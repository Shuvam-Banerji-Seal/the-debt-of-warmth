Days 246–248 · Smoke 7–9, Y.H. 813 · Low Kiln, Sill

Smoke 7, Seventhday, Y.H. 813

The hand lay against the fold where it had come to rest on the river’s next low note, and the boy let the coordination go the way a surgeon lets an instrument go at the end of a case — not dropped, not put away with ceremony, but set down where the next hand will find it without looking. It had not been a reach. It had not been a hearing. It was the sixty-sixth answer on the sixth place, the last of two rhythms crossed in one inch of air across a wall that does not touch; now the inch was finished and the hand was a hand again, small, soft-nailed, outside the wool, on the bed at the low angle of the loaf shelf, with the fold at his ear and one crust lying two inches from it. He had once reduced a shoulder in the dark by feel alone, and now he could not lift that crust two inches to his mouth. Both were true. Neither stood up to call itself a sign, and the surgeon took the inventory of both and entered it nowhere.

The file lay open on the towel where he had left it, exact, complete and worth nothing. There was no towel, no instruments and no case, only the order of the laying, sharp by sharp and blunt by blunt, every edge where a hand would look for it first. Six hearings stood beside the full note and never among, with no seventh and none invented to fill the empty place, for a finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged. Three near-full answers stood advanced and unpaid. Three shapings of the pot saying stood as sayings and no fourth likeness had been made a cause, because a likeness made a cause wants a payer and none stood to any. The fourth burned-slate telling stood on its own place and the two tellings of the room shut in the war stood at two and did not become three. The told bell lay beside the heard, the told figure beside the paid. Nine stoppings stood with no tenth before the seventh year, which stood at its very end and had still not given. The discipline was the whole of it, and a discipline is a thing a hand uses, not a thing a man believes.

He was two hundred and forty-five days old and six months and sixty-five days, and nobody in Sarn, Sill or Grath said either number in his hearing or over his head. The count had come down out of the dark exact and complete and unspent, and it went forward now into two hundred and forty-six, six months and sixty-six days, with no hand to enter it and no voice to say so. He had stopped expecting to be told, which is not the same as not wanting it, and the wanting went on under the expectation the way a coal goes on under ash. Smoke went on the way a firing goes, without a stroke drawn across it, neither of the district’s two names for the months set right or left wrong in the telling. There was no market on a Seventhday, no Cross and no bell. Smoke 10 (Day 249), the first Account-day and market of the second firing, stood three mornings ahead and had not been brought into a day that had not earned it. A day not yet earned keeps its own counsel, and the yard kept it.

Hot clay came first under the Vail kiln-house roof in Low Kiln, hot clay under tallow and wet wool under that, and under all three the sour Ash breath of the unswept corner the house called the thaw. The day’s first sound was not a bell. Behind the wall the kiln drew in four low pulls and stopped where the fifth should have stood, and the stopping stood where it stood with nobody under any roof to put a name to what stood in it. He took the smells in the order they arrived and the sound in the order it did not, the way he had once taken a room in the dark: what was under the hand first, what was behind it second, and what the ear wanted to hear last. A man who had learned to find the bleeder before the bleed knew that the ear lies and the hand does not, and he had learned it here again in a house where the ear was told nothing and the hand was given everything it could not use.

One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from the ear. The empty place beside it kept its name to itself, and no third came to lie down and make the row come out even, because a kindness with a figure on it is the beginning of an account and this house had learned to give without entering. The thread of drool at his mouth had frozen in the dry heat and was unwiped, and would stay unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. Cold had stopped at cheek, temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, as though it had come to the end of a line written out for it and had neither an order to go further nor an order to go away. It went no further and it did not go away. The afterdrop waited beyond all three, past a warming nobody in this house would perform. He knew the severe face cold wears is a quiet face, knew a cold patient cannot clot, knew a man must be warmed from the outside and slowly. He knew all of it and wanted the crust. Both were true and the second was louder, because a child’s hunger keeps no hours at all while the register goes on being exact inside a body that goes on being hungry.

Through the finger’s-width door the river kept its note of water over water, a fifth low thing never counted among the hearings. Behind the floor the carried fire answered four low pulls with the silence where the fifth should have been, and its payer had been without a name for two hundred and forty-six days. Overhead the boards kept four nails on the near board, four on the next, and a thumb-width knot where the fifth nail should have stood. Fire under the floor, river through the door, nails overhead: three counts in three places, and none rose into another. A man who wanted them to agree would have to put a payer under every one, and no payer stood to any, so the boy left them on three trays and the trays lay where they lay. Three instruments that will not read against one another are still three instruments, and an instrument is a thing a hand uses, not a thing a man believes.
