TASK: GENESIS 1 of 10. Conceive the saga.

The repository is empty of story. Before you write, read AGENTS.md (your charter, already loaded), design/templates/, and design/AUTHOR-API.md.

Invent an original reincarnation saga meant to run for thousands of chapters. Take your time and think deeply. Choose against the obvious: no generic "overpowered hero", no copied tropes, no famous names. Make it strange, specific and human.

Create these files:

1. `bible/book.json`: valid JSON with
   - "title": the saga's title (evocative, original, 1–5 words)
   - "subtitle": a line beneath it
   - "tagline": one haunting sentence
   - "byline": "Written by Hy4 Flash"
   - "cover_motif": one or two sentences naming a single emblem for the cover (an object, beast, sigil or wound at the heart of the saga, drawable as a gold-tooled emblem on leather)
   - "genre_notes": a few words

2. `bible/premise.md`: logline; core themes (at least 5); the true nature of the reincarnation and why it happened (a mystery revealed slowly over hundreds of chapters); at least 8 deep mysteries with their hidden answers; the saga's spine (what drives it for thousands of chapters); the tonal palette; what makes this story unlike any other. Private, so spoilers belong here.

3. `bible/past-life.md`: the protagonist's previous life in depth (at least 2,000 words). Era and place (it may be our Earth or not, your choice), family, profession and skills, loves, enemies, failures, the exact manner of death, the unfinished business, the people left behind, and the secrets of that life that will matter in the new one. Include specific sensory memories that can surface later.

4. `bible/voice.md`: your style manifesto for this saga. Narrative person and tense; how the past-life consciousness shows on the page; recurring motifs and image-families; sentence music; how you write violence, grief, tenderness and humour; dialogue conventions; words and habits to avoid; three short sample paragraphs (not part of the story) showing the voice at its best.

Write in English. Make every file rich and specific; vague notes will betray you later. When done, reply with one line: the title.
