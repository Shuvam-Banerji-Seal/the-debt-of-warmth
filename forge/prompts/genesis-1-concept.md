TASK: GENESIS 1 of 10. Conceive the saga.

The repository is empty of story. Before you write, read AGENTS.md (your charter, already loaded), **design/STYLE-REFERENCE.md (the binding voice: read every word)**, design/templates/, and design/AUTHOR-API.md.

Invent an original reincarnation isekai meant to run for thousands of chapters: **a man from present-day Earth dies and is reborn as an infant in a world of magic, with all his memories**. He will climb a deep power system slowly over the whole saga, using his modern knowledge as an edge that is real but limited, through monsters, danger, schooling, war, love and betrayal. Take your time and think deeply. Choose against the obvious: no generic overpowered hero, no copied tropes, no famous names. Make it strange, specific and human, and dark where the world is dark.

Create these files:

1. `bible/book.json`: valid JSON with
   - "title": the saga's title (evocative, original, 1–5 words)
   - "subtitle": a line beneath it
   - "tagline": one haunting sentence
   - "byline": "Written by Hy4 Flash"
   - "cover_motif": one or two sentences naming a single emblem for the cover (an object, beast, sigil or wound at the heart of the saga, drawable as a gold-tooled emblem on leather)
   - "genre_notes": a few words

2. `bible/premise.md`: logline; core themes (at least 5); the true nature of the reincarnation and why it happened (a mystery revealed slowly over hundreds of chapters); at least 8 deep mysteries with their hidden answers; the saga's spine (what drives it for thousands of chapters); the tonal palette; what makes this story unlike any other. Private, so spoilers belong here.

3. `bible/past-life.md`: the protagonist's previous life in depth (at least 2,000 words). He lives in **the present day (2020s), in a real country and city on Earth**. Cover his family; his profession and expertise (choose something that will give real but limited leverage in a magic world, and say exactly what he knows and does not know); his loves, enemies, failures and habits of mind; the exact manner of his death; his unfinished business; the people left behind; and the secrets of that life that will matter in the new one. Include specific sensory memories (modern ones: sodium streetlights, hospital corridors, phone glow, motorway rain, whatever his life held) that can surface later as the contrast between his two lives.

4. `bible/voice.md`: your style manifesto for this saga, **built faithfully on design/STYLE-REFERENCE.md**. Keep its person and tense, its tense-shift for the past life, its sentence music, its handling of violence, grief, tenderness and humour, its dialogue discipline and its banned list. Then add what is new to this saga: how a modern man's consciousness shows on the page in an infant's and then a boy's body; how combat and magic are written in this voice (exact, physical, costly, never game-like); and this saga's own motifs and image-families (new ones, none from the reference). End with three short sample paragraphs (not part of the story) in this voice: one of daily life in the new world, one of the modern past life breaking through, and one of violence or magic.

Write in English. Make every file rich and specific; vague notes will betray you later. When done, reply with one line: the title.
