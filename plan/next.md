The clay-heavy rise has come up in the night and taken the woman who stood at the end of nineteen steps. The boy, thirty-eight days old, is held on the bank in the basket with the fold at his chin and cannot speak, cannot move, cannot look away. He hears the small flat knock of a body cracked all the way through --- the **sixth hearing and the second from a person**, the payoff of the plant from Stone 25 (T061, T087) and the first edge of T008/T073. 2. **Point of view stays with Dan.** No Court scene, no inside-the-collection scene, not yet. The first such departure is far off.
3. **Present tense only for Chicago breaking through**, one or two sentences at a time, never for anticipation. Day 38 pays the held hand-on-basket detail in past tense and untagged, as in Chapter 9. Do not make the present-tense break a habit, and never tag a `\memory{}` to it.

6. **The body humiliates him** in every chapter: drool, the useless head, the fold, the hand that cannot reach the cup or lift to its own chin. At the bank on Day 38 he cannot move, speak or look away. Play it for exactness, with the dry joke living in it, never for easy pathos.

Mira's short eels and Berr's short marks are the same arithmetic at a different stall.
