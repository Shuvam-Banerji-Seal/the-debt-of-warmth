## Where Chapter 113 left the hand and the house

Small hours of Day 392's very end, the Long Night, Y.H. 813, under the Vail kiln-house roof at the low angle of the loaf shelf. The boy, three hundred and ninety-two days old and six months and two hundred and twelve days, told by nobody, lay on his bed and not in the Debt Loft; one stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from his ear with its corner a hair off the wool, and beside it the wool Sella put into the empty place on the evening of Stone 15 lay folded once, found where put on the mornings and goings of Kindling, Weighing and the Long Night and changed by nobody. **The keeping was not made a habit, a way, a sign, a rule, a gift, a payment or a payer**: each finding from a hand that only read adds no placing; a second placing would make a habit and a third a way, and both want a payer where none stands. The far-off finger that will one day lay shelf and fold together and call the joining an account is still far off, not his, not come. The thread of drool at his mouth lay frozen in the dry kiln heat and unwiped, because nobody under any roof has ever been given an instruction about the thread; cold stopped at cheek, temple and the bare wrist-joint where the wool rode back, as at the end of a line written out for it, and the afterdrop waited past a warming nobody here will perform. The surgeon's knowing and the infant's want of the crust lifted, the thread wiped and a hand on the pot handle were both true, the second louder, neither winning, the quarrel the work. The hand came up an inch toward the river's note under the finger's-width door, took a small intake, held the interval whole on the note's own finishing, let it go as the note ended and laid itself flat against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. Answer **360** was filed exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer. Count **392 exact**; **360 honest answers** on the sixth place; the carried fire's fifth unpaid and Garl's name unspoken the same number. There was no fire after dusk. The year's fire had come up the Clay Yard in a clay dish from hearth to hearth from a source nobody under the roof names or prices; the Kindling collector's ordinary writing hand with no pause and his belief that he keeps the world alive by taking what is already going, the clean voice's missing name and the Long Night story's empty name all sat on trays of their own; the told bell was kept four, four and a gap in its square beside the bell he had heard; six hearings stood beside and never among with the seventh place empty and the Drawing's flat knock outside; nine record-first hands kept no tenth and nine stoppings kept no tenth; the four rooms with the same gap were held apart; the widow's mark was untouched; Tarn's seventh year had ended with the loan uncalled and the Seed 16 breath unspent; Jorra's promise was neither given nor withdrawn. One present-tense break in eighty-eight chapters had opened the seam on the clean voice's form — a page over the speakers, a white room that does not look up, hands staying in the field — untagged, paying nothing.
