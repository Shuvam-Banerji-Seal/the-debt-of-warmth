3. **Present tense only for Chicago breaking through**, one or two sentences at a time, never for anticipation and never more than once in a chapter. 4. **`\memory{...}` for remembered words only.** Chapters 4–25 tagged none; let the market voice and the hospital stand on their own until a remembered line earns the tag.
5. **No recap, no exposition speech, no modern simile in Sarn.** A face is not like a screen here. It may be like one in Chicago, where the screen is part of the texture. A fist of seed may be read like the surgeon's ninety-second sorting; that is a comparison inside his own head, never a spoken speech to a neighbour.
6. **The body humiliates him** in every chapter: drool, the useless head, the fold, the hand that cannot reach the cup or lift to its own chin, the frozen thread at the mouth nobody wipes. Play it for exactness, with the dry joke living in it, never for easy pathos.
