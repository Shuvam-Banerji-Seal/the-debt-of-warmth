## Continuity rules for these chapters

1. **Days 132 onward only**, in order, with no gaps and no day poured into another: Ch 31 = 132–134 (Reed 13–15), Ch 32 = 135–137 (Reed 16–18), Ch 33 = 138–140 (Reed 19–21, Reed 20 an Account-day), Ch 34 = 141–143 (Reed 22–24), Ch 35 = 144–146 (Reed 25–27). 2. **Point of view stays with Dan.** No Court scene, no inside-the-collection scene, not yet. The first such departure is far off.
3. **Present tense only for Chicago breaking through**, one or two sentences at a time, never for anticipation and never more than once in a chapter. Chapter 30 paid none and wrote no `\memory{}` tag; let the surgeon's register carry the old life until a remembered line earns a break. Do not tag a `\memory{}` to it.
4. **`\memory{...}` for remembered words only.** Chapters 4–30 tagged none; let the market voice and the hospital stand on their own until a remembered line earns the tag.
5. **No recap, no exposition speech, no modern simile in Sarn.** A face is not like a screen here. It may be like one in Chicago, where the screen is part of the texture. An arm carrying a bundle may be read like the surgeon's ninety-second sorting; that is a comparison inside his own head, never a spoken speech to a neighbour.
6. **The body humiliates him** in every chapter: drool, the useless head, the fold, the hand that cannot reach the cup or lift to its own chin, the frozen thread at the mouth nobody wipes, cold at cheek--temple--wrist with the wool ridden back. Play it for exactness, with the dry joke living in it, never for easy pathos.
7. **Every cost has a payer.** The carried fire, the stone, the gruel, the eels and the crusts came from somebody's fire and were entered nowhere; Sella paid twice and has taken a measure from the district.
