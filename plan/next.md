## Chapter 6 — Counting What There Is

The boy counts the stack four, four, a place where the fifth should be, and files it beside the bench and the interval. Nobody has said a word to the basket. Do not explain the loan that can hear, the debt, the mark or the missing note.

## Chapter 7 — The Number They Give Out

## Chapter 8 — The Five Cold Days

**Days covered.** Days 25–30 --- Kindling, Weighing, the Long Night, the Remembering and the Last Day (Days 25–29, Y.H. 812), then Ilmen 1, Y.H. 813 (Day 30, Firstday, the Opening of the Slates). Give **each of the five holy days its own dated entry of at least a paragraph**, and Day 30 its own entry too. No day is poured into another, and no childhood is summarised. Check `bible/calendar.md` before writing; the Five have names, not tend-days, and no market is held during them.

**Dated spine, one entry each.**
- **Day 25, Kindling.** Every hearth in Sill lit from the Hearth-flame. The boy watching a fire come into the house from somewhere he cannot see and cannot reach; the count extended to the light it makes. Sella touches the widow's mark before the kiln and at no other time.
- **Day 26, Weighing.** The year's measures read aloud in a clean voice. Somebody else's whole return announced for somebody who is not in the room; the grammar Berr used at the Cross now wearing a temple's clothes (T047 advance). The boy hears a figure and says nothing. Plant the reading of a name the household will not speak.
- **Day 27, the Long Night.** No fire after dusk. Stories of the Return told in the dark; the boy listens to the voices instead of the flames and counts the gaps between them. The bell's four-four-and-a-gap comes back in the telling of the year.
- **Day 28, the Remembering.** Names of the dead spoken. Garl's name is not spoken in the Vail house; the coat stays on the peg and the ridge stays unswept. The boy counts the silence where the name should be and files it with the other gaps.
- **Day 29, the Last Day.** Bread baked; the grey Ash in the corners left where it lies to be burned next morning and called dealt with. Sella will not let anyone sweep early. The boy watches the ridge measured by a thumb's width and understands that a thing can be left in place and still be accounted for.
- **Day 30, Ilmen 1, Y.H. 813.** The new year opens with the banked coals still banked and the Ash burned and called gone. The count goes on into a year the district has already entered on its slates. Forward motion into the thaw and the work of the slip pits, with the stone still unmentioned in House Silt and the figure still one in twelve.

**Characters on stage.** Sella Vail, Tarn, Jorra Vail, Alda Reed (in the Remembering and the second notes), Dessa Silt (the cough worse, still called weather), Mira Silt (a crust, briskly), the household and market voices. **Threads advanced.** T077 (the number the district gives out, now the year's measure), T013 (the Ash left and burned and called dealt with), T009 (what was left where it lies), T031 (the charred slate still in the kiln ash, untouched), T071/T072 (the count carried into a new year), T060 (love as maintenance through the Five), T073 (the name not spoken in the Remembering), T036 (Tarn's term, one year now gone), T081 (the Weighing Hall planted as another room with a bench, a draught and a gap --- not recognised as such yet), T082 (the gaps in the stories counted like intervals).

## Chapter 9 — The River Under the Ice

**Days covered.** Days 31–44 --- Ilmen 2 to Ilmen 15, Y.H. 813, Secondday to Fifthday. A passage chapter: **every day gets its own dated entry of at least a paragraph**, fourteen entries, no day poured into another. The Fish Blessing falls on Day 44. Dessa dies on Day 38 and stays dead.

**Dated spine, one entry each.**
- **Day 31, Ilmen 2, Secondday.** The thaw begins in the little things: water in the \place{Slip Brook}, oiled paper that no longer lifts quite so hard. Mira touches a cord when a number is spoken at the yard. The boy is a month old tomorrow and still carried everywhere.
- **Day 32, Ilmen 3, Thirdday.** Tarn has one year of the seven left and will not count it; he agrees with everything so thoroughly that there is nowhere for an argument to go. The boy's hand outside the fold goes up an inch toward the broom and comes down.
- **Day 33, Ilmen 4, Fourthday.** Jorra comes in from the back room with slate dust and says nothing. The men whose names are not spoken are not named; the Broken Slates are planted toward Day ~160 without being called by that name. Tarn's ear is not spoken of.
- **Day 34, Ilmen 5, Fifthday.** Alda touches the tin once and writes what she turns down; a third hidden note goes with the other two, still unread. The exact dates come faster than the question needs them.
- **Day 35, Ilmen 6, Sixthday.** A pot in the yard answers nearly full under nobody's knuckle and the boy hears the difference between nearly and full. No power used, no rank change, no hand to tap it. This is the first earthly answer to the interval he filed on Stone 13 (T082 advanced, not paid).
- **Day 36, Ilmen 7, Seventhday.** The ice on the Ilm shows a hairline crack. Somebody comes back from looking at it with a number they do not care to give (T077, now a habit of the district rather than a mystery to be explained).
- **Day 37, Ilmen 8, Eighthday.** Dessa's cough is worse and is still weather. Her thumb goes to the sieve-wire scar, the nineteen steps go into the thumb, the door-shut smile comes down. The boy cannot name what he sees in her face.
- **Day 38, Ilmen 9, Ninthday.** Dessa drowns in the clay-heavy rise of the Slip Brook, uncollected. The boy, thirty-eight days old, is held on the bank and hears the small flat knock of a body cracked all the way through --- the sixth hearing and the second from a person. The collector's clerk is at the Fishstairs; Alda arrives after the vessel has given. He cannot speak, cannot move, cannot look away. He will remember the exact shape of her hand on the basket handle. This is his first intimate encounter with the Waste and it is not explained to him. Do not let him understand what the sound was; he only files it.
- **Day 39, Ilmen 10, Tenthday.** Account-day at the Cross. The market goes on; the boy is carried through it. No recap of the figure, no new joining of the pauses. The count goes on because there is nothing else that is his.
- **Day 40, Ilmen 11, Firstday.** Sella does not send for a collector for the woman in the brook, and nobody remarks on it. The Vail ridge is measured against another house's by the width of a thumb and left where it lies. Plant the first heresy toward ~150: warmth left where it is, and the world does not end.
- **Day 41, Ilmen 12, Secondday.** Mira gives the boy a crust briskly, before anyone can make a speech of it, and touches a knot when a figure is spoken. The separate cord for the thin child in Nine Pots is glimpsed again (T059).
- **Day 42, Ilmen 13, Thirdday.** A tin above Dessa's bed holds a scrap of cloth from the birth; the boy notices the object without knowing what it is and does not enter it anywhere. Sella's private roll remains unshown.
- **Day 43, Ilmen 14, Fourthday.** The Weir is prepared for the Fish Blessing. Orra Weir's cords are in the Eel House; Fenna Clay is glimpsed again across the market with green at her mouth and no dialogue. Alda and Orra do not discuss the third day.
- **Day 44, Ilmen 15, Fifthday.** The Fish Blessing at the Weir. Orra Weir ties a forty-first knot she cannot account for, says the word *forty* within the boy's hearing and watches what he does with it. He is too young to answer. He does not forget the shape of the cord. Payoff of the withheld number; opens T024 toward ~300 and ~1400.

## Continuity rules for these five chapters

1. **Days 10–47 only**, in order, with no gaps: Ch 6 = 10–14, Ch 7 = 15–24 with ten dated entries, Ch 8 = 25–30 with six dated entries, Ch 9 = 31–44 with fourteen dated entries, Ch 10 = 45–47. Day 9 is finished and must not be revisited as a new scene. Check `bible/calendar.md` for every tend-day: Day 20 is Stone 26 and is not Account-day; Day 42 is Ilmen 13, Thirdday; Day 44 is Ilmen 15, Fifthday. No day is skipped and no childhood is summarised.
2. **Point of view stays with Dan.** No Court scene, no inside-the-collection scene, not yet. The first such departure is far off.
3. **Present tense only for Chicago breaking through**, one or two sentences at a time, never for anticipation. Ch 5 used it for a cafeteria and an eaten wrapper; do not make it a habit, and never tag a `\memory{}` to it.
4. **`\memory{...}` for remembered words only** --- Ostrowski's two duties in Ch 3, a half-line of a call. Chapter 5 tagged none; let the market voice and the cafeteria stand on their own until a remembered line earns the tag.
5. **No recap, no exposition speech, no modern simile in Sarn.** A face is not like a screen here. It may be like one in Chicago, where the screen is part of the texture.
6. **The body humiliates him** in every chapter: drool, the useless head, the fold, the hand that cannot reach the cup or lift to its own chin. Play it for exactness, with the dry joke living in it, never for easy pathos.
7. **Every cost has a payer.** The stone, the gruel, the eels and the crust came from somebody's fire and were entered nowhere; Sella paid twice and has taken a measure from the district. Mira's short eels and Berr's short marks are the same arithmetic at a different stall. When somebody pays in Chapters 6–10, let the reader be able to name who. Dessa's death is a taking nobody enters and the boy cannot refuse.
8. **The two hands stay separate in his mind.** He has set the pause at the flare beside the pause on the birth-slate and the pause on the clerk's slate, and has not joined them. Ch 6 gives a fourth sighting, Ch 7 a fifth; do not do the joining for him. The Drawing collector's neat hand has no pause at all and stays in its own place. It is paid off in Slate Row years away.
9. **The ear keeps its objects in order.** Cup (Ch 2), pot (Ch 2), Vail pot through the wall (Ch 4), dying man at the Drawing (Ch 4), district's slate twice (Ch 3), the district's slate again (Ch 6 and Ch 7), a pot cracking in the yard (Ch 7), a nearly full pot (Ch 9), Dessa on the bank (Ch 9). Five hearings from four sources stand at the close of Ch 5; Stone 13 added an interval, not a hearing. Tarn hears some of them and says nothing; he has now seen the boy's ear and kept that too. Each is useless and none is named in Grathic.
10. **When a chapter closes, update `memory/state.md`, add one line per day to `memory/timeline.md`, append to `memory/story-so-far.md`, give every character who appeared a Chapter Log line, move planted threads from `open` to `planted` in `plan/threads.md`, and add any new coined terms to `bible/glossary.md`.
