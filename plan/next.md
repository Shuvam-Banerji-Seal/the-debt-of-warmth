## Continuity rules for these chapters

1. **Days 55 onward only**, in order, with no gaps and no day poured into another: Ch 13 = 55–60 (six dated entries), Ch 14 = 61–67 (seven), Ch 15 = 68–74 (seven), Ch 16 = 75–77 (three), Ch 17 = 78–84 (seven). Day 54 is finished and must not be revisited as a new scene. Check `bible/calendar.md` for every tend-day: Day 59 is Account-day, Day 60 is Silt 1, Day 69 is Silt 10 and Account-day, Day 74 is Silt 15, Day 75 is Silt 16, Day 79 is Silt 20 and Account-day, Day 90 is Seed 1. A passage chapter never exceeds seven lived days.
2. **Point of view stays with Dan.** No Court scene, no inside-the-collection scene, not yet. The first such departure is far off.
3. **Present tense only for Chicago breaking through**, one or two sentences at a time, never for anticipation and never more than once in a chapter. The old life was paid in past tense and untagged on Day 38 and again in past tense on Days 48–54; do not make a habit of it, and never tag a `\memory{}` to it.
4. **`\memory{...}` for remembered words only.** Chapters 4–12 tagged none; let the market voice and the hospital stand on their own until a remembered line earns the tag.
5. **No recap, no exposition speech, no modern simile in Sarn.** A face is not like a screen here. It may be like one in Chicago, where the screen is part of the texture.
6. **The body humiliates him** in every chapter: drool, the useless head, the fold, the hand that cannot reach the cup or lift to its own chin. Play it for exactness, with the dry joke living in it, never for easy pathos.
7. **Every cost has a payer.** The carried fire, the stone, the gruel, the eels and the crusts came from somebody's fire and were entered nowhere; Sella paid twice and has taken a measure from the district. Mira's short eels and Berr's short marks are the same arithmetic at a different stall. Dessa's death is a taking nobody enters and the boy cannot refuse. The coal the gull took and the crust that went out of the fold are two more subtractions entered in no account, and the boy keeps both.
8. **The hands stay separate in his mind.** The five pauses in the neat dark hand must not be joined; the Drawing collector's hand has no pause and stays in its own place; the officer's clean voice is a third thing filed apart; the twice-wiped thumb of Berr stays on its own tray. The clerk at the Fishstairs and the clerk at the Court's table are two more record-first hands on separate trays (T080, T090), not Marra and not joined to one another. Sella's, Alda's and Mira's hands sit on a fourth tray as possible takers of the crust and must not become a cause (T091).
9. **The ear keeps its objects in order.** Six hearings from four sources plus the sixth from a person on the Slip Brook; three intervals (Stone 13, Stone 17, Stone 25), the told bell beside the heard bell, and TWO nearly-full answers (Ilmen 6 and Ilmen 23) filed as answers to the Stone 13 interval on their own tray, advanced and not paid. The word *forty* is a saying on a tray of its own; the forty-first knot is a shape legible without its name, and the weight of a thumb is apart from both. Every one is useless and none is named in Grathic. No seventh hearing in Chapters 13–17.
10. **The crusts are accounted for.** One spent crust from Stone 15 remains stiff in the fold; the smoky crust from Stone 28 went out of it on Day 46 and nobody said who took it, that it is gone, or what became of it. No third crust. Do not solve it; the reader must be able to suspect every woman in the yard, including the girl who put the crust there. The taking is a keeping that no slate will enter, and the boy carries the empty place as he carried the gift.
11. **Dessa stays dead.** No recognition of the mother, no name from her, no number from her thumb, no one in three, no Marra, no explanation of the Forty, no visit to a bank turned into a quest, no ending. Her tin is seen and not opened; the lie told for mercy stands as a fact nobody can correct. She has been dead sixteen mornings at the close of Chapter 12 and the count keeps rising.
12. **When a chapter closes, update `memory/state.md`, add one line per day to `memory/timeline.md`, append to `memory/story-so-far.md`, give every character who appeared a Chapter Log line, move planted threads from `open` to `planted` in `plan/threads.md`, and add any new coined terms to `bible/glossary.md`.
