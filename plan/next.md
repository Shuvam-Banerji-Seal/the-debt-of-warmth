## Where Chapter 110 left the hand and the house

Small hours of Day 383's very end, Stone 24, Fourthday, under the Vail kiln-house roof at the low angle of the loaf shelf. The boy, three hundred and eighty-three days old and six months and two hundred and three days, told by nobody, lay on his bed and not in the Debt Loft; one stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from his ear with its corner a hair off the wool, and beside it the wool Sella put into the empty place on the evening of Stone 15 lay folded once, found where put on the seventh, eighth and ninth mornings and at the seventh, eighth and ninth goings of the light and changed by nobody. No second fold, no third crust, no name spoken over the place. **The keeping was not made a habit, a way, a sign, a rule, a gift, a payment or a payer**: nine findings from a hand that only read add no ninth placing; a second placing would make a habit and a third a way, and both want a payer where none stands. He filed it on a place of its own beside the crust's gap, the shelf's two gaps, the gone pan's round ring and the field's empty place, gathered once and gathering interest toward the far-off finger that will one day lay shelf and fold together and call the joining an account — that hand is not his and has not come. The thread of drool at his mouth lay frozen in the dry kiln heat and unwiped, because nobody under any roof has ever been given an instruction about the thread; cold stopped at cheek, temple and the bare wrist-joint where the wool rode back, as at the end of a line written out for it, and the afterdrop waited past a warming nobody here will perform. The surgeon's knowing and the infant's want of the crust lifted, the thread wiped and a hand on the pot handle were both true, the second louder, neither winning, the quarrel the work. Outside the wool the hand came up an inch toward the river's note under the finger's-width door, took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole on the note's own finishing under the winter skin, let it go as the note ended and laid itself flat against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. Answer **342** was filed: exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the eleven readings, the bell's gap, the flat knock, the covered grain, the held breath, the two bites of iron, the river's skin, the widow's mark, the fourth direction nor the keeping at the fold. **Eleven arithmetics of the year — the three of Frost, the first, second and third of Bone, Ash's first, second and third, Stone's first and Stone's second — stand behind one unsigned figure**; the figure read is still not the figure paid, no signature stands on the space and no doctrine comes out of eleven.
