## Continuity rules for these chapters

1. **Days 78 onward only**, in order, with no gaps and no day poured into another: Ch 17 = 78–84 (seven dated entries), Ch 18 = 85–90 (six), Ch 19 = 91–93 (three), Ch 20 = 94–100 (seven). Day 77 is finished and must not be revisited as a new scene. A passage chapter never exceeds seven lived days.
2. **Point of view stays with Dan.** No Court scene, no inside-the-collection scene, not yet. The first such departure is far off.
3. **Present tense only for Chicago breaking through**, one or two sentences at a time, never for anticipation and never more than once in a chapter. Chapter 16 paid none and wrote no `\memory{}` tag; let the surgeon's register carry the old life until a remembered line earns a break. Do not tag a `\memory{}` to it.
4. **`\memory{...}` for remembered words only.** Chapters 4–16 tagged none; let the market voice and the hospital stand on their own until a remembered line earns the tag.
5. **No recap, no exposition speech, no modern simile in Sarn.** A face is not like a screen here. It may be like one in Chicago, where the screen is part of the texture.
6. **The body humiliates him** in every chapter: drool, the useless head, the fold, the hand that cannot reach the cup or lift to its own chin, the frozen thread at the mouth nobody wipes. Play it for exactness, with the dry joke living in it, never for easy pathos.
7. **Every cost has a payer.** The carried fire, the stone, the gruel, the eels and the crusts came from somebody's fire and were entered nowhere; Sella paid twice and has taken a measure from the district. Mira's short eels and Berr's short marks are the same arithmetic at a different stall. Dessa's death is a taking nobody enters and the boy cannot refuse.
