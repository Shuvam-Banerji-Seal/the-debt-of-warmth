2. **Point of view stays with Dan.** No Court scene, no inside-the-collection scene, not yet. The first such departure is far off.
3. **Present tense only for Chicago breaking through**, one or two sentences at a time, never for anticipation. Ch 7 used it for flare light at the market's edge; do not make it a habit, and never tag a \memory{} to it.
