## Chapter 43 — the turn into Ember

**Days covered.** Days 180–182, Ember 1–3, Firstday to Thirdday. The calendar names Ember the kiln month; the district keeps calling Tallow the firing month through these chapters, so keep the scenes' own usage and never correct it in narration.

Do not recap the close of Chapter 42. The first smell is hot clay under tallow and wet wool; the first sound is the kiln's breath in fours with a gap, not a bell — the firing month is named for what the kilns did, and the kilns go on breathing the same way into a month named for what they are. Under the floor the carried fire answers four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have been, its payer unnamed for a hundred and eighty days; through the finger's-width door the river keeps its note of water over water, a fifth low thing never counted among the hearings; overhead the boards keep four nails, four, and a knot the width of a thumb — three counts in three places that do not become one. The crust lies stiff and leathery two inches from his ear with its empty place unnamed and no third; the thread of drool is frozen and unwiped; his hand outside the wool goes up an inch toward the note of water and comes down against the fold. Sella gives the instruction to the wool, the fire, the pot, the broom and the running water and never to the basket, says *the house*, keeps her half-pace, and leaves the widow's mark where it hung, because a morning and a shelf are not openings. The yard goes around the clean dark line, the finer grey called the thaw, the rack space with its broken birth-slates and the empty place in the returning line where a hand went in on Seed 15, with a look and a wide step and no number. Tarn takes the broom only after it is instructed; six counted where the house can check, the seventh held where it cannot, nine stoppings still nine objects with no tenth, the Seed 16 breath unspent and the loan uncalled. The three held changes stay on three separate places and are not repeated or joined; the Day 166 beat beneath the shelf stays where it was left and the charcoal remains unread.
