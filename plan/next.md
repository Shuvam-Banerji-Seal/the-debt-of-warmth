## Chapter 44 — the second working week of Ember

**Days covered.** Days 183–185, Ember 4–6, Fourthday to Sixthday. The month settles into its working week.

**Opening moment of Chapter 44.** Open on Day 183, Ember 4, Fourthday, before first grey under the Vail kiln-house roof, with the boy one hundred and eighty-three days old and six months and three days, and nobody under any roof saying so. Do not recap the close of Chapter 43. The first smell is hot clay under tallow and wet wool with the sour mineral breath of the unswept corner; the first sound is the kiln drawing breath in fours with a gap, not a bell. Under the floor the carried fire answers four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have been, its payer unnamed for a hundred and eighty-three days; through the finger's-width door the river keeps its note of water over water, a fifth low thing never counted among the hearings and never invented to stand up as a seventh; overhead the boards keep four nails, four, and a knot the width of a thumb — three counts in three places that do not become one. The crust lies stiff and leathery two inches from his ear with its empty place unnamed and no third, the thread of drool frozen and unwiped, his hand outside the wool going up an inch toward the note of water and coming down against the fold. Sella gives the instruction to the wool, the fire, the pot, the broom and the running water and never to the basket, says *the house*, keeps her half-pace, glances once at the clay jar cold under its glaze without touching it, and leaves the widow's mark where it hung, because a morning and a shelf are not openings and the one opening was before the kiln on Ember 1. The yard goes around the clean dark line, the finer grey called the thaw, the rack space with its broken birth-slates, the pan's gone place, the bare dark trough whose water is called weather and the empty place in the returning line where a hand went in on Seed 15, with a look and a wide step and no number. Tarn takes the broom only after it is instructed; six counted where the house can check and the seventh held where it cannot, nine stoppings still nine objects with no tenth, the Seed 16 breath unspent and the loan uncalled. The three held changes stay on three separate places and are not repeated or joined; the Day 166 beat beneath the shelf stays where it was left and the charcoal waits unread for a hand that is not his and a day that has not come. His hand comes down against the fold with one hundred and eighty-three exact, complete and unspent.

**Intentions.** Keep the count exact, the fire's payer unnamed, the one opening behind the household, the absences on their separate trays and the old life offstage. Let the week's work be ordinary and let the danger stay gathered without an edge being paid. Forward, not closed.
