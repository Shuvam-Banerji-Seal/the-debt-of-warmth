## Chapter 11 — After the Blessing

**Days covered.** Days 45–47 — Ilmen 16 to Ilmen 18, Y.H. 813, Sixthday to Eighthday. A normal chapter of three lived days. Days 48 onward belong to Chapter 12.

**Opening moment.** Open on Day 45, Ilmen 16, Sixthday, at the Fishstairs after the Blessing, not on a thought and not on a recap. The forty-first knot is already tied and nobody can say what it counts. The boy is carried below the north bridge at dawn; a brine-gull goes off with a coal not yet cold, and the eel-bones move from hand to hand while Mira decides whether to lie. Do not explain the knot, do not speak the number again, do not let the boy answer Orra's watching. Forward, not closed.

**Working scenes.**
- **Scene 1 (Day 45).** Orra's cord as an object the boy can now read in shape if not in name; Mira's grey cord and the separate cord twisted twice under it still unjoined in his private file. The clerk set at the Fishstairs for the uncalled measure is at the steps with his cuff and slate; the six hearings stay six and the pauses stay five. Orra watches him watching and says nothing further. The boy's hand outside the wool goes up an inch and comes down.
- **Scene 2 (Day 46).** Back under the Vail roof. Sella goes around the days to Kindling without a figure; the household's stores are thinner than they were and nobody says so. Tarn's seventh year is closer and he will not count it; his broom still goes over the road to the Fishstairs without turning, and no collector goes down it. Jorra's forearms carry bone-coloured slip and the back room is not mentioned. **By now one of the two crusts is gone --- eaten by nobody, given to the fire or the eels, never spoken of --- and neither woman says who took it; this is the first edge of T086's payoff.** Record the decision in continuity and do not solve who took it; the reader must be able to suspect every woman in the yard, including the girl who once put the crust there.
- **Scene 3 (Day 47).** At the yard well, a frost-bloom where the Warm has lately left and talk of the ice going soft before its time. A number goes around and the boy cannot spend it. The weak board in the plank walk at the Weir is heard of but not yet trodden (T083). Close on the empty place in the fold where a crust was, and on the count four, four and a gap carried into a year the district has already entered.

**Characters on stage.** Sella Vail, Tarn, Jorra Vail, Orra Weir, Mira Silt, Alda Reed (short), Berr Clay (short), market and Fishstairs voices, the clerk set for the uncalled measure (walk-on, not Marra). Dessa is dead and stays dead; no visit to a bank becomes a quest; Fenna remains unnamed and offstage.

**Threads advanced.** T024 (the forty-first knot held unread, the long vigil begun), T025/T059 (two cords held side by side), T077 and T090 (the number that goes around, the uncalled measure at the steps), T061/T082 (six hearings, no seventh; the nearly-full note still on its own tray), T013/T009 (the clean dark line left where it lies; the heresy planted but not performed), T071/T072 (the private count carried forward), T083 (the weak board held against a river beginning to stir), T081 (the Weir as another room, still not recognised), T086 (one crust gone, unspoken), T089 (Dessa's tin still unopened).

## Chapter 12 — The River Stirring, part one

**Days covered.** Days 48–54 — Ilmen 19 to Ilmen 25, Y.H. 813, Ninthday to Fifthday. A passage chapter: **every day gets its own dated entry**, seven lived days at the cap, no day poured into another. Vary the well and thaw material from Chapter 9; do not repeat the same scene with different dates.

**Opening moment.** Open on Day 48, Ilmen 19, at the yard well with a frost-bloom on the stone lip where the Warm has lately left, the oiled paper slack in its frame, and a neighbour who used to say the ice held now saying nothing at all. The boy is forty-eight days old, carried in the basket with the fold at his chin, and his hand outside the wool goes up an inch toward the bucket and comes down. Forward, not closed.

**Dated spine, one entry each.**
- **Day 48, Ilmen 19, Ninthday.** Frost-bloom at the well and slack paper at the Vail window; the man who said the ice was honeyed now says nothing; Sella calls it the thaw and feeds the fire (T085).
- **Day 49, Ilmen 20, Tenthday.** Account-day at the Cross. Berr reads one in twelve; the levy is spoken around; the figure goes over the top of the basket. No seventh hearing, no joining of the pauses, no new sighting of the neat dark hand. The crowd goes back to the pigs.
- **Day 50, Ilmen 21, Firstday.** Jorra comes in from the back room with slate dust and says nothing; the men are not named and the broken birth-slates stay under the drying racks (T035). Tarn's ear is not spoken of.
- **Day 51, Ilmen 22, Secondday.** Alda touches the tin once inside her bodice and writes a fifth note she turns down toward the floorboard; the exact dates come faster than the question needs them, and the lie told for Dessa still stands (T023, T008).
- **Day 52, Ilmen 23, Thirdday.** A pot in the yard answers nearly full again; nearly and full are still two sounds and the boy has no hand to tap either. The intervals from Stone 13, Stone 17 and Stone 25 remain objects, not hearings (T082 advanced, still not paid).
- **Day 53, Ilmen 24, Fourthday.** Mira's grey cord and the separate cord twisted twice under it are both glimpsed as she decides whether to lie about the eels; eel-bones flick against her thumb. The two cords stay side by side in the boy's file, unjoined (T059).
- **Day 54, Ilmen 25, Fifthday.** More hairlines in the Ilm ice; the Singing Sill gives its low note a little earlier than it should and the yard calls it weather. Tarn still wants the late thaw, because a late thaw puts off the paying (T085, T077).

**Characters on stage.** Sella Vail, Tarn, Jorra Vail, Alda Reed, Mira Silt, Berr Clay (short), market voices. Orra and Fenna stay offstage until the story earns them again. Dessa is dead and stays dead.

**Threads advanced.** T085 (thaw in frost-bloom, honeyed ice and widened hairlines), T077 (the number that goes around and is never given), T083 (the missing step and the weak plank held against a stirring river), T065 (the closed counting room planted in Chapter 13), T013 (the ridge left where it lies), T009, T061/T082 (hearing and interval kept apart, no seventh), T035 (Jorra's dust and the unnameable back room), T023/T008 (Alda's sign, dates and tin), T059 (the two cords), T071/T072 (the private count carried toward the Breaking), T086 (the empty place in the fold).

## Chapter 13 — The Approach to Silt

**Days covered.** Days 55–60 — Ilmen 26 to Ilmen 30, then Silt 1, Y.H. 813, Sixthday to Firstday. A passage chapter: **every day gets its own dated entry**, six lived days, no day poured into another. Day 60 is Silt 1 and opens the breaking; Day 61 onward belongs to Chapter 14.

**Opening moment.** Open on Day 55, Ilmen 26, at the yard well: a man come downriver from Greybank speaks of a counting room shut during the last war where men died of the breath with the damper closed, and nobody in the yard connects it to the kiln. The boy is fifty-five days old and files the saying beside the kiln's own missing breath without writing a cause. Forward, not closed.

**Dated spine, one entry each.**
- **Day 55, Ilmen 26, Sixthday.** The closed counting room from Greybank; men died of the breath with the damper closed. Nobody connects it to the Vail kiln. Plant T065 toward ~650.
- **Day 56, Ilmen 27, Seventhday.** The tin above Dessa's bed still holds its scrap of birth-cloth and nobody visits the bank; Sella's private roll is still unshown and the wall has no door. The boy notices the object without knowing what it is (T089).
- **Day 57, Ilmen 28, Eighthday.** The household's stores are thinner again and nobody says so; the days are days to Silt now and the household keeps the number badly while the boy keeps it exactly (T071/T072).
- **Day 58, Ilmen 29, Ninthday.** The yard prepares for a sweeping that has already happened; the clean dark line where the ridge was is measured by a thumb against another house's and left where it lies; Garl's coat keeps its peg (T013, T009).
- **Day 59, Ilmen 30, Tenthday.** Account-day and the last market before Silt. The river stirs under the ice; the Singing Sill's note comes up through the boards; a barrow of grey goes out toward the Bone Orchard and nobody looks twice. Close on the boy carried up the yard under the Vail roof with the count four, four and a gap, and the Ilm beginning to move beneath the ice.
- **Day 60, Silt 1, Firstday.** Mud; the month is named for what the river does. A new hairline of grey has gathered in the corner and Sella will not sweep it early, saying it is the thaw. **Characters on stage.** Sella Vail, Tarn, Jorra Vail, Alda Reed, Mira Silt, Berr Clay (short), market voices and a man come down from Greybank (walk-on, one line, no sheet unless he speaks twice). Dessa is dead and stays dead.

## Chapter 14 — The First Breaking, part one

**Days covered.** Days 61–67 — Silt 2 to Silt 8, Y.H. 813, Secondday to Eighthday. A passage chapter: **every day gets its own dated entry**, seven lived days at the cap. The flood in this arc is the year's flood, not the great one of Arc 3 years hence; nobody explains what the boy hears in a district under strain. Days 68 onward belong to Chapter 15.

**Opening moment.** Open on Day 61, Silt 2, at the drying racks: the brook runs higher behind them and the clay comes down heavier than it did in Ilmen, and the boy's hand outside the wool goes up an inch toward the sound of water going over water and comes down. Sella says it is the thaw and gives the instruction to the fire. Forward, not closed.

**Dated spine, one entry each.**
- **Day 61, Silt 2, Secondday.** Brook higher and clay heavier behind the racks; Mira touches the grey cord when a number is spoken; the boy is two months old and still carried everywhere.
- **Day 62, Silt 3, Thirdday.** Tarn's seventh year is nearly gone and he will not count it; he wants the water down before the paying. His broom checks at the place where the cracked pot stood and went unswept.
- **Day 63, Silt 4, Fourthday.** Jorra comes in from the back room with slate dust and says nothing; the men are not named and the broken slates stay where they are. He knows the full note before a Vail pot leaves the wheel and does not say it.
- **Day 64, Silt 5, Fifthday.** Alda touches the tin once and writes a fifth note she turns down; the hidden notes under the floorboard join the others, unread. The exact dates come faster than the question needs them.
- **Day 65, Silt 6, Sixthday.** A pot in the yard answers nearly full under nobody's knuckle; nearly and full are still two sounds and the boy can tap neither. The three intervals stay objects, not hearings (T082, still not paid).
- **Day 66, Silt 7, Seventhday.** The Singing Sill gives its low note earlier than it should and the yard calls it weather; the boy sets it beside the told bell, the heard bell and the kiln's breath and keeps all four apart (T078, T065, T071/T072).
- **Day 67, Silt 8, Eighthday.** The hairlines in the Ilm widen; a man come back from looking says the ice is honeyed all through. The boy lies with his hand outside the wool going up an inch toward the water and coming down.

**Characters on stage.** Sella Vail, Tarn, Jorra Vail, Alda Reed, Mira Silt, Berr Clay (short), market and weir voices. Orra appears only at the flood's edge in Chapter 15; Fenna is still not named. Dessa is dead and stays dead.

**Threads advanced.** T083/T085 (hairline and honeyed ice carried into the year's flood), T077 (the number that goes around and is never given), T013/T009 (the ridge left where it lies and gone around), T061/T082 (hearing and interval kept apart, no seventh), T035 (Jorra's dust and the unnameable back room), T059 (the two cords), T023/T089 (notes under the floorboard, tin above the bed, both unread), T036 (Tarn's seventh nearly gone), T071/T072 (private count carried through the rise).

## Chapter 15 — The First Breaking, part two (sketch)

**Days covered.** Days 68–74 — Silt 9 to Silt 15, Y.H. 813, Ninthday to Fifthday. Seven lived days at the cap. Day 68 the Ilm comes up over the Fishstairs and takes the low houses; Day 69 is Account-day at the steps with Berr reading one in twelve over the water; Day 70 the water goes around the ridge rather than taking it (plant the later heresy that a house can be left where it is and still be kept); Day 71 a child wading below the sill comes up with a foot that stays cold after the water falls (seed the silt-leach of Arc 3 without naming it); Day 72 the water falls and Mira's two cords are glimpsed again with the knot for the thin child upriver not touched and Dessa's tin above the bed still where it was put; Day 73 the sweeping that has happened twice already goes around the ridge; Day 74 the flood recedes into mud and the district counts what it took in loaves and glances, not figures. On Day 68 the boy is carried to the edge and hears the note of a whole district under strain --- a vessel with an enormous capacity and a slow, certain failure --- and has no word and no hand to point with; do not explain it, he only files it. The weak board in the plank walk at the Weir is either trodden or gone by the flood; do not make an accident of it yet, and let the reader remember it. Close on the boy under the Vail roof with the fire keeping four, four and a gap, the count carried into Seed, nothing joined, explained or closed. Day 75 opens Chapter 16 on Seed 1.

## Continuity rules for these chapters

1. **Days 45–74 only**, in order, with no gaps and no day poured into another: Ch 11 = 45–47, Ch 12 = 48–54 (seven dated entries), Ch 13 = 55–60 (six entries), Ch 14 = 61–67 (seven entries), Ch 15 = 68–74 (seven entries). Day 44 is finished and must not be revisited as a new scene. Check `bible/calendar.md` for every tend-day: Day 45 is Ilmen 16, Day 49 and Day 59 are Account-days, Day 60 is Silt 1, Day 69 is Silt 10 and Account-day, Day 74 is Silt 15, Day 75 opens Chapter 16. A passage chapter never exceeds seven lived days.
2. **Point of view stays with Dan.** No Court scene, no inside-the-collection scene, not yet. The first such departure is far off.
3. **Present tense only for Chicago breaking through**, one or two sentences at a time, never for anticipation and never more than once in a chapter. The old life was paid in past tense and untagged on Day 38; do not make a habit of it, and never tag a `\memory{}` to it.
4. **`\memory{...}` for remembered words only.** Chapters 4–10 tagged none; let the market voice and the hospital stand on their own until a remembered line earns the tag.
5. **No recap, no exposition speech, no modern simile in Sarn.** A face is not like a screen here. It may be like one in Chicago, where the screen is part of the texture.
6. **The body humiliates him** in every chapter: drool, the useless head, the fold, the hand that cannot reach the cup or lift to its own chin. Play it for exactness, with the dry joke living in it, never for easy pathos.
7. **Every cost has a payer.** The carried fire, the stone, the gruel, the eels and the crusts came from somebody's fire and were entered nowhere; Sella paid twice and has taken a measure from the district. Mira's short eels and Berr's short marks are the same arithmetic at a different stall. Dessa's death is a taking nobody enters and the boy cannot refuse; her tin above the bed and her jar on Sella's shelf are the things of hers the district has not entered either.
8. **The hands stay separate in his mind.** The five pauses in the neat dark hand must not be joined; the Drawing collector's hand has no pause and stays in its own place; the officer's clean voice is a third thing filed apart; the twice-wiped thumb of Berr stays on its own tray. Day 38 added a hearing from a person, not a seventh pause. The clerk at the Fishstairs and the clerk at the Court's table are two more record-first hands on separate trays (T080, T090), not Marra and not joined to one another.
9. **The ear keeps its objects in order.** Six hearings from four sources plus the sixth from a person on the Slip Brook; three intervals (Stone 13, Stone 17, Stone 25), the told bell beside the heard bell, and the nearly-full note of Ilmen 6 filed as an answer to the Stone 13 interval on its own tray. The word *forty* is a saying on a tray of its own, not a sound of a vessel; the forty-first knot is a shape, not a number the boy can read. Every one is useless and none is named in Grathic. No seventh hearing in Chapters 11–15.
10. **The crusts are accounted for.** Both crusts lay in the fold through Chapter 10; neither was eaten, removed or named and no third was given. One is gone by Day 46 (Chapter 11), eaten by nobody and spoken of by nobody --- the first edge of T086. Do not solve who took it; the reader must be able to suspect every woman in the yard.
11. **Dessa stays dead.** No recognition of the mother, no name from her, no number from her thumb, no one in three, no Marra, no explanation of the Forty, no visit to a bank turned into a quest, no ending. Her tin is seen and not opened; the lie told for mercy stands as a fact nobody can correct.
12. **When a chapter closes, update `memory/state.md`, add one line per day to `memory/timeline.md`, append to `memory/story-so-far.md`, give every character who appeared a Chapter Log line, move planted threads from `open` to `planted` in `plan/threads.md`, and add any new coined terms to `bible/glossary.md`. Record any new place in `memory/places.md` before it appears on a map.** Walk-ons go in `characters/_minor.md`, and `meta.json` "characters" lists only slugs that have sheets.
13. **Keep the engines turning.** Alda's lie curdles against Dessa's death; Mira's knot-string and Sella's silence are both second records, and Dessa's tin above the bed is a third (T089); Tarn's ear makes him valuable and therefore unsafe; Berr's box stays locked and his reason stays good; the clerk set at the Fishstairs for an uncalled measure is a new kind of Court presence (T090); Jorra's dust and the unnameable back room point at the Broken Slates; the carried fire of Kindling keeps its unnamed payer (T088); Orra's knot and the word *forty* are watched and unread (T024); the birth room, the kiln-house, the death-house, the Court's room, the neighbour's room two doors down, the room with the tin and the Eel House are one room repeated (T081); Fenna's green at the mouth is filed in silence years before her ear is spoken (T050/T051). Twists, betrayals and romances must be in the ground before they are harvested, and every one of them is logged in `plan/threads.md`.
