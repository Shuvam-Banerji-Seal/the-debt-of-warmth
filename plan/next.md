2. **Point of view stays with Dan.** No Court scene, no inside-the-collection scene, not yet. The first such departure is far off.
3. **Present tense only for Chicago breaking through**, one or two sentences at a time, never for anticipation and never more than once in a chapter. 4. **`\memory{...}` for remembered words only.** Chapters 4–28 tagged none; let the market voice and the hospital stand on their own until a remembered line earns the tag.
5. **No recap, no exposition speech, no modern simile in Sarn.** A face is not like a screen here. It may be like one in Chicago, where the screen is part of the texture. An arm carrying a bundle may be read like the surgeon's ninety-second sorting; that is a comparison inside his own head, never a spoken speech to a neighbour.
6. **The body humiliates him** in every chapter: drool, the useless head, the fold, the hand that cannot reach the cup or lift to its own chin, the frozen thread at the mouth nobody wipes, cold at cheek--temple--wrist with the wool ridden back.
