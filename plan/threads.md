# The ledger of threads (private)

*Every mystery, seed, Chekhov's gun, grudge, promise, prophecy and romance, with where it is planted and where it is paid. Nothing is written yet, so every status is **open**. As chapters land, a thread becomes **planted** when its seed is in the text, and **paid** when the payoff scene is written — a thread can be planted and paid more than once, and some payoffs open a new thread in the same line. Plant chapters are planned anchors, reconciled with the day-count in `bible/calendar.md`; the provisional `first_appearance` numbers on character sheets are treated the same way.*

**Status key:** open = planned, not yet in the text; planted = the seed is written; paid = the payoff is written.
