# The ledger of threads (private)

*Every mystery, seed, Chekhov's gun, grudge, promise, prophecy and romance, with where it is planted and where it is paid. Chapter 1 is written and its seeds are marked **planted** below; the payoffs remain planned until their scenes are written. As chapters land, a thread becomes **planted** when its seed is in the text, and **paid** when the payoff scene is written — a thread can be planted and paid more than once, and some payoffs open a new thread in the same line. Plant chapters are planned anchors, reconciled with the day-count in `bible/calendar.md`; the provisional `first_appearance` numbers on character sheets are treated the same way. Chapter 4 is written and closed: the Hearth Drawing, the fifth hearing of the flat note from a person, the second chill with its withheld cause, the two hidden notes and the Drawing collector's reason are in the text and marked against it below. Chapter 5 is written and closed: the quiet morning under the Vail roof where a pot was turned toward and gave nothing, the second ear witnessed and kept silent, the Fishstairs and the market edge, the brown-edged crust and the two cords at Mira's waist are in the text and marked against it below. Chapter 6 is written and closed: the household's arithmetic in loaves, glances and a kindling stack four, four and an empty fifth, a second interval, a fourth sighting of the pause and Alda's second hidden note are in the text. Chapter 7 is written and closed: the last tend and the year's last market, a number carried in a shut fist, a third interval from a cracked pot, a fifth sighting of the pause, two crusts in the fold and a further note toward the tin are in the text and marked against it below; no payoff has been written, and the fifth sighting is a count and nothing more. Chapter 8 is written and closed: the carried fire from an unspoken source, the clean voice and the whole return announced for somebody absent, the told bell kept beside the heard bell, the silence after an unspoken name, the ridge left where it lay and then burned and called dealt with, and the count carried into Y.H. 813 are in the text and marked against it below. Nothing was paid off, no sixth hearing was added, no pause joined, and T088 was opened.*

Chapter 10 is written and closed: Days 38–44, Ilmen 9–15, Y.H. 813. Dessa Silt dies on Day 38 in the clay-heavy Slip Brook and stays dead, uncollected; the sixth hearing of the full note comes out of a person, the hand-on-the-basket detail is paid in past tense and untagged, no collector is called and nobody names the omission, a pewter tin is seen above her empty bed, and on Day 44 Orra Weir ties a forty-first knot and says *forty* low into the basket and watches. Two new threads carry what the chapter opened --- the tin above the bed (T089) and the clerk set at the Fishstairs for an uncalled measure (T090) --- while the watched word is folded into T024. T024 moves from open to planted and T087 pays its Day 38 payoff; nothing else is closed, and no hearing becomes a seventh.

Chapter 11 is written and closed: Days 45–47, Ilmen 16–18, Y.H. 813. The boy, forty-five to forty-seven days old, watched a brine-gull carry a not-yet-cold coal off upriver with no slate the poorer; read the shape of the forty-first knot without its name while the word forty went unsaid; and, on the afternoon of Day 46, lost the smoky crust from the fold with nobody saying it was gone or who took it --- the first edge of T086 paid. Frost wrote on the well-lip where the Warm had lately gone out and was called weather by noon; a number went from fist to mouth to shoulder and arrived nowhere; a weak board at the Weir was heard of and not trodden; the clerk at the Fishstairs and the uncollected woman stayed two facts on separate trays. The six hearings stayed six; no seventh came, no sixth sighting of the neat dark hand, no joining of word, shape, cords or trays. T083 moves from open to planted, and T091 is planted over the three hands the boy set on a tray as the possible takers of the crust.

**Status key:** open = planned, not yet in the text; planted = the seed is written; paid = the payoff is written.
