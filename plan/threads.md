# The ledger of threads (private)

*Every mystery, seed, Chekhov's gun, grudge, promise, prophecy and romance, with where it is planted and where it is paid. Chapter 1 is written and its seeds are marked **planted** below; the payoffs remain planned until their scenes are written. As chapters land, a thread becomes **planted** when its seed is in the text, and **paid** when the payoff scene is written — a thread can be planted and paid more than once, and some payoffs open a new thread in the same line. Plant chapters are planned anchors, reconciled with the day-count in `bible/calendar.md`; the provisional `first_appearance` numbers on character sheets are treated the same way. Chapter 4 is written and closed: the Hearth Drawing, the fifth hearing of the flat note from a person, the second chill with its withheld cause, the two hidden notes and the Drawing collector's reason are in the text and marked against it below. Chapter 5 is written and closed: the quiet morning under the Vail roof where a pot was turned toward and gave nothing, the second ear witnessed and kept silent, the Fishstairs and the market edge, the brown-edged crust and the two cords at Mira's waist are in the text and marked against it below.*

**Status key:** open = planned, not yet in the text; planted = the seed is written; paid = the payoff is written.
