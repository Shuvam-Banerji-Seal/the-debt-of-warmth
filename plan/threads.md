# The ledger of threads (private)

*Every mystery, seed, Chekhov's gun, grudge, promise, prophecy and romance, with where it is planted and where it is paid. Chapter 1 is written and its seeds are marked **planted** below; the payoffs remain planned until their scenes are written. As chapters land, a thread becomes **planted** when its seed is in the text, and **paid** when the payoff scene is written — a thread can be planted and paid more than once, and some payoffs open a new thread in the same line. Plant chapters are planned anchors, reconciled with the day-count in `bible/calendar.md`; the provisional `first_appearance` numbers on character sheets are treated the same way.
