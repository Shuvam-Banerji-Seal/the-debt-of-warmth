## Contradictions not fixed here, with a plan to reconcile in the story

1. **Dan's and Nina's birth years.** `characters/dan-marchetti.md` gives Dan born 1984, died 2024 at forty, and says he married Elena Rossi in 2007 and "their daughter Nina was born in 2000"; `characters/nina-marchetti.md` also gives 2000. That makes Dan sixteen at Nina's birth and the birth seven years before the marriage. The old life's other dates (Tasha at thirty-one, the twelve-year Cook County career) depend on 1984/2024, so those must stay. *Plan:* in the next character-sheet pass set Nina's birth to 2002–2004 (Dan 18–20, Nina 20–22 at his death, still a grown woman, with the nine-year-old boat promise plausible), and correct both sheets together; alternatively move the marriage earlier if 2000 is to stand. Until then no chapter may state Nina's age or birth year, or Dan's age at her birth, on the page. Keep the child under the dashboard a separate person from Nina, as `bible/premise.md` requires.

2. **Orra Weir's presence at the birth.** `characters/orra-weir.md` History says she "was in the room on Stone 7 when the child with the branching red flower was born" and "saw the collector's slate that night"; Bonds says "Dessa Silt — they were both in the room on Stone 7." The written birth scene (Ch 1) does not put her in the room, and Ch 10 canon has her at the Weir, seeing Sella carry a small warm bundle with a flower up the Clay Yard on the night of the taking, with no slate in her hands. *Plan:* never place Orra in the birth room. Reconcile in a later chapter by having her knowledge come from the night of the taking (Day 3): she saw the bundle go up the Clay Yard and glimpsed a strange hand and a name on a collector's slate among the men who took the Forty, which is why she remembers the shape of the name and cannot spell it. Then amend her sheet's History and Bonds to that night. Her forty-first knot stays tied over the bundle, not over a birth she witnessed.

3. **The warm stone above Dessa's bed.** `characters/dessa-silt.md`, Secrets #4: "a stain on the ceiling above the bed where she kept a warm stone for the baby for a year after he was gone." Canon puts the single pale river stone in the fold of grey wool on the abandoned board-bed in House Silt on Day 3, where it remains unmentioned through Day 44; the pewter tin above that bed holds a pale fold, unopened. *Plan:* keep the pale river stone where canon left it. In a later chapter establish that her ceiling stain came from a different stone or a later warmth, or amend the secret to say she kept the empty fold rather than the stone. The tin's contents may remain a private secret; on the page they stay unidentified.

4. **Ulda's first-appearance marker.** `characters/ulda.md` gives `first_appearance: 6`, but he is not on stage or named in Chapter 6; `plan/threads.md` T033 places the loaf sale around Chapter 50 and `plan/arcs.md` introduces him in Arc 2. *Plan:* treat 6 as a stale planting marker (the sheets themselves call these provisional). Either correct `first_appearance` to the chapter where he first speaks, or deliberately bring him on as a named walk-on in Chapter 11 or 12 if the earlier marker is wanted; record which choice is made.

5. **The rank list.** `bible/premise.md` gives a provisional six (Whole, Held, Drawn, Banked, Hearthward, Kept); `plan/arcs.md` gives nine, adding Thin, Opened and Welled. No written chapter uses a rank name in narration, so nothing on the page is broken. `memory/story-so-far.md` currently carries the nine-name list. *Plan:* settle the list in the magic bible when the power system is built, then align premise.md, arcs.md and the story-so-far list in one pass.
