## Contradictions not fixed, with a plan to reconcile in the story

### Carried over from the Chapter 10 audit

1. **Dan's and Nina's birth years.** `characters/dan-marchetti.md` gives Dan born 1984, died 2024 at forty, married Elena Rossi in 2007 and "their daughter Nina was born in 2000"; `characters/nina-marchetti.md` also gives 2000. That makes Dan sixteen at Nina's birth and the birth seven years before the marriage. The other old-life dates (Tasha at thirty-one, twelve years at Cook County) depend on 1984/2024. *Plan:* set Nina's birth to 2002–2004 in the next sheet pass (Dan 18–20, Nina 20–22 at his death, nine-year boat promise still plausible) and correct both sheets together, or move the marriage earlier if 2000 stands. Until then no chapter may state Nina's age or birth year, or Dan's age at her birth, on the page. Keep the minivan child separate from Nina.
2. **Orra Weir's presence at the birth.** `characters/orra-weir.md` History says she "was in the room on Stone 7 when the child with the branching red flower was born" and "saw the collector's slate that night"; Bonds says "Dessa Silt — they were both in the room on Stone 7." Written canon puts her onstage first at Ch 10 (Ilmen 14–15), seeing Sella carry a small warm bundle with a flower up the Clay Yard on the night of the taking and tying the forty-first knot over that sight on Day 44. *Plan:* never place Orra in the birth room. When her knowledge is reconciled, establish that it came from the night of the taking (Day 3): the bundle, a strange hand and a name on a slate among the men who took the Forty, which is why she remembers the shape of the name and cannot spell it. Then amend History and Bonds. Her knot stays tied over the bundle, not a birth she witnessed.
3. **The warm stone above Dessa's bed.** `characters/dessa-silt.md`, Secrets #4: "a stain on the ceiling above the bed where she kept a warm stone for the baby for a year after he was gone." Canon puts the single pale river stone in the grey wool fold on the abandoned board-bed in House Silt from Day 3 and confirms it **gone by Day 64**, with only the hollow remaining and nobody saying it is gone; the pewter tin above that bed holds an unidentified pale fold, unopened. *Plan:* keep the stone's disappearance as written. In a later chapter establish that the ceiling stain came from a different stone or a later warmth, or amend the secret to say she kept the empty fold. The tin's contents may remain private; on the page stay unidentified.
4. ~~Ulda's first-appearance marker.~~ **Resolved.** `characters/ulda.md` now gives `first_appearance: 16`, matching his first plain sighting at the well on Silt 16, and its History reconciles his years-hence dialogue. ### New in the Chapter 20 audit

6. **How many mornings Dessa has been dead.** Ch 16 says "thirty-eight mornings dead" (Day 75; Day 75 − Day 38 = 37) and Ch 17 says "forty mornings dead" (Day 84; actual difference 46). From Ch 18 onward the narration deliberately stops counting and says "more mornings dead than any living person keeps count of," and `memory/timeline.md` Day 84 carries the Ch 17 number. *Plan:* treat the two exact numbers as authorial slips, not a hidden calendar. Keep the vague phrasing from Ch 18 onward and never put a precise count on the page; if a chapter genuinely needs one, compute from Day 38 rather than from either slip. Do not rewrite finished timeline entries.
7. **The cold foot appears twice.** T093 plants the staying-cold foot on Day 71 (Silt 12, Ch 15) and Ch 20's Day 98 describes "a child came out of the low water with a foot that stayed dark." The prose reads like a fresh emergence, but no new thread was opened and no cure or cause was written. *Plan:* treat Day 98 as the same foot re-exposed as water leaves the low houses, not a second child, so T093/T094/T096 remain one line; do not name silt-leach, cause or cure before Ch 25, and do not multiply the shapes on the boy's trays.

If a count is unavoidable, four half-beats, three later silent appearances, and no sale.
9. **The Account-day bell slip in Ch 20, scene 6.** One paragraph calls the missing stroke the "fourth stroke," the rest of the scene and all other canon say four strokes, four, and a missing **fifth**. Already logged in Ch 20's summary and in T078. *Plan:* canon is the missing fifth; treat "fourth stroke" as a scribe's slip and do not propagate it into Ch 21–25.
10. **Alda's sign count.** The pre-rewrite story-so-far said she crossed the flower's red edge "five times unawares" by Ch 10, while the T001/T007 plant lists record more crossings than five. *Plan:* treat "five" as a local count at the Ch 10 snapshot, never a cumulative total; put no total on the page, only "again and again, each covered with a date."
