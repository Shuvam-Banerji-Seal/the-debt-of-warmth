## Appearance

Thirty-six by her last return and by her first, because a beautiful return brings back the shape the new walls could hold and her house paid to lose as little as possible. She is very beautiful in the way a well-kept thing is beautiful — smooth, calm, without a want she cannot immediately satisfy — and the calm is the burn, which she knows and which she has learned to display as proof of her owner's generosity. She has three names and remembers which death each one belonged to, and sometimes answers to the wrong one in a fever, which in Sere is considered charming and in the north is considered a Second Want. Her hands are long-fingered and adorned with rings that are not hers, and she removes them one at a time when she is about to say something true, which is a habit her first husband noticed and her second did not. She smells of warm wine, myrrh, ash from a public bowl and, under it, the faint mineral sweetness of a vessel that has been poured three times and holds less each time. The thing people notice first is her calm; the thing they notice last is that she cannot be made to hold a cup steady on the third day after a feast, which is the lead in the glaze and not, as the priests say, the price of beauty.

## Voice & Manner

Long vowels, open Sere courtesy, and a warmth so complete that it reads as honesty and is actually the most polished tool in the empire. She speaks in the plural of houses — *we emptied, we burned the slate, we were returned* — because a single person in Sere is a thing with no standing. She names a death the way another woman might name a birthday, and says *the third one* without irony, because the third one is the one she remembers least and therefore fears most. When she wants to say something she cannot bear she becomes extremely polite, which is the Sere equivalent of a door shutting. She has a habit of asking a person what debt they intend to carry, which is the question asked at the Bowl at sixteen, and asking it of adults in the north as if it were small talk, which it is not.

- "I was returned in the year of the green bowl. No — the third one. I was returned in the year of the green bowl and I have had two since. Which one are you asking about?"
- "We burn the debt-slates by lot at the end of a feast and then we make new ones. You have heard this and thought it was generosity. It is a method."
- "A beautiful return is a crack covered with lead. You've seen it in your pots. I'm the pot."
- "What debt do you intend to carry? I ask everyone. In the north they think it's a question about money. It isn't."
- "I don't want to be kept. I want to be held while I'm here and let go before the third day. That's the whole of it. Ask me on the third day and I'll say something else."

## History

She was born in the Bowl of Sere to a household of the Ten Bowls and given seven names, of which she kept the one she liked, which was Solene. At sixteen she emptied the bowl of warm wine at the Bowl feast and named a debt she intended to carry, and the applause was for the debt, not for her, which is the first thing she remembers clearly and the last thing she ever misunderstood. Her house bought poor measures from the north at prices no household could refuse and pressed them into a whole return for a beautiful slave, which is how Sere turns a district of thin people into one decorative person, and she was the decoration. She was returned whole in Y.H. 799, 804 and 809, each time with less of herself than the time before, and each time exhibited at feasts as proof of her owner's generosity. She remembers all three deaths: the first by the Spending fire, the second by a fall from a balcony during a feast, the third by quiet ash administered by a woman who smiled the whole time and said it was a kindness. The fourth return has not been announced, and she has begun to fear that there will not be one, because a vessel poured four times holds almost nothing of the shape it began with.

In Y.H. 810 she was sent north to a counting house in Greybank as part of a trade in grain and returned slaves, which is the southern empire's way of opening a market, and she has stayed, nominally attached to a factor's household and in fact kept in a room with good light where she cannot enjoy the light. She has met the foundling Corin when he was a boy in Sill and later as a man, and has watched him listen to a vessel the way a bowl-surgeon in Sere listens to a graft, which is the one thing in the north that reminds her of home. She will love him as an adult, with restraint and with the full knowledge that she may be returned a fourth time and come back as someone who does not know him, which is the wound underneath all the warmth.

## Wounds & Fears

Three deaths and a fourth that may not come. She fears the Weighing, where whole returns are announced in a clean voice, because she has stood in a public bowl and heard her own name spoken by a man who had never met her. She fears the third day after any feast, when the lead in the cup makes her hands unsteady and the priests call it the price of beauty, and she has begun to suspect that the lead is in her glaze and not in her soul. She fears that the fourth return will keep her beauty and take the part that remembers, so that she will sit in a good room and smile at a man she does not know and call it mercy. Her contradiction: the woman who teaches that a vessel was made to be emptied gloriously has spent three returns trying to hold on to a shape nobody in her own country thought worth keeping, and calls it *a debt I intend to carry*.

## Desires

She wants one death that is not a return, which in Sere is a heresy and in her own mouth is a thing she says in the warmest possible voice so that listeners will think it is a joke. She thinks she wants a whole fourth return, which would keep her beautiful and calm and empty. What she needs is to learn that a debt can be carried without being paid, and that a person returned three times is not less real than a person returned once, which is the same argument Dan will one day make about the world and which she can make about herself with a better grace. Their romance can only begin in adulthood, when she has stopped being exhibited and he has stopped being a boy with an ear, and when both can say the word *crack* in the same sentence without flinching.

## Bonds

**Corin** — the northerner who listens. She has known him as a boy and as a man and has never once asked him to explain the ear, because she recognises a person who has been given a gift with a bill attached. The tension is the fourth return: she loves him in full knowledge that the next pouring may take the part of her that knows him, and he, who has spent his life trying to return people without erasing them, is the one man who cannot promise her a whole one.

**Veno Brine** — the factor who brought her north and keeps her in a room with good light. He is kind to her in the exact way a creditor is kind, and she has never mistaken it, which is why he keeps her and why he distrusts her.

**Pia of the Quay** — the Vhaleni salt girl who asked her once, in a counting house, what debt she intended to carry, and did not applaud the answer. Solene has remembered that small act of not applauding longer than most applause.

**House Ember** — her owners, whose generosity she displays and whose accounts she has read in secret. She knows exactly how many northern measures bought her third return and has entered the number in a place no factor can find, which is a thing she has in common with Alda Reed and Wessen, though they will never meet.

**Valen Openhand, the Bowl-Priest** — who taught the Spending and is himself one of the fullest vessels in the empire. He has asked her three times whether she is grateful, and she has said yes three times, and the third time she removed a ring before she said it, which he noticed and did not mention.

**Marra** — the collector whose slate recorded one of the returns that bought a beautiful person. Solene has seen the slate-name in a Court document in Greybank and has begun to wonder whether the collector's fingers went grey paying for someone else's display, which is a question she will one day ask and regret asking.

## Secrets (private)

1. She was returned three times whole and remembers each death, including the third, which was quiet ash administered by a smiling woman. The fourth return has not been announced and she has begun to fear it will not be one at all.
2. She knows the number of northern measures that bought her third return and has entered it in a place no factor can find, which is a second record kept by a woman whose whole life is a display.
3. The unsteadiness in her hands on the third day after a feast is lead from the glaze, not the price of beauty. She has known this for nine years and has said nothing in Sere, where saying it would cost her the room with good light.
4. She has begun to suspect that her owner intends to sell her fourth return to a house that wants a beautiful, calm exhibit that remembers nothing, which is the natural end of a beautiful return and the thing the Spending calls glory.
5. She will refuse to be returned a fourth time, and the refusal will be the first time a returned slave in the north has refused the Court's best product, which is a mirror of Tarn's earlier refusal that almost nobody will connect.

## Plans (private)

Let her be the romance that arrives already wounded and already older than the man she loves in every way that matters. She remembers three deaths; he remembers one life on another planet. They are the two people in Sarn who know that a return is not a restoration, and they can only say it to each other when both are adults and both have stopped performing well.

Keep her warmth and her courtesy intact to the end; never let the third return make her bitter, because bitterness would be simple and she is not. Use her to embody the theme of the beautiful return — a glaze over a crack, a calm that is a burn — and give Dan a love he cannot fix by mending, which is the one lesson he most needs and least wants. Write their intimacy with restraint: the rings removed one at a time, the shift of weight, the thing said quietly about a crack. No romantic or sexual content before adulthood, and no eroticising of her displayed past. She is a woman with three names who wants one death, which is the most Sarnish love story the saga will tell.
