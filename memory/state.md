## The moment

Day 251, Smoke 12, Secondday, Y.H. 813, the small hours, under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof in \place{Low Kiln}. \person[Corin]{the boy} lies on \place{the bed} at the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf}, awake and silent, in the room that does not touch the room of the fire, with the fold at his ear and one stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust two inches from it; the empty place beside the crust keeps its name to itself and no third came to make the row come out even. ## The protagonist's condition
