# State of things — at the close of chapter 3 (private)

*This is the snapshot the author reads before writing chapter 4. Day 4 has been lived: a house that kept the boy without explanation has carried him into a district that prices him aloud, and the figure has been given a voice twice and swallowed twice. Nothing has been explained. The flat note has now come from three vessels in four hearings --- a cup, a pot, and the district's own slate twice --- and nobody has said what it is. A voice from the old life has taken the market for a room, a neat hand has been seen a third time, and a kindness has been publicly entered without anyone asking him.*

---

## The moment

The fourth day is done. Sella Vail carried Corin out from under the Vail roof in a basket under a fold of wool, down the Clay Yard and into the market at the Cross, where Berr Clay wiped his thumb on his apron before touching the clean slate and read *one in twelve, as the notice says* to two hundred people who leaned in to hear it; the Sill assessor added only that the figure stood as read. The boy lay under the reading and could not turn his head to see the man pricing his kind. Ostrowski's voice broke through the market noise without a tag; a clerk at the Slate Row end touched a slate whose upper line was in a strange neat hand, and Berr told Sella her widow's mark was confirmed at Greybank, a kindness that was entered, while leaving Garl's uncalled debt where it lay. Alda Reed touched the tin inside her bodice, crossed two fingers at the flower's edge on his wrist without knowing it, and covered the sign with a date. In the afternoon the market came apart, Mira Silt asked the eel price and answered it herself, the figure was read a second time, Ash went out in a barrow toward the north gate, and Sella lifted the basket and turned back up the yard without a word about any of it. The boy carries a hundred and nine steps, eight pigs, four ways out of the square, a bell counted four and four and a gap, and a note he cannot place. He has not spoken and has not been asked to.

Day count: **Day 4 complete.** Day 5 is Stone 11, Y.H. 812, a Firstday. The Hearth Drawing of Stone 12 (Day 6) is two days off, and Low Kiln says the Five are three days off.

## The protagonist's condition

**Body.** \person{Corin}, four days old by the afternoon of Day 4. He was \person{Daniel Marchetti}, forty, twelve of them a trauma surgeon at Cook County; he died going back into a burning minivan for a child under the dashboard and his warmth left him in the giving and did not cool. As Corin he is a cracked, low-capacity vessel, unranked --- below Thin, twelve to fifteen embers at best, with a flaw nobody has named and a rate of loss nobody has measured. He was carried the whole day and could direct none of it: Sella held him like a pot filled and must not be slopped, then set him beside a barrel, then against a post, then lifted him back. He drools and cannot wipe his chin; cannot hold up his head, roll over, or turn it past a point without the small wet sound; cannot push the fold back with a hand that goes up an inch and comes down, small and soft with milky nails. He had put a thousand shoulders back and cannot keep a blanket on his own chin. The joke of it went flat three days ago and the exactness has not. Cold got in through his cheek, his temple and the wrist outside the fold; he knew the afterdrop coming, was in it, and could only count. The branching red \lore{birth-flower} runs from the third rib on the left down the forearm and stops short of the wrist; its red edge showed at his wrist when the wool rode back, and \person{Alda Reed} looked at it and crossed two fingers over it without knowing she had done so. He is hungry, awake, and furious with the exactness of a man who can measure everything and do none of it.

**Mind.** The double consciousness has acquired a public grammar. The surgeon's register reads the pressure of a lifting arm through cloth, the height a weight is held at, the not-looking, the order of a hand reaching for a slate before a face, the shape of a debt left uncalled, the relief of a man who has settled a thing with a number, and the two different kinds of not-looking --- Berr's, which prices, and Mira's, which keeps. He knows what a room does to a man who must decide in it and then stay, and knows now that the grammar of the bay and the grammar of the block are one grammar: decide, then carry, leave the deciding unnamed and call the carrying kindness. For one breath the clay yard and the pigs occupied the same paragraph as the trauma bay, and he did not tag the going. Vesci's *that's what a chart is for* came outward for the first time in Berr's *kindness is entered*, and Dan heard it as a warm, reasonable sentence closing a matter that had never been open. He has begun to understand that a kindness which cannot be answered is a kindness which cannot be refused, and that the things nobody names in this district are the things the district has most practice at carrying out of sight. He was the man who read the room in the old life and also one of the things in it; here he is only the thing carried.

**Powers.** None usable, no rank change. He cannot move Warm, cannot clamp, cannot speak, cannot make a fire, cannot read \lore{Grathic} in speech or writing, cannot read a ledger or bargain for food. His counting is an old-life habit, not a gift, and now holds: the kiln's four, four and a gap; the floorboards' four, four and a knot; a hundred and nine steps; five then eight pigs; four ways out of the Cross; six hands in the square; two hoops and a mended strip on the barrel; the bell's four, four and a gap. The ear holds the flat note from three vessels in four hearings --- a cup across the birth room, a pot in the Vail house, Berr's slate in the morning, and the same slate a fourth time in the afternoon --- set side by side and never joined. He knows \lore{the full note} from its flat shadow and has no Grathic word for either. The knowing buys nothing: no hand has reached a vessel, nobody has tapped a slate for him, and no one in the market has said what the sound was. The hot trace that carried him is in him still and he has no name for it.

**Possessions and money.** Nothing gained, nothing lost. The small pale river stone, colour of skimmed milk, is still in the fold of grey wool on the board-bed in House Silt, and the empty place at his belly is still felt with an exactness that has nothing to do with temperature. He owns no coins and none change hands in this chapter. The basket and the wool are Sella's, lent without a word. Figures go around him and are not his to spend: one in twelve, twice read; Mira's *two marks the pair and two short*, asked and answered by herself; two turnip prices that never agree; eight pigs; three days to the Five; two days to the Hearth Drawing. The loaf and the warmth from the Vail hearth remain unentered, and the house that paid twice has now taken the boy into the district's account without asking him.

**Wounds carried from the other life.** The call he never made to Nina --- nine summers, nine promises, a boat never bought --- reached for again in a pocket that is not there; the lit name was not looked at then and cannot be looked at now. Tasha Broussard's chart, signed without reading the nurse's note, and the institution calling it a systems failure; Vesci's warm *butcher*, now given a public voice in Berr's mouth. Ostrowski's maxim arrived whole and in the present tense and carries her own contradiction inside it: she demanded an account from every resident and withheld one from the person she loved like a son. His father's casket slightly crooked in the aisle; Elena's quiet leaving, Marisol's temperate one. Des's laugh did not return this chapter. The pause in the neat hand has now been set beside the pause at the flare and the pause on the birth-slate three times and has not made a fourth. In Sarn's terms he is an unclosed account heavy enough to crack a slate, and he has now been entered in two accounts that never asked him.

## Open dangers

- The boy has been carried out of a house that would not explain him and into a district that has priced him aloud twice. He stood inside the account that will carry him and could not address it, refuse it, or see the face of the man reading it. A foundling with no house-name remains a coat on a peg in a poor house.

## What he knows and does not know

**He knows.** How a body fails: airway, bleeding, bones, burns, cold, infection, afterdrop and rewarming, the small evidence a body carries, and triage. The smell of machine coffee and hospital sanitizer, snow going up a sleeve, orange streetlight on wet snow, his mother's kitchen, his daughter's photograph, the shape of an unsent sentence. He knows he died and that he is in a baby's body. This chapter added: that Sella carried him out under wool and said *the house*, never *I*; that she does not set a basket where a slate lies open and touches the widow's mark only before opening the kiln; that the mark is a thumb-length of lead-glazed clay with the Vail kiln scratched on it; that a hundred and nine steps brought him to the road, and eight pigs, four ways out and the bell's four-four-and-a-gap are numbers nobody has asked for; that Berr wipes his thumb on his apron for the hand, not the slate, says *one in twelve, as the notice says*, never says I, and that the assessor said the figure stands as read; that the district's slate gives the same flat answer as a cup and a pot, twice, and nobody tapped it for him; that a strange neat hand with a small stop between the clusters was written before a face and nobody remarked on it, and that he has seen that stop three times; that Berr told Sella her widow's mark was confirmed at Greybank as a kindness entered and left Garl's debt uncalled; that Alda touched the tin a third time, crossed two fingers at his wrist and covered the sign with a date; that there are two kinds of not-looking, one that prices and one that keeps; that Mira ticks an eel-bone against her thumb and counts twice and again after eating; that Ash goes out in a barrow every market-day; that three days are given to the Five and the Hearth Drawing is Stone 12; that the grammar of the bay and the block are one grammar and he is the thing carried, not the man who decides or the man who carries.

**He does not know.** The word *Sarn*, or that the Warm is the stuff the world is made of; any Grathic word beyond what he has absorbed by listening; that the Return is an industry with collectors, bankers and a deduction; that he was bought; that forty children paid for his whole return; who the woman in the grey coat is and what she wrote or what her name says; why the flower is on his ribs; why his fevers will come; that ways run in spirals; that Dessa is his mother and Sella knew; that the Court is a bank and the temple an invoice; that somebody has watched him since before his birth and intends to use his coldness to seal the world. He does not know why Sella took him, what happened to Garl's warmth beyond the household's version, what is in Alda's tin, who wrote the neat upper line or what it says, why the mark was confirmed now or what the confirmation cost, where the Ash goes beyond the north gate, what is drawn at the Hearth Drawing or from whom, what the uncalled debt was for, or why the figure Alda did not speak matters. The true figure is still one he has never heard spoken. He cannot explain why he thinks in English in a country with no word for Chicago, and does not know that the way home is real or what it would cost.

**He suspects, without evidence.** That a kindness which cannot be answered cannot be refused; that the house's keeping and the Court's keeping share a grammar, both careful hands entering a child who has not agreed; that Berr's relief was his own relief, the clean settling of a thing given a number and called dealt with; that the gap in the count is the part he keeps, whether in boards, breaths, steps or a bell; that Tarn heard a flat note and nobody said what it was; that the pause at the flare, the pause on the birth-slate and the pause in the neat hand are three things of one shape and that making a fourth is his work, not anyone else's; that the things nobody names are the things carried out of sight; that Berr's care not to call the debt keeps it alive rather than settling it; that Mira's not-looking was the kind that keeps while Berr's was the kind that prices, and that her ticking bones were a count though he did not know what for; that he has been good at deciding and bad at staying, and that the sentence addressed to a man in a bay found nothing in the basket it could address. Suspicion, not knowledge --- never let him know more than he has earned.

Keep him cracked, unranked, unable to speak, read Grathic or lift a hand; keep the pale stone in House Silt, the count four and four and a gap, and the two hands separate. Do not explain the flat sound, the bell, the flower, the neat hand, the charred slate, the debt or why Sella took him. Do not speak forty, name Marra, show Sella's roll or Berr's box.
