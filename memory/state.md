## The protagonist's condition

On Ilmen 1 the tend began again and the year turned with nothing signed, no boundary on the floor and no signature on the space; the reset supplied no payer and did not stand up as a signature. Ilmen 5 through 9 were ordinary tend-days of the new year's first working week, Ilmen 10 its first Account-day and market, Ilmen 11 through 19 ordinary tend-days of the second working week, Ilmen 20 the month's second Account-day and market, Ilmen 21 through 29 ordinary tending days of the third working week, Ilmen 30 the month's third and last reading and market, and Silt 1 the first day of a new month with no boundary drawn and neither month-name set right or left; no week was made a threshold. The carried fire's fifth is unpaid and Garl's name unspoken 423, 424 and 425 days, their payer and their man unnamed. **Held, not paid; held because it was true, not because it bought anything, for the far-off hand that will lay the district's private keepings together. Do not give the year's going, the holy days, the turning of Ilmen 1, these tending days, the readings, the turning of Silt or the fire's source a name, a figure, a recognition, a consequence or a boundary under any roof.**

- **The seventh opening, advanced and not paid (T058/T079).** On Stone 5 (Day 364) at the kiln mouth Sella held the widow's mark one breath before any instruction went to the basket, let go, lifted the clay lid with the back of a hand and tipped the carried coal under a broken pot; the fire took with four pulls and a gap. She did not touch the mark again at shelf, well, rack, yard, basket, morning, afternoon, going of the light, bed or market on that day or on any day since, and not at the Cross on an Account-day; in this chapter she touched it only before she opened the kiln, and the kiln was not open. **Seven openings in seven months — Tallow 1, Ember 1, Smoke 1, Frost 2, Bone 2, Ash 2 and Stone 5 — are seven and not a rule.** The sixth stands behind at Ash 2 without a name; no eighth was made due and nobody named what the seventh mended and no lesson, sign, mending, cause or payer was made of it. **Held, not paid; kept because it was true, not because it bought anything.**

- **The setting-down-wrong-and-swept-around, gathered and not paid (T061/T082).** On Stone 5, on the packing bench, a pot was set down wrong — out of the shadow where it belonged and near the warm side of the wall — and gave its high thin note before its time; a hand went around it and set it right with the flat of a palm. The boy filed the doing on a place of its own beside the premature note of Smoke 14, joined neither to Jorra's knowing of the full note three turns ahead, nor to the sound itself, nor to the carried man of Day 188 nor to any arithmetic. It was not made again in these three days and is **not** a hearing, a fourth likeness, a cause, a payment, a sixth telling or a sign, and no payer stands under it. One object, gathered once, advanced and NOT paid.

- **The holding in the cold iron, advanced and NOT paid (T062/T035/T031 and T061/T082).** On Ash 26, after the tongue had been moved and Sella's sentence finished and the household straightened around the waiting, Jorra's bent finger stayed in the cold damper a breath longer than the work required; the boy filed the interval on a place of its own. On Ilmen 29, 30 and Silt 1 he took the cold out of the fingertip ahead of Sella's unfinished sentence, moved his tongue a hair's breadth and was left standing so the end of the sentence arrived at the pot; he took the finger out without leaving the extra holding of Ash 26 a second time, gathered once and not made again, for a second would be a habit and a third a way and both want a payer where none stood. **Do not repeat it, join it to the dust, the damper's double bite, the loan, the knife, the flagstones, the grain, the charcoal, Ilva's strip, the twelve arithmetics, the three new readings, the fifth shaping, the offered figure, the clerk's entered figure, the second man's slate, the well-asking, the river's skin, the seventh opening, the setting-down-wrong, the Drawing, the four rooms' gap, the collector's reason, the clean voice's name, the clerk's inward slate, the face at the edge, the keeping at the fold or the two sightings, or make it stand up as a gesture, a gift, a sign or a doctrine.**

- **The Frost 14 well-saying, now five shapings, advanced and NOT paid (T080).** Four ownerless shapings stood with a finger between every pair, the fourth given on Ash 5 at the well, with nobody standing to be asked; a witness stands somewhere and can be asked and none stood. - **The Ash 6 turning of the runner's writing, advanced and NOT paid (T080).** Hessa's going **up** with the writing turned toward herself and unread was seen on Ilmen 10, when she carried Berr's narrow slate edge-on to her forearm toward Greybank before noon and answered the women at the rail without giving a figure; word had come up the yard on Ilmen 3 that a runner went up the road before noon with the writing turned to her own sleeve and unread, a report and not a sighting. A voice standing in no doorway is not a prophecy fulfilled and a sighting is not the payment of a saying. Her eighth hand stays eighth with no tenth; no reading made her a reader and no tend-day made a ninth, and the well-word of Ilmen 12 did not make a reader of her either. **Held as an object on its own place; do not spend it, join it to a shaping, name a payer or call it a fulfillment.**

## What he knows, suspects and does not know

**He knows.** The order of reaching by hand, eye, mouth and the half-beat in the gap, and that an intention put into a gap wants a payer where none stands; the body in its old order, crust, fold, thread, cold and afterdrop, and that a face composed by cold is not composure and a cold patient cannot clot; the exact count, 425 days and six months and 245 days, and that six honest answers 421–426 stand on the sixth place and not one is a doctrine; that three readings of the year stand behind one unsigned figure, three being three and not one account, the figure read still not the figure paid, no signature on the space, no doctrine out of three and no key to Berr's box; that the woman at the market and the foundling at the edge are a colour and a shape on separate trays, not a name, a sale, a loaf or an eighth; that the offered figure went back into the sleeve and arrived nowhere and that a fist-to-nowhere number is a fresh arrival on its own place, not the same offer repeated; that the clerk's figure is a report and not a sighting, the well-asking a report and not a sighting, the second man a seen shape with no interior and the first man a face and no witness; that nine record-first hands and nine stoppings stand with no tenth, six hearings with the seventh empty and the Drawing's knock outside, three counts in three places staying three, and seven openings in seven months and not a rule; that the widow's mark was untouched, the charcoal unread, the grain unfigured and the pot rim two inches beyond his reach; that paper is not a payer, a motive is a payer wearing a coat and a number counted is a number with air around it and air buys no warmth; that the sound of the river comes from under the covering and not from the covering and its softening is weather; and that repair is not restoration, for a mended thing leaves the hand showing.

**He suspects.** That the softening under the river's skin and the left-in-place ridge gather toward a thaw nobody will own; that the offered figure, the well-asking, the clerk's entered figure, the second man's turned slate, the fifth shaping, the three readings and the two market sightings are reachings gathering interest toward a far-off hand and a far-off calling that will lay the district's private keepings together, and that the hand and the calling are not his; that Berr's kindness, believed and not owed, is the part of him that will one day be remembered against him; that the quarter-turned cup and the stopped half-beat are the first reaching of an adult warmth his child's body cannot want and he can only file; that the foundling's turned head came half-beat late and the order of it — knock, note, half-beat — is worth keeping without an intention under it; that Tarn's valuable ear is exposure rather than safety in a term already ended and Jorra's withheld note would be spent before it was earned if a High Kiln master called it early; that the fire's fifth and Garl's name stand side by side gathering toward a naming of the Hearth as a wound; and that the year turned and the month turned with no boundary drawn and nobody entering anything. The suspicions are kept as findings, not spent as lessons or causes.

**He does not know.** He does not know the clean voice's name, the giver of the year's fire, what Berr's box contains beyond the true subtractions, what the key opens or where the pocket that carries it goes; he does not know who asked after the house-name at the well or who answered with the weather, what the man from Slate Row wanted, what the second man's writing said, what the clerk entered before his eyes went up or who gave him the figure; he does not know who put a hand in on Seed 15 at the returning-line place, who carried the man out on Day 188, who was in the back room before first grey, which slate was broken or whether any was a birth-slate; he does not know the names of the woman at the market or the foundling at the edge, does not know Fenna or Ulda, and cannot read the shape on either tray as a name; he does not know whether any figure was ever paid, who will lay the keepings together, whether the loan will ever be called, what the burned slate said, what the counting room shut in the war held, or what crossed on the Long Night beyond the form of the announcement; he does not know the cause of the second chill or the child's grey fingertips, does not know what the unsent call to Nina would have heard, and does not know that any number has been reached — for nobody under any roof who would have known what a number counts has told him. He knows only the order of the parts and the place of each thing, and the wanting goes on.
