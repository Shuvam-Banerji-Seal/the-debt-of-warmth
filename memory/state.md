## The moment

Day 266, Smoke 27, Seventhday, Y.H. 813, the small hours, under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof in \place{Low Kiln}. \person[Corin]{the boy} lies on \place{the bed} at the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf}, awake and silent, in the room that does not touch the room of the fire, with the fold at his ear and one stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust two inches from it; the empty place beside the crust keeps its name to itself and no third came in the afternoon to make the row come out even. The thread of drool at his mouth has frozen in the dry heat and is unwiped, because nobody under any roof has ever been given an instruction about the thread and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. Cold has stopped at cheek, temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rides back, as though it had come to the end of a line written out for it and had neither an order to go further nor an order to go away; the afterdrop waits beyond all three, past a warming nobody in this house will perform. ## What he knows, suspects and does not know

- **Knows, exactly and uselessly.** His count: two hundred and sixty-four through two hundred and sixty-six days --- six months and eighty-four days through six months and eighty-six days --- kept edge to edge against a house that keeps its days in loaves, glances and unnamed spaces; the more exact it grows the more it isolates him, and he has no hand to lay it down and no voice with which to offer it. The order in which cold arrives --- cheek, temple, wrist, the wool riding back --- and the order in which a body takes heat back, the small joint first and the core last; that a cold patient cannot clot, that you do not rub a frozen foot and do not set it to the fire, and that all of it buys nothing without a hand two paces long. The shape of a reading said over a body that cannot answer, now said at five readings from Grain 10 through Smoke 20 and still not the figure paid, and the grammar of taking the speaker out of a sentence with *your house* rather than \emph{I} --- heard at every reading over the same basket from the same man, and called a kindness. That a district's arithmetic can be changed in a square where two hundred people stand and carried home in a locked box to two hundred tables with two figures in it, one printed and one paid, and a space between them nobody signed; that the reading knows the cover of the thing under the wool and not the thing, and that a thing covered is not therefore paid for. That an arm can know what a handful weighs and not what it cost, and a slate can know the figure and not the grain. That a thing can be examined as closely as a hand can examine it and still enter nothing --- the grain turned piece by piece for damp and rot and set down where it lay, the not-entering as complete as the examining --- and that a week can go out having learned nothing from a reading and keep the nothing as a finding with no payer. That a report of an asking is not an asking and that taking it apart finds no witness at the bottom of it. That the extremity goes before the core and the afterdrop waits beyond a warming nobody here will perform, and that having no word for it does not make it cease. That a pot set down wrong gives its note before its time and that the note is neither a hearing nor a seventh; that a fist arriving nowhere is a chart turned face-down and a supplied figure a forged one; that frost called weather is a permission to stop looking, and a sweet glaze wind touching the fold a direction arrived nowhere; that two intervals laid apart are not one interval laid across another, and a hand going on past a note is not thereby a longer reach or a power.
