## What he knows, suspects and does not know

- **Knows, exactly and uselessly.** His count: three hundred and twenty-seven through three hundred and twenty-nine days --- six months and one hundred and forty-seven through one hundred and forty-nine days --- kept edge to edge against a house that keeps its days in loaves, glances and unnamed spaces; the round three hundred was a threshold kept on a place of its own and not spent, and he has stopped expecting to be told what any of the numbers count, which is not the same as not wanting it. The order in which cold arrives --- cheek, temple, wrist, the wool riding back --- and the order in which a body takes heat back, the small joint first and the core last; that a cold patient cannot clot, that you do not rub a frozen foot and do not set it to the fire, that a tourniquet let go too soon weeps into the dressing, and that all of it buys nothing without a hand two paces long. That a person's face can be composed because the cold has composed it, and that the pink returning to the skin is the core still cold. The shape of a reading said over a body that cannot answer, now said at the account-days from Grain 10 through Bone 30 (twice on Frost 30, Bone 10, Bone 20 and Bone 30) and still not the figure paid, and the grammar of taking the speaker out of a sentence with *your house* rather than *I* --- heard at every reading over the same basket from the same man, and called a kindness; and now the smaller movement of taking himself out of the sentence as a surgeon draws a hand from a field before the dressing goes on. That a district's arithmetic can be changed in a square where two hundred people stand and carried home in a locked box to two hundred tables with two figures in it, one printed and one paid, and a space between them nobody signed; that a figure can be sent over a basket twice, once for those who stood and once for those who came late, and be one figure spoken two times; that **six readings are six and not one account**, and a rule made from six wants a payer put under all six and none stood, so no rule stood up; that the finding kept on a place of its own with no payer is not a lesson, a grievance, a mending, a doctrine or a rule; that a person can carry a record for years and still not be its reader, and say so with *I don't read it. I carry it. There's a difference, and the road doesn't care which*; that a thane going up the road with his box locked and the cord of his office folded is not thereby a collector; that a house keeps by not-doings, and that the keeping is maintenance and not a story; that a touch which took nothing and paid nothing can be kept because it was true and not because it bought anything; that two people keeping the same sound under one roof are not one sound, and two likenesses are not a cause and a cause wants a payer standing under it, and none stood; that attention is the one thing no institution can tax.
