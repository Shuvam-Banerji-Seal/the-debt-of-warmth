## The moment

Day 290, Frost 21, Firstday, Y.H. 813, the small hours, under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill}, \place{Grath}. \person[Corin]{The boy} lies on \place{the bed} at the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf}, awake and silent, carried there at the going of the light by \person{Sella Vail} and set under the roof and never in \place{the Debt Loft}, with the fold at his ear and one stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust two inches from it; the empty place beside the crust keeps its name to itself and no third came in these three days to make the row come out even. The thread of drool at his mouth has frozen in the dry heat and is unwiped, because nobody under any roof has ever been given an instruction about the thread and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. Cold has stopped at cheek, temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rides back, as though it had come to the end of a line written out for it and had neither an order to go further nor an order to go away; the afterdrop waits beyond all three, past a warming nobody in this house will perform. He knows the composed face that is not composure, knows a cold patient cannot clot and will seem better before he is worse, and that a man must be warmed from the outside and slowly. He wants the crust lifted the two inches to his mouth and cannot close two inches on the pot handle set beyond his reach. Both are true and the second is louder; the surgeon takes the inventory in its old order --- crust, gap, thread, cold, the waiting --- and enters it nowhere, not even on the place where he keeps the things he does not enter. He had once reduced a shoulder in the dark by feel alone and now cannot lift a crust to his mouth; the thought came and went without bitterness, which is its own small betrayal. There are two men under the roof and only one of them has a body: the older keeps the inventory, the younger keeps the wanting, and neither has authority over the other.

Through the finger's-width door the river keeps its note of \lore{water over water}, a low thing over a lower thing, never numbered among the hearings; under the floor \lore{the carried fire} gives four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer without a name for two hundred and ninety days, and nobody lifts the floor and nobody supplies the pull; overhead \place{the boards} keep four nails on the near board, four on the next and a thumb-width knot where the fifth nail should have stood. Three counts in three places, and none rises into another, because agreeing any two would want a payer put under both and no payer stands to any. No fire-sitter stands over the coals and no house count comes to be borrowed. Six hearings of \lore{the full note} stand beside and never among, five from four sources and the sixth, the small flat knock out of \person[Dessa Silt] as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go and never with the things that came from vessels; the empty place keeps its emptiness and none was invented to fill it. A woman's low counting comes from the next room whose floor does not touch his --- the small intake, the hold, the letting-go --- and he takes only what the hand can take and leaves the letting-go where it was; it is her breath and her count and not his.

At the close he has laid the hand down against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. On the hundred and fifty-sixth answer it took the held interval and the empty interval from the file, set them side by side with a finger between, borrowed no house count, supplied no fifth to the kiln, crossed no silence that belonged to another's breathing and came down on the river's next low note of water over water. Neither interval was promoted into a reach, a hearing, a gift, a keeping, a seventh, a stopping, a payment, a held change, a key, a power, a freedom, a doctrine or a rule; he made nothing of them and would not let them stand up as a gift or a doctrine, for a gift wants a taker and a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stands. **One hundred and fifty-six** honest answers stand on the sixth place, each an object and not a power, exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air; a round threshold did not stand up and call itself a doctrine. The file lies open on \place{the towel} at the head of the bed with every gap left gap, every tray parted by a finger's width and nothing that had moved. The river's low note goes quiet after the hand comes down.

Two hundred and ninety stands exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in \place{Sarn}, \place{Sill} or \place{Grath}; six months and one hundred and ten days are told by nobody; the fire's fifth is unpaid the same number of days. \lore{The widow's mark} was touched once before the kiln on Frost 2, held one breath, let go, and touched at no shelf, well, rack, road, basket, yard, morning, hearth, afternoon, dusk, reading or going of the light on Frost 19, 20 or 21, because none of these is an opening and the hand goes to the mark before the kiln and at no other hour. Frost goes on without a boundary drawn and neither of the district's two habits of naming its months --- the older women by the weather, the slatemen by the number of Account-days --- was set right or left wrong in so many words. Frost's two arithmetics stand behind the household with no lesson, mending anybody would name or payer; Frost 30 (Day 299) stands nine mornings ahead and was not brought into any day that had not earned it. ## The protagonist's condition

**Body.** \person{Corin}, two hundred and ninety days old at the close of Day 290 and six months and one hundred and ten days (288 and six months and one hundred and eight on Frost 19, 289 and six months and one hundred and nine on Frost 20, 290 and six months and one hundred and ten on Frost 21), carried everywhere: on all three days about the yard at the hearth, loaf shelf, drying racks and rack end, and down and up at the going of the light; on Frost 20 down the clay road to the Cross and back; set on his bed under the roof each night, never in \place{the Debt Loft}; the instruction went to the fire, the wool, the pot, the broom and the running water and never to the basket. He was \person{Daniel Marchetti}, forty, twelve of them a trauma surgeon at Cook County; he died going back into a burning minivan for a child under the dashboard and his warmth left him in the giving and did not cool. As Corin he is a cracked, low-capacity vessel, unranked --- below Thin, twelve to fifteen embers at best, with a flaw nobody has named and a rate of loss nobody has measured. He cannot roll, stand, speak, read \lore{Grathic} in speech or writing, reach a cup, close two inches on the rim of the basket that holds him, lift a hand to his own chin or lift a crust two inches to his mouth. He drools; the thread at his mouth froze in the dry heat and was left unwiped on all three days. Frost 22 is an ordinary tending day with no market, Cross or bell: the yard goes around the seven walked-around places, the two unswept lines and the returning-line hand; the grain turns for damp with no figure; Tarn's seventh year stands at its very end and still ungiven; Jorra's promise stays sealed and \emph{he's not Vail} stays sealed; the row stays at five askings with one shaping and no witness; the two shapings of the Frost 14 saying stay held with a finger between them and between the second and Hessa's eighth hand, and **no third shaping is to be supplied**. The hand will answer six times, the **one hundred and fifty-seventh** through the **one hundred and sixty-second**, and the count will reach **291 exact**. Frost 30 (Day 299), the month's third and last Account-day and market, stands eight mornings ahead and is not to be brought into a day that has not earned it. Do not let a round number of answers, four months' openings, two shapings, two arithmetics or a hundred and sixty-two hands stand up as a doctrine, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stands. Nothing may be opened, joined, named, called, paid or ended on an unearned day.
