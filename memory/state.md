## What he knows, suspects and does not know

**He knows.** The order of reaching by hand, eye, mouth and the half-beat in the gap, and that an intention put into a gap wants a payer where none stands; the body in its old order, crust, fold, thread, cold and afterdrop, and that a face composed by cold is not composure and a cold patient cannot clot; the exact count, 428 days and six months and 248 days, and that six honest answers 427–432 stand on the sixth place and not one is a doctrine; that three readings of the year stand behind one unsigned figure, three being three and not one account, the figure read still not the figure paid, no signature on the space, no doctrine out of three and no key to Berr's box; that the woman at the market and the foundling at the edge are a colour and a shape on separate trays, not a name, a sale, a loaf or an eighth; that the neighbour's finger went from the shelf toward the ear and stopped a finger's width short, that she made two rooms one absence in one breath without seeing it and named both gaps wrongly, and that the reaching is a shape on its own place and not a joining, a name, a figure, an account or a payer; that the offered figure went back into the sleeve and arrived nowhere and that a fist-to-nowhere number is a fresh arrival on its own place, not the same offer repeated; that the clerk's figure is a report and not a sighting, the well-asking a report and not a sighting, the second man a seen shape with no interior and the first man a face and no witness; that nine record-first hands and nine stoppings stand with no tenth, six hearings with the seventh empty and the Drawing's knock outside, three counts in three places staying three, and seven openings in seven months and not a rule; that the widow's mark was untouched, the charcoal unread, the grain unfigured and the pot rim two inches beyond his reach; that paper is not a payer, a motive is a payer wearing a coat and a number counted is a number with air around it and air buys no warmth; that the sound of the river comes from under the covering and not from the covering and its softening is weather; that repair is not restoration, for a mended thing leaves the hand showing; and that a line that looks joined can still be a line that will not hold.

**He suspects.** That the neighbour's near-reaching at the shelf gathers toward the far-off hand that will lay shelf and fold together and call the joining an account, and that hand is not his; that the softening under the river's skin and the left-in-place ridge gather toward a thaw nobody will own; that the offered figure, the well-asking, the clerk's entered figure, the second man's turned slate, the fifth shaping, the three readings and the two market sightings are reachings gathering interest toward a far-off hand and a far-off calling that will lay the district's private keepings together, and that the hand and the calling are not his; that Berr's kindness, believed and not owed, is the part of him that will one day be remembered against him; that the quarter-turned cup and the stopped half-beat are the first reaching of an adult warmth his child's body cannot want and he can only file; that the foundling's turned head came half-beat late and the order of it — knock, note, half-beat — is worth keeping without an intention under it; that Tarn's valuable ear is exposure rather than safety in a term already ended and Jorra's withheld note would be spent before it was earned if a High Kiln master called it early; that the fire's fifth and Garl's name stand side by side gathering toward a naming of the Hearth as a wound; and that the year turned and the month turned with no boundary drawn and nobody entering anything. The suspicions are kept as findings, not spent as lessons or causes.

**He does not know.** He does not know the clean voice's name, the giver of the year's fire, what Berr's box contains beyond the true subtractions, what the key opens or where the pocket that carries it goes; he does not know who asked after the house-name at the well or who answered with the weather, what the man from Slate Row wanted, what the second man's writing said, what the clerk entered before his eyes went up or who gave him the figure; he does not know who put a hand in on Seed 15 at the returning-line place, who carried the man out on Day 188, who was in the back room before first grey, which slate was broken or whether any was a birth-slate; he does not know the neighbour's name or what she truly meant by the month's turn and a child's appetite, does not know that what she said joined two rooms, does not know the names of the woman at the market or the foundling at the edge, does not know Fenna or Ulda, and cannot read the shape on either tray as a name; he does not know whether any figure was ever paid, who will lay the keepings together, whether the loan will ever be called, what the burned slate said, what the counting room shut in the war held, or what crossed on the Long Night beyond the form of the announcement; he does not know the cause of the second chill or the child's grey fingertips, does not know what the unsent call to Nina would have heard, and does not know that any number has been reached — for nobody under any roof who would have known what a number counts has told him. He knows only the order of the parts and the place of each thing, and the wanting goes on.
