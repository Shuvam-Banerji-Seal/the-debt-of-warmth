## The protagonist's condition

**Body.** \person{Corin}, four hundred and seven days old at the close of Day 407 — six months and two hundred and twenty-seven days (405 and six months and 225, 406 and six months and 226, 407 and six months and 227) — carried everywhere: down the nineteen steps to the Clay Yard on the mornings and afternoons of Ilmen 11, 12 and 13 and up again at each going of the light, set under the roof and never in \place{the Debt Loft}; the instruction went to the wool, the river wind, the pot, the fire and the banked fire and never to the basket, and on no day was the basket told what the day was or told to be still. He was \person{Daniel Marchetti}, forty, twelve of them a trauma surgeon at Cook County; he died going back into a burning minivan for a child under the dashboard and his warmth left him in the giving and did not cool. As Corin he is a cracked, low-capacity vessel, unranked — below Thin, twelve to fifteen embers at best, with a flaw nobody has named and a rate of loss nobody has measured. He cannot roll, stand, speak, read \place{Grathic} in speech or writing, reach a cup, close two inches on the rim of the basket that holds him, lift a hand to his own chin or lift a crust two inches to his own mouth. He drools; the thread at his mouth froze in the dry kiln heat, went stiffer through the dry nights and was left unwiped on all three days, because nobody under any roof has ever been given an instruction about the thread. Cold comes to cheek, temple and the small bare joint at the wrist where the wool rides back and stops there as if it had reached the end of its own instruction; the afterdrop waits past a warming nobody in this house will perform. He knows that a face composed by cold is not composure, that the grimace of severe cold wears the shape of a smile, that pink returning is the surface still warm over a cold core, that a cold patient cannot clot and must be warmed from outside and slowly, never hurried, never rubbed, never set too near the fire, and that the order of giving up heat is the extremities before the core and the order of taking it back the reverse, but there is no hand two paces long to put any of it to work. A surgeon who cannot lift a crust two inches to his own mouth is a surgeon who has become a field, and a field does not dress itself.

**Mind.** The double consciousness has acquired three more days of objects and six more answers from the hand, and still no hand to enter any of them. - **Corin/Dan** — on his bed under the roof with the fold at his ear, one crust with its corner hair-lifted two inches off, the empty place beside it holding the fold put there once on the fifteenth and found where put through Ilmen 11, 12 and 13, still without a name and with no third and no fourth crust, the thread frozen and unwiped, cold stopped at cheek, temple and bare wrist-joint and the afterdrop waiting. Awake, silent, hand outside the wool having just let a small intake go whole on the river's note under its winter skin and laid itself back against the fold on the **three hundred and ninetieth** answer — exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air and filed on the sixth place with no payer named, not spent or explained, the sixth answer in a row and not one of them a doctrine. Four hundred and seven days old and six months and two hundred and twenty-seven days, told by nobody that a number has been reached. Wants the crust lifted two inches, the thread wiped, a hand on the pot handle and a number spoken by somebody who would have known what a number counted; gets none and cannot ask for any. Holds six hearings with no seventh and the Drawing's flat knock outside, the heard bell beside the told with both gaps in their own squares, nine record-first hands and nine stoppings with no tenth, the fifth shaping on its own tray between the eighth hand and the road saying, the clerk's inward slate and Tarn's degree-turn each on its own place, Berr's low saying about kindness and his securely wiped thumb each on a place of its own, the man from Slate Row's face filed and not counted among the hands, the green at the pot board and the shape at the market's edge with no eighth of either, twelve arithmetics behind one unsigned figure beside the first of a fresh count with no key to Berr's box, the figure read over covered grain that does not know its cost, the silence after Weighing's name, the Long Night story's empty name, the clean voice's missing name and the place the Remembering did not fill on the gap tray, the neighbour's called-gone grey beside the left-in-place ridge, Jorra's promise neither given nor withdrawn, Garl's unforged collector sentence beside the seventh notch, the covered grain and the two bites of iron, and the keeping at the fold found where put. - **Sella Vail**, 33, keeper of the Vail fire — under the roof at the close after the going of the light, her hands never still when they had nothing to do, counting spoons, nails and the breaths of a child nobody had numbered and coming back to the hairline at the wire; what she said low went to the pot and the fire, and when she spoke of the boy it was *the boy* and when she spoke of what she meant to do it was *the house*, because a thing said aloud becomes a thing the Court can enter. On all three days she went to the hearth before the basket, told the coals it was the thaw in the voice another woman would have used for a child who could not hear her, turned them with the back of a broken pot, gave the small intake, the hold and the letting-go toward no opening with neither gap figured, kept her finger along the top of the loaf shelf so the charcoal tally stayed unread, moved her lips on a two-gapped row unfigured, glanced once at the clay jar with the hand half over nothing and the little-door burn closing on air, and did not touch the widow's mark. She lifted the twice-mended pot thumb-first where the wire crossed the crack, left the hairline showing, said both proverbs to pot and fire and never to a person, turned the dressed grain under wool with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child and no figure or handful, and set the pot rim two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat and no look toward the bed. She carried him down and up the nineteen steps at the half-pace from every open slate, went wide around the seven walked-around places with a look and a wide step once and not twice, widest around the returning-line place, set him under the roof and never in the Debt Loft, put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face and added no second fold, and went back to her fire saying nothing at all — not of the well-word, not of the arithmetic up the road, not of the pot's crack. She wants the house kept as a house keeps what it has, with care and one crack nobody mentions, and she does not want a thing said aloud that the Court could enter.

- **Tarn**, 22, the Vail's clay-bound hand — at the kindling and flagstones on all three days, offstage at the close and found where put. Six pieces stood in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check and the seventh at the lip beyond it, beside \person{Garl Vail}'s unforged collector sentence with no name on the man who turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on \lore{the tile debt}; the stack kept its own arithmetic of four, four and a space, and the space was not a fifth and was not filled. The small knife lay at the very edge of its sill and his hand went over blade and sill as a man passes a hand over a tool put back where it belongs, and came away with no breath held at the top of the reach — the coming away the whole of the checking. He cut nothing, counted nothing, spent nothing, and said to the wood in the tone of a man stating the weather, *Six counted. The next I don't count.* He took the broom only after the flagstones had been given their instruction and said *Yes, house* to the question and to the thing behind it, agreeing so thoroughly the question had nowhere left to put a foot, and went over the stones without a word and without a turn of the head toward the landing. His chest had been tight since the dark, Ash worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line, the pale clip-ring at his left wrist catching and giving back what little light there was, his stroke over the flagstones going without the half-check; he was helpful when he was frightened and on these days he was not frightened, and the household read the quiet of him the way it read the quiet of Sella. The ear-sentence about an ear under this roof being worth more than the debt came all the way to the word that would call the loan and stopped there: a remembered ninth stopping with no tenth, no figure and no name. The \emph{Seed 16} breath was drawn and left unspent at the top of the reach; the seventh year had run to its very end and the term was already ended, a term ending being exposure and not freedom, the loan uncalled and a man more exposed than the day before, the fear of being entered again for vagrancy his without a figure, and a want he did not voice for a slate with his own name and no mark against it. ### The Account-days and the world asleep

## Knows, exactly and uselessly

His count: 405 through 407 days — six months and 225 through 227 days — kept edge to edge against a house that keeps its days in loaves, glances and unnamed spaces; **three hundred and ninety answers** stand on the sixth place, six honest answers (385 through 390) from these three days and not one a doctrine, and 390 is a hundred and ninety more than the year's two hundred, a number that crosses from Y.H. 813 into Y.H. 814 with no threshold anybody crosses, and he has stopped expecting to be told what any of the numbers count, which is not the same as not wanting it. That a holy day arrives the way weather arrives, with a name and no tend-day and no market, and that an hour earns what comes in it and not what is coming to it — he did not bring the landing's word, the face at the edge, the arithmetic of Ilmen 10, the kinder saying at the block, the well-word or the morning's glaze into a day that had not earned them; that the Five ended without a boundary drawn and the year turned on Ilmen 1 with nothing signed; that the district's arithmetic changed on Ilmen 10, began again and cannot be undone, and that one reading is one and not an account, a reset is not a signing and a figure read is not a figure paid. The order in which cold arrives — cheek, temple, wrist, the wool riding back — and the order in which a body takes heat back and gives it up, the reverse and sooner than anybody likes; that pink returning is the surface still warm and the core still cold; that a cold patient cannot clot, that you do not rub a frozen foot and do not set it to the fire, that a tourniquet let go too soon weeps into the dressing, and that all of it buys nothing without a hand two paces long.
