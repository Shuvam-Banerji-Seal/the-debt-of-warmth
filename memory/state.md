## The moment

Day 67 is done. On Silt 8, Eighthday, Y.H. 813, at dusk, \person{Sella Vail} carried the boy up \place{the Clay Yard} from the edge of \place{the Ilm} at a half-pace from every open slate, set him on his bed under the \place{Vail kiln-house} roof, not in \place{the Debt Loft}, and put the fold right without looking at his face. He is \person{Corin}, sixty-seven days old, two months and seven days, carried everywhere in a basket that is not his, with **one** brown-edged crust in the fold where two had lain and the empty place at his ear beside it, still carried without a name. There was a dryness in the small of his back at the last that had nothing to do with the cold and everything to do with the day, and he entered it nowhere, because no slate in this house would take it and he has no hand to put it on one. Behind him the carried fire lies under blind ash and the back of a broken pot, warming the boards by degrees and missing one breath in every four. Four, four and a gap. The door stands a finger's width open; through the gap comes the low note of the river moving under the ice, nearer than it had been at the well or under the boards in the afternoon, and through the floor comes the fire, the two sounds kept apart as they have been for two months. In the corner the new hairline of grey lies finer than the old clean dark line beside it, unswept because the house says it is the thaw and it will go when it goes. His hand outside the wool goes up an inch toward the water and comes down. No opening came in any of the seven days, and the widow's mark was not touched all chapter.

## The protagonist's condition

**Body.** \person{Corin}, sixty-seven days old at the close of Day 67, two months and seven days, carried everywhere: at the rack's edge in the yard, under the north bridge at the Fishstairs, three doors down just inside a finger's-width-open door in House Silt, in the well's thin shade, at the edge of the Ilm, and lifted onto the bed under the roof, never into the Debt Loft. He was \person{Daniel Marchetti}, forty, twelve of them a trauma surgeon at Cook County; he died going back into a burning minivan for a child under the dashboard and his warmth left him in the giving and did not cool. As Corin he is a cracked, low-capacity vessel, unranked --- below Thin, twelve to fifteen embers at best, with a flaw nobody has named and a rate of loss nobody has measured. He cannot roll, stand, speak, read \lore{Grathic}, reach a cup or lift a hand to his own chin. He drools; the thread at his mouth freezes in the small hours and nobody wipes it, because a basket set down is a pot set down and left. His head goes where it wants and makes the small wet sound when it goes too far; the hand outside the wool goes up an inch toward the warmth or the water and comes down; nothing within two paces can be reached. Cold arrives in its known order --- cheek, temple, wrist --- and the wool does less than the wind wants, riding back from his wrist so the cold finds the place. The branching red birth-flower runs from the third rib on the left down the forearm and stops short of the wrist; Alda crossed two fingers at it again on Day 64 without knowing it and covered the sign with a date. He wants the crust, wants the breast and wants the bucket, and can reach neither: the spent crust lies stiff in the fold and the warmth he most wanted is an empty place in the fold. A dryness in the small of his back on the last day is entered nowhere. Twenty-nine mornings ago a woman went into the water two paces from where he lay and he knew exactly how the vessel gave and could not lift a hand toward her.

**Mind.** The double consciousness has acquired seven more days of objects and still no hand to enter any of them. He knows the order of reaching --- the hand to the record before the hand to the person --- and has now seen it in a midwife, a neighbour at a well, a clerk behind a Court table, a man who priced him, Berr passing a finger over a figure, the soft-cuffed clerk at the Fishstairs, Alda going to her slate before the child, Mira moving eel-bones and then her thumb to the grey cord while deciding whether to lie. This chapter added: that water going over water is the river's own note, a low turning with no vessel in it, and the yard has no word for it; that *weather* does not name the water but names the permission to stop looking at it; that a district can say *honeyed all through* to the boards and let nobody own it; that a debt left lying is not a debt forgiven, only a debt nobody has picked up; that a loan that can hear is a loan that can be called, and two men under one roof can keep the same sentence unspoken; that a weak board is a gap in a place that must one day carry weight, a missing step a gap in stairs, a hairline stopping short a gap in a sheet of ice; that a man can move a damper with his anger and let the household straighten around the silence; that a sixth page can be written and turned down and change nothing for the woman who writes it or the child it is written about; and that a new hairline of grey can lie finer than a left-in-place line and be kept on two places without a word in Grathic for the difference. He knows the difference between a likeness and a cause because a better-lit room taught him the cost of getting it wrong, and he keeps the sealed-room saying beside but never on the kiln's gap; the told bell beside the heard bell, and both beside the early note and the kiln's missing breath and now the river's own note; six like rooms in six places; the six hearings beside and never among; **three** near-full answers advanced and not paid; the word on its tray, the shape of the double turn and the weight of a thumb apart from one another; and the clerk, the board, the stopping and widening hairlines, the barrow, the two ridges, the new grey and the honeyed saying in separate places. He knows Tarn turned his head a degree at a note and said nothing, and that two silences under one roof are nearer an equality than anything the house has a name for.

**Powers.** None usable, no rank change, no experiment, no clamp, no heresy. He cannot move Warm, cannot clamp, cannot speak, cannot make a fire, cannot read \lore{Grathic} in speech or writing, cannot enter a figure anywhere. Counting is an old-life habit, not a gift: the kiln's four, four and a gap; the bell's four, four and a gap with nobody crossing himself; the told bell in the dark; the Fishstairs' four, four and a landing where a step should have been; the boards' four, four and a knot; the death-house bench's four, four and a knot; the well-roof's four, four and a stone that does not ring; the kindling's four, four and a space; the firelight's four, four and an empty shelf. The ear still holds six hearings of \lore{the full note}: five from four sources --- a cup across the birth room, a pot in the \place{Vail wheel-shed} through the wall, the district's slate twice at \place{the Cross}, and a man giving at the Drawing --- and the sixth, the small flat knock out of \person{Dessa Silt} as the men lifted, from a person, filed beside the five and never among them, not a seventh. The near-full answer of Day 35 (Ilmen 6), the second of Day 52 (Ilmen 23) and the **third of Day 65** stand on their own tray, advanced and not paid, neither hearings nor the third thing. The three intervals (Stone 13, Stone 17, Stone 25) stay objects on a third tray. The word forty remains a saying, not a vessel's sound, on its own tray with the readable shape of the double turn beside it and the weight of a thumb on wicker apart from both; it was not spoken again this chapter. Water-over-water, the early Sill note, the told bell, the heard bell and the kiln's gap each kept their own place and no cause was written across any of them; four low sounds in four places stayed four. No Grathic word for the flat note, for *nearly*, for the river's own note, for the failure in a vessel, for a thing taken and not spoken of, or for the difference between a left line and a burned one.

**Possessions and money.** Nothing he can use. **Held/changed:** the one brown-edged barley crust put into the fold on Stone 15 remains, stiff and gone past smelling of anything, uneaten, unlifted, unnamed, carried through Silt; the empty place beside it has still not been named. The smoky crust from Mira on Stone 28 is gone since the afternoon of Day 46, taken quietly by a hand nobody will name, and neither the taking nor the absence was spoken of; Sella's, Alda's and Mira's hands stay on their tray and do not become a cause. The coal the brine-gull took on Day 45 is entered in no account and missed in none, twenty-two days ago now. **Newly gone, unmentioned:** the small pale river stone is no longer in the fold of grey wool on the abandoned board-bed in House Silt (observed on Day 64: only the fold and the hollow remained); nobody said it was gone, who took it or what became of it, and the boy let the house leave it unmentioned. **Held/unchanged:** the small bench knife is on its sill; the charred slate still lies in the kiln ash, black on one side and eaten into the shape of a strip of bark; the basket and wool are Sella's, lent without a word; the clay jar Sella fetched from House Silt stands on the wall shelf and is the house's. Alda's little tin is inside her bodice, touched again on Day 64 without opening; her **sixth** narrow slate is turned face-down toward a floorboard in a house the boy cannot name, and the hidden record now stands at six pages, all unread (the sixth paid on Day 64, not to be paid again). Sella's private roll is unshown; Berr's box is locked in his house with the true deduction inside; Mira's grey wool cord and the second cord twisted twice beneath it have knots uncounted and have never gone upriver to Nine Pots or been lifted at a door; Orra's knot-string with its forty-first knot hangs in the Eel House, unread, its shape legible to the boy without its name; \lore{the Ash} in the Vail corner is gone, burned on Ilmen 1 and called dealt with, and a new hairline of grey lies finer beside the old clean dark line and is not swept, because the house says it is the thaw.

**Wounds carried from the other life.** The unsent call to Nina --- nine summers, nine promises, a boat never bought --- was paid on Day 38, in past tense and untagged; nothing like it returned in chapter 14, and the old life stayed offstage. The chart with its note turned down and the hand going past it to a signature stood behind the order of reaching again; the flare light and the hand that stopped between two clusters had a fifth sighting on Stone 30 and have not had a sixth, and the Fishstairs clerk filed apart on Day 61 was not joined to that writer or to the grey-fingered drawing collector. His father's casket slightly crooked in the aisle; Elena's quiet leaving, Marisol's temperate one; Des's laugh did not return. In Sarn's terms he is an unclosed account heavy enough to crack a slate, and he has been entered in three accounts that never asked him --- the house's, the Court's and Dessa's count of bits --- of which the third is the quietest because nobody thinks it is an account at all, and is gone into the water with the number still in her thumb.

- **\person{Berr Clay}**, 48, Thane of Clay --- came up short at the Fishstairs on Day 61 with the red wool cord of his office folded under his coat and green of tasted glaze at one corner of his mouth, smelling of hot clay over nothing at all, wiped his thumb twice before touching nothing, held a narrow slate unopened and passed a finger over what was written, said *a number goes into a slate, not into a well; there'll be a slate at the watch*, took himself out of his own sentence and went off toward High Kiln with his box still locked and the true deduction inside. ## Open dangers

- The Slip Brook now runs clay-heavy and higher behind the racks, with a low note of water going over water that the district has no word for and calls the thaw. The Ilm runs under a skin so thin the dark current shows through in veins, the ice is honeyed all through and not only at the edge, more hairlines run in and stop a thumb's breadth short of the open water, the Singing Sill gives its note early, and a new hairline of grey lies beside the old clean dark line in the Vail corner, unswept because the house says it is the thaw. A weak board in the plank walk at the Weir is still there, will not carry a man when the river comes up, and has been trodden by nobody. The clerk at the Fishstairs has sat with his slate for a measure nobody called and his hand to slate before eyes to the stalls. The district has one word, *weather*, that lets a person go home warm without owning a day. He knows the anatomy of a vessel going thin and has no Grathic word for the failure and no hand to point at it. The year's flood is close; nobody has joined any of these signs to a house.
- A man described, over a bucket at the well in the last chapter, a room shut in the last war with the damper closed and the men inside dead of the breath, their heat gone out with no way to feed it. The yard received it as history with a date; Sella, who lost a husband to the same danger, heard it and told the fire it was the thaw. The boy filed the saying on its own tray beside the told bell, the heard bell, the early note and the kiln's gap and wrote no cause across them. It is a description of the danger under his own roof carried into a yard that had no use for it, and no one to receive it; planted toward ~650 (T065). The house keeps its own low word for the kiln's breath, which names the danger and the mercy in one breath. The carried fire is still under blind ash and the back of a broken pot, warming the boards by degrees and missing one breath in every four; the coal that went up with the brine-gull on Day 45 is entered nowhere and missed in none.
- No collector came for the woman in the brook and nobody named the omission; twenty-nine mornings gone and still unentered. Warmth can be left where it fell and the morning still comes: the first edge of a heresy that will one day refuse to collect, planted as something simply not done, with no word, no doctrine and no argument. The boy is inside three accounts that never asked him --- the house's, the Court's and Dessa's count of bits --- and the third is gone into the water with the number still in her thumb.
- The number still goes around in fists, mouths, shoulders, loaves, glances and weather: one in twelve printed and unchallenged, the true subtraction unspoken, Berr's figure that goes into a slate and not into a well, the number for the thaw passed from fist to mouth to shoulder and given to nobody, the eel figure that went over the basket into the noise of the stalls, and the house's arithmetic with a space at the end of the loaf row that nobody calls a number. Sella's private roll is still unshown; Berr's box stays locked; Mira's two cords and Orra's knot-string are records no Court can confiscate and no slate can enter, and the knots are still uncounted; Alda's **six** written notes lie turned down where no mother or officer will read them; the pewter tin above the empty bed holds its pale fold and thumb's bright rub, unopened, and the little tin in Alda's bodice is shut. The neat upper line has been seen five times and the writer still has no name in his file; the Drawing collector's ordinary hand and the Fishstairs clerk's ordinary order stay filed apart. The word forty was spoken once, low, by a woman who watched him, and was not spoken again this chapter.
- Orra tied a forty-first knot over the story of a small warm bundle with a flower and said the number into the basket and watched; the cord hangs in the Eel House where he cannot enter or read it, though he can read its shape without its name. A woman has kept a count in wool for three years and knows something about a child carried up the Clay Yard in the year of the flower, and she is watching still to see what the boy does with a word he cannot answer. The shape of the knot is on its tray beside the word and the weight of a thumb apart from both, and the watching has had no answer.
- One of the two crusts in the fold is gone and nobody says who took it, that it is gone, or what became of it; the spent one lies stiff beside an empty place that has crossed into Silt without a name. The pale river stone is gone from the fold in House Silt and nobody says it is gone either. A coal went up with a brine-gull on Day 45 and one warmth less went out of the world with no slate the poorer. These are subtractions entered in no account, and the boy keeps all three without a word in \lore{Grathic} for any of them. A kindness that could not be answered has become an absence that cannot be answered.
- Sella's widow's mark stands confirmed by a factor at Greybank and entered as a kindness; it was not touched once in seven days, because no opening came and a rack, a basket, a well and a door are not an opening. Garl's uncalled debt against the tiles remains never paid, never settled, never done with; his name was not said under the Vail roof on the Remembering and his objects keep his place. His clean dark line is not swept, because a line left where it is is still a line kept, and the new finer grey beside it is not swept either, because the house says it is thaw and will go when it goes.
- Tarn has heard flat notes and said nothing, has seen the boy hear one before it happened and said nothing about that either, has swept the road to a collector without turning and noticed that he has noticed. A loan that can hear is a loan that can be called; two people keeping the same silence under one roof is an equality neither can name, and a man whose ear is worth more than his debt is a man a house might one day learn to count. The seventh year is nearly gone and the one entry the house cannot check presses toward its end. Jorra's grey dust and the unnameable back room, the space under the rack where slate should have stood, Sella's half-pace and her unnamed moving of the basket, Mira's question-answers and two cords, Orra's knot-on-a-lie, the green at a mouth and the weakness of a board are all keepings no slate can enter. The boy is the only person who has seen them and has no standing to ask.
- The kiln's breath is named as the thing that takes a man in his sleep, and Mud End called the death merciful; what the house cannot measure it calls weather or mercy: the cough that killed a woman, the debt, the numb hand, the ridge burned and called dealt with, the sealed counting room eighteen leagues east, and the thaw itself are all called something that lets a person go home warm without owning a day of it.
- The Court's room and the Eel House have the same bench, pot, draught and gap as the birth room, the kiln-house and the death-house, and the boy has kept six likenesses apart on purpose; no cause has been written in any of them. Berr takes the speaker out of his sentences as neatly as a surgeon takes a hand out of a field, and the district's grammar of weather does the same work.
- The flat note has six hearings --- five from four sources and one from a person --- and has bought him nothing; nobody has tapped a vessel for him and there is no Grathic word for it. The nearly full note now has three answers on its own tray, advanced and not paid, and is not a hearing and not the third thing. Water-over-water is a fifth low thing on a place of its own and not a hearing at all. The bell is a Court instrument doing a temple's work, still unexplained, four, four and a gap in the square and in a story told in the dark, kept in two places; the early note is kept beside but never on them. The count is four, four and a gap in the kiln, the boards, the bell, the bench, the well-roof, the river-prints, the kindling and the firelight, and the gap is the part nobody has thought to enter.
- A woman is dead and stays dead, uncollected, her number in her thumb and her name under her scar; the mercy told her became a fact nobody alive can correct, and she believed him spent among children she never saw to the last. The word *the Breaking* has not been said in his hearing; the word forty was said once, low, is not explained, and was not repeated.

## What he knows and does not know

**He knows.** How a body fails: airway, bleeding, bones, burns, cold, infection, afterdrop and rewarming, the small evidence a body carries, and triage; the way a room cools from the edges inward and a small body goes at the extremities before the core admits to it; how a man pulled from cold water can worsen before he settles; the exact look and sound of a vessel handing its work to the next thing until the skin holds more than it was made to hold and the face arranges itself in the ordinary way; and the anatomy of a room sealed against its own heat. The smell of machine coffee and hospital sanitizer, snow going up a sleeve, orange streetlight on wet snow, his mother's kitchen, his daughter's photograph, the shape of an unsent sentence. He knows he died and that he is in a baby's body. This chapter added: that water going over water is a sound the river makes when it has more of itself than the channel will carry, and no vessel gives it; that a district's single word can be permission to stop looking rather than a name; that ice can be honeyed all the way through and the current can show through its skin in veins; that a debt left lying is not a debt forgiven; that a loan that can hear is a loan that can be called and a silence can be shared as an equality; that a gap in a board, a step and a sheet of ice are three gaps in three places and must not be made into one cause; that a sixth page can be written and turned down and buy nobody anything; that the pale stone he last felt against his belly is gone from the fold and the house has said nothing; and that a finer hairline can lie beside a kept line and be kept the same way. He knows Tarn turned his head a degree at a note and said nothing, and that two silences under one roof are nearer an equality than anything the house has a name for.

**He does not know.** The word *Sarn*, or that the Warm is the stuff the world is made of; any Grathic word beyond what he has absorbed by listening; that the Return is an industry with collectors, bankers and a deduction; that he was bought; that forty children paid for his whole return; who the woman in the grey coat is, what she wrote, or what her name says; why the flower is on his ribs; why his fevers will come; that ways run in spirals; that Dessa is his mother and Sella knew; that the Court is a bank and the temple an invoice; that somebody has watched him since before his birth and intends to use his coldness to seal the world. He does not know why Sella took him, what happened to Garl's warmth beyond the household's version, where the carried fire was first lit and who pays to keep it giving, what is in Alda's tin or what she wrote six times and turned down, which house holds the floorboard under which the notes go, what goes on in the back room whose door is kept shut, what the missing slates under the drying rack were for, why the second cord has never gone upriver, where the Ash goes beyond the north gate, where the measure goes after it is entered, what the uncalled debt was for, why the mark was confirmed, why Sella's roll is kept private, or what the Court's soft sentences actually do. He does not know why no collector came for the woman in the brook or that the omission will one day have a name. He has heard the word forty spoken once, low, by a woman who watched him, and does not know that it is the number of children who paid for him or that he is one of them, nor what the forty-first knot counts, nor that Orra tied it the night a woman carried him up the Clay Yard with a flower on the bundle. He can read the shape of the knot but not its name and has no word to spend it on. He does not know who rubbed the pewter lid bright, who took the crust from the fold, who took the stone from the board-bed, or that any of these takings will be read years hence as gifts entered on no slate. He does not know that the note of a whole district under strain is a thing he will hear in two days, or that no one will explain it.

**He suspects, without evidence.** That the gap in the count is the part he keeps, whether in boards, breaths, steps, kindling, a bell, a landing, a woman's thumb, a hairline in ice that stops short of an edge, an empty shelf, the space between a woman's thumb and her scar, a word said low and watched, an empty place in the fold, a slack frame, a silence where an argument used to stand, a coal gone up with a bird, a stone gone from a fold, or a new hairline of grey beside a left-in-place line; that the ridges, the charred slate, the barrows of grey, the benches, the dusts, the cords, the crusts, the stone, the intervals, the honeyed ice, the frost-bloom, the higher brook, the oiled paper, the knot-string, the prepared and performed sweepings, the sealed room, water-over-water and the pauses are one thing seen many ways, and that joining them would be a deciding he has no standing to make; that the passed fire has a payer somewhere beyond the yard, as the stone and the gruel and the gone crust have payers inside it; that Tarn's silence and his own are the same object held from two sides; that the near-full note, now three answers, is a reply to the interval he has carried since Stone 13 and not yet the third thing; that the six like rooms with a bench, a pot, a draught and a gap are a likeness he must not turn into a cause, because he has made that mistake once in a better-lit room and carried it for the rest of a life; that the told bell and the heard bell and the early note and the river's own note must be kept apart for the same reason, and so must the word on its tray and the sound in his ear, and the Greybank saying and the gap under his own roof; that a left thing and a burned thing are two ways of keeping, and a thing called gone may still be in the room as a smell or a hollow in a fold; that a kindness which cannot be answered cannot be refused, and that the clerks' reasons are more dangerous than cruelties would have been because they are easier to live beside; that the district's one word for the weather is a permission to stop looking, and that everyone who goes home warm on it has paid somebody else for the warmth; that a man who can hear a vessel's note and says nothing is worth more to a house than his debt, and that the back room has a record the house cannot enter.
