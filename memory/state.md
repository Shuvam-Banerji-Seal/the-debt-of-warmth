## What he knows, suspects and does not know

- **Knows, exactly and uselessly.** His count: 396 through 398 days — six months and 216 through 218 days — kept edge to edge against a house that keeps its days in loaves, glances and unnamed spaces; **three hundred and seventy-two answers** stand on the sixth place, and 372 is a hundred and seventy-two more than the year's two hundred, a number that crosses from Y.H. 813 into Y.H. 814 with no threshold anybody crosses, and he has stopped expecting to be told what any of the numbers count, which is not the same as not wanting it. That a holy day arrives the way weather arrives, with a name and no tend-day and no market, and that an hour earns what comes in it and not what is coming to it — he did not bring the landing's word or the morning's glaze into a day that had not earned them, nor Ilmen 10's arithmetic into a tend-day; that the Five ended without a boundary drawn and the year turned on Ilmen 1 with nothing signed. The order in which cold arrives — cheek, temple, wrist, the wool riding back — and the order in which a body takes heat back and gives it up, the reverse and sooner than anybody likes; that pink returning is the surface still warm and the core still cold; that a cold patient cannot clot, that you do not rub a frozen foot and do not set it to the fire, that a tourniquet let go too soon weeps into the dressing, and that all of it buys nothing without a hand two paces long. That a person's face can be composed because the cold has composed it, and that a basket set level out of the draught where the heat comes sideways is the rewarming he would choose and cannot ask for. That a hand at the fold's gap which closes nothing is exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air; that a keeping found where put is still a keeping however many hands find it and that a finding is not a placing; that the small alarm at a double count is itself only a finding; that a keeping found again on three mornings is not a habit, for a habit wants a hand that placed twice and a way a third. That a district's arithmetic can be changed where two hundred people stand and carried home in a locked box with one figure printed and one paid and a space between that no signature fills; that twelve readings are twelve and not one account and that no doctrine comes out of twelve even when the tend resets. That a voice in a yard is a voice in a yard and not a room entered, and a pause is not a person; that a report of a man at a landing is a shape in a place and not a sighting, a room, a witness or a door; that a cracked vessel's flat answer is a fact about the vessel and not a confession from whoever held it; that a name passing door to door is a keeping in the yard's mouths and that its not coming under a roof is another keeping, and neither mends anything. That a thing called finished is not finished for being called so, and that the called-gone and the left-in-place can stand on two places with a finger between; that a term ending is not a payment, a forgiveness or a freedom and leaves a man more exposed. That a promise neither given nor withdrawn is still a promise, and that a loan held at the teeth nine times is still owed.
