## The moment

Day 257, Smoke 18, Eighthday, Y.H. 813, the small hours, under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof in \place{Low Kiln}. \person[Corin]{the boy} lies on \place{the bed} at the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf}, awake and silent, in the room that does not touch the room of the fire, with the fold at his ear and one stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust two inches from it; the empty place beside the crust keeps its name to itself and no third came to make the row come out even. The thread of drool at his mouth has frozen in the dry heat and is unwiped, because nobody under any roof has ever been given an instruction about the thread and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. Cold has stopped at cheek, temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rides back, as though it had come to the end of a line written out for it and had neither an order to go further nor an order to go away; the afterdrop waits beyond all three, past a warming nobody in this house will perform. He knows the severe face cold wears, knows a cold patient cannot clot and that a man must be warmed from the outside and slowly, and that the cold patient will seem to improve before he worsens. He wants the crust. Both are true and the second is louder; the surgeon takes the inventory and enters it nowhere.

Through the finger's-width door the river keeps its note of \lore{water over water}, a low thing over a lower thing, never numbered among the hearings; under the floor \lore{the carried fire} gives four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have been, its payer without a name for two hundred and fifty-seven days; overhead \place{the boards} keep four nails on the near board, four on the next, and a thumb-width knot where the fifth nail should have stood. Three counts in three places, and none rises into another, because agreeing any two would want a payer put under both and no payer stands to any. The bell's four, four and a gap, heard at the Cross on Smoke 10, stays in the square beside the told bell and is not folded into the three and not heard as a seventh of the full note. Sella has gone to her own bed before the small hours; there is no breathing number anywhere in the house and no fire-sitter standing over the coals, and he borrows no rhythm from the room that does not touch his except the little silence after a count that has already ended there.

At the close he has laid the hand down against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. His sixth question went up an inch toward the low note, held for one rhythm, took the little silence after a count that had ended in the other room and carried that rhythm across the empty interval as an object in its own right --- neither filling it to make a fifth stroke in a row that had none nor crossing a second count against it, borrowing no house count and making no two counts agree --- and came down on the river's next low note. It was an answer and not an asking. **Ninety** honest answers stand on the sixth place, each an object and not a power, exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air: not a longer reach, a miss entered as a finding, a hearing, a seventh of the full note, a tenth stopping, a payment, one of the held changes, a key, a power or a rule. The file lies open on the towel with every gap left gap and every tray parted from its neighbour, and the count goes forward into Smoke 19, a day that has not earned its heading.

Two hundred and fifty-seven stands exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in \place{Sarn}, \place{Sill} or \place{Grath}; six months and seventy-seven days are told by nobody; the fire's fifth is unpaid the same number of days; \lore{the widow's mark} was touched once before the kiln on Smoke 1, held one breath, let go, and touched at no shelf, well, rack, road, basket, morning or dusk on Smoke 16, 17 or 18; the seventh year stands at its very end and has still not given. Smoke went out without a boundary drawn and neither of the district's two month-names was set right or left wrong; Smoke 19 (Day 258) stands **one morning ahead** and has not been brought into a day that has not earned it, and Smoke 20 (Day 259), the second Account-day and market of the second firing, stands two mornings from the close and was not reached for early. The next real opening, the first firing of Frost (Day 271), is far off and unbrought.

## The protagonist's condition

**Body.** \person{Corin}, two hundred and fifty-seven days old at the close of Day 257 and six months and seventy-seven days (two hundred and fifty-five and six months and seventy-five days on Smoke 16, two hundred and fifty-six and six months and seventy-six on Smoke 17, two hundred and fifty-seven and six months and seventy-seven on Smoke 18), carried everywhere: on Smoke 16 up and down the yard and at the hearth, kiln mouth, drying racks and loaf shelf; on Smoke 17 and 18 under the roof, and on Smoke 16 and 17 up \place{the clay road} at the going of the light and set on his bed under the roof and not in \place{the Debt Loft}. He was \person{Daniel Marchetti}, forty, twelve of them a trauma surgeon at Cook County; he died going back into a burning minivan for a child under the dashboard and his warmth left him in the giving and did not cool. As Corin he is a cracked, low-capacity vessel, unranked --- below Thin, twelve to fifteen embers at best, with a flaw nobody has named and a rate of loss nobody has measured. He cannot roll, stand, speak, read \lore{Grathic} in speech or writing, reach a cup, close two inches on the rim of the basket that holds him, lift a hand to his own chin or lift a crust two inches to his mouth. He drools; the thread at his mouth froze in the dry heat and was left unwiped on all three days. Cold comes cheek, temple, the small bare joint at the wrist where the wool rides back, and stops there as if it had reached the end of its own instruction; the afterdrop waits past a warming nobody in this house will perform. One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust lies two inches from his ear with its empty place unnamed and no third to make the row come out even. The one change is the hand: it can go up an inch toward a sound and stay for a small intake, for an interval held between intake and letting-go, for a whole counted breath, past the end of a counted breath into the little silence after it, and can carry one rhythm across such an empty interval without crossing a second count or filling the silence to make a row come out even. It is an answering, not a reach, a hearing, a seventh of the full note, a payment, a held change, a key, a power or a rule. He took the humiliation of the two inches as a fact rather than as a punishment, which was its own smaller defeat, and wanted the crust on all three mornings without ever learning to want it less, the wanting going on under the exactness the way a coal goes on under ash.

**Mind.** The double consciousness has acquired three more days of objects and six more answers from the hand, and still no hand to enter any of them. He knows the order of reaching --- the hand to the record before the hand to the person --- and has now seen it in a midwife laying slates before turning to a fold, a thane passing a finger over a figure before the crowd, a clerk with hand to slate before eye to stalls, Sella reaching a finger beneath the shelf before her eyes went to the row, Sella's glance at the clay jar before anything else, Hessa settling her strap and turning her wrist before her eyes took in the crowd, Berr wiping his thumb twice and passing a finger over a figure before his eyes took the square, the soft-cuffed clerk entering a buyer's number before he looked up to find the man who had said it, and Ilva at the yard well passing a thumb-nail over notches before her eyes went to the asker. Hessa's reaching stays filed as the **eighth** such hand; Ilva's, taken from the district's firing work and not from any house or Court, is the **ninth**, joined to none, and nine hand-shapes must not stand up and call themselves a rule. ## What he knows, suspects and does not know

- **Knows, exactly and uselessly.** His count: two hundred and fifty-five through two hundred and fifty-seven days --- six months and seventy-five days through six months and seventy-seven days --- kept edge to edge against a house that keeps its days in loaves, glances and unnamed spaces; the more exact it grows the more it isolates him, and he has no hand to lay it down and no voice with which to offer it. The order in which cold arrives --- cheek, temple, wrist, the wool riding back --- and the order in which a body takes heat back, the small joint first and the core last; that a cold patient cannot clot, that you do not rub a frozen foot and do not set it to the fire, and that all of it buys nothing without a hand two paces long. The reading of a body with lead in it --- the pale line at the gum, the held or curled hand, the colour of the nail beds, the short breath with the pause at its top, the tremor in the wrist, the care with which a dipper is set down when the fingers have stopped telling the man where it is --- exact, complete and worth nothing at two paces. The shape of a reading said over a body that cannot answer, now said at four readings from Grain 10 through Smoke 10 and still not the figure paid, and the grammar of taking the speaker out of a sentence with *your house* rather than \emph{I} --- heard at every reading over the same basket from the same man, and called a kindness. That a pot set down wrong gives its note before its time and that the note is neither a hearing nor a cause nor a seventh; that an almost-note can be filed and not spent. That frost-bloom is the shape a vessel makes when the heat in it has gone somewhere it was not meant to go, though he has no \lore{Grathic} word for it and the yard calls it weather; that the word grants a person permission to stop looking.
