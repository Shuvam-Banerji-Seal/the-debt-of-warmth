## The protagonist's condition

**Body.** \person{Corin}, four hundred and nineteen days old at the close of Day 419 — six months and two hundred and thirty-nine days (417 and six months and 237, 418 and six months and 238, 419 and six months and 239) — carried everywhere: down the nineteen steps to the Clay Yard on the mornings and afternoons of all three days and up again at each going of the light, set under the roof and never in \place{the Debt Loft}; the instruction went to the wool, the river wind, the pot, the fire and the banked fire and never to the basket, and on no day was the basket told what the day was or told to be still. He was \person{Daniel Marchetti}, forty, twelve of them a trauma surgeon at Cook County; he died going back into a burning minivan for a child under the dashboard and his warmth left him in the giving and did not cool. That a holy day arrives the way weather arrives, with a name and no tend-day and no market, and that an hour earns what comes in it and not what is coming to it — he did not bring the clerk's entered figure, the second man's turned slate, the face at the edge, the well-asking, the readings of Ilmen 10 or 20, the kinder saying at the block or the softening at the river into a day that had not earned them; that the Five ended without a boundary drawn and the year turned on Ilmen 1 with nothing signed; that the district's arithmetic changed on Ilmen 10, began again on Ilmen 20 and cannot be undone, and that one reading is one and not an account, a reset is not a signing, two are two and not one account and a figure read is not a figure paid. That an order of reaching — hand to the record, eye to the person, mouth after both, the half-beat in the gap — is an order and not an answer, whether it stands in a midwife, a thane, a keeper, a runner, a collector, Berr's thumb, Hessa's wrist, Ilva's sleeve, the clerk's inward slate, the second man's turned and unopened slate or the two women at the well; and that an order can be broken in a new place without an answer coming out of the break. That a report travelling a yard has no body under it and a word without a witness is a shape and not a fact, and that a reaching with no hand under it gathers interest toward a calling without being that calling; that a thing left in place is not a thing forgiven and looking twice is how a finding becomes a habit; that a figure read over grain covered at home does not know what the grain costs and paper is not a payer; that a face composed by cold is not composure and a cold patient cannot clot and must be warmed slowly from the outside; that a thing surface-together over a cold cavity is not closed; that a term ending leaves a man exposed and a forgiveness uncalled; and that exactness is the whole of the tray and worth nothing at two inches of air, because a field read correctly does not bleed less for being read. He knows the difference between a repair and a restoration, between a keeping and a sign, between a finding and a placing, and between a voice in a yard and a door; and he knows that wanting a crust lifted, a thread wiped and a hand on a pot handle is a want, in the old-life register, even under the small animal hunger that does not know it wants.

## Holds as shapes, not facts

Three places that do not touch: the shelf's two gaps, the fold's one crust with its empty place, and the field's empty place in the returning line, with the sayings set beside them. **A fourth place:** the pan's gone place on the wall, held on a tray of its own with no scrape, crumb, print, chip or word; the trough's water-line and the gone pan's round print stay on places of their own and are not laid across the loaf's stopping or the ridge. **A fifth place:** the pale line at the gum, exact and worth nothing at two paces, where a danger of the month goes and not among the hearings or the near-full answers. **A sixth place:** the held inch, with answers 121 through **four hundred and fourteen** from Frost 4 through the small hours of Ilmen 25, filed on its own place beside the others and never laid across them, not a reaching, not a power, not a key, not a sign, not a freedom, with no payer named. The staying-at-the-inch object, the early letting-go, the handful, the thumb's resting, the sixth opening, the pot-crack resting, the breath at the sill, the holding in the iron, the river's winter skin, the seventh opening, the setting-down-wrong, Sella's wide step around the returning-line place, the fourth room with the gap, the completed year, the turned year, the tending days after the turn, the working weeks, the readings of Ilmen 10 and Ilmen 20, the fire's unnamed source, the keeping at the fold with no placing, the collector's flat hand and belief, Berr's wiped thumb, his *your house* and his low saying about kindness entered, the clean voice's missing name, the Long Night story's empty name, the place the Remembering did not fill, the told bell's gap, the clerk's inward slate, Tarn's degree held as a shape, Tarn's unspoken sentence of the eighteenth on its own place, the softening at the river's skin on its own place, the man from Slate Row's face on its own tray, the second man's slate turned to the sleeve and his direction down the bridge road on places of their own with a finger between them and every other kept thing, **the well-asking answered with the weather on a place of its own beside that direction and slate**, the green at the pot board, the shape at the market's edge, the figure read over covered grain, Ilva's strip unread in her sleeve, Hessa's slate unread up the road, the fifth shaping of the well-saying on its own tray, the clerk's entered figure on its own place, the four ownerless directions, the burned slate at five, the row at five askings, the shut room at two tellings, the three counts in three places, the heard bell beside the told, and the sixth hearing with the Drawing's knock outside stand where put, each on a place of its own with a finger between every pair, gathered toward the far-off hand that is not his, not joined, named, called, paid or ended.

## Does not know

That he is four hundred and nineteen days old, or six months and two hundred and thirty-nine days, or that the year has turned into Y.H. 814, or that the Five are over, or that a number was reached at all: nobody has told him, nobody under the roof knows he has a number, and there is no \place{Grathic} word in which to say what a life would count; 419 is exact only in the old-life register and he would not say so to anyone. Where the year's fire was first lit, what it cost to keep, or who supplies the fifth pull under the floor; why each house says it kept the flame and nobody says where the dish was filled. Who the man from Slate Row is, what he wants, whether his name is on any roll, whether he came about the boy, the promise under the shed, Tarn's ear, the burned slate or the tile debt — nobody asked him and he did not say, and a shape seen from a low angle is not a history or a hand laid on a vessel; on all three of these days he was not on stage at all. Who the second man from Slate Row is, what he carries written on his inward slate, where he was going down the bridge road, whether he truly asked after a house-name under this roof or was answered with the weather, whom he meant to ask or answer, and whether the road was a door; the asking is a word with nobody standing under it, a report and not a sighting, and no word from it carried into the yard; a shape going down a road with its writing turned away is not a name, an errand, an answer or a witness, and the slate was not opened within reach of the basket. Who the soft-cuffed clerk at the landing is, what the measure he sits for would measure, whether he has written any name, who the man was who gave the figure entered on his inward slate, and whether the landing is a door; he has the word of a neighbour from Ilmen 3 and the report from Ilmen 15 and no sighting, and a report is not a witness he can ask; no figure came under any roof and no slate was opened within reach of the basket. Who the Kindling collector was, what the measure he banked was a measure of, or why the neighbour's pale round place was not a wound; the collector's belief he did not know and would not have called by a name. What the woman who carries the slate once carried that named a person she knew, or whether the fifth shaping of the well-saying is true; no name, figure, date or owner stood under the word and Hessa is not a reader. Why the grain covered at home was read over by a figure that did not know its cost, and who signed the space between the figure and the price; nobody signed it and no key came under the roof. What the clerk's entered figure wanted of the man who gave it, whether Tarn's degree toward the landing was a door, whether the loan can truly hear, and what calling it would cost; the measure is uncalled and the term already ended. What the softening at the river's skin means, whether the thaw belongs to anybody, and what the seventh opening mended; the yard has no word that would make a thing left in place a thing forgiven. What hand went into the returning line on Seed 15 and did not come out; no collection, reading or figure answered for it. Whether the green at the pot board or the shape at the market's edge has a name; they are sightings with no eighth and no owner. Whether the words at the well about the rope taking a finger meant anything beyond the cold; the boy gave the women no interior and the words no owner beyond the mouths they came out of. Whether any of the kept things touch; he has spent three more days not laying one across another, and does not know what a hand would find if it did. And he does not know that any of this is a story that will be read, or that the answering of the well was a question that will go on toward a bridge.
