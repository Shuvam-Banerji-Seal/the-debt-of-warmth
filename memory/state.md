## The moment

Day 269, Smoke 30, Tenthday, Y.H. 813, the small hours, under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof in \place{Low Kiln}. \person[Corin]{the boy} lies on \place{the bed} at the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf}, awake and silent, in the room that does not touch the room of the fire, with the fold at his ear and one stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust two inches from it; the empty place beside the crust keeps its name to itself and no third came in the afternoon to make the row come out even. The thread of drool at his mouth has frozen in the dry heat and is unwiped, because nobody under any roof has ever been given an instruction about the thread and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. Cold has stopped at cheek, temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rides back, as though it had come to the end of a line written out for it and had neither an order to go further nor an order to go away; the afterdrop waits beyond all three, past a warming nobody in this house will perform. He knows the severe face cold wears, knows a cold patient cannot clot and will seem better before he is worse, and that a man must be warmed from the outside and slowly. He wants the crust lifted the two inches to his mouth. Both are true and the second is louder; the surgeon takes the inventory and enters it nowhere, not even on the place where he keeps the things he does not enter.

Through the finger's-width door the river keeps its note of \lore{water over water}, a low thing over a lower thing, never numbered among the hearings; under the floor \lore{the carried fire} gives four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have been, its payer without a name for two hundred and sixty-nine days; overhead \place{the boards} keep four nails on the near board, four on the next, and a thumb-width knot where the fifth nail should have stood. Sella has gone to her own bed before the small hours; there is no breathing number anywhere in the house and no fire-sitter standing over the coals, and he borrows no rhythm from the room that does not touch his.

At the close he has laid the hand down against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. On the hundred and fourteenth answer it went up an inch toward the river's note, held for the small intake, the hold and the letting-go of one of the breaths the woman counted in the room that does not touch, did not come down with her breath, kept the small height through the little silence after her count had ended, went on past the river's next low note to the one after it --- carrying two of the house's rhythms across the empty interval with no second house count borrowed for the second. The house's count is on its own place, the river's two notes on another, and the little silence between is kept empty on a third, with no stroke supplied to agree any two, for agreeing would want a payer and none stands. The bell's gap stayed in the square where the bell struck it, with a finger between it and the held inch. He made nothing of the likeness and would not let it stand up as a gift; it was an answer and not an asking. **One hundred and fourteen** honest answers stand on the sixth place, each an object and not a power, exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air. The file lies open on the towel with every gap left gap and every tray parted from its neighbour, and the count goes forward into Frost 1, a day that has not earned its heading.

Two hundred and sixty-nine stands exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in \place{Sarn}, \place{Sill} or \place{Grath}; six months and eighty-nine days are told by nobody; the fire's fifth is unpaid the same number of days; \lore{the widow's mark} was touched once before the kiln on Smoke 1, held one breath, let go, and touched at no shelf, well, rack, road, basket, yard, market, morning, afternoon, dusk or going of the light on Smoke 28, 29 or 30, because a market and a reading and a bed and a basket and a going of the light are not openings. ## The protagonist's condition

**Body.** \person{Corin}, two hundred and sixty-nine days old at the close of Day 269 and six months and eighty-nine days (two hundred and sixty-seven and six months and eighty-seven days on Smoke 28, two hundred and sixty-eight and six months and eighty-eight on Smoke 29, two hundred and sixty-nine and six months and eighty-nine on Smoke 30), carried everywhere: on Smoke 28 and 29 about the yard, at the hearth, kiln mouth, drying racks, loaf shelf and rack end, and set on his bed under the roof at the going of the light each day; on Smoke 30 carried to the Cross, set in the moot-post's shade through the reading and the bell, and carried home at the going of the light. ## What he knows, suspects and does not know

- **Knows, exactly and uselessly.** His count: two hundred and sixty-seven through two hundred and sixty-nine days --- six months and eighty-seven days through six months and eighty-nine days --- kept edge to edge against a house that keeps its days in loaves, glances and unnamed spaces; the more exact it grows the more it isolates him, and he has no hand to lay it down and no voice with which to offer it. The order in which cold arrives --- cheek, temple, wrist, the wool riding back --- and the order in which a body takes heat back, the small joint first and the core last; that a cold patient cannot clot, that you do not rub a frozen foot and do not set it to the fire, and that all of it buys nothing without a hand two paces long. The shape of a reading said over a body that cannot answer, now said at six readings from Grain 10 through Smoke 30 and still not the figure paid, and the grammar of taking the speaker out of a sentence with *your house* rather than \emph{I} --- heard at every reading over the same basket from the same man, and called a kindness. That a district's arithmetic can be changed in a square where two hundred people stand and carried home in a locked box to two hundred tables with two figures in it, one printed and one paid, and a space between them nobody signed; that the reading knows the cover of the thing under the wool and not the thing, and that a thing covered is not therefore paid for; that a figure read a third time is no nearer the thing under the cover than a chart laid over a covered body is nearer the wound beneath it, and that three readings are three and not one account. That an arm can know what a handful weighs and not what it cost, and a slate can know the figure and not the grain. That a thing can be examined as closely as a hand can examine it and still enter nothing --- the grain turned under wool and set down where it lay --- and that a week and a month can go out having learned nothing from a reading and keep the nothing as a finding with no payer. That a report of an asking is not an asking and that taking it apart finds no witness at the bottom of it; that a silence in which no asking comes is a keeping and not a sixth witness; that a fist arriving nowhere has not therefore been withheld; that a bell's gap is a gap in a bell and not a hearing; that the extremity goes before the core and the afterdrop waits past a warming nobody here will perform.

- **Holds as shapes, not facts.** Three places that do not touch: the shelf's two gaps, the fold's one crust with its empty place, and the field's empty place in the returning line, with the sayings set beside them. **A fourth place:** the pan's gone place on the wall, held on a tray of its own with no scrape, crumb, print, chip or word. **A fifth place:** the pale line at the gum, exact and worth nothing at two paces, where a danger of the month goes and not among the hearings or the near-full answers; ## What Chapter 73 must not forget

Chapter 73 opens on Day 270, Frost 1, Firstday, in the small hours under the Vail roof with the hand still on the **one hundred and fourteenth** answer and the count at **269** exact, six months and eighty-nine days told by nobody --- the fire's fifth unpaid the same number of days with its payer unnamed, Smoke's third arithmetic behind and unread up the clay road, the grain still covered and unfigured at the rack end, the widow's mark touched once before the kiln on Smoke 1 and at no other hour since, the seventh year at its very end and still ungiven, the burned-slate word at four, the shut-room tellings at two, the row question at **five askings with one further shaping**, no witness and no row named, the ninth hand still nine with Ilva's stick unread in her sleeve, the seventh green a colour and the seventh shape a shape with no eighth, the findings that close handling is not entry, that the house keeps itself by not-doings and that three readings are three and not one account each on a place of its own, the house's not-doings kept apart from the boy's own gaps with a finger between, the little silence kept empty beside the held inch, the bell's gap left in the square, and no likeness drawn between the three readings and any of his other findings.
