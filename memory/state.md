## What he knows, suspects and does not know

- **Knows, exactly and uselessly.** His count: 384 through 386 days — six months and 204 through 206 days — kept edge to edge against a house that keeps its days in loaves, glances and unnamed spaces; **three hundred and forty-eight answers** stand on the sixth place, and 366 was a whole year stood edge to edge in the only count that is exact, the month he was born in come round again, a date named to nobody, no threshold anybody crosses, and he has stopped expecting to be told what any of the numbers count, which is not the same as not wanting it. The order in which cold arrives — cheek, temple, wrist, the wool riding back — and the order in which a body takes heat back and gives it up, the reverse and sooner than anybody likes; that pink returning is the surface still warm and the core still cold; that a cold patient cannot clot, that you do not rub a frozen foot and do not set it to the fire, that a tourniquet let go too soon weeps into the dressing, and that all of it buys nothing without a hand two paces long. That a person's face can be composed because the cold has composed it, and that a basket set level out of the draught where the heat comes sideways is the careful rewarming he would choose and cannot ask for. That a hand at the fold's gap which closes nothing is exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air, a warmth laid against the cold side of a vessel that will not hold it; that twelve findings by a hand that only read add no twelfth placing, that a keeping found where put is still a keeping however many hands find it, and that the small alarm at a double-digit count is itself only a finding. That the small satisfaction in a field taken as found and left as found costs nothing, which is how he knows it is not peace. That a district's arithmetic can be changed where two hundred people stand and carried home in a locked box with one figure printed and one paid and a space between that no signature fills; that eleven readings are eleven and not one account and no doctrine comes out of eleven. That a voice over the boards with nobody standing under it can shape a warning four ways and still supply no witness, and that four likenesses do not make an account without a hand to lay them together and a payer under them all. That a term ending is not a payment, a forgiveness or a freedom, and that a fear kept without a figure is still kept. That an inch toward a note is not a reach, and that a letting-go ended by the note's own finishing is neither early nor stayed.

- **Suspects, without proof and without making a figure of it.** That the house keeps by not-doings and that the keeping may be maintenance rather than mercy; that Sella's hand going toward the crust and coming back inside the wool, her half-beat at the pot's setting, her thumb at the wire, her finger beneath and then on top of the shelf, her glance at the clay jar and her hand half over nothing, and her refusal to touch the mark are a grammar with an order and no speaker; that Tarn's breath at the sill, his stopping at the calling word and his stroke over the bright flagstones without the half-check belong to the loan that can hear; that Jorra's dust, his forearms parted as clot from vessel, his knowing of the full note three turns ahead and his finger's double bite in the iron belong to the room nobody names and the promise neither given nor withdrawn; that Ilva's strip in her sleeve and Hessa's turned writing up the road are keepings of the same order as the charcoal under the shelf, held by hands that put the record before the person; that the fourth direction of Stone 23 and the three older Ash movements are further shapings of one finding about a room and a slate and a man nobody names, and that the finding wants a witness who can be asked.
