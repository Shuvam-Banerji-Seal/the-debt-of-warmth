## The moment

Day 248, Smoke 9, Ninthday, Y.H. 813, the small hours, under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof in \place{Low Kiln}. \person[Corin]{the boy} lies on \place{the bed} at the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf}, awake and silent, in the room that does not touch the room of the fire, with the fold at his ear and one stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust two inches from it; the empty place beside the crust keeps its name to itself and no third came to make the row come out even. The thread of drool at his mouth has frozen in the dry heat and is unwiped, because nobody under any roof has ever been given an instruction about the thread and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. Cold has stopped at cheek, temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rides back, as though it had come to the end of a line written out for it and had neither an order to go further nor an order to go away; the afterdrop waits beyond all three, past a warming nobody in this house will perform. He knows the severe face cold wears, knows a cold patient cannot clot and that a man must be warmed from the outside and slowly. He wants the crust. Both are true and the second is louder; the surgeon takes the inventory and enters it nowhere.

Through the finger's-width door the river keeps its note of \lore{water over water}, a fifth low thing never counted among the hearings; under the floor \lore{the carried fire} gives four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have been, its payer without a name for two hundred and forty-eight days; overhead \place{the boards} keep four nails on the near board, four on the next, and a thumb-width knot where the fifth nail should have stood. Three counts in three places, and none rises into another, because agreeing them would want a payer put under all three and no payer stands to any. Sella has gone to her own bed before the small hours and left the coals to keep themselves; there is no breathing number anywhere in the house and no fire-sitter standing over the coals, and he borrows no rhythm from the room that does not touch his except the little silence after a count that has already ended there.

At the close he has laid the hand down against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. He had put the sixth question to it: the hand outside the wool went up an inch toward the low note through the finger's-width door, went through the small intake, the hold and the letting-go of one of Sella's counted breaths in the room that does not touch his, kept through the little silence after her count, crossed the river's note against the borrowed rhythm in the inch and nowhere else, and came down on the river's next low note. The coordination was remembered and not a change made again. It was filed on the sixth place with no payer named, not spent or explained: not a longer reach, a miss entered, a hearing, a seventh of the full note, a tenth stopping, a payment, one of the held changes, a key, a power or a rule. **Seventy-two** answers stand there now, each an object and not a power, exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air. The file lies open on the towel and the count goes forward into Smoke 10, a day that has not earned its heading.

Two hundred and forty-eight stands exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in \place{Sarn}, \place{Sill} or \place{Grath}; six months and sixty-eight days are told by nobody; the fire's fifth is unpaid the same number of days; \lore{the widow's mark} was touched once before the kiln on Smoke 1, held one breath, let go, and touched at no shelf, well, rack, road, basket, afternoon or dusk on Smoke 7, 8 or 9; the seventh year stands at its very end and has still not given. Smoke went out without a boundary drawn and neither of the district's two month-names was set right or left wrong; Smoke 10 (Day 249), the first Account-day and market of the second firing, stands **one morning ahead** and has not been brought into a day that has not earned it.

## The protagonist's condition

**Body.** \person{Corin}, two hundred and forty-eight days old at the close of Day 248 and six months and sixty-eight days (two hundred and forty-six and six months and sixty-six days on Smoke 7, two hundred and forty-seven and six months and sixty-seven days on Smoke 8), carried everywhere: under the Vail roof at the bed, the hearth, the loaf shelf's low angle and the kiln mouth; in the Clay Yard, the drying racks and the yard well; at the wheel-shed's edge and the cold damper; down the clay road at the going of the light and home again. He was \person{Daniel Marchetti}, forty, twelve of them a trauma surgeon at Cook County; he died going back into a burning minivan for a child under the dashboard and his warmth left him in the giving and did not cool. As Corin he is a cracked, low-capacity vessel, unranked --- below Thin, twelve to fifteen embers at best, with a flaw nobody has named and a rate of loss nobody has measured. He cannot roll, stand, speak, read \lore{Grathic} in speech or writing, reach a cup, close two inches on the rim of the basket that holds him, lift a hand to his own chin or lift a crust two inches to his mouth. He drools; the thread at his mouth froze in the dry heat and was left unwiped on all three days. Cold comes cheek, temple, the small bare joint at the wrist where the wool rides back, and stops there as if it had reached the end of its own instruction; the afterdrop waits past a warming nobody in this house will perform. One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust lies two inches from his ear with its empty place unnamed and no third to make the row come out even. The one change is the hand: it can go up an inch toward a sound and stay for a small intake, for an interval held between intake and letting-go, for a whole counted breath, past the end of a counted breath and down on a second rhythm, for the river's own low interval with no count in the house to hold it against, for the little silence after a count has ended, and down on the next low note; six ways of asking the same inch across this chapter, the sixth the two-rhythm crossing remembered. It remains a fraction of air and no longer a reach; each movement was filed as an object with no payer named.

**Mind.** The double consciousness has acquired three more days of objects and six more answers from the hand, and still no hand to enter any of them. He knows the order of reaching --- the hand to the record before the hand to the person --- and has now seen it in a midwife laying slates before turning to a fold, a thane passing a finger over a figure before the crowd, a clerk with hand to slate before eye to stalls, Sella reaching a finger beneath the shelf before her eyes went to the row, Sella's glance at the clay jar before anything else, Hessa settling her strap and turning her wrist before her eyes took in the crowd, Berr wiping his thumb twice and passing a finger over the figure before his eyes took the crowd, the soft-cuffed clerk entering a buyer's number before he looked up to find the man who had said it, and Ilva at the yard well on Smoke 5 passing a thumb-nail over notches before her eyes went to the asker and before her mouth answered; on Smoke 8 her hand stayed where it was and he filed no second shape from a not-reaching. Hessa's reaching stays filed as the **eighth** such hand; Ilva's, taken from the district's firing work and not from any house or Court, is the **ninth**, joined to none, and nine hand-shapes must not stand up and call themselves a rule. This chapter added: that a question asked a fifth time is still a question and five askings with no witness are not an account, for an account wants a payer and none stands to any; that a row is a place, a place wants a witness, and a witness stands somewhere and can be asked; that a thing not priced is not therefore free but a thing whose payer has not been named; that a sleeve is not a slate and a figure kept is not a figure lost; that the sweet edge of glaze on a wrong wind is a direction arrived nowhere and not a second edge paid; that one rhythm held across a borrowed silence is not two rhythms crossed; that a hand that does not reach cannot yield a second shape to make a row come out even; and that a discipline is a thing a hand uses, not a thing a man believes.

**Powers.** None usable, no rank change, no experiment, no clamp, no heresy. He cannot move Warm, cannot clamp, cannot speak, cannot make a fire, cannot read Grathic in speech or writing, cannot enter a figure anywhere. Counting is an old-life habit, not a gift: the kiln's four-and-a-gap, the boards' four nails, four and a knot, the carried fire's four pulls and missing fifth, the river's fifth low note, the told bell beside the heard, the told figure beside the paid, three counts in three places that do not become one, and 246 through 248 exact days set edge to edge against a house that keeps its days in loaves, glances and unnamed spaces. The ear still holds six hearings of \lore{the full note} --- five from four sources (a cup across a birth room, a pot in the wheel-shed through a finger's width of door, the district's slate twice at the Cross) and the sixth, the small flat knock out of \person[Dessa Silt] as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go and never among the sources --- and **no seventh came in three days** and none was invented to fill the empty place, for an empty place kept empty is a finding and a finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged. Three near-full answers stand advanced and unpaid, and beside them three shapings of the pot saying on their own place, advanced and unpaid, no fourth likeness made a cause; none of the sayings was made a cause by the fifth asking. The burned-slate word stands at four and the two tellings of the counting room shut in the war stand at two and did not become three or a fifth. The older intervals lie at Stone 13, Stone 17 and Stone 25 with the half-beat behind a standing finger, the unchecking stroke over the bright patch and his own held breath from a market morning; **nine** stoppings stand on Reed 5, 8, 11, 14, 17, 20, 23, 26 and 29 as objects and not a rule, with no tenth before the seventh year, which now stands at its very end and has still not given. The Seed 16 breath is drawn at the top of every reach and not spent, and the loan is uncalled. The seventh green stands a colour at a silent woman's mouth and the seventh shape a shape in cast-offs, with no eighth of either and no name. The question about the row keeps **five** askings (Grain 21, 23, 26, 29 and the afternoon of Smoke 8) on a seventh place of its own with a finger between each pair, still not one witness, not a hearing, not a cause, not a name and not a row named.

**Possessions and money.** Nothing he can use, and no money at any point; no purchase, no sale, no bond, no loaf passed and no slate opened within reach of the basket on any of the three days. The chart with its note turned down and the hand going past it to a signature stood behind the file turned out as instruments on a towel, behind the body inventoried at two inches, behind the surgeon's knowledge of the afterdrop and of a cold patient who cannot clot, behind the notched bark read as an order of reaching and not as a figure, behind the closed fist read as a chart turned face-down, behind the sweet edge of glaze that arrived and went out on one wind, behind the figure that arrived nowhere and had no signature between it and the figure paid, behind the bent finger held in the damper until Sella's sentence finished, behind the half-beat on the flagstones measured as the distance between intention and arrest, behind the ear-sentence brought to the word that would call the loan and stopped, behind the small dry scrape from the loft filed on its own place, behind Hessa's edge-on slate and her refusal to read, behind Berr's twice-wiped thumb, finger over a figure and *your house*, behind the clerk's number entered before the eyes found the man who had said it, behind the reed-cutter's cord turned so its knots stood, behind Orra's date laid over the levy question, behind Sella's glance at the clay jar before anything else, behind the pan's gone ring named from the shape it left, behind the finger held under the board for a counted breath, behind the thumb-length lead-glazed mark at a widow's throat and the one held breath before the kiln, behind the four askings of the ownerless question and now the fifth, behind the ninth record-first hand at the well and the hand that did not reach a second time, and behind the six ways of asking the hand across these three days. ## What he knows, suspects and does not know

- **Knows, exactly and uselessly.** His count: two hundred and forty-six through two hundred and forty-eight days --- six months and sixty-six days through six months and sixty-eight days --- kept edge to edge against a house that keeps its days in loaves, glances and unnamed spaces; the more exact it grows the more it isolates him, and he has no hand to lay it down and no voice with which to offer it.
