# State of things — at the close of chapter 5 (private)

*This is the snapshot the author reads before writing chapter 6. Days 7, 8 and 9 have been lived: the quiet morning after the Drawing under one roof, where a pot was set down and did not answer and a man with a broom saw the boy expect a note and said nothing; a dawn at the Fishstairs where eels, rumours and a cord at a woman's waist went together; and a morning at the market's edge where a woman counted nineteen into a thumb and put a crust into the fold without looking in. Nothing has been explained. The boy is nine days old, and his private file has grown by a cord, a crust, an interval and a silence witnessed by another person. The Drawing is behind him; the figure read aloud at the Cross is still a figure nobody will give him. Low Kiln still says three days to the Five and will not say what it counts.*

## The moment

Day 9 is done. On Stone 15, Fifthday, Sella Vail carried the boy down to the market's edge at dawn and set the basket in the bridge's shade, in a patch no slate had reached, as she had set it at the edge of the light at the Drawing. \person{Dessa Silt} stood at the end of nineteen steps with her shoulder against nothing, slip-grey nails and clay at her hem, cough from \lore{the slip pits} called weather and the pale sieve-wire scar smoothed whenever a truth came near. She said nineteen out and nineteen back, four loaves on the seventh of Stone in eight hundred and twelve, three to \lore{the levy} and one eaten with her feet up, and bits to the woman who came with a basket; her eyes went over the basket and past it and never into it. Across the trade a girl with lead-glaze green at her mouth stood a silhouette and looked at pots. \person{Mira Silt}, slime dried pale on her sleeves and the top of her left ear gone, answered a price with a question and her own answer, and when a figure was spoken her thumb went to the grey cord at her waist --- the third time in two mornings --- and never to the second cord twisted twice beneath it, which has never gone up the river to \place{Nine Pots} and has never been lifted at a door. She put a brown-edged barley crust into the fold at the boy's side in two brisk movements, called it a crust and not a sermon, and went back to the eels. He could smell the smoke and feel the warmth and could not lift it to his mouth. Somewhere back toward \place{the Cross} the market bell went four, four and a gap.

At dusk Sella carried him up the yard with the eel bucket knocking at her knee and said nothing about the market, the woman at the eels or the crust. Under the roof she put the fold right without looking at his face, set the basket on the bed under the roof and not in the \place{Debt Loft} above the shed, banked the coals with the back of a broken pot and said *that'll hold*, and did not touch \lore{the widow's mark} --- that is for the hour before an opening and no other. Then she counted loaves and bits with her lips and came back to the same place twice, keeping the shape of the number of days to \lore{Kindling} without giving the number. \person{Tarn} swept the threshold twice, put the small bench knife back on the sill and told the yard he had done so, unasked, and said nothing about the note that did not come. \person{Jorra Vail} came in with bone-coloured slip and no slate dust, spoke to a pot and moved the damper before she had finished telling him the fire was low, and did not say the back room was cold. \person{Garl Vail}'s coat kept its salt shape on the peg, his seat its hollow on the left, his proverb its place in the house's mouth. Three doors down the threshold of \place{House Silt} stood in the angle of the wall.

Day count: **Day 9 complete.** Day 10 is Stone 16, Y.H. 812, a Sixthday. The household has begun, without saying it, to count what it has against the days before \lore{Kindling}; \place{Low Kiln} still gives three days to \lore{the Five}; \lore{Account-day} and the slate at the Cross are ahead.

The two earlier days of this chapter, held in full:

- **Day 7, Stone 13, Thirdday.** First light after the Drawing. Sella touched the widow's mark before she opened the kiln and at no other time, fed the boy barley gruel thinned with milk from the house's own dish, and gave every instruction to the fire and never to the basket. In the shed the boy turned his head toward a pot *before* Jorra set it down --- expectation, not hearing --- and Jorra set it down softly and it gave nothing. He kept the interval where the note should have been; the hearing count stayed at five from four sources. Tarn came up the yard with his broom, the knife already back on its sill, saw the turning, stopped half a stroke at the empty set-down and said nothing: two ears under one roof, each kept out of the other's reach. Jorra had slip, not slate dust. Through the day the number of days to Kindling went around as a closed fist; Tarn's sixth year was counted and the seventh held back; he stopped altogether for two breaths when Sella said he had done well. The boy extended his file to the death-house bench's four, four and a knot and to the interval. At dusk she banked the coals with a broken pot and did not touch the mark, and nobody said what had been taken two doors down on Stone 12.
- **Day 8, Stone 14, Fourthday.** Sella carried him down to the \place{the Fishstairs} before light because there was nobody in the yard to leave him with. The town woke in layers --- gulls, wet rope and salted perch, water under ice, then the cold. \person{Mira Silt} filleted eels with pale slime on her sleeves and the top of her left ear gone, a hook by her story and a knife by the scar, flicked \lore{eel-bones} against her thumb while deciding whether to lie, and gave \person{Berr Clay} four eels where six should go against his two marks short of six years; neither acknowledged it. Asked whose was the basket, she answered *warm*, off the slate and not on one. Berr gave out *three days to \lore{the Five}* from the top of the steps and passed his finger over the figure; her hand went under her apron to a cord at her waist. The boy saw the hand and not the knots. A collector crossed \place{the north bridge} with a soft cuff and a hand to his satchel clasp before his eyes to the stalls, and two present-tense sentences of \place{Cook County Hospital} at four in the morning broke through --- the wrong light, the hum, the paper wrapper eaten to the last crumb because waste offended him, the hand going to a pocket where a phone lay lit with a name he would not look at --- and the river closed over it. He counted four steps down and four and a landing where a step should have been. Sella bought two pair of eels, counted them with her eyes, and carried him home with the bucket swinging at her knee and the river smell on the wool.

## The protagonist's condition

**Body.** \person{Corin}, nine days old by the night of Day 9. He was \person{Daniel Marchetti}, forty, twelve of them a trauma surgeon at Cook County; he died going back into a burning minivan for a child under the dashboard and his warmth left him in the giving and did not cool. As Corin he is a cracked, low-capacity vessel, unranked --- below Thin, twelve to fifteen embers at best, with a flaw nobody has named and a rate of loss nobody has measured. He is carried everywhere: in the basket at Sella's hip, set down in the shade at the market's edge, lifted back onto the bed under the roof. He cannot roll, stand, speak, read \lore{Grathic}, reach a cup or lift a hand to his own chin. He drools; the thread at his mouth froze on the walk down and nobody wiped it, because a basket at the edge of a board is a pot set down and left, not a person who needs wiping. The head goes where it wants and makes a small wet sound when it goes too far, and the hand outside the wool goes up an inch toward a pot, a cord, a crust, the place where a pocket should have been, and comes down; nothing within two paces can be reached. The branching red \lore{birth-flower} runs from the third rib on the left down the forearm and stops short of the wrist; it was not shown this chapter. Cold comes in at cheek, temple and wrist, in the order he knows and cannot argue with. He wanted the breast and the crust at once and got neither; the crust lies warm in the fold at his side and cannot be eaten.

**Mind.** The double consciousness has acquired a second kind of keeper and a new precision about absence. He knows the rule of Sella's mark holds at the bank as well as before an opening, and that she gives instructions to the fire and never to the basket. He knows that a pot can be turned toward and give nothing, and that a thing which does not happen can still be kept --- an interval with the shape of a sound in it. He knows that Tarn saw him anticipate the note and chose silence, which makes two people under one roof keeping the same thing without a word; he cannot enter that silence and cannot hand it back, and it is not safety and not yet a threat. He knows that a number can go around a district as a closed fist and arrive in a house as an absence, and that a woman can keep accounts in grey wool where no collector's thumb would think to look. He has read two kinds of not-looking --- the kind that prices and the kind that keeps, the door shut and the door held on the latch --- and has no word in Grathic for either. He knows the surgeon's habit of laying objects in a row before anyone writes them down, and that here it buys him nothing. He is the only adult in every room and the least use in it; correctness is the whole humiliation, and he has learned to find it funny without laughing.

**Powers.** None usable, no rank change. He cannot move Warm, cannot clamp, cannot speak, cannot make a fire, cannot read Grathic in speech or writing, cannot enter a figure anywhere. Counting is an old-life habit, not a gift: the kiln's four, four and a gap; the floorboards' four, four and a knot the width of a thumb; the death-house bench's four, four and a knot; the bell's four, four and a gap; the Fishstairs steps' four, four and a landing that is not a step; seven doors to the place where the eighth should have been; nineteen out and nineteen back in a woman's thumb, which does not fit the shape and will not be made to fit. The ear holds \lore{the full note} in five hearings from four sources --- a cup across the birth room, a pot in the Vail wheel-shed through the wall, the district's slate twice at \place{the Cross}, and a man giving at the Drawing --- with this chapter adding no sixth. The boy's turning toward the pot on the bench was expectation, not hearing. The knowing buys nothing: no hand has reached a vessel, nobody has tapped a slate for him, and no one has said what the sound was. The hot trace that carried him is in him still and he has no name for it.

**Possessions and money.** Nothing he can use. **Gained:** a brown-edged barley crust with a little smoke in it, put into the fold at his side by Mira on Stone 15; he can smell and feel it but cannot lift or eat it, and Sella neither thanked nor refused it. **Lost/unchanged:** the small pale river stone, colour of skimmed milk, is still in the fold of grey wool on the abandoned board-bed in \place{House Silt}, unmentioned. The small bench knife is back on the sill where it can be checked; the charred slate still lies in the kiln ash; the basket and wool are Sella's, lent without a word. He owns no coins and none change hands to him: Sella laid marks for two pair of eels at the stall and counted them with her eyes, and Mira has paid Berr two marks short against two eels short for six years. Figures go around him and are not his to spend: one in twelve, the year's first measure, three days to the Five, a finger's width, nineteen steps, four loaves on the seventh of Stone. The gruel, the crust, the eels, the barley and the warmth of the hearth remain entered nowhere; every kindness has a payer who can be named and a recipient who cannot refuse it.

**Wounds carried from the other life.** The call he never made to Nina --- nine summers, nine promises, a boat never bought --- was reached for again in the place where a pocket should have been, this time in a market with a collector's cuff crossing a bridge; the lit name was not looked at then and cannot be looked at now. He ate a paper wrapper to its last crumb in a cafeteria he could not name, and the hunger of the infant body and the thrift of the grown man turned out to be the same appetite wearing two faces. Tasha Broussard's chart, signed without reading the nurse's note, and the institution calling it a systems failure, came back in the exactness of Alda's dates in the chapter before, not in this one. Vesci's *that's what a chart is for* was not spoken; Ostrowski's voice stayed offstage. His father's casket slightly crooked in the aisle; Elena's quiet leaving, Marisol's temperate one; Des's laugh did not return. The pause in the neat hand has been set beside the pause at the flare and the pause on the birth-slate three times and has not made a fourth; the Drawing collector's ordinary hand, with no pause in it, stays in its own place. In Sarn's terms he is an unclosed account heavy enough to crack a slate, and he has now been entered in three accounts that never asked him --- the house's, the Court's, and Dessa's count of bits --- of which the third is the quietest because nobody thinks it is an account at all.

## Who is where, and what they want now

### Under the Vail roof at night

- **Corin/Dan** --- on his bed under the roof with the fold at his chin and the crust warm in the fold at his side, awake, silent, hand outside the wool going up an inch and coming down. Wants the breast, wants the crust, wants a sentence that would let him correct the room; gets none of the three and cannot ask for any.
- **Sella Vail**, 33, keeper of the Vail fire --- at the fire after banking the coals with the back of a broken pot, having counted her loaves twice with her lips. Her hand went to the widow's mark before the opening on Stone 13 and at no other time, not at the bank on Stone 13 or Stone 15. She fed him from the house's dish, carried him down twice and to the market edge once, kept a half-pace from every open slate, put the fold right without looking at his face, and did not see the crust go in or made nothing of it, which is the same thing where she is concerned. She did not say that the woman at the stall was her sister's sister, or that the marks in the basket were short, or where they had been. Has not written him as Vail, keeps a private roll in a careful hand she will not show, and will not let anyone fill Garl's seat or take his coat down. Wants the fire kept, the house kept, the keeping never explained; the Greybank confirmation has made that harder without giving her anyone to answer.
- **Tarn**, 22, the Vail's clay-bound hand --- in the yard and under the roof, broom set against the wall, small knife back on the sill and announced unasked: *I took the small knife. I put it back. You can check.* Six years of a seven counted; he will not count the seventh, the one entry in this house the house cannot check. He stopped altogether for two breaths when Sella said he had done well, then said *Yes, house* and began again. On Stone 13 he saw the boy turn his head toward a pot before it was set down, and his broom stopped half a stroke at the soft set-down that gave nothing; he has said nothing about either and will say nothing. Wants the exact account of what he is owed and what he owes, and grudges the house the loan of a bed for somebody else. A loan that can hear is a loan that can be called; the sentence has never been said in the house and lies in it all the same.
- **Jorra Vail**, 16 --- in from the wheel with bone-coloured slip dried to old bone up both forearms, bent little finger, and no slate dust on Day 7 or Day 9. Said *you'll do* to a pot and *it doesn't care whose hands* to the wheel, and shifted the damper before Sella had finished telling him the fire was low. Did not say the back room was cold tonight. Still wants the foundling outside the name Vail and the hollow seat for himself. The sentence *he's not Vail* is as sealed as on the hour it was spoken; the broken slates under the drying rack and the men whose names are not spoken remain unshown.
- **\person{Garl Vail}** --- absent and the shape of most of the house. Not collected in the Long Cold; his warmth lies where it fell, the \lore{the Ash} ridge is its shape, the coat keeps his broad shape on the peg, the seat keeps his twenty-year leftward lean, and the ridge is unswept and will not be swept before the Last Day. His debt against a firing of tiles was left uncalled at the Cross and remains neither paid nor done with. His proverbs live in the house's mouth: *feed the fire before you feed yourself*, and *leave the crack showing; people trust a thing they can see has been handled.*

## Open dangers

- The year's first measure was taken at the Drawing out of a dying neighbour and entered in the figure the Cross read aloud; the boy watched and could not address, refuse, reach or speak to it. Nobody in the yard has said what was taken or where it went. The household has now begun, silently, to count fuel and bread against the days before Kindling, and the number of those days goes around as a closed fist and enters the house only as an absence.
- Two accounts entered him without asking before this chapter; at the market's edge a third entered him without anyone thinking it an account at all, and he does not know he is inside it. Sella's private roll is still unshown and the wall around it held; the Court's neat upper line was seen a third time before this chapter, the Drawing collector's ordinary hand is filed separately with no pause, and Mira's grey wool is a record no Court can confiscate and no slate can enter.
- Sella's widow's mark stands confirmed by a factor at Greybank and entered as a kindness; it is held while the fire never goes out and is touched only before an opening. Garl's uncalled debt against the tiles hangs where Berr left it: never paid, never settled, never done with.
- Tarn has heard a flat note and said nothing, and has now seen the boy hear one before it happened and said nothing about that either; a loan that can hear is a loan that can be called, and two people keeping the same silence under one roof is an equality neither can name. Jorra's no-dust forearms and the back room, Sella's half-pace from open slates, Mira's question-answers and both not-lookings, and Dessa's count in her thumb are all keepings no slate can enter. The boy is the only person who has seen them and he has no standing to ask.
- The child on the quilt in the death-house was worse on the third day of its return and the room called it a graft settling. Alda's two written notes are turned down where no mother or officer will read them; the second cause stays in the tin and in the struck word nobody speaks.
- The Ash in the death-house is thicker by a finger's width than the ridge at Vail, and both turn corners without a break; the Vail ridge has not been swept and will not be before the Last Day. The charred slate in the kiln ash and the barrow that went out through the north gate are unread; the things nobody names in this district are the things it has most practice at carrying out of sight.
- The flat note has five hearings from four sources and has bought him nothing; nobody has tapped a vessel for him and there is no Grathic word for it. The bell is a Court instrument doing a temple's work, still unexplained. The count is four, four and a gap in the kiln, the boards, the bell, the bench, the steps and the space between a woman's thumb and her scar, and the gap is the part nobody has thought to enter --- the only place in any room where nothing has been written down.
- Low Kiln says three days to the Five and will not say what it counts; the Ilm is under ice and the Singing Sill goes on giving its low note without explanation. The Drawing is past and the year's first measure is gone; the district will call these days ordinary. Account-day and the Cross are ahead, and with them a fourth sighting of the neat hand still unjoined to the other pauses.

## What he knows and does not know

**He knows.** How a body fails: airway, bleeding, bones, burns, cold, infection, afterdrop and rewarming, the small evidence a body carries, and triage. The smell of machine coffee and hospital sanitizer, snow going up a sleeve, orange streetlight on wet snow, his mother's kitchen, his daughter's photograph, the shape of an unsent sentence. He knows he died and that he is in a baby's body. This chapter added: that the rule of the mark holds at the bank as well as before the opening; that a pot set down softly can give nothing and the interval can still be kept; that expectation is not hearing and a thing can be kept without happening; that Tarn saw him turn toward the pot and chose silence, which makes two of them keeping the same thing; that the days to Kindling go around as a closed fist and arrive as an absence; that Tarn's sixth year is counted and the seventh held; that Jorra's forearms carried no slate dust on Days 7 and 9; that a meal is entered nowhere when the house says *the house*; that the Fishstairs sell eels at dawn and rumours at noon; that Mira's hook is a knife, that her eel-bones move while she decides whether to lie, and that she pays Berr two eels short against his two marks short; that she calls the boy warm, off the slate and not on one; that her hand goes to a cord when a number is spoken; that a collector's cuff goes to a satchel clasp before his eyes go to a stall; that the hunger of the infant and the thrift of the surgeon are the same appetite; that the Fishstairs steps run four, four and a landing where a step should have been, and that the river puts them back out of line in the Breaking; that Dessa counts nineteen in her thumb, calls the pits' cough weather, keeps a door-shut smile and did not look into the basket; that a second cord at Mira's waist has never gone up the river; that a crust can be given into a fold and cannot be lifted out of it.

**He does not know.** The word *Sarn*, or that the Warm is the stuff the world is made of; any Grathic word beyond what he has absorbed by listening; that the Return is an industry with collectors, bankers and a deduction; that he was bought; that forty children paid for his whole return; who the woman in the grey coat is and what she wrote or what her name says; why the flower is on his ribs; why his fevers will come; that ways run in spirals; that Dessa is his mother and Sella knew; that the Court is a bank and the temple an invoice; that somebody has watched him since before his birth and intends to use his coldness to seal the world. He does not know why Sella took him, what happened to Garl's warmth beyond the household's version, what is in Alda's tin or what she wrote twice and turned down, who wrote the neat upper line or what it says, where the Ash goes beyond the north gate, where the measure goes after the collector banks it, what the uncalled debt was for, why the mark was confirmed, why Sella's roll is kept private, why Jorra's back room is cold, what the second cord is for, or what the collector's soft sentence actually does. He does not know that Dessa has put him among the bits she counts, that Mira is kin to the house three doors down, or that either woman knows more about him than she will say on a slate. The true figure is still one he has never heard spoken; the word forty has not been said in his hearing. He cannot explain why he thinks in English in a country with no word for Chicago, and does not know that the way home is real or what it would cost.

**He suspects, without evidence.** That the gap in the count is the part he keeps, whether in boards, breaths, steps, a bell, a landing, a woman's thumb or the space between her thumb and her scar; that the two ridges, the charred slate, the barrow of grey, the bench, the cord, the crust and the intervals are one thing seen seven ways, and that joining them would be a deciding he has no standing to make; that Tarn's silence and his own are the same object held from two sides; that the pause at the flare, the pause on the birth-slate and the pause in the neat hand are three things of one shape and that making a fourth is his work, not anyone else's; that a kindness which cannot be answered cannot be refused, and that the collector's reason is more dangerous than a cruelty would have been because it is easier to live beside; that Mira's cord is the only record worth keeping because the institution does not know it exists; that Dessa's count keeps something larger out of the room in the same way his own count keeps the gap; that two not-lookings with the same shape can be opposite things; that knowing a thing exactly has never once given him a hand to act with. Suspicion, not knowledge --- never let him know more than he has earned.

## Relationships at the close of chapter 5

- **Nina** --- love and resentment braided; the debt that made him portable. The unread name in a lit pocket came back in a cafeteria at four in the morning, and the hand found wool where the phone had been. Nine promises, no boat.
- **Des, Ostrowski, Vesci** --- the dead life's voices. None returned; the market voice and the collector's cuff stood alone. The shape of Vesci's chart sentence came back in Alda's exact, kind, wrong dates in the chapter before, not this one.
- **Sella** --- the woman who took him and will never ask where his memory came from. She carried him down twice and to the market edge, fed him from the house's dish, kept the mark's rule and the half-pace, left the crust where it was put, and said nothing at all about what she had seen or where they had been. Kindness as a wall, confirmed at Greybank and still unanswered.
- **Tarn** --- the house's debt, now visibly a second ear. The knife is back where it can be checked; he saw the boy anticipate a note and kept silent, and the boy kept silent too. The equality is closer than it was and still has no name on either side.
- **Jorra** --- the house's wound; slip on his forearms and no dust from the back room, *he's not Vail* still sealed by the boy's arrival. He speaks to pots and the wheel and shifts the damper, not to Sella about the boy.
- **Mira** --- the aunt who keeps accounts in counts, bones and grey wool and says nothing. She answered a question about the basket with *warm*, touched the cord when figures were spoken, and gave a crust into the fold without looking in. She has not said she knows and must not.
- **Dessa** --- his mother, who stood at the end of her nineteen steps with the shade kept off him and counted him among the bits without knowing it. She will die believing him dead. She has not said the number or the name in three years.
- **Alda** --- offstage; the woman who lied over his flower on Day 1, over his life on Day 3, over his birth date in a market on Day 4, and twice over a dying child with dates that answered nothing. Both notes remain unread and the tin unopened.
- **Berr Clay** --- the local face of the Court, wiping his thumb before his slate and speaking the passive grammar; his short payment and her short eels are a six-year war nobody fights aloud. His careful not-calling of Garl's debt keeps it alive.
- **The collectors** --- the officer with the soft cuff who crossed the bridge is an ordinary man going home to be warm; the one with the cold iron tube took the year's first measure two doors down. Neither is Marra, and the boy could no more address either than the slate under his hand.
- **Fenna** --- green at the mouth across the market, looking at pots, silent and unnamed. A shape filed without knowing it was filed.
- **Garl** --- an absence arranged around, now with his uncalled debt beside the measure that was called and the ridge nobody will sweep. *Leave the crack showing.*
