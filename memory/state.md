## The moment

## The protagonist's condition

**Body.** \person{Corin}, two hundred and thirty-nine days old at the close of Day 239 and six months and fifty-nine days (two hundred and thirty-seven and six months and fifty-seven days on the first morning, two hundred and thirty-eight and six months and fifty-eight days on the second), carried everywhere: under the Vail roof at the bed, the hearth, the loaf shelf's low angle and the kindling; in the Clay Yard, the drying racks, the yard well and the returning line; at the wheel-shed's edge, the cold damper and the worked strips' edge; down to the Cross and market at the half-pace in the moot-post's thin shade, and home again at the going of the light. He was \person{Daniel Marchetti}, forty, twelve of them a trauma surgeon at Cook County; he died going back into a burning minivan for a child under the dashboard and his warmth left him in the giving and did not cool. As Corin he is a cracked, low-capacity vessel, unranked --- below Thin, twelve to fifteen embers at best, with a flaw nobody has named and a rate of loss nobody has measured. He cannot roll, stand, speak, read \lore{Grathic} in speech or writing, reach a cup, close two inches on the rim of the basket that holds him, lift a hand to his own chin or lift a crust two inches to his mouth.
