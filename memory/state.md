## The moment

Day 368, Stone 9, Ninthday, Y.H. 813, the very end of the day --- when the small hours have settled under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill}, \place{Grath} and the river's note has come down to its lowest under its winter skin --- \person[Corin]{the boy} lies on \place{the bed} at the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf}, awake and silent, carried there at the going of the light by \person{Sella Vail} from the Clay Yard with the whole of her attention on the basket's level, set under the roof and never in \place{the Debt Loft}, with the fold at his ear and one stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust two inches from it, its corner standing a hair off the wool; the empty place beside the crust keeps its name to itself and no third came in these three days to make the row come out even. The thread of drool at his mouth has frozen in the dry kiln heat and is unwiped, because nobody under any roof has ever been given an instruction about the thread and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. Cold has stopped at cheek, temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rides back, as though it had come to the end of a line written out for it with neither an order to go farther nor an order to go away; \lore{the Afterdrop} waits beyond all three, past a warming nobody in this house will perform. He knows the composed face that is not composure, that a cold patient cannot clot and will seem better before he is worse and must be warmed from the outside and slowly, never hurried, never rubbed, never set too near the fire; he knows the order in which a body takes heat back, the small joint first and the core last, and the order in which it gives it up, the reverse, which comes sooner than anybody likes, and that pink returning to a face is the surface still cold and not the core. He wants the crust lifted the two inches to his mouth, the thread wiped and a hand that would close on the pot handle set beyond his reach. Both are true, the surgeon's register and the infant's want, the second louder, neither winning, and the quarrel between them is the work. ## The protagonist's condition

## What he knows, suspects and does not know

- **Knows, exactly and uselessly.** His count: 366 through 368 days --- six months and 186 through 188 days --- kept edge to edge against a house that keeps its days in loaves, glances and unnamed spaces; **three hundred and twelve answers** stand on the sixth place, and 366 is a whole year stood edge to edge in the only count that is exact, the month he was born in come round again, a date named to nobody, no threshold anybody crosses, and he has stopped expecting to be told what any of the numbers count, which is not the same as not wanting it. The order in which cold arrives --- cheek, temple, wrist, the wool riding back --- and the order in which a body takes heat back and gives it up, the reverse and sooner than anybody likes; that a cold patient cannot clot, that you do not rub a frozen foot and do not set it to the fire, that a tourniquet let go too soon weeps into the dressing, and that all of it buys nothing without a hand two paces long. That a person's face can be composed because the cold has composed it, and that pink returning to the skin is the surface still cold and not the core; that a basket set level out of the draught where the heat comes sideways is the careful rewarming he would choose and cannot ask for. That the small satisfaction in a field taken as found and left as found costs nothing, which is how he knows it is not peace. That a house can go a whole year without marking it and not one district account alters by a mark --- the truest and least useful thing he knows about the number. That an absence of recognition is neither a slight nor a mercy and cannot be entered on a slate, for a slate wants a hand to enter it and no hand came. The grammar of taking the speaker out of a sentence with *your house* rather than *I*, and a reading called a kindness, entered to a block that cannot answer. That a district's arithmetic can be changed where two hundred people stand and carried home in a locked box with one figure printed and one paid and a space between nobody signed; that a figure can be sent over a basket twice and be one figure spoken two times; that nine such readings are nine and not one account, and that averaging them would want a payer put under all nine and none stands; that a finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged and a doctrine is an account that has found a payer. That a person can carry a record for years and still not be its reader and can carry a figure up a road unread; that a rule is an account that has found a payer and nine hand-shapes must not stand up and call themselves one. That a sign is a thing that points to a payer and no payer stood under the returning-line place; that a wide step can be a keeping without being a sign.

- **Holds as shapes, not facts.** Three places that do not touch: the shelf's two gaps, the fold's one crust with its empty place, and the field's empty place in the returning line, with the sayings set beside them. **A fourth place:** the pan's gone place on the wall, held on a tray of its own with no scrape, crumb, print, chip or word. **A fifth place:** the pale line at the gum, exact and worth nothing at two paces, where a danger of the month goes and not among the hearings or the near-full answers. **A sixth place:** the held inch, with answers one hundred and twenty-one through **three hundred and twelve** from Frost 4 through the small hours of Stone 9, filed on its own place beside the others and never laid across them, not a reaching, not a power, not a key, not a sign, not a freedom, with no payer named; the repeats through Chapters 94 through 105 add no new form. The staying-at-the-inch object, the early letting-go, the handful, the thumb's resting, the sixth opening, the pot-crack resting, the breath at the sill, the holding in the iron, the river's winter skin, the seventh opening, the setting-down-wrong, Sella's wide step around the returning-line place and the completed year sit each on its own place with a finger between every pair; none is a habit, a way, a hearing, a near-full answer, a sign, a mending, a cause, a payer or a rule. The nine arithmetics stand as nine on nine places and not one account, the finding on a place of its own with no payer and no doctrine stood up. The heard bell, the told figure, the seventh colour, the seventh shape, the nine hands, the nine stoppings, the five askings, the burned slate at five, the shut-room tellings at two, the Ash 5 joining, the Ash 17 direction, the Ash 23 direction, the fourth shaping, the Ash 6 turning, the sweet glaze wind that touched the fold and went out, the thickened ridge, the breath at the sill, the river's skin, the setting-down-wrong and the unmarked birthday stand each on a place of its own with a finger between, and were not laid across one another.

- **Does not know.** That he is three hundred and sixty-eight days old, or six months and one hundred and eighty-eight days, or that a whole year has finished, or that a number was reached at all: nobody has told him, nobody under the roof knows he has a number, and there is no \place{Grathic} word in which to say what a life would count; 368 is exact only in the old-life register and he would not say so to anyone. What the clay jar on the wall shelf is for, though Sella glanced at it and shut her hand half over nothing on these mornings and evenings, and her little-door burn closing on air. What the widow's mark mended, why the hand goes to it before the kiln and at no other hour, or why the household's first firing of Stone came on Stone 5 and not on the turning of the month, or why a year could finish without a hand going to the mark; he has seen the acts and set them down as maintenance on a vessel, not as signs or payments. Why another loaf went off the shelf on Seed 6, or who carried it out; **what the charcoal tally under the loaf shelf says, or why Sella reached a finger beneath it for one counted breath on Day 166 and for two on Frost 5 and came away both times without a word;** he read the change in the hand and not the charcoal, and does not know what she counts under her breath at the fire, and put a finger between her sound and his three. Why Sella's thumb stayed one breath longer at the wire crossing on Ash 9, why Tarn's hand stayed a breath longer at the knife-sill on Ash 14, why Jorra left his bent finger a breath longer in the cold iron on Ash 26, and whose hand went around the wrongly set pot on Stone 5 --- whether any meant anything, whether any was a habit of the hand or a thing a man would do again, and who might one day read them; he has the intervals and their edges and nothing else, made no payer of them and no lesson, and none was made again on these three days. What the men want who come before light to the back room each afternoon and who keep the room open and low-voiced, or why the room is a finger's width open; the boy has not joined the room to the kiln, the widow's mark, any reading, the burned slate, the counting room shut in a war, the glaze-room hand carried out on Day 188, Ilva's notches, Tarn's loan, Jorra's dust, the handful, the thumb's resting, the pot-crack resting, the breath at the sill, the holding in the iron, the river's skin, the seventh opening, the setting-down-wrong, the wide step, the finished year or the silence held empty after a count. Who put the winter skin on the river and who will take it off --- weather answers, and weather is not a source standing to be asked. What happened to the hand that went in on Seed 15 and did not come out. What the collector's grey fingertips will one day have a reason for, and what the cold foot below the sill means (T093/T094/T096 carried, uncured); Alda's six pages and both tins and Dessa's pewter tin stay unopened (T023/T089 carried). The afterdrop's second cause stays unnamed. Marra is not in Sill and none of the watchers was seen in these three days; no eighth sighting came. The reincarnation and the old life stay behind his eyes, named to nobody and never tagged.

## What Chapter 106 must not forget

Chapter 106 opens in the small hours of Day 369, Stone 10, Tenthday, with the hand still laid down on answer **three hundred and twelve** and the count exact at **three hundred and sixty-eight** --- six months and one hundred and eighty-eight days told by nobody, a whole year finished and named by no roof, the carried fire's fifth unpaid and Garl's name unspoken the same number. **Nothing from Stone 7, 8 or 9 crosses forward as sign, lesson, mending, cause, payment or payer:** the birthday was not recognized and its non-recognition is not a sign, a slight or a cause; the widow's mark was untouched and no eighth opening made due; the river went under its winter skin on the first afternoon and the boy spent nothing on it, not on the birthday, not on a finished year and not on any date; the Ash 5 joining, the Ash 17 direction and the Ash 23 direction stood where put and did not repeat; Sella's wide step around the returning-line place stays a keeping and not a sign; the setting-down-wrong stays gathered once on its own place beside the premature note of Smoke 14; Ilva's ninth hand at the well on Stone 4 came and went without a tenth and her absence on Stone 5 through 9 promoted nothing. Carry forward unchanged and unspent: **three hundred and twelve honest answers** on the sixth place, each an object and not a power and not one standing up and calling itself a doctrine; **nine arithmetics of the year --- the three of Frost, the first, second and third of Bone, Ash's first, Ash's second and Ash's third --- behind one unsigned figure, nine and not one account**, with all three Ash subtractions locked and unread in Berr Clay's box up the clay road, the key in a coat pocket that does not come back down, the figure read still not the figure paid and no signature on the space; six hearings with the seventh place empty; the heard bell beside the told, four, four and a gap; nine record-first hands with no tenth (Ilva's ninth, Hessa's eighth); nine stoppings with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end and the loan uncalled; five held objects with the pot-crack resting beside them, the hand-at-the-sill breath beside that, the holding of Ash 26 beside all and the setting-down-wrong beside the premature Smoke 14 note, each found where put and not made again with a finger between every pair; the burned slate at five, the shut-room tellings at two, the well-saying at four shapings, the row at five askings with no witness and expressly no sixth; the Ash 5 joining, the Ash 17 direction and the Ash 23 direction apart; river's skin beside water-over-water, not a fourth covering, not a hearing and not spent on any date; three counts in three places that do not become one, with the bell's gap in its square; seven openings in seven months and no eighth made due; the promise under the wheel-shed ungiven and \emph{he's not Vail} sealed. Day 369 earns its own Account-day and market: the tenth arithmetic, Berr up short off the clay road wiping his thumb twice, the figure read twice over the basket, the levy called and denied with *your house*, Hessa's slate up the road edge-on and unread, the bell four, four and a gap --- ten readings are ten and not one account, the figure read still not the figure paid, no signature on the space and no doctrine out of ten. Day 371, Stone 12, the Hearth Drawing, stands three mornings further and must not be brought into a day that has not earned it. Forward, not closed.
