## What he knows, suspects and does not know

- **Knows, exactly and uselessly.** His count: 357 through 359 days --- six months and 177 through 179 days --- kept edge to edge against a house that keeps its days in loaves, glances and unnamed spaces; 359 is not round and not a threshold, and he has stopped expecting to be told what any of the numbers count, which is not the same as not wanting it. The order in which cold arrives --- cheek, temple, wrist, the wool riding back --- and the order in which a body takes heat back and gives it up, the reverse and sooner than anybody likes; that a cold patient cannot clot, that you do not rub a frozen foot and do not set it to the fire, that a tourniquet let go too soon weeps into the dressing, and that all of it buys nothing without a hand two paces long. That a person's face can be composed because the cold has composed it, and that pink returning to the skin is the surface still cold and not the core. The grammar of taking the speaker out of a sentence with *your house* rather than *I*, and a reading called a kindness, entered to a block that cannot answer. That a district's arithmetic can be changed where two hundred people stand and carried home in a locked box with one figure printed and one paid and a space between nobody signed; that a figure can be sent over a basket twice and be one figure spoken two times; that nine such readings are nine and not one account, and that averaging them would want a payer put under all nine and none stands; that a finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged and a doctrine is an account that has found a payer. That a person can carry a record for years and still not be its reader and can carry a figure up a road unread; that a voice may arrive nowhere and a sighting is not a payment; that weather and a price can answer a saying first and last and leave it standing nowhere; that a direction is not a witness and a reason spoken by nobody is not a reason given to him; that nine record-first hands and nine stoppings are nine and no tenth has stood up; that six openings are six and not a rule and no seventh came due; that an interval held a breath too long in a piece of cold iron is a keeping and not a habit, a way or a rule. That attention is the one thing no institution here has thought to tax, and that his small clean satisfaction in a field left as found costs him nothing, which is how he knows it is not the same as peace.

- **Holds as shapes, not facts.** Three places that do not touch: the shelf's two gaps, the fold's one crust with its empty place, and the field's empty place in the returning line, with the sayings set beside them. **A fourth place:** the pan's gone place on the wall, held on a tray of its own with no scrape, crumb, print, chip or word. **A fifth place:** the pale line at the gum, exact and worth nothing at two paces, where a danger of the month goes and not among the hearings or the near-full answers. **A sixth place:** the held inch, with answers one hundred and twenty-one through **two hundred and ninety-four** from Frost 4 through the small hours of Ash 30, filed on its own place beside the others and never laid across them, not a reaching, not a power, not a key, not a sign, not a freedom, with no payer named; the repeats through Chapters 94 through 102 add no new form. The staying-at-the-inch object, the early letting-go, the handful, the thumb's resting, the sixth opening, the pot-crack resting, the breath at the knife-sill and the holding in the cold iron sit each on its own place on the sixth with a finger between every pair, eight objects and none a sixth of anything; none is a habit, a way, a hearing, a near-full answer, a sign, a mending, a cause, a payer or a rule. The nine arithmetics stand as nine on nine places and not one account, the finding on a place of its own with no payer and no doctrine stood up. The heard bell, the told figure, the seventh colour, the seventh shape, the nine hands, the nine stoppings, the five askings, the burned slate at five, the shut-room tellings at two, the Ash 5 joining, the Ash 17 direction, the Ash 23 direction, the fourth shaping, the Ash 6 turning, the sweet glaze wind that touched the fold and went out and the breath at the sill stand each on a place of its own with a finger between, and were not laid across one another.

- **Does not know.** That he is three hundred and fifty-nine days old, or six months and one hundred and seventy-nine days, or that a number was reached at all: nobody has told him, nobody under the roof knows he has a number, and there is no \place{Grathic} word in which to say what a life would count. What the clay jar on the wall shelf is for, though Sella glanced at it and shut her hand half over nothing on these mornings and evenings, and her little-door burn closing on air. What the widow's mark mended, why the hand goes to it before the kiln and at no other hour, or why the household's first firing of Ash came on Ash 2 and not on the turning of the month; he has seen the act and set it down as maintenance on a vessel, not as a sign or a payment. Why another loaf went off the shelf on Seed 6, or who carried it out; **what the charcoal tally under the loaf shelf says, or why Sella reached a finger beneath it for one counted breath on Day 166 and for two on Frost 5 and came away both times without a word;** he read the change in the hand and not the charcoal, and does not know what she counts under her breath at the fire, and put a finger between her sound and his three. Why Sella's thumb stayed one breath longer at the wire crossing on Ash 9, why Tarn's hand stayed a breath longer at the knife-sill on Ash 14, and why Jorra left his bent finger a breath longer in the cold iron on Ash 26 --- whether any meant anything, whether any was a habit of the hand or a thing a man would do again, and who might one day read them; he has the intervals and their edges and nothing else, made no payer of them and no lesson, and the first two were not made again on these three days, nor was the third on any day since. What the men want who come before light to the back room each afternoon and who keep the room open and low-voiced, or why the room is a finger's width open, or whether there were three or four of them; whether the men in that room and the men shut in the counting room of the ownerless sayings are the same men, or whether there is any room at all; he has no name, no hand, no figure and no witness standing to be asked. What the nine true subtractions in Berr's locked box say, or what Berr read on the slate he held inward, or why a kindness had to be entered to a block that answered nothing; he knows the figure printed and the figure sent over the basket twice, and not the figure paid. Who put the hand into the worked strips on Seed 15 and whether it ever came out, who was carried out on Day 188, who stood at the pot board with the sweet green at her mouth and who wore the green sliver at the market's edge; a colour is not a name and a shape is not a house, and he did not make either into Fenna or Ulda. Why Ilva's notches are cut in the wood and what count she keeps that no slate can take a figure off; what Hessa's slate says and why she will carry it and not read it; what the soft-cuffed clerk's inward measure was that nobody called. Whether any of the three ownerless words came from the men in the back room or from Slate Row or from nowhere at all, and whether the burned slate and the room shut with the damper closed have anything to do with the man nobody names. He does not know that a far-off hand will one day lay the district's private keepings together, or that the hand is not his. He does not know what is coming on in the dark beyond the door, or that the month in which he was born will bring a first firing, a first birthday and a Hearth Drawing; none of those dates was named to him under any roof. The old life stays asleep behind his eyes and was named to nobody in these three days.

## What Chapter 103 must not forget

Chapter 103 opens on Day 360, Stone 1, Firstday, with the hand laid down on answer **294** and the count still exact at **359** --- six months and one hundred and seventy-nine days told by nobody, the carried fire's fifth unpaid and Garl's name unspoken the same number. Nothing from Ash 28, 29 or 30 crosses over as sign, lesson, mending, cause, payment or payer; the ninth reading bought no lesson under the Vail roof, and the nine arithmetics stand as nine and not one account. Carry forward unchanged and unspent: **294 honest answers** on the sixth place; **nine arithmetics of the year --- the three of Frost, the first, second and third of Bone, Ash's first, Ash's second and Ash's third --- behind one unsigned figure, nine and not one account**, with all three Ash subtractions locked and unread in Berr Clay's box up the clay road, the key in a coat pocket that does not come back down, the figure read still not the figure paid and no signature on the space; six hearings with the seventh place empty; the heard bell beside the told; nine record-first hands with no tenth (Ilva's ninth, Hessa's eighth); nine stoppings with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end and the loan uncalled; the row at five askings with no witness; the burned slate at five, the shut-room tellings at two, the well-saying at four shapings, and the Ash 5 joining, the Ash 17 direction and the Ash 23 direction held apart with a finger between every pair, all advanced and not paid; the holding of Ash 26 on its own place, gathered once and not made again; the promise under the wheel-shed neither given nor withdrawn and *he's not Vail* sealed. The deep-winter month in which he was born comes on without a boundary drawn and neither of the district's two habits of naming the months corrected; its first firing stands far off and unbrought, the first birthday on Stone 7 (Day 366) beyond that and the Hearth Drawing on Stone 12 (Day 371) further still, and none is named to him under any roof. **Do not** bring Stone into a day that has not earned it, supply a tenth arithmetic, a signature, a payer or a doctrine out of nine, repeat the Ash 26 holding, make Ilva or Hessa a reader or a speaker, name the green-mouthed woman or the foundling, call the loan, make a seventh opening, hearing or held object, or let an answer stand up as a power. No collector came up or down the clay road in this chapter and none is to be summoned by a reading; the debt stays at the teeth. The hand goes on being laid down where the next hand will find it without looking, and answers 295 through 300 will be entered in Chapter 103 with the count reaching 362 exact. Forward, not closed.
