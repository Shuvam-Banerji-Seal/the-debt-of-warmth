Days 45, 46 and 47 have been lived, one to a dated entry: the morning after the Blessing below the north bridge, a day under the Vail roof in which the household's arithmetic went on around a boy keeping the count exactly, a day in which a crust went out of the fold and nobody said who took it, and a day in which frost wrote on the well-lip where the Warm had lately gone out and the yard called it weather by noon. ## The moment

Day 47 is done. On Ilmen 18, Eighthday, Y.H. 813, at dusk, \person{Sella Vail} carried the boy up \place{the Clay Yard} from \place{the yard well} at a half-pace from every open slate, set him on his bed under the \place{Vail kiln-house} roof, not in \place{the Debt Loft}, and put the fold right without looking at his face. ## The protagonist's condition

**Body.** \person{Corin}, forty-seven days old at the close of Day 47. He was \person{Daniel Marchetti}, forty, twelve of them a trauma surgeon at Cook County; he died going back into a burning minivan for a child under the dashboard and his warmth left him in the giving and did not cool. As Corin he is a cracked, low-capacity vessel, unranked --- below Thin, twelve to fifteen embers at best, with a flaw nobody has named and a rate of loss nobody has measured. He is carried everywhere: under the north bridge in the bridge's shade, up the Clay Yard, at the yard well in the shade out of the draught, and lifted onto the bed under the roof, never into \place{the Debt Loft}. He cannot roll, stand, speak, read \lore{Grathic}, reach a cup or lift a hand to his own chin. He drools; the thread at his mouth freezes in the small hours and nobody wipes it, because a basket set down is a pot set down and left. His head goes where it wants and makes the small wet sound when it goes too far; the hand outside the wool goes up an inch toward the warmth and comes down; nothing within two paces can be reached. Cold arrives in its known order --- cheek, temple, wrist --- and the wool does less than the wind wants. The branching red birth-flower runs from the third rib on the left down the forearm and stops short of the wrist; Alda crossed two fingers at its red edge again on Day 46 without knowing she had done it and covered the sign with a date. He wants the breast and wants the crust even cold, and gets neither: now only the spent crust remains, stiff past smelling, and the warmth he most wanted is an empty place in the fold. A woman went into the water nine mornings ago two paces from where he lay and he knew exactly how the vessel gave and could not lift a hand toward her.

**Mind.** The double consciousness has acquired three more days of objects and still no hand to enter any of them. He knows the order of reaching --- the hand to the record before the hand to the person --- and has now seen it in a midwife, a neighbour at a well, a clerk behind a Court table, a man who priced him, Berr passing a finger over a figure, the soft-cuffed clerk at the Fishstairs, Alda going to her slate before the child, and Berr again at the well. This chapter added: that a shape can be read without its name and a word can be kept beside the thing it names without the two touching; that a warmth can leave a fire's account with a bird and no slate show the loss and no house miss it; that a taking can be a keeping, and the absence of a kindness that could not be answered has the same shape as the kindness itself; that a number can pass from fist to mouth to shoulder and arrive nowhere; that a weakness spoken of in the third person leaves a board where nobody's weight has been; that frost can write on a stone lip where a slate should stand and be called weather by noon; that a thing that does not happen leaves a space with the outline of a sound that did not come. He knows the difference between a likeness and a cause because a better-lit room taught him the cost of getting it wrong, and he keeps the honeyed ice, the hairline, the higher brook and this frost-bloom in four places, the told bell beside but never on the heard bell, six like rooms in six places, the six hearings beside and never among, and the three suspecting hands on a tray that does not become a cause. He knows that a kindness which cannot be answered cannot be refused, that knowing a thing exactly has never once given him a hand to act with, and that he is the only adult in every room and the least use in it.

**Powers.** None usable, no rank change, no experiment, no clamp, no heresy. He cannot move Warm, cannot clamp, cannot speak, cannot make a fire, cannot read \lore{Grathic} in speech or writing, cannot enter a figure anywhere. Counting is an old-life habit, not a gift: the kiln's four, four and a gap; the bell's four, four and a gap with nobody crossing himself; the Fishstairs' four, four and a missing step; the boards' four, four and a knot; the death-house bench's four, four and a knot; the well-roof's four, four and a stone that does not ring; the kindling's four, four and a space; the firelight's four, four and an empty shelf; the told bell's four, four and a gap; the officer's four measures, four measures and a missing closing line; four ways out of the Cross; four oars on the collector's boat in Orra's story. The ear still holds six hearings of \lore{the full note}: five from four sources --- a cup across the birth room, a pot in the Vail wheel-shed through the wall, the district's slate twice at \place{the Cross}, and a man giving at the Drawing --- and the sixth, the small flat knock out of Dessa as the men lifted, from a person, filed beside the five and never among them, not a seventh. The nearly full answer of Day 35 remains a third sound on its own tray, advanced and not paid. The word forty remains a saying, not a vessel's sound, on its own tray with the readable shape of the double turn beside it and the weight of a thumb on wicker apart from both; it was not spoken again in three days. The trays do not touch: intervals, gaps, told bell, absence-shaped-like-a-collection, word, shape and knot. No Grathic word for the flat note, for nearly, for the failure in a vessel or for a thing taken and not spoken of, and no word of his own for the taking.

**Possessions and money.** Nothing he can use. **Held/changed:** only the brown-edged barley crust put into the fold on Stone 15 remains, stiff and gone past smelling of anything, uneaten, unlifted, unnamed. The fresh smoky crust from Mira, given on Day 22, is **gone** as of the afternoon of Day 46: taken quietly, not eaten in his sight, not lifted in it, not named by anyone, and not solved; whether Sella's hand, Alda's hand or Mira's hand took it is left open, and the boy set all three on a tray and would not let the tray become a cause. His hand outside the wool reaches an inch toward the empty place and comes down. The coal the brine-gull took on Day 45 is gone from the carried fire's account, entered nowhere and missed in no account. The shape of Dessa's thumb on the wicker is still a held thing, paid and kept where things with no hour are kept. **Held/unchanged:** the small pale river stone remains in the fold of grey wool on the abandoned board-bed in House Silt, unmentioned; the small bench knife is back on its sill, announced to the empty yard on Days 45 and 46 and to nobody on Day 47; the charred slate still lies in the kiln ash, black on one side and eaten into the shape of a strip of bark; the basket and wool are Sella's, lent without a word; the clay jar Sella fetched from House Silt stands on the wall shelf and is the house's, not his. Alda's little tin is inside her bodice, touched on Day 46 without opening, and her fourth narrow slate is turned face-down toward a floorboard in a house the boy cannot name; Sella's private roll is unshown; Berr's box is locked in his house; Mira's grey wool cord and the second cord twisted twice beneath it have knots uncounted; Orra's knot-string with its forty-first knot hangs in the Eel House, unread, its shape now legible to the boy without its name; the \lore{Ash} ridge is gone from the Vail corner, burned on Ilmen 1 and called dealt with, and both of Garl's proverbs are still said to the fire and the pot. **Filed but not possessed:** the honeyed ice, the hairline that stops short of the edge, the higher clay-heavy brook, the frost-bloom on the well-lip, the Ilm's rise under a thinning skin, the weak board heard of at the Weir, the missing Fishstairs step, the green at a mouth and the looking, Orra's knot and the word forty, Dessa's nineteen in her thumb, the two not-lookings, the paid thumb on wicker, the clerk's record-first order, Berr's twice-wiped thumb among the record-first hands, Jorra's clean forearms, the cracked pot, the told bell, the nearly-full note, the absence with the outline of a collection, the five unjoined pauses, the clean dark line, the empty fifth in the kindling and the shape of a thumb pressed into wicker. Every figure goes past him unspendable, uncorrectable and unreturnable.

**Wounds carried from the other life.** The unsent call to Nina --- nine summers, nine promises, a boat never bought --- was paid on Day 38, in past tense and untagged, in the shape of Dessa's thumb on the wicker reaching toward a pocket that held a small cold rectangle of light with a name on it; the name was not looked at and the hand came back with wool, and nothing like it returned in chapter 11. The chart with its note turned down and the hand going past it to a signature stood behind that reaching; the flare light and the hand that stopped between two clusters had made a fifth sighting on Stone 30 and have not made a sixth, and the Fishstairs clerk on Days 45 and 47 was filed apart, not joined to that writer or to the grey-fingered Drawing collector. Tasha Broussard's chart, signed without reading the nurse's note, and the institution calling it a failure of the system, were recognised in Berr's passive sentences, the Court clerk's soft *it's entered as it stands*, the district's grammar of weather and the shape of a hand going to a slate before a person. Vesci's *that's what a chart is for* was not spoken; Ostrowski's voice stayed offstage. His father's casket slightly crooked in the aisle; Elena's quiet leaving, Marisol's temperate one; Des's laugh did not return. In Sarn's terms he is an unclosed account heavy enough to crack a slate, and he has been entered in three accounts that never asked him --- the house's, the Court's and Dessa's count of bits --- of which the third is the quietest because nobody thinks it is an account at all, and is gone into the water with the number still in her thumb.

## Who is where, and what they want now

### Under the Vail roof at night

- **Corin/Dan** --- on his bed under the roof with the fold at his ear, **one** crust in it and an empty place beside it, awake, silent, drool frozen and unwiped, hand outside the wool going up an inch toward the warmth and coming down. Wants the breast, wants the crust that is gone, wants a hand to lift to the cord upriver; gets none of the three and cannot ask for any. Holds the readable shape of the knot, the kept word, the coal gone with the gull and the empty place where the crust had been, and can spend none of them. His count goes on into a year the district has entered and has not entered him.
- **Sella Vail**, 33, keeper of the Vail fire --- at the fire and the bed, having carried him at a half-pace from every open slate at the well and set him on his bed at dusk. She says *the house* where another woman says *I*, gives every instruction to the fire, the pot and the wool and never to the basket, fed him from the house's own dish on Day 46 without wiping his mouth, and did not name the warmth paid or either crust or the one that is gone. She touched the widow's mark once before opening the kiln on Day 45 and has not touched it since, not at a bank, a reading, a market, a well or the carrying of a basket; she turns the coals with the back of a broken pot. Her private roll stays unshown, she has not written him as Vail, and she will not let anyone fill Garl's seat or take his coat down. Wants the fire kept, the house kept, the keeping never explained; the thaw coming on in plain sight --- frost on the well-lip, the ice going soft before its time, the brook up --- has made that harder without giving her anyone to answer.
- **Tarn**, 22, the Vail's clay-bound hand --- in the yard and under the roof, broom set away, small knife back on its sill: announced to the empty yard on Days 45 and 46 and told nobody on Day 47 because the yard had already been told. Six years of the seven are counted; he will not count the seventh, the one entry in this house the house cannot check. Ash is worked into the creases of his knuckles that no scrubbing lifts; the pale clip-ring at his left wrist and the mark worn almost flat on the knife handle are his own records. His broom went over the road a man takes to fetch a collector, over and over, without once turning, and no collector went down it. He agreed with the early-thaw party and the late-thaw party so thoroughly that there was nowhere for an argument to put its foot, and went on wanting the late one, because a late thaw puts off the paying. He has heard a flat note and seen the boy hear one and said nothing about either. A loan that can hear is a loan that can be called; the sentence has never been said under this roof and lies under it all the same.
- **Jorra Vail**, 16 --- in and out of the wheel-shed with slip dried to the colour of old bone up both forearms and no grey worked past the bend of the little finger on Days 45, 46 and 47, the bent finger going in ahead of his hand at the damper on each of them, moving it before Sella has finished telling him the fire is low. He tells pots they will do and the wheel it does not care whose hands, only if they are steady. He knows the full note of a Vail pot before it leaves the wheel and would not say so before a kiln-master. He has not once looked toward the bed. The room off the shed keeps its finger's width of open and nobody pushes it wider. Still wants the foundling outside the name Vail and the hollow seat for himself; *he's not Vail* is as sealed as on the hour it was spoken, and the broken slates under the drying rack and the men whose names are not spoken remain unshown.
- **\person{Garl Vail}** --- absent and the shape of most of the house, though the ridge that was his ash-marker is now a clean dark line. His warmth lies where it fell, the coat keeps his broad shape on the peg, the seat keeps his twenty-year leftward lean, and the debt against a firing of tiles remains neither paid nor settled nor done with. Under the Vail roof his name was not spoken on the Remembering, and the house went around the word as Sella goes around ash in a corner. His proverbs live in the house's mouth: *feed the fire before you feed yourself*, and *leave the crack showing; people trust a thing they can see has been handled* --- the second went on being said to the pot after the crack was gone and the ridge was a clean dark line.

### Three doors down and farther offstage

- **\person[Marra]{Collector Marra}**, still not in Sill --- not present, not named in Sarn narration, not mistaken for any of this chapter's walk-ons. She forged an assessment with the deduction entered as zero and has been paying with her fingers ever since. The clerk at the Fishstairs is an ordinary hand with no pause that the boy could see, filed apart from her pause and from the flare hand.
- **Rusk of the Weir**, 70, walking the traps at dusk in ice-mouse fur, unbanked since Y.H. 675; in his locked drawer Haldre's *The Sealed Vessel*, nine names with eight struck out, and a rubbing of a branching red mark from Y.H. 588. He has not seen the boy and has already decided what to make of him. \place{Nine Pots} was named in chapter 11 only as the place Mira's second cord has never visited; nothing else about it is shown.
- A Waiting Room clerk in \place{Slate Row} whose name is on no roll takes small secrets from children and pays in loaves and ale. Ulda will sell him the boy's ear in a few years and, later, the mark.

### The wider world, asleep or waking

- **\place{Greybank}** (18 leagues east): \person{Veno Brine} holds a damp copy of the slate for a whole return and cannot spell the purchaser's name; a factor at Greybank confirmed Sella's widow's mark on Stone 10, as Berr reported it, and the confirmation has been spoken around through another turning of the year. \person{Solene Ember}, kept in a room with good light, removes a ring one at a time and does not know the boy exists.
- **The Hearthseat** (150 leagues south-east): Steward Derren has authorised a private whole return of forty unspent measures without recording the purchaser; Provost Wessen has a slate corrected in a second hand in his second file and has entered nothing in any report. Rennick, 26, posted to Sill in Y.H. 810, is not yet in town and is already asking why forty unspent measures are needed for one intact one.
- **Keld** (120 leagues west-north-west): Hald Iron reads Haldre's paper on the Last Day from a tin lined with warm-stone; Siv of the First Seam keeps the crossed-out Y.H. 588 survey and the shape of the mark on the back of his tally.
- **Vhalen** (80 leagues north-east): Pia of the Quay, 14, does not yet know the boy. **The Waste** lies beyond the Rim, taking what nobody keeps; the Ash in the Vail corner is gone and called dealt with, and the death-house corner was burned the same morning --- unkept promises already made.

### On Earth

- **\person{Nina Marchetti}**, grown, lives a life organised around not waiting; the missed call from the night of the crash is on her phone still, and the name under the lit screen was reached for on Day 38 in the shape of a woman's thumb on a wicker basket and was not looked at. She will never learn that the warmth that crossed was not hers.
- **\person{Deshawn Carter}** took the 3 a.m. call and laughed for exactly one minute before he put the radio down; he has not yet told Nina her father was working late.
- **\person{Lena Ostrowski}**, retired, has a photograph of Dan as a junior and a little book of every patient he lost, with Tasha Broussard's name in his own handwriting. Her voice stayed offstage; she withheld one careful question from the person she loved like a son and has never said why.
- **\person{Paul Vesci}**, in private practice in Evanston in a room with good light, keeps a file on Dan's career with one page about Tasha's chart, and has forgotten the unkind thing he said about Nina.

## Open dangers

- The thaw has stopped being a taste in the water and has taken a person. Dessa Silt was carried away uncollected in the Slip Brook behind the racks and stays dead; the Ilm runs higher under a skin so thin the current shows through it in dark veins; the Singing Sill gives its low note early through the boards of both kiln-house and Weir; the Slip Brook runs clay-heavy a little higher than it should; frost-bloom wrote on the well-lip where the Warm had lately gone out and the ice is said to be going soft before its time; a board in the plank walk will not bear a man's weight when the river comes up in Silt and a man steps over it without comment. The district has one word for all of it --- weather --- that lets a person go home warm without owning a day. He knows the anatomy of a vessel going thin and has no Grathic word for the failure and no hand to point at it.
- No collector came for the woman in the brook and nobody named the omission. Warmth can be left where it fell and the morning still comes: the first edge of a heresy that will one day refuse to collect, planted as something simply not done, with no word, no doctrine and no argument. The clerk at the Fishstairs has now sat for seven mornings with a slate for a measure nobody called, his hand going to the slate before his eyes to the stalls; the absence of a draw for the dead woman is entered nowhere. The boy is inside three accounts that never asked him --- the house's, the Court's and Dessa's count of bits --- and the third is gone into the water with the number still in her thumb.
- The number still goes around in fists, mouths, shoulders and weather: one in twelve printed and unchallenged, the true subtraction unspoken, Berr's figure that goes into a slate and not into a well, the number for the thaw passed from fist to mouth to shoulder and never given, marks for eels given six for five, the house's arithmetic in loaves and glances with no figure. Sella's private roll is still unshown; Berr's box stays locked; Mira's two cords and Orra's knot-string are records no Court can confiscate and no slate can enter, and the knots are still uncounted; Alda's four written notes lie turned down where no mother or officer will read them; the pewter tin above the empty bed holds a pale fold nobody has opened. The neat upper line has been seen five times and the writer still has no name in his file; the Drawing collector's ordinary hand and the Fishstairs clerk's ordinary order stay filed apart. The word forty was spoken once, low and watched, and is now two days unspoken.
- Orra tied a forty-first knot over the story of a small warm bundle with a flower and said the number into the basket and watched; the cord hangs in the Eel House where he cannot enter or read it, though as of Day 45 he can read its shape without its name. A woman has kept a count in wool for three years and knows something about a child carried up the Clay Yard in the year of the flower, and she is watching still to see what the boy does with a word he cannot answer. The shape of the knot is on its tray beside the word and the weight of a thumb apart from both, and the watching has had no answer.
- One of the two crusts in the fold is gone and nobody says who took it, that it is gone, or what became of it. A kindness that could not be answered has become an absence that cannot be answered; the reader may suspect Sella, Alda or Mira, including the girl who put the crust there. A coal went up with a brine-gull on Day 45 and one warmth less went out of the world with no slate the poorer. This is how the district keeps what it never enters, and the boy keeps both subtractions without a word in \lore{Grathic} for either.
- Sella's widow's mark stands confirmed by a factor at Greybank and entered as a kindness; it is held while the fire never goes out and touched only before an opening, never at a bank, a market, a reading, a well or the carrying of a basket. Garl's uncalled debt against the tiles has been spoken around through another turning of the year and remains never paid, never settled, never done with; his name was not said under the Vail roof on the Remembering and his objects keep his place. His clean dark line is not swept, because a line left where it is is still a line kept.
- Tarn has heard a flat note and said nothing, has seen the boy hear one before it happened and said nothing about that either, has swept the road to a collector without turning and noticed that he had noticed. A loan that can hear is a loan that can be called; two people keeping the same silence under one roof is an equality neither can name, and a man whose ear is worth more than his debt is a man a house might one day learn to count. Jorra's clean forearms and the unnameable back room, the space under the rack where slate should have stood, Sella's half-pace and her unnamed moving of the basket, Mira's question-answers and two cords, Orra's knot-on-a-lie, the green at a mouth and the weakness of a board are all keepings no slate can enter. The boy is the only person who has seen them and has no standing to ask.
- The kiln's breath is named as the thing that takes a man in his sleep, and Mud End called the death merciful; the carried fire is still under blind ash and the back of a broken pot, warming the boards by degrees and missing one breath in every four. What the house cannot measure it calls weather or mercy: the cough that killed a woman, the debt, the numb hand, the ridge burned and called dealt with, and the thaw itself are all called something that lets a person go home warm without owning a day of it.
- The child on the quilt in the death-house was worse on the third day of its return and the room called it a graft settling; Alda told the story in the dark with dates where wonder should stand. The boy's own flower was crossed at again on Day 46 by a woman who did not know she did it and covered with a date. The death-house ridge was measured a finger's width thicker than Vail's and both were burned on Ilmen 1.
- The Court's room and the Eel House have the same bench, pot, draught and gap as the birth room, the kiln-house and the death-house, and the boy has kept six likenesses apart on purpose; no cause has been written in any of them. Berr takes the speaker out of his sentences as neatly as a surgeon takes a hand out of a field, and the district's grammar of weather does the same work.
- The flat note has six hearings --- five from four sources and one from a person --- and has bought him nothing; nobody has tapped a vessel for him and there is no Grathic word for it. The nearly full note lies on its own tray as an answer to the Stone 13 interval, advanced and not paid, and is not a hearing and not the third thing. The bell is a Court instrument doing a temple's work, still unexplained, four, four and a gap in the square and in a story told in the dark, kept in two places; the count is four, four and a gap in the kiln, the boards, the bell, the bench, the well-roof, the river-prints, the kindling, the firelight and the space between a woman's thumb and her scar, and the gap is the part nobody has thought to enter.
- A woman is dead and stays dead, uncollected, her number in her thumb and her name under her scar; the mercy told her became a fact nobody alive can correct, and she believed him spent among children she never saw to the last. The word *the Breaking* has not been said in his hearing; the word forty was said once, low, and is not explained and was not repeated.

## What he knows and does not know

**He knows.** How a body fails: airway, bleeding, bones, burns, cold, infection, afterdrop and rewarming, the small evidence a body carries, and triage; the way a room cools from the edges inward and a small body goes at the extremities before the core admits to it; how a man pulled from cold water can worsen before he settles; the exact look and sound of a vessel handing its work to the next thing until the skin holds more than it was made to hold and the face arranges itself in the ordinary way. The smell of machine coffee and hospital sanitizer, snow going up a sleeve, orange streetlight on wet snow, his mother's kitchen, his daughter's photograph, the shape of an unsent sentence. He knows he died and that he is in a baby's body. This chapter added: that a shape can be read without a name and kept beside the word without the two touching; that warmth can leave a fire's account with a bird and no slate or house miss it; that a taking can be a keeping and an absence can have the same shape as an unanswerable kindness; that one crust where two had lain is felt with the exactness of a wound and yields no cause; that a number can travel from fist to mouth to shoulder and arrive nowhere; that a weakness spoken of in the third person leaves a board with nobody's weight on it; that frost can write where a slate should stand and be called weather by noon; that three likenesses laid in a row will invent a cause for a man who wants one. He knows Tarn has turned his head at a note and said nothing, and that two silences under one roof are nearer an equality than anything the house has a name for.

**He does not know.** The word *Sarn*, or that the Warm is the stuff the world is made of; any Grathic word beyond what he has absorbed by listening; that the Return is an industry with collectors, bankers and a deduction; that he was bought; that forty children paid for his whole return; who the woman in the grey coat is, what she wrote, or what her name says; why the flower is on his ribs; why his fevers will come; that ways run in spirals; that Dessa is his mother and Sella knew; that the Court is a bank and the temple an invoice; that somebody has watched him since before his birth and intends to use his coldness to seal the world. He does not know why Sella took him, what happened to Garl's warmth beyond the household's version, where the carried fire was first lit and who pays to keep it giving, what is in Alda's tin or what she wrote four times and turned down, which house holds the floorboard under which the notes go, what goes on in the back room whose door is kept shut, what the missing slates under the drying rack were for, why the second cord has never gone upriver, where the Ash goes beyond the north gate, where the measure goes after it is entered, what the uncalled debt was for, why the mark was confirmed, why Sella's roll is kept private, or what the Court's soft sentences actually do. He does not know why no collector came for the woman in the brook or that the omission will one day have a name. He has heard the word forty spoken once, low, by a woman who watched him, and does not know that it is the number of children who paid for him or that he is one of them, nor what the forty-first knot counts, nor that Orra tied it the night a woman carried him up the Clay Yard with a flower on the bundle. He can read the shape of the knot but not its name and has no word to spend it on. He does not know who took the crust from the fold or that the taking will be read years hence as a gift entered on no slate; he does not know that the green-mouthed woman who looked at pots has a name or an ear, that Dessa put him among the bits she counted, that Mira is kin to the house three doors down, or that either woman knows more about him than she will say on a slate. He does not know that the clerk at the Fishstairs will sit there for a standing charge on a district that has already spent its dead, or that the weak board at the Weir is where the river will put the Fishstairs out of line.

**He suspects, without evidence.** That the gap in the count is the part he keeps, whether in boards, breaths, steps, kindling, a bell, a landing, a woman's thumb, a hairline in ice that stops short of an edge, an empty shelf where a fifth would stand, the space between a woman's thumb and her scar, a word said low and watched, an empty place in the fold or a coal gone up with a bird; that the ridges, the charred slate, the barrows of grey, the benches, the dust, the cords, the crusts, the intervals, the honeyed ice, the frost-bloom, the higher brook, the knot-string and the pauses are one thing seen many ways, and that joining them would be a deciding he has no standing to make; that the passed fire has a payer somewhere beyond the yard, as the stone and the gruel and the gone crust have payers inside it; that Tarn's silence and his own are the same object held from two sides; that the five pauses are five objects of one shape and making a sixth is not his work either; that the nearly full note is an answer to the interval he has carried since Stone 13 and not yet the third thing; that the six like rooms with a bench, a pot, a draught and a gap are a likeness he must not turn into a cause, because he has made that mistake once in a better-lit room and carried it for the rest of a life; that the told bell and the heard bell must be kept apart for the same reason, and so must the word on its tray and the sound in his ear; that a left thing and a burned thing are two ways of keeping, and a thing called gone may still be in the room as a smell; that a kindness which cannot be answered cannot be refused, and that the clerks' reasons are more dangerous than cruelties would have been because they are easier to live beside; that the things a house calls weather or mercy are the things it has entered nowhere; that Dessa's count kept something larger out of the room in the same way his own count keeps the gap; that two not-lookings with the same shape can be opposite things; that a word watched is a thing someone expects a reaction to, and the watching has not stopped.

## Relationships at the close of chapter 11

- **Nina** --- love and resentment braided; the debt that made him portable. The unread name in a lit pocket was reached for on Day 38 through the paid shape of a woman's thumb on a basket, in past tense, and the hand came back with wool. Nine promises, no boat.
- **Des, Ostrowski, Vesci** --- the dead life's voices. None returned in voice this chapter; the district's grammar of weather, Berr's passive sentences and the shape of a hand going to a slate before a person stood in for them. Still no tag on a remembered line.
- **Orra Weir** --- a watching that has lasted from the day he was carried up the Clay Yard to the knot tied at the Blessing and the word said low into the basket, and through three more days of no answer. The shape is legible to him now; the word was not said again. She is not a friend and not an enemy; she is a person who set a thing down in front of him and is waiting to see whether it will be picked up.
- **Sella Vail** --- the hand that puts the fold right and never looks at his face, that says *the house* and gives the instruction to the wool; a keeping with no figure and no explanation. One of three hands the boy set on a tray as possible takers of the crust, which is not the same thing as a cause.
- **Alda Reed** --- the hand that presses the fold too long and covers a sign with a date, exact and not true; another of the three hands on the tray.
- **Mira Silt** --- the hand that put the crust there and has kept two cords no officer can read; the third of the three hands, and the one he has wanted the crust from longest.
