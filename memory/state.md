## The moment

Day 254, Smoke 15, Fifthday, Y.H. 813, the small hours, under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof in \place{Low Kiln}. \person[Corin]{the boy} lies on \place{the bed} at the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf}, awake and silent, in the room that does not touch the room of the fire, with the fold at his ear and one stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust two inches from it; the empty place beside the crust keeps its name to itself and no third came to make the row come out even. The thread of drool at his mouth has frozen in the dry heat and is unwiped, because nobody under any roof has ever been given an instruction about the thread and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. Cold has stopped at cheek, temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rides back, as though it had come to the end of a line written out for it and had neither an order to go further nor an order to go away; the afterdrop waits beyond all three, past a warming nobody in this house will perform. He knows the severe face cold wears, knows a cold patient cannot clot and that a man must be warmed from the outside and slowly, and that the cold patient will seem to improve before he worsens. He wants the crust. Both are true and the second is louder; the surgeon takes the inventory and enters it nowhere.

Through the finger's-width door the river keeps its note of \lore{water over water}, a fifth low thing never counted among the hearings; under the floor \lore{the carried fire} gives four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have been, its payer without a name for two hundred and fifty-four days; overhead \place{the boards} keep four nails on the near board, four on the next, and a thumb-width knot where the fifth nail should have stood. Three counts in three places, and none rises into another, because agreeing them would want a payer put under all three and no payer stands to any. The bell's four, four and a gap, heard at the Cross on Smoke 10, stays in the square beside the told bell and is not folded into the three and not heard as a seventh of the full note. Sella has gone to her own bed before the small hours; there is no breathing number anywhere in the house and no fire-sitter standing over the coals, and he borrows no rhythm from the room that does not touch his except the little silence after a count that has already ended there.

At the close he has laid the hand down against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. He had put the sixth question to it: the hand outside the wool went up an inch toward the low note through the finger's-width door, took that borrowed count and the little silence after it, crossed the two against the river's note in the inch and nowhere else, and came down on the river's next low note. The two-rhythm crossing was remembered and not done anew. It was filed on the sixth place with no payer named, not spent or explained: not a longer reach, a miss entered, a hearing, a seventh of the full note, a tenth stopping, a payment, one of the held changes, a key, a power or a rule. **Eighty-four** answers stand there now, each an object and not a power, exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air. The file lies open on the towel with every gap left gap and every tray parted from its neighbour, and the count goes forward into Smoke 16, a day that has not earned its heading.

Two hundred and fifty-four stands exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in \place{Sarn}, \place{Sill} or \place{Grath}; six months and seventy-four days are told by nobody; the fire's fifth is unpaid the same number of days; \lore{the widow's mark} was touched once before the kiln on Smoke 1, held one breath, let go, and touched at no shelf, well, rack, road, basket, afternoon or dusk on Smoke 13, 14 or 15; the seventh year stands at its very end and has still not given. Smoke went out without a boundary drawn and neither of the district's two month-names was set right or left wrong; Smoke 16 (Day 255) stands **one morning ahead** and has not been brought into a day that has not earned it, and Smoke 20 (Day 259), the second Account-day and market of the second firing, stands five mornings from the close and was not reached for early. The next real opening, the first firing of Frost (Day 271), is far off and unbrought.

## The protagonist's condition

**Body.** \person{Corin}, two hundred and fifty-four days old at the close of Day 254 and six months and seventy-four days (two hundred and fifty-two and six months and seventy-two days on Smoke 13, two hundred and fifty-three and six months and seventy-three on Smoke 14, two hundred and fifty-four and six months and seventy-four on Smoke 15), carried everywhere: on Smoke 13 up and down the yard and at the hearth, kiln mouth, drying racks and loaf shelf; on Smoke 14 and 15 under the roof, and on Smoke 15 up \place{the clay road} at the going of the light and set on his bed under the roof and not in \place{the Debt Loft}. He was \person{Daniel Marchetti}, forty, twelve of them a trauma surgeon at Cook County; he died going back into a burning minivan for a child under the dashboard and his warmth left him in the giving and did not cool. As Corin he is a cracked, low-capacity vessel, unranked --- below Thin, twelve to fifteen embers at best, with a flaw nobody has named and a rate of loss nobody has measured. He cannot roll, stand, speak, read \lore{Grathic} in speech or writing, reach a cup, close two inches on the rim of the basket that holds him, lift a hand to his own chin or lift a crust two inches to his mouth. He drools; the thread at his mouth froze in the dry heat and was left unwiped on all three days. Cold comes cheek, temple, the small bare joint at the wrist where the wool rides back, and stops there as if it had reached the end of its own instruction; the afterdrop waits past a warming nobody in this house will perform. One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust lies two inches from his ear with its empty place unnamed and no third to make the row come out even. The one change is the hand: it can go up an inch toward a sound and stay for a small intake, for an interval held between intake and letting-go, for a whole counted breath, past the end of a counted breath into the little silence after it, and can cross two such intervals in the inch across a wall that does not touch, as it did on Days 251, 254 and now again --- but each crossing is remembered and filed as an object, not made anew, and none of it is a reach, a hearing, a key, a power or a rule. The surgeon's knowledge of the cold body buys nothing at two inches.

**Mind.** The double consciousness has acquired three more days of objects and six more answers from the hand, and still no hand to enter any of them. He knows the order of reaching --- the hand to the record before the hand to the person --- and has now seen it in a midwife laying slates before turning to a fold, a thane passing a finger over a figure before the crowd, a clerk with hand to slate before eye to stalls, Sella reaching a finger beneath the shelf before her eyes went to the row, Sella's glance at the clay jar before anything else, Hessa settling her strap and turning her wrist before her eyes took in the crowd, Berr wiping his thumb twice and passing a finger over a figure before his eyes took the square, the soft-cuffed clerk entering a buyer's number before he looked up to find the man who had said it, and Ilva at the yard well passing a thumb-nail over notches before her eyes went to the asker. Hessa's reaching stays filed as the **eighth** such hand; Ilva's, taken from the district's firing work and not from any house or Court, is the **ninth**, joined to none, and nine hand-shapes must not stand up and call themselves a rule. This chapter added: that a pot set down wrong gives its high thin note before its time and can be filed on a tray of its own without being spent as a warning, made a fourth likeness, made a cause, joined to the sweet glaze wind or made to name what ailed the man carried out on Day 188; that an almost-note can be kept without being an account, because an account wants a payer and none stands under the note; that the ownerless question can be left at five askings with no witness and no row named without a sixth being supplied to make the row come out even; that five likenesses are not one witness and do not make a sixth; that a thing not priced is not therefore free but a thing whose payer has not been named; that a figure read over a covered thing knows the cover and not the thing, and the space between figure and cost wants a signature nobody set there; and that two rhythms crossed in the inch across a wall that does not touch, remembered, are still two objects on a tray and not a change made again. The wanting of a number goes on under the not-expecting the way a coal goes on under ash.

**Powers.** None usable, no rank change, no experiment, no clamp, no heresy. He cannot move Warm, cannot clamp, cannot speak, cannot make a fire, cannot read Grathic in speech or writing, cannot enter a figure anywhere. Counting is an old-life habit, not a gift: the kiln's four-and-a-gap, the boards' four nails, four and a knot, the carried fire's four pulls and missing fifth, the river's fifth low note, the told bell beside the heard, the told figure beside the paid, three counts in three places that do not become one, and 252 through 254 exact days set edge to edge against a house that keeps its days in loaves, glances and unnamed spaces.
