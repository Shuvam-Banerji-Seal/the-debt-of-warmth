## The protagonist's condition

**Body.** \person{Corin}, four hundred and forty-three days old at the close of Day 443 — six months and two hundred and sixty-three days (441 and six months and 261, 442 and six months and 262, 443 and six months and 263) — carried everywhere: down the nineteen steps to \place{the Clay Yard} on the afternoons of Silt 17, 18 and 19, to the yard on the seventeenth, to the yard well on the eighteenth and to the Vail corner on the nineteenth, and up again at each going of the light; set under the roof and never in \place{the Debt Loft}; the instruction went to the wool, the river wind, the pot, the fire and the banked fire and never to the basket, and on no day was the basket told what the day or the hour was or told to be still.
