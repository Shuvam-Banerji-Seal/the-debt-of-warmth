## What he knows, suspects and does not know

- **Knows, exactly and uselessly.** His count: three hundred and three through three hundred and eight days --- six months and one hundred and twenty-three through six months and one hundred and twenty-eight days --- kept edge to edge against a house that keeps its days in loaves, glances and unnamed spaces; the round three hundred was a threshold kept on a place of its own and not spent, and he has stopped expecting to be told what any of the numbers count, which is not the same as not wanting it. The order in which cold arrives --- cheek, temple, wrist, the wool riding back --- and the order in which a body takes heat back, the small joint first and the core last; that a cold patient cannot clot, that you do not rub a frozen foot and do not set it to the fire, and that all of it buys nothing without a hand two paces long. The shape of a reading said over a body that cannot answer, now said seven times from Grain 10 through Frost 30 (twice on Frost 30) and still not the figure paid, and the grammar of taking the speaker out of a sentence with *your house* rather than \emph{I} --- heard at every reading over the same basket from the same man, and called a kindness; and now the smaller movement of taking himself out of the sentence as a surgeon draws a hand from a field.
