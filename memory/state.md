## The protagonist's condition

**Body.** \person{Corin}, sixty days old at the close of Day 60, two months, carried everywhere: at the yard well in the shade out of the draught, down the Clay Yard at a half-pace from open slates, in the moot-post's shade at the Cross, in the lee of the eel creels at the Fishstairs, three doors down just inside a finger's-width-open door in House Silt, and lifted onto the bed under the roof, never into the Debt Loft. He was \person{Daniel Marchetti}, forty, twelve of them a trauma surgeon at Cook County; he died going back into a burning minivan for a child under the dashboard and his warmth left him in the giving and did not cool. As Corin he is a cracked, low-capacity vessel, unranked --- below Thin, twelve to fifteen embers at best, with a flaw nobody has named and a rate of loss nobody has measured. He cannot roll, stand, speak, read \lore{Grathic}, reach a cup or lift a hand to his own chin. He drools; the thread at his mouth freezes in the small hours and nobody wipes it, because a basket set down is a pot set down and left. His head goes where it wants and makes the small wet sound when it goes too far; the hand outside the wool goes up an inch toward the warmth and comes down; nothing within two paces can be reached. Cold arrives in its known order --- cheek, temple, wrist --- and the wool does less than the wind wants. The branching red birth-flower runs from the third rib on the left down the forearm and stops short of the wrist; Alda crossed two fingers at its red edge on Day 56 without knowing she had done it and covered the sign with a date. He wants the crust and wants the breast and gets neither: one spent crust lies in the fold and the warmth he most wanted is an empty place in the fold. Twenty-two mornings ago a woman went into the water two paces from where he lay and he knew exactly how the vessel gave and could not lift a hand toward her.

**Mind.** The double consciousness has acquired six more days of objects and still no hand to enter any of them. He knows the order of reaching --- the hand to the record before the hand to the person --- and has now seen it in a midwife, a neighbour at a well, a clerk behind a Court table, a man who priced him, Berr passing a finger over a figure, the soft-cuffed clerk at the Fishstairs, Alda going to her slate before the child, and Mira moving eel-bones and then her thumb to the grey cord while deciding whether to lie. This chapter added: that a room can be sealed and the bill paid by the bodies inside, and that a man can carry that description into a yard that has no use for it; that a danger under his own roof can be spoken aloud by a stranger and received as history; that two private records in two materials can stand shut in one room on trays that do not touch; that a house can keep its days badly in loaves and glances because to keep them exactly would be to own them; that a repair which pretends the break never happened is the district's ordinary kindness, and that a proverb can outlive the thing it was true about; that a month can come in on mud with no figure given; and that a new hairline of grey called thaw can lie beside a left-in-place line and be kept on two places without a word in Grathic for the difference. He knows the difference between a likeness and a cause because a better-lit room taught him the cost of getting it wrong, and he keeps the sealed counting room beside but never on the kiln's gap; the told bell beside the heard bell, and both beside the early note and the kiln's missing breath; six like rooms in six places; the six hearings beside and never among; two nearly-full answers advanced and not paid; the word on its tray, the shape of the double turn and the weight of a thumb apart from one another; and the clerk, the board, the stopping hairlines, the barrow, the two ridges and the new grey in separate places. He knows that a kindness which cannot be answered cannot be refused, that knowing a thing exactly has never once given him a hand to act with, and that he is the only adult in every room and the least use in it.

**Powers.** None usable, no rank change, no experiment, no clamp, no heresy. He cannot move Warm, cannot clamp, cannot speak, cannot make a fire, cannot read \lore{Grathic} in speech or writing, cannot enter a figure anywhere. Counting is an old-life habit, not a gift: the kiln's four, four and a gap; the bell's four, four and a gap with nobody crossing himself; the told bell in the dark; the Firestairs' four, four and a missing step; the boards' four, four and a knot; the death-house bench's four, four and a knot; the well-roof's four, four and a stone that does not ring; the kindling's four, four and a space; the firelight's four, four and an empty shelf. The ear still holds six hearings of \lore{the full note}: five from four sources --- a cup across the birth room, a pot in the \place{Vail wheel-shed} through the wall, the district's slate twice at \place{the Cross}, and a man giving at the Drawing --- and the sixth, the small flat knock out of \person{Dessa Silt} as the men lifted, from a person, filed beside the five and never among them, not a seventh. The nearly full answer of Day 35 (Ilmen 6) has a **second** answer beside it as of Day 52, both a third sound on their own tray, advanced and not paid, neither a hearing nor the third thing. The word forty remains a saying, not a vessel's sound, on its own tray with the readable shape of the double turn beside it and the weight of a thumb on wicker apart from both; it was not spoken again through the turning of the month. The trays do not touch: intervals, gaps, told bell, heard bell, early note, sealed-room saying, tin, word, shape, cords, crust, three hands, three near-misses and low sounds. No Grathic word for the flat note, for nearly, for the failure in a vessel, for a thing taken and not spoken of, or for the difference between a left line and a burned one.

**Possessions and money.** Nothing he can use. **Held/changed:** only the brown-edged barley crust put into the fold on Stone 15 remains, stiff and gone past smelling of anything, uneaten, unlifted, unnamed, carried into Silt. The fresh smoky crust from Mira, given on Day 22, is **gone** as of the afternoon of Day 46: taken quietly, not eaten in his sight, not lifted in it, not named by anyone, and not solved; whether Sella's hand, Alda's hand or Mira's hand took it is left open, and the boy set all three on a tray and would not let the tray become a cause. The empty place has been carried into a new month without a name. The coal the brine-gull took on Day 45 is gone from the carried fire's account, entered nowhere and missed in none. The shape of Dessa's thumb on the wicker is still a held thing, paid and kept where things with no hour are kept. **Held/unchanged:** the small pale river stone remains in the fold of grey wool on the abandoned board-bed in House Silt, unmentioned; the small bench knife is on its sill; the charred slate still lies in the kiln ash, black on one side and eaten into the shape of a strip of bark; the basket and wool are Sella's, lent without a word; the clay jar Sella fetched from House Silt stands on the wall shelf and is the house's, not his. Alda's little tin is inside her bodice, touched again on Day 56 without opening, and her **fifth** narrow slate is turned face-down toward a floorboard in a house the boy cannot name (four went before it; no sixth was written this chapter); Sella's private roll is unshown; Berr's box is locked in his house; Mira's grey wool cord and the second cord twisted twice beneath it have knots uncounted; Orra's knot-string with its forty-first knot hangs in the Eel House, unread, its shape legible to the boy without its name; the \lore{Ash} ridge is gone from the Vail corner, burned on Ilmen 1 and called dealt with, and both of Garl's proverbs are still said to the fire and the pot; a **new** hairline of grey lies beside the old clean dark line in the Vail corner, called thaw and not swept. **Filed but not held:** six hearings of the full note; the Greybank saying; the pewter tin and its pale fold; two nearly-full answers; three intervals; five unjoined pauses; the told and heard bells; the early note; the word, the shape and the weight of a thumb; two cords; the crust's empty place; the clerk, the board, the barrow and the new grey.

**Wounds carried from the other life.** The unsent call to Nina --- nine summers, nine promises, a boat never bought --- was paid on Day 38, in past tense and untagged; nothing like it returned in chapter 13, and the old life stayed offstage throughout. The chart with its note turned down and the hand going past it to a signature stood behind the order of reaching again; the flare light and the hand that stopped between two clusters had made a fifth sighting on Stone 30 and have not made a sixth, and the Fishstairs clerk filed apart on Day 59 was not joined to that writer or to the grey-fingered drawing collector. Vesci's *that's what a chart is for* was not spoken; Ostrowski's voice stayed offstage. His father's casket slightly crooked in the aisle; Elena's quiet leaving, Marisol's temperate one; Des's laugh did not return. In Sarn's terms he is an unclosed account heavy enough to crack a slate, and he has been entered in three accounts that never asked him --- the house's, the Court's and Dessa's count of bits --- of which the third is the quietest because nobody thinks it is an account at all, and is gone into the water with the number still in her thumb.

### Under the Vail roof at night

## Open dangers

- A man described, over a bucket at the well, a room shut in the last war with the damper closed and the men inside dead of the breath, their heat gone out with no way to feed it. The yard received it as history with a date; Sella, who had lost a husband to the same danger, heard it and told the fire it was the thaw. The boy filed the saying on its own tray beside the told bell, the heard bell and the kiln's gap and wrote no cause across them. It is a description of the danger under his own roof carried into a yard that had no use for it, and no one to receive it; planted toward ~650 (T065). The house keeps its own low word for the kiln's breath, which names the danger and the mercy in one breath.
- The thaw has stopped being a taste in the water and has written itself on mud, ice, oiled paper, well-stone and now the skirting of the Vail kiln-house. Silt came in on mud, named for what the river leaves and with no name for what it takes; the Ilm runs under a skin so thin the dark current shows through in veins, the hairlines run in and stop a thumb's breadth short of the edge, the Singing Sill gives its low note early through the boards, the Slip Brook runs clay-heavy a little higher than it should, and a new hairline of grey lies in the Vail corner beside the old clean dark line, called thaw and left unswept. A weak board in the plank walk at the Weir is still there, will not carry a man when the river comes up, and has been trodden by nobody. The district has one word, *weather*, that lets a person go home warm without owning a day. He knows the anatomy of a vessel going thin and has no Grathic word for the failure and no hand to point at it.
- No collector came for the woman in the brook and nobody named the omission; twenty-two mornings gone and still unentered. Warmth can be left where it fell and the morning still comes: the first edge of a heresy that will one day refuse to collect, planted as something simply not done, with no word, no doctrine and no argument. The clerk at the Fishstairs has now sat for more than eleven mornings with a slate for a measure nobody called, his hand going to the slate before his eyes to the stalls; the absence of a draw for the dead woman is entered nowhere. The boy is inside three accounts that never asked him --- the house's, the Court's and Dessa's count of bits --- and the third is gone into the water with the number still in her thumb.
- The number still goes around in fists, mouths, shoulders, loaves, glances and weather: one in twelve printed and unchallenged, the true subtraction unspoken, Berr's figure that goes into a slate and not into a well, the number for the thaw passed from fist to mouth to shoulder and given to nobody, a number brought to the well in a fist and thought better of, eels changing hands at a figure, and the house's arithmetic with a space at the end of the loaf row that nobody calls a number. Sella's private roll is still unshown; Berr's box stays locked; Mira's two cords and Orra's knot-string are records no Court can confiscate and no slate can enter, and the knots are still uncounted; Alda's five written notes lie turned down where no mother or officer will read them, with no sixth yet; the pewter tin above the empty bed holds its pale fold and thumb's bright rub, unopened, and the little tin in Alda's bodice is shut. The neat upper line has been seen five times and the writer still has no name in his file; the Drawing collector's ordinary hand and the Fishstairs clerk's ordinary order stay filed apart. The word forty was spoken once, low, by a woman who watched him, and was not spoken again through the turning of the month.
- Orra tied a forty-first knot over the story of a small warm bundle with a flower and said the number into the basket and watched; the cord hangs in the Eel House where he cannot enter or read it, though he can read its shape without its name. A woman has kept a count in wool for three years and knows something about a child carried up the Clay Yard in the year of the flower, and she is watching still to see what the boy does with a word he cannot answer. The shape of the knot is on its tray beside the word and the weight of a thumb apart from both, and the watching has had no answer.
- One of the two crusts in the fold is gone and nobody says who took it, that it is gone, or what became of it. A kindness that could not be answered has become an absence that cannot be answered, now carried into Silt without a name; the reader may suspect Sella, Alda or Mira, including the girl who put the crust there. A coal went up with a brine-gull on Day 45 and one warmth less went out of the world with no slate the poorer. This is how the district keeps what it never enters, and the boy keeps both subtractions without a word in \lore{Grathic} for either.
- Sella's widow's mark stands confirmed by a factor at Greybank and entered as a kindness; it is touched only before an opening, as on the opening of the kiln on Silt 1, and never at a well, a market, a reading, a bank or the carrying of a basket. Garl's uncalled debt against the tiles has been spoken around through another turning of the year and remains never paid, never settled, never done with; his name was not said under the Vail roof on the Remembering and his objects keep his place. His clean dark line is not swept, because a line left where it is is still a line kept, and the new grey beside it is not swept either, because the house says it is thaw.
- Tarn has heard flat notes and said nothing, has seen the boy hear one before it happened and said nothing about that either, has swept the road to a collector without turning and noticed that he has noticed. A loan that can hear is a loan that can be called; two people keeping the same silence under one roof is an equality neither can name, and a man whose ear is worth more than his debt is a man a house might one day learn to count. Jorra's bone-coloured forearms and the unnameable back room, the space under the rack where slate should have stood, Sella's half-pace and her unnamed moving of the basket, Mira's question-answers and two cords, Orra's knot-on-a-lie, the green at a mouth and the weakness of a board are all keepings no slate can enter. The boy is the only person who has seen them and has no standing to ask.
- The kiln's breath is named as the thing that takes a man in his sleep, and Mud End called the death merciful; the carried fire is still under blind ash and the back of a broken pot, warming the boards by degrees and missing one breath in every four. What the house cannot measure it calls weather or mercy: the cough that killed a woman, the debt, the numb hand, the ridge burned and called dealt with, the sealed counting room eighteen leagues east, and the thaw itself are all called something that lets a person go home warm without owning a day of it.
- The child on the quilt in the death-house was worse on the third day of its return and the room called it a graft settling; Alda told the story in the dark with dates where wonder should stand. The boy's own flower was crossed at again on Day 56 by a woman who did not know she did it and covered with a date. The death-house ridge was measured a finger's width thicker than Vail's and both were burned on Ilmen 1.
- The Court's room and the Eel House have the same bench, pot, draught and gap as the birth room, the kiln-house and the death-house, and the boy has kept six likenesses apart on purpose; no cause has been written in any of them. Berr takes the speaker out of his sentences as neatly as a surgeon takes a hand out of a field, and the district's grammar of weather does the same work.
- The flat note has six hearings --- five from four sources and one from a person --- and has bought him nothing; nobody has tapped a vessel for him and there is no Grathic word for it. The nearly full note has a second answer on its own tray, advanced and not paid, and is not a hearing and not the third thing. The bell is a Court instrument doing a temple's work, still unexplained, four, four and a gap in the square and in a story told in the dark, kept in two places; the early note is kept beside but never on them. The count is four, four and a gap in the kiln, the boards, the bell, the bench, the well-roof, the river-prints, the kindling and the firelight, and the gap is the part nobody has thought to enter.
- A woman is dead and stays dead, uncollected, her number in her thumb and her name under her scar; the mercy told her became a fact nobody alive can correct, and she believed him spent among children she never saw to the last. The word *the Breaking* has not been said in his hearing; the word forty was said once, low, is not explained, and was not repeated.

## What he knows and does not know

**He knows.** How a body fails: airway, bleeding, bones, burns, cold, infection, afterdrop and rewarming, the small evidence a body carries, and triage; the way a room cools from the edges inward and a small body goes at the extremities before the core admits to it; how a man pulled from cold water can worsen before he settles; the exact look and sound of a vessel handing its work to the next thing until the skin holds more than it was made to hold and the face arranges itself in the ordinary way; and the anatomy of a room sealed against its own heat. The smell of machine coffee and hospital sanitizer, snow going up a sleeve, orange streetlight on wet snow, his mother's kitchen, his daughter's photograph, the shape of an unsent sentence. He knows he died and that he is in a baby's body. This chapter added: that a room can be sealed and the bill paid by the bodies inside; that a danger under his own roof can be spoken aloud by a stranger and received as history with a date; that two private records in two materials can stand shut in one room on trays that do not touch; that a thumb's rub is evidence of a hand and a fold evidence of a folding and neither read past itself; that a house keeps its days badly in loaves and glances because to keep them exactly would be to own them; that a repair which pretends the break never happened is the district's ordinary kindness and that a proverb can outlive the thing it was true about; that a prepared sweeping and a performed sweeping are two objects on two trays; that the month named for what the river leaves has no name for what it takes; and that a new hairline of grey called thaw can lie beside a left-in-place line without either becoming the other. He knows Tarn has turned his head at a note and said nothing, and that two silences under one roof are nearer an equality than anything the house has a name for.

**He does not know.** The word *Sarn*, or that the Warm is the stuff the world is made of; any Grathic word beyond what he has absorbed by listening; that the Return is an industry with collectors, bankers and a deduction; that he was bought; that forty children paid for his whole return; who the woman in the grey coat is, what she wrote, or what her name says; why the flower is on his ribs; why his fevers will come; that ways run in spirals; that Dessa is his mother and Sella knew; that the Court is a bank and the temple an invoice; that somebody has watched him since before his birth and intends to use his coldness to seal the world. He does not know why Sella took him, what happened to Garl's warmth beyond the household's version, where the carried fire was first lit and who pays to keep it giving, what is in Alda's tin or what she wrote five times and turned down, which house holds the floorboard under which the notes go, what goes on in the back room whose door is kept shut, what the missing slates under the drying rack were for, why the second cord has never gone upriver, where the Ash goes beyond the north gate, where the measure goes after it is entered, what the uncalled debt was for, why the mark was confirmed, why Sella's roll is kept private, or what the Court's soft sentences actually do. He does not know why no collector came for the woman in the brook or that the omission will one day have a name. He has heard the word forty spoken once, low, by a woman who watched him, and does not know that it is the number of children who paid for him or that he is one of them, nor what the forty-first knot counts, nor that Orra tied it the night a woman carried him up the Clay Yard with a flower on the bundle. He can read the shape of the knot but not its name and has no word to spend it on. He does not know who rubbed the pewter lid bright or at what fire its fold was warmed, who took the crust from the fold or that the taking will be read years hence as a gift entered on no slate; he does not know that the green-mouthed woman who looked at pots at the market is named, or that the sealed room at Greybank and the breathing of his own roof will one day be read as the same fact. He does not know that the new grey in the corner is a signature of the flood coming, or that he will one day have a hand to point with.

**He suspects, without evidence.** That the gap in the count is the part he keeps, whether in boards, breaths, steps, kindling, a bell, a landing, a woman's thumb, a hairline in ice that stops short of an edge, an empty shelf, the space between a woman's thumb and her scar, a word said low and watched, an empty place in the fold, a slack frame, a silence where an argument used to stand, a coal gone up with a bird, or a new hairline of grey beside a left-in-place line; that the ridges, the charred slate, the barrows of grey, the benches, the dusts, the cords, the crusts, the intervals, the honeyed ice, the frost-bloom, the higher brook, the oiled paper, the knot-string, the prepared and performed sweepings, the sealed room and the pauses are one thing seen many ways, and that joining them would be a deciding he has no standing to make; that the passed fire has a payer somewhere beyond the yard, as the stone and the gruel and the gone crust have payers inside it; that Tarn's silence and his own are the same object held from two sides; that the five pauses are five objects of one shape and making a sixth is not his work either; that the nearly full note, now two answers, is a reply to the interval he has carried since Stone 13 and not yet the third thing; that the six like rooms with a bench, a pot, a draught and a gap are a likeness he must not turn into a cause, because he has made that mistake once in a better-lit room and carried it for the rest of a life; that the told bell and the heard bell and the early note must be kept apart for the same reason, and so must the word on its tray and the sound in his ear, and the Greybank saying and the gap under his own roof; that a left thing and a burned thing are two ways of keeping, and a thing called gone may still be in the room as a smell; that a kindness which cannot be answered cannot be refused, and that the clerks' reasons are more dangerous than cruelties would have been because they are easier to live beside; that the things a house calls weather or mercy are the things it has entered nowhere; that Dessa's count kept something larger out of the room in the same way his own counting does; that the man at the well carried a true description of this house out of it again without knowing what he had carried, and that a description spoken as history is no safer than one spoken as a threat.

## Relationships at the close of chapter 13

- **Nina** --- love and resentment braided; the debt that made him portable. The unread name in a lit pocket was not reached for again; the old life stayed offstage, no present-tense break and no tagged remembered line. Nine promises, no boat.
