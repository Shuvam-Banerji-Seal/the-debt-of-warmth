## The moment

Day 152, Tallow 3, Thirdday, Y.H. 813, the small hours. In \place{Low Kiln}, under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof, \person{Sella Vail} has carried \person[Corin]{the boy} up the clay road from the wheel-shed at the half-pace she keeps from every open slate, woodsmoke going before her and the river wind coming up to meet it, the two passing on the stair in the dark the way two people pass without speaking. The kiln breathed behind the wall in fours with a gap and the house took no notice. Halfway up she went around the place on the wall where the pan had stood out to dry through Reed and was not standing now, tilting shoulder and basket so nothing knocked and saying nothing; at \place{the yard well}{the well} she went around the trough whose skin of ice had thinned and gone, the water standing bare and dark, and called it weather to nobody; at the yard she went wide around the space beneath \place[the drying racks]{the lowest rack} where the broken birth-slates lie, as wide as a woman goes around a pail of hot water set where a child might knock it, and did not explain the step.

Under the roof she lifted him out of the basket without looking into it, set him on \place{the bed} and not in \place{the Debt Loft}, base first and weight settled, put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without once looking at his face, left the thread of drool frozen and unwiped because nobody under any roof has been given an instruction about the thread, and rested her hand a half-beat longer than the setting needed before it went away --- the half-beat she learned in the winter she sat up three nights with a child of her own who cooled while everybody was kind, and never told anybody. She told the fire it was \lore{the thaw}, turned the coals with the back of a broken pot, said *The house will keep it* where another woman would have said \emph{I}, stood a moment with the back of the pot in her hand counting under her breath toward no opening, pushed coal-black hair back with the wrist that carries the little-door burn (the grey streak pale off the coals), set the pot rim out with its handle standing two inches beyond the reaching hand that would not close on it, did not look toward the bed, did not touch \lore{the widow's mark} because a dusk is not an opening, and went to her own bed without explaining the count. The two rooms do not touch.

In the small hours he turned out his file the way a man lays instruments on a towel before a case begins, each thing going into its own hollow and nothing resting across anything else. Six hearings of \lore{the full note} lay beside and never among --- five from four sources and the sixth, the small flat knock out of \person{Dessa Silt} as the men lifted, from a person --- with no seventh and none invented to fill the place; three near-full answers stood on their own tray, advanced and not paid, with Jorra's withheld knowing among them and not among the hearings; the older intervals lay at Stone 13, Stone 17 and Stone 25 with the half-beat behind a standing finger, the unchecking stroke, his own held breath from a market; and on the widened tray lay **nine** stoppings --- Reed 5, 8, 11, 14, 17, 20, 23, 26 and 29 --- objects and not a rule, intervals and not payments, no payer named to any, held apart by hand because a doctrine is an account that has found a payer. \lore{The damper} lay beside them as one iron with two bites joined to no kiln; the two shut-room tellings stood side by side with no third; the told bell stood beside the heard bell. Through the finger's-width door came the river's note of water over water, a fifth low thing never counted among the hearings; through the floor the carried fire gave four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have been, its payer unnamed for **a hundred and fifty-two days**; overhead the boards kept four nails, four, and a fifth that was a knot the width of a thumb. Three counts in three places, and none became one. In the other room the loaf shelf kept its two unnamed gaps and its unread charcoal, one stiff brown-edged crust lay two inches from his ear with its empty place unnamed and no third, the pan's gone place was held on a place of its own beside them all, the sixth green was a colour joined to no ear and the foundling a shape with no sale, and no fourth likeness was made a cause. Cold arrived cheek, temple, wrist, the wool riding back from the small bare joint. One hundred and fifty-two stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody as his hand outside the wool went up an inch toward the note of water and came down against the fold.

## The protagonist's condition

**Body.** \person{Corin}, one hundred and fifty-two days old at the close of Day 152 and five months and two days, carried everywhere: at the Clay Yard and the edge of the worked strips, at the kiln mouth for the first opening, under the Vail kiln-house roof at the loaf shelf's low angle and the hearth, at the wheel-shed's edge (back room not entered), \lore{the damper} and the flagstones, down the river road to the Weir's plank walk, blessing stone and fish-trade shade, and lifted onto the bed under the roof each night, never into the Debt Loft. He was \person{Daniel Marchetti}, forty, twelve of them a trauma surgeon at Cook County; he died going back into a burning minivan for a child under the dashboard and his warmth left him in the giving and did not cool. As Corin he is a cracked, low-capacity vessel, unranked --- below Thin, twelve to fifteen embers at best, with a flaw nobody has named and a rate of loss nobody has measured. He cannot roll, stand, speak, read \lore{Grathic} in speech or writing, reach a cup, close two inches on the rim of the basket that holds him or lift a hand to his own chin. He drools; the thread at his mouth froze in the dry heat of the yard and was left unwiped on all three days, because nobody under any roof has been given an instruction about the thread and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. His head goes where it wants and makes the small wet sound when it goes too far, and nobody under the roof has been given an instruction about that sound either. The hand outside the wool goes up an inch toward warmth or the note of water and comes down; nothing within two paces can be reached --- not the crust two inches from his ear, the bucket, the pot with its handle turned out, the loaf shelf, the charcoal under it, the clay jar, the cord in the cutter's coat, the knot-string at Orra's waist, the second twist beneath Mira's apron, the clerk's inward slate or the warm fact of the kiln's heat, which came across his cheek and was gone.

**Mind.** The double consciousness has acquired three more days of objects and still no hand to enter any of them. He knows the order of reaching --- the hand to the record before the hand to the person --- and has now seen it in a midwife laying slates before turning to the fold, a thane passing a finger over a figure before looking at the crowd, a clerk with hand to slate before eye to stalls, a reed-cutter straightening his cord before lifting his trap, Sella reaching a finger beneath the shelf before her eyes went to the row, and Sella glancing once at the clay jar before anything else; five hands performing one grammar in one district and not one explaining it. This chapter added: that an opening is a thing done and gone past the way a door is gone past once a man is through it; that a theatre keeps its danger behind a screen and the hands that know where the screen stands are the hands that must not go behind it; that the kiln's breath is the month's danger and has changed address; that a thing ground small as meal got there by being broken while a thing mixed was put together on purpose; that a cost asked as a direction is answered with a direction and leaves no entry; that a smile kept like a door on the latch can be shut without anybody seeing the movement; that a keeping put where nobody expects to be read tells more by the care of its hiding than the writing would; that a knowing withheld from a kiln-master is not a hearing given; that one pan's gone place is one absence held on a tray of its own and a fourth likeness would make a cause and a cause would want a payer; that **nine** likenesses in a row will stand up and call themselves a doctrine if nobody keeps a finger between them, and a doctrine is an account that has found a payer; that a tenth standing up before the seventh year has been earned is a debt invented to make the other nine into a bill, and the absence of a tenth is a keeping, not a payment; that two ears have kept their equality under this roof for a hundred and fifty-two days --- Tarn's, which hears a pot before a knuckle touches it, and his, which turns toward the note before anybody makes it --- and a loan that can hear has never been called. The surgeon's perfect reading of the cold foot, the extremity going before the core, the almost-smile that is not peace and the afterdrop waiting past a warming nobody will perform, worked as exactly as the day he learned it and bought nothing.

**Powers.** None usable, no rank change, no experiment, no clamp, no heresy. He cannot move Warm, cannot clamp, cannot speak, cannot make a fire, cannot read Grathic in speech or writing, cannot enter a figure anywhere. Counting is an old-life habit, not a gift: the kiln's four, four and a gap; the boards' four nails, four, and a fifth that is a knot the width of a thumb; the river's note as a fifth low thing never counted among the hearings; three counts in three places that do not become one; and 150, 151 and 152 exact days set edge to edge against a house that keeps its days in loaves, glances and unnamed spaces, carried unspent. The ear still holds six hearings of \lore{the full note}: five from four sources --- a cup across a birth room, a pot in the \place{Vail wheel-shed} through a finger's width of door, the district's slate twice at \place{the Cross}, and a man giving at \lore[the Hearth Drawing]{the Drawing} --- and the sixth, the small flat knock out of \person{Dessa Silt} as the men lifted, from a person, filed beside the five and never among them. **No seventh came in three days** and he did not invent one to fill the empty place. Three near-full answers (a pot under the wheel-shed wall, a rack pot lifted a hand's breadth too high and set down again, and Jorra's withheld knowledge of a Vail pot's full note) stand on their own tray, advanced and not paid, not among the hearings. The interval tray holds the intervals from Stone 13, Stone 17 and Stone 25 with Tarn's half-beat behind the standing finger, the stroke that went on over the flagstones without the check, his own held breath from a market morning and the unchecking stroke, and **nine** stoppings of the near-saying --- Reed 5, 8, 11, 14, 17, 20, 23, 26 and 29 --- objects and not a rule, intervals and not payments, no payer named to any, the loan uncalled, the Seed 16 breath unspent and the seventh year one tend from its end and still held. Beside them, on no tray of their own: the damper as one iron with two bites joined to no kiln, with no Grathic word for both mercies; the two tellings of the shut counting room with no third; the told bell of \lore{the Long Night} beside the heard bell out of the yard. An inventory is not a key and a list is not a hand.

**Possessions and money.** Nothing he can use, and no money at any point; no purchase, no sale, no loaf passed and no slate seen near the basket. **Held/changed:** the shallow clay pan that stood mouth-up against the wall through all of Reed is **gone** --- no scrape, no crumb, no print in the dust, no chip of a rim, no word --- and its round empty ring is held on a tray of its own, not laid across the shelf's two gaps, the fold's one crust and its gap, the field's empty place, the sixth green or the sixth shape. **T097 stands advanced and unpaid; do not solve, name or join the absences in Chapter 38, let no third gap come, let no fourth likeness make a cause and let no finger of his draw the line.** The one brown-edged barley crust put into the fold on Stone 15 remains stiff, uneaten and past smelling of anything, two inches from his ear, with the empty place beside it still unnamed and no third. **Wounds carried from the other life.** The unsent call to Nina --- nine summers, nine promises, a boat never bought --- was paid on Day 38, in past tense and untagged; nothing like it returned in chapter 37, and the old life stayed offstage. The chart with its note turned down and the hand going past it to a signature stood behind the charcoal under the shelf on Day 151 (a note turned down behind a chart, where the care of the hiding told more than the writing would), behind the reed-cutter's cord turned so its knots stood, behind Orra's date laid over the levy question, behind Sella's glance at the clay jar before anything else, and behind the pan's gone ring named from the shape it left. The damper was read again as the clamp of the old life --- a clamp is a clamp and not a doctrine, set where it is needed and not asked to mean more --- and here it is one piece of iron with two bites, the bite that holds a vessel whole and the bite that can take a hand off a man, with no Grathic word for both mercies. A theatre is a room in which heat is kept and counted and a man stands at the gate and does not go in; he had stood in such rooms with a towel in his hands while somebody else held the field. His father's casket slightly crooked in the aisle; Elena's quiet leaving, Marisol's temperate one; Des's laugh did not return. In Sarn's terms he is an unclosed account heavy enough to crack a slate, and he has been entered in three accounts that never asked him --- the house's, the Court's and Dessa's count of bits --- of which the third is the quietest because nobody thinks it is an account at all, and is gone into the water with the number still in her thumb.

## Open dangers

- **The kiln's breath, the danger of the month.** It has changed address with the firing season: four low draws and a gap where the fifth should have been, coming through the wall. A yard woman raking the mouth spoke sideways of a man in Mud End who went in his sleep, quiet as a lamb, and them that found him called it merciful; nobody made a lesson of it and nobody put a figure to him. The boy knows the order in which a man goes when the breath takes him wrong --- head first, the skin doing what the vessels under it do, the almost-smile that is not peace --- and the knowing is worth nothing with the heat two paces off.
- **The dust and the glaze.** Fine grey slate dust ground small as meal under the nails, on the sill and the backs of hands; a thing ground is a thing that got there by being broken. The sweetish edge of \lore{lead glaze} comes up from \place{High Kiln} on the wrong wind and puts a false sweetness in the mouth. A pot that sings before its time generally sings itself out early, and Jorra's knowing of the Vail pot's note was withheld from the kiln-master who said it.
- **The collector road and the loan that can hear.** Tarn's seventh year stands one tend from its end and still held, six counted where the house can check and the seventh where it cannot, the knife at the lip of its sill and the unforged collector sentence beside it. The ear-sentence about an ear under this roof being worth more than the debt that holds it came to the word that would call the loan on Day 152 and stopped short; nine stoppings are held, a tenth has not been stood up before the seventh year was earned, the Seed 16 breath is unspent, and the collector has not come. A thing held nine times is still owed.
- **The back room and the counting room.** Three or four unnamed men went low-voiced before first grey into the room off the wheel-shed that keeps a finger's width of open; Sella gave them no name and did not say the room was cold. Two tellings of a counting room shut in a war stand side by side with no third, planted toward ~650. The boy has not joined them and must not.
- **The cold foot and the inward slate.** Below the sill a child's foot stays dark in three turns of wool, the cold to one woman and weather to the yard, with the afterdrop waiting past a warming nobody will perform; no \emph{silt-leach}, cause or cure may be named in this arc. The soft-cuffed clerk stays set at the landing for a measure nobody called, his slate turned inward, and nobody has taken him off.
- **The absences gathering.** The pan's gone place, the shelf's two gaps, the fold's crust and its gap and the field's empty place where a hand went in on Seed 15 stand on separate trays; no third gap has come and no fourth likeness has been made a cause. Absences standing in a row will call themselves a doctrine if nobody keeps a hand between them, and a doctrine is an account that has found a payer. No finger of his has drawn the line.

## What he knows, suspects and does not know

- **Knows, exactly and uselessly.** His count: one hundred and fifty, one hundred and fifty-one and one hundred and fifty-two days, five months, five months and a day, five months and two days, kept edge to edge against a house that keeps its days in loaves, glances and unnamed spaces; the more exact it grows the more it isolates him, and he has no hand to lay it down and no voice with which to offer it. The order in which cold arrives --- cheek, temple, wrist, the wool riding back --- and the order in which a body takes heat back, the small joint first and the core last; the extremity going before the core, the almost-smile that is not peace and the afterdrop waiting on the far side of a warming nobody would perform; that a cold patient cannot clot, that you do not rub a frozen foot and do not set it to the fire, and that all of it buys nothing without a hand two paces long. The order of reaching for the record before the person, and the grammar of taking the speaker out of a sentence. That an opening is a thing done and gone past, that a theatre keeps its danger behind a screen, that a cost asked as a direction is answered with a direction and leaves no entry, that a date given where a figure was asked has had the question taken out of it, that a bundle judged by the arm that carries it tells what was carried and not what it cost, that the arm keeps its record in muscle with no nerve that can enter a figure, that a figure read aloud is not the figure paid, that a figure put into a slate does not know the hand that did not come back, and that a tally kept in cord or charcoal is a record no Court thumb can read. That a keeping put where nobody expects to be read tells more by the care of its hiding than the writing would, and a hand under a board without looking either knows the board by heart or has been told not to look; that a knowing withheld is not a hearing given; that a stopping is not a hearing and a ninth stopping no more a ninth hearing; that a thing ground is a thing broken and a thing mixed is a thing put together; that **nine** likenesses in a row will stand up and call themselves a doctrine if nobody holds them apart, and a doctrine is an account that has found a payer; that a tenth stood up too early is a debt invented to make the other nine into a bill, and the absence of a tenth is a keeping and not a payment; that an inventory is not a key and a list is not a hand. The damper as the clamp of the old life --- one iron with two bites, the holding and the taking, with no Grathic word for both mercies.
- **Holds as shapes, not facts.** Three places that do not touch: the shelf's two gaps, the fold's one crust with its empty place, and the field's empty place in the returning line, with the sayings set beside the first two and the saying of the loaf-shelves --- two gaps stay two gaps until a finger lays them together and an account then wants a name --- set beside them all. **A new fourth place:** the pan's gone place on the wall, held on a tray of its own with no scrape, crumb, print, chip or word, not laid across the shelf's gaps, the fold's crust, the field's empty place, the sixth colour or the sixth shape; no third gap came and no fourth likeness was made a cause (T097 advanced). **T098 carried and unpaid:** the field's empty place, gone around with a look and a wide step and no figure, no name, cause, body or count. **T099 carried and unpaid:** the reed-cutter's cord, knotted with teeth on the blessing stone and closed inside his coat, held beside Mira's grey cord and second twist, Orra's knot-string with its legible unnamed double turn, Alda's six hidden pages and both tins, Berr's locked box with the true subtraction, the clerk's inward slate and Dessa's pewter tin, touching none of them. **T100 carried and unpaid:** the charcoal clusters under the Vail shelf and the finger-under-board habit. Five record-first hands in one district performing one grammar and not one explaining it. The sixth green as a colour joined to no ear and the foundling's sixth half-turn as a shape with no sale, no loaf, no slate and no payer. The near-number of hands that went from strip to teeth and back, on the interval tray; Tarn's stroke going on without the half-check and his chest's small interval, the unchecking stroke beside the earlier half-beat, set beside the bent finger, the held breath and **nine** stoppings without any of them joined. The near-saying itself: a sentence brought to the threshold of the word nine times --- Reed 5, 8, 11, 14, 17, 20, 23, 26 and 29 --- and stopped, with no figure and no name, held as nine intervals and not nine payments; on Day 152 it came to the word and stopped short of it, and no tenth standing up was a keeping, not a payment. The two shut-room tellings with no third, the told bell beside the heard bell, the damper's double bite joined to no kiln, three counts in three places that do not become one. He keeps a finger between all of them without moving anything.
- **Does not know.** That he is one hundred and fifty-two days old: nobody has told him, nobody under the roof knows he has a number, and there is no Grathic word in which to say what a life would count. What the clay jar on the wall shelf is for, though he has watched Sella glance at it and not touch it for a hundred and fifty-two days. **Who took the pan that stood out to dry through Reed, when, why, or whether the not-saying would have been the same had a woman set it back** --- the round ring is all that is left and the house went around it. Who took the smoky crust, who took the pale stone, what became of the weak board, or what the broken birth-slates were. Why another loaf went off the shelf on Seed 6, or who carried it out; **what the charcoal tally under the loaf shelf says, or why Sella reaches a finger beneath the board without looking**, though he has now seen the hand go under and come back on Days 132, 134, 135, 138, 140, 141, 143, 144, 147 and 151 and in the night inventories of 140, 145, 146, 147, 148, 149, 150 and 152. The name Dessa held under the sieve-wire scar or the number in her thumb, and no one can take either off a dead face now. What the men want who come before light to the back room, or why the room is a finger's width open, or whether there were three or four of them; whether the men in that room and the men shut in the counting room eighteen leagues east are in any way the same fact, and he has not joined them. Which house holds the floorboard under Alda's sixth page, and what the second hand says. What the promise of the foundling's name means, or who the men who burn slates are. Why the foundling's head went half a beat late, whether he will ever speak, and that the sale is years ahead. What the reed-cutter's knots count, what the cord says beyond *No more can the Court*, who the reed-cutter is, what the word forty counts, who Orra's forty-first knot was tied over and who the man at the stern of the collector's boat was. What the silt-leach is, or that the cold in the child's foot has a name and a habit Arc 3 away. Which hand went into the strips on Seed 15 and did not come out on Seed 27, whether it is a hand or a body that will be found or a figure that will be made of it, and what a slate would find if a hand came up in a month or a year --- nothing has come up. Why the collector's boat came round the bend on the flood with six oars and one man at the stern who never touched them, and whether Orra's date was a lie or the habit of counting. What the soft-cuffed clerk's inward slate carries, why the measure was set down before anybody called it, and who set him there. What Berr's true subtraction is, and that the figure read is not the figure paid. Why the tiny bend in the low, low note under the floor is a fifth pull that misses, and who has not paid for the warmth it gave. What the widow's mark cost when it was taken, or who was named under the foot of the account when the fire was first carried. Whether the opening he watched at the kiln mouth will be paid for, by whom, and in what.
