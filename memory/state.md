## The moment

Day 203, Ember 24, Fourthday, Y.H. 813, the small hours. In \place{Low Kiln}, under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof, \person[Corin]{the boy} lies on \place{the bed}, awake and silent, with the fold at his ear, one leathery brown-edged crust two inches from that ear and its empty place beside it unnamed, no third, and the thread of drool at his mouth frozen and unwiped because nobody under any roof has been given an instruction about the thread and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. Cold has stopped at cheek, temple and the small bare joint at the wrist where the wool rides back, as if it had reached the end of its own instruction; the afterdrop waits on the far side of a warming nobody in this house will perform. Through the finger's-width door the river keeps its note of \lore{water over water}, a fifth low thing never counted among the hearings; under the floor \lore{the carried fire} gives four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have been, its payer without a name for two hundred and three days; overhead the boards keep four nails, four, and a knot the width of a thumb. Three counts in three places, and none rises up and becomes the others, because a man who wanted them to agree would have to put a payer under them and no payer stands to any of them.

At the close he has put the question to his hand and been answered as he was never answered before. The hand outside the wool went up an inch toward the river's note, stayed up through the small intake, the hold and the letting go of one of the breaths Sella counts at her fire in the room that does not touch his --- and **did not come down with her breath**. It kept the small height through the little silence after her count had ended and came down on the river's next low note: a coordination with a second rhythm, the one through the door, which nobody counts and which was never given to him. It is not a longer reach, not a stronger hand, not a hearing (water over water remains a fifth low thing never counted among the hearings), not a seventh, not a stopping, not a payment, not one of the three held changes, not a key, not a power and not a rule; no payer is named to it and nothing stood up out of it to call itself a sign. He filed it on a sixth place beside the others and laid nothing across them. The river went on without noticing that an inch had ridden one of its low notes for the length of one breath, and the fold took the hand without a word. Two hundred and three stood exact in the dark, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody.

He turned out the file as a man lays instruments on a towel --- no towel, no instruments and no case, but the order of the laying the whole of the discipline --- and found it exact, complete and worth nothing. ## The protagonist's condition

**Body.** \person{Corin}, two hundred and three days old at the close of Day 203 and six months and twenty-three days (two hundred and one and six months and twenty-one days on the first morning, two hundred and two and six months and twenty-two days on the second), carried everywhere: under the Vail roof at the bed, the hearth, the loaf shelf's low angle and the kindling; in the Clay Yard, at the kiln mouth, the drying racks and the yard well; at the wheel-shed's edge, the cold damper and the flagstones (the back room and the Debt Loft never entered by him or the narration); up and down the clay road at the going of the light. No market, no Cross and no bell in these three days. He was \person{Daniel Marchetti}, forty, twelve of them a trauma surgeon at Cook County; he died going back into a burning minivan for a child under the dashboard and his warmth left him in the giving and did not cool. As Corin he is a cracked, low-capacity vessel, unranked --- below Thin, twelve to fifteen embers at best, with a flaw nobody has named and a rate of loss nobody has measured. He cannot roll, stand, speak, read \lore{Grathic} in speech or writing, reach a cup, close two inches on the rim of the basket that holds him, lift a hand to his own chin or lift a crust two inches to his mouth. He drools; the thread at his mouth froze in the small hours and was left unwiped on all three days. Cold comes cheek, temple, the small bare joint at the wrist where the wool rides back, and stops there as if it had reached the end of its own instruction; the afterdrop waits on the far side of a warming nobody in this house will perform. The one change is the hand: it can go up an inch toward a sound and stay for a small intake, for a whole counted breath, or --- now --- past the end of a counted breath and down on a second rhythm, and on other days refuse to stay at all.

**Mind.** The double consciousness has acquired three more days of objects and one more small physical first, and still no hand to enter any of them. He knows the order of reaching --- the hand to the record before the hand to the person --- and has seen it in a midwife laying slates before turning to the fold, a thane passing a finger over a figure before looking at the crowd, a clerk with hand to slate before eye to stalls, a reed-cutter straightening his cord before lifting his trap, Sella reaching a finger beneath the shelf before her eyes went to the row, Sella's glance at the clay jar before anything else, Hessa Rale settling her strap and turning her wrist before her eyes took in the crowd, Berr Clay wiping his thumb twice and passing a finger over the figure before his eyes took the crowd, and the soft-cuffed clerk below the bridge entering a buyer's number before he looked up to find the man who had said it. ## What he knows, suspects and does not know

- **Knows, exactly and uselessly.** His count: two hundred and one through two hundred and three days --- six months and twenty-one days through six months and twenty-three days --- kept edge to edge against a house that keeps its days in loaves, glances and unnamed spaces; the more exact it grows the more it isolates him, and he has no hand to lay it down and no voice with which to offer it. The order in which cold arrives --- cheek, temple, wrist, the wool riding back --- and the order in which a body takes heat back, the small joint first and the core last; that a cold patient cannot clot, that you do not rub a frozen foot and do not set it to the fire, and that all of it buys nothing without a hand two paces long. The reading of a body with lead in it --- the pale line at the gum, the held or curled hand, the colour of the nail beds, the short breath with the pause at its top, the tremor in the wrist, the care with which a dipper is set down when the fingers have stopped telling the man where it is --- exact, complete and worth nothing at two paces. The shape of a reading said twice over a body that cannot answer, and the grammar of taking the speaker out of a sentence with *your house* rather than \emph{I} --- heard twice now from the same man over the same basket. That an opening is a thing done before the kiln by a hand that will not name what it mends, and that a bed, a shelf, a well, an afternoon, a basket, a yard, a market, a carrying, a morning, a dusk and a reading are not openings. That a cost asked as a direction goes to the pot, the house and the weather and arrives nowhere; that a date given where a figure was asked has had the question taken out of it; that a bundle judged by the arm that carries it tells what was carried and not what it cost; and that a district's arithmetic can change twice in a month without one mark of it crossing the wall into this house. And now: that a hand can go up an inch toward a sound and come down at once, or let it go early, or hold it for a small intake, or for a whole counted breath and down with the breath, or --- newest --- keep it past the end of a count and down on a rhythm nobody gave; that none of these is a failure, a reach, a hearing or a power, and each is worth an inch.
- **Holds as shapes, not facts.** Three places that do not touch: the shelf's two gaps, the fold's one crust with its empty place, and the field's empty place in the returning line, with the sayings set beside them. **A fourth place:** the pan's gone place on the wall, held on a tray of its own with no scrape, crumb, print, chip or word. **A fifth place:** the pale line at the gum, exact and worth nothing at two paces, where a danger of the month goes and not among the hearings or the near-full answers. **A sixth place:** the held inch, with answers from Day 198 through the small hours of Day 203, filed on its own place beside the others and never laid across them, not a reaching, not a power, not a key, not a sign, with no payer named. **T098 carried and unpaid:** the field's empty place and the place the carriers crossed, gone around with a look and a wide step and no figure. **T099 carried and unpaid:** the reed-cutter's cord tied once low with teeth and closed inside his coat, held beside Mira's grey cord and second twist, Orra's knot-string with its legible unnamed double turn, Alda's six hidden pages and both tins, Berr's locked box with the true subtraction, Hessa's edge-on slate with its unread figure, the clerk's inward slate and Dessa's pewter tin, touching none of them. **T100 carried and advanced, unpaid:** the charcoal clusters under the Vail shelf, the finger-under-board habit and the breath held under the board on Day 166, with the reason still standing between knowing the row by heart and being told not to look. **T097 advanced and unpaid:** the pan's gone ring and its empty round place, held with no third gap and no fourth likeness made a cause.
- **Does not know.** That he is two hundred and three days old, or six months and twenty-three days, or that a number was reached at all: nobody has told him, nobody under the roof knows he has a number, and there is no Grathic word in which to say what a life would count. What the clay jar on the wall shelf is for, though he has watched Sella glance at it and not touch it on each of these three days, her hand starting toward it and shutting half over nothing. Why another loaf went off the shelf on Seed 6, or who carried it out; **what the charcoal tally under the loaf shelf says, or why Sella reaches a finger beneath the board without looking and on Day 166 held it there for a counted breath.** What the men want who come before light to the back room, or why the room is a finger's width open, or whether there were three or four of them; whether the men in that room and the men shut in the counting room eighteen leagues east are in any way the same fact, and he has not joined them. What Jorra wants of the men who come to the room, what the holding back and then the holding of his finger at the damper means beyond a sentence finished, and what went on in the space between the two silences under one roof. What the stone is that Tarn took out of its pot and set on the sill in the light, or why he put it back. What ails a glaze-room hand, and what the man carried out on Day 188 cost, and whether he lived or died after he went up the road breathing --- and the narration has not invented an ending for him; neither reading gave a figure, name or cause for him. Why his own hand can hold an inch past the end of a counted breath and down on a second rhythm, and cannot close two inches on a pot handle. And the printed one in twelve, the locked subtraction, Hessa's unread slate and the clerk's uncalled measure all stand beyond his reach and have not been challenged under any roof in these three days.

## What Chapter 51 must not forget

Chapter 51 opens on Day 204, Ember 25, Fifthday, with the kiln month's last working week closing in the household's own keeping of Garl --- the salt-stiff coat on its peg, the seat with its twenty-year leftward lean, the clean dark line where the Ash ridge had turned with its finer grey called the thaw beside it, the proverb gone to the pot and the uncalled debt --- no boundary drawn, no lesson made, no collector called and no ending. Keep the count exact and unspent at **two hundred and three**, six months and twenty-three days, spoken by nobody, and the fire's fifth missing with no payer for two hundred and three days; Ember's second reading gone up the clay road locked in Berr's box with the printed one in twelve unchallenged and the true subtraction inside, the figure read twice still not the figure paid, and the month's arithmetic changed twice and not changeable back; Hessa's slate unread at Greybank; the clerk still set at the landing for his uncalled measure. Keep the **one opening** behind the household --- the mark touched once before the kiln on Ember 1, held one breath, at no shelf, hearth, well, rack, road, basket, yard, market, morning, afternoon or dusk since --- and do not touch it again unless there is another opening; nobody praised it and nobody saw what it mended. Keep the nine stoppings as objects with no tenth before the seventh year is earned, the seventh nearer its end than ever and not given, the Seed 16 breath unspent, the loan uncalled, the ear-sentence short of the word that would call it, no seventh hearing invented, six hearings beside and never among with the sixth from a person, three near-full answers on their own tray, and any note out of season filed as a danger and not promoted into a hearing. Keep the **three** held changes on three separate places --- the Day 161 damper fraction, the Day 166 held beat beneath the shelf and the Day 171 flagstone half-check --- and never let them stand up as a pair, a doctrine or a rule with a payer; Jorra's holdings in this chapter were remembered repetitions and Tarn's stroke checked half a beat on no day. Keep the **stone's small loft-sill sound on a fourth place**, the stone itself back in its pot in the loft, cold and unreconciled by not trying, which is not forgiving. Keep the carried glaze-room hand's **pale line on a fifth place of its own**: an exact reading worth nothing at two paces, not a hearing, not a seventh, not a stopping, not one of the three held changes and not joined to the sweet wind, the dust, the early note, the sixth green, the foundling's sliver, the pan's ring or any absence; no collector was called for him and no cause was named. Keep the **held inch on a sixth place of its own** --- down at once on a morning, held for a small intake, down before the breath finished, held through a whole breath and down with it, and now held into the silence after a count and down on the river's next low note --- and never let it stand up as a reaching, a hearing, a seventh, a stopping, a payment, a key, a power or a rule; no payer named to it, and do not pair it with Tarn's held breath at the knife's lip. Keep the sixth green a colour and the foundling's sixth glimpse a shape, with no seventh sighting and no seventh hearing. The market does not get to read the inch. **Do not invent a seventh hearing, a tenth stopping, a third telling of the counting room, a third gap, a fourth likeness made a cause, a calling of the loan, a named payer, an offstage fate for the carried man or any ending.** Plant nothing, pay nothing, open nothing. Forward, not closed.
