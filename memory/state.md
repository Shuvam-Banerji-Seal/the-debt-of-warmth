## The moment

Day 54 is done. On Ilmen 25, Fifthday, Y.H. 813, at dusk, \person{Sella Vail} carried the boy up \place{the Clay Yard} from \place{the yard well} at a half-pace from every open slate, set him on his bed under the \place{Vail kiln-house} roof, not in \place{the Debt Loft}, and put the fold right without looking at his face. He is \person{Corin}, fifty-four days old, carried everywhere in a basket that is not his, with **one** crust in the fold where two had lain and the empty place at his ear beside it, carried through all seven days without a name. The spent crust from Stone 15 is stiff past smelling and was not lifted, eaten or named; the smoky crust \person{Mira Silt} put in the fold on Stone 28 is gone since Day 46, taken quietly by a hand nobody will name, and neither the taking nor the absence was spoken of. Behind him the carried fire lies under blind ash and the back of a broken pot, warming the boards by degrees and missing one breath in every four. Four, four and a gap. The coal that went up with the brine-gull on Day 45 is entered in no account and missed in none, nine days ago now. His hand outside the wool goes up an inch toward the warmth and comes down.

The seven days of this chapter, each lived in full:

- **Day 48, Ilmen 19, Y.H. 813 (Ninthday).** Frost-bloom wrote itself again in white fronds on the stone lip of the well where \lore{the Warm} had lately gone out; the woman who used to say the ice held now said nothing at all and the man who had called it honeyed kept his counsel, so the argument ended with nobody carrying it. The oiled paper in the Vail window sat slack in its frame and \person{Tarn}'s eye went a degree to it and back to the road without a word. Sella told the fire it was the thaw and the bucket there was a skin on it, gave every instruction to fire, wool and bucket and never to the basket, and went around a slate propped at the rack end as wide as a pail of hot water. One crust in the fold and the empty place beside it; when the wind turned off the water the thin breath of barley did not come and he wrote no cause across the absence. The hand went up an inch toward the bucket and came down. Six hearings stayed six, the nearly-full note of Ilmen 6 lay advanced and not paid, and the three hands stayed on their tray.
- **Day 49, Ilmen 20, Y.H. 813 (Tenthday, Account-day).** Sella carried him down at a half-pace from every open slate and set the basket in the \place{moot-post}'s shade at \place{the Cross}. \person{Berr Clay} came with the red wool cord of his office folded under his coat and green of glaze at one corner of his mouth, wiped his thumb twice before touching nothing, read *one in twelve, as the notice says*, said a household that had heard otherwise had heard wrong, called the levy for the thaw and took himself out of his own sentence; the true subtraction went over the top of the basket and the number for the thaw went from fist to mouth to shoulder and arrived nowhere. He read the figure a second time for the late households. The bell went four, four and a gap and nobody crossed himself. Word came up that the clerk was still at \place{the Fishstairs}, the eleventh morning, with his hand to his slate before his eyes to the stalls, for a measure nobody called; the clerk did not speak and was not \person[Marra]{Collector Marra}, not the drawing collector and not the soft-cuffed man at the Court's table. Sella carried him home at midday without a word about what had been read over him.
- **Day 50, Ilmen 21, Y.H. 813 (Firstday).** \person{Jorra Vail} came out of the room off \place{the Vail wheel-shed} with fine grey slate dust worked into the creases past his bent little finger, not bone-coloured slip, and said nothing about where he had been; the men were not named and the broken birth-slates stayed under the drying racks. He told a pot it would do and the wheel it did not care whose hands, only if they were steady, and moved the damper before Sella had finished telling him the fire was low. \person{Tarn}'s knife was on its sill with six years counted and the seventh held; a vessel gave its note past the yard door and his head did not turn, and the two silences under one roof kept their equality. His broom went over the collector's road without turning; no collector went down it. \person{Garl Vail}'s coat, hollow seat, clean dark line, charred slate and proverbs kept their places. Sella went around the house's days in loaves and glances with no figure spoken.
- **Day 51, Ilmen 22, Y.H. 813 (Secondday).** \person{Alda Reed} came short under the roof with soap-and-milk gone cold under river silt and lye under the soap, birth-slates under her arm, and laid the writing-down on the bench before she turned to the fold. She pressed the fold flat at his shoulder a breath too long, touched the little tin once inside her bodice without opening it, and made the two-finger sign at the red edge of \lore{the birth-flower} without knowing it, covering it with the same unasked-for date she gave \person{Dessa Silt}: *Stone seven, the account went up on the eighth.* She went on with a cooper's seventh in \place{Mud End}, a potter's third child in \place{Stonemouth} and the last high water after there was no question left to stop her, said *Warm and breathing --- that's all I've set down*, which was exact and not true, and did not name the woman or the third day. On a narrow slate she wrote a **fifth** note in the second hand and turned it face-down into her shawl toward a floorboard in a house the boy cannot name; the tin went back inside her bodice, shut. Sella stood a half-pace off and did not ask. Sella fed him barley gruel thinned with milk from the house's own dish earlier, two fingers behind his ear, spoon wiped on her sleeve and his mouth not wiped, and did not say who paid for the milk. At the dusk bank she turned the coals with a broken pot and did not touch \lore{the widow's mark}.
- **Day 52, Ilmen 23, Y.H. 813 (Thirdday).** A rack-woman set a pot down in the yard out of her shadow and, under nobody's knuckle, it answered **nearly full** --- a note with air under it and a want in it, neither the flat answer of the six hearings nor the silence of a pot turned toward that gives nothing. The boy filed it on its own tray as a second earthly answer to the interval carried since Stone 13, beside the nearly-full note of Ilmen 6, advanced and not paid; no Grathic word holds *nearly* and he has no hand to tap either pot and ask it to say the thing again. The three intervals from Stone 13, Stone 17 and Stone 25 stayed objects on their own tray; six hearings stayed six and no seventh came; the loan that can hear stayed uncalled. Jorra's bent finger led at the damper ahead of Sella's instruction. The clean dark line was not swept and nobody had found a word for the not-calling of a collector.
- **Day 53, Ilmen 24, Y.H. 813 (Fourthday).** Mira worked the eels at the outer end of the Fishstairs with pale slime dried on her sleeves, silt in the hollow of her neck and her head held right where the top of her left ear had been, her small smile kept like a door on the latch. Asked about the run she answered the question and her own: *Does it come now? It comes when it comes. Ask the river* --- moving eel-bones against her thumb while deciding whether to lie and putting one away when the deciding was done. When a buyer said four marks the pair her thumb went under her apron to the grey wool cord and stayed; shifting a basket showed the **second cord twisted twice beneath it**, never carried upriver to \place{Nine Pots}, never lifted at a door, the knots on neither counted. The boy set the two cords on a board side by side and unjoined, beside the board \person{Orra Weir} keeps, not touching it; the shape of the forty-first knot was legible without its name and the word was unspoken. The thin child upriver was not spoken of and no door was knocked; he filed the not-knocking as an interval. Sella carried him home at the turn of the wind, saying nothing about the figure or the cords.
- **Day 54, Ilmen 25, Y.H. 813 (Fifthday).** More hairlines ran into the ice of \place{the Ilm} and stopped a thumb's breadth short of the edge; a man put a knuckle on the ice, brought a number in his fist and thought better of giving it, and the hairlines were weather by the time the bucket came up. \place{the Singing Sill} gave its low note early through the boards and was called the water running fast under the ice. The thaw number went from fist to mouth to shoulder and arrived nowhere; Tarn agreed with early and late and went on wanting the late one. A weir woman with salt white at her wrists said plainly, for the first time, that the weak board in the plank walk at \place{the Weir} was **still there**, would not carry a man when the river came up, and had been trodden by nobody; the boy set it beside the missing Fishstairs step and the stopping hairline and let none of them touch, and no man stepped on the board and no accident was made of it. Behind the racks \place{the Slip Brook} ran clay-heavy a little higher than it should, kept with the frost-bloom, the honeyed ice and the new hairlines in four places that did not touch. At dusk Sella set him on his bed with one crust and the empty place carried through seven days, the fire missing one breath in every four.

Day count: **Day 54 complete.** Day 55 is Ilmen 26, Y.H. 813, Sixthday, and opens chapter 13 at the yard well. No day was skipped and no day was poured into another.

## The protagonist's condition

**Body.** \person{Corin}, fifty-four days old at the close of Day 54. He was \person{Daniel Marchetti}, forty, twelve of them a trauma surgeon at Cook County; he died going back into a burning minivan for a child under the dashboard and his warmth left him in the giving and did not cool. As Corin he is a cracked, low-capacity vessel, unranked --- below Thin, twelve to fifteen embers at best, with a flaw nobody has named and a rate of loss nobody has measured. He is carried everywhere: at the yard well in the shade out of the draught, down the Clay Yard at a half-pace from open slates, in the moot-post's shade at the Cross, at the end of the drying racks, at the Fishstairs in the lee of the eel creels, and lifted onto the bed under the roof, never into the Debt Loft. He cannot roll, stand, speak, read \lore{Grathic}, reach a cup or lift a hand to his own chin. He drools; the thread at his mouth freezes in the small hours and nobody wipes it, because a basket set down is a pot set down and left. His head goes where it wants and makes the small wet sound when it goes too far; the hand outside the wool goes up an inch toward the warmth and comes down; nothing within two paces can be reached. Cold arrives in its known order --- cheek, temple, wrist --- and the wool does less than the wind wants. The branching red birth-flower runs from the third rib on the left down the forearm and stops short of the wrist; Alda crossed two fingers at its red edge again on Day 51 without knowing she had done it and covered the sign with a date. He wants the breast and wants the crust even cold, and gets neither: now only the spent crust remains, stiff past smelling, and the warmth he most wanted is an empty place in the fold. A woman went into the water sixteen mornings ago two paces from where he lay and he knew exactly how the vessel gave and could not lift a hand toward her.

**Mind.** The double consciousness has acquired seven more days of objects and still no hand to enter any of them. He knows the order of reaching --- the hand to the record before the hand to the person --- and has now seen it in a midwife, a neighbour at a well, a clerk behind a Court table, a man who priced him, Berr passing a finger over a figure, the soft-cuffed clerk at the Fishstairs, Alda going to her slate before the child, and Mira moving eel-bones and then her thumb to the grey cord while deciding whether to lie. This chapter added: that a season can lose its arguer and leave a sentence-shaped silence nobody fills; that a frame going slack is a signature a district will not enter; that knowing a note exactly is a tool with no handle and a clamp with nothing to bite on; that two cords can be held side by side and unjoined and a knot's shape read without its name; that a weakness said plainly in the third person still leaves a board with nobody's weight on it and no accident made of it; that a note can come early and be called weather; and that four witnesses to one thing can be kept in four places that do not touch. He knows the difference between a likeness and a cause because a better-lit room taught him the cost of getting it wrong, and he keeps the honeyed ice, the frost-bloom, the stopping hairlines and the higher brook in four places; the told bell beside but never on the heard bell, and both beside the early note and the kiln's missing breath; six like rooms in six places; the six hearings beside and never among; three near-misses (board, missing step, hairline) in three places; and the three suspecting hands on a tray that does not become a cause. He knows that a kindness which cannot be answered cannot be refused, that knowing a thing exactly has never once given him a hand to act with, and that he is the only adult in every room and the least use in it.

**Powers.** None usable, no rank change, no experiment, no clamp, no heresy. He cannot move Warm, cannot clamp, cannot speak, cannot make a fire, cannot read \lore{Grathic} in speech or writing, cannot enter a figure anywhere. Counting is an old-life habit, not a gift: the kiln's four, four and a gap; the bell's four, four and a gap with nobody crossing himself; the Fishstairs' four, four and a missing step; the boards' four, four and a knot; the death-house bench's four, four and a knot; the well-roof's four, four and a stone that does not ring; the kindling's four, four and a space; the firelight's four, four and an empty shelf; the told bell's four, four and a gap; the officer's four measures, four measures and a missing closing line; four ways out of the Cross; four oars on the collector's boat in Orra's story. The ear still holds six hearings of \lore{the full note}: five from four sources --- a cup across the birth room, a pot in the \place{Vail wheel-shed} through the wall, the district's slate twice at \place{the Cross}, and a man giving at the Drawing --- and the sixth, the small flat knock out of \person{Dessa Silt} as the men lifted, from a person, filed beside the five and never among them, not a seventh. The nearly full answer of Day 35 (Ilmen 6) has a **second** answer beside it as of Day 52, both a third sound on their own tray, advanced and not paid, neither a hearing nor the third thing. The word forty remains a saying, not a vessel's sound, on its own tray with the readable shape of the double turn beside it and the weight of a thumb on wicker apart from both; it was not spoken again in seven days. The trays do not touch: intervals, gaps, told bell, absence-shaped-like-a-collection, word, shape and knot, near-misses and low sounds. No Grathic word for the flat note, for nearly, for the failure in a vessel or for a thing taken and not spoken of, and no word of his own for the taking.

**Possessions and money.** Nothing he can use. **Held/changed:** only the brown-edged barley crust put into the fold on Stone 15 remains, stiff and gone past smelling of anything, uneaten, unlifted, unnamed. The fresh smoky crust from Mira, given on Day 22, is **gone** as of the afternoon of Day 46: taken quietly, not eaten in his sight, not lifted in it, not named by anyone, and not solved; whether Sella's hand, Alda's hand or Mira's hand took it is left open, and the boy set all three on a tray and would not let the tray become a cause. The empty place has now been carried seven days without a name. The coal the brine-gull took on Day 45 is gone from the carried fire's account, entered nowhere and missed in no account. The shape of Dessa's thumb on the wicker is still a held thing, paid and kept where things with no hour are kept. **Held/unchanged:** the small pale river stone remains in the fold of grey wool on the abandoned board-bed in House Silt, unmentioned; the small bench knife is on its sill; the charred slate still lies in the kiln ash, black on one side and eaten into the shape of a strip of bark; the basket and wool are Sella's, lent without a word; the clay jar Sella fetched from House Silt stands on the wall shelf and is the house's, not his. Alda's little tin is inside her bodice, touched once on Day 51 without opening, and her **fifth** narrow slate is turned face-down toward a floorboard in a house the boy cannot name (four went before it); Sella's private roll is unshown; Berr's box is locked in his house; Mira's grey wool cord and the second cord twisted twice beneath it have knots uncounted; Orra's knot-string with its forty-first knot hangs in the Eel House, unread, its shape now legible to the boy without its name; the \lore{Ash} ridge is gone from the Vail corner, burned on Ilmen 1 and called dealt with, and both of Garl's proverbs are still said to the fire and the pot. **Filed but not held:** six hearings of the full note; three intervals; five unjoined pauses; the nearly-full answer and its second; the word and the shape of the knot and the weight of a thumb; Mira's two cords on a board beside Orra's; the weak board at the Weir beside the missing Fishstairs step and the stopping hairline; the early note beside the told and heard bells and the kiln's gap; four witnesses to the thaw in four places; the told and heard bells; the not-calling of a collector for the woman in the brook; and the slate of the clerk still set at the Fishstairs.

**Wounds carried from the other life.** The unsent call to Nina --- nine summers, nine promises, a boat never bought --- was paid on Day 38, in past tense and untagged; nothing like it returned in chapter 12, and the old life stayed offstage throughout. The chart with its note turned down and the hand going past it to a signature stood behind that reaching; the flare light and the hand that stopped between two clusters had made a fifth sighting on Stone 30 and have not made a sixth, and the Fishstairs clerk on Day 49 was filed apart, not joined to that writer or to the grey-fingered drawing collector. Tasha Broussard's chart, signed without reading the nurse's note, and the institution calling it a failure of the system, were recognised again in Berr's passive sentences, the Court clerk's soft *it's entered as it stands*, the district's grammar of weather, the order of reaching and the shape of a hand going to a slate before a person. Vesci's *that's what a chart is for* was not spoken; Ostrowski's voice stayed offstage. His father's casket slightly crooked in the aisle; Elena's quiet leaving, Marisol's temperate one; Des's laugh did not return. In Sarn's terms he is an unclosed account heavy enough to crack a slate, and he has been entered in three accounts that never asked him --- the house's, the Court's and Dessa's count of bits --- of which the third is the quietest because nobody thinks it is an account at all, and is gone into the water with the number still in her thumb.

## Who is where, and what they want now

### Under the Vail roof at night

- **Corin/Dan** --- on his bed under the roof with the fold at his ear, **one** crust in it and the empty place beside it carried through seven days, awake, silent, drool frozen and unwiped, hand outside the wool going up an inch toward the warmth and coming down. Wants the breast, wants the crust that is gone, wants a hand to lift to the cord upriver; gets none of the three and cannot ask for any. Holds the readable shape of the knot, the kept word, the coal gone with the gull and the empty place where the crust had been, and can spend none of them. His count goes on into a year the district has entered and has not entered him.
- **Sella Vail**, 33, keeper of the Vail fire --- at the fire and the bed, having carried him at a half-pace from every open slate and set him on his bed at dusk. She says *the house* where another woman says *I*, gives every instruction to the fire, the pot, the wool and the bucket and never to the basket, fed him from the house's own dish on Day 51 without wiping his mouth, and did not name the warmth paid or either crust or the one that is gone. She did not touch the widow's mark at the well, a reading, a carrying or a bank; she turns the coals with the back of a broken pot. Her private roll stays unshown, she has not written him as Vail, and she will not let anyone fill Garl's seat or take his coat down. Wants the fire kept, the house kept, the keeping never explained; the thaw now writing itself on ice, paper and well-stone in plain sight has made that harder without giving her anyone to answer.
- **Tarn**, 22, the Vail's clay-bound hand --- in the yard and under the roof, broom set away, small knife back on its sill, said to the sill rather than the yard because the yard had already been told. Six years of the seven are counted; he will not count the seventh, the one entry in this house the house cannot check. Ash is worked into the creases of his knuckles that no scrubbing lifts; the pale clip-ring at his left wrist and the mark worn almost flat on the knife handle are his own records. His broom went over the road to a collector without turning and no collector went down it. His head did not turn at a vessel's note on Days 50 and 52, and he did not say what the pot gave. He agrees with the early-thaw party and the late-thaw party so thoroughly that there is nowhere for an argument to put its foot, and goes on wanting the late one, because a late thaw puts off the paying. A loan that can hear is a loan that can be called; the sentence has never been said under this roof and lies under it all the same.
- **Jorra Vail**, 16 --- in and out of the wheel-shed: grey slate dust worked past his bent little finger on Day 50, clean bone-coloured slip on the other days; the bent finger going in ahead of his hand at the damper, moving it before Sella has finished telling him the fire is low. He tells pots they will do and the wheel it does not care whose hands, only if they are steady. He knows the full note of a Vail pot before it leaves the wheel and would not say so before a kiln-master. He has not once looked toward the bed. The room off the shed keeps its finger's width of open and nobody pushes it wider; the men are unnamed and the broken slates stay under the rack. Still wants the foundling outside the name Vail and the hollow seat for himself; *he's not Vail* is as sealed as on the hour it was spoken.
- **\person{Garl Vail}** --- absent and the shape of most of the house, though the ridge that was his ash-marker is now a clean dark line. His warmth lies where it fell, the coat keeps his broad shape on the peg, the seat keeps his twenty-year leftward lean, and the debt against a firing of tiles remains neither paid nor settled nor done with. Under the Vail roof his name was not spoken on the Remembering, and the house went around the word as Sella goes around ash in a corner. His proverbs live in the house's mouth: *feed the fire before you feed yourself*, and *leave the crack showing; people trust a thing they can see has been handled* --- the second went on being said to the pot after the crack was gone and the ridge was a clean dark line.

The clay would not wipe out of the creases beside her mouth; the man from the pits kept his fingers at her neck longer than a pulse would have needed and took them away without saying what he had found. The number stayed in her thumb and the name under the scar and neither came up; to the last she believed him spent among children she had never seen, gone in the levy three winters ago for a whole return she could not have read the writing of, which was a mercy when it was told and is a fact now that nobody alive can correct. Her thumb on the wicker for one breath on Day 37 was the detail paid on Day 38 and kept. She will not say the name, and no visit to the bank may become a quest.

## Open dangers

- The thaw has stopped being a taste in the water and has written itself on ice, oiled paper and well-stone. Dessa Silt was carried away uncollected in the Slip Brook behind the racks, sixteen mornings gone and still without a word; the Ilm runs higher under a skin so thin the current shows through it in dark veins and the new hairlines stop a thumb's breadth short of the edge; the Singing Sill gives its low note early through the boards of both kiln-house and Weir; the Slip Brook runs clay-heavy a little higher than it should; frost-bloom writes on the well-lip and goes by noon; the oiled paper has gone slack in its frame; and a weak board in the plank walk at the Weir is now said plainly to be still there, to be unable to carry a man when the river comes up, and to have been trodden by nobody. The district has one word for all of it --- weather --- that lets a person go home warm without owning a day. He knows the anatomy of a vessel going thin and has no Grathic word for the failure and no hand to point at it.
- No collector came for the woman in the brook and nobody named the omission. Warmth can be left where it fell and the morning still comes: the first edge of a heresy that will one day refuse to collect, planted as something simply not done, with no word, no doctrine and no argument. The clerk at the Fishstairs has now sat for eleven mornings or more with a slate for a measure nobody called, his hand going to the slate before his eyes to the stalls; the absence of a draw for the dead woman is entered nowhere. The boy is inside three accounts that never asked him --- the house's, the Court's and Dessa's count of bits --- and the third is gone into the water with the number still in her thumb.
- The number still goes around in fists, mouths, shoulders and weather: one in twelve printed and unchallenged, the true subtraction unspoken, Berr's figure that goes into a slate and not into a well, the number for the thaw passed from fist to mouth to shoulder and given to nobody, a number brought to the well in a fist and thought better of, eels changing hands four marks the pair, the house's arithmetic in loaves and glances with no figure. Sella's private roll is still unshown; Berr's box stays locked; Mira's two cords and Orra's knot-string are records no Court can confiscate and no slate can enter, and the knots are still uncounted; Alda's five written notes lie turned down where no mother or officer will read them; the pewter tin above the empty bed holds a pale fold nobody has opened. The neat upper line has been seen five times and the writer still has no name in his file; the Drawing collector's ordinary hand and the Fishstairs clerk's ordinary order stay filed apart. The word forty was spoken once, low and watched, and is now seven days unspoken.
- Orra tied a forty-first knot over the story of a small warm bundle with a flower and said the number into the basket and watched; the cord hangs in the Eel House where he cannot enter or read it, though he can read its shape without its name. A woman has kept a count in wool for three years and knows something about a child carried up the Clay Yard in the year of the flower, and she is watching still to see what the boy does with a word he cannot answer. The shape of the knot is on its tray beside the word and the weight of a thumb apart from both, and the watching has had no answer.
- One of the two crusts in the fold is gone and nobody says who took it, that it is gone, or what became of it. A kindness that could not be answered has become an absence that cannot be answered, now carried seven days without a name; the reader may suspect Sella, Alda or Mira, including the girl who put the crust there. A coal went up with a brine-gull on Day 45 and one warmth less went out of the world with no slate the poorer. This is how the district keeps what it never enters, and the boy keeps both subtractions without a word in \lore{Grathic} for either.
- Sella's widow's mark stands confirmed by a factor at Greybank and entered as a kindness; it is held while the fire never goes out and touched only before an opening, never at a bank, a market, a reading, a well or the carrying of a basket. Garl's uncalled debt against the tiles has been spoken around through another turning of the year and remains never paid, never settled, never done with; his name was not said under the Vail roof on the Remembering and his objects keep his place. His clean dark line is not swept, because a line left where it is is still a line kept.
- Tarn has heard a flat note and said nothing, has seen the boy hear one before it happened and said nothing about that either, has swept the road to a collector without turning and noticed that he had noticed. A loan that can hear is a loan that can be called; two people keeping the same silence under one roof is an equality neither can name, and a man whose ear is worth more than his debt is a man a house might one day learn to count. Jorra's returned grey dust and the unnameable back room, the space under the rack where slate should have stood, Sella's half-pace and her unnamed moving of the basket, Mira's question-answers and two cords, Orra's knot-on-a-lie, the green at a mouth and the weakness of a board are all keepings no slate can enter. The boy is the only person who has seen them and has no standing to ask.
- The kiln's breath is named as the thing that takes a man in his sleep, and Mud End called the death merciful; the carried fire is still under blind ash and the back of a broken pot, warming the boards by degrees and missing one breath in every four. What the house cannot measure it calls weather or mercy: the cough that killed a woman, the debt, the numb hand, the ridge burned and called dealt with, and the thaw itself are all called something that lets a person go home warm without owning a day of it.
- The child on the quilt in the death-house was worse on the third day of its return and the room called it a graft settling; Alda told the story in the dark with dates where wonder should stand. The boy's own flower was crossed at again on Day 51 by a woman who did not know she did it and covered with a date. The death-house ridge was measured a finger's width thicker than Vail's and both were burned on Ilmen 1.
- The Court's room and the Eel House have the same bench, pot, draught and gap as the birth room, the kiln-house and the death-house, and the boy has kept six likenesses apart on purpose; no cause has been written in any of them. Berr takes the speaker out of his sentences as neatly as a surgeon takes a hand out of a field, and the district's grammar of weather does the same work.
- The flat note has six hearings --- five from four sources and one from a person --- and has bought him nothing; nobody has tapped a vessel for him and there is no Grathic word for it. The nearly full note now has a second answer on its own tray, advanced and not paid, and is not a hearing and not the third thing. The bell is a Court instrument doing a temple's work, still unexplained, four, four and a gap in the square and in a story told in the dark, kept in two places; the early note is kept beside but never on them. The count is four, four and a gap in the kiln, the boards, the bell, the bench, the well-roof, the river-prints, the kindling, the firelight and the space between a woman's thumb and her scar, and the gap is the part nobody has thought to enter.
- A woman is dead and stays dead, uncollected, her number in her thumb and her name under her scar; the mercy told her became a fact nobody alive can correct, and she believed him spent among children she never saw to the last. The word *the Breaking* has not been said in his hearing; the word forty was said once, low, and is not explained and was not repeated.

## What he knows and does not know

**He knows.** How a body fails: airway, bleeding, bones, burns, cold, infection, afterdrop and rewarming, the small evidence a body carries, and triage; the way a room cools from the edges inward and a small body goes at the extremities before the core admits to it; how a man pulled from cold water can worsen before he settles; the exact look and sound of a vessel handing its work to the next thing until the skin holds more than it was made to hold and the face arranges itself in the ordinary way. The smell of machine coffee and hospital sanitizer, snow going up a sleeve, orange streetlight on wet snow, his mother's kitchen, his daughter's photograph, the shape of an unsent sentence. He knows he died and that he is in a baby's body. This chapter added: that a season can lose its arguer and leave a space shaped like a sentence; that a slack frame and a well-lip writing are signatures a district will not enter; that knowing a note exactly is a tool with no handle; that nearly and full are two sounds and no word in Grathic holds the first; that two cords can be held side by side and unjoined and a knot's shape read without its name; that a weakness said plainly in the third person still leaves a board with nobody's weight on it and no accident made of it; that a note can come early and be called weather; and that four witnesses to one thing can be kept in four places that do not touch. He knows Tarn has turned his head at a note and said nothing, and that two silences under one roof are nearer an equality than anything the house has a name for.

**He does not know.** The word *Sarn*, or that the Warm is the stuff the world is made of; any Grathic word beyond what he has absorbed by listening; that the Return is an industry with collectors, bankers and a deduction; that he was bought; that forty children paid for his whole return; who the woman in the grey coat is, what she wrote, or what her name says; why the flower is on his ribs; why his fevers will come; that ways run in spirals; that Dessa is his mother and Sella knew; that the Court is a bank and the temple an invoice; that somebody has watched him since before his birth and intends to use his coldness to seal the world. He does not know why Sella took him, what happened to Garl's warmth beyond the household's version, where the carried fire was first lit and who pays to keep it giving, what is in Alda's tin or what she wrote five times and turned down, which house holds the floorboard under which the notes go, what goes on in the back room whose door is kept shut, what the missing slates under the drying rack were for, why the second cord has never gone upriver, where the Ash goes beyond the north gate, where the measure goes after it is entered, what the uncalled debt was for, why the mark was confirmed, why Sella's roll is kept private, or what the Court's soft sentences actually do. He does not know why no collector came for the woman in the brook or that the omission will one day have a name. He has heard the word forty spoken once, low, by a woman who watched him, and does not know that it is the number of children who paid for him or that he is one of them, nor what the forty-first knot counts, nor that Orra tied it the night a woman carried him up the Clay Yard with a flower on the bundle. He can read the shape of the knot but not its name and has no word to spend it on. He does not know who took the crust from the fold or that the taking will be read years hence as a gift entered on no slate; he does not know that the green-mouthed woman who watched the pots is a name on a slate in Greybank, nor that the Counting Room was shut during the last war. He knows the shape of a body going thin and does not know the word the district has for it, which is weather.

**He suspects, without evidence.** That the gap in the count is the part he keeps, whether in boards, breaths, steps, kindling, a bell, a landing, a woman's thumb, a hairline in ice that stops short of an edge, an empty shelf, the space between a woman's thumb and her scar, a word said low and watched, an empty place in the fold, a slack frame, a silence where an argument used to stand, or a coal gone up with a bird; that the ridges, the charred slate, the barrows of grey, the benches, the dusts, the cords, the crusts, the intervals, the honeyed ice, the frost-bloom, the higher brook, the slack frame, the knot-string and the pauses are one thing seen many ways, and that joining them would be a deciding he has no standing to make; that the passed fire has a payer somewhere beyond the yard, as the stone and the gruel and the gone crust have payers inside it; that Tarn's silence and his own are the same object held from two sides; that the five pauses are five objects of one shape and making a sixth is not his work either; that the nearly full note, now two answers, is a reply to the interval he has carried since Stone 13 and not yet the third thing; that the six like rooms with a bench, a pot, a draught and a gap are a likeness he must not turn into a cause, because he has made that mistake once in a better-lit room and carried it for the rest of a life; that the told bell and the heard bell and the early note must be kept apart for the same reason, and so must the word on its tray and the sound in his ear; that a left thing and a burned thing are two ways of keeping, and a thing called gone may still be in the room as a smell; that a kindness which cannot be answered cannot be refused, and that the clerks' reasons are more dangerous than cruelties would have been because they are easier to live beside; that the things a house calls weather or mercy are the things it has entered nowhere; that Dessa's count kept something larger out of the room in the same way his own count keeps the gap; that two not-lookings with the same shape can be opposite things; that the board, the missing step and the stopping hairline are one warning spoken three ways, and that warning given plainly and warning kept on a tray are the same warning because nobody can step on either from a basket.

## Relationships at the close of chapter 12

- **Nina** --- love and resentment braided; the debt that made him portable. The unread name in a lit pocket was not reached for again; the old life stayed offstage, no present-tense break and no `\memory{}` tag. Nine promises, no boat.
- **Des, Ostrowski, Vesci** --- the dead life's voices. None returned in voice this chapter; the district's grammar of weather, Berr's passive sentences and the order of reaching --- slate before child, date before wonder, record before person --- stood in for them. Still no tag on a remembered line.
- **Orra Weir** --- a watching that has outlasted seven more days of no answer; she is offstage now, her board and knot-string only a presence, the shape of the knot legible to him and the word still unspoken. She is not a friend and not an enemy; she is a person who set a thing down in front of him and is waiting to see whether it will be picked up.
- **Sella Vail** --- the hand that puts the fold right and never looks at his face, that says *the house* and gives the instruction to the wool; a keeping with no figure and no explanation. One of three hands the boy set on a tray as possible takers of the crust, which is not the same thing as a cause.
- **Alda Reed** --- the hand that presses the fold too long, crosses the sign without knowing it, covers it with a date and writes a fifth note she will not say; another of the three hands on the tray.
- **Mira Silt** --- the hand that put the crust there and keeps two cords no officer can read; the third of the three hands and the one he has wanted the crust from longest. The hand that went to the cord when a figure was spoken now stands on the tray with the other two, kept and not entered.
