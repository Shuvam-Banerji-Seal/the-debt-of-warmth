- **Corin/Dan** — on his bed under the roof with the fold at his ear, **one** crust in it and the empty place beside it still without a name, awake, silent, drool frozen and unwiped, hand outside the wool having just let a small intake go whole on the river's note at the very end of the day and laid itself back against the fold on the **two hundred and eighty-second** answer — exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air and filed on the sixth place with no payer named, not spent or explained. Three hundred and fifty-three days old and six months and one hundred and seventy-three days, told by nobody that a number has been reached; 353 is not round and not a threshold, only one more in a row nobody counts aloud, and he has long ago stopped expecting to be told, which is not the same as not wanting it. Wants the crust lifted two inches, the thread wiped, a hand on the pot handle and a number spoken by somebody who would have known what a number counted; gets none and cannot ask for any. Holds the six hearings with no seventh, the heard bell beside the told, the told figure beside the paid with **eight** arithmetics locked away and the finding on a place of its own with no payer, **six openings in six months** with the sixth held behind at Ash 2 and no seventh made due, the dressed grain covered and unfigured under a number that did not know its cost, the **handful** let fall back at the rack end on Bone 22 as one object and not made again, the stayed-at-the-inch object and the early letting-go as two objects on two places and not made again, **Tarn's thumb's resting on the seventh notch** as one object on its own place and not made again, the **pot-crack resting of Ash 9** as one object on a place of its own beside the five and not a sixth, and **the held breath at the knife-sill of Ash 14** as one object on a place of its own beside the pot-crack resting, checked again on the twenty-second, twenty-third and twenty-fourth and found where it was put and not made again; beside these, on its own place with a finger between, the Ash 23 direction stands with the Ash 5 joining and the Ash 17 direction, advanced and not paid. Whatever equality his noticing finds among the private keepings of Tarn, Ilva and Jorra, he refuses to turn into a cause, because a cause wants a payer standing and none stands; the edges come near to touching and the finger goes between.

- **Sella Vail**, 33, keeper of the Vail fire — at the close she carried him up at the going of the light on Ash 24 at the half-pace from every open slate, went wide around the seven walked-around places, set him on his bed under the roof and never in \place{the Debt Loft}, put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face, her hand resting the half-beat longer than the setting needed before it went away, and left the thread where it lay. On all three days the instruction went to the fire, the wool, the pot and the river wind and never to the basket, and she said nothing of where they had been. She went to the hearth before the basket and said *The house will keep it* where another woman would have said I. On all three mornings and at all three goings of the light she told the coals it was the thaw, turned them with the back of a broken pot, and stood a moment counting small intake, hold and letting-go toward no opening, giving neither gap a figure; her finger lay on top of the loaf shelf's front edge so the charcoal tally stayed unread; her lips moved along a two-gapped row and figured neither gap; she glanced once at the clay jar standing cold under lead glaze and shut her hand half over nothing, the little-door burn on the back of her left hand closing on air, the cord of her widow's mark oiled where a working thumb had touched it ten thousand times; she lifted the twice-mended pot with her thumb first where the wire crossed the crack, said both proverbs to the pot and the fire and never to a person and left the hairline showing; she turned the dressed grain under wool with a still lighter touch and no figure and no handful, her fingers going in and coming out without closing; she set the pot rim two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat and no look toward the bed. When the Ash 23 voice went over the boards she gave it no name, no figure and no lesson and did not answer on behalf of the man nobody had named. The widow's mark was not touched on any of the three days; she answered nothing on behalf of the hand that went in on Seed 15, the man carried out on Day 188, the covered grain or the room shut with the damper closed.

- **Tarn**, 22, the Vail's clay-bound hand — last seen on Ash 24 in the Clay Yard at \place{the kindling}, six years standing in the worn thumb-mark where the house can check and the seventh at the lip beyond it where it cannot, and \person{Garl Vail}'s unforged collector sentence lying beside the seventh with no name on the man who turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on the debt. - **\person{Jorra Vail}**, 16 — last seen on Ash 24 at the shed's edge and \place{the damper}, coming through the finger's-width door off \place{the Vail wheel-shed} with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bent little finger and white in the creases where a fist had opened, and it was not the bone-coloured \lore{slip} of the wheel. - **\person{Garl Vail}** — absent and the shape of most of the house, kept in objects and not in a story on all three days: the salt-stiff coat with the crease at the left shoulder keeps his broader shape on its peg behind the door; the wheel-seat in the shed keeps its twenty-year hollow on that side and nobody is allowed to fill it; the ridge that was his ash-marker is now the clean dark line at \place{the Vail corner} with the finer grey beside it called \lore{the thaw}, unswept both; the twice-mended pot on \place{the pot board} carries the hairline of the original break between the old-brass staple and the tighter wire, with both proverbs gone to the pot and the fire and never to a person — *Leave the crack showing. ### The Account-days and the world asleep

The district's arithmetic changed for the eighth time on Ash 20 (Day 349) and cannot be changed back; the printed one in twelve stood unchallenged over the basket with the figure read still not the figure paid, and the true subtraction went up the clay road locked in Berr's box. **Eight arithmetics of the year now stand behind one unsigned figure, eight and not one account**, with the finding held on a place of its own and no payer standing. Ash 22, 23 and 24 were ordinary tending days with no Cross, market or bell, no collector on the clay road and no runner on any road; Ash 30 (Day 359), the month's third and last Account-day, stands six mornings ahead and is named only as a date, not brought into a day that has not earned it. Far-off in the dark: Marra is not in Sill; the foundling's seventh half-turn gathers toward a sale years away; Alda's mercy-lie has curdled into furniture; the waiting man from Slate Row and the Waiting Room clerk take small secrets for loaves and ale; Rennick is already asking why forty unspent measures are needed for one intact one; Solene Ember removes a ring one at a time in a room with good light and does not know the boy exists; Hald Iron reads Haldre's paper from a tin lined with warm-stone and Siv keeps the crossed-out survey; Pia of the Quay does not yet know him; the Waste takes what nobody keeps. The reincarnation's old life stays asleep behind the boy's eyes and was named to nobody in these three days.

## Open dangers

- **The danger of a year begun, paid small and non-fatally, now with eight arithmetics gone up the road and a sixth opening held behind the household's firings.** The kilns' heat thinned into the ordinary tending of a working week, and the danger keeps an order and no name that cannot be carried into a chart: hot clay under tallow, fine grey slate dust ground small as meal sitting white in a knuckle when a fist opens, and a pot set down wrong giving its high thin note before its time. ## What he knows, suspects and does not know

- **Knows, exactly and uselessly.** His count: 351 through 353 days — six months and 171 through 173 days — kept edge to edge against a house that keeps its days in loaves, glances and unnamed spaces; 353 is not round and not a threshold, and he has stopped expecting to be told what any of the numbers count, which is not the same as not wanting it. The order in which cold arrives — cheek, temple, wrist, the wool riding back — and the order in which a body takes heat back and gives it up, the reverse and sooner than anybody likes; that a cold patient cannot clot, that you do not rub a frozen foot and do not set it to the fire, that a tourniquet let go too soon weeps into the dressing, and that all of it buys nothing without a hand two paces long. That a person's face can be composed because the cold has composed it, and that pink returning to the skin is the surface still cold and not the core. The grammar of taking the speaker out of a sentence with *your house* rather than *I*, and a reading called a kindness. ## What Chapter 101 must not forget

Chapter 101 opens on Day 354, Ash 25, Fifthday, with the hand laid down on answer **282** and the count at **353 exact** — six months and one hundred and seventy-three days told by nobody, the carried fire's fifth unpaid and Garl's name unspoken the same number. **Nothing from Ash 22, 23 or 24 crosses over as sign, lesson, mending, cause, payment or payer** — the voice that came over the boards on the twenty-third stood nowhere to be asked and bought no lesson under this roof, and the eight arithmetics behind the unsigned figure answered none of the held questions. Carry unchanged and unspent: **282 honest answers** on the sixth place, each an object and not a power; **eight arithmetics of the year — the three of Frost, the first, second and third of Bone, Ash's first and Ash's second — behind one unsigned figure, eight are eight and not one account**, with both of Ash's subtractions locked and unread in Berr Clay's box up the clay road, the figure read still not the figure paid, no signature on the space, the finding kept on a place of its own with no payer and not laid across the house's not-doings, the covered grain, the five held objects, the pot-crack resting, the hand-at-the-sill breath, the handful, the thumb's resting, the two small objects of the hand, the sixth opening, the three Ash 5/6 movements, the Ash 17 direction, the Ash 23 direction, the fourth shaping, the turned writing, the strip in the sleeve or his gaps; six hearings of the full note beside and never among with the seventh place empty; the heard bell beside the told; nine record-first hands with no tenth; nine stoppings with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end and the loan uncalled; the row at five askings with no witness; the burned slate at five, the shut-room tellings at two, the well-saying at four shapings, and the Ash 5 joining, the Ash 17 direction and the Ash 23 direction held apart with a finger between every pair. **The Ash 23 direction stands on its own place beside the Ash 5 joining and the Ash 17 direction, advanced and not paid, still gathering and still unspent** — a room shut against the cold and a slate burned so a count would not go up, a further shaping of that joining and an advancement of the finding that a place wants a witness, expressly **not** a sixth telling of the burned slate, **not** a third shut-room telling, **not** a sixth asking of the row, **not** a fifth shaping of the well-saying, **not** a hearing, **not** a seventh of the full note, **not** a witness, **not** a reader, **not** a name, **not** a cause and **not** a payment, with no payer standing under it; do not supply a stand-in, a name, a witness, a sixth or a payment (T062). The promise under the wheel-shed is neither given nor withdrawn and goes on gathering interest against the claim; *he's not Vail* stays sealed. Ash 30 (Day 359) stands six mornings ahead and is named only as a date; on that day the month's third reading will put a ninth arithmetic behind the same figure, and nine will be nine and not one account — keep it as a date until its day is earned, and supply no ninth arithmetic early, no seventh opening, no sixth hearing, sixth held object, sixth telling, third shut-room telling, sixth asking, fifth shaping, tenth hand or tenth stopping to make a row come out even. No answer becomes a power, no reading answers a held question, no doctrine stands up out of eight or nine, and the hand goes on being laid down where the next hand will find it without looking. Forward, not closed.
