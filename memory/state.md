## The moment

Day 128, Reed 9, Ninthday, Y.H. 813, the going of the light into the small hours. In \place{Low Kiln}, under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof, \person{Sella Vail} has carried \person[Corin]{the boy} up from \place{House Silt} and then the clay road from \place{the Fishstairs} at the half-pace she keeps from every open slate, going around the space beneath \place[the drying racks]{the lowest rack} as wide as a pail of hot water set where a child might knock it, and set him on his bed and not in \place{the Debt Loft}. She put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without once looking at his face and left the thread of drool at his mouth frozen and unwiped, because nobody under any roof in the district has been given an instruction about the thread, and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. Her hand rested a half-beat longer than the setting needed before it went away, the way a hand rests on a thing it has carried a long way and cannot bring itself to let go of. She told the fire it was \lore{the thaw}, turned the coals with the back of a broken pot, said “The house will keep it” where another woman would have said \emph{I}, stood a moment with the back of the broken pot in her hand counting something under her breath toward no opening, pushed a strand of coal-black hair back with the wrist that carries the little-door burn (the grey streak pale off the coals), set the pot where it could be found in the dark, rim out and handle toward the reaching hand, did not look toward the bed, and went to her own bed. She did not touch \lore{the widow's mark}; the clean dark line where the \lore{Ash} ridge turned and the new finer hairline of grey beside it lay unswept and were called the thaw. The door stands a finger's width open. Through the gap comes the river's note of water over water, beyond the reed beds and the Weir, a sound he has kept on a place of its own since he was six days old, never a hearing and never counted among the six; through the floor under blind ash the carried fire takes its coal and gives back four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have been, its payer unnamed for a hundred and twenty-eight days and to be unnamed on the morrow; overhead the boards keep four nails, four, and a fifth that is a knot the width of a thumb. Three counts in three places; none became one. He turned out his file as a man lays instruments on a towel: six hearings of \lore{the full note} stand beside and never among with no seventh, three near-full answers advanced and not paid, the told bell beside the heard bell, the intervals and the two stoppings of the near-saying on their tray as objects and not a rule, the word forty unspoken with the shape of Orra's forty-first double turn legible beside it, the cold foot below the sill the cold to one woman and weather to the yard, the green at a mouth's corner still a colour and not a name, the clerk at the landing still holding his slate for a measure nobody called. The reed-cutter's cord has gone back inside his coat with its knots unread, Mira's grey cord and the second twist beneath it have not been lifted at a door, Alda's six pages lie turned toward a floorboard and both tins are shut, and Berr's narrow slate holds a figure that does not know the hand that did not come back. Nothing has been joined, named, paid, opened, called or ended. Nobody under this roof said he was a hundred and twenty-eight days old, four months and eight days. His small hand outside the wool went up an inch toward the note of water over water and came down, and the count crossed into the morrow.

## The protagonist's condition

**Body.** \person{Corin}, one hundred and twenty-eight days old at the close of Day 128 and four months and eight days, carried everywhere: at the Clay Yard and the edge of the worked strips, the stalls beyond the Cross, the wheel-shed's edge (back room not entered), the damper, the clay road and market edge, the hearth and kindling, the road downriver and the plank walk at the Weir, the fish-trade shade, three doors down in House Silt, and lifted onto the bed under the roof each night, never into the Debt Loft. He was \person{Daniel Marchetti}, forty, twelve of them a trauma surgeon at Cook County; he died going back into a burning minivan for a child under the dashboard and his warmth left him in the giving and did not cool. As Corin he is a cracked, low-capacity vessel, unranked --- below Thin, twelve to fifteen embers at best, with a flaw nobody has named and a rate of loss nobody has measured. He cannot roll, stand, speak, read \lore{Grathic} in speech or writing, reach a cup, close two inches on the rim of the basket that holds him or lift a hand to his own chin. He drools; the thread at his mouth froze in the small hours and nobody wiped it on any of the three days, because nobody under any roof has been given an instruction about the thread and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. His head goes where it wants and makes the small wet sound when it goes too far, and nobody under the roof has been given an instruction about that sound either. The hand outside the wool goes up an inch toward warmth or the note of water and comes down; nothing within two paces can be reached. Cold arrives in its known order, cheek, temple, wrist, and the wool does less than the wind wants, riding back from the joint so the cold finds the bare place and goes under the fold at his ear. The branching red birth-flower runs from the third rib on the left down the forearm and stops short of the wrist; Alda crossed two fingers at its red edge on Day 128 without knowing she had done it and covered the sign with a date. Nobody in the yard, the sheds, the stalls, the Weir or House Silt has any use for the number of his days.

**Mind.** The double consciousness has acquired three more days of objects and still no hand to enter any of them. He knows the order of reaching --- the hand to the record before the hand to the person --- and has seen it in a midwife, a neighbour at a well, a clerk behind a Court table, a man who priced him, Berr wiping his thumb twice and passing a finger over a figure, the soft-cuffed clerk at the landing holding his slate to his forearm, Mira moving eel-bones and then her thumb to the cord while deciding, Orra pulling a knot down with her thumb as she gives a date, the reed-cutter turning his cord where no slate lay, Jorra speaking to the wheel before the pots and the people, Alda laying her birth-slates on the bench before she turned to the fold, and Sella giving the instruction to the bucket, broom, wool, water, fire, pot and wind before she gives it to anybody. This chapter added: that a record kept in a carrying arm tells what was carried and not what it cost; that a cost asked in the form of a direction can be answered with a direction and arrive nowhere as a figure; that a fist shut on two fingers and a fist put back in a sleeve are two holdings of the same kind in two places that do not touch; that a cup turned a quarter and set back where it stood is care of the same order as the yard going around its empty place and a market walking over a step that is not a step; that three likenesses in a row will grow a cause unless a hand holds them apart, and the labour of holding them apart is invisible and worth nothing to anyone who could see it; that a man who washes his own cuff has a hunger or a habit and a man who holds a slate where nobody else can see the figure has a use, and a use wants a payer; that a man standing at the stern and keeping his hands off the oars counts other men's pulling; that a date given where a figure was asked is an answer with the question taken out of it, a chart closed over a note nobody opened; that a smile held like a door on the latch is a door somebody must stand beside all day; that taking yourself out of your own sentence with *your house* is the same shape as taking a hand out of a field before the dressing goes on; that covering a field with a cloth before bringing the instrument down and lifting a hand out before the dressing goes on are one motion performed on a person who cannot object; that a second stopping makes a second interval, not a second payment, and a kept word is a debt that can hear and be called; that two truths can be held at once --- the surgeon noting a fold pressed a breath too long and the child cold at the ear wanting a crust that is not in that room --- both true, the second louder, neither handable back; and that the hollow in grey wool where a small pale stone had lain is the only object in that room his sorrow can attach to. The more exact the private file grows the more it isolates the only adult in the room with the hands of a child.

**Powers.** None usable, no rank change, no experiment, no clamp, no heresy. He cannot move Warm, cannot clamp, cannot speak, cannot make a fire, cannot read \lore{Grathic} in speech or writing, cannot enter a figure anywhere. Counting is an old-life habit, not a gift: the kiln's four, four and a gap; the boards' four nails, four, and a fifth that is a knot the width of a thumb; the three counts in three places that do not become one; the bell's four, four and a gap heard under the moot-post and kept beside the told bell; and now 126, 127 and 128 exact days set edge to edge against the house's loaves, glances and unnamed spaces, carried at one hundred and twenty-eight unspent. The ear still holds six hearings of \lore{the full note}: five from four sources --- a cup across a birth room, a pot in the \place{Vail wheel-shed} through a wall, the district's slate twice at \place{the Cross}, and a man giving at \lore[the Hearth Drawing]{the Drawing} --- and the sixth, the small flat knock out of \person{Dessa Silt} as the men lifted, from a person, filed beside the five and never among them. No seventh came in three days: the bell was a told bell, the wheel and the pots under the roof were notes he had learned to leave where they sounded, and he did not invent one to fill the empty place. Three near-full answers (a cup across a birth room, a pot under the wheel-shed wall, a rack pot lifted a hand's breadth too high and set down again) stand on their own tray, advanced and not paid. The interval tray holds the intervals from Stone 13, Stone 17 and Stone 25 with Tarn's half-beat behind the standing finger, the stroke that went on over the flagstones without the check, his own held breath from the market, and **two** stoppings of the near-saying --- the sentence about an ear worth more than the debt brought to the threshold of the word that would call the loan on Reed 5 and again on Reed 8, stopped twice with no figure and no name --- as objects and not a rule. ## What he knows, suspects and does not know

- **Knows, exactly and uselessly.** His count: one hundred and twenty-eight days, four months and eight days, kept edge to edge against a house that keeps its days in loaves, glances and an unnamed space; the more exact it grows the more it isolates him, and he has no hand to lay it down and no voice with which to offer it. The order in which warmth is given out and the order in which a body loses it, especially the extremity going before the core, the face putting on an almost-smile that is not peace, and the afterdrop waiting on the far side of a warming nobody would perform. The order of reaching for the record before the person, and the grammar of taking the speaker out of a sentence. That a figure read aloud is not the figure paid, that a figure put into a slate is not a figure put into a strip or a bundle, that a bundle judged by the arm that carries it tells what was carried and not what it cost, that an armful held for a month is a record kept in muscle that no Court thumb can enter, and that a tally kept in cord is a record no Court thumb can read. That a cost with no figure is still a cost and every cost in Low Kiln has a payer nobody will name; that a cost asked in the form of a direction can be answered with a direction and leave no entry, and the withholding be called the weather. That a cost asked as weather is still asked, and a fist shut on two fingers is a holding as surely as a fist put back in a sleeve. That care leaves no entry either: a full dish carried level, a cup turned a quarter and set back where it stood, a market walking over a step that is not a step, the yard going around a place with a look. That three likenesses in a row will grow a cause unless a hand holds them apart, and that the labour of holding them apart is invisible and worth nothing to anybody who could see it; that a fourth likeness makes no cause merely by standing there. That a hand washing its own cuff has a hunger or a habit and a hand held to a forearm where nobody can see the figure has a use, and a use wants a payer. That a date given where a figure was asked is an answer with the question taken out of it, and a man standing at the stern who never touches the oars counts other men's pulling. That a smile held like a door on the latch is a door somebody must stand beside all day, and the keeping is the same gesture as the pricing face turned the opposite way with no Grathic word for either. That taking yourself out of your own sentence with *your house* is the same shape as taking a hand out of a field before the dressing goes on; that covering a field with a cloth before the instrument comes down and lifting the hand out before the dressing goes on are one motion on a person who cannot object; that a clean line at the foot of a page says nothing about the note under it. That every warmth has a payer, so ask who paid before asking what a thing is worth. That a breath stopped behind a clamp is a breath spent whether it goes in or not and the price is in the hand that holds the iron; that a word held at the teeth is kept, a kept thing is owed, a kept word is a debt, and a debt that can hear is a debt that can be called. That a loan that can hear can be called and the unsaid sentence is kept with the year a man will not count; that a near-saying is an interval on a tray and not a payment, not a hearing and not a seventh, and a **second** stopping makes a second interval and not a second payment. That a household straightens around a silence the way cloth straightens when a weight is lifted, obedience arriving before the instruction and being also a correction nobody will name. That the bite that holds a vessel whole and the bite that takes a hand off a man are one piece of iron with no Grathic word for both, and that a stroke checked one day and going on the next are two intervals on one tray and not a cause. That a truth can be two truths at once --- the surgeon counting the pressure of a fold and the child cold and wanting a crust --- and that the second is louder and neither can be handed back. That the hollow in grey wool where a stone had lain is the only object a grief with no name in the country it was spoken in can attach to.
- **Holds as shapes, not facts.** Three places that do not touch: the shelf's two gaps, the fold's one crust with its empty place, and the field's empty place in the returning line, with the neighbours' one-breath joinings set beside the first two and the new saying of the loaf-shelves --- two gaps stay two gaps until a finger lays them together and an account then wants a name --- set beside them all. **T098 planted and unpaid:** the field's empty place, gone around with a look and no figure, no name, cause, body or count, beside the woman's shut fist of Day 126 and the man's fist of Seed 24, two holdings on two places. **T099 planted and unpaid:** the reed-cutter's cord, carried again on Day 128 and not counted, knots unread, grey with the oil of a working thumb, held beside Mira's grey cord and second twist, Orra's knot-string with its legible unnamed double turn, Alda's six hidden pages and both tins, and Dessa's pewter tin, touching none of them. **T097 advanced and unpaid:** the two neighbours' tellings on two places beside the earlier tellings and the watcher's late turn, no fourth likeness and no third gap. The near-number of hands that went from strip to teeth and back, on the interval tray; Tarn's check on one day and his stroke going on without it on the next, set beside the bent finger, the held breath and **two** stoppings without any of them joined. The near-saying itself: a sentence that came twice to the threshold of the word and stopped twice, with no figure and no name, held as an interval. The greens: four shapes in four places --- three at a mouth (Silt 30, Seed 28, Reed 7) and Berr's at his own mouth --- with the pale cord-line on the green-mouthed woman's hand and the cup set back a quarter on places of their own, no name, no ear and no trade. The cold foot, uncured, uncaused and unnamed, the cold and the weather never in the same sentence. The soft hands and the loaf on separate trays, not joined and not called a sale, and the soft hands a sixth shape kept off every record-first tray. Berr's true subtraction, which he knows exists because the figure said is not the figure paid, but which he cannot read or carry. The sixth hearing out of a person, the three near-full answers advanced and not paid, the unspoken word with Orra's readable shape beside it, the two shut-room tellings beside the damper's double bite, the told bell beside the heard bell, the river's note as a fifth low thing on its own place, and the thumb-width knot kept out of the count of nails.
- **Does not know.** That he is one hundred and twenty-eight days old: nobody has told him, nobody under the roof knows he has a number, and there is no Grathic word in which to say what a life would count. What the clay jar on the wall shelf is for, though he has watched Sella glance at it for a hundred and twenty-eight days. Who took the smoky crust, who took the pale stone, what became of the weak board, or what the broken birth-slates were. Why another loaf went off the shelf on Seed 6, or who carried it out; what the charcoal tally under the loaf shelf says, the tally not yet seen. The name Dessa held under the sieve-wire scar or the number in her thumb, and no one can take either off a dead face now. What the men want who come before light to the back room, or why the room is a finger's width open, or whether there were three or four of them. What the promise of the foundling's name means, or who the men who burn slates are. Why the foundling's head went half a beat late, and that the sale is years ahead. What the reed-cutter's knots count, what the cord says beyond *No more can the Court*, who the reed-cutter is, what the word forty counts, who Orra's forty-first knot was tied over and who the man at the stern of the collector's boat was. What the silt-leach is, or that the cold in the child's foot has a name and a habit Arc 3 away. Which hand went into the strips on Seed 15 and did not come out on Seed 27, whether it is a hand or a body that will be found or a figure that will be given, and whether anybody will ever enter the place in the line. What the green on the silent woman's mouth will one day be read as, or whose mouth it will be joined to, and what the pale line on the back of her hand once held. Whether the loan that can hear can be called, what word would call it, and what must give at the end of Tarn's seventh year; there have been two stoppings now and he does not know how many more it will take. Whether the two tellings of the shut room belong under his own roof. Who paid for the fire, the gruel, the seed, the hands, the reeds and the loaves, and why every cost in Low Kiln has a payer nobody will name. He does not know that his exactness is the only arithmetic in the district, or that keeping it is at once his whole dignity and the proof that he has no standing to enter any of it.
