## The moment

Day 278, Frost 9, Ninthday, Y.H. 813, the small hours, under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof in \place{Low Kiln}. \person[Corin]{The boy} lies on \place{the bed} at the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf}, awake and silent, in the room whose floor does not touch the room of the fire, with the fold at his ear and one stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust two inches from it; the empty place beside the crust keeps its name to itself and no third came in the afternoon to make the row come out even. The thread of drool at his mouth has frozen in the dry heat and is unwiped, because nobody under any roof has ever been given an instruction about the thread and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. Cold has stopped at cheek, temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rides back, as though it had come to the end of a line written out for it and had neither an order to go further nor an order to go away; the afterdrop waits beyond all three, past a warming nobody in this house will perform. He knows the composed face that is not composure, knows a cold patient cannot clot and will seem better before he is worse, and that a man must be warmed from the outside and slowly. He wants the crust lifted the two inches to his mouth. Both are true and the second is louder; the surgeon takes the inventory and enters it nowhere, not even on the place where he keeps the things he does not enter. He had once reduced a shoulder in the dark by feel alone and now cannot close two inches on a pot handle; the thought came and went without bitterness, which was its own small betrayal.

Through the finger's-width door the river keeps its note of \lore{water over water}, a low thing over a lower thing, never numbered among the hearings; under the floor \lore{the carried fire} gives four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer without a name for two hundred and seventy-eight days, and nobody lifts the floor and nobody supplies the pull; overhead \place{the boards} keep four nails on the near board, four on the next, and a thumb-width knot where the fifth nail should have stood. Three counts in three places, and none rises into another, because agreeing any two would want a payer put under both and no payer stands to any. No fire-sitter stands over the coals and no house count comes to be borrowed. The bell's four, four and a gap, heard at \place{the Cross} on the last market-day of Smoke, stays in the square beside the told bell of \lore{the Long Night}; it is not brought under this roof on a Ninthday, not folded into the three and not heard as a seventh of \lore{the full note}. Six hearings of the full note stand beside and never among, five from four sources and the sixth, the small flat knock out of \person[Dessa Silt] as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go and never with the things that came from vessels; the empty place keeps its emptiness and none was invented to fill it. A woman's low counting comes from the next room whose floor does not touch his --- the small intake, the hold, the letting-go --- and the boy takes only what the hand can take and leaves the letting-go where it was.

At the close he has laid the hand down against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. On the hundred and thirty-second answer it took the small intake of the river's own going and the hold of one of the counted breaths from the room whose floor does not touch his, set them side by side with a finger between, borrowed no house count, supplied no fifth to the kiln, crossed no silence that did not belong to the river, took nothing from the two-gapped row, the charcoal under the shelf or the grain under the wool, and came down on the river's next low note. Neither interval was promoted into a reach, a hearing, a gift or a keeping; he made nothing of the week's small movements and would not let any stand up as a gift, a freedom or a claim; a gift wants a taker and nobody under this roof has taken anything. **One hundred and thirty-two** honest answers stand on the sixth place, each an object and not a power, exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air. The file lies open on \place{the towel} at the head of the bed with every gap left gap, every tray parted from its neighbour and nothing that had moved. The river's low note goes quiet after the hand comes down.

Two hundred and seventy-eight stands exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in \place{Sarn}, \place{Sill} or \place{Grath}; six months and ninety-eight days are told by nobody; the fire's fifth is unpaid the same number of days. \lore{The widow's mark} was touched once before the kiln on Frost 2, held one breath, let go, and touched at no shelf, well, rack, road, basket, yard, morning, hearth, afternoon, dusk or going of the light on Frost 7, 8 or 9, because none of these is an opening and the hand goes to the mark before the kiln and at no other hour. The first firing of Frost stands behind the household with no lesson, sign, mending anybody would name or payer, and the next opening stands in the first firing of a later month, far off and not hoped for; four openings in four months are four and not a rule. Frost went out without a boundary drawn on the afternoon of the Ninthday and neither of the district's two month-names was set right or left wrong in so many words. Smoke's third arithmetic went up \place{the clay road} in \person[Berr Clay]{Berr}'s locked box and is unread under this roof; the figure read is still not the figure paid; the dressed grain lies covered and unfigured on the cool stone at \place{the rack end}, turned **twice** on Frost 9 beneath the wool for damp with a lighter touch each time because the stone had gone cold, and given no figure, with the space between figure and price unsigned. The seventh year stands at its very end and has still not given; the loan stands uncalled in the ruts. Nothing was opened, named, called, paid or ended on this Ninthday; only the hand was placed down, forward and unresolved.

## The protagonist's condition

**Body.** \person{Corin}, two hundred and seventy-eight days old at the close of Day 278 and six months and ninety-eight days (two hundred and seventy-six and six months and ninety-six on Frost 7, two hundred and seventy-seven and six months and ninety-seven on Frost 8, two hundred and seventy-eight and six months and ninety-eight on Frost 9), carried everywhere: on all three days about the yard, at the hearth, loaf shelf, drying racks and rack end, and set on his bed under the roof at the going of the light each day, never in \place{the Debt Loft}; the instruction went to the fire, the wool, the pot, the broom and the running water and never to the basket. He was \person{Daniel Marchetti}, forty, twelve of them a trauma surgeon at Cook County; he died going back into a burning minivan for a child under the dashboard and his warmth left him in the giving and did not cool. As Corin he is a cracked, low-capacity vessel, unranked --- below Thin, twelve to fifteen embers at best, with a flaw nobody has named and a rate of loss nobody has measured. He cannot roll, stand, speak, read \lore{Grathic} in speech or writing, reach a cup, close two inches on the rim of the basket that holds him, lift a hand to his own chin or lift a crust two inches to his mouth. He drools; the thread at his mouth froze in the dry heat and was left unwiped on all three days. **Mind.** The double consciousness has acquired three more days of objects and six more answers from the hand, and still no hand to enter any of them. ## What he knows, suspects and does not know
