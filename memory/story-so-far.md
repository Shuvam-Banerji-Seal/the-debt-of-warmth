## A. The hidden frame (author only — nothing on the page)

Daniel Marchetti, forty, twelve a trauma surgeon at Cook County, died going back into a burning minivan for a child; his warmth left him in the giving and did not cool. The hot trace and an unpaid promise carried him into an infant vessel in Low Kiln. Sarn's Warm is living heat: vessels hold, leak, clamp, graft, pour, bank it; Hearth source, Waste sink, Court's Return an industry with a deduction, Ash a spent self. Ranks: Thin, Whole, Opened, Held, Drawn, Banked, Welled, Hearthward, Kept; Corin below Thin (`bible/premise.md` older six-name list). Marra, grey-coated at flare, forged deduction to zero, paid whole return with forty Low Kiln children, pays with greying fingers. Unsent nine-year-old call to Nina about a boat; debt crossed, not phone or words. Red flower is fire writing into the trace. Warm runs in spirals, not trees; Sarn cracked vessel, Hearth wound, Ash oldest repair; Second Want a former owner resuming in the Afterdrop (his fevers will be one); Kept retentive and numb; Rusk, unbanked since Y.H. 675, means to seal world; temple's creation story a founding invoice; earlier menders beautiful and lying, honest and letting world break, one Court. ## C. Corin at the close (Day 323)
