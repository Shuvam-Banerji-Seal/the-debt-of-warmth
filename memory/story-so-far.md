## A. The hidden frame (author only — nothing on the page)

Daniel Marchetti, forty, twelve a trauma surgeon at Cook County, died at 2:17 on a February expressway going back into a burning minivan for a child under the dashboard; his warmth left him in the giving and did not cool. The hot trace and an unpaid promise carried him into an infant vessel in Low Kiln. Sarn's Warm is living heat: vessels hold, leak, clamp, graft, pour, bank it; Hearth source, Waste sink, Court's Return an industry with a deduction, Ash a spent self. Ranks as trades: Thin, Whole, Opened, Held, Drawn, Banked, Welled, Hearthward, Kept; Corin starts below Thin (`premise.md` still carries an older six-name list). Marra, the grey-coated woman at the flare, wrote his measure, forged the deduction to zero, paid for his whole return with forty Low Kiln children and pays with her greying fingers. The phone held an unsent nine-year-old call to Nina about a Lake Michigan boat; the debt crossed, not the phone or words. The red flower is fire writing into the trace. Warm runs in spirals, not trees; Sarn is a cracked vessel, Hearth a wound, Ash the oldest repair; the Second Want is a former owner resuming in the Afterdrop (his fevers will be one); the Kept are retentive and numb; Rusk, unbanked since Y.H. 675, means to seal the world; the minivan child grew on Earth and going home costs her warmth; the temple's creation story is a founding invoice; earlier menders were beautiful and lying, honest and letting the world break, one become the Court. No Grathic word for the flat note or *nearly*; *the Breaking*, *one in three*, Marra's name and these truths never spoken in his hearing.

## F. Debts, lies and oaths (who knows what)

- **Alda's lie:** he went in levy among Forty. Alda and reader know; Dessa believed; Mira suspects Dessa knows more; Orra knows Dessa lies about child; Sella's knowledge unstated. Never corrected, curdled into fact in dead woman's face.
- **Name:** Marra's hand before face, Alda's chalk beneath; Orra remembers shape, cannot spell; boy read neither. Dessa would not say a name --- a thing with no name cannot be claimed by woman with no house.
- **Forty:** forty Low Kiln children paid for whole return; Orra's wool count plus forty-first for bundle with flower; Sella took him that week; Marra's assessment zero; Derren authorised forty unspent measures. He heard *forty* once, low; does not know it counts children or that he is one.
- **Warmth unentered:** stone and crust from Vail hearth, gruel from house dish, carried fire, Mira's crusts, seed, warm loaf from Slate Row, beds and folds --- each with payer and recipient who cannot refuse.
- **Subtractions in no account:** gull's coal (45), smoky crust (46), pale stone (by 64), weak board, loaf off shelf (Seed 6), broken birth-slates, Dessa's death and number. None solved, named or avenged.
- **Garl's tile debt** uncalled, neither paid nor settled; **Tarn's seventh year** uncounted; loan that can hear uncalled. **Sella's roll** private; mark held while fire never goes out. **Berr's box** locked; **Alda's six notes and two tins** hidden; **pewter tin** unopened; **Mira's cords**, **Orra's knot-string** unread.
- **Omission:** no collector for Dessa, nobody named it, first edge of heresy with no word or doctrine; water going around left things and sweeping around what water left.
- **No promise made on page to boy.** Nine boat promises old life, paid as shape Day 38. Jorra's promise of foundling's name to men who burn slates ungiven, not withdrawn.

## I. What he knows, does not know and suspects

**Knows.** How a body fails --- airway, bleeding, burns, cold, afterdrop, triage, extremity before core --- and a vessel handing its work on; record before person; body can be warm and still be gone; count can keep a larger thing out of room; unanswerable kindness cannot be refused, absence cannot be answered; district can call ruin weather and rename it past tense; figure read aloud not figure paid, figure in slate not figure in strip; iron can hold and take; likeness grows a cause unless hand holds it apart; two faces turned same way mean opposite things; thing left where it lies may still be kept. Added: flat knock out of cracked person; watched word as count; knot's shape without name; accounts in wool, tin, cord, bread, shelf-gaps; two tellings apart; three near-full answers advanced; half-beat after knock; eels blessed over uncollected woman; hundredth day kept, unspent.

**Does not know.** Word *Sarn*; Warm as world-stuff; Return's machinery, deduction, purchase, forty children; grey-coated woman; Marra's forgery; why flower or fevers; spirals; Dessa was his mother and Sella knew; Court as bank and invoice; Rusk's design; minivan child; why Sella took him, Garl's warmth, fire's payer, tin contents, Alda's floorboard, back room, second cord's errand, Ash's destination, why mark confirmed, why no collector came or that omission will be named. Not that he is one hundred days old --- nobody under roof knows he has a number, no Grathic word for a life's count. Not fates of stone, crust, coal, weak board, slates, loaf, second shelf gap; not Dessa's name or number or *one in three*; not cords' counts or forty-first knot's subject; not why foundling's head went late or that sale is years ahead; not silt-leach or that cold foot has name and habit Arc 3 away; not whether loan can be called or what must give at Tarn's seventh year's end; not that his exactness is district's only arithmetic and keeping it is both dignity and proof of no standing.

**Suspects without evidence.** Gap in every count is the part he keeps; joining ridges, slates, barrows, benches, cords, crusts, shelf-gaps, intervals, pauses would be deciding he has no standing to make; carried fire has payer beyond yard; Tarn's silence and his own one object held from two sides; nearly full is answer to long interval, not third thing; six like rooms are likeness not cause, better-lit room taught cost of that mistake; unrefusable kindness more dangerous than cruelty; what a house calls weather or mercy is what it entered nowhere; watched word is one someone expects reaction to.
