## A. The hidden frame (author only — nothing on the page)

Daniel Marchetti, forty, twelve a trauma surgeon at Cook County, died at 2:17 on a February expressway going back into a burning minivan for a child under the dashboard; his warmth left him in the giving and did not cool. The hot trace and an unpaid promise carried him into an infant vessel in Low Kiln. Sarn's Warm is living heat: vessels hold, leak, clamp, graft, pour, bank it; Hearth source, Waste sink, Court's Return an industry with a deduction, Ash a spent self. Ranks as trades: Thin, Whole, Opened, Held, Drawn, Banked, Welled, Hearthward, Kept; Corin starts below Thin (`bible/premise.md` still carries an older six-name list). Marra, grey-coated at the flare, wrote his measure, forged deduction to zero, paid for his whole return with forty Low Kiln children and pays with greying fingers. The phone held an unsent nine-year-old call to Nina about a Lake Michigan boat; the debt crossed, not phone or words. The red flower is fire writing into the trace. Warm runs in spirals, not trees; Sarn a cracked vessel, Hearth a wound, Ash the oldest repair; the Second Want a former owner resuming in the Afterdrop (his fevers will be one); the Kept retentive and numb; Rusk, unbanked since Y.H. 675, means to seal the world; the minivan child grew on Earth and going home costs her warmth; the temple's creation story a founding invoice; earlier menders beautiful and lying, honest and letting the world break, one become the Court. No Grathic word for flat note or *nearly*; *the Breaking*, *one in three*, Marra's name and these truths never spoken in his hearing.

## C. Corin at the close (Day 169)

**Body.** 169 days, five months nineteen days; cracked, low-capacity, unranked, below Thin, twelve to fifteen embers at best. Carried in Sella's basket, never into Debt Loft. Cannot roll, stand, speak, read Grathic, reach cup or close two inches on pot handle or lift hand to chin. Drools; thread frozen and unwiped; head wanders with small wet sound; hand outside wool up an inch toward warmth or river note and down; nothing within two paces reachable. Cold cheek-temple-wrist, wool riding back. Flower third rib to left forearm short of wrist, Alda's sign covered with date. Wants crust two inches from ear, breast, pot handle, number spoken by somebody who would know what a number was; gets none.

**Mind.** Surgeon reads hand-grips, afterdrop, record-first reaching, extremity before core, double bite of iron, two not-lookings of opposite things, likeness growing a cause unless hand holds it apart, hand held back from iron knowing where iron stands, hand under board telling what withdrawing hand cannot, cost as direction answered with direction, date given where figure asked as chart closed over finding, read figure apart from paid figure, two records by two hands not one account, inventory not a key. Infant body wins by being louder. Lessons: record before person; non-event leaves interval shaped like sound; watched low word is count; accounts in wool, tin, cord, bread, shelf-gaps, charcoal; figure read aloud and written privately two objects; bell heard and told two objects, three likenesses call themselves a cause; keeping hidden tells more by hiding; arm's record tells what carried, not cost; holding stoppings from doctrine worth nothing, doctrine an account that found a payer; two fractions on two places not a pair. Stopped expecting number spoken, not same as not wanting it. Eight record-first hands (Hessa eighth) on places of their own; Sella's, Alda's, Mira's on another, not a cause.

**Powers and holdings.** No power, rank, experiment, clamp, heresy, Warm moved; counting old-life habit, not gift.
