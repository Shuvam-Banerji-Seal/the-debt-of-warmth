## A. The hidden frame (author only — nothing on the page)

Daniel Marchetti, forty, twelve a trauma surgeon at Cook County, died going back into a burning minivan for a child; his warmth left him in the giving and did not cool. The hot trace and an unpaid promise carried him into an infant vessel in Low Kiln. Sarn's Warm is living heat: vessels hold, leak, clamp, graft, pour, bank it; Hearth source, Waste sink, Court's Return an industry with a deduction, Ash a spent self. Ranks: Thin, Whole, Opened, Held, Drawn, Banked, Welled, Hearthward, Kept; Corin below Thin (`bible/premise.md` has older six-name list). Marra, grey-coated at flare, forged deduction to zero, paid whole return with forty Low Kiln children, pays with greying fingers. Unsent nine-year-old call to Nina about a boat; debt crossed, not phone or words. Red flower is fire writing into the trace. Warm runs in spirals, not trees; Sarn cracked vessel, Hearth wound, Ash oldest repair; Second Want a former owner resuming in the Afterdrop (his fevers will be one); Kept retentive and numb; Rusk, unbanked since Y.H. 675, means to seal world; temple's creation story a founding invoice; earlier menders beautiful and lying, honest and letting world break, one Court. No Grathic word for flat note or *nearly*; *the Breaking*, *one in three*, Marra's name never in his hearing.

## C. Corin at the close (Day 263)

**Body.** 263 days, six months eighty-three days; cracked, low-capacity, unranked below Thin, twelve to fifteen embers. Carried in basket, never Debt Loft. Cannot roll, stand, speak, read Grathic, reach cup, close two inches on pot handle or lift chin two inches to crust. Drools; thread frozen and unwiped, no instruction ever given about it. Cold stopped at cheek, temple and bare wrist-joint as if at end of an order; afterdrop waits on warming nobody will perform. Flower third rib to forearm, Alda's sign covered with date. Wants crust, breast, pot handle, a number spoken by somebody who would know a number; gets none; stopped expecting, not same as not wanting.

**Mind.** Surgeon reads hand-grips, afterdrop, record-first reaching, extremity before core, double bite of iron, two not-lookings, likeness growing cause unless held apart, cost as direction, date where figure asked, read figure apart from paid, two records not one account, inventory not key, dust parted from slip, body read at two paces worth nothing, pressure held in cavity to one inch.
