## A. The hidden frame (author only — nothing on the page)

Daniel Marchetti, forty, twelve a trauma surgeon at Cook County, died at 2:17 on a February expressway going back into a burning minivan for a child under the dashboard; his warmth left him in the giving and did not cool. The hot trace and an unpaid promise carried him into an infant vessel in Low Kiln. Sarn's Warm is living heat: vessels hold, leak, clamp, graft, pour, bank it; Hearth source, Waste sink, Court's Return an industry with a deduction, Ash a spent self. Ranks as trades: Thin, Whole, Opened, Held, Drawn, Banked, Welled, Hearthward, Kept; Corin starts below Thin (`bible/premise.md` still carries an older six-name list). Marra, the grey-coated woman at the flare, wrote his measure, forged the deduction to zero, paid for his whole return with forty Low Kiln children and pays with her greying fingers. The phone held an unsent nine-year-old call to Nina about a Lake Michigan boat; the debt crossed, not the phone or words. The red flower is fire writing into the trace. Warm runs in spirals, not trees; Sarn is a cracked vessel, Hearth a wound, Ash the oldest repair; the Second Want is a former owner resuming in the Afterdrop (his fevers will be one); the Kept are retentive and numb; Rusk, unbanked since Y.H. 675, means to seal the world; the minivan child grew on Earth and going home costs her warmth; the temple's creation story is a founding invoice; earlier menders were beautiful and lying, honest and letting the world break, one become the Court. No Grathic word for the flat note or *nearly*; *the Breaking*, *one in three*, Marra's name and these truths never spoken in his hearing.

## C. Corin at the close (Day 131)

**Body.** One hundred and thirty-one days, four months eleven days; cracked, low-capacity, unranked, below Thin. Carried in Sella's basket, never into Debt Loft. Cannot roll, stand, speak, read Grathic, reach cup or lift hand to chin. Drools; thread frozen and unwiped, head wandering with small wet sound, nobody instructed; hand outside wool up an inch toward warmth or water and down; nothing within two paces reachable. Cold cheek-temple-wrist, wool riding back. Flower third rib to left forearm short of wrist; Alda's unknowing sign covered with date. Wants crust, breast, bucket's lip; reaches none.

**Mind.** Surgeon reads hand-grips, afterdrop, record-first reaching, extremity before core, double bite of iron, two not-lookings of opposite things, likeness growing a cause unless hand holds it apart. Infant body wins by being louder. Learned: record before person; non-event leaves interval shaped like sound; watched low word is count; accounts in wool, tin, cord, bread, shelf-gaps, charcoal; figure read aloud and figure written privately are two objects; bell heard and told are two objects and three likenesses will call themselves a cause; keeping hidden tells more by its hiding; hand under board knows it by heart or was told not to look; record in arm tells what was carried, not cost; date given where figure asked has question taken out; holding three stoppings from becoming a doctrine is worth nothing, and a doctrine is an account that found a payer. He has stopped expecting the number spoken, which is not the same as not wanting it. Five record-first hands (Marra, Drawing collector, Court-table clerk, Fishstairs clerk, Slate Row man) on five trays; Sella's, Alda's and Mira's on a sixth, not a cause.

**Powers and holdings.** No power, rank, experiment, clamp, heresy, Warm moved; counting old-life habit. One brown-edged crust Stone 15 stiff, uneaten, unlifted; empty place unnamed; no third.
