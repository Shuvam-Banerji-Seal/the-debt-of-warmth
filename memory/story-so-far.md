## A. The hidden frame (author only — none of this is on the page)

Daniel Marchetti, forty, twelve a trauma surgeon at Cook County, died at 2:17 on a February expressway going back into a burning minivan for a child under the dashboard; his warmth left him in the giving and did not cool. That hot trace, tangled with an unpaid promise, carried him into an infant vessel in Low Kiln. Sarn's Warm is living heat: vessels hold, leak, clamp, graft, pour, bank it; Hearth source, Waste sink, the Court's Return an industry with a deduction, the Ash a spent self. Ranks as trades: below Thin, Thin, Whole, Opened, Held, Drawn, Banked, Welled, Hearthward, Kept. The grey-coated woman at the flare was Marra, who wrote his measure, forged the assessment to zero and paid for his whole return with forty poor children of Low Kiln, paying ever since with her greying fingers. The phone held an unsent nine-year-old call to Nina about a boat on Lake Michigan; the debt crossed, not the phone or words. The red flower is fire writing itself into the trace. Warm runs in spirals, not trees; Sarn is a cracked vessel, the Hearth a wound, the Ash remembers and is the oldest repair; the Second Want is a former owner resuming in the Afterdrop (his fevers will be one); the Kept are perfectly retentive and numb; Rusk the teacher is Unbanked and means to seal the world; the minivan child grew on Earth and going home costs her warmth; the temple's creation story is a founding invoice; other menders came before (beautiful and lying, honest and letting the world break, become the Court). No Grathic word for the flat note or *nearly*; *the Breaking*, *one in three*, Marra's name and these truths have not been spoken in his hearing.

## B. Arc 1 — The Cold Stone (Chapters 1–10)

### B1. Birth and taking (Ch 1–2; Days 1–3)

**Day 1, Stone 7, Y.H. 812, Seventhday.** Born in House Silt's front room, three doors down the Clay Yard in Low Kiln, with Dan's full memory. Alda Reed delivers; a pale river stone warmed at the Vail hearth by Dessa and carried in her apron is spent against his belly, unremarked. Alda sees the branching red flower from third rib to left forearm, short of the wrist, crosses two fingers unawares, covers it with *birth-flower* and an unasked-for Nine Pots history. On the slate a strange neat dark hand wrote a name before the face; its pause matches the Chicago flare hand, filed side by side, unjoined. Alda's chalk and little tin are on her person. Dessa, spent, face to wall, counts nineteen steps to the slip rack and back, specific about four loaves on the seventh of Stone in 812 (three to the levy), rubs the sieve-wire scar up her left forearm, calls him *the little hot one* and *the flower*, says no name. Mira Silt keeps to the patch of floor the slate does not reach — eel slime on her sleeves, top of left ear gone — answers a question with a question, gives a crust briskly, face arranged. Sella Vail's hand comes in ahead of her, a burn like a little door across its back, feels for heat at his belly, hovers, withdraws; she never enters or speaks. Four nails, four, a knot the width of a thumb; Ilm under ice, Five three days off. He drools; cannot roll, hold up his head, wipe his chin, reach a cup two paces off or turn his head past a point without a small animal sound; cold arrives cheek, temple, wrist.

**Day 2, Stone 8.** The one scene away from him: the Vail kiln breathes four, four, misses the fifth; Garl Vail is only a hollow wheel-seat (twenty years' lean), salt-stiff coat on its peg, unswept Ash ridge, two proverbs (*feed the fire before you feed yourself*; *leave the crack showing*) said to fire and pots. Jorra says *he's not Vail*; Sella answers the pot. Tarn calls her *house*, says *I owe*, replaces the small bench knife, six years into a seven-year clay debt from Y.H. 806. Alda washes him, slate first, signs the flower again, covers it with dates; Dessa coughs from the pits and calls it weather. A cup gives the first flat note. Des's car-park laugh comes from Chicago and goes out. Sella touches the widow's mark before opening the kiln, at no other time.

**Day 3, Stone 9.** Sella takes the covered bundle up the yard; Dessa stands at the end of nineteen steps, thumb on scar, door-shut smile, does not follow. Alda tells her he went in the levy among the children taken for the account, dates arriving too fast, no figure — a mercy-lie believed three years. The cold stone stays in the fold on the abandoned bed in House Silt. Under the Vail roof he is laid on fresh straw; Tarn gets the Debt Loft above the wheel-shed with *he's a foundling, I'm a debt*, the boy the bed. A pot in the shed gives the second flat note. Nobody writes him as Vail — Jorra's sentence sealed. The charred edge of a slate lies in the kiln ash, black on one side, writing gone off its end, swept around, never lifted. Heat comes back to his hands and feet with the itch and sting of rewarming.

### B2. Cross and Drawing (Ch 3–4; Days 4–6)

**Day 4, Stone 10, Account-day.** Sella carries him a hundred and nine steps to the Cross in a basket under wool, a half-pace from open slates, says *the house*, puts the fold right without looking. Berr Clay, Thane of Clay, wipes his thumb twice, reads *one in twelve, as the notice says* twice to two hundred people, never says *I*; the Sill assessor says *the figure stands as read*. The district's slate answers flat twice — third and fourth hearings, three vessels (cup, Vail pot, slate). Bell four four and a gap, nobody crossing. Ostrowski's *decide, then carry* and Vesci's grammar break outward in Berr's *kindness is entered*; he reaches a pocket with no phone, name unlooked-at. A soft-cuffed Court clerk touches slate before woman, says *it's entered as it stands*; the neat hand's stop is seen a third time, unjoined. Berr tells Sella her widow's mark is confirmed at Greybank as a kindness entered, leaves Garl's tile debt uncalled; she answers the wool. Alda crosses the sign and covers it with a date; Mira asks the eel price and answers it herself, never looking at the basket, shoves a loose board clear. Ash goes out in a barrow toward the north gate. Drawing two days off, Five three.

**Day 5, Stone 11.** Coals held under ash; mark before opening only; fold right without a look. Tarn's broom stops two strokes at a Vail pot's flat note, no turn, no name. Jorra speaks to pots in bone-pale slip. Alda reaches for her slate before a neighbour's rekindled child, gives dates where comfort was asked, calls grey fingertips *a graft settling*; Dan knows the afterdrop — cold that worsens at rescue, the smile of a body giving up — and is corrected without anyone knowing he spoke. Her slate goes writing-down.

**Day 6, Stone 12, Hearth Drawing.** Sella sets the basket at the edge of the light in a neighbour's death-house. A collector with soft cuff turned back twice and fingertips the colour of skimmed milk gone off takes a cold iron tube from its cloth, finds the way under a dying man's arm and holds it without pressing; the man gives with the small flat knock — fifth hearing, first from a person. The collector says *the measure is banked; the account stands; the return is recorded* in a neat ordinary hand with no pause; not Marra, not the Cross clerk. For two sentences a white hospital light takes the room: a nurse holding pressure, a hand past a turned note to a signature (Tasha Broussard's chart). The child worsens on the third day; Alda turns a second note under a milk cup; the death-house Ash ridge is a finger's width thicker than Vail's, turning the corner unbroken. At dusk Sella banks the coals, feeds him from the house's own dish; Dessa passes at her threshold counting nineteen into her thumb, not looking in. Low Kiln says three days to the Five without saying what it counts.

### B3. Household arithmetic and the turn of the year (Ch 5–8; Days 7–30)

**Days 7–9, Stone 13–15 (Ch 5).** A pot turned toward before the set-down gives nothing — the first interval; Tarn sees the boy turn and stops his broom half a stroke at the empty place, saying nothing: two ears under one roof. Days to Kindling go around as a closed fist. At the Fishstairs Mira works among eels, slime on her sleeves, silt at her neck, left ear top gone, flicks eel-bones while deciding, gives Berr four eels where six should go against his two marks short for six years, calls the basket warm and off the slate, touches a grey cord at a figure; a second cord twisted twice beneath it has never gone to Nine Pots or a door, knots uncounted. A collector's cuff brings two present-tense sentences of a cafeteria at four and a wrapper eaten to the last crumb. Four steps down, four, a landing where a step should have been. In the north bridge's shade Dessa stands at the end of nineteen steps, cough called weather, four loaves on the seventh of Stone in 812, puts him among the bits, never looks in; a green-mouthed silhouette looks at pots; Mira puts a brown-edged barley crust into the fold — *a crust, not a sermon* — which he cannot lift. Sella counts her loaves twice, Tarn replaces the knife, Jorra moves the damper.

**Days 10–14, Stone 16–20 (Ch 6).** Kindling four four and a space; Alda touches her tin, signs his wrist's red edge unawares, covers it with a date, writes a note face-down toward an unnamed floorboard, says *warm and breathing* — exact and untrue. Second pot gives nothing: second interval. Tarn still two breaths at *you've done well*, holds his clip-ring up; Sella names the kiln's breath, Tarn tells of a Mud End death called merciful; Jorra's forearms free of dust. At the well a neighbour puts her slate before her child; two sentences of a ceiling light at four, a chart's turned note, a hand past it to a signature, then a pocket holding wool where a lit name lay. A man holds up Ilm ice with a hairline stopping short of the edge; a High Kiln woman with lead-glaze at her mouth says the thaw will come early, those wanting it late contradict her because a late thaw puts off paying; a neighbour describes the Court's room — bench, pot, draught, missing line. Sella measures the ridges, two thumbs at Vail, a finger thicker two doors down, neither swept before the Last Day. Account-day: bell four four and a gap, nobody crossing; Berr reads one in twelve, levy *not called by me*, goes around Garl's debt, no look in; Court clerk record-first; neat hand's stop a fourth time, unjoined. Crust cold. Five likenesses in five places, no cause. Five eleven days off.

**Days 15–24, Stone 21–30 (Ch 7).** Fuel and bread counted into the last tend in loaves and glances; a man brings a number in a shut fist, Sella answers with the fire. Tarn fetches a Day 16 collector: grey fingertips, slate before bed, *the measure is taken and the account stands*; a vessel gives a flat note past the door, Tarn's head turns a degree. Jorra's grey dust returns on Day 17 after five clean mornings; the empty space under the rack is gone around, not entered. On Day 19 a pot cracks two paces off with the flat knock, is called a pot gone wrong, becomes a third object on the interval tray — still no sixth hearing. Alda writes another hidden note, signs and covers the flower; Mira adds a fresh crust on Day 22 and shows the second cord; Dessa's cough stays weather. On Stone 30 the neat hand's pause comes a fifth time at the market's edge in one untagged beat of orange flare light and joins nothing; Berr reads one in twelve; the Five are three days off. Sella carries him home with both crusts, fire four four and a gap.

**Days 25–30, Kindling to Ilmen 1 (Ch 8).** Kindling: a coal under ash comes up the Clay Yard in a clay dish, hearth to hearth; Sella touches the mark once before tipping it in, payer unspoken. He counts the firelight four four and a hypothetical fifth; the first measure is taken offstage without a figure. Weighing: an officer reads the measures in Cross grammar wearing temple clothes, announces a whole return for someone absent and an unspoken name; silence among the gaps; Court's room as a voice, fourth likeness; Berr off with the box locked. Long Night: no fire after dusk, the house tells the Return in the dark, a told bell four four and a gap kept beside the heard bell, cold at cheek, temple, wrist, Tarn holding his seventh year back. Remembering: names pass door to door but not under the Vail roof; Alda lays birth-slates, touches her tin, signs and covers the flower; a grey sleeve finds wool where a lit name lay, boat promise past tense untagged. Last Day: bread baked, no third crust; Mira shows the second cord, knots uncounted; Sella measures both ridges and leaves them. Ilmen 1, Y.H. 813: the Ash is burned before light and called gone and dealt with by a neighbour, not Sella; no opening, mark untouched, fire under ash; Slate Row a direction, one in twelve printed, true subtraction unspoken. At thirty days old he carries five hearings from four sources and no sixth, five unjoined pauses, told bell beside heard bell and the empty fifth into the new year.

### B4. The thaw, the death and the word (Ch 9–10; Days 31–44)

**Day 31, Ilmen 2.** A woman at the well says the water tastes different; a man from the Ilm says the ice is *honeyed under the mud*. Nobody says the word. Mira answers a silence with her own answer, touches her grey cord, second beneath; neither counted.

**Day 32, Ilmen 3.** A month old, nothing marks it. Tarn sets the knife on its sill, six of seven counted, wants the late thaw, which puts off the paying; he agrees with both thaw parties so thoroughly no argument can stand. Sella says *the house*, instructs the fire and the wool, never the basket.

**Day 33, Ilmen 4.** Jorra's grey dust comes from the back room past his bent little finger; the space under the rack where slate should have stood is gone around, not entered; clean-slip and dusty forearms on separate trays.

**Day 34, Ilmen 5.** Alda lays birth-slates on the bench before the fold, touches her unopened tin, signs the flower's red edge unawares, covers it with the same unasked-for date, writes a fourth hidden note toward a floorboard, and says *warm and breathing* — exact and not true.

**Day 35, Ilmen 6.** A pot set down out of a woman's shadow answers *nearly full*, air under the note and a want in it: neither hearing nor silence, filed as first earthly answer to the Stone 13 interval, advanced and not paid. Tarn's head goes a degree and he sweeps on. Sella touches the mark before the opening only. Missing breath, empty fifth and nearly full note kept in three places.

**Day 36, Ilmen 7.** A hairline opens in the Ilm's ice, stops short of the edge, set beside the missing Fishstairs step and told bell in three places. A man brings a figure in his fist and thinks better; Berr wipes his thumb twice before touching nothing, leaves box locked. Singing Sill early; all weather.

**Day 37, Ilmen 8.** Dessa stands at the end of nineteen steps, worse, cough still weather, puts nineteen into her thumb, does not look in or say number or name. Mira passes her sister without looking, brings no crust. As Dessa's thumb goes along the wicker for one breath, an old hand in a grey sleeve reaches for a pocket and a small cold rectangle with a name, does not look, comes back with wool; a boat nine promises old on cold water is not said. The Slip Brook runs clay-heavy a little high. Carried up at dusk, thirty-seven days old, two crusts in the fold, no third; hand up an inch and down.

**Day 38, Ilmen 9, Ninthday.** The clay-heavy rise comes up behind the drying racks in the night and takes Dessa from the end of nineteen steps. The yard lifts her out with hands and no words; the boy, held on the bank, cannot move, speak or look away. As they lift, a small flat knock comes out of her body cracked all the way through — sixth hearing, second from a person — filed beside the five from four sources, never among them, no seventh. The detail held since Ilmen 8 is paid in past tense, untagged: grey sleeve, pocket, small cold rectangle with a name unlooked-at, boat unsaid, hand back with wool, nothing crossing into the basket. Alda comes after the vessel has given, reaches for her slate before the body, lays dates where a wonder should stand; her tin is touched, not opened. A soft-cuffed clerk with a slate is set at the Fishstairs for an uncalled measure. No collector comes for Dessa and nobody names the omission. She dies believing him spent among children she never saw; number in her thumb, name under the sieve-wire scar; the lie is now a fact nobody alive can correct.

**Day 39, Ilmen 10, Account-day.** Market resumes over her uncollected body: Berr wipes his thumb twice, reads one in twelve as the notice says, takes himself out with *there'll be a slate at the watch*, true subtraction over the basket; thaw's cost fist to mouth to shoulder, never given; soft-cuffed clerk's hand to slate before woman; bell four four and a gap; fold right without a look.

**Day 40, Ilmen 11.** No collector came for the woman in the brook and nobody found a word for it: warmth may be left where it fell and the morning still comes — first edge of the later heresy, an omission with no doctrine. Bucket goes round with no slate following. Sella measures the clean dark line where the Ash ridge turned against the death-house ridge by a thumb and leaves both. Tarn sets the knife back (sixth counted, seventh held) and sweeps the collector's road without turning, noticing that he noticed; a late thaw puts off paying. Jorra's bone-coloured slip goes to the damper ahead of his hand. Sella feeds him barley gruel from the house's dish and touches neither crust.

**Day 41, Ilmen 12.** Mira comes up with pale slime on her sleeves, silt in her neck, answers a question with her own answer, shows the second cord twisted twice beneath the grey with neither knot counted nor door lifted, brings no crust, does not look in. The weir women's talk of the blessing and its number ends in weather; the brook stays clay-heavy and high.

**Day 42, Ilmen 13.** Sella carries a clay jar three doors down and sets the basket just inside a door a finger's width open. From the height at which shelves are highest the boy sees a round pewter tin above the empty bed with a pale fold at its seam and a thumb's bright rub on the lid; he knows neither what it is nor that it was warmed at another fire, and enters it nowhere. Nobody says the woman's name or the number in her thumb. Sella opens no slate, does not turn the errand into a walk to a bank, sets the jar on her own shelf.

**Day 43, Ilmen 14.** Preparations upriver: eels salted, traps lifted, Orra's knot-accounts in the Eel House, not on slate. A green-mouthed woman looks at pots across the market and is gone unnamed. Orra comes onstage at the top of the rise, rope-handed, pale eel scars uncovered, grey streak through black hair, smelling of silt, cold fish and tallow, slow and half-sung in the Fen way; she tells of a collector's boat with four oars where a direct answer should stand and gives a date instead of an opinion. Alda stands with her in the lee of the Eel House; the third day is not spoken of. The Ilm runs higher under a thinning skin called weather; a weak board in the plank walk will not bear a man and is filed as a gap.

**Day 44, Ilmen 15, Fifthday, Fish Blessing.** Hearth-maids bless the traps from the flat stone; an old weir woman says the saying — the brook takes what no slate will enter, the knot keeps what no clerk can spell, the eels run whether anybody blesses them or not. Traps come up full, eels going head-first into the mouth and not finding it. Orra will not sit where a slate lies open and says names into the cord rather than the air. She ties a forty-first knot over the sideways story of a small warm covered bundle with a flower carried up the Clay Yard in the year of the flower, says *forty* low into the basket and watches. He does nothing. The surgeon notes the double turn tied left over right, the extra pressure of her thumb, that the word was watched — a count before he knows what it counts. The word goes on its own tray as a saying, not a vessel's sound; the knot's shape beside it, the weight of the thumb apart from both. Mira comes up with her two cords, brings no crust, does not look in. The river rises under its thinning skin, the Singing Sill's note comes early through the boards, called weather. At dusk Sella carries him home, sets him on his bed under the roof and not in the Debt Loft, puts the fold right without looking at his face. Both crusts still in the fold; the carried fire keeps four, four and a gap. Nothing joined, paid or explained.

## C. Corin at the close (Day 44)

**Body.** Forty-four days old; cracked, low-capacity, unranked, below Thin, twelve to fifteen embers at best, flaw unnamed, loss rate unmeasured. Carried in Sella's basket, set in shade or on boards, never into the Debt Loft. Cannot roll, stand, speak, read Grathic, reach a cup or lift a hand to his chin; drools, thread freezing unwiped; head goes where it wants with a small wet sound; hand outside the wool rises an inch toward warmth and comes down. Cold arrives cheek, temple, wrist. Red flower from third rib to forearm, short of the wrist; Alda crossed its red edge five times unawares, each covered with a date. Wants the breast and the smoky crust, gets neither.

**Mind.** The surgeon reads hand-grips, pulses, afterdrop, record-first reaching and a vessel going thin; the infant body wins every argument by being louder. Learned: record before person; a non-event leaves an interval shaped like a sound; a watched low word is a count; accounts in wool, tin, cord and bread no officer reads; a district blesses eels over an uncollected woman; an unanswerable kindness cannot be refused; he is the only adult in every room and the least use in it.

**Powers and holdings.** No usable power, rank change, experiment, clamp or heresy; no Warm moved. Counting is an old-life habit. Basket and wool are Sella's; two barley crusts lie in the fold — one from Stone 15, stiff past smelling, one from Mira on Day 22, faint with barley smoke — neither eaten, lifted or named, no third. The pale river stone lies in the fold of grey wool on the abandoned bed in House Silt, unmentioned since Day 3; knife on its sill, charred slate in the kiln ash, clay jar on Sella's shelf. Dessa's thumb on the wicker is kept where things with no hour are kept; tin, cords, knot-string and Alda's notes are seen and unread.

**Old wounds.** Nina's missed call, nine promises, no boat; Tasha's chart signed over the unread nurse's note and the *systems failure*; the crooked casket; Elena's quiet leaving, Marisol's temperate one; Des's timed minute of laughter; Ostrowski's two duties; Vesci's grammar; mother's kitchen, sister in Florida, brother on the force, father who fixed things instead of saying them. The unsent call was paid on Day 38 as a shape in past tense, untagged; the phone never crossed.

## E. Places

## F. Debts, lies and oaths (who knows what)

- **Alda's lie:** he went in the levy among the Forty. Known to Alda and reader; believed by Dessa; Mira suspects Dessa knows more; Orra knows Dessa lies about the child; Sella's knowledge unstated. Never corrected on the page.
- **The name:** Marra's hand wrote it before the face, Alda's chalk beneath; Orra remembers its shape, cannot spell it; the boy has read neither. Dessa will not say a name — a thing with no name cannot be claimed by a woman with no house.
- **The Forty:** forty Low Kiln children paid for his whole return; Orra keeps their count in wool plus a forty-first for the bundle with the flower; Sella took him that week; Marra's assessment shows zero; Derren's file authorised forty unspent measures. He heard *forty* once, low, and does not know it counts children or that he is one.
- **Warmth unentered:** stone and crust from the Vail hearth, gruel from the house's dish, carried fire, Mira's crusts, every bed and fold — each with a payer and a recipient who cannot refuse.
- **Garl's tile debt** uncalled, neither paid nor settled; **Tarn's seventh year** uncounted; a loan that can hear, uncalled by both. **Sella's roll** private; her mark held while the fire never goes out. **Berr's box** locked; **Alda's tin and four notes** hidden; **the pewter tin** unopened; **Mira's cords** and **Orra's knot-string** unread.
- **The omission:** no collector called for Dessa, nobody named it — first edge of a heresy with no word, doctrine or argument.

## I. What he knows, does not know and suspects

**Knows.** How a body fails — airway, bleeding, burns, cold, afterdrop, triage — and the look of a vessel handing its work on; record before person; a body can be warm and still be gone; a count can keep a larger thing out of the room; an unanswerable kindness cannot be refused; a district can call its own ruin weather; knowing exactly has never given him a hand. Added: the flat knock out of a person cracked through; a watched word as a count; a thumb's weight on wicker; accounts in wool, tin and cord; nearly full as its own kind of answer; blessing eels over an uncollected woman; two silences as an unnamed equality.

**Does not know.** The word *Sarn*; that Warm is the stuff of the world; the Return's machinery, deduction, his purchase or the forty children; who the grey-coated woman is; Marra's forgery; why the flower is on his ribs; why fevers will come; that ways run in spirals; that Dessa was his mother and Sella knew; that Court is bank and temple invoice; that Rusk means to seal the world. Not why Sella took him, what became of Garl's warmth, where the carried fire was lit or who pays, what is in either tin, whose floorboard holds Alda's notes, what is in the back room, why the second cord never went upriver, where the Ash goes, what the uncalled debt was for, why the mark was confirmed, why no collector came for the woman in the brook or that the omission will one day have a name. He heard *forty* once, does not know it counts children or that he is one, or what the forty-first knot records; does not know the green-mouthed woman has a name or ear, that Dessa put him among the bits she counted, or that Mira and Orra know more than they will say.

**Suspects without evidence.** That the gap in every count is the part he keeps, and joining ridges, slates, barrows, benches, cords, crusts, intervals and pauses would be a deciding he has no standing to make; that the carried fire has a payer beyond the yard; that Tarn's silence and his own are one object held from two sides; that nearly full is an answer to a long interval, not yet the third thing; that the six like rooms are a likeness and not a cause, because a better-lit room taught him the cost of that mistake; that an unrefusable kindness is more dangerous than a cruelty; that what a house calls weather or mercy is what it has entered nowhere; that a watched word is one someone expects a reaction to.

## J. Open dangers and immediate next

The thaw has taken a person: brook high and clay-heavy, Ilm rising under thinning skin, Singing Sill early, weak board — all called weather. A woman is dead, uncollected, the omission unnamed; the Fishstairs clerk waits for a measure nobody called; the number goes around in fists, mouths and weather; Orra's cord hangs in the Eel House with a knot nobody can read and a word already in the room. Sella's mark, Garl's debt and the clean dark line hold; Tarn's seventh year, Jorra's back room, Mira's cords, Alda's notes and the two crusts are keepings no slate can enter. He has no standing to ask.

**Chapter 11 opens Day 45, Ilmen 16, Y.H. 813, Sixthday, at the Fishstairs after the Blessing:** the forty-first knot tied and nobody able to say what it counts; the boy carried below the north bridge at dawn; a brine-gull going off with a coal not yet cold; eel-bones moving hand to hand while Mira decides whether to lie; a crust about to go missing with nobody saying who took it. Keep six hearings beside and never among, nearly full on its own tray, told bell beside heard bell, both cords side by side unjoined, Dessa dead with the omission unnamed, clean dark line unswept, fire at four four and a gap. No recognition of the mother, no name or number from her, no bank becoming a quest, no one in three, no Marra, no explanation of the Forty, no joining, no ending. Forward, not closed.
