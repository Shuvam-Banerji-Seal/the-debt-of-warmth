# Digest — Chapters 41–50 (Days 170–203)

*Tallow 21 to Ember 24, Y.H. 813: the firing month's last working week and third Account-day, then the kiln month come on without a boundary, its two Account-days and last tend, still Arc 1, "The Cold Stone." Day 203: the boy two hundred and three days old, one crust, two shelf gaps, three held changes and an inch on a sixth, the count exact against a house that keeps it badly. Nothing joined, paid, named, opened, called or ended.*

**Ch 41 (Days 170–172, Tallow 21–23).** The last working week opens with the kilns at heat and the carried fire missing its fifth under the floor, its payer unnamed for 170–172 days. Sella Vail instructs wool, fire, pot, broom and running water, never the basket, says *the house*, keeps her half-pace and leaves the widow's mark hung, for a morning and a shelf are not openings. Men go low into the back room unnamed on Day 171; Jorra Vail comes through with dust ground small as meal and withholds a pot's note from a High Kiln kiln-master on a wrong wind. Then the chapter's change: while Jorra's bent finger stands in the cold damper and Sella's sentence finishes, Tarn's broom checks half a beat behind it on the flagstones and goes on without a word --- the Seed 22 half-check returned after a hundred and fifty days, filed **beside** the Day 161 damper fraction and the Day 166 held beat as three held changes on three places, not a rule, for a rule is an account that found a payer. Tarn tells the kindling *Six counted. The next I don't count*, brings the ear-sentence to the word and stops; nine stoppings stay nine and no tenth stands. Count 172.

**Ch 42 (Days 173–179, Tallow 24–30; seven-day passage chapter).** Seven days the month takes to die with no boundary drawn: hot clay, the sweet edge of glaze on a wrong wind, costs going to the pot and the weather. Men go low into the back room on Days 174, 176 and 178, unnamed; the two tellings stay two. Jorra's finger waits out Sella's sentence on two mornings, a remembered holding; Tarn wants the late thaw because it puts off the paying. On Day 179, the third Account-day and last market, Sella sets the basket in the moot-post's shade where no slate lay and stands half a pace off without a reason; Berr Clay wipes his thumb twice, reads *one in twelve, as the notice says* a third time that month, calls the levy and denies it with *your house*, never *I*, going off with the box locked and the true subtraction inside, believing he has done a kindness. The bell goes four, four and a gap; Hessa Rale carries the slate up the road unread: *I don't read it. I carry it. There's a difference.* Count 179, the month out.

**Ch 43 (Days 180–182, Ember 1–3).** Ember opens with no boundary drawn, neither month-name corrected; the boy is six months exactly and told by nobody. On the morning of Day 180 Sella puts her hand to the widow's mark at the kiln mouth, holds it one breath, lifts the lid and tips the carried coal under a broken pot; the fire takes, still answering four with the fifth missing and no payer, and nobody praises it. She does not touch the mark again at any shelf, well, road, basket, afternoon or dusk --- the hand goes to it before the kiln and at no other hour. A pot set down wrong gives its note early; the boy files it as a danger, not among the hearings. Tarn's ear-sentence stops at the word; Jorra's finger stands in the damper until the sentence reaches the pot. No Account-day or market: the first of Ember waits on Day 189. Count 182.

**Ch 44 (Days 183–185, Ember 4–6).** Tarn keeps six checked and the seventh at the lip, says *Yes, house* to early and late while wanting the late because it puts off the paying, and holds the ear-sentence short of the Seed 16 breath. On the afternoon of Day 183 he climbs to the Debt Loft, takes the warm stone given him in the winter Garl died out of its unmarked pot, holds it, sets it on the sill in the light, looks a long while and puts it back. No word, figure, payer or slate: he does not reconcile the stone with the debt and does not forgive it, for not trying is not forgiving. Below, the boy hears only a small dry scrape of a pot's foot on stone and files it on **a fourth place**, not across the three held changes and not suffered to stand up as a cause; the next two days file the remembering as a remembering. Jorra's damper finger is a remembered repetition. Count 185.

**Ch 45 (Days 186–188, Ember 7–9).** The month's danger is paid small, non-fatally and entered nowhere. On Day 187 a glaze-room hand from High Kiln drinks at the well with the sweet on his breath and a pale line wet white at his gum; the boy reads line, held hand and pale nails from two paces --- exact, complete, worth nothing. On Day 188 a rack-worker's pot sings itself out early and the woman makes no lesson of it; two men carry the glaze-room hand out across the yard, alive and breathing, one thumb hooked under his arm to keep the head from lopping. Sella turns the boy's face as a pot is turned to the light, goes wide around the place the carriers crossed and gives no name for what ails him. No collector, figure, Grathic cause or slate; the pale line lies on **a fifth place** and the cost stays entered nowhere. T064 moves from open to planted. The man went up the road breathing; no offstage fate is invented. Count 188.

**Ch 46 (Days 189–191, Ember 10–12; first Account-day).** Sella sets the boy in the moot-post's shade; two hundred lean to the block, the bell four, four and a gap with nobody crossing. Berr wipes his thumb twice, passes a finger over the figure and reads *one in twelve, as the notice says*, going twice over the basket; *The levy is called. It is not called by me*, *your house*, never *I*, and off up the road with the box locked and the subtraction inside, believing he has done a kindness. Hessa takes the slate up before noon, edge-on: *I don't read it. I carry it. There's a difference, and the road doesn't care which.* The soft-cuffed clerk stays set below the bridge; a silent woman at the pot board turns a cup a quarter, green at her mouth, and a small shape in cast-offs catches light on a green sliver and goes dull. Colour gets no ear, shape no name. The reading does not answer the man carried out on Day 188. Count 191.

**Ch 47 (Days 192–194, Ember 13–15).** Three quiet days. Costs go to pot and weather, the charcoal stays unread and no mark is touched; Tarn stops at the word, nine stoppings still nine; Jorra's damper finger is a remembered repetition. Then the boy's hand goes up an inch toward the river's note and does not come down at once: on Day 193 it hesitates for the small pause in the kiln's breath, on Day 194 morning it goes up and down at once, and in the small hours it stays up for one of the breaths Sella counts at her fire in the room that does not touch his, then comes down against the fold. Each answer is filed on **a sixth place** --- not a hearing, seventh, stopping, payment, held change, key, power or rule, no payer named, no doctrine stood up. He still cannot close two inches or speak a number; the body humiliates him and moves by an inch at once. Count 194.

**Ch 48 (Days 195–197, Ember 16–18).** The inch is asked again: Day 195 for one counted breath, Day 196 down before the breath finished, Day 197 morning up and down at once, Day 197 small hours up through the whole breath and into the letting go, down with the breath --- the longest holding, a coordination, not a reach. Each is filed apart on the sixth place; three like holdings are not a trend or a power. The sweet edge comes off High Kiln on a wrong wind and goes out on it, a direction; no lesson, no second edge, no pot lost. The nine stoppings, the Seed 16 breath, the uncalled loan, the cold stone and the unread charcoal stand unchanged. Count 197.

**Ch 49 (Days 198–200, Ember 19–21; second Account-day).** On Day 199 the kiln month's arithmetic changes a second time and cannot be changed back. Berr reads *One in twelve, as the notice says* low into the crowd, the figure twice over the basket, calls the levy, denies it with *your house*, never *I*, and goes off with the box locked and the subtraction inside; a figure read aloud is still not the figure paid. Hessa's slate goes up unread; the clerk stays set. The sixth green remains a colour at the pot board and the foundling's sixth half-turn a shape at the market's edge, no seventh of either. The reading does not answer the man carried out on Day 188. The hand is asked four times and held four ways --- down before the breath was done, down at once, held for the small intake, held through the whole breath and down with it --- each on its own place, laid across nothing. Count 200.

**Ch 50 (Days 201–203, Ember 22–24).** The last tend opens, no boundary drawn; the seventh year stands nearer its end than ever at the knife's lip and has not given. Five answers: down at once, a small intake, down before the breath finished, a whole breath down with it, a small intake again. Then in the small hours of Day 203 the hand goes up toward the river's note and stays through the small intake, the hold and the letting go of one of Sella's breaths at her fire --- **and does not come down with her breath**, kept through the silence after her count and down on the river's next low note: a coordination with a second rhythm nobody counts and never gave him. Not a reach, hearing (water over water is still a fifth low thing never counted among the hearings), seventh, stopping, payment, held change, key, power or rule; no payer named, nothing stood up as a sign. The river goes on without noticing an inch rode one of its low notes. Count 203.

**Turns and changes.** No death occurs; Dessa stays dead since Day 38, uncollected and unnamed, and the man carried out on Day 188 went up breathing, no fate invented. No power gained: Corin stays cracked, unranked below Thin, mute, unable to reach two paces; the inch is a physical first, not a gift. No thread opens after T064 is planted in Ch 45 and none is paid; the tally stands at one hundred (T001–T100), 65 planted, 34 open, 1 paid (T087). T036/T076 holds at nine stoppings; T062/T035/T031 and T100 gain the Day 171 half-check as a **third** held change, T006 the loft-sill scrape on a fourth, T064 the pale line on a fifth and T061/T082 the inch on a sixth, ending in a coordination with the river's rhythm. T058/T079 records the second opening on Ember 1; T015/T041/T047/T077 sees the month's arithmetic changed twice by readings that are not payments. The count crosses 170 to 203 exact and unspent. Six hearings stay six, three near-full answers advanced, the word forty unspoken, the sixth green a colour and the foundling a shape with no seventh of either, Marra out of Sill, Fenna unnamed, *he's not Vail* sealed and the old life offstage. Forward, not closed.
