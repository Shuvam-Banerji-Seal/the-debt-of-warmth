# Digest of Chapters 91–100 (private)

*Consolidated after Chapter 100. Days 324–353, Bone 25 through Ash 24, Y.H. 813. Every day was lived in its own `\daymark` and two full scenes. The chapter Chronicles and `memory/timeline.md` hold the day-by-day detail.*

These ten chapters are one run of ordinary tending days, broken by three Account-day readings, one firing and three arrivals of a voice that stood in no doorway. Bone went out without a boundary drawn and Ash came on without one; neither habit of naming the months was set right or left wrong. Corin passed from three hundred and twenty-four days old (six months, 146 days) to three hundred and fifty-three (six months, 173 days), told by nobody. Honest answers on the sixth place went from 222 to 282, two a day, each an object and not a power. No death occurred: Dessa Silt stays dead and uncollected, her name and number unspoken. No rank changed, no power used, no experiment made, and no sale, bond or slate-opening came within reach of the basket.

**Ch 91 — Days 324–326, Bone 25–27.** The house woke by small sounds: boards giving back heat, the fourth pull under the floor with no fifth, the river low over lower under the finger's-width door. Sella Vail went to the hearth before the basket, told the coals it was the thaw, turned them with a broken pot's back and said *The house will keep it* where another woman would have said I; she lifted the twice-mended pot thumb-first where the wire crossed the crack and set its rim two inches beyond the reaching hand. The yard went around its seven walked-around places with a look and no number; three coverings at the well stayed weather; three counts stood in three places and did not become one. On Bone 26 Ilva drew at the well, answered *Ask the pot what it cost. I keep the count, not the account*, and kept the notched strip in her sleeve; the boy filed her reaching as the **ninth** record-first hand beside Hessa Rale's eighth, with no tenth. At the kindling Tarn rested his thumb on the uncut seventh notch at the lip for one breath, drew back to the six and said *Six counted. The next I don't count* — the chapter's one meaningful change, advanced and NOT paid (T036/T076/T006), a held object on its own place, not a tenth stopping, not the seventh year given, not a calling of the loan. Answers 223–228. A Scene 5 tally reads "two hundred and twenty-five" where 226 would follow; the canonical ledger is **228**.

**Ch 92 — Days 327–329, Bone 28–30.** Bone 30 brought the month's third and last Account-day. Berr Clay came up off the clay road with his red cord folded under his coat and green of tasted lead glaze at one mouth-corner, wiped his thumb twice before touching nothing, read *One in twelve, as the notice says* twice over the basket — once for the moot, once for the late households — called the levy and denied it with *your house*, never I, and went off with the box locked believing he had done a kindness. **Six arithmetics stood behind one unsigned figure**, six and not one account, the figure read still not the figure paid, the finding on a place of its own with no payer. Hessa carried the slate toward Greybank unread; the bell went four, four and a gap; Ilva answered at the well on the twenty-ninth. Answers 229–234; count 329.

**Ch 93 — Days 330–332, Ash 1–3.** Ash came on as a dark room comes on. On Ash 2, before any word or instruction to the basket, Sella put her left hand to the widow's mark at her throat, held it one breath, lifted the clay lid with the back of a hand and tipped the carried coal under a broken pot; the fire took with four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer unnamed three hundred and thirty-one days. This was the **sixth opening in six months** — Tallow 1, Ember 1, Smoke 1, Frost 2, Bone 2, Ash 2 — six and not a rule, no seventh made due, nobody praising it or naming what it mended (T058/T079 advanced, not paid). The mark was not touched again at any shelf or hour. Answers 235–240; count 332.

**Ch 94 — Days 333–335, Ash 4–6.** Three movements arrived and none was spent. On Ash 5 an ownerless voice over the boards joined the burned slate to the room with the damper shut — not a sixth telling of the slate (five), not a third telling of the shut room (two), no room named, no hand at any flame, no figure, date, cause or payer (T035/T031/T065/T095 advanced, not paid). Also on Ash 5 an ownerless voice at the well gave the Frost 14 well-saying a **fourth shaping** — the runner had come back down the road with the writing turned toward her; Ilva drawing a pace away was expressly not the speaker and Hessa was not made a reader (T080 advanced, not paid). On Ash 6 Hessa came **down** the clay road with the writing turned toward herself for the first time, still edge-on and unread, a runner and not a collector: *Ask the road. It knows the way and I know the weight.* The boy filed the turning beside the fourth shaping with a finger between and made no fifth shaping, reader or payment. Answers 241–246; count 335.

**Ch 95 — Days 336–338, Ash 7–9.** No voice came over the boards. On Ash 9 at the going of the light Sella's thumb stayed one breath longer than the lift required at the wire crossing on the twice-mended pot, then let the weight come on the handle with the hairline showing, saying both proverbs to pot and fire. Repair stayed apart from restoration: a restored thing hides the hand that handled it, a mended thing leaves that hand showing. The boy filed the interval **beside** the staying at the inch, the early letting-go, the handful, Tarn's thumb's resting and the sixth opening — not a sixth held object, not a habit, sign, mending, cause, payer or rule, not made again (T009/T013/T092 advanced, not paid). Answers 247–252; count 338.

**Ch 96 — Days 339–341, Ash 10–12.** Ash 10 was Tenthday, the month's first Account-day. Berr wiped his thumb twice, sent the figure twice over the basket, denied the levy with *It is not called by me* and *your house*, and went off with Ash's first subtraction locked in the box. **Seven arithmetics stood behind one printed figure**, seven and not one account. Hessa took the slate up unread; the soft-cuffed clerk stayed set at the landing; a seventh green and a seventh shape stayed sightings with no eighth, neither named. Ilva answered at the well on the eleventh. Answers 253–258; count 341.

**Ch 97 — Days 342–344, Ash 13–15.** On Ash 14 at the kindling Tarn set the knife at the edge of its sill where it could be checked and also slipped off, passed his hand over it as the house checks, and let the hand stay a breath longer than the check required. He cut nothing, called no loan, spent no Seed 16 breath and did not repeat the thumb's resting. The boy filed the held breath beside the five held objects and the pot-crack resting — **not** a sixth held object, not a seventh of anything, not a tenth stopping, not a cause, payment or rule, with no payer standing (T036/T076/T006 advanced, not paid). Answers 259–264; count 344.

**Ch 98 — Days 345–347, Ash 16–18.** On Ash 17 an ownerless voice over the boards from the direction of Slate Row gave a word about a room shut with the damper closed and a slate burned over a man nobody named — no room named, no hand at any flame, no figure, date or person standing to be asked. Weather answered first, the price of barley after it, and the voice went up the terrace. The boy filed it beside the Ash 5 joining as a further shaping advancing the finding that a place wants a witness — not a sixth telling, third shut-room telling, sixth asking, fifth shaping, hearing, cause, reader, name or payment (T035/T031/T065/T095 with T101 advanced, not paid). Jorra grew quieter and the household straightened around the quiet. Answers 265–270; count 347.

**Ch 99 — Days 348–350, Ash 19–21.** Ash 20 was the second Account-day. Berr read one in twelve twice over the basket, denied the levy with *It is not called by me* and *your house*, and went off with Ash's second subtraction locked in the box beside Ash's first, the three of Frost and the first, second and third of Bone. **Eight arithmetics stand behind one unsigned figure** — eight and not one account — the figure read still not the figure paid and no signature on the space; no doctrine stood up out of eight, for a doctrine wants a payer and none stood. Answers 271–276; count 350.

**Ch 100 — Days 351–353, Ash 22–24.** On Ash 23 a third ownerless voice over the boards from the direction of Slate Row shaped the burned-slate / shut-room joining one direction further: a room shut with the damper closed, a slate burned over a man nobody named, the room shut against the cold and the slate burned so a count would not go up — still no room, hand at flame, figure, date, name or person standing to be asked. A wrong wind off High Kiln brought the sweet edge of lead glaze over the boards and touched the fold, the price of barley answered after it, and the voice went up the terrace. The boy filed it beside the Ash 5 joining and the Ash 17 direction with a finger between every pair — a further shaping, not a sixth telling, third shut-room telling, sixth asking, fifth shaping, witness, cause, reader, name or payment (T035/T031/T065/T095 and T101 advanced, not paid). It did not repeat on the twenty-fourth. Answers 277–282; count 353.

**Turns, deaths, revelations, relationships.** No deaths, no revelation given a figure; every meaningful movement was a held object filed on its own place and refused standing up as a sign, lesson, mending, cause, payment or rule. The new objects are Tarn's thumb's resting (Bone 26), the sixth opening (Ash 2), Sella's pot-crack resting (Ash 9), Tarn's hand-at-the-sill breath (Ash 14), and the Ash 5 joining and Ash 17 and Ash 23 directions, with the well-saying's fourth shaping and the Ash 6 turning held beside them and joined to none. Jorra's silence deepened after each voice; Sella gave none a name or figure; Hessa carried and did not read; Ilva kept her count in wood. No relationship changed hands: Jorra's promise stays ungiven and unwithdrawn, *he's not Vail* stays sealed, Garl's name unspoken, Marra still not in Sill.

**Power and threads.** Corin remains a cracked, low-capacity vessel below Thin, unable to roll, stand, speak, read Grathic, close the two inches, lift a crust or wipe his mouth; counting is an old-life habit, not a gift, and growth is the discipline of attention. One hundred and one threads (T001–T101) stand unchanged at 66 planted, 34 open, 1 paid (T087): none opened since T101 (Ch 59) or paid since T087 (Day 38). Advanced and NOT paid: T036/T076/T006, T015/T041/T047 (sixth, seventh, eighth arithmetics), T058/T079, T035/T031/T065/T095 and T101, T080, T009/T013/T092, T061/T082. Held unchanged: five burned-slate tellings, two shut-room tellings, five askings of the row with one shaping and no witness, four well-saying shapings with no fifth, nine hands and nine stoppings with no tenth, six hearings with the seventh place empty, four held changes on four places, two small objects of the hand, the handful, seven greens and seven shapes with no eighth, the loan uncalled and the fire's fifth unpaid. Ash 30 (Day 359) stands six mornings ahead as a date only; no ninth arithmetic early.
