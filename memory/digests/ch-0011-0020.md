# Digest — Chapters 11–20 (Days 45–100)

*Ilmen 16 to Seed 11, Y.H. 813: the Flood, its lean week, the turn into Seed and a planting month that becomes waiting. All still Arc 1, "The Cold Stone." Day 100: the boy one hundred days old, one crust, a second shelf-gap in another room, the count exact against a house that keeps it badly. Nothing joined, paid or ended.*

**Ch 11 (Days 45–47).** A brine-gull carries a not-yet-cold coal upriver: one warmth gone, entered in no slate. Orra's forty-first knot is now readable --- double turn, tail tucked, left over right, thumb-weight --- but nameless; *forty* is not spoken again while she watches. Sella keeps the month in loaves and glances, instructs fire, pot and wool, never the basket, says *the house*, touches the mark once before an opening. Tarn's knife lies with six counted and the seventh held; his broom crosses the collector's road without turning, no collector comes, a late thaw puts off paying. Jorra's bent finger reaches the damper ahead of her sentence. Alda presses the fold too long, dates where a wonder should stand, says *warm and breathing*, exact and not true. Mira's smoky crust (Stone 28) is gone --- eaten, lifted and named by nobody, unsolved; the boy sets Sella's, Alda's and Mira's hands on a tray and refuses a cause. Frost writes on the well-lip where the Warm went out, called weather by noon; a number goes fist to mouth to shoulder and arrives nowhere; Berr wipes his thumb twice, says a number goes into a slate, not into a well, and leaves his box locked. The Fishstairs clerk still sits for an uncalled measure.

**Ch 12 (Days 48–54).** Seven lived days. Frost writes back on the well-lip, the oiled paper hangs slack, the ice's defenders fall silent: the argument that ended in weather has no arguer. Hairlines stop short of the edge, the Singing Sill is early, the weak board is there and trodden by nobody. On Account-day Berr reads one in twelve over the basket, the true subtraction goes unspoken, the bell goes four, four and a gap. Jorra comes out with grey dust past his bent finger; a vessel's note passes the door and his head does not turn. Alda covers her unaware sign with a date and turns a **fifth** note toward a floorboard. A rack pot answers *nearly full* a second time, filed beside Ilmen 6 as advanced and not paid. Mira shows the second cord twisted twice beneath the grey, never upriver, knots uncounted. The boy carries one crust and an empty place through seven days.

**Ch 13 (Days 55–60).** A Greybank man tells of a counting room shut in the war with the damper closed and its men dead of the breath. Sella, who lost a husband so, tells the fire it is the thaw; the boy files the saying beside the bells and the kiln's gap, joined to none. At House Silt he sees the pewter tin above the empty bed, pale fold and thumb-bright lid; Alda's tin is touched shut and no sixth note written. Sella lays one thumb in Vail's clean dark line and one in the death-house ridge, and fetches no broom. Berr reads one in twelve twice at the last market, Mira brings no crust, a barrow of grey goes out unread, and Silt comes in on mud. A **new, finer grey hairline** gathers in the Vail corner; Sella stands over it with a broom and will not sweep.

**Ch 14 (Days 61–67).** The Slip Brook goes over itself behind the racks with a weight nobody names; the yard calls it weather. Jorra brings dust past his bent finger and leads at the damper. Three doors down Alda writes what she will not leave lying and turns it toward a floorboard: the hidden record grows **from five pages to six**, unread, and T023 pays once. The **pale river stone is gone** from the wool fold on the abandoned bed and only the hollow remains, unmentioned. A rack pot answers *nearly full* a third time, beside Ilmen 6 and Ilmen 23, advanced and not paid; six hearings stay six, no seventh. Ice honeyed through, current in veins, mud in the well rope, early Sill note, weak board untrodden. Nothing becomes an accident.

**Ch 15 (Days 68–74).** The year's flood comes up as a thin brown sheet; the district calls it weather and keeps using the steps. He hears a whole quarter under strain --- many small failures inside one slow, certain one --- and files it without a word. At the Cross water sits on the second step; Berr reads one in twelve, the true subtraction goes over the basket, the number arrives nowhere. The water goes around both lines and stops short; a neighbour says the house was saved and Sella does not. Line, water and new grey go on three separate trays, with the fact nobody said: a thing left where it lies may still be kept. Below the sill a child's foot stays dark --- the cold to one woman, weather to the yard; the surgeon's reading works and buys nothing. Orra watches from the flood's edge and gets no answer. The weak board is gone or trodden flat and no fall is made of it.

**Ch 16 (Days 75–77).** Mud takes a boot and gives it back slowly; the yard sweeps around the two lines and the rack-space, Sella telling the broom to go around before anybody. At the well a foundling in cast-offs finishes a kiln-hand's sentence and makes the yard laugh; when a pot is set down his head goes a half-beat after the boy's, then stops; he rubs the green sliver at his neck and cannot say why. A man from Slate Row gives him a warm loaf that goes into a pocket holding no slate; the boy does not call it a sale, and the shape gathers interest in an account he cannot read. At the Fishstairs the steps come back four, four and an out-of-line landing nobody makes a fall of; the clerk is still set. Mira shows both cords; Alda covers her sign and keeps her sixth slate face-down; Jorra names neither man in the back room and leads at the damper.

**Ch 17 (Days 78–84).** The lean week comes in on mud holding a boot-print; the house keeps its days badly, the boy keeps seventy-eight through eighty-four. At a thinner Account-day Berr reads one in twelve and takes himself out of his sentence; the bell goes four, four and a gap. The steps come back out of line, walked over without a fall; the clerk is still set; Mira shows both cords. Alda covers the sign with a dead woman's date and says *warm and breathing*; two private records stand unread in one room. The weak board is gone; ice and hairlines are *the thaw that was*. A **second man from Greybank** retells the shut-room story, eighteen leagues east and dated; the boy files it beside the first without letting them touch --- nose-breath against throat-breath, both saying *not the war's hand* --- and joins neither to a kiln.

**Ch 18 (Days 85–90).** Silt goes out on the mud it came in on; the boy keeps eighty-five through ninety into Seed, nobody telling his age. Tarn tells the yard where his knife is, six counted, the seventh nearly gone: *The next I don't count.* Jorra brings grey dust, tells a pot it will do, leads at the damper and is heard at the wheel on the last day. Alda covers her unaware sign with the date, touches her tin unopened; her sixth slate stays face-down. Mira answers *Ask the river* and shows the second cord; Berr reads one in twelve on the last market; the foundling's head goes half a beat late toward a pot. No third telling of the shut room comes. Seed opens on mud and the first work is sweeping; one crust crosses into the planting month with its empty place still unnamed.

**Ch 19 (Days 91–93).** The strips east of Low Kiln become worked ground: seed goes out of uncounted fists, and Berr says a number goes into a slate, not a strip, the levy called but not by him. At the well Sella leaves the row's end unfigured and sends the questioner to the pot: *the house has said it*. Jorra comes through the finger's-width-open door with grey dust past his bent finger and leads at the damper; the boy reads the iron as the bite that holds a vessel whole and can take a hand off a man, with no Grathic word for both --- worth nothing. Tarn sweeps the collector's road. Mira shows both cords; the steps lie out of line; the clerk sits; the weak board is gone or trodden flat and nobody has looked. Alda covers the sign with a dead woman's date and keeps her tin and sixth page shut.

**Ch 20 (Days 94–100).** The planting becomes waiting: a fist lets seed out and comes back with chaff, read as a chart closed with a signature, worth nothing. On Seed 6 the shelf stands with **two** empty places --- one old, one new with no mark --- while the fold keeps one crust and one gap in a room that does not touch it. Jorra gives the back-room men no name and leads at the damper; Mira shows her second cord; Alda covers the sign with a dead woman's date. On Seed 9 a child's foot stays dark --- the cold to one woman, weather to the yard --- and a foundling turns half a beat late to a pot. On Account-day Berr reads one in twelve twice, the bell goes four, four and a gap, and a soft-handed man hands a figure to a slate --- filed apart, not a sale. On Seed 11 the boy is one hundred days old and nobody says so. River's note through the door, fire's gap through the floor, in two places as for a hundred days.

**Turns and changes.** No power gained or spent: still cracked, unranked, below Thin, without speech, Grathic or a hand to lift. What changed is the record. Crust (Day 46), gull's coal (Day 45), stone (Day 64) and weak board are subtractions entered in no account; a sixth hidden note was written on Day 64, so T023 paid once and must not be paid again; a second and third *nearly full* answer were filed advanced and not paid (Ch 12, Ch 14); new grey (Ch 13), flood (Ch 15), second shut-room telling (Ch 17), damper's bite (Ch 19–20), shelf gap (Ch 20) and cold foot (Ch 15 onward) went onto separate trays. The hundredth day was kept, not paid. Orra's vigil held; Sella's kindness stayed a wall; Tarn's seventh year held; Jorra's sentence stayed sealed; Alda's lie curdled past correction; Dessa stayed dead, uncollected, with no collector. New threads: T091 (Ch 11), T092 (Ch 13), T093 (Ch 15), T094 and T095 (Ch 17), T096 (Ch 18); T083 planted in Ch 11, T033/T034 in Ch 16, T062 in Ch 20. Nothing closed; the count crossed into Seed 12 unspent.
