# Digest — Chapters 71–80 (private)

*Chapters 71–80, Days 264–293, Smoke 25, Fifthday, to Frost 24, Fourthday, Y.H. 813. From the Chronicle and Continuity sections of `book/chapters/ch-0071`–`ch-0080/summary.md`; summaries and `memory/timeline.md` hold the day-by-day texture. Thirty days lived, each with its own `\daymark` and two scenes. Nothing was opened, named, called, paid, joined or ended: no thread opened, paid or closed, the ledger at 101 (66 planted, 34 open, 1 paid — T087), no death, and no power, rank or experiment.*

## The house and the boy

Smoke went out without a boundary and Frost came on in its own light; neither of the district's two month-names was set right or left wrong. Corin was carried everywhere at the half-pace, set on his bed under the Vail roof at the going of the light and never in the Debt Loft, with one stiff brown-edged crust two inches from his ear, its empty place unnamed and no third, the frozen thread unwiped because nobody had ever been instructed about it, cold stopping at cheek, temple and wrist as at the end of a line written out for it, the afterdrop waiting past a warming nobody would perform. He wanted the crust lifted and a number spoken by somebody who would know what a number counted; got neither and could not ask. The surgeon kept the inventory; the infant kept the wanting, the second louder, and the checking of one by the other was the work, not a virtue.

Sella went to the fire before the basket each morning and going of the light, turned the coals with a broken pot, said *The house will keep it* where another woman would say *I*, kept her finger above the charcoal so the tally stayed unread, moved her lips along a two-gapped row without a figure, set the pot rim two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat, and instructed fire, wool, pot, broom and running water, never the basket. Tarn kept six checkable and a seventh at the knife's lip beside Garl's unforged collector sentence, said *Yes, house* to early and late thaw while wanting the late because it puts off paying, and stopped the ear-sentence at the calling word on all three days of every chapter — a remembered **ninth** stopping, no tenth before the seventh year at its very end, Seed 16 breath unspent, loft stone cold and unreconciled by not trying, which is not forgiveness. Jorra parted dusty forearms from clean as a surgeon parts clot from vessel, spoke to wheel and pot before any person, withheld the note he knew three turns ahead, stood his bent finger in the cold damper ahead of Sella's sentence, gave the anonymous back-room men no name, and kept *he's not Vail* sealed and the promise of the boy's name neither given nor withdrawn. Garl stayed in objects, not a story — salt coat, hollow wheel-seat, clean dark line with its finer grey thaw unswept, tile debt worse for being uncalled — his name unspoken from 264 to 293 days, no collector on the clay road.

The hand's honest answers rose from **102** at Day 263 to **162** at Day 293, six each chapter: intakes taken, rhythms carried across intervals, two intakes held apart, bridges laid down, endings made early, letting-go kept whole, held interval beside empty interval. None became a reach, hearing, seventh, payment, power or rule; a round number did not stand up as a doctrine, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood. Three counts in three places did not become one; six hearings stayed beside and never among, heard bell beside told, told figure beside paid. The yard went around seven walked-around places with a look and no number, among them the returning-line place where a hand went in on Seed 15 and did not come out; the house's not-doings stayed apart from the boy's own gaps with a finger between.

## Chapter by chapter

**Ch 71 — Days 264–266, Smoke 25–27.** Sella drew the wool wholly back on the 26th, turned the grain piece by piece for damp and rot, set each down and covered it without a figure; the boy kept the finding that a thing can be handled closely and still enter nothing, and on the 27th kept the not-learning. Ilva at the well answered *Ask the pot what it cost. I keep the count, not the account*, her birch unread and no tenth hand filed. A wrong glaze wind came and went; an ownerless voice gave the row question one further shaping — a report of an asking, not a fresh asking — beside the five askings, still no witness or row named (T101 advanced). Answers **103–108**. Count **266**.

**Ch 72 — Days 267–269, Smoke 28–30.** Ordinary tending on the 28th and 29th; Ilva at the well on the 29th; no voice over the boards on the 29th, the silence a keeping and not a sixth witness. On Smoke 30 Sella set the basket in the moot-post's shade; Berr read *one in twelve*, denied the levy with *your house*, never *I*, and went off with the box locked. Smoke's arithmetic changed a third time and cannot be changed back; Hessa's slate went up unread, the bell went four, four and a gap. The boy kept the finding that three readings are three and not one account. Answers **109–114**. Count **269**.

**Ch 73 — Days 270–272, Frost 1–3.** On the morning of Frost 2 (Day 271), before word or basket, Sella put her hand to the widow's mark for one breath, let go, lifted the clay lid with the back of a hand and tipped the coal under a broken pot; the fire took four with the fifth missing, no slate, payer, lesson or mending. This is the **fourth opening**, after Tallow 1, Ember 1 and Smoke 1; four are four and not a rule, and the mark was untouched at every other hour. Grain turned under wool with no figure; Ilva at the well on Frost 1 kept her count in wood. Answers **115–120**; the held interval and the empty interval lay side by side with a finger between. Count **272**.

**Ch 74 — Days 273–275, Frost 4–6.** On Frost 5 Sella's finger went beneath the shelf's front edge for **two** counted breaths where on Day 166 it stayed for one, then came away without looking, reading or a word; the boy filed the returned beat on the shelf's own place beside the Day 161 fraction, the Day 166 beat and the Tallow 22 flagstone half-check, not a fourth likeness, gift or rule (T100 advanced, unpaid). On Frost 6 the finger stayed on top and the two breaths were not repeated. Answers **121–126**, among them two intakes held apart and an ending made early. Count **275**.

**Ch 75 — Days 276–278, Frost 7–9.** Rime wrote white on the well's north lip; on Frost 8 the fronds did not go at sun-up as they had on Frost 4, and the women called the weather. By Frost 9 the trough had skinned over; Sella lifted a plate of ice clouded with silt with the back of the broken pot and set it on a flat stone out of the path of feet. The rope stiffened, coals were banked an hour earlier, the grain was turned a second time because the stone had gone cold — nothing entered, no figure, cause, mending or payer (T083/T085 advanced, unpaid). Answers **127–132**. Count **278**.

**Ch 76 — Days 279–281, Frost 10–12.** Frost's first Account-day: the arithmetic changed for the month and cannot be changed back. Berr read *one in twelve* into the two hundred, denied the levy with *your house*, never *I*, and went off with Frost's first true subtraction in the locked box, believing he had done a kindness. The reading knew nothing of what it read over — no figure in the basket, nothing for the man carried out on Day 188 or the hand that went in on Seed 15, and no knowledge of the grain's cost, the space between figure and price unsigned. One gesture ran at different scales: thane's finger over figure, runner's hand to slate, clerk's number before eye to buyer, widow's basket at half-pace. Answers **133–138**. Count **281**.

**Ch 77 — Days 282–284, Frost 13–15.** On Frost 14 an unnamed woman at the well spoke to the boards: the runner who carries Berr's slate once carried a figure that named a person she knew — no owner standing, room, hand at the writing, name, figure, confession or cause. The boy filed it beside Hessa's eighth record-first hand with a finger between, a shaping toward the far-off day she must choose whether to read what she has carried, not a hearing, witness, joining or payment and not making her a reader (T080 advanced, unpaid). Ilva answered only the question put; no second shaping came on Frost 15. Answers **139–144**. Count **284**.

**Ch 78 — Days 285–287, Frost 16–18.** Ordinary tending. Ilva drew water on Frost 17, her birch unread. On the 18th the file was turned out on the towel as instruments on a tray — six hearings with no seventh, five askings with no witness, nine stoppings, nine hands, each on its own place with a finger between. One hundred and fifty answers stood beside one hundred and forty-nine; a round number did not stand up as a doctrine, for no payer stood. Answers **145–150**. Count **287**.

**Ch 79 — Days 288–290, Frost 19–21.** An ownerless voice over the boards gave the well-saying a **second shaping** — what a runner carries in a slate that has a name in it — filed beside the first with a finger between, Hessa not made a reader and two shapings not a witness. On Frost 20 the arithmetic changed a second time: Berr sent the figure over the basket once for the moot and once for late households, denied the levy with *It is not called by me*, and went up the road with the box locked; figure read still not figure paid. Hessa carried the slate unread; a cup turned a quarter and a half-turn after a knock were a colour and a shape with no eighth. Answers **151–156**. Count **290**.

**Ch 80 — Days 291–293, Frost 22–24.** Ordinary tending. On Frost 23 an ownerless voice gave the well-saying a **third shaping** — the slate-runner once carried a name the district spent and did not say whose — no owner, room, hand, name, figure, confession or cause. Sella heard it and brought nothing under the roof as lesson, figure, mending, cause or payment; the boy filed it beside the first two with a finger between every pair, on a place of its own beside Hessa's eighth hand. Three shapings did not stand up as a witness, for a witness stands somewhere and can be asked and none stood; it was not a sixth asking of the row. Answers **157–162**, the last setting held and empty intervals side by side. Count **293**. Frost 30 (Day 299) stood six mornings ahead and was not brought in.

## The close

No death, no settled revelation, no relationship or power change. The count stood at **293 exact**, six months and one hundred and thirteen days told by nobody; the fire's fifth unpaid that many days with its payer unnamed, Garl's name unspoken the same number. Three shapings, five askings with one shaping, nine stoppings, nine hands with Ilva's ninth unread, burned slate at four, shut-room tellings at two, three pot sayings with the Smoke 14 note apart, seventh green a colour and seventh shape a shape with no eighth, and stayed frost and plate of ice called weather went forward unchanged and unjoined. T080 advanced; no thread paid. The file lay open on the towel with every gap left gap, the hand back on the one hundred and sixty-second answer, and the river's low note went quiet after it. Forward, not closed.
