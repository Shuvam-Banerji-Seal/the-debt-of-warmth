# Digest — Chapters 51–60 (Days 204–233)

*Ember 25 to Grain 24, Y.H. 813: the kiln month's last week and its third Account-day, then Grain come on without a boundary, its first two Account-days and the year's first harvest, still Arc 1, "The Cold Stone." Day 233: the boy two hundred and thirty-three days old, one crust, three held changes, an inch on a sixth, an ownerless question on a seventh, the count exact. One new thread planted (T101), none paid, nothing joined, named, opened, called or ended.*

**Ch 51 (Days 204–206, Ember 25–27).** The fire misses its fifth with no payer for 204–206 days, the river keeps water over water, the boards keep four nails, four and a knot — three counts in three places, for agreeing them would want a payer and none stands to any. On the second morning a word comes up from Slate Row over the boards and not to the basket: a slate had burned in the night, no owner, room, hand at the flame or reason, the voice going on to the price of barley and the weather. The boy takes it apart as a surgeon takes a wound apart and files it beside Jorra's grey dust and the two shut-room tellings, a hand between the three — a direction arrived nowhere, not a hearing, seventh, stopping, payment, cause or third telling (T035/T031/T065/T095 advanced, unpaid). Jorra grows quieter; *he's not Vail* stays sealed, the promise of the boy's name to the men who burn slates neither given nor withdrawn. Sella comes to the fire before the basket, instructs wool, fire, pot, broom, water and river wind and never the child, keeps her finger from beneath the charcoal and leaves the widow's mark alone — no opening in twenty-six days. Six times the hand is asked: down at once, a small intake twice, a whole breath down with the breath (the old coordination remembered), down before the breath finished; then in the small hours, with no count at Sella's fire to hold against, up toward the river's note and held for the river's own low interval, down on the next low note — a coordination with the **one** rhythm through the door, filed on the sixth place, no payer named. Count 206.

**Ch 52 (Days 207–209, Ember 28–30; third and last Account-day).** The burned-slate word goes around a second time, still not a third telling of the counting room. On Day 209 two hundred lean to the reading block, eight pigs go over the wet boards, the bell goes four, four and a gap. Berr Clay comes up short off the clay road, red cord folded under his coat, wipes his thumb twice, passes a finger over the figure and reads *One in twelve, as the notice says* low into the crowd, the figure twice over the basket; *The levy is called. It is not called by me*, *your house*, never *I*, and off with the box locked and the true subtraction inside, believing he has done a kindness. Hessa carries the slate up unread, the soft-cuffed clerk stays set, and a figure read is still not the figure paid — answering nothing for the man carried out on Day 188. In the small hours, with no breathing number in the house, the hand goes up an inch toward the river's note and comes down **at once** — a miss under the very conditions that gave the holding on Day 206, filed on the sixth place and laid across nothing. Ember goes out without a boundary drawn. Count 209.

**Ch 53 (Days 210–212, Grain 1–3).** Ember gives way to Grain in the dark with no boundary; the firing thins into the ordinary tending of a year begun. The yard goes around the broken birth-slates, the pan's gone ring, the bare trough and the field's empty place, each with a look and a wide step and no number. Cost-questions go to the pot and the weather; a man's number goes back into his coat. Tarn tells the kindling *Six counted. The next I don't count*, brings the ear-sentence to the word and stops; nine stoppings, no tenth before the seventh year at its very end, the Seed 16 breath unspent, no collector. Jorra parts dust from slip, tells a pot *You'll do*, withholds its note from a wrong wind. Six answers, the last through a whole counted breath at Sella's fire and down with it, the old two-rhythm coordination remembered. Count 212.

**Ch 54 (Days 213–215, Grain 4–6).** A kiln-hand up from High Kiln on a wrong wind says the pot that sang before its time last month has not been heard from since: the saying turns from a proverb into the report of one pot's silence, set on the near-full tray beside the hearings and never among them — a direction with no hand, owner, cause or figure (T061/T082 advanced, unpaid). Jorra, who knew that pot's full note three turns before it left his hands, lets the saying stand; no pot is lost, no name given to what ailed the carried man. The sweet edge of glaze goes out on the same wind. On Day 215 the burned-slate word comes a third time, still not a third telling of the counting room. Six answers, the last held through the intake and hold of one of Sella's breaths and down on the letting go. Count 215.

**Ch 55 (Days 216–218, Grain 7–9).** A wrong-wind kiln-hand lets fall a third shaping of the old saying — a pot gone quiet on every row come the thaw — so three likenesses stand on the near-full tray with a finger between them; the boy will not let them stand up and call themselves a rule, for a rule is an account that has found a payer and none stands to any shaping (T061/T082 advanced, unpaid). On Day 218 the burned-slate word comes a fourth time, four tellings of a slate still not a third of the shut room. Jorra grows quieter and the household straightens around the quiet without an order. Six answers, the last held through a counted breath, past the silence after the count, and down on the river's next low note — two rhythms across a wall, remembered, not a reach. Count 218.

**Ch 56 (Days 219–221, Grain 10–12; first Account-day and market).** Sella sets the basket in the moot-post's thin shade; Berr reads *one in twelve* twice over the basket, calls the levy, denies it with *your house*, never *I*, and goes off with the locked box; Hessa carries the slate up unread; the bell goes four, four and a gap. The one change comes as two sightings, each **paid as a sighting only**: a seventh green at the mouth of a silent woman at the pot board (clay in the nail crescents, a pale cord-mark on one hand, sweet lead glaze beside the lip, a cup turned a quarter and set back) and a seventh sighting of the foundling at the market's edge (a man's shoulders on a child's frame, breeches cut at the knee, silt in the wrist creases, a gap where a front tooth had been, a green sliver catching light and going dull, a head a half-beat late toward a set-down pot and stopping). One is a colour, the other a shape on places that do not touch — never Fenna or Ulda in the narration, joined to no ear, cause, name or romance; the sale to the Waiting Room clerk stays years ahead (T050/T051, T033/T034 paid as sightings only, deeper senses unpaid). Six answers; count 221.

**Ch 57 (Days 222–224, Grain 13–15).** The harvest comes in: hands come out of the worked strips with the year's first cut grain, Sella sets it under a fold of wool near the hearth, and nobody gives a figure — arms know weight and not cost, the yard counts in glances and handfuls. With the returning hands the yard goes around the empty place where a hand went in on Seed 15 and did not come out, with a look and a wide step and no number, and he keeps bundle and absence on separate trays (T098 advanced, not paid). Six answers; count 224.

**Ch 58 (Days 225–227, Grain 16–18).** The first grain is taken out of its covering, beaten out on the flagstones with an ash-wood flail, winnowed on the river wind, spread thin to dry, gathered by the handful and poured back under wool — three changes of state and no figure, laid across none of the absences. A fist's number goes back into its sleeve; the yard still goes around the returning-line place (T098 advanced, not paid). Six answers; count 227.

**Ch 59 (Days 228–230, Grain 19–21; second Account-day; T101 planted).** Sella sets the basket in the moot-post's shade; Berr reads *One in twelve, as the notice says*, the figure twice over the basket, goes off with the box locked; Hessa carries the slate up unread; the bell goes four, four and a gap; Grain's arithmetic changes for the second time. Then over the boards a voice with no owner, room, hand at the kiln or cause asks which row the pot went quiet on; the weather answers and the price of barley after it, and the voice goes off up the terrace while the row stands where it stood. The boy files the question on a **seventh place of its own**, a finger between it and the pot sayings, the burned-slate telling, the two shut-room tellings, the six hearings and the count — not a fourth shaping, hearing, seventh, cause, name or fifth telling of the slate, but a seed for the far-off day a witness stands somewhere and can be asked. **T101 planted and unpaid**; the ledger rises to 101 (66 planted, 34 open, 1 paid). Six answers, the thirty-sixth a remembered two-rhythm coordination; count 230.

**Ch 60 (Days 231–233, Grain 22–24).** Sella turns the dressed grain under its wool for damp, spreads it thin and gathers it back by the handful — changed in state twice more, counted in glances and handfuls, still unfigured. In the second afternoon the ownerless voice asks the row question a **second** time, and the weather and the price of barley answer again; the boy sets the second asking on the seventh place beside the first with a finger between the two. **Two askings are not one witness** — a witness stands somewhere and can be asked, and none does (T101 advanced, not paid). Six answers, the thirty-seventh through the forty-second, the last held two rhythms across the wall — through one of Sella's counted breaths, past the silence after her count, and down on the river's next low note, remembered, not a change made again. Count 233.

**Turns and changes.** No death occurs: Dessa stays dead since Day 38, uncollected and unnamed, and the man carried out on Day 188 went up breathing with no fate invented. No revelation the house would enter, no lie told, no debt paid. No power gained: Corin stays cracked, unranked below Thin, mute, unable to close two inches or lift the crust; the inch is a physical answer, not a gift. No relationship changes: the equality holds where it moved, the two silences unjoined and the loan uncalled; Sella's instruction grammar, *the house* and the half-pace, Tarn's sixth and seventh, Jorra's sealed *he's not Vail* and ungiven promise stand as they stood. T101 is the first new thread since T100; T050/T051 and T033/T034 pay seventh sightings only, T098 advances with the harvest. Three held changes, the loft scrape, the pale line and the held inch stay on separate places; six hearings, three near-full answers, three pot sayings, four burned-slate tellings, two shut-room tellings, nine stoppings, the Seed 16 breath and the uncalled loan hold at their old counts. The count crosses 203 to 233 exact; the widow's mark is untouched for fifty-three days; the old life stays offstage. Forward, not closed.
