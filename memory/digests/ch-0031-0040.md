# Digest — Chapters 31–40 (Days 132–169)

*Reed 13 to Tallow 20, Y.H. 813: the reed month's middle tend and last weeks, then the firing month's first three working weeks, still Arc 1, "The Cold Stone." Day 169: the boy one hundred and sixty-nine days old, one crust, two shelf gaps, a charcoal tally under that shelf, the count exact against a house that keeps it badly. Nothing joined, paid, named, opened, called or ended.*

**Ch 31 (Days 132–134, Reed 13–15).** Bundles are judged by the arm that carried them; a cost asked as a direction is answered with weather and river. Sella Vail carries the boy at the half-pace from every open slate, instructs wool and river wind, never the basket, and goes around the clean dark line, the finer grey called the thaw, the rack space with its broken birth-slates and the place where a hand went in on Seed 15 and did not come out. Under the loaf shelf she reaches a finger beneath the board where the charcoal tally lies, without looking or a word, on Days 132 and 134 (T100 carried). At the Weir a reed-cutter ties one knot with his teeth and puts his grey cord inside his coat: *You can ask the cord* --- *No more can the Court* (T099 carried). Orra Weir gives a date where a figure is asked; Mira Silt answers a thin run with her own answer and keeps her thumb to a second cord twisted twice. Before first grey men go low into the finger's-width-open back room and get no name. Jorra Vail comes through with grey dust past the bent finger, tells a pot *You'll do* and sets that finger in the cold damper ahead of Sella's unfinished sentence. Tarn takes the broom only after instruction, wants the late thaw because it puts off the paying, and on Day 133 brings the sentence about an ear worth more than the debt a **fourth** time to the word that would call the loan, no figure, no name, drawing the breath he does not spend; the boy files a fourth interval, an object and not a payment. At the Cross a silent woman turns a cup a quarter: the fourth green at a mouth, the fifth shape with Berr Clay's glaze, a colour joined to no ear and never Fenna. Berr pays two marks short; the dark foot stays the cold to one woman and weather to the yard. Six hearings, no seventh, four stoppings, 134 exact.

**Ch 32 (Days 135–137, Reed 16–18).** The reed month enters its last third; the same arithmetic holds in arms and weather, the same places gone around with no number. Sella's finger goes beneath the shelf again on Day 135; the reed-cutter straightens his cord on the blessing stone on Day 137, ties one low with his teeth, bites the end off. The greens stay at four and no fifth comes to make the count even. On Day 136 Tarn brings the ear-sentence a **fifth** time to the calling word and stops, breath unspent; the boy files a fifth stopping beside four, five likenesses not suffered to stand up as a doctrine, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer. Jorra's finger leads at the damper; a High Kiln kiln-master's word about a pot singing early arrives at no figure. Count 137.

**Ch 33 (Days 138–140, Reed 19–21; second Account-day on Reed 20).** The last bundles are judged by the arm that carried them. On Day 139 Berr comes up short under the moot-post, wipes his thumb twice, reads *one in twelve, as the notice says*, calls the levy and denies it with *your house*, never *I*, and goes off with the true subtraction locked in his box; the figure read is not the figure paid. Across the square a silent woman turns a cup a quarter: the **fifth** green at a mouth and the sixth shape with Berr's glaze, unnamed, silent and never Fenna. That night Tarn brings the ear-sentence a **sixth** time (Reed 20) and stops; the boy files a sixth interval. Men go low into the back room on Day 140; Jorra's dust and damper finger are unchanged. Count 140.

**Ch 34 (Days 141–143, Reed 22–24).** A neighbour under the Vail roof names the shelf's old gap and the empty place at the boy's ear under one appetite in a single breath, not seeing she has made two rooms one absence: a further shaping of T097, on its own place and not paid. Sella sends her to the pot and reaches beneath the shelf. On Day 142 Tarn tells the kindling *Six counted. The next I don't count* and brings the ear-sentence a **seventh** time (Reed 23) to the word, stops with no figure or name, draws his Seed 16 breath and does not spend it; the boy files a seventh stopping and will not let seven likenesses call themselves a doctrine. At the Cross Berr reads and denies again; downriver the reed-cutter, Orra and Mira keep their private records unread. Count 143.

**Ch 35 (Days 144–146, Reed 25–27).** The reed month goes out without a boundary drawn. On Day 145 Tarn tells the kindling *Six counted. The next I don't count* and brings the ear-sentence an **eighth** time (Reed 26) to the word and stops. Men go low into the back room; Jorra's dust goes past the bent finger, a pot's full note is withheld from a High Kiln master and the damper's iron keeps its two bites joined to no kiln. On Day 146 Alda lays her birth-slates before the fold, crosses two fingers at the flower unknowing and covers the sign with the date she gave Dessa, *Stone seven. The account went up on the eighth*; *Warm and breathing* stays exact and not true, six pages lie toward a floorboard and both tins stay shut. Count 146.

**Ch 36 (Days 147–149, Reed 28–30; third Account-day and last market).** On Day 148 Tarn brings the ear-sentence a **ninth** time (Reed 29) to the word and stops; the boy files a ninth stopping and holds nine likenesses apart. At the last market on Day 149 every withholding stands together and none is read: Berr reads and denies with *your house*, pays two marks short and goes off with the box locked; a silent woman turns a cup a quarter, a **sixth** green joined to no ear; at the market's edge the foundling's head turns half a beat late and stops, a **sixth** shape with no loaf, no slate and no sale. The bell goes four, four and a gap, the heard bell beside the told; the soft-cuffed clerk stays set for a measure nobody called. Count 149, the month out with no boundary.

**Ch 37 (Days 150–152, Tallow 1–3).** The calendar reconciliation settles these as **Tallow**, not Smoke, by the binding rule, though the district calls Tallow the firing month. The kilns are brought up for the year's last working season and the danger changes address to the kiln's breath in fours with a gap, hot clay under tallow, slate dust ground small as meal and the sweetish edge of lead glaze on a wrong wind. The first meaningful change is a real **opening**: for the first firing Sella puts her hand to the widow's mark *before* the kiln mouth, holds it one breath, lets go and does not touch it again --- the rule held exactly after a hundred and forty-nine days of going around. The shallow clay pan that stood out through Reed is gone without scrape or word; its round place is held on its own tray apart from the shelf's gaps, the fold's crust, the field's empty place, the sixth green and the sixth shape. No third gap comes, no fourth likeness is made a cause. Tarn tells the kindling *Six counted. The next I don't count* and the ear-sentence comes to the word on Day 152, but **no tenth stopping stands up** before the seventh year has been earned. Count 152.

**Ch 38 (Days 153–159, Tallow 4–10; first Account-day of Tallow).** A new named character arrives: **Hessa Rale** of Slate Row, who carries Berr's narrow slate edge-on between Cross and Greybank. Her hand goes to the slate before her eyes to the yard; *I don't read it. I carry it. There's a difference, and the road doesn't care which*; *Cross to Greybank. Reading goes up before noon.* The boy files the order of reaching as an **eighth record-first hand** on a place of its own (T080 advanced, not paid); she is a runner, not a reader, and no figure comes off the slate. The first Account-day of Tallow goes up in Berr's locked box with the printed figure unchallenged and the true subtraction inside. A kiln-mouth woman speaks sideways of the Mud End man taken in his sleep; nobody makes a lesson. Count 159.

**Ch 39 (Days 160–162, Tallow 11–13).** The change is a fraction, not a settlement. On Day 161, for the first time under the Vail roof, Jorra's bent finger goes to the cold damper and **stops** until Sella has finished *It wants feeding*, then moves the tongue a hair's breadth and leaves it standing. The household straightens around a sentence allowed to finish and nobody speaks of the waiting; the boy files it on a place of its own as a change held and unspoken --- not a reconciliation, not a hearing, not a payment, no payer named and no slate entered --- apart from Tarn's unchecking stroke and the nine stoppings. Tarn's stroke goes on without the half-check; the loan stays uncalled. Count 162.

**Ch 40 (Days 163–169, Tallow 14–20; second Account-day, passage chapter at the seven-day cap).** On Day 164 Jorra's finger goes to the damper again and waits out Sella's sentence, so the fraction is held and not repeated as a new change. On Day 166 Sella, standing with her back to the basket, reaches a finger **beneath** the loaf shelf and **holds it for one counted breath** where the charcoal tally lies, without looking, reading, copying, a word or a return to the shelf that night. The boy files the held beat on a place of its own and keeps it from standing up with the Day 161 fraction and the older intervals into a rule, for a rule is an account that has found a payer. Downriver the reed-cutter's cord, Orra's date and Mira's second twist are carried unread. On Day 169 Berr reads one in twelve on the second Account-day, denies the calling with *your house*, and goes off with the box locked; Hessa goes up the clay road with the slate edge-on and her figure unread. Kiln, dust, the withheld singing pot, the Mud End man, the fire's unnamed fifth and the charcoal all gather interest unpaid.

**Turns and changes.** No death occurs in these chapters; Dessa stays dead since Day 38, uncollected and unnamed to the boy. No power gained or spent: still cracked, low-capacity, unranked below Thin, mute, unable to read Grathic or reach two paces. No new thread is opened and none is paid --- the tally stays one hundred (T001–T100), with T087 still the only paid thread. T036/T076 advances through the **fourth to ninth stoppings** (Reed 14, 17, 20, 23, 26, 29) and then holds at nine; T050/T051 gathers three greens (six at mouths, seven shapes with Berr's, never Fenna); T097 gains a Day 141 shaping; T098–T100 are carried unread; T080 advances with Hessa's eighth hand; T062/T035/T031 and T100 gain the held fraction and held beat, kept apart so no likeness makes a rule. The count crosses 132 to 169 exact and unspent. Six hearings stay six, three near-full answers advanced, the word forty unspoken, the cold foot the cold to one woman and weather to the yard, the foundling a shape with no sale, Marra out of Sill, the old life offstage without a `\memory{}` tag. Forward, not closed.
