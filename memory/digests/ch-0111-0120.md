# Digest of Chapters 111–120 (private)

*Consolidated after Chapter 120. Days 384–413, Stone 25 through Ilmen 19, Y.H. 813 turning to 814. Every day was lived in its own `\daymark` and two full scenes; the chapter Chronicles and `memory/timeline.md` hold the detail.*

These ten chapters carry the saga out of its first year without a figure standing up as an account. Corin passes from 384 days old (six months, 204 days) to 413 (six months, 233 days), told by nobody; answers go from 342 to 402, two a day, each an object and not a power. Dessa Silt stays dead and uncollected; Garl Vail stays absent, his name unspoken as many days as the fire's unpaid fifth. No rank, power, experiment, sale or bond comes near the basket. The 101 threads stand unchanged at 66 planted, 34 open, 1 paid (T087).

**Ch 111 — Days 384–386, Stone 25–27.** The year's last working week runs out. **Change (T097/T060/T086, advanced, NOT paid):** the keeping at the fold is found where put a tenth, eleventh and twelfth time — wool folded once in the crust's empty place, no second fold, third crust, name or payer. These add no placing: a second placing makes a habit and a third a way, both wanting a payer where none stands. Ilva drew at the well on Stone 26, ninth hand; Tarn kept six and the seventh, ninth stopping with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end, loan uncalled; Jorra's finger went into the damper ahead of an unfinished sentence and out without the extra holding. Answers 343–348; count 386.

**Ch 112 — Days 387–389, Stone 28–30.** **Change (T015/T041/T047, advanced, NOT paid):** Stone 30, the year's last Account-day and market. Berr Clay wiped his thumb twice, read *One in twelve, as the notice says*, sent the figure twice over the basket, called the levy and denied it with *your house*, never *I*, and went off with the box locked and Stone's last subtraction inside, believing he had done a kindness. **Twelve arithmetics stand behind one unsigned figure** — three Frost, three Bone, three Ash, Stone's first, second and last — twelve and not one account, figure read not figure paid, no signature, no doctrine; the subtractions ride in his box and the boy has no key. Hessa carried the slate up unread; bell four, four and a gap. Answers 349–354; count 389. The Five stand beyond.

**Ch 113 — Days 390–392, Kindling, Weighing, the Long Night.** The first three of the Five — names, not tend-days, no market. On Kindling the year's fire came up the Clay Yard in a clay dish, hearth to hearth before first light, source nobody names or prices. A collector took the first measure: hand to slate before eyes to bed, *The measure is banked. The account stands. The return is recorded.* His ordinary writing hand, no pause, went on its own tray — not Marra, not the Court-table, Fishstairs or Drawing collectors, no tenth — and the reason planted against him: he keeps the world alive by taking what is going. On Weighing a clean voice in the Cross grammar wearing a temple's clothes announced a whole return for somebody absent, said the name once, left a silence the household would not fill, and took the speaker out of his own sentences. Tarn's seventh year ended with the loan uncalled; Jorra's promise stayed ungiven. On the Long Night no fire after dusk, Sella banked the coals, the told bell went four, four and a gap beside the heard, and neighbours told the Return with gaps shaped like vessels' intervals, one leaving the name empty. **For two sentences the grammar broke** — *The page over the speakers says a name. The white room does not look up, and his hands stay in the field.* — then fell back: the *form* of the announcement, not the unsent call to Nina (paid Day 38, not paid again), paying nothing. Answers 355–360; count 392.

**Ch 114 — Days 393–395, the Remembering, the Last Day, Ilmen 1, Y.H. 814.** The last two of the Five and the turn of the year, nothing signed. Names of the dead went door to door; one went among them and did not come under the Vail roof — Garl's, unspoken its 393rd day. On the Last Day bread was baked and the grey left where it lay, thickening where no broom had gone in two years; a neighbour called it *dealt with* because it would burn in the morning, Sella did not, and the boy kept the called-gone grey beside the left-in-place ridge with a finger between. Tarn passed his hand over blade and sill and away and said *Six counted. The next I don't count* — term ended, loan uncalled; Jorra took his finger from the damper without the extra holding; neither promise given. On Ilmen 1 the yard burnt its grey and called it gone, Sella told the coals it was the thaw, lifted the pot with the hairline showing and did not sweep the ridge. Answers 361–366; count 395. No present-tense break or `\memory{}` tag.

**Ch 115 — Days 396–398, Ilmen 2–4.** Tarn stood at the end of an ended term, six and seventh, ear-sentence stopping at the word that would call the loan. On Ilmen 3 a wrong wind off High Kiln brought the sweet edge of lead glaze over the boards, and costs came and went as weather. **Change (T090/T080, T036/T076/T006, advanced, NOT paid):** word came up that the soft-cuffed clerk was still set at the out-of-line landing below the north bridge, slate turned inward for a measure nobody called — seen by nobody under the roof, no witness. The boy filed the order of reaching on a place of its own, no tenth hand; Tarn's head went a degree toward the landing and back to the broom without a word. Answers 367–372; count 398.

**Ch 116 — Days 399–401, Ilmen 5–7.** **Change (T080/T090, T062/T035/T031, T036/T076/T006, advanced, NOT paid):** on Ilmen 6, before first grey, men unnamed went low into the room off the wheel-shed and Jorra came through with grey slate dust white in a fist-crease; then a man from Slate Row whose name was on no roll stood at the yard's edge with soft hands and a patient face. Nobody asked, no loaf passed, no word carried. The boy filed the face beside the older hands, made no tenth of it, called the waiting no sale and the man no witness, and kept the face, Jorra's promise and Tarn's degree on three places with a finger between each pair. On Ilmen 7 the house kept its days badly; Tarn and Jorra offstage. Answers 373–378; count 401.

**Ch 117 — Days 402–404, Ilmen 8–10.** A wrong wind off High Kiln on the ninth was filed as weather. **Change (T015/T041/T047, advanced, NOT paid):** on Ilmen 10, the year's first Account-day, Sella set the basket in the moot-post's shade and stood half a pace off. Berr Clay, red cord folded, wiped his thumb twice, passed a finger over the figure through his sleeve and read low *One in twelve, as the notice says*; he sent it twice over the basket, called the levy and denied it with *your house*, never *I*, said low to the block that kindness was entered, and went off with the box locked. The district's arithmetic began again and cannot be undone. The boy read it as the first of a fresh count and **not a thirteenth** — one reading not an account, a reset not a signing, figure read not figure paid, no signature, the twelve old findings not laid across it, for paper is not a payer. Hessa went up unread; bell four, four and a gap. Answers 379–384; count 404.

**Ch 118 — Days 405–407, Ilmen 11–13.** **Change (T080, advanced, NOT paid):** on Ilmen 12 a neighbour at the yard well said once that the woman who carries the slate had once carried a figure that named a person she knew — no owner standing, no name, no figure, nobody to ask. The boy filed it as a **fifth shaping of the well-saying** on its own place, a finger between it and Hessa's eighth hand, the road saying and the four directions; not a witness, confession, reader or sixth shaping, and no sixth came due. Ilva drew beside it, strip unread, answering *Ask the pot what it cost. I keep the count, not the account*, expressly not the speaker. Answers 385–390; count 407.

**Ch 119 — Days 408–410, Ilmen 14–16.** The man from Slate Row stood again at the yard's edge on the fourteenth, face filed and not counted. **Change (T090/T080, advanced, NOT paid):** on the fifteenth word travelled the yard that the soft-cuffed clerk below the north bridge had entered a figure on his inward slate before looking up to find the man who gave it — hand to slate, eye to person after, half-beat in the gap, a report and not a sighting. The boy filed it on a place of its own with a finger between it and Tarn's degree and another between it and the face at the edge — the next reaching toward the loan that can hear, expressly **not** the calling of it. Ilva offstage, ninth hand nine; Hessa's eighth stayed eighth. Answers 391–396; count 410.

**Ch 120 — Days 411–413, Ilmen 17–19.** The house kept its days badly, loaves pushed a finger's width and stopped. **Change (T036/T076/T006, advanced, NOT paid):** on the afternoon of the eighteenth Tarn's hand went over blade and sill as a man puts a tool back and came away with no breath held and **no word to the wood**; said on the seventeenth and again on the nineteenth, the silence stood as a change found where put, not a custom — not a tenth stopping, payment, hearing or rule. The checking was the work; the sentence never the work. Second change, of weather (T083/T085/T092, T009/T013/T092, advanced, NOT paid): on the nineteenth air less cold came off the river, the sour tang of Ash thinned at the Vail corner and the river's skin softened at one edge where the low note ran nearer under it; the yard called it weather, Sella told the fire it was the thaw, and the boy filed the softening beside the clean dark line and called-gone grey, no hand or payer, not made again — not a sign, fourth covering, sixth shaping, hearing or payment. Answers 397–402; count 413.

**Turns, deaths, revelations, relationships.** No deaths and no revelation given a figure; each movement was a held object filed on its own place and refused standing as sign, lesson, mending, cause, payment or rule. New objects: the tenth–twelfth findings, the twelfth arithmetic, the year's unnamed fire, the Kindling collector's hand and reason, the clean voice's missing name, the Slate Row face, the first arithmetic of Y.H. 814, the fifth shaping, the clerk's entered figure, Tarn's withheld sentence and the softening under the river's skin. The present-tense break is the form of an announcement from the old life and pays nothing. No relationship changed hands: Jorra's promise ungiven, *he's not Vail* sealed, Garl's name unspoken, Marra still not in Sill.

**Power and threads.** Corin remains a cracked vessel below Thin, unable to roll, stand, speak, read Grathic or close the two inches; counting is an old-life habit, not a gift. Advanced and NOT paid: T097/T060/T086, T015/T041/T047, T080/T090, T036/T076/T006, T062/T035/T031, T083/T085/T092, T009/T013/T092. Held unchanged: six hearings with the seventh place empty and the Drawing's knock outside, nine hands and nine stoppings with no tenth, seven openings with no eighth, burned slate at five, shut-room tellings at two, row at five askings with no witness, well-saying now at **five** shapings, four directions and four rooms held apart, the loan uncalled and the fire's fifth unpaid. No thread opened, paid or closed. Ilmen 20 (Day 414), the month's second Account-day and market, stands one morning beyond the close.
