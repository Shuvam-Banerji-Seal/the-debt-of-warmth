# Digest — Chapters 81–90 (private)

*Chapters 81–90, Days 294–323, Frost 25, Fifthday, to Bone 24, Fourthday, Y.H. 813. From the Chronicle and Continuity sections of `book/chapters/ch-0081`–`ch-0090/summary.md`; the summaries and `memory/timeline.md` hold the day-by-day texture. Thirty days lived, each with its own `\daymark`. No thread opened, paid or closed — the ledger stands at 101 (66 planted, 34 open, 1 paid — T087), unchanged since Chapter 59. No death, revelation, power, rank or experiment. Frost went out and Bone came on without a boundary drawn.*

## The house and the boy

Corin was carried at the half-pace, set on his bed under the Vail roof at the going of the light and never in the Debt Loft. Each morning the body was inventoried in its old order: one stiff brown-edged crust two inches from the ear, its empty place unnamed and no third; the thread frozen in the dry heat and unwiped, for nobody had ever been instructed about it; cold stopping at cheek, temple and wrist-joint as at the end of a line written out for it, the Afterdrop waiting past a warming nobody would perform. He wanted the crust lifted, the thread wiped, a hand on the pot handle and a number spoken by someone who would know what a number counted; got none and could not ask. The surgeon's knowledge — a cold patient cannot clot, a frozen part warmed from outside and slowly — bought nothing at two inches of air. The infant's want was louder; neither won; the checking of one by the other was the work.

Sella went to the fire before the basket, told the coals it was the thaw, turned them with the back of a broken pot, said *The house will keep it* where another woman would say *I*, kept her finger on top of the loaf shelf so the charcoal stayed unread, lifted the twice-mended pot on the thumb where the wire crossed the crack and said both proverbs to the pot and the fire and never to a person, turned the dressed grain beneath the wool with a lighter touch and no figure, and set the pot rim two inches beyond the reaching hand; the instruction never went to the basket. Tarn kept six in the worn thumb-mark and a seventh at the knife's lip beside Garl's unforged collector sentence, said *Six counted. The next I don't count*, and stopped the ear-sentence at the calling word as a **remembered ninth stopping**, no tenth before the seventh year at its very end, Seed 16 breath unspent, the warm loft stone cold and unreconciled by not trying, which is not forgiveness; he wanted the late thaw because it puts off the paying, and no collector came. Jorra parted dusty forearms from clean as a surgeon parts clot from vessel, spoke to wheel and pot before any person, withheld the note a pot would give three turns ahead, stood his bent finger in the cold damper ahead of Sella's sentence, gave the back-room men no name, and kept *he's not Vail* sealed and the promise of the foundling's name neither given nor withdrawn. Garl stayed in objects, not a story, his name unspoken from 294 to 323 days.

The hand's answers rose from **162** at Day 293 to **222** at Day 323, six each chapter: small intakes let go whole on the river's low note. None became a reach, hearing, seventh, stopping, payment, power or rule, and no payer was named to any. Three counts in three places — the fire's four pulls and missing fifth, the river's low over lower, the boards' four nails, four and a knot — did not become one, for agreeing any two would want a payer and none stood. Six hearings stayed beside and never among with the seventh place empty; the heard bell beside the told and the told figure beside the paid. The yard went around seven walked-around places, among them the returning-line place where a hand went in on Seed 15 and did not come out; the house's not-doings stayed apart from his gaps.

## Chapter by chapter

**Ch 81 — Days 294–296, Frost 25–27.** No market, Cross or bell. On Frost 26 Ilva drew at the well; asked the month's cost, her hand went to her sleeve and stayed, the notched birch unread: *Ask the pot what it cost. I keep the count, not the account.* The boy filed her order of reaching as the **ninth** record-first hand beside Hessa's eighth and filed **no tenth** (T080 advanced, unpaid). Answers **163–168**. Count **296**.

**Ch 82 — Days 297–299, Frost 28–30.** Frost's **third arithmetic** went up on the last Account-day: Berr wiped his thumb twice, read *one in twelve* into the two hundred, sent the figure over the basket twice, denied the levy with *your house*, never *I*, and went off with the box locked and the true subtraction inside, believing he had done a kindness. Three Frost arithmetics stood behind one printed figure, three and not one account; the figure read was still not the figure paid, and answered nothing for the covered grain, the carried man or the missing hand. Answers **169–174**. Count **299**.

**Ch 83 — Days 300–302, Bone 1–3.** Bone came on without a boundary; three hundred went over on Bone 1 and was kept as a threshold, spent by nobody. A Cold Acre hunter judged the hare's carrying by the arm, answered the cost with the weather and went off. On Bone 2 (Day 301) Sella touched the widow's mark for one breath before the kiln and tipped the coal under a broken pot: the **fifth opening**, five in five months and not a rule. On Bone 3 the white went off the well's north lip while the yard looked elsewhere; the trough's skin and the silt-clouded plate stayed and were called weather. Answers **175–180**. Count **302**.

**Ch 84 — Days 303–305, Bone 4–6.** On Bone 5 an ownerless voice from Slate Row said over the boards, on the breath of barley and weather, that a slate had been burned again: no owner, room, hand at the flame, reason, name or witness. The boy filed it beside the four earlier tellings with a finger between every pair — a **fifth telling of the burned slate**, still not a third telling of the shut counting room, not a hearing, payment, cause, witness or rule (T035/T031/T065/T095 advanced, unpaid). Jorra grew quieter at the saying; *he's not Vail* stayed sealed. Answers **181–186**. Count **305**.

**Ch 85 — Days 306–308, Bone 7–9.** At the going of the light on Bone 8, while Jorra's finger stood in the cold damper ahead of Sella's sentence, Tarn's broom checked half a beat behind it on the flagstones and went on without a word — the Seed 22 half-check returned changed after a hundred and thirty-six days. Filed on the flagstone's own place beside the Day 161 damper fraction, the Day 166 shelf beat and the Frost 5 returned beat: **four held changes on four places**, not a rule, for a rule is an account that has found a payer and none stood (T062/T035/T031, T036/T076 advanced, unpaid). Not made again on Bone 7 or 9. Answers **187–192**. Count **308**.

**Ch 86 — Days 309–311, Bone 10–12.** Bone's first reading on Bone 10: Berr sent the figure twice over the basket, denied the levy with *It is not called by me* and *your house*, never *I*, and went up the road with the box locked and Bone's first subtraction beside the three of Frost. **Four arithmetics** stood behind one printed figure, four and not one account; the finding stayed on a place of its own with no payer, not laid across the house's not-doings, the covered grain, the four held changes or his gaps. Hessa's slate went up unread; the bell went four, four and a gap. Answers **193–198**. Count **311**.

**Ch 87 — Days 312–314, Bone 13–15.** On Bone 15 the hand, having let its intake go whole, did not come straight down: it **stayed at the inch** for the space of the note's fading, held and not reaching, the two inches to the crust unclosed. The surgeon read a clamp left after bleeding; the infant, a hand still wanting. Both true; neither won; filed as answer **203** on its own place, not made again, for one staying is a keeping and two a habit, and a habit wants a payer (T060/T086 advanced, unpaid). Answers **199–204**. Count **314**.

**Ch 88 — Days 315–317, Bone 16–18.** On Bone 17 the hand let its intake go **before the note had done**, while the low sound was still going under the door. The surgeon read a clamp off before the field had dried; the infant, a hand that gave up too soon. Filed as answer **207**, a second object with a finger between it and the stayed inch; not made again, for one early letting-go is a letting-go and two a way, and both habit and way want a payer and none stood. Answers **205–210**. Count **317**.

**Ch 89 — Days 318–320, Bone 19–21.** Bone's second Account-day: Berr read *one in twelve*, sent the figure twice over the basket, denied the levy with *your house*, never *I*, and went off with the box locked, believing it a kindness. **Five arithmetics of the year** — three of Frost, Bone's first and second — stood behind one printed figure, five and not one account, the figure read still not the figure paid, no signature on the space, a rule made from five wanting a payer where none stood. Ilva's ninth hand was seen again, strip unread, no tenth; Hessa's slate went up unread; the bell went four, four and a gap. Answers **211–216**. Count **320**.

**Ch 90 — Days 321–323, Bone 22–24.** On Bone 22, at the rack end, Sella lifted the wool from the dressed grain, took a small handful and let it fall back through her fingers onto the heap instead of spreading it — said nothing, gave no figure, and did not do it again, her fingers going in and coming out without closing. The surgeon read a dressing's edge lifted and let fall; the infant wanted his crust lifted and got no lifting, both true, neither winning. The boy filed the **handful** on a place of its own with a finger between it and the four held changes and between it and his gaps: not a fifth held change, sign, lesson, mending, cause, sixth arithmetic or rule, no payer standing, kept because it was true and not because it bought anything (T077 advanced, unpaid). Ilva was seen again at the well on Bone 23, her strip unread and no tenth. Answers **217–222**. Count **323**.

## The close

No death, revelation or power change; no debt paid, no promise withdrawn. The count stood at **323 exact**, six months and one hundred and forty-three days told by nobody; the fire's fifth unpaid that number of days with its payer unnamed and Garl's name unspoken the same number. Five arithmetics behind one unsigned printed figure, five readings five and not one account; five openings, five burned-slate tellings, four held changes on four places, two small objects of the hand, one handful, nine hands with Ilva's strip unread and no tenth, nine stoppings, six hearings, five askings of the row with one shaping and no witness, three shapings of the well-saying, two shut-room tellings, three pot sayings with the Smoke 14 note apart, seventh green a colour and seventh shape a shape with no eighth, the stayed frost gone, trough and plate called weather — all forward unchanged and unjoined. T080, T015/T041/T047, T058/T079, T035/T031/T065/T095, T062, T060/T086, T061/T082 and T077 advanced, unpaid; no thread paid. The file lay open with every gap left gap, the hand back on the two hundred and twenty-second answer, and the river's low note went quiet. Bone 30 (Day 329), the month's third and last reading, stood six mornings ahead and was not brought early. Forward, not closed.
