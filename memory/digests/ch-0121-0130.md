# Digest of Chapters 121–130 (private)

*Consolidated after Chapter 130. Days 414–443, Ilmen 20 through Silt 19, Y.H. 814. Every day was lived in its own `\daymark` and two full scenes; the chapter Chronicles and `memory/timeline.md` hold the detail.*

These ten chapters carry the boy from 414 days old (six months, 234 days) to 443 (six months, 263 days), told by nobody; answers go from 403 to 462, two a day, each an object on the sixth place and not a power. The year's arithmetic grows from two readings behind one unsigned figure to four; the ledger grows from 101 to **102 IDs (67 planted, 34 open, 1 paid — T087)**, the new one opened in Ch 121. There are no deaths (Dessa stays dead since Day 38), no revelations given a figure, no rank or power moved, no sale, no bond, no collector for the carried man, no Marra in Sill, no ending. One shape is opened, eight are advanced, none paid. Nothing crosses under the Vail roof as sign, lesson, mending, cause, payment or payer, and the not-crossing is itself kept.

**Ch 121 — Days 414–416, Ilmen 20–22.** Ilmen 20 is the month's second Account-day and market, earned by its morning. Sella sets the basket in the moot-post's shade and stands half a pace off; Berr Clay wipes his thumb twice, reads *One in twelve, as the notice says* twice, calls the levy, denies it with *your house*, never *I*, sends a fist-number to a slate at the watch where it arrives nowhere, says low to the block that kindness is entered, and goes off with the box locked and the second subtraction inside, believing he has done a kindness. **Two readings of the year stand behind one unsigned figure** — two are two and not one account, the first not made a thirteenth by the second, the figure read still not the figure paid; the twelve of Y.H. 813 are not laid across them. Hessa goes up unread; the clerk stays a report; the bell goes four, four and a gap. **T102 is opened and NOT paid:** before first grey on Ilmen 21 a second man from Slate Row goes down the north bridge road with a narrow slate under his arm and the writing turned to his sleeve, no name, errand, interior or word carrying; the boy files the direction and the slate on places of their own beside the first man's face, the clerk's entered figure and the fifth shaping, a finger between every pair. Answers 403–408; count 416; softening under the river's skin called weather; crust old horn, thread unwiped, fire's fifth unpaid and Garl's name unspoken the same number.

**Ch 122 — Days 417–419, Ilmen 23–25.** **T102 advanced, NOT paid:** word comes up once at the yard well, mouth to mouth with nobody standing under it, that a man with a narrow slate asked after a house-name here and was answered with the weather; a report, not a sighting. Ilva draws with her pail against her knee, hand to sleeve before eye to asker, strip unread, ninth hand nine, expressly not the speaker. The boy files the asking beside the direction and the turned slate, the face, the clerk's figure and the fifth shaping, joined to none and not the calling of the loan. A fist-number goes toward the well and back into a sleeve; a woman sent for a pot's cost gets a note, not a price. The corner keeps the called-gone grey beside the left-in-place ridge. Answers 409–414; count 419.

**Ch 123 — Days 420–422, Ilmen 26–28.** **T080 advanced, NOT paid:** at the well a woman brings a figure up out of a shut fist toward Ilva's birch strip; Ilva's hand goes flat over the fold before her eyes go to the asker and her mouth after both, and she answers the question put and not the one behind it — *Ask the pot what it cost. I keep the count, not the account.* The figure looks at the light, goes back into the sleeve and arrives nowhere; *Then it's a stick*, and nobody turns twice. No figure comes off the wood, no reader made. The offer and its return are filed beside Ilva's ninth hand and the fifth shaping, not a sale, tenth hand, sixth shaping or calling of the loan; her fear is advanced and unpaid. Tarn keeps six and the seventh at the lip and lets his hand go over blade and sill and away; Jorra parts dusty from clean as clot from vessel. Answers 415–420; count 422.

**Ch 124 — Days 423–425, Ilmen 29–30, Silt 1.** On Ilmen 30 Berr reads twice again and goes up the road with the third subtraction locked in his box; **three readings stand behind one unsigned figure**, three and not one account. At the market **T050/T051 with T033/T034 advance, NOT paid, years ahead:** a woman with clay in her nail crescents and a pale cord-mark turns a cup a quarter and sets it back, and a foundling in cast-offs with a green sliver at his throat turns his head a half-beat after a pot's note and stops mid-turn — a colour and a shape on separate trays, never Fenna or Ulda, no loaf, slate, sale, word, name or eighth. Silt 1 comes on without a boundary drawn, neither month-name set right. Answers 421–426; count 425.

**Ch 125 — Days 426–428, Silt 2–4.** **T097 advanced, NOT paid:** on Silt 3 a neighbour with a pot reaches past the bare board before her eyes find the basket, traces the shelf's top where the charcoal lies beneath without going under it, then lets her finger go, unseen, toward the empty place at the boy's ear and stop a finger's width short of the fold. In one breath she calls the shelf's old gap a thing the month's turn brought in and the empty place a child's appetite, making two rooms one absence without seeing it. The boy files the near-reaching beside the earlier shapings and the far-off finger: a further shaping, no joining, name, figure, third gap, fourth likeness, reading of the charcoal or payer. Sella weighs a fist of barley and sets it back, saying no number. Answers 427–432; count 428.

**Ch 126 — Days 429–431, Silt 5–7.** Two advances, NOT paid. **T080 with T090/T102:** a word goes around the well that the woman who carries Berr's slate has taken up a second, narrower one, edge-on with the writing to her sleeve, hand to slates before eye to yard and mouth after both; a report and a shape, no name, no figure off either slate, no reader or witness. The runner's reaching comes nearer to a joining than ever and is refused in the same breath. **T083/T085/T092:** at the Vail corner the finer grey called the thaw lies a hair less distinct along one unswept stretch and the river's skin is softer at one edge where the low note runs nearer; found where put, not looked twice, called weather, no sign, fourth covering or payment. Answers 433–438; count 431.

**Ch 127 — Days 432–434, Silt 8–10.** Silt 10 is the month's first Account-day and the year's fourth reading. Berr reads twice, denies the levy with *your house*, sends a fist-number to the watch, says kindness is entered, and goes up the road with the fourth subtraction locked away. **Four readings stand behind one unsigned figure** — four are four and not one account, first not made a thirteenth, second no signature, third no doctrine, fourth no payer, the twelve not laid across the four. Hessa goes up before noon with the slate edge-on and unread, answering the question put and not the one behind it (*I don't read it. I carry it*), not made a reader by four figures. Answers 439–444; count 434.

**Ch 128 — Days 435–437, Silt 11–13.** Two exposures, NOT paid. **T080 with T090/T102:** on Silt 12 a second neighbour, carrying the bucket-round's fear of the figure that went back into the fist, asks whether a count kept in wood will be taken at the next reading; Ilva answers pot and weather and the asking is filed beside the offer and its return, her ninth hand and the fifth shaping — not a second offer, custom, tenth hand, witness, sale or calling of the loan. **T090/T080:** on Silt 13 a man from Slate Row stands at the yard's edge with loose hands, a patient face, mud pale at the toe and dark at the heel; he is asked nothing, says nothing, and gives no loaf, slate, word, threshold, name, errand, interior or witness's standing. Answers 445–450; count 437.

**Ch 129 — Days 438–440, Silt 14–16.** Two advances, NOT paid. **T036/T076/T006 with T061/T082:** Tarn tells the wood *Six counted. The next I don't count* on the fourteenth, withholds the word on the fifteenth so that the space stays a space, and says it again on the sixteenth so the silence is found where put and not made a habit, a tenth stopping, payment, hearing or rule; three sentences are three and not one doctrine, the loan uncalled in an ended term. **T083/T085/T092:** on Silt 16 the corner's finer grey is a hair less distinct along one stretch with the sour tang of Ash, and the river's air is less cold at one edge; both called weather, with no payer and not made again. Answers 451–456; count 440.

**Ch 130 — Days 441–443, Silt 17–19.** Two advances, NOT paid. **T077 with T061/T082:** on Silt 18 a man waiting at the well opens a shut fist not toward the water but toward the clay road going up to High Kiln, looks at the light, shuts the fist and puts it back into his sleeve, the number arriving nowhere; no name, figure, money, room or relation to the boy, and Ilva at the well is expressly not the speaker. The direction is filed beside the fist-to-nowhere arrivals and the offered figure and its return, not a second offer, custom, payment, reader, sale, sixth shaping or calling of the loan. **T083/T085/T092:** on Silt 19 the corner keeps its finer grey a hair less distinct along a **second** unswept stretch and the river air is softer again at one edge; both found where put, not looked twice, called weather, with no hand or payer and not made again. Answers 457–462; count 443.

**Turns, deaths, revelations and relationships.** No death and no revelation given a figure. No relationship changed hands: Jorra's promise stays neither given nor withdrawn and *he's not Vail* sealed, Tarn's loan uncalled in an ended term, Garl's name unspoken and his tile debt uncalled, the mercy-lie uncorrected, no promise made to the boy. The green and the foundling remain sightings, never named; the adult romance stays planted. No power moved: Corin is still a cracked vessel below Thin, unable to roll, stand, speak, read Grathic, reach his crust or close two inches; counting is an old-life habit.

**Threads.** T102 opened in Ch 121; T080, T090, T097, T077, T083/T085, T036/T076/T006, T015/T041/T047 and T050/T051 advanced, none paid. Held unchanged: six hearings with the seventh place empty and the Drawing's knock outside, nine record-first hands and nine stoppings with no tenth, seven openings in seven months with no eighth, burned slate at five, shut-room tellings at two, the row at five askings with one shaping and no witness, the well-saying at five shapings, four ownerless directions and four rooms held apart, the river under its winter skin, the keeping found where put, the fire's fifth unpaid. **Silt 20 (Day 444), the month's second reading and the year's fifth, waits one morning beyond the close** — five will then stand and five will be five and not one account.
