# Digest of Chapters 101–110 (private)

*Consolidated after Chapter 110. Days 354–383, Ash 25 through Stone 24, Y.H. 813. Every day was lived in its own `\daymark` and two full scenes. The chapter Chronicles and `memory/timeline.md` hold the day-by-day detail.*

These ten chapters are one run of ordinary tending days, broken by three Account-day readings, one firing, one unmarked birthday, one Hearth Drawing and four ownerless arrivals. Ash went out and Stone came on without a boundary drawn. Corin passed from 354 days old (six months, 174 days) to 383 (six months, 203 days), told by nobody. Honest answers on the sixth place went from 282 to 342, two a day, each an object and not a power. No death occurred: Dessa Silt stays dead and uncollected, her name and number unspoken. No rank, power, experiment, sale, bond or slate-opening came near the basket. The 101 threads stand unchanged at 66 planted, 34 open, 1 paid (T087).

**Ch 101 — Days 354–356, Ash 25–27.** Sella Vail kept the hearth grammar and the yard went around its seven walked-around places; Tarn checked the Bone 26 thumb's resting and the Ash 14 held breath and found each where put; Jorra's bent finger went into the cold damper ahead of a sentence unfinished. **The one meaningful change (T062/T035/T031 and T061/T082, advanced and NOT paid):** on Ash 26, after the work was done, Jorra's finger stayed in the cold iron a breath longer than the work required — gathered once, not a sixth held object, habit, way, sign, cause, payment or rule, no payer standing; on Ash 27 he took it out and did not repeat it. At the well on Ash 26 Ilva answered *Ask the pot what it cost. I keep the count, not the account*, her strip unread, the **ninth** record-first hand with no tenth and not the speaker of any voice. Answers 283–288; count 356, six months and 176 days.

**Ch 102 — Days 357–359, Ash 28–30.** Ash 30 spent the month's last Account-day: Berr Clay wiped his thumb twice, read *One in twelve, as the notice says* twice over the basket, called the levy and denied it with *your house*, never I, and went off with the box locked and Ash's third subtraction inside. **Nine arithmetics now stand behind one unsigned figure**, nine and not one account, the figure read still not the figure paid, no signature, no doctrine out of nine. Hessa carried the slate toward Greybank unread. Answers 289–294; count 359. Ash went out without a boundary and Stone came on beyond the door. (The summary's "Paid off: the ninth arithmetic, a date" means a planted date earned its own morning, not a thread-status change.)

**Ch 103 — Days 360–362, Stone 1–3.** **The one meaningful change (T083/T085 and T078, advanced and NOT paid):** on the first afternoon the river's note came low under a skin — the Ilm had taken its winter ice bank to bank. A Cold Acre hare-carrier told the woman who asked what the ice would cost to *Ask the weather. It laid it on. It'll take it off*; no slate opened. The boy filed the note beside water-over-water, a covering put on and taken off by the cold, entered nowhere — not a sixth low thing, not a hearing, not a seventh of the full note and not a fourth of the three coverings; found where put on Stone 2 and 3, not made again. Ilva drew at the well on Stone 2, her ninth hand, strip unread. Answers 295–300; count 362 — **three hundred honest answers**, a round number nobody speaks. Six months stood exactly at Stone 1's close.

**Ch 104 — Days 363–365, Stone 4–6.** **The one meaningful change (T058/T079, advanced and NOT paid):** on the morning of Stone 5, before any instruction to the basket, Sella carried him to the kiln mouth, put her left hand to the widow's mark for one breath, lifted the lid with the back of a hand and tipped the coal under a broken pot; the fire took with four pulls and a gap. This was the **seventh opening in seven months** — Tallow 1, Ember 1, Smoke 1, Frost 2, Bone 2, Ash 2, Stone 5 — seven and not a rule, no eighth made due, no lesson or payer named; the mark was not touched again. On the packing bench a pot set down wrong gave its note before its time and a hand swept around it (T061/T082), filed beside Smoke 14's premature note and not made again. Answers 301–306; count 365, a round number no threshold crossed.

**Ch 105 — Days 366–368, Stone 7–9.** **The one meaningful change (T071/T072 with T088, advanced and NOT paid):** the boy's first birthday fell on Stone 7 (Day 366) and was lived as an ordinary tending day — no party, cake, figure, recognition or threshold, no loaf set aside. Stone 8 proved no residue; Stone 9 closed a whole year finished and named by no roof. He kept 366, 367 and 368 exactly. The widow's mark stayed untouched and no eighth opening was due. Answers 307–312; count 368. The recurring figures were offstage; walk-ons brought directions, never figures.

**Ch 106 — Days 369–371, Stone 10–12.** Stone 10 brought the tenth arithmetic: Berr read one in twelve twice over the basket and went off with the box locked and Stone's first subtraction inside; Hessa took the slate up unread; the bell went four, four and a gap. Ilva answered at the well on Stone 11. **On Stone 12 the Hearth Drawing:** Sella set the basket at the edge of the light. The collector, fingertips grey as skimmed milk gone off, set a cold iron tube against the neighbour's forearm by touch; the neighbour gave under it with a small flat knock and a pale round place, not a wound and no blood. A rekindled child was worse on the third day, grey at the fingertips and red at the hairline, while the room called it a graft settling; the mother held a cup of warm milk she never drank. The surgeon knew pink returning is the surface still cold and not the core and could not speak, reach or warm. The flat knock stayed outside the six hearings and the seventh place stayed empty. **Four rooms keep the same gap in their count** — the birth room, the Vail kiln-house, the market-day slate room and the Drawing house — held apart with a finger between every pair, four likenesses and not one account (T081 advanced). Answers 313–318; count 371.

**Ch 107 — Days 372–374, Stone 13–15.** Three deliberately ordinary days. Ilva drew at the well on Stone 13; Hessa came **down** the road on Stone 14 with the writing to her sleeve, unread. **The one meaningful change (T097 with T060/T086, advanced and NOT paid):** on the evening of Stone 15, setting him on his bed and righting the fold, Sella tucked the wool into the empty place beside the crust — no word, no third crust, no name, no look toward his face, her hand resting the half-beat longer than the setting needed. The boy filed it beside the crust's gap, the shelf's two gaps, the gone pan's ring and the field's empty place: not a joining, gift, payment, sixth held object, habit or rule, gathered once and not made again. Earlier days held the place untouched so this would be a change, not a habit. Answers 319–324; count 374.

**Ch 108 — Days 375–377, Stone 16–18.** **The one test (T097 with T060/T086, advanced and NOT paid):** the wool was found folded once in the empty place on three mornings and at three goings of the light, changed by nobody. Finding is not placing: a second placing would make a habit and a third a way, and both want a payer where none stands. Ilva drew at the well on Stone 16, her ninth hand; Hessa went **up** the road on Stone 17, unread and not made a reader; Tarn kept six and the seventh and Jorra took his finger from the damper without a second holding. Answers 325–330; count 377.

**Ch 109 — Days 378–380, Stone 19–21.** **The one meaningful change (T015/T041/T047, advanced and NOT paid):** on Stone 20, the month's second Account-day, Berr read one in twelve twice over the basket and went off with the box locked and Stone's second subtraction inside. **Eleven arithmetics stand behind one unsigned figure** — the three of Frost, the first, second and third of Bone, the first, second and third of Ash, Stone's first and Stone's second — eleven and not one account, the figure read still not the figure paid, no signature and no doctrine out of eleven. Hessa carried the slate up unread; a seventh green and a seventh shape stayed sightings with no eighth. The keeping was found where put on Stone 19, 20 and 21 — fourth, fifth and sixth findings; six findings added no seventh placing. Answers 331–336; count 380.

**Ch 110 — Days 381–383, Stone 22–24, the last full working week.** **The one meaningful change (T035/T031/T065/T095 and T101, advanced and NOT paid):** on Stone 23 a fourth ownerless direction came over the boards from Slate Row with nobody standing under it — a room shut against the cold, a slate burned so a count would not go up, the cold and the count going out through the same gap, no room named, no hand at any flame, no figure, no date and nobody to be asked. The boy filed it as a surgeon files a wound, on a place of its own beside the Ash 5 joining and the Ash 17 and Ash 23 directions, a finger between every pair — not a sixth telling of the slate (five), not a third telling of the shut room (two), not a fifth shaping of the well-saying (four), not a hearing, witness, payment or rule, for a rule wants a payer and none stood. It did not repeat on Stone 24. Ilva drew at the well on Stone 22, her ninth hand; Hessa went up the road on Stone 23, her eighth still eight. The keeping was found where put on Stone 22, 23 and 24 — seventh, eighth and ninth findings; nine added no ninth placing. Answers 337–342; count 383. The week ran out with nothing standing up and calling itself an account.

**Turns, deaths, revelations, relationships.** No deaths, no revelation given a figure; every movement was a held object filed on its own place and refused standing up as a sign, lesson, mending, cause, payment or rule. The new objects are Jorra's extra breath in the cold iron, the river under its winter skin, the seventh opening, the setting-down-wrong, the Hearth Drawing and four rooms' shared gap, the keeping at the fold and its nine findings, the ninth, tenth and eleventh arithmetics, and the fourth ownerless direction. Jorra grew quieter after each voice; Sella named nothing; Hessa carried and did not read; Ilva kept her count in wood. No relationship changed hands: Jorra's promise stays ungiven, *he's not Vail* sealed, Garl's name unspoken 383 days, Marra still not in Sill.

**Power and threads.** Corin remains a cracked vessel below Thin, unable to roll, stand, speak, read Grathic or close the two inches; counting is an old-life habit, not a gift. Advanced and NOT paid: T062/T035/T031, T083/T085, T058/T079, T061/T082, T015/T041/T047, T035/T031/T065/T095 and T101, T080, T097/T060/T086. Held unchanged: six hearings with the seventh place empty and the Drawing's knock outside, nine hands and nine stoppings with no tenth, seven openings with no eighth, burned slate at five, shut-room tellings at two, row at five askings with no witness, well-saying at four shapings, four held changes on four places, seven single objects on the sixth place, seven greens and shapes with no eighth, the loan uncalled and the fire's fifth unpaid. Stone 30 (Day 389), the year's last reading and twelfth arithmetic, stands six mornings ahead; the Five stand beyond.
