## The house and the boy

All thirty days were lived under the Vail kiln-house roof in Low Kiln, each with its own `\daymark` and two full scenes. The carried fire gave four pulls and kept the silence where the fifth should stand, its payer unnamed for Days 234–263 (T088); the river through the finger's-width door kept its note of water over water; the boards held four nails, four and a knot. Three counts in three places, never agreed, for agreeing any two would want a payer and none stood (T061/T082). The heard bell's four, four and a gap was kept beside the told bell, the told figure beside the paid.

Corin was carried everywhere, from 234 days, six months and fifty-four days, to 263 days, six months and eighty-three days, nobody saying either figure. One stiff brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from his ear, its empty place unnamed and no third; the drool thread froze unwiped, nobody ever having been instructed about it; cold stopped at cheek, temple and wrist with the afterdrop waiting past a warming nobody would perform. He wanted the crust lifted and a number spoken by somebody who would know what a number counted; got neither and could not ask. No power, rank, experiment or heresy: counting stayed an old-life habit, not a gift. The old life was wholly offstage — no present-tense Chicago break, no `\memory{}` tag, no departure from the boy's eyes.

Sella went to the fire before the basket, instructing wool, fire, pot, broom, water and river wind, never the basket; *the house*, *The house will keep it*, half-pace, jar-glance with hand shut half over nothing, finger on top of the shelf so the charcoal stayed unread, pot handle two inches out with a half-beat and no look at the bed. She set him on his bed, not the Debt Loft, and left the thread frozen. Tarn took the broom only when instructed, said *Yes, house* to early and late thaw, told the wood *Six counted. The next I don't count*, kept six where the house could check and the seventh at the lip with Garl's unforged collector sentence beside the knife. The ear-sentence stopped at the word that would call the loan: the ninth stopping, remembered, no tenth before the seventh year at its very end and still ungiven, Seed 16 breath unspent, loan uncalled, no collector on the clay road. Jorra parted meal-fine slate dust from slip, told a pot *You'll do* and withheld the note he knew three turns ahead, kept the burned slate at four and the shut-room tellings at two, and held his bent finger in the cold damper ahead of Sella's unfinished sentence. Garl was kept in objects, not a story, his name unspoken from 236 to 263 days.

## Chapter by chapter

**Ch 61 — Days 234–236, Grain 25–27.** Grain was turned under wool, spread thin and gathered by the handful, changed in state, given no figure. The ownerless voice asked over the boards **a third time** which row the pot had gone quiet on; a wrong glaze wind answered first, barley's price after, and the voice went up the terrace while the row stood. The boy opened it as a surgeon opens a wound and found no witness: three askings on the seventh place, a finger between each pair (T101 advanced, not paid). Answers **43–48**, the fifth a miss entered without shame, the last a remembered two-rhythm coordination.

**Ch 62 — Days 237–239, Grain 28–30.** The voice asked **a fourth time** on Grain 29; weather and barley answered, no witness stood. On Grain 30, third and last Account-day and market of Grain, Sella set the basket in the moot-post's shade; Berr wiped his thumb twice, read *one in twelve* twice over the basket, denied the levy with *your house*, never *I*, and went off with the box locked. **Ch 66 — Days 249–251, Smoke 10–12.** On Smoke 10, first Account-day and market of the second firing, Berr read one in twelve twice over the basket, once for the moot and once for late households, and went off with the true subtraction locked in his box believing he had done a kindness. **Ch 67 — Days 252–254, Smoke 13–15.** A pot set down wrong gave its high thin note before its time and a woman swept around it; the boy filed the premature note beside the three sayings and made no fourth likeness and no cause. **Ch 68 — Days 255–257, Smoke 16–18.** On Smoke 17 the dressed grain was gathered in for the last time before the second reading, covered under wool away from the hearth on the cool stone at the rack end; the arithmetic to be read over it would not know what it cost (T077 advanced, not paid). **Ch 70 — Days 261–263, Smoke 22–24.** The one meaningful change was small and interior: through Smoke 23's plain work the boy registered that the house keeps itself by **not-doings** — two lines unswept, the mark untouched, the charcoal unread, the fifth pull unsupplied, the loan uncalled, no number spoken over the basket, the crust unlifted. On Smoke 24 glaze came over the boards and out again, a direction arrived nowhere; Sella turned the grain under wool with no figure; Tarn's ear-sentence stopped as a remembered ninth stopping. Ilva came once on Smoke 23, answered *I keep the count, not the account*, and filed no tenth hand. Answers **97–102**: on the last, the little silence after a count in the other room was left whole and empty on its own place beside the held one — an empty object, a keeping and not a miss, not a reach, hearing, seventh, payment, held change, key, power or rule. The count closed at **263** exact, unspent and spoken by nobody; the hand lay back against the fold.
