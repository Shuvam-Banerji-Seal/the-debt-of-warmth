## Chronicle
Three to six paragraphs (250–500 words) for the reader, printed at the end of the chapter in a framed box. Write it as a chronicler would set it down: past tense, beautiful and exact, naming who did what and what it cost. Do not tease the future. Do not reveal secrets the chapter did not reveal.

## Continuity
Private notes for your future self (never printed):
- Exact final moment: where the chapter stops, mid-action or not, and who is present.
- Day and time at the end.
- Where every on-stage character is now, and their condition: wounds, exhaustion, mood.
- Items gained, lost or used; money; powers used and their cost.
- Facts revealed, lies told, promises made, debts incurred.
- Threads advanced or opened, and foreshadowing planted.
- Anything the next chapter must not forget.
