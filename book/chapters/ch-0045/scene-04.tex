The middle hours came on and did not turn into anything a man could set a figure against. \place{the Clay Yard} held the flat dry glare that has no shadow in it to measure by, and the heat came up out of the clay in a fine trembling that set the far racks a finger's width above the ground. Hot clay lay under tallow, and tallow lay under wet wool, and beneath all three the sour mineral breath of the unswept corner, where \lore{Ash} had turned the corner for two years and the new finer grey called \lore{the thaw} lay beside it, neither swept and neither given an instruction. Slate dust was ground small as meal and lay white in the creases of a fist when the fist opened. The sweetish edge of \lore{lead glaze} rode a wrong wind off \place{High Kiln} and went over the basket and on up the yard. \person[Corin]{The boy} turned toward it and got no further. None of it was paid. None of it was given a name that could be carried into a chart.

He lay at the low angle where the underside of \place{the loaf shelf} became a roof, his back to the fire and his face to the boards. He was one hundred and eighty-seven days old and six months and seven days, and nobody under any roof said so. The crust lay two inches from his ear, one stiff brown-edged barley cake gone leathery in the dry heat, with its empty place beside it unnamed and no third. The thread at his mouth had frozen and was unwiped. His head went where it wanted and made the small wet sound when it went too far. He wanted the crust. He wanted the breast. He wanted a number spoken by somebody who would have known what a number counted. He got none of them and had stopped expecting to be told, which was not the same as not wanting it.

\person{Sella Vail} stood with her back to the basket at the shelf and moved her lips along a row standing with two unnamed gaps, the old space at the row's end and the palm's breadth that had come in on the night of Seed six. She gave nobody a figure. Her eye went once to the clay jar standing cold under its glaze and her hand shut half over nothing and came away without touching it, the way a hand closes on a tool it has decided not to lift. Her finger did not go beneath the front edge. The charcoal lay under the board where it had lain since the night she held a finger beneath it for one counted breath, unread, uncounted, uncopied; an afternoon and a woman's back turned were not the same as that night. The held beat stayed where it had been left, on a place of its own, and she gave no instruction to the basket about either the row or the board.

At \place{the hearth}, with the coals banked and the small knife back at the very edge of its sill at \place{the kindling} where a man could check it and find it where it should be, \person[Tarn Vail]{Tarn} told the wood what he told it at every going out of the light. \emph{Six counted. The next I don't count.} Six years stood in the worn thumb-mark where the house could put a finger and verify them, and the seventh stood at the lip where no thumb of the house had gone; beside the knife lay \person{Garl Vail}'s unforged collector sentence, kept with the seventh year and entered in nothing. The breath from Seed sixteen stood unspent at the top of every reach with its small interval where a breath should have gone in and had not. \lore{Ash} was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line and no scrubbing lifted it, and the pale clip-ring at his left wrist held what light there was and gave it back. His stroke went on over \place{the flagstones} and past the shallow bright patch without the half-check; the stones kept the stopped place and were not given it again. He said \emph{Yes, house} to an early thaw and a late, agreed so entirely that no argument could find a foot, and went on wanting the late one because it puts off the paying, though the paying had stood in the ruts since the water went.

The sentence about an ear under this roof being worth more than the debt that holds it came to his teeth and stopped short of the word that would call the loan. No figure came with it and no name. He drew the breath he should have spent at the top of the reach and did not spend it, the way a clamp is left standing on a vessel that will not bear the taking off, and went on with the broom. Nine stoppings stood as objects and not a rule, intervals with no payer named to any, and no tenth stood up before the seventh year was earned. The seventh year pressed nearer its end than it had ever stood and had not given. The loan stayed uncalled. Sella, within hearing, gave the end of her instruction to the pot and asked nothing; the wheel-keeper did not ask about the ear and Tarn did not ask about the room, and the two silences stayed unjoined.

The warm stone lay back in its unmarked clay pot in \place{the Debt Loft} above the shed, two years gone and cold, unreconciled with the debt by not trying, which is not forgiving. From below came no sound now, and the boy kept the remembering on its own place as a remembering and not as a holding done again; he did not stand it up with the Day 161 fraction at the iron, the Day 166 beat beneath the shelf or the Day 171 half-check on the flagstones, because three likenesses in a row will call themselves a truth if a man leaves them standing, and a fourth likeness stood up would call itself a cause and a cause would want a payer under it.

In \place{the Vail wheel-shed} \person{Jorra Vail} kept his dusty forearms and his clean ones on one tray, the dust ground small as meal past the bent little finger and under the nails, white in the creases where a fist had opened, not bone-coloured \lore{slip}. The broken birth-slates lay beneath \place[the drying racks]{the lowest rack} and were entered by nobody. He gave the men who had gone low into \place{the back room} before first grey no name and did not say the room was cold, and the two tellings of a counting room shut in the war stayed two beside the iron with no third. He spoke to the wheel before he spoke to anyone. \emph{It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady,} he said, flat and low, and to the pot under his hands he said \emph{You'll do} in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either. He knew \lore{the full note} that pot would give three turns before it left the wheel and withheld the knowing from the men come up on a wrong wind with the sweet edge of lead glaze at one mouth-corner; the knowing went to the near-full tray beside the hearings and never among them, advanced and not paid, and no pot was lost in the yard.

When Sella's voice went low and unfinished in the middle of a sentence to the fire, Jorra's bent finger went to the cold iron of \lore{the damper} ahead of his hand and waited. He did not look toward her and did not answer her. He held the iron while the sentence found its end and the end of it arrived at the pot, and only then did he move the tongue a hair's breadth and leave the finger standing in the cold. The household straightened around the waiting and nobody spoke of it. It was a remembering of a holding and not a holding made again, with no word in \lore{Grathic} that would carry both mercies of the one piece of iron in a single mouthful, joined to no kiln and entered in no slate. The fraction stayed on its place and was not laid across the Day 166 beat or the Day 171 half-check, and the promise of the foundling's name to the men who burn slates was neither given nor withdrawn.

At the going of the light Sella carried him home up \place{the clay road}, woodsmoke going before her in long low bars out of the chimneys and the river wind coming up behind with the taste of \lore{water over water}, the two passing on the stair of the evening as they had passed every evening of his life without taking each other's colour or spending a word on either. The instruction went to the wool and the wind and never to the basket. The arm that had carried him a hundred and eighty-seven mornings kept the height of the handle, the give of the shoulder and the turn of the wrist at a step's end, a record in muscle that remembered weight and gave no account of cost. At the edge of the yard she went around the pan's gone place on the wall, with no scrape, no crumb and no word; around \place{the trough} at \place[the yard well]{the well}, bare and dark, whose water was called weather; wide around the rack space where the broken birth-slates lay and nobody entered; and around the empty place in the returning line where a hand had gone in on Seed fifteen and had not come out. Each got a look that asked for nothing and found nothing, a wide step and no number and no explanation to the basket.

Under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof she set him on \place{the bed} and not in \place{the Debt Loft}, base first and the weight settled the way a woman sets a vessel that must not rock, put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without once looking at his face, left the thread frozen and unwiped, and let her hand rest a half-beat longer than the setting needed before it went away. She told \lore{the carried fire} it was \lore{the thaw}, turned the coals with the back of a broken pot, said \emph{The house will keep it} where another woman would say \emph{I}, stood a moment with the back of the pot in her hand counting under her breath toward no opening --- the small intake at the top, the hold, the letting go --- pushed coal-black hair back with the wrist that carries the little-door burn so the grey streak showed pale off the coals, set the pot rim out on the board with its handle standing two inches beyond the reaching hand, and did not look toward the bed. \lore{The widow's mark} hung on its cord at her throat where it had hung since the first firing; it had been touched once, before the kiln on the first morning of Ember, held one breath, let go, and at no shelf, well, rack, road, basket, afternoon or dusk since, because the hand goes to the mark before the kiln and at no other hour. The two rooms did not touch: hers held a pot, a fire and a woman counting under her breath, and his held a crust and an empty place with no name.

In the small hours he turned out the file the way a man lays instruments on a towel before a case begins, each thing going into its own hollow and nothing resting across anything else, the order of the laying being the whole of the discipline and the discipline older than any word in this country. Six hearings of the full note lay beside and never among --- five from four sources and the sixth, the small flat knock out of a person as the men lifted, filed where a thing out of a person must go and never among the sources --- and no seventh came in a hundred and eighty-seven days and none was invented to fill the empty place, for an empty place kept empty is a finding and a finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged. Three near-full answers stood on their own tray, advanced and not paid; the older intervals lay at Stone thirteen, Stone seventeen and Stone twenty-five with the half-beat behind a standing finger, the unchecking stroke and his own held breath from a market morning; and nine stoppings stood as objects on the widened tray with no payer named to any, the damper lying beside them as one piece of iron with two bites and no Grathic word for both mercies.

To this laying he added the reading of the body at the well, which the day had carried forward one more hour without spending it. The pale line at the gum, wet white where the colour should have been; the hand held curled at the side rather than used; the pewter-pale nails; the breath that went in short and came out shorter with a small pause at the top where something hurt; the care with which the dipper was set down because the fingers had stopped telling the man where it was. Each thing lay in its own hollow of the register, exact, complete and worth nothing at two paces. He filed the whole where a danger of the month went, on a place of its own: not among the six hearings, not a seventh, not one of the three near-full answers, not a stopping, not one of the three held changes and not a fourth likeness stood up to call itself a cause, for a rule is an account that has found a payer and no payer stood to this. He could not reach the hand to turn it over. He could not reach his own chin to stop the thread. Nobody under any roof had asked him to read it and nobody would have understood the reading if he had given it.

The two tellings of the shut counting room stayed two with no third; the told bell stood beside the heard bell, four, four and a gap with nobody crossing himself; the shelf's two unnamed gaps lay in the other room and did not touch the fold's crust and its empty place, and the pan's gone round ring and the field's empty place lay on trays of their own. No third gap came, no fourth likeness was made a cause and no finger of his drew the line, because he had spent a hundred and eighty-seven days taking likenesses down one at a time with nobody to help and nobody to see. He wanted the crust two inches from his ear, the breast, the pot handle standing two inches beyond his hand and a number spoken by somebody who would have known what a number counted; he had stopped expecting to be told, which was not the same as not wanting it, and the surgeon's register read the whole of it as an inventory that was not a key and a list that was not a hand.

Through the finger's-width door the river kept its note of water over water, a fifth low thing never counted among the hearings and never made to stand up as a seventh; through the floor the carried fire gave four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have been, its payer without a name for a hundred and eighty-seven days; overhead the boards kept four nails, four, and a knot the width of a thumb. Three counts in three places, and none rose up and became the others. Cold had come to cheek, temple and the small bare joint at the wrist where the wool rode back, and stopped as if it had reached the end of its own instruction; the afterdrop waited on the far side of a warming nobody in this house would perform. His hand outside the wool went up an inch toward the note of water over water and came down against the fold. One hundred and eighty-seven stood exact and complete and unspent in the dark of the wool, spoken by nobody under any roof, the pale line at the gum held on a place of its own and the fire's fifth still missing under the floor with no payer. Nothing had been joined, named, opened, called, spent or ended, and the night went on toward morning without a figure.
