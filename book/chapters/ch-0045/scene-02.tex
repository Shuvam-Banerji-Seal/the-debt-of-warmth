The afternoon thinned without ending. It lost its edges the way water goes off a stone that has held the sun --- first the bright at the yard's far side, then the small distinct heat at the kiln's lip, then the last of the separate smells, each going off the stone and leaving the stone the same stone it had been. Hot clay lay under tallow under wet wool, and below the three the sour mineral breath of \lore{Ash} in the corner nobody had swept in two years, which did not go with the light but stayed and gathered in the quiet. The work wound down into the smaller sounds a house makes once it has stopped being listened to: the rasp of a pot eased down on boards, the click of a tool put back where it belonged, the pull of a breath a man had not known he was holding, let go at last into the room. \person[Corin]{The boy} lay at the low angle where the underside of \place{the loaf shelf} becomes a roof, his back to the fire and his face to the boards, and took the going of the light in as a shape rather than a colour. The new finer grey called \lore{the thaw} lay beside the old clean dark line in the corner and was neither of them swept, and the surgeon's register set the two lines down as a finding laid beside a finding and went no further with it.

\person{Sella Vail} stood at the shelf with her back to the basket and moved her lips along a row that stood with two unnamed gaps. The old space sat at the row's end; the palm's breadth that had come in on the night of Seed six sat beside it. She gave neither a figure and gave nobody a figure. Her eye went once to the clay jar standing cold under its glaze and her hand shut half over nothing and came away without touching it, the way a hand closes over an instrument it has decided not to lift. She kept her finger from beneath the front edge of the board, where the charcoal tally lay where it had lain since the night she held a finger under it for one counted breath. An afternoon was not that night. A woman's back turned was not the same as a hand going in. The tally stayed unread, uncounted, uncopied, and the beat she had left under the board on that night stayed where it was left, on its own place under its own roof, and the two rooms under this one roof did not touch.

The widow's mark hung on its cord at her throat where it had hung since the first firing, a thumb-length of lead-glazed clay with the Vail kiln scratched into it. She did not touch it. A shelf and an afternoon and the going of a day's light were not openings; the one opening lay behind the household on the first morning of Ember, and nobody had praised it and nobody had seen what it mended. People noticed the burn first and the mark last, which was the order she preferred.

\scenebreak

At \place{the kindling} the small knife lay at the very edge of its sill where a man could put a thumb and find it where it should be. Six years stood in the worn thumb-mark, in the place where the house could check; the seventh stood at the lip, where no thumb of the house had gone. \person{Garl Vail}'s unforged collector sentence lay beside it --- the one about the man sent for a collector who turned back at the frozen ford --- kept where the seventh year was kept and entered in nothing. \person[Tarn Vail]{Tarn} came up with the broom and took it only after the broom had been given its instruction, as a man takes up a tool already named. \lore{Ash} was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line and no scrubbing lifted it, and the pale clip-ring at his left wrist held what light there was and gave it back.

At the top of the next reach the breath from Seed sixteen stood unspent, with its small interval showing where a breath should have gone in and had not. He had carried the interval for a hundred and eighty-six days and had never spent it, as a man carries a sum he will not write down.

The sentence came to his teeth again as he put the knife right: that an ear under this roof was worth more than the debt that held it. It came all the way to the word that would call the loan and stopped there, with no figure in it and no name. He drew the breath he should have spent at the top of the reach and did not spend it. \emph{Yes, house,} he said to the fire, and went on with his work, taking the broom over \place{the flagstones} as the house had told the broom to go. No tenth stopping stood up before the seventh year was earned. The nine stood where they had always stood, objects and not a rule, intervals and not payments, with no payer named to any of them; the loan that could hear stayed uncalled; the seventh year stood nearer its end than it had ever stood and had not given. He swept the road a house sends a man down when it wants a collector and did not once turn along it. No collector came up the clay road and no collector went down it.

\scenebreak

Above the shed, in \place{the Debt Loft}, the warm stone lay back in its unmarked clay pot, two days gone and cold. Tarn had taken it out on the afternoon of Day 183 and held it and set it on the sill in the light and looked at it a long while and put it back; the debt had not moved by the width of a hair and he had not forgiven the stone. From below the boy heard nothing now, not a scrape, not a foot, only the house going on around its own kept things. He filed the remembered holding as a remembering and not as a holding made again. He did not stand it up with the fraction of the Day 161 hand at \lore{the damper}, nor with the beat that waited under the shelf from Day 166, nor with the half-check the broom made on the flagstones on Day 171, because he had spent a hundred and eighty-six days taking likenesses down one at a time and he knew what three of them standing in a row would do if a man left them there to call themselves a truth. The loft-sill sound lay on a fourth place, of its own and joined to none. A remembering was a remembering. It did not become a second holding by being remembered twice.

Within hearing Sella gave the end of her instruction to the pot and asked nothing of the man at the kindling and nothing of the room off the shed. \person{Jorra Vail} did not ask about the ear and Tarn did not ask about the room. The two silences stayed where they had always stayed, side by side and unjoined, and the two tellings of the counting room shut in the war stood beside them with no third, one from a man at a well and one from a man going up the clay road with a narrow slate edge-on to his sleeve. Nobody said the room was cold.

At the going of the light she carried him home up \place{the clay road}. Woodsmoke went before her in long low bars out of the chimneys and the river wind came up behind with the taste of \lore{water over water}, the two passing on the stair of the evening as they had passed every evening of his life without taking each other's colour or spending a word on either. The instruction went to the wool and to the river wind and never to the basket: \emph{Keep it off his ears. It's the river wind.} The arm that had carried him a hundred and eighty-six mornings kept the height of the handle, the give of the shoulder, and the turn of the wrist at a step's end --- a record in muscle that gave no account of the cost and would have said, if a man could ask it, that the weight was the same on this evening as on the first, which was true, and that the cost was not the same, which was also true and not entered.

At the edge of the yard she went around the round place on the wall where the shallow clay pan had stood mouth-up to dry through Reed and was standing there no longer, with no scrape, no crumb, no print in the dust and no chip of a rim. She went around \place{the trough} at \place[the yard well]{the well}, bare and dark, whose water was called weather. She went wide beneath \place[the drying racks]{the lowest rack}, where the broken birth-slates lay and nobody entered. She went around the empty place in the returning line where a hand had gone in on Seed fifteen and had not come out. Each got the same look, a look that asked for nothing and found nothing; each got a wide step and no number and no explanation to the basket of why a pot set down here should be carried wide of there. The surgeon's register set the pan's gone round ring on a tray of its own and the field's empty place on another, and did not lay one across the other, because three likenesses in a row will stand up and call themselves a truth if a man leaves them standing.

Under the roof she set him on \place{the bed} and not in the Debt Loft, base first and the weight settled the way a woman sets a vessel that must not rock. She put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without once looking at his face. The thread of drool at his mouth stayed frozen and unwiped, because nobody under any roof had been given an instruction about the thread. Her hand rested a half-beat longer at the setting than the setting needed before it went away --- the half-beat she had learned in the winter she sat up three nights with a child of her own who cooled while everybody was kind, and had never told anybody and would not call kindness now if a slate were held under it and a clean hand set to the figure. She told \lore{the carried fire} it was the thaw, turned the coals with the back of a broken pot, and said \emph{The house will keep it} where another woman would have said \emph{I}. She stood a moment with the back of the pot in her hand counting under her breath toward no opening --- the small intake at the top, the hold, the letting go --- pushed coal-black hair back with the wrist that carries the little-door burn so the grey streak showed pale off the coals, and set the pot rim out on the board with its handle standing two inches beyond the reaching hand. She did not look toward the bed. The mark hung where it hung, because a dusk and a bed and a basket were not openings and the hand went to the mark before the kiln and at no other hour. The two rooms did not touch: hers held a pot, a fire and a woman counting under her breath, and his held a crust two inches from an ear and an empty place beside it with no name.

\scenebreak

In the small hours he turned out the file as a man lays instruments on a towel, each thing going into its own hollow and nothing resting across anything else. He had no towel and no instruments and no case, and laid them out anyway in the dark with hands that could not have lifted the least of them; the order of the laying was the whole of the discipline, and the discipline was older than any word in \lore{Grathic}, older than the house and older than the woman asleep in the room that did not touch this one.

Six hearings of \lore{the full note} lay beside and never among. Five came from four sources: a cup across a birth room, a pot in \place{the Vail wheel-shed} through a finger's width of door, the district's slate twice at \place{the Cross}, and a man giving at \lore[the Hearth Drawing]{the Drawing}. The sixth, the small flat knock out of \person{Dessa Silt} as the men lifted, lay where a thing out of a person must go and never among the sources. No seventh had come in a hundred and eighty-six days and none had been invented to fill the empty place, for an empty place kept empty was a finding and a finding filled to make a row come out even was a finding forged. Three near-full answers stood on their own tray, advanced and not paid. The older intervals lay at Stone 13, Stone 17 and Stone 25 with the half-beat behind a standing finger, the unchecking stroke that went on over the bright patch, and his own held breath from a market morning. Nine stoppings stood as objects and not a rule, with no tenth before the seventh year was earned. \lore{The damper} lay beside them as one piece of iron with two bites, the holding and the taking, with no word in Grathic that would carry both mercies in one mouthful, joined to no kiln and entered in no slate.

Three held changes stood on three places. The fraction from the morning of Day 161 lay where it was left; the beat beneath the shelf from Day 166 lay under its board; the half-check on the flagstones from Day 171 lay in the yard. They were not a pair and not a doctrine, and no payer stood to any of them. The loft-sill scrape lay on a fourth place. The two shut-room tellings stood with no third. The told bell lay beside the heard bell, four, four and a gap with nobody crossing himself; the told figure lay beside the paid, and the figure read over a basket was not the figure that would be paid. The sixth green was a colour joined to no ear and the foundling a shape with no sale, both offstage and unspent. The shelf's two gaps, the fold's crust and its gap, the pan's gone round ring and the field's empty place lay on trays that did not touch. No third gap came. No fourth likeness stood up to call itself a cause. No finger of his drew the line, because three likenesses in a row will call themselves a truth if a man leaves them standing, and he had spent a hundred and eighty-six days taking them down one at a time with nobody to help and nobody to see.

Through the finger's-width door the river kept its note of water over water, a fifth low thing never counted among the hearings and never made to stand up as a seventh. Through the floor \lore{the carried fire} gave four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have been, its payer without a name for a hundred and eighty-six days. Overhead the boards kept four nails, four, and a knot the width of a thumb. Three counts in three places, and none rose up and became the others.

Cold came to cheek, temple, the small bare joint at the wrist where the wool rode back, and stopped as if it had reached the end of its own instruction. The afterdrop waited on the far side of a warming nobody in this house would perform. Nothing had been joined, named, opened, called, spent or ended: the six hearings still six with no seventh invented, the nine stoppings still nine, the Seed sixteen breath still held, the loan still uncalled, the claim of the ear still short of the word that would call it, the mark touched once before the kiln and at no other hour, the charcoal still waiting under the shelf for a hand that was not hers and a day that had not come.

His hand outside the wool went up an inch toward the note of water over water through the finger's-width door and came down against the fold. One hundred and eighty-six stood exact, complete and unspent in the dark of the wool, spoken by nobody under any roof, the fire's fifth still missing under the floor with no payer, and the stone back in its pot in the loft still cold and unreconciled.
