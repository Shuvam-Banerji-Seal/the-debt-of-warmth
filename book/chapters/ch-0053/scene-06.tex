The afternoon thinned without ending, the way a fire thins in a room nobody is watching: off the high air first, off the peg where the salt coat hung, off the top of the wall, then down into the low places where a body lies --- behind the pot, beneath \place{the loaf shelf}, in the fold at the ear. Under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof in \place{Low Kiln} the heat came down the underside of the shelf in a slow slant and stopped at the edge where \person[Corin]{the boy}'s hand stopped. Above that edge the shelf was a roof; beyond it, where a woman stood with her back turned, it was only a shelf with a row of loaves on it. He lay at the angle where one thing becomes the other and neither told him so.

One leathery brown-edged crust lay two inches from his ear, stiff now past the point at which a child would have eaten it, and the empty place beside it kept its empty name. No third came to stand there. The thread of drool at his mouth had frozen in the dry heat and was unwiped, because nobody under this roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread, and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. Cold had come to cheek and temple and the small bare joint at the wrist where the wool rode back, and had stopped there as if it had reached the end of its own order. The afterdrop waited on the far side of a warming nobody in this house would perform. The surgeon's register took the reading without being asked --- surface warm, core not, the order exact --- and the reading bought nothing at two paces. He knew the sequence of a body taking its heat back and could not alter one step of it. The child's body went on being cold while the man's mind recorded how cold, and the two occupied him without either buying the other a thing.

\scenebreak

At the shelf \person{Sella Vail} stood with her back to the basket and moved her lips along the row, which stood with two unnamed gaps and gave nobody a figure. Her finger lay on the top of the shelf's front edge where any woman in the district could see it lying; the charcoal under the board stayed unread, uncounted and uncopied, and an afternoon and a woman's back turned were not the same thing as a finger beneath it. Her eye went once to the clay jar cold under its glaze on the wall shelf, and her hand started toward it and shut half over nothing without a touch, the little-door burn closing on air as it closed on everything that came within reach of it. She said *the house* where another woman would have said I, and gave the instruction to the wool, the pot, the broom, the running water and the river wind, and never to the basket.

At \place{the hearth} she told the fire it was \lore{the thaw}, and turned the coals with the back of a broken pot. “The house will keep it,” she said, and stood a moment with the back of the pot in her hand counting under her breath toward no opening --- small intake, hold, letting go --- the count arriving nowhere as it had arrived nowhere every day of his life. \lore{The widow's mark} hung on its cord at her throat where it had hung since the first firing, touched once before the kiln on Ember 1 and at no hour since. A shelf and an afternoon are not openings; there had been no opening in thirty-two days. Her private roll stayed unshown, and \person[Dessa Silt]{Dessa}'s cost stayed where it had been put down and never entered. She pushed a strand of coal-black hair back with the wrist that carried the burn, and the grey streak at her temple showed pale off the coals, and she did not discuss the streak or the winter it had come in. Then she set the pot rim out with its handle two inches beyond the reaching hand and let her hand rest a half-beat longer at the setting than the setting required, and did not look toward \place{the bed}.

\scenebreak

\person[Garl Vail]{Garl} was kept to the third day without a story. His salt-stiff coat held a broader man's shape on its peg behind the door with the crease at the left shoulder still in it, and the seat in the shed kept its twenty-year hollow and its leftward lean that nobody filled. He had been a big man who moved slowly and arrived early, which in a kiln-house is the same thing, and the boy had never seen him and read him only as a crease in salt-stiff wool. The clean dark line where \lore{Ash} had turned the corner lay at the skirting board with the finer hairline beside it that the house called the thaw, and the household went around both with a look and a wide step and no broom taken to either; a line left where it is is still a line kept. His proverb about leaving the crack showing had gone to the pot and was said over a pot with no crack in it, which cost more now than it had when he was alive to say it. The debt against a firing of tiles was circled without a name, worse for being uncalled, because it could neither be paid nor settled nor stopped. No lesson was made of any of it and no collector was called. His name had not been spoken under this roof for two hundred and twelve days; the keeping of him was maintenance and not a story, and the house went on holding its shape around a man who was not in it.

\scenebreak

At \place{the kindling} the small knife lay at the very edge of its sill, six counted in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check and the seventh at the lip where it could not. Beside it lay Garl's unforged collector sentence, the one about the man sent for a collector who turned back at the frozen ford, kept where the seventh year was kept and entered in nothing. \person[Tarn Vail]{Tarn} took the broom only after the broom had been instructed. His chest had been tight since the dark; grey Ash was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line and no scrubbing lifted it, and the pale clip-ring at his left wrist, in place since an older binding, held what light there was and gave it back.

“Six counted. The next I don't count,” he told the wood, and set the knife back where it could be checked.

The sentence about an ear under this roof being worth more than the debt that held it came to his teeth and went all the way to the word that would call the loan, and stopped there with no figure and no name. He drew the breath he should have spent at the top of the reach and did not spend it; the Seed 16 breath stood where it should have gone in, unspent and carried into the new tend like a tool he had been told to keep and had never used. Nine stoppings stood as nine objects with no tenth stood up before the seventh year was earned; the seventh year stood at its very end now, nearer than it had ever stood, and had not given. The loan that could hear stayed uncalled. His stroke went over \place{the flagstones} past the shallow bright patch without the half-check the stones remembered, and over the road a house sends a man down when it wants a collector, without once turning along it. No collector came up or went down \place{the clay road} on the third day of Grain. Above the shed, in \place{the Debt Loft}, the warm stone lay cold and unreconciled in its unmarked clay pot, and the small loft-sill scrape stayed on its fourth place with no word spoken over it and no payer named to it.

\scenebreak

At the edge of \place{the Vail wheel-shed} \person[Jorra Vail] came through with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bent little finger and under the nails, white in the creases where a fist had opened, and not bone-coloured \lore{slip}. He gave no name to the men who had gone low into the finger's-width-open \place{the back room} before first grey, and did not say the room was cold; the two tellings of the room shut in the war stayed two with no third, and he did not join them to the burned-slate word that had come over the boards and arrived nowhere. He kept that word on a place of its own beside the dust and the two shut-room tellings, with a hand between the three, and made no cause of it. The broken birth-slates beneath \place{the lowest rack} were gone around by the yard and entered by nobody, and he entered none of them. He kept his dusty forearms and his clean ones on one tray, as a surgeon keeps a field and the instruments that must not touch it, parting dust from slip as a surgeon parts clot from vessel, and asked neither which slate had been broken nor whether it had been a birth-slate. \emph{He's not Vail} stayed sealed as tightly as a lid nobody lifts, and the promise of the boy's name to the men who burn slates was neither given nor withdrawn.

He spoke to the wheel before he spoke to anyone. “It doesn't care whose hands,” he said. “It cares if they're steady.” He told a pot, “You'll do,” in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either, and knew the \lore{full note} the pot would give three turns before it left his hands, and withheld the knowing from a man come up from \place{High Kiln} on a wrong wind. The man said that a pot singing before its time generally sings itself out early, and the saying stayed a saying about a pot and was not made true as a lesson, and cost no pot, and no pot was lost in the yard. Jorra grew quieter whenever the burned-slate word went around the boards, and the household straightened around the quiet without an order; the quiet went before him into the shed the way a cold air goes before a door.

Then Sella's voice went low and unfinished at the hearth --- \emph{The fire's low. It wants---} --- and his bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper} ahead of his hand and held until the sentence reached \emph{feeding} and the end was given to the pot; then he moved the tongue a hair's breadth and left the finger standing in the iron. It was a remembered holding and not a change made again, filed where the old fraction lay and not made twice over. There was no word in \lore{Grathic} that would carry both mercies in one mouthful: the iron was one piece with two bites, the holding and the taking, joined to no kiln and entered in no slate. A clamp was a clamp and was not asked to mean more. He did not look toward the bed and did not ask about the ear, and Tarn did not ask about the room, and the two silences kept their old shape under one roof with nobody putting a word between them.

\scenebreak

At the going of the light Sella carried him home at the half-pace, woodsmoke before her in long low bars and river wind behind with the taste of \lore{water over water}. At every open slate she kept the half-pace and went wide. She went around the pan's gone round ring, the bare dark \place{the trough} at \place{the yard well} whose water was called weather, the rack space beneath the lowest rack at \place{the drying racks} where the broken birth-slates lay, the field's empty place and the place the carriers crossed, each with a look and a wide step and no number. The carrying arm was a record in muscle that remembered weight and not cost: it knew the height of the handle, the give of the shoulder and the turn of the wrist at a step's end, and could not have told any slate what any of them had cost her. Under the roof she set him on his bed and not in the Debt Loft, put the fold right without a look at his face and left the thread frozen. One crust lay two inches from his ear with its empty place unnamed, and no third came in the carry.

\scenebreak

Sella had gone to her own bed and left the coals to keep themselves, and there was no breathing number anywhere in the house, only the rhythm that came through the finger's-width door. In the small hours he turned out the file as a man lays instruments on a towel --- no towel, no instruments and no case, but the order of the laying the whole of the discipline --- and found it exact, complete and worth nothing.

Six hearings of the full note stood beside and never among: five from four sources, a cup across a birth room, a pot in the wheel-shed through a finger's width of door, the district's slate twice at \place{the Cross}, and the sixth, the small flat knock out of Dessa as the men lifted her, from a person, filed where a thing out of a person must go and never among the sources. No seventh came in two hundred and twelve days and none was invented to fill the empty place, for an empty place kept empty is a finding and a finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged. Three near-full answers stood on their own tray, advanced and unpaid: a pot under the shed wall that answered nearly full with air under the note and a want in it, a rack pot lifted a hand's breadth too high and set down again, and the knowing the wheel-keeper keeps back from a man come up on a wrong wind. None was promoted into the hearings and no pot was lost in the yard. The told bell lay beside the heard, four, four and a gap with nobody crossing himself; the told figure lay beside the paid, one in twelve read three times over a basket and still not the figure paid, the third reading gone up the road in a locked box with the true subtraction inside, answering nothing for the man carried out on Day 188. On the widened tray nine stoppings stood --- Reed 5, 8, 11, 14, 17, 20, 23, 26 and 29 --- objects and not a rule, intervals and not payments, no payer named to any, held apart by hand because a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and no payer stands to any of them. A tenth had not stood up before the seventh year was earned, and the seventh year now stood at its very end and had still not given.

Three held changes stood on three places. The fraction from the morning of Day 161 --- the hand that waited at the iron for a sentence to finish --- lay where it was left, held and never spoken, no payer named to it and no slate entered; it had been held again through the firing month and into Grain as a remembered repetition, not a change made twice over. Beneath the loaf shelf the beat that stayed under the board on Day 166 stood on a place of its own, the reason standing between knowing the board by heart and being told not to look; Sella had kept her finger on the top of the front edge through all three days and had not gone beneath it. The flagstone half-check made on Tallow 22 stood on a third place beside the other two, a place in a yard as the others are places in a wall and in a room that does not touch either; it had not been repeated in these three days. Three held changes, not a pair and not a doctrine, no word spoken over any and no payer named; he would not let them stand up together and call themselves a rule, because a rule is an account that has found a payer.

A fourth place: the small dry scrape of a pot's foot set down on stone, heard from the loft on Day 183, with no word spoken over it and no payer named. A fifth: the pale line at the gum of the glaze-room hand, read at the well and on the carried body, exact, complete and worth nothing at two paces, not joined to the sweet wind, the dust, the pot that sang early, the sixth green or the foundling's sliver. Neither the third reading nor either of the earlier readings answered for that man; no figure, collector or slate knows what he cost, and no cause was named over him in Grathic.

And beside the dust and the two shut-room tellings, with a hand between the three, the burned-slate word: a direction from a place that passed over boards and arrived nowhere, advanced and not paid, not a hearing, not a seventh, not a stopping, not a payment, not a fourth likeness and not a third telling of the room shut in the war. Two tellings stayed two. The crust's gap here, the shelf's two gaps there, the pan's gone ring on its own tray, the field's empty place on another --- he took them one at a time and drew no finger across them. No third gap came, no fourth likeness stood up to call itself a cause, and nothing was filled to make a row come out even. Then the held inch on a sixth place beside the others, with five answers filed across the three days and this night not yet asked.

\scenebreak

Then he put the question to his hand. Sella had gone to her own bed and left the coals to keep themselves; the two rooms did not touch. There was no breathing number anywhere in the house, nothing to hold against but the rhythm that came through the finger's-width door, the low note of water over water drawing out and slackening, and the next low note coming when it came. In the other room her breaths went small intake, hold, letting go at her fire, a count she kept toward no opening and had never offered to any ear under this roof. The hand outside the wool went up an inch toward the note and stayed.

The old coordination took it through the small intake, rode the hold, and brought it down on the letting go, down with the breath rather than before it, as it had done once before in this house. It was remembered, not a change made again. He filed it on the sixth place beside the others and laid nothing across them and named no payer to it. Nothing stood up out of it to call itself a longer reach, a stronger hand, a hearing, a seventh, a stopping, a payment, one of the three held changes, a key, a power or a rule. The asking is not always answered and the answer here had been given; both are objects on their own trays and neither is a sign.

He still could not close the two inches to the pot handle set out for him, lift his chin the two inches to the crust, or speak a number that anybody under any roof had been asked to believe. The body humiliated him and moved by an inch in the same motion, and both were true. Grain had come on without a boundary drawn and neither of the district's two month-names had been corrected; two hundred and twelve stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody under any roof, the \lore{carried fire}'s fifth had had no payer for two hundred and twelve days, the seventh year stood at its very end and had still not given, and the count went forward into Day 213, Grain 4, Fourthday, with Grain 10 held as the first Account-day and market of the new month, where the seventh green and the seventh sighting were owed and must not come early. The seventh green stayed a colour and the foundling a shape, with no loaf, no slate and no sale.

The hand came down with the breath counted at Sella's fire in the room that does not touch his. The thread at his mouth was still frozen and unwiped, one stiff leathery brown-edged crust lay two inches from his ear with its empty place unnamed and no third, and the fire went on in the room that warmed him while the river went on in the room he could not enter, and neither stopped for the ear under the roof.
