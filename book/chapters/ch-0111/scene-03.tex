\daymark{385}{Stone 26, Sixthday, Y.H. 813}

The hand had lain flat on the fold since the last going of the light, and the dark had thinned out of the room under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill}, \place{Grath} by the slow subtraction that brings a morning in these parts --- not a lifting but a letting-up, as a banked fire lets up its heat under ash long before anyone lifts the lid. \person[Corin]{The boy} lay at the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf} and took the field as it came to him, in the old order, by the small things first. Three hundred and eighty-four answers stood on the sixth place with the three hundred and eighty-fifth not yet begun, and six months and two hundred and four days were behind him with a two hundred and fifth to be earned before the light went. He had once read a trauma bay in the space between a page and a breath; here he read a crust, and a gap, and a thread, and the place where cold had come to a stop, and found the reading no less exact for being worth nothing at two inches of air.

Three things lay where they had been laid and were found as found. The crust had gone harder and browner in the month's dry cold, stiff as old leather at the edge, lying two inches from the ear with its corner lifting a hair off the wool; beside it the empty place kept its emptiness, with no second crust and no name spoken over it; the thread of drool at his lip had frozen in the dry heat and was left unwiped, because nobody under any roof has ever been given an instruction about the thread. Cold had come cheek, temple and the small bare joint at the wrist where the wool rode back, and stopped there as a line stops at the end of a ruled page, with no order to go farther and no order to go away. \lore{The Afterdrop} waited past a warming nobody in this house would perform. He knew the composed face that is not composure, knew pink returning for the surface still lying over a cold core, knew a cold patient cannot clot and will seem better before he is worse and must be warmed from the outside and slowly, never hurried, never rubbed. A basket set level out of the draught where the heat came to him sideways was the careful rewarming he would choose and could not ask for. Under the wool the infant wanted the crust lifted, the thread wiped and a hand on the pot handle. Both were true. The second was louder. Neither won. The quarrel was the work, and he had long ago stopped looking for a verdict, which was not the same as finding one.

Beside the crust the wool lay folded once in the empty place, as it had lain when a hand put it there on the fifteenth, changed by nothing that had gone over it in the night. Ten mornings and ten goings of the light had found it folded there before this one, and this was the eleventh finding; the hand that found it was the same hand that had only ever read it, and eleven readings from that hand added no eleventh placing to the first. A second placing would make a habit and a third would make a way, and a habit and a way both want a payer standing under them, and no payer stood. He filed the finding beside the crust's gap, the two gaps of the loaf shelf in the room whose floor does not touch this one, the gone pan's round ring and the field's empty place, with a finger between every pair so that no row came out even by his doing. It was not a joining of rooms, not a gift, not a payment, not a sixth held object, not a habit, a way, a sign, a hearing, a mending, a cause or a rule. Eleven is two figures, and two figures is the shape a doctrine wears when a hand stands under it to count them. No hand stood. He felt the small alarm of that and filed it too on a place of its own, and did not let eleven likenesses stand up together and call themselves an account, for an account wants a hand to lay them together and a payer under them all, and under this roof there was neither. The far-off finger that would one day lay shelf and fold together and call the joining an account was still far off, was not his, and had not come.

Nothing in the room had been asked to carry the finding and nothing did. The fold lay where the fold lay, the count lay where the count lay, and the two did not join.

\scenebreak

\person{Sella Vail} came to \place{the hearth} before the basket, as she had at the first of every day for three years, and told the coals it was \lore{the thaw}. The hairline in the yard's corner and the word in the mouth of the house were one thing to her, and the coals took the word the way coals take any instruction, by taking it and giving nothing back. She turned them with the back of a broken pot and said “The house will keep it” where another woman would have said I. Then she stood a moment on a small intake, a hold and a letting-go toward no opening, and figured neither of the two gaps. Her finger lay along the top of the loaf shelf so that the charcoal tally beneath stayed unread; her lips moved along a two-gapped row and gave neither gap a number. She glanced once at \place{the clay jar} standing cold under \lore{lead glaze}, with the sweetish edge of it coming and going on a wrong wind past \place{the pot board}, and shut her hand half over nothing, the little-door burn on the back of her left hand closing on air. She did not touch \lore{the widow's mark} at her throat. A morning is not an opening; seven openings in seven months are seven and not a rule, and no eighth was made due.

On the pot board the twice-mended pot came up thumb-first where the wire crossed the crack, before the weight went on the handle, the hairline of the original break showing between the old-brass staple and the tighter wire. Repair was not restoration: a restored thing hides the hand that handled it, and a mended thing leaves that hand showing. “Leave the crack showing. People trust a thing they can see has been handled,” she told the pot. “If it's worth mending it's worth mending twice. The second time is the real one,” she told the fire, and the fire, which had heard both sayings a thousand times, answered with its low pull and nothing more. Both proverbs went to the pot and the fire and never to a person, because a thing said to a person becomes a thing the Court can enter. She turned the dressed grain once under wool with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child, lifting no figure and taking no handful, and set the pot rim two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat and no look toward the bed. The half-beat was the distance between intention and arrest, bought at nothing and not a gift. Inside the wool his hand went toward the rim and stopped where the half-beat had stopped it.

Under the floor \lore{the carried fire} gave four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer without a name for three hundred and eighty-four days. A basket set down is a pot set down, and a pot is told where to stand and not why.

\scenebreak

She carried him down the nineteen steps at the half-pace she had kept from every open slate for three years, correcting the tilt at every turn of the landing before it had time to settle into the wicker, so that he felt the small correction before he felt the stair. The instruction went to the wool, to the river wind, to the fire she would bank against their coming back, and never to the basket. “Keep his ear covered,” she told the wool. She told the wind nothing, because a wind is owed no instruction it cannot keep. Out in \place{the Clay Yard} the morning stood in the dry cold and went about its work without a boundary drawn, and the yard went around its seven walked-around places with a look and a wide step and no number: the clean dark line at \place{the Vail corner} with its finer grey hairline called the thaw, unswept and thickened in the month's dry cold, carrying the faint sour tang of \lore{Ash} up off it where no broom had gone in two years; the space beneath \place{the lowest rack} where \lore{the broken birth-slates} lay and were entered by nobody; the round ring at \place{the rack end} where the shallow clay pan had once stood, keeping no scrape, no crumb, no print, no chip and no word; the field's empty place out on \place{the worked strips}; \place{the trough} with its skin drawn edge to edge; the silt-clouded plate of ice on \place{the flat stone by the well}; and, widest of all, \place{the returning-line place} where a hand had gone in on \place{Seed} 15 and had not come out. The going-around there was so wide that her elbow cut the corner and the basket canted the other way to balance it, and she righted it without looking down. Once. Not twice. A second going-around would have made a figure of once, and a figure wants a payer standing under it and none stood.

The three coverings stayed where the weather had put them, with a finger between every pair: the white frost gone off the north lip of \place{the yard well}, the skin drawn edge to edge across the trough, the plate of ice clouded with silt standing on its flat stone out of the way of feet. Weather entered nowhere and was not made a cause, a sign, a payment or a promise. Beyond the yard \place{the river} kept \lore{the winter skin} on its own place beside \lore{water over water}, found where put on the first day of the month and found again this morning and not made again, not spent on the week's work and not made to buy a lesson under any roof. No hand had laid the skin, no payer stood under it and no source stood to be asked. The satisfaction of a field gone around as found and left as found cost nothing, which was how the boy knew it was not peace.

\person{Ilva} was at the well once, drawing with the pail against her knee. The rope stood stiff in the \place{Stone} cold and gave its small dry rasp as the bucket came up, and the water went dark and moving below without a sound; the pail knocked the stone rim, and the sound went off across the yard and was gone. Clay had dried pale in the crescents of her nails; the finer grey rack dust was worked into the seam of one thumb, and she did not scrub it out. Her hands knew the weight of a pail and the edge of a wet board and had never been soft. The people going about the yard noticed the pail first and the sleeve not at all, which was the order she preferred. A woman waiting her turn, with a pot on her hip and her shawl drawn tight against the river wind, asked what Stone's firings had taken.

Ilva's hand went toward her sleeve before her eyes went to the asker, and it stayed there. The short strip of notched birch bark lay in the fold where it always lay, pale where a thumb had rubbed the notches and dark in the uncut grain between; it was not taken out, read, copied, counted or joined to the open slate a pace away on the stone lip, nor to the charcoal under the loaf shelf, nor to the cord, nor to the knot-string, nor to any saying that had arrived nowhere, and no figure came off the wood. Her eyes went to the woman and her mouth opened after both, with the half-beat lying in the gap where a lesser observer would have put an intention.

“Ask the pot what it cost,” she said. “I keep the count, not the account.”

She put the flat of her hand once more on the sleeve and went back to the pail, and nobody turned twice. The woman took the answer as she took the weather, with no thanks and no figure, and turned back to her pot; the well kept its dark moving water under the stiff rope, and the yard went on around its work.

He took the order of her reaching apart as a surgeon takes a field apart --- hand to the record before eyes to the person, eyes before mouth, the half-beat in the gap where a lesser observer would have put an intention --- and filed it on a place of its own as her ninth record-first hand, with a finger between it and \person{Hessa Rale}'s eighth. He filed no tenth, made no reader of her keeping, joined her to none and made no figure of the slate's nearness. She had not looked into the basket and had no notion she had been read. Her fear that one day a slate would be put to her stick and a figure taken off it stayed hers and was not spoken; she would not have known what there was to explain. She was expressly not the speaker of any voice that came over \place{the boards}: the Frost 14 well-saying stayed at one saying and four shapings with no fifth, the burned slate stood at five, the row at five askings with no witness, and the fourth direction of Stone 23 stood where put and did not repeat and bought no lesson under any roof. The count stayed in wood where no slate could take a figure off it.

\scenebreak

In the small hours \place{the towel} came out at the head of \place{the bed} as instruments come out when a case is done and before the next is called, with every gap left gap. Six hearings of \lore{the full note} stood beside and never among, the sixth the small flat knock out of \person{Dessa Silt} as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go and never among the sources, and the seventh place stayed empty and was not dug for; \lore[The Hearth Drawing]{The Drawing}'s flat knock stayed outside them on its own place. The heard bell of \lore{the Long Night} stayed in its square beside the told, four, four and a gap, not folded into any count. The five held objects stood with the pot-crack resting and the hand-at-the-sill breath and the holding in \lore{the damper} beside them, each on a place of its own with a finger between every pair, and the keeping at the fold stood beside the crust's gap, the shelf's two gaps, the gone pan's round ring and the field's empty place and was expressly not a tenth held object. Nine record-first hands kept no tenth; nine stoppings kept no tenth before the seventh year at its very end; the burned slate stood at five, the shut-room tellings at two, the row at five askings with no witness and the well-saying at four shapings. The finding that eleven arithmetics are not one account sat on a place of its own, with no signature on the space and no doctrine standing up out of eleven; their eleven true subtractions rode locked in a box up \place{the clay road} whose key had gone up in a coat pocket and did not come back down, and the boy had no key. No sixth hearing, tenth hand, tenth stopping, eighth opening, twelfth arithmetic or witnessing voice was supplied to make a row come out even, for a finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged. Four rooms kept the same gap in their count held apart with a finger between every pair, four likenesses and not one account, an account wanting a hand to lay them together and a payer under them all, and no such hand standing and no such payer.

Through the \place{finger's-width door} the river kept its low note of water over water under the winter skin, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming, muffled as a voice is muffled by a shut door. The note had done before any hand came down. The Afterdrop waited past a warming nobody in this house would perform, and the infant's want of the crust lifted, the thread wiped and a hand on the pot handle went on quarrelling without verdict, both true, the second louder, neither winning. The surgeon's small satisfaction in a field taken as found and left as found cost nothing, which was how he knew it was not peace.

He filed answer three hundred and forty-five: exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the eleven readings, the bell's gap, the flat knock, the child's grey fingertips, the mother's cup, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the held breath, the two bites of iron, the river's skin, the widow's mark, the fourth direction nor the keeping at the fold. Stone kept its twenty-sixth day without a boundary drawn, the deep-winter month holding both its names, older women by weather and slatemen by \lore{Grathic} Account-days, neither set right nor left in so many words; \lore{the Five} stood beyond and was not brought into a day that had not earned it. Four mornings stood between this day and the month's last reading and were not brought in. The count stood at three hundred and eighty-five exact, six months and two hundred and five days told by nobody who would have known what a number counted, the carried fire's fifth unpaid the same number, and \person[Garl Vail]{Garl}'s name unspoken the same number. Eleven findings were eleven findings and not a habit; eleven arithmetics were eleven and not one account; four directions were four and not a witness. The wool lay folded once in the crust's empty place beside the crust gone harder and browner in the dry cold, its corner standing a hair off the wool, with no second fold, no third crust and no name spoken over the place, and the thread was frozen and unwiped. The hand lay flat on the fold on answer three hundred and forty-five, the eleventh finding counted and still not placed, and the count went on under the roof in Low Kiln, Sill, Grath.
