\daymark{384}{Stone 25, Fifthday, Y.H. 813}

\dropcap{T}{he hand} had lain flat on the fold since the going of the last light, and the dark had gone out of the room under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill}, \place{Grath} by degrees, the way a fire goes out under blind ash --- not lifted away but thinned, the coals behind the wall giving back their heat in fours and leaving the fifth where it had been left since the night a boy came under this roof to stay. \person[Corin]{The boy} took the field in its old order, the way a case is read off a towel before the light is good enough to trust: the crust first, stiff and leathery-brown at the edge, two inches from the ear with its corner standing a hair off the wool; the empty place beside it; the thread of drool frozen at the lip in the dry kiln heat and wiped by nobody, because nobody under any roof has ever been given an instruction about the thread. Three things found as they had been left, and none of them the better for his finding them.

He was carried everywhere and told by nobody, and the telling he did himself was in the only register he had: three hundred and forty-two honest answers stood on the sixth place, with the three hundred and forty-third not yet begun, and six months and two hundred and three days behind him with two hundred and four to be earned before the light went. He had once reduced a thousand shoulders in a room that smelled of antiseptic and hot metal; he could not lift a crust two inches to his own mouth, and the fact had long ago stopped being funny without ever quite stopping being true. Cold had stopped at cheek, temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, as though it had come to the end of a line written out for it with neither an order to go farther nor an order to go away. \lore{The Afterdrop} waited past a warming nobody in this house would perform. He knew the face that cold composes is not composure, and that pink returning is the surface warm over a core that has not warmed at all; he knew a basket set level out of the draught where the heat came to a body sideways was the careful rewarming he would choose and could not ask for, and that a cold patient cannot clot and will seem better before he is worse. Under the wool the infant wanted the crust lifted, the thread wiped and a hand on the pot handle. Both registers were true. The second was louder. Neither won. The quarrel was the work, and he had stopped looking for a verdict in it, which was not the same thing as having found one.

Beside the crust the wool lay folded once in the empty place, as it had lain when a hand put it there on the fifteenth, changed by nothing that had passed over it in the night. He found it where it was put. He did not put it there again. Nine mornings and nine goings of the light had found it folded there before this one, and this was the tenth finding; the hand that found it was the same hand that had only ever read it, and ten readings from that hand added no tenth placing to the first. A second placing would make a habit and a third would make a way, and a habit and a way both want a payer standing under them, and no payer stood. He filed the tenth beside the crust's gap, the two gaps of \place{the loaf shelf}, the gone pan's round ring and the field's empty place, with a finger between every pair so that no row came out even by his doing. It was not a joining of rooms, not a gift, not a payment, not a sixth held object, not a sign, a hearing, a mending, a cause or a rule. It was a finding. Ten was the first of the findings to need two figures to say it, and two figures is the shape a doctrine wears when a hand stands under it to count them. No hand stood. He felt the small alarm of that and kept it as a finding too, on a place of its own, and did not let ten likenesses stand up together and call themselves an account, for an account wants a hand to lay them together and a payer under them all, and under this roof there was neither. The far-off finger that would one day lay shelf and fold together and call the joining an account was still far off, was not his, and had not come.

\scenebreak

\person{Sella Vail} came to \place{the hearth} before she came to the basket, as she had at both ends of every day for three years, and told the coals it was \lore{the thaw}. She turned them with the back of a broken pot.

“The house will keep it,” she said to the coals, where another woman would have said I. She stood a moment on a small intake, a hold and a letting-go toward no opening, and figured neither of the two gaps. Her finger lay along the top of the loaf shelf so the charcoal tally on its underside stayed unread; her lips moved along a two-gapped row of the shelf's front edge and gave neither gap a number. She glanced once at \place{the clay jar} standing cold under \lore{lead glaze} and shut her hand half over nothing, the little-door burn on the back of her left hand closing on air; the cord of her \lore{widow's mark} hung oiled where a working thumb had touched it ten thousand times, and she did not touch it now, for a morning is not an opening and seven openings in seven months are seven and not a rule. Her hands, which had nothing to do for a moment, counted the spoons on the bench and went back to the coals without having told anybody what they counted.

On \place{the pot board} the twice-mended pot stood with the hairline of the original break showing between the old-brass staple and the tighter wire. She lifted it thumb-first where the wire crossed the crack, the thumb arriving at the fault before the weight came on the handle.

“Leave the crack showing,” she told the pot. “People trust a thing they can see has been handled.”

“If it's worth mending it's worth mending twice. The second time is the real one,” she told the fire. The sayings had been improved in the keeping and were very likely not as good as \person{Garl Vail} had thought them when he first said them, and they went to the pot and the fire and never to a person. Repair stayed apart from restoration: a restored thing hides the hand that handled it, and a mended thing leaves that hand showing. His salt-stiff coat kept his broader shape on its peg behind the door with the crease at the left shoulder where a hand had long ago pulled it down, and the hollow wheel-seat in the shed kept its twenty-year shape on that side and was not filled. His name had not been spoken under this roof for three hundred and eighty-four days, and the keeping of him was maintenance and not a story.

The dressed grain she turned once under wool with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child, lifting no figure and no handful. Then she set the pot rim two inches beyond the reaching hand, with a half-beat and no look toward \place{the bed}. Inside the wool his hand went toward the rim and stopped where the half-beat had stopped it, and the interval was bought at nothing and was not a gift.

\scenebreak

She carried him down \place{the nineteen steps} at the half-pace she keeps from every open slate. The instruction went to the wool, to the river wind, to the fire she would bank against their coming back, and never to the basket; she corrected the tilt at every turn of the landing before it had time to settle into the wicker, so that he felt the small correction before he felt the stair. The kiln stood behind its wall with the last firing long gone cold and bought no word from any of them.

Out in \place{the Clay Yard} the morning stood in the dry cold, smelling of woodsmoke and wet wool under the faint sour mineral tang of the corner. The yard went around its seven walked-around places with a look and a wide step and no number. It went widest around \place{the returning-line place}, where a hand had gone in on Seed 15 and had not come out, so wide that her elbow cut the corner and the basket canted the other way to balance it and was righted without a look down. Once, and not twice. A second going-around would have made a figure of once, and a figure wants a payer standing under it and none stood. The keeping cost a step each time and nobody entered the steps and he did not enter them either.

The clean dark line at \place{the Vail corner} kept its finer grey hairline called the thaw, unswept and thickened in the month's dry cold with the faint sour tang of \lore{Ash} coming up off it where no broom had crossed in two years. Beneath \place{the lowest rack} \lore{the broken birth-slates} lay and were entered by nobody. At \place{the rack end} the round ring where the shallow pan once stood kept no scrape, no crumb, no print, no chip and no word. The gone white frost off the north lip of \place{the yard well}, the skin drawn edge to edge across \place{the trough}, and the silt-clouded plate of ice on \place{the flat stone by the well} set a little out of the path of feet stayed three coverings entered nowhere, with a finger between every pair. Out on \place{the worked strips} the field kept its empty place and did not fill it. At \place{the kindling} Garl's unforged collector sentence lay beside the seventh notch with no name on the man who turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on the debt, and \lore{the tile debt} against a firing of tiles circled without a name and was worse for being uncalled.

Through \place{the finger's-width door} \place{the river} went on with its note of \lore{water over water}, a low thing over a lower thing, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming, muffled under its covering as a voice is muffled by a shut door. Over that, from bank to bank since the first day of \place{Stone}, lay \lore{the winter skin}, a covering put on by the cold and taken off by the cold and entered nowhere, found where put on the first day of the month and found again this morning and not made again, not spent on the week's work and not made to buy a lesson under any roof. No hand had laid the skin, no payer stood under it and no source stood to be asked. A sign points to a payer; a covering left where it is is weather and not a promise. He kept the covering on a place of its own beside the note and put a finger between the two, so that no row came out even by his doing.

\scenebreak

In the small hours \place{the towel} came out at the head of the bed as instruments come out when a case is done and before the next is called, with every gap left gap. Three hundred and forty-two honest answers stood on the sixth place and the three hundred and forty-third was to come. The five held objects stood with the pot-crack resting and the hand-at-the-sill breath and the holding in \lore{the damper} beside them, each on a place of its own with a finger between every pair, and the keeping at the fold stood beside all of them and was expressly not a tenth held object. It had been found where put on ten mornings now --- a tenth finding and still not a placing --- and ten findings from a hand that only read added no tenth placing to the first, and no hand had laid shelf and fold together and called them an account. No name had been spoken over the place, no third crust stood against the two gaps to make a row come out even, and the far-off finger was still far off, was not his, and had not come. Three counts in three places stood and did not become one: \lore{the carried fire} giving four low pulls under the floor with the silence where the fifth should have stood, the river's note under its skin, and \place{the boards} overhead keeping four nails on the near board, four on the next and a thumb-width knot where the fifth might have been driven and was not.

Nine record-first hands kept no tenth; nine stoppings kept no tenth before the seventh year at its very end; \lore{The burned-slate word} stood at five, the shut-room tellings at two, the row at five askings with no witness and expressly no sixth, the well-saying at four shapings with no fifth. Four rooms kept the same gap in their count --- the birth room in \place{House Silt}, the Vail kiln-house, the room where the slate was read on market-day and \place{the Drawing house} seen two doors down --- held apart with a finger between every pair, four likenesses and not one account, an account wanting a hand to lay them together and a payer under all four, and no such hand standing and no such payer. The burned-slate word stood where it had been put on Ash 5, and the Ash 17, Ash 23 and Stone 23 directions stood on places of their own beside it and did not repeat and bought no lesson. No sixth hearing, seventh opening, sixth held object, sixth telling, third shut-room telling, sixth asking, fifth shaping, tenth hand, tenth stopping or twelfth arithmetic was supplied to make a row come out even, for a finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged. The finding that eleven are not one account sat on a place of its own with a finger between it and every other kept thing.

Six hearings of \lore{the full note} stood beside and never among, the sixth the small flat knock out of \person{Dessa Silt} as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go and never among the sources, and the seventh place stayed empty and was not dug for. \lore[The Hearth Drawing]{The Drawing}'s small flat knock out of the neighbour on the pallet stayed on its own place outside them, not a seventh and not a filling of the empty place, for a hearing wants a source standing and that room had been seen from a basket and not entered. The heard bell of \lore{the Long Night} stayed in its square beside the told, four, four and a gap, and was not folded into any of the counts. Eleven arithmetics stood behind one unsigned figure: the three of Frost, the first, second and third of Bone, the first, second and third of Ash, Stone's first and Stone's second. The figure had been read and was still not the figure paid; no signature stood on the space between, and no doctrine had come out of eleven. Their eleven true subtractions rode locked in \person{Berr Clay}'s box up \place{the clay road}, the key gone up in a coat pocket that did not come back down and the boy having no key.

He filed answer three hundred and forty-three: exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the eleven readings, the bell's gap, the flat knock, the child's grey fingertips, the mother's cup, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the held breath, the two bites of iron, the river's skin, the widow's mark, the fourth direction nor the keeping at the fold. The surgeon took his small clean satisfaction in a field taken as found and left as found, and it cost him nothing, which was how he knew it was not peace. Under it, louder, the infant wanted the crust lifted, the thread wiped and a hand on the pot handle, and got none of the three. Both were true. The second was louder. Neither won.

Stone kept its twenty-fifth day without a boundary drawn, the deep-winter month holding both its names, older women by weather and slatemen by \lore{Grathic} Account-days, neither set right nor left in so many words, and five mornings stood between this day and the month's last reading and were not brought into it. \lore{The Five} stood beyond that. The count stood at three hundred and eighty-four exact, six months and two hundred and four days told by nobody who would have known what a number counted, the carried fire's fifth unpaid the same number and Garl's name unspoken the same number. The wool lay folded once in the crust's empty place beside the crust gone harder and browner in the dry cold, its corner standing a hair off the wool, with no second fold, no third crust and no name spoken over the place, and the thread was frozen and unwiped. Ten findings were ten findings and not a rule; eleven arithmetics were eleven and not one account; four directions were four and not a witness. The hand lay flat on the fold on answer three hundred and forty-three, the tenth finding counted and still not placed, and the count went on under the roof in Low Kiln, Sill, Grath.
