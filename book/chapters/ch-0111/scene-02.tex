The afternoon came on without a boundary drawn, the light subtracted from the boards a little at a time, so that no hour could say where the morning had ended and the going of the light would begin. It went out of the room the way warmth goes out of a pot set off the heat, not all at once but by degrees the hand cannot name until the hand is cold. \person{Sella Vail} carried \person[Corin]{the boy} down the nineteen steps at the half-pace she had kept from every open slate for three years, correcting the tilt at every turn of the landing before it had time to settle into the wicker, so that he felt the small correction before he felt the stair. The instruction went to the wool, to the river wind, to the fire she would bank against their coming back, and never to the basket. She told the wool to keep his ear covered; she told the wind nothing, because a wind is owed no instruction it cannot keep. A basket set down is a pot set down, and a pot is told where to stand and not why.

He took the descent as a surgeon takes a field before the light, by the small things first, because the small things do not lie: the scrape of a heel on the second step, the draught that came up at the fifth and went out again at the sixth, the particular hollow the basket made against her hip when she shifted her weight at the turn. Under the wool the infant wanted the tilt corrected the other way, toward the crust two inches off and the thread frozen at his lip, and got none of it. Both wants were true and the second was louder and neither won. That was the quarrel, and the quarrel was the work.

Out in \place{the Clay Yard} the afternoon went about its ordinary work and left its accounts where they lay. Nothing from the eleven arithmetics standing behind the household, nor from the keeping found at the fold that morning, had crossed under the roof as sign, lesson, mending, cause, payment or payer, and nothing crossed out here either. The yard went around its seven walked-around places with a look and a wide step and no number: the clean dark line at \place{the Vail corner} with its finer grey hairline that the house called \lore{the thaw}, unswept and thickened in the month's dry cold, carrying the faint sour tang of \lore{Ash} up off it where no broom had gone in two years; the space beneath \place{the lowest rack} where \lore{the broken birth-slates} lay and were entered by nobody; the round ring at \place{the rack end} where the shallow clay pan had once stood, keeping no scrape, no crumb, no print, no chip and no word; the field's empty place out on \place{the worked strips}; \place{the trough} with its skin drawn edge to edge; the silt-clouded plate of ice on \place{the flat stone by the well}, set a little out of the path of feet; and, widest of all, \place{the returning-line place} where a hand had gone in on \place{Seed} 15 and had not come out. The going-around there was so wide that her elbow cut the corner and the basket canted the other way to balance it, and she righted it without looking down. Once. Not twice. A second going-around would have made a figure of once, and a figure wants a payer standing under it and none stood, so the step was taken and not entered, and the satisfaction of a field gone around as found and left as found cost nothing, which was how the boy knew it was not peace.

The three coverings stayed where the weather had put them, with a finger between every pair. The white frost was gone off the north lip of \place{the yard well}; the skin lay drawn edge to edge across the trough; the plate of ice clouded with silt stood on its flat stone out of the way of feet. Weather entered nowhere and was not made a cause, a sign, a payment or a promise. Out on the worked strips the field kept its empty place and did not fill it.

\scenebreak

The day's costs arrived as directions and went away as weather. A man down from \place{Cold Acre} shifted a hare across his shoulders and answered the question of its price with the weather which had carried the snow and could carry the price, and went off toward \place{the clay road} with the hare unweighed and no slate opened. A man with a cracked jar in a rope sling was sent to a pot, and the pot, being a pot, said nothing. To a woman with a basket of kindling who asked what the seventh year would want when it came, and to two fence-menders who said the thaw would be early and the thaw would be late, \person{Tarn} said “Yes, house” to each and kept the late, because a late thaw puts off the paying. He agreed so thoroughly that there was nowhere for an argument to find a foot. No collector came up or went down the clay road, and no bell came on any road.

At \place{the kindling} the account stood in its old exactness. Six pieces lay in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check, and the seventh lay at the lip beyond it, beside \person{Garl Vail}'s unforged collector sentence, with no name on the man who had turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on the debt. \lore{The tile debt} against a firing of tiles circled without a name and was worse for being uncalled. The small knife lay at the very edge of its sill, where a hand could check it and where a hand could also slip it off. Tarn passed his hand over blade and sill as a man passes a hand over a tool put back where it belongs; the hand went up and came away, and the coming away was the whole of the checking. He held no breath at the top of the reach. The boy read the movement the way he had once read a hand drawn from a field before the dressing went on --- neither the hand that works nor the hand that only rests, but the hand that checks and goes away.

“Six counted. The next I don't count,” he told the wood, and cut nothing, counted nothing, spent nothing.

The Ash 14 held breath and the Bone 26 thumb's resting were found where they had been put and were not made again, standing beside the five held objects and the pot-crack resting, each on a place of its own with a finger between every pair. They were not a sixth held object, not a seventh, not a tenth stopping, not the calling of the loan. The pale clip-ring at his left wrist caught what little light there was and gave it back. His chest had been tight since the dark; the Ash was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line at the Vail corner; and his stroke went over the shallow bright patch of \place{the flagstones} without the half-check, the patch keeping the place where the stroke had once stopped and joined to no cause.

He brought the ear-sentence up to the word that would call the loan and stopped there, as a remembered ninth stopping with no tenth before the seventh year now at its very end. The Seed 16 breath was unspent and the loan uncalled. He did not look along the clay road. Two ears, two silences, a loan that could hear and had never been called, with nobody standing to enter it. A term ending is not a payment, not a forgiveness, not a freedom. A man with no house stands where the road runs, and the fear that he will be entered again for vagrancy stays his, kept without a figure and without asking anybody to carry it.

\person{Jorra Vail} came through the \place{finger's-width door} off \place{the Vail wheel-shed} with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bent little finger and white in a fist-crease where a hand had opened, and it was not the bone-coloured \lore{slip} of the wheel. Three or more men unnamed had gone low into \place{the back room} before first grey; he gave the house no name for them and no word that the room was cold. He kept his dusty forearms and his clean ones on one tray and parted them as clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had been broken nor whether it had been a birth-slate, while the broken ones lay beneath the lowest rack and were entered by nobody.

He spoke to the wheel before he spoke to any person. “It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady.” To a pot going past on a bench he said “You'll do,” in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either, knowing \lore{the full note} that pot would give three turns before it left his hands, with air under it and a want in it. He withheld the knowing from any master come up from \place{High Kiln} who would call it early and sing a pot before its time. The boy kept that knowing on a tray of its own beside the hearings and never among them: a pot's note known before its time is not a hearing, no source stands under it, and no seventh hearing stood up to fill the empty place.

When Sella had not finished telling him the fire was low, his bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper} ahead of her sentence, took the cold out of the fingertip, moved the tongue a hair's breadth and was left standing, so that the end of her instruction arrived at the pot and the household straightened around the waiting as cloth straightens when a weight is lifted. The boy read one piece of iron with two bites, the holding and the taking, the same clamp that holds a vessel and stops the flow through it, and no \lore{Grathic} word covered both mercies. The finger came out without leaving the extra holding of Ash 26 a second time, gathered once on a place of its own and standing where nobody entered it, for a second holding would make a habit and a third a way and both want a payer where none stood. Neither looked at the other and neither said what the iron had been asked to carry, because a thing said aloud becomes a thing the Court can enter.

“He's not Vail” stayed sealed, and the promise of the boy's name to the men who burn slates was neither given nor withdrawn. \lore{The burned-slate word} stood at five with a finger between the fifth and the four and no witness; the shut-room tellings at two; the row at five askings with no sixth. The Ash 5 joining and the Ash 17, Ash 23 and Stone 23 directions stood where they had been put, none repeating, none buying a lesson, all gathering interest against the promise without joining it, as dust gathers in a corner nobody sweeps. The fourth direction of Stone 23 stood where put and did not repeat; nothing that came over the boards this afternoon bought a teaching under this roof.

At the going of the light Sella carried him up the nineteen steps under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof and never into \place{the Debt Loft}. She set him on \place{the bed} at the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf}, said nothing of where they had been, put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face, and added no second fold to the crust's empty place. He was found where put, changed by nothing, and the ten findings were ten findings still and not a habit and not a way; a habit wants a hand that placed twice and a way a third, and both want a payer where none stood. The thread stayed frozen and unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread.

Under the roof the grammar of the hearth was renewed as it had been at both ends of every day for three years. She came to \place{the hearth} before the basket, told the coals it was the thaw and turned them with the back of a broken pot, and said “The house will keep it” where another woman would have said I, standing a moment to count a small intake, a hold and a letting-go toward no opening and figuring neither of the two gaps. Her finger lay along the top of the loaf shelf so that the charcoal tally on its underside stayed unread; her lips moved along a two-gapped row and figured neither gap. She glanced once at \place{the clay jar} standing cold under \lore{lead glaze} and shut her hand half over nothing, the little-door burn on the back of her left hand closing on air, and did not touch \lore{the widow's mark} hanging at her throat: a going of the light and a bed are not openings, seven openings in seven months are seven and not a rule, and no eighth was made due.

On \place{the pot board} the twice-mended pot was lifted thumb-first where the wire crossed the crack, the hairline of the original break showing between the old-brass staple and the tighter wire. “Leave the crack showing. People trust a thing they can see has been handled,” she told the pot. “If it's worth mending it's worth mending twice. The second time is the real one,” she told the fire. Both proverbs went to the pot and the fire and never to a person, because a thing said to a person becomes a thing the Court can enter. Repair stayed apart from restoration: a restored thing hides the hand that handled it and a mended thing leaves that hand showing. The dressed grain she turned under wool with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child, lifting no figure and taking no handful, and she set the pot rim two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat and no look toward the bed. The half-beat was the distance between intention and arrest, bought at nothing and not a gift. Inside the wool his hand went toward the rim and stopped where the half-beat had stopped it.

Under the floor \lore{the carried fire} gave four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer without a name for three hundred and eighty-four days. \lore{The Afterdrop} waited beyond the warm room the boy could not reach, past a rewarming nobody in this house would perform; and the infant still wanted the crust lifted, the thread wiped and a hand on the pot handle, and got none of the three. Both were true. The second was louder. Neither won.

In the small hours \place{the towel} came out at the head of the bed as instruments come out when a case is done and before the next is called, with every gap left gap. The five held objects stood with the pot-crack resting and the hand-at-the-sill breath and the holding in the damper beside them; the keeping at the fold stood beside the crust's gap, the shelf's two gaps in the room whose floor does not touch this one, the gone pan's round ring and the field's empty place, with a finger between it and every other kept thing. Six hearings of \lore{the full note} stood beside and never among, the sixth the small flat knock out of \person[Dessa Silt] as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go and never among the sources, and the seventh place stayed empty and was not dug for; \lore[The Hearth Drawing]{The Drawing}'s flat knock stayed outside them on its own place. The heard bell of \lore{the Long Night} stayed in its square beside the told, four, four and a gap, not folded into any count. Nine record-first hands kept no tenth; nine stoppings kept no tenth before the seventh year at its very end; the burned slate stood at five, the shut-room tellings at two, the row at five askings with no witness and the well-saying at four shapings. The finding that eleven arithmetics are not one account sat on a place of its own, with no signature on the space and no doctrine standing up out of eleven; their eleven true subtractions rode locked in a box up the clay road whose key had gone up in a coat pocket and did not come back down and the boy had no key. No sixth hearing, tenth hand, tenth stopping, eighth opening, twelfth arithmetic or witnessing voice was supplied to make a row come out even, for a finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged.

Through the finger's-width door \place{the river} kept its note of \lore{water over water}, a low thing over a lower thing, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming, muffled under its covering as a voice is muffled by a shut door. Over it from bank to bank lay \lore{the winter skin}, a covering put on by the cold and taken off by the cold and entered nowhere, found where put on the first day of \place{Stone} and found again this afternoon and not made again, not spent on the week's work or on the going of the light and not made to buy a lesson under any roof. No hand had laid the skin, no payer stood under it and no source stood to be asked. A sign points to a payer; a covering left where it is, is weather. He kept the covering on a place of its own beside the note and put a finger between the two, so that no row came out even by his doing.

He filed answer three hundred and forty-four: exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the eleven readings, the bell's gap, the flat knock, the child's grey fingertips, the mother's cup, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the held breath, the two bites of iron, the river's skin, the widow's mark, the fourth direction nor the keeping at the fold. Stone kept its twenty-fifth day without a boundary drawn, the deep-winter month holding both its names, older women by weather and slatemen by Grathic Account-days, neither set right nor left in so many words; \lore{the Five} stood beyond and was not brought into a day that had not earned it. The count stood at three hundred and eighty-four exact, six months and two hundred and four days told by nobody who would have known what a number counted, the carried fire's fifth unpaid the same number, the payer unnamed, and Garl's name unspoken the same number. Ten findings were ten findings and not a rule; eleven arithmetics were eleven and not one account; four directions were four and not a witness. The hand lay flat on the fold on answer three hundred and forty-four, the tenth finding counted and still not placed, and the count went on under the roof in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill}, \place{Grath}.
