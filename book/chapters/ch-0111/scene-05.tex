\daymark{386}{Stone 27, Seventhday, Y.H. 813}

The hand had lain flat on the fold since the last going of the light, and the dark went out of the room by the slow subtraction that brings a morning to these parts --- not a lifting but a letting-up, as a banked fire lets up its heat under ash long before anybody lifts the lid. \person[Corin]{The boy} lay at the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf} under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill}, \place{Grath}, and took the field as it came to him in the old order, by the small things first, because a small thing cannot lie to a man the way a large thing can, being mostly weather. Three hundred and eighty-five answers stood on the sixth place with the three hundred and eighty-sixth not yet earned, and six months and two hundred and five days were behind him with a two hundred and sixth to be got through before the light went. Nothing in the room had been told what the day was for, and nothing in the room asked.

Three things lay where they had been laid and were found as found. The barley crust had gone harder and browner in the dry cold, stiff as old leather at the edge, lying two inches from the ear with its corner standing a hair off the wool; beside it the empty place kept its emptiness, with no second crust and no name spoken over it; the thread of drool at his lip had frozen in the dry kiln heat and was left unwiped, because nobody under any roof has ever been given an instruction about the thread, and a basket set down is a pot set down. Cold had come cheek, temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, and stopped there the way a line stops at the end of a ruled page, with no order to go farther and no order to go away. \lore{The Afterdrop} waited past a warming nobody in this house would perform. He knew what a face composed by cold looks like, and that it is not composure; knew that pink returning is the surface still lying over a cold core; knew that a patient cold as this cannot clot, will seem better before he is worse, and must be warmed from the outside and slowly, never hurried, never rubbed. A basket set level out of the draught where the heat came to him sideways was the careful rewarming he would choose and could not ask for. Under the wool the infant wanted the crust lifted, the thread wiped and a hand on the pot handle. Both were true. The second was louder. Neither won. The quarrel was the work, and he had stopped looking for a verdict years before he had learned to speak one.

Then the twelfth finding. The wool lay folded once in the crust's empty place, exactly where a hand had put it on the fifteenth, unchanged by the night: no second fold, no third crust, no name over the place, no new hand at the setting. Eleven mornings and eleven goings of the light had found it folded there before this one, and this was the twelfth finding; the hand that found it was the hand that had only ever read it, and twelve readings from that hand added no twelfth placing. A second placing would make a habit and a third a way, and both want a payer where none stands. He filed the finding beside the crust's gap, the shelf's two gaps in the room whose floor does not touch this one, the gone pan's round ring and the field's empty place, with a finger between it and every other kept thing. It was expressly not a joining of rooms, not a gift, for a gift wants a giver and nobody gave, not a payment, payer, sixth held object, habit, way, sign, hearing, mending, cause or rule. He did not lay it across the eleven readings, the four rooms with the same gap, or any of the four directions standing where they had been put. The far-off finger that would one day lay shelf and fold together and call the joining an account was still far off, not his, not come. Twelve was a number a man could say over a thing; it was not a hand under it.

Twelve findings were twelve findings, and the day had not earned a doctrine out of them.

\person{Sella Vail} came to \place{the hearth} before the basket, as she had come at both ends of every day for three years. She told the coals it was \lore{the thaw} and turned them with the back of a broken pot, and said “The house will keep it” where another woman would have said I, standing a moment to count a small intake, a hold and a letting-go toward no opening and figuring neither of the two gaps. Her finger lay along the top of the loaf shelf so that the charcoal tally on its underside stayed unread. Her lips moved along a two-gapped row and gave neither gap a number. She glanced once at the clay jar standing cold under \lore{lead glaze}, with the sweetish edge of it coming and going on a wrong wind past the pot board, and shut her hand half over nothing, the little-door burn on the back of her left hand closing on air. She did not touch \lore{the widow's mark} at her throat, the cord oiled where a working thumb had touched it ten thousand times: a morning is not an opening, seven openings in seven months are seven and not a rule, and no eighth was made due.

On \place{the pot board} the twice-mended pot was lifted thumb-first where the wire crossed the crack, before the weight came on the handle, so that the hairline of the original break showed between the old-brass staple and the tighter wire. “Leave the crack showing. People trust a thing they can see has been handled,” she told the pot. “If it's worth mending it's worth mending twice. The second time is the real one,” she told the fire. Both proverbs had been \person{Garl Vail}'s before the man was gone and had been improved in the keeping, and were very likely not as good as he had said them; they went to the pot and the fire and never to a person, because a thing said to a person becomes a thing the Court can enter. Repair stayed apart from restoration: a restored thing hides the hand that handled it and a mended thing leaves that hand showing. The dressed grain she turned once under wool with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child, lifting no figure and taking no handful. She set the pot rim two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat and no look toward \place{the bed}. The half-beat was the distance between intention and arrest, bought at nothing and not a gift. Inside the wool his hand went toward the rim and stopped where the half-beat had stopped it; the stopping was his and the interval was not a reach.

Under the floor \lore{the carried fire} gave four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer without a name for three hundred and eighty-five days. Then Sella took him down the nineteen steps at the half-pace she had kept from every open slate for three years, correcting the tilt at every turn of the landing before it settled into the wicker. “Keep his ear covered,” she told the wool. To the wind off \place{the river} she said nothing, because a wind is owed no instruction it cannot keep. The instruction went to the wool, to the wind and to the fire she would bank against their coming back, and never to the basket. She set him under the roof at the going of the light and never in \place{the Debt Loft}; that was the grammar of the house and wanted no announcement.

Out in \place{the Clay Yard} the morning went about its ordinary tending and left its accounts where they lay. Nothing from the eleven arithmetics standing behind the household, nor from the keeping found at the fold, had crossed under the roof as sign, lesson, mending, cause, payment or payer, and nothing crossed out here either. The yard went around its seven walked-around places with a look and a wide step and no number: the clean dark line at \place{the Vail corner} with its finer grey hairline that the house called the thaw, unswept and thickened in the month's dry cold, carrying the faint sour tang of \lore{Ash} up off it where no broom had gone in two years; the space beneath \place{the lowest rack} where \lore{the broken birth-slates} lay and were entered by nobody; the round ring at \place{the rack end} where the shallow clay pan had once stood, keeping no scrape, no crumb, no print, no chip and no word; the field's empty place out on \place{the worked strips}; \place{the trough} with its skin drawn edge to edge; the silt-clouded plate of ice on \place{the flat stone by the well}, set a little out of the path of feet; and, widest of all, \place{the returning-line place} where a hand had gone in on \place{Seed} 15 and had not come out. The going-around there was so wide that her elbow cut the corner and the basket canted the other way to balance it, and she righted it without looking down. Once. Not twice. A second going-around would have made a figure of once, and a figure wants a payer standing under it and none stood. The satisfaction of a field gone around as found and left as found cost nothing, which was how the boy knew it was not peace.

The three coverings stayed where the weather had put them, with a finger between every pair. The white frost was gone off the north lip of \place{the yard well}; the skin lay drawn edge to edge across the trough; the plate of ice clouded with silt stood on its flat stone out of the way of feet. Weather entered nowhere and was not made a cause, a sign, a payment or a promise. Beyond the yard the river kept its low note of \lore{water over water}, a low thing over a lower thing, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming; over it from bank to bank since the afternoon of \place{Stone} 1 lay \lore{the winter skin}, a covering put on by the cold and taken off by the cold and entered nowhere, found where put on the first day of the month and found again this morning and not made again, not spent on the week's work and not made to buy a lesson under any roof. No hand had laid the skin, no payer stood under it and no source stood to be asked. A sign points to a payer; a covering left where it is, is weather. He kept the note and the covering on separate places with a finger between them, so that no row came out even by his doing.

In the small hours \place{the towel} came out at the head of the bed as instruments come out when a case is done and before the next is called, with every gap left gap. The five held objects stood with the pot-crack resting and the hand-at-the-sill breath and the damper-holding beside them, each on a place of its own with a finger between every pair; the keeping at the fold stood beside them and was expressly not a tenth held object, not a sixth, not a rule. Six hearings of \lore{the full note} stood beside and never among, the sixth the small flat knock out of \person[Dessa Silt] as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go and never among the sources; the seventh place stayed empty and was not dug for. \lore[The Hearth Drawing]{The Drawing}'s flat knock stayed outside them on its own place, not a seventh and not a filling of the empty place, for a hearing wants a source standing and that room was seen from a basket and not entered. The heard bell of \lore{the Long Night} stayed in its square beside the told, four, four and a gap, not folded into any count. Nine record-first hands kept no tenth; nine stoppings kept no tenth before the seventh year now at its very end; \lore{The burned-slate word} stood at five, the shut-room tellings at two, the row at five askings with no witness and the well-saying at four shapings with no fifth. The four rooms with the same gap were held apart with a finger between every pair, four likenesses and not one account, an account wanting a hand to lay them together and a payer under all four, and no such hand standing and no such payer. Stone 23's fourth direction stood where put on a place of its own beside the Ash 5 joining and the Ash 17 and Ash 23 directions, did not repeat and bought no lesson; no ownerless voice came over \place{the boards} this morning, and none of the four grew into a witness by standing still.

Eleven arithmetics of the year --- the three of \place{Frost}, the first, second and third of \place{Bone}, the first, second and third of \place{Ash}, and Stone's first and second --- stood behind one unsigned figure: one in twelve, read twice over the basket on Stone 20 and sent twice, once for the moot and once for the late households. \person{Berr Clay}'s eleven true subtractions rode locked in his box up \place{the clay road}, the key gone up in a coat pocket that did not come back down and the boy having no key. The figure read was still not the figure paid, no signature on the space between, no doctrine standing up out of eleven, eleven readings eleven and not one account. The finding that eleven are not one account sat on a place of its own with no hand under it. No sixth hearing, tenth hand, tenth stopping, eighth opening or twelfth arithmetic was supplied to make a row come out even, for a finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged. Twelve findings were twelve findings and not a rule; the far-off finger was far off and had not come.

Through \place{the finger's-width door} the river kept its note of water over water under the winter skin, muffled as a voice is muffled by a shut door. The hand outside the wool came up an inch toward it --- did not reach, and the inch is not a reach --- took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole on the note's own finishing under the covering, let the intake go as the note ended and came straight down and laid itself flat against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. The note had done before the hand came down: the letting-go was neither early nor stayed, and the hand did not stay. Under the floor the fourth low pull was where it had been put and the fifth was silent, and the two sounds kept their places.

He filed answer three hundred and forty-seven: exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the eleven readings, the bell's gap, the flat knock, the child's grey fingertips, the mother's cup, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the held breath, the two bites of iron, the river's skin, the widow's mark, the fourth direction nor the keeping at the fold. Stone kept its twenty-seventh day without a boundary drawn, the deep-winter month holding both its names, older women by weather and slatemen by \lore{Grathic} Account-days, neither set right nor left in so many words. \lore{The Five} stood beyond and was not brought into a day that had not earned it; three mornings stood between this day and the month's last reading and were not brought in. The count stood at three hundred and eighty-six exact, six months and two hundred and six days told by nobody who would have known what a number counted, the carried fire's fifth unpaid the same number, its payer unnamed, and Garl's name unspoken the same number. The surgeon's small satisfaction in a field taken as found and left as found cost nothing, which was how he knew it was not peace; under it, louder, the infant wanted the crust lifted, the thread wiped and a hand on the pot handle, and got none of the three, both true, the second louder, neither winning, no verdict in the quarrel and none looked for. Twelve findings are twelve findings and not a habit; eleven arithmetics are eleven and not one account; four directions are four and not a witness. The hand lay flat on the fold on answer three hundred and forty-seven, the twelfth finding counted and still not placed, and the count went on under the roof in Low Kiln, Sill, Grath.
