## Chronicle

Three days the year's last working week ran itself out under the Vail roof, and the boy kept a count no roof could read. Stone twenty-five, twenty-six and twenty-seven came without a boundary drawn. Each morning the surgeon took the body in its old order: barley crust two inches from the ear with its corner a hair off the wool; the empty place beside it; the thread frozen and unwiped, because nobody under any roof has ever been given an instruction about the thread; cold stopping at cheek, temple and bare wrist-joint; the afterdrop waiting past a warming nobody would perform. He knew a face composed by cold is not composure, and that pink returning is the surface still lying over a cold core; he knew a cold patient cannot clot and must be warmed from the outside, slowly, never hurried, never rubbed. Under the wool the infant wanted the crust lifted, the thread wiped, a hand on the pot handle. Both true, the second louder, neither winning; the quarrel was the work.

On the twenty-fifth the wool was found folded in the crust's empty place a tenth time, and ten findings from a hand that only read added no tenth placing. Sella Vail went to the hearth before the basket, told the coals it was the thaw, said the house would keep it where another woman would say I, carried him down the nineteen steps, and set the pot rim two inches beyond his reach. At the kindling Tarn kept six where the house could check and the seventh at the lip, told the wood *Six counted. The next I don't count*, and brought his ear-sentence to the word that would call the loan and stopped. Jorra's bent finger went into the cold damper ahead of an unfinished sentence and came out without the extra holding. Answers 343 and 344; the count stood at 384.

On the twenty-sixth the fold was found an eleventh time. Ilva at the well answered the woman who asked what Stone's firings had taken: *Ask the pot what it cost. I keep the count, not the account* — her hand to the sleeve before her eyes to the asker, the notched strip unread. Answers 345 and 346; the count stood at 385.

On the twenty-seventh the fold was found a twelfth time, and twelve were still twelve. The yard went around its seven places, widest around the returning-line place, once and not twice; the pot's crack showed between old brass and tighter wire; the widow's mark was untouched. In the small hours the file came out and every gap was left a gap. Answers 347 and 348; the count stood at 386 exact, six months and two hundred and six days told by nobody, the fire's fifth unpaid and Garl's name unspoken the same number. The surgeon's satisfaction in a field taken as found and left as found cost nothing, which was how he knew it was not peace.
