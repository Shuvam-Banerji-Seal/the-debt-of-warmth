The afternoon came on without a boundary drawn, the light thinned from the boards a little at a time, so that no hour among them could have said where the morning had ended and the going of the light would begin. It left the room the way warmth leaves a pot set off the fire --- not in one go, but by degrees the hand cannot name until the hand is cold. \person[Corin]{The boy} lay at the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf} under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill}, \place{Grath}, and took the thin afternoon as a surgeon takes a first look into a field, by the small things first, because the small things do not lie and the large ones are mostly weather. Three hundred and forty-seven answers stood on the sixth place, the last of them filed in the dark that had just gone, and the three hundred and forty-eighth was not yet earned. Three hundred and eighty-six days stood exact behind him, six months and two hundred and six, with no end of a sixth month to make a figure of. Twelve findings were twelve findings at the fold, and the week had run out without a teaching under this roof about what a twelfth finding might be for. Nothing in the room had been told what the afternoon was for, and nothing in the room asked.

Outside, \person{Sella Vail} carried him down the nineteen steps at the half-pace she had kept from every open slate for three years, correcting the tilt at every turn of the landing before it had time to settle into the wicker, so that he felt the small correction before he felt the stair. The instruction went to the wool, to the river wind, to the fire she would bank against their coming back, and never to the basket. “Keep his ear covered,” she told the wool. To the wind she said nothing, because a wind is owed no instruction it cannot keep. A basket set down is a pot set down, and a pot is told where to stand and not why. He took the descent as a wound is taken apart, by the small things first: the scrape of a heel on the second step, the draught that came up at the fifth and went out again at the sixth, the particular hollow the basket made against her hip when she shifted her weight at the turn. Under the wool the infant wanted the tilt corrected the other way, toward the crust standing its hair off two inches from his ear, and wanted the thread at his lip wiped, and got neither. Both wants were true and the second was louder and neither won. That was the quarrel, and the quarrel was the work.

Out in \place{the Clay Yard} the afternoon went about its ordinary tending and left its accounts where they lay. Nothing from the eleven arithmetics standing behind the household, nor from the keeping found at the fold that morning, had crossed under the roof as sign, lesson, mending, cause, payment or payer, and nothing crossed out here either. The yard went around its seven walked-around places with a look and a wide step and no number: the clean dark line at \place{the Vail corner} with its finer grey hairline that the house called \lore{the thaw}, unswept and thickened in the month's dry cold, carrying the faint sour tang of \lore{Ash} up off it where no broom had gone in two years; the space beneath \place{the lowest rack} where \lore{the broken birth-slates} lay and were entered by nobody; the round ring at \place{the rack end} where the shallow clay pan had once stood, keeping no scrape, no crumb, no print, no chip and no word; the field's empty place out on \place{the worked strips}; \place{the trough} with its skin drawn edge to edge; the silt-clouded plate of ice on \place{the flat stone by the well}, set a little out of the path of feet; and, widest of all, \place{the returning-line place} where a hand had gone in on \place{Seed} 15 and had not come out. The going-around there was so wide that her elbow cut the corner and the basket canted the other way to balance it, and she righted it without looking down. Once. Not twice. A second going-around would have made a figure of once, and a figure wants a payer standing under it and none stood, so the step was taken and not entered. The satisfaction of a field gone around as found and left as found cost nothing, which was how the boy knew it was not peace.

The three coverings stayed where the weather had put them, with a finger between every pair. The white frost was gone off the north lip of \place{the yard well}; the skin lay drawn edge to edge across the trough; the plate of ice clouded with silt stood on its flat stone out of the way of feet. Weather entered nowhere and was not made a cause, a sign, a payment or a promise. Out on the worked strips the field kept its empty place and did not fill it. Beyond the yard \place{the river} kept its low note of \lore{water over water}, a low thing over a lower thing, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming; over it from bank to bank since the afternoon of \place{Stone} 1 lay \lore{the winter skin}, a covering put on by the cold and taken off by the cold and entered nowhere, found where put on the first day of the month and found again this afternoon and not made again, not spent on the week's work or on the going of the light and not made to buy a lesson under any roof. No hand had laid the skin, no payer stood under it and no source stood to be asked. A sign points to a payer; a covering left where it is, is weather. He kept the note and the covering on separate places with a finger between them, so that no row came out even by his doing.

The day's costs arrived as directions and went away as weather. A man down from \place{Cold Acre} shifted a hare across his shoulders and answered the question of its price with the weather which had carried the snow and could carry the price, and went off toward \place{the clay road} with the hare unweighed and no slate opened. A man with a cracked jar in a rope sling was sent to a pot, and the pot, being a pot, said nothing. To a woman with a basket of kindling who asked what the seventh year would want when it came, and to two fence-menders who said the thaw would be early and the thaw would be late, \person{Tarn} said “Yes, house” to each and kept the late, because a late thaw puts off the paying. He agreed so thoroughly that there was nowhere for an argument to find a foot. No collector came up or went down the clay road, and no bell came on any road, on this afternoon or on either of the two that stood behind it.

At \place{the kindling} the account stood in its old exactness. Six pieces lay in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check; the seventh lay at the lip beyond it, beside \person{Garl Vail}'s unforged collector sentence, with no name on the man who had turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on the debt. The small knife lay at the very edge of its sill. Tarn's hand passed over blade and sill and away as a man passes a hand over a tool put back where it belongs, and the coming away was the whole of the checking, with no breath held at the top of the reach. “Six counted. The next I don't count,” he told the wood, in the tone of a man stating the weather, and cut nothing, counted nothing, spent nothing. The Ash 14 held breath and the Bone 26 thumb's resting were found where put and not made again, beside the five held objects and the pot-crack resting, each on a place of its own with a finger between every pair, not a sixth held object, not a tenth stopping. He brought the ear-sentence up to the word that would call the loan and stopped there, the ninth stopping with no tenth before the seventh year now at its very end; the Seed 16 breath stayed unspent, the loan stayed uncalled, and he did not look along the road. His chest had been tight since the dark; the Ash was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line at the Vail corner, and the pale clip-ring at his left wrist caught what little light there was and gave it back, and his stroke went over the shallow bright patch of \place{the flagstones} without the half-check. A term ending is not a payment, not a forgiveness, not a freedom. The fear of being entered again for vagrancy stayed his, kept without a figure.

\person{Jorra Vail} came through the finger's-width door off \place{the Vail wheel-shed} with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bent little finger and white in a fist-crease where a hand had opened, and it was not the bone-coloured \lore{slip} of the wheel. Three or more men unnamed had gone low into \place{the back room} before first grey; he gave the house no name for them and did not say the room was cold. He kept his dusty forearms and his clean ones on one tray and parted them as a surgeon parts clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had been broken nor whether it had been a birth-slate, while the broken ones lay under the lowest rack and were entered by nobody. He spoke to the wheel before he spoke to any person: “It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady.” To a pot going past he said “You'll do,” in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either. He knew \lore{the full note} that pot would give three turns ahead, with air under it and a want in it, and kept the knowing on a tray of its own beside the hearings and never among them, withheld from any master come up from \place{High Kiln} who would call it early and sing a pot before its time. When Sella had not finished telling him the fire was low, his bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper} ahead of her sentence, took the cold out of the fingertip, moved the tongue a hair's breadth and was left standing, so that the end of her instruction arrived at the pot and the household straightened around the waiting as cloth straightens when a weight is lifted --- one piece of iron with two bites, the holding and the taking, with no \place{Grathic} word covering both mercies. The finger came out without leaving the extra holding of Ash 26 a second time; that holding had been gathered once and stood where nobody entered it.

“He's not Vail” stayed sealed as a lid nobody had tried to lift in three years. The promise of the boy's name to the men who burn slates was neither given nor withdrawn, and went on gathering interest against \lore{the burned-slate word} standing at five, the shut-room tellings at two, the row at five askings with no witness, the Ash 5 joining and the Ash 17, Ash 23 and Stone 23 directions standing where put --- none repeating, none buying a lesson, none joined to Jorra's promise, to the keeping at the fold or to the eleven readings. No collector came up or went down the clay road on any of the three days; the green and the shape stayed sightings with no eighth, and the year's last market stood beyond the week and was not brought in.

At the going of the light Sella carried him up the nineteen steps and never into \place{the Debt Loft}, set him on his bed under the roof at the low angle of the shelf, put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face and added no second fold. The thread stayed frozen and unwiped; the wool was found where put a twelfth time and was not made again. She went to the hearth before the basket and told the coals it was the thaw and turned them with the back of a broken pot, and said “The house will keep it” where another woman would have said I, standing a moment to count a small intake, a hold and a letting-go toward no opening and figuring neither of the two gaps. Her finger lay along the top of the loaf shelf so that the charcoal tally on its underside stayed unread; her lips moved along a two-gapped row and gave neither gap a number; she glanced once at the clay jar standing cold under \lore{lead glaze}, with the sweetish edge of it coming and going on a wrong wind past the pot board, and shut her hand half over nothing, the little-door burn on the back of her left hand closing on air. She did not touch \lore{the widow's mark} at her throat, the cord oiled where a working thumb had touched it ten thousand times: a going of the light and a bed are no openings, seven openings in seven months are seven and not a rule, and no eighth was made due.

On \place{the pot board} the twice-mended pot was lifted thumb-first where the wire crossed the crack, before the weight came on the handle, so that the hairline of the original break showed between the old-brass staple and the tighter wire. “Leave the crack showing. People trust a thing they can see has been handled,” she told the pot. “If it's worth mending it's worth mending twice. The second time is the real one,” she told the fire. Both proverbs had been Garl's before the man was gone and had been improved in the keeping, and were very likely not as good as he had said them; they went to the pot and the fire and never to a person, because a thing said to a person becomes a thing the Court can enter. Repair stayed apart from restoration: a restored thing hides the hand that handled it and a mended thing leaves that hand showing. The dressed grain she turned once under wool with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child, lifting no figure and taking no handful. She set the pot rim two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat and no look toward the bed. Inside the wool his hand went toward the rim and stopped where the half-beat had stopped it; the interval was bought at nothing and was not a gift, and the stopping was his. Under the floor \lore{the carried fire} gave four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer without a name for three hundred and eighty-six days.

In the small hours at the very end of the day \place{the towel} came out at the head of the bed as instruments come out when a case is done and before the next is called, with every gap left gap. He went over the field by the place of each thing, and checked every object and left every gap a gap. The twelve findings at the fold were twelve findings and not a placing, not a habit, way, sign, mending, cause, rule or payer. The five held objects stood with the pot-crack resting and the hand-at-the-sill breath and the damper-holding beside them, each on a place of its own with a finger between every pair, and the keeping stood beside them and was expressly not a tenth held object, not a sixth. Nine record-first hands kept no tenth; nine stoppings kept no tenth before the seventh year now at its very end; the burned-slate word stood at five, the shut-room tellings at two, the row at five askings with no sixth and no witness, the well-saying at four shapings with no fifth. The Ash 5 joining and the Ash 17, Ash 23 and Stone 23 directions stood each on its own place with a finger between every pair, none joined to Jorra's promise, to the keeping or to the eleven readings, none grown into a witness by standing still. The finding that eleven are not one account sat on a place of its own with no hand under it.

Six hearings of the full note stood beside and never among, the sixth the small flat knock out of \person[Dessa Silt] as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go and never among the sources; the seventh place stayed empty and was not dug for or filled. \lore[The Hearth Drawing]{The Drawing}'s flat knock stayed outside them on its own place, not a seventh and not a filling of the empty place, for a hearing wants a source standing and that room had been seen from a basket and not entered. The heard bell of \lore{the Long Night} stayed in its square beside the told, four, four and a gap, not folded into any count. The four rooms with the same gap were held apart with a finger between every pair --- the birth room in \place{House Silt}, this kiln-house, the room where the slate was read on market-day and the Drawing house seen two doors down --- four likenesses and not one account, an account wanting a hand to lay them together and a payer under all four, and no such hand standing and no such payer.

No sixth hearing, seventh opening, sixth held object, sixth telling, third shut-room telling, sixth asking, fifth shaping, tenth hand, tenth stopping or twelfth arithmetic was supplied to make a row come out even, for a finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged. Eleven arithmetics of the year --- the three of \place{Frost}, the first, second and third of \place{Bone}, the first, second and third of \place{Ash}, and Stone's first and second --- stood behind one unsigned figure: one in twelve, read twice over the basket on Stone 20 and sent twice, once for the moot and once for the late households. \person{Berr Clay}'s eleven true subtractions rode locked in his box up the clay road, the key gone up in a coat pocket that did not come back down and the boy having no key. The figure read was still not the figure paid, no signature on the space between, no doctrine standing up out of eleven, eleven readings eleven and not one account. Twelve findings were twelve findings and not a rule; the far-off finger that would one day lay shelf and fold together and call the joining an account was still far off, not his, not come.

Through \place{the finger's-width door} the river kept its note of water over water under the winter skin, muffled as a voice is muffled by a shut door, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming. The hand outside the wool came up an inch toward it --- did not reach, and the inch is not a reach --- took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole on the note's own finishing under the covering, let the intake go as the note ended and came straight down and laid itself flat against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. The note had done before the hand came down: the letting-go was neither early nor stayed, and the hand did not stay.

He filed answer three hundred and forty-eight: exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the eleven readings, the bell's gap, the flat knock, the child's grey fingertips, the mother's cup, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the held breath, the two bites of iron, the river's skin, the widow's mark, the fourth direction nor the keeping at the fold. Stone kept its twenty-fifth, twenty-sixth and twenty-seventh days without a boundary drawn, the deep-winter month holding both its names, older women by weather and slatemen by \lore{Grathic} Account-days, neither set right nor left in so many words. \lore{The Five} stood beyond and was not brought into a week that had not earned it; three mornings stood between this day and the month's last reading, and the twelfth arithmetic stood three mornings beyond the close, unbrought. The year's last working week ran out with nothing in it standing up and calling itself an account. Stone 28, 29 and 30 stood ahead, and the count went on toward them.

The count stood at three hundred and eighty-six exact, six months and two hundred and six days told by nobody who would have known what a number counted, the carried fire's fifth unpaid the same number, its payer unnamed, and Garl's name unspoken the same number. The surgeon's small clean satisfaction in a field taken as found and left as found cost nothing, which was how he knew it was not peace; under it, louder, the infant wanted the crust lifted, the thread wiped and a hand on the pot handle, and got none of the three, both true, the second louder, neither winning, no verdict in the quarrel and none looked for. Twelve findings are twelve findings and not a rule; eleven arithmetics are eleven and not one account; four directions are four and not a witness. The hand lay flat on the fold on answer three hundred and forty-eight, the crust's corner standing a hair off the wool, and the count went on under the roof in Low Kiln, Sill, Grath.
