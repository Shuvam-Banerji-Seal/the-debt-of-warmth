**Arc:** Arc 1, *The Cold Stone* — “a warm stone, already going cold.”

## Scene 1 — The Tenth Finding

- **Day and time:** Day 384, Stone 25, Fifthday, Y.H. 813. Small hours opening the day, then the morning inventory, the hearth grammar and the carry down into the Clay Yard; a second small-hours passage at the scene's end for the answer.
- **First line of the day:** the chapter's only `\dropcap` and its first `\daymark{384}{Stone 25, Fifthday, Y.H. 813}`. Open on the hand laid flat against the fold on answer **342** and the count exact at **383**, six months and two hundred and three days, carried out of Chapter 110. Do not recap the fourth direction or the eleven readings; simply find them in the file as the surgeon finds each thing on the towel.
- **Place:** the Vail kiln-house under the loaf shelf and at the hearth; the pot board; the nineteen steps; the Clay Yard and its seven walked-around places.
- **Present:** Corin/Dan, Sella Vail. Garl Vail in objects only: salt-stiff coat with its left-shoulder crease on the peg, hollow wheel-seat, clean dark line with its finer grey called the thaw thickened in the dry cold with the sour tang of Ash, twice-mended pot's hairline, unforged collector sentence at the kindling, uncalled tile debt. No Tarn, Jorra, Ilva, Hessa, Berr, collector, runner, market, Cross or bell.

### Beats, in order

1. The surgeon takes the body in its old order: leathery brown-edged barley crust two inches from the ear, corner a hair off the wool; cold stopping at cheek, temple and the bare wrist-joint where the wool rides back, as at the end of a line written out for it; pink returning is the surface still cold and not the core; a basket set level out of the draught where the heat comes sideways is the careful rewarming he would choose and cannot ask for; the afterdrop waits past a warming nobody here will perform. The infant's want of the crust lifted, the thread wiped and a hand on the pot handle quarrels without verdict — both true, the second louder, neither winning. The thread stays frozen and unwiped; nobody under any roof has ever been given an instruction about the thread.
2. **The tenth finding (T097/T060/T086, advanced, NOT paid).** The wool is found folded once in the crust's empty place, exactly where it was put on the fifteenth, unchanged by the night. No second fold, no third crust, no name spoken over the place. He files it beside the crust's gap, the shelf's two gaps, the gone pan's round ring and the field's empty place. Ten findings from a hand that only read add no tenth placing: a second placing would make a habit and a third a way, and both want a payer where none stands. He does not let ten likenesses stand up and call themselves an account. This is the chapter's one meaningful change and it buys nothing.
3. Sella comes to the hearth before the basket. She tells the coals it is the thaw, turns them with the back of a broken pot, says *The house will keep it* where another woman would say I, stands a moment on a small intake, a hold and a letting-go toward no opening and figures neither gap. Her finger lies along the top of the loaf shelf so the charcoal tally stays unread; her lips move along a two-gapped row and give neither gap a number; she glances once at the clay jar standing cold under lead glaze and shuts her hand half over nothing, the little-door burn on the back of her left hand closing on air. The widow's mark, oiled where a working thumb has touched it ten thousand times, is not touched: a morning is not an opening, and seven openings in seven months are seven and not a rule.
4. On the pot board the twice-mended pot is lifted thumb-first where the wire crosses the crack before the weight comes on the handle, hairline showing between old-brass staple and tighter wire. Both proverbs go to the pot and the fire and never to a person: *Leave the crack showing. People trust a thing they can see has been handled*; *If it's worth mending it's worth mending twice. The second time is the real one* — improved in the keeping and very likely not as good as the man thought them. Repair stays apart from restoration. The dressed grain is turned once under wool with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child, no figure and no handful. The pot's rim is set two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat and no look toward the bed; the interval is bought at nothing and is not a gift.
5. She carries him down the nineteen steps at the half-pace from every open slate, correcting the tilt at every turn before it settles into the wicker. The instruction goes to the wool, the river wind and the fire she will bank against their coming back, never to the basket. In the yard the seven walked-around places are gone around with a look and a wide step and no number, widest around the returning-line place where a hand went in on Seed 15 and did not come out, once and not twice: a second going-around would make a figure of once and a figure wants a payer standing under it.
6. The clean dark line at the Vail corner keeps its finer grey called the thaw, unswept and thickened, with the faint sour tang of Ash coming up where no broom has crossed in two years. The broken birth-slates beneath the lowest rack are entered by nobody; the round ring at the rack end keeps no scrape, crumb, print, chip or word. The gone white frost off the well's north lip, the skin across the trough and the silt-clouded plate of ice on its flat stone stay three coverings entered nowhere with a finger between every pair. Out on the worked strips the field keeps its empty place and does not fill it. The river keeps its winter skin on its own place beside water over water, found where put on the first day of the month and found again this morning, not made again, not spent.
7. Small hours: the file comes out on the towel at the head of the bed as instruments come out when a case is done, with every gap left gap. Six hearings stand beside and never among, seventh place empty, the Drawing's flat knock outside them; the heard bell stays in its square beside the told, four, four and a gap; the four rooms with the same gap stay held apart with a finger between every pair. He files answer **343** — exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the eleven readings, the bell's gap, the flat knock, the child's grey fingertips, the mother's cup, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the held breath, the two bites of iron, the river's skin, the widow's mark, the fourth direction nor the keeping at the fold. Count: **384 exact**, six months and two hundred and four days told by nobody; fire's fifth unpaid and Garl's name unspoken the same number. Five mornings stand between this day and Stone 30; do not bring the reading in.

- **Emotional turn:** from the plain relief of a body inventoried as found and left as found, to the small, held alarm that a double-digit count of findings is the very shape a doctrine would wear if a hand stood under it — and none stands. The surgeon's satisfaction costs nothing, which is how he knows it is not peace; underneath, the infant's louder want gets none of the three things it wants.
- **Sensory anchors:** leathery barley crust, hair-lifted corner, sour Ash tang, coals turned with a broken pot's back, lead-glaze green at the jar's mouth, little-door burn closing on air, brass staple and tighter wire, wet wool and woodsmoke, stiff well rope, river note low under its winter skin.
- **Threads advanced, none paid:** T097/T060/T086 (tenth finding); T071/T072 (384 exact, answer 343, six months 204 days); T061/T082 (answer filed; six hearings, seventh empty, Drawing's knock outside, heard bell beside told); T058/T079/T100 (hearth grammar, charcoal unread, widow's mark untouched, no eighth opening); T077 (grain covered, no handful); T009/T013/T092 and T098 (seven places, returning-line widest, once); T083/T085 (three coverings and the river's skin found where put); T081 (four rooms held apart); T088 (fire's fifth unpaid, payer unnamed); T015/T041/T047 held.
- **Foreshadowing planted, not paid:** the tenth finding gathers interest toward the far-off finger that will one day lay shelf and fold together and call the joining an account — that hand is not his and has not come. Stone 30's last reading gathers five mornings ahead without answering any held question. No sixth hearing, tenth hand, tenth stopping, eighth opening, twelfth arithmetic, witness, reader, payment, name for the green or the shape, third crust or ending.
- **Exact last image:** the hand lies flat on the fold on answer 343, the crust's corner standing its hair off the wool, the tenth finding counted and still not placed; the count goes on under the roof in Low Kiln, Sill, Grath.

## Scene 2 — Six Counted, the Seventh Held

- **Day and time:** Day 384, Stone 25, Fifthday. Afternoon through the going of the light, then the small hours closing the day with answer 344. No new dated heading.
- **Place:** the Clay Yard; the kindling; the Vail wheel-shed edge and the cold damper; the kiln-house bed, hearth, pot board and towel.

### Beats, in order

1. The afternoon goes on without a boundary drawn, the light subtracted slowly from the boards. The yard goes around its seven places once and not twice. The day's costs are asked as directions and answered with weather, the pot and the house, leaving no entry: hare-carrier's price to the weather that carried the snow, cracked jar to a pot that says nothing, kindling woman's question about the seventh year and the fence-menders' early and late thaw to *Yes, house* — Tarn keeping the late because it puts off the paying, agreeing so thoroughly that no argument finds a foot.
2. At the kindling six pieces lie in the worn thumb-mark where the house can check, the seventh at the lip beside Garl's unforged collector sentence — no name on the man who turned back at the frozen ford, no figure on the debt. The small knife lies at the very edge of its sill. Tarn's hand passes over blade and sill and away with no breath held at the top of the reach; the coming away is the whole of the checking. *Six counted. The next I don't count* — cut nothing, count nothing, spend nothing.
3. The Ash 14 held breath and the Bone 26 thumb's resting are found where put and not made again, beside the five held objects and the pot-crack resting with a finger between every pair — not a sixth held object, not a tenth stopping. He brings the ear-sentence to the word that would call the loan and stops there as a remembered ninth stopping with no tenth before the seventh year now at its very end. The Seed 16 breath stays unspent; the loan stays uncalled; he does not look along the clay road. Chest tight since the dark, Ash in the knuckle creases the colour of the clean dark line, pale clip-ring at the left wrist catching and giving back the little light, stroke over the bright flagstones without the half-check. A term ending is not a payment, a forgiveness or a freedom; the fear of being entered again for vagrancy stays his, kept without a figure.
4. Jorra comes through the finger's-width door off the wheel-shed with meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bent little finger and white in a fist-crease where a hand had opened — not the bone-coloured slip of the wheel. Three or more men unnamed have gone low into the back room before first grey; he gives the house no name for them and no word that the room is cold. Dusty forearms and clean ones are kept on one tray and parted as clot from vessel; he asks neither which slate had been broken nor whether it was a birth-slate while the broken ones under the lowest rack stay entered by nobody.
5. He speaks to the wheel before any person: *It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady.* To a pot going past, *You'll do* — no welcome, no unkindness. He knows the full note it will give three turns ahead, with air under it and a want in it, and withholds the knowing from any master come up from High Kiln who would call it early and sing a pot before its time. The boy keeps that knowing on a tray beside the hearings and never among them; no source stands under it and it is not a seventh hearing.
6. When Sella has not finished telling him the fire is low, his bent finger goes into the cold iron of the damper ahead of her sentence, takes the cold out of the fingertip, moves the tongue a hair's breadth and is left standing, so that the end of her instruction arrives at the pot and the household straightens around the waiting as cloth straightens when a weight is lifted. One piece of iron has two bites, the holding and the taking, and no Grathic word covers both mercies. The finger comes out without leaving the extra holding of Ash 26 a second time — gathered once, standing where nobody enters it. Neither looks at the other; a thing said aloud becomes a thing the Court can enter.
7. *He's not Vail* stays sealed. The promise of the boy's name to the men who burn slates is neither given nor withdrawn; the burned slate stands at five, shut-room tellings at two, row at five askings with no witness, Ash 5 joining, Ash 17, Ash 23 and Stone 23 directions standing where put, none repeating, none buying a lesson, all gathering interest against the promise without joining it.
8. At the going of the light Sella carries him up the nineteen steps and never into the Debt Loft, sets him at the shelf's low angle, puts the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face and adds no second fold. Thread stays frozen and unwiped; wool found where put and not made again. Hearth grammar renewed: fire before basket, coals told the thaw and turned with a broken pot's back, *The house will keep it*, intake/hold/letting-go unfigured, finger on the shelf so charcoal stays unread, lips on a two-gapped row unfigured, one glance at the clay jar with the hand half over nothing, widow's mark untouched. Both proverbs to pot and fire, crack showing; dressed grain turned under wool, no figure, no handful; pot rim two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat and no look toward the bed. Under the floor four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood, payer unnamed for 384 days.
9. Small hours: file out on the towel; answer **344** filed, exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, laid across neither the eleven readings, the bell's gap, the flat knock, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the held breath, the two bites of iron, the river's skin, the widow's mark, the fourth direction nor the keeping at the fold. Count stays **384 exact**, six months and two hundred and four days; fire's fifth unpaid and Garl's name unspoken the same number. Stone 25 kept without a boundary drawn, month holding both names.

- **Emotional turn:** from the exactness of a knife checked and not touched, through the nearly invisible holding of a finger in cold iron, to the comprehension that the household's straightening around a silence is itself a keeping no slate can enter — and that the loan can hear and has never been called. The fear of a seventh year ending without freedom is kept, not resolved.
- **Sensory anchors:** worn thumb-mark, seventh notch at the lip, small knife at sill edge, Ash in knuckle creases, pale clip-ring, meal-fine grey slate dust white in a fist-crease, sweetish lead-glaze edge on a bad wind, cold iron of the damper taking the cold out of a fingertip, banked coals, sour Ash ridge.
- **Threads advanced, none paid:** T036/T076/T006 (six and seventh, checkings not made again, ninth stopping with no tenth, Seed 16 breath unspent, loan uncalled, fear kept); T062/T035/T031 (dust, forearms parted, damper's two bites, Ash 26 not repeated, *he's not Vail* sealed, promise ungiven); T035/T031/T065/T095 and T101 (four directions held, not repeated); T058/T079/T100 (hearth grammar, charcoal unread, mark untouched); T077, T088, T081, T061/T082 held; T071/T072 (answer 344, 384 exact).
- **Foreshadowing planted, not paid:** Tarn's term at its very end with the loan uncalled and the fear of being entered for vagrancy; Jorra's promise gathering interest against the burned slate and the four directions; the damper's double bite with no Grathic word covering both mercies; the man at the frozen ford still unnamed. - **First line of the day:** the third dated `\daymark{386}{Stone 27, Seventhday, Y.H. 813}`, in the first scene of Day 386 only. No recap; no likeness from yard or shed crosses under the roof as sign, lesson, mending, cause, payment or payer.
- **Place:** Vail kiln-house bed, hearth, pot board; nineteen steps; Clay Yard.
- **Present:** Corin/Dan, Sella Vail; Garl Vail in objects. No Ilva, Tarn, Jorra, Hessa, Berr, collector, runner, market, Cross or bell.

### Beats, in order

1. Surgeon's inventory in the old order, with the added clinical knowing: a cold patient cannot clot, will seem better before he is worse, must be warmed from outside and slowly, never hurried, never rubbed. The thread stays frozen and unwiped; nobody has ever been given an instruction about the thread. Infant's want quarrels without verdict, second louder.
2. **Twelfth finding (T097/T060/T086, advanced, NOT paid):** the wool found folded once in the crust's empty place, unchanged by the night. Twelve findings add no twelfth placing; a second placing would make a habit and a third a way and both want a payer where none stands. Filed beside crust's gap, shelf's two gaps, gone pan's ring and field's empty place; expressly not a joining, gift, payment, payer, tenth finding made into a habit, way, sign, hearing, mending, cause or rule; not laid across the eleven readings, the four rooms' gap or any of the four directions. Far-off finger still far off, not his, not come.
3. Sella's morning grammar unchanged: fire before basket, coals told the thaw, turned with a broken pot's back, *The house will keep it*, intake/hold/letting-go with neither gap figured, finger on the shelf so the charcoal stays unread, lips on a two-gapped row, one glance at the clay jar with the hand half over nothing and the burn closing on air, widow's mark untouched — a morning is not an opening, seven openings in seven months are seven and not a rule, no eighth made due. Both proverbs to pot and fire, repair apart from restoration, crack showing between old-brass staple and tighter wire; grain turned under wool without figure or handful; pot rim two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat.
4. Carry down at the half-pace, instruction to wool, river wind and fire, never basket; seven walked-around places, returning-line widest, once and not twice; clean dark line and finer grey thaw unswept with sour Ash; broken birth-slates under lowest rack entered by nobody; round ring at rack end keeping no scrap; three coverings weather entered nowhere; field's empty place unfilled; river's winter skin found where put beside water over water, not made again.
5. Small hours: file out on the towel, every gap left gap. Five held objects with pot-crack resting, hand-at-sill breath and damper-holding beside them, each on a place of its own with a finger between; the keeping beside them and expressly not a tenth held object. Six hearings beside and never among, seventh empty, Drawing's knock outside; heard bell beside told; four rooms' gap held apart. Eleven arithmetics behind one unsigned figure — three of Frost, first, second and third of Bone, first, second and third of Ash, Stone's first and Stone's second — figure read still not figure paid, no signature, no doctrine out of eleven, eleven true subtractions locked in Berr's box up the clay road with the key gone up in a coat pocket that does not come back down and the boy having no key.
6. Answer **347** filed, exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, laid across neither the eleven readings, the bell's gap, the flat knock, the child's grey fingertips, the mother's cup, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the held breath, the two bites of iron, the river's skin, the widow's mark, the fourth direction nor the keeping at the fold. Count **386 exact**, six months and two hundred and six days told by nobody; fire's fifth unpaid and Garl's name unspoken the same number. Three mornings to Stone 30, not brought in; the Five stand beyond.

- **Emotional turn:** from the daily exactness of the twelfth finding to the quiet recognition that a count has reached the number the week will close on while every held thing remains unspent — the satisfaction of a field taken as found and left as found, which costs nothing and so is not peace.
- **Sensory anchors:** leathery crust darkening in dry cold, frozen thread, sour Ash tang, broken-pot back on coals, lead glaze and the little-door burn, brass staple and wire, stiff rope, river under skin, locked box up the clay road (only as a fact of the holding offstage).
- **Threads advanced, none paid:** T097/T060/T086 (twelfth finding), T071/T072 (answer 347, 386 exact), T058/T079/T100, T077, T009/T013/T092, T098, T083/T085, T081, T061/T082, T088; T015/T041/T047 held.
- **Foreshadowing planted, not paid:** eleven arithmetics gather toward a twelfth on Stone 30 without answering any held question; the keeping's twelve findings gather toward the far-off hand; Stone 30 and the Five stand unbrought. No sixth hearing, tenth hand, tenth stopping, eighth opening, twelfth arithmetic, witness, reader, payment, third crust or ending.
- **Exact last image:** the hand lies flat on the fold on answer 347 with the twelfth finding counted and not placed; the count goes on under the roof in Low Kiln, Sill, Grath.

## Scene 6 — The Count Goes On

- **Day and time:** Day 386, Stone 27, Seventhday. Afternoon through the going of the light, then the small hours at the very end of the day with answer 348 and the chapter close. No new dated heading.
- **Place:** Clay Yard and its seven places; kindling; wheel-shed edge and damper; kiln-house bed, hearth, pot board, towel; the finger's-width door and the river beyond.
- **Present:** Corin/Dan, Sella Vail, Tarn, Jorra Vail in the afternoon; Garl Vail in objects. Walk-ons: hare-carrier, cracked-jar man, kindling woman, two fence-menders. No Ilva, Hessa, Berr, collector, green-mouthed woman, foundling, market, Cross or bell.

### Beats, in order

1. Sella carries him down at the half-pace, instruction to wool, river wind and fire, never basket, level and out of draught; the yard goes around its seven places with a look and a wide step and no number, widest around the returning-line place where a hand went in on Seed 15 and did not come out, once and not twice, because a going-around twice would make a figure of once and a figure wants a payer standing under it.
2. Clean dark line and finer grey thaw unswept with sour Ash; broken birth-slates under lowest rack entered by nobody; round ring at rack end with no scrape, crumb, print, chip or word; gone white frost, trough skin and silt-clouded plate of ice on the flat stone stay three coverings entered nowhere with a finger between every pair; field keeps its empty place; river's winter skin found where put on the first day of the month and found again this afternoon, not spent on the week's work or the going of the light and not made to buy a lesson. A sign points to a payer; a covering left where it is is weather and not a promise.
3. At the kindling the account stands in its old exactness: six in the worn thumb-mark, seventh at the lip beside Garl's unforged collector sentence, no name on the man at the frozen ford, no figure on the debt. Tarn's hand over blade and sill and away with no breath held at the top of the reach; *Six counted. The next I don't count*; nothing cut, counted or spent. Ash 14 breath and Bone 26 thumb's resting found where put and not made again; ear-sentence to the calling word and stopped as a remembered ninth stopping with no tenth before the seventh year now at its very end; Seed 16 breath unspent, loan uncalled, no look along the clay road. Chest tight, Ash in knuckles, pale clip-ring, stroke over bright flagstones without the half-check. A term ending is not a payment, a forgiveness or a freedom; fear of being entered again for vagrancy stays his.
4. Jorra through the finger's-width door with meal-fine grey dust white in a fist-crease, not bone-coloured slip; men unnamed gone low into the back room before first grey, no name and no word that the room is cold. Forearms parted as clot from vessel; wheel and pot spoken to before any person; full note known three turns ahead with air under it and a want in it, withheld from a High Kiln master. Bent finger into the cold damper ahead of Sella's unfinished sentence, two bites of iron, household straightening around the waiting, finger out without the extra holding of Ash 26 a second time.
5. *He's not Vail* sealed; promise neither given nor withdrawn, gathering interest against burned slate at five, shut-room tellings at two, row at five askings with no witness, Ash 5 joining and Ash 17, Ash 23 and Stone 23 directions — none repeating, none buying a lesson, none joined to the keeping or the eleven readings. No collector came up or down the clay road on any of the three days.
6. At the going of the light: carry up, bed under roof not Debt Loft, fold righted without a look at his face, no second fold, thread frozen and unwiped, wool found where put a twelfth time and not made again. Hearth grammar renewed: fire before basket, coals told the thaw and turned with broken pot's back, *The house will keep it*, intake/hold/letting-go unfigured, finger on shelf so charcoal stays unread, lips on a two-gapped row unfigured, one glance at clay jar with hand half over nothing and burn closing on air, widow's mark untouched because a going of the light and a bed are no openings. Twice-mended pot lifted thumb-first with hairline showing and both proverbs to pot and fire, repair apart from restoration; grain turned under wool without figure or handful; pot rim set two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat and no look toward the bed; inside the wool his hand goes toward the rim and stops where the half-beat stopped it, and that interval is bought at nothing and is not a gift. Four low pulls under the floor and the silence where the fifth should have stood, payer unnamed for 386 days.
7. Small hours at the very end: the towel comes out as instruments come out when a case is done, every gap left gap. Check every object and leave every gap a gap: twelve findings at the fold and not a placing; five held objects with pot-crack resting, hand-at-sill breath and damper-holding beside them and the keeping expressly not a tenth held object; nine hands with no tenth; nine stoppings with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end; burned slate at five, shut-room tellings at two, row at five askings with no sixth, well-saying at four shapings with no fifth; Ash 5 joining and Ash 17, Ash 23 and Stone 23 directions with a finger between every pair, none joined to Jorra's promise, the keeping or the eleven readings; finding that eleven are not one account on a place of its own; six hearings beside and never among, sixth the small flat knock out of Dessa Silt as the men lifted her filed where a thing out of a person must go and never among the sources, seventh place empty and not dug for, Drawing's knock outside on its own place; heard bell in its square beside the told, four, four and a gap; four rooms with the same gap held apart, four likenesses and not one account, an account wanting a hand to lay them together and a payer under all four and no such hand standing and no such payer. No sixth hearing, seventh opening, sixth held object, sixth telling, third shut-room telling, sixth asking, fifth shaping, tenth hand, tenth stopping or twelfth arithmetic supplied to make a row come out even, for a finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged. Eleven arithmetics behind one unsigned figure, figure read still not figure paid, no signature, no doctrine out of eleven, eleven true subtractions locked in Berr's box up the clay road, key gone up in a coat pocket that does not come back down and the boy having no key.
8. Surgeon's small clean satisfaction in a field taken as found and left as found costs nothing, which is how he knows it is not peace; under it, louder, the infant wants crust lifted, thread wiped and a hand on the pot handle, and gets none of the three, both true, second louder, neither winning, no verdict in the quarrel and none looked for. Through the finger's-width door the river keeps its note of water over water under its winter skin, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming, muffled as a voice is muffled by a shut door. The hand outside the wool comes up an inch toward it — does not reach, and the inch is not a reach — takes a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, holds the interval whole on the note's own finishing under the skin, lets the intake go as the note ended and comes straight down and lays itself flat against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. The note had done before the hand came down: letting-go neither early nor stayed, and the hand did not stay.
9. He files answer **348**: exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the eleven readings, the bell's gap, the flat knock, the child's grey fingertips, the mother's cup, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the held breath, the two bites of iron, the river's skin, the widow's mark, the fourth direction nor the keeping at the fold. Stone keeps its twenty-fifth, twenty-sixth and twenty-seventh days without a boundary drawn, the month holding both names, older women by weather and slatemen by Account-days, neither set right nor left in so many words. Count stands at **three hundred and eighty-six exact**, six months and two hundred and six days told by nobody who would have known what a number counted, fire's fifth unpaid the same number and Garl's name unspoken the same number. Twelve findings are twelve findings and not a rule; eleven arithmetics are eleven and not one account; four directions are four and not a witness. The year's last working week runs out with nothing in it standing up and calling itself an account. Stone 28, 29 and 30 stand ahead; Stone 30's last reading and twelfth arithmetic gathers three mornings beyond the close, unbrought; the Five stand beyond that. Forward, not closed.

- **Emotional turn:** from the surgeon's exact, unsentimental inventory of a week held unchanged, through the infant's unappeased want, to the laying down of a tool on a fold that will be found by another hand — no verdict, no payment, no ending, only the count going on toward a reading nobody under this roof has earned yet.
- **Sensory anchors:** frozen thread, dry kiln heat, sour Ash ridge, stiff rope, silt-clouded plate of ice, worn thumb-mark and seventh notch, pale clip-ring, grey dust white in a fist-crease, cold damper iron, banked coals and four pulls with the fifth missing, leathery crust, river note under winter skin, towel at the bed's head.
- **Threads advanced, none paid:** T097/T060/T086 (twelfth finding), T071/T072 (answer 348, 386 exact), T061/T082 (answer; hearings, bell, Drawing's knock), T058/T079/T100, T077, T036/T076/T006, T062/T035/T031, T035/T031/T065/T095 and T101, T009/T013/T092 and T098, T083/T085, T081, T088; T015/T041/T047 held; T012 and T093/T094/T096 carried.
- **Foreshadowing planted, not paid:** Stone 30's twelfth arithmetic, Berr's figure read twice and denied with *your house*, Hessa's slate carried up unread, the bell's four-four-and-a-gap, and the year's last market gather three mornings ahead without answering any held question; the keeping, the four rooms' gap, the eleven subtractions in the locked box and Jorra's promise all gather toward a far-off hand that is not his and has not come; Tarn's term stands at its end with the fear of vagrancy and the loan uncalled; the green and the shape remain sightings with no eighth. Paid off: nothing.
- **Exact last image:** the hand lies flat on the fold on answer 348 with the crust's corner standing a hair off the wool and the count going on under the roof in Low Kiln, Sill, Grath.
