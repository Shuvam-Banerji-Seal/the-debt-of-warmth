## Chronicle

Three days came under the Vail roof and the house kept them in loaves pushed a finger's width and stopped. No market, Cross, bell or runner came on Silt 2, 3 or 4. The boy carried four hundred and twenty-six, four hundred and twenty-seven and four hundred and twenty-eight. Each morning the surgeon took the body in the old order and the infant wanted the crust lifted, the thread wiped and a hand on the pot handle. Both were true, the second louder, neither won, and the quarrel was the work.

At both ends of all three days Sella came to the hearth before the basket, told the coals it was the thaw and said *The house will keep it* to the fire. Her finger lay along the shelf's top so the charcoal tally stayed unread; she lifted the twice-mended pot thumb-first, and both proverbs went to the pot and fire. She turned the dressed grain under wool, set the rim two inches from the reaching hand and never touched the widow's mark, for a morning is not an opening and seven openings are not a rule. On the third morning she weighed a fist of barley and set it back, saying no number.

At the kindling Tarn kept six pieces in the worn thumb-mark and the seventh at the lip, and told the wood *Six counted. The next I don't count*; his sentence about an ear stopped at the word that would call the loan. At the wheel-shed Jorra parted dusty from clean as clot from vessel and withheld the full note from any master who would call it early. The two quiet men stayed unjoined.

In the afternoon of the third day a neighbour with a pot reached past the bare board before her eyes found the basket, traced the shelf's top where the charcoal lay beneath, and let her finger go, unseen, toward the empty place at the boy's ear, stopping a finger's width short. She named the old gap a thing the month's turn brought in and the empty place a child's appetite in one breath, made two rooms one absence without seeing it, and went off with her pot. The boy filed it on a place of its own beside the far-off finger — a shape, advanced and not paid, with no joining, name, figure or payer standing.

Costs went away as weather and the river's softening was called weather. Six answers, four hundred and twenty-seven to four hundred and thirty-two, stood worth nothing at two inches of air and not one a doctrine. The count reached four hundred and twenty-eight exact, told by nobody; the fire's fifth was unpaid and Garl's name unspoken. In the small hours the towel came out as instruments do when a case is done. The hand went up an inch toward the river's note — the inch not a reach — took a small intake and came down flat on the fold. There was no next hand in the room.
