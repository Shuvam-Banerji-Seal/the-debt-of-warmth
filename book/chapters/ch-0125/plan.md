# Beat sheet — Chapter 125: *A Finger's Width Short*

**Days 426–428, Silt 2–4, Y.H. 814, Secondday to Fourthday. Normal mode, six scenes, two per day. Count 426 exact at the opening hand on answer 426; 428 exact at the close on answer 432. Six honest answers 427–432 on the sixth place, two to each day, not one a doctrine.**

Opening state carried in, not restated on the page: the hand laid flat on the fold on answer 426 and no next hand in the room; count 425, six months and two hundred and forty-five days told by nobody; three readings of the year behind one unsigned figure, three being three and not one account, the figure read still not the figure paid, the key gone up in a coat pocket that never comes back down; Berr up the clay road believing he did a kindness, Hessa's slate unread, Ilva's count in wood; the two market sightings on separate trays with no eighth; the offered figure, the well-asking, the second man's turned slate and direction, the clerk's entered figure, the fifth shaping, Tarn's withheld sentence, Jorra's promise, the softening at the river's skin, the called-gone grey, the left-in-place ridge and the keeping at the fold all found where put; six hearings with the seventh empty and the Drawing's knock outside; nine hands and nine stoppings with no tenth; seven openings in seven months and not a rule; the carried fire's fifth unpaid and Garl's name unspoken the same number. Silt 10 (Day 434), the month's first reading and the year's fourth, waits six mornings beyond the close as a date and is not brought.

The house takes up its grammar as though nothing had turned. Nothing from Ilmen 29, 30 or Silt 1 crosses under the roof as sign, lesson, mending, cause, payment or payer. No recap. No present-tense break in this chapter and no `\memory{}` tag; the old life rides the surgeon's register alone, in past tense, untagged.

## Scene 1 — The Second Morning of Silt

- **Day and time.** Day 426, Silt 2, Y.H. 814, Secondday. Small hours at the very opening, then first grey and the morning's work.
- **Place.** The Vail kiln-house under the roof at the low angle of the loaf shelf; the hearth and pot board; the nineteen steps down; the Clay Yard; the kindling and flagstones; the wheel-shed edge.
- **Present.** Corin/Dan (carried), Sella Vail, Tarn, Jorra Vail; Garl Vail in objects only (salt-stiff coat, hollow wheel-seat, clean dark line with its finer grey, the twice-mended pot, the unforged collector sentence). Ilva, Hessa, Berr offstage with their keepings found where put. Walk-ons at the yard grammar, unnamed and interiorless.
- **Beats, in order.**
  1. Only `\dropcap` of the chapter and first dated `\daymark{426}{Silt 2, Y.H. 814, Secondday}`. Open in the small hours on the hand laid flat against the fold on answer 426, the count exact at 425, six months and two hundred and forty-five days told by nobody, and no next hand in the room. Do not recap the reading or the sightings; the room holds only its own objects.
  2. The surgeon takes the body in the old order, in the order it arrives: horn-hard brown-edged crust two inches from the ear with its corner a hair off the wool; the fold found where put in the empty place, still a finding and not a placing; the thread stiff and unwiped because nobody under any roof has ever been given an instruction about the thread; cold stopped at cheek, temple and the bare wrist-joint where the wool rides back as at the end of a line written out for it; the Afterdrop waiting past a warming nobody here will perform. The infant's three wants — crust lifted, thread wiped, hand on the pot handle — quarrel without verdict, both true, the second louder, neither winning; the quarrel is the work.
  3. One untagged past-tense fragment from the surgeon's register is earned by the horn-hard crust and the cold joint — the old life's familiarity with a body cooling where a warm room says it is warm — written in past tense, no present-tense break, no `\memory{}` tag. It says nothing about the white room and pays nothing (T004).
  4. Sella comes to the hearth before the basket. The whole morning grammar: coals told it was the thaw and turned with the back of a broken pot; intake, hold and letting-go toward no opening with neither gap figured; finger along the top of the loaf shelf so the charcoal tally beneath stays unread; lips on a two-gapped row; one glance at the cold clay jar with the hand shut half over nothing and the little-door burn closing on air; widow's mark untouched. Twice-mended pot lifted thumb-first where the tighter wire crosses the crack, hairline showing, both proverbs to pot and fire and never to a person. Dressed grain turned under wool with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child and with no figure or handful. Pot rim set two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat and no look toward the bed. (T058/T079/T100)
  5. Carrying-down of the nineteen steps at the half-pace from every open slate; instruction to the wool, the river wind, the pot, the fire and the banked fire and never to the basket; the basket not told what the day is and not told to be still.
  6. At the kindling Tarn keeps six pieces in the worn thumb-mark where the house can check and the seventh at the lip beside Garl's unforged collector sentence; small knife at the very edge of its sill; hand over blade and sill and away with no breath held at the top of the reach. He tells the wood *Six counted. The next I don't count* once, in the tone of a man stating the weather; the ear-sentence stops at the word that would call the loan. (T036/T076/T006)
  7. At the wheel-shed edge Jorra comes through the finger's-width door with meal-fine grey slate dust past the bent little finger and white in a fist-crease, parts dusty forearms from clean as clot from vessel, speaks to the wheel and a pot before any person, knows the full note three turns ahead and withholds it from any High Kiln master who would call it early. *He's not Vail* sealed; the promise neither given nor withdrawn. (T035/T031/T062)
  8. Answer 427 comes out and is filed on the sixth place. Count reaches 426 exact, six months and two hundred and forty-six days, told by nobody; he does not stand the number up as a threshold or lay the twelve old arithmetics and the three readings across one another, for paper is not a payer. (T071/T072)
- **Emotional turn.** From the still, filed restraint of the opening hand to the small animal impatience of the carried body at the pot rim; from the infant's louder wanting to the surgeon's refusal to turn two inches of air into a figure.
- **Sensory anchors.** Tallow, woodsmoke, wet wool; the faint sour tang of the unswept corner; the horn-hard crust; the half-beat at the setting; the broken pot's back scraping the coals; the cold-stiffened rope; the sweetish edge of lead glaze on a wrong wind off High Kiln, arrived nowhere.
- **Threads advanced.** T060/T086/T097; T004; T058/T079/T100; T036/T076/T006; T035/T031/T062; T071/T072. Held, not spent: T015/T041/T047, T080/T090/T102, T050/T051.
- **Foreshadowing planted / paid off.** Planted, unpaid: nothing new; the far-off finger, Berr's kindness, the two sightings, the softening under the river's skin all gather interest where put. Paid off: nothing — no sixth hearing, tenth hand, tenth stopping, eighth opening, sixth shaping or eleventh arithmetic.
- **Exact last image.** The boy, set under the roof at the edge of the light where the heat comes sideways and never in the Debt Loft, with the crust two inches from his ear, the thread unwiped, and the small hand outside the wool laid flat on the fold on answer 427.

## Scene 2 — What the Second Left

- **Day and time.** Day 426, Silt 2, Secondday. Afternoon through the going of the light and the small hours closing the day.

- **Beats, in order.**

The afternoon takes its small sounds first because they do not lie and the large ones are mostly weather: pail on the flat stone, rope settling into its knot with the cold-stiffened rasp, barley wall to wall without a number, a loaf pushed a finger's width and stopped with nobody calling the stopping a figure.   2. One wide circuit of the seven walked-around places, once and not twice, widest at the returning-line place where a hand went in on Seed 15 and did not come back; no name, body, figure or accident. (T098, T009/T013/T092)
  3. The trough keeps the gone pan's shallow round print on a place of its own; not laid across the loaf's stopping or the Vail-corner ridge. The water-line read level in a vessel nobody calibrated.
  4. At the Vail corner the clean dark line keeps its finer grey called the thaw beside the called-gone grey of the ridge with a finger between — a thing left in place is not a thing forgiven. The air off the river is less cold at one edge where the low note runs nearer under the winter skin; found where put, called weather, not looked twice, not a fourth covering, sign, payment or custom. (T083/T085/T092, T009/T013/T092)
  5. Costs arrive as directions and go away as weather and leave no entry: a fist opens toward the well, looks at the light, shuts and goes back into a sleeve, arriving nowhere; a woman sent for a pot's cost is sent to the pot and gets a note and not a price; the figure read over the covered grain does not know what the grain cost and the ink-neat space between figure and price has no signature. (T077)
  6. The offered figure, the asking answered with the weather, the second man's turned slate and direction, the clerk's entered figure and the fifth shaping are found where put on their own places with a finger between every pair; none is told again, made a custom, made a witness, made a reader or joined to another on a day that earned only its second. (T080/T090/T102 held)
  7. Going of the light: Sella comes to the hearth before the basket; the whole grammar returns and the household straightens around the order. Widow's mark untouched — a going of the light is not an opening and seven openings in seven months are seven and not a rule. Twice-mended pot thumb-first, both proverbs to pot and fire, grain turned under wool, pot rim two inches beyond the reaching hand with its half-beat and no look toward the bed. (T058/T079)
  8. The keeping at the fold is found where put with no second fold, third crust, name, gift, payment or payer. Under the boards the carried fire gives four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer without a name for four hundred and twenty-six days; nobody lifts the floor and nobody supplies the pull. (T097, T088)
  9. Small hours: the towel comes out at the head of the bed as instruments do when a case is done. The surgeon's exactness and the infant's want stand side by side; neither buys the other. Answer 428 filed, exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer. (T061/T082, T071/T072)
- **Emotional turn.** From the boy's preference for small honest sounds over large weather-talk to the long undignified interval of the evening in which nothing is entered and nothing is mended; the day closes not in settlement but in the filing of a second exact, useless answer.
- **Sensory anchors.** Flat knock of the pail, dry rasp of the rope, barley wall to wall, the finger's-width stopping of the loaf; the sour Ash tang thinning at one edge; water over water under a winter skin; tallow and low smoke on the towel; four low pulls under the floor.
- **Threads advanced.** T098; T009/T013/T092; T083/T085/T092; T077; T080/T090/T102 (held); T058/T079; T097; T088; T061/T082; T071/T072.
- **Foreshadowing planted / paid off.** Planted, unpaid: the softening at the river's skin gathers toward a thaw nobody will own; the missing fifth pull gathers toward the later naming of the Hearth as a wound. Paid off: nothing.
- **Exact last image.** The hand outside the wool comes straight down and lies flat against the fold on answer 428, with the crust unmoved and the thread unwiped, while four low pulls breathe under the floor and the fifth is not supplied.

## The pull into Chapter 126

The chapter ends with the hand put away where a next hand would find it without looking, and with no next hand in the room. The count goes on past the turning of the month and is not stood up as a threshold: 428 exact, six months and two hundred and forty-eight days told by nobody, six honest answers 427 through 432 on the sixth place and not one a doctrine. The neighbour's finger that went to the shelf and toward the ear stopped a finger's width short and was filed as a shape, not an account — it gathers interest toward the far-off hand that is not his and has not come, and nothing in this chapter joined shelf to fold, named a gap or paid a payer. Three readings still stand behind one unsigned figure; Silt 10 (Day 434), the month's first reading and the year's fourth, waits six mornings off as a date, the fourth waiting beyond Chapter 126's close. Chapter 126 opens on Day 429, Silt 5, Fifthday, with the hand still laid down on answer 432 and the count exact at 428, the house taking up its grammar as though nothing had turned, and the runner's reaching still unspent — on Silt 6 a word will come up at the yard well that the woman who carries Berr Clay's slate has begun carrying a second, narrower slate edge-on under her arm with its writing turned the same way, and no figure will come off either; the boy will file the second slate on a place of its own beside her eighth hand, Ilva's offered figure and its return, the clerk's entered figure and the three likenesses of Slate Row, not a reader made, not a ninth or tenth hand, not a witness, not a sale, not a sixth shaping and not the calling of the loan. Forward, not closed.
