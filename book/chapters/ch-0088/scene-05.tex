\daymark{317}{Bone 18, Eighthday, Y.H. 813}

The small hours went off without a hand lifted anywhere to announce them, the eighteenth coming on the way a vessel comes to the top of a kiln --- from the inside, by a change in the air that no hand had made. \person[Corin]{The boy} came into his three hundred and seventeenth day under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof with nobody saying so. Six months and one hundred and thirty-seven days were told by nobody in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill} and \place{Grath}. The count went from three hundred and sixteen to three hundred and seventeen exact, complete, unspent and spoken by no man under any roof. \lore{The carried fire}'s fifth stood unpaid the same number of days with its payer unnamed, and \person{Garl Vail}'s name had not been spoken under the roof the same number. There was no market, no Cross and no bell on an Eighthday. The older women of the district named their months by the weather and the slatemen by the number of Account-days, and neither habit was set right or left wrong under this roof; the month carried both names the way a pot carries two handles and is none the worse for it. Nothing out of the day behind had come across the dark as a sign or a lesson or a mending or a payment, and nothing out of this day would be asked to carry one.

He read the room the way a man reads a field at its edge, by the small sounds first, because the small sounds do not lie. The coals breathed under blind ash with the dry, sour smell of hot clay gone cold, and they gave four low pulls and then the silence where the fifth should have stood, a silence with a shape now, familiar as a gap in a row of teeth is familiar to the tongue that keeps going back to it. Tallow and wet wool lay over everything, and under them the faint mineral breath of the \lore{Ash} ridge along the skirting board, which nobody had swept in two years and nobody had named in three, and which he set down as a smell and nothing more. The river went on \lore{water over water} under the finger's-width door, a low thing over a lower thing, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming, and was not numbered among the hearings. Out of \place{the Debt Loft} came the small dry scrape that had been filed on its own place and was not made a seventh of anything. Overhead \place{the boards} kept four nails on the near board, four on the next, and a thumb-width knot where a fifth nail might have been driven and was not. Three counts in three places, and none of them rose into another, because agreeing any two would have wanted a payer put under both and none stood.

He took the inventory in its old order and entered it nowhere, not even on the place where he kept the things he did not enter. One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from his ear, gone stiffer in the dry heat of the kiln with its corner standing a hair off the wool; the empty place beside it kept its name to itself and no third had come in to make the row come out even. The thread of drool at his mouth had frozen and was unwiped, for nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread, and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. Cold had stopped at cheek, temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, as though it had come to the end of a line written out for it, with neither an order to go further nor an order to go away. \lore{The Afterdrop} waited beyond all three, past a warming nobody in this house would perform.

He knew the composed face that was not composure, knew that the ease cold puts on a face is no ease, that a cold patient cannot clot and will seem better before he is worse and must be warmed from the outside and slowly, never hurried, never rubbed, never set too near the fire. The knowing bought nothing at two inches of air. Underneath it the infant wanted the crust lifted the two inches to his mouth and the thread wiped and the pot handle closed on, and the wanting had no words and did not need any and was louder than the knowing. Both were true and the second was louder and neither won; the argument between them was the work, and it went on in the dark without either voice giving ground.

\scenebreak

\person{Sella Vail} went to the fire before the basket, as she went to it before everything, and the house woke in the order she gave it. She told the coals it was the thaw, in the flat tone of one stating the weather to a thing that could not answer and might not need to, and turned them with the back of a broken pot, lifting the ash without disturbing the shape underneath. \emph{The house will keep it}, she said, where another woman would have said \emph{I}, and the sentence went to the fire and stayed there. Then she stood a moment with the back of the pot in her hand, counting something under her breath --- small intake, hold, letting-go --- toward no opening and with no figure on any of the three. The grey streak at her temple showed pale where she pushed the coal-black hair back with the wrist that carried the burn, and the little door of that burn closed on nothing when the hand had nothing to hold. Under the fold the boy heard the three small words of her counting and knew them for his own three, intake and hold and letting-go, kept in a woman's breath and in a boy's file without either being the other's cause. They lay side by side as the two ears under one roof lay, near enough to be mistaken for one keeping if a hand had been less careful; the finger went between them. Neither count borrowed from the other and no payer stood under either, and she did not look toward the bed and he could not have told her what he kept.

Her finger went to the top of \place{the loaf shelf}'s front edge and lay there, so the charcoal tally beneath stayed unread; her lips moved along a row that stood with two gaps in it and figured neither gap, and no number came off the shelf to stand between the two bare boards. She glanced once at the clay jar standing cold under \lore{lead glaze} and shut her hand half over nothing, and did not say why she had looked. On \place{the pot board} stood the twice-mended pot with the hairline of the original break showing dark between the staples and the second wire drawn tighter and left longer than it needed. She put her thumb first where the wire crossed the crack, felt what the thumb had to feel, and only then let the weight come on the handle. Both proverbs went to the pot and the fire and never to a person: \emph{If it's worth mending it's worth mending twice. The second time is the real one}; and \emph{Leave the crack showing. People trust a thing they can see has been handled}. The sayings had improved in the keeping and were very likely not as good as the man had said them. The crack was left showing. Repair was not restoration and a mended vessel carried its break, but she did not say either, because a saying wants somebody standing under it to be asked.

At \place{the rack end} the dressed grain lay under wool away from the hearth, and she turned it with a still lighter touch than the day before --- a corner lifted, a handful let fall back, the wool drawn over again --- and gave it no figure. It had been read over on Bone 10 by a number that did not know what it cost; the number had gone up \place{the clay road} in a locked box and had not come back as a lesson or a mending, and the grain lay as it lay, changed in state and uncounted. The instruction went to the wool and the river wind and never to the basket, and a basket set down under a roof is a pot set down and left. She set the pot rim two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat and no look toward the bed, and the small flat sound of the set-down went into the room as an interval and not a note, a thing that had stopped where it stopped and owed nothing for the stopping. She put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face, and left the thread where it lay. \lore{The widow's mark} hung where it hung on its cord at her throat, a thumb-length of lead-glazed clay with the Vail kiln scratched into it, and she did not touch it, because a morning and a hearth are not openings; five openings in five months are five and not a rule, and the next stands in a later month's first firing, far off, not hoped for and not counted toward.

\scenebreak

Under the floor the carried fire gave four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should stand, its payer without a name for three hundred and seventeen days; over \place{the boards} four nails stood on the near board and four on the next with the thumb-width knot where a fifth nail might have been driven and was not; at the finger's-width door the river kept water over water, a low thing over a lower thing, and was not numbered among the hearings. Three counts in three places that did not become one. The bell's gap left behind in the square was not fetched in to make a fourth, and the heard bell stayed in its square beside the told bell of \lore{the Long Night}; six hearings of \lore{the full note} stood beside and never among with the seventh place empty, and none was invented to fill it; the told figure stood beside the paid figure with the space between them unsigned. Each keeping stayed in its own square. A fifth pull supplied, a seventh hearing named, a signature found for the space --- any of them would have made the row come out even, and a row that comes out even has always found somebody to stand under it.

Four arithmetics of the year stood behind one printed figure --- the three of Frost and Bone's first --- one in twelve unchallenged, the figure read still not the figure paid, no signature on the space between, four readings four and not one account. The four true subtractions rode locked up the clay road in \person{Berr Clay}'s box toward \place{High Kiln}, unread under this roof. The finding sat on a place of its own with no payer under it, not a lesson, grievance, mending, doctrine or rule, and he did not lay it across the house's \lore{not-doings}, the dressed grain covered under wool at the rack end, the four held changes on four places, the stayed-at-the-inch object, the early letting-go or his own kept gaps. The edges of these things came near to touching, so near that a less careful hand would have pushed them together and called the touching a cause; the finger went between them instead. He had stopped asking for a number that would make one thing of them, which was not the same as not wanting one, and the wanting went on under the not-asking the way a coal goes on under ash.

The staying of Bone 15 stayed one object and not a habit, and the early letting-go of Bone 17 stayed one object on its own place beside it with a finger between, and neither was made again. The four held changes stood four and not a rule: the returned half-check of the eighth on its flagstone, the Day 161 damper fraction, the Day 166 shelf beat, the Frost 5 returned beat. Neither of the hand's two small objects joined them, and neither stood up as a fifth held change, a doctrine or a payer. One staying is a keeping and two would be a habit, and a habit is an account that has found a payer; one early letting-go is a letting-go and two would be a way of it, and a way wants a payer under it. None stood. He left them where they lay and asked nothing of any of them.

He turned out the file the way he had once turned instruments out on a towel at the end of a list, laying each thing on its own place and asking nothing of it: the stayed-at-the-inch object, the early letting-go, the four held changes with a finger between every pair, the burned slate at five with no sixth and no witness, the row at five askings with one shaping, the well-saying at three shapings, the shut-room tellings at two, the pot sayings at three with the Smoke 14 note on its own place, nine record-first hands with the ninth unread in a sleeve and no tenth, nine stoppings with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end, six hearings with the seventh place empty, the heard bell beside the told, the told figure beside the paid with four arithmetics behind it and the finding on a place apart, seven green a colour and seven shape a shape with no eighth. Nothing on the towel stood up and called itself a reach, a hearing, a seventh, a stopping, a payment, a held change, a gift, a doctrine or a rule, and the older man took a clean, cold satisfaction in finding every gap left gap.

\scenebreak

The hand outside the wool came up an inch toward the river's note. It took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his. It let the intake go whole on the same low note, exact and complete and worth nothing at two inches of air, and came straight down and laid itself back against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. The note had done before the hand came down. The letting-go was not early again, and the hand did not stay at the inch; the early letting-go and the staying stayed where they were, two objects on two places and not a second of either. He filed the morning's small work as answer two hundred and nine, a restraint and not a payment, borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, and neither the number nor the small hand-movings that made it stood up and called themselves a doctrine, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood.

Two hundred and nine honest answers stood on the sixth place, each an object and not a power. He had stopped expecting anybody to tell him what the number counted; that was a habit of a better-lit room, a man with a chart in his hands waiting for somebody in a clean coat to say the word that would make the chart mean something, and no clean coat had come under this roof in three hundred and seventeen days and no word had been spoken over the basket. The older man took his satisfaction in the keeping; the infant underneath wanted the crust lifted two inches and the thread wiped and a hand that would close on the pot handle. Both were true and the second was louder and neither won, and the argument went on in the dark with no payer named to either side.

Three hundred and seventeen stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in Low Kiln, Sill and Grath; six months and one hundred and thirty-seven days were told by nobody; the carried fire's fifth was unpaid the same number of days with its payer unnamed; and Garl's name had not been spoken under the roof the same number. Nothing on the eighteenth had been opened, joined, named, called, paid or ended, and the keeping of the number was the only honest work there was to do and bought nothing at two inches of air. The answer lay back against the fold and the file lay open on \place{the towel} at the head of the bed with every object on its own place and every gap left gap, and the river went on low over lower under the finger's-width door while Bone went on without a boundary drawn.
