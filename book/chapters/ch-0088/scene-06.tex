The going of the light came down into the yard without any hand to announce it, the way heat goes out of a pot that has been set down and left: nobody struck the pot and nobody struck the hour, and still the air of the place altered. \person{Sella Vail} took \person[Corin]{the boy} up from \place{the bed} under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof at the half-pace she kept from every open slate, and carried him down into \place{the Clay Yard} with her burn half-closed on the basket handle and the instruction going out ahead of her to the wool and the river wind and never to the basket. A basket set down is a pot set down, and she set him where the light would not lie full on his face and went away about the yard as though she had set a pot there and had no further word to spend on it. Dry dust of the hunting month lifted at the hem of her skirt and did not settle, clay ground small as meal under the nails of every hand that worked there.

She went wide around the places the yard goes around, with a look and a wide step and no number. The clean dark line at the Vail corner lay where it lay, and the finer grey hairline beside it that the yard called \lore{the thaw} lay beside it unswept, the two of them near enough for a careless broom to take them up together; she gave the broom its instruction and the broom went around. Under \place[the drying racks]{the lowest rack} the broken birth-slates kept their own shadow. The shallow clay pan had been gone from its place so long that the round ring it left had the look of something made on purpose, with no scrape, no crumb, no print, no chip and no word on it. \place{the trough} had a skin over it. Out on \place{the worked strips} the field kept its empty place, and in the returning line the place where a hand had gone in on Seed 15 and had not come out with the last hands kept its own emptiness. Seven places, gone around with a look and a wide step and no figure spoken over any of them; no eighth likeness was stood up and no cause laid across two of them. A thing a household goes around is a thing it still keeps, and the keeping was maintenance and not a story.

The month went on without a boundary drawn anywhere in the yard, and neither of the district's two habits of naming its months was set right or left wrong under that roof. The older women named the months by the weather and the slatemen by the number of Account-days, and the house carried both names the way a pot carries two handles and is none the worse for it. Hunts and repairs went on as directions judged by the carrying arm and the weather, never figures. A man come down from \place{Cold Acre} with a hare across his shoulders shifted the weight when a woman with a pot asked what the carrying had cost, and told her to ask the weather, which had carried the snow and could carry the price, and went off toward \place{the clay road} with nobody weighing the hare and nobody opening a slate. Another, with a cracked jar in a sling of rope, asked what the mending of the month would want and was told to ask the pot; the pot, being a pot, said nothing, and the question went off with the man and arrived at no figure. The boy lay at the edge of the work and took the answers apart as he took apart a voice over the boards, by what was there and by what was not, and entered neither the hare's weight nor the jar's cost.

At \place{the yard well} the three coverings stayed called weather and entered nowhere. The frost that had stayed had gone off the north lip on Bone 3 while the yard was looking elsewhere, and the place where it had been was not a fourth likeness to set beside the two that remained. \place{the flat stone by the well} kept the plate of ice clouded with silt standing out of the path of feet, a piece of a depth taken up and set aside where no foot would go. The boy set the gone white, the skin on the water and the lifted plate on three places with a finger between every pair, and made no cause of the going or the staying: a covering over a depth, the same covering over another depth, and a piece of the depth lifted out and stood where nobody would step. No fourth likeness stood up, no payer was named, and the yard went on drawing its buckets and calling it weather.

At \place{the rack end} the dressed grain lay under wool away from \place{the hearth}, and she turned it once more with a lighter touch than the morning --- a corner lifted, a handful let fall back, the wool drawn over again --- and gave it no figure. It had been read over on Bone 10 by an arithmetic that did not know what it cost; the arithmetic had gone up the road in a locked box and had not come back as a lesson or a mending, and the grain lay as it lay, changed in state and uncounted. A covering drawn over a thing and a number spoken over it are two acts, and the finger went between them.

On \place{the flagstones} the shallow bright patch lay where the yard had left it and neither broom nor foot went into it. The returned half-check of the eighth stood on the stone's own place with the Day 161 damper fraction, the Day 166 shelf beat and the Frost 5 returned beat beside it: four held changes on four places and not a rule. \person{Tarn}'s stroke went over the bright patch without the half-check again at the going of the light, and the place where it had stopped kept its shape without anybody entering it. A fifth likeness would have stood up and called itself a habit, and a habit is an account that has found a payer; none stood. A thing kept by not being made again is still kept, and the keeping bought nothing at two inches of air.

Under the roof the staying of Bone 15 stayed one object, and the early letting-go of Bone 17 stayed one object on its own place beside it, with a finger between them and a finger between each and every other object on the sixth. Neither was made again. One staying is a keeping and two would be a habit, and a habit wants a payer; one early letting-go is a letting-go and two would be a way of it, and a way wants a payer under it. None stood. He did not ask either object to stand for something, and the older man took a cold, clean satisfaction in finding them still apart, each on its own square of the towel with nothing borrowed across.

Four arithmetics of the year stood behind one printed figure --- the three of Frost and Bone's first --- one in twelve unchallenged, the figure read still not the figure paid, no signature on the space between, four readings four and not one account. The four true subtractions rode locked up the clay road in \person{Berr Clay}'s box toward \place{High Kiln}, unread under this roof. The finding sat on a place of its own with no payer under it, not a lesson, a grievance, a mending or a doctrine, and he did not lay it across the house's \lore{not-doings}, the dressed grain covered under wool at the rack end, the four held changes on four places, the stayed-at-the-inch object, the early letting-go or his own kept gaps. The edges of these things came near to touching, so near that a less careful hand would have pushed them together and called the touching a cause; the finger went between them instead. He had stopped asking for a number that would make one thing of them, which was not the same as not wanting one, and the wanting went on under the not-asking the way a coal goes on under ash.

He turned out the file the way he had once turned instruments out on a towel at the end of a list, laying each thing on its own place and asking nothing of it. The \lore{burned slate} stood at five with no sixth and no witness and no hand named at any flame. The shut-room tellings stood at two. The row stood at five askings with one shaping and no witness, and no sixth asking had come into the yard on a day that had not earned one. The well-saying stood at three shapings, and three shapings were three and not a witness. \person{Hessa Rale}'s eighth hand stood where it stood, with \person{Ilva}'s ninth beside it and a finger between; the notched strip stayed in the woman's sleeve unread and no tenth hand was supplied. Nine stoppings stood with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end and the loan uncalled. At \place{the Vail wheel-shed} \person{Jorra Vail} came through the finger's-width door with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bent little finger and white in the creases where a fist had opened, not bone-coloured \lore{slip}. He kept dusty forearms and clean ones on one tray and parted them as a surgeon parts clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had broken nor whether it was a birth-slate. He spoke to the wheel before he spoke to any person --- \emph{It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady} --- and told a pot \emph{You'll do} in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either, keeping the full note he knew that pot would give three turns ahead, with air under it and a want in it, on the near-full tray beside the hearings and never among them, because a kiln-master from High Kiln would have called it early and made something of it. His bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper} ahead of Sella's unfinished sentence and left the tongue a hair's breadth over so the end of the instruction arrived at the pot; one piece of iron with two bites, the holding and the taking, with no \lore{Grathic} word for both mercies, joined to no kiln and entered in no slate. The absent man kept his place in objects and not in a story. The salt-stiff coat with the crease at the left shoulder held his broader shape on its peg behind the door; the wheel-seat in the shed kept its twenty-year hollow on that side and nobody filled it; the clean dark line with its finer grey beside it lay unswept; the twice-mended pot on \place{the pot board} stood with the hairline of the original break showing dark between the old-brass staple and the tighter wire, and its note hung where no knuckle struck it. Both proverbs went to the pot and the fire and never to a person, and had improved in the keeping and were very likely not as good as he had said them. The debt against a firing of tiles circled without a name, neither paid nor settled nor done with and worse for being uncalled, and his name had not been spoken under the roof three hundred and seventeen days. The keeping of him was maintenance, not a lesson, and nobody stood under it to be asked.

Sella carried him up at the going of the light at the half-pace and set him on his bed under the roof and never in \place{the Debt Loft}, with the instruction going to the wool and the river wind and never to the basket. Under the roof the three counts kept their three places. \lore{The carried fire} gave four low pulls under the floor and the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer without a name for three hundred and seventeen days; over \place{the boards} four nails stood on the near board and four on the next with a thumb-width knot where a fifth might have been driven and was not; at the finger's-width door the river kept \lore{water over water}, a low thing over a lower thing, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming, and was not numbered among the hearings. The hand outside the wool came up an inch toward the river's note. It took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his.
