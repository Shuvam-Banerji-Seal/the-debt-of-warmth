## Chronicle

Bone came on without a boundary drawn, and beneath the Vail roof the boy came into his three hundred and fifteenth, sixteenth and seventeenth mornings, untold. The count went from three hundred and fourteen to three hundred and seventeen; the fire's fifth stayed unpaid, Garl's name unspoken likewise. Each morning the body was inventoried in its old order: crust, unnamed gap, frozen thread, cold, the waiting afterdrop. His knowledge that a cold patient cannot clot bought nothing at two inches of air. The infant wanted the crust lifted and the thread wiped. Both were true, the second louder; neither won, and the argument was the work.

Sella went to the fire before the basket, told the coals it was the thaw, and said *The house will keep it* where another woman would have said I. She counted intake, hold and letting-go toward no opening, lifted the twice-mended pot on the thumb where the wire crossed the crack, turned the dressed grain beneath the wool with a lighter touch and no figure, and set the pot rim two inches beyond the reaching hand. She did not touch the widow's mark: a morning and a hearth are not openings, and five openings in five months are five and not a rule. Garl stayed in objects and his name was not spoken.

In the yard she went around seven walked-around places with a look and a wide step; the three coverings at the well stayed weather and entered nowhere. Tarn kept six where the house could check and left the Seed 16 breath unspent. Jorra put his bent finger into the cold damper ahead of Sella's unfinished sentence. On Bone 17 Ilva drew at the well; her hand went to her sleeve, the strip did not come out, and she answered *Ask the pot what it cost. I keep the count, not the account.* The boy filed her reaching as the ninth record-first hand and filed no tenth.

Six answers went whole on the river's note --- the two hundred and fifth through the two hundred and tenth --- each worth nothing at two inches of air, no payer named. On Bone 17, for the first time, the hand let its intake go before the note had done, while the low sound was still going under the door. The surgeon read a clamp taken off too soon; the infant, a hand that gave up before it was done wanting. Both were true, neither won, and he filed the early letting-go as answer two hundred and seven on a place of its own with a finger between it and every other object there. He did not make it again.

At the close the file lay open on the towel with every gap left gap, the staying and the early letting-go two objects on two places, two hundred and ten honest answers standing on the sixth, and three hundred and seventeen exact and spoken by nobody. Bone went on without a boundary drawn.
