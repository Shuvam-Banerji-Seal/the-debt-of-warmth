# Chapter 88 — beat sheet: *The Note Let Go Early*

**Days covered.** Days 315–317, Bone 16–18, Sixthday to Eighthday, Y.H. 813. A normal chapter, three lived days, no day skipped, poured into another or summarised. Bone 20 (Day 319), the month's second Account-day and reading, stands two mornings ahead and is not brought into any of these three days. No market, Cross or bell on Bone 16, 17 or 18. Point of view never leaves Dan/Corin; no Court scene, no inside-the-collection scene, no back-room interior. No present-tense break and no `\memory{}` tag in this chapter — the old life is carried by the surgeon's register alone.

## How the final moment pulls into the next chapter

The last image is deliberately not an ending: the count stands at **three hundred and seventeen exact**, the hand laid down on the **two hundred and tenth** answer, the file open with every gap left gap, and Bone going on without a boundary drawn. Chapter 89 opens on Day 318, Bone 19, Ninthday, with Bone 20 (Day 319), the month's second Account-day and market, **one morning ahead** — the second reading will come to the Cross and change the district's arithmetic for the month a second time and cannot be changed back, making **five arithmetics of the year** behind one printed figure, five and not one account. Everything this chapter held is carried into that morning unchanged and unspent: the stayed-at-the-inch object and the new early letting-go as two separate objects on the sixth and not a habit; the four held changes on four places and not a rule; four arithmetics in Berr's locked box with the figure read still not the figure paid and no signature on the space; the burned slate at five with no sixth; nine hands and nine stoppings with no tenth; six hearings with the seventh place empty; Ilva's strip unread; the grain covered under wool and unfigured. Nothing in this chapter reaches the reading as sign, lesson, mending, cause, payment or payer, and no payer stands to any of it. The forward pull is the arithmetic the district is about to change over a basket that cannot answer, and the boy's private count going on toward a number nobody will speak.
