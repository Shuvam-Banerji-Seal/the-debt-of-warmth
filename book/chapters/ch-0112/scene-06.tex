The small hours went on under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill}, \place{Grath}, and the hand outside the wool lay flat on the fold on answer three hundred and fifty-three, where the going of the light had left it. The count stood at three hundred and eighty-nine, exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air, and no voice under any roof would have known to speak it. This was the deep of \place{Stone} 30, the last dark of the year, the hour before \place{the Clay Yard} and \place{the Vail wheel-shed} remembered their morning; the twelfth arithmetic had gone up behind one unsigned figure in the light that was gone, and twelve were twelve and not one account, and nothing in the room had stood up and called itself a doctrine about it. \person[Corin]{The boy} lay where \person{Sella Vail} had set him, under the roof and never in \place{the Debt Loft}, with \place{the boards} overhead holding four nails on the near board, four on the next, and a thumb-width knot where a fifth might have been driven and was not. \person{Garl Vail} was not in the room and had not been in it for three hundred and eighty-nine days; he was kept in the salt-stiff coat on its peg behind the door with the crease at the left shoulder, in the hollow of the wheel-seat nobody filled, in the clean dark line at \place{the Vail corner} unswept, and in the hairline on \place{the pot board}, and none of these had been given his name in all that time.

The surgeon took the body in its old order, as a man takes a field off a towel in the dark and reads it by the fingers because the light would only show him what he already knows. The crust came first, stiff and leathery-brown at the edge, two inches from the ear with its corner standing a hair off the wool. Beside it the empty place held the fold put there on the fifteenth, found where put on every morning and at every going of the light since, not made again, still a finding and not a placing. The thread of drool at the lip had frozen in the dry kiln heat and was wiped by nobody, because nobody under any roof has ever been given an instruction about the thread. Three things found as they had been left, and none the better for his finding them. Cold had come cheek, temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, and stopped there as though it had come to the end of a line written out for it, with neither an order to go farther nor an order to go away. \lore{The Afterdrop} waited past a warming nobody in this house would perform. He knew a face composed by cold is not composure; that pink returning is the surface still warm over a core still cold; that a cold patient cannot clot and must be warmed from the outside and slowly, never hurried, never rubbed. He knew that a basket set level out of the draught where the heat came sideways was the careful rewarming he would choose and could not ask for. The knowledge was exact and belonged to a man with a name in another country. The body it was spent on was three hundred and eighty-nine days old and could not lift its own head.

Under the exactness the infant wanted the crust lifted two inches, the thread wiped, and a hand on the pot handle, and wanted them without knowing that wanting them was a want. The two registers quarrelled without verdict. Both were true. The second was louder. Neither won, and no verdict was looked for; the quarrel was the work, and the work went on in silence in a room whose floor does not touch his.

Sella came to \place{the hearth} before the basket, as she had at the going of every day since the boy came under the roof, and this was the last time she would come to it in the year that was going out. She told the coals it was \lore{the thaw} and turned them with the back of a broken pot, and said \emph{The house will keep it} where another woman would have said I, standing a moment to count a small intake, a hold and a letting-go toward no opening and figuring neither of the two gaps. Her finger lay along the top of \place{the loaf shelf} so that the charcoal tally on its underside stayed unread; her lips moved along a two-gapped row and figured neither gap. The instruction went to the wool, to the fire and to the river wind, and never to the basket. One glance went to the clay jar standing cold under \lore{lead glaze} beyond the pot board, the sweetish edge of it coming and going on a wrong wind; her hand shut half over nothing, the little-door burn on the back of her left hand closing on air. The cord of her \lore{widow's mark} at her throat was oiled where a working thumb had touched it ten thousand times and was not touched now. A market, a going of the light and a bed are not openings. Seven openings in seven months are seven and not a rule, no eighth was made due on the year's last day, and the empty place where one might have stood was not dug for and not filled. The sour mineral tang of \lore{Ash} came up off the unswept line at the Vail corner where the dry cold had thickened it, and was a smell and not a cause.

The charcoal under the shelf's front edge carried its own small arithmetic, a tally she had never read in the three years he had lain under the roof and would not read now on the year's last dark; her finger lay along the top of the shelf so the marks stayed unturned, and the boy did not turn them either, having no key to any count but his own. Above them the two gaps in the row of her lips stood where they stood, and the shelf's two gaps stood beneath, and the room whose floor does not touch this one kept its own two gaps in another house; likenesses enough for a rule if a hand stood to lay them together, but no hand stood and he did not stand in for one. He kept them apart with a finger between every pair, as a surgeon keeps two fields apart on one towel, and the keeping cost him nothing and bought him nothing.

Then she came to the bed and stood over it a moment with her hands gone still, as a woman stands over a thing she has carried across a room and is about to put down. She put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face, and added no second fold to the crust's empty place. Her hand did only what the setting needed and went away. The wool lay where she had put it on the fifteenth and was found where put, not made again, a keeping and not a filling, not a habit and not a way, with no third crust, no name spoken over the place, no gift, no payment and no payer. The thread stayed frozen at his lip and unwiped. The small keeping sat on a place of its own beside the crust's gap, the shelf's two gaps in the room whose floor does not touch this one, the gone pan's round ring and the field's empty place, with a finger between every pair. The far-off finger that would one day lay shelf and fold together and call the joining an account was still far off, not his, and not come; the boy did not reach toward it and did not bring it into the dark by wanting it. Twelve findings from a hand that only read had added no twelfth placing to the first, and the small alarm at a double-digit count had itself been filed as a finding, on a place of its own.

She said nothing of where they had been or of what had been read over the wicker, and the silence was not a refusal to tell a basket, for nobody under any roof tells a basket what a thing is worth and she was not the woman who would say a thing to please the speaker. Her hands, which counted when they had nothing to do, had nothing to do now and did not count; a basket set down is a pot set down and left, and nobody counts the breaths of a pot. Nothing from \place{the Cross} crossed under the roof as sign, lesson, mending, cause, payment or payer: the twelfth arithmetic and the figure sent twice over the basket stayed up the road in the locked box, and the boy did not bring them in by wanting an answer from them. No ownerless voice came over the boards; the Ash 5 joining and the Ash 17, Ash 23 and Stone 23 directions stood where they had been put, did not repeat on the year's last day and bought no lesson. The heard bell stayed in its square beside the told, and \lore[The Hearth Drawing]{The Drawing}'s flat knock stayed outside the six hearings on its own place. The house kept itself by not-doings as it had for three years --- the two unswept lines, the charcoal unread beneath the shelf's front edge, the fifth pull unsupplied under the floor, the loan uncalled at the kindling, the crust in the fold not lifted --- and the not-doings were maintenance and not a story, and the boy filed them as findings with no payer standing, gathering interest toward a hand not his and not come.

On the pot board the twice-mended pot stood with the hairline of the original break showing between the older brass staple and the tighter wire. Sella lifted it thumb-first where the wire crossed the crack before she let the weight come on the handle, and the hairline showed dark; she left it showing. \emph{Leave the crack showing. People trust a thing they can see has been handled} and \emph{If it's worth mending it's worth mending twice. The second time is the real one} went to the pot and the fire and never to a person, improved in the keeping and very likely not as good as the man had said them, for a saying kept in a house grows smoother than the mouth that made it. Repair stayed apart from restoration: a restored thing hides the hand that handled it and a mended thing leaves that hand showing. The dressed grain under wool at the cool stone was turned once with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child, changed in state and lifting no figure and taking no handful. The pot rim was set two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat and no look toward the bed; his hand went toward it and stopped where the half-beat had stopped it. The interval was bought at nothing and was not a gift, and the stopping was his.

Under the floor \lore{the carried fire} gave four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer without a name for three hundred and eighty-nine days, and nobody lifted the floor and nobody supplied the pull. The four pulls and the missing fifth were a count in one place; the boards' four and four and a knot were a count in another; the bell's four, four and a gap was a count in a third. Three counts in three places, and none rose into another, because agreeing any two would have wanted a payer put under both and no payer stood. Twelve arithmetics stood behind one unsigned figure up \place{the clay road} in \person[Berr Clay]{Berr}'s box, locked toward \place{High Kiln} with the key gone up in a coat pocket that does not come back down and the boy having no key: the three of \place{Frost}, the first, second and third of \place{Bone}, the first, second and third of \place{Ash}, and Stone's first, second and last. One in twelve had been read twice over the basket on the thirtieth and sent twice, once for the moot and once for the households that came late; the figure read was still not the figure paid, no signature stood on the space between, twelve were twelve and not one account, and no doctrine came out of twelve. The boy set the finding on its own place with a finger between it and every other kept thing, and did not lay it across the keeping at the fold, across the four rooms with the same gap, across the four directions standing where put, or across any of his own gaps; he did not let a row come out even by his doing. An account wants a hand to lay twelve likenesses together and a payer standing under all twelve, and no such hand stood and no such payer stood. The hand that would one day lay the district's private keepings together and call the joining an account was far off, not his, and not come.

The check ran in the order the day had arrived, as a file goes out on a towel in the order the instruments were used and not in the order the surgeon would have wished them used. Six hearings of \lore{the full note} stood beside and never among, the sixth the small flat knock out of \person[Dessa Silt] as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go and never among the sources, and the seventh place stayed empty and was not dug for or filled. The Drawing's flat knock stayed outside them on its own place, not a seventh and not a filling of the empty place. The heard bell stayed in its square beside the told bell of \lore{the Long Night}, four, four and a gap, its gap not folded into any count nor made a seventh hearing. The four rooms with the same gap were held apart with a finger between every pair, four likenesses and not one account, an account wanting a hand to lay them together and a payer under all four, and no such hand standing. Nine record-first hands kept no tenth and nine stoppings kept no tenth; the eighth stayed eight and the ninth stayed nine, and the ordinary writing hand two doors down stayed on its tray joined to none. The burned-slate word stood at five, the shut-room tellings at two, the row at five askings with no witness and expressly no sixth, the well-saying at four shapings with no fifth. The Ash 5 joining and the Ash 17, Ash 23 and Stone 23 directions stood where put and did not repeat and bought no lesson; the fourth direction was a shape and not a fact, and the boy did not make it stand up and call itself a witness. The five held objects stood with the pot-crack resting, the hand-at-the-sill breath and the holding in the damper beside them, each with a finger between, and the keeping at the fold stood beside them and expressly not a tenth. The handful let fall back through her fingers onto the heap at the rack end on Bone 22 stood as one object and was not made again; the stayed-at-the-inch object and the early letting-go stood as two objects on two places and were not made again; the Ash 14 breath and the Bone 26 thumb's resting were found where put and not made again; the extra holding in the cold damper was gathered once and not left a second time. He did not make the twelve findings at the fold a thirteenth payment, nor the twelve arithmetics a doctrine, nor the four directions a rule, for a rule is an account that has found a payer and none stood to any of them. No sixth hearing, seventh opening, tenth hand, tenth stopping, eighth opening, thirteenth arithmetic, witness, reader or payment was supplied to make a row come out even, for a finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged.

Through \place{the finger's-width door} \place{the river} kept its note of \lore{water over water}, a low thing over a lower thing under \lore{the winter skin}, a covering put on by the cold and taken off by the cold and entered nowhere, found where put and not made again, not spent on the reading or the going of the light and not made to buy a lesson. It was muffled in the going as a voice is muffled by a shut door, and had no hand that laid it, no payer under it and no source standing to be asked. The note had done before any hand in the room came down.

The hand outside the wool came up an inch toward the note, did not reach, took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole on the note's own finishing under the covering, let it go as the note ended, and came straight down and laid itself flat against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. The note had done before the hand came down, so the letting-go was neither early nor stayed, and the hand did not stay.

\place{the towel} came out at the head of the bed as instruments come out when a case is done and before the next is called, with every gap left gap. He filed answer three hundred and fifty-four: exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the twelve readings, the bell's gap, the flat knock, the child's grey fingertips, the mother's cup, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the held breath, the two bites of iron, the river's skin, the widow's mark, the fourth direction nor the keeping at the fold.

Stone kept its twenty-eighth, twenty-ninth and thirtieth days without a boundary drawn, the deep-winter month holding both its names the way a pot carries two handles and is none the worse for it: older women called it by the weather and slatemen by \lore{Grathic} Account-days, and neither habit was set right or set left in so many words under any roof. The year went out as it had come in, without a line across it. The reading at the Cross had changed the district's arithmetic for the twelfth time and it could not be changed back; twelve readings stood behind one unsigned figure with no signature on the space and no doctrine out of twelve, and nothing under the Vail roof stood up and called itself an account. The surgeon's only victory was a refusal, and it cost him nothing: twelve were twelve, and the hand that would lay them together and the payer standing under them did not stand, and no row came out even by his doing.

\lore{The Five} stood beyond the last of Stone and were not brought into a dark that had earned only the year's thirtieth day. They belonged to no month and had no name under this roof, and the boy did not reach toward them by wanting them or make a boundary out of the year's going that would be a door to them. The loan at the kindling stood uncalled at the very end of the seventh year, the fear of being entered again for vagrancy still his without a figure; the promise under the wheel-shed was neither given nor withdrawn and went on gathering interest against the burned slate at five and the four directions standing where put, buying no payment and naming no room. The cold foot was uncured and the cause of the second chill was withheld, and neither was spent on the year's going or made to open anything; they crossed into the days beyond as they had crossed every day of the year, unentered and unspent. Nothing under the roof had been opened, joined, named, called, paid, forgiven or ended.

The count stood at three hundred and eighty-nine exact, six months and two hundred and nine days told by nobody who would have known what a number counted; the carried fire's fifth was unpaid the same number and its payer unnamed the same number; Garl Vail's name was unspoken the same number, kept in the coat and the hollow seat and the hairline and the unswept line and nowhere spoken. Three counts that stood side by side and did not become one, and the boy set no hand to join them, for a joining wants a payer standing and none stood. He took his small clean satisfaction in a field taken as found and left as found, and it cost him nothing, which was how he knew it was not peace; under it, louder, the infant wanted the crust lifted, the thread wiped and a hand on the pot handle, and got none of the three. Both were true. The second was louder. Neither won, no verdict in the quarrel and none looked for.

The hand lay flat on the fold on answer three hundred and fifty-four, the crust's corner standing a hair off the wool, with the year going out without a boundary drawn while the river kept its note under the winter skin beyond the finger's-width door, and the count went on under the roof in Low Kiln, Sill, Grath.
