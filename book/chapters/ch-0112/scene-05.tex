\daymark{389}{Stone 30, Tenthday, Y.H. 813}

The small hours of the thirtieth of \place{Stone} came down under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill}, \place{Grath}, and the hand outside the wool lay flat on the fold on answer three hundred and fifty-two, where the going of the light had left it. The count stood at three hundred and eighty-eight, exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air, and nobody under any roof would have known to speak it. This was the tenth day of the month's last tend, the year's third Account-day and its last market; the twelfth arithmetic stood in this morning and had been earned by it, and nothing from the twenty-eighth or the twenty-ninth had crossed under the roof as sign, lesson, mending, cause, payment or payer. Eleven were eleven and not one account. Twelve would be twelve before the light went and not one account either. The boy lay in the dark with the morning waiting on him the way an uncalled case waits on a surgeon --- known in outline, unwanted in particular, certain to arrive.

\person[Corin]{The boy} took his body in its old order, as a surgeon takes a field off a towel before the light is good enough to trust: the crust first, stiff and leathery-brown at the edge, two inches from the ear with its corner standing a hair off the wool; the empty place beside it, holding the fold of wool put there on the fifteenth and found where put on every morning since, not made again, still a finding and not a placing; the thread of drool frozen at the lip in the dry kiln heat and wiped by nobody, because nobody under any roof has ever been given an instruction about the thread. Three things found as they had been left, and none the better for his finding them. Cold had come cheek, temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, and stopped there as though it had come to the end of a line written out for it, with neither an order to go farther nor an order to go away. \lore{The Afterdrop} waited past a warming nobody in this house would perform.

He knew a face composed by cold is not composure; that pink returning is the surface still warm over a core still cold; that a cold patient cannot clot and must be warmed from the outside and slowly, never hurried, never rubbed. He knew that a basket set level out of the draught where the heat came sideways was the careful rewarming he would choose for such a patient and could not ask for. The knowledge was exact and belonged to a man with a name in another country. The body it was spent on was three hundred and eighty-eight days old and could not lift its own head.

Under the exactness the infant wanted the crust lifted two inches, the thread wiped, and a hand on the pot handle, and wanted them without knowing that wanting them was a want. The two registers quarrelled without verdict. Both were true. The second was louder. Neither won, and no verdict was looked for; the quarrel was the work, and the work went on in silence in a room whose floor does not touch his.

\person{Sella Vail} went to \place{the hearth} before the basket, as she had at both ends of every day since the boy came under the roof. She told the coals it was the thaw and turned them with the back of a broken pot, and said \emph{The house will keep it} where another woman would have said I, standing a moment to count a small intake, a hold and a letting-go toward no opening and figuring neither of the two gaps. Her finger lay along the top of \place{the loaf shelf} so that the charcoal tally on its underside stayed unread; her lips moved along a two-gapped row and figured neither gap. The instruction went to the wool, to the fire and to the river wind, and never to the basket.

Before first light she carried him down the nineteen steps at the half-pace from every open slate, correcting the tilt at every turn of the landing before it had time to settle into the wicker. Out in \place{the Clay Yard} the dark still held the seven walked-around places, gone around with a look and a wide step and no number, once and not twice, widest around \place{the returning-line place} where a hand had gone in on \place{Seed} 15 and had not come out; but a going-around on a market morning was not a lesson, and the boy took none. Through \place{the finger's-width door} \place{the river} kept its note of \lore{water over water}, a low thing over a lower thing under \lore{the winter skin}, found where put and not made again and not spent on the reading, muffled as a voice is muffled by a shut door.

At \place{the Cross} the market had begun to assemble in the cold before the top of the morning: boards going up, a rope of pigs somewhere behind the square, the first of the two hundred finding places to lean. Sella set the basket in \place{the moot-post}'s finger's width of shade where no slate lay, the cold iron of the post at his cheek, and stood half a pace off through the reading and the bell, giving no instruction and no reason to the basket. She did not look into it and did not tell it what the morning was. A basket set down is a pot set down and left, and nobody under any roof instructs a basket about what goes over it.

\scenebreak

Eight pigs went over the wet \place{the reading block under the moot-post} while a Sill assessor wrote them down before anybody could ask which eight he meant, his hand going to the slate before his eyes to the rail. The two hundred leaned in and the boards took their weight, and the morning went on without a boundary drawn between the coming of the light and its standing still.

The boy took the square the way a surgeon takes a field by its small sounds first --- the rope under a pig's belly, the rasp of a slate laid on a board, the small flat knock of an iron rim on stone --- because the small sounds do not lie and the large ones are mostly weather. The square had four ways out: the road down to the south bridge and \place{Slate Row}, the steep street up to \place{High Kiln}, \place{the north bridge}, and the road toward \place{the Bone Orchard}. People brought figures to a heap of turnips and left them standing side by side without making them agree, and a Court table at the Slate Row fringe took a name before it took a look at the woman waiting. Nothing in the four ways out was a direction the boy had not already been given, and none of them repeated on this morning or bought a lesson.

By the time the top of the crowd had begun to lean, \person{Berr Clay} came up short off \place{the clay road} to the block with the red wool cord of his office folded square under his coat instead of worn across it and the sweetish green of tasted \lore{lead glaze} at one mouth-corner; under the hot clay of him was the smell of nothing at all, which is the most expensive smell in Grath. His hands were large and clean. He wiped the thumb twice on his apron before touching nothing, once for the slate and once for the figure that would be on it, held a narrow slate unopened against his forearm with the writing inward, passed a finger over the figure through the cloth of his own sleeve, and let his eyes rest on the writing a breath longer than the writing required. Then he read low into the goers-by, \emph{One in twelve, as the notice says}, and sent the figure twice over the basket --- once for the moot and once for the households that came late --- never looking into it.

He called the levy and denied it. \emph{The levy is called. It is not called by me}; \emph{Your house wants it again, there'll be a slate at the watch}; \emph{If your household has heard otherwise, your household has heard wrong} --- \emph{your house}, never I --- taking himself out of his own sentence as a surgeon draws a hand from a field before the dressing goes on. A man who had brought a number up out of a fist was sent to a slate at the watch, and the number went from the fist to a mouth to a shoulder going up the road and arrived nowhere. Something low Berr said to the block about kindness being entered, in the tone of a man setting a cup where a cup belongs, and the block answered nothing.

He went off up the clay road toward High Kiln with the cord folded, the narrow slate under his arm and the box locked, Stone's last true subtraction riding inside beside the eleven before it, believing he had done a kindness, with no anger in him and no triumph, nothing a crowd could fasten on. The boy watched the going as he had watched the coming, by the order of the parts: the thumb wiped before the slate was touched, the finger over the figure before the eyes to the crowd, the sentence said and the speaker taken out of it before the dressing went on. It was the order of a man who removes his hands from a field so that somebody else must close it. Berr had done a kindness and believed it, and that belief, not anger, was the part of him that would one day be remembered against him; the boy filed it on a place of its own without anger and without triumph, because a finding filed in anger is a finding forged.

The boy took Berr's order of reaching apart as a surgeon takes a field apart --- the thumb wiped before the slate was touched, the finger passed over the figure through the sleeve before the eyes went to the goers-by, the sentence said with the speaker taken out of it --- and filed it on a tray of its own beside the other record-first hands, with a finger between it and the runner's eighth and between it and the ordinary writing hand two doors down. No tenth stood up in the empty place. He made no doctrine of the twice-wiped thumb and no payer of the man who had wiped it; he kept the order as an order, the way a surgeon keeps a hand that was in a field and went away again, and held the twelve readings on their own place where they could not touch the keeping at the fold or the four rooms' shared gap.

He filed the twelve arithmetics of the year: the three of \place{Frost}, the first, second and third of \place{Bone}, the first, second and third of \place{Ash}, Stone's first, Stone's second and Stone's last. Twelve were twelve and not one account. The figure read was still not the figure paid. No signature stood on the space between, and no doctrine came out of twelve. Their twelve true subtractions rode locked in Berr's box up the clay road, the key gone up in a coat pocket that does not come back down and the boy having no key. He did not lay the finding across the keeping at the fold, across the four rooms with the same gap, across the four directions standing where put, or across any of his own gaps; he did not let a row come out even by his doing. An account wants a hand to lay twelve likenesses together and a payer standing under all twelve, and no such hand stood and no such payer stood. The hand that would one day lay the district's private keepings together and call the joining an account was far off, not his, and not come; the boy did not reach toward it and did not bring it into the day by wanting it.

The bell went four, four and a gap with nobody crossing himself, the strokes striking through the morning and the afternoon and longer than a church bell. The heard bell stayed in its square beside the told bell of \lore{the Long Night}, its gap not folded into any count and not made a seventh hearing. Three counts in three places did not become one, and agreeing any two would have wanted a payer put under both where none stood.

\person{Hessa Rale} went up the clay road toward \place{Greybank} before noon with Berr's narrow slate held edge-on to her forearm and the writing turned to her sleeve, the thumb along the grain of the frame and nothing pressing where the writing was, the second strap let out for the long road and dried pale scurf of road-mud unbrushed on the shortened skirt. The small flat correction of the strap and the turn of the wrist came first, her hand to the slate before her eyes to the crowd, and no figure came off the face of it. To a woman who asked what she carried she answered the question put and not the one behind it: \emph{I don't read it. I carry it. There's a difference, and the road doesn't care which}; and to the one who wanted more, \emph{Ask the road. It knows the way and I know the weight. That's the whole of what either of us knows}. She gave no figure, read nothing aloud and did not look into the basket, and went on at the pace of a person who has made a bargain with distance and keeps it without thinking. The year's last figure did not make her a reader; her eighth record-first hand stayed eight with no tenth, and she was joined to none --- not to a strip in a sleeve at a well a mile away, not to Berr's twice-wiped thumb, not to the charcoal under the loaf shelf, not to the clerk's inward slate.

Below the north bridge the soft-cuffed clerk stayed set at \place{the out-of-line landing}, where a fifth step should have stood, for a measure nobody called, his cuff grey from washing and the grey worked into the seam at the wrist, his slate turned inward and his hand to the slate before his eyes to the stalls. The market sold over him as water goes over a stone and went away leaving him where he stood, and nobody took him off. Across the square at \place{the pot board} a silent woman turned a cup a quarter and set it back where it stood, the sweetish green of lead glaze at one corner of her mouth staying a colour joined to no ear; at the market's edge a small shape in cast-offs caught the light on a green sliver and went dull, a shape with no name and no house. Neither was given a loaf, a slate or a sale. There was no eighth of either, and the boy never called the colour by a woman's name or the shape by a child's; a colour is not a name and a shape is not a house. He filed the grey cuff and the inward slate on a place of its own, the hand going to the slate before the eyes to the stalls set beside Berr's twice-wiped thumb with a finger between and not joined to it; he made no reader of the clerk and no measure of the market that sold over him. The green at the pot board went on its own place beside the greens behind it, a colour and no eighth, and the shape at the market's edge went on another, a shape and no eighth; neither was named, joined, given a loaf, a slate or a sale, and neither was made to stand for a hearing or a cause. The reading gave him nothing he could enter. He had no key, no slate within reach of the basket, and no number of his own that anybody had asked to spend; the only arithmetic he could keep was the count of his own days, and that count was told by nobody. He set the twelve readings on their place and let the market go on over them, and the hour after the reading lay in his keeping exactly as the hour before it had lain.

The dressed grain covered under wool at home lay turned once and unfigured, read over on this morning by a figure that did not know what it cost. The space between that figure and its price had no signature, and the boy, who could not spend a single one of his own numbers, set no figure against it.

At the going of the light she carried him home, saying nothing of what had been read over the wicker, up the nineteen steps and never into \place{the Debt Loft}, correcting the tilt at every turn of the landing, and set him on \place{the bed} under the roof. She put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face and added no second fold to the crust's empty place; the thread at his mouth stayed frozen in the dry kiln heat and unwiped, because nobody under any roof has ever been given an instruction about the thread.

Behind them the market went off the boards in no order anybody entered: the pigs gone south, the assessor's slate shut, the heap of turnips left where the figures had stood side by side and agreed at nothing. The two hundred thinned into the four ways out and were gone, and the moot-post kept its finger's width of shade with nobody in it. In the Clay Yard the seven walked-around places were gone around once and not twice, widest around the returning-line place, and the dressed grain lay turned once under wool at home with no figure lifted from it and no handful taken.

\scenebreak

In the small hours \place{the towel} came out at the head of the bed as instruments come out when a case is done and before the next is called, with every gap left gap. The file went over the day in the order the day had arrived, and the order was the check: twelve arithmetics behind one unsigned figure --- the three of Frost, the first, second and third of Bone, the first, second and third of Ash, Stone's first, Stone's second and Stone's last --- one in twelve read twice over the basket on the thirtieth and sent twice, once for the moot and once for the late households; the figure read still not the figure paid, no signature on the space, twelve and not one account, no doctrine out of twelve, twelve subtractions locked in Berr's box up the road with the key in a coat pocket that does not come back down and the boy having no key. Six hearings of \lore{the full note} stood beside and never among, the sixth the small flat knock out of \person[Dessa Silt] as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go and never among the sources, and the seventh place stayed empty and was not dug for or filled. \lore[The Hearth Drawing]{The Drawing}'s flat knock stayed outside them on its own place, not a seventh and not a filling of the empty place; the heard bell stayed in its square beside the told, four, four and a gap; the four rooms with the same gap were held apart with a finger between every pair. Nine record-first hands kept no tenth and nine stoppings kept no tenth; the burned slate stood at five, the shut-room tellings at two, the row at five askings with no witness and expressly no sixth, the well-saying at four shapings with no fifth. The five held objects stood with the pot-crack resting, the hand-at-the-sill breath and the holding in the damper beside them, each with a finger between, and the keeping at the fold stood beside the crust's gap, the shelf's two gaps in the room whose floor does not touch this one, the gone pan's round ring and the field's empty place, expressly not a tenth and not a joining. The fourth direction stood where put beside the Ash 5 joining and the Ash 17, Ash 23 and Stone 23 directions, and none of the four repeated and bought no lesson. No sixth hearing, seventh opening, tenth hand, tenth stopping, eighth opening, thirteenth arithmetic, witness, reader, signature or payment had been supplied to make a row come out even, for a finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged.

Under the floor \lore{the carried fire} gave four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer without a name for three hundred and eighty-nine days, and nobody lifted the floor and nobody supplied the pull. Through the finger's-width door the river kept its note of water over water under the winter skin, a covering put on by the cold and taken off by the cold and entered nowhere, found where put and not made again and not spent on the reading or the going of the light. \lore{The widow's mark} was untouched: a market, a going of the light and a bed are not openings, seven openings in seven months are seven and not a rule, and no eighth was made due on a day that had changed the district's arithmetic for the twelfth time and could not change it back.

The hand outside the wool came down and lay flat against the fold, as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. He filed answer three hundred and fifty-three: exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the twelve readings, the bell's gap, the flat knock, the child's grey fingertips, the mother's cup, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the held breath, the two bites of iron, the river's skin, the widow's mark, the fourth direction nor the keeping at the fold.

Stone kept its thirtieth day without a boundary drawn, the deep-winter month holding both its names, older women by weather and slatemen by \lore{Grathic} Account-days, neither set right nor left in so many words. The year went out with its arithmetic changed for the twelfth time and not changeable back, twelve readings standing behind one unsigned figure with no signature on the space and no doctrine out of twelve, and nothing under the roof standing up and calling itself an account. The surgeon's only victory was a refusal, and it cost him nothing: twelve were twelve, and the hand that would lay them together and the payer under them did not stand, and no row came out even by his doing. The count stood at three hundred and eighty-nine exact, six months and two hundred and nine days told by nobody who would have known what a number counted, the carried fire's fifth unpaid the same number, its payer unnamed, and \person{Garl Vail}'s name unspoken the same number.

He took his small clean satisfaction in a field taken as found and left as found, and it cost him nothing, which was how he knew it was not peace; under it, louder, the infant wanted the crust lifted, the thread wiped and a hand on the pot handle, and got none of the three. Both were true. The second was louder. Neither won, no verdict in the quarrel and none looked for. \lore{The Five} stood beyond the last of Stone and were not brought into a day that had earned only its thirtieth. The hand lay flat on the fold on answer three hundred and fifty-three, the crust's corner standing a hair off the wool, with twelve readings standing behind one unsigned figure and the boy having no key to the box going up the road, and the count went on under the roof in Low Kiln, Sill, Grath.
