\daymark{388}{Stone 29, Ninthday, Y.H. 813}

The small hours came down under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill}, \place{Grath}, and the hand outside the wool lay flat against the fold on answer three hundred and fifty, where the going of the light had left it. The count stood at three hundred and eighty-seven, exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air, and nobody under any roof would have known to speak it. It was the twenty-ninth of \place{Stone}, the last ordinary day the year had, and the year's last reading stood one morning off with the twelfth arithmetic waiting beyond it, unbrought and not earned by any hour of this day. Nothing of the eleven behind the unsigned figure had crossed under the roof since the twentieth as sign, lesson, mending, cause, payment or payer, and nothing crossed in these small hours. Eleven were eleven and not one account, and no doctrine had come out of eleven.

\person[Corin]{The boy} lay where he had been set, at the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf} on \place{the bed}, and the surgeon took the body in its old order because there was nothing else to take and the order was a way of being awake. One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust stood two inches from his ear, gone harder and browner in the dry kiln heat with its corner a hair off the wool. Beside it the empty place held the fold of wool a hand had put there on the fifteenth, found where put yesterday at both ends of the day and not made again; a keeping and not a filling, twelve findings added to twelve findings with no twelfth placing among them. The thread of drool at his lip had frozen in the dry heat and was unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. Cold had stopped at cheek, temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, as though it had come to the end of a line written out for it with neither an order to go farther nor an order to go away.

He knew a face composed by cold is not composure, and that pink returning is the surface still cold over the core, a better colour on a worse patient. A cold patient cannot clot; he must be warmed from the outside and slowly, never hurried, never rubbed, and the afterdrop waits past a warming that nobody in this house would perform. A basket set level out of the draught where the heat comes sideways is the careful rewarming he would choose and cannot ask for. \lore{The Afterdrop} waited beyond the warm room he could not reach, and the infant wanted the crust lifted, the thread wiped and a hand on the pot handle, and got none of the three. Both were true. The second was louder. Neither won, and the quarrel was the work.

The keeping was found where put and was not made again. A hand that only reads adds no placing to what it reads: a habit wants a hand that placed twice and a way wants a third, and both want a payer standing under them where none stood. Twelve findings from one finding were still twelve findings and no rule; the small alarm at a double count had been filed on a place of its own as a finding too, and the far-off finger that would one day lay shelf and fold together and call the joining an account was still far off, not his, not come. He put a finger between the keeping and the crust's gap and between the keeping and every other kept thing, and did not lay it across the eleven readings to make a row come out even, for a finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged.

\scenebreak

Before first grey \person{Sella Vail} went to \place{the hearth} before the basket, told the coals it was \lore{the thaw} and turned them with the back of a broken pot, and said “The house will keep it” where another woman would have said I, standing a moment to count a small intake, a hold and a letting-go toward no opening and figuring neither of the two gaps. Her finger lay along the top of the loaf shelf so that the charcoal tally on its underside stayed unread; her lips moved along a two-gapped row and figured neither gap. She glanced once at \place{the clay jar} standing cold under \lore{lead glaze} with the sweetish edge coming and going on a wrong wind past \place{the pot board}, shut her hand half over nothing, the little-door burn on the back of her left hand closing on air, and did not touch \lore{the widow's mark} at her throat: a morning is not an opening, seven openings in seven months are seven and not a rule, and no eighth was made due.

She carried him down the nineteen steps at the half-pace she had kept from every open slate for three years, correcting the tilt at every turn of the landing before it had time to settle into the wicker, so that he felt the small correction before he felt the stair. The instruction went to the wool, to the river wind and to the fire she banked against their coming back, and never to the basket. She told the wool to keep his ear covered; she told the wind nothing, because a wind is owed no instruction it cannot keep. Out in \place{the Clay Yard} the morning went about its ordinary work and left its accounts where they lay, and the yard went around its seven walked-around places with a look and a wide step and no number, once and not twice, widest around \place{the returning-line place} where a hand had gone in on \place{Seed} 15 and had not come out.

At \place{the yard well} \person{Ilva} drew with the pail against her knee. The rope was stiff in the Stone cold and gave its small dry rasp as the bucket came up, and the water below moved dark and without a sound. Clay had dried pale in the crescents of her nails and the finer grey dust of the racks was worked into the seam of one thumb, and she did not scrub the seam out. Her hands knew the weight of a pail and the edge of a wet board and had never been soft. People going about the yard noticed the pail first and the sleeve not at all, which was the order she preferred.

From the basket at the edge of the light the boy saw the well as a surgeon sees a field before the light, by the small things first because the small things do not lie: the dry rasp of the rope going up, the small knock of the pail's iron rim against the stone lip, the dark water moving without a sound under the little roof and giving back no reflection of the grey sky. An open slate lay on the lip a pace from where Ilva stood, the writing turned toward its owner and nobody reading it aloud; it was a nearness and nothing more, a thing set down for its own reasons and not for hers, and he kept the nearness as an absence and did not make a figure of it. The yard went on about its work around the two women as water goes around a stone, and the morning came down thin and went on without a boundary drawn.

A woman waiting her turn, with a pot on her hip and her shawl drawn tight against the river wind, asked what Stone's firings had taken. Ilva's hand went toward her sleeve before her eyes went to the asker, and her mouth opened after both, with the half-beat lying in the gap where a lesser observer would have put an intention. The short strip of notched birch lay in the fold where it always lay, pale where a thumb had rubbed the notches and dark in the uncut grain between; it was not taken out, read, copied, counted or joined to the open slate a pace away on \place{the stone lip}, to the charcoal beneath the loaf shelf, to the cord or the knot-string, and no figure came off the wood.

“Ask the pot what it cost. I keep the count, not the account,” she said, and answered the question put and not the one behind it. Then she put the flat of her hand once more on the sleeve and went back to the pail, and nobody turned twice.

Her thumb did not pass over the notches while the asker waited, nor after she had gone; the flat of the hand on the sleeve had been the whole of her answer to the thing behind the question, and it cost her nothing that a slate could enter. The woman took up her pot and went about her turning, and the well went back to its small sounds --- rope, iron rim, dark water --- and Ilva went back to the drawing as a woman goes back to work that has not been interrupted.

The boy took the order of her reaching apart as a surgeon takes a field apart --- hand to the record before eyes to the person, eyes before mouth, the half-beat in the gap --- and filed it on a place of its own as her ninth record-first hand, with a finger between it and \person{Hessa Rale}'s eighth. He filed no tenth, made no reader of her keeping, joined her to none and made no figure of the slate's nearness. The count she kept stayed in wood. Even with an open slate a pace away on the stone lip, nothing came off her stick that a slate could take, and the boy, who could not spend a single one of his own numbers, knew the small practical courage of it without calling it courage or setting it up as a lesson under a roof that would have entered it.

\scenebreak

She was expressly not the speaker of any voice. The Frost 14 well-saying stood at one saying and four shapings with no fifth; \lore{the burned-slate word} stood at five, the shut-room tellings at two, the row at five askings with no witness. No ownerless voice had come over \place{the boards} on Stone 28 or this morning; the Ash 5 joining and the Ash 17, Ash 23 and Stone 23 directions stood where put and did not repeat and bought no lesson, and the fourth direction stood where put beside them with a finger between every pair. Nothing that arrived nowhere under this roof was made to arrive at her well.

Under the floor \lore{the carried fire} gave four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer without a name for three hundred and eighty-eight days. Nobody lifted the floor and nobody supplied the pull; the house went on over it as water goes over a stone, and the missing pull was not asked to stand as the missing nail in the boards or the gap in the bell, for three counts in three places do not become one and agreeing any two would want a payer put under both where none stood.

In the small hours \place{the towel} came out at the head of the bed as instruments come out when a case is done and before the next is called, with every gap left gap. The five held objects stood with the pot-crack resting and the hand-at-the-sill breath and the holding in the damper beside them; the keeping at the fold stood beside the crust's gap, the shelf's two gaps in the room whose floor does not touch this one, the gone pan's round ring and the field's empty place, with a finger between it and every other kept thing. Six hearings of \lore{the full note} stood beside and never among, the sixth the small flat knock out of \person[Dessa Silt] as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go and never among the sources, and the seventh place stayed empty and was not dug for; \lore[The Hearth Drawing]{The Drawing}'s flat knock stayed outside them on its own place. The heard bell of \lore{the Long Night} stayed in its square beside the told, four, four and a gap, not folded into any count and not made a seventh. Nine record-first hands kept no tenth and nine stoppings kept no tenth; the burned slate stood at five, the shut-room tellings at two, the row at five askings with no witness and the well-saying at four shapings. The four rooms with the same gap were held apart with a finger between every pair, four likenesses and not one account, and the handful let fall back at the rack end stayed one object and was not made again. The finding that eleven arithmetics are not one account sat on a place of its own with no signature on the space and no doctrine standing up out of eleven, and their eleven true subtractions rode locked in a box up the clay road whose key had gone up in a coat pocket and did not come back down and the boy had no key. The twelfth stood one morning off, outside the day, and was not brought in to make a row come out even.

Through \place{the finger's-width door} \place{the river} kept its note of \lore{water over water}, a low thing over a lower thing, muffled under \lore{the winter skin} as a voice is muffled by a shut door, found where put and not made again and not spent on this morning's work or on the going of the light that was still a day away. No hand had laid the skin, no payer stood under it and no source stood to be asked; a sign points to a payer, and a covering left where it is, is weather.

The hand outside the wool came up an inch toward the note --- did not reach, and the inch is not a reach --- took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole on the note's own finishing under the covering, let the intake go as the note ended and came straight down and laid itself flat against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. The note had done before the hand came down: the letting-go was neither early nor stayed, and the hand did not stay.

He filed answer three hundred and fifty-one: exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the eleven readings, the bell's gap, the flat knock, the child's grey fingertips, the mother's cup, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the held breath, the two bites of iron, the river's skin, the widow's mark, the fourth direction nor the keeping at the fold. Stone kept its twenty-ninth day without a boundary drawn, the deep-winter month holding both its names, older women by weather and slatemen by Grathic Account-days, neither set right nor left in so many words. The count stood at three hundred and eighty-eight exact, six months and two hundred and eight days told by nobody who would have known what a number counted, the carried fire's fifth unpaid the same number, its payer unnamed, and \person[Garl Vail]{Garl}'s name unspoken the same number. Twelve findings were twelve findings and not a rule; eleven arithmetics were eleven and not one account; four directions were four and not a witness. The surgeon took his small clean satisfaction in a field taken as found and left as found, and it cost him nothing, which was how he knew it was not peace; under it, louder, the infant wanted the crust lifted, the thread wiped and a hand on the pot handle, and got none of the three. Both were true. The second was louder. Neither won, no verdict in the quarrel and none looked for. \lore{The Five} stood beyond the one morning and were not brought into a day that had not earned them. The hand lay flat on the fold on answer three hundred and fifty-one, the crust's corner standing a hair off the wool, with the strip in Ilva's sleeve unread a mile away in a fold nobody under the Vail roof can see; and the count went on under the roof in Low Kiln, Sill, Grath.
