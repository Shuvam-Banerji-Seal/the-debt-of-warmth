The afternoon came down thin through \place{the finger's-width door} and went on without a boundary drawn. It did not arrive all at once but accumulated, a little grey on \place{the flagstones}, a little grey on the backs of hands, the same light that had lain on the well at morning thinning into the light that would lie on the bed at the going of the light, with no seam anywhere between the one and the other. \person[Corin]{The boy}, set level out of the draught against \place{the Vail kiln-house} wall, took it the way a surgeon takes a field after the instruments are out, by the small signs first --- the dry rasp of a rope going up, the small knock of an iron rim on stone, the way dust moved where nobody had moved it --- because the small signs do not lie and the large ones are mostly weather. \place{The Clay Yard} went around its seven walked-around places with a look and a wide step and no number, once and not twice, widest around \place{the returning-line place} where a hand had gone in on \place{Seed} 15 and had not come out. A second going-around would have made a figure of once, and a figure wants a payer standing under it, and none stood.

He went around them as they were: the clean dark line at \place{the Vail corner} with the finer grey beside it called \lore{the thaw}, thickened in the month's dry cold with the faint sour tang of \lore{Ash} coming up off it where no broom had gone in two years; the space beneath \place[the drying racks]{the lowest rack} where \lore{the broken birth-slates} lay and were entered by nobody; the round ring at \place{the rack end} where the shallow clay pan had once stood, keeping no scrape, crumb, print, chip or word; the bare \place{the trough}; the field's empty place out on \place{the worked strips}; and the returning-line place again at the end of the round, which he did not enter and did not count. The three coverings stayed weather entered nowhere with a finger between every pair --- the gone frost off the well's north lip, the skin on the trough, the silt-clouded plate on \place{the flat stone by the well} --- and the dressed grain under wool at home stayed turned once and unfigured. Nothing in the round was a lesson and nothing was a sign, and nothing crossed under the roof as either.

Costs came to the yard as directions and went away as weather. A man down from \place{Cold Acre} shifted a hare across his shoulders and asked what it would fetch, and \person{Tarn}, not looking at the animal, told him to ask the weather which had carried the snow and could carry the price, and went off toward \place{the clay road} with the hare unweighed and no slate opened. A man with a cracked jar in a rope sling was sent to a pot, and the pot, being a pot, said nothing, and the man went away with the question still in his hands. A woman with a basket of kindling asked what the seventh year would want when it came, and two fence-menders, one wanting the thaw early and one wanting it late, put their case at the same time; to the woman and to both men he said “Yes, house” to each and kept the late, because a late thaw puts off the paying, agreeing so thoroughly that there was nowhere for an argument to find a foot. The boy, who could not spend a single one of his own numbers, watched the prices go off in the directions they had come from and filed no figure of any of them.

At \place{the kindling} the exact account held. Six pieces stood in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check and the seventh lay at the lip beyond it beside \person[Garl Vail]{Garl}'s unforged collector sentence, with no name on the man who had turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on \lore{the tile debt} against the firing of tiles. The small knife lay at the very edge of its sill where a hand could be checked and where a hand could also slip it off. Tarn's hand went up over blade and sill and away, and the coming away was the whole of the checking; there was no breath held at the top of the reach. “Six counted. The next I don't count,” he told the wood, and cut nothing, counted nothing and spent nothing. The held breath he had left at the sill on \place{Ash} 14 was found where it had been put, and his thumb's resting on the uncut seventh of \place{Bone} 26 was found where it had been put, and neither was made again; both stood beside the five held objects with a finger between every pair, not a sixth held object, not a tenth stopping, and with no payer standing to either.

He brought the ear-sentence all the way to the word that would call the loan and stopped there, as a man stops at the edge of a field he has walked nine times and will not walk a tenth before the year is out. It was a remembered ninth stopping with no tenth, and the seventh year stood at its very end with \place{Seed} 16's breath still unspent and the loan uncalled; he did not look along the road, because a term ending is not a payment, a forgiveness or a freedom, and the fear of being entered again for vagrancy stayed his without a figure. His chest had been tight since the dark. Ash was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line, the pale clip-ring at his left wrist caught what little light there was and gave it back, and his stroke went over the shallow bright patch of the flagstones without the half-check, the returned half-check of Bone 8 staying on the stones as a held change and not a rule. The boy took the hand going up and away apart as a surgeon takes a field apart and filed it on a place of its own beside the other keepings, not as a sign of anything and not as a payment.

\person{Jorra Vail} came through the finger's-width door off \place{the Vail wheel-shed} with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bent little finger and white in a fist-crease where a hand had opened, and it was not the bone-coloured \lore{slip} of the wheel. Three or more men unnamed had gone low into \place{the back room} before first grey, and he gave the house no name for them and said nothing about whether the room was cold. He kept his dusty forearms and his clean ones on one tray and parted them as a surgeon parts clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had been broken nor whether it had been a birth-slate while the broken ones under the lowest rack stayed entered by nobody. He spoke to the wheel before he spoke to any person --- “It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady” --- and told a pot “You'll do” in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either. He knew the \lore{full note} that pot would give three turns before it left his hands, with air under it and a want in it, and kept the knowing on a tray of its own beside the hearings and never among them, withheld from any master come up from \place{High Kiln} who would call it early and sing a pot before its time.

When \person{Sella Vail} had not finished telling him the fire was low, his bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper} ahead of her sentence, took the cold out of the fingertip, moved the tongue a hair's breadth and was left standing, so that the end of her instruction arrived at the pot and the household straightened around the waiting as cloth straightens when a weight is lifted. One piece of iron had two bites, the holding and the taking, and no \lore{Grathic} word covered both mercies. He took the finger out without leaving the extra holding of \place{Ash} 26 a second time; that holding had been gathered once and stayed where nobody entered it, found where put, not made again. Neither looked at the other and neither said what both knew: that \emph{he's not Vail} stayed sealed as a lid nobody had tried to lift in three years, and the promise of the boy's name to the men who burn slates was neither given nor withdrawn on the day before the year's last reading.

The burned slate stood at five with a finger between the fifth and the four, and no sixth telling had come over \place{the boards} on this afternoon to make it six. The shut-room tellings stood at two, the row at five askings with no witness and expressly no sixth, the well-saying at four shapings with no fifth, nine record-first hands with no tenth and nine stoppings with no tenth. \lore{The burned-slate word} went on gathering interest against the ungiven promise without joining it and without paying it, and the Ash 5 joining and the Ash 17, Ash 23 and Stone 23 directions stood where put and did not repeat and bought no lesson. No ownerless voice came over the boards on \place{Stone} 28 or this afternoon; the fourth direction stood where put beside the other three with a finger between every pair, and nothing in any of them stood up and called itself a witness. The boy kept them on places of their own and did not lay one across another to make a row come out even, for a finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged.

At the going of the light she carried him up the nineteen steps at the half-pace from every open slate, correcting the tilt at every turn of the landing before it had time to settle into the wicker, and set him on \place{the bed} under the roof and never in \place{the Debt Loft}. The instruction went to the wool, to the river wind and to the fire she banked against their coming back, and never to the basket. She put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face and added no second fold to the crust's empty place; the thread at his mouth stayed frozen in the dry kiln heat and unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread. Before the basket she went to \place{the hearth}, told the coals it was the thaw and turned them with the back of a broken pot, and said “The house will keep it” where another woman would have said I, standing a moment to count a small intake, a hold and a letting-go toward no opening and figuring neither of the two gaps. Her finger lay along the top of \place{the loaf shelf} so that the charcoal tally on its underside stayed unread; her lips moved along a two-gapped row and figured neither gap. She glanced once at \place{the clay jar} standing cold under \lore{lead glaze} with the sweetish edge coming and going on a wrong wind past \place{the pot board}, shut her hand half over nothing, the little-door burn on the back of her left hand closing on air, and did not touch \lore{the widow's mark} at her throat: a going of the light and a bed are not openings, seven openings in seven months are seven and not a rule, and no eighth was made due on a day that had not earned one.

On the pot board the twice-mended pot was lifted thumb-first where the wire crossed the crack before the weight came on the handle, the hairline of the original break showing between the old-brass staple and the tighter wire. “Leave the crack showing. People trust a thing they can see has been handled” and “If it's worth mending it's worth mending twice. The second time is the real one” went to the pot and the fire and never to a person, improved in the keeping and very likely not as good as the man had said them; repair stayed apart from restoration, because a restored thing hides the hand that handled it and a mended thing leaves that hand showing. The dressed grain was turned once under wool with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child, lifting no figure and taking no handful. The pot rim was set two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat and no look toward the bed; his hand went toward it and stopped where the half-beat had stopped it, and the interval was bought at nothing and was not a gift and the stopping was his. Under the floor \lore{the carried fire} gave four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer without a name for three hundred and eighty-eight days, and nobody lifted the floor and nobody supplied the pull.

In the small hours \place{the towel} came out at the head of the bed as instruments come out when a case is done and before the next is called, with every gap left gap. Six hearings of the full note stood beside and never among, the sixth the small flat knock out of \person[Dessa Silt] as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go and never among the sources, and the seventh place stayed empty and was not dug for; \lore[The Hearth Drawing]{The Drawing}'s flat knock stayed outside them on its own place, not a seventh and not a filling of the empty place. The heard bell of \lore{the Long Night} stayed in its square beside the told, four, four and a gap, not folded into any count and not made a seventh hearing. Three counts in three places did not become one --- the kiln's breathing, the boards' four nails and a thumb-width knot, the fire's four pulls and missing fifth --- and agreeing any two would have wanted a payer put under both where none stood. The four rooms with the same gap were held apart with a finger between every pair, four likenesses and not one account. The five held objects stood with the pot-crack resting, the hand-at-the-sill breath and the holding in the damper beside them, each with a finger between, and the keeping at the fold stood beside the crust's gap, the shelf's two gaps in the room whose floor does not touch this one, the gone pan's round ring and the field's empty place, expressly not a tenth and not a joining.

The finding that eleven arithmetics are not one account sat on a place of its own with no signature on the space and no doctrine standing up out of eleven: the three of \place{Frost}, the first, second and third of Bone, the first, second and third of Ash, Stone's first and second, and their eleven true subtractions riding locked in \person{Berr Clay}'s box up the clay road with the key gone up in a coat pocket that did not come back down and the boy having no key. The twelfth stood one morning off, outside the day, waiting to be earned by a morning that had not yet come; it was not brought in to make a row come out even and bought no lesson in advance. Nothing under the roof had stood up and called itself an account, and no hand had come to lay shelf and fold together.

Through the finger's-width door \place{the river} kept its note of \lore{water over water}, a low thing over a lower thing, muffled under \lore{the winter skin} as a voice is muffled by a shut door, found where put and not made again and not spent on this afternoon or on the going of the light that had just passed. No hand had laid the skin, no payer stood under it and no source stood to be asked; a sign points to a payer, and a covering left where it is, is weather.

The hand outside the wool came up an inch toward the note --- did not reach, and the inch is not a reach --- took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole on the note's own finishing under the covering, let the intake go as the note ended and came straight down and laid itself flat against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. The note had done before the hand came down: the letting-go was neither early nor stayed, and the hand did not stay.

He filed answer three hundred and fifty-two: exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the eleven readings, the bell's gap, the flat knock, the child's grey fingertips, the mother's cup, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the held breath, the two bites of iron, the river's skin, the widow's mark, the fourth direction nor the keeping at the fold. Stone kept its twenty-ninth day without a boundary drawn, the deep-winter month holding both its names, older women by weather and slatemen by Grathic Account-days, neither set right nor left in so many words. The count stood at three hundred and eighty-eight exact, six months and two hundred and eight days told by nobody who would have known what a number counted, the carried fire's fifth unpaid the same number, its payer unnamed, and Garl's name unspoken the same number. Twelve findings were twelve findings and not a rule; eleven arithmetics were eleven and not one account; four directions were four and not a witness. The surgeon took his small clean satisfaction in a field taken as found and left as found, and it cost him nothing, which was how he knew it was not peace; under it, louder, the infant wanted the crust lifted, the thread wiped and a hand on the pot handle, and got none of the three. Both were true. The second was louder. Neither won, no verdict in the quarrel and none looked for. \lore{The Five} stood beyond the one morning and were not brought into a day that had not earned them. The hand lay flat on the fold on answer three hundred and fifty-two, the crust's corner standing a hair off the wool, with the twelfth arithmetic still outside the day and waiting, and the count went on under the roof in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill}, \place{Grath}.
