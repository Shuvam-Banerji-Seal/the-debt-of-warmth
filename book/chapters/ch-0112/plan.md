## Scene 5 — The Twelfth Arithmetic

- **Present.** Corin/Dan, Sella Vail, Berr Clay, Hessa Rale; Garl Vail in objects. - **Beats in order.**
  1. Open on answer 352 and the count exact at 388 carried in; new day, new date, no recap; nothing from Stone 28 or 29 crosses under the roof as sign, lesson, mending, cause, payment or payer.
  2. The surgeon takes the body in its old order — crust, empty place with the fold, frozen thread, cold stopping at cheek/temple/wrist, afterdrop waiting — and knows the level basket out of the draught is the rewarming he would choose and cannot ask for; the infant's want quarrels without verdict, both true, the second louder, neither winning.
  3. Sella goes to the hearth before the basket, carries him down the nineteen steps before first light at the half-pace from every open slate, instruction to the wool and the river wind and never to the basket; at the Cross she sets the basket in the moot-post's finger's width of shade where no slate lay and stands half a pace off through the reading and the bell, giving no instruction and no reason to the basket (T058/T079).
  4. **The twelfth arithmetic (T015/T041/T047, advanced and NOT paid).** Berr Clay comes up short off the clay road to the reading block under the moot-post with the red wool cord of his office folded square under his coat and the sweetish green of tasted lead glaze at one mouth-corner, under the hot clay of him the smell of nothing at all. Large clean hands wipe the thumb twice before touching nothing, once for the slate and once for the figure; a narrow slate is held unopened to the forearm with the writing inward; a finger passes over the figure through the cloth of the sleeve and the eyes rest on the writing a breath longer than the writing required. He reads *One in twelve, as the notice says* low into the goers-by and sends the figure twice over the basket — once for the moot, once for the households that come late — never looking into it. He calls the levy and denies it with *The levy is called. It is not called by me* and *your house*, never *I*, taking himself out of his own sentence as a surgeon draws a hand from a field before the dressing goes on; a man with a number in his fist is sent to a slate at the watch; something low is said to the block about kindness being entered, in the tone of a man setting a cup where a cup belongs, and the block answers nothing. He goes off up the clay road toward High Kiln with the cord folded, the narrow slate under his arm and the box locked, Stone's last true subtraction riding inside beside the eleven before it, believing he has done a kindness, with no anger and no triumph — the belief that will one day be remembered against him.
  5. The boy files the twelve arithmetics: the three of Frost, the first, second and third of Bone, the first, second and third of Ash, and Stone's first, second and last. Twelve are twelve and not one account; the figure read is still not the figure paid; no signature stands on the space; no doctrine comes out of twelve. The twelve true subtractions ride locked in Berr's box, the key gone up in a coat pocket that does not come back down and the boy having no key. He does not lay the finding across the keeping, the four rooms' gap, the four directions or any of his own gaps, and does not let a row come out even by his doing.
  6. The bell goes four, four and a gap with nobody crossing himself; the heard bell stays in its square beside the told bell of the Long Night, its gap not folded into any count and not made a seventh (T078).
  7. Hessa Rale goes up the clay road toward Greybank before noon with the narrow slate edge-on to her forearm and the writing turned to her sleeve, thumb along the grain of the frame and nothing pressing where the writing is, hand to slate before eyes to crowd; no figure comes off it; she is not made a reader by the year's last figure, her eighth record-first hand stays eight with no tenth and she is joined to none (T080 held).
  8. Below the north bridge the soft-cuffed clerk stays set at the out-of-line landing for a measure nobody called, cuff grey from washing, slate to forearm, hand to slate before eyes to stalls, the market selling over him as water goes over a stone (T090 held). Across the square at the pot board a silent woman's sweetish lead-glaze green stays a colour and at the market's edge a small shape in cast-offs stays a shape, with no eighth of either, neither named, joined, given a loaf, a slate or a sale, and never Fenna or Ulda in the narration (T050/T051, T033/T034 held). The dressed grain covered under wool at home is read over by a figure that does not know what it cost (T077).
  9. Sella carries him home at the going of the light saying nothing of what has been read over the wicker, never into the Debt Loft, fold right at ear and chin, no second fold.
  10. Small hours: towel out, file out, every gap left gap; answer **353** filed exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer.
- **Emotional turn.** The year's arithmetic changes for the twelfth time and cannot be changed back, and the boy's only victory is a refusal: twelve readings are readings, not an account, and the signature that would make them one does not exist. Berr goes home believing he has done a kindness; that belief, not anger, is what will be remembered against him.

## Scene 6 — The Year Goes Out Without a Boundary

- **Day and time.** Day 389, Stone 30, Tenthday. The end of the day: the carry home, the going of the light, and the small hours at the very close of the year. Ends with answer **354** and the count at **389 exact**.
- **Place.** The Vail kiln-house under the roof — the bed at the low angle of the loaf shelf, the boards, the hearth, the pot board, the towel at the head of the bed, the finger's-width door and the river beyond.
- **Present.** Corin/Dan, Sella Vail; Garl Vail in objects. No Berr, Hessa, Ilva, Tarn, Jorra, collector, runner, market, Cross or bell after the carry home. No ownerless voice over the boards.
- **Beats in order.**
  1. Sella sets him on his bed under the roof and never in the Debt Loft, puts the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face and adds no second fold; the thread stays frozen and unwiped; the wool stays found where put and is not made again — still a finding, not a placing, no habit, way, sign, hearing, mending, cause or rule, with no second fold, third crust, name, gift, payment or payer; the far-off finger that would lay shelf and fold together and call the joining an account is still far off, not his, not come (T097/T060/T086 held).
  2. She goes to the hearth before the basket, tells the coals it is the thaw, turns them with the back of a broken pot, says *The house will keep it* where another woman would say *I*, counts a small intake, a hold and a letting-go toward no opening with neither gap figured; finger on top of the loaf shelf so the charcoal tally stays unread; lips along a two-gapped row unfigured; one glance at the clay jar cold under lead glaze with the sweetish edge coming and going on a wrong wind past the pot board and the hand half over nothing, the little-door burn closing on air.
  3. The widow's mark at her throat, the cord oiled where a working thumb has touched it ten thousand times, is not touched: a market, a going of the light and a bed are not openings, seven openings in seven months are seven and not a rule, and no eighth was made due (T058/T079).
  4. On the pot board the twice-mended pot is lifted thumb-first where the wire crosses the crack before the weight comes on the handle, the hairline of the original break showing between old-brass staple and tighter wire. *Leave the crack showing. People trust a thing they can see has been handled* and *If it's worth mending it's worth mending twice. The second time is the real one* go to the pot and the fire and never to a person, improved in the keeping and very likely not as good as Garl said them; repair stays apart from restoration. The dressed grain is turned once under wool with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child, lifting no figure and taking no handful. The pot rim is set two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat and no look toward the bed; his hand goes toward it and stops where the half-beat stopped it, the interval bought at nothing and not a gift and the stopping his (T077).
  5. Under the floor the carried fire gives four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer unnamed three hundred and eighty-nine days (T088).
  6. In the small hours the towel comes out as instruments come out when a case is done and before the next is called, with every gap left gap. The check runs: twelve arithmetics behind one unsigned figure — the three of Frost, the first, second and third of Bone, the first, second and third of Ash, and Stone's first, second and last — one in twelve read twice over the basket on Stone 30 and sent twice, once for the moot and once for the late households; figure read still not figure paid, no signature on the space, twelve and not one account, no doctrine out of twelve, twelve subtractions locked in Berr's box up the road with the key in a coat pocket that does not come back down and the boy having no key. Six hearings of the full note stand beside and never among, the sixth the small flat knock out of Dessa Silt as the men lifted her, the seventh place empty and not dug for; the Drawing's flat knock stays outside them on its own place; the heard bell stays in its square beside the told, four, four and a gap; the four rooms with the same gap are held apart with a finger between every pair; nine record-first hands and nine stoppings keep no tenth; burned slate at five, shut-room tellings at two, row at five askings with no witness, well-saying at four shapings; the five held objects with pot-crack resting, hand-at-sill breath and damper holding beside them, each with a finger between, and the keeping beside them and expressly not a tenth. No sixth hearing, seventh opening, tenth hand, tenth stopping, eighth opening, thirteenth arithmetic, witness, reader or payment was supplied to make a row come out even, for a finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged.
  7. Through the finger's-width door the river keeps its note of water over water under the winter skin, a covering put on by the cold and taken off by the cold and entered nowhere, found where put and not made again, not spent on the reading or the going of the light and not made to buy a lesson (T083/T085 held).
  8. The hand outside the wool comes up an inch toward the note, does not reach, takes a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, holds the interval whole on the note's own finishing under the covering, lets it go as the note ended and comes straight down and lays itself flat against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking; the note had done before the hand came down, so the letting-go was neither early nor stayed, and the hand did not stay.
  9. Answer **354** filed exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the twelve readings, the bell's gap, the flat knock, the child's grey fingertips, the mother's cup, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the held breath, the two bites of iron, the river's skin, the widow's mark, the fourth direction nor the keeping at the fold.
  10. Stone keeps its twenty-eighth, twenty-ninth and thirtieth days without a boundary drawn, the deep-winter month holding both its names — older women by weather, slatemen by Grathic Account-days — neither set right nor left in so many words; the year ends without a boundary drawn and the Five stand beyond, unbrought. Count **389 exact**, six months and two hundred and nine days told by nobody, fire's fifth unpaid the same number and its payer unnamed, Garl's name unspoken the same number. Surgeon's small clean satisfaction costs nothing, which is how he knows it is not peace; under it, louder, the infant wants crust lifted, thread wiped and hand on pot handle, and gets none of the three — both true, second louder, neither winning, no verdict and none looked for.
- **Emotional turn.** The file goes out with every gap left gap and the year goes out without a boundary drawn. Twelve findings, twelve arithmetics and four directions are counts, not an account; the surgeon's satisfaction is not peace, and the infant's wants are not answered. Forward, not closed.
- **Sensory anchors.** Banked coals turned with a broken pot's back; woodsmoke, wet wool and the sour tang of Ash; the twice-mended pot's hairline between brass and wire; lead glaze's sweetish edge on a wrong wind; the little-door burn closing on air; four low pulls under the floor; the river's note muffled under its winter skin; the towel at the head of the bed.
- **Threads advanced, none paid.** T097/T060/T086 held (keeping found where put, still a finding); T058/T079/T100 (hearth grammar, charcoal unread, grain unfigured, widow's mark untouched); T077; T088 (fire's fifth unpaid 389 days); T015/T041/T047 (twelve behind one unsigned figure, no signature, no doctrine, locked box); T061/T082 (six hearings, seventh place empty, Drawing's knock outside, heard bell beside told); T081 (four rooms' gap held apart); T080/T090/T050/T051/T033/T034 held; T083/T085 (river's skin found where put and unspent); T009/T013/T092 and T098 carried; T036/T076/T006, T062/T035/T031, T035/T031/T065/T095/T101 held; T012 and T093/T094/T096 carried.
- **Foreshadowing planted.** Twelve readings and twelve findings gather toward the far-off hand that will lay shelf and fold together and call the joining an account, a hand not his and not yet come; Tarn's loan uncalled at the very end of the seventh year; Jorra's promise gathering interest against the burned slate and four directions; the second chill's withheld cause and the cold foot uncured carry into the Five. **Paid off: nothing.**
- **Exact last image.** The hand lies flat on the fold on answer 354, the crust's corner standing a hair off the wool, with count 389 exact and the year going out without a boundary drawn while the river keeps its note under the winter skin beyond the finger's-width door.

## How the final moment pulls into Chapter 113

The chapter closes with the hand laid down on answer **354** and the count exact at **389** — six months and two hundred and nine days told by nobody, the carried fire's fifth unpaid and its payer unnamed, Garl's name unspoken the same number, twelve arithmetics behind one unsigned figure with the figure read still not the figure paid and no doctrine out of twelve. The year has gone out without a boundary drawn and nothing under the roof has stood up and called itself an account. Chapter 113 opens on **Kindling, Y.H. 813 (Day 390)**, the first of the Five: the year's fire comes up the Clay Yard in a clay dish from hearth to hearth and enters the house from a source nobody names or prices — the carried fire's payer still unnamed and the fifth pull still unpaid even though the year is counted complete, planting T088 toward the later naming of the Hearth as a wound. A collector takes the year's first measure: the flat leather satchel, the cuff soft from washing and folded back twice, the cold iron tube from a roll of wool, the grey fingertips, the hand to the slate before the eyes to the bed and the flat sentence *The measure is banked. The account stands. The return is recorded* — an ordinary writing hand with no pause in it, on its own tray, joined to none. Weighing brings the Cross grammar in a temple's clean voice; the Long Night brings stories with gaps the shape of vessels' intervals and a bell told four, four and a gap kept beside the heard bell and never made a seventh. The keeping, the four rooms' gap, the charcoal under the shelf, Ilva's strip, Hessa's unread slate, Jorra's promise, Tarn's uncalled loan, the cold foot and the second chill all cross into the Five unchanged and unspent. The hand goes on answering; the count goes on.
