\daymark{387}{Stone 28, Eighthday, Y.H. 813}

\dropcap{T}{he hand} had lain flat on the fold since the going of the last light, and the dark went out of the room under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill}, \place{Grath} by degrees, the way a fire goes out under blind ash --- not lifted away but thinned, the coals behind the wall giving back their heat in fours and leaving the fifth where it had been left since the night a boy came under this roof to stay. \person[Corin]{The boy} took the field as it came, in the order it came, as a surgeon reads a case off a towel before the light is good enough to trust: the crust first, stiff and leathery-brown at the edge, two inches from the ear with its corner standing a hair off the wool; the empty place beside it; the thread of drool frozen at the lip in the dry kiln heat and wiped by nobody, because nobody under any roof has ever been given an instruction about the thread. Three things found as they had been left, and none the better for his finding them. Dry heat had taken the moisture out of the room overnight and left the crust harder and browner than it had been at the going of the light, a small tough thing with a burnt edge; the wool lay folded once in the empty place and had not been folded again by any hand in the dark.

He was carried everywhere and told by nobody, and the telling he did himself was in the only register he had: three hundred and forty-eight honest answers stood on the sixth place, with the three hundred and forty-ninth not yet begun, and three hundred and eighty-six days behind him, six months and two hundred and six, with the twenty-eighth of Stone to be lived before the light went. The year's last reading stood two mornings off. It did not stand in this room. Nothing that had been read over a basket on the twentieth of this month had crossed under this roof as sign, lesson, mending, cause, payment or payer, and nothing crossed now; the eleven arithmetics standing behind one unsigned figure kept their distance from a morning that had not earned a twelfth. He had once reduced a thousand shoulders in a room that smelled of antiseptic and hot metal, with a resident holding the light and a nurse calling the time; he could not lift a crust two inches to his own mouth, and the fact had long ago stopped being funny without ever quite stopping being true.

Cold had stopped at cheek, temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, as though it had come to the end of a line written out for it with neither an order to go farther nor an order to go away. \lore{The Afterdrop} waited past a warming nobody in this house would perform. He knew the face that cold composes is not composure, and that pink returning is the surface warm over a core that has not warmed at all; he knew a cold patient cannot clot and will seem better before he is worse, and must be warmed from the outside and slowly, never hurried, never rubbed, the way one turns a body toward a fire and waits for the fingers before trusting the colour. A basket set level out of the draught where the heat came to a body sideways was the careful rewarming he would choose and could not ask for. Under the wool the infant wanted the crust lifted, the thread wiped and a hand on the pot handle. Both registers were true. The second was louder. Neither won. The quarrel was the work, and he had stopped looking for a verdict in it, which was not the same thing as having found one.

\scenebreak

First grey came thin through the finger's-width door and laid no boundary on anything. \person{Sella Vail} went to \place{the hearth} before she went to the basket, as she had gone at both ends of every day for three years. “It's the thaw,” she told the coals, and turned them with the back of a broken pot so that the ash lifted and the red showed and went under again. She stood a moment with her hands gone still, counting a small intake, a hold and a letting-go toward no opening, and gave neither of the two gaps a figure; a thing counted aloud becomes a thing the Court can enter. “The house will keep it,” she said where another woman would have said \emph{I}, and the sentence went to the fire and not to the wicker. Her finger lay along the top of \place{the loaf shelf} so that the charcoal tally on its underside stayed unread, and her lips moved along a two-gapped row and figured neither gap. She glanced once at the clay jar standing cold under \lore{lead glaze} beyond \place{the pot board}, with the sweetish edge of it coming and going on a wrong wind, and shut her hand half over nothing, the little-door burn on the back of her left hand closing on air. She did not touch \lore{the widow's mark} at her throat, the cord oiled where a working thumb had touched it ten thousand times: a morning and a hearth are no openings, seven openings in seven months are seven and not a rule, and no eighth was made due.

On the pot board the twice-mended pot was lifted thumb-first where the wire crossed the crack, before the weight came on the handle, so that the hairline of the original break showed between the old-brass staple and the tighter wire. \person{Garl Vail}'s two proverbs went with the lifting, improved in the keeping and very likely not as good as the man had said them: “Leave the crack showing. People trust a thing they can see has been handled,” to the pot; “If it's worth mending it's worth mending twice. The second time is the real one,” to the fire. They went to the pot and the fire and never to a person, because a thing said to a person becomes a thing a Court can enter. Repair stayed apart from restoration: a restored thing hides the hand that handled it and a mended thing leaves that hand showing. The dressed grain she turned once under wool with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child, lifting no figure and taking no handful; it lay covered and unfigured on the cool stone at the rack end and did not know what it cost. She set the pot rim two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat and no look toward the bed. Inside the wool his hand went toward the rim and stopped where the half-beat had stopped it. The interval was bought at nothing and was not a gift, and the stopping was his.

She carried him down the nineteen steps at the half-pace she had kept from every open slate for three years, correcting the tilt at every turn of the landing before it had time to settle into the wicker, so that he felt the small correction before he felt the stair. The instruction went to the wool, to the river wind, to the fire she would bank against their coming back, and never to the basket. “Keep his ear covered,” she told the wool. To the wind she said nothing, because a wind is owed no instruction it cannot keep. A basket set down is a pot set down, and a pot is told where to stand and not why. He took the descent as a wound is taken apart, by the small things first: the scrape of a heel on the second step, the draught that came up at the fifth and went out again at the sixth, the particular hollow the basket made against her hip when she shifted her weight at the turn. Under the wool the infant wanted the tilt corrected the other way, toward the crust standing its hair off two inches from his ear, and wanted the thread at his lip wiped, and got neither. Both wants were true and the second was louder and neither won. That was the quarrel, and the quarrel was the work.

\scenebreak

Out in \place{the Clay Yard} the morning went about its ordinary tending and left its accounts where they lay. The yard went around its seven walked-around places with a look and a wide step and no number: the clean dark line at \place{the Vail corner} with its finer grey hairline that the house called \lore{the thaw}, unswept and thickened in the month's dry cold, carrying the faint sour tang of \lore{Ash} up off it where no broom had gone in two years; the space beneath \place{the lowest rack} where \lore{the broken birth-slates} lay and were entered by nobody; the round ring at \place{the rack end} where the shallow clay pan had once stood, keeping no scrape, no crumb, no print, no chip and no word; the field's empty place out on \place{the worked strips}; \place{the trough} with its skin drawn edge to edge; the silt-clouded plate of ice on \place{the flat stone by the well}, set a little out of the path of feet; and, widest of all, \place{the returning-line place} where a hand had gone in on \place{Seed} 15 and had not come out. The going-around there was so wide that her elbow cut the corner and the basket canted the other way to balance it, and she righted it without looking down. Once. Not twice. A second going-around would have made a figure of once, and a figure wants a payer standing under it and none stood, so the step was taken and not entered. The satisfaction of a field gone around as found and left as found cost nothing, which was how the boy knew it was not peace.

Beside the last of the seven lay \person{Garl Vail} in objects and not in a story: the salt-stiff coat with its left-shoulder crease on the peg behind the door, the hollow wheel-seat at \place{the Vail wheel-shed} that nobody was allowed to fill, the clean dark line that had been his ash-marker, the twice-mended pot's hairline, the unforged collector sentence beside the knife at \place{the kindling}, and \lore{the tile debt} circling without a name and worse for being uncalled. He was a big man with clay in the creases of his hands and a laugh that ended in a cough, who mended pots for a living and would not let anyone mend him; his name had not been spoken under this roof for three hundred and eighty-six days, and the keeping of him was maintenance and not a story. Nothing in the yard stood up and called any of it a lesson.

The three coverings stayed where the weather had put them, with a finger between every pair. The white frost was gone off the north lip of \place{the yard well}; the skin lay drawn edge to edge across the trough; the plate of ice clouded with silt stood on its flat stone out of the way of feet. Weather entered nowhere and was not made a cause, a sign, a payment or a promise. Out on the worked strips the field kept its empty place and did not fill it. Beyond the yard \place{the river} kept its low note of \lore{water over water}, a low thing over a lower thing, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming; over it from bank to bank since the afternoon of \place{Stone} 1 lay \lore{the winter skin}, a covering put on by the cold and taken off by the cold and entered nowhere, found where put on the first day of the month and found again this morning and not made again, not spent on the week's work or on the going of the light and not made to buy a lesson under any roof. No hand had laid the skin, no payer stood under it and no source stood to be asked. A sign points to a payer; a covering left where it is, is weather. He kept the note and the covering on separate places with a finger between them, so that no row came out even by his doing.

\scenebreak

The keeping in the crust's empty place was found where put and was not made again: the wool folded once, exactly where a hand had put it on the fifteenth, changed by nobody in the night, with no second fold, no third crust, no name spoken over the place, no gift, no payment and no payer. It stayed a finding. A habit wants a hand that placed twice and a way that a third, and both want a payer where none stands; a hand that only reads adds no placing however many times it reads. The finding sat on a place of its own beside the crust's gap, the shelf's two gaps, the gone pan's round ring and the field's empty place, with a finger between every pair, and was not a joining of rooms, not a sixth held object, not a sign, a hearing, a mending, a cause or a rule. The far-off finger that would one day lay shelf and fold together and call the joining an account was still far off, not his, and not come.

Three counts stood in three places and none rose into another. Behind the wall \place{the kiln mouth} kept its last firing long gone cold and answered nothing. \place{The boards} fronting the shelf kept four nails on the near board, four on the next and a thumb-width knot where a fifth nail might have been driven and was not. Under the floor \lore{the carried fire} gave four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer without a name for three hundred and eighty-six days. Agreeing any two of the three would have wanted a payer put under both, and none stood to any of them. The heard bell of \lore{the Long Night} stayed in its square beside the told, four, four and a gap, and its gap was not folded into any count and not made a seventh. Six hearings of \lore{the full note} stood beside and never among, the sixth the small flat knock out of \person[Dessa Silt] as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go and never among the sources, and the seventh place stayed empty and was not dug for or filled. \lore[The Hearth Drawing]{The Drawing}'s small flat knock out of the neighbour on the pallet stayed outside them on its own place, not a seventh and not a filling of the empty place, for a hearing wants a source standing and that room had been seen from a basket and not entered.

Eleven arithmetics of the year --- the three of \place{Frost}, the first, second and third of \place{Bone}, the first, second and third of \place{Ash}, \place{Stone}'s first and Stone's second --- stood behind one unsigned figure: one in twelve, read twice over the basket on the twentieth and sent twice, once for the moot and once for the late households. The figure read was still not the figure paid; no signature stood on the space between, no doctrine came out of eleven, and eleven readings were eleven and not one account. Their eleven true subtractions rode locked in \person{Berr Clay}'s box up \place{the clay road} toward \place{High Kiln}, the key gone up in a coat pocket that did not come back down and the boy having no key. No twelfth arithmetic was brought into a morning that had not earned it, and none of the Ash 5 joining, the Ash 17, Ash 23 and Stone 23 directions repeated or bought a lesson; they stood where put. No ownerless voice came over the boards.

In the small hours \place{the towel} came out at the head of the bed as instruments come out when a case is done and before the next is called, with every gap left gap. He went over the field by the place of each thing and checked every object and left every gap a gap, and supplied nothing to make a row come out even, for a finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged. No sixth hearing, seventh opening, tenth hand, tenth stopping, eighth opening, thirteenth arithmetic, witness, reader or payment was supplied; the burned-slate word stood at five, the shut-room tellings at two, the row at five askings with no witness, the well-saying at four shapings, nine record-first hands and nine stoppings with no tenth, four rooms with the same gap held apart with a finger between every pair --- the birth room in \place{House Silt}, this kiln-house, the room where the slate was read on market-day and \place{the Drawing house} seen two doors down --- four likenesses and not one account, an account wanting a hand to lay them together and a payer under all four, and no such hand standing and no such payer.

Through the finger's-width door the river kept its note of water over water under the winter skin, muffled as a voice is muffled by a shut door. The hand outside the wool came up an inch toward it --- did not reach, and the inch is not a reach --- took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole on the note's own finishing under the covering, let the intake go as the note ended and came straight down and laid itself flat against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. The note had done before the hand came down: the letting-go was neither early nor stayed, and the hand did not stay.

He filed answer three hundred and forty-nine: exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the eleven readings, the bell's gap, the flat knock, the child's grey fingertips, the mother's cup, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the held breath, the two bites of iron, the river's skin, the widow's mark, the fourth direction nor the keeping at the fold. Stone kept its twenty-eighth day without a boundary drawn, the deep-winter month holding both its names, older women by weather and slatemen by \lore{Grathic} Account-days, neither set right nor left in so many words. Two mornings stood between this day and the month's last reading and were not brought into it, and \lore{The Five} stood beyond that. The count stood at three hundred and eighty-seven exact, six months and two hundred and seven days told by nobody who would have known what a number counted, the carried fire's fifth unpaid the same number, its payer unnamed, and Garl's name unspoken the same number. The surgeon took his small clean satisfaction in a field taken as found and left as found, and it cost him nothing, which was how he knew it was not peace; under it, louder, the infant wanted the crust lifted, the thread wiped and a hand on the pot handle, and got none of the three. Both were true. The second was louder. Neither won, no verdict in the quarrel and none looked for. The keeping is a finding and not a rule; eleven arithmetics are eleven and not one account; four directions are four and not a witness. The hand lay flat on the fold on answer three hundred and forty-nine, the crust's corner standing a hair off the wool, and the count went on under the roof in Low Kiln, Sill, Grath.
