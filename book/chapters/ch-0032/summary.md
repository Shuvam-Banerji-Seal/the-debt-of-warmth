## Chronicle

The reed month went into its last third in wet-wool light. Sella Vail carried the boy at the half-pace from every open slate, giving the instruction to the wool and the river wind and never to the basket. The reeds came in armfuls judged by the arm that carried them; a woman at the racks who asked what the hands would be paid was answered with the weather and the river. The yard went around the clean dark line, the thaw, the rack space with its broken birth-slates and the empty place where a hand went in on Seed 15 and did not come out, with a look and no number. Under the loaf shelf Sella reached a finger beneath the board where the charcoal tally lies, without looking or a word, and brought it away.

Before first grey three or four men went low into the back room, and Sella gave them no name. Jorra Vail came through with grey slate dust past the bent finger, told a pot *You'll do* and withheld its full note from a kiln-master who said it was singing before its time, then put that finger into the cold damper ahead of Sella's unfinished sentence. At the hearth Tarn brought the ear-sentence a fifth time to the word that would call the loan and stopped there, no figure, no name, drawing the breath he did not spend. The boy laid a fifth interval on the tray beside four.

At the Weir a reed-cutter tied a knot with his teeth, bit the end off and put his grey cord inside his coat: *You can ask the cord* --- *No more can the Court*. Orra Weir gave a date where a figure was asked, the forty-first double turn legible and its name unspoken; Mira Silt answered a thin run with her own answer, her thumb under her apron to a grey cord with a second twist beneath it, her board beside Orra's and not touching. At the Cross Berr Clay wiped his thumb twice, paid two marks short for eels and said a number goes into a slate, not a bundle. Below the sill a child's foot stayed dark in three turns of wool, the cold to one woman and weather to the yard; the reading bought nothing.

At the going of the light Sella set the boy on his bed, put the fold right without looking at his face and left the thread frozen. In the small hours his file held six hearings and no seventh, three near-full answers advanced and not paid, five stoppings as objects and not a rule. The river's note came through the door, the carried fire gave four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have been, the boards kept four nails, four, and a knot: three counts in three places, none become one. His hand went up an inch toward the water and came down with one hundred and thirty-seven exact and unspent.
