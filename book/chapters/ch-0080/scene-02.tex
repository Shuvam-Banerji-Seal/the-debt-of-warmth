The afternoon thinned without ending. It went out of the fold the way heat goes out of a pot set off the coals and left on a cold stone, drawing back by degrees and sitting behind the pot where a hand might still have found it if a hand had been given to put there. \person[Corin]{The boy} lay at the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf} under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof with the pot rim standing two inches beyond him and the file still turned out on \place{the towel}, every tray parted by a finger's width and nothing moved. Two hundred and ninety-one stood exact and complete and was spoken by nobody; six months and one hundred and eleven days were told by nobody in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill} and \place{Grath}. \person{Sella Vail} came in, went to the fire before the basket, gave it its instruction in the low voice she kept for tools, and carried him down at the half-pace from every open slate, out of the kiln-house and into \place{the Clay Yard}, where the light was going thin over the worked ground and the day was wearing on without drawing to a close. There were two men under the roof and only one of them had a body; the older kept the inventory and the younger kept the wanting, and carrying him down did not put the two in the same sentence. No bell had come on this Secondday and no reading at \place{the Cross}, and the yard went about its goings as water goes over a stone that lies where a stone lies.

He took the body as it presented itself, in the order the facts arrived, because that order had never lied to him and no other had ever promised to. One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from his ear, gone past the point where a child would eat it, and the empty place beside it kept its name to itself; no third had come in the afternoon to make the row come out even. The thread of drool at his mouth had frozen in the dry heat and was unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread, and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. Cold had come to cheek and temple and the bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, and stopped there as at the end of a line written out for it, with neither an order to go forward nor an order to go away. The afterdrop waited beyond all three, past a warming nobody in this house would perform. He knew the composed face that is not composure; he knew a cold patient cannot clot and will seem better before he is worse, and that a man must be warmed from the outside and slowly. He wanted the crust lifted the two inches to his mouth and could not close two inches on the pot handle. Both were true and the second was louder; the surgeon took the inventory in its old order --- crust, gap, thread, cold, the waiting --- and entered none of it, not even on the place where he kept the things he did not enter.

\scenebreak

She went wide around the seven walked-around places with a look and a wide step and no number. At the Vail corner the clean dark line lay where the \lore{Ash} ridge had turned, and the finer grey hairline beside it, called \lore{the thaw}, lay finer still; neither had been swept, and the broom went around both as a woman goes around a sleeping animal. Beneath \place{the lowest rack} the broken birth-slates lay where they had been laid, and she gave the space under the rack the same wide berth she gave a pail of hot water set where a child might knock it, without looking at the slates and without entering them. On the flat ground near \place{the drying racks} the round ring where the shallow pan had once stood kept its shape with no scrape, no crumb, no print and no word; a pan gone is known by the ring it leaves, and the ring was not a message and had no hand to send it. Beside the well \place{the trough} had skinned over from edge to edge and its water was called weather by everybody who passed it, and the weather was not asked what it cost. Out on \place{the worked strips} the field's empty place stood where it had stood since the planting, and the returning-line place, where a hand had gone in on Seed 15 and had not come out, was gone around with a look and no number.

Seven were seven and not an account. She did not count them under her breath and he did not count them for her; the missing hand was carried, not named, not paid and not joined to the one crust in the fold, or to the dressed grain covered and unfigured under wool at the rack end, or to the man carried out on Day 188. A house that keeps by going around things keeps its accounts in the feet, and a man who reads such a house learns to count the steps and to call no total. He had once reduced a shoulder in the dark by feel alone; here he could not turn his head far enough to see the space under the rack and still keep the crust in view, and the two things stood on different places with a finger between. The yard's goings-around went on over him the way water goes over a stone that lies where a stone lies, and he was the stone and not the water. A man who has spent his life putting things right in rooms where somebody always called for him finds it a strange discipline to be carried past seven absences and call none of them; he had been learning it since his first afternoon under this roof and it had not got easier.

\scenebreak

The stayed frost lay white on the lip of \place{the yard well} where the Warm had lately gone out, and the rope stiffened as the bucket came up and gave its small dry rasp. On \place{the flat stone by the well}, set out of the path of feet where nobody would knock it, the plate of ice stood clouded with silt where the silt had risen into it, a covering laid over a depth that the yard called weather and asked nothing of. The boy kept the white lip on a place of its own and the plate on another, and he made no fourth likeness between them and the trough's skin and the frost on \place{the boards}, and he named no payer to any of them. A cause is a thing that wants a payer standing under it, and nobody stood. He had once known the exact number of instruments laid on a tray without deciding to, and here he kept three coverings that nobody had asked him to keep and would not make them one, because one covering wants one cause and no cause stood to any of them. The first instinct of the man he had been in another room was to find which two things agreed and call the third the error; here the error would have been to agree at all. He let the three be three. The bucket went down and came up; the rope gave its dry note; the weather went on being weather, entered nowhere.

\scenebreak

\person{Garl Vail} was present in objects and not in a story. His salt-stiff coat kept a broader man's shape on its peg behind the door, with the crease at the left shoulder where a collector's hand had once rested or where a man had leaned; the boy had never decided which, and the not-deciding kept the coat honest. The wheel-seat in the shed kept its twenty-year lean and its hollow on that side, and Sella let no one fill it. The clean dark line at the corner kept its finer grey beside it, unswept; the proverb had gone years ago to a pot and the fire and never to a person --- feed the fire before you feed yourself, leave the crack showing, people trust a thing they can see has been handled --- and the debt against a firing of tiles stood neither paid nor settled nor done with, and was worse for being uncalled. His name had not been spoken under this roof in two hundred and ninety-one days. No collector came up \place{the clay road} and none went down it; Frost's first arithmetic and Frost's second rode locked in a box toward \place{High Kiln} with the printed figure unchallenged and the figure read still not the figure paid, and the keeping of a man by \lore{not-doings} stayed on its own place, maintenance and not a story. The house went around his absence the way it went around the line on the floor, and there was no lesson in it for anybody who cared to learn one.

\scenebreak

\person{Tarn} was at \place{the kindling} with the small knife at the very edge of its sill. Six stood in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check and the seventh stood at the lip where the house could not; Garl's unforged collector sentence lay beside it, with no name on the man who had turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on the debt. He told the wood \emph{Six counted. The next I don't count} in the tone of a man stating the weather, and when a woman went by him wanting the thaw early and another wanting it late he said \emph{Yes, house} to each so thoroughly that no argument could find a foot, and went on wanting the late because it puts off the paying, though the paying stood in the ruts of the road and had stood there since the water went.

The sentence about an ear under this roof being worth more than the debt that held it came to his teeth and went as far as the word that would call the loan, and stopped there with no figure and no name, a remembered ninth stopping and no tenth before the seventh year at its very end and still ungiven. He drew the breath that should have gone in at the top of the reach and did not spend it, and his chest was tight as it had been since the dark. Ash was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line and no scrubbing lifted it; the pale clip-ring at his left wrist held what light there was and gave it back. His stroke went on over \place{the flagstones} past the shallow bright patch without the half-check, the stones keeping the place where it had stopped, and from above came only a sound with a direction out of \place{the Debt Loft}, where the warm stone in its unmarked pot was cold and unreconciled by not trying, which is not forgiveness. He took the broom only after it had been given its instruction and asked nothing of the sky, and the boy, who had once counted the exits in a room without deciding to, kept the seventh where Tarn had set it and entered no figure against it.

\scenebreak

\person{Jorra Vail} came through the finger's-width-open door at the edge of \place{the Vail wheel-shed} with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal into both forearms past the bend of his little finger and under the nails, not bone-coloured \lore{slip}. He kept dusty forearms and clean ones on one tray and parted them as a surgeon parts clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had broken nor whether it was a birth-slate. He spoke to the wheel before he spoke to any person: \emph{It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady}. To a pot on the wheel he said \emph{You'll do} in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either. He knew the \lore{full note} that pot would give three turns before it left his hands, with air under it and a want in it, and withheld the knowing on the near-full tray beside the hearings and never among them, advanced and not paid.

Men unnamed had gone low into \place{the back room} before first grey and he gave the house no name for them and did not say the room was cold; the boy took it as a direction and a sound. When Sella's sentence came low and unfinished, his bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper} ahead of the end of it, took the cold out of the fingertip, moved the tongue a hair's breadth and left it standing, so the end of her instruction arrived at the pot and the household straightened around his silence. The burned-slate word stood at four from \place{Slate Row}, a direction over the boards and not a fifth telling; the two tellings of the counting room shut in the war stood at two and had not become three; three sayings about a pot stood together with the premature Smoke 14 note on its own place and no fourth likeness made a cause; the ownerless row question stood at five askings with one shaping and no witness, and was not asked a sixth time. No voice came over the boards this afternoon and the two shapings of the Frost 14 saying stayed where they had been filed, beside \person{Hessa Rale}'s eighth record-first hand with a finger between, and the boy did not make three of two or let the well's quiet stand up as an answer to the row. \emph{He's not Vail} stayed sealed as tightly as a lid nobody had tried to lift in three years, and the promise of the boy's name to the men who burn slates was neither given nor withdrawn.

\scenebreak

At the going of the light Sella carried him up at the half-pace from every open slate, set him on \place{the bed} under the roof and not in the Debt Loft, put the fold right without a look at his face and left the thread frozen where it lay. She told the fire it was the thaw, turned the coals with the back of a broken pot and said \emph{The house will keep it} where another woman would have said \emph{I}, and gave every instruction to the fire, the wool, the pot, the broom and the running water and never to the basket. \lore{The widow's mark} hung where it hung, because a going of the light is not an opening; at the loaf shelf her finger stayed on top of the front edge so the charcoal tally was unread, her lips moved along a row standing with two gaps and gave neither gap a figure, and she glanced once at the clay jar cold under its \lore{lead glaze} and shut her hand half over nothing.

In the small hours a woman's low counting came from the next room whose floor does not touch his --- the small intake, the hold, the letting-go --- and he took only what the hand could take and left the letting-go where it was, for it was her breath and her count and not his. Outside the wool the hand went up an inch and took two intakes from that room, held them in two places with a finger between, and set them side by side on the file; the letting-go between them was left where it had been, and neither intake was made to agree with \lore{the carried fire}'s missing fifth or with the river's \lore{water over water}. The hundred and fifty-eighth answer was filed exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air, and neither intake was promoted into a reach, a hearing, a seventh, a stopping, a payment, a held change, a gift, a doctrine or a rule. The surgeon's clean satisfaction at the exact coordination was checked by the infant's louder want of the crust two inches off, and the checking was the work and he did not call it a virtue. Frost the thirtieth stood six mornings ahead and was not brought into a day that had not earned it; he made nothing of the nearness and did not let it stand up as a sign, for a sign wants somebody to read it and nobody under this roof had been given the office. Two hundred and ninety-one stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in Low Kiln, Sill and Grath; six months and one hundred and eleven days were told by nobody; the carried fire's fifth was unpaid the same number of days with its payer unnamed. Nothing was opened, named, called, paid or ended. The hand came down against the fold with the two intakes side by side and a finger between them, the letting-go left where it was, the file open on the towel and the river's low note of water over water going thin under the finger's-width door and quiet in its own time.
