The afternoon went on from the morning the way heat goes out of a pot set off the coals and left on a cold stone. It did not end; it thinned. The light drew back across the worked ground by degrees and sat behind the things it had left, where a hand might still have found it if a hand had been given to put there. \person[Corin]{The boy} lay at the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf} under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof with the crust two inches from his ear and the file still turned out on \place{the towel}, every tray parted by a finger's width and nothing moved. Two hundred and ninety-two stood exact and complete and was spoken by nobody in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill} and \place{Grath}; six months and one hundred and twelve days were told by nobody. Under the floor \lore{the carried fire} kept its four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer unnamed the same number of days, and nobody lifted the floor and nobody supplied the pull. There were two men under the roof and only one of them had a body; the older kept the inventory and the younger kept the wanting, and the afternoon did not put the two into one sentence.

He took the body as it presented itself, in the order the facts arrived, because that order had never lied and no other had promised to. One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust lay two inches off, gone past the point where a child would eat it, and the empty place beside it kept its name to itself; no third had come in the afternoon to make the row come out even. The thread of drool at his mouth had frozen in the dry heat and was unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread, and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. Cold had come to cheek and temple and the bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, and stopped there as though it had come to the end of a line written out for it, with neither an order to go forward nor an order to go away. The afterdrop waited beyond all three, past a warming nobody in this house would perform. He knew the composed face that is not composure. He knew a cold patient cannot clot and will seem better before he is worse, and that a man must be warmed from the outside and slowly. He wanted the crust lifted the two inches and could not close two inches on the pot handle. Both were true and the second was louder; the surgeon took the inventory in its old order --- crust, gap, thread, cold, the waiting --- and entered none of it, not even on the place where he kept the things he did not enter. He had once reduced a shoulder in the dark by feel alone and now could not lift a crust to his mouth; the thought came and went without bitterness, which was its own small betrayal.

\scenebreak

\person{Sella Vail} came in and went to the fire before the basket, gave it its instruction in the low voice she kept for tools, and carried him down at the half-pace from every open slate, out of the kiln-house and into \place{the Clay Yard}, where the light was going thin over the worked ground. She went wide around the seven walked-around places with a look and a wide step and no number. At the Vail corner the clean dark line lay where the \lore{Ash} ridge had turned, and the finer grey hairline beside it, called \lore{the thaw}, lay finer still; neither had been swept and the broom went around both as a woman goes around a sleeping animal. Beneath \place{the lowest rack} the broken birth-slates lay where they had been laid, and she gave the space under the rack the wide berth she gave a pail of hot water set where a child might knock it, without looking at the slates and without entering them. On the flat ground near \place{the drying racks} the round ring where the shallow pan had once stood kept its shape with no scrape, no crumb, no print and no word. Beside \place{the yard well} \place{the trough} had skinned over from edge to edge and its water was called weather by everybody who passed it, and the weather was not asked what it cost. Out on \place{the worked strips} the field's empty place stood where it had stood since the planting, and the returning-line place, where a hand had gone in on Seed 15 and had not come out, was gone around with a look and no number.

Seven were seven and not an account. She did not count them under her breath and he did not count them for her; the missing hand was carried, not named, not paid and not joined to the one crust in the fold, or to the dressed grain covered and unfigured under wool at \place{the rack end}, or to the man carried out on Day 188. A house that keeps by going around things keeps its accounts in the feet, and a man who reads such a house learns to count the steps and to call no total. Here he could not turn his head far enough to see the space under the rack and still keep the crust in view, and the two things stood on different places with a finger between. The yard's goings-around went on over him the way water goes over a stone that lies where a stone lies, and he was the stone and not the water.

The stayed frost lay white on the lip of the well where the Warm had lately gone out, and the rope stiffened as the bucket came up and gave its small dry rasp. On \place{the flat stone by the well}, set out of the path of feet where nobody would knock it, the plate of ice stood clouded with silt where the silt had risen into it, a covering laid over a depth that the yard called weather and asked nothing of. The boy kept the white lip on a place of its own and the plate on another, and he made no fourth likeness between them and the trough's skin and the frost on \place{the boards}, and he named no payer to any of them. A cause is a thing that wants a payer standing under it, and nobody stood. He had once known the exact number of instruments laid on a tray without deciding to, and here he kept three coverings that nobody had asked him to keep and would not make them one, because one covering wants one cause and no cause stood to any of them. In the other room he had been trained to make three disagreeing facts agree by leaving one of them out; here the leaving out would have been the fraud. He let the three be three. The bucket went down and came up; the rope gave its dry note; the weather went on being weather, entered nowhere.

\scenebreak

The yard went about its goings the way water goes over a stone that lies where a stone lies. The rope gave its dry note and went quiet; a woman told a pot what it cost; the light went on thinning without drawing to a close. Nothing in the afternoon had stood up to be asked, and then a voice came over the boards and the well-roof.

It had no owner standing under it. There was no room behind it, no hand at a slate, no figure in it, no name for the person it concerned, no confession and no cause. It said that the slate-runner had once carried a name the district had spent, and it did not say whose. Then it went on with the day the way the yard says the weather --- low, practical, passing over the boards and down the terrace --- and nobody turned twice to look for a speaker, because nobody in a clay yard turns twice at the weather. A woman went on with her bucket. A pot got its word. The thing the voice had said lay where a great many sayings lie in Low Kiln, said once and gone on from, with nobody standing to be asked about it.

The boy took it apart as a surgeon takes a wound apart, by what was there and by what was not. There was a voice with no throat in it that anybody could find; a runner; a slate; a name that had been spent; a district that had spent it; and a question of whose that the voice had left lying where it lay. There was no owner, no room, no hand at a slate, no figure spoken, no person named, no confession, no cause, no witness and no payer. He had spent his life opening wounds to find the one small thing that explained the bleeding, and here the finding was the absence: a saying arrived nowhere, from nobody, with its edge taken off by the way the yard took it --- said and gone on from. He did not make the absence into a person. An absence is not a man standing somewhere who can be asked; the throat of it could not be found, and a finding with no throat is not a witness however often it is said.

On \place{the towel} the file lay open with every gap left gap. The Frost 14 saying stood on a place of its own. It had come first from a woman waiting her turn at the well, who said the runner carrying \person{Berr Clay}'s slate had once carried a figure that named a person she knew; it had come a second time from a voice over the boards with no owner to be asked, asking what a runner carries in a slate that has a name in it. Now a third came, and he filed it beside the first and the second with a finger between every pair, on a place of its own beside \person{Hessa Rale}'s eighth record-first hand. He made of it a third shaping toward the far-off day when she must choose whether to read what she had carried for years. He did not make her a reader by it. She was a runner who carried and did not read, and a shaping is not an opening of the slate and does not put a figure in her hand.

He kept it from joining. It was not joined to the notched strip in the sleeve of \person{Ilva}, a woman of the firing work offstage this afternoon with her count still in wood where no slate can take a figure off it; not to Berr's twice-wiped thumb and his finger passed over a figure; not to the clerk's inward slate held writing-inward at a landing nobody called; not to the charcoal tally under the front edge of \place{the loaf shelf} that Sella kept unread with a finger on top of the board. Four hands and a voice, and a finger between it and every one. Three shapings were three and not a witness, not a cause, not a confession, not a joining and not paid; they did not stand up and call themselves a rule, for a rule is an account that has found a payer and no payer stood to any of them. He had once made two disagreeing accounts agree by leaving one of them out and signing the bottom of a chart; the habit of that correction was still in him, quick and shameful and ready, and he did not give it a slate. Nothing under this roof had opened, named, called or paid any of it.

\scenebreak

The row question stood where it had stood. Five askings, one shaping, no witness, and no row named; the shape of the well-saying that had just come over the boards was not a sixth asking and was not the row's shaping, and the boy kept the two on separate places with a finger between, because a sixth asking wants a sixth asking's own shape and this was a saying about a name. The last asking had come low into the boards on Smoke 8 with no owner and no room and no hand at the kiln, and the shaping after it had been a report that somebody had been asking which row it was that went quiet, not a fresh asking; five were five and not a witness, and the ownerless question was not the well-saying and the well-saying was not it. Three had not come of two here and six had not come of five there. The well's quiet was not an answer to the row and the row's quiet was not a confirmation of the name. He wrote no cause across either.

He went through the rest of the file the way a man checks a tray before he puts an instrument away. The burned-slate word stood at four from \place{Slate Row}, a direction over the boards and not a fifth telling; the two tellings of the counting room shut in the war stood at two and had not become three, and the burned slate was not a third telling of the room; three sayings about a pot stood together with the premature Smoke 14 note on its own place, and the note that had come before its time was not a fourth likeness made a cause; the seventh green stood as a colour at a silent woman's mouth and the seventh shape as a shape in cast-off clothes at the market's edge, a colour and a shape with no eighth and no name spoken over either. The nine record-first hands stood nine with the ninth, Ilva's, unread, and no tenth came into the yard this afternoon to make a row of them; the nine stoppings stood nine with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end and still ungiven. He kept the unequal counts unequal. A round number standing up out of a string of likenesses is an account that wants a payer, and no payer stood to any of them.

\scenebreak

\person{Tarn} was at \place{the kindling} with the small knife at the very edge of its sill. Six stood in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check and the seventh stood at the lip where it could not; \person{Garl Vail}'s unforged collector sentence lay beside it, with no name on the man who had turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on the debt. He told the wood \emph{Six counted. The next I don't count} in the tone of a man stating the weather, and when a woman went by wanting the thaw early and another wanting it late he said \emph{Yes, house} to each so thoroughly that no argument could find a foot, and went on wanting the late because it puts off the paying, though the paying stood in the ruts of \place{the clay road} and had stood there since the water went.

The sentence about an ear under this roof being worth more than the debt that held it came to his teeth and went as far as the word that would call the loan, and stopped there with no figure and no name, a remembered ninth stopping and no tenth before the seventh year at its very end and still ungiven. He drew the breath that should have gone in at the top of the reach and did not spend it, and his chest was tight as it had been since the dark. Ash was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line and no scrubbing lifted it; the pale clip-ring at his left wrist held what light there was and gave it back. His stroke went on over \place{the flagstones} past the shallow bright patch without the half-check, the stones keeping the place where it had stopped, and from above came only a sound with a direction out of \place{the Debt Loft}, where the warm stone in its unmarked pot was cold and unreconciled by not trying, which is not forgiveness. He took the broom only after it had been given its instruction, and the boy, who had once counted the exits in a room without deciding to, kept the seventh where Tarn had set it and entered no figure against it. The voice over the boards had not changed any of this; Tarn went on with his count as the yard went on with its weather, and the boy did not lay the one across the other, for an ear worth more than a debt and a name spent by a district are two things with no payer between them.

\scenebreak

\person{Jorra Vail} came through the finger's-width-open door at the edge of \place{the Vail wheel-shed} with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal into both forearms past the bend of his little finger and under the nails, not bone-coloured \lore{slip}. He kept dusty forearms and clean ones on one tray and parted them as a surgeon parts clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had broken nor whether it was a birth-slate. He spoke to the wheel before he spoke to any person: \emph{It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady}. To a pot on the wheel he said \emph{You'll do} in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either. He knew the \lore{full note} that pot would give three turns before it left his hands, with air under it and a want in it, and withheld the knowing on the near-full tray beside the hearings and never among them, advanced and not paid.

Men unnamed had gone low into \place{the back room} before first grey and he gave the house no name for them and did not say the room was cold; the boy took it as a direction and a sound. When Sella's sentence came low and unfinished, his bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper} ahead of the end of it, took the cold out of the fingertip, moved the tongue a hair's breadth and left it standing, so the end of her instruction arrived at the pot and the household straightened around his silence. The burned-slate word stood at four, the shut-room tellings at two, three pot sayings with the Smoke 14 note apart, the row at five askings with one shaping and no witness; nothing in the afternoon's saying had moved any of them, and \emph{he's not Vail} stayed sealed as tightly as a lid nobody had tried to lift in three years. The promise of the boy's name to the men who burn slates was neither given nor withdrawn. He did not look toward the basket, and the boy did not lay the new shaping across the sealed sentence: a name spent by a district and a name withheld under a roof are two keepings, and there was no hand in the yard to join them.

Garl stayed in objects and not in a story. His salt-stiff coat with the crease at the left shoulder kept a broader man's shape on its peg behind the door; the wheel-seat in the shed kept its twenty-year lean and its hollow on that side, and Sella let no one fill it. The clean dark line at the corner kept its finer grey beside it, unswept; the proverb had gone years ago to a pot and the fire and never to a person --- feed the fire before you feed yourself, leave the crack showing, people trust a thing they can see has been handled --- and the debt against a firing of tiles stood neither paid nor settled nor done with, and was worse for being uncalled. His name had not been spoken under this roof in two hundred and ninety-two days. No collector came up the clay road and none went down it; Frost's first arithmetic and Frost's second rode locked in Berr Clay's box up the road toward \place{High Kiln} with the printed figure unchallenged and the figure read still not the figure paid, and the keeping of a man by \lore{not-doings} stayed on its own place, maintenance and not a story. The house went around his absence the way it went around the line on the floor, and there was no lesson in it for anybody who cared to learn one.

\scenebreak

At the going of the light Sella carried him up at the half-pace from every open slate, set him on \place{the bed} under the roof and not in \place{the Debt Loft}, put the fold right without a look at his face and left the thread frozen where it lay. She told the fire it was the thaw, turned the coals with the back of a broken pot and said \emph{The house will keep it} where another woman would have said \emph{I}, and gave every instruction to the fire, the wool, the pot, the broom and the running water and never to the basket. \lore{The widow's mark} hung where it hung on its cord at her throat, a thumb-length of lead-glazed clay with the Vail kiln scratched into it, and she did not touch it, because a going of the light is not an opening and the hand goes to the mark before the kiln and at no other hour. At \place{the loaf shelf} her finger stayed on top of the front edge so the charcoal tally was unread; her lips moved along a row standing with two gaps and gave neither gap a figure, and she glanced once at the clay jar cold under its \lore{lead glaze} and shut her hand half over nothing.

She had heard the voice over the boards and went on with the house the way the yard had gone on with the weather. She did not bring it under the roof as a lesson, a figure, a mending, a cause or a payment; she did not say whose name had been spent or say that any name had been spent at all, and she gave the house no news of it. If she put the saying in the private roll nobody is shown, she put it there without a figure, and the boy did not ask and was not told. The finding that a house keeps by its not-doings stayed on a place of its own apart from the boy's own gaps, with a finger between the two keepings, and the shaping was not laid across either. The two gaps above the shelf, the one crust in the fold and its unnamed empty place, the gone pan's round ring, the field's empty place and the returning-line place stayed where they were, and no third gap came and no fourth likeness was made a cause. The house's keeping was maintenance; the boy's gaps were his own; a finger between them kept the two from standing up as one account, because one account wants one payer and none stood.

In the small hours, while the house kept its number in loaves and glances and unnamed spaces, the hand outside the wool went up an inch toward the river's own going. The boards overhead kept four nails on the near board, four on the next and a thumb-width knot where the fifth should have stood; under the floor the carried fire kept its four low pulls and the silence; through the finger's-width door the river kept its note of \lore{water over water}, a low thing over a lower thing. Three counts in three places, and none rose into another, because agreeing any two would have wanted a payer put under both and none stood. Six hearings of the full note stood beside and never among, five from four sources and the sixth, the small flat knock out of \person[Dessa Silt] as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go and never with the things that came from vessels; the seventh place kept its emptiness and none was invented to fill it. The heard bell's four, four and a gap stayed in its square beside the told bell of \lore{the Long Night}, heard and not borrowed. The Frost 14 saying stood with its first, second and third shapings on a place of its own beside Hessa Rale's eighth hand, a finger between every pair, and the three did not stand up and call themselves a witness; a witness stands somewhere and can be asked, and none of the three stood anywhere.

The hand took two intakes from the room whose floor does not touch his --- the small intake, the hold, the letting-go --- and kept them in two places with a finger between, where the three shapings stood on their own place with a finger between every pair. It left the letting-go between the intakes where it had been, for it was her breath and her count and not his, and borrowed no house count and supplied no fifth to the carried fire. The hundred and sixtieth answer was filed exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air: two intakes held apart with a finger between them, three shapings held apart with a finger between every pair, and neither intake promoted into a reach, a hearing, a joining, a payment, a gift, a doctrine or a rule. The surgeon's clean satisfaction at the exact coordination was checked by the infant's louder want of the crust two inches off, and the checking was the work and he did not call it a virtue. A finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged, and he had learned to leave the row unfilled even when the hand itched to close it.

Nothing of the saying came back under the roof. The voice had gone over the boards the way weather goes, and weather is not brought in and entered; no lesson was made of it, no figure taken off it, no cause laid under it, no payment made to it. It did not make a witness stand anywhere, and nobody stood to be asked. The finding that a house keeps by its not-doings stayed on a place of its own apart from the boy's own gaps with a finger between, and the third shaping was not laid across either keeping; it waited on its own place for a far-off day when a runner must choose whether to read what she had carried for years, and that day was not brought nearer by the saying and was not made a sign. Frost the thirtieth, the month's third and last Account-day, stood six mornings ahead and was not brought into a day that had not earned it; the boy made nothing of the nearness and did not let it stand up as a sign, for a sign wants somebody to read it and nobody under this roof had been given the office. Two hundred and ninety-two stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in Low Kiln, Sill and Grath; six months and one hundred and twelve days were told by nobody; the carried fire's fifth was unpaid the same number of days with its payer unnamed. Nothing was opened, named, called, paid or ended. The hand came down against the fold with two intakes held apart and a finger between them and the three shapings on their own place with a finger between every pair, the file open on the towel with every gap left gap, the crust two inches off with its empty place unnamed, and the river's low note thinning under the finger's-width door and quiet in its own time.
