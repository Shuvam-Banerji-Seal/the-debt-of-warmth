## The pull into Chapter 81

The final image is deliberately small and unclosed: 293 exact, six months and one hundred and thirteen days told by nobody; the fire's fifth unpaid the same number of days; the hand laid on the 162nd answer; Frost 30 six mornings ahead and explicitly not brought into this day. Chapter 81 opens on **Day 294, Frost 25, Fifthday**, in the small hours under the same Vail roof with the hand laid down on the 162nd answer and the count still at 293, the first and second arithmetics locked up the clay road, the covered grain still read over by numbers that do not know its cost, the **three** shapings of the Frost 14 well-saying held beside Hessa's eighth hand with a finger between and none of them standing up as a witness, the row at five askings with one shaping and no witness, the ninth stopping ungiven and the loan uncalled, Garl's name unspoken 294 days, and the last working week of Frost gathering toward the third reading without any of it being called a sign, lesson, mending or payment. The forward pressure is the arithmetic itself: two readings of Frost are behind and cannot be changed back, the third stands on Frost 30, and the boy is the only person in Low Kiln keeping the days exactly while the house keeps its number in loaves, glances and unnamed spaces. Nothing in this chapter opens, joins, names, pays or ends; the hand simply goes down on the next answer and the river goes quiet, which is how the next chapter must begin.
