\daymark{291}{Frost 22, Secondday, Y.H. 813}

\dropcap{T}{he hand} lay where it had been laid, against the fold at the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf}, as a man puts a tool down where the next hand will find it without looking, and the small hours came on under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof the way cold comes into a banked house, by degrees and from the outside. \person[Corin]{The boy} came into his two hundred and ninety-first day with nobody under any roof to tell him so. A hundred and fifty-six honest answers stood behind him, and a hundred and fifty-six hand-movings had gone into the making of them, and neither the number nor the movements stood up and called themselves a doctrine, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood. Two hundred and ninety had gone to two hundred and ninety-one the way one board laid beside another gives a dry knock and asks nothing of the seam; six months and one hundred and eleven days were told by nobody in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill} and \place{Grath}. Under the floor \lore{the carried fire}'s fifth stood unpaid the same number of days with its payer unnamed, and nobody lifted the floor and nobody supplied the pull. Frost 22 had come on its own morning, an ordinary Secondday with no market, no bell and no reading at \place{the Cross}, and it asked nothing of the boy under the fold except that he lie there and be warm where he could.

He took the body as it presented itself, in the order the facts arrived, because that order had never lied to him and no other had ever promised to. One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from his ear on \place{the bed}, gone past the point where a child would eat it, and the empty place beside it kept its name to itself; no third had come in the night to make the row come out even. The thread of drool at his mouth had frozen in the dry heat and was unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread, and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. Cold had come to cheek and temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, and stopped there as at the end of a line written out for it, with neither an order to go forward nor an order to go away. The afterdrop waited beyond all three, past a warming nobody in this house would perform. He knew the composed face that is not composure; he knew that a cold patient cannot clot and will seem better before he is worse, and that a man must be warmed from the outside and slowly, and that the ease cold puts on a face is not ease at all. He wanted the crust lifted the two inches to his mouth. Both were true, and in a body six months and one hundred and eleven days old the second was louder; the surgeon took the inventory in its old order --- crust, gap, thread, cold, the waiting --- and entered none of it, not even on the place where he kept the things he did not enter. He had once reduced a shoulder in the dark by feel alone and now could not close two inches on the pot handle set beyond his reach; the thought came and went without bitterness, which was its own small betrayal.

There were two men under the roof and only one of them had a body. The older kept the inventory and the younger kept the wanting, and neither had authority over the other; the older could name the afterdrop and the younger could only be cold, and the naming did not warm him. It was an old habit, this standing ready with a fact nobody would ask for, and he kept it the way a house keeps its charcoal under a finger and does not look. He had signed a chart once without reading the note in the margin and had carried that shape into a country where notes are turned face-down toward floorboards; here he read every note he was not given and entered none of them. The dry heat had drawn the water out of the thread at his mouth and left it stiff as wire drawn through tallow; he could not lift a hand to wipe it, and no instruction had ever been given to anybody to wipe it, and the two facts stood side by side without either becoming a complaint. A man who has spent his life putting things right in rooms where somebody always called for him finds it a strange discipline to lie under a shelf and call nothing. He had been learning it since his first morning under this roof and it had not got easier.

\scenebreak

Three counts stood in three places and none rose into another. Under the floor the carried fire gave four low pulls with the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer without a name for two hundred and ninety-one days; at the finger's-width door the river went \lore{water over water}, a low thing over a lower thing; overhead \place{the boards} kept four nails on the near board, four on the next and a thumb-width knot where the fifth nail should have stood. Agreeing any two would want a payer put under both and none stood to any, so the three stayed where they were, exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air. Six hearings of \lore{the full note} stood beside and never among, five from four sources and the sixth, the small flat knock out of a woman as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go and never with the things that came from vessels; the seventh place kept its emptiness and none was invented to fill it, for an empty place kept empty is a finding and a finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged. The bell's four, four and a gap, heard at the Cross on Frost 10 and again at the market on Frost 20, stayed in the square beside the told bell of \lore{the Long Night}; no bell had come on this morning and he would not lend an ear to one that had not earned it, for a bell comes on its own day the way a month comes, and a man who borrows one to make a row come out even has already put a payer under it.

Water over water was not a vessel's note and he had never entered it as one; it was the river going under its own skin, a low thing laid over a lower thing, and the ear at the door took it as weather takes a sound, without asking who had set it there. The kiln's four pulls came up through the floor as a man's breathing comes through a wall, and the silence after them had the shape of a held hand. He had once known the exact number of instruments laid on a tray without deciding to, and here he kept three counts that nobody had asked him to keep and would not make them one, because one count wants one payer and no payer stood to any of them. The first instinct of the man he had been in another room was to find which two things agreed and call the third the error; here the error would have been to agree at all. He let the three be three.

\scenebreak

On \place{the towel} at the head of the bed the file lay turned out as instruments are turned out for a man who will never be called to use them, each thing in its own place with room between, known by weight before the eye fell on it. The surgeon's hand went over it in the dark and found every object where it had been laid. Six hearings lay first with the empty seventh place beside them kept empty; the heard bell lay beside the told in its square; three pot sayings lay together with the premature Smoke 14 note on a place apart and no fourth likeness made a cause; the burned-slate word stood at four from \place{Slate Row}, a direction over the boards and not a fifth telling; the two tellings of the counting room shut in the war stood at two and had not become three; the ownerless row question lay with five askings and one shaping beside them, a finger between every pair, no witness under any and no row named. It was not asked a sixth time, and the shaping of the well-saying was not its shaping; the boy kept the two things on two places and would not let a neighbourly likeness carry a question across from one tray to the other. The nine stoppings stood with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end and still ungiven, and the nine record-first hands stood with \person{Ilva}'s ninth in her sleeve unread and no tenth, the count kept in wood where no slate could take a figure off it.

He set the Frost 14 well-saying on a place of its own beside \person{Hessa Rale}'s eighth record-first hand with a finger between: one saying and one second shaping, no more, not a hearing, not a seventh, not a tenth hand, not a cause, not a confession, not a joining, not a witness and not paid. The first had been said three days back by a woman waiting her turn at \place{the yard well}, to the boards and the well-roof and not into the basket and not to any person: the runner who carried \person{Berr Clay}'s slate once carried a figure that named a person she knew. The second had come from a voice over the boards with no owner standing anywhere to be asked, no room, no hand at a slate, no name for the person, no figure spoken, no confession and no cause, asking what a runner carries in a slate that has a name in it; the voice had gone on with the day the way the yard says the weather and nobody had turned twice. Neither made Hessa a reader of what she had carried for years, and neither was joined to the strip in a sleeve a pace away, nor to Berr's twice-wiped thumb, nor to the soft-cuffed clerk's inward slate, nor to the charcoal under the shelf. Two shapings must not stand up as a rule, for a rule is an account that has found a payer and none stood to either. Frost's first arithmetic and Frost's second lay locked in Berr's box up \place{the clay road} toward \place{High Kiln}, the printed one in twelve unchallenged and the figure read still not the figure paid, and the space between the figure and the price kept its unsigned edge. The dressed grain lay covered and unfigured on the cool stone at \place{the rack end}, read over by numbers that did not know what it cost.

He kept the finding that a house keeps by its \lore{not-doings} on a place of its own, and his own gaps on another with a finger between --- two gaps above the shelf, one crust in the fold and its unnamed empty place, the gone pan's round ring, the field's empty place out on the worked strips, the returning-line place where a hand went in and did not come out --- no third gap made a row and no fourth likeness made a cause. The stayed frost on the well lip, the trough skinned over and the plate of ice clouded with silt on its flat stone out of the path of feet were called weather and entered nowhere. The two keepings stayed two and not one account for want of a payer under either, and he did not lay the finding across any absence. It was maintenance and not a story, and he left it in its own place with the file open around it and room between.

\scenebreak

\person{Sella Vail} went to the fire before the basket. She gave it its instruction in the low voice she kept for tools, told it \lore{the thaw} when the sour breath came off the unswept corner, turned the coals with the back of a broken pot and said \emph{The house will keep it} where another woman would have said \emph{I}. The grey streak at her temple showed pale off the coals as she pushed a strand of coal-black hair back with the wrist that carries the burn, and the little-door burn shut on nothing when her hand had nothing to hold. The dressed grain lay covered under wool away from \place{the hearth} on the cool stone at the rack end; she lifted a corner and turned it once with a lighter touch than the morning before, put the wool back and gave it no figure. At the loaf shelf her finger stayed on top of the front edge while her lips moved along a row standing with two gaps, and neither gap got a figure; she glanced once at the clay jar standing cold under its \lore{lead glaze} and shut her hand half over nothing. \lore{The widow's mark} hung where it hung, a thumb-length of lead-glazed clay with the Vail kiln scratched into it, and she did not touch it, because a morning is not an opening and the hand goes to the mark before the kiln and at no other hour; she would sooner swallow the mark than touch it at a basket or a bed. To the wool she said \emph{Keep it off his ears. It's the river wind}, and to the broom she gave its instruction before anybody could, \emph{Go around}. The pot rim stood on \place{the pot board} two inches beyond the reaching hand with its handle toward him and a half-beat between the setting and her going away, and she did not look toward the bed. Every instruction at that hour went to the fire, the wool, the pot, the broom and the running water, and none of it went to the basket.

He heard her grammar as a man hears a language he has not been taught but has learned to parse from the order of the words: fire before basket, the instruction to the tools and never to the thing carried, \emph{the house} where another woman would say \emph{I}. Nothing of the day went into the fold and no figure came to the shelf with the coals. The house kept itself by its not-doings, and he had learned to tell that keeping from a story the way he had learned to tell a sound vessel from a cracked one: by what answered when you tapped it. She stood a moment with the back of the broken pot in her hand counting something under her breath toward no opening, and went away without looking toward the fold.

\scenebreak

Outside the wool the hand came up an inch toward the river's note and stayed for a small intake. It was not a reach; a reach would have wanted the crust or the pot handle and neither stood within it. The intake was of the fire's silence rising through the floor under the four low pulls, carried across the interval to the first morning's own going, and the going was not brought back to stand beside it as a cause; he took the note and the silence in two places, borrowed no house count and supplied no fifth to the kiln, crossed no silence that belonged to another's breathing and came down on the river's next low note of water over water with the sound still whole. The hundred and fifty-seventh answer was filed exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air, and a round threshold of answers did not stand up and call itself a doctrine, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood. The surgeon's clean satisfaction at the exact coordination was checked by the infant's plain want of the crust two inches off, and the checking was the work and he did not call it a virtue. The satisfaction was small and clean and he did not spend it; the wanting was large and damp and would not be answered by any counting.

Frost the thirtieth, the month's third and last Account-day and market, stood six mornings ahead and was not brought into a day that had not earned it. He made nothing of the nearness and did not let it stand up as a sign; a sign wants somebody to read it, and nobody under this roof had been given the office. The hand laid itself back against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. Two hundred and ninety-one stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in Low Kiln, Sill and Grath; six months and one hundred and eleven days were told by nobody; the carried fire's fifth was unpaid the same number of days with its payer unnamed. Nothing was opened, named, called, paid or ended. The file lay open on the towel with every gap left gap, every tray parted by a finger's width and nothing that had moved. The pot rim stood two inches out and the river's low note went quiet after the hand came down.
