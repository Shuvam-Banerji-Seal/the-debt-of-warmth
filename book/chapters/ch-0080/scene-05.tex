\daymark{293}{Frost 24, Fourthday, Y.H. 813}

Frost the twenty-fourth came on its own morning, as a board laid beside a board comes to lie flat without either asking the other, and \person[Corin]{the boy} came into his two hundred and ninety-third day with nobody under any roof to tell him so. Two hundred and ninety-two had gone to two hundred and ninety-three the way a dry knock goes and asks nothing afterward; six months and one hundred and thirteen days were told by nobody in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill} and \place{Grath}. It was an ordinary Fourthday with no market, no reading at \place{the Cross} and no bell, and a day like that asks to be let alone. Under the floor \lore{the carried fire} kept its four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer unnamed two hundred and ninety-three days, and nobody lifted the floor and nobody supplied the pull. The hand lay against the fold where it had been laid in the small hours, on the hundred and sixtieth answer, as a man puts a tool down where the next hand will find it without looking. Nothing of the day behind was brought into this one. The voice that had gone over the boards and the well-roof yesterday, and the third shaping it had left lying, were not brought under the roof as a witness, a cause, a confession or a payment; a witness stands somewhere and can be asked, and none had stood. The morning asked only that he lie there and be warm where he could, which was the one thing nobody under this roof had been given an instruction to make him.

The smells came in their old order, because the house had no notion of order and so could not disturb it: hot clay cooled under tallow, wet wool, and under both the faint sour mineral breath of the unswept corner that the house called \lore{the thaw}. Through the finger's-width door the river kept its note of \lore{water over water}, a low thing over a lower thing, never numbered among the hearings; under the floor the fire answered in four low pulls with the silence where the fifth should have stood, and overhead \place{the boards} kept four nails on the near board, four on the next, and a thumb-width knot where the fifth nail should have stood. Four, four and a gap; four and the silence; water over water. The kiln answered in fours and was not a bell, and no bell had been brought under the roof on this morning and none was lent an ear it had not earned. There were two men under the roof and only one of them had a body. The older kept the inventory and the younger kept the wanting, and neither had authority over the other; the older could name the afterdrop and the younger could only be cold, and the naming did not warm him. It was an old arrangement and a costly one, this standing ready with a fact nobody would ask for, and he kept it the way a house keeps its charcoal under a finger and does not look.

\scenebreak

He took the body as it presented itself, in the order the facts arrived, because that order had never lied to him and no other had ever promised to. One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from his ear at the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf} on \place{the bed}, gone past the point where a child would eat it, and the empty place beside it kept its name to itself; no third had come in the night to make the row come out even. The thread of drool at his mouth had frozen in the dry heat and was unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread, and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. Cold had come to cheek and temple and the bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, and stopped there as though it had come to the end of a line written out for it, with neither an order to go forward nor an order to go away. The afterdrop waited beyond all three, past a warming nobody in this house would perform.

He knew the composed face that is not composure. He knew a cold patient cannot clot and will seem better before he is worse, and that a man must be warmed from the outside and slowly, never hurried, never rubbed; the grimace that cold puts on a face is not ease, and he had learned to read it in other men and could not read it off his own. He wanted the crust lifted the two inches to his mouth and could not close two inches on the pot handle set beyond his reach. Both were true and the second was louder; the surgeon took the inventory in its old order --- crust, gap, thread, cold, the waiting --- and entered none of it, not even on the place where he kept the things he did not enter. He had once reduced a shoulder in the dark by feel alone and now could not lift a crust to his mouth; the thought came and went without bitterness, which was its own small betrayal. The dry heat had drawn the water out of the thread and left it stiff as wire drawn through tallow, and he could not lift a hand to wipe it, and no instruction had ever been given to anybody to wipe it, and the two facts stood side by side without either becoming a complaint. Two inches of air and an infant's reach were the whole of the distance, and the distance did not care who had once held a clamp.

\scenebreak

Three counts stood in three places and none rose into another. The carried fire kept its four low pulls and the silence; the river kept its water over water; the boards kept their four, four and the knot. Agreeing any two would have wanted a payer put under both and no payer stood to any of them. A man who has spent his life finding the one error that makes a set of numbers agree finds it a strange trade to stand before three honest counts and refuse to make them one, but he had been at it two hundred and ninety-three days and had not grown skilful at it, only obedient to the discipline of it. Six hearings of \lore{the full note} stood beside and never among, five from four sources and the sixth, the small flat knock out of \person[Dessa Silt] as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go and never with things that came from vessels; the seventh place kept its emptiness and none was invented to fill it, for a finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged. The bell's four, four and a gap stayed in its square beside the told bell of \lore{the Long Night}, heard and not borrowed, not folded into the three counts and not heard as a seventh of the full note. He had once made two disagreeing accounts agree by leaving one of them out and signing the bottom of a chart; the habit of that correction was still in him, quick and shameful and ready, and he did not give it a slate.

On \place{the towel} at the head of the bed the file lay open with every gap left gap. The Frost 14 saying stood on a place of its own with its first, second and third shapings, a finger between every pair, filed beside \person{Hessa Rale}'s eighth record-first hand with a finger between that and the nearest shaping; not a witness, not a cause, not a confession, not a joining and not paid. Three were three and did not stand up and call themselves a rule, for a rule is an account that has found a payer and no payer stood to any of them. The nine stoppings stood nine with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end and still ungiven; the nine record-first hands stood nine with the ninth, \person{Ilva}'s strip in a sleeve a pace off, unread and joined to none; the row question stood at five askings with one shaping and no witness, and was not asked a sixth time. The burned-slate word stood at four from \place{Slate Row}, a direction over the boards and not a fifth telling; the two tellings of the counting room shut in the war stood at two and had not become three. Frost's first arithmetic and Frost's second rode locked in \person{Berr Clay}'s box up \place{the clay road} toward \place{High Kiln}, the printed one in twelve unchallenged and the figure read still not the figure paid, the space between figure and price unsigned. The boy kept the finding that a house keeps by its \lore{not-doings} on a place of its own, and his own gaps on another with a finger between the two keepings --- two gaps above the shelf, one crust in the fold and its unnamed empty place, the gone pan's round ring, the field's empty place out on the worked strips, the returning-line place where a hand went in and did not come out --- no third gap made a row and no fourth likeness made a cause. Nothing in the file had moved in the night, and nothing in this morning's quiet had earned the right to move it.

\scenebreak

\person{Sella Vail} went to the fire before the basket. That was the grammar of the house and it held exactly, as it had held every morning since he came under the roof: the instruction went to the fire, the wool, the pot, the broom and the running water, and never to the basket. She said \emph{The house will keep it} where another woman would have said \emph{I}, in the low voice she kept for tools, told the fire it was the thaw when the sour breath came off the unswept corner, and turned the coals with the back of a broken pot. She was broad and impossible to move, with her hair coal-black and the one grey streak at her temple showing pale off the coals as she pushed a strand back with the wrist that carried the little-door burn; the burn shut on nothing when her hand had nothing to hold, and the grey did not go away. She stood a moment with the back of the broken pot in her hand counting something under her breath toward no opening, and did not ask the fire what it had cost or who had paid for the fifth.

At \place{the loaf shelf} her finger stayed on top of the front edge so the charcoal tally beneath it was unread; she moved her lips along a row standing with two gaps and gave neither gap a figure. \lore{The widow's mark} hung where it hung on its cord at her throat, a thumb-length of lead-glazed clay with the Vail kiln scratched into it, and she did not touch it, because a morning is not an opening and the hand goes to the mark before the kiln and at no other hour; she would sooner swallow the mark than touch it at a basket or a bed. When she set the pot down on \place{the pot board} she set the rim two inches beyond the reaching hand with its handle turned toward it, and there was a half-beat between the setting and her going away, and she did not look toward the bed. The half-beat was not a gift and the handle turned toward the hand was not an instruction to take; she had never given an instruction to the basket at any hour of any day, and she gave none now. The boy watched the going of her burn-wrist the way he had learned to watch a surgeon's hand leave a field --- for what it did not touch --- and filed the not-touching on its own place, maintenance and not a message.

The broom got its instruction before any person did, and so did the running water: go around what the water left, it cost enough to come in and will cost more to take out; keep the draught off the grain; let the pot stand where the pot stands. To the wool she said \emph{Keep it off his ears. It's the river wind}. She said the words into the yard and the sink and the fire and never once turned them toward the basket, as though the basket were a stone that had no ears and needed none. Later, in the turn of the morning, she uncovered the dressed grain at \place{the rack end} and turned it beneath the wool with a progressively lighter touch, giving it no figure. It lay on the cool stone away from \place{the hearth}, covered against the draught, and the numbers that had been read over it twice did not know what it cost; the space between the figure and the price had no signature on it and remained unsigned. She glanced once at the clay jar standing cold under its \lore{lead glaze} and shut her hand half over nothing, and carried nothing slopped. When she carried him past a slate set out to dry she kept the half-pace without appearing to measure it, and the fold went right at his ear without a look at his face. It would have been easy to read care in it and easier still to read the absence of care; he read the distance instead, which was the only thing in the room that was honestly there. A woman who says the house where another woman says I has taken the first person out of her own sentence, and a boy who lies under the sentence learns to notice where the gap is.

He heard the grammar as a man hears a language he has not been taught but has learned to parse from the order of the words: fire before basket, the instruction to the tools and never to the thing carried, the house where another woman says I. Nothing of the day went into the fold and no figure came to the shelf with the coals. The house kept itself by its not-doings, and he had learned to tell that keeping from a story the way he had learned to tell a sound vessel from a cracked one: by what answered when you tapped it. The morning was almost a relief after yesterday's ownerless voice, which had had no throat to find and no owner standing to be asked; the relief was undercut by the body's smaller humiliations, by the thread stiff at his mouth and the crust two inches off, and by the cold that had stopped where it had stopped and would go no further on its own. He did not call the relief a relief. He had no word in this house that would carry it, and a thing named aloud is a thing that can be entered.

\scenebreak

In the small hours, while the house kept its number in loaves and glances and unnamed spaces, the hand outside the wool came up an inch toward the river's own going. It took the letting-go of an intake on the same note, kept the letting-go whole as it had been left and set it beside the held interval with a finger between --- not an ending made early, not a freedom and not a gift, for a gift wants a taker and a freedom wants a man who can move, and neither stood under the fold. The boards overhead kept four nails on the near board, four on the next and a thumb-width knot where the fifth should have stood; under the floor the carried fire kept its four low pulls and the silence; through the finger's-width door the river kept its low thing over a lower thing. Three counts in three places, and none borrowed to make the fourth come out even. Six hearings stood beside and never among with the seventh place kept empty; the heard bell stayed in its square beside the told; the three shapings of the Frost 14 saying stood on their own place with a finger between every pair and did not stand up and call themselves a witness; the finding that a house keeps by its not-doings stayed on its place and the boy's own gaps on another with a finger between the two keepings. He borrowed no house count, supplied no fifth to the fire, crossed no silence belonging to another's breathing and came down on the river's next low note with the letting-go kept whole and the interval kept whole and the two parted by a finger's width.

The hundred and sixty-first answer was filed exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air; neither the letting-go nor the interval was promoted into a reach, a hearing, a gift, a keeping, a seventh, a stopping, a payment, a held change, a key, a power, a freedom, a doctrine or a rule. The surgeon's clean satisfaction at the exact coordination was checked by the infant's plain want of the crust two inches off, and the checking was the work and he did not call it a virtue. The satisfaction was small and clean and he did not spend it; the wanting was large and damp and would not be answered by any counting. It was restraint and not attainment. A man may keep a letting-go whole beside a held interval and still not have moved the two inches to the crust, and the keeping did not make him freer than he had been at the first answer on the first morning.

Frost the thirtieth, the month's third and last Account-day and market, stood six mornings ahead and was not made a sign of or brought into a day that had not earned it. He made nothing of the nearness and did not let it stand up as a sign, for a sign wants somebody to read it and nobody under this roof had been given the office. Two hundred and ninety-three stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in Low Kiln, Sill and Grath; six months and one hundred and thirteen days were told by nobody; the carried fire's fifth was unpaid the same number of days with its payer unnamed. Nothing was opened, named, called, paid or ended. The hand came down from keeping the letting-go beside the held interval and laid itself back against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking, the crust two inches off with its empty place unnamed, the file open on the towel with every gap left gap, and the river going low over lower under the finger's-width door.
