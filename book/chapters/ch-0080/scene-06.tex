The afternoon of Frost the twenty-fourth came on without a boundary drawn, the way one board comes to lie flat beside another and neither asks the other to move. \person[Corin]{The boy} lay at the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf} under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof with the hand laid back against the fold on the hundred and sixty-first answer, and the heat went out of the fold by degrees as heat goes out of a pot set off the coals and left on a cold stone. It did not end; it thinned. Two hundred and ninety-three stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill} and \place{Grath}; six months and one hundred and thirteen days were told by nobody. Frost went on without a line drawn across it, and neither of the district's two habits of naming its months was set right or left wrong in so many words --- the older women named it by the weather and the slatemen by the count of Account-days, and the two went on side by side like two hands on one rope. No lesson was made of Frost's first arithmetic or Frost's second, both locked away up \place{the clay road} in \person{Berr Clay}'s box with the printed one in twelve unchallenged and the figure read still not the figure paid, and no payer was named to either. Four openings in four months were four and not a rule; the next opening stood in the first firing of a later month, far off, not hoped for and not counted toward. A rule is an account that has found a payer, and none stood to any of these.

He took the body as it presented itself, in the order the facts arrived, because that order had never lied to him and no other had promised to. One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from his ear, gone past the point where a child would eat it, and the empty place beside it kept its name to itself; no third had come in the afternoon to make the row come out even. The thread of drool at his mouth had frozen in the dry heat and was unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread, and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. Cold had come to cheek and temple and the bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, and stopped there as though it had come to the end of a line written out for it, with neither an order to go forward nor an order to go away. The afterdrop waited beyond all three, past a warming nobody in this house would perform. He knew the composed face that is not composure. He knew a cold patient cannot clot and will seem better before he is worse, and that a man must be warmed from the outside and slowly, never hurried, never rubbed; the grimace that cold puts on a face is not ease, and he had learned to read it in other men and could not read it off his own. He wanted the crust lifted the two inches to his mouth and could not close two inches on the pot handle set beyond his reach. Both were true and the second was louder; the surgeon took the inventory in its old order --- crust, gap, thread, cold, the waiting --- and entered none of it, not even on the place where he kept the things he did not enter. He had once reduced a shoulder in the dark by feel alone and now could not lift a crust to his mouth; the thought came and went without bitterness, which was its own small betrayal. There were two men under the roof and only one of them had a body. The older kept the inventory and the younger kept the wanting, and neither had authority over the other.

\scenebreak

\person{Sella Vail} carried him down at the half-pace from every open slate and went wide around the seven walked-around places with a look and a wide step and no number. At the Vail corner the clean dark line lay where the \lore{Ash} ridge had turned, and the finer grey hairline beside it, called \lore{the thaw}, lay finer still; neither had been swept and the broom went around both as a woman goes around a sleeping animal. Beneath \place{the lowest rack} the broken birth-slates lay where they had been laid, and she gave the space under the rack the wide berth she gave a pail of hot water set where a child might knock it, without looking at the slates and without entering them. On the flat ground near \place{the drying racks} the round ring where the shallow pan had once stood kept its shape with no scrape, no crumb, no print and no word. Beside \place{the yard well} \place{the trough} had skinned over from edge to edge and its water was called weather by everybody who passed it, and the weather was not asked what it cost. Out on \place{the worked strips} the field's empty place stood where it had stood since the planting, and the returning-line place, where a hand had gone in on Seed 15 and had not come out, was gone around with a look and no number. Seven were seven and not an account; she did not count them under her breath and he did not count them for her. The missing hand was carried, not named, not paid and not joined to the one crust in the fold, or to the dressed grain covered and unfigured under wool at \place{the rack end}, or to the man carried out on Day 188. A house that keeps by going around things keeps its accounts in the feet, and a man who reads such a house learns to count the steps and to call no total. Here he could not turn his head far enough to see the space under the rack and still keep the crust in view, and the two things stood on different places with a finger between. The yard's goings-around went on over him the way water goes over a stone that lies where a stone lies, and he was the stone and not the water.

The stayed frost lay white on the lip of the well where the Warm had lately gone out, and the rope stiffened as the bucket came up and gave its small dry rasp. On \place{the flat stone by the well}, set out of the path of feet where nobody would knock it, the plate of ice stood clouded with silt where the silt had risen into it, a covering laid over a depth that the yard called weather and asked nothing of. The boy kept the white lip on a place of its own and the plate on another, and he made no fourth likeness between them and the trough's skin and the frost on \place{the boards}, and he named no payer to any of them. A cause is a thing that wants a payer standing under it, and nobody stood. He had once known the exact number of instruments laid on a tray without deciding to, and here he kept three coverings that nobody had asked him to keep and would not make them one, because one covering wants one cause and no cause stood to any of them. In the other room he had been trained to make three disagreeing facts agree by leaving one of them out; here the leaving out would have been the fraud. He let the three be three. The bucket went down and came up, the rope gave its dry note, and the weather went on being weather, entered nowhere.

\scenebreak

\person{Tarn} was at \place{the kindling} with the small knife at the very edge of its sill. Six stood in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check and the seventh stood at the lip where it could not; \person{Garl Vail}'s unforged collector sentence lay beside it, with no name on the man who had turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on the debt. He told the wood \emph{Six counted. The next I don't count} in the tone of a man stating the weather, and when a woman went by wanting the thaw early and another wanting it late he said \emph{Yes, house} to each so thoroughly that no argument could find a foot, and went on wanting the late because it puts off the paying, though the paying stood in the ruts of the clay road and had stood there since the water went. His chest had been tight since the dark, and the sentence about an ear under this roof being worth more than the debt that held it came to his teeth and went as far as the word that would call the loan, and stopped there with no figure and no name --- a remembered ninth stopping and no tenth before the seventh year at its very end and still ungiven. He drew the breath that should have gone in at the top of the reach and did not spend it. Ash was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line and no scrubbing lifted it; the pale clip-ring at his left wrist held what light there was and gave it back. His stroke went on over \place{the flagstones} past the shallow bright patch without the half-check, the stones keeping the place where it had stopped, and from above came only a sound with a direction out of \place{the Debt Loft}, where the warm stone in its unmarked pot was cold and unreconciled by not trying, which is not forgiveness. He took the broom only after it had been given its instruction, and the boy, who had once counted the exits in a room without deciding to, kept the seventh where Tarn had set it and entered no figure against it.

\scenebreak

\person{Jorra Vail} came through the finger's-width-open door at the edge of \place{the Vail wheel-shed} with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal into both forearms past the bend of his little finger and under the nails, not bone-coloured \lore{slip}. He kept dusty forearms and clean ones on one tray and parted them as a surgeon parts clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had broken nor whether it was a birth-slate. He spoke to the wheel before he spoke to any person: \emph{It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady}. To a pot on the wheel he said \emph{You'll do} in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either. He knew the \lore{full note} that pot would give three turns before it left his hands, with air under it and a want in it, and withheld the knowing on the near-full tray beside the hearings and never among them, advanced and not paid. Men unnamed had gone low into \place{the back room} before first grey and he gave the house no name for them and did not say the room was cold; the boy took it as a direction and a sound. When Sella's sentence came low and unfinished, his bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper} ahead of the end of it, took the cold out of the fingertip, moved the tongue a hair's breadth and left it standing, so the end of her instruction arrived at the pot and the household straightened around his silence. The burned-slate word stood at four from \place{Slate Row}, a direction over the boards and not a fifth telling; the two tellings of the counting room shut in the war stood at two and had not become three; three sayings about a pot stood together with the premature Smoke 14 note on its own place and no fourth likeness made a cause; the row question stood at five askings with one shaping and no witness; \emph{he's not Vail} stayed sealed as tightly as a lid nobody had tried to lift in three years, and the promise of the boy's name to the men who burn slates was neither given nor withdrawn. He did not look toward the basket, and the boy did not lay any of it across the sealed sentence: a count held and a name withheld are two keepings, and there was no hand in the yard to join them.

Garl stayed in objects and not in a story. His salt-stiff coat with the crease at the left shoulder kept a broader man's shape on its peg behind the door; the wheel-seat in the shed kept its twenty-year lean and its hollow on that side, and Sella let no one fill it. The clean dark line at the corner kept its finer grey beside it, unswept; the proverb had gone years ago to a pot and the fire and never to a person --- feed the fire before you feed yourself, leave the crack showing, people trust a thing they can see has been handled --- and the debt against a firing of tiles stood neither paid nor settled nor done with, and was worse for being uncalled. His name had not been spoken under this roof in two hundred and ninety-three days. No collector came up the clay road and none went down it; Frost's first arithmetic and Frost's second rode locked in Berr Clay's box up the road toward \place{High Kiln}, and the keeping of a man by \lore{not-doings} stayed on its own place, maintenance and not a story, not joined to any shaping that had come over the boards and not made to answer for a name a district had spent. The house went around his absence the way it went around the line on the floor, and there was no lesson in it for anybody who cared to learn one.

\scenebreak

At the going of the light Sella carried him up at the half-pace from every open slate, set him on \place{the bed} under the roof and not in the Debt Loft, put the fold right without a look at his face and left the thread frozen where it lay. She told the fire it was the thaw, turned the coals with the back of a broken pot and said \emph{The house will keep it} where another woman would have said \emph{I}, and gave every instruction to the fire, the wool, the pot, the broom and the running water and never to the basket. At the loaf shelf her finger stayed on top of the front edge so the charcoal tally beneath it was unread; her lips moved along a row standing with two gaps and gave neither gap a figure, and she glanced once at the clay jar cold under its \lore{lead glaze} and shut her hand half over nothing. When she set the pot down on \place{the pot board} she set the rim two inches beyond the reaching hand with its handle turned toward it, and there was a half-beat between the setting and her going away, and she did not look toward the bed. The half-beat was not a gift and the handle turned toward the hand was not an instruction to take. \lore{The widow's mark} hung where it hung on its cord at her throat, a thumb-length of lead-glazed clay with the Vail kiln scratched into it, and she did not touch it, because a going of the light is not an opening and the hand goes to the mark before the kiln and at no other hour. No voice had come over the boards this afternoon with an owner to be asked, and she brought nothing under the roof as a lesson, a figure, a mending, a cause or a payment; if she put the day in the private roll nobody is shown, she put it there without a figure, and the boy did not ask and was not told. He watched the going of her burn-wrist the way he had learned to watch a surgeon's hand leave a field --- for what it did not touch --- and filed the not-touching on its own place, maintenance and not a message.

In the small hours the file was turned out on \place{the towel} as instruments are turned out on a tray, each thing in its own place with room between. Six hearings of the full note stood beside and never among, five from four sources and the sixth, the small flat knock out of \person[Dessa Silt] as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go and never with the things that came from vessels; the seventh place kept its emptiness and none was invented to fill it, for a finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged. The heard bell's four, four and a gap stayed in its square beside the told bell of \lore{the Long Night}, heard and not borrowed and not folded into the three counts in three places that did not become one. Three sayings about a pot stood together with the premature Smoke 14 note apart on its own place, and no fourth likeness was made a cause; the burned-slate word stood at four from Slate Row and the two tellings of the counting room shut in the war stood at two. The row question stood at five askings with one shaping and no witness, and the well-saying's shaping was not a sixth asking and was not the row's. Nine stoppings stood nine with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end and still ungiven; the nine record-first hands stood nine with the ninth, \person{Ilva}'s notched strip in a sleeve a pace off, unread and joined to none, and no tenth came into the small hours to make a row of them. The seventh green stood a colour at a silent woman's mouth and the seventh shape a shape in cast-off clothes at the market's edge, a colour and a shape with no eighth and no name spoken over either. The Frost 14 saying stood on a place of its own with its first, second and third shapings, a finger between every pair, filed beside \person{Hessa Rale}'s eighth record-first hand with a finger between that and the nearest shaping; not a witness, not a confession, not a joining and not paid. Three were three and did not stand up and call themselves a rule, for a rule is an account that has found a payer and no payer stood to any of them; a witness stands somewhere and can be asked, and none of the three stood anywhere to be asked. Frost's first arithmetic and Frost's second stayed locked in Berr Clay's box up the clay road with the printed one in twelve unchallenged and the figure read still not the figure paid; the dressed grain lay covered and unfigured at the rack end, and the stayed frost and the plate of ice clouded with silt on its flat stone lay where they lay, entered nowhere. The finding that a house keeps by its not-doings stayed on a place of its own, and the boy's own gaps on another, with a finger between the two keepings --- two gaps above the shelf, one crust in the fold and its unnamed empty place, the gone pan's round ring, the field's empty place out on the worked strips and the returning-line place where a hand went in and did not come out --- no third gap made a row and no fourth likeness was made a cause. Nothing in the file had moved; he kept maintenance and not a story.

\scenebreak

Outside the wool the hand went up an inch toward the river's own going. It took the held interval and the empty interval from the file and set them side by side with a finger between, borrowed no house count, supplied no fifth to \lore{the carried fire} and crossed no silence belonging to another's breathing, and came down on the river's next low note of \lore{water over water}. The boards overhead kept four nails on the near board, four on the next and a thumb-width knot where the fifth nail should have stood; under the floor the carried fire kept its four low pulls and the silence; through the finger's-width door the river kept its low thing over a lower thing. Three counts in three places, and none rose into another, because agreeing any two would have wanted a payer put under both and none stood. Neither interval was promoted into a reach, a hearing, a gift, a keeping, a seventh, a stopping, a payment, a held change, a key, a power, a freedom, a doctrine or a rule; the hand held two objects and made no total of them. The hundred and sixty-second answer was filed exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air. A round threshold does not stand up and call itself a doctrine, and three shapings do not stand up and call themselves a witness, for a witness stands somewhere and can be asked and none of the three stood anywhere to be asked. The surgeon's clean satisfaction at the exact coordination was checked by the infant's plain want of the crust two inches off, and the checking was the work and he did not call it a virtue. He had spent a life finding the one error that makes a set of numbers agree, and here the finding was to leave them as they stood.

The hand laid itself back against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. Two hundred and ninety-three stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in Low Kiln, Sill and Grath; six months and one hundred and thirteen days were told by nobody; the carried fire's fifth was unpaid the same number of days with its payer unnamed. Nothing was opened, named, called, paid or ended. The file lay open on the towel with every gap left gap, every tray parted by a finger's width and nothing that had moved. Frost the thirtieth, the month's third and last Account-day and market, stood six mornings ahead and was not brought into a day that had not earned it; the boy made nothing of the nearness and did not let it stand up as a sign, for a sign wants somebody to read it and nobody under this roof had been given the office. He lay with the crust two inches off and its empty place unnamed, the hand laid back against the fold on the hundred and sixty-second answer, the file open on the towel with every gap left gap, the frost white on the well lip and the plate of ice clouded on its flat stone beyond the finger's-width door, and the river's low note going quiet under the door after the hand came down.
