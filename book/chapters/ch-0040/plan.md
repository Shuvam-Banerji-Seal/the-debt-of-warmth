# Chapter 40 — *The Road Knows the Weight*

**Days 163–169, Tallow 14–20, Y.H. 813, Fourthday to Tenthday.** A **passage chapter at the seven-day cap**: seven lived days, each with its own dated `\daymark` heading and each lived in full for at least 800 words, none skipped, poured into another or summarised. Day 162, Tallow 13, is finished and is not revisited or recapped. Seven scenes, one to a day, 17,600 words planned. Drop capital belongs to Scene 1. The second Account-day and market-day of Tallow falls on Day 169, Tallow 20; the Cross is silent through Days 163–168. Day 170 opens Chapter 41, the month's last tend.

**Arc:** Arc 1 — *The Cold Stone*, “a warm stone, already going cold,” as in the metas of Chapters 35–39; `plan/arcs.md` needs no revision, because nothing in this chapter opens a new long-range direction. The arc's work here is to carry the exact count across a second reading, keep the absences and the private records separate, and let the firing month's danger gather toward an edge that can be paid later.

**Meaningful change of the chapter:** a fraction in the boy's private file, held and unspoken. On Day 166, under the Vail roof at the loaf shelf, **Sella's finger goes BENEATH the front edge where the charcoal tally lies and stays there for the length of one of the breaths she counts before she brings it away**. On Days 151, 157 and 160 the finger went under and came back in the same motion, without looking and without a word. This time it waits. She does not look, does not read, does not copy, does not go back to the shelf that night, and gives no explanation. The boy sees the held beat and files it on a place of its own as a change held and unspoken --- not a reading of the charcoal, not a payment, no payer named to it --- and keeps it from standing up with the Day 161 fraction at the damper and the older intervals into a rule, for a rule is an account that has found a payer and no payer stands to any of them (T100 carried and advanced, not paid; T061/T082). The reason still stands between knowing the board by heart and being told not to look. Nothing is joined: the shelf's two gaps, the crust's gap, the pan's gone place and the field's empty place stay on separate trays, no third gap comes and no fourth likeness is made a cause.

**Secondary texture, not a resolution:** the district's arithmetic gathers toward the second reading. Figures go up out of fists and mouths, loaves and knots; the carried fire keeps its missing fifth with its payer unnamed; the river keeps its note over water; the High Kiln saying about a pot that sings before its time, the dust ground small as meal, the man in Mud End taken quiet in his sleep and the back room go on gathering interest. No pot is lost in the yard, no payer is named, no lesson is made of the Mud End death, and no debt is paid.

**Hard withholdings (continue from Ch 39):** no calling of the loan, no tenth stopping, no spending of the Seed 16 breath, no giving or cutting of the seventh year, no seventh hearing of the full note, no name or number from Dessa, no silt-leach/cause/cure for the cold foot, no sale or betrayal paid, no opening of tin or box, no reading of charcoal or any private tally, no joining of the pan's gone place to the shelf's two gaps, the fold's crust or the field's empty place; no third gap, no fourth likeness made a cause. **The Day 161 fraction is held and unspoken and is not repeated**: if it is remembered by anyone, it is in the holding, not in a word. Marra stays out of Sill; Alda offstage and her mercy-lie uncorrected; Dessa stays dead and unnamed to the boy; the foundling stays offstage (the sixth glimpse has landed and must not be spent); the green is never Fenna in narration; *he's not Vail* stays sealed; the promise of the foundling's name stays ungiven. ## Scene 1 — The Third Week's First Morning

- **Place:** under the Vail kiln-house roof, the hearth, the kiln mouth, the Clay Yard, the yard well and the drying racks.

### Beats, in order
1. Open with the boy on his bed under the roof, fold at his ear and under his chin, one stiff brown-edged crust two inches away with its empty place unnamed, the thread of drool frozen and unwiped, his hand outside the wool going up an inch toward the note of water over water through the finger's-width door and coming down against the fold. 2. The kiln has been brought up for the third working week. 5. The boy reads the firing as he reads an operating theatre he cannot enter: heat kept and counted behind a screen, a gate with two bites, a cost paid by a man whose name is not spoken. He reaches an inch toward the water and comes down with one hundred and sixty-three exact and unspent.

## Scene 2 — The Pot That Sings Before Its Time

2. 6. Through the finger's-width door the river keeps its fifth low thing, water over water; the boy files it on a place of its own, not a hearing, not a seventh, and not joined to the kiln's gap or the boards' knot.
7. One hundred and sixty-four days old, eleven days to the reading; nobody says either number. His hand goes up an inch toward the note of water and comes down with the count unspent.

## Scene 5 — The River's Note Over Water

- **Day and time:** Day 167, Tallow 18, Eighthday, morning into the going of the light downriver, and the small hours under the Vail roof.
