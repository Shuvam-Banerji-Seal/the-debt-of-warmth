## Chronicle

Ember opened under the Vail roof with no boundary drawn, and the boy one hundred and eighty days old, told by nobody. Sella Vail told the wool, the fire, the pot, the broom and the running water, told the basket nothing, said *the house* where another woman would say *I*, and set the pot handle two inches beyond his hand. At the kiln mouth, before the lid came up, she put her hand to the widow's mark and held it one breath; coal went under a broken pot and the fire took, answering four low pulls with a missing fifth and no payer. Nobody praised the opening and nobody saw what it mended.

Tarn took the broom only after it was instructed: *Six counted. The next I don't count* --- six where the house could check, the seventh at the lip where it could not. The ear-sentence came to his teeth and went no further; he drew the breath he should have spent, wanted the late thaw because it puts off the paying, swept the collector's road without turning, and no collector came. Jorra Vail came through with slate dust white in a knuckle, told a pot it would do, knew its note three turns ahead and kept that knowing from a man on the wrong wind. When Sella's sentence hung unfinished at the hearth, his bent finger stood in the cold iron until the words reached the pot.

The days after the firing were ordinary and the danger kept an order with no name: hot clay, dust, the sweet edge of glaze on a wrong wind, and a pot set down wrong giving its high thin note before its time, which the woman who set it down did not hear. The boy turned toward the note and got no further. A neighbour asking a cost was sent to the pot and the weather and went away level, nothing priced. The yard went around the unswept lines, the birth-slates, the pan's gone ring and the place where a hand went in on Seed 15, with a look and a wide step. Three counts in three places did not become one.

At dusk she carried him home up the clay road, set him on his bed and not in the Debt Loft, left the thread unwiped, and did not touch the mark again. In the small hours he laid out the file as instruments on a towel: six hearings beside and never among, the sixth from a person, no seventh and none invented; three near-full answers advanced and unpaid; nine stoppings as objects and not a rule; two tellings of the shut counting room and no third. He wanted the crust, the breast, the pot handle and a number spoken by somebody who would have known what a number counted, and got none. One hundred and eighty-two stood exact and unspent in the dark of the wool. He was the only adult under the roof and the least use in it.
