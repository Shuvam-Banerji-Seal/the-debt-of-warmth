## Chronicle

Three days came under the Vail roof and the house kept them in loaves pushed a finger's width. No market, Cross, bell or runner came. The boy carried four hundred and twenty-nine, four hundred and thirty and four hundred and thirty-one. Each morning the surgeon took the body in its old order — the crust gone into old horn two inches from the ear, the fold found where put, the thread stiff and unwiped, the cold stopping at the wrist, the Afterdrop waiting past a warming nobody would perform — and the infant wanted the crust lifted, the thread wiped and a hand on the pot handle. Both were true, the second louder, neither won, and the quarrel was the work.

At both ends of all three days Sella came to the hearth before the basket, told the coals it was the thaw and said *The house will keep it* to the fire. Her finger held the shelf's top so the charcoal stayed unread; she lifted the twice-mended pot thumb-first, gave both proverbs to pot and fire, turned the dressed grain under wool, set the rim two inches from the reaching hand and never touched the widow's mark, for seven openings are not a rule. At the kindling Tarn kept six where the house could check and the seventh at the lip, and said *Six counted. The next I don't count*; his sentence about an ear stopped at the word that would call the loan. Jorra at the wheel-shed parted dusty from clean and withheld the full note.

On the sixth afternoon a word went around the well from mouth to mouth: the woman who carries the slate had taken up a second, narrower one, edge-on under her arm, the writing turned to her sleeve. Her hand went to the slates before her eye to the yard and her mouth after both, the half-beat in the gap. The boy filed it on a place of its own beside the eighth hand and the clerk's entered figure; a report and not a sighting, two slates the road carried and read neither. The reaching had come nearer to a joining than ever, and the refusal was in the same breath.

On the seventh afternoon the grey called the thaw at the Vail corner lay a hair less distinct along one unswept stretch, and the air off the river was less cold at one edge where the low note ran nearer. He found both where put, called them weather and did not look twice; Sella told the fire it was the thaw and did not tell the basket. Six answers stood worth nothing at two inches of air, not one a doctrine. The count reached four hundred and thirty-one exact, told by nobody; the fire's fifth was unpaid and Garl's name unspoken. The hand went up an inch toward the note, took a small intake and came down flat on the fold. There was no next hand in the room.
