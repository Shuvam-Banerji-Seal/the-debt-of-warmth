The afternoon came on without a bell and without a hand to call it, the way cold comes into a room under a door --- not at the door and not all at once, but along the floor first and up the skirting and only afterward at the chair. \person{Sella Vail} came to \place{the hearth} before she came to the basket, and the household straightened around the order of it as a table straightens when a weight is lifted off it. She carried \person[Corin]{the boy} down \place{the nineteen steps} at the half-pace from every open slate, correcting the tilt at the turn of the landing before the wicker settled, and the instruction went to the wool, the river wind, the pot, the fire and the banked fire and never to the basket. In \place{the Clay Yard}, \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill}, \place{Grath}, she set him down out of the draught where she could keep an eye on him between her pail and her broom, and went back to the small honest work.

The small sounds came first, and he took them first because they did not lie. A pail went down on \place{the flat stone by the well} with a flat knock that had no note in it and wanted none. The rope at \place{the yard well} settled into its knot with the cold-stiffened rasp, complaining while the weight came on it, going quiet when the weight was taken; the knot held what the knot was given and asked for nothing back. Barley went wall to wall without a number. A loaf was pushed a finger's width along a board and stopped, and nobody called the stopping a figure. He took each apart by fist and eye and mouth and the half-beat in the gap. The fist was the part that arrived; the eye was the part that checked; the mouth was the part that said or held its peace. Between them stood the half-beat, a place and not a person, an interval with no intention in it, for an intention wants a payer standing under it and none stood.

One wide circuit went around \place{the seven walked-around places}, once and not twice, with a look and a wide step and no number; widest around \place{the returning-line place}, where a hand had gone in on \emph{Seed 15} and had not come back out. No name went with the wide step, no body, no figure for what any month had cost, no accident written across the space. The step was a keeping and not a sign. At \place{the trough} the bare dark water kept the shallow round print of the clay pan lifted away, a ring on a place of its own, not laid across the loaf's stopping and not laid across the ridge at the corner. The water-line stood level in a vessel nobody had calibrated; level is not honest and a level is not a promise.

Costs came as directions and went away as weather and left no entry. A man waiting his turn opened a shut fist toward the water, looked at what was in it, shut the fist again and put it back into a sleeve, the number arriving nowhere. A woman sent to ask what a pot cost was sent to the pot and got a note and not a price. The figure read over the covered grain did not know what the grain had cost, and the ink-neat space between figure and price carried no signature. He had worked in a room where a price was a thing that had to be said aloud before it could be entered, and a number arrived at a desk with the hand that had held it gone clean out of the sentence; here the numbers went fist to sleeve and figure to weather and nobody turned them into a row. Walk-ons came and went at the well and the rail with pails and pots, going about an ordinary Seventhday grammar, and none looked into the wicker or gave the basket a name, an hour or an instruction. He took their not-seeing as findings and not as slights; a finding is what a surgeon takes when he cannot ask and nobody in the room can answer. There was no market, no \place{Cross}, no bell, no Account-day, no called collector and no runner on a day that had earned only its seventh.

At \place{the Vail corner} the clean dark line kept its finer grey called \lore{the thaw} a hair less distinct along one stretch where no broom had gone in two years. The grey had thickened in the dry cold and carried the faint sour tang of \lore{Ash} coming up off it, the mineral smell of a corner where something had been left too long and nobody had swept it into an account. Beside it the called-gone grey of the ridge lay left in place with a finger between; a thing left in place is not a thing forgiven. Through \place{the finger's-width door} the air off \place{the river} was less cold at one edge, where the low note of \lore{water over water} ran nearer under the winter skin and the tang came thinner. Both were found where put. He did not look twice. He called them weather, and weather is what a thing is called when no hand stands under it to own it.

Not a sign. Not a fourth covering. Not a fourth likeness. Not a sixth shaping. Not a payment. There was no payer under either grey and he did not supply one, and he did not make the thinner air again on a day that had earned only its seventh. The two greys at the corner kept their finger between them, the called-gone beside the left-in-place, and he did not lay one across the other to make a row come out even. In the other life he had stood at a ward window and watched frost leave the glass in two lines that met at a corner, and a nurse had called it the weather and gone on with her count; he had learned to read such lines by the place where they stopped, not by the name anybody gave them. These lines stopped where they stopped. He wrote nothing down that any Court could read.

The word that had gone around the well on the sixth day was not told again. The woman who carried the second slate was not standing in the yard to be asked, and the second slate stayed a shape in a mouth with no figure off it, narrow or wide, on a place of its own. Nobody made a reader of her out of a saying, and nobody made a witness of the half-beat in the gap. Silt 7 had its own work and had not earned a telling of the sixth. Nothing crossed under the roof from the corner or the well as sign, lesson, mending, cause, payment or payer, and the house took up its grammar as though nothing had turned.

\scenebreak

The going of the light came without anyone calling it. Sella came to the hearth before she came to the basket, and the household straightened around the order.

\emph{It's the thaw}, she told the coals, and did not tell the basket. She said it in the voice another woman would have used for a child who could not hear her, and turned the coals with the back of a broken pot; the back scraped over them with the small dry sound of a tool that had done this work before and expected to do it again, and left the blind ash where it lay. She gave the small intake, the hold and the letting-go toward no opening, figuring neither of the two gaps. Her finger lay along the top of \place{the loaf shelf} so the charcoal tally beneath stayed unread; no hand went beneath, no mark was copied, no row was numbered. Her lips moved on a two-gapped row. One glance went to the clay jar standing cold under \lore{lead glaze} beyond \place{the pot board}; the hand shut half over nothing, and the little-door burn on the back of her left hand, two straight sides with a lintel and a threshold, closed on air and went back to the pot.

Her hands were never still. When they had nothing to do they counted --- spoons, nails, the breaths of a child nobody numbered --- and came back to the hairline at the wire. He had worked in a room where an instrument count was called aloud before a case and again after, and the count was the only thing in that room that could not lie, because a sponge is a sponge and a needle is a needle and neither has a reason to flatter you. Here a woman counted spoons and the breaths of a child nobody numbered and came back to the hairline, and the count came back right, and nobody looked under anything. He read the work and not the woman, which was the whole of what he was allowed. One grey streak at her temple said winter, and she said nothing about the winter. She smelled of tallow and woodsmoke and wet wool, and under it the faint sour tang of the corner nobody had swept, and she carried the smell the way she carried the mark, without comment.

\lore{The widow's mark} hung where it hung and she did not touch it; a going of the light is not an opening, seven openings in seven months are seven and not a rule, and no eighth came due on a Seventhday that had earned only its seventh. She touched the mark before she opened \place{the kiln mouth} and at no other hour, and the kiln mouth was not open. She lifted the twice-mended pot thumb-first where the tighter wire crossed the crack. The hairline of the original break showed between the older brass staple and the wire, a dark thread with a hand's work on either side, and both proverbs went to the pot and the fire and never to a person: \emph{Leave the crack showing. People trust a thing they can see has been handled}; \emph{If it's worth mending it's worth mending twice. The second time is the real one}. Repair was not restoration. A restored thing hides the hand that handled it; a mended thing leaves the hand showing, so that a vessel carrying its break can be read by the next woman who takes it up. \person{Garl Vail}'s laugh had ended in a cough and his proverbs had survived him improved by use, and neither the hairline nor the sayings bought the absence a payment, a name or a mending.

At \place{the rack end} she turned the dressed grain under wool with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child, set each piece where it had lain and drew the wool back with the flat of her hand; no figure went over it and no handful was counted. Then she set the pot rim two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat and no look toward \place{the bed}. The half-beat was the distance between the intention and the arrest; she did not measure it and did not name it, for a named gap wants a payer put under it and none stood. Two inches of air bought no warmth and she knew it as well as he did, and neither of them said so. The pot stood where she set it and the warmth in it went the way warmth goes in a cold room --- out, slowly, by the whole of its surface, and faster where the metal met the air.

She carried him up the nineteen steps at the half-pace and set him under the roof at the low angle of the loaf shelf, at the edge of the light where the heat came sideways, never in \place{the Debt Loft}. She put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face and added no second fold. \lore{The keeping at the fold} in its empty place was found where put and stayed a finding, with no third crust, no name, no gift, no payment and no payer. The crust stood unmoved two inches from his ear, gone into old horn in the dry cold with its corner a hair off the wool. The thread at his mouth stayed stiff and unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread. He could not speak a word of \lore{Grathic} and had no way to ask for any of the three things the body wanted, and the wanting was complete and therefore useless. Under \place{the boards} \lore{the carried fire} kept four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer without a name for four hundred and thirty-one days; nobody lifted the floor and nobody supplied the pull. The kiln behind the wall breathed in fours with its gap, and the river under the door kept its low over lower under a skin, three counts in three places that did not become one, for agreeing any two would want a payer put under both and none stood.

In the small hours \place{the towel} came out at the head of the bed as instruments come out when a case is done and before the next is called, rough from the wash and smelling of tallow and low smoke. He went over the day by the place of each thing with every gap left a gap: the pail's flat knock on the stone, the rope's cold-stiffened rasp, barley wall to wall without a number, the loaf stopped a finger's width; the seven walked-around places once and not twice, widest at the returning-line place; the trough's round print on its own place; the two unswept lines at the corner with a finger between; the softening at the river's skin found where put and called weather; the fist arriving nowhere, the note that was not a price, the figure that did not know what the covered grain cost with the ink-neat space unsigned. The file turned out exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer.

Six hearings stood beside and never among, with the seventh place empty and \place{the Drawing}'s flat knock outside; the heard bell stood beside the told bell with both gaps in their own squares, and three counts in three places stayed three, for agreeing any two would want a payer put under both and none stood. Nine record-first hands and nine stoppings stood with no tenth. Seven openings in seven months were seven and not a rule. Three readings of the year stood behind one unsigned figure, three being three and not one account, the twelve not laid across the three, no signature in the second and no doctrine out of the third, the figure read still not the figure paid. Silt 10, the month's first reading and the year's fourth, waited three mornings off as a date and was not brought into this day. \person{Berr Clay}'s \emph{your house}, never \emph{I}, and his low saying about kindness entered among the bucket-round kept their own places with a finger between, believed and not owed; he was up \place{the clay road} toward \place{High Kiln} with the box locked and the key gone up in a coat pocket that never came back down. The surgeon's unsent call stayed where it had been put and was not paid again, a small thing with a name on it that he did not look at.

Beside all these, on places of their own with a finger between every pair, stood the things kept but not spent. The reported second slate of the sixth stood near, but did not touch, the eighth record-first hand that had gone up the road with \person{Hessa Rale} and her first slate unread; near, but not touching, the offered figure that had come out of a shut fist, looked at the light and gone back into its sleeve, arriving nowhere, and its return; near, but not touching, \person{Ilva}'s ninth hand with her strip of notched birch bark unread in its fold and the fifth shaping of \lore{the well-saying}. The well-asking answered with the weather, the second man's turned slate and direction, the first man's face, the soft-cuffed clerk's entered figure brought up the yard from \place{the out-of-line landing} below \place{the north bridge} as a report and never as a sighting, the three likenesses of \place{Slate Row}, the neighbour's near-joining of shelf and fold from Silt 3 stopped a finger's width short on its own place, the green and the shape of the two sightings on separate trays with no eighth of either, the burned slate at five and the four ownerless directions. \person{Tarn} was offstage at \place{the kindling} with six where the house could check and the seventh at the lip beside \person{Garl Vail}'s unforged collector sentence and \lore{the tile debt} circling without a name; \person{Jorra Vail} was offstage at \place{the Vail wheel-shed}, his bent finger's cold taken out of \place{the damper} ahead of a sentence nobody finished. Neither was made a rule or a compact. None of the kept things was told again, made a custom, a witness, a reader, a tenth hand, a payment, a calling of the loan or a paying. The hand that will one day lay the district's private keepings together is far off and is not his.

The surgeon's exactness and the infant's want stood side by side, both true, the second louder, neither winning, the quarrel the work. He wanted the crust lifted two inches, the thread wiped, a hand on the pot handle where the warmth lived; he knew the crust was old horn and would not nourish, the thread a small dry nothing, the pot handle a hand arriving at two inches of air and stopping. He finished on his plate what was put before him and could not reach the plate.

Answer four hundred and thirty-eight came out and was filed on the sixth place. Six honest answers, four hundred and thirty-three through four hundred and thirty-eight, stood on the sixth place, two to each of the three days, and not one was a doctrine. The answer was exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, laid across neither the reported second slate nor the thaw at the corner, the eighth hand, the offered figure and its return, the clerk's entered figure, the three likenesses, the twelve arithmetics, the three readings, the fifth shaping, the neighbour's near-reaching, the two sightings, the widow's mark nor the keeping at the fold.

Four hundred and thirty-one exact, six months and two hundred and fifty-one days, told by nobody who would have known what a number counted. The carried fire's fifth was still unpaid and Garl Vail's name still unspoken the same number, the fire and the man standing side by side without becoming one; his name had not crossed a threshold under this roof in four hundred and thirty-one days and no account had gone up the road to call his debt. He did not stand the number up as a threshold, a doctrine or a figure, for a number counted is a number with air around it and air buys no warmth, and a number stood up as a doctrine is an account that has found a payer where none stood.

Through the finger's-width door the river kept its low note of water over water, a low thing over a lower thing under the winter skin, softer at one edge where the low note ran nearer under it, the tang thinner there. The note came from under the covering and not from the covering, and ended on its own finishing. The hand outside the wool came up an inch toward it --- the inch not a reach, not a wanting made into a claim, only the body's small assent to a sound --- took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole on the note's own finishing, let it go as the note ended, and came straight down and laid itself flat against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. There was no next hand in the room. He did not wipe the thread and did not lift the crust. Four low pulls breathed under the floor and the fifth was not supplied. Forward, into an eighth day not yet lived and not brought into this one.
