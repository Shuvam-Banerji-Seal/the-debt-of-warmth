# Beat sheet — Chapter 126: *What the Road Carries*

**Days 429–431, Silt 5–7, Y.H. 814, Fifthday to Seventhday. Normal mode, six scenes, two per day. Count 428 exact at the opening hand on answer 432; 431 exact at the close on answer 438. Six honest answers 433–438 on the sixth place, two to each day, not one a doctrine.**

Opening state carried in, not restated on the page: the hand laid flat on the fold on answer 432 and no next hand in the room; count 428, six months and two hundred and forty-eight days told by nobody; three readings of the year behind one unsigned figure, three being three and not one account, the figure read still not the figure paid, the key gone up in a coat pocket that never comes back down; Berr up the clay road believing he did a kindness, Hessa's single slate unread up the road, Ilva's count in wood; the neighbour's near-reaching of Silt 3 filed on a place of its own beside the earlier shapings and the far-off finger, not repeated and not made a custom; the two market sightings on separate trays with no eighth; the offered figure, the well-asking, the second man's turned slate and direction, the clerk's entered figure, the fifth shaping, Tarn's withheld sentence, Jorra's promise, the softening at the river's skin, the called-gone grey, the left-in-place ridge and the keeping at the fold all found where put; six hearings with the seventh empty and the Drawing's knock outside; nine hands and nine stoppings with no tenth; seven openings in seven months and not a rule; the carried fire's fifth unpaid and Garl's name unspoken the same number. Silt 10 (Day 434), the month's first reading and the year's fourth, waits three mornings beyond the close as a date and is not brought.

The house takes up its grammar as though nothing had turned. Nothing from Silt 3 or 4 — not the neighbour's near-joining at the shelf, not any yard saying — crosses under the roof as sign, lesson, mending, cause, payment or payer. No recap. No present-tense break in this chapter and no `\memory{}` tag; the old life rides the surgeon's register alone, in past tense, untagged. The single present-tense break of Chapter 113 goes on gathering interest toward the later discovery of what crossed and what did not, and is not spent here.

## Scene 1 — The Fifth Morning of Silt

- **Day and time.** Day 429, Silt 5, Y.H. 814, Fifthday. Small hours at the very opening, then first grey and the morning's work.
- **Place.** The Vail kiln-house under the roof at the low angle of the loaf shelf; the hearth and pot board; the nineteen steps down; the Clay Yard; the kindling and flagstones; the wheel-shed edge.
- **Present.** Corin/Dan (carried), Sella Vail, Tarn, Jorra Vail; Garl Vail in objects only (salt-stiff coat with its left-shoulder crease, hollow wheel-seat, clean dark line with its finer grey, twice-mended pot with the hairline between staple and wire, unforged collector sentence). Ilva offstage with her count still in wood; Hessa's single slate unread up the road; Berr up the clay road with the box locked and the key gone up in a coat pocket. Walk-ons at the yard grammar, unnamed and interiorless. No market, Cross, bell, reading, runner or collector.
- **Beats, in order.**
  1. Only `\dropcap` of the chapter and first dated `\daymark{429}{Silt 5, Y.H. 814, Fifthday}`. Open in the small hours on the hand laid flat against the fold on answer 432, the count exact at 428, six months and two hundred and forty-eight days told by nobody, and no next hand in the room. Do not recap the reading, the two sightings or the neighbour's near-reaching; the room holds only its own objects.
  2. The surgeon takes the body in the old order, in the order it arrives: horn-hard brown-edged barley crust two inches from the ear with its corner a hair off the wool; the fold found where put in the empty place, still a finding and not a placing; the thread stiff in the dry kiln heat and unwiped because nobody under any roof has ever been given an instruction about the thread; cold stopped at cheek, temple and the bare wrist-joint where the wool rides back as at the end of a line written out for it; the Afterdrop waiting past a warming nobody here will perform. The infant's three wants — crust lifted, thread wiped, hand on the pot handle — quarrel without verdict, both true, the second louder, neither winning; the quarrel is the work. (T060/T086/T097)
  3. One untagged past-tense fragment from the surgeon's register is earned by the horn-hard crust and the cold joint — a warm room insisting a body is warm while the extremities go first — written in past tense, no present-tense break, no `\memory{}` tag. It says nothing of the white room and pays nothing. (T004)
  4. Sella comes to the hearth before the basket. The whole morning grammar: coals told it was the thaw and turned with the back of a broken pot; intake, hold and letting-go toward no opening with neither gap figured; finger along the top of the loaf shelf so the charcoal tally beneath stays unread; lips on a two-gapped row; one glance at the cold clay jar with the hand shut half over nothing and the little-door burn closing on air; widow's mark untouched. Twice-mended pot lifted thumb-first where the tighter wire crosses the crack, hairline showing, both proverbs to pot and fire and never to a person. Dressed grain turned under wool with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child and with no figure or handful. Pot rim set two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat and no look toward the bed. (T058/T079/T100)
  5. Carrying-down of the nineteen steps at the half-pace from every open slate; instruction to the wool, the river wind, the pot, the fire and the banked fire and never to the basket; the basket not told what the day is and not told to be still.
  6. At the kindling Tarn keeps six pieces in the worn thumb-mark where the house can check and the seventh at the lip beside Garl's unforged collector sentence; small knife at the very edge of its sill; hand over blade and sill and away with no breath held at the top of the reach, the coming away the whole of the checking. He tells the wood *Six counted. The next I don't count* once, in the tone of a man stating the weather; the ear-sentence stops at the word that would call the loan, the Seed 16 breath unspent, the loan uncalled in a term already ended, his valuable ear exposure and not safety. (T036/T076/T006)
  7. At the wheel-shed edge Jorra comes through the finger's-width door with meal-fine grey slate dust past the bent little finger and white in a fist-crease, parts dusty forearms from clean as clot from vessel, speaks to the wheel and a pot before any person, knows the full note three turns ahead and withholds it from any High Kiln master who would call it early. *He's not Vail* sealed; the promise neither given nor withdrawn. (T035/T031/T062)
  8. Answer 433 comes out and is filed on the sixth place. Count reaches 429 exact, six months and two hundred and forty-nine days, told by nobody; he does not stand the number up as a threshold or lay the twelve old arithmetics and the three readings across one another, for paper is not a payer and no key came under any roof. - **Sensory anchors.** Tallow, woodsmoke, wet wool; the faint sour tang of the unswept corner; the horn-hard crust with its hair-lifted corner; the half-beat at the setting; the broken pot's back scraping the coals; the cold-stiffened rope; the cold iron of the damper; the river under its skin through the finger's-width door.

  9. Small hours: the towel comes out at the head of the bed as instruments do when a case is done. The surgeon's exactness and the infant's want stand side by side; neither buys the other. Answer 434 filed, exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer. (T061/T082, T071/T072)

## The pull into Chapter 127

The chapter ends with the hand put away where a next hand would find it without looking, and with no next hand in the room. The count goes on through an ordinary seventh day and is not stood up as a threshold: 431 exact, six months and two hundred and fifty-one days told by nobody, six honest answers 433 through 438 on the sixth place and not one a doctrine. Nothing crossed under the roof from the runner's reported second slate or the hair-thin thaw, and neither was made a sign, a lesson, a sixth shaping, a payment or a payer; both were filed on places of their own, gathering interest toward the far-off hand that will lay the district's private keepings together and that is not his. Three readings still stand behind one unsigned figure, three being three and not one account, and the neighbour's near-reaching of Silt 3 still stops a finger's width short on its own place. Silt 10 (Day 434), the month's first reading and the year's fourth, waits three mornings off as a date. Chapter 127 opens on Day 432, Silt 8, Y.H. 814, Eighthday, with the hand still laid down on answer 438 and the count exact at 431, the house taking up its grammar as though nothing had turned, and the reported second slate, the softening called weather and the keeping at the fold found where put. Forward, not closed.
