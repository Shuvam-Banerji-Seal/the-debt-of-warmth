The afternoon carry came down the nineteen steps with the basket level and the fold set right at his ear, the weight taken before the handle as a woman takes the weight of a pot filled to the brim before she lets it come on the grip. \person{Sella Vail} did not hurry and did not stop. She gave the instruction to the wool, the river wind and the fire she would bank against their coming back, and never to the basket. “Keep it off his ears,” she told the wool. “It's the river wind.” The half-pace said a slate might be open anywhere and a slate was an account and an account had a hand waiting at it; the level of the wicker said a pot filled must not be slopped. Nothing in the step carried the morning's figure down into \place{the Clay Yard}. A figure read twice over a basket stayed where it had been read, and the house went on with its own grammar, which had no room in it for a tenth arithmetic and did not miss one. \person[Corin]{The boy} had stopped asking the step to say what it did not say, which was not the same as not listening.

Then the yard, and the going around. She went wide around seven places with a look and a wide step and no number: the clean dark line at \place{the Vail corner} with the finer grey hairline beside it called \lore{the thaw}, unswept both and thickened in the month's dry cold; the space beneath \place{the lowest rack} where the broken birth-slates lay and were entered by nobody; the round ring where the shallow clay pan had stood, with no scrape, no crumb, no print and no word; the well with its stiff rope, the skinned trough and the silt-clouded plate of ice out of the path of feet; the field's empty place; and the returning-line place where a hand had gone in on \place{Seed} 15 and had not come out. She was widest at the returning-line place, by nothing measurable and by no longer look, and she did not go around it twice to make a figure of going around it once. A line left where it is is still a line kept and not a lesson. A sign is a thing that points to a payer, and no payer stood under that ground.

Three coverings stayed where the weather had put them, with a finger between every pair and none entered anywhere: the gone white frost off the north lip of \place{the yard well}, the skin on \place{the trough}, the clouded plate on \place{the flat stone by the well}. He read them as dressings put on by cold and left as found, by what was there and by what was not, and did not make a fourth likeness of the three or a fifth of anything. Through \place{the finger's-width door} \place{the Ilm} went low over lower under its winter skin, the note muffled in the going as a voice is muffled by a shut door, found where it had been put and not made again, sitting on its own place beside \lore{water over water}. It was not a fourth covering, not a sixth low thing, not a hearing and not spent on the reading behind them or on the Drawing two mornings ahead. Under the floor \lore{the carried fire} kept four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer unnamed three hundred and sixty-nine days; overhead \place{the boards} kept four nails on the near one, four on the next and a thumb-width knot where a fifth might have been driven and was not. Three counts in three places and none rose into another, and the bell's gap stayed in its square.

At \place{the rack end} the dressed grain lay turned under wool, and her fingers went in and came out without closing. She lifted a corner to the dry air, turned what lay beneath with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child, and put the wool back; no figure came off it and no handful was lifted. The fingers that came out empty stood beside the one handful let fall back on \place{Bone} 22 as a keeping and not a second handful, for a second would be a habit and a third a way, and both want a payer and none stood. The grain was covered and unfigured and read over by a number that did not know what it cost, and the space between the figure and its price had no signature on it.

The yard's doings went by as directions judged by the carrying arm and the weather. A man come down from \place{Cold Acre} shifted a hare across his shoulders at the well and, to a woman who asked what the carrying had cost, said, “Ask the weather. It carried the snow. It can carry the price.” He went off toward \place{the clay road} with the hare unweighed and nobody opening a slate. Another with a cracked jar in a sling of rope asked what the month's mending would want and was told to ask the pot; the pot, being a pot, said nothing. A woman with a basket of kindling asked what the seventh year would want when it came, and two men mending a strip of fence said the thaw would be early and the thaw would be late. \person{Tarn} said, “Yes, house,” to each, and kept the late because a late thaw puts off the paying, agreeing so thoroughly that no argument found a foot anywhere. No hare was weighed, no jar entered and no figure came off a thaw. The boy filed the three answers on separate places by what was there and by what was not, and wrote no cause across any of them.

At \place{the kindling} the account stood in the old exactness. Six lay in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check, and the seventh lay at the lip beyond it where it could not, beside \person{Garl Vail}'s unforged collector sentence with no name on the man who had turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on the debt. The small knife lay at the very edge of its sill, where it could be checked and where a hand could also slip it off. Tarn passed his hand over blade and sill as a man passes a hand over a tool put back where it belongs, and the hand went away when the checking was done, and no breath stayed at the top of the reach. “Six counted. The next I don't count,” he told the wood, in the tone of a man stating the weather. He cut nothing and counted nothing.

The held breath he had left at the sill on \place{Ash} 14 was where it had been put: found where put and not made again. The thumb's resting on the uncut seventh of Bone 26 was likewise found and not made again. Each lay on a place of its own beside the five held objects and the pot-crack resting, with a finger between every pair --- not a sixth held object, not a seventh of anything, not a tenth stopping, not the seventh year given, not the calling of the loan, not a habit, a way, a sign, a hearing, a mending, a cause, a payment or a rule, with no payer standing under any of them. He brought the ear-sentence up to the word that would call the loan and stopped there, as a remembered ninth stopping with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end; the Seed 16 breath stayed unspent at the top of the reach and the loan stayed uncalled. His chest had been tight since the dark. The Ash was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line at the corner, and the pale clip-ring at his left wrist caught what little light there was and gave it back, and his stroke went over the shallow bright patch of \place{the flagstones} without the half-check.

\person{Jorra Vail} came through the finger's-width door off \place{the Vail wheel-shed} with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bent little finger and white in a fist-crease where a hand had opened, and it was not the bone-coloured \lore{slip} of the wheel. Three or more men unnamed had gone low into \place{the back room} before first grey and got no name from him and no word that the room was cold. He kept his dusty forearms and his clean ones on one tray and parted them as a surgeon parts clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had been broken nor whether it was a birth-slate while the broken ones lay under the lowest rack and were entered by nobody. He spoke to the wheel before he spoke to any person. “It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady,” he said, and told a pot, “You'll do,” in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either. He knew the \lore{full note} that pot would give three turns before it left his hands, with air under it and a want in it, and kept the knowing where nobody could make a song of it, withheld from any master come up from \place{High Kiln} who would call it early and sing a pot before its time.

Sella had not finished telling him the fire was low when his bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper} ahead of her sentence, took the cold out of the fingertip, moved the tongue a hair's breadth and was left standing so that the end of her instruction arrived at the pot and the household straightened around the waiting as cloth straightens when a weight is lifted. One piece of iron had two bites, the holding and the taking, and no Grathic word covered both mercies. He took the finger out without leaving the extra holding of Ash 26 a second time; it had been gathered once, on a place of its own with no payer standing, and a second would be a habit and a third a way. “He's not Vail,” stayed sealed as a lid nobody had tried to lift in three years, and the promise of the boy's name to the men who burn slates was neither given nor withdrawn under the shed.

No ownerless voice came over the boards on this afternoon. The Ash 5 joining, the Ash 17 direction and the Ash 23 direction stood where they had been put and did not repeat and bought no lesson: a room shut against the cold, a slate burned so a count would not go up, all of it with no room named, no hand at any flame, no figure and nobody standing to be asked. He took the dust, the two bites of iron and the unspent breath apart by what was there and by what was not and filed them on separate places, writing no cause across any of them and supplying no sixth telling, third shut-room telling, witness, name, room, hand, figure or payer to make a row come out even. A finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged.

At the going of the light she came to \place{the hearth} before she came to the basket, the way she had come to it at every morning and evening for three years. Fire before basket; the instruction to the wool, the pot, the wind and the river wind, never to the basket. “It's \lore{the thaw},” she told the coals, and turned them with the back of a broken pot. “The house will keep it,” she said, where another woman would have said I. The faint sour mineral tang of Ash lay along the skirting board; the ridge was unswept and thickened in the dry cold, and she had not swept it in two winters and did not sweep it now. She stood a moment counting a small intake, a hold and a letting-go toward no opening, giving neither gap a figure. Her finger lay on top of \place{the loaf shelf} so the charcoal tally underneath stayed unread, and her lips moved along a two-gapped row and figured neither gap. One glance went to \place{the clay jar} standing cold under \lore{lead glaze}, and the hand shut half over nothing, the little-door burn on the back of her left hand closing on air. \lore{The widow's mark} hung where it hung on the cord at her throat, the cord oiled where a working thumb had touched it ten thousand times, and it was not touched at an afternoon, a going of the light or a bed, because none of these was an opening and seven openings in seven months were seven and not a rule, with no eighth made due and the empty place where one might have stood not dug for or filled.

At \place{the pot board} she lifted the twice-mended pot with her thumb first where the wire crossed the crack, held the weight a half-beat before she let it come on the handle, and left the hairline showing between the old-brass staple and the tighter wire. “Leave the crack showing. People trust a thing they can see has been handled,” she told the pot. “If it's worth mending it's worth mending twice. The second time is the real one,” she told the fire. Both sayings went to the pot and the fire and never to a person, improved in the keeping and very likely not as good as the man had said them, and repair stayed apart from restoration, for a restored thing hides the hand that handled it and a mended thing leaves that hand showing. The salt-stiff coat kept a broader man's shape on its peg behind the door, the wheel-seat its twenty-year hollow that nobody was allowed to fill, and the clean dark line at the corner kept the place where a ridge had turned; the unforged collector sentence lay at the kindling with no name and no figure, and the debt against a firing of tiles circled without a name and was worse for being uncalled. His name had not been spoken under this roof three hundred and sixty-nine days, and the carried fire's fifth had stood unpaid the same number with its payer unnamed. She turned the dressed grain under wool with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child, fingers in and out without closing, and set the pot rim two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat at the setting and no look toward \place{the bed}. The half-beat was the distance between intention and arrest, bought at nothing and not a gift, for a gift wants a giver standing and none stood.

In the small hours the surgeon took a small clean satisfaction in a field taken as found and left as found, and it cost him nothing, which was how he knew it was not peace. Underneath, the infant wanted the crust lifted the two inches to his mouth, the thread at his mouth wiped, and a hand that would close on the pot handle set where the fingers could reach it. Both were true, the second was louder, neither won, and the quarrel between them was the work. One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from his ear with its corner standing a hair off the wool, its empty place unnamed and no third to make the row come out even; the thread had frozen in the dry kiln heat and was unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread. Cold had stopped at cheek, temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, as at the end of a line written out for it with neither an order to go farther nor an order to go away, and \lore{the Afterdrop} waited past all three beyond a warming nobody in this house would perform.

The room's small sounds came in their own order: a coal settling in the hearth, clay ticking along the shelf as it cooled, the river low under its skin through the finger's-width door. Three counts in three places, and none rose into another; the bell's gap stayed in its square beside the told bell of \lore{the Long Night} and was not folded into any of them. Six hearings of the full note stood beside and never among, the seventh place empty and not dug for; the heard bell of the morning stayed in its square and was not made a seventh hearing.

The file came out on \place{the towel} at the head of the bed as instruments come out on a towel when a case is done and before the next is called, and he went over it by the place of each thing. Three hundred and twelve honest answers from the days behind, and three hundred and thirteen from the morning, stood on the sixth place, each an object and not a power and not one standing up and calling itself a doctrine. Six hearings with the seventh place empty; the heard bell beside the told; ten readings behind one unsigned figure, the figure read still not the figure paid and no signature on the space; nine record-first hands with no tenth and nine stoppings with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end; the five held objects with the pot-crack resting, and the Ash 14 breath, the Bone 26 thumb's resting and Jorra's gathered holding each on a place of its own with a finger between every pair; the river's winter skin beside water over water and not spent on the Drawing; the three Ash directions apart; the promise ungiven and the loan uncalled. No sixth hearing, seventh opening, sixth held object, eleventh arithmetic, tenth hand or tenth stopping was supplied to make a row come out even, for a finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged.

The hand outside the wool came up an inch toward the river's note under the finger's-width door, the way a hand goes up in a field it has learned not to disturb. It did not reach, and the inch was not a reach. He took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole on the note's own finishing under the winter skin, let the intake go as the note ended, and came straight down and laid itself back flat against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. The note had done before the hand came down, so the letting-go was neither early nor stayed, and the hand did not stay; it was the note's own finishing under the covering that ended the interval and not any decision of the hand. He filed it as answer three hundred and fourteen: exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the ten arithmetics, the bell's gap, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the held breath at the sill, the two bites of iron, the dust in the fist-crease, the unspent breath, the river's winter skin, the setting-down-wrong, the eighth hand at the rail, the three Ash directions, the widow's mark nor his own kept gaps. The crust kept its hair-lifted corner and its empty place with no third; cold stopped at cheek, temple and wrist; through the finger's-width door the river went low over lower under its winter skin, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming. Three hundred and fourteen honest answers stood on the sixth place and not one was a doctrine. Count three hundred and sixty-nine exact, and the Drawing two mornings off, unbrought. Forward, not closed.
