The hand lay flat on the fold where it had been put, and the count stood at three hundred and seventy-one exact. It did not carry itself forward; \person[Corin]{the boy} carried it, as he carried the room's small sounds and the crust's hair-lifted corner, without deciding to and without telling anybody. He was three hundred and seventy-one days old, six months and one hundred and ninety-one days told by nobody who would have known what a number counted. \lore{The carried fire}'s fifth stood unpaid three hundred and seventy-one days with its payer unnamed, and \person{Garl Vail}'s name had not been spoken under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof the same number. \lore[The Hearth Drawing]{The Drawing} stood behind the household with no lesson made of it.

The surgeon took the body in the old order, as a man takes a field before the light is good enough to work in it. One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from the ear, gone harder and browner in the dry cold, its corner standing a hair off the wool; the empty place beside it kept its name to itself and no third had come to make the row come out even. The thread of drool at the mouth had frozen in the dry kiln heat and lay unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread, and a basket set down was a pot set down and left. Cold had come to cheek, temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, and stopped there as though it had reached the end of a line written out for it, with neither an order to go farther nor an order to go away. \lore{The Afterdrop} waited past all three, beyond a warming nobody in this house would perform. He knew a face composed by cold from a face composed by choice, and knew that a cold patient cannot clot and will seem better before he is worse. The infant wanted the crust lifted the two inches to his mouth, the thread wiped, and a hand that would close on the pot handle set where the fingers could reach it. Both were true. The second was louder. Neither won, and the quarrel between them was the work.

\person{Sella Vail} came to \place{the hearth} before the basket, as she had come to it at every going of the light since the boy came under the roof. “It's \lore{the thaw},” she told the coals, and turned them with the back of a broken pot. “The house will keep it,” she said, where another woman would have said I, and stood a moment counting a small intake, a hold and a letting-go toward no opening, giving neither gap a figure. The instruction went to the wool and the river wind and to the fire she would bank against their coming back, and never to the basket. “Keep it off his ears,” she told the wool. “It's the river wind.” She said nothing of where they had been, nothing of the room, the tube, the child or the cup, and that was not a refusal to tell him, for nobody under any roof tells a basket what a keeping is worth and she was not the woman who would say a thing to please the speaker. Her hand did not go to \lore{the widow's mark}. A Drawing was not an opening, and an afternoon, a going of the light and a bed were not openings; seven openings in seven months were seven and not a rule, and no eighth was made due. The cord at her throat was oiled where a working thumb had touched it ten thousand times, the little-door burn on the back of her left hand shut on nothing, and the mark hung where it hung.

At \place{the pot board} the twice-mended pot kept the hairline of the original break showing between the old-brass staple and the tighter wire. She put her thumb first where the wire crossed the crack before she let the weight come on the handle, and said both proverbs to the pot and the fire and never to a person: *Leave the crack showing. People trust a thing they can see has been handled* and *If it's worth mending it's worth mending twice. Repair stayed apart from restoration, because a restored thing hides the hand that handled it and a mended thing leaves that hand showing, and the hairline was the hand.

The dressed grain lay covered and unfigured, turned under wool with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child, her fingers going in and coming out without closing, no figure lifted and no handful.
