The afternoon came on without a figure in it. \person{Sella Vail} carried him down at the half-pace she kept from every open slate, the weight taken before the handle and the basket level and unslopped, and the instruction went to the wool and the river wind and to the fire she would bank against their coming back. “Keep it off his ears,” she told the wool. “It's the river wind.” She told the basket nothing, and no basket under that roof had ever been told anything; a basket set down was a pot set down and left. \person[Corin]{The boy} went where he was carried and kept the half-pace without being told, as he kept the household's whole small grammar without being told, and the slate that might have been open anywhere went unopened wherever they passed it. Three hundred and seventy days stood exact behind him and six months and one hundred and ninety days were told by nobody who would have known what a number counted. The \lore{carried fire}'s fifth stood unpaid three hundred and seventy days with its payer unnamed, and \person{Garl Vail}'s name had not been spoken under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof the same number of days. The morning's ordinary tending had left no residue in the yard; nothing had crossed under any roof as sign, lesson, mending, cause, payment or payer, and the \place{Stone} reading that stood behind them went up the road in a locked box and did not come down into an afternoon that had not earned a second figure.

She went wide around the seven walked-around places with a look and a wide step and no number. The clean dark line at \place{the Vail corner} lay with its finer grey hairline beside it called \lore{the thaw}, unswept both and thickened in the month's dry cold; the space beneath \place{the lowest rack} kept its broken birth-slates and was entered by nobody; the round ring where the shallow clay pan had stood kept its place with no scrape, no crumb and no print; at \place{the yard well} the rope stood stiff, \place{the trough} was skinned from edge to edge, and the silt-clouded plate on \place{the flat stone by the well} sat a little out of the path of feet; the field kept its empty place; and the returning-line place, where a hand had gone in on Seed 15 and had not come out, was gone around widest of all --- by nothing measurable and with no longer look, not gone around twice to make a figure of going around once. A line left where it was was still a line kept and not a lesson, for a sign is a thing that points to a payer and no payer stood under that ground.

The gone white frost was off the well's north lip, the skin lay on the trough, and the silt-clouded plate kept its flat stone; three coverings of weather entered nowhere, with a finger between every pair. Through \place{the finger's-width door} of the house behind them the river went low over lower under its winter skin, found where put and not made again, sitting on its own place beside \lore{water over water}, not a fourth covering, not a sixth low thing, not a hearing and not spent on \lore[the Hearth Drawing]{the Drawing} one morning ahead. It was a covering put on by the cold and taken off by the cold, with no hand that laid it and no source standing to be asked; the boy found it where it had been put and left it there.

The yard was at its ordinary work. A man come down from \place{Cold Acre} shifted a hare across his shoulders at the well and, to a woman who asked what the carrying had cost, said, “Ask the weather. It carried the snow. It can carry the price,” and went off toward \place{the clay road} with the hare unweighed and nobody opening a slate. Another, with a cracked jar in a sling of rope, asked what the month's mending would want and was told to ask the pot; the pot, being a pot, said nothing. A woman with a basket of kindling asked what the seventh year would want when it came, and two men mending a strip of fence said the thaw would be early and the thaw would be late. To each of them \person{Tarn} said, “Yes, house,” and kept the late, because a late thaw puts off the paying, agreeing so thoroughly that there was nowhere for an argument to find a foot. These were the month's hunts and repairs, directions judged by the carrying arm and the weather, never figures. The boy took them as they stood and wrote no cost across any of them.

At \place{the kindling} the account stood in the old exactness: six in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check, and the seventh at the lip beyond it where it could not, with Garl's unforged collector sentence lying beside the seventh --- no name on the man who had turned back at the frozen ford, no figure on the debt. The small knife lay at the very edge of its sill. Tarn passed his hand over blade and sill as a man passes a hand over a tool put back where it belongs, and the hand went away when the checking was done, with no breath held at the top of the reach and nothing left standing where a hand had been. “Six counted. The next I don't count,” he told the wood, in the tone of a man stating the weather. He cut nothing and counted nothing.

The Ash 14 held breath at the sill was where it had been put; the Bone 26 thumb's resting on the uncut seventh was where it had been put. He found each as a man finds a tool laid down the day before, passed over each without making it again, and left each on its own place with a finger between every pair --- beside the five held objects and the pot-crack resting, not a sixth held object, not a seventh of anything, not a tenth stopping, not the calling of the loan, not a habit, a way, a sign, a hearing, a mending, a cause, a payment or a rule, with no payer standing under any of them. The pale clip-ring at his left wrist caught what little light there was and gave it back.

He brought the ear-sentence up to the word that would call the loan and stopped there, as he had stopped nine times before and would stop a tenth only at the very end of the seventh year, which had not come. The sentence about an ear under this roof being worth more than the debt that held the house going went as far as the threshold and no farther, with no figure after it and no name after it, and nobody stood there to enter it. The Seed 16 breath stayed unspent at the top of the reach, the loan uncalled, and the equality under the roof held at the place to which it had moved --- two ears, two silences, a loan that could hear and had never been called --- with nobody to enter it and nobody to call it. His chest had been tight since the dark. The Ash was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line, and his stroke went over \place{the flagstones}' shallow bright patch without the half-check; the Bone 8 returned half-check lay where it lay and was not made a fifth of anything. He did not look along the road.

The boy read the hand as he had learned to read it, by what it did and by what it did not: the hand that checks and goes away is not the hand that only rests and is not the hand that works. He filed the stopping on a place of its own as one more of nine, and filed no tenth. Nine stoppings standing in a row might call themselves a rule if a hand were there to lay them together, but a rule is an account that has found a payer and none stood. The filing bought him nothing and he knew it.

Three or more men had gone low into \place{the back room} before first grey, and they got no name from \person{Jorra Vail} and no word that the room was cold. They went as men go into a room whose door is kept from closing, heads down, shoulder-brushes drawn in, and the house went on around them as a pot goes on holding its shape when the hand comes off the handle. He came through \place{the finger's-width door} off \place{the Vail wheel-shed} with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bent little finger and white in a fist-crease where a hand had opened; it was not the bone-coloured \lore{slip} of the wheel. He kept his dusty forearms and his clean ones on one tray and parted them as a surgeon parts clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had been broken nor whether it was a birth-slate, while the broken ones lay under the lowest rack and were entered by nobody. He spoke to the wheel before he spoke to any person. “It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady,” he told it, and told a pot, “You'll do,” in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either.

He knew \lore{the full note} that pot would give three turns before it left his hands, with air under it and a want in it, and kept the knowing where nobody could make a song of it, withheld from any master come up from \place{High Kiln} who would call it early and sing a pot before its time. The boy kept that knowing on a tray of its own beside the hearings and never among them, and no seventh hearing stood up in the empty place to take it.

Sella had not finished telling him the fire was low when his bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper} ahead of her sentence. It took the cold out of the fingertip, moved the tongue a hair's breadth, and was left standing there, so that the end of her instruction arrived at the pot and the household straightened around the waiting as cloth straightens when a weight is lifted. One piece of iron had two bites, the holding and the taking, and no Grathic word covered both mercies. Then the finger came out. The extra holding of Ash 26 was not left a second time; it stayed gathered once on a place of its own with no payer standing under it, not a sixth held object, not a seventh, not an eighth, not a habit, a way, a sign, a hearing, a mending, a cause, a payment or a rule. A second would have made a habit and a third a way, and both want a payer and none stood. \emph{he's not Vail} stayed sealed as a lid nobody had tried to lift in three years, and the promise of the boy's name to the men who burn slates was neither given nor withdrawn.

The boy took the dust, the two bites of iron and the unspent breath apart by what was there and by what was not and filed them on separate places, with a finger between every pair, writing no cause across any of them. Grey dust in a fist-crease was not the same thing as a cold finger in iron, and a cold finger in iron was not the same thing as a breath held at a word and not spent; to lay them together would want a hand that stood and a payer under all three, and no such hand stood and no such payer stood. The finding gathered interest against a day not come and was not paid on this one. A cold foot carried from a woman who had wrapped it stayed a cold to that woman and weather to the yard, uncured and unnamed; no silt-leach, cause, cure, recognition or treatment came near it, and the boy wrote no lesson over it, for the foot was a foot and the weather was weather and neither had been asked to agree.

At the going of the light she carried him back up the nineteen steps at the same half-pace from every open slate, went wide around the seven walked-around places with a look and no number and widest around the returning-line place, and set him on \place{the bed} under the roof and never in \place{the Debt Loft}. She put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face, let her hand rest the half-beat longer than the setting needed before it went away, and left the thread frozen and unwiped. She said nothing of where they had been, and that was not a refusal to tell him, for nobody under any roof tells a basket what a keeping is worth and she was not the woman who would say a thing to please the speaker. The half-beat was the distance between intention and arrest, bought at nothing and not a gift; a gift wants a giver and nobody gave. The infant wanted the crust lifted the two inches to his mouth, the thread wiped, and a hand that would close on the pot handle set where the fingers could reach it. The surgeon knew a basket set level out of the draught where the heat came sideways was the careful rewarming he would choose and could not ask for, and knew that pink returning to a face is the surface still cold and not the core. Both were true. The second was louder. Neither won, and the quarrel between them was the work.

She went to \place{the hearth} before the basket. “It's \lore{the thaw},” she told the coals, and turned them with the back of a broken pot. “The house will keep it,” she said, where another woman would have said I, and stood a moment counting a small intake, a hold and a letting-go toward no opening, giving neither gap a figure. The faint sour mineral tang of Ash lay along the skirting board, the ridge unswept and thickening in the dry cold, and she did not sweep it and did not say why. Her finger lay on top of \place{the loaf shelf} so the charcoal tally underneath stayed unread; her lips moved along a two-gapped row and figured neither gap. She glanced once at the clay jar standing cold under lead glaze and shut her hand half over nothing, the little-door burn on the back of her left hand closing on air. \lore{The widow's mark} hung on the cord at her throat, oiled where a working thumb had touched it ten thousand times, and she did not touch it --- a morning, a yard, a going of the light and a bed are not openings, seven openings in seven months are seven and not a rule, and no eighth was made due.

At \place{the pot board} she lifted the twice-mended pot with her thumb first where the wire crossed the crack, held the weight a half-beat, and left the hairline of the original break showing between the old-brass staple and the tighter wire. “Leave the crack showing. People trust a thing they can see has been handled,” she told the pot. “If it's worth mending it's worth mending twice. The second time is the real one,” she told the fire. Both sayings went to pot and fire and never to a person, improved in the keeping and very likely not as good as the man had said them; repair stayed apart from restoration, for a restored thing hides the hand that handled it and a mended thing leaves that hand showing. She turned the dressed grain under wool with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child, fingers in and out without closing, and lifted no figure and no handful. The salt-stiff coat kept its broader shape on its peg, the wheel-seat kept its hollow that nobody filled, the clean dark line at the corner kept its finer grey beside it, the unforged collector sentence kept its place beside the seventh, and the debt against the firing of tiles lay where it lay, circled without a name and worse for being uncalled. Then she set the pot rim two inches beyond the reaching hand, with a half-beat at the setting and no look toward the bed, and went away about the banked fire.

In the small hours the file came out on \place{the towel} at the head of the bed as instruments come out on a towel when a case is done and before the next is called, and he went over it by the place of each thing and not by any figure the places might have made standing together. Three hundred and fourteen honest answers on the sixth place, and three hundred and fifteen from the morning; six hearings of \lore{the full note} standing beside and never among with the seventh place empty, and not one of them a stopping; the heard bell in its square beside the told bell of \lore{the Long Night}; the told figure beside the paid figure with nine arithmetics between and no signature on the space; nine record-first hands with a finger between every pair and no tenth; nine stoppings with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end; five held objects with the pot-crack resting beside them, and the hand-at-sill breath and the holding in the cold iron beside those, each on its own place; the setting-down-wrong gathered once beside the premature note of Smoke 14; the burned slate at five, the shut-room tellings at two, the well-saying at four shapings, the row at five askings with no witness; the Ash 5 joining and the Ash 17 direction and the Ash 23 direction each where it had been put, with a finger between every pair; the river's winter skin beside \lore{water over water}, found where put and not spent on this day or on the Drawing; three counts in three places not rising into one, the bell's gap staying in its square and not folded into any of them; the promise under the wheel-shed neither given nor withdrawn and the loan uncalled. No sixth hearing, no seventh opening, no sixth held object, no sixth telling, no third shut-room telling, no sixth asking, no fifth shaping, no tenth hand and no tenth stopping was supplied to make a row come out even, for a finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged.

The surgeon took a small clean satisfaction in a field taken as found and left as found, and it cost him nothing, which was how he knew it was not peace. The room kept its small sounds --- coal settling, clay ticking along the shelf, the river low under its skin --- three counts in three places and no hand to lay them together; and overhead \place{the boards} kept four nails on the near one, four on the next, and a thumb-width knot where a fifth might have been driven and was not.

The hand outside the wool came up an inch toward the river's note under the finger's-width door, the way a hand goes up in a field it has learned not to disturb. It did not reach, and the inch was not a reach. He took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole on the note's own finishing under the winter skin, let the intake go as the note ended, and came straight down and laid itself back flat against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. The note had done before the hand came down, so the letting-go was neither early nor stayed, and the hand did not stay. He filed it as answer three hundred and sixteen: exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment, borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the arithmetics, the bell's gap, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the held breath at the sill, the two bites of iron, the dust in the fist-crease, the river's winter skin, the setting-down-wrong, the three Ash directions, the widow's mark nor his own kept gaps. The crust kept its hair-lifted corner and its empty place with no third; cold stopped at cheek, temple and the bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back; \lore{the Afterdrop} waited past all three, beyond a warming nobody in this house would perform. Three hundred and sixteen honest answers stood on the sixth place and not one was a doctrine. Through the finger's-width door the river went low over lower under its winter skin, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming. Count three hundred and seventy exact, with one morning between it and the Drawing, and that morning not yet earned. Forward, not closed.
