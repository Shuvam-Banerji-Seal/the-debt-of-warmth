\daymark{370}{Stone 11, Firstday, Y.H. 813}

The hand outside the wool had come straight down on answer three hundred and fourteen and lain flat against the fold since the small hours, and the room went on around it as a yard goes on around a stone set in its place and forgotten. \person[Corin]{The boy} lay on \place{the bed} at the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf}, under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill}, \place{Grath}. The morning had a date no roof under that sky would speak aloud: \place{Stone} 11, the first day of the tend, a Firstday with no market, no Cross and no bell and nothing due at any post. He kept the date exactly, as he kept all of them, and did not stand it up as a line for anybody's foot to cross. Three hundred and sixty-nine days stood exact behind him and the three hundred and seventieth was come in, six months and one hundred and eighty-nine days turned into one hundred and ninety, told by nobody who would have known what a number counted. The \lore{carried fire}'s fifth had stood unpaid three hundred and seventy days with its payer unnamed, and \person{Garl Vail}'s name had not been spoken under this roof the same number of days. Nothing in the room had been moved for him or taken away, and the small satisfaction of that cost him nothing, which was how he knew it was not peace.

The surgeon took the body in the old order, by the small sounds first because the small sounds do not lie: the coal settling in the hearth, the clay ticking along the shelf as it cooled, the river low under its skin through \place{the finger's-width door}. Three counts in three places, and none rose into another. Then the body itself. A stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from his ear, gone harder and browner in the dry cold, its corner standing a hair off the wool; the empty place beside it kept its name to itself and no third had come in the night to make the row come out even. The thread of drool at his mouth had frozen in the dry kiln heat and lay unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread, and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. Cold had come to cheek, temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, and stopped there as at the end of a line written out for it with neither an order to go farther nor an order to go away. \lore{The Afterdrop} waited past all three, beyond a warming nobody in this house would perform. He knew the order in which a body takes heat back --- the small joint first, the core last --- and the order in which it gives it up, the reverse, which arrives sooner than anybody likes, and that pink returning to a face is the surface still cold and not the core. He had once reduced a shoulder in the dark, an arm in his two hands and a room held quiet around it, and he could not lift a crust two inches to his own mouth. Both were true. The infant wanted the crust lifted, the thread wiped and a hand that would close on the pot handle set where the fingers could reach it. The second was louder. Neither won, and the quarrel between them was the work.

\scenebreak

Before the light came proper \person{Sella Vail} went to \place{the hearth} before she went to the basket, the way she had gone at both ends of every day for three years. Fire before basket. “It's \lore{the thaw},” she told the coals, and turned them with the back of a broken pot. “The house will keep it,” she said, where another woman would have said I. She stood a moment counting a small intake, a hold and a letting-go toward no opening, giving neither gap a figure, and the household went on about its business as a pot goes on holding its shape when the hand comes off the handle. The instruction went to the wool, the pot, the wind and the river wind, and never to the basket. “Keep it off his ears,” she told the wool. “It's the river wind.” She lifted him with the weight taken before the handle, the way a woman takes the weight of a pot filled to the brim before she lets it come on the grip, and carried him down at the half-pace from every open slate, level and unslopped. The half-pace said a slate might be open anywhere and a slate was an account and an account had a hand waiting at it. Nothing in the step carried yesterday's arithmetic down into the yard; a figure read twice over a basket stayed where it had been read, and the house went on with its own grammar, which had no room in it for a tenth arithmetic and did not miss one.

At \place{the pot board} she lifted the twice-mended pot with her thumb first where the wire crossed the crack, held the weight a half-beat, and left the hairline of the original break showing between the old-brass staple and the tighter wire. “Leave the crack showing. People trust a thing they can see has been handled,” she told the pot. “If it's worth mending it's worth mending twice. The second time is the real one,” she told the fire. Both sayings went to the pot and the fire and never to a person, improved in the keeping and very likely not as good as the man had said them; repair stayed apart from restoration, for a restored thing hides the hand that handled it and a mended thing leaves that hand showing. She turned the dressed grain under wool with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child, fingers in and out without closing, and lifted no figure and no handful. \lore{The widow's mark} hung where it hung on the cord at her throat, the cord oiled where a working thumb had touched it ten thousand times, and she did not touch it at a well or a yard, for neither was an opening and seven openings in seven months were seven and not a rule, with no eighth made due and the empty place where one might have stood not dug for or filled. Then she carried him out.

The yard was at its ordinary work. Water went down to the racks and came back on the pails; clay dried pale on every lip and edge; somewhere a board was being mended and a man answered the weather for the price of it. An open slate lay on the stone lip a pace from the well with its writing turned toward its owner, and nobody took a figure off it. The boy kept his half-pace from it without being told, and the woman carrying him kept it without looking, the two of them moving around the writing as around a sleeping animal that might wake and want something. Through the finger's-width door of the house behind them the river went low over lower under its winter skin, found where put and not made again, sitting on its own place beside \lore{water over water}, not a hearing and not spent on the morning.

\scenebreak

\person{Ilva} stood at \place{the yard well} drawing with the pail against her knee, the rope stiff in the \place{Stone} cold and giving its small dry rasp as the bucket came up, the water dark and moving without a sound. Clay had dried pale in the crescents of her nails and the finer grey dust of the racks was worked into the seam of one thumb, which she did not scrub out. Her hands knew the weight of a pail and the edge of a wet board and had never been soft. The people going about the yard noticed the pail first and the sleeve not at all, which was the order she preferred; a woman of the firing work is of the yard the way \place{the trough} and the rack are of the yard, and nobody turns twice to look at one.

A woman waiting her turn, with a pot on her hip and her shawl tight against the river wind, asked what Stone's firings had taken. Ilva's hand went toward her sleeve and stayed. The short strip of notched birch lay in the fold where it always lay, pale where a thumb had rubbed the notches and dark in the uncut grain between; it was not taken out, not read, not copied, not counted, and not joined to the open slate a pace away, to the charcoal beneath the loaf shelf, to the cord, to the knot-string or to any saying that had arrived nowhere. No figure came off the wood. Her eyes went to the asker, and her mouth opened after both with a half-beat between the looking and the word --- the half-beat a lesser observer would have filled with an intention, but there was none there to fill, only the space where the record stood before the person and the person before the answer. “Ask the pot what it cost. I keep the count, not the account,” she said. It was the question put that she answered and not the one behind it, and the woman went off with her answer level in her hands.

Ilva put the flat of her hand once more on the sleeve and went back to the pail, and nobody turned twice. The rope gave its small dry rasp; the dark water moved without a sound and gave back nothing. She had not looked into the basket and had no notion she had been read. To her he was a child at the edge of the work, carried by a house-woman who kept her half-pace and said nothing. The count stayed in wood where no slate could take a figure off it, and the well went back to being a well.

\scenebreak

The boy took the order of her reaching apart as a surgeon takes a field apart, by what was there and by what was not: hand to the record before eyes to the person, eyes before mouth, the half-beat in the gap where another man would have put an intention and found none standing. He filed it on a place of its own as the ninth record-first hand, with a finger between it and \person{Hessa Rale}'s eighth, and he filed no tenth. He made no reader of her keeping and joined her to none, not to the runner with her edge-on slate, not to the clerk at the landing with his inward one, not to the charcoal beneath the loaf shelf, and he made no figure of the slate's nearness to her well. Nine hand-shapes standing in a row might call themselves a rule if a hand were there to lay them together, but a rule is an account that has found a payer and none stood; the filing bought him nothing and he knew it.

She was expressly not the speaker of any voice. No ownerless word had come over \place{the boards} this morning; no fifth shaping had come of the \place{Frost} 14 well-saying, no sixth asking of the row, no third telling of the shut room, and no witness stood to any of them. The \place{Ash} 5 joining, the Ash 17 direction and the Ash 23 direction stood each where they had been put, with a finger between every pair, and did not repeat and bought no lesson. He did not promote her answer into a saying or her strip into a record that a clerk's thumb could read; the far-off hand that would one day lay the district's private keepings together was still far off and was not his.

Behind the household, up \place{the clay road}, the year's arithmetics stood where they had been put --- nine true subtractions locked in \person{Berr Clay}'s box with all three of Ash inside, the key gone up in a coat pocket and not coming back down, and behind them the one printed figure with no signature on the space between the figure and the price. Ten readings were ten and not one account, and the boy did not bring an eleventh arithmetic into a morning that had not earned one. The figure read was still not the figure paid, and the space between kept its own place with no hand entering it and no payer standing under it; yesterday's arithmetic stayed behind the household and did not cross as sign, lesson, mending, cause, payment or payer.

At the going of the morning light she carried him back up the nineteen steps at the same half-pace and set him on his bed under the roof and never in \place{the Debt Loft}, righted the fold at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face, let her hand rest the half-beat longer than the setting needed before it went away, and left the thread frozen and unwiped. She said nothing of the well or of what had been asked there, and that was not a refusal to tell him, for nobody under any roof tells a basket what a keeping is worth. The basket lay level and out of the draught, the crust two inches off with its hair-lifted corner, and the room came down again to its small sounds.

The file came out on \place{the towel} at the head of the bed as instruments come out on a towel when a case is done and before the next is called, and he went over it by the place of each thing: the honest answers on the sixth place, six hearings of \lore{the full note} standing beside and never among with the seventh place empty, the heard bell in its square beside the told bell of \lore{the Long Night}, the nine arithmetics behind one unsigned figure, the ninth record-first hand with a finger between it and the eighth, the nine stoppings with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end, the river's winter skin beside water over water, the three Ash directions apart, the promise ungiven and the loan uncalled. No tenth hand, tenth stopping, eleventh arithmetic, seventh hearing, sixth asking, fifth shaping or eighth opening was supplied to make a row come out even, for a finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged.

The hand outside the wool came up an inch toward the river's note under the finger's-width door, the way a hand goes up in a field it has learned not to disturb. It did not reach, and the inch was not a reach. He took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole on the note's own finishing under the winter skin, let the intake go as the note ended, and came straight down and laid itself back flat against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. The note had done before the hand came down, so the letting-go was neither early nor stayed, and the hand did not stay. He filed it as answer three hundred and fifteen: exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment, borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the arithmetics, the bell's gap, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the held breath at the sill, the two bites of iron, the dust in the fist-crease, the river's winter skin, the setting-down-wrong, the strip in her sleeve, the three Ash directions, the widow's mark nor his own kept gaps. The crust kept its hair-lifted corner and its empty place with no third; cold stopped at cheek, temple and wrist; through the finger's-width door the river went low over lower under its winter skin, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming. Three hundred and fifteen honest answers stood on the sixth place and not one was a doctrine. Count three hundred and seventy exact, and the Drawing one morning ahead and unbrought. Forward, not closed.
