## Scene 1 — The Tenth Figure
**Day and time:** Day 369, Stone 10, Tenthday, Y.H. 813; small hours under the roof, then the morning of the first Account-day and market. First `\daymark{369}{Stone 10, Tenthday, Y.H. 813}` and the chapter's only `\dropcap`.
**Place:** the Vail kiln-house and bed; down the nineteen steps to the Cross, the reading block under the moot-post, the moot-post's finger's width of shade, the pot board, the out-of-line landing below the north bridge.
**Present:** Corin/Dan (carried), Sella Vail, Berr Clay, Hessa Rale, the soft-cuffed clerk, the green-mouthed woman and the foundling as sightings only; Garl Vail in objects. No Ilva, no Tarn, no Jorra this morning, no collector on the clay road.

**Beats in order:**
1. Open mid-action from the previous close: the hand has just come down on answer 312 and lies flat against the fold as a tool put away; the file lies open on the towel with every gap left gap. The surgeon takes the body in the old order --- crust two inches off with its corner a hair off the wool, its empty place unnamed and no third; frozen thread unwiped, nobody ever given an instruction about it; cold stopping at cheek, temple and wrist-joint, afterdrop waiting past a warming nobody will perform --- while the infant wants the crust lifted, the thread wiped and a hand on the pot handle. Both true, the second louder, neither winning.
2. Sella goes to the hearth before the basket, tells the coals it is the thaw, turns them with the back of a broken pot, says *The house will keep it* where another woman would say I; instruction to the wool, the river wind and the fire, never to the basket. Carries him down at the half-pace from every open slate. No recap of the birthday; the year finished is a keeping nobody can be asked about.
3. At the Cross she sets the basket in the moot-post's finger's width of shade where no slate lay and stands half a pace off through the reading and the bell. Eight pigs go over the wet boards; figures are brought to a heap of turnips and left side by side without agreeing.
4. Berr comes up short off the clay road with the red wool cord folded square under his coat and the sweet green of tasted glaze at one mouth-corner, the smell of nothing at all under the hot clay. Large clean hands; thumb wiped twice before touching nothing; narrow slate held inward; a finger passed over the figure through his sleeve; eyes resting a breath longer than the writing requires. Reads *One in twelve, as the notice says* low and sends it twice over the basket --- once for the moot, once for the households that came late --- never looking into the basket. Calls the levy, denies it with *The levy is called. It is not called by me* and *your house*, never I; takes himself out of his own sentence as a surgeon draws a hand from a field before the dressing goes on. Says something low to the block about kindness being entered; the block answers nothing; goes off with the box locked and Stone's first true subtraction inside, believing he has done a kindness, no anger and no triumph in him.
5. Hessa takes the slate up the road before noon, edge-on to her forearm, writing to her sleeve, the small flat correction of the strap and the turn of the wrist first, her hand to the slate before her eyes to the crowd; the second strap let out, dried pale scurf of road-mud unbrushed on the shortened skirt; answers the woman who asks with *I don't read it. I carry it. There's a difference, and the road doesn't care which* and, to one who wants more, *Cross to Greybank. Reading goes up before noon. I've got a road to walk.* No figure comes off the slate.
6. Below the north bridge the soft-cuffed clerk stays set at the out-of-line landing, cuff grey from washing, hand to slate before eyes to stalls, for a measure nobody called; the market sells over him as water over a stone. Across the square a silent woman turns a cup a quarter and sets it back; at the market's edge a small shape in cast-offs catches the light on a green sliver and goes dull. Each a seventh with no eighth, not joined to Berr's green, to each other, to a loaf, a slate, a sale, a hearing, a cause or a romance; never Fenna, never Ulda in the narration.
7. The bell goes four, four and a gap with nobody crossing himself. The heard bell stays in its square beside the told bell of the Long Night. The boy's own count goes to **369 exact**, six months and 189 days told by nobody; the tenth arithmetic rides up the road in Berr's box behind the first nine and is a tenth reading, not a tenth account.
8. The hand outside the wool comes up an inch, takes a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, holds the interval whole on the note's own finishing under the morning, lets it go as the note ends and lays itself back on the fold --- answer **313**.

**Emotional turn:** from the private exactness of a year nobody names to the public arithmetic read twice over the basket; the turn is not outrage but the colder finding that ten readings are ten and not one account, that the figure read is still not the figure paid, that a doctrine is an account which has found a payer and none stands here. The surgeon's register reads Berr's self-removal as a hand withdrawn from a field before the dressing; the infant only registers the cold iron at his cheek and the level of the basket.

**Sensory anchors:** wet boards under the pigs; the moot-post's cold iron at his cheek; the finger's width of shade; hot clay and the sweet lead-glaze green at Berr's mouth; the smell of nothing at all; the twice-wiped thumb; Hessa's strap-correction and dried pale road-mud; the grey cuff at the landing; the green sliver catching and going dull; the bell's four, four and a gap; the river low over lower under winter ice through the finger's-width door.

**Threads advanced, all not paid:** T015/T041/T047 (one in twelve, figure twice over basket, true subtraction in locked box, self-removal with *your house*); T077 (cost sent to the pot and the weather, figure read not paid); T080 (Hessa's eighth record-first hand, slate unread, no tenth; soft-cuffed clerk on its own tray); T090 (uncalled measure); T078 (heard bell beside told); T050/T051 and T033/T034 (seventh green and seventh shape held as sightings, no eighth, no name); T061/T082 (answer 313, sixth place); T071/T072 (369 exact, six months and 189 days); T088 (fire's fifth unpaid 369 days, payer unnamed); T009/T013/T092 (Garl's name unspoken 369 days).

**Foreshadowing planted / paid off:** plants the tenth arithmetic toward the eleventh at Stone 20 (Ch 109) and the twelfth at Stone 30; advances Hessa toward the far-off day a carrier's hand must choose whether to read what it carries; advances the clerk's inward slate toward the far-off hand that lays the private records together. No thread opened, none paid, no witness, no tenth hand, no eighth green or shape, no seventh hearing.

**Exact last image:** the small hand outside the wool comes up an inch, takes its small intake on the bell's finishing under the morning, comes straight down and lays itself flat against the fold on answer **313** as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking; the basket stays in the moot-post's shade and the figure already stands twice over it with no signature on the space.

## Scene 2 — What the Reading Left
**Day and time:** Day 369, Stone 10; afternoon in the Clay Yard, the going of the light, the hearth and the bed, then the small hours at the very end of the day. No new dated heading.
**Place:** the Clay Yard and its seven walked-around places, the yard well, trough and flat stone, the drying racks and rack end, the kindling, the flagstones, the Vail wheel-shed, the damper; under the roof at the pot board, loaf shelf, bed, boards and towel.
**Present:** Corin/Dan, Sella Vail, Tarn, Jorra Vail; Garl Vail in objects. Walk-ons only: the Cold Acre hare-carrier, the cracked-jar man, the woman with kindling, two fence-menders. No Ilva, Berr, Hessa, runner, collector, green-mouthed woman or foundling; no ownerless voice.

**Beats in order:**
1. Sella carries him down at the half-pace, instruction to the wool, the river wind and the fire she will bank against their coming back, never to the basket. Goes wide around seven walked-around places with a look and a wide step and no number: the clean dark line at the Vail corner with the finer grey hairline beside it called the thaw, the space beneath the lowest rack with broken birth-slates entered by nobody, the round ring where the shallow clay pan stood, the yard well with stiff rope, skinned trough and silt-clouded plate of ice out of the path of feet, the field's empty place and the returning-line place where a hand went in on Seed 15 and did not come out. Widest at the returning-line place, by nothing measurable and no longer look; not gone around twice to make a figure of going around once. A line left where it is is still a line kept and not a lesson; a sign is a thing that points to a payer and no payer stands under that ground.
2. Three coverings stay weather entered nowhere with a finger between every pair: gone white frost off the well's north lip, skin on the trough, clouded plate on its flat stone. The river under the finger's-width door goes low over lower beneath its winter skin, found where put and not made again, not a fourth covering, not a sixth low thing, not a hearing and not spent on the reading or the Drawing two mornings off.
3. Dressed grain turned under wool with fingers going in and coming out without closing, no figure lifted and no handful; the empty fingers stand beside the one handful let fall at the rack end on Bone 22 as a keeping and not a second handful, for a second would be a habit and a third a way, and both want a payer and none stands. Hunts and repairs stay directions judged by the carrying arm and the weather: hare unweighed, cracked jar answered with the pot, the seventh year's cost answered by two fence-menders with an early and a late thaw, Tarn saying *Yes, house* to each and keeping the late because it puts off the paying, agreeing so thoroughly no argument finds a foot.
4. At the kindling the account stands in the old exactness: six in the worn thumb-mark where the house can check, the seventh at the lip beyond where it cannot, Garl's unforged collector sentence beside the seventh with no name on the man who turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on the debt. Tarn passes his hand over blade and sill and the hand goes away when the checking is done, no breath held at the top of the reach. *Six counted. The next I don't count* to the wood, in the tone of a man stating the weather. Checks the Ash 14 held breath and the Bone 26 thumb's resting, finds each where put and makes neither again, each on its own place beside the five held objects and the pot-crack resting with a finger between every pair --- not a sixth held object, not a seventh of anything, not a tenth stopping, not the calling of the loan, not a habit, way, sign, hearing, mending, cause, payment or rule. Ear-sentence to the word that would call the loan and stops as a remembered ninth stopping with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end; Seed 16 breath unspent, loan uncalled, chest tight since the dark, Ash in the knuckle creases the colour of the clean dark line, pale clip-ring catching and giving back the little light, stroke over the flagstones' shallow bright patch without the half-check.
5. Jorra comes through the finger's-width door off the wheel-shed with meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bent little finger and white in a fist-crease, not the bone-coloured slip of the wheel; three or more men unnamed gone low into the back room before first grey and no name given and no word that the room is cold. Dusty forearms and clean ones on one tray, parted as a surgeon parts clot from vessel, asking neither which slate broke nor whether it was a birth-slate while the broken ones lie under the lowest rack entered by nobody. *It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady* to the wheel; *You'll do* to a pot; the full note known three turns ahead withheld from any High Kiln master who would call it early and sing a pot before its time. When Sella has not finished telling him the fire is low his bent finger goes into the cold damper ahead of her sentence, takes the cold out of the fingertip, moves the tongue a hair's breadth and is left standing so the end of her instruction arrives at the pot and the household straightens around the waiting as cloth straightens when a weight is lifted --- one iron with two bites, the holding and the taking, no Grathic word covering both mercies --- then taken out without leaving the extra holding of Ash 26 a second time. *He's not Vail* sealed; promise under the shed neither given nor withdrawn.
6. No voice over the boards; the Ash 5 joining, Ash 17 direction and Ash 23 direction stand where put and do not repeat and buy no lesson. The boy takes the dust, the two bites of iron and the unspent breath apart by what is there and what is not and files them on separate places, writing no cause across any.
7. Going of the light: she comes to the hearth before the basket, *The house will keep it*, coals told the thaw and turned with the broken pot's back; faint sour mineral tang of Ash along the skirting board, ridge unswept in the dry cold; her finger on top of the loaf shelf so the charcoal tally underneath stays unread; lips on a two-gapped row figuring neither gap; one glance at the clay jar under lead glaze and the hand shut half over nothing, the little-door burn closing on air; widow's mark oiled by a working thumb and untouched, because an afternoon, a going of the light and a bed are not openings. Twice-mended pot lifted with thumb first where the wire crosses the crack and the hairline showing between old-brass staple and tighter wire; both proverbs to the pot and the fire and never to a person; repair apart from restoration. Salt-stiff coat, hollow wheel-seat, clean dark line, unforged sentence, uncalled tile debt; his name unspoken 369 days, fire's fifth unpaid the same. Pot rim set down two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat and no look toward the bed --- the distance between intention and arrest, bought at nothing and not a gift.
8. Small hours: the surgeon takes a small clean satisfaction in a field taken as found and left as found, and it costs him nothing, which is how he knows it is not peace. Room's small sounds --- coal settling, clay ticking along the shelf, river low under its skin --- three counts in three places not becoming one, bell's gap staying in its square. File out on the towel: 312 honest answers plus answer 313 from the morning, six hearings with seventh place empty, nine arithmetics behind one figure, nine hands, nine stoppings, held objects each on its own place, river's skin beside water-over-water. Hand takes a small intake on the river's note, holds it whole on the note's own finishing, comes straight down and lays itself back on the fold --- answer **314**. Count 369 exact.

**Emotional turn:** the morning's public figure gives way to a household that keeps itself by not-doings; the turn is the boy's cold recognition that the tenth reading changed the district's arithmetic but bought nothing under this roof, and that the house's quiet is maintenance, not mercy and not a slight. The surgeon's clean satisfaction is exact and worth nothing; underneath, the infant's louder wanting keeps the scene from settling.

**Sensory anchors:** wet wool and woodsmoke; sour Ash in the skirting board; stiff well rope and skinned trough; the hairline between staple and wire; the little-door burn closing on air; the damper's cold iron; grey slate dust white in a fist-crease; the pale clip-ring; the river's muffled note; the pot rim two inches out; the half-beat at the setting.

**Threads advanced, all not paid:** T009/T013/T092 and T098 (Garl in objects, seven places, returning-line widest, name unspoken 369 days); T083/T085 and T078 (three coverings apart, river's skin found where put and not spent, bell's gap in its square); T060/T086 and T077 (body inventory, surgeon/infant quarrel, grain unfigured, no handful, hunts and repairs as directions); T058/T079/T100 (hearth grammar, charcoal unread, lips figured neither gap, widow's mark untouched, no eighth opening made due); T036/T076/T006 (six and seventh, checked keepings, ninth stopping with no tenth, loan uncalled); T062/T035/T031 (dust, the damper's two bites, Ash 26 not repeated, promise ungiven); T061/T082 (answer 314; 314 honest answers on the sixth place); T071/T072 (369 exact); T088 (fire's fifth unpaid 369 days); T035/T031/T065/T095 and T101 held (no voice, three directions apart).

**Foreshadowing planted / paid off:** Jorra's ungiven promise gathers interest against the burned slate and the three Ash directions, still neither paid nor called; Tarn's ear held valuable and unsafe with the loan uncalled; the held changes stay on their own places and are not allowed to stand up as a rule. Nothing opened, joined, named, called, paid or ended.

**Exact last image:** the hand outside the wool comes straight down and lays itself flat against the fold on answer **314**, the file open on the towel with every gap left gap; cold stopped at cheek, temple and wrist; the crust's empty place still unnamed and no third; through the finger's-width door the river low over lower under its winter skin, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming; count 369 exact and the Drawing two mornings off.
