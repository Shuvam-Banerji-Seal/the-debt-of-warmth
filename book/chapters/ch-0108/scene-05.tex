\daymark{377}{Stone 18, Eighthday, Y.H. 813}

The hand outside the wool lay flat on the fold where it had been laid at the close of the day before, and the count stood at three hundred and seventy-six exact. \person[Corin]{The boy} did not lift it by an act of will; attention goes where the field is, and his field was two inches of air above a barley crust and the small sounds of a house coming awake in its own order. He lay on \place{the bed} at the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf} under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill}, \place{Grath}, and this eighteenth day of \place{Stone} carried a date no roof under that sky would speak aloud. Three hundred and seventy-six days, six months and one hundred and ninety-six days, told by nobody who would have known what a number counted. \lore{The carried fire}'s fifth stood unpaid the same number with its payer unnamed, and \person{Garl Vail}'s name had not been spoken under that roof the same number. The file had come out on \place{the towel} at the head of the bed as instruments come out when a case is done and before the next is called, and he went over it by the place of each thing with every gap left gap.

Nothing from the sixteenth or the seventeenth crossed into this morning as a sign, a lesson, a mending, a cause, a payment or a payer. A night is a thing that happens to a house and not a hand that writes in it.

He took the body in the old order, because a field is checked in a sequence and a surgeon trusts a sequence more than an impression. First the crust: one stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust two inches from the ear, gone harder and browner in the dry kiln heat, its corner standing a hair off the wool. It had stood two inches off for three hundred and seventy-six days and would stand two inches off on this one; the distance was not a wish and had no giver. Beside it the wool lay folded once in the empty place, pale in the dry light, exactly as a hand had left it on the fifteenth, and found where put now for the third morning. Then the cold. It had come cheek, temple, the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, and it had stopped there as though it had reached the end of a line written out for it with neither an order to go farther nor an order to go away. \lore{The Afterdrop} waited beyond all three, past a warming nobody in this house would perform. The thread of drool at his lip had frozen in the dry kiln heat and was unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread and a basket set down is a pot set down and left.

He knew what the house had done without being asked and could not ask for it. A basket set level out of the draught where the heat came sideways was the careful rewarming he would have chosen for a patient who could not shiver. A cold patient cannot clot; he will seem better before he is worse; he warms at the extremities before the core, which is the wrong order and the dangerous one, and pink returning to a face is the surface still cold and not the core. The order in which heat comes back is the reverse of the order in which it goes, and it comes sooner than anybody likes. All of it was true and all of it was useless here, for the body in question was his own and the hands were the hands of an infant.

The infant wanted the crust lifted two inches, the thread wiped, and a hand on the pot handle. Both registers were true. The second was louder. Neither won, and the quarrel between them was the work; he had stopped expecting it to end, which was not the same as being used to it.

\scenebreak

The test came with the light, as it had come on the two mornings before. The wool was folded once in the empty place beside the crust, changed by nobody in the third night. He took the interval apart by what was there and by what was not. There was no second fold, no third crust, no name spoken over the place, no gift (a gift wants a giver and nobody gave), no payment, no payer. No hand under this roof had laid the keeping across the shelf's two gaps in the room whose floor does not touch this one, or across the gone pan's round ring at \place{the rack end}, or across the field's empty place out on \place{the worked strips}. He kept a finger between it and every other kept thing — between it and the pot-crack resting of \place{Ash} 9, between it and the hand-at-the-sill breath of Ash 14, between it and the holding \person[Jorra Vail]{Jorra} left in the cold \place{the damper} — so that no row came out even by his doing.

A small fear had come with the first finding, that a second finding might make a habit; a small fear had come with the second, that a third might make a way. Neither stood up now. A hand that finds is not a hand that places, and a habit wants a hand that places twice while a way wants a third placing, and both want a payer where none stands. The wool had been put down once, by a hand that did not look at his face and said nothing over the place, and it had been found three times by a hand that only read it. Finding is the trade of a surgeon and placing is the trade of a payer, and no payer stood here. A second placing would make a habit and a third a way; if these three findings were three keepings a fourth would be due, and none had come. Three findings without a placing leave the keeping gathered once and not made again, sitting on a place of its own beside the crust's gap, gathering interest against the far-off hand that is not his and has not come. He made no fourth likeness into a cause and no name for the place.

Under \place{the boards} the four nails kept their count and the thumb-width knot kept the place where a fifth might have been driven and was not; under the floor the carried fire gave four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer without a name three hundred and seventy-seven days. Three counts in three places, and none rose into another, because agreeing any two would have wanted a payer put under both and no payer stood.

\scenebreak

\person{Sella Vail} came to \place{the hearth} before she came to the basket, as she had at both ends of every day for three years. She told the coals it was \lore{the thaw} and turned them with the back of a broken pot. “The house will keep it,” she said where another woman would have said I. She stood a moment counting a small intake, a hold and a letting-go toward no opening, and gave neither gap a figure, so that the count went into the room and not onto a slate. Her finger lay along the top of the loaf shelf's front edge so the charcoal tally underneath stayed unread; her lips moved along a two-gapped row and figured neither gap; she glanced once at the clay jar standing cold under \lore{lead glaze} and shut her hand half over nothing, the little-door burn on the back of her left hand closing on air. The cord of her \lore{widow's mark} was oiled where a working thumb had touched it ten thousand times, and the mark hung where it hung, untouched, because a morning and a hearth are not openings and seven openings in seven months are seven and not a rule and no eighth was made due.

On \place{the pot board} the twice-mended pot kept the hairline of the original break showing between the old-brass staple and the tighter wire. Her thumb went first where the wire crossed the crack before the weight came on the handle; both proverbs went to the pot and the fire and never to a person — “Leave the crack showing. People trust a thing they can see has been handled,” and “If it's worth mending it's worth mending twice. The second time is the real one.” They were improved in the keeping and very likely not as good as the man had said them. Repair stayed apart from restoration: a restored thing hides the hand that handled it and a mended thing leaves that hand showing. She turned the dressed grain under wool with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child and lifted no figure and no handful, and set the pot rim two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat and no look toward the bed, the half-beat the distance between intention and arrest, bought at nothing and not a gift.

The wider file was checked without standing up, as a man checks a field by its small sounds before he lets the light into it. Six hearings of \lore{the full note} stood beside and never among, the sixth the small flat knock out of \person[Dessa Silt] as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go and never among the sources; the seventh place stayed empty and was not dug for or filled, and the flat knock of \lore[the Hearth Drawing]{the Drawing} stood on its own place outside them. The heard bell of \lore{the Long Night} stayed in its square beside the told, four, four and a gap, and was not folded into any of them. Nine record-first hands kept no tenth — \person{Hessa Rale}'s eighth, \person{Ilva}'s ninth and the collector's ordinary hand on its own tray joined to none — and nine stoppings kept no tenth before the seventh year at its very end. Ten arithmetics stood behind one unsigned figure — the three of \place{Frost}, the first, second and third of \place{Bone}, Ash's first, second and third and Stone's first — the figure read still not the figure paid, no signature on the space and no doctrine out of ten; their ten true subtractions rode in \person{Berr Clay}'s box up \place{the clay road} locked toward \place{High Kiln}, unread under this roof, the key gone up in a coat pocket and not coming back down. Four rooms kept the same gap in their count held apart with a finger between every pair, four likenesses and not one account, an account wanting a hand to lay them together and a payer under all four, and no such hand standing and no such payer.

No sixth hearing, seventh opening, sixth held object, tenth hand, tenth stopping, eleventh arithmetic or eighth opening was supplied to make a row come out even, for a finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged. The burned slate stood at five, the shut-room tellings at two, the row at five askings with no witness and the well-saying at four shapings, and none was made a sixth of on a day that had not earned one. The finding that ten are not one account sat on a place of its own with a finger between it and every other kept thing, and was not a grievance, a sign, a power or a lesson. The keeping at the fold sat beside all of these and was not laid across any of them.

\scenebreak

Through \place{the finger's-width door} \place{the river} kept its note of \lore{water over water}, a low thing over a lower thing, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming; over it from bank to bank lay \lore{the winter skin} that the cold had put on and that the cold would take off, found where put and not made again, not spent on this morning's work and not made to buy a lesson under any roof. He kept the covering on its own place beside the note and put a finger between the covering and the note it muffled, so that no row came out even by his doing.

In the small hours the file came out on the towel at the head of the bed as instruments come out when a case is done, and the hand outside the wool came up an inch toward the low note under the door as a hand goes up in a field it has learned not to disturb. It did not reach, and the inch was not a reach. He took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole on the note's own finishing under the winter skin, let the intake go as the note ended and came straight down and laid itself flat against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. The note had done before the hand came down, so the letting-go was neither early nor stayed, and the hand did not stay.

He filed it as answer three hundred and twenty-nine: exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the ten readings, the bell's gap, the flat knock, the child's grey fingertips, the mother's cup, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the held breath, the two bites of iron, the river's skin, the widow's mark nor the keeping at the fold. Three hundred and twenty-nine honest answers stood on the sixth place and not one was a doctrine, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood. The surgeon took his small clean satisfaction in a field taken as found and left as found, and it cost him nothing, which was how he knew it was not peace. The infant wanted the crust lifted two inches, the thread wiped and a hand on the pot handle. Both were true. The second was louder. Neither won, and the quarrel was the work.

The hand outside the wool had come up an inch and not reached, and came straight down again. The wool lay folded once in the empty place beside the crust gone harder and browner in the dry cold, its corner a hair off the wool; no second fold, no third crust and no name spoken over the place. The thread stood frozen and unwiped. The hand lay flat against the fold on answer three hundred and twenty-nine, and the count stood at three hundred and seventy-seven exact, six months and one hundred and ninety-seven days told by nobody who would have known what a number counted. Stone kept its eighteenth day without a boundary drawn, the deep-winter month held both its names, and nothing under the roof stood up to call the day an account.
