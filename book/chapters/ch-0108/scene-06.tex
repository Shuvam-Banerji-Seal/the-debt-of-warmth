The afternoon came on without a boundary drawn, the dry cold settling into \place{the Clay Yard} the way a lid settles on a pot, and nothing under it stood up to say what hour it was. \person{Sella Vail} carried \person[Corin]{the boy} down \place{the nineteen steps} at the half-pace she keeps from every open slate, the basket level as a pot that must not be slopped, the instruction going before her into the wool, the river wind and the fire she would bank against their coming back and never into the basket. “Keep it off his ears. It's the river wind,” she said to the wool, and when they came under the wall she told the fire it was the thaw. Three hundred and seventy-seven days stood exact, six months and one hundred and ninety-seven days, and the eighteenth of \place{Stone} went about its work in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill}, \place{Grath}, under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof and out in the yard. No roof under that sky would speak the date aloud. The month carried both its names the way a pot carries two handles and is none the worse, older women by weather and slatemen by Account-days, neither set right nor left in so many words. \lore{The carried fire}'s fifth stood unpaid the same number of days with its payer unnamed, and \person{Garl Vail}'s name had not been spoken under that roof the same number. Nothing from the morning crossed into the afternoon as a sign, a lesson, a mending, a cause, a payment or a payer. A morning is a thing that happens to a house and not a hand that writes in it.

The yard went around \place{the seven walked-around places} with a look and a wide step and no number. The look was not a counting and the step was not a measurement, and neither stood up and called itself an account. She went widest around \place{the returning-line place}, where a hand had gone in on Seed 15 and had not come out, wider by nothing that could be measured; the basket tipped a hair against her forearm at the wide part of the step and came level again before the wool knew it had moved. She went around the place once and not twice, because a second going-around would have made a figure of once, and a figure wants a payer standing under it and none stood. At \place{the Vail corner} the clean dark line kept its finer grey hairline beside it, the thing the house called \lore{the thaw}, unswept and thickened in the month's dry cold, with the faint sour tang of \lore{Ash} coming up off it where it had not been swept in two years. Under \place{the lowest rack} the broken birth-slates lay where they lay and were entered by nobody. At \place{the rack end} the round ring where the shallow pan had once stood kept no scrape, no crumb, no print, no chip and no word.

The three coverings stayed weather entered nowhere, with a finger between every pair. The gone white frost off the north lip of \place{the yard well}, the skin drawn edge to edge across \place{the trough}, and the silt-clouded plate of ice on \place{the flat stone by the well} a little out of the path of feet were not a fourth covering laid over the three and not a sixth low thing set beside the five held objects, not spent on the afternoon's work and not made to buy a lesson under any roof. Through \place{the finger's-width door} \place{the river} kept its note of \lore{water over water}, a low thing over a lower thing, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming; over it from bank to bank lay \lore{the winter skin} that the cold had put on and that the cold would take off, found where put beside the note and on its own place, not spent on this afternoon or on the going of the light and not made again. He kept the covering on its own place and put a finger between the covering and the note it muffled, so that no row came out even by his doing. A line left where it is is still a line kept; a sign is a thing that points to a payer, and no payer stood under that ground. The boy took the yard as a field taken as found and left as found. It cost him nothing, which was how he knew it was not peace.

At \place{the kindling} the account stood where it had been left. Six pieces lay in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check them and the seventh lay at the lip beyond it where the house could not, and beside the seventh lay \person{Garl Vail}'s unforged collector sentence about the man who turned back at the frozen ford, with no name on the man and no figure on the debt. The small knife lay at the very edge of its sill, where it could be checked and where a hand could also slip it off. \person{Tarn} passed his hand over blade and sill as a man passes a hand over a tool put back where it belongs, and the hand went away when the checking was done and no breath stayed at the top of the reach. “Six counted. The next I don't count,” he told the wood, in the tone of a man stating the weather, and cut nothing, counted nothing and spent nothing. The pale clip-ring at his left wrist caught what little light there was and gave it back. His chest had been tight since the dark; the Ash was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line; his stroke went over the shallow bright patch of \place{the flagstones} without the half-check.

The Ash 14 held breath and the Bone 26 thumb's resting were where they had been put, found this afternoon and not made again. They lay beside the five held objects and the pot-crack resting with a finger between every pair: not a sixth held object, not a seventh, not a tenth stopping, not the calling of the loan, with no payer standing under any of them. He brought the ear-sentence up to the word that would call the loan and stopped there, as a remembered ninth stopping with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end. The Seed 16 breath stayed unspent at the top of the reach and the loan stayed uncalled, and he did not look along the road. Two ears, two silences, a loan that can hear and has never been called, with nobody standing to enter it. The boy took the equality as he took the rest, as a field taken as found and left as found, and made no fifth shaping of it and brought no word of it under the roof. Garl's name had gone unspoken under that roof three hundred and seventy-seven days and the carried fire's fifth had gone unpaid the same number with its payer unnamed, and the two counts stood side by side and did not become one.

\person{Jorra Vail} came through the finger's-width door off \place{the Vail wheel-shed} with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bent little finger and white in a fist-crease where a hand had opened, and it was not the bone-coloured \lore{slip} of the wheel. Three or more men unnamed had gone low into \place{the back room} before first grey; he gave the house no name for them and no word that the room was cold. He kept his dusty forearms and his clean ones on one tray and parted them as clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had been broken nor whether it had been a birth-slate, while the broken ones lay under the lowest rack and were entered by nobody. He spoke to the wheel before he spoke to any person. “It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady.” To a pot he said, “You'll do,” with no welcome in the voice and no unkindness either in it, knowing the \lore{full note} that pot would give three turns before it left his hands, with air under it and a want in it, and keeping the knowing where no master come up from \place{High Kiln} could call it early and sing a pot before its time.

Sella had not finished telling him the fire was low when his bent finger went into the cold iron of \place{the damper} ahead of her sentence, took the cold out of the fingertip, moved the tongue a hair's breadth and was left standing there, so that the end of her instruction arrived at the pot and the household straightened around the waiting as cloth straightens when a weight is lifted. One piece of iron had two bites, the holding and the taking, and there was no \lore{Grathic} word covering both mercies. He brought the finger out without leaving the extra holding of Ash 26 a second time; that holding had been gathered once and stayed where nobody had entered it, for a second would make a habit and a third a way and both want a payer where none stood. \emph{He's not Vail} stayed sealed as a lid nobody had tried to lift in three years, and the promise of the boy's name to the men who burn slates was neither given nor withdrawn, gathering interest against the burned slate at five and the three Ash directions, which stood where they were put and did not repeat and bought no lesson on this afternoon of all afternoons. The boy filed the two silences on separate places with a finger between, and joined the wheel-shed to nothing under the roof.

At the going of the light she carried him up the nineteen steps under the roof and never into \place{the Debt Loft}, and the instruction went before her to the wool, the pot and the fire she would bank against their coming back and not to the basket. She set him on \place{the bed} at the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf}, put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face, and did not tuck a second fold into the empty place. Her hand did only what the setting needed and went away; the little-door burn on the back of her left hand closed on air; the thread at his mouth was left frozen and unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread; and she said nothing of where they had been.

He took the keeping apart by what was there and by what was not. The wool lay folded once in the crust's empty place exactly where a hand had put it on the fifteenth, found where put on three mornings and at three goings of the light and changed by nobody in between. There was no second fold, no third crust, no name spoken over the place, no gift (a gift wants a giver and nobody gave), no payment, no payer, not a joining of rooms, not a sixth held object, not a habit, a way, a sign, a hearing, a mending, a cause or a rule. He filed it on a place of its own beside the crust's gap, the shelf's two gaps in the room whose floor does not touch this one, the gone pan's round ring at the rack end and the field's empty place out on \place{the worked strips}, with a finger between it and every other. It had been gathered once and not made again. A second would have made a habit and a third a way, and both want a payer where none stands; finding a thing is not placing it, and the three findings from three hands that only read had not added a fourth placing to the first.

The far-off finger that would one day lay shelf and fold together and call the joining an account was still far off and was not his; it had not come. The account wants a hand to lay the places together and a payer under all of them, and no hand stood and no payer stood. What the keeping kept was its own place. It gathered interest against that hand without being paid, promoted into nothing, named to nobody, and it did not stand up and say what it was worth. He had stopped expecting the quarrel underneath it to end. The infant wanted the crust lifted two inches, the thread wiped and a hand on the pot handle. Both registers were true. The second was louder. Neither won, and the quarrel was the work.

Under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof in Low Kiln, Sill, Grath, the hearth grammar was renewed at the going of the light as it was renewed at both ends of every day for three years. \person{Sella Vail} came to \place{the hearth} before she came to the basket. “It's \lore{the thaw},” she told the coals, and turned them with the back of a broken pot. “The house will keep it,” she said where another woman would have said I. She stood a moment counting a small intake, a hold and a letting-go toward no opening, and gave neither gap a figure, so that the count went into the room and not onto a slate. Her finger lay along the top of the loaf shelf's front edge so the charcoal tally underneath stayed unread; her lips moved along a two-gapped row and figured neither gap; she glanced once at the clay jar standing cold under \lore{lead glaze} and shut her hand half over nothing, the little-door burn closing on air. The cord of her \lore{widow's mark} was oiled where a working thumb had touched it ten thousand times, and the mark hung where it hung, untouched, because a going of the light and a hearth are not openings and seven openings in seven months are seven and not a rule and no eighth was made due.

On \place{the pot board} the twice-mended pot kept the hairline of the original break showing between the old-brass staple and the tighter wire. Her thumb went first where the wire crossed the crack before the weight came on the handle; both proverbs went to the pot and the fire and never to a person — “Leave the crack showing. People trust a thing they can see has been handled,” and “If it's worth mending it's worth mending twice. The second time is the real one.” They were improved in the keeping and very likely not as good as the man had said them. Repair stayed apart from restoration: a restored thing hides the hand that handled it and a mended thing leaves that hand showing. She turned the dressed grain under wool with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child and lifted no figure and no handful, and set the pot rim two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat and no look toward the bed, the half-beat the distance between intention and arrest, bought at nothing and not a gift.

Under the floor the carried fire gave four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer without a name three hundred and seventy-seven days, and nobody lifted the floor to supply the pull; overhead \place{the boards} kept four nails on the near one, four on the next and a thumb-width knot where a fifth might have been driven and was not. Three counts in three places, and none rose into another, because agreeing any two would have wanted a payer put under both and no payer stood.

The hand outside the wool came up an inch toward the low note under the finger's-width door as a hand goes up in a field it has learned not to disturb. It did not reach, and the inch was not a reach. He took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole on the note's own finishing under the winter skin, let the intake go as the note ended and came straight down and laid itself flat against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. The note had done before the hand came down, so the letting-go was neither early nor stayed, and the hand did not stay.

He filed it as answer three hundred and thirty: exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the ten readings, the bell's gap, the flat knock, the child's grey fingertips, the mother's cup, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the held breath, the two bites of iron, the river's skin, the widow's mark, the keeping at the fold nor his own kept gaps. The surgeon took his small clean satisfaction in a field taken as found and left as found, and it cost him nothing, which was how he knew it was not peace.
