## Chronicle

For three days in deep winter's middle the house under the Vail roof went about its tending as a surgeon goes about a field taken as found and left as found, and nothing crossed under it as sign, lesson, mending, cause or payment. The yard went around its seven walked-around places with a look and a wide step and no number, widest around the returning line. Sella Vail came to the coals before she came to the basket, told them it was the thaw, turned them with the back of a broken pot, and said *The house will keep it* where another woman would have said I. She gave both proverbs to the pot and the fire, never to a person; the dressed grain lay turned under wool with no figure and no handful, the charcoal stayed unread, and the widow's mark hung untouched, for a morning is not an opening.

On the sixteenth Ilva drew at the well with the rope stiff in the cold; her hand went to her sleeve before her eyes to the asker, and she answered the question put with *Ask the pot what it cost. I keep the count, not the account*, the notched birch strip unread in its fold. On the seventeenth Hessa Rale went up the clay road with Berr Clay's slate held edge-on to her forearm and the writing turned toward her sleeve, the second strap let out; she settled the strap and turned her wrist before her eyes went to the yard, told the woman she carried a slate and did not read it, and gave no figure. At the kindling Tarn kept six where the house could check and the seventh at the lip, brought his ear-sentence to the calling word and stopped, and did not look along the road. At the wheel-shed Jorra parted dusty forearms from clean as clot from vessel and took his bent finger from the cold damper without a second holding.

The keeping at the fold was tested, and it held. The wool Sella had tucked into the crust's empty place on the fifteenth lay folded once there on three mornings and at three goings of the light, changed by nobody, with no second fold, third crust or name. A hand that finds is not a hand that places: a second keeping would be a habit and a third a way, and both want a payer where none stands. He filed it beside the crust's gap, the shelf's two gaps, the gone pan's ring and the field's empty place, and made no row. Six answers went down in the small hours, the three hundred and twenty-fifth to the three hundred and thirtieth, each worth nothing at two inches of air. Three days went exact and told by nobody, six months and one hundred and ninety-seven days, the fire's fifth unpaid and Garl's name unspoken the same number. Nothing under the roof called the keeping an account, and the count went on.
