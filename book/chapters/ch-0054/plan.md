# Beat sheet — Chapter 54, *The Pot Not Heard From*

Days 213–215, Grain 4–6, Fourthday to Sixthday, Y.H. 813. Normal chapter, three lived days, two scenes per day, six scenes, about 17,700 words. Each day opens with its own `\daymark` in its first scene only; the second scene of a day has no new dated heading. Point of view never leaves Daniel Marchetti (Corin). The old life stays offstage: no present-tense Chicago break, no `\memory{}` tag. The one meaningful change is a saying that turns by a hair — the pot that sang early last month has not been heard from since — gathered and **not paid**. Everything else advances without being spent.

## Scene 1 — The Count Goes Forward

- **Day and time:** Day 213, Grain 4, Fourthday. Small hours under the roof, then before first grey and into the morning.
- **Place:** The bed at the loaf shelf's low angle; the hearth; the loaf shelf; the edge of the Clay Yard; the Vail threshold.
- **Present:** Corin (alone in the small hours), Sella Vail (fire, shelf, door), Tarn and Jorra passing at the morning work, one neighbour at the door (walk-on). Garl held in objects.
- **Only drop cap of the chapter; first `\daymark{213}{Grain 4, Y.H. 813 (Fourthday)}`.**

**Beats, in order**

1. The hand lies down against the fold after the two-rhythm holding of Day 212. Do not recall that holding as a sign, growth or reaching: "a miss was a miss" in chapter 53; here "a holding was a holding" and is set down like a tool. The boy is two hundred and thirteen days old and six months and thirty-three days, told by nobody (T071/T072).
2. The day arrives first as smell, then as a gap in sound: hot clay under tallow, wet wool under that, the sour Ash breath of the unswept corner the house calls the thaw; the kiln drawing in fours with a gap, not a bell. The carried fire gives four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have been, payer unnamed for two hundred and thirteen days (T088). Through the finger's-width door the river keeps water over water; overhead the boards keep four nails, four and a thumb-width knot. Three counts in three places that do not become one, because agreeing them would want a payer and none stands to any (T061/T082).
3. The body: one leathery brown-edged crust two inches from the ear, empty place unnamed, no third; frozen drool thread unwiped; cold stopped at cheek, temple, bare wrist-joint where the wool rides back, afterdrop waiting past a warming nobody will perform. The surgeon's register takes the reading and buys nothing at two paces.
4. The file is turned out as instruments on a towel: six hearings beside and never among, sixth out of a person, no seventh; three near-full answers advanced and unpaid; told bell beside heard; told figure beside paid with Ember's third reading locked up the road and answering nothing for the man carried out on Day 188; nine stoppings with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end; three held changes on three places, loft scrape on a fourth, pale line on a fifth, held inch on a sixth; burned-slate word told twice (Days 205, 208) on its own place beside Jorra's dust and the two shut-room tellings with a hand between the three (T061/T082, T036/T076, T006, T035/T031/T065/T095). An empty place kept empty is a finding; a finding filled to make a row even is forged.
5. The morning asks the hand: up an inch toward the note, **down at once** against the fold. A miss, no failure in it. Filed on the sixth place, laid across nothing, no payer named (T061/T082).
6. Sella comes to the fire before the basket. Instruction to wool, fire, pot, broom, running water, river wind — never the basket. "The house." Half-pace from every open slate; glance at the clay jar cold under its glaze and hand shut half over nothing without a touch; lips along a two-gapped row with no figure; finger on top of the shelf's front edge so the charcoal stays unread; pot rim set out with handle two inches beyond the reaching hand, a half-beat longer at the setting, no look toward the bed; widow's mark untouched — no opening in thirty-three days (T058/T079, T100).
7. A neighbour's cost-question at the door goes to the pot and the weather, goes away level, nothing priced, refused or entered (T077). The yard goes around the clean dark line, the finer grey called thaw, the rack space with broken birth-slates, the pan's gone round ring, the bare dark trough whose water is called weather, the field's empty place — each with a look and a wide step and no number (T009/T013/T092/T098).
8. No market, no Account-day: Grain 10 (Day 219) held, with the seventh green and the seventh sighting owed and not come early.

- **Emotional turn:** From a night that gave a coordination (which must not be made into a promise) into a morning that asks and refuses. The old man's exactness and the infant's refusal occupy the same inch; neither buys the other anything.
- **Sensory anchors:** Hot clay under tallow; wet wool; sour Ash breath called thaw; kiln in fours with a gap; river water over water through a finger's-width door; stiff crust two inches off; frozen thread; grey streak pale off the coals; the walnut-dark shelf and the neat charcoal hidden under its front edge.

## Scene 3 — The Saying Changes by a Hair

- **Day and time:** Day 214, Grain 5, Fifthday. Small hours, then before first grey and into the morning and midday.
- **Place:** Bed; hearth; loaf shelf; Clay Yard; Vail wheel-shed edge; yard well.
- **Present:** Corin, Sella, Tarn, Jorra; a kiln-hand come up on a wrong wind (walk-on); a rack woman at the well and a man off the clay road with a number in his fist (walk-ons).
- **Second `\daymark{214}{Grain 5, Y.H. 813 (Fifthday)}`.**

**Beats, in order**

1. New day, no recap. Count 214, six months and thirty-four days, nobody told (T071/T072); fire's fifth unpaid 214 days (T088); fire, river, boards three counts in three places (T061/T082). First smell hot clay under tallow; first sound the kiln in fours with a gap. Grain's first working week has no market and no Account-day until Day 219.
2. **The chapter's one meaningful change:** a kiln-hand come up on a wrong wind says the pot that sang early last month has not been heard from since. The saying turns by a hair from "mostly sings itself out early" to "has not been heard from since." The boy takes it apart as a surgeon takes apart a wound: a direction with no hand at the foot of it, no owner, no room, no cause, no figure. The sweet edge of lead glaze rides up off High Kiln on the wrong wind and goes out on it — a direction, not a second edge paid (T064).
3. Jorra, who knows the note that pot would give three turns before it left his hands and holds the near-full answer with air under it and a want in it, lets the saying stand as a saying. He gives no name to what ailed the man carried out on Day 188 and makes no lesson of the green on the wind. No pot is lost. The knowing goes to the near-full tray beside the hearings and never among them; no seventh hearing comes, no cause stands up, nothing is spent (T061/T082 advanced and unpaid, T035/T031).
4. At the yard well a rack woman with slip dried pale in her wrists asks what the month's pots will want; a man off the clay road keeps a number shut in his fist. 5. Sella's instruction grammar, the house, half-pace, jar-glance and hand half-shut, lips along the two-gapped row, finger above the charcoal, pot handle two inches beyond the hand with a half-beat and no look toward the bed; widow's mark untouched, no opening in thirty-four days (T058/T079, T100).
6. Morning hand: up an inch, **down before the counted breath has finished** — an honest miss filed on its sixth place and laid across nothing (T061/T082).

- **Emotional turn:** A saying changes by a hair and becomes a question nobody asks. The district's arithmetic stays in loaves, glances and the weather, while a pot's silence gathers interest on a tray nobody can read.
- **Sensory anchors:** Wrong-wind sweetness at the back of the throat; meal-fine slate dust white in a knuckle; the nearly-full note with air under it; bare dark trough and its water called weather; a number closing in a fist; the grey streak pale off the coals.

- **Foreshadowing planted / held:** The pot that sang early and is now not heard from — a small non-fatal edge gathering toward the Broken Slates and Jorra's withheld knowledge; do not join it to the carried man on Day 188 or name what ailed him; no seventh green, no seventh sighting before Grain 10.
- **Exact last image:** The wrong wind going out of the yard with the sweet edge of green behind it, and the saying standing where it had been set down — no pot lost, no lesson made, no name given.

## Pull into Chapter 55

The final fraction is held and let go, not spent and not explained; nothing stands up out of it as a power, a rule or a payer. The wrong-wind saying about the pot not heard from has gathered one more telling without a cause, the burned-slate word has come over the boards a third time and arrived nowhere, Tarn's ninth stopping still stands with the seventh year at its very end and the Seed 16 breath unspent, and the widow's mark has not been touched since before the kiln on Ember 1. Two hundred and fifteen goes forward exact and unspent into Day 216, Grain 7, Seventhday, the count carried by the only person who would have known what it counted and spoken by nobody. Chapter 55 opens on that ordinary working day of the second tend of Grain with no market and no Account-day, every long thread still open, the seventh green and the seventh sighting held for Grain 10 on Day 219, and the hand asked again as the body answers — forward, not closed.
