## Chronicle

Three days went over the Vail roof in the first working week of Grain, and the house kept them in loaves, glances and the space where a thing had stood. The boy at the low angle of the loaf shelf was two hundred and thirteen days old on the fourth of the month and two hundred and fifteen on the sixth, and nobody said either number, and no slate in Sarn had a place to enter them. His hand went up an inch toward the river's note and came down: at once on the fourth morning, before the counted breath on the fifth, at once again on the sixth under conditions that had sometimes answered. In the small hours it held for a small intake, kept the river's low interval with no count to hold it against, and on the last night stayed through the intake and hold of one of the widow's counted breaths, coming down on the letting go. Each fraction was filed and named no payer.

A kiln-hand come up from High Kiln on a wrong wind told the yard that the pot which had sung before its time last month had not been heard from since. The saying turned by a hair from a proverb into the report of one pot's silence, and the boy took it apart as a surgeon takes apart a wound: a direction with no hand at the foot of it, no owner, no room, no cause, no figure. He set it beside the nearly-full answers and never among the hearings. Jorra, who knew the note it would give three turns before it left his hands, let the saying stand; no pot was lost. The sweet edge of lead glaze came up off High Kiln and went out on the wind that brought it.

On the sixth morning the burned-slate word went over the boards a third time, from Slate Row on the breath of the barley price and the weather, with no owner, room, flame or reason. It was a third telling of the slate and still not a third telling of the counting room shut in the war. Jorra grew quieter and the household straightened around the quiet without an order. Cost-questions at the door and the well went to the pot and the weather and away level. Sella kept her back to the basket and her finger above the charcoal, set the pot handle two inches beyond the reaching hand, rested a half-beat longer at the setting and did not look toward the bed. Tarn brought the ear-sentence to the word that would call the loan and stopped. No collector came, no slate was opened, and the count went forward exact and unspent, spoken by nobody.

## Continuity

- **Exact final moment:** Small hours of Day 215, Grain 6, Sixthday. Corin on his bed at the low angle of the loaf shelf under the Vail roof, alone; Sella gone to her own bed before the small hours, the coals left to keep themselves, the two rooms not touching. The hand outside the wool went up an inch toward the river's note, was taken through the small intake and the hold of one of the breaths Sella counts at her fire, and came down on the letting go --- a coordination shorter than the whole breath of the night before, filed on the sixth place with nothing laid across it and no payer named. Thread of drool frozen and unwiped; one stiff brown-edged crust two inches from the ear, its empty place unnamed, no third. Fire in the room that warms him, river in the room he cannot enter, neither stopping for the ear. Forward, not closed.
- **Day and time at the end:** Day 215, Grain 6, Y.H. 813 (Sixthday), small hours. Count two hundred and fifteen exact, complete, unspent, spoken by nobody; six months and thirty-five days. Fire's fifth unpaid 215 days. Seventh year at its very end and still ungiven.
- **Where everyone is now:** Corin on his bed under the roof. Sella Vail in her own bed, coals left to keep themselves; she had carried him home at the going of the light at the half-pace and set him on his bed, not in the loft. Tarn at the kindling/sill earlier, knife at the very edge with six counted and the seventh at the lip, ear-sentence stopped, no collector on Grain 6. Jorra Vail at the wheel-shed with meal-fine dust, quiet going before him into the shed. Garl held in objects: salt coat, hollow seat, clean dark line and finer grey called the thaw unswept, proverb gone to the pot, tile debt neither paid nor settled nor named. Three or more unnamed men went low into the back room before first grey on all three mornings, given no name.
- **Items, money, powers:** Nothing gained, lost or used within reach; no money, no purchase, no sale, no loaf, no slate, no entry. Pot handle stays two inches beyond the hand. Powers: none usable, no rank change, no experiment; the hand's six answers (three days, morning and small hours) filed as objects, not powers. Seed 16 breath unspent; loan uncalled.

Widow's mark untouched, no opening in thirty-five days. *He's not Vail* sealed; promise of the boy's name neither given nor withdrawn. Sella's private roll unshown; Dessa's cost unentered. No collector called for Garl's tile debt or the carried man.
