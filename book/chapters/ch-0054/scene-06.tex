The afternoon thinned without ending under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof. Heat went off the high air first and settled into the low places, under \place{the loaf shelf}, behind the pot, into the fold where \person[Corin]{the boy} lay with the grey wool at his ear. It was the heat of the day finding the places that had held it, and no kindness done to anybody. One leathery brown-edged crust lay two inches from that ear and kept the empty place beside it unnamed, and no third came to stand there. The thread of drool at his mouth had frozen in the dry air and was unwiped. Nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread, and a basket set down was a pot set down and left.

Cold had come to cheek and temple and the small bare joint at the wrist where the wool rode back, and had stopped there as if it had reached the end of its own order. It went no further and it did not go away. The surgeon's register took the reading without being asked --- surface warm, core not, the order exact --- filed it with the others and asked the room what it was for. The room gave back hot clay under tallow, wet wool under that, and the sour mineral breath of the unswept corner the house called \lore{the thaw}. That was the whole answer. It was enough to live on and worth nothing at two paces.

The underside of the shelf made a roof that ran above him and stopped where his hand stopped, and he did not ask the roof to be longer. Through the finger's-width door the river kept its note of \lore{water over water}, a fifth low thing never counted among the hearings; under the floor \lore{the carried fire} gave four low pulls and withheld the fifth, and overhead the boards kept four nails on the near board, four on the next, and a thumb-width knot where the fifth nail should have stood. Three counts in three places. They lay in their three places and kept their own company, because a man who wanted them to agree would have had to put a payer under all three and no payer stood to any of them. The afterdrop waited on the far side of a warming nobody in this house would perform.

\scenebreak

\person{Sella Vail} came to the fire before she came to the basket, because that was the order of the house and had been the order since the first firing, and she kept it the way a widow keeps a wound clean: daily, without comment, with a hand that knew exactly how much pressure the thing would bear. She stood with her back to the basket at \place{the hearth} and moved her lips along the loaf row, and the row stood with two unnamed gaps --- the old space at the end and the palm's breadth that had come in on a night years back with no scrape, crumb, print or word --- and she gave nobody a figure. Her eye went once to the clay jar cold under its glaze on the wall shelf, and her hand started toward it and shut half over nothing without a touch, the little-door burn closing on air the way it closed on everything that came within reach of it. Her finger lay on the top of the shelf's front edge where any woman in the district could see it lying; the charcoal under the board stayed unread, uncounted and uncopied, and a woman's back turned was not the same thing as a finger beneath it. A hand that knows a board by heart and a hand that has been told not to look leave the same mark on a room, and the reason stood between the two, unspoken.

She told the fire it was the thaw and turned the coals with the back of a broken pot. The fire answered four low pulls and withheld the fifth, and the space where the fifth should have stood went on being a space with no payer named to it for two hundred and fifteen days. “The house will keep it,” she said, where another woman would have said \emph{I}, and stood a moment with the back of the pot in her hand counting under her breath toward no opening --- small intake, hold, letting go --- the count arriving nowhere as it had arrived nowhere every day of his life. She pushed a strand of coal-black hair back with the wrist that carried the burn, and the grey streak at her temple showed pale off the coals, and she did not discuss the streak or the winter it had come in.

“Don't set that near the door,” she told the pot, which had not moved and had no intention of moving. “You'll pay for it in the thaw.” The pot gave back nothing, which was the answer she had wanted of it. Every instruction in the room went to the wool, the fire, the pot, the broom, the running water and the river wind, and never to the basket. She set the pot rim out with its handle two inches beyond the reaching hand and let her hand rest a half-beat longer at the setting than the setting required, and did not look toward \place{the bed}. \lore{The widow's mark} hung on its cord at her throat where it had hung since the first firing, touched once before the kiln on Ember 1 and at no hour since. An afternoon is not an opening and a dusk is not an opening, and there had been no opening in thirty-five days. Her private roll stayed unshown, and \person[Dessa Silt]{Dessa}'s cost stayed where it had been put down and never entered.

\scenebreak

\person{Garl Vail} was kept to the sixth day without a story, the way the house kept most of what it would not set down in words. His salt-stiff coat held a broader man's shape on its peg behind the door with the crease at the left shoulder, and the seat in \place{the Vail wheel-shed} kept its twenty-year hollow and its lean to the left that nobody filled. Where the \lore{Ash} ridge had turned the corner there was a clean dark line, and beside it the new, finer hairline of grey the house called the thaw, and neither had been swept. His proverb had gone to a pot with no crack in it --- \emph{leave the crack showing; people trust a thing they can see has been handled} --- a saying about honest repairs said over a corner where the repair had been rubbed out, and it cost more now than it had when he was alive to say it in his own voice. The debt against a firing of tiles lay where it lay, neither paid nor settled nor named, circled without a word and worse for being uncalled. No lesson was made of any of it and no collector was called. His name had not been spoken under this roof for two hundred and fifteen days, and the keeping of him was maintenance, not a story. A line left where it is is still a line kept.

\scenebreak

At \place{the kindling} \person[Tarn Vail]{Tarn} took the broom only after the broom had been instructed. He came at the going of the light with his chest tight and grey Ash worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line, and no scrubbing lifted it; the pale clip-ring at his left wrist held what light there was and gave it back. He set the small knife at the very edge of its sill, six counted in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check and the seventh at the lip where it could not, and the unforged collector sentence that belonged to Garl lay beside it where it had lain for six years. “Six counted. The next I don't count,” he told the wood, and put the knife back where any man could see it was back. He said \emph{Yes, house} to the ones who wanted the thaw early and to the ones who wanted it late, agreed so entirely that no argument could find a foot to stand on, and went on wanting the late because a late thaw puts off the paying, though the paying had stood in the ruts since the water went.

The sentence about an ear under this roof being worth more than the debt that held it came to his teeth and went all the way to the word that would call the loan. There it stopped. It stopped as it had stopped before, as a remembered stopping and not a new one, with no figure and no name. He drew the breath he should have spent at the top of the reach and did not spend it. If the thing was paid at all, it was paid as an interval and not as a calling: a breath taken and held at the threshold, a word stopped short of the air, nothing entered and nobody called. Nine stoppings stood as nine objects with no tenth stood up before the seventh year, which now stood at its very end and had still not given; the Seed 16 breath stood where it should have gone in, unspent, a small interval at the top of every reach; the loan that could hear was not called.

His stroke went over \place{the flagstones} past the shallow bright patch without the half-check the stones remembered, and over the road a house sends a man down when it wants a collector, without once turning along it. No collector came up or went down \place{the clay road} on the sixth day of Grain. Above the shed the warm stone lay cold and unreconciled in its unmarked pot in \place{the Debt Loft}, and the small loft-sill scrape stayed on its fourth place with no word spoken over it and no payer named.

\scenebreak

\person[Jorra Vail]{Jorra} came through from the shed with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bent little finger and under the nails, white in the creases where a fist had opened, and not bone-coloured \lore{slip}. He kept his dusty forearms and his clean ones on one tray, parting dust from slip as a surgeon parts clot from vessel, and asked neither which slate had been broken nor whether it had been a birth-slate. The broken birth-slates under \place{the lowest rack} stayed entered by nobody. Three or four men had gone low into the finger's-width-open \place{the back room} before first grey and he gave them no name and did not say the room was cold.

He spoke to the wheel before he spoke to anyone. “It doesn't care whose hands,” he said. “It cares if they're steady.” He told a pot, “You'll do,” in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either, and withheld \lore{the full note} --- the note he knew it would give three turns before it left his hands --- and said nothing about where the slate had burned or why. Both wrong-wind sayings stood where they had been set down: the pot that sang before its time generally sings itself out early, and the pot that had not been heard from since. Both were sayings about a pot and nothing more, with no lesson made and no name given to what ailed the man carried out on Day 188. The knowing went to the near-full tray beside the hearings and never among them, advanced and unpaid, and no pot was lost in the yard.

When Sella's voice went low and unfinished at the hearth --- “The fire's low. It wants---” --- his bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper} ahead of his hand and held until her sentence reached the word for feeding and gave the end of it to the pot, then moved the tongue a hair's breadth and was left standing there, a remembered holding and not a change made again. The cold iron took the cold out of his fingertip while he waited. The iron was one piece with two bites, and there was no \lore{Grathic} word that carried both mercies in one mouthful: the bite that holds a vessel whole and the bite that can take the hand off a man. He held the two bites apart as he held the dust from the slip, and joined neither to the burned slate nor to the two tellings of the room shut in the war. \emph{He's not Vail} stayed sealed as tightly as a lid nobody lifts, and the promise of the boy's name to the men who burn slates was neither given nor withdrawn. The burned-slate word had come over the boards a third time and arrived nowhere, a direction with no hand at the foot of it; the two shut-room tellings stayed two with no third, and he kept a hand between them and the slate word as he kept a hand between dust and slip.

\scenebreak

At the going of the light Sella carried him home at the half-pace. Woodsmoke went before her in long low bars and the river wind came behind with the taste of water over water, and between the two she went around the places the yard kept without naming. In \place{the Clay Yard} she went wide around the pan's gone round ring, the bare dark \place[the trough]{trough} at \place{the yard well} with its water called weather, the rack space beneath the lowest rack with its broken birth-slates, the field's empty place and the place the carriers crossed, each with a look and a wide step and no number. The \place[the drying racks]{drying racks} stood to the east with the light going off them and she did not count those either. Her carrying arm was a record in muscle that remembered weight and not cost; it had carried him up and down this yard for two hundred and fifteen days and had never once asked what he weighed in anything but the arm's own knowing. It set him on his bed under the roof and not in the loft, put the fold right without a look at his face and left the thread frozen. The one crust lay two inches from his ear with its empty place unnamed and no third. The door stood a finger's width open and the river kept its note through the gap, and under the floor the fire went on in the room that warmed him. She let her hand rest a half-beat longer at the setting, then took it away and went to her own bed before the small hours, leaving the coals to keep themselves. The two rooms did not touch.

\scenebreak

In the small hours he turned out the file as a man lays instruments on a towel --- no towel, no instruments and no case, but the order of the laying the whole of the discipline --- and found it exact, complete and worth nothing. Six hearings of the full note stood beside and never among: five from four sources, a cup across a birth room, a pot in the wheel-shed through a finger's width of door, the district's slate twice at \place{the Cross}, and the sixth, the small flat knock out of Dessa as the men lifted her, from a person, filed where a thing out of a person must go and never among the sources. No seventh came in two hundred and fifteen days and none was invented to fill the empty place, for an empty place kept empty is a finding and a finding filled to make a row come out even is forged.

Three near-full answers stood on their own tray, advanced and unpaid: a pot under the shed wall that answered nearly full with air under the note and a want in it, a rack pot lifted a hand's breadth too high and set down again, and the knowing the wheel-keeper kept back from a man come up on a wrong wind whose saying about a pot singing before its time and then not heard from stayed two sayings about a pot and cost no pot. None was promoted into the hearings and no pot was lost in the yard. The told bell lay beside the heard, four, four and a gap with nobody crossing himself; the told figure lay beside the paid, one in twelve read three times over a basket and still not the figure paid, the third reading gone up the clay road in \person[Berr Clay]{Berr}'s locked box with the true subtraction inside, answering nothing for the man carried out on Day 188, and the soft-cuffed clerk still set at the out-of-line landing for a measure nobody called.

On the widened tray nine stoppings stood --- Reed 5, 8, 11, 14, 17, 20, 23, 26 and 29 --- objects and not a rule, intervals and not payments, with no tenth stood up before the seventh year, which now stood at its very end and had still not given. The Seed 16 breath was unspent and the loan uncalled. The damper was one iron with two bites, the dust kept apart from the slip, and the two shut-room tellings kept at two with no third.

Three held changes stood on three places. The fraction from the morning of Day 161 lay where it had been left, held and never spoken, no payer named to it and no slate entered. Beneath the loaf shelf the beat that stayed under the board on Day 166 stood on a place of its own, the reason standing between knowing the board by heart and being told not to look. The flagstone half-check made on Tallow 22 stood on a third place beside the other two, a place in a yard as the others were places in a wall and in a room that does not touch either. Three held changes, not a pair and not a doctrine, no word spoken over any and no payer named. A fourth place: the small dry scrape of a pot's foot set down on stone from the loft sill on Day 183, not a hearing, not a seventh, not a stopping, not made into a cause. A fifth: the pale line at the gum of the glaze-room hand, read at the well on Day 187 and on the carried body on Day 188, exact, complete and worth nothing at two paces, joined to no sweet wind and no dust and no pot that sang early. And beside them all, with a hand between, the burned-slate word on its own place beside the wheel-keeper's dust and the two shut-room tellings --- a direction arrived nowhere, not a cause, not a hearing, not a seventh, not a stopping, not a payment, nothing a clerk could chart.

He did not let them stand up together and call themselves a rule, because a rule is an account that has found a payer and no payer stood to any of them. There was no third gap in the fold and no fourth likeness made a cause, and nothing was filled to make a row come out even. Two hundred and fifteen stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody under any roof; the carried fire's fifth was unpaid for two hundred and fifteen days; the seventh year stood at its very end and had still not given. Grain 10 stood four mornings off, the first Account-day and the first market of the month, with the seventh green still a colour and the foundling still a shape, and no loaf, no slate and no sale coming early into the small hours of a working day.

\scenebreak

He put the question to his hand. Sella had gone to her own bed and left the coals to keep themselves, and there was no breathing number anywhere in the house --- nothing to hold against but the rhythm that came through the finger's-width door, the low note of water over water drawing out and slackening, and the next low note coming when it came. In the other room her breaths had gone small intake, hold, letting go at a fire, a count she kept toward no opening and had never offered to any ear under this roof. The hand outside the wool went up an inch toward the note and stayed. He took it through the small intake and the hold of one of those breaths, and brought it down with the letting go --- down with the breath rather than before it, a coordination shorter than the whole breath of the night before and on a place of its own.

Was it longer? No. It was another fraction, held to a shorter count and let go at the same end. He filed it on the sixth place beside the other answers and laid nothing across them and named no payer to it. Nothing stood up out of it to call itself a longer reach, a stronger hand, a hearing, a seventh, a stopping, a payment, one of the three held changes, a key, a power or a rule. He still could not close the two inches to the pot handle Sella had set out for him, lift his chin the two inches to the crust, or speak a number that anybody under any roof would take down. The body humiliated him and answered him in the same motion, and both were true.

The asking was not always answered and the answer here had been given; both were objects on their own trays and neither was a sign. Two hundred and fifteen went forward exact and unspent and spoken by nobody under any roof, carried by the only person who would have known what the number counted and by no slate in \place{Sarn}, \place{Sill} or \place{Grath}. The carried fire's fifth stayed unpaid; the seventh year stayed at its very end and ungiven; Grain 10 held four mornings off with the seventh green a colour and the foundling a shape, and Grain 7 would come up under this roof as another ordinary working day with no market and no Account-day, the hand asked again as the body answered --- forward, not closed. The hand came down on the letting go of a breath counted at a fire in a room that does not touch his, the thread at his mouth frozen and unwiped, the one stiff crust two inches from his ear with its empty place unnamed and no third. The fire went on in the room that warmed him and the river in the room he could not enter, and neither stopped for the ear under the roof.
