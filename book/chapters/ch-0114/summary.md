## Chronicle

The last two of the Five came under the Vail roof — the Remembering and the Last Day — and then Ilmen 1 of a new year, with nothing signed. Each morning the surgeon inventoried the body: the crust two inches from the ear, its corner a hair off the wool; the fold in the empty place, found where put; the thread frozen and unwiped, for nobody had been told to wipe it; cold stopping at cheek, temple and wrist; the afterdrop waiting past a warming nobody would perform. The infant wanted the crust lifted, the thread wiped, a hand on the pot handle. Both true; the second louder; neither winning.

On the Remembering the names of the dead went up the Clay Yard from door to door, passed mouth to mouth the way a coal goes in a dish. One name went among them and did not come under the Vail roof; Garl's name stayed unspoken its three hundred and ninety-third day. Sella carried the boy to House Silt on an errand, and from the basket's low angle he saw the pewter tin above the empty bed, its pale fold and thumb-bright lid, and it was not opened.

On the Last Day bread was baked and the grey in the corners was left where it lay, thickening in the dry cold where no broom had gone in two years. A neighbour called it dealt with because it would be burnt in the morning; Sella did not. The boy kept the called-gone grey beside the left-in-place ridge with a finger between. At the kindling Tarn passed his hand over blade and sill and away with no breath held at the top of the reach, and told the wood, *Six counted. The next I don't count.* His term had ended and the loan stood uncalled. Jorra put a bent finger into the cold damper and took it out without the extra holding; the promise under the wheel-shed was neither given nor withdrawn.

On Ilmen 1 the yard burnt its grey and called it gone, and the household went back to its grammar. Sella told the coals it was the thaw, lifted the twice-mended pot with the hairline showing, covered the grain without a figure and set the pot rim two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat and no look toward the bed. She did not sweep the ridge; the widow's mark hung where it hung.

Six answers were filed, 361 through 366. The count stood at 395 exact, six months and two hundred and fifteen days told by nobody; the fire's fifth was unpaid and Garl's name unspoken the same number. Twelve arithmetics crossed into the new year behind one unsigned figure. His satisfaction in a field taken as found and left as found cost nothing; it was not peace. The infant wanted the crust lifted, the thread wiped and a hand on the pot handle, and got none of the three.
