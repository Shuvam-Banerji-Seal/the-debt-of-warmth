  % thing. Left, the ridge left where it lay, thickening where no broom has
  % gone in two years, with its finer grey above it. Right, the grey the yard

  % a thin smoke going up that is not spent. Between them a finger's width,
  % with a dashed would-be joining broken at its middle: no fourth likeness
