## Scene 1 — The Names Going Up the Yard

**Day and time.** Day 393, the Remembering, Y.H. 813: small hours opening the day, then first light and the morning's passing of names up the Clay Yard.
**Place.** The Vail kiln-house — the bed at the low angle of the loaf shelf, the hearth, the pot board, the finger's-width door; the Clay Yard and the doorsteps along it.

**Beats, in order.**

2. The boy counts the spaces between the sounds without knowing which are names and gives no interior to any voice. **Beats, in order.**
1.
