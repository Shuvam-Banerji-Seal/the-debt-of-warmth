The afternoon came on in the same key as the morning, which was the house's way of answering a year that had turned. \person[Corin]{The boy} lay where the morning had left him at the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf}, and the light went across \place{the boards} fronting it as a hand goes over a pot to find the crack and does not find a new one: four nails on the near board, four on the next, and a thumb-width knot where a fifth might have been driven and was not. He had counted that knot so many mornings that it had stopped being a finding and had become the shape of the board itself. He counted it anyway. A man who stops counting a thing has already decided what it means, and nothing under this roof had been asked to decide anything.

\person{Sella Vail} went to \place{the hearth} before the basket at the going of the light, as she had gone at its coming, and told the coals it was \lore{the thaw} in the voice another woman would have used for a child who could not hear her. The small red eye under the ash showed and went dark and showed again under the back of the broken pot. A small intake, a hold, and a letting-go toward no opening, with neither gap figured. Her finger lay along the top of the shelf where the charcoal tally stayed unread; her lips moved on a two-gapped row and figured neither gap; one glance went to the clay jar cold under \lore{lead glaze} beyond \place{the pot board}, the sweetish edge coming and going on a wrong wind, and her hand shut half over nothing, the little-door burn on the back of it closing on air. The twice-mended pot went up thumb-first where the wire crossed the crack, the hairline of the old break showing between the older brass and the tighter wire, and both proverbs went to the pot and the fire and never to a person: \emph{Leave the crack showing. People trust a thing they can see has been handled}, and the other about mending twice, the second time being the real one, improved in the keeping and very likely not as good as the man had said it. She turned the dressed grain under wool with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child, with no figure over it and no handful lifted, and set the pot rim two inches beyond the reaching hand. There was a half-beat at the setting and no look toward \place{the bed}. He filed the half-beat as the distance between intention and arrest, bought at nothing and not a gift; it was the same interval he had once bought with a clamp and called by another name, and the other name bought nothing here either.

What she said low went to the pot and the fire, and when she spoke of the boy it was \emph{the boy}, and when she spoke of what she meant to do it was \emph{the house}: a grammar that had outlasted three holy days and a turning of the year without losing a word. The instruction went to the wool, the pot, the fire and the river wind and never to the basket. \lore[the widow's mark]{The widow's mark} hung where it hung on its cord of working thumbs and she did not touch it; a new year's Firstday was not an opening, seven openings in seven months were seven and not a rule, and a rule is an account that has found a payer and none stood. Under the floor \lore{the carried fire} breathed four low pulls with the silence where the fifth should have stood, its giver without a name and its fifth unpaid three hundred and ninety-five days, and nobody lifted the floor to supply what was wanting below it. \person{Garl Vail}'s coat kept its salt-stiff crease at the left shoulder on its peg behind the door, and his name was not spoken in the grammar of this afternoon.

Her hands were never still when they had nothing to do; they went over the spoons on the board, the nails in the boards, the breaths of a child nobody had given a number to, and came back to the hairline at the wire without having decided to. The boy read the order of it as anatomy: hand to the fire before eye to the basket, mouth after both, and never a small stop in the writing where another hand would have put one. It was the order he had filed from a flat satchel and a cold iron tube and a cuff soft from washing, and it stood on its own place here as a woman's grammar and not as a tenth hand. The half-beat at the pot's setting was the space between the reaching and the arrest of it, and a surgeon knows that space by feel: it is where a clamp is put down gently so that the field is not startled, and it buys nothing and costs nothing, and nobody under this roof would have known what it was.

Before she came back to the basket she carried him down the nineteen steps at the half-pace she keeps from every open slate, and the instruction went again to the wool and the river wind and never to him. Out in \place{the Clay Yard} the neighbours were settling into the ordinary grammar of the new year, which meant that they went about their work as if \lore{the Five} had been a week of weather and the year had turned in the night without anybody's noticing: a woman set a pail down where she always set it, a man counted kindling into a stack and did not count the stack, two voices passed the price of barley from one end of the wall to the other and neither voice made a figure of it. She went around the seven walked-around places with a look and a wide step and no number, once and not twice, and widest around \place{the returning-line place} where a hand had gone in on Seed 15 and had not come out. She set him under the roof and never in \place{the Debt Loft}, put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face, and added no second fold. The thread at his mouth was frozen and unwiped because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread.

He had carried twelve arithmetics across the turn of the year and found them unchanged on the far side. The three of \place{Frost}, the first, second and third of \place{Bone}, the first, second and third of \place{Ash}, and \place{Stone}'s first, second and last: twelve readings of one small printed figure, twelve and not one account. The printed figure stood at one in twelve, unchallenged, and twelve true changes to the district's figures had not moved that one mark. The figure read was still not the figure paid; the space between them had no signature on it and no doctrine came out of twelve, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood. Their twelve true subtractions rode locked in \person{Berr Clay}'s box up \place{the clay road} toward \place{High Kiln}, the key gone up in a coat pocket that never came back down and the boy with no key to anything. The tend had begun again at Firstday, which was a fact of \lore{the Court}'s counting and not an act of anybody under this roof, and the reset supplied no payer to the old twelve or to a new one. He did not lay his twelve old findings across a new figure to make a row come out even. \place{Ilmen 10} stood nine mornings off with the year's first arithmetic waiting there, and he did not bring it into an afternoon that had not earned it. No thirteenth reading stood in the space of the Five and no signature stood at the turn; the year had gone over like a pot set down in the same place on a board, and the board had not changed shape under it.

He had learned to take a series of readings as a surgeon takes instruments out of a case: each one laid out by its own place, none standing in for the next, and no clean arrangement of twelve making a thirteenth thing that had not been taken. The first of Frost and the last of Stone had the same small print at the top of them and different figures underneath, and the difference was the whole of the work; a man who laid them edge to edge and called them one account would have had to put a hand under the account to pay it, and no hand stood. The box up the road held the true subtractions the way a closed case holds instruments after a case is done, out of reach and not disposed of, and the boy had no key and did not ask for one. He kept the finding on the place where he had found it and did not carry it down the road to lay it across the printed figure.

At the going of the light \place{the towel} came out at the head of the bed as instruments come out when a case is done and before the next is called. He went over the field by the place of each thing and left every gap a gap. The crust's gap first: one stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust two inches from his ear, its corner standing a hair off the wool, the empty place beside it holding the fold found where put and not made again. \lore[the keeping at the fold]{The keeping at the fold} was a finding still and not a placing; a hand that finds is not a hand that places, and a habit wants a hand that placed twice and a way wants a third, both wanting a payer where none stood. The shelf's two gaps stayed where the loaves had not been set; the gone pan's round ring stayed on the board where a pan had stood and stood no longer; \place{the field's empty place} kept its own shape out beyond the door. \lore[the told bell]{The told bell}'s gap sat in its square beside \lore{the heard bell} from \place{the Cross}, four, four and a gap in two places and a gap in a third, three counts in three places staying three because agreeing any two would want a payer put under both. The silence after the name from \place{Weighing} lay on the \lore{gap tray} with the clean voice's missing name and the empty name the neighbours had left in the story of \place{the Remembering}, each with a finger between, none filled, none made a witness, a reader or a payer. The neighbour's called-gone grey lay on its own place beside the left-in-place ridge at \place{the Vail corner}, and a thing called finished was not finished for being called so. \person{Tarn}'s ear-sentence stood stopped at the word that would call the loan, at the very end of a term already ended, with no figure on it and no name on the man who turned back at the frozen ford. \person{Jorra Vail}'s promise under \place{the Vail wheel-shed} stood neither given nor withdrawn; \person{Garl Vail}'s unforged collector sentence lay beside the seventh notch at \place{the kindling}, and the debt against a firing of tiles went on circling without a name and worse for being uncalled. Each lay where it had been laid with a finger between, and he did not let the stopped sentence, the ungiven promise and the place the Remembering did not fill stand up together and call themselves a rule.

The towel was rough under the place where his hand rested, and the room smelled of tallow, clay dust and the faint sour tang that came up off the unswept ridge where no broom had gone in two years. He took the field in that order --- the near things first by their exact places, then the sounds beyond the door --- because a field taken by likeness is a field already decided. The crust's gap was an edge that had come together over a cold cavity and called itself closed; the shelf's two gaps were absences with the shape of loaves; the gone pan's round ring was a vessel's interval left on the board after the vessel went. None of them was a fifth object, a sixth hearing or a sign, and none borrowed a name from the things beside it. The surgeon's register took them as found and had no suggestion to make about them, which was the only way he had ever managed to leave a field alone.

Nine record-first hands stood with no tenth, each on a tray of its own. \person{Hessa Rale}'s eighth had gone up \place{the clay road} with her narrow slate turned to her sleeve and unread; \person{Ilva}'s ninth stayed in the fold of her sleeve with the notched strip untouched; Berr's twice-wiped thumb had passed over a figure through cloth and gone off up the road believing it had done a kindness. The soft-cuffed clerk's slate lay turned inward at \place{the out-of-line landing} below \place{the north bridge}, set for a measure nobody called. The Drawing collector's ordinary hand and the Kindling collector's ordinary hand each kept its own tray, and the belief of the second --- that he keeps the world alive by taking what is already going --- went on gathering interest against the day it would be remembered against him, whole and without anger. The neat dark hand at the Court table, \person{Marra}'s, and the finger beneath the shelf sat each on its own place as well. The clean-voice speaker of Weighing stayed taken out of his own sentences on a place of his own and was not made a tenth hand of, for a clean voice is not a hand. Sella's hands, \person{Alda Reed}'s and \person{Mira Silt}'s sat on another tray and were not made a cause: the woman who keeps the fire, the woman who told a mercy-lie and the woman last seen at the lower boards each kept her own interval, and a row of three would still want a payer standing under it.

The order of reaching was the same in nine hands and made no tenth of any of them: hand to the record, eye to the person, mouth after both, so that a figure stood between a man and the person he had come to measure. He had watched it in a midwife laying slates before turning to a fold, in a thane passing a finger over a figure before the crowd, in a keeper's finger beneath a board, and in a collector who wrote flat and went away believing he kept the world alive. The clean voice's speaker had taken himself out of his own sentences as a hand is drawn from a field before the dressing goes on, and the missing name stayed on the gap tray with nothing standing under it. Nine hands were nine hands, a trade learned the same way in nine rooms, and no hurry of his could make one of them a reader.

The ledger of holdings was checked and unchanged. Six hearings of \lore{the full note} stood beside and never among, the seventh place empty and not dug for, \lore{the flat knock} of \place{the Drawing house} outside them on its own place; the small flat knock out of \person{Dessa Silt} as the men lifted her stayed where a thing out of a person must go and was not made a seventh hearing. Nine stoppings stood with no tenth. Seven openings in seven months were seven and no eighth was made due on a Firstday, a morning, a yard, a well, a going of the light or a bed. \lore[the burned-slate word]{The burned-slate word} stood at five, the shut-room tellings at two, the row at five askings with one shaping and no witness, and the well-saying at four shapings; none was added to on a day that had not earned it. \lore[the four directions]{The four directions} --- the joining to \lore{the counting room} shut in the war and the three shapings from the direction of \place{Slate Row} --- stood apart with a finger between every pair, and none repeated or bought a lesson. Green was a colour and the foundling a shape, with no eighth of either and no name given to either under this roof. The cold foot below the sill stayed uncured and unnamed, the cause of \lore{the second chill} withheld; \place{the river} under \lore{the winter skin} stayed beside \lore{water over water} on its own place; \lore{the four rooms} with the same gap stayed four with a finger between every pair and no Court room brought under the roof as a fifth; the three counts in three places stayed three. Every one advanced and none paid, and no hand under any roof drew a boundary across the afternoon to make them stand as a doctrine.

\scenebreak

Through \place{the finger's-width door} the river kept its note of water over water under the winter skin, a low thing over a lower thing, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming. The skin was a covering put on by the cold and taken off by the cold, entered nowhere, found where put, not spent on the turning of a year and not a hearing or a sign. The note had done before any hand in the room came down; it ended on its own finishing under the covering and not on any decision of the hand.

The note came from under the covering and not from the covering: a surgeon knows the difference between the sound a hand makes and the sound a thing makes under a hand, and he had never once mistaken the one for the other, though he had often been the one holding the hand steady. The covering had been put on by the cold and would be taken off by the cold, and there was no hand under it to be asked who paid for it. He let the note be the note and did not give it a source, for a source is an account that has found a payer and none stood beyond the door.

The hand outside the wool came up an inch toward the note. The inch was not a reach. He took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole on the note's own finishing under the covering, let it go as the note ended --- neither early nor stayed --- and came straight down and laid itself flat against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking.

Answer three hundred and sixty-six was filed exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the twelve readings, the bell's gap, the flat knock, the child's grey fingertips, the mother's cup, the covered grain, \lore{the handful}, \lore{the thumb's resting}, \lore{the held breath}, \lore{the two bites of iron}, the river's skin, the widow's mark, \lore{the fourth direction} nor the keeping at the fold. Six honest answers stood on the sixth place from these three days and three hundred and sixty-six stood since the first, not one a doctrine. The surgeon took his small clean satisfaction in a field taken as found and left as found, every object in its place and nothing laid across anything else, and it cost him nothing, which was how he knew it was not peace. Peace in the old life had always come with something spent --- a shift ended, hands washed, a chart signed; this cost nothing because nobody had been asked to pay. Under it, louder, the infant wanted the crust lifted two inches, the thread at his mouth wiped, and a hand on \place{the pot board}'s handle. He got none of the three, and the wanting and the having-not went on being the work, the way a wound goes on being a wound under a dressing that does not close it.

The count came to three hundred and ninety-five exact, six months and two hundred and fifteen days told by nobody who would have known what a number counted. The carried fire's fifth was unpaid the same number and Garl's name was unspoken the same number, the two standing side by side without becoming one. \lore[the Afterdrop]{The Afterdrop} waited past a warming nobody in this house would perform; cold had stopped at cheek, temple and the bare wrist-joint where the wool rode back, as at the end of a line written out for it, and the thread of drool at his mouth had gone stiff and unwiped because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread.

Nothing had been opened, joined, named, called, paid, forgiven or ended. No slate had been opened within reach of the basket, no figure given for the boy, no door drawn across the turning of the year, no payer set under any of the kept things. \lore[the Opening of the Slates]{The Opening of the Slates} had come and gone with a yard burning its grey and a household that said nothing, and had left no signature on the space it opened. The new year's first arithmetic waited nine mornings off where it had been left and was not brought into a day that had not earned it. Beyond the finger's-width door the river went on under its skin, water over water, and the hand lay flat on the fold on answer three hundred and sixty-six with the crust's corner standing a hair off the wool, the thread frozen and unwiped, and four low pulls breathing below the floor with the fifth unsupplied. The house went on as a house goes when a pot is set down in its place and the fire kept, forward, with nothing signed.
