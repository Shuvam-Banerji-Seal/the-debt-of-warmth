\daymark{393}{the Remembering, Y.H. 813}

\dropcap{T}{he hand} lay flat on the fold where it had been put down, and the small hours came on under the roof the way weather comes, without a door opened for them and without anybody saying what they had crossed. Three hundred and ninety-two days stood behind it exact and unspent, six months and two hundred and twelve of them told by nobody who would have known what a number counted. Under the floor \lore{the carried fire} gave four low pulls and kept the fifth where it had always been kept, and \person[Corin]{the boy} lay at the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf} in \place{the Vail kiln-house}, in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill}, \place{Grath}, with the dry kiln heat coming to him sideways and the smell of woodsmoke and wet wool in the fold and, under that, the faint sour tang of \lore{Ash} lifting out of \place{the Vail corner} where no broom had gone in two years. The room held its heat the way a cup holds water, with a crack in it nobody mentioned. Outside, the dark was going about its own work along \place{the Clay Yard}, and the fourth of \lore{the Five} had a name and no tend-day and no market, and was not a door or a boundary or a thing paid.

The surgeon took the body in the order it arrived, because a field read out of order is a field read wrong. One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust stood two inches from his ear with its corner a hair off the wool; beside it the empty place held the fold put there on the fifteenth, found where put again in the dark and not made again, with no second fold, no third crust and no name spoken over it. The thread of drool at his mouth had gone stiff in the dry heat and was unwiped, because nobody under any roof has ever been given an instruction about the thread. Cold had come to cheek, temple and the bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, and stopped there as though it had reached the end of a line written out for it; \lore{the Afterdrop} waited past a warming nobody in this house would perform. A face composed by cold is not composure. Pink returning is the surface still lying over a cold core, and a cold patient cannot clot, and the rewarming he would choose is a basket set level out of the draught where the heat comes sideways, slow and from the outside, never hurried and never rubbed, and he could not ask for it. He had once stood at a bay door and sorted twenty people by what they would not survive, and his knowledge of cold was of the same order and as useless here: he knew the order in which a body gives up heat and the order in which it takes it back, with the extremities before the core and the surface warm while the middle is still cold, and there was no hand two paces long to put any of it to work. Under the exactness the infant wanted the crust lifted, the thread wiped and a hand on the pot handle. Both were true. The second was louder. Neither won, and no verdict was looked for; the quarrel was the work.

\person{Sella Vail} came to \place{the hearth} before she came to the basket, as she had come to it every morning of his life, and gave the coals their instruction first. “It's the thaw,” she said, in the voice another woman would have used for a child who could not hear her, and the low red eye under the back of the broken pot answered her with a small breathing that was not an answer to anything anybody had asked. She turned the coals with the back of the pot and stood a moment with her hands not still, counting a small intake, a hold and a letting-go toward no opening, the count arriving nowhere as it had arrived nowhere every day of his life, with neither gap figured and neither made to stand for a number. Her finger lay along the top of \place{the loaf shelf} where any woman in the district could see it lying, and the charcoal tally underneath stayed unread; her lips moved along the two gaps in the loaf row and gave nobody a figure. Her eye went once to the clay jar cold under \lore{lead glaze} beyond \place{the pot board}, with the sweetish edge of a wrong wind coming and going over it, and her hand started toward it and shut half over nothing, the little-door burn on the back of her left hand closing on air. She lifted the twice-mended pot thumb-first where the tighter wire crossed the crack, and the hairline of the original break showed between the old-brass staple and the new binding, and she said both proverbs to the pot and the fire and never to a person, keeping repair apart from restoration as a surgeon keeps a graft apart from a cure; a restored thing hides the hand that handled it and a mended thing leaves that hand showing. The pot gave back a small flat note as she set it down --- not the full clear note of a sound vessel and not the dead note of one cracked through, but the note of a thing that carries its break and goes on holding --- and she had heard it every morning of his life and had never once called it mended. A strand of coal-black hair came loose at her temple where the one grey streak was, and she pushed it back with the wrist that carried the burn and did not discuss either the hair or the winter it had come in. The dressed grain came out of \place{the drying racks} and went back under wool with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child, changed in state and given no figure and lifted in no handful. The pot rim went down two inches beyond the reaching hand, with a half-beat at the setting and no look toward \place{the bed}. The half-beat stood where it stood and asked nothing and was not entered. The cord of her \lore{widow's mark} lay against her throat with the oil of ten thousand working thumbs in it, and her hand did not go to it; a holy day is not an opening, seven openings in seven months are seven and not a rule, and no eighth was made due on a morning that had not earned one.

\scenebreak

The names began going up the yard before the sun cleared the eaves, and they went the way a coal goes in a dish from hearth to hearth, passed from door to door along the row of houses and set down at the last threshold with nobody saying where the dish had been filled. It was \lore{the Remembering}, and the yard said it the way the yard says everything it has kept a long time: practically, low, with the words flattened by work. A woman said a name to a pail. A man said one to the latch of his door as he lifted it, and a girl further down said one to the dust at her feet, and each house took the sound and passed it on and kept its own; nobody put a date to any of them and nobody said where the count began. The boy lay under the fold and counted the spaces between the sounds without knowing which of the sounds were names and which were the small noises of a yard waking in the cold; he gave no interior to any of the voices, for a voice in a yard is a voice in a yard and not a room entered, and a pause is not a person. He had learned that from pots: a cracked vessel answers flat, and the flatness is a fact about the vessel and not a confession from whoever held it.

A door opened up the yard and a woman's voice went out of it into the cold and came back to the person on the step as a thing handed over and taken; the person on the step said nothing, only set her hand on the pail and held it, and then the voice went on down the row. At the next house a man stood with his coat held closed at the throat and said a name into the gap between his hand and the lintel, and a child inside said it back wrong, and the man did not correct the child, because in the yard the saying is the keeping and the exactness of the sound is not what is kept. Nearer the well another man went around a place in the trodden ash with his pail held out from his body, took the long way past it without looking, and the boy had learned not to ask what the place was called or what had gone into it; a wide step is a wide step, and the yard had no number for it and gave him none. He followed the intervals rather than the words, as he followed the fire under the floor by its pulls and its silences: a sound, a space, a sound, a longer space where a door shut, a sound again from a house further up with the wind taking the top off it. The yard was doing the work it had always done with what it had, passing its dead from mouth to mouth the way it passed its fire in the dark, and nobody in any of the houses stood under a sound to be asked who had begun it or what it cost to keep going.

One name went among the others and did not come under this roof. It passed a door, went down a step and was taken up by another mouth, and the boy did not know which of the sounds it was, and no roof said it to him, and no number went with it. Among the names that travelled that morning was one that had belonged to \person{Dessa Silt}, dead since the opening of the year and uncollected, carried now in the mouths of neighbours who had known her as the yard knows all its dead, by sound and by the shape of a silence rather than by any slate; and the boy did not know it was hers, and her number stayed where it had been put, in a thumb gone into the water, and the name under the sieve-wire scar stayed where it had been put too. The mercy-lie stood as a fact nobody under any roof could correct, and no telling of it under the Vail roof corrected it now. The house kept its own quiet. No whole return was announced for \person{Garl Vail}; his name went unspoken its three hundred and ninety-third day, kept in the salt-stiff coat with its left-shoulder crease on the peg behind the door, in the hollow wheel-seat nobody filled, in the clean dark line at the Vail corner with the finer grey beside it called \lore{the thaw}, thickened in the dry cold with the faint sour tang of Ash coming up off it where no broom had gone in two years, in the hairline between old brass and tighter wire and in the unforged collector sentence lying at \place{the kindling} with no name on the man who turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on \lore{the tile debt}. He was kept there and nowhere spoken, and the keeping was maintenance and not a story.

The clean voice's missing name came up in the ear as the yard's voices went past, not as a voice now but as the space a voice had left, and the story's empty name came up beside it out of the dark behind \place{the finger's-width door}. The boy set them on the gap tray beside the told bell's gap with a finger between every pair, and did not fill either. He did not make a witness of a space, for a witness wants a person standing somewhere who can be asked; did not make a reader of it, for a reader wants a hand on a slate; did not make a payment of it, for a payment wants somebody paid; and did not make a doctrine of it, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood. The heard bell stayed in its square beside the told bell of \lore{the Long Night}, four, four and a gap, and the gap stayed in its own square, not folded into the fire's four and a gap under the floor, nor into \place{the boards}' four nails and four and a thumb-width knot overhead, nor into the twelve arithmetics standing behind one unsigned figure, nor made a seventh hearing of \lore{the full note}. Six hearings stood beside and never among with the seventh place empty and \place{the Drawing house}'s flat knock outside them; \place{House Silt}, this kiln-house, the room where the slate was read on market-day and the Drawing house kept the same gap in four places with a finger between every pair and no Court room brought under the roof as a fifth likeness. Three counts in three places stayed three, because agreeing any two would want a payer put under both and none stood to either. The Ash 5 joining and the Ash 17, Ash 23 and Stone 23 directions stood where they had been put, did not repeat and bought no lesson.

Sella carried him down the nineteen steps at the half-pace she keeps from every open slate, with the fold right at his ear and his chin and the instruction going to the wool and the river wind and the banked fire and never to the basket. She went wide around the seven walked-around places with a look and a wide step and no number, once and not twice, widest around \place{the returning-line place} where a hand had gone in on Seed 15 and had not come out, and the look stayed the length of a step before it went off the place. The three coverings stayed weather entered nowhere with a finger between every pair, and \place{the river} kept its note of \lore{water over water} under \lore{the winter skin} beyond the door, a covering put on by the cold and taken off by the cold, found where put and not made again. She set him under the roof and never in \place{the Debt Loft}, righted the fold without a look at his face, added no second fold, and left the thread where it lay. Under the floor the carried fire answered four low pulls with the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer unnamed and its fifth unpaid three hundred and ninety-three days; nobody lifted the floor and nobody supplied the pull, and the fire that warmed the house was a finding and not a cause.

\scenebreak

He turned out the file in the hush after the names had gone past, as instruments come out on \place{the towel} between cases, and went over the field by the place of each thing. It was not put away for good; another case would come and the towel would come out again, and the hand would find the place of every instrument without looking for it. The crust's gap, the shelf's two gaps, the gone pan's round ring, the field's empty place, the keeping at the fold found where put and still a finding, the told bell's gap, the silence after the name from \place{Weighing}, the place this remembering had not filled, Dessa's name going among the yard's others and not under this roof, the stopped ear-sentence brought to the calling word at the very end of a term already ended, the promise under \place{the Vail wheel-shed} neither given nor withdrawn, and Garl's unforged collector sentence beside the seventh notch --- each lay on its own place with a finger between, and none was made to stand up and call itself a rule. He did not let the silences stand up together and call themselves a rule, for a rule is an account that has found a payer and none stood. Answer three hundred and sixty-one was filed exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the twelve readings, the bell's gap, the flat knock, the child's grey fingertips, the mother's cup, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the held breath, the two bites of iron, the river's skin, the widow's mark, the fourth direction nor the keeping at the fold.

The count came to three hundred and ninety-three exact, six months and two hundred and thirteen days told by nobody who would have known what a number counted. The carried fire's fifth was unpaid the same number and its payer unnamed the same number; Garl's name was unspoken the same number, and the two counts stood side by side without becoming one. The surgeon took his small clean satisfaction in a field taken as found and left as found, and it cost him nothing, which was how he knew it was not peace. Under it, louder, the infant wanted the crust lifted, the thread wiped and a hand on the pot handle, and got none of the three. The hand outside the wool lay flat on the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking, and the fold lay where it had been put under the crust's corner standing a hair off the wool, with the name that had not come under the roof kept on its own place and a finger between it and every other gap, while the yard went on passing its names from door to door and no voice under this roof said one of them aloud.
