The hand lay flat on the fold and the afternoon came down over \place{the Clay Yard} the way dry heat comes off a banked kiln, by degrees nobody called and with no door opened for it. \person[Corin]{The boy} was set in the finger's width of shade where no slate lay, and the instruction went to the wool and the river wind and never to the basket; \person{Sella Vail} stood half a pace off and said nothing over him. The day kept the name it had been given and did not become a gate or a payment or a thing signed. In \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill}, \place{Grath}, the last of \lore{the Five} was going out with the same face it had worn coming in, and \lore{the Last Day} was a loaf set on \place{the boards}, not a boundary drawn under anybody's hand.

The yard went around its seven walked-around places with a look and a wide step and no number, once and not twice, and widest around \place{the returning-line place} where a hand had gone in on Seed 15 and had not come out. The three coverings --- the stayed frost, the skinned trough and the silt-clouded plate on \place{the flat stone by the well} --- stayed weather entered nowhere with a finger between every pair, and \lore{the winter skin} lay on \place{the river} with \lore{water over water} in its own note, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming. The boy spent none of it and made no fourth likeness of the three and called it a cause, for a cause is an account that has found a payer and none stood.

The day's costs went out as directions and went away as weather. A man down from \place{Cold Acre} shifted a hare across his shoulders and put a price into the space in front of \person{Tarn}'s face, and Tarn sent the price to the weather, which had carried the snow and could carry the price, and the man went off up \place{the clay road} with the hare unweighed and no slate opened. A man with a cracked jar in a rope sling was told to ask a pot, and the pot, being a pot, said nothing. A woman with a basket of kindling asked what the seventh year would want when it came, and two fence-menders said \lore{the thaw} would be early and the thaw would be late; to each of them Tarn said, “Yes, house,” and kept the late because a late thaw puts off the paying, agreeing so thoroughly that no argument found a foot. The boy filed the order of the answering without making a tenth hand of any of them: the weather before the price, the pot before the asker, the house before the man, and the mouth coming last of all.

\scenebreak

At \place{the kindling} six pieces stood in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check and the seventh lay at the lip beyond it, beside \person{Garl Vail}'s unforged collector sentence, with no name on the man who turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on \lore{the tile debt}. The stack kept its own arithmetic --- four, four and a space --- and the space was not a fifth and was not filled. The small knife lay at the very edge of its sill where a hand could be checked and where a hand could also slip it off. Tarn's hand went over blade and sill as a man passes a hand over a tool put back where it belongs, and came away with no breath held at the top of the reach. The coming away was the whole of the checking. He cut nothing, counted nothing and spent nothing, and said to the wood in the tone of a man stating the weather, “Six counted. The next I don't count.” The words went into the kindling and stayed there, and no ear under any roof was called to answer them.

The boy watched the hand go up and away from the low angle of the basket and read the interval by what was there and by what was not. There was no intake at the top of the reach, no second checking, no held breath left lying on the sill; the hand went over the place where a man might have stopped and did not stop, and that was the whole of it. He had once known a man who checked a blade the same way before the dressing went on --- the hand that works, the hand that only rests, and the hand that checks and goes away --- and the knowing was useless here, where the checking was all there was and no dressing would follow. The kindling took the sentence and gave nothing back, and the space at the lip kept its place beside the debt with a finger between.

The ear-sentence came around again in the yard's mouths and came to the word that would call the loan, and stopped there. An ear under this roof was worth more than the debt that held it, and the sentence had brought the worth all the way to the calling and left the calling standing unspoken --- a remembered ninth stopping with no tenth, no figure and no name. The \emph{Seed 16} breath was drawn and left unspent at the top of the reach, and the loan was not called. The seventh year had run to its very end and the term was already ended, and a term ending is not a payment, a forgiveness or a freedom; it leaves a man more exposed than it found him, and Tarn stood more exposed in the afternoon light with no figure put to the exposure and nobody standing to enter it.

The fear that he would be entered again for vagrancy stayed his without a figure, and so did a want he did not voice --- a slate with his own name on it and no mark against it, a thing a man could hold up and be called by. He did not look along the clay road. His chest had been tight since the dark. \lore{Ash} was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line at \place{the Vail corner}, and the pale clip-ring at his left wrist gave back what little light the afternoon had, and his stroke went over the shallow bright patch of \place{the flagstones} without the half-check that had once been returned to it.

The held breath of \emph{Ash 14} at the sill and the thumb's resting of \emph{Bone 26} were found where they had been put and were not made again, beside the five held objects and the pot-crack resting with a finger between every pair --- not a sixth held object, not a seventh of anything, not a tenth stopping and with no payer standing. The boy left them on their places. He did not lay the stopped sentence across the unspent breath and call the two one account, and he did not let the exposure stand up and ask for a figure, for a figure would want a payer and no payer stood under the yard.

Toward the going of the light \person{Jorra Vail} came through the finger's-width door off \place{the Vail wheel-shed}, with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past his bent little finger and white in a fist-crease where a hand had opened, and it was not the bone-coloured \lore{slip} of the wheel. He kept dusty forearms and clean ones on one tray and parted them as a surgeon parts clot from vessel, and he asked neither which slate had been broken nor whether it had been a birth-slate. Three or more men unnamed had gone low into \place{the back room} before first grey, and he gave the house no name for them and no word that the room was cold. The door took them in and gave nothing back, and the boy made no hearing of the going in and no source of the room's cold.

Jorra spoke to the wheel before he spoke to any person. “It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady,” he said, and the wheel took the sentence the way a pot takes a hand and gives back only the note. To a pot on the rack he said, “You'll do,” in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either. He knew the \lore{full note} that pot would give three turns before it left his hands, with air under it and a want in it, and he kept the knowing where nobody could make a song of it, withholding it from any master come up from \place{High Kiln} who would call it early and sing a pot before its time. The boy filed the knowing on a tray of its own beside the hearings and never among them; no seventh hearing stood up in the empty place, and \place{the Drawing house}'s small flat knock stayed outside on its own place.

Sella had not finished telling him the fire was low when his bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper} ahead of her sentence. He took the cold out of the fingertip, moved his tongue a hair's breadth, and was left standing so that the end of her instruction arrived at the pot and the household straightened around the waiting, as cloth straightens when a weight is lifted. One piece of iron had two bites, the holding and the taking, and no \lore{Grathic} word covered both mercies; a surgeon would have read it as one clamp that both holds and stops flow, and neither mercy was entered on any slate. He took the finger out without leaving the extra holding of \emph{Ash 26} a second time. That holding had been gathered once and stayed where nobody would enter it, found where put and not made again, for a second would be a habit and a third a way and both want a payer and none stood. Neither of them looked at the other and neither said what the iron had been asked to carry.

The boy read the two hands on two places and kept them apart. Tarn's hand had gone up over blade and sill and away with no breath spent at the top of the reach; Jorra's finger had gone into the iron and come out without the extra holding. Two silences, unjoined; one iron with two bites; a loan uncalled at the very end of a term. He did not make the coming-away and the coming-out into one likeness and call it a rule, because a rule is an account that has found a payer and none stood.

\scenebreak

“He's not Vail” stayed sealed as a lid nobody had tried to lift in three years. The promise of the boy's name under the wheel-shed was neither given nor withdrawn; it went on gathering interest against the burned slate the way a coal goes on giving off heat under a pot nobody lifts. The burned slate stood at five, the shut-room tellings at two, the row at five askings with one shaping and no witness, the well-saying at four shapings, and the four Ash/Slate directions held apart with a finger between every pair --- none added to on a holy day, none repeated, none buying a lesson. The Ash 5 joining of \lore{the burned-slate word} to \lore{the counting room} shut in the war, the Ash 17 and Ash 23 directions from \place{Slate Row} and the Stone 23 direction stood where they had been put and said nothing new under the going-out light.

No ownerless voice came over the boards on this last of the Five, and no fifth shaping stood up out of the yard's stories to make a row come out even. \lore{The Hearth Drawing} kept its small flat knock outside the six hearings on its own place, and \lore{the carried fire} under the floor gave four low pulls with the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer unnamed and its fifth unpaid three hundred and ninety-four days. Nobody lifted the floor and nobody supplied the pull, and the fifth stood where it had always stood, gathering toward a day that had not come.

At the going of the light she carried him up the nineteen steps at the half-pace from every open slate, and set him under the roof and never in \place{the Debt Loft}, and put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face and added no second fold. Overhead the boards kept four nails on the near board, four on the next and a thumb-width knot where a fifth might have been driven and was not; the scrape of the loft came from above and behind and went off again without a figure put to it. The twice-mended pot on \place{the pot board} kept the hairline of the original break showing between the old-brass staple and the tighter wire, and she had lifted it thumb-first and given both proverbs to the pot and the fire and never to a person. The dressed grain lay covered under wool with no figure over it and no handful lifted. \lore{The widow's mark} hung at her throat on its cord of working thumbs and she did not touch it: a holy day had not made an eighth due, and seven openings in seven months were seven and not a rule.

The surgeon took the body in its old order and found it as he had found it: one stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust two inches from the ear with its corner standing a hair off the wool, the empty place beside it holding the fold found where put and not made again, the thread at the mouth frozen in the dry heat and unwiped because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread, cold stopped at cheek and temple and the bare wrist-joint where the wool rode back, \lore{the Afterdrop} waiting past a warming nobody in this house would perform. A cold patient cannot clot; pink returning is the surface still lying over a cold core; the rewarming he would choose is slow and from the outside and never rubbed, and he could not ask for it. Under the exactness the infant wanted the crust lifted, the thread wiped and a hand on the pot handle. Both were true. The second was louder. Neither won, and no verdict was looked for; the quarrel was the work.

He lay with the three kept things in the ear and refused to let them stand up together and call themselves a rule. Tarn's stopped ear-sentence at the very end of a term already ended, Jorra's promise under the wheel-shed neither given nor withdrawn, the gap where a name had not stood on \lore{the Remembering} --- each lay on its own place with a finger between, and none was a sixth hearing, a tenth stopping, an eighth opening, a witness, a reader or a payer, for a rule is an account that has found a payer and none stood. He did not lay the exposure across the promise and call the two one debt, and he did not set the kept gap beside the told bell's gap and make the pair a count, for three counts in three places stay three and agreeing any two wants a payer put under both.

\place{The towel} came out at the head of \place{the bed} as instruments come out when a case is done and before the next is called, and he went over the field by the place of each thing and left every gap a gap: the crust's gap, \place{the loaf shelf}'s two gaps, the gone pan's round ring, \place{the field's empty place}, the keeping at the fold found where put and still a finding, the told bell's gap, the silence after the name from \place{Weighing}, the place the remembering had not filled, the called-gone grey and the left-in-place ridge on two places, Tarn's sentence stopped at the calling word, Jorra's promise ungiven, Garl Vail's unforged collector sentence beside the seventh notch. Each stayed where it was put and none joined another.

Answer three hundred and sixty-four was filed exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the twelve readings, the bell's gap, the flat knock, the child's grey fingertips, the mother's cup, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the held breath, the two bites of iron, the river's skin, the widow's mark, the fourth direction nor the keeping at the fold. The count came to three hundred and ninety-four exact, six months and two hundred and fourteen days told by nobody who would have known what a number counted, and the carried fire's fifth was unpaid the same number and Garl's name was unspoken the same number, and the two stood side by side without becoming one. The surgeon took his small clean satisfaction in a field taken as found and left as found, every object in its place and nothing laid across anything else, and it cost him nothing, which was how he knew it was not peace. Under it, louder, the infant wanted the crust lifted, the thread wiped and a hand on the pot handle, and got none of the three. The fifth of the Five went out with no boundary drawn and no eighth opening made due, and the hand outside the wool came down and laid itself flat on the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking.
