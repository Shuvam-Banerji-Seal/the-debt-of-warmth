\daymark{394}{the Last Day, Y.H. 813}

The small hours came down under the roof the way dry heat goes out of a kiln after the last firing, by degrees nobody announced and with no door opened for them. Three hundred and ninety-three days stood behind the hand flat on the fold, exact and unspent, six months and two hundred and thirteen of them told by nobody who would have known what a number counted; the three hundred and sixty-second answer lay where it had been filed, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air, and the dry smell of the kiln lay under the wet wool and woodsmoke in the room. \person[Corin]{The boy} lay at the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf} in \place{the Vail kiln-house}, in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill}, \place{Grath}, and the day had a name and no tend-day and no market and was not a door or a boundary or a thing paid. Under the floor \lore{the carried fire} gave four low pulls and kept the fifth where it had always been kept, and beyond \place{the finger's-width door} \place{the river} went on under \lore{the winter skin} with \lore{water over water} in its own note, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming, entered nowhere.

The surgeon took the body in the order it arrived, because a field read out of order is a field read wrong. One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust stood two inches from his ear with its corner a hair off the wool; beside it the empty place held the fold put there on the fifteenth, found where put again in the dark and not made again, with no second fold, no third crust and no name spoken over the place. The thread of drool at his mouth had gone stiff in the dry heat and was unwiped, because nobody under any roof has ever been given an instruction about the thread. Cold had come to cheek, temple and the bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, and stopped there as though it had reached the end of a line written out for it; \lore{the Afterdrop} waited past a warming nobody in this house would perform. A face composed by cold is not composure. Pink returning is the surface still lying over a cold core, and a cold patient cannot clot, and the rewarming he would choose is a basket set level out of the draught where the heat comes sideways, slow and from the outside, never hurried and never rubbed, and he could not ask for it. He had once known a man brought in off the expressway in February whose hands had gone the colour of old rope, and had made the same judgement then that he made now of himself: the surface lies, the core is still cold, and the only way through is slow and from outside and with no rubbing, and no haste. He had known the order in which a body gives up heat and the order in which it takes it back, with the extremities before the core and the surface warm while the middle is still cold, and there was no hand two paces long to put any of it to work. Under the exactness the infant wanted the crust lifted, the thread wiped and a hand on the pot handle. Both were true. The second was louder. Neither won, and no verdict was looked for; the quarrel was the work.

Before she took the basket down \person{Sella Vail} came to \place{the hearth} first, as a woman goes to a door before she opens it. The coals were low from the banked fire and she told them it was \lore{the thaw} in the voice another woman would have used for a child who could not hear her, and turned them with the back of a broken pot, and gave the small intake, the hold and the letting-go toward no opening with neither gap figured and neither made to stand for a number. Her finger went along the top of the loaf shelf as she passed and the charcoal tally stayed unread beneath it, and her lips moved on a two-gapped row and figured neither gap. She glanced once at the clay jar standing cold under \lore{lead glaze} beyond \place{the pot board}, with the sweetish edge of it coming and going on a wrong wind, and shut her hand half over nothing, the little-door burn on the back of her left hand closing on air. The \lore{widow's mark} hung at her throat on its cord of working thumbs and she did not touch it: the dark was not an opening, a baking was not an opening, seven openings in seven months were seven and not a rule, and no eighth was made due on a day that had not earned one.

Then the baking began. On \lore{the Last Day} bread is baked under every roof in Low Kiln and the grey in the corners is left where it lies, and the house went about it the way it went about all its work, without announcement and without a word said over the basket. She set a bowl on the pot board and worked the dough with the heel of her hand, and the tallow went into it in a little firm piece, and the dry kiln heat took the moisture off the top of the dough as fast as she could work it in. The room smelled of dough and tallow and the ash going cold in the corners. From the low angle of the basket the boy watched the heel of her hand come down and turn and come down again, a rhythm as old as the house and older than any collector's slate, and the surgeon in him noted that she worked the dough the way she turned the coals, with a pressure that knew exactly what the thing would bear and never a stroke more. He had watched a nurse hold pressure in a cavity with both hands and known the same exactness, and the knowing was useless to him here, where his own hands could not close on the rim of the basket that held him.

\scenebreak

The grey lay where it lay. At \place{the Vail corner} the clean dark line kept its finer grey hairline beside it called the thaw, thickened in the dry cold and carrying the faint sour tang of \lore{Ash} coming up off it where no broom had gone in two years, and along the skirting board the same ridge stood in the places the yard had left it, neither swept nor covered nor counted. Sella did not sweep it early. Other houses along \place{the Clay Yard} were sweeping, the boy could hear the brooms going on the trodden-ash floors and the small dry rasp of them at the corners, but under the Vail roof the ridge kept its place and the clean dark line kept its hairline beside it, and the sour tang went on coming up off both where the dry heat had drawn the moisture out of everything else. A woman passing the door with a basket of kindling looked in at the grey and said what the yard says on the Last Day, that it would be burnt in the morning and so was dealt with. “That'll be dealt with come morning,” she said, and went on with her kindling, and nobody under the roof answered her.

At the next door a woman turned a loaf on a peel and called across that there was no sense fretting over the grey, it would be dealt with in the morning, and the kindling woman said aye and shifted her basket, and the two went back to their work. The boy heard the whole of it from the low angle of the basket and gave the voices no faces and no interiors, for a voice is a voice and the yard had a hundred of them and nothing in any of them stood up to be asked. The sour tang came up off the ridge at the Vail corner and went into the dry air with the smell of bread from four doorways, and the two smells kept their two places and did not join.

Sella said nothing. She did not say it was dealt with and she did not say it was not; she put the dough to rise under a cloth and came back to the hearth and told the coals again it was the thaw, and the women's sentences went off with the light as sentences do in this yard, leaving the ridge where it was put. The boy kept the two sayings on two places with a finger between. On one place stood the neighbour's dealt-with grey, the grey that would be burnt in the morning and called finished, and on the other stood the ridge at the Vail corner thickened in the dry cold with the sour tang coming up off it where no broom had gone in two years. Both were true. They were not the same grey and he did not make them the same, and he did not make a fourth likeness of them and call it a cause, for a cause is an account that has found a payer and none stood. A thing called finished is not finished for being called, and a thing left where it lies is still kept; the two stand on their own places and no broom goes between them. The surgeon in him had once been asked to sign a chart that said a patient was stable when the patient was not, and had learned what a word does when a hand puts it on a record --- it does not change the patient, and it does not change the record's owing, and it stays on the record all the same. Here the record was a neighbour's sentence going up the yard and the patient was a ridge of grey in a corner, and neither the sentence nor the ridge had a hand that would join them, and he gave no interior to either.

When the loaves had risen she shaped them on the board and set them in the mouth to brown, and the crust came up brown and hard in the dry heat, and the smell of bread went out through the finger's-width door and down the nineteen steps into the yard where the neighbouring houses were baking their own. The loaf shelf kept its two gaps and its charcoal unread beneath her finger, and no third crust went into the fold. The spent crust stood two inches from his ear with its corner standing a hair off the wool, and beside it the empty place held the fold found where put and not made again, and the thread at his mouth stayed frozen and unwiped. She lifted a loaf from the mouth and set it on \place{the boards} to cool, and the loaf shelf kept its gaps and the loaf kept its place, and no figure was set over any of it, and no handful was lifted and given.

At the pot board she lifted the twice-mended pot thumb-first where the tighter wire crossed the crack, with the hairline of the original break showing between the old-brass staple and the newer wire, and she said both proverbs to the pot and to the fire and never to a person, the words improved in the keeping and very likely not as good as the man had said them. “\emph{Leave the crack showing. People trust a thing they can see has been handled},” she said to the pot, and to the fire, “\emph{If it's worth mending it's worth mending twice. The second time is the real one}.” Repair stayed apart from restoration, for a restored thing hides the hand that handled it and a mended thing leaves that hand showing, and the hairline was left where it could be seen. \person{Garl Vail} was in the pot and in the crack and in the clean dark line at the corner and in the hollow seat in the shed and in the salt-stiff coat on its peg behind the door, and his name was not spoken under the roof and his tile debt was not called and no proverb was said to a person, and the fifth pull under the floor stayed unsupplied the same number of days as his name. The dressed grain she turned under wool with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child, changed in state and left unfigured, with no figure over it and no handful lifted, and then she set the pot rim two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat and no look toward the bed. The half-beat was the distance between the intention and the arrest, bought at nothing and not a gift, and the boy filed it on its own place and left it a gap.

\scenebreak

Later, when the bread was cooling and the morning's work was put by, she took the basket down the nineteen steps at the half-pace she keeps from every open slate, and the instruction went to the wool and the river wind and the fire she had banked against their coming back and never to the basket. Out in the Clay Yard she went around the seven walked-around places with a look and a wide step and no number, once and not twice and widest around \place{the returning-line place} where a hand had gone in on Seed 15 and had not come out, and the three coverings --- the stayed frost, the skinned trough and the silt-clouded plate on \place{the flat stone by the well} --- stayed weather entered nowhere with a finger between every pair. The river kept its note under its winter skin, a covering put on by the cold and taken off by the cold, found where put and not spent on the baking and not made a sign of the last of \lore{the Five}. The clean dark line at the Vail corner kept its hairline beside it unswept under the thin light, and the loaf cooled on the boards without a figure and without a handful, and the boy kept the neighbour's called-gone grey on its own place beside the left-in-place ridge with a finger between and neither judging the other.

Under the floor the carried fire answered four low pulls with the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer unnamed and its fifth unpaid three hundred and ninety-four days, and nobody lifted the floor and nobody supplied the pull. The seventh pull stood where it had always stood, gathering toward a day when \lore{the Hearth} would be named for what it is, a wound that a district keeps warm and asks no question of the payer; but that day had not come and no name was spoken over the gap here, and the fifth stayed as unsupplied as the first day the fire came up the yard. The heard bell stayed in its square beside the told bell, four, four and a gap, and the told bell's gap stayed in its own square and was not folded into the fire's four and a gap or the boards' four and four and a knot or the twelve arithmetics behind one unsigned figure, and no bell was rung in the yard on this day. Three counts in three places stayed three, because agreeing any two would want a payer put under both and no payer stood. Six hearings of \lore{the full note} stood beside and never among with the seventh place empty and \place{the Drawing house}'s small flat knock outside them on its own place, and no story's gap was made a hearing and no fourth likeness made a cause.

Up the nineteen steps again at the half-pace she carried him, and set him under the roof and never in \place{the Debt Loft}, and put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face and added no second fold. Overhead the boards kept four nails on the near board, four on the next, and a thumb-width knot where a fifth might have been driven and was not, and the scrape of the loft came from above and behind and went off again without a figure put to it. \place{The towel} came out at the head of \place{the bed} as instruments come out on a towel when a case is done and before the next is called, and he went over the field by the place of each thing and left every gap a gap: the crust's gap, the shelf's two gaps, the gone pan's round ring, \place{the field's empty place}, the keeping at the fold found where put and still a finding, the told bell's gap, the silence after the name from \place{Weighing}, the place the remembering had not filled, \person{Tarn}'s stopped ear-sentence at the very end of a term already ended, \person{Jorra Vail}'s promise under \place{the Vail wheel-shed} neither given nor withdrawn, Garl Vail's unforged collector sentence beside the seventh notch with no name on the man who turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on the debt. Each lay on its own place with a finger between and none stood up to call itself a rule, for a rule is an account that has found a payer and none stood.

Answer three hundred and sixty-three was filed exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the twelve readings, the bell's gap, the flat knock, the child's grey fingertips, the mother's cup, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the held breath, the two bites of iron, the river's skin, the widow's mark, the fourth direction nor the keeping at the fold. The count came to three hundred and ninety-four exact, six months and two hundred and fourteen days told by nobody who would have known what a number counted, and the carried fire's fifth was unpaid the same number and Garl's name was unspoken the same number, and the two stood side by side without becoming one. The surgeon took his small clean satisfaction in a field taken as found and left as found, every object in its place and nothing laid across anything else, and it cost him nothing, which was how he knew it was not peace; peace in the old life had always arrived with something spent, a shift ended, a hand washed, a chart signed, and this cost nothing because nobody had been asked to pay. Under it, louder, the infant wanted the crust lifted, the thread wiped and a hand on the pot handle, and got none of the three. The clean dark line at the Vail corner kept its finer grey beside it unswept under the last of the year's light, the loaf stood on the boards without a figure, and the hand outside the wool came down and laid itself flat on the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking, with the thread frozen and unwiped and the crust's corner standing a hair off the wool.
