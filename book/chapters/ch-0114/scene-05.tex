\daymark{395}{Ilmen 1, Y.H. 814, Firstday}

The hand lay flat on the fold and the small hours went over \place{Low Kiln} in \place{Sill}, \place{Grath}, the cold at the window doing its slow work with nobody lifting the floor to supply what was wanting below it. \person[Corin]{The boy} lay at the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf} under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof, with one stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust two inches from his ear and its corner standing a hair off the wool, and with the empty place beside it holding the fold found where it had been put. Morning stood beyond the door as a new ten stands beyond a man's counting, and he carried three hundred and ninety-four exact into it the way a surgeon carries an instrument across a threshold and does not set it down. Nothing in the night had been signed. The house kept its days in loaves and glances and the space where a loaf had stood; he kept his in numbers nobody under this roof would have known what to call, and he held the two counts on separate places with a finger between, because agreeing any two wants a payer put under both and none stood.

Before first light the surgeon took the body in its old order, as he had taken it under the going-out light and would take it again when the day was grown: crust, gap, thread, cold, the waiting past the warming. Cold had come to cheek and temple and the small bare joint at the wrist where the wool rode back, and stopped there as at the end of a line written out for it; \lore{the Afterdrop} waited past a rewarming nobody in this house would perform. He knew a face composed by cold is not composure, and that pink returning is the surface still lying over a cold core, and that the slow outside warming he would choose for a patient --- level, out of the draught, never hurried, never rubbed --- was the very thing this basket could not ask for. Under the exactness the infant wanted the crust lifted two inches, the thread at his mouth wiped, and a hand on \place{the pot board}'s handle. Both were true. The second was louder. Neither won, and no verdict was looked for; the quarrel was the work.

Below the floor \lore{the carried fire} breathed four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood. Nobody lifted the floor and nobody supplied the pull; the payer had no name under this roof and no figure anywhere, and the fifth was unpaid three hundred and ninety-five days, which the boy counted because he could not stop counting and entered on no slate because no hand here would take a figure from him. The fire that kept the room from going cold had come up the yard from a source nobody named or priced, and he kept it as a finding and not a cause, for a cause is an account that has found a payer and none stood. The tend began again at Firstday, which was a fact of the Court's counting and not an act of anybody under this roof; no boundary was drawn on the floor, no signature stood on the space, and the reset supplied no payer to an old account or a new one. The year's first arithmetic stood nine mornings off at \place{Ilmen 10}, and he did not bring it into a morning that had not earned it.

\person{Sella Vail} came to \place{the hearth} before the basket, as she had come at the going of the light the night before, and told the coals it was \lore{the thaw} in the voice another woman would have used for a child who could not hear her. She turned them with the back of a broken pot, and the small red eye under the ash showed and went dark and showed again. A small intake, a hold, and a letting-go toward no opening, with neither gap figured; her finger along the top of the shelf kept the charcoal where it lay unread; her lips moved on a two-gapped row and figured neither gap. One glance went to the clay jar cold under \lore{lead glaze} beyond the board, the sweetish edge of it coming and going on a wrong wind, and her hand shut half over nothing, the little-door burn on the back of it closing on air. The twice-mended pot went up thumb-first where the wire crossed the crack, the hairline of the original break showing between the older brass and the tighter wire, and both proverbs went to the pot and the fire and never to a person: \emph{Leave the crack showing. People trust a thing they can see has been handled}; and the other about mending twice, the second time being the real one, improved in the keeping and very likely not as good as the man had said it. She turned the dressed grain under wool with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child, with no figure over it and no handful lifted, and set the pot rim two inches beyond the reaching hand. There was a half-beat at the setting and no look toward \place{the bed}. The boy filed the half-beat as the distance between intention and arrest, bought at nothing and not a gift; the surgeon's hand that had once done the same thing with a clamp had known that interval by a different name, and the name bought nothing here either. \person{Garl Vail}'s coat kept its salt-stiff crease at the left shoulder on its peg behind the door, and his name was not spoken in the grammar of this morning.

Later, when the thin light had come properly up off the yard, she carried him down the nineteen steps at the half-pace she keeps from every open slate, and the instruction went to the wool and the river wind and never to the basket. Out in \place{the Clay Yard} the neighbours were burning the grey that \lore{the Last Day} had left in the corners. It had been swept in the small hours into a low ridge along the wall, and a woman with a rake fed it into a shallow heap on the bare clay; a man turned it with a stick and set a coal to it, and the grey took with a dry small crinkle and a smell like wet stone heated too fast. A thread of smoke went up straight in the morning cold and then lay almost flat along the ground. It was not a bonfire and nobody made it one. A neighbour with her sleeves pulled down over her hands looked at the heap and said, “There, then. Gone and dealt with.” Another said the yard could sleep easier of a morning now. Nobody said what the grey had been before it was swept, and nobody put a figure to what the burning cost. The woman with the rake noticed the ridge still lying along the Vail wall and looked toward Sella with the basket at her knee. “You'll have that too, Widow, now the rest is up,” she said, and the man with the stick rested on it and waited for the answer, because a woman who keeps a fire is a woman who might be expected to say when a thing is finished. Sella told the broom where to stand, in the tone she used for a child being told where to put its hands: go around what the water left, go around the line, it cost enough to come in and it will cost more to take out. She gave no answer to the woman and none to the man, and neither pressed her, for the house had said what it meant to say and a person who keeps a fire is allowed to keep the rest of her sentences. The rake went back to the heap and the stick turned it once, and the small dry smell went along the ground with the flat smoke.

The boy watched the heap from the low angle of the basket and took it as work done and not as a thing decided. The grey that had been gathered was gone where it had been gathered; that much was plain to the eye and he granted it. The grey at \place{the Vail corner} was not gathered and not gone, and the clean dark line beside it had kept its finer hairline through the dry cold without a hand on it, and he granted that too. He did not set the two grants against each other and call the pair an account, because an account wants a payer standing under it and none stood in the yard this morning. The burning cost something in coal and in the morning's breath of the woman with the rake, and neither figure was spoken; what the yard called dealt with was a heap that had ceased to smoke, and the boy left the saying where he found it, on its own place with a finger between it and the unswept ridge.

She went around the seven walked-around places with a look and a wide step and no number, once and not twice, and widest around \place{the returning-line place} where a hand had gone in on Seed 15 and had not come out. She did not sweep \place{the Vail corner}. The clean dark line kept its finer grey beside it called the thaw, thickened in the dry cold with the faint sour tang of \lore{Ash} coming up where no broom had gone in two years, and the ridge stood where a hand had left it and where the yard had gone around it for a year and more. The boy kept the neighbour's called-gone grey on its own place beside the left-in-place ridge with a finger between, and did not let either stand in for the other. A thing called finished was not finished for that; the fine grey beside the clean dark line had not been burnt and had not been named, and the Ash's memory was not spent, explained or promoted into a sign. He had once signed a chart that said a wound was closed when the surface had only come together over a cold cavity, and the memory of that came up now without being asked and went back without a payer, borrowing no house count and buying no lesson.

The three coverings --- the stayed frost, the skinned trough and the silt-clouded plate on \place{the flat stone by the well} --- stayed weather entered nowhere with a finger between every pair, and \place{the river} kept its note of \lore{water over water} beneath \lore{the winter skin}, a covering put on by the cold and taken off by the cold, found where put and not spent on the burning. The neighbour with the rake said something about a clean sweep for the new year, and Sella let the saying stand where it stood as she let the frost and the trough stand, and did not answer the year. Through \place{the finger's-width door} the boy heard the low note going under the skin and heard it come back nearer than it went, and he left it on its own place. It was not a hearing and not a sign, and the note had done before any hand in the room came down.

Up the nineteen steps she carried him at the half-pace, set him under the roof and never in \place{the Debt Loft}, and the instruction went to the wool, the pot, the fire and the river wind and never to the basket. The household went back to its grammar as a pot goes back to the same place on a board: fire before basket, coals told the thaw and turned with the broken pot's back, the small intake and hold and letting-go toward no opening with neither gap figured, the finger along the shelf where the charcoal lay unread, the lips on the two-gapped row, the one glance at the jar with the hand half over nothing. The twice-mended pot went up thumb-first again and the hairline showed again and both proverbs went to the pot and the fire and never to a person, as though repair were a thing done at the same hour every day and not a thing decided. The dressed grain lay covered under wool with no figure over it and no handful lifted, and the rim stood two inches beyond the reaching hand with the half-beat and no look toward the bed.

\lore{The widow's mark} hung where it hung on its cord of working thumbs and she did not touch it. The burning of a yard's grey was not an opening; the Firstday of a new year had not made an eighth due; seven openings in seven months were seven and not a rule, and a rule is an account that has found a payer and none stood. The charcoal beneath the loaf shelf stayed unread beneath her finger. Overhead \place{the boards} kept four nails on the near board, four on the next, and a thumb-width knot where a fifth might have been driven and was not, and the scrape of the loft came from above and behind and went off again with no figure put to it. \lore[the Five]{The Five} had had names and no tend-day and no market; the tend had Firstday again now, and nothing in this room stood up between the two and called itself a gate.

When the burnt smell had gone off the yard and the morning's work was put by, \place{the towel} came out at the head of the bed as instruments come out when a case is done and before the next is called. He went over the field by the place of each thing with the surgeon's exactness and left every gap a gap: the crust's gap, the shelf's two gaps, the gone pan's round ring, \place{the field's empty place}, the keeping at the fold found where put and still a finding, the told bell's gap in its square, the silence after the name from \place{Weighing}, the place \place{the Remembering} had not filled, the neighbour's called-gone grey and the left-in-place ridge on two places, the stopped ear-sentence at the end of a term already ended, the promise under the wheel-shed neither given nor withdrawn, Garl Vail's unforged collector sentence beside the seventh notch. Each lay where it had been laid with a finger between, and none stood up to call itself a rule. He did not lay the twelve old findings across a new figure to make a row come out even, and he did not bring the first arithmetic of the year into a day that had not earned it. The twelve arithmetics of Y.H. 813 rode across into Y.H. 814 unchanged behind one unsigned figure: twelve were twelve and not one account, the figure read was still not the figure paid, and the reset of the tend supplied no payer to them or to any new one.

Answer three hundred and sixty-five was filed exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the twelve readings, the bell's gap, the flat knock, the child's grey fingertips, the mother's cup, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the held breath, the two bites of iron, the river's skin, the widow's mark, the fourth direction nor the keeping at the fold. Her hand put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face and added no second fold; the wool lay where a hand had put it on the fifteenth, found again and not made again, with no third crust, no name and no payer. The surgeon took the body once more in its old order and found it as he had found it: crust gone harder in the dry heat with its corner a hair off the wool, thread frozen and unwiped because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread, cold stopped at cheek and temple and bare wrist-joint, the Afterdrop waiting past a warming nobody would perform. The new year had come on its own morning and none of the three wants had been answered by it. The infant wanted the crust lifted, the thread wiped and a hand on the pot handle. Both registers were true; the second was louder; neither won, and the quarrel went on being the work.

The count came to three hundred and ninety-five exact, six months and two hundred and fifteen days told by nobody who would have known what a number counted, and the carried fire's fifth was unpaid the same number and Garl's name was unspoken the same number, and the two stood side by side without becoming one. Under the floor the fire breathed four low pulls with the silence where the fifth should have stood, its giver without a name and its source advancing toward a day that had not come; here it was only the warmth under the house, kept as a finding and not a cause. The surgeon took his small clean satisfaction in a field taken as found and left as found, every object in its place and nothing laid across anything else, and it cost him nothing, which was how he knew it was not peace. Peace in the old life had always come with something spent, a shift ended, hands washed, a chart signed; this cost nothing because nobody had been asked to pay.

The yard's thin skin of grey was gone from the corners it had been swept toward, and the fine grey beside the clean dark line at the Vail corner was where a hand had left it. Nothing had been opened, joined, named, called, paid, forgiven or ended, and no hand had drawn a boundary across the morning. Beyond the finger's-width door the river went on under its winter skin, water over water, and the hand outside the wool came straight down and laid itself flat against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking, with the year turned and nothing signed, forward, not closed.
