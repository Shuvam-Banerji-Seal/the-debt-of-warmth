\daymark{279}{Frost 10, Tenthday, Y.H. 813}

\dropcap{T}{he hand} lay back against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. Outside the wool the small hours went on at their own pace under the roof of \place{the Vail kiln-house} in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill}, \place{Grath}, and \person[Corin]{the boy} lay carried, awake and silent, with the hundred and thirty-second answer put down behind him the way a pot is set on a shelf where nobody will knock it. Two hundred and seventy-eight stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in \place{Sarn}; six months and ninety-eight days were told by nobody; the fire's fifth was unpaid the same number of days with its payer unnamed. The hand had gone up an inch toward the river's note, taken the small intake of the river's own going and the hold of one counted breath from the room whose floor does not touch his, set them side by side with a finger between, borrowed no house count and supplied no fifth to the kiln, and come down on the next low note. That was done. It was not recalled as growth, a gift, a reaching or a sign; it was a thing put away in its place, and the place was the sixth, and the sixth was a place and not a power. Under the floor \lore{the carried fire} kept its four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood, and the lean quarter of its warmth stayed where it was with nobody to lift the floor and nobody to supply the pull.

He took the body as it presented itself, in the order the facts arrived, because that was the only order he had ever found trustworthy. One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from his ear, gone past the point where a child would eat it, and the empty place beside it kept its name to itself; no third had come in the night to make the row come out even. The thread of drool at his mouth had frozen in the dry heat and was unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. Cold had come to cheek and temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, and stopped there as at the end of a line written out for it, with neither an order to go forward nor an order to go away. The afterdrop waited beyond all three, past a warming nobody in this house would perform. He knew the composed face that is not composure; he knew a cold patient cannot clot and will seem better before he is worse, and that a man must be warmed from the outside and slowly. He wanted the crust lifted the two inches to his mouth. Both were true, and in a body six months and ninety-nine days old the second was louder; the surgeon took the inventory in its old order --- crust, gap, thread, cold, the waiting --- and entered none of it, not even on the place where he kept the things he did not enter. He had once reduced a shoulder in the dark by feel alone and now could not close two inches on a pot handle; the thought came and went without bitterness, which was its own small betrayal.

Three counts stood in three places and none rose into another. Through the finger's-width door the river kept its note of \lore{water over water}, a low thing over a lower thing, never numbered among the hearings; under the floor the carried fire gave four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer without a name, and nobody lifted the floor and nobody supplied the pull; overhead \place{the boards} kept four nails on the near board, four on the next, and a thumb-width knot where the fifth nail should have stood. Agreeing any two would want a payer put under both and no payer stood to any. No fire-sitter stood over the coals and no house count came to be borrowed. Six hearings of \lore{the full note} stood beside and never among, five from four sources and the sixth, the small flat knock out of a woman as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go and never with the things that came from vessels; the empty place kept its emptiness and none was invented to fill it. The heard bell's four, four and a gap stayed in \place{the Cross} beside the told bell of \lore{the Long Night}, not brought under this roof on a market morning, not folded into the three and not heard as a seventh of the full note.

Then the count crossed from two hundred and seventy-eight to two hundred and seventy-nine the way a board laid beside a board gives a dry knock and asks nothing of the seam. Two hundred and seventy-nine stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody; six months and ninety-nine days were told by nobody; the fire's fifth was unpaid the same number of days with its payer unnamed. The house kept its days in loaves, glances and unnamed spaces, and gave nobody a number to carry. Even the crossing made no sound anybody could have entered. The small hours went on, and the room whose floor did not touch his gave its low counting without a word addressed to the basket --- the small intake, the hold, the letting-go --- and the boy took only what the hand could take and left the letting-go where it was.

\person{Sella Vail} came to \place{the hearth} before she came to anything else, as she had come to it on the mornings behind and would come to it on the mornings ahead. The little-door burn on the back of her left hand closed on nothing as she reached the coals, and the grey streak at her temple showed pale in the low light. She told them it was \lore{the thaw}. \emph{You'll go when you go}, she told them, and turned them with the back of a broken pot, and said \emph{The house will keep it} where another woman would have said \emph{I}. Her instruction went to the fire, the wool, the pot, the broom and the running water and never to the basket. \lore{The widow's mark} hung where it hung on its cord at her throat, and her hand did not go to it at a morning and in a basket, because neither is an opening and the hand goes to the mark before the kiln and at no other hour. The first firing of Frost stood behind the household with its face turned away; she did not look at it again to make a lesson of it, and four openings were four and not a rule, and the next stood in the first firing of a later month, far off, not hoped for and not counted toward. At \place{the loaf shelf} her finger stayed on top of the front edge so the charcoal beneath remained unread, and her lips moved along a row standing with two gaps and gave neither gap a figure; she glanced once at the clay jar standing cold under its \lore{lead glaze} and shut her hand half over nothing. She set the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face, left the thread frozen where it lay, and stood the pot rim two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat and no look toward \place{the bed}. She gave no instruction at any hour to the basket. He was set under the roof and never in \place{the Debt Loft}; this was a keeping and not a kindness anybody had named.

Beyond the finger's-width door the stayed frost lay white on the north lip of \place{the yard well}, where the yard had left it white on Frost 8 and had gone on calling it weather on Frost 9, and the plate of ice that Sella had lifted off the skin on \place{the trough} stood on its flat stone out of the path of feet, clouded where the silt had risen into it. Nobody had given either a figure and nobody had carried either away. The surgeon's reading of a skin laid over a depth stayed on a place of its own beside the gone white of Frost 4, with a finger between; he made no fourth likeness of the three and no cause of any two, and the first light coming up over \place{the Clay Yard} did not pay any of it. Out at \place{the rack end} the dressed grain lay covered and unfigured under wool on the cool stone, turned twice the day before because the stone had gone cold and turned with a lighter touch each time, and given no figure at any turning. Three readings of Smoke stood behind it with their true subtractions locked in \person[Berr Clay]{Berr}'s box going up \place{the clay road}, and the printed one in twelve stood unchallenged; the figure read was still not the figure paid, and the space between the figure that would be read over the covered grain and what that grain had cost had no signature on it. Nothing under this roof had opened the box or copied the slate, and nothing under this roof would.

Frost 10 came on in its own light and was not brought into the day behind it. No figure from the reading had reached the small hours. The market was a direction beyond the door and the visits were coming, and neither was a date spoken under this roof; Frost 20 stood eight mornings ahead and was not brought into a morning that had not earned it. The light went first along the well's white lip, then across \place{the flagstones}, then in under the shelf where the boy lay, thin and cold and exact as a blade laid on a towel. The kiln answered in fours with its gap and not a bell. He had counted the day before without being told what the count was for, and he counted this morning without asking anybody to tell him; the wanting of a number spoken went on under the not-expecting the way a coal goes on under ash, and there was nobody in the room to hear it and nobody who would have known what a number counted if it had been spoken.

\person{Garl Vail} stayed in objects and not in a story, his name unspoken under this roof two hundred and seventy-nine days: the salt-stiff coat with the crease at the left shoulder on its peg behind the door, the wheel-seat in the shed keeping its twenty-year lean and its hollow on that side, the clean dark line at the corner with the finer grey beside it unswept, and the debt against a firing of tiles neither paid nor settled nor done with, and worse for being uncalled. His sayings lived in the house's mouth, spoken to the pot and the fire and never to a person. The unforged sentence about a man sent for a collector who turned back at the frozen ford lay where it had been laid at the kindling, with no name on the man and no figure on the debt. No lesson had been made of any of it, and the light coming up the yard did not make one now.

On \place{the towel} at the head of the bed the file lay as it had been laid in the small hours of the day behind, open with every gap left gap and every tray parted from its neighbour, each thing known by its weight before the eye fell on it, exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air. One hundred and thirty-two honest answers stood on the sixth place, each an object and not a power, and the hundred and thirty-second lay uppermost where the hand had put it down. The surgeon knew the value of a clean coordination the way he had once known the value of a reduction done in the dark without a light to look at: it was right, and it bought nothing, and the two inches between the hand and the crust did not shorten because the answer was right. He had learned that early and had not yet learned to be indifferent to it.

The hand went up an inch toward the river's note. It took the small intake of the river's own going and carried it across the little silence left by one of the counted breaths in the room whose floor does not touch his, and came down on the river's next low note. One house rhythm had gone into a day that would be full of figures by noon; the interval was given no figure, no house count was borrowed, no fifth was supplied to the kiln, and the little silence was carried across and not filled. The surgeon felt the small correctness of it, the intake going over the gap in its own order and coming down where it belonged; the boy wanted the crust lifted two inches to his mouth; both were true, and the second was louder, and the checking of the one by the other was the work and he did not call it a virtue. The answer came down on the sixth place as one hundred and thirty-three, beside one hundred and thirty-two with a finger between, exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air. It was not promoted into a reach, a hearing, a seventh, a payment, a held change, a key, a power, a doctrine or a rule, and he made nothing of it and would not let it stand up as a gift, because a gift wants a taker and nobody under this roof had taken anything.

The hand laid back against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. Beyond the finger's-width door the first light came up on a market the basket had not yet been carried to, and the frost lay white on the well lip where the yard had left it, called weather and entered nowhere. The crust lay two inches from his ear with its empty place beside it unnamed, and there was no third.
