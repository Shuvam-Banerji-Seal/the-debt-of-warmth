## Ground rules held in every scene

- **Body inventory, in the old order every time:** one stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust two inches from the ear, gone past the point where a child would eat it, its empty place unnamed and no third; the thread of drool frozen in the dry heat and unwiped, because nobody under any roof has ever been given an instruction about the thread and a basket set down is a pot set down and left; cold stopped at cheek, temple and the bare wrist-joint where the wool rides back, as at the end of a line written out for it; the afterdrop waiting past a warming nobody in this house will perform. He knows the composed face that is not composure, that a cold patient cannot clot and will seem better before he is worse, and that a man must be warmed from the outside and slowly. He wants the crust lifted two inches. Both are true; in this body the second is louder; he enters neither, not even on the place where he keeps the things he does not enter (T060/T086).
- **Three counts in three places:** the carried fire's four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should stand under the floor, its payer unnamed; the river's *water over water* at the finger's-width door; the boards' four nails, four and a thumb-width knot. No fire-sitter supplies the fifth and no house count is borrowed; agreeing any two would want a payer put under both and none stands (T088, T061/T082). The heard bell's four, four and a gap stays in the square beside the told bell of the Long Night, not folded into the three and not a seventh of the full note (T078).
- **Six hearings of the full note** stand beside and never among, five from four sources and the sixth, the small flat knock out of Dessa Silt as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go and never with the things that came from vessels.
