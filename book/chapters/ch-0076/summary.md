## Chronicle

On Frost 10 the district's arithmetic changed at the Cross and could not be changed back. Berr Clay wiped his thumb twice, passed a finger over a figure, read *one in twelve, as the notice says* low into the two hundred, called the levy and denied it --- *your house*, never *I* --- and went off to High Kiln with Frost's first true subtraction locked in his box, believing he had done a kindness. The bell went four, four and a gap and nobody crossed himself. Hessa Rale carried his slate up the road unread; the soft-cuffed clerk sat below the north bridge for a measure nobody called.

The reading knew nothing of what it read over. It went over the basket in the moot-post's shade and left no figure in it, answered nothing for the man carried out on Day 188 or the hand that went in on Seed 15. The dressed grain lay covered under wool on the cool stone, read over by a number that did not know what it cost, the space between figure and price unsigned. One gesture ran through the square at different scales: a thane's finger over a figure, a runner's hand to a slate, a clerk's number entered before his eyes found the buyer, a widow carrying a basket at a half-pace. Each hand went to its object first.

The two mornings after were ordinary: no market, no Cross, no bell. Sella went to the fire before the basket, said *The house will keep it*, gave neither gap of the row a figure, turned the dressed grain beneath the wool and set the pot rim two inches beyond the reaching hand. Ilva drew water at the well, her hand toward her sleeve, and answered only *Ask the pot what it cost. I keep the count, not the account.* Tarn kept six where the house could check and the seventh at the lip, stopped the ear-sentence short of calling the loan; Jorra kept dusty forearms from clean and withheld his knowing. The frost lay white on the well lip and the plate of ice stood on its stone; the yard called both weather.

Through three days the hand rose an inch six times and came down six times on the river's note --- a rhythm carried across a silence, the heard bell's gap beside a breath, a letting-go left whole, two intakes bridged, a silence kept empty, the held interval beside the empty one --- each filed exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air, not a reach, not a hearing, not a gift. At the close two hundred and eighty-one stood unspent and spoken by nobody; six months and one hundred and one days were told by nobody; the fire's fifth was unpaid the same number of days, its payer unnamed. The file lay open on the towel with every gap left gap, the crust two inches off, and the river's note went quiet.

- **Corin/Dan:** body unchanged in the old order --- one stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust two inches from the ear with its empty place unnamed and no third, the thread frozen in the dry heat and unwiped, cold stopped at cheek, temple and the bare wrist-joint, the afterdrop waiting past a warming nobody here will perform; he wants the crust lifted and cannot close two inches on the pot handle, both true and the second louder. - **Where everyone is and what they want:** Sella Vail has set him on his bed under the roof, not the loft, put the fold right without a look at his face, left the thread frozen, told the fire it was the thaw, turned the coals with the back of a broken pot, kept her finger above the charcoal, moved her lips along the two-gapped row without a figure, glanced once at the clay jar and shut her hand half over nothing, set the pot rim two inches out with a half-beat and no look toward the bed, and never instructed the basket; the widow's mark hangs untouched at a going of the light, for that is not an opening. She wants the house kept whole by its not-doings. Tarn at the kindling keeps six checkable and the seventh at the lip beside Garl's unforged sentence, stops the ear-sentence at the word that would call the loan (a remembered **ninth** stopping, no tenth before the seventh year at its very end and still ungiven), draws the Seed 16 breath unspent, chest tight, Ash in the knuckles, pale clip-ring at the wrist, his stroke over the bright patch without the half-check, the loft stone cold and unreconciled by not trying, which is not forgiveness; he wants the paying put off though it stands in the ruts. Jorra at the shed edge parts dusty forearms from clean, speaks to the wheel and a pot before any person, withholds the note he knows three turns ahead, puts his bent finger into the cold damper ahead of Sella's sentence, gives the back-room men no name, keeps burned slate at four, shut-room tellings at two, three pot sayings with the premature Smoke 14 note apart, the row at five askings with one shaping and no witness, *he's not Vail* sealed and the promise neither given nor withdrawn. Garl stays in objects with his name unspoken **281** days, the salt coat, hollow wheel-seat, clean dark line with its finer grey thaw unswept and the tile debt neither paid nor settled nor done with and worse for being uncalled; no collector came up or down the clay road on Frost 10, 11 or 12. Ilva was glimpsed once, on Frost 11 (Day 280), at the yard well with clay pale in her nail crescents and rack dust in one thumb seam, her hand toward her sleeve and back into it, answering the question put and not the one behind it; her ninth hand stays unread, no tenth was filed, and she does not know she was read.
