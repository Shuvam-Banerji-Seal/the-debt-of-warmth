\daymark{272}{Frost 3, Thirdday, Y.H. 813}

Frost came on its own morning, earned by the light in the door, and was not brought into a day that had not yet earned it. There was no market and no Cross and no bell on a Thirdday; the first firing stood behind the household with no lesson made of it, no thing mended that anybody would name as mended, and the next opening stood in the first firing of a later month, far off and not hoped for. The count went from two hundred and seventy-one to two hundred and seventy-two the way a man lays one board beside another without measuring the seam, six months and ninety-two days told by nobody under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof in \place{Low Kiln}. Under the floor \lore{the carried fire} kept its four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer carrying no name, the fifth unpaid two hundred and seventy-two days running.

\person[Corin]{The boy} lay at the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf} in the room that did not touch the room of the fire, carried and awake and silent, and took the body in the order the facts presented themselves, as a surgeon takes a field in the order the wounds present themselves. One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from his ear, gone past the point where a child would have eaten it, and the empty place beside it kept its name to itself; no third had come in the night to make the row come out even. The thread of drool at his mouth had frozen in the dry heat and was unwiped, because nobody under any roof in \place{Sarn} had ever been given an instruction about the thread and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. Cold had come to cheek and temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, and stopped there as at the end of a line written out for it, with neither an order to go forward nor an order to go away. The afterdrop waited beyond all three, past a warming nobody in this house would perform. He knew the composed face that is not composure; he knew a cold patient cannot clot and will seem better before he is worse. He wanted the crust lifted the two inches to his mouth. Both were true, and in a body two hundred and seventy-two days old the second was the louder; the surgeon took the inventory and let the boy want, and entered neither, not even on the place where he kept the things he did not enter. He had once reduced a shoulder in the dark by feel alone and now could not close two inches on a pot handle.

The room smelled of hot clay cooled under tallow, of wet wool, and under those of the sour breath of the unswept corner called \lore{the thaw}, which \person{Sella Vail} had told the coals and left standing where it stood. Through the finger's-width door the river kept its note of \lore{water over water}, a low thing over a lower thing. The kiln answered in fours and withheld its fifth, not a bell; the bell's four, four and a gap stayed in the square where it struck and was not brought under this roof on a Thirdday. Overhead \place{the boards} kept four nails on the near board, four on the next, and a thumb-width knot where the fifth nail should have stood. Three counts in three places, and none rose into another, because agreeing any two would want a payer put under both and no payer stood to any. There was no fire-sitter to supply the fifth and no house count to borrow. Six hearings of \lore{the full note} stood beside and never among, five from four sources and the sixth, the small flat knock out of a woman as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go and never with the things that came from vessels; no seventh stood in the empty place and none was invented to fill it, for an empty place kept empty is a finding and a finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged.

The boy did not make a doctrine of the four months' openings. He kept the not-making of yesterday's firing as a finding with no payer, on a place of its own with a finger between it and the house's \lore{not-doings} and a finger between it and his own kept gaps --- the missing sixth hearing, the tenth stopping that had not come, the two inches of air between his hand and a pot handle. A joining would want a payer and none stood, and he would not let the finding stand up as a lesson.

Sella went to the fire before she went to the basket and before any instruction. Every instruction went to the fire and the wool and the pot and the broom and the running water, and never to the basket; she said \emph{The house will keep it} where another woman would have said \emph{I}, and \emph{the boy} where another would have said my son. At \place{the hearth} the pot rim went out with its handle two inches beyond the reaching hand, with a half-beat at the setting and no look toward \place{the bed}. At the loaf shelf her finger stayed on top of the front edge so the charcoal tally on the underside remained unread, and her lips moved along a row standing with two gaps and gave neither gap a figure. She put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face, and left the thread where it was. \lore{The widow's mark} hung where it hung on its cord at her throat, and she did not touch it at a morning, a hearth or a shelf, because none of these is an opening and the hand goes to the mark before the kiln and at no other hour. She pushed coal-black hair back with the wrist that carries the little-door burn, and the one grey streak at her temple showed pale off the coals.

\scenebreak

Out on \place{the Clay Yard} the dressed grain lay covered on the cool stone at \place{the rack end}, away from the hearth. Sella lifted a corner of the wool with two fingers and turned the grain once beneath it for damp with a touch lighter than the one she had used the day before, as a surgeon turns a wound to see the part that lies hidden, and drew the wool back over it and set it down where it had lain. The grain was changed in state and given no figure. Smoke's third arithmetic stood behind and unread in a thane's locked box gone up \place{the clay road}, and the figure that would be read over the grain in the days to come did not know what the turning cost or what the wool had kept from it; the space between the figure and the price stayed unsigned, with no mark on it and no hand to put one there. She gave no instruction to the basket and did not look toward the work's edge.

She carried him at the half-pace from every open slate and went wide around seven things with a look and a wide step and no number: the clean dark line at the Vail corner and the finer grey hairline called the thaw beside it, the space beneath \place{the lowest rack} where the broken birth-slates lay, the round ring where the shallow clay pan had stood with no scrape, crumb, print, chip or word, the bare dark \place{the trough} beside \place{the yard well} with its water called weather, the field's empty place out on \place{the worked strips}, and the returning-line place where a hand had gone in on Seed 15 and had not come out. Seven were seven and not an account, because an account wants a payer standing under it and none stood. The hand in the returning line was carried and not named, not paid and not joined to the shelf's two gaps, the fold's one crust or the dressed grain under wool. She did not touch the widow's mark at a rack or a well or a road, because a rack, a well and a road are not openings.

\person{Garl Vail}'s debt against a firing of tiles went around without a name, neither paid nor settled nor done with, and worse for being uncalled; his name had not been spoken under this roof for two hundred and seventy-two days. The salt-stiff coat kept his broader shape on its peg and the proverb said over a corner where the repair had been rubbed out went on being said and not mended. Garl was kept in objects and not in a story, a keeping that was maintenance and not an argument, and no lesson was made of any of it.

At \place{the kindling} \person{Tarn} had the small knife at the very lip of its sill, six counted in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check and the seventh at the lip where it could not. Garl's unforged collector sentence lay beside it: a man sent for a collector who turned back at the frozen ford, with no name on the man and no figure on the debt. Tarn said \emph{Yes, house} to those who wanted the thaw early and those who wanted it late, agreeing with both so thoroughly that no argument could find a foot, and keeping the late because it puts off the paying, though the paying stood in the ruts. He told the wood \emph{Six counted. The next I don't count} in the tone of a man stating the weather. The sentence about an ear under this roof being worth more than the debt that held it came to his teeth and went as far as the word that would call the loan before stopping there, with no figure and no name --- the ninth stopping remembered, not a tenth stood up before the seventh year at its very end and still ungiven. He drew the breath that should have gone in at the top of the reach and did not spend it, his chest tight as it had been since the dark, \lore{Ash} worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line and no scrubbing lifting it, the pale clip-ring at his left wrist holding what light there was and giving it back. His stroke went on over \place{the flagstones} past the shallow bright patch without the half-check. Up in \place{the Debt Loft}, which came to the boy as a sound with a direction and never as a room he was taken into, the warm stone was cold and unreconciled in its unmarked pot, and the small dry scrape of the sill lay on a place of its own. The loan stayed uncalled and no collector came up the clay road on this Thirdday and none went down it.

Before first grey three or more men went low into the finger's-width-open \place{the back room} and got no name from \person{Jorra Vail}; he did not say the room was cold, and Sella gave the house no name for them either. At the edge of \place{the Vail wheel-shed} he had the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bent little finger and white in the creases of a fist that had opened, not bone-coloured slip. He kept dusty forearms and clean ones on one tray and parted them as a surgeon parts clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had broken nor whether it was a birth-slate. He spoke to the wheel before he spoke to any person --- \emph{It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady} --- and told a pot \emph{You'll do} in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either. He knew the full note that pot would give three turns before it left his hands, with air under it and a want in it, and withheld the knowing on the near-full tray beside the hearings and never among them, advanced and not paid. His bent finger went into the cold \lore{damper} ahead of Sella's unfinished sentence, and the end of it arrived at the pot; he read the iron as one piece with two bites, joined to no kiln and named in no \lore{Grathic} word for both. The burned-slate word stood at four from \place{Slate Row}, a direction over the boards arrived nowhere and not a fifth telling; the two tellings of the counting room shut in the war stood at two and did not become three; three sayings about a pot stood as sayings with the premature Smoke 14 note on its own place and no fourth likeness made a cause; the five askings of the row question stood with one further shaping beside them and no witness under any and no row named. The promise of the boy's name to the men who burn slates was neither given nor withdrawn, and \emph{he's not Vail} stayed sealed as tightly as a lid nobody had tried to lift in three years.

\scenebreak

The morning thinned without ending, the way heat goes out of a pot set off the coals; it drew no boundary and left none behind it. The body held its inventory in the order the facts had presented: one crust two inches off with its empty place unnamed and no third, the thread frozen and unwiped, cold stopped at cheek and temple and the bare wrist-joint, the afterdrop waiting past a warming nobody here would perform. The boy kept the seven places and the Seed 15 hand and the two-gapped row and the fold's one crust as objects on separate places, with a finger between every pair; a joining would want a payer and none stood. He made no cause of the wrong wind or the sweet edge of glaze, no fourth likeness, no eighth of the green or the shape; neither came under this roof on a Thirdday. What the day offered was an ordinary morning with nothing to settle, and the only thing the boy did with it was keep an interval empty.

In the small hours the hand outside the wool went up an inch toward the river's note through the finger's-width door. It took the small intake of a count from the room whose floor did not touch his --- a woman's low counting in the next room, a thing he borrowed only the beginning and the holding of --- and it came down before that count's letting-go. It did not borrow a house count and did not supply the kiln's fifth; it crossed no silence that did not belong to the river and took nothing from the two-gapped row on the shelf. The little silence after the borrowed count ended was left whole and empty on its own place, crossed by no rhythm and filled by no stroke, made into no gift, no hearing, no sixth of anything and no payment. One hundred and nineteen, filed on the sixth place beside one hundred and eighteen with no payer named, exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air: an honest coordination and not a reach, not a hearing, not a seventh of the full note, not a stopping, not one of the held changes, not a key, a power or a rule. He made nothing of it and would not let it stand up as a gift. The restraint was the work, and it was the whole of the day's event, and he did not promote it.

The hand lay back against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. Behind the wall the fire kept four low pulls and withheld its fifth under a floor nobody would lift, the lean quarter of its warmth staying where it was with its payer unnamed toward the far-off accounting of \lore{the Keeping}. The count stood at two hundred and seventy-two, exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in \place{Grath} or \place{Sill}; six months and ninety-two days were told by nobody; the fire's fifth was unpaid the same number of days. The dressed grain lay covered and unfigured at the rack end with the price unsigned, the heard bell stood beside the told in two places, the seventh year stood at its very end and still ungiven, and the widow's mark hung where it hung. Nothing had been opened, paid or named on this Thirdday, and Frost went toward its fourth morning without a boundary drawn and without a lesson made of four months' openings, the next opening standing far off in a later month's first firing. Frost 4 waited in the dark beyond the door, unearned and not brought early.
