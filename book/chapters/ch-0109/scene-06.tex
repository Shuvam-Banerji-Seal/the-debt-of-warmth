In the afternoon \person{Sella Vail} carried him down the nineteen steps from under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof into \place{the Clay Yard}, in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill}, \place{Grath}, at the half-pace she kept from every open slate. The instruction went to the wool, the river wind and the fire she would bank against their coming back, and never to the basket. She set the basket level as a pot that must not be slopped and out of the draught, and at each turn of the landing corrected the tilt before it had time to settle into the wicker, so that the boy felt the small correction under him before he felt the stair. \person[Corin]{The boy} took the order of it as a surgeon takes a field before the light, by the small things first, because the small things do not lie. The infant under the wool wanted the correction to go another way, toward the crust two inches off and the frozen thread at his lip, and got none of it.

She went around the seven walked-around places with a look and a wide step and no number. At \place{the Vail corner} she went around the clean dark line where the ridge had turned and the finer grey hairline beside it that the house called \lore{the thaw}, and swept neither. The dry cold had thickened the line and the faint sour tang of \lore{Ash} came up off it where it had not been swept in two years, and she did not turn her head toward the smell, having settled long ago what the corner was and what it was not. Under \place[the drying racks]{the lowest rack} the broken birth-slates lay entered by nobody, and she went around the space above them as wide as a pail of hot water set where a child might knock it. At \place{the rack end} the round ring where the shallow pan had once stood kept no scrape, no crumb, no print, no chip and no word; the pan had gone out of the house and the ring stayed, which is how a vessel is kept after it is gone. Out on \place{the worked strips} she went around the field's empty place, and around \place{the trough}, and around the silt-clouded plate of ice on \place{the flat stone by the well} that stood a little out of the path of feet. Widest of all she went around the returning-line place where a hand had gone in on \place{Seed} 15 and had not come out, so wide that her elbow cut the corner of the yard and the basket tilted the other way to balance it, and she righted it without looking down, once and not twice.

He took the wide step apart as he took a field apart, by what was there and by what was not. Seven places gone around with a look and no figure; the widest gone around by nothing measurable. A second going-around would have made a figure of once, and a figure wants a payer standing under it, and none stood. The yard kept its places by walking around them; the keeping cost a step each time and nobody entered the steps, and he did not enter them either. A line left where it is is still a line kept. He had learned to read a place by the marks that were not on it, the way he had once read a chart by a note turned face down, and the yard gave him the same small trouble it had always given him: it was exact, and it told him nothing he could spend. The clean dark line kept its finer grey beside it unswept; the broken slates stayed entered by nobody; the round ring at the rack end kept its emptiness without a word written into it. The afternoon had a thin light in it, the kind that shows the shape of a thing without its heat, and the yard went about under it with the carefulness of people who know the cold is only a little way off and has not yet arrived.

\scenebreak

The three coverings stayed weather entered nowhere, with a finger between every pair: the gone white frost off the well's north lip, the skin drawn edge to edge across the trough, and the plate of ice on the flat stone a little out of the path of feet. They were three and not a fourth covering laid over the three and not a sixth low thing set beside the five held objects. Through \place{the finger's-width door} \place{the river} kept its note of \lore{water over water}, a low thing over a lower thing, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming, muffled under \lore{the winter skin} as a voice is muffled by a shut door. That skin the cold had put on and the cold would take off, found where put on the first day of the month and found again this afternoon and not made again, not spent on the going of the light or the day's work and not made to buy a lesson under any roof. No hand had laid the skin, no payer stood under it and no source stood to be asked. A sign is a thing that points to a payer; a covering left where it is is weather and not a promise. He kept the covering on its own place beside the note and put a finger between the two, so that no row came out even by his doing.

That was the whole of the afternoon's weather. No one came over \place{the boards} with a direction, no hare was shifted across a shoulder, no cracked jar asked the price of mending, and the small doings of the yard went about themselves without a hand reaching for a slate. It was not the quiet of an empty place, but the quiet of a place going about its work and leaving its accounts where they lay. The boy took it as a field taken at rest and let it go at that. The infant under the wool was not so easily dismissed: he wanted the crust lifted two inches, the thread wiped, a hand on the pot handle, and the wanting went on under the exactness of the reading as water goes on under ice, with nowhere to go and no leave to stop. The surgeon's small account and the child's small hunger lay in the same basket and did not reconcile, and he had stopped expecting them to, which was not the same as being at ease with it.

At \place{the kindling} the account stood in its old exactness: six pieces in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check, and the seventh at the lip beyond it beside \person{Garl Vail}'s unforged collector sentence, with no name on the man who turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on the debt. The small knife lay at the very edge of its sill, where it could be checked and where a hand could also slip it off. \person{Tarn} passed his hand over blade and sill as a man passes over a tool put back where it belongs. He did not hold the breath at the top of the reach; the hand went up and came away, and the coming away was the whole of the checking.

“Six counted. The next I don't count,” he told the wood, and cut nothing, counted nothing, spent nothing.

The Ash 14 held breath and the \place{Bone} 26 thumb's resting were found where they had been put and not made again, beside the five held objects and the pot-crack resting with a finger between every pair — not a sixth held object, not a seventh, not a tenth stopping, not the calling of the loan, with no payer standing. The pale clip-ring at his left wrist caught what little light there was and gave it back. His chest had been tight since the dark; the Ash was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line, and his stroke went over the shallow bright patch of \place{the flagstones} without the half-check. He said nothing of the tightness, and the house asked nothing, having long ago settled that a clay-bound hand's body was the house's business only when it stopped work.

He brought the ear-sentence up to the word that would call the loan and stopped there, as a remembered ninth stopping with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end, the Seed 16 breath unspent and the loan uncalled. He did not look along the road. Two ears, two silences, a loan that could hear and had never been called, with nobody standing to enter it. The seventh year at its very end was not a payment, a forgiveness or a freedom; it was a man with no house standing where the road ran, and the fear that he would be entered again for vagrancy stayed his, and he kept it as he kept the rest, without a figure on it and without asking anybody to carry it. Garl's name had gone unspoken under this roof three hundred and eighty days and \lore{the carried fire}'s fifth had gone unpaid the same number, and the two counts stood side by side and did not become one. The boy took the equality apart: two ears that had heard, two silences that had kept, and a debt that could be called but had not been, with no hand to call it and no hand to forgive it. The equality held where it had moved to, and he did not lift it out of its place to stand it under a doctrine.

\person{Jorra Vail} came through the finger's-width door off \place{the Vail wheel-shed} with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bent little finger and white in a fist-crease where a hand had opened, and it was not the bone-coloured \lore{slip} of the wheel. Three or more men unnamed had gone low into \place{the back room} before first grey; he gave the house no name for them and no word that the room was cold. Dusty forearms and clean ones he kept on one tray and parted as clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had been broken nor whether it was a birth-slate while the broken ones lay under the lowest rack and were entered by nobody. He spoke to the wheel before he spoke to any person.

“It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady.”

To a pot going past on a bench he said, “You'll do,” in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either, knowing the \lore{full note} that pot would give three turns before it left his hands, with air under it and a want in it, and withholding the knowing from any master come up from \place{High Kiln} who would call it early and sing a pot before its time. The boy kept that knowing on a tray of its own beside the hearings and never among them; a pot's note known before its time was not a hearing and no source stood under it to be asked.

When Sella had not finished telling him the fire was low, his bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper} ahead of her sentence, took the cold out of the fingertip, moved the tongue a hair's breadth and was left standing so that the end of her instruction arrived at the pot and the household straightened around the waiting as cloth straightens when a weight is lifted — one piece of iron with two bites, the holding and the taking, with no \place{Grathic} word covering both mercies. The finger came out without leaving the extra holding of Ash 26 a second time; that holding had been gathered once and stayed where nobody entered it. He did not look at Sella when he did it, and she did not look at him, and neither said what the iron had been asked to carry, because a thing said aloud becomes a thing the Court can enter.

“He's not Vail” stayed sealed as a lid nobody had tried to lift in three years. The promise of the boy's name to the men who burn slates was neither given nor withdrawn, gathering interest against the burned slate at five and the three Ash directions, which stood where put and did not repeat and bought no lesson. The boy filed Jorra's two silences on separate places with a finger between and joined the wheel-shed to nothing under the roof, for a silence kept in one room is not the same silence kept in another, and both want a hand to lay them together and no hand stood.

At the going of the light Sella carried him up the nineteen steps under the roof and never into \place{the Debt Loft}, and set him on \place{the bed} at the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf}. She said nothing of where they had been. She put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face, and did not tuck a second fold into the empty place. Her hand did only what the setting needed and went away; the little-door burn closed on air; the thread was left frozen and unwiped. The wool lay folded once in the crust's empty place exactly where a hand had put it on the fifteenth, found where put again at the going of the light as at each going and each morning since, and not made again.

The boy filed it on a place of its own beside the crust's gap, the two gaps in the shelf in the room whose floor does not touch this one, the gone pan's round ring at the rack end and the field's empty place out on the worked strips, with a finger between it and every other kept thing so that no row came out even by his doing. It was gathered once and not made again: not a joining of rooms, not a sixth held object, not a gift, for a gift wants a giver and nobody gave, not a payment, not a sign, not a hearing, not a mending, not a cause and not a rule. Six findings from a hand that only read had added no seventh placing to the first. A habit wants a hand that placed twice and no such hand had stood; a way wants a third, and both want a payer where none stood. The far-off finger that would one day lay shelf and fold together and call the joining an account was still far off, was not his, and had not come. The account wants a hand to lay the places together and a payer standing under all of them, and no hand stood and no payer stood. The keeping had gathered interest all afternoon without being spent, as dust gathers in a corner nobody sweeps, and a corner is not kept by a hand for all that it is left lying.

Under the roof Sella went to \place{the hearth} before the basket as she had at both ends of every day for three years. She told the coals it was the thaw and turned them with the back of a broken pot, said the house would keep it where another woman would have said I, counted a small intake, a hold and a letting-go toward no opening with neither gap figured, kept her finger along the top of the loaf shelf so the charcoal tally stayed unread, and moved her lips along a two-gapped row and figured neither gap. She glanced once at \place{the clay jar} standing cold under \lore{lead glaze} and shut her hand half over nothing, the little-door burn on the back of her left hand closing on air. On \place{the pot board} the twice-mended pot kept the hairline of the original break showing between the old-brass staple and the tighter wire; her thumb went first where the wire crossed the crack, and both proverbs went to the pot and the fire and never to a person — leave the crack showing, people trust a thing they can see has been handled; if it's worth mending it's worth mending twice, the second time is the real one, improved in the keeping and very likely not as good as the man said them. Repair stayed apart from restoration: a restored thing hides the hand that handled it and a mended thing leaves that hand showing. The dressed grain she turned under wool with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child, with no figure lifted and no handful, and the pot rim she set two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat and no look toward the bed; the half-beat was the distance between intention and arrest, bought at nothing and not a gift. The cord of her \lore{widow's mark} hung untouched at her throat, oiled where a working thumb had touched it ten thousand times. A going of the light and a hearth are not openings; seven openings in seven months are seven and not a rule, and no eighth was made due. Under the floor \lore{the carried fire} gave four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer unnamed three hundred and eighty days, and nobody lifted the floor to supply the pull; overhead \place{the boards} kept four nails on the near one, four on the next and a thumb-width knot where a fifth might have been driven and was not. Three counts in three places, and none rose into another, because agreeing any two would have wanted a payer put under both and no payer stood.

The five held objects stood with the pot-crack resting, the hand-at-the-sill breath and the holding in the damper beside them, each on a place of its own with a finger between every pair. No sixth hearing, seventh opening, sixth held object, sixth telling, third shut-room telling, sixth asking, fifth shaping, tenth hand, tenth stopping or twelfth arithmetic was supplied to make a row come out even, for a finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged. The finding that eleven are not one account sat on a place of its own with the finger between it and every other kept thing, and the keeping at the fold sat beside all of these and was not laid across any of them. The surgeon took his small clean satisfaction in a field taken as found and left as found, and it cost him nothing, which was how he knew it was not peace. The infant wanted the crust lifted two inches, the thread wiped and a hand on the pot handle. Both were true. The second was louder. Neither won, and the quarrel was the work, and he had stopped looking for a verdict in it, which was not the same as having found one.

The hand outside the wool came up an inch toward the low note under the finger's-width door, as a hand goes up in a field it has learned not to disturb. It did not reach, and the inch was not a reach. He took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole on the note's own finishing under the winter skin, let the intake go as the note ended and came straight down and laid itself flat against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. The note had done before the hand came down, so the letting-go was neither early nor stayed, and the hand did not stay.

He filed it as answer three hundred and thirty-six: exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the eleven readings, the bell's gap, the flat knock, the child's grey fingertips, the mother's cup, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the held breath, the two bites of iron, the river's skin, the widow's mark nor the keeping at the fold. The count stood at three hundred and eighty exact, six months and two hundred days told by nobody who would have known what a number counted, the carried fire's fifth unpaid the same number and Garl's name unspoken the same number. Stone kept its nineteenth, twentieth and twenty-first days without a boundary drawn, the deep-winter month holding both its names — older women by weather, slatemen by Account-days — neither set right nor left in so many words. The wool lay folded once in the empty place beside the crust gone harder and browner in the dry cold, its corner a hair off the wool, with no second fold, no third crust and no name spoken over the place, and the thread frozen and unwiped. Eleven arithmetics stood behind one unsigned figure with no signature on the space and no doctrine out of eleven, and the keeping at the fold was not one of them and had not been laid across any of them. The hand lay flat on the fold. The count went on under the roof in Low Kiln, Sill, Grath.
