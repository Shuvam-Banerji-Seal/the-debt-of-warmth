  5. ## How the close pulls into Chapter 110

The final moment leaves the hand laid down on answer **336** and the count at **380 exact** — six months and two hundred days told by nobody — with the fire's fifth unpaid and Garl's name unspoken the same number. Eleven arithmetics now stand behind one unsigned figure, the figure read still not the figure paid and no signature on the space; twelve (Stone 30, Day 389) stands three chapters ahead, and the Five stand beyond it.
