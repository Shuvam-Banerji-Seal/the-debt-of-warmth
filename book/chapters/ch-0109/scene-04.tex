In the afternoon \person{Sella Vail} carried him down the nineteen steps from under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof into \place{the Clay Yard}, in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill}, \place{Grath}, at the half-pace she kept from every open slate. The instruction went to the wool, the river wind and the fire she would bank against their coming back, and never to the basket. The basket went level as a pot that must not be slopped and out of the draught, and the whole of her attention was on the keeping of that level, so that at every turn of the landing she corrected the tilt before the tilt had time to settle into the wicker. \person[Corin]{The boy} felt the small correction under him before he felt the stair, the hand's small exactness under a tray, and the infant in him wanted the correction to go another way, toward the crust two inches off and the frozen thread at his lip, and got none of it.

She went around the seven walked-around places with a look and a wide step and no number. At \place{the Vail corner} she went around the clean dark line where the ridge had turned and the finer grey hairline beside it that the house called \lore{the thaw}, and swept neither, because a line left where it is is still a line kept and a thaw goes when it goes. The dry cold had thickened it and the faint sour tang of \lore{Ash} came up off it where it had not been swept in two years, and she did not turn her head toward the smell, having settled long ago what the corner was and what it was not. Under \place[the drying racks]{the lowest rack} the broken birth-slates lay entered by nobody, and she went around the space above them as wide as a pail of hot water set where a child might knock it. At \place{the rack end} the round ring where the shallow pan had once stood kept no scrape, no crumb, no print, no chip and no word; the pan had gone out of the house and the ring stayed, which is how a vessel is kept after it is gone. Out on \place{the worked strips} she went around the field's empty place, and around \place{the trough}, and around the silt-clouded plate of ice on \place{the flat stone by the well} that stood a little out of the path of feet. Widest of all she went around the returning-line place where a hand had gone in on \place{Seed} 15 and had not come out, so wide that her elbow cut the corner of the yard and the basket tilted the other way to balance it, and she righted it without looking down.

He took the wide step apart as he took a field apart, by what was there and by what was not. Seven places gone around with a look and no figure; the widest gone around by nothing measurable, and once and not twice. A second going-around would have made a figure of once, and a figure wants a payer standing under it, and none stood. The yard kept its places by walking around them. The keeping cost a step each time and nobody entered the steps, and he did not enter them either. The clean dark line kept its finer grey beside it unswept, thickened in the dry cold; the broken slates stayed entered by nobody; the round ring at the rack end kept its emptiness without a word written into it. He had learned to read a place by the marks that were not on it, the way he had once read a chart by the note turned face down, and the yard gave him the same small trouble it had always given him: it was exact, and it told him nothing he could spend.

The three coverings stayed weather entered nowhere, with a finger between every pair: the gone white frost off the well's north lip, the skin drawn edge to edge across the trough, and the plate of ice on the flat stone a little out of the path of feet. They were three, and not a fourth covering laid over the three, and not a sixth low thing set beside the five held objects. Through \place{the finger's-width door} \place{the river} kept its note of \lore{water over water}, a low thing over a lower thing, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming. Over it from bank to bank lay \lore{the winter skin} that the cold had put on and that the cold would take off, found where put on the first day of the month and found again this afternoon and not made again, not spent on the reading, the market or the going of the light. A line left where it is is still a line kept; a sign is a thing that points to a payer, and no payer stood under that ground. He kept the covering on its own place beside the note and put a finger between the two, so that no row came out even by his doing.

No one came over the boards this afternoon with a direction and no hare was shifted across a shoulder and no cracked jar asked the price of mending. The yard delivered itself of its small doings without a hand reaching for a slate. That was not the quiet of an empty place; it was the quiet of a place going about its work and leaving its accounts where they lay. The boy took it as a field taken at rest and let it go at that. The infant under the wool was not so easily dismissed. He wanted the crust lifted two inches, the thread wiped, a hand on the pot handle, and the wanting went on under the exactness of the reading as water goes on under ice, with nowhere to go and no leave to stop.

At \place{the kindling} the account stood in its old exactness: six pieces in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check, and the seventh at the lip beyond it beside \person{Garl Vail}'s unforged collector sentence, with no name on the man who turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on the debt. The small knife lay at the very edge of its sill, where it could be checked and where a hand could also slip it off. \person{Tarn} passed his hand over blade and sill as a man passes over a tool put back where it belongs, told the wood, “Six counted. The next I don't count,” and cut nothing, counted nothing and spent nothing. The Ash 14 held breath and the \place{Bone} 26 thumb's resting were found where they had been put and not made again, beside the five held objects and the pot-crack resting with a finger between every pair — not a sixth held object, not a seventh, not a tenth stopping, not the calling of the loan, with no payer standing.

The pale clip-ring at his left wrist caught what little light there was and gave it back; his chest had been tight since the dark, and the Ash was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line. His stroke went over the shallow bright patch of \place{the flagstones} without the half-check. He brought the ear-sentence up to the word that would call the loan and stopped there as a remembered ninth stopping with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end, the Seed 16 breath unspent and the loan uncalled; he did not look along the road. Two ears, two silences, a loan that could hear and had never been called, with nobody standing to enter it. Garl's name had gone unspoken under this roof three hundred and seventy-nine days and \lore{the carried fire}'s fifth had gone unpaid the same number, and the two counts stood side by side and did not become one.

\person{Jorra Vail} came through the finger's-width door off \place{the Vail wheel-shed} with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bent little finger and white in a fist-crease where a hand had opened, and it was not the bone-coloured \lore{slip} of the wheel. Three or more men unnamed had gone low into \place{the back room} before first grey; he gave the house no name for them and no word that the room was cold. Dusty forearms and clean ones he kept on one tray and parted as clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had been broken nor whether it was a birth-slate while the broken ones lay under the lowest rack and were entered by nobody. He spoke to the wheel before he spoke to any person.

“It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady.”

To a pot going past on a bench he said, “You'll do,” with no welcome in it and no unkindness either, knowing \lore{the full note} that pot would give three turns before it left his hands, with air under it and a want in it, and withholding the knowing from any master come up from \place{High Kiln} who would call it early and sing a pot before its time. When Sella had not finished telling him the fire was low his bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper} ahead of her sentence, took the cold out of the fingertip, moved the tongue a hair's breadth and was left standing so that the end of her instruction arrived at the pot and the household straightened around the waiting as cloth straightens when a weight is lifted — one piece of iron with two bites, the holding and the taking, with no \lore{Grathic} word covering both mercies. The finger came out without leaving the extra holding of Ash 26 a second time; that holding was gathered once and stays where nobody entered it.

“He's not Vail” stayed sealed as a lid nobody had tried to lift in three years. The promise of the boy's name to the men who burn slates was neither given nor withdrawn, and eleven arithmetics going up \place{the clay road} in a locked box toward High Kiln brought no loosening of it and no word under the wheel-shed. The boy filed the wheel-shed's two silences on separate places with a finger between and joined nothing under that roof to the figure that had gone up the road.

At the going of the light Sella carried him up the nineteen steps under the roof and never into \place{the Debt Loft}, and set him on \place{the bed} at the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf}. She said nothing of where they had been and nothing of the morning's shade at the moot-post. She put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face, and did not tuck a second fold into the empty place. Her hand did only what the setting needed and went away; the little-door burn closed on air; the thread was left frozen and unwiped. The wool lay folded once in the crust's empty place exactly where a hand had put it on the fifteenth, found where put again at the going of the light as at each going since, and not made again.

The boy filed it on a place of its own beside the crust's gap, the two gaps in the shelf in the room whose floor does not touch this one, the gone pan's round ring at the rack end and the field's empty place out on the worked strips, with a finger between it and every other kept thing so that no row came out even by his doing. It was gathered once and not made again: not a joining of rooms, not a sixth held object, not a gift, for a gift wants a giver and nobody gave, not a payment, not a sign, not a hearing, not a mending, not a cause, not a rule. The far-off finger that would one day lay shelf and fold together and call the joining an account was still far off, was not his, and had not come. The account wants a hand to lay the places together and a payer standing under all of them, and no hand stood and no payer stood. Nothing that had passed under \place{the moot-post} that morning had crossed into the afternoon as sign, lesson, mending, cause, payment or payer, and the keeping at the fold was not joined to it.

Under the roof Sella went to the hearth before the basket as she had at both ends of every day for three years. At \place{the hearth} she told the coals it was the thaw and turned them with the back of a broken pot, said the house would keep it where another woman would have said I, counted a small intake, a hold and a letting-go toward no opening with neither gap figured, and kept her finger along the top of the loaf shelf so the charcoal tally stayed unread. On \place{the pot board} the twice-mended pot kept the hairline of the original break showing between the old-brass staple and the tighter wire; her thumb went first where the wire crossed the crack, and both proverbs went to the pot and the fire and never to a person. Repair stayed apart from restoration: a restored thing hides the hand that handled it and a mended thing leaves that hand showing. The dressed grain lay covered and unfigured under wool with no figure lifted and no handful, and the cord of her \lore{widow's mark} hung untouched, for a going of the light and a hearth are not openings and no eighth was made due. Under the floor the carried fire gave four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer unnamed three hundred and seventy-nine days, and nobody lifted the floor to supply the pull; overhead \place{the boards} kept four nails on the near one, four on the next and a thumb-width knot where a fifth might have been driven and was not. Three counts in three places, and none rose into another, because agreeing any two would have wanted a payer put under both and no payer stood.

The wider file was checked without standing up, by the place of each thing and every gap left gap. Six hearings of the full note stood beside and never among, the seventh place empty; the sixth was the small flat knock out of \person[Dessa Silt] as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go and never among the sources; the flat knock of \lore[the Hearth Drawing]{the Drawing} stayed on its own place outside them. Nine record-first hands stood with no tenth — \person{Hessa Rale}'s eighth, \person{Ilva}'s ninth, the collector's ordinary writing hand on a tray of its own joined to none — and nine stoppings stood with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end. The burned slate stood at five, the shut-room tellings at two, the row at five askings with no witness, the well-saying at four shapings; four rooms kept the same gap held apart with a finger between every pair; eleven arithmetics stood behind one unsigned figure — the three of \place{Frost}, the first, second and third of Bone, Ash's first, Ash's second, Ash's third, \place{Stone}'s first and Stone's second — the figure read still not the figure paid, no signature on the space and no doctrine out of eleven; their eleven true subtractions rode locked in \person{Berr Clay}'s box up the clay road toward High Kiln, the key gone up in a coat pocket that did not come back down and the boy having no key. Nothing was lifted out of its place to make a row come out even, and the finding that eleven are not one account sat on a place of its own.

In the small hours \place{the towel} came out at the head of the bed as instruments come out on a towel when a case is done and before the next is called, and the hand outside the wool came up an inch toward the river's note under the finger's-width door. It did not reach, and the inch was not a reach. He took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole on the note's own finishing under the winter skin, let the intake go as the note ended and came straight down and laid itself flat against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. The note had done before the hand came down, so the letting-go was neither early nor stayed, and the hand did not stay.

He filed it as answer three hundred and thirty-four: exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer. The surgeon took his small clean satisfaction in a field taken as found and left as found, and it cost him nothing, which was how he knew it was not peace. The infant wanted the crust lifted two inches, the thread wiped and a hand on the pot handle. Both were true. The second was louder. Neither won, and the quarrel was the work.

The hand lay flat on the fold on answer three hundred and thirty-four, and the count stood at three hundred and seventy-nine exact, six months and one hundred and ninety-nine days told by nobody who would have known what a number counted, the carried fire's fifth unpaid the same number and Garl's name unspoken the same number. Eleven arithmetics stood behind one unsigned figure with no signature on the space and no doctrine out of eleven, and the keeping at the fold was not one of them and had not been laid across any of them. The wool lay folded once in the empty place beside the crust gone harder and browner in the dry cold, its corner a hair off the wool, with no second fold, no third crust and no name spoken over the place. The count went on under the roof in Low Kiln, Sill, Grath.
