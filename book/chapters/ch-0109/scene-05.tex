\daymark{380}{Stone 21, Firstday, Y.H. 813}

In the small hours under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill}, \place{Grath}, the hand lay flat on the fold on answer three hundred and thirty-four and the count stood at three hundred and seventy-nine, told by nobody who would have known what a number counted. \lore{The carried fire}'s fifth was unpaid the same number and \person{Garl Vail}'s name unspoken the same number, and the two counts stood side by side in the dark without becoming one. \place{Stone} had kept its twentieth day behind the household and bought no teaching under this roof: eleven arithmetics stood behind one unsigned figure, the figure read still not the figure paid, no signature on the space, no doctrine out of eleven, and the finding that eleven were not one account sat on a place of its own with a finger between it and every other kept thing. \person[Corin]{The boy} did not go back over it. A morning is not an opening, and an account wants a hand at the mark and none stood.

The surgeon took the body in its old order, as a man takes a field before the light, by the small things first because the small things do not lie. One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from the ear, gone harder and browner in the dry cold, its corner standing a hair off the wool. Beside it the wool lay folded once in the empty place, where a hand had put it on the fifteenth and where no hand had been since. The thread of drool at the lip had frozen in the dry kiln heat and was unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. Cold had stopped at cheek, temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, as though it had come to the end of a line written out for it with neither an order to go farther nor an order to go away. \lore{The Afterdrop} waited beyond all three, past a warming nobody in this house would perform.

He knew the order in which heat comes back is the reverse of the order in which it goes; that a basket set level out of the draught where the heat came sideways was the careful rewarming he would choose and could not ask for; that a cold patient cannot clot, will seem better before he is worse, and warms extremity before core; that pink returning to a face is the surface still cold and not the core. The composed face was not composure. There was a wrong familiarity in it, the sense of having come back to a room after it had been rearranged: the weight of the crust at two inches, the particular dry heat, the low note through the gap. He had learned not to make a home of it. The old life was held in the mouth like instruments between the lips, always there, almost never spoken. He had once reduced a shoulder in the dark and could not lift a hand to his own chin. The infant wanted the crust lifted two inches, the thread wiped and a hand on the pot handle. Both were true. The second was louder. Neither won, and the quarrel was the work, and he had stopped expecting it to end, which was not the same as being used to it.

\scenebreak

The sixth finding came before the light, as the five before it had come, and was no different from them. The wool was folded once in the crust's empty place exactly where it had been put, changed by nobody in the night. He took the place apart by what was there and by what was not: a second fold was not there, a third crust was not there, no name had been spoken over the place, no gift laid, no payment made and no payer stood. A sixth finding makes no sixth placing. A habit would want a hand that placed twice, and no such hand had stood under this roof; a way would want a third, and both want a payer where none stood. Six findings from a hand that only read added no seventh placing to the first. No fourth likeness was made a cause.

He filed it on a place of its own beside the crust's gap, the two gaps in \place{the loaf shelf} in the room whose floor does not touch this one, the gone pan's round ring at \place{the rack end} and the field's empty place out on \place{the worked strips}, with a finger between it and every other kept thing so that no row came out even by his doing. It was not a joining of rooms, not a sixth held object, not a gift --- a gift wants a giver and nobody gave --- not a payment, not a sign, not a hearing, not a mending, not a cause and not a rule. He did not lay it across the eleven readings or the four rooms with the gap. The far-off finger that would one day lay shelf and fold together and call the joining an account was still far off, was not his, and had not come. The account wants a hand to lay the places together and a payer standing under all of them, and no hand stood and no payer stood.

The house kept its days in loaves pushed along a shelf, in glances at a clay jar, in the space where a weight had stood and in the shape a room takes when a weight is lifted out of it. It could not say that a number had been reached, and its not saying was neither a cruelty nor a mercy that could be entered on a slate. He kept his days another way, exact, edge to edge, and told nobody; the two keepings lay in the same house and did not touch, as the shelf's room does not touch this one. Six findings had gathered on their own place like dust in a corner that nobody swept, and a corner is not kept by a hand for all that it is left where it lies. Nothing from the twentieth crossed into this morning as sign, lesson, mending, cause, payment or payer, and the keeping at the fold was not joined to it.

\scenebreak

\person{Sella Vail} went to \place{the hearth} before the basket, as she had at both ends of every day for three years. She told the coals it was \lore{the thaw} and turned them with the back of a broken pot, said the house would keep it where another woman would have said I, stood a moment counting a small intake, a hold and a letting-go toward no opening with neither gap figured. Her finger lay along the top of the loaf shelf so the charcoal tally stayed unread; her lips moved along a two-gapped row and figured neither gap. She glanced once at \place{the clay jar} standing cold under \lore{lead glaze} and shut her hand half over nothing, the little-door burn on the back of her left hand closing on air. On \place{the pot board} the twice-mended pot kept the hairline of the original break showing between the old-brass staple and the tighter wire; her thumb went first where the wire crossed the crack, and both proverbs went to the pot and the fire and never to a person --- leave the crack showing, people trust a thing they can see has been handled; if it's worth mending it's worth mending twice, the second time is the real one, improved in the keeping and very likely not as good as the man said them. Repair stayed apart from restoration: a restored thing hides the hand that handled it and a mended thing leaves that hand showing. The dressed grain she turned under wool with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child, with no figure lifted and no handful. The pot rim she set two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat and no look toward the bed; the half-beat was the distance between intention and arrest, bought at nothing and not a gift.

Garl stayed in objects and not in a story. The salt-stiff coat with its left-shoulder crease kept his broader shape on its peg behind the door; the hollow wheel-seat in the shed kept its twenty-year hollow that nobody was allowed to fill; the clean dark line at \place{the Vail corner} with its finer grey hairline beside it called the thaw stayed unswept, thickened in the dry cold with the faint sour tang of \lore{Ash} coming up off it where it had not been swept in two years; the unforged collector sentence lay beside the seventh notch at the kindling with no name on the man who turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on the debt. His name had gone unspoken under this roof three hundred and eighty days and the carried fire's fifth gone unpaid the same number, and the two counts stood side by side and did not become one.

The \lore{widow's mark} hung untouched at her throat, the cord oiled where a working thumb had touched it ten thousand times. A morning and a hearth are not openings; seven openings in seven months are seven and not a rule, and no eighth was made due. Under the floor the carried fire gave four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer unnamed three hundred and eighty days, and nobody lifted the floor to supply the pull. Overhead \place{the boards} kept four nails on the near one, four on the next and a thumb-width knot where a fifth might have been driven and was not. Three counts in three places, and none rose into another, because agreeing any two would want a payer put under both and no payer stood. The heard bell of \lore{the Long Night} stayed in its square beside the told, four, four and a gap, and was not folded into any of them or made a seventh hearing. Six hearings of \lore{the full note} stood beside and never among, the seventh place empty; the flat knock of \lore[the Hearth Drawing]{the Drawing} stayed on its own place outside them. Eleven arithmetics stood behind one unsigned figure --- the three of \place{Frost}, the first, second and third of \place{Bone}, Ash's first, Ash's second, Ash's third, Stone's first and Stone's second --- the figure read still not the figure paid, no signature on the space and no doctrine out of eleven, and the finding that eleven were not one account was kept on a place of its own and was not promoted into a doctrine.

\scenebreak

In the small hours \place{the towel} came out at the head of \place{the bed} as instruments come out on a towel when a case is done and before the next is called, with every gap left gap. The hand outside the wool came up an inch toward \place{the river}'s note under \place{the finger's-width door} as a hand goes up in a field it has learned not to disturb. The inch was not a reach. He took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole on the note's own finishing under the winter skin, let the intake go as the note ended and came straight down and laid itself flat against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. The note had done before the hand came down, so the letting-go was neither early nor stayed, and the hand did not stay. Through the gap the river kept its note of \lore{water over water}, a low thing over a lower thing, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming, muffled under \lore{the winter skin} the cold had put on and the cold would take off, found where put and not spent on the day's work or the going of the light.

He filed it as answer three hundred and thirty-five: exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the eleven readings, the bell's gap, the flat knock, the child's grey fingertips, the mother's cup, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the held breath, the two bites of iron, the river's skin, the widow's mark nor the keeping at the fold. The surgeon took his small clean satisfaction in a field taken as found and left as found, and it cost him nothing, which was how he knew it was not peace. The infant wanted the crust lifted two inches, the thread wiped and a hand on the pot handle. Both were true. The second was louder. Neither won, and the quarrel was the work.

The hand lay flat on the fold on answer three hundred and thirty-five, and the count stood at three hundred and eighty exact, six months and two hundred days told by nobody who would have known what a number counted, the carried fire's fifth unpaid the same number and Garl's name unspoken the same number. Stone kept its twenty-first day without a boundary drawn, the deep-winter month holding both its names --- older women by weather, slatemen by Account-days --- neither set right nor left in so many words. The wool lay folded once in the empty place beside the crust gone harder and browner in the dry cold, its corner a hair off the wool, with no second fold, no third crust and no name spoken over the place, and the thread frozen and unwiped. Six findings and still a keeping, not a habit and not a way. The count went on under the roof in Low Kiln, Sill, Grath.
