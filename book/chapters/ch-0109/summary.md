## Chronicle

Three days the house under the Vail roof kept its shape, and the boy kept a count no roof could read. Stone nineteen, twenty and twenty-one came on without a boundary drawn. Each morning the surgeon took the body in its old order: leathery barley crust two inches from the ear, corner a hair off the wool; pale wool folded in the empty place where a hand put it on the fifteenth; thread frozen and unwiped; cold stopping at cheek, temple and bare wrist-joint; the afterdrop waiting past a warming nobody would perform. The infant wanted the crust lifted, the thread wiped, a hand on the pot handle. Both true, the second louder, neither winning; the quarrel was the work.

On the nineteenth the fold was found where put a fourth time. Sella Vail went to the hearth before the basket, told the coals it was the thaw, said the house would keep it where another woman would say I, and carried him down at a half-pace. The yard went around its seven places, widest around the returning-line place where a hand went in on Seed 15 and did not come out. Ilva at the well answered: Ask the pot what it cost. I keep the count, not the account — ninth record-first hand, no tenth. Tarn told the wood Six counted. The next I don't count; Jorra put a bent finger into the cold damper ahead of an unfinished sentence. Answers 331 and 332 ; the count stood at 378.

On the twentieth the district's arithmetic changed for the eleventh time. Berr Clay came up off the clay road, wiped his thumb twice before touching nothing, passed a finger over the figure through his sleeve and read one in twelve twice over the basket. He called the levy and denied it with your house, never I, and went off with the box locked and Stone's second true subtraction inside, believing he had done a kindness. Eleven arithmetics stood behind one unsigned figure; eleven were eleven and not one account, the figure read still not the figure paid, no signature on the space, no doctrine out of eleven. Hessa carried his slate up unread; the bell went four, four and a gap. Answers 333 and 334 ; the count stood at 379.

On the twenty-first the fold was found where put a sixth time, and six findings from a hand that only read added no seventh placing. The yard went around its places, Tarn's hand went to the knife-sill and away with no breath held at the top of the reach, and Jorra's finger came out of the iron without the extra holding made again. Answers 335 and 336 ; the count stood at 380 exact, six months and two hundred days told by nobody, the fire's fifth unpaid and Garl's name unspoken the same number. The surgeon's satisfaction in a field taken as found cost nothing, which was how he knew it was not peace.

## Continuity

- **Exact final moment.** The chapter closes in the small hours at the very end of Day 380, Stone 21, Firstday, under the Vail kiln-house roof in Low Kiln, Sill, Grath. The hand outside the wool has come up an inch toward the river's note under the finger's-width door, not reaching, taken a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole on the note's own finishing under the winter skin, let it go as the note ended and laid itself flat against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. Answer **336** filed: exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer. Present: Corin/Dan alone on the bed, with Sella Vail somewhere under the roof after the evening's hearth grammar. Not mid-action; the hand has come down and does not stay.

- **Where everyone is at the close.** Sella Vail — under the roof, having carried him up the nineteen steps at the going of the light, set him on the bed, put the fold at his ear and under his chin with no look at his face and no second fold, renewed the hearth grammar and left the twice-mended pot, dressed grain and unread charcoal as found; wants the house kept by not-doings and gives no instruction to the basket. Tarn — last seen on Stone 21 afternoon at the kindling: six in the worn thumb-mark, seventh at the lip, knife at the sill edge, hand up and away with no breath held at the top of the reach; chest tight since the dark, Ash in his knuckle creases, pale clip-ring at his left wrist; the loan uncalled at the very end of his seventh year and the Seed 16 breath unspent. Jorra Vail — last seen at the wheel-shed edge and damper on Stone 21 afternoon with meal-fine grey dust white in a fist-crease, three or more unnamed men gone low into the back room before first grey, dusty and clean forearms parted as clot from vessel, bent finger into the cold iron ahead of Sella's unfinished sentence and out again without a second holding; *He's not Vail* sealed, the promise of the boy's name neither given nor withdrawn. Garl Vail — absent, kept in objects only: salt-stiff coat on its peg, hollow wheel-seat, the clean dark line with its finer grey called the thaw, the twice-mended pot's hairline, the unforged collector sentence at the kindling. Berr Clay — gone up the clay road toward High Kiln with the red cord folded under his coat, the narrow slate under his arm and the box locked, Stone's second true subtraction inside, believing he has done a kindness. Hessa Rale — gone up the clay road before noon with Berr's narrow slate edge-on, the writing to her own sleeve, unread, her eighth record-first hand still eight. Ilva — last on stage at the yard well on Stone 19 with the notched birch strip in her sleeve, unread, her ninth hand still nine. The soft-cuffed clerk, the green-mouthed woman and the small shape were sightings only on Stone 20, with no eighth of either green or shape; no Drawing collector, runner, ownerless voice or witness on any of the three days.
