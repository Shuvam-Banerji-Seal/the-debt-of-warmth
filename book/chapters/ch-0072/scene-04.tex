The afternoon came on the way heat comes off a pot set aside from the coals --- not with a stroke or a boundary, but with a lessening that went first behind the pot and then into the fold where \person[Corin]{the boy} lay, so gradual that the room could not have said at what hour it had changed. Smoke went on under its own name and neither of the district's two names for the months was set right or left wrong by it. There would be no market, no Cross and no bell on a Ninthday. Smoke 30 stood one morning ahead, the third and last Account-day and market of the second firing, and was not reached for; the first firing of Frost stood far off and unbrought. Two hundred and sixty-eight stood where it had stood, exact, complete and unspent, six months and eighty-eight days told by nobody in \place{Sarn}, \place{Sill} or \place{Grath}. He had long since stopped expecting anyone to tell him what the number counted, which was not the same thing as not wanting it; the wanting went on under the expectation the way a coal goes on under ash, giving out nothing and going out not at all.

He took the body as the facts presented it, in the order he had learned in a country that had no word for any of it. One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from his ear, gone past the point where a child would have eaten it, and the empty place beside it kept its name to itself; no third had come into the afternoon to make the row come out even. The thread at his mouth had frozen in the dry heat and was unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread, and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. Cold had come to cheek and temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, and had stopped there as at the end of a line written out for it, neither going forward nor going away. The afterdrop waited beyond all three, past a warming nobody in this house would perform. He knew the composed face that is not composure, knew a cold patient cannot clot and will seem better before he is worse, knew a man must be warmed from the outside and slowly. He wanted the crust lifted the two inches to his mouth. Both were true, and in a body two hundred and sixty-eight days old the second was the louder; he entered neither, not even on the place where he kept the things he did not enter. The surgeon took the inventory. The boy lay under the fold and wanted his breakfast. There was no third hand in the room to lift either thing for him.

\lore[the widow's mark]{The widow's mark} hung where it hung, touched once before the kiln on Smoke 1 and at no shelf, well, rack, road, basket, yard, afternoon or going of the light since, because an afternoon and a yard and a bed are not openings. The one opening before Smoke 1 stayed behind the household with no lesson, no sign, no mending and no payer named. Under the floor \lore{the carried fire} kept four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer without a name for two hundred and sixty-eight days, and through the finger's-width door the river kept its note of \lore{water over water}, a low thing over a lower thing, never numbered among the hearings.

\person{Sella Vail} carried him out at the half-pace from every open slate, and in \place{the Clay Yard} she went around the seven places again with a look and a wide step and no number, as a woman goes around a pot left where a child might knock it. There was the clean dark line at the Vail corner where the \lore{Ash} ridge had turned and been burned and called dealt with; the finer grey hairline beside it that the house called \lore{the thaw}; the space beneath \place{the lowest rack} where the broken birth-slates lay and the yard entered them into nothing; the round ring on the boards where the shallow clay pan had stood, with no scrape, no crumb, no print, no chip and no word in it; \place{the yard well} with \place{the trough} beside it bare and dark and its water called weather; the field's empty place out on \place{the worked strips}; and the returning-line place where a hand had gone in on Seed 15 and had not come out. She went around that last one as wide as a pail of hot water set where a child might reach, and gave it no name and no figure. Seven in a row were seven and not an account, because an account wants a payer standing under it and none stood. The hand was carried and not named, not paid, not joined to the shelf's two gaps or the fold's one crust or the dressed grain lying covered under wool at \place{the rack end} or any other kept absence.

Below the sill a child's foot that had stayed cold after the flood was wrapped in a woman's wool and called the cold by her and weather by the yard. He kept it as the extremity going before the core and the afterdrop waiting beyond, and named no cause, no silt-leach and no cure, because there was no \lore{Grathic} word for the thing the foot was doing and nobody had asked him for one. It lay on a place of its own, and no lesson was made of it and no payer put under it.

No voice with no owner came over the boards that afternoon, and the question about which row had gone quiet was not asked a sixth time. It gained no second shaping on this day. The five askings stood on the seventh place with the one further shaping beside them, and a finger stood between each pair and another between the shaping and the nearest asking, so that five and one were five and one and not an account. No witness stood anywhere to be asked and no row was named. The boy took the silence of it as a keeping and not as a sixth witness, and made no cause of it. He took the report apart as a surgeon takes a wound apart and found no witness at the bottom of it; a silence kept was one thing and a silence filled to make a row come out even was a finding forged, and he had learned the difference in a country that had no word for either. The report of the asking lay on its own tray where it had been laid, with nobody standing under it.

At \place{the kindling} the broom was instructed before any person, the instruction going to the tool and not to the man, and \person[Tarn Vail]{Tarn} took it up as a man takes up an instrument already named. “Yes, house,” he said to those who wanted the thaw early and “Yes, house” to those who wanted it late, agreeing with both so entirely that no argument could find a foot to stand on, and going on wanting the late one, for a late thaw puts off the paying, though the paying had stood in the ruts since the water went. He told the wood, “Six counted. The next I don't count,” in the tone of a man stating the weather. The small knife lay at the very lip of its sill, six in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check and the seventh at the edge where it could not, and \person[Garl Vail]{Garl}'s unforged collector sentence lay beside it --- the one about a man sent for a collector in the Long Cold who turned back at the frozen ford, with no name on the man and no figure on the debt. The sentence about an ear under this roof being worth more than the debt that held it came to his teeth and went all the way to the word that would call the loan and stopped there with no figure and no name, a remembered ninth stopping and not a new one, with no tenth stood up before the seventh year at its very end, which had still not given. He drew the breath he should have spent at the top of the reach and did not spend it.

His chest had been tight since the dark. \lore{Ash} was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line and no scrubbing lifted it, and the pale clip-ring at his left wrist held what light there was and gave it back. Up in \place{the Debt Loft}, which came to the boy only as a sound with a direction and never as a room he was taken into, the warm stone Sella had given him in the winter Garl died lay cold in its unmarked pot, two years gone, not tried and not forgiveness, and he did not go up to it. His stroke went over \place{the flagstones} past the shallow bright patch without the half-check and over the road a house sends a man down when it wants a collector without once turning along it. No collector came up \place{the clay road} on Smoke 29 and none went down it, and the loan stayed uncalled. The boy kept the two ears under one roof on their old equality, two ears and two silences and a loan that could hear, and put no word between them, because a word put there would want a payer standing under it and none stood.

At the edge of \place{the Vail wheel-shed} \person[Jorra Vail]{Jorra} came through with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bent little finger and white in the creases of a fist that had opened, not the bone colour of slip. He kept dusty forearms and clean ones on one tray and parted them as a surgeon parts clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had broken nor whether it was a birth-slate. The broken birth-slates lay beneath the lowest rack where the yard went around them and entered them into nothing. Three or more men unnamed had gone low into the finger's-width-open \place{the back room} before first grey, and he gave the house no name for them and did not say the room was cold; its sound came to the basket only as a direction, low and without words the boy could have repeated. He spoke to the wheel before he spoke to any person. “It doesn't care whose hands,” he said. “It cares if they're steady.” He told a pot “You'll do” in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either, and knew the \lore{full note} that pot would give three turns before it left his hands, with air under it and a want in it, and withheld the knowing on the near-full tray beside the hearings and never among them.

The burned-slate word stood at four from \place{Slate Row}, a direction over the boards arrived nowhere and not a fifth telling. The two tellings of the counting room shut in the war stood at two and did not become three. The three sayings about a pot stayed three, with the premature Smoke 14 note on its own place and no fourth likeness made a cause; no pot was lost and no name was given to what ailed the man carried out on Day 188. The promise of the boy's name to the men who burn slates was neither given nor withdrawn, and “he's not Vail” stayed sealed as tightly as a lid nobody had tried to lift in three years. When Sella's sentence went low, his bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper} ahead of it, took the cold out of the fingertip and held until the end of her instruction arrived at the pot; the household straightened around his silence the way cloth straightens when a weight is lifted. The boy read one piece of iron with two bites, the bite that holds a vessel whole and the bite that can take the hand off a man, mercy and mutilation in one object with no Grathic word for both, joined to no kiln.

Under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof she set him on \place{the bed} at the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf} and not in the Debt Loft. She put the fold right without a look at his face, left the thread frozen, told the fire it was the thaw, turned the coals with the back of a broken pot, said \emph{The house will keep it} where another woman would have said \emph{I}, counted under her breath toward no opening --- the small intake, the hold, the letting-go --- and went to her own bed. Her finger stayed on top of the shelf's front edge so the charcoal tally lying beneath stayed unread, and her lips moved along a row standing with two gaps without giving either gap a figure. The dressed grain lay covered and unfigured at the rack end with one morning between it and the reading, and it did not wait for the reading as a thing waits for an answer, because nothing about the house waited. Nobody under the roof found it remarkable that a figure would be read over it in the morning and would not know what it cost. He kept the not-knowing as a finding with no payer; it was not a lesson, not a grievance and not a doctrine, and he did not lay it across the house's \lore{not-doings} or across his own kept gaps, for a joining would want a payer standing under it and none stood.

In the small hours he turned the file out on the towel and found it exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air. Six hearings of the full note stood beside and never among, with the seventh place kept empty and none invented to fill it; three near-full answers stood on their own place; three sayings about a pot stood with the premature Smoke 14 note apart and no fourth likeness made a cause. Four low things stood with water over water a fifth; the told bell stood beside the heard, and the told figure beside the paid, with Smoke's first two arithmetics gone up the clay road in \person[Berr Clay]{Berr}'s locked box and unread under this roof. Nine stoppings stood with no tenth, the seventh year at its very end and still ungiven; three held changes stood on three places, the loft scrape on a fourth and the pale line of the carried man on a fifth, and the held inch on a sixth with one hundred and eleven answers. Nine record-first hands stood with \person{Ilva}'s ninth on its own tray and her strip unread; the seventh green was a colour and the seventh shape a shape with no eighth. The pan's gone ring, the shelf's two gaps and the fold's one crust lay on trays that did not touch. The finding that a thing handled closely still enters nothing stood on a place of its own, and the finding that the house keeps itself by not-doings stood apart from his own gaps with a finger between, a finding with no payer and not a doctrine. The report of the asking lay on its own tray with nobody standing under it, and the five askings stood with the one shaping beside them and no sixth and no witness and no row named.

Through the finger's-width door the river kept its low note; under the floor the carried fire kept four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer unnamed for two hundred and sixty-eight days; overhead \place{the boards} kept four nails on the near board, four on the next and a thumb-width knot where the fifth nail should have stood. Three counts in three places, and none rose into another, because agreeing any two would want a payer put under both and none stood. No fire-sitter stood over the coals to supply a count and no house count was there to borrow. In the room that does not touch a count had ended and a little silence stood after it, and the little silence was left empty on a place of its own and not crossed. The arithmetic that would be read over the covered grain in the morning would not know what it cost, and the space between the figure and its price stayed unsigned.

The hand outside the wool went up an inch toward the river's low note. It held for the small intake. It held past the letting-go of one of the breaths counted in the room that does not touch, and did not come down with that breath, and took the little silence after the count as an object on its own place, crossing it with nothing and borrowing no stroke, and came down on the river's next low note. It was one hundred and twelve, filed on the sixth place beside one hundred and eleven with no payer named, exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air: an honest coordination and not a reach, not a hearing, not a seventh of the full note, not a stopping, not a payment, not one of the held changes, not a key, a power or a rule. He made nothing of it and would not let it stand up as a gift. The inch was still an inch; it had grown by four honest objects and had not once closed two inches of air.

Two hundred and sixty-eight stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody; six months and eighty-eight days were told by nobody; the fire's fifth was unpaid the same number of days with its payer unnamed. The hand lay back against the fold as a tool put away where the next hand will find it without looking. The little silence behind the count lay whole on its own place, and the report of the asking lay on its tray with nobody standing under it. The thread was frozen, the crust two inches from his ear with its empty place unnamed and no third, the towel on the fold with every tray parted and every gap left gap. Smoke went on without a boundary drawn; Smoke 30 stood one morning ahead and was not brought into a day that had not earned it, and the count moved forward into that morning with the hand come down on the river's next low note and no stroke supplied to agree any three.
