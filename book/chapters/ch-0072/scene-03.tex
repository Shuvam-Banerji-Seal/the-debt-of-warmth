\daymark{268}{Smoke 29, Ninthday, Y.H. 813}

The small hours under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof went on as water goes around a stone that lies where a stone lies, and \person[Corin]{the boy} came into his two hundred and sixty-eighth day with nobody in \place{Sarn}, \place{Sill} or \place{Grath} saying so. Six months and eighty-eight days were told by nobody and never had been. The hand lay against the fold where it had been put down, the way a man lays a tool on a bench at the end of a shift, not because the work is done but because the next hand will find it without looking. He had stopped expecting to be told what a number counted, which was not the same thing as not wanting it; the wanting went on under the expectation the way a coal goes on under ash, giving out nothing and going out not at all. Two hundred and sixty-seven had stood exact and unspent; two hundred and sixty-eight stood where it stood now, exact in the same way, with no figure anywhere in the house to agree with it.

He took the body as the facts presented it, in the order he had learned in a country that had no word for any of it. One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from his ear, gone past the point where a child would have eaten it, and the empty place beside it kept its name to itself; no third had come in the night to make the row come out even. The thread at his mouth had frozen in the dry heat and was unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread, and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. Cold had come to cheek and temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, and had stopped there as at the end of a line written out for it, neither going forward nor going away. The afterdrop waited beyond all three, past a warming nobody in this house would perform. He knew the composed face that is not composure; he knew a cold patient cannot clot and will seem better before he is worse. He wanted the crust lifted the two inches to his mouth. Both were true, and in a body two hundred and sixty-eight days old the second was the louder, and he entered neither, not even on the place where he kept the things he did not enter.

Through the finger's-width door the river kept its note of \lore{water over water}, a low thing over a lower thing, never numbered among the hearings; under the floor \lore{the carried fire} gave four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer without a name for two hundred and sixty-eight days; overhead \place{the boards} kept four nails on the near board, four on the next, and a thumb-width knot where the fifth nail should have stood. Three counts in three places, and none rose into another, because agreeing any two would want a payer put under both and none stood to either. \lore[the widow's mark]{The widow's mark} hung where it hung, touched once before the kiln on Smoke 1 and at no shelf, well, rack, road, basket, morning or going of the light since, because a rack and a well and a yard and a bed are not openings. The one opening before Smoke 1 stayed behind the household with no lesson, no sign, no mending and no payer named.

Smoke 29 came on its own morning, earned by that light and not brought into a day that had not earned it. There would be no market, no Cross and no bell on a Ninthday. Smoke 30 stood one morning ahead, the third and last Account-day and market of the second firing, and was not reached for; the first firing of Frost stood far off and unbrought. The house woke into the smells it always woke into --- hot clay under tallow, wet wool sour with lodging, the faint sour mineral breath of the unswept corner the house called \lore{the thaw} --- and the kiln drew in fours behind the wall with the gap where a fifth pull should have stood, not a bell, and the river went low over lower through the door.

\person{Sella Vail} went to the fire before the basket, as she went to it every morning, and told it the thaw. She turned the coals with the back of a broken pot and said \emph{The house will keep it} where another woman would have said \emph{I}. She stood a moment with the back of the pot in her hand, counting under her breath toward no opening --- the small intake, the hold, the letting-go --- and pushed a strand of coal-black hair back with the wrist that carries the burn, so that the one grey streak at her temple showed pale off the coals. The burn was shaped like a little door, two straight sides and a lintel and a threshold, and it closed on nothing when her hand had nothing to hold. She did not look toward \place{the bed}. No instruction went to the basket, and none had ever gone to it.

At \place{the loaf shelf} she kept her finger on top of the front edge so that the charcoal tally lying beneath stayed unread, and moved her lips along a row standing with two gaps without giving either gap a figure. She glanced once at the clay jar cold under its glaze and shut her hand half over nothing. Then she set the pot rim out with its handle two inches beyond the reaching hand, with a half-beat at the setting and no look toward the bed: a distance measured not as a kindness and not as a cruelty but as the place where a hand that could not close two inches of air would find the thing set down for it and leave it where it lay. He could not close the distance. He had once reduced a shoulder in the dark by feel alone. He kept the two facts on separate places and made no cause of either.

\scenebreak

At \place{the rack end}, on the cool stone away from \place{the hearth}, the dressed grain lay covered under wool. She drew the wool back only far enough to feel for damp and turned the grain once in the flat of her hand, and laid it back with a lighter touch than she had used the day before, drawing the wool over it again. The grain changed in state and gained no figure. The arithmetic that would be read over it in the morning --- the third reading of Smoke, with two hundred people leaning in below the bridge --- would not know what it cost, and the space between the figure and its price stayed unsigned, as it had stayed unsigned through two readings already and would stay through three. He kept the seeing as a finding with no payer; it was not a lesson, not a grievance and not a doctrine, and he did not lay it across the house's \lore{not-doings} or across his own kept gaps, for a joining would want a payer standing under it and none stood.

She carried him out at the half-pace from every open slate. In \place{the Clay Yard} the morning stood in the dry hot smell of clay giving up its water, and the dust of the racks lay ground small as meal on the boards. She went around the clean dark line where the \lore{Ash} ridge had turned and been burned and called dealt with, and around the finer grey hairline beside it that the house called the thaw, with a look and a wide step and no number; the ridge had not been swept in two years and would not be swept today. He kept the two lines on places of their own, with a finger between, and did not join them to the empty place at his ear or to the two gaps on the shelf.

At \place{the yard well} \person{Ilva} was drawing water with the pail against her knee. Clay was dried pale in the crescents of her nails and the finer grey dust of the racks was worked into the seam of one thumb, and she had not scrubbed it out. The pail knocked the stone lip and the sound went across the yard. A woman with a pot asked what the firings of Smoke had cost.

Her hand went toward her sleeve and stayed there. The short strip of birch bark with its notches did not come out; it was not taken out, not read, not rubbed with a thumb, not asked about, and no figure came off it. Her eyes went to the woman who had asked, and her mouth answered the question put and not the one behind it.

“Ask the pot what it cost. I keep the count, not the account.”

She said no figure and gave no name to the thing she kept, and went on with her water while nobody turned to look at her twice. The boy took the order of her reaching --- hand toward the record, eyes to the asker, mouth last --- and filed it as a shape on a place of its own. He did not make it a tenth. The ninth stayed nine on its tray with a finger between it and the eighth, and nine hand-shapes were nine and not a rule, because a rule is an account that has found a payer and none stood to any of these. The notches stayed in her sleeve; he neither read them nor asked about them nor joined the stick to the reed-cutter's cord turned so its knots stood, the knots, the six pages, the tins, the charcoal under the shelf, the missing hand or the pale line. Her count kept in wood and his count kept nowhere stood on separate places and did not meet, and the cost of two exact keepings that could not be laid together was his to feel and not hers to know.

\scenebreak

At the door a cost-question went to the pot and the weather and came back with neither, and the woman who asked it went off level with the question still on her, nothing priced, nothing refused, nothing entered. A man come down \place{the clay road} with dried pale scurf of mud on his coat skirt brought a number up out of a fist toward the well, his thumb tucked over it as over a thing that might be read; he looked at it, and put it back into his sleeve. The figure arrived nowhere. It was not a missing figure and not a withheld one, only a figure that had not arrived, and the boy read it as a surgeon reads a chart turned face-down: complete, exact, and giving up nothing to two inches of air. No figure was supplied, and none was wanted under this roof.

At the going of the light she carried him up at the half-pace from every open slate and went around the places she always went around, each with a look and a wide step and no number. Under the roof she set him on \place{the bed} at the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf} and not in the Debt Loft. She put the fold right without a look at his face, and left the thread at his mouth frozen and unwiped. She told the fire it was the thaw, turned the coals with the back of a broken pot, said \emph{The house will keep it} where another woman would have said \emph{I}, counted under her breath toward no opening, and went to her own bed. The grain lay covered and unfigured at the rack end with Smoke's arithmetic not yet read over it; one morning stood between it and the reading, and it did not wait for the reading as a thing waits for an answer, because nothing about the house waited.

In the small hours he turned the file out on the towel and found it exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air. Six hearings of \lore{the full note} stood beside and never among, with the seventh place kept empty and none invented to fill it; three near-full answers stood on their own place; three sayings about a pot stood with the premature Smoke 14 note apart and no fourth likeness made a cause. Four low things stood with water over water a fifth; the told bell stood beside the heard, and the told figure beside the paid, with Smoke's first two arithmetics gone up the clay road in \person[Berr Clay]{Berr}'s locked box and unread under this roof. Nine stoppings stood with no tenth, the seventh year at its very end and still ungiven; nine record-first hands stood with Ilva's ninth on its own tray and her strip unread; the seventh green was a colour and the seventh shape a shape with no eighth. The pan's gone ring, the shelf's two gaps and the fold's one crust lay on trays that did not touch. The ownerless question stood at five askings with one shaping beside them and no witness under any and no row named. The held inch stood on a sixth place with one hundred and ten answers, and the little silence after the count that had ended in the room that does not touch was left empty on a place of its own and not crossed.

Through the door the river kept its low note; under the floor the carried fire kept four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer unnamed for two hundred and sixty-eight days; overhead the boards kept four, four and a knot. No fire-sitter stood over the coals to supply a count, and no house count was there to borrow.

The hand outside the wool went up an inch toward the river's low note. It held. It held through one whole interval of the river's own going --- a low note, the water over water between, and the small rise toward the next --- and came down on the river's next low note, borrowing no house count and taking no stroke from the room that does not touch. It was one hundred and eleven, filed on the sixth place beside one hundred and ten with no payer named, exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air: an honest coordination and not a reach, not a hearing, not a seventh of the full note, not a stopping, not a payment, not one of the held changes, not a key, a power or a rule. He made nothing of it and would not let it stand up as a gift.

Two hundred and sixty-eight stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody; six months and eighty-eight days were told by nobody; the fire's fifth was unpaid the same number of days with its payer unnamed. The hand lay back against the fold as a tool put away where the next hand will find it without looking. At the rack end the grain lay under the wool with the corner laid back and no figure on it, and the arithmetic that would come in the morning would go over it without knowing what it cost. Smoke went on without a boundary drawn; Smoke 30 stood one morning ahead and was not brought into a day that had not earned it, and the count moved forward into that day with the hand come down on the river's next low note and no stroke supplied to agree any three.
