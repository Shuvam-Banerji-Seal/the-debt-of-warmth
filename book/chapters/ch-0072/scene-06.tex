The day went on around him without a boundary drawn in it, the afternoon thinning the way slip thins under a spoon, by degrees and with no edge where the light gave out and the dark began. Smoke passed out of the yard as water passes out of a shallow pan left in the sun, and neither of the district's two names for the months was set right or left wrong by the going of it. Frost came on in the dark beyond the door and was not brought into a day that had not earned it; the first firing of Frost stood far off and unbrought. \person[Corin]{The boy} lay on \place{the bed} at the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf} under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof in \place{Low Kiln}, carried and awake and silent, in the room that does not touch the room of the fire. The day had been lived to the month's last tend and the month would spend no fourth; there was no further market, no Cross and no bell under this roof, and nothing that had happened in the square had come home with him except the arithmetic, which had gone up the road in a locked box and was unread.

Heat drew back out of the fold and went behind the pot, where it sat the way a coal sits under ash, giving out nothing and going out not at all. He took the body in the order the facts presented themselves, as a surgeon takes a field in the order the wounds present themselves, and entered none of it. One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from his ear, gone past the point where a child would have eaten it, and the empty place beside it kept its name to itself; no third had come in the afternoon to make the row come out even. The thread of drool at his mouth had frozen in the dry heat and was unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread, and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. Cold had come to cheek and temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, and had stopped there as at the end of a line written out for it, neither going forward nor going away. The afterdrop waited beyond all three, past a warming nobody in this house would perform. He knew the composed face that is not composure; he knew a cold patient cannot clot and will seem better before he is worse, and that a man must be warmed from the outside and slowly. He wanted the crust lifted the two inches to his mouth. Both were true, and in a body two hundred and sixty-nine days old the second was the louder; the surgeon took the inventory and let the boy want, and entered neither, not even on the place where he kept the things he did not enter.

\person{Garl Vail} was kept in objects and not in a story, as he had been kept for two hundred and sixty-nine days under this roof and his name spoken by nobody in all of them. The salt-stiff coat held a broader man's shape on its peg with the crease at the left shoulder; the wheel-seat kept its twenty-year lean and its hollow on that side and no one filled it; the proverb about leaving the crack showing had gone years ago to a pot with no crack in it and was said now over a corner where the repair had been rubbed out. The debt against a firing of tiles went around without a name, neither paid nor settled nor done with, and worse for being uncalled. No collector came up \place{the clay road} on Smoke 28, 29 or 30 and none went down it; the road kept its ruts and gave back nothing. A keeping, not a grief; no lesson had been made of it and no payer stood to it.

\person{Sella Vail} carried the basket at the half-pace from every open slate, and went wide around seven things with a look and a wide step and no number. She went around the clean dark line at the Vail corner and the finer grey hairline beside it called \lore{the thaw}; around the space beneath \place{the lowest rack} at \place{the drying racks} where the broken birth-slates lay; around the round ring on \place{the boards} where the shallow clay pan had stood, with no scrape, crumb, print, chip or word in it; around \place{the trough} beside \place{the yard well}, bare and dark, with its water called weather as the grey lip was called weather; around the field's empty place out on \place{the worked strips}; and around the returning-line place where a hand had gone in on Seed 15 and had not come out. Seven in a row were seven and not an account, because an account wants a payer standing under it and none stood. The hand in the returning line was carried and not named, not paid, not joined to the shelf's two gaps, the fold's one crust or the dressed grain lying covered under wool on the cool stone at \place{the rack end}. Below the sill a child's foot that had stayed cold after the flood was wrapped in a woman's wool and called the cold by her and weather by the yard; he kept it as the extremity going before the core with the afterdrop waiting beyond, and named no cause, no silt-leach and no cure, because there was no word in \lore{Grathic} for the thing it was doing and nobody had asked for one.

\scenebreak

At the going of the light she came for the basket and carried him up the yard, still at the half-pace, going wide of the seven places as a woman goes wide of a pail of hot water set where a child might knock it. Under the floor of the kiln-house \lore{the carried fire} gave four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer unnamed; through the finger's-width door the river kept its note of \lore{water over water}, a low thing over a lower thing, never numbered among the hearings; overhead the boards kept four nails on the near board, four on the next, and a thumb-width knot where the fifth nail should have stood. Three counts in three places, and none rose into another, because agreeing any two would want a payer put under both and none stood to any. Six hearings of \lore{the full note} stood beside and never among, five from four sources and the sixth, the small flat knock out of a woman as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go and never with the things that came from vessels. No seventh stood in the empty place and none had been invented to fill it, for an empty place kept empty is a finding and a finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged. The heard bell stood beside the told bell and the told figure beside the paid, with Smoke's third arithmetic gone up the road in a locked box and unread under this roof.

At \place{the kindling} \person{Tarn} had the small knife at the very lip of its sill, six counted in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check and the seventh where it could not, and Garl Vail's unforged collector sentence lying beside it, with no name on the man who had turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on the debt. He took the broom only after it had been given its instruction, said \emph{Yes, house} to an early thaw and a late and agreed with both so thoroughly that no argument could find a foot, and kept wanting the late because a late thaw puts off the paying, though the paying had stood in the ruts since the water went. \lore{Ash} was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line and no scrubbing lifted it; the pale clip-ring at his left wrist held what light there was and gave it back; his chest was tight as it had been since the dark and the breath from Seed 16 lay unspent at the top of each reach. He told the wood \emph{Six counted. The next I don't count} in the tone of a man stating the weather. Then the sentence about an ear under this roof being worth more than the debt that held it came to his teeth and went as far as the word that would call the loan and stopped there. It was a remembered stopping, the ninth, with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end and still ungiven. He would not stand a tenth up to make a rule of the nine, because a rule is an account that has found a payer and none stood. The stone Sella had given him in the winter Garl died lay cold in its unmarked pot up in \place{the Debt Loft}, which came to the boy only as a sound with a direction; it was not tried and not forgiveness. The loan stayed uncalled, and his stroke went on over \place{the flagstones} without the half-check, the stones keeping the shallow bright patch where it had stopped.

At the edge of \place{the Vail wheel-shed} \person{Jorra Vail} came through with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bent little finger and white in the creases of a fist that had opened, not bone-coloured slip. He kept dusty forearms and clean ones on one tray and parted them as a surgeon parts clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had been broken nor whether it was a birth-slate; the broken birth-slates lay beneath the lowest rack and the yard entered them into nothing. He spoke to the wheel before he spoke to any person --- \emph{It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady} --- and told a pot \emph{You'll do} in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either. He knew the full note that pot would give three turns before it left his hands, with air under it and a want in it, and withheld the knowing on the near-full tray beside the hearings and never among them, advanced and not paid. Three or more unnamed men had gone low into \place{the back room} before first grey and got no name from him, and he did not say the room was cold; it kept its finger's width of open and its sound of low voices, which arrived at the basket as a direction and nothing more. When Sella's sentence went low, his bent finger stood in the cold iron of \lore{the damper} ahead of it, took the cold out of the fingertip and held until the end arrived at the pot; the boy read one piece of iron with two bites, mercy and mutilation, with no word in Grathic for both, joined to no kiln. The burned-slate word stood at four from \place{Slate Row}, a direction over the boards and not a fifth telling; the two shut-room tellings stood at two and did not become three; the three sayings about a pot stood with the premature Smoke 14 note on its own place and no fourth likeness made a cause; the five askings of the row question stood with the one further shaping beside them and no witness under any and no row named. The promise of the boy's name to the men who burn slates was neither given nor withdrawn and \emph{he's not Vail} stayed sealed as tightly as a lid nobody had tried to lift in three years.

\scenebreak

Under the Vail kiln-house roof she set him on the bed at the low angle of the shelf and not in the Debt Loft. She put the fold right without a look at his face and left the thread frozen, told the fire it was the thaw, turned the coals with the back of a broken pot, said \emph{The house will keep it} where another woman would have said \emph{I}, stood a moment with the back of the pot in her hand counting under her breath toward no opening --- the small intake, the hold, the letting-go --- and pushed coal-black hair back with the wrist that carries the burn, so that the one grey streak at her temple showed pale off the coals. The burn was shaped like a little door closed on nothing; her hand shut half over nothing when her eye went once to \place{the clay jar} standing cold under its \lore{lead glaze} on the shelf by the wall. At the loaf shelf her finger stayed on top of the front edge so the charcoal tally on the underside remained unread, and her lips moved along a row standing with two gaps and gave neither gap a figure. The pot rim went out with its handle two inches beyond the reaching hand, with a half-beat at the setting and no look toward the bed. No instruction came to the basket at any hour; the instruction went to the fire, the wool, the pot and the water.

\lore[The widow's mark]{The widow's mark} hung where it hung on its cord at her throat, a thumb-length of lead-glazed clay with the Vail kiln scratched into it, touched once before the kiln on Smoke 1, held one breath, let go, and touched at no shelf, well, rack, road, basket, yard, market, afternoon, dusk or going of the light on Smoke 28, 29 or 30, because a bed and a basket and a market and a dusk are not openings. The one opening before Smoke 1 stayed behind the household with no lesson, no mending, no sign and no payer named to it; the next real opening stood far off in the first firing of Frost. Then Sella went to her own bed, and the house kept itself by leaving things where they lay.

In the small hours he turned the file out on the towel and found the whole of it exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air. Six hearings of the full note stood beside and never among with no seventh and none invented to fill the empty place; three near-full answers stood advanced and unpaid; three sayings about a pot stood with the premature Smoke 14 note on its own place and no fourth likeness made a cause; four low things stood with water over water a fifth; the told bell stood beside the heard and the told figure beside the paid, with Smoke's third arithmetic gone up the road in a locked box and unread under this roof. Nine stoppings stood with no tenth; three held changes stood on three places, the loft-sill scrape on a fourth and the pale line of the carried man on a fifth, and the held inch on a sixth. Nine record-first hands stood with \person{Ilva}'s ninth on its own tray and her strip unread in her sleeve, and the seventh green was a colour and the seventh shape a shape with no eighth. The dressed grain lay covered and unfigured on the cool stone at the rack end with the third reading gone over it and not knowing what it cost; the space between the figure and its price stayed unsigned. The shallow pan's gone ring, the shelf's two gaps and the fold's one crust with its unnamed place beside it lay on trays that did not touch. The row question stood at five askings with one further shaping, no witness under any and no row named. The house's \lore{not-doings} stood beside his own kept gaps with a finger between and no joining, for a joining would want a payer and none stood. And the finding of the third reading stood on a place of its own: a figure read a third time over the same basket was no nearer the covered thing than a chart laid over a covered body is nearer the wound under it, and three readings were three and not one account. It was not laid across the house's not-doings or across his own kept gaps, and it was not a lesson, a grievance or a doctrine; it was a finding with no payer, exact, complete and worth nothing to anybody under this roof. He checked it all and found it whole, and the wholeness bought him nothing he could lift, speak or spend.

Through the finger's-width door the river kept its low note; under the floor the fire kept four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood; over his head the boards kept four, four and a knot. No fire-sitter stood over the coals to supply a count and no house count was there to borrow. In the room that does not touch, a count had ended some time before and a little silence stood after it, whole and empty on a place of its own. In the square the bell's gap stood where the bell had struck it, and it was not brought under the roof and not heard as a seventh of the full note.

The hand outside the wool went up an inch toward the river's note. It held for the small intake, and for the hold, and for the letting-go of one of the breaths the woman counted in the room that does not touch. It did not come down with that breath. It kept the small height through the little silence after her count had ended, and went on past the river's note to the one after it, carrying two of the house's rhythms across the empty interval with no second house count borrowed for the second. The house's count was kept on its own place; the river's two notes were kept on another; the little silence between them was kept empty on a third. The bell's gap stayed in the square with a finger between it and the held inch, and no stroke was supplied to agree any three, for agreeing would want a payer and none stood. It was one hundred and fourteen, filed on the sixth place beside one hundred and thirteen with no payer named, exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air: an honest coordination and not a longer reach, not a hearing, not a seventh of the full note, not a stopping, not a payment, not one of the held changes, not a key, a power or a rule. He made nothing of it and would not let it stand up as a gift. The hand had carried two rhythms across a silence where a third count stood in a square, and he set the likeness on no tray and drew no line between it and anything else. The hand lay back against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking.

Two hundred and sixty-nine stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in \place{Sarn}, \place{Sill} or \place{Grath}; six months and eighty-nine days were told by nobody. One hundred and eight honest answers had become one hundred and fourteen and none was a reach. The carried fire stopped at four with its fifth unpaid two hundred and sixty-nine days and its payer unnamed; the widow's mark hung where it hung; the grain lay covered and unfigured with Smoke's third arithmetic gone up the road in a locked box and the figure read still not the figure paid; the heard bell stood beside the told. The seventh year stood at its very end and had still not given. Smoke went out without a boundary drawn and neither of the district's two month-names was set right or left wrong; Frost stood one morning ahead and was not reached for, and the first firing of Frost stood far off and unbrought. The file lay open on the towel with every tray parted and every gap left gap, and the count went forward into Frost 1, a day that had not earned its heading. Nothing was opened, named, called, paid or ended; only the hand was placed down, forward and unresolved, and the river's second low note went quiet after it.
