# Chapter 72 — beat sheet: *The Figure Goes Up the Road*

**Frame.** Days 267–269, Smoke 28–30, Eighthday to Tenthday, Y.H. 813. Normal chapter, three lived days, six scenes (two per day), each day opened by its own `\daymark` in the first scene of that day and each lived in full in two scenes of well over eight hundred words. Smoke 30 (Day 269), the third and last Account-day and market of the second firing, is earned by its own morning in Scene 5 and is not reached for on Smoke 28 or 29. There is no market, Cross or bell on an Eighthday or a Ninthday; there is no further market after Smoke 30 in this chapter. Smoke goes out without a boundary drawn and neither of the district's two names for the months is set right or left wrong. Frost comes on in the dark beyond the door; Frost 1 (Day 270) and its first firing (Day 271) stand far off and unbrought. Close third-person past tense; point of view never leaves Dan's eyes; no present-tense Chicago break, no `\memory{}` tag, no Court, collector, Waiting Room or back-room interior, no recap and no ending.

**Counts carried into the first line.** 266 exact, six months and eighty-six days, told by nobody; one hundred and eight honest answers on the sixth place, the hundred and eighth holding across two of the river's intervals with the silence between kept empty and no second house count borrowed; fire's fifth unpaid 266 days, payer unnamed; six hearings beside and never among with no seventh; three near-full answers; three pot sayings with the premature Smoke 14 note on its own place; burned slate at four; shut-room tellings at two; told bell beside heard, told figure beside paid; nine stoppings with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end and still ungiven; nine record-first hands with Ilva's ninth on its own tray and her strip unread; seventh green a colour and seventh shape a shape with no eighth; five askings of the row question with one further shaping, no witness and no row named; the finding that close handling is not entry, and the finding that the house keeps itself by not-doings, each on a place of its own with a finger between and no joining; grain covered and unfigured on the cool stone at the rack end; widow's mark touched once before the kiln on Smoke 1 and at no other hour; Smoke's first and second arithmetic gone up the clay road in Berr's locked box and unread under this roof; Smoke 30 three mornings ahead of the opening line.

**Count at close.** 269 exact, six months and eighty-nine days, told by nobody; one hundred and fourteen honest answers on the sixth place; fire's fifth unpaid 269 days; all other counts unchanged — six hearings still six with no seventh, burned slate still four, shut-room tellings still two, pot sayings still three with the Smoke 14 note apart, row question still five askings with the one shaping and no witness, nine stoppings with no tenth, nine record-first hands with Ilva's strip unread, seventh green a colour and seventh shape a shape with no eighth. Answers: **109, 110** on Day 267; **111, 112** on Day 268; **113, 114** on Day 269. Each is an honest coordination of the held inch, an object on the sixth place and not a reach, hearing, seventh, stopping, payment, held change, key, power or rule. On the 114th the hand carries two of the house's rhythms across the empty interval and leaves the bell's gap in the square with a finger between, supplying no stroke to agree any three.

**The chapter's one meaningful change, advanced and not paid.** On Smoke 30, the third and last reading of Smoke comes to the Cross and the district's arithmetic changes for the third time in that month and cannot be changed back. The boy registers that a figure read a third time over the same basket is no nearer the covered thing it is read over: the arithmetic changes three times where two hundred people stand and goes home locked in Berr's box, while the printed figure stands unchallenged, the figure read is still not the figure paid, and the price of the grain under wool remains unsigned. The not-knowing is as complete after the third reading as after the first, and three readings are three and not one account. He keeps this as a finding with no payer — not a lesson, grievance or doctrine — and does not lay it across the house's not-doings, the covered grain or his own kept gaps, for a joining would want a payer and none stands. Nothing under the Vail roof is settled by the reading; no thread is opened, paid or closed; nothing is joined, named, opened, called, spent or ended.
