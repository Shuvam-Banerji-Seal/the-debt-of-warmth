The morning spent itself and the afternoon came on without an edge to it, the way heat goes out of a pot set off the coals --- no stroke struck, no boundary drawn, only a lessening that settled first behind the pot and then into the fold where \person[Corin]{the boy} lay. Smoke went on under its own name and neither of the district's two names for the months was set right or left wrong by it. There would be no market, no Cross and no bell on an Eighthday; Smoke 30 stood two mornings ahead and was not reached for, and the first firing of Frost stood far off and unbrought.

He took the body as the facts presented it, in the order he had learned in a country that had no word for any of it. One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from his ear, gone past the point where a child would have eaten it, and the empty place beside it kept its name to itself; no third had come into the afternoon to make the row come out even. The thread at his mouth had frozen in the dry heat and was unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread, and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. Cold had come to cheek and temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, and had stopped there as at the end of a line written out for it, neither going forward nor going away. The afterdrop waited beyond all three, past a warming nobody in this house would perform.

He knew the composed face that is not composure. He knew a cold patient cannot clot, and will seem better before he is worse, and that a man must be warmed from the outside and slowly. He wanted the crust lifted the two inches to his mouth. Both were true, and in a body two hundred and sixty-seven days old the second was the louder, and he entered neither, not even on the place where he kept the things he did not enter. The surgeon took the inventory; the boy lay under the fold and wanted his breakfast. There was no third hand in the room to lift either thing for him.

\person[Garl Vail]{Garl} was kept in objects and not in a story, and his name had not been spoken under this roof for two hundred and sixty-seven days. The salt-stiff coat with the crease at the left shoulder held a broader man's shape on its peg behind the door; the wheel-seat in \place{the Vail wheel-shed} kept its twenty-year lean and its hollow on that side, and nobody filled it. The proverb about leaving the crack showing had gone years ago to a pot with no crack in it and was said now over a corner where the repair had been rubbed out --- a saying about honest repairs said in a house that kept its warmth by leaving things where they lay. The debt against a firing of tiles went around the household's talk without a figure put on it, circled the way a woman goes around a pot left on the floor in the dark, neither paid nor settled nor done with. No collector came up \place{the clay road} on Smoke 28 and none went down it.

\person{Sella Vail} carried him out at the half-pace from every open slate. In \place{the Clay Yard} she went around seven things with a look and a wide step and no number: the clean dark line at the Vail corner where the ridge had turned and been burned and called dealt with; the finer grey hairline beside it that the house called \lore{the thaw}; the space beneath \place{the lowest rack} where the broken birth-slates lay and the yard entered them into nothing; the round ring on the boards where the shallow clay pan had stood, with no scrape, no crumb, no print, no chip and no word in it; \place{the yard well} with \place{the trough} beside it bare and dark and its water called weather; the field's empty place out on \place{the worked strips}; and the returning-line place where a hand had gone in on Seed 15 and had not come out. She went around that last one as wide as a pail of hot water set where a child might knock it, and gave it no name and no figure. Seven in a row were seven and not an account, because an account wants a payer standing under it and none stood. The hand that had gone in and not come back was carried and not named, not paid, not joined to the shelf's two gaps or the fold's one crust or the dressed grain under wool or any other kept absence.

Below the sill a child's foot that had stayed cold after the flood was wrapped in a woman's wool and called the cold by her and weather by the yard. He kept it as the extremity going before the core and the afterdrop waiting beyond, and named no cause, no silt-leach and no cure, because there was no \lore{Grathic} word for the thing the foot was doing and nobody had asked him for one. It lay on a place of its own, and no lesson was made of it and no payer put under it.

A wrong wind came up off \place{High Kiln} and brought the sweet edge of \lore{lead glaze} over \place{the boards}. It touched the fold and went out again on the wind that brought it, a direction arrived nowhere, not a second edge paid, not joined to the man who had been carried out on Day 188 or to any saying about a pot. The yard went on with its work as though nothing had crossed it, and the boy kept the sweetness on its own tray and let it lie.

\scenebreak

At \place{the kindling} the broom was instructed before any person, the instruction going to the tool and not to the man, and \person[Tarn Vail]{Tarn} took it up as a man takes up an instrument already named. “Yes, house,” he said to those who wanted the thaw early and “Yes, house” to those who wanted it late, agreeing with both so entirely that no argument could find a foot to stand on, and going on wanting the late one, for a late thaw puts off the paying, though the paying had stood in the ruts since the water went. He told the wood, “Six counted. The next I don't count,” in the tone of a man stating the weather. The small knife lay at the very lip of its sill, six in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check and the seventh at the edge where it could not, and Garl's unforged collector sentence lay beside it --- the one about a man sent for a collector in the Long Cold who turned back at the frozen ford, with no name on the man and no figure on the debt. The sentence about an ear under this roof being worth more than the debt that held it came to his teeth and went all the way to the word that would call the loan and stopped there with no figure and no name, a remembered ninth stopping and not a new one, with no tenth stood up before the seventh year at its very end, which had still not given. He drew the breath he should have spent at the top of the reach and did not spend it.

His chest had been tight since the dark. \lore{Ash} was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line and no scrubbing lifted it, and the pale clip-ring at his left wrist held what light there was and gave it back. Up in \place{the Debt Loft}, which came to the boy only as a sound with a direction and never as a room he was taken into, the warm stone Sella had given him in the winter Garl died lay cold in its unmarked pot, two years gone, not tried and not forgiveness, and he did not go up to it. His stroke went over \place{the flagstones} past the shallow bright patch without the half-check and over the road a house sends a man down when it wants a collector without once turning along it; no collector came up or went down the clay road on Smoke 28.

At the edge of \place{the Vail wheel-shed} \person{Jorra Vail} came through with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bent little finger and white in the creases of a fist that had opened, not the bone colour of slip. He kept dusty forearms and clean ones on one tray and parted them as a surgeon parts clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had broken nor whether it was a birth-slate. The broken birth-slates lay beneath the lowest rack where the yard went around them and entered them into nothing. Three or more men unnamed had gone low into the finger's-width-open \place{the back room} before first grey, and he gave the house no name for them and did not say the room was cold; its sound came to the basket only as a direction, low and without words the boy could have repeated. He spoke to the wheel before he spoke to any person. “It doesn't care whose hands,” he said. “It cares if they're steady.” He told a pot “You'll do” in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either, and knew the \lore{full note} that pot would give three turns before it left his hands, with air under it and a want in it, and withheld the knowing on the near-full tray beside the hearings and never among them. The boy kept the withheld knowing where questions could last for years, and did not call it a hearing.

The burned-slate word stood at four from \place{Slate Row}, a direction over the boards arrived nowhere and not a fifth telling. The two tellings of the counting room shut in the war stood at two and did not become three. The three sayings about a pot stayed three, with the premature Smoke 14 note on its own place and no fourth likeness made a cause; no pot was lost and no name was given to what ailed the man carried out on Day 188. The promise of the boy's name to the men who burn slates was neither given nor withdrawn, and “he's not Vail” stayed sealed as tightly as a lid nobody had tried to lift in three years. When Sella's sentence went low, his bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper} ahead of it, took the cold out of the fingertip and held until the end of her instruction arrived at the pot; the household straightened around his silence the way cloth straightens when a weight is lifted. The boy read one piece of iron with two bites, the bite that holds a vessel whole and the bite that can take the hand off a man, mercy and mutilation in one object with no \lore{Grathic} word for both, joined to no kiln.

Under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof she set him on \place{the bed} at the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf} and not in the Debt Loft. She put the fold right without a look at his face, left the thread frozen, told the fire it was the thaw, turned the coals with the back of a broken pot, said \emph{The house will keep it} where another woman would have said \emph{I}, counted under her breath toward no opening --- the small intake, the hold, the letting-go --- and went to her own bed. \lore[the widow's mark]{The widow's mark} hung where it hung, touched once before the kiln on Smoke 1 and at no shelf, well, rack, road, bed or going of the light since, because a bed and a basket and a dusk are not openings. The one opening before Smoke 1 stayed behind the household with no lesson, no sign, no mending and no payer named; the next real opening stood far off in the first firing of Frost.

In the small hours he turned the file out on the towel and found it exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air. Six hearings of the full note stood beside and never among, with the seventh place kept empty and none invented to fill it; three near-full answers stood on their own place; three sayings about a pot stood with the premature Smoke 14 note apart and no fourth likeness made a cause. Four low things stood with \lore{water over water} a fifth; the told bell stood beside the heard, and the told figure beside the paid, with Smoke's second arithmetic gone up the clay road in \person[Berr Clay]{Berr}'s locked box and unread under this roof. Nine stoppings stood with no tenth; three held changes stood on three places, the loft scrape on a fourth and the pale line of the carried man on a fifth, and the held inch on a sixth with one hundred and nine answers. Nine record-first hands stood with \person{Ilva}'s ninth on a place of its own and her strip unread in her sleeve; the seventh green was a colour and the seventh shape a shape with no eighth. The pan's gone ring, the shelf's two gaps and the fold's one crust lay on trays that did not touch. The ownerless question stood at five askings with one shaping beside them and no witness under any and no row named. The house's \lore{not-doings} stood on a place apart from his own gaps with a finger between, a finding with no payer and not a doctrine. The arithmetic that would be read over the covered grain two mornings off would not know what it cost, and the space between the figure and its price stayed unsigned.

Through the finger's-width door the river kept its note of water over water, a low thing over a lower thing, never numbered among the hearings; under the floor \lore{the carried fire} gave four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer without a name for two hundred and sixty-seven days; overhead the boards kept four nails on the near board, four on the next and a thumb-width knot where the fifth nail should have stood. Three counts in three places, and none rose into another, because agreeing any two would want a payer put under both and none stood. In the room that does not touch a count had ended and a little silence stood after it, and the little silence was left empty on a place of its own and not crossed.

The hand outside the wool went up an inch toward the river's low note. It held for the small intake. It carried one of the house's rhythms across the little silence after the count that had ended in the room that does not touch, taking it as an object in its own right, crossing no second count and filling nothing to make a stroke in a row that had none, and came down on the river's next low note. It was one hundred and ten, filed on the sixth place beside one hundred and nine with no payer named, exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air: an honest coordination and not a reach, not a hearing, not a seventh of the full note, not a stopping, not a payment, not one of the held changes, not a key, a power or a rule. An object among objects. He made nothing of it and would not let it stand up as a gift.

Two hundred and sixty-seven stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in \place{Sarn}, \place{Sill} or \place{Grath}; six months and eighty-seven days were told by nobody; the fire's fifth was unpaid the same number of days with its payer unnamed. The hand lay back against the fold as a tool put away where the next hand will find it without looking. The towel lay on the fold with every tray parted and every gap left gap, the little silence uncrossed, the thread still frozen, the crust two inches from his ear with its empty place unnamed and no third. Smoke went on without a boundary drawn and Smoke 30 stood two mornings ahead; the count moved forward into a day that had not earned its name, and the hand lay where the next hand would find it.
