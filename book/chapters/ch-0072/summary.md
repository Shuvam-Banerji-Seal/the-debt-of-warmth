## Chronicle

On the twenty-eighth of Smoke the boy came into his two hundred and sixty-seventh day and the house took the hand back as a bench takes back a tool. Sella Vail went to the fire before the basket, told the coals it was the thaw and said the house would keep it. Fire, river and boards kept three counts that did not agree, for agreeing any two would want a payer and none stood. The grain lay under wool on the cool stone, turned once for damp and given no figure. The hand held for the small intake and came down on the next note (one hundred and nine), then carried one of the house's rhythms across the silence after a count in the other room (one hundred and ten).

On the twenty-ninth the grain was turned once more under the wool and learned nothing. At the well Ilva drew with the pail against her knee and answered, Ask the pot what it cost. I keep the count, not the account; her notched birch stayed in her sleeve and no tenth hand was filed. No voice came over the boards and the row question was not asked a sixth time --- five askings stood with one shaping and no witness. The hand held through one interval of the river (one hundred and eleven), then kept the silence after a counted breath as an object of its own (one hundred and twelve).

On the thirtieth Sella set the basket in the moot-post's shade at the Cross and stood half a pace off while two hundred leaned in. Berr Clay came up short off the clay road, the red cord of his office folded square, the green of tasted glaze at one mouth-corner; he wiped his thumb twice, read one in twelve over the basket, denied the levy with your house, never I, and went off with the box locked, believing he had done a kindness. The arithmetic changed for the third time and could not be changed back, yet the figure read was still not the figure paid. Hessa carried the narrow slate up the road unread; the bell went four, four and a gap. The boy kept the finding that three readings are three and not one account, laid across nothing.

The hand went up an inch toward the river's note, held through a counted breath, kept the small height through the silence after it and went on past the next low note, carrying two rhythms across the empty interval and leaving the bell's gap in the square with a finger between (one hundred and fourteen). Two hundred and sixty-nine stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody; six months and eighty-nine days were told by nobody. The file lay open with every tray parted, and the hand lay back against the fold as a tool put away, the count going forward into Frost 1.
