\daymark{365}{Stone 6, Sixthday, Y.H. 813}

The hand lay back against the fold on answer three hundred and four as a tool lies where the next hand will find it without looking, and the file lay open on \place{the towel} at the head of \place{the bed} with every gap left gap. The small hours had come on under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill}, \place{Grath}, and \person[Corin]{the boy} took the room in as a field is taken before work, by the small sounds first because the small sounds do not lie and the large ones are mostly weather: the dry rasp of a coal settling under its ash at \place{the hearth}, the small tick of cooling clay somewhere along \place{the loaf shelf}, the river's note low under its winter skin through \place{the finger's-width door}, and over all of them the roof's own silence, which is not the absence of sound but the sound of a house holding itself level. Three hundred and sixty-four had become three hundred and sixty-five exact. Six months and one hundred and eighty-four days had become six months and one hundred and eighty-five days, told by nobody under any roof. He kept the number because keeping was what he had, and had long since stopped expecting anybody to say what a number counted, which was not the same thing as not wanting it. Nobody said it. Nobody under this roof kept days in numbers at all, but in loaves, in glances, in the space where a loaf had stood and in the shape a room took when a weight was lifted out of it.

Three hundred and sixty-five stood round and complete and meant nothing to the house. It was a round number nobody spoke and nobody marked, and no threshold was crossed in it, because a threshold wants a line drawn and a foot set over and a hand to draw the line, and no hand under this roof had done it. He did not stand it up as a line crossed. He had known a year as a thing men put on slates in \place{Slate Row} and as a thing a district paid for in figures and could not change back; he knew it here as the number of times a basket had been set down and taken up and set down again, which was a different arithmetic and not one anybody had entered. \lore{The carried fire} kept four low pulls with the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer unnamed three hundred and sixty-five days. The name of \person{Garl Vail}, whose salt-stiff coat kept a broader man's shape on its peg behind the door and whose wheel-seat kept its twenty-year hollow that nobody filled, had not been spoken under this roof the same number. Two counts stood exact and unspent and nobody joined them, for agreeing any two would want a payer put under both and none stood.

The file came out on the towel as instruments come out on a towel, and he went over it as a surgeon goes over a tray before the lights come up, by the place of each thing and not by its name. Three hundred and four honest answers stood on the sixth place, each an object and not a power, and not one had stood up and called itself a doctrine, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood. Six hearings of \lore{the full note} stood beside and never among, the sixth the small flat knock out of \person[Dessa Silt] as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go and never among the sources, and the seventh place stayed empty and none was invented to fill it. The heard bell, four, four and a gap, stayed in its square beside the told bell of \lore{the Long Night}, and the told figure stood beside the paid figure with no signature on the space between and no doctrine standing up out of nine. Nine record-first hands kept no tenth, with \person{Ilva}'s ninth on a place of its own and \person{Hessa Rale}'s eighth a finger's width away; nine stoppings kept no tenth before the seventh year at its very end, and the loan stood uncalled. Five held objects sat each on its place with a finger between every pair, the staying at the inch, the early letting-go, the handful let fall, the thumb's resting on the uncut seventh, the sixth opening at the kiln mouth on \place{Ash} 2; the pot-crack resting of Ash 9 lay beside them, the hand-at-the-sill breath of Ash 14 beside that, and the extra holding \person{Jorra Vail} left in the cold iron on Ash 26 beside all, each found where put and not made again, none a sixth, a seventh or an eighth, no payer standing under any. The burned slate stood at five, the shut-room tellings at two, the well-saying at four shapings, the row at five askings with no witness and expressly no sixth. The Ash 5 joining, the Ash 17 direction and the Ash 23 direction stood where they had been put and did not repeat and bought no lesson. The river's winter skin lay on its own place beside \lore{water over water}, not a fourth of the three coverings, not a sixth low thing, not a hearing and not spent on the firing behind the wall or on any date. Seven openings in seven months were seven and not a rule, the widow's mark touched once before the kiln on \place{Stone} 5 and at no other hour, no eighth made due. The setting-down-wrong-and-swept-around stood on a place of its own beside the premature note of \place{Smoke} 14, gathered once and not made again. Nine arithmetics of the year stood behind one unsigned figure with all three Ash subtractions locked and unread in \person{Berr Clay}'s box up \place{the clay road}, the key gone up in a coat pocket and not coming back down, the figure read still not the figure paid. Every gap stayed gap.

The body took itself in the surgeon's old order, and the old order did not flatter it. One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from his ear, gone harder and browner through the long cold, its corner standing a hair off the wool, its empty place beside it unnamed and no third come to make the row come out even. The thread of drool at his mouth had frozen in the dry kiln heat and was unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. Cold had stopped at cheek, temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, as though it had come to the end of a line written out for it with neither an order to go farther nor an order to go away. \lore{The Afterdrop} waited past all three, past a warming nobody in this house would perform. He knew the composed face that was not composure, knew a cold patient cannot clot and will seem better before he is worse, knew the order in which a body takes heat back, the small joint first and the core last, and the order in which it gives it up, the reverse, which comes sooner than anybody likes, and that pink returning to a face is the surface still cold and not the core. He had once taken a core temperature with a glass under a tongue and read the number off it in a white room; now he could not lift a crust two inches to his own mouth. The infant wanted the crust lifted, the thread wiped and a hand that would close on the pot handle set where the fingers could reach it. Both were true, the second was louder, neither won, and the quarrel between them was the work.

\person{Sella Vail} came to \place{the hearth} before she came to the basket, fire before basket, as she had come at every opening of every day for three years. “It's the thaw,” she told the coals, and turned them with the back of a broken pot, the way a woman turns a thing that has been left to keep overnight and must not be woken all at once. “The house will keep it,” she said, where another woman would have said I, and the sentence went to the fire and not to the wicker, for nobody under any roof tells a basket what a keeping is worth. She stood a moment counting a small intake, a hold and a letting-go toward no opening, giving neither gap a figure; the two gaps stayed where they were, and a finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged. Her finger lay on top of the loaf shelf's front edge so the charcoal tally underneath stayed unread, and her lips moved along a two-gapped row with neither gap figured, so that the shape of the days kept itself without being counted out loud. Her eye went once to \place{the clay jar} standing cold under \lore{lead glaze}, and her hand shut half over nothing, the little-door burn on the back of her left hand closing on air; the cord of \lore{the widow's mark} at her throat was oiled where a working thumb had touched it ten thousand times, and the mark hung where it hung and was not touched, because a morning and a hearth are not openings --- the hand goes to the mark before the kiln and at no other hour, and it had been touched once before the firing on Stone 5 and at no hour since. Seven openings in seven months were seven and not a rule, and no eighth was made due.

At \place{the pot board} she lifted the twice-mended pot with her thumb first where the wire crossed the crack, held the weight a half-beat before she let it come on the handle, and left the hairline showing between old-brass staple and tighter wire. “Leave the crack showing. People trust a thing they can see has been handled,” she told the pot. “If it's worth mending it's worth mending twice. The second time is the real one,” she told the fire. Both sayings went to the pot and the fire and never to a person; neither was improved in the keeping and neither was wasted, and repair stayed apart from restoration, for a restored thing hides the hand that handled it and a mended thing leaves that hand showing. The sayings had been Garl's once, improved by twenty years of use and very likely not as good as he had said them, and his name went unspoken while they went on being said. She turned the dressed grain under wool with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child, her fingers going in and coming out without closing, no figure lifted off it and no handful; the empty fingers went on a place of its own beside the one object of \place{Bone} 22, a keeping and not a second handful. Then she set the pot rim two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat at the setting and no look toward the bed. The half-beat was the distance between intention and arrest, bought at nothing and not a gift, for a gift wants a giver standing and none stood; he had once known a surgeon who could not resist closing that distance in a patient and had learned, by closing it, what it cost. The rim stayed where it was set. The hand could not close two inches on it and did not try, and the wanting was quieter than the crust at his ear and did not win.

The boy's first birthday stood one morning beyond the close of this day, on \place{Stone} 7, and was not brought into a day that had not earned it. He kept it as a date and only as a date, inside the exact keeping where the firing of Stone 5 stood behind the household with no lesson anybody named and \lore{the Hearth Drawing} on Stone 12 stood further off. No party stood behind that date and no cake, no figure, no recognition from anybody under any roof when it came; a Court that could count a district's deductions to the tenth of an ember would not notice that a basket had been carried under this roof for a year, and a house that kept its days in loaves and glances would mark the morning by the weather and by nothing more. He had long ago given up expecting to be told what a number counted. The fired kiln stood cool behind its wall with nothing made of its opening that any hand could name, and the river's skin lay where it had been put and was not spent on the morning that was coming or on any date, for a thing joined to a date wants a hand to join it and none had done it. The round number at the month's end was a round number and not a threshold; he did not stand it up and call it a line crossed, and no roof above him did either.

The first light came on about the room without a boundary drawn, the way warmth comes off a fired wall into a yard, by degrees and with no hand to mark the degree. It found the pot brush in its notch, the wooden dish upside down to drain, the twice-mended pot on the pot board with the hairline of the original break showing dark between old-brass staple and tighter wire; it found the fold at his ear and the crust two inches off with its corner standing a hair above the wool, and it did not find the empty place beside the crust, because a space has no surface for light to lie on. He took the room as a field is taken before work, by what was there and by what was not. The coals held under their ash and gave the fire's four low pulls with the silence where the fifth should have stood, and the note through the finger's-width door went low over lower under its skin, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming, a covering put on by the cold and taken off by the cold and entered nowhere. Overhead \place{the boards} kept four nails on the near one, four on the next and a thumb-width knot where a fifth might have been driven and was not. Three counts in three places, and none rose into another, because agreeing any two would want a payer put under both and none stood; the bell's gap stayed in its square and was not folded into any of them. Attention was the one thing no institution in Grath had thought to tax, and it cost nobody anything and bought nothing that could be entered on a slate. He had spent the whole of his attention on two inches of air and it had bought him the exact use of his own noticing, which was the only honest work available and worth nothing at the distance.

In the small hours the hand outside the wool came up an inch toward the river's note under the finger's-width door. It was low under its skin, found where put and not made again. The hand did not reach, and the inch was not a reach; most of what a hand does in a field is wait, and he had once taken a hand into a cavity slowly, with the whole of the attention, and knew the difference between the moving and the waiting. It took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole on the note's own finishing under the ice, let the intake go, and came straight down and laid itself back against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. The note had done before the hand came down, so the letting-go was neither early nor stayed, and the hand did not stay; it was the note's own finishing under the covering that ended the interval and not any decision of the hand. The intake was not a breath kept for warmth, the interval not a stopping of the river, the note not his. Nothing crossed under the roof in that inch as sign, lesson, mending, cause, payment or payer, and no sixth hearing, seventh opening, sixth held object, sixth telling, third shut-room telling, sixth asking, fifth shaping, tenth hand or tenth stopping was invented to make a row come out even.

He filed it as answer three hundred and five: exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the nine arithmetics, the bell's gap, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the holding in the iron, the held breath at the sill, the staying at the inch, the early letting-go, the seventh opening, the pot-crack resting, the river's skin, the ninth hand, the Ash 5 joining, the Ash 17 direction, the Ash 23 direction, the setting-down-wrong-and-swept-around, the fourth shaping, the strip in the sleeve nor his own kept gaps. Three hundred and five honest answers stood on the sixth place, each an object and not a power, and not one stood up and called itself a doctrine. The carried fire kept four low pulls with the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer unnamed three hundred and sixty-five days; the name of the man whose salt-stiff coat kept his shape on the peg behind the door had not been spoken under this roof the same number. Six months and one hundred and eighty-five days were told by nobody, and the round number stood round and unmarked and was no threshold anybody crossed.

The hand lay back against the fold on answer three hundred and five; the file lay open on the towel with every gap left gap; one stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from the ear with its empty place unnamed and no third, the thread frozen and unwiped; and through the finger's-width door the river, \place{the Ilm}, went low under its winter skin, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming. Cold had stopped at cheek, temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, as at the end of a line written out for it with neither an order to go farther nor an order to go away, and the afterdrop waited past all three. Stone kept its sixth day without a boundary drawn and neither of the district's two habits of naming its months was set right or left in so many words; the deep-winter month held. Forward, not closed.
