## Chronicle

Stone came on in the deep of winter, and on the fifth morning Sella Vail went to the hearth before the basket, told the coals it was the thaw and banked her fire against their coming back. She carried the boy down at the half-pace she keeps from every open slate and set him beside the kiln mouth, level and out of the draught, where the heat came from the flank and not full in the face. Then, her hands gone still, she put her left hand to the widow's mark at her throat, held it one breath, let go, lifted the clay lid with the back of her hand and tipped the carried coal under a broken pot. The fire took, answering four low pulls with the silence where the fifth should have stood. Nobody praised the opening, named what it mended or made a lesson of it. Seven openings in seven months were seven and not a rule, and no eighth was made due.

The yard went around its seven walked-around places each afternoon, widest around the place in the returning line where a hand had gone in on Seed 15 and had not come out; the gone frost, the skin on the trough and the plate of ice on the flat stone kept their separate places, and the river under the finger's-width door went low over lower beneath its winter skin. Ilva came once to the well and, asked what the firings cost, answered that she kept the count and not the account, her strip staying in her sleeve. Tarn kept six where the house could check and the seventh at the lip beyond, said *Six counted. The next I don't count*, and brought his ear-sentence to the word that would call the loan and stopped there, the ninth stopping with no tenth. Jorra parted dusty forearms from clean as clot from vessel, spoke to the wheel before any person and put his bent finger into the cold damper ahead of a sentence unfinished. On the packing bench a pot set down wrong gave its thin note before its time and a hand swept around it and set it right; the boy filed the doing on a place of its own and spent nothing on it.

Through the three days the hand outside the wool went up an inch toward the river's note, took a small intake, held it whole on the note's own finishing under the ice and came straight down. Six answers were entered, three hundred and one through six, and the count stood at three hundred and sixty-five --- a round number nobody under any roof spoke or marked, and no threshold anybody crossed. What attention bought was the exact use of a man's noticing, worth nothing at two inches of air; what it cost was nothing anyone could enter on a slate. Stone kept its sixth day without a boundary drawn, and the deep-winter month held.
