- **Day/time:** Day 365, Stone 6, Sixthday; afternoon through the going of the light and the bed, then the small hours at the close of the day and the chapter.
- **Place:** Clay Yard seven places, yard well and flat stone, rack end, kindling and flagstones, wheel-shed edge and damper; then hearth, pot board, loaf shelf, bed, boards, towel under the Vail roof.
- **Present:** Corin/Dan, Sella Vail, Tarn, Jorra Vail; Garl in objects. Walk-ons only: hare-carrier, cracked-jar man, kindling woman, two fence-menders. No Ilva this afternoon (offstage since Stone 4), no Berr, no Hessa, no collector, no runner, no market/Cross/bell, no green-mouthed woman or foundling sighting, no ownerless voice.
- **Beats, in order:**
  1. Sella carries him home at the going of the light at the half-pace, goes wide around the seven places with a look and a wide step and no number and widest around the returning-line place, sets him on his bed under the roof and never in the Debt Loft, righted the fold without a look at his face, hand resting the half-beat longer than the setting needed, thread left frozen and unwiped, and says nothing of where they had been.
  2. Yard takes its afternoon tending: three coverings weather entered nowhere with a finger between every pair and no fourth likeness made a cause; river's winter skin found where put and not made again on its own place beside water-over-water, not spent on the firing, the birthday or the Drawing; dressed grain covered and unfigured, no handful; hunts and repairs as directions judged by the carrying arm and the weather.
  3. Tarn at the kindling: six/seventh, *Six counted. The next I don't count*, Ash 14 breath and Bone 26 thumb's resting checked and found where put and not made again, ear-sentence to the calling word and stopped as the remembered ninth stopping with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end, Seed 16 breath unspent, loan uncalled, chest tight, Ash in the knuckle creases in the colour of the clean dark line, pale clip-ring, stroke over the bright patch without the half-check.
  4. Jorra at the wheel-shed edge and damper: meal-fine slate dust white in a fist-crease, men unnamed gone low before first grey, dusty and clean forearms parted as clot from vessel, wheel spoken to before any person (*It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady*), pot told *You'll do*, full note known three turns ahead and withheld from a High Kiln master, bent finger into the cold iron ahead of Sella's unfinished sentence — one iron with two bites — then taken out without leaving the extra holding of Ash 26 a second time; *he's not Vail* sealed, promise of the boy's name to the men who burn slates neither given nor withdrawn.
  5. Going of the light: hearth grammar closes the day at both ends — fire before basket, coals told the thaw and turned with a broken pot's back, *The house will keep it*, intake-hold-letting-go toward no opening with neither gap figured, finger on top of the shelf so the charcoal stays unread, lips on the two-gapped row unfigured, one glance at the clay jar and the little-door burn closing on air, twice-mended pot lifted thumb-first with both proverbs and hairline showing between old-brass staple and tighter wire, dressed grain turned under wool with no figure or handful, pot rim set two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat and no look toward the bed; the boy set on his bed under the roof and never in the Debt Loft, fold righted without a look at his face, hand resting the half-beat longer than the setting needed before it went away, thread left frozen and unwiped. The widow's mark was touched only before the kiln on Stone 5 and at no other hour; seven openings in seven months are seven and not a rule with no eighth made due.
  6. **Small hours — the surgeon's satisfaction and the full ledger:** a field taken as found and left as found, costing nothing and therefore not peace. 364 became 365 exact, six months and 184 became 185 days, a round number nobody speaks and no threshold crossed; fire's fifth unpaid 365 days, payer unnamed; Garl's name unspoken 365 days. Ledger gone over as instruments on a towel: **306 honest answers** on the sixth place, six hearings with seventh empty, heard bell beside told, nine hands no tenth, nine stoppings no tenth before the seventh year at its very end and loan uncalled, five held objects plus pot-crack resting plus hand-at-sill breath plus Ash 26 holding, burned slate five, shut-room tellings two, well-saying four shapings, row five askings, Ash 5/17/23 apart, setting-down-wrong-and-swept-around on its own place beside the premature note of Smoke 14, nine arithmetics behind one unsigned figure with all three Ash subtractions locked in Berr's box, figure read not figure paid, no signature, no doctrine out of nine, river skin on its own place beside water-over-water, first firing behind the household with no lesson and no payer.
  7. Three counts in three places do not become one: fire's four pulls with its fifth unpaid under the floor, boards' four nails and four and a thumb-width knot overhead, river's water under its skin through the finger's-width door; agreeing any two would want a payer put under both and none stood, so the bell's gap stays in its square. Attention is the one thing no institution in Grath has thought to tax, and it cost nobody anything and bought nothing that could be entered on a slate. No sixth hearing, seventh opening, sixth held object, sixth telling, third shut-room telling, sixth asking, fifth shaping, tenth hand or tenth stopping was invented to make a row come out even. The far-off hand that will one day lay the private keepings together is still far off and is not his.
  8. Underneath the exactness the infant still wants the crust lifted, the thread wiped and a hand on the pot handle; both true, the second louder, neither winning, the quarrel the work.
  9. **Final answer and close:** in the small hours the hand goes up an inch toward the river's note under the finger's-width door, the way a hand goes up in a field it has learned not to disturb; it does not reach and the inch is not a reach, for most of what a hand does in a field is wait, and he had once taken a hand into a cavity slowly, with the whole of the attention, and knew the difference between the moving and the waiting. It takes a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, holds the interval whole on the note's own finishing under the ice, lets the intake go and comes straight down and lays itself back against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. The note had done before the hand came down, so the letting-go was neither early nor stayed and the hand did not stay; the note's own finishing ended the interval and not any decision of the hand. The intake is not a breath kept for warmth, the interval not a stopping of the river, the note not his. He files it as answer **306** — exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across none of the held objects. Three hundred and six honest answers stand on the sixth place, each an object and not a power and not one standing up and calling itself a doctrine. Stone kept its sixth day without a boundary drawn and neither habit of naming its months was corrected; the deep-winter month held, the birthday on Stone 7 (Day 366) one morning beyond the close as a date named to nobody, the Hearth Drawing on Stone 12 further still.
- **Emotional turn:** from the field taken as found and left as found, through the full ledger held at exactly 306 objects and 365 days, to the small restraint of the hand and the infant's still-louder want — satisfaction that costs nothing, a number that means nothing to anyone who could say it, and a quarrel that does not resolve.
- **Sensory anchors:** the twice-mended pot's dark hairline between old-brass staple and tighter wire; frozen thread and leathery crust; Ash in knuckle creases the colour of the clean dark line; meal-fine slate dust white in a fist-crease; the river low over lower under its winter skin, nearer at the going of the water; the boards' four nails, four and a thumb-width knot.
- **Threads advanced:** **T058/T079 advanced and NOT paid** (seven openings not a rule, mark touched only before the kiln, no eighth made due, nothing made of the firing); T061/T082 (answers 305–306; **306** honest answers; six hearings with seventh empty; setting-down-wrong filed and not paid); T071/T072 (**365 exact**, six months and 185 days, round number nobody speaks); T088 (fire's fifth unpaid 365 days); T009/T013/T092 and T098 (Garl in objects, name unspoken 365 days, yard's seven places, returning-line widest); T060/T086 (body inventory, surgeon versus infant); T077 (grain unfigured, hunts and repairs as directions); T083/T085 (coverings, river skin found and not spent); T036/T076/T006 (six/seventh, checked keepings, ninth stopping, loan uncalled); T062/T035/T031 (Jorra's dust, damper's two bites, Ash 26 not repeated, promise ungiven); T100 (charcoal unread, finger on top of the shelf); T080 (Ilva's ninth carried, no tenth); T015/T041/T047 held (nine arithmetics, one unsigned figure, no doctrine out of nine); T035/T031/T065/T095 and T101 held (Ash 5 joining, Ash 17 and Ash 23 directions apart, no ownerless voice); T023/T089 and T093/T094/T096 carried (tins shut, cold foot uncured and unnamed).
- **Foreshadowing planted / paid off:** nothing paid. Plants the birthday of Chapter 105 as a date nobody marks and the Drawing of Chapter 106 further off; keeps the seventh opening, the river skin, the setting-down-wrong, the nine arithmetics and the far-off hand all gathering and unspent. No doctrine stands up out of the round number.
- **Exact last image:** the hand laid back against the fold on answer 306; the file open on the towel at the head of the bed with every gap left gap; the twice-mended pot on the pot board with the hairline of the original break showing between old-brass staple and tighter wire; one stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust two inches from the ear with its empty place unnamed and no third, the thread frozen and unwiped; nine arithmetics locked and unread up the clay road in Berr Clay's box with the key gone up in a coat pocket and not coming back down; and through the finger's-width door the river under its winter skin, low over lower, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming, while the boards overhead keep four nails on the near one, four on the next and a thumb-width knot where a fifth might have been driven and was not. Stone 6 was done and the deep-winter month held. Forward, not closed.

## The pull into Chapter 105

The chapter closes on the small restraint of the hand at answer 306 and the count exact at **three hundred and sixty-five** — six months and one hundred and eighty-five days told by nobody, a round number nobody under any roof speaks and a threshold nobody crosses. Nothing crossed in these three days as sign, lesson, mending, cause, payment or payer: the first firing of Stone stands behind the household with no lesson anybody names, the river's skin stays on its own place beside water-over-water and is not spent on the firing or on any date, the setting-down-wrong-and-swept-around stays gathered once on its own place, seven openings are seven and not a rule with no eighth made due, and the far-off hand that will lay the private keepings together is still far off and not his. Chapter 105 opens in the small hours of **Day 366, Stone 7, Seventhday**, with the hand laid down on answer 306 and the count still exact at 365 — the boy's first birthday lived as an ordinary day with no party, no cake, no figure and no recognition from anybody under any roof, nothing made due by a round number or by the return of the month he was born in, and the Hearth Drawing on Stone 12 (Day 371) standing three mornings beyond that close, unbrought. The hand goes on being laid down where the next hand will find it without looking.
