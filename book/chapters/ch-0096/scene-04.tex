In the afternoon \person{Sella Vail} carried the basket down under the finger's-width door and took \person[Corin]{the boy} out into \place{the Clay Yard}, and the instruction went to the wool and the river wind and never to the basket. “Keep it off his ears,” she told the wind, and set the fold right under his chin without a look at his face. She carried him level at the half-pace she kept from every open slate, the way a woman carries a pot filled to the brim and does not slop it. Three hundred and forty stood exact behind him, six months and one hundred and sixty days told by nobody in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill}, \place{Grath}. Under the floor \lore{the carried fire} had given four low pulls in the morning with its fifth unpaid the same number of days and its payer unnamed; the salt-stiff coat on its peg had kept a man's shape the same number of days without a name spoken over it. Nothing from the first reading of \place{Ash} crossed the yard with him as sign, lesson, mending, cause or payment. The figure had gone up where figures go up and had answered nothing under this roof, and the yard was not its echo. He took the afternoon as a field taken in the order it offered itself, by the small sounds first, because the small sounds do not lie and the large ones are mostly weather.

The crust lay two inches from his ear in the dry cold, stiff and leathery, its corner standing a hair off the wool and the empty place beside it keeping its name to itself; no third had come in the morning to make the row come out even. The thread at his mouth had frozen and would stay frozen, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread. Cold had come to cheek and temple and the small bare joint of his wrist and had stopped there as at the end of a line written out for it; \lore{the Afterdrop} waited past the stopping for a warming nobody in this house would perform. He knew the composed face that is not composure, and that a cold patient cannot clot and must be warmed from the outside and slowly, never hurried, never rubbed, never set too near the fire. He wanted the crust lifted the two inches to his mouth, the thread wiped away, and a hand that would close on the pot handle brought within reach. Both were true and the second was louder and neither won, and the argument went on under the wool without a verdict. He had once reduced a shoulder in a room that smelled of disinfectant and cold metal and could not lift a crust two inches to his own mouth; the joke cost him nothing and was not funny.

She went around the yard as the yard went around itself, with a look and a wide step and no number. At \place{the Vail corner} the clean dark line lay where it had lain and the finer grey hairline beside it that the district called \lore{the thaw} lay beside it unswept, and she went wide of both and swept neither. Beneath \place{the lowest rack} the broken birth-slates lay where they lay and she stepped around them, entering nothing; the round ring where the shallow clay pan had once stood kept its place with no scrape, no crumb, no print, no chip and no word, and a man stepping over it did not look down. At \place{the yard well} the \place{trough} kept its skin, and on \place{the flat stone by the well} the silt-clouded plate of ice stood out of the path of feet; out on \place{the worked strips} the field kept its empty place; and in the returning line, where a hand had gone in on Seed 15 and had not come out, she went around the place the way everybody went around it, with no name spoken. Seven places, and no eighth likeness and no cause across two of them and no payer under any. He entered none, because a thing entered is a thing that wants a payer and none stood.

\person{Ilva} drew at the yard well with the pail against her knee, the rope stiff in the Ash cold giving its small dry rasp as the bucket came up. Clay had dried pale in the crescents of her nails and the finer grey rack dust was worked into the seam of one thumb, which she did not scrub out; her hands knew the weight of a pail and the edge of a wet board and had never been soft, and people noticed the pail first and the sleeve not at all. Nothing under the Vail roof that morning had come to her well as sign or lesson or payment; the arithmetic that had gone up the clay road the day before stood nowhere near the water, and the three movements of the fifth and sixth of Ash stood each on a place of its own with a finger between and were not brought to the water.

A woman waiting her turn asked what Ash's first reading had taken. It was a question about a figure, and a figure was the one thing the yard kept where no slate could come at it. Ilva's hand went toward her sleeve and stayed. The short strip of notched birch bark, pale where a thumb had rubbed the notches and dark in the uncut grain between, was not taken out, read, copied, counted or joined to the open slate a pace away on the stone lip, the charcoal beneath \place{the loaf shelf}, the cord, the knot-string or any saying that had arrived nowhere, and no figure came off the wood. Her eyes went to the asker and her mouth opened after both with a half-beat. “Ask the pot what it cost. I keep the count, not the account,” she said, answering the question put and not the one behind it. She put the flat of her hand once more on the sleeve and went back to the pail, and nobody turned twice.

The boy took the order of her reaching apart as a surgeon takes a field apart, by what was there and by what was not: hand to the record before hand to the person, eyes before mouth, and a small interval between the looking and the word. He filed it as the ninth record-first hand on a place of its own with a finger between it and \person{Hessa Rale}'s eighth. He filed no tenth, made no figure of the slate's nearness, made her no reader of her own keeping and joined her to none --- not to the runner, not to the clerk at his landing, not to the charcoal, not to the figure that had gone up the road and not to any voice that stood in no doorway to be asked.

There was no ownerless saying at this well on the eleventh of Ash. The fourth shaping of the old well-saying stayed four shapings and no fifth came; the turning of the runner's writing toward herself on the sixth stayed a sighting and not a prophecy; the woman waiting for water was made no witness and no speaker of either. Four shapings are four and not a witness, and a sighting is not the payment of a saying. The boy kept the three movements on three places with a finger between and paid no claim out of any of them. A count kept in wood is still a keeping; it was none of his to enter, and he did not enter it. Nor did the reading become a cause because a woman asked after it at a well, for a cause wants a hand to stand under it and none stood.

The hunts and repairs of the afternoon stayed directions judged by the carrying arm and the weather, never figures. A man come down from \place{Cold Acre} shifted a hare across his shoulders at the well and, to a woman who asked what the carrying had cost, told her to ask the weather, which had carried the snow and could carry the price; he went off toward \place{the clay road} with the hare unweighed and nobody opening a slate. Another with a cracked jar in a sling of rope asked what the month's mending would want and was told to ask the pot, and the pot, being a pot, said nothing. The boy read each as a chart left blank where the number should stand and entered none of the blanks. A cost-question answered with the weather was a direction arrived nowhere, and the arriving was the whole of it.

The three coverings stayed weather and were entered nowhere. The \lore{frost-bloom} had gone off the well's north lip on Bone 3 while the yard looked elsewhere, and it was not a sign now and had not been a sign then; the skin on the trough broke under the knock of a pail and closed again over what it had covered, and nobody lifted a finger to it; the silt-clouded plate on the flat stone stood where no foot went. A covering over a depth, the same covering over another depth, a piece of the depth lifted and set where no foot would go --- three things on three places with a finger between every pair, called weather, and no fourth likeness made a cause. The figure in the box up the road had not entered any of them and was not made to.

At \place{the rack end} the dressed grain lay turned under wool away from the hearth, and Sella lifted a corner with a touch lighter than she had used in the morning and turned it once and gave no figure. Her fingers went in and came out and did not close, so that no handful was lifted. The boy filed the empty fingers on a place of their own beside the one object that was the handful let fall on Bone 22 --- a keeping, not a second handful, not a fifth held change, not a rule, with no payer under it. Wool over turning grain, the dry rasp of it, the smell of the yard coming up through it: the small sounds and the small smells, taken first.

At \place{the kindling} \person{Tarn} kept his account in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check and in the lip beyond it where it could not: six standing, the seventh at the lip beside the unforged collector sentence that \person{Garl Vail} had left there with no name on the man who turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on the debt. The small knife lay at the very edge of its sill, where a man could check it and where a man's hand could also slip it off. “Six counted. The next I don't count,” he told the wood, in the tone of a man stating the weather, and cut nothing and counted nothing and called no loan. Ash was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line, and the pale clip-ring at his left wrist held what little light there was and gave it back.

The sentence about an ear under this roof being worth more than the debt that held it came up to the word that would call the loan and stopped there, as it had stopped on a ninth morning and would stop again; there were nine stoppings and no tenth before the seventh year at its very end, and the loan was uncalled. The breath that should have gone in at the top of the reach stayed unspent, a small interval at the top of every reach, and his chest had been tight since the dark. At \place{the flagstones} his stroke went over the shallow bright patch without the half-check, and the returned half-check of Bone 8 stayed on its own place with the Day 161 fraction, the Day 166 shelf beat and the Frost 5 returned beat, four held changes on four places and not a rule. The warm loft stone lay cold in its unmarked pot above the shed, unreconciled by not trying, which is not forgiveness. The boy checked the resting of a thumb on the uncut seventh of Bone 26 and found it where it had been laid, and did not make it again on the eleventh of Ash: not a tenth stopping, not the seventh year given, not a payment, not a fifth held change, not a hearing, not a mending and not a rule, with no payer standing under it.

\person[Jorra Vail]{Jorra} came through the finger's-width door off \place{the Vail wheel-shed} with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bent little finger and white in the creases where a fist had opened, and it was not the bone-coloured \lore{slip} of the wheel. He kept his dusty forearms and his clean ones on one tray and parted them as a surgeon parts clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had been broken nor whether it was a birth-slate. Three or more men had gone low into \place{the back room} before first grey and he gave the house no name for them and did not say the room was cold. To the wheel he spoke before he spoke to any person: “It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady.” To a pot he said, “You'll do,” in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either, and knew the \lore{full note} it would give three turns before it left his hands, with air under it and a want in it, and kept the knowing where nobody could make a song of it, withheld from a kiln-master come up from \place{High Kiln} who would call it early and make a song of it.

Sella had not finished telling him the fire was low when his bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper}, took the cold out of the fingertip, moved the tongue a hair's breadth and was left standing; the end of her instruction arrived at the pot and the household straightened around his silence the way cloth straightens when a weight is lifted. One piece of iron had two bites, the holding and the taking, and there was no \lore{Grathic} word for both mercies. “He's not Vail” stayed sealed as a lid nobody had tried to lift in three years, and the promise of the boy's name to the men who burn slates was neither given nor withdrawn. The burned-slate word stood at five with a finger between the fifth and the four and no witness standing; the joining said over the boards on the fifth of Ash stood beside it on a place of its own, not a sixth telling, not a third telling of the shut room, not a cause, not a confession and not a rule. The shut-room tellings stood at two and the row of askings at five with no witness and no sixth, and no sixth was due on a day that had not earned one. The boy set the five down where they lay and did not look for a sixth standing beside them.

Garl stayed in objects and not in a story. His salt-stiff coat held a broader man's crease at the left shoulder on its peg behind the door; the wheel-seat in the shed kept its twenty-year hollow on that side and nobody was allowed to fill it; the ridge that had been his ash-marker was now the clean dark line at the Vail corner with the finer grey beside it unswept; the twice-mended pot stood on \place{the pot board} with the hairline of the original break showing between the old-brass staple and the tighter wire, and both proverbs had gone to the pot and the fire and never to a person --- “Leave the crack showing. People trust a thing they can see has been handled,” and “If it's worth mending it's worth mending twice. The second time is the real one,” improved in the keeping and very likely not as good as the man had said them. Repair stayed apart from restoration because a restored thing hides the hand that handled it and a mended thing leaves that hand showing. The debt against a firing of tiles circled without being called, neither paid nor settled nor done with and worse for being uncalled; his name had not been spoken under this roof three hundred and forty days, and no lesson had been made of any of it.

At the going of the light Sella carried him up at the half-pace and set him on \place{the bed} under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof and never in \place{the Debt Loft}. She put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face, let her hand rest the half-beat longer than the setting needed before it went away, and left the thread where it lay. She said “The house will keep it” where another woman would have said I. \lore{The widow's mark} hung where it hung and was not touched, because a going of the light and a bed are not openings; six openings in six months were six and not a rule, and no seventh was made due on a day that had not earned one.

She went to \place{the hearth} before the basket, told the coals it was the thaw and turned them with the back of a broken pot. Her finger lay on top of the loaf shelf's front edge so the charcoal tally stayed unread, her lips moved along a two-gapped row and figured neither gap, and her eye went once to the clay jar standing cold under \lore{lead glaze} with the hand half over nothing and the little-door burn closing on air. From the pot board she lifted the twice-mended pot with her thumb first where the wire crossed the crack and only then let the weight come on the handle; both proverbs went to the pot and the fire and never to a person, and the crack was left showing. At the rack end the dressed grain lay turned under wool away from the hearth; she lifted a corner with a lighter touch and turned it once and gave no figure, and her fingers went in and came out without closing, so that no handful was lifted. The pot's rim went down two inches beyond the reaching hand, with a half-beat at the setting and no look toward the bed. The heard bell stayed in its square beside the told bell of \lore{the Long Night}, four, four and a gap with nobody crossing himself, and was not fetched in to make a fourth count or a seventh hearing. It was not laid across the house's \lore{not-doings}, the dressed grain covered under wool and read over by a number that did not know its cost, the five held objects, the handful, the thumb's resting, Ilva's strip, the three movements of the fifth and sixth of Ash or his own kept gaps. In the small hours, with the river's note low and going lower under the finger's-width door, the hand outside the wool went up an inch toward the sound. It took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole, let the intake go on the same low note, and came straight down and laid itself back against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. The note had done before the hand came down, so the letting-go was neither early again nor stayed, and the hand did not stay; it was the note's own finishing that ended the interval and not any decision of the hand, and that was the whole of the honesty in it. The inch was not a reach and the intake was not a breath kept for warmth; the interval was not a stopping of the river and the note was not his. He filed it as answer two hundred and fifty-six: exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment, borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the seven arithmetics, the bell's gap, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the stayed object, the early letting-go, the sixth opening, the pot-crack resting, the joining over the boards, the fourth shaping, the turned writing, Ilva's wood nor his own kept gaps. Two hundred and fifty-six honest answers stood on the sixth place, each an object and not a power, and neither the number nor the small hand-movings that had made it stood up and called themselves a doctrine, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood.

The surgeon took his small clean satisfaction in a field left as found, and the infant underneath took none of it and went on wanting the crust lifted, the thread wiped and a hand on the pot handle, both true, the second louder, neither winning, the two registers quarrelling without verdict in the small hours. Three hundred and forty stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in Low Kiln, Sill and Grath; six months and one hundred and sixty days were told by nobody; the carried fire's fifth was unpaid the same number with its payer unnamed; and the name of the man the salt-stiff coat kept was unspoken the same number. Nothing on the eleventh of Ash had been opened, joined by a standing hand, named, called, paid or ended. The keeping of the number was the only honest work there had been to do, bought nothing at two inches of air, was not for sale, and went on because attention is the one thing no institution can tax. The file lay open on \place{the towel} at the head of the bed with every gap left gap; the silt-clouded plate of ice stood on its flat stone out of the path of feet; Ilva's strip stayed in her sleeve with no figure off the wood; the fourth shaping stayed four shapings on a place of its own; no runner had been seen on any road since the tenth of Ash; and the river's low note went quiet under the finger's-width door. Seven arithmetics rode locked and unread up the clay road behind one printed figure, the figure read still not the figure paid. Ash 20 stood nine mornings ahead and was named nowhere under this roof, a date and not a promise, belonging to a day that had not earned it. Forward, not closed.
