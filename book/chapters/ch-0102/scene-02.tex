The afternoon came on without a boundary drawn, the light drawing off \place{the loaf shelf} and going round to the far side of \place{the hearth} the way water goes round a stone, and the house kept its shape as a pot keeps its shape when the heat goes out of it, by the places that have not moved. \person{Sella Vail} came to the basket where it lay under the fold and gave the instruction to the wool and the river wind and to the fire, that it was to be banked against their coming back, and never to the basket. She said \emph{the house} where another woman would have said I, and her hands did not pause at the little-door burn on the back of her left hand, because an afternoon is not an opening and \lore{the widow's mark} hung where it hung at her throat with the cord oiled by a working thumb. She lifted the basket as a woman lifts a pot filled to the brim, with the weight taken before the handle, and carried him down out of \place{the Vail kiln-house} into \place{the Clay Yard} at the half-pace she keeps from every open slate.

\person[Corin]{The boy} went with her in the basket and took the yard in the order a field is taken before the light, by the small sounds first because the small sounds do not lie: the rasp of the well rope, the tick of cooling clay, the low run of the river under everything, and under that \lore{the carried fire} keeping four low pulls with the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer unnamed three hundred and fifty-seven days. Air came through the wool at his ear in a thread. He counted the fingers of his own hand without deciding to, because counting was the old habit of a man who had once counted pulses in a room that smelled of disinfectant, and here there was nothing to count except what other people left undone. The composed face is not composure; he knew that the way he had once known it across a table, and knew that a cold patient cannot clot and must be warmed from the outside and slowly, never hurried, never rubbed. He knew it exactly and it was worth nothing at two inches of air. The want underneath was louder: the crust lifted, the thread wiped, a hand on the handle. Both were true, the second louder, neither won, and the quarrel between them went on without a verdict all the way down the yard.

She went wide around the seven walked-around places with a look and no number. At \place{the Vail corner} she went around the clean dark line where the ridge had turned and the finer grey hairline beside it that the house called \lore{the thaw}, and she swept neither, because a line left where it is is still a line kept and a thaw goes when it goes. She went around the space beneath \place[the drying racks]{the lowest rack} where the broken birth-slates lay, as wide as a pail of hot water set where a child might knock it, and entered it none. She went around the round ring where the shallow clay pan had stood, with no scrape in it, no crumb, no print, no chip and no word. She went around \place{the trough} with its skin of ice thick enough to hold a poured spoon. Out on \place{the worked strips} she went around the field's empty place. On \place{the flat stone by the well} she went around the silt-clouded plate of ice lifted a little out of the path of feet. And widest of all she went around the returning-line place where a hand had gone in on \place{Seed} 15 and had not come out, going so wide that her elbow cut the corner of the yard and the basket tilted the other way to balance it, and she righted it without looking down.

Seven places, entered none, for a thing entered wants a payer standing and none stood. The boy kept the seven where the yard kept them, each on its own place with a finger between every pair, and did not make an eighth of the space between them.

Three coverings stayed weather entered nowhere with a finger between every pair and no fourth likeness made a cause: the gone white frost on the north lip of \place[the yard well]{the well}, the skin on the trough that had broken under a hand and closed again, and the plate of ice that had lifted its clouded face a little out of the path of feet. The yard called all three weather and gave nobody a figure; the boy kept them where they lay and made no cause of the likeness between them, for a fourth likeness would want a payer standing and none stood.

At the rack end she turned the dressed grain once under wool with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child, her fingers going in and coming out without closing, no figure lifted off it and no handful. When the empty fingers came away she set them on a place of their own beside the one object of \place{Bone} 22 as a keeping and not a second handful, with a finger between; a second would be a habit and a third a way, and both want a payer standing and none stood. The grain lay covered and unfigured. A figure would have known the cost of none of it.

The day's hunts and repairs came and went as directions judged by the carrying arm and the weather, never as figures. A man come down from \place{Cold Acre} shifted a hare across his shoulders at the well and, to a woman who asked what the carrying had cost, told her to ask the weather, which had carried the snow and could carry the price; he went off toward \place{the clay road} with the hare unweighed and nobody opening a slate. Another with a cracked jar in a sling of rope asked what the month's mending would want and was told to ask the pot, and the pot, being a pot, said nothing. A woman with a basket of kindling asked what the seventh year would want when it came, and two men mending a strip of fence said the thaw would be early and late, to each of whom \person{Tarn}, the house's clay-bound hand, said \emph{Yes, house} and kept the late because a late thaw puts off the paying, agreeing so thoroughly that there was nowhere for an argument to find a foot. The directions of the fifth, the seventeenth and the twenty-third stood where they had been put and did not repeat and bought no lesson under this roof. Nothing from the twenty-fifth, twenty-sixth or twenty-seventh crossed the yard as sign, lesson, mending, cause or payment, and the word that had gone over the boards stood where it was put.

\scenebreak

At \place[the yard well]{the well} \person{Ilva} drew with the pail against her knee, the rope stiff in the dry cold and giving its small dry rasp as the bucket came up, clay dried pale in the crescents of her nails and the finer grey dust of the racks worked into the seam of one thumb she did not scrub out. Her hands knew the weight of a pail and the edge of a wet board and had never been soft, and people noticed the pail first and the sleeve not at all, which was the order she preferred. A slate lay open a pace away on the stone lip with the writing turned toward whoever had left it, and nobody was reading it aloud.

To a woman waiting her turn who asked what \place{Ash}'s firings had taken, Ilva's hand went toward her sleeve and stayed; the short strip of notched birch bark, pale where a thumb had rubbed the notches and dark in the uncut grain between, was not taken out, read, copied, counted or joined to the open slate a pace away, to the charcoal beneath the loaf shelf, to the cord, to the knot-string or to any saying that had arrived nowhere, and no figure came off the wood. Her eyes went to the asker and her mouth opened after both with a half-beat between the looking and the word. \emph{Ask the pot what it cost. I keep the count, not the account}, she said, the question put and not the one behind it. She put the flat of her hand once more on the sleeve and went back to the pail, and nobody turned twice.

The boy took the order of her reaching apart as a surgeon takes a field apart: hand to the record before eyes to the person, eyes before mouth, the half-beat in the gap where a lesser observer would put an intention. He filed it on a place of its own as the \emph{ninth} record-first hand in the district, with a finger between it and \person{Hessa Rale}'s eighth and \emph{no tenth}; he made no reader of her own keeping, no figure of the slate's nearness, and joined her to none. She was expressly not the speaker of any voice: no ownerless word came over the boards, no fifth shaping came of the well-saying, no sixth asking of the row and no third telling of the shut room. Her count stayed in wood where no slate could take a figure off it, and the ninth stayed nine.

\scenebreak

At \place{the kindling} Tarn kept six in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check and the seventh at the lip beyond it where it could not, beside \person{Garl Vail}'s unforged collector sentence with no name on the man who had turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on the debt. The small knife lay at the very edge of its sill, where a hand could be checked and where a hand could also slip it off, and the pale clip-ring at his left wrist caught what little light there was and gave it back. \emph{Six counted. The next I don't count}, he told the wood, and cut nothing, counted nothing, called no loan and spent no breath. He had said \emph{Yes, house} to the early thaw and the late and kept the late because it puts off the paying.

He passed his hand over the Bone 26 thumb's resting and the Ash 14 held breath and found each where it had been put and not made again, beside the five objects and the pot-crack resting with a finger between every pair, not a sixth held object, not a seventh, not a tenth stopping, not the calling of the loan and with no payer standing. He brought the ear-sentence all the way up to the word that would call the loan and stopped there, a remembered ninth stopping with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end, the Seed 16 breath unspent at the top of the reach and the loan uncalled, and his stroke went on over the shallow bright patch on \place{the flagstones} without the half-check. His chest had been tight since the dark and Ash was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line; he did not look along the road.

\person{Jorra Vail} came through \place{the finger's-width door} off \place{the Vail wheel-shed} with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bent little finger and white in a fist-crease where a hand had opened, and it was not the bone-coloured \lore{slip} of the wheel. Men unnamed had gone low into \place{the back room} before first grey and he gave the house no name for them and did not say the room was cold. He kept his dusty forearms and his clean ones on one tray and parted them as a surgeon parts clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had been broken nor whether it was a birth-slate. He spoke to the wheel before he spoke to any person, \emph{It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady}, and told a pot \emph{You'll do} in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either; he knew the \lore{full note} that pot would give three turns ahead, with air under it and a want in it, and withheld the knowing from any \place{High Kiln} master who would call it early and make a song of it.

When Sella had not finished telling him the fire was low his bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper} ahead of her sentence, took the cold out of the fingertip, moved the tongue a hair's breadth and left it standing, so the end of her instruction arrived at the pot and the household straightened around the waiting as cloth straightens when a weight is lifted, one piece of iron with two bites, the holding and the taking, with no Grathic word for both mercies. He did not leave the extra interval of the twenty-sixth a second time. The holding of Ash 26 stayed where nobody would enter it, gathered once and not made again, for a second would be a habit and a third a way, both of which want a payer standing and none stood. He made no song of the note he knew, gave the back-room men no name and did not look toward the basket.

\scenebreak

At the going of the light she carried him up at the half-pace and went wide around the seven places again, set him on \place{the bed} under the roof and never in \place{the Debt Loft}, put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face, let her hand rest the half-beat longer than the setting needed before it went away, and left the thread of drool frozen and unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread. The widow's mark was untouched; an afternoon and a going of the light are not openings, and six openings in six months were six and not a rule with no seventh made due. She told the coals it was the thaw and said \emph{The house will keep it} where another woman would have said I, and said nothing of where they had been or what the yard had given or withheld.

In the small hours the hand outside the wool came up an inch toward the river's note under the finger's-width door, the way a hand goes up in a field it has learned not to disturb. It did not reach, and the inch was not a reach; most of what a hand does in a field is wait, and he had once taken a hand into a cavity slowly, with the whole of the attention, and knew the difference between the moving and the waiting. The note went \lore{water over water}, a low thing over a lower thing, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming, never numbered among the hearings, for a hearing wants a source standing and none stood. The hand took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole on the note's own finishing, let the intake go and came straight down and laid itself back against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. The note had done before the hand came down, so the letting-go was neither early again nor stayed, and the hand did not stay; it was the note's own finishing that ended the interval and not any decision of the hand, and that was the whole of the honesty in it. The intake was not a breath kept for warmth, the interval not a stopping of the river, the note not his.

He filed it as answer two hundred and ninety: exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the eight arithmetics, the bell's gap, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the holding in the iron, the held breath at the sill, the staying at the inch, the early letting-go, the sixth opening, the pot-crack resting, the Ash 5 joining, the Ash 17 direction, the Ash 23 direction, the word that stood in no doorway, the fourth shaping, the turned writing, the strip in the sleeve nor his own kept gaps. Two hundred and ninety honest answers stood on the sixth place, each an object and not a power, and not one stood up and called itself a doctrine, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood.

The surgeon took his small clean satisfaction in a field taken as found and left as found, and it cost him nothing, which was how he knew it was not the same as peace. Underneath it the infant wanted the crust lifted the two inches to his mouth, the thread wiped and a hand that would close on the pot handle set beyond the reaching fingers. Both were true, the second was louder, neither won, and the two registers quarrelled without verdict in the ordinary small hours of the twenty-eighth. Nothing on this day had been opened, joined by a standing hand, named, called, paid or ended; three hundred and fifty-seven stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody, and the keeping of it, bought at two inches of air and not for sale, went on because attention is the one thing no institution here has thought to tax. Ash kept its twenty-eighth day without a boundary drawn, Ash 30 stood two mornings ahead and was named only as a date, and no ninth arithmetic was supplied early, no seventh hearing, sixth held object, seventh opening, sixth telling, third shut-room telling, sixth asking, fifth shaping, tenth hand or tenth stopping invented to make a row come out even.

The hand lay back against the fold on answer two hundred and ninety; the twice-mended pot stood on \place{the pot board} with the hairline of the original break showing between old-brass staple and tighter wire; and the holding of Ash 26 stayed where nobody would enter it, a keeping gathered once and not made again. Forward, not closed.
