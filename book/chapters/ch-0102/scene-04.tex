The afternoon came on without a boundary drawn, the light going round \place{the hearth} the way a hand goes round a pot it does not want to disturb, and \place{the loaf shelf} keeping its long shadow a little longer than it had kept it in the morning. It was the twenty-ninth, and the day offered nothing. That was itself an exact result, and \person[Corin]{the boy} lay in the basket under the fold and entered it nowhere. \place{Ash} 30 stood one morning ahead and was named only as a date, never as a promise, and was not brought into an afternoon that had not earned it; the ninth arithmetic that would stand behind the figure when its own morning was earned stayed unnamed for one more day. Outside, the river went on with its low note and the clay cooled ticking on the wall, and the house kept its shape the way a pot keeps its shape after the heat goes out of it, by the places that have not moved.

\person{Sella Vail} came to the basket where it lay and gave the instruction to the wool and the river wind and to the fire, that it was to be banked against their coming back, and never to the basket. She said \emph{the house} where another woman would have said I, and her hands did not pause at the little-door burn on the back of her left hand, because an afternoon is not an opening and \lore{the widow's mark} hung where it hung at her throat with the cord oiled by a working thumb. She lifted as a woman lifts a pot filled to the brim, weight taken before the handle, and carried him down out of \place{the Vail kiln-house} into \place{the Clay Yard} at the half-pace she keeps from every open slate. The light went off the brown edge of the crust and came back on the wool. She did not look down.

He took the yard in the order a field is taken before the light, by the small sounds first because the small sounds do not lie and the large ones are mostly weather: the small dry rasp of the well rope, the tick of cooling clay, the low run of the river under everything, and under that \lore{the carried fire} keeping four low pulls with the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer unnamed three hundred and fifty-eight days. Air came through the wool at his ear in a thread. The surgeon's register took its small inventory of what the afternoon had given it: one stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust two inches from the ear with its corner standing a hair off the wool, its empty place unnamed and no third; the thread of drool frozen in the dry kiln heat and unwiped because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread; cold stopped at cheek, temple and the bare wrist-joint where the wool rode back, as at the end of a line written out for it with neither an order to go farther nor an order to go away; \lore{the Afterdrop} waiting past a warming nobody in this house would perform. The composed face was not composure; a cold patient cannot clot and must be warmed from outside and slowly, never rubbed, never set too near the fire. He knew it exactly and it was worth nothing at two inches of air. The want underneath was louder: the crust lifted, the thread wiped, a hand that would close on the pot handle. Both were true, the second louder, neither won, and the quarrel between them went on without a verdict all the way down the yard.

She went wide around the seven walked-around places with a look and no number. At \place{the Vail corner} she went around the clean dark line where the ridge had turned and the finer grey hairline beside it that the house called \lore{the thaw}, and swept neither, because a line left where it is is still a line kept and a thaw goes when it goes. She went around the space beneath \place[the drying racks]{the lowest rack} where the broken birth-slates lay, as wide as a pail of hot water set where a child might knock it, and entered it none. She went around the round ring where the shallow clay pan had stood, with no scrape in it, no crumb, no print, no chip and no word. She went around \place{the trough} with its skin of ice thick enough to hold a poured spoon. Out on \place{the worked strips} she went around the field's empty place. On \place{the flat stone by the well} she went around the silt-clouded plate of ice lifted a little out of the path of feet. And widest of all she went around the returning-line place where a hand had gone in on \place{Seed} 15 and had not come out, so wide that her elbow cut the corner of the yard and the basket tilted the other way to balance it, and she righted it without looking down.

Seven places, entered none, for a thing entered wants a payer standing and none stood. The boy kept the seven where the yard kept them, each on its own place with a finger between every pair, and did not make an eighth of the space between them. Nobody in the yard had spoken his name or given him a number; the day went around its absences as a pot goes around a flaw, and left them showing.

Three coverings stayed weather entered nowhere with a finger between every pair and no fourth likeness made a cause: the gone white frost on the north lip of \place[the yard well]{the well}, the skin on the trough that had broken under a hand and closed again, and the plate of ice that had lifted its clouded face a little out of the path of feet. The yard called all three weather and gave nobody a figure, and the boy kept them where they lay; a fourth likeness would want a payer standing and none stood.

At \place{the rack end} she turned the dressed grain once under wool with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child, her fingers going in and coming out without closing, no figure lifted off it and no handful. When the empty fingers came away she set them on a place of their own beside the one object of \place{Bone} 22 as a keeping and not a second handful, with a finger between; a second would be a habit and a third a way, and both want a payer standing and none stood. The grain lay covered and unfigured. A figure would have known the cost of none of it, and no figure came.

The day's hunts and repairs came and went as directions judged by the carrying arm and the weather, never as figures. A man come down from \place{Cold Acre} shifted a hare across his shoulders at the well and, to a woman who asked what the carrying had cost, told her to ask the weather, which had carried the snow and could carry the price; he went off toward \place{the clay road} with the hare unweighed and nobody opening a slate. Another with a cracked jar in a sling of rope asked what the month's mending would want and was told to ask the pot, and the pot, being a pot, said nothing. A woman with a basket of kindling asked what the seventh year would want when it came, and two men mending a strip of fence said the thaw would be early and late, to each of whom \person{Tarn}, the house's clay-bound hand, said \emph{Yes, house} and kept the late because a late thaw puts off the paying, agreeing so thoroughly that there was nowhere for an argument to find a foot. The directions of the fifth, the seventeenth and the twenty-third stood where they had been put and did not repeat and bought no lesson under any roof. No sixth telling of the burned slate came over \place{the boards}, no third telling of the shut room, no sixth asking of the row, no fifth shaping of the well-saying, no tenth record-first hand and no tenth stopping; none was made due on a day that had not earned one. There was no runner on any road, no collector on the clay road, no market, no Cross and no bell, and no ownerless voice came over the boards on the twenty-ninth.

\scenebreak

At \place{the kindling} Tarn kept six in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check and the seventh at the lip beyond it where it could not, beside \person{Garl Vail}'s unforged collector sentence with no name on the man who had turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on the debt. The small knife lay at the very edge of its sill, where a hand could be checked and where a hand could also slip it off, and the pale clip-ring at his left wrist caught what little light there was and gave it back. \emph{Six counted. The next I don't count}, he told the wood, and cut nothing, counted nothing, called no loan and spent no breath. He had said \emph{Yes, house} to the early thaw and the late and kept the late because it puts off the paying.

He passed his hand over the Bone 26 thumb's resting and the Ash 14 held breath and found each where it had been put and not made again, beside the five objects and the pot-crack resting with a finger between every pair, not a sixth held object, not a seventh, not a tenth stopping, not the calling of the loan and with no payer standing. He brought the ear-sentence all the way up to the word that would call the loan and stopped there, a remembered ninth stopping with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end, the Seed 16 breath unspent at the top of the reach and the loan uncalled. His stroke went on over the shallow bright patch on \place{the flagstones} without the half-check. The returned half-check of Bone 8 stayed on the flagstone's own place with the Day 161 fraction, the Day 166 beat and the \place{Frost} 5 beat --- four held changes on four places and not a rule; the twenty-ninth made no fifth of them and supplied no order to the four. The warm loft stone lay cold in its unmarked pot, unreconciled by not trying, which is not forgiveness. His chest had been tight since the dark and Ash was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line; he did not look along the road a house sends a man down when it wants a collector.

\person{Jorra Vail} came through \place{the finger's-width door} off \place{the Vail wheel-shed} with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bent little finger and white in a fist-crease where a hand had opened, and it was not the bone-coloured \lore{slip} of the wheel. Men unnamed had gone low into \place{the back room} before first grey and he gave the house no name for them and did not say the room was cold. He kept his dusty forearms and his clean ones on one tray and parted them as a surgeon parts clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had been broken nor whether it was a birth-slate. He spoke to the wheel before he spoke to any person, \emph{It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady}, and told a pot \emph{You'll do} in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either. He knew the \lore{full note} that pot would give three turns ahead, with air under it and a want in it, and withheld the knowing from any \place{High Kiln} master who would call it early and make a song of it.

When Sella had not finished telling him the fire was low his bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper} ahead of her sentence, took the cold out of the fingertip, moved the tongue a hair's breadth and left it standing, so the end of her instruction arrived at the pot and the household straightened around the waiting as cloth straightens when a weight is lifted, one piece of iron with two bites, the holding and the taking, with no Grathic word for both mercies. He did not leave the extra interval of the twenty-sixth a second time. The holding of Ash 26 stayed where nobody would enter it, gathered once and not made again, for a second would be a habit and a third a way, both of which want a payer standing and none stood. He made no song of the note he knew, gave the back-room men no name and did not look toward the basket.

At the going of the light she carried him up at the half-pace and went wide around the seven places again, set him on \place{the bed} under the roof and never in \place{the Debt Loft}, put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face, let her hand rest the half-beat longer than the setting needed before it went away, and left the thread of drool frozen and unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread. The widow's mark was untouched; an afternoon and a going of the light are not openings, and six openings in six months were six and not a rule with no seventh made due. She told the coals it was the thaw and said \emph{The house will keep it} where another woman would have said I, and said nothing of where they had been or what the yard had given or withheld. On \place{the pot board} the twice-mended pot stood with the hairline of the original break showing between old-brass staple and tighter wire, and the crack was left showing there too.

In the small hours the hand outside the wool came up an inch toward the river's note under the finger's-width door, the way a hand goes up in a field it has learned not to disturb. It did not reach, and the inch was not a reach; most of what a hand does in a field is wait, and he had once taken a hand into a cavity slowly, with the whole of the attention, and knew the difference between the moving and the waiting. The note went \lore{water over water}, a low thing over a lower thing, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming, never numbered among the hearings, for a hearing wants a source standing and none stood. The hand took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole on the note's own finishing, let the intake go and came straight down and laid itself back against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. The note had done before the hand came down, so the letting-go was neither early again nor stayed, and the hand did not stay; it was the note's own finishing that ended the interval and not any decision of the hand, and that was the whole of the honesty in it. The intake was not a breath kept for warmth, the interval not a stopping of the river, the note not his.

He filed it as answer two hundred and ninety-two: exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the eight arithmetics, the bell's gap, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the holding in the iron, the held breath at the sill, the staying at the inch, the early letting-go, the sixth opening, the pot-crack resting, the Ash 5 joining, the Ash 17 direction, the Ash 23 direction, the word that stood in no doorway, the fourth shaping, the turned writing, the strip in the sleeve nor his own kept gaps. Two hundred and ninety-two honest answers stood on the sixth place, each an object and not a power, and not one stood up and called itself a doctrine, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood.

The day had offered no new thing, and that was itself an exact result entered nowhere; the last quiet holding was kept without being turned into a habit, a way or a rule. The surgeon took his small clean satisfaction in a field taken as found and left as found, and it cost him nothing, which was how he knew it was not the same as peace. Underneath it the infant wanted the crust lifted the two inches to his mouth, the thread wiped and a hand that would close on the pot handle set beyond the reaching fingers. Both were true, the second was louder, neither won, and the two registers quarrelled without verdict in the ordinary small hours of the twenty-ninth. Nothing on this day had been opened, joined by a standing hand, named, called, paid or ended; three hundred and fifty-eight stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody, six months and one hundred and seventy-eight days told by nobody, the carried fire's fifth unpaid the same number with its payer unnamed, and the name of the man whose coat kept his shape on the peg behind the door unspoken the same number. Ash kept its twenty-ninth day without a boundary drawn, and neither of the district's two habits of naming its months was set right or left wrong; the month carried both names the way a pot carries two handles and is none the worse. \place{Stone} came on in the dark beyond the door and was not brought into a day that had not earned it, and no seventh hearing, sixth held object, seventh opening, sixth telling, third shut-room telling, sixth asking, fifth shaping, tenth hand or tenth stopping was supplied to make a row come out even.

The hand lay back against the fold on answer two hundred and ninety-two; under the floor the carried fire kept four low pulls with the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer unnamed three hundred and fifty-eight days. Forward, not closed.
