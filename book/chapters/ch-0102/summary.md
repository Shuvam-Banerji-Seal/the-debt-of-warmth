## Chronicle

Three days of Ash were lived under the Vail roof, the boy carried in the basket at the half-pace Sella Vail keeps from every open slate. The twenty-eighth and twenty-ninth were ordinary tending days. Sella went to the hearth before the basket, told the coals it was the thaw, turned them with a broken pot's back and said *The house will keep it*; the charcoal stayed unread and the grain lay covered with no figure or handful. She went wide around seven yard places, widest around the place where a hand went in on Seed 15 and did not come out. Tarn kept six where the house could check and the seventh at the lip, said *Six counted. The next I don't count*, and stopped at the calling word. Jorra put his bent finger into the cold damper ahead of Sella's unfinished sentence --- one iron with two bites. At the well Ilva's hand went to her sleeve, the birch strip unread and her reaching filed as the ninth record-first hand; she was the speaker of no voice.

On the thirtieth the month spent its last Account-day and market. Sella set the basket in the moot-post's finger's width of shade and stood half a pace off through the reading. Berr Clay came up short off the clay road with the sweet green of lead glaze at his mouth and the smell of nothing under the hot clay of him; he wiped his thumb twice, read *One in twelve, as the notice says* twice over the basket, called the levy and denied it with *your house*, never I. He said something low to the block about kindness entered and went off up the road with the box locked and Ash's third subtraction inside, believing he had done a kindness. Nine arithmetics now stand behind one unsigned figure --- nine are nine and not one account, the figure read still not the figure paid and no signature on the space. Hessa carried the slate up toward Greybank unread, and the soft-cuffed clerk stayed at the landing while the market sold over him as water over a stone.

Each night the hand went up an inch toward the river's note, took a small intake, held it whole on the note's own finishing and came straight down --- answers 289 through 294, exact and worth nothing at two inches of air. Three hundred and fifty-nine days stood exact and spoken by nobody, six months and one hundred and seventy-nine days; the carried fire kept four pulls and a gap, the boards four, four and a knot, the river low over lower. The reading bought no lesson and no doctrine stood up out of nine, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood. Ash went out without a boundary drawn and Stone came on in the dark beyond the door. What the days cost was attention, which no institution here has thought to tax.
