  4. The boy takes the order of reaching apart as a surgeon takes a field apart and files it as the **ninth** record-first hand on a place of its own with a finger between it and Hessa Rale's eighth and **no tenth**; he makes no reader of her own keeping, no figure of the slate's nearness and joins her to none.   6. Jorra comes through the finger's-width door off the wheel-shed with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bent little finger and white in a fist-crease, not the bone-coloured slip of the wheel; men unnamed went low into the back room before first grey and he gives the house no name for them and does not say the room was cold. - **Sensory anchors.** Stiff rope rasping the well-rod, pale clay in nail crescents and grey rack dust in a thumb seam, silt-clouded ice lifted out of the path, meal-fine slate dust white in a fist-crease, cold iron taking the sting out of a fingertip, the little-door burn closing on air.

## Scene 6 — Ash Going Out

- **Day and time.** Day 359, Ash 30, Tenthday; afternoon through the going of the light and the bed, then the small hours. Chapter close, no new dated heading.
- **Place.** The Clay Yard's seven places, yard well and flat stone, rack end, kindling and flagstones, wheel-shed edge and damper; then hearth, pot board, loaf shelf, bed, boards and towel.
- **Present.** Corin/Dan, Sella Vail, Tarn, Jorra Vail; Garl in objects. Walk-ons only: the Cold Acre hare-carrier, the cracked-jar man, the woman with kindling, two fence-menders. No Ilva this afternoon, no Berr and no Hessa on any road (the runner went up before noon), no collector, no green-mouthed woman or foundling sighting, no second reading, no ownerless voice.
- **Beats, in order.**
  1.   2. Tarn keeps six where the house can check and the seventh at the lip beside Garl's unforged collector sentence, knife at the edge of its sill, tells the wood *Six counted.
