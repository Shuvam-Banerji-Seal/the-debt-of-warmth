The afternoon of the thirtieth came on after the market had gone off it the way an afternoon comes after floodwater goes down a street --- leaving the marks and taking the noise. \place{the Cross} was a place again and not an event; the pigs had gone back to being pigs and the two hundred to being households, and the bell's long note had spent itself into the common air until there was nothing in it but the river under everything. Nothing had been carried home from the reading except the box going up the road, and the box was a direction now and not a man standing anywhere to be asked. \person{Sella Vail} came to the basket where it lay under the fold and gave the instruction to the wool and the river wind and to the fire, that it was to be banked against their coming back, and never to the basket. She said \emph{the house} where another woman would have said I, and her hands did not pause at the little-door burn on the back of her left hand, because an afternoon is not an opening and \lore{the widow's mark} hung where it hung at her throat with the cord oiled by a working thumb. She lifted the basket as a woman lifts a pot filled to the brim, weight taken before the handle, and carried \person[Corin]{the boy} down out of \place{the Vail kiln-house} into \place{the Clay Yard} at the half-pace she kept from every open slate.

He took the yard in the order a field is taken before the light, by the small sounds first, because the small sounds do not lie: the rasp of the well rope, the tick of cooling clay, the low run of the river under everything, and under that \lore{the carried fire} keeping four low pulls with the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer unnamed three hundred and fifty-nine days through the floor of \place{the Vail kiln-house}. Air came through the wool at his ear in a thread and carried the sour mineral tang of the ridge along the skirting board, which had not been swept in two years and would not be swept now on an afternoon that was no opening. The composed face is not composure; he knew it as he had once known it across a table, and knew a cold patient cannot clot and must be warmed from the outside and slowly, never hurried, never rubbed, never set too near the fire. \lore{The Afterdrop} waited past the stopping, beyond a warming nobody in this house would perform. He knew it exactly and it was worth nothing at two inches of air. The want underneath was louder: the crust lifted to his mouth, the thread wiped, a hand that would close on the pot handle. Both were true, the second louder, neither won, and the quarrel went on under the wool without a verdict all the way down the yard.

She went wide around the seven walked-around places with a look and no number. At \place{the Vail corner} she went around the clean dark line where the ridge had turned and the finer hairline beside it that the house called \lore{the thaw}, and swept neither, because a line left where it is is still a line kept and a thaw goes when it goes. She went around the space beneath \place[the drying racks]{the lowest rack} where the broken birth-slates lay, as wide as a pail of hot water set where a child might knock it, and entered it none. She went around the round ring where the shallow clay pan had stood, with no scrape in it, no crumb, no print, no chip and no word; a ring named from the shape it left and not from anything that had stood there. She went around \place{the trough} with its skin of ice thick enough to hold a poured spoon. Out on \place{the worked strips} she went around the field's empty place. On \place{the flat stone by the well} she went around the silt-clouded plate of ice lifted a little out of the path of feet. And widest of all she went around the returning-line place where a hand had gone in on \place{Seed} 15 and had not come out, so wide that her elbow cut the corner of the yard and the basket tilted the other way to balance it, and she righted it without looking down. Seven places entered none, for a thing entered wants a payer standing and none stood; the directions of the fifth, the seventeenth and the twenty-third stood where they had been put and did not repeat and bought no lesson under any roof.

Three coverings stayed weather entered nowhere with a finger between every pair and no fourth likeness made a cause: the gone white frost off the north lip of \place{the yard well}, the skin on the trough that had broken and closed, the plate of ice that had lifted its clouded face a little out of the path of feet. Each was a covering put on by the cold and taken off by the cold, and a man who laid the three together and called them one thing would have wanted a payer under them, and none stood. At \place{the rack end} the dressed grain lay covered and unfigured under its fold of wool, and she turned it once with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child, her fingers going in and coming out without closing, no figure lifted off it and no handful. When the empty fingers came away she set them on a place of their own beside the one object of \place{Bone} 22 as a keeping and not a second handful, with a finger between; a second would be a habit and a third a way, and both want a payer standing and none stood. The grain lay covered. A figure would have known the cost of none of it, and no figure came.

The day's hunts and repairs came and went as directions judged by the carrying arm and the weather, never as figures. A man come down from \place{Cold Acre} shifted a hare across his shoulders at the well and, to a woman who asked what the carrying had cost, told her to ask the weather, which had carried the snow and could carry the price; he went off toward \place{the clay road} with the hare unweighed and nobody opening a slate. Another with a cracked jar in a sling of rope asked what the month's mending would want and was told to ask the pot, and the pot, being a pot, said nothing. A woman with a basket of kindling asked what the seventh year would want when it came, and two men mending a strip of fence said the thaw would be early and late; to each \person{Tarn} said \emph{Yes, house} and kept the late because a late thaw puts off the paying, agreeing so thoroughly that there was nowhere for an argument to find a foot. A wrong wind off \place{High Kiln} brought the sweet edge of \lore{lead glaze} over the boards and went on about its business; nobody tasted it twice. No collector came up or down the clay road, no runner came on any road, and nothing was opened, joined by a standing hand, named, called, paid or ended on the afternoon of the thirtieth.

At \place{the kindling} Tarn kept six in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check and the seventh at the lip beyond it where it could not, beside \person{Garl Vail}'s unforged collector sentence with no name on the man who had turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on the debt. The small knife lay at the very edge of its sill, where a hand could be checked and where a hand could also slip it off, and the pale clip-ring at his left wrist caught what little light there was and gave it back. \emph{Six counted. The next I don't count}, he told the wood, and cut nothing, counted nothing, called no loan and spent no breath. He passed his hand over the Bone 26 thumb's resting and the Ash 14 held breath and found each where it had been put and not made again, beside the five objects and the pot-crack resting with a finger between every pair, not a sixth held object, not a seventh, not a tenth stopping, not the calling of the loan and with no payer standing. He brought the ear-sentence all the way up to the word that would call the loan and stopped there, a remembered ninth stopping with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end, the Seed 16 breath unspent at the top of the reach and the loan uncalled. His stroke went on over the shallow bright patch on \place{the flagstones} without the half-check; the returned half-check of the eighth stayed on its own place with the Day 161 fraction, the Day 166 beat and the \place{Frost} 5 beat, four held changes on four places and not a rule, and the thirtieth made no fifth of them. His chest had been tight since the dark and Ash was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line; he did not look along the road a house sends a man down when it wants a collector. The warm loft stone lay cold in its unmarked pot in \place{the Debt Loft}, unreconciled by not trying, which is not forgiveness.

\person{Jorra Vail} came through \place{the finger's-width door} off \place{the Vail wheel-shed} with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bent little finger and white in a fist-crease where a hand had opened, and it was not the bone-coloured \lore{slip} of the wheel. Men unnamed had gone low into \place{the back room} before first grey and he gave the house no name for them and did not say the room was cold. He kept his dusty forearms and his clean ones on one tray and parted them as clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had been broken nor whether it was a birth-slate, while the broken birth-slates under the lowest rack stayed entered by nobody. He spoke to the wheel before he spoke to any person, \emph{It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady}, and told a pot \emph{You'll do} in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either; he knew the \lore{full note} that pot would give three turns ahead, with air under it and a want in it, and withheld the knowing from any \place{High Kiln} master who would call it early and make a song of it. When Sella had not finished telling him the fire was low, his bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper} ahead of her sentence, took the cold out of the fingertip, moved the tongue a hair's breadth and left it standing, so the end of her instruction arrived at the pot and the household straightened around the waiting as cloth straightens when a weight is lifted --- one piece of iron with two bites, the holding and the taking, with no Grathic word for both mercies. He did not leave the extra interval a second time. The holding of \place{Ash} 26 stood where nobody would enter it, gathered once and not spent, and on the thirtieth he did not make a habit of it, for a second would be a way and a third a custom and both want a payer standing and none stood. \emph{He's not Vail} stayed sealed as a lid nobody had tried to lift in three years, and the promise of the boy's name to the men who burn slates was neither given nor withdrawn. The burned slate stood at five with a finger between the fifth and the four and no witness, the shut-room tellings at two, the row at five askings, the well-saying at four shapings, and no ownerless voice came over the boards on the thirtieth to make a sixth of any of them.

\scenebreak

At the going of the light Sella lifted the basket out of the yard with the whole of her attention on its level, as if the thing to be got right were the balance of a pot on a shelf and not the figure that had been sent twice over its top that morning. She went back around the seven places with a look and a wide step and no number, going widest around the returning-line place, and gave the instruction to the wool and the river wind and never to the basket. She said nothing of where they had been and nothing of what had been read over the wicker. That was not a refusal to tell him. There was nobody under any roof who told a basket what a figure meant, and a story told to a child who could not answer back would have been a thing said to please the speaker; she was not that woman. She carried him as a pot is carried that must not be slopped, up off \place{the clay road} and under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof, and did not look along the road where the box had gone and the key with it.

Under the roof she came to \place{the hearth} before she came to the basket, and told the coals it was \lore{the thaw} and turned them with the back of a broken pot, and said \emph{The house will keep it} where another woman would have said I. She stood a moment counting small intake, hold and letting-go toward no opening with neither gap figured; her finger lay along the top of \place{the loaf shelf} so the charcoal tally stayed unread; her lips moved along a two-gapped row and figured neither gap; her eye went once to the clay jar standing cold under its glaze and her hand shut half over nothing, the little-door burn on the back of her left hand closing on air. At \place{the pot board} she lifted the twice-mended pot with her thumb first where the wire crossed the crack, said both proverbs to the pot and the fire and never to a person, and left the hairline of the original break showing between old-brass staple and tighter wire, so that repair stayed apart from restoration, for a restored thing hides the hand that handled it and a mended thing leaves that hand showing. The dressed grain lay covered and unfigured; wherever she put her fingers they went in and came out without closing, and no handful was lifted. She set the pot rim two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat and no look toward the bed, then set him on \place{the bed} under the roof and never in the Debt Loft, put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face, let her hand rest the half-beat longer than the setting needed before it went away, and left the thread of drool frozen in the dry kiln heat and unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread.

\lore{The widow's mark} hung where it hung at her throat and was not touched. A market, a Cross, a going of the light, a hearth and a bed are not openings; the hand goes to the mark before the kiln and at no other hour, and six openings in six months are six and not a rule, with the sixth held behind at \place{the kiln mouth} on Ash 2 without a name and no seventh made due on a day that had not earned one. The mark answered nothing this evening for the crust at two inches, the empty place beside it, the fire's missing fifth, the hand that had gone in on \place{Seed} 15 and not come out, or the nine arithmetics riding away in a locked box; none of those stood under it asking to be answered, and she did not ask on their behalf.

In the small hours the whole file came out on \place{the towel} at the head of the bed as instruments come out on a towel, and was checked and left as it lay. Nine arithmetics of the year stood behind one unsigned figure --- the three of Frost, the first, second and third of Bone, Ash's first, Ash's second and Ash's third --- nine and not one account. The printed figure stood one in twelve, unchallenged; the figure read was still not the figure paid; no signature stood on the space between. Both of Ash's subtractions rode locked and unread up the clay road in \person{Berr Clay}'s box toward \place{High Kiln}, the key gone up in a coat pocket and not coming back down. The finding sat on a place of its own with no payer under it and no doctrine standing out of nine, and it was not laid across the house's \lore{not-doings}, the dressed grain lying covered under wool with no figure and no handful, the five held objects, the pot-crack resting of Ash 9, the hand-at-the-sill breath of Ash 14, the holding of Ash 26 in the cold iron, the Ash 5 joining, the Ash 17 direction, the Ash 23 direction or his own kept gaps. The edges came near to touching, so near that a less careful hand would have called the touching a cause and the cause a debt and set a payer under both; the finger went between, not a decision and not a doctrine, only the placing of a small clean interval where an interval was due. He had learned that a finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged. No sixth hearing, seventh opening, sixth held object, sixth telling, third shut-room telling, sixth asking, fifth shaping, tenth hand or tenth stopping was invented to make a row come out even.

Six hearings of \lore{the full note} stood beside and never among, the seventh place empty, the sixth the small flat knock out of \person[Dessa Silt] as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go and never among the sources; an empty place kept empty is a finding and a finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged. The heard bell stood four, four and a gap in its square beside the told bell of \lore{the Long Night}, not folded into the three counts and not made a seventh of the full note. Nine record-first hands stood with no tenth, \person{Ilva}'s ninth on a place of its own with a finger between it and \person{Hessa Rale}'s eighth, and nine stoppings stood with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end, the loan uncalled. The burned-slate word stood at five, the shut-room tellings at two, the well-saying at four shapings, the row at five askings with no witness; the Ash 5 joining, the Ash 17 direction and the Ash 23 direction stood each apart with a finger between every pair, advanced and not paid. Her short strip of notched birch bark stayed in her sleeve with no figure coming off the wood, and she was not made a reader of her own keeping and not made the speaker of any voice; the soft-cuffed clerk at \place{the out-of-line landing} stayed set for a measure nobody called, his slate turned inward, the market that had sold over him now gone away and leaving him where he had stood. Nothing in the file had been promoted by the reading. Nine were nine and no doctrine stood up out of them, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood.

Three counts in three places did not become one. Under the floor \lore{the carried fire} kept four low pulls with the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer unnamed; overhead \place{the boards} kept four nails on the near one, four on the next and a thumb-width knot where a fifth might have been driven and was not; through \place{the finger's-width door} the river kept \lore{water over water}, a low thing over a lower thing, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming, never numbered among the hearings, for a hearing wants a source standing and none stood. Agreeing any two of the three would have wanted a payer put under both, and none stood to any, so the three stayed where they were and the bell's gap stayed in its square and was not folded into them. Attention was the one thing no institution in \place{Grath} had thought to tax, and it was the whole of what he had spent, and it cost nobody anything and bought nothing that could be entered on a slate.

Three hundred and fifty-eight had stood exact behind the day; three hundred and fifty-nine stood exact at the going of the light and stood exact now in the small hours, six months and one hundred and seventy-eight days becoming one hundred and seventy-nine, told by nobody in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill}, Grath. The count closed because the day closed and not because anybody called it; 359 was not round and not a threshold, only one more in a row nobody counted aloud, and he had long ago stopped expecting to be told what it counted, which was not the same as not wanting it. The carried fire's fifth was unpaid the same number of days with its payer unnamed, and the name of the man the salt-stiff coat kept on its peg behind the door was unspoken the same number; the coat kept its broader shape and the crease at the left shoulder and gave nothing away. Nothing on the thirtieth had been opened, joined by a standing hand, named, called, paid or ended. There had been a market and a Cross and a bell and they belonged to the thirtieth and not to any day that had not earned them, and the reading had bought no lesson under the Vail roof and answered none of the held questions.

The surgeon took his small clean satisfaction in a field left as found and in an answer entered where nobody could see it --- nine entries set down on nine places and not averaged into one account, every gap left gap, every instrument laid back where the next hand would find it without looking. It was a cold satisfaction and it cost him nothing, which was how he knew it was not the same as peace. Underneath it the infant went on wanting the crust lifted the two inches to his mouth, the thread wiped, a hand that would close on the pot handle. Both were true, the second louder, neither winning, the two registers quarrelling without verdict in the ordinary small hours of the thirtieth, and the argument was the work.

The hand outside the wool came up an inch toward the river's note under the finger's-width door, the way a hand goes up in a field it has learned not to disturb. It did not reach, and the inch was not a reach; he had long ago stopped calling it one. Most of what a hand does in a field is wait, and he had once taken a hand into a cavity slowly, with the whole of the attention, and knew the difference between the moving and the waiting. The note went water over water under the door, a low thing over a lower thing, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming, never numbered among the hearings, for a hearing wants a source standing and none stood. The hand took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole on the note's own finishing, let the intake go and came straight down and laid itself back against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. The note had done before the hand came down, so the letting-go was neither early again nor stayed, and the hand did not stay; the note's own finishing ended the interval and not any decision of the hand, and that was the whole of the honesty in it. The intake was not a breath kept for warmth, the interval not a stopping of the river, the note not his. He filed it as answer two hundred and ninety-four: exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the nine arithmetics, the bell's gap, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the held breath at the sill, the staying at the inch, the early letting-go, the sixth opening, the pot-crack resting, the Ash 5 joining, the Ash 17 direction, the Ash 23 direction, the word that stood in no doorway, the fourth shaping, the turned writing, the strip in the sleeve nor his own kept gaps. Two hundred and ninety-four honest answers stood on the sixth place, each an object and not a power, and not one stood up and called itself a doctrine, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood.

\place{Ash} went out without a boundary drawn. Neither of the district's two habits of naming its months --- the older women by the weather, the slatemen by the number of Account-days --- was set right or left wrong in so many words; the month carried both names the way a pot carries two handles and is none the worse for having them. \place{Stone} came on in the dark beyond the door and was not brought into a day that had not earned it. The first firing of Stone stood far off and unbrought; the boy's first birthday on Stone 7 (Day 366) stood beyond that, and the Hearth Drawing on Stone 12 (Day 371) further off still, and none was named to him under any roof. No ninth doctrine, no seventh hearing, sixth held object, seventh opening, sixth telling, third shut-room telling, sixth asking, fifth shaping, tenth hand or tenth stopping was supplied to make a row come out even. The far-off hand that would one day lay the district's private keepings together was still far off and was not his.

The hand lay back against the fold on answer two hundred and ninety-four; the file lay open on the towel at the head of the bed with every gap left gap; the twice-mended pot stood on the pot board with the hairline of the original break showing between old-brass staple and tighter wire; one stiff brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from his ear with its empty place unnamed and no third, the thread frozen and unwiped; nine arithmetics rode locked and unread up the clay road in Berr Clay's box with the key gone up in a coat pocket and not coming back down; and through the finger's-width door the river went water over water while the boards overhead kept four nails on the near one, four on the next and a thumb-width knot where a fifth might have been driven and was not. Ash 30 was done and Stone came on in the dark beyond the door. Forward, not closed.
