The afternoon came on without a sound of its own, the way heat comes into a room from a wall that has been holding it all morning. From the low angle of the basket \person[Corin]{the boy} took the small sounds first, because they did not lie and the large ones were mostly weather. A pail went down on its stone where a pail always stood and the stone gave back the same small wooden knock it gave back every day of the year, neither more nor less than the day before. A rope settled into its own knot with a rasp as stiff as the week's cold, the fibres arguing among themselves a moment and then giving the argument up. A loaf went a finger's width along a board and stopped; nobody called the stopping a figure, and the board kept the shallow place where the loaf had lain. Barley passed from wall to wall in the breath between two women's voices and was never made into a number, the price carried on the sound of it like silt held in water and let go again before it could settle. He had learned early that a yard keeps its accounts in such things --- a knock, a rasp, a finger's width, a breath between voices --- and that none of them is a figure until some hand goes looking for one.

He took the field as he found it and left it as he found it, reading by the edges and not by the middle. At \place{the Vail corner} the clean dark line kept its finer grey beside it, the hairline the yard called \lore{the thaw}, thickened in the dry cold and carrying the faint sour tang of \lore{Ash} up off it where no broom had gone in two years. He kept the called-gone grey beside the left-in-place ridge with a finger between, and no agreement had been made to stop looking at this one, so the looking stayed a looking and did not turn into a custom. Out on \place{the worked strips} the field's empty place held the shape of a thing taken out, the way a bed holds the shape of a body that has got up; he did not lay that shape across the loaf's stopping or across the ridge, and did not set one absence beside another and call the two a row. \place{the trough} stood bare and dark with its skin gone, and the gone pan's round ring gave back its shallow print on \place{the flat stone by the well}, on a place of its own out of the path of feet. A figure set against any of these would want a payer standing, and none stood.

He went around \place{the seven walked-around places} once and not twice, with a look and a wide step and no number: the clean dark line and its grey, the space beneath the lowest rack where the broken birth-slates lay, the gone pan's ring, the bare trough, the field's empty place out on the strips, and \place{the returning-line place} where a hand had gone in on \emph{Seed 15} and had not come back. The seventh he went widest around, giving it the whole width of the basket's small world, as though a wide enough step might keep a hand from ever having gone in there. The wide step was a keeping and not a sign. He kept the step and gave the figure up, for a keeping that stands up and calls itself a rule is an account that has found a payer, and none stood.

Costs came to the yard as directions and went away as weather, and left no entry behind them. A man come down \place{the clay road} brought a number up out of a shut fist toward \place{the yard well}, held it where the light could see it, looked at it, and put it back into his sleeve. It arrived nowhere. The boy took the order apart as he took every field apart: hand first, then eye, then the mouth that did not open, and the half-beat lying in the gap between the hand and the eye. He gave the fist no figure and the sleeve no motive; a sleeve is a sleeve and not an intention, and a half-beat is a place where a thing might have stood and did not. A woman came asking what a pot cost and was sent to the pot with a practical word; from the pot she got a note and not a price, for pots keep their own accounts and do not speak them. The dressed grain lay covered under wool on the cool stone at \place{the rack end}, and the arithmetic read on \emph{Ilmen 10} over a basket in another yard had not known what it cost. No signature stood on the space between the figure and the price. He did not set one there.

The soft-cuffed clerk's figure entered on his inward slate before he looked up stayed on a place of its own, a report brought up the yard and never a sighting from the basket. \person{Tarn}'s degree toward \place{the out-of-line landing} below \place{the north bridge} stayed a shape found where put and was not repeated in the afternoon; \person{Jorra Vail}'s promise under the shed stayed sealed. The boy kept a finger between every pair and did not lay them across one another, for no witness stood under any of them, no reader, no payer and no tenth hand; three nothings standing in three places are three nothings still, and an account that has found no payer has not yet found its standing.

At \place{the kindling} Tarn kept six pieces in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check and the seventh at the lip beyond it, beside \person{Garl Vail}'s unforged collector sentence with no name on the man who turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on \lore{the tile debt}. The small knife lay at the very edge of its sill. His hand went over blade and sill as a man passes a hand over a tool put back where it belongs and came away with no breath held at the top of the reach --- the coming away was the whole of the checking. He cut nothing, counted nothing, spent nothing, and said to the wood in the tone of a man stating the weather, \emph{Six counted. The next I don't count}. Ash was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line, and the pale clip-ring at his left wrist caught and gave back what little light there was. His chest had been tight since the dark. He was helpful when he was frightened and on this afternoon he was not frightened, and the household read the quiet of him the way it reads the quiet of \person[Sella Vail]{Sella}.

At \place{the Vail wheel-shed} Jorra came through \place{the finger's-width door} with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past his bent little finger and white in a fist-crease where a hand had opened, and it was not the bone-coloured \lore{slip} of the wheel. He kept dusty forearms and clean on one tray and parted them as clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had been broken nor whether it had been a birth-slate, and gave the men unnamed in \place{the back room} no name and no word that the room was cold. He spoke to the wheel before any person --- \emph{It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady} --- and told a pot \emph{You'll do} in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either. He knew \lore{the full note} that pot would give three turns before it left his hands, with air under it and a want in it, and withheld the knowing from any master come up from \place{High Kiln} who would call it early and sing a pot before its time. His bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper} ahead of a sentence nobody had finished and came out without the extra holding of \emph{Ash 26}, gathered once and not made again, for a second would be a habit and a third a way and both want a payer where none stood. Nothing was said of the boy, and the boy asked for nothing to be said.

The going of the light came down the yard the way a hand comes down over a pot to feel whether it is still warm, and she went to \place{the hearth} before she went to the basket. This was the order of the house and had been the order since the boy was carried under the roof, and she kept it the way a woman keeps a wound clean --- daily, without comment, with a hand that knows exactly how much the thing will bear. She told the coals it was the thaw in the voice another woman would have used for a child who could not hear her, and turned them with the back of a broken pot. The small intake, the hold and the letting-go went toward no opening, and she figured neither of the two gaps: the one between the intake and the hold, the one between the hold and the going-away. Her finger lay along the top of \place{the loaf shelf} so the charcoal tally on its underside stayed unread; her lips moved on a two-gapped row and figured neither gap. One glance went to the clay jar standing cold under \lore{lead glaze} beyond \place{the pot board}, and the hand shut half over nothing, the little-door burn on the back of her left hand --- two straight sides, a lintel and a threshold --- closing on air, and went back to the pot. \emph{The house will keep it} went to the fire and not to the basket, because a thing said aloud becomes a thing the Court can enter.

She lifted the twice-mended pot thumb-first where the tighter wire crossed the crack, and the hairline of the original break showed between the old-brass staple and the wire, a dark thread with a hand's work on either side of it. Garl was in that hairline and in the salt-stiff coat with its crease at the left shoulder on the peg behind the door and in the hollow wheel-seat in the shed nobody was allowed to fill, and not in a story anybody told. His name had gone unspoken under this roof four hundred and eleven days, and \lore{the carried fire}'s fifth had gone unpaid the same number, the fire and the man standing side by side without becoming one. She gave both proverbs to the pot and the fire and never to a person: \emph{Leave the crack showing. People trust a thing they can see has been handled} and \emph{If it's worth mending it's worth mending twice. The second time is the real one}. Repair was not restoration; a restored thing hides the hand that handled it and a mended thing leaves the hand showing, and a surgeon keeps a graft apart from a cure for the same reason a potter leaves a staple in the light.

At the rack end she lifted a corner of the wool and turned the dressed grain piece by piece with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child, setting each down where it had lain, and took no figure from it and no handful for herself. The wool went back over the grain on \place{the drying racks} and the space stayed as it was, covered and unfigured and not knowing what it cost. Then the pot went down on the board again with its rim two inches beyond the reaching hand, and there was a half-beat at the setting and no look toward \place{the bed}. The half-beat was the distance between an intention and an arrest; it cost her nothing and bought the boy nothing, and neither of them called it a gift.

She carried him up the nineteen steps from \place{the Clay Yard} to \place{the Vail kiln-house} in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill}, \place{Grath}, at the half-pace from every open slate, and the instruction went to the wool, the river wind, the pot, the fire and the banked fire and never to the basket; the basket was not told what the day was and was not told to be still. In the yard she had gone once around the seven walked-around places with a look and a wide step and no number, once and not twice, and widest around the returning-line place where a hand had gone in on \emph{Seed 15} and had not come back. She set him under the roof and never in \place{the Debt Loft}, put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face, and added no second fold to the empty place. \lore{The keeping at the fold} was found where put and stayed a finding, not a placing, with no third crust, no name, no gift, no payment and no payer. \lore{The widow's mark} hung where it hung and was not touched: a morning, a hearth, a shelf, a yard, a well, a going of the light and a bed are not openings, and seven openings in seven months are seven and not a rule, with no eighth made due on a day that had earned only its seventeenth. Then she went back to her fire and said nothing at all --- not of the number in the fist that arrived nowhere, not of the woman sent to the pot for a price, not of the figure entered before a face, not of the arithmetic gone up the road in a box with no key under any roof.

In the small hours \place{the towel} came out at the head of the bed as instruments come out when a case is done and before the next is called, rough from the wash and smelling of tallow and low smoke, and he went over the field by the place of each thing with every gap left a gap. Six hearings of the full note stood beside and never among with the seventh place empty and \place{the Drawing house}'s small flat knock outside them on its own place, not a seventh filed where a hearing should have gone and not a payer standing under the silence. \lore{The told bell} kept its square in the ear beside the bell he had heard from \place{the Cross}, four, four and a gap, and its gap was not folded into the gap of the carried fire, which breathed four low pulls under \place{the boards} with the fifth unsupplied, or into the gap of the boards themselves, which kept four nails on the near board, four on the next and a thumb-width knot where a fifth might have been driven and was not, or into the twelve arithmetics of the last year behind one unsigned figure beside the first of a fresh count. Three counts in three places stayed three, because agreeing any two would want a payer put under both and none stood. The carried fire's payer had been unnamed four hundred and eleven days and Garl's name had been unspoken the same number, the fire and the man standing side by side and never becoming one.

Nine record-first hands stood with no tenth and nine stoppings stood with no tenth: the soft-cuffed clerk's reported hand on its own tray, \person{Hessa Rale}'s eighth unread up the road, \person{Ilva}'s ninth in her sleeve with the notched birch strip unread, \person{Berr Clay}'s thumb wiped twice before he touched nothing, the Drawing collector's ordinary hand, the Kindling collector's ordinary hand, the neat dark hand, \person{Marra}'s and the finger beneath the shelf. Four rooms kept the same gap with a finger between every pair, and no Court room was brought under the roof as a fifth likeness. The clerk's entered figure stayed on its own place with a finger between it and Tarn's degree toward the landing and another between it and the face at the yard's edge; the fifth shaping of \lore{the well-saying} sat on its own tray between Hessa's eighth hand and the saying of the road from the Cross to \place{Greybank}, with a finger between every pair and a third between it and the four ownerless directions; Berr's low saying about kindness stayed beside neither the twelve findings nor the new figure; the green at the pot board and the shape at the market's edge stayed sightings with no eighth of either. The first arithmetic of the year was up the clay road in a locked box with no key in any hand under this roof, one reading and not an account, not a signature, not a payer and not a thirteenth, and he did not lay the twelve old findings across the first of the new count to make a row come out even, for paper is not a payer. The arithmetic read over grain covered at home had not known what it cost, and the space between figure and price had no signature and he set none against it.

The small keepings gathered toward a far-off hand that was not his and had not come, and he took a small clean satisfaction in a field taken as found and left as found. It was an exact satisfaction, and because it cost nothing he knew it was not peace. He had once stood at a bay door and sorted twenty people by what they would not survive, and the knowing stood side by side with the knowing that a face composed by cold is not composure and that a cold patient cannot clot, and neither bought the other. Under all of it, louder than the register, the wanting went on: the crust lifted two inches, the thread wiped, a hand on the pot handle. He got none of the three. Both were true. The second was louder. Neither won, and the quarrel was the work.

Answer three hundred and ninety-eight came out and was filed on the sixth place: exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the twelve readings, the first of the new count, the printed figure, the fifth shaping, the clerk's entered figure, Tarn's degree, the face at the yard's edge, the widow's mark nor the keeping at the fold. Two honest answers stood there now, three hundred and ninety-seven and three hundred and ninety-eight, and not one of them was a doctrine. Count four hundred and eleven exact, six months and two hundred and thirty-one days told by nobody who would have known what a number counted; the carried fire's fifth unpaid the same number and Garl's name unspoken the same number, the fire and the man standing side by side without becoming one. He did not stand four hundred and eleven up as a threshold, a doctrine or a figure.

Through the finger's-width door \place{the river} kept its note of \lore{water over water}, a low thing over a lower thing under \lore{the winter skin}, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming, found where put on a day that had earned only its seventeenth. The sound came from under the covering and not from the covering; it was not a hearing, not a sign, not a fourth of the three coverings, not a rule and not a payer, and the note ended on its own finishing before any hand came down. The hand outside the wool came up an inch toward the note. The inch was not a reach. He took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole on the note's own finishing under the covering --- neither early nor stayed --- and let it go as the note ended. Then the hand came straight down and laid itself flat against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking.

The crust's corner stood a hair off the wool and the thread was stiff and unwiped; cold had come to cheek, temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rides back and stopped there as at the end of a line written out for it, and \lore{the Afterdrop} waited past a warming nobody here would perform. Four low pulls breathed below the floor with the fifth unsupplied. The first arithmetic of the year was up the clay road in a locked box and no key lay in his hand. The fifth shaping sat on its own tray with a finger between it and every other kept thing. Nothing had been joined, named, called, paid or ended, and the house went on as a house goes when a pot is set down in its place and the fire kept --- forward, into a morning not yet earned and not brought into this one.
