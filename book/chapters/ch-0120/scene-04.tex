The afternoon came on with no more ceremony than a second smoke rising from a banked fire: it lay over the yard, and the yard went on under it. It had not been earned and had not been asked for, and \person[Corin]{the boy}, carried low under the eaves in his basket, took it by its small sounds first, because the small sounds do not lie and the large ones are mostly weather. A pail went down on its stone at \place{the yard well} where a pail always stood; a rope settled into its own knot with a rasp as stiff as the week's cold; a loaf went a finger's width along a board and stopped, and nobody called the stopping a figure; barley passed from wall to wall in the breath between two women's voices and was never made into a number. At \place{the Vail corner} a neighbour raised a rake at the ridge, looked at the clean dark line and the finer grey beside it that the yard called \lore{the thaw}, and set the rake down with nothing done and nothing said. \lore{Ash} had come up off that line in the dry cold where no broom had gone in two years.

At \place{the kindling} \person{Tarn} kept six pieces in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check and the seventh at the lip beyond it, beside \person{Garl Vail}'s unforged collector sentence with no name on the man who turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on \lore{the tile debt}. The stack kept its own arithmetic of four, four and a space, and the space was not a fifth and had never been filled. The small knife lay at the very edge of its sill.

His hand went over blade and sill as a man passes a hand over a tool put back where it belongs. It came away.

That was all. The hand went up and came away, and the coming away was the whole of the checking. No breath was held at the top of the reach. No word went to the wood.

In the morning he had said \emph{Six counted. The next I don't count} in the tone of a man stating the weather. Now, with the same six standing in the mark and the same seventh at the lip, he said nothing at all to the wood. The small knife stayed where it could be checked, checked by the hand and not by a sentence. The sixth was still six; the seventh was still at the lip; the breath he had drawn on \emph{Seed 16} was still unspent at the top of a reach nobody in the yard could see; the loan was still uncalled at the very end of a term that had already ended. He cut nothing, counted nothing, spent nothing, and took no more out of the afternoon than the afternoon would give.

The boy took the silence apart as he took every field apart, by the place of what was there and the place of what was not. What was there: six in the mark, the seventh at the lip, a hand over blade and sill and away. What was not: the words, the held breath at the top of the reach, a figure, a name, a man standing under any of it to pay. He put the absence on a place of its own. It was not a tenth stopping, for the nine stoppings stood where they had been set and this was not brought among them to make a row come out even. It was not a payment, for nothing had been handed over and no hand had received. It was not a hearing, for no note had sounded and the ear had been given nothing to keep. It was not a rule, for a rule is an account that has found a payer, and none had stood. He read the withheld sentence as a hand withdrawn from a field before the dressing goes on: exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air. The checking was the work. The sentence had never been the work, and the absence of it bought nobody anything.

He did not lay the silence across the soft-cuffed clerk's figure entered on an inward slate before a face was looked at, reported up the yard and not witnessed; he did not lay it across Tarn's degree toward \place{the out-of-line landing} below \place{the north bridge}, held as a shape and not repeated; he did not lay it across the face at the yard's edge filed among the older record-first hands and not counted among them; and he did not lay it across \person{Jorra Vail}'s promise under the shed, neither given nor withdrawn. Five places, a finger between every pair, and the space between kept as a space. A doctrine is an account that has found a payer. There was no doctrine here, and nothing had been made by standing the five together.

Tarn took the broom only after \place{the flagstones} had been given their instruction. “Yes, house,” he said to the question and to the thing behind it, agreeing so thoroughly that the question had nowhere left to put a foot, and went over the stones without a word and without a turn of the head toward the landing. His chest had been tight since the dark. Ash was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line, and the pale clip-ring at his left wrist caught and gave back what little light there was. His stroke went over the shallow bright patch without the half-check. He was helpful when he was frightened, and on this afternoon he was not frightened; the household read the quiet of him the way it read the quiet of \person{Sella Vail}, as a weather signal and not a summons. A man who owns nothing knows where every small thing is, and the small thing he had put back where it belonged was the only one that had moved.

Under the wheel-shed edge Jorra kept his own silence, which was an older one and not the same. He came through \place{the finger's-width door} with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past his bent little finger and white in a fist-crease where a hand had opened, and it was not the bone-coloured \lore{slip} of the wheel. He parted dusty forearms from clean as clot from vessel, spoke to the wheel before any person and told a pot \emph{You'll do} in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either; he knew the \lore{full note} that pot would give three turns ahead, with air under it and a want in it, and kept the knowing where no master come up from \place{High Kiln} could call it early and sing a pot before its time. His bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper} ahead of an unfinished sentence and came out without leaving the extra holding of \emph{Ash 26} a second time. \emph{He's not Vail} stayed sealed as a lid nobody had tried to lift in three years, and the promise stayed neither given nor withdrawn. The boy kept that silence on its own place too, with a finger between it and the silence at the kindling. Two quiet men under one roof were two quiet men and not a custom, and no argument between them had found a foot.

The afternoon went on at the grammar of an ordinary Eighthday and gave nobody a figure to carry. The rake went up at the ridge and came down with nothing done; the loaf stayed stopped where it had stopped; \lore{the widow's mark} hung where it hung at a shelf, a well and an afternoon, untouched, and no eighth opening came due on a day that had earned only its eighteenth. Six hearings of the full note stood beside and never among, with the seventh place empty and the Drawing's flat knock outside it on its own ground. The fifth shaping of \lore{the well-saying} sat on its tray. Nine record-first hands stood with no tenth, and nine stoppings with no tenth. \person{Hessa Rale}'s eighth hand stayed eight up the road, \person{Ilva}'s strip of notched birch bark stayed in her sleeve with her ninth hand nine, and \person{Berr Clay}'s box went on up \place{the clay road} with the first arithmetic of the year locked inside and no key under any roof. Nothing was made a sixth of, nothing was joined, and the boy took a small clean satisfaction in a field taken as found and left as found, and knew it was not peace because it cost nothing. Under it, louder, the wanting went on, and the two kinds of knowing stood side by side without either buying the other.

At the going of the light Sella came to \place{the hearth} before she came to the basket, as she had come at the beginning of every day since the boy was brought under the roof. She told the coals it was the thaw in the voice another woman would have used for a child who could not hear her, and turned them with the back of a broken pot. The small intake, the hold and the letting-go went toward no opening, and she figured neither of the two gaps: the one between the intake and the hold, the one between the hold and the going-away. Her finger lay along the top of \place{the loaf shelf} so the charcoal tally on its underside stayed unread; her lips moved on a two-gapped row and figured neither gap. One glance went to the clay jar standing cold under \lore{lead glaze} beyond \place{the pot board}, and the hand shut half over nothing, the little-door burn on the back of her left hand --- two straight sides, a lintel and a threshold --- closing on air, and went back to the pot. \emph{The house will keep it} went to the fire and not to the basket, because a thing said aloud becomes a thing the Court can enter.

She lifted the twice-mended pot thumb-first where the tighter wire crossed the crack, and the hairline of the original break showed between the old-brass staple and the wire, a dark thread with a hand's work on either side of it. Both proverbs went to the pot and the fire and never to a person: \emph{Leave the crack showing. People trust a thing they can see has been handled} and \emph{If it's worth mending it's worth mending twice. The second time is the real one}. Repair was not restoration; a restored thing hides the hand that handled it and a mended thing leaves the hand showing, and a surgeon keeps a graft apart from a cure for the same reason a potter leaves a staple in the light. At \place{the rack end} she lifted a corner of the wool and turned the dressed grain piece by piece with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child, setting each down where it had lain, and took no figure from it and no handful for herself. The wool went back over the grain on \place{the drying racks} and the space stayed as it was, covered and unfigured and not knowing what it cost. Then the pot went down on the board again with its rim two inches beyond the reaching hand, and there was a half-beat at the setting and no look toward the child. The half-beat was the distance between an intention and an arrest; it cost her nothing and bought the boy nothing, and neither of them called it a gift. The widow's mark hung where it hung and was not touched: a carrying, a hearth, a shelf, a going of the light and a basket are not openings, and seven openings in seven months are seven and not a rule.

She carried him up the nineteen steps from \place{the Clay Yard} into \place{the Vail kiln-house} in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill}, \place{Grath}, at the half-pace from every open slate, and the instruction went to the wool, the river wind, the pot, the fire and the banked fire and never to the basket. The basket was not told what the day was and was not told to be still. She went once around \place{the seven walked-around places} with a look and a wide step and no number, once and not twice, and widest around \place{the returning-line place} where a hand had gone in on \emph{Seed 15} and had not come back. The wide step was a keeping and not a sign, and a figure set against it would want a payer standing and none stood. At the yard well a pail stood on its stone where a pail always stood, and \place{the trough} was dark and bare, and the gone pan's shallow ring kept its round print on a place of its own. She set him under the roof and never in \place{the Debt Loft}, put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face, and added no second fold. The keeping was found where put and stayed a finding, with no third crust, no name spoken over the place, no gift, no payment and no payer. The thread of drool at his mouth was stiff in the dry kiln heat and unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread.

Garl stayed where Garl stays, in objects and not in a story. He was in the salt-stiff coat with its crease at the left shoulder on the peg behind the door, in the hollow wheel-seat in the shed keeping its twenty-year hollow that nobody was allowed to fill, in the clean dark line at the Vail corner with its finer grey beside it called the thaw, unswept and thickened in the dry cold with the sour tang of Ash where no broom had gone in two years, in the hairline of the pot between staple and wire, and in the unforged collector sentence lying beside the seventh notch at the kindling. The debt against a firing of tiles went on circling without a name, neither paid nor settled nor done with and worse for being uncalled. His name had gone unspoken under this roof four hundred and twelve days, and \lore{the carried fire}'s fifth had gone unpaid the same four hundred and twelve days, the fire and the man standing side by side without ever becoming one. Neither the figure entered before a face on \emph{Ilmen 15} nor the first arithmetic gone up the road had supplied a payer to either count, and the boy did not make the two counts a third thing by setting them in the same breath. \place{The boards} overhead kept four nails on the near board, four on the next, and a thumb-width knot where a fifth might have been driven and was not, and the fire below gave four low pulls with the silence where the fifth should have stood, two counts in two places that did not become one.

In the small hours \place{the towel} came out at the head of \place{the bed} as instruments come out when a case is done and before the next is called, rough from the wash and smelling of tallow and low smoke, and he went over the day by the place of each thing with every gap left a gap. The soft-cuffed clerk's figure entered before a face was looked at stayed on a place of its own beside his inward slate, reported and not witnessed, with a finger between it and Tarn's degree toward the landing and another between it and the face at the yard's edge filed among the older record-first hands and not counted among them. The order of it stood hand, eye, mouth with the half-beat lying in the gap, and no man was put under any of the three; it was the next reaching toward the loan that can hear and not the calling of it, and he did not call it. The fifth shaping of the well-saying sat on its tray between Hessa's eighth record-first hand and the saying of the road from \place{the Cross} to \place{Greybank}, with a finger between every pair and a third between it and the four ownerless directions; Ilva's short strip of notched birch bark stayed in her sleeve unread, her count in wood and her ninth hand nine. The carried fire's fifth was unpaid four hundred and twelve days and Garl's name was unspoken the same number, the fire and the man standing side by side and never becoming one; the first arithmetic of the year was up the clay road in a locked box with no key in any hand under this roof, and the figure read was still not the figure paid.

Six hearings of the full note stood beside and never among, with the seventh place empty and the Drawing's flat knock outside on its own ground; \person{Dessa Silt} was not made a seventh and the empty place was not dug for or filled. The heard bell kept its square beside the told, four, four and a gap, its gap not folded into the fire's four and a gap, the boards' four and four and a thumb-width knot, or the twelve arithmetics behind one unsigned figure beside the first of a fresh count --- three counts in three places staying three, because agreeing any two would want a payer put under both and none stood. Nine record-first hands stood with no tenth, and nine stoppings stood with no tenth; the ear-sentence about an ear under this roof being worth more than the debt that holds it came all the way to the word that would call the loan and stopped there as an object and not a rule, with no figure and no name and the Seed 16 breath still unspent. Tarn's silence of the afternoon was filed on a place of its own beside them, never among: a hand withdrawn from a field before the dressing went on, found where put, a change and not a custom owing anything to a morrow, not a tenth stopping, not a payment, not a hearing and not a rule. He had said the sentence in the morning so that a withholding would be a change, and the change had come; it stood now as a finding and asked no day that had not earned it to answer for it. \emph{Ilmen 20} stood one morning off, a date and not a day that had earned anything, and he did not bring it under the roof. \lore{The keeping at the fold} was found where put and stayed a finding, with no second fold, no third crust, no name, no gift and no payer.

Answer four hundred came out and was filed on the sixth place: exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the twelve readings, the first of the new count, the printed figure, the fifth shaping, the clerk's entered figure, Tarn's degree, the face at the yard's edge, Jorra's ungiven promise, the afternoon's withheld sentence, the widow's mark nor the keeping at the fold. Four honest answers stood there now, three hundred and ninety-seven, three hundred and ninety-eight, three hundred and ninety-nine and four hundred, beginning what the next days would carry on, and not one of them was a doctrine. Count four hundred and twelve exact, six months and two hundred and thirty-two days told by nobody who would have known what a number counted; the fire's fifth unpaid the same number and Garl's name unspoken the same number, the fire and the man standing side by side without becoming one. He did not stand four hundred and twelve up as a threshold, a doctrine or a figure, and he borrowed no payer from the arithmetics gone up the road, for paper is not a payer and a row made even on paper still wants a hand under it before it can be called an account. The house kept its days badly, in loaves pushed a finger's width and stopped, in glances at a cold clay jar, in the unnamed space at the row's end, and his exactness borrowed none of its ways and taught it none.

Cold had come to cheek, temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, and stopped there as at the end of a line written out for it --- neither an order to go farther nor an order to go away. \lore{The Afterdrop} waited past a warming nobody in this house would perform. A basket set level out of the draught where the heat came to him sideways was the careful rewarming he would have chosen and could not ask for; the asking would have gone to nobody who would have understood what was being asked. A face composed by cold is not composure, and a cold patient cannot clot and must be rewarmed from outside, slowly, never hurried, never rubbed, never set too near the fire. He had once stood at a bay door and sorted twenty people by what they would not survive, and that knowing stood side by side with the knowing of the extremities, which give up heat before the core and take it back the same way, and neither bought the other. Under all of it, louder than the register, the wanting went on: the crust lifted two inches, the thread wiped, a hand on the pot handle. He wanted the three things without knowing that wanting them was a want, and he got none of the three. Both were true. The second was louder. Neither won, and no verdict was looked for; the quarrel was the work, and the work went on in silence in a room whose floor does not touch his.

Through the finger's-width door \place{the river} kept its note of \lore{water over water}, a low thing over a lower thing under \lore{the winter skin}, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming, found where put on a day that had earned only its eighteenth. The sound came from under the covering and not from the covering; it was not a hearing, not a sign, not a fourth of the three coverings, not a rule and not a payer, and the note ended on its own finishing before any hand came down. The hand outside the wool came up an inch toward the note. The inch was not a reach. He took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole on the note's own finishing under the covering --- neither early nor stayed --- and let it go as the note ended. Then the hand came straight down and laid itself flat against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking.

The crust's corner stood a hair off the wool and the thread was stiff and unwiped; four low pulls breathed below the floor with the fifth unsupplied; the first arithmetic of the year was up the clay road in a locked box and no key lay in his hand; the fifth shaping sat on its own tray with a finger between it and every other kept thing. Nothing had been joined, named, called, paid or ended, and the house went on as a house goes when a pot is set down in its place and the fire kept --- forward, into a morning not yet earned and not brought into this one.
