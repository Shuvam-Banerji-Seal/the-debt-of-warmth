## Chronicle

Three days came under the Vail roof — Ilmen 17, 18 and 19 — and the house kept them badly, in loaves pushed a finger's width along a board and stopped. The boy kept four hundred and eleven, then twelve, then thirteen exact against it, and stood no number up as a threshold. He was carried down the nineteen steps and up again at the going of the light, under the roof, never in the Debt Loft. Sella came to the hearth before the basket at both ends of every day, told the coals it was the thaw, turned them with a broken pot's back, lifted the twice-mended pot thumb-first with the hairline showing, and gave both proverbs to pot and fire and never to a person. Repair was not restoration.

At the kindling Tarn kept six pieces in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check and the seventh at the lip beside Garl Vail's unforged sentence. On the seventeenth he said, *Six counted. The next I don't count*. On the eighteenth his hand went over blade and sill and away and no word went to the wood; on the nineteenth he said it again, so the silence stood as a change and not a habit. Jorra parted dusty forearms from clean as clot from vessel, spoke to the wheel before any person, and kept the full note three turns ahead from any master who would call a pot early.

In the small hours the towel came out at the head of the bed as instruments come out, and the boy went over each day by the place of its thing with every gap left a gap: six hearings with the seventh empty and the Drawing's knock outside, three counts in three places staying three, nine hands and nine stoppings with no tenth, the fifth shaping of the well-saying on its own tray, the clerk's entered figure on its own place with a finger between it and the degree and another between it and the face. Six answers, 397 through 402, were filed on the sixth place, worth nothing at two inches of air; not one was a doctrine.

On the last afternoon air less cold came off the river. The sour tang of Ash thinned at the Vail corner; the river's skin softened at one edge where the low note ran nearer under it. The yard called it weather, and Sella told the fire it was the thaw. The boy filed the softening beside the clean dark line and the called-gone grey, gave it no hand and no payer, and did not look twice. He took a small clean satisfaction in a field taken as found and left as found, and knew it was not peace because it cost nothing. The crust's corner stood a hair off the wool; the thread was stiff and unwiped; four low pulls breathed below the floor with the fifth unsupplied. The hand came straight down on the fold.
