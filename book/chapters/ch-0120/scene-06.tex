The afternoon came up off \place{the river} as air less cold than the morning's, moving in under the eaves and through \place{the finger's-width door} before it reached the yard, so that a body standing in \place{the Clay Yard} felt the change at the back of the hand before anybody had a word for it. It was not warmth. It was a lessening. \person[Corin]{The boy}, carried low in the basket with the wool riding back from the small bare joint of his wrist, had once known the difference between a patient who was returning and a patient who had only stopped falling: the one was a thing happening, the other a thing ceasing, and a roomful of people might call them by the same name without either being mended. The cold at the wrist did not go. It stopped arriving with the same instruction it had carried all morning, and that was all. At \place{the Vail corner} the sour tang of \lore{Ash} thinned under the back of a hand where it had lain thick in the dry cold, and the clean dark line kept its finer grey beside it that the yard called \lore{the thaw}, unswept and unowned; only the grey lay a hair less distinct along one stretch, as though a finger had passed over it and not quite smoothed it away. Nobody had passed a finger over it. Nobody had swept it. Two years of not sweeping had made that line a feature of the yard, like the hollow in the wheel-seat, and a feature of the yard is not an instruction and has no hand under it.

The river under its \lore{winter skin} softened at one edge on that afternoon. The low note of \lore{water over water} ran nearer under the covering there, a thing over a lower thing, and the surgeon knew a covering put on by the cold and taken off by the cold when he felt one: it was entered nowhere, had no payer, and was not one of \lore{the three coverings} anybody in this house had named. He filed the softening on a place of its own, beside the clean dark line, the new finer grey, the called-gone grey at the row's end and the river under its skin --- not a sign, not a fourth covering, not a sixth shaping of the well-saying, not a hearing, not a payment, with no hand or payer under it and not made again on a day that had earned only its nineteenth. A second softening would be a custom and a third a way, and both want a payer standing where none stood. He did not look at the place twice to make sure of it. Looking twice is how a finding becomes a habit.

The yard called it weather. At \place{the yard well} a woman tipped her bucket and said, \emph{The air has turned}; a man with his hands in his sleeves said, \emph{It'll be back before morning}. Nobody asked them what they meant by it, and they did not ask each other. The argument ended where such arguments end in that yard, with the bucket on its stone and nobody owning the day. \person{Sella Vail} came to \place{the hearth} before she came to the basket and told the coals it was the thaw in the voice another woman would have used for a child who could not hear her. She did not tell the basket. A basket told what the day was would be a basket with an opinion about the day, and the house had no use for one.

He took the yard as he found it and left it as he found it, taking the small sounds first because the small sounds do not lie and the large ones are mostly weather. A pail went down on its stone where a pail always stood. A rope settled into its own knot with a rasp the week's cold had stiffened. A loaf went a finger's width along a board and stopped, with nobody calling the stopping a figure. Barley passed from wall to wall in the breath between two women's voices and was never made into a number. He went once around \place{the seven walked-around places} with a look and a wide step and no number, once and not twice, and widest around \place{the returning-line place} where a hand had gone in on \emph{Seed 15} and had not come back. The wide step was a keeping and not a sign; a figure set against it would want a payer standing, and none stood. Beside it \place{the trough} stood dark and bare, and the gone pan kept its shallow round print on a place of its own. The called-gone grey stayed beside the left-in-place ridge with a finger between, and there had been no agreement to stop looking at that one: a thing left in place is not a thing forgiven, and the looking at it cost nobody anything and bought nobody anything.

Costs arrived as directions and went away as weather, leaving no entry. A man come down \place{the clay road} brought a number up out of a shut fist toward the well, held it where the light could see it, looked at it, and put it back into his sleeve; it arrived nowhere. He took the order apart as he took every field apart --- hand, eye, mouth, and the half-beat lying in the gap --- and gave the fist no figure and the sleeve no motive, for a motive is a payer wearing a coat. A woman sent to ask what a pot cost was sent to the pot and got a note and not a price. The dressed grain lay covered. The arithmetic read on \emph{Ilmen 10} did not know what the grain cost, and no signature stood on the space between the figure and the price; he set none there himself. Twelve arithmetics of \emph{Y.H. 813} stood unchanged behind one unsigned figure beside the first of a fresh count, and the first arithmetic of \emph{Y.H. 814} was up the clay road in \person{Berr Clay}'s locked box with the key gone up in a coat pocket that never came back down and no key under any roof. He did not lay the twelve across the new one to make a row come out even, for paper is not a payer and a row made even on paper still wants a hand under it before it can be called an account. The figure read was still not the figure paid. The printed figure stood at one in twelve, unchallenged, and the boy's exact count ran on under it without borrowing from it.

At the going of the light she kept the whole grammar as though the afternoon had been any afternoon and the air had not turned at all. She told the coals it was the thaw, turned them with the back of a broken pot, and gave the small intake, the hold and the letting-go toward no opening, figuring neither of the two gaps: the one between the intake and the hold, the one between the hold and the going-away. Her finger lay along the top of \place{the loaf shelf} so the charcoal tally on its underside stayed unread, and her lips moved on a two-gapped row with neither gap figured. One glance went to the clay jar standing cold under \lore{lead glaze} beyond \place{the pot board}; the hand shut half over nothing, the little-door burn on the back of her left hand --- two straight sides, a lintel and a threshold --- closing on air, and went back to the pot. \emph{The house will keep it} went to the fire and not to the basket, because a thing said aloud becomes a thing the Court can enter. Her thumb went first to where the tighter wire crossed the crack, and only then did the weight come on the handle; the hairline of the original break showed between the old-brass staple and the wire, a dark thread with a hand's work on either side of it. Both proverbs went to the pot and the fire and never to a person: \emph{Leave the crack showing. People trust a thing they can see has been handled} and \emph{If it's worth mending it's worth mending twice. The second time is the real one}. Repair was not restoration. A restored thing hides the hand that handled it; a mended thing leaves the hand showing, and a surgeon keeps a graft apart from a cure for the same reason a potter leaves a staple in the light. At \place{the rack end} she lifted a corner of the wool and turned the dressed grain piece by piece with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child, set each down where it had lain, took no figure from it and no handful for herself, and drew the wool back over \place{the drying racks}. Then the pot went down with its rim two inches beyond the reaching hand, and there was a half-beat at the setting and no look toward the child. The half-beat was the distance between an intention and an arrest. It cost her nothing and bought the boy nothing, and neither of them called it a gift.

\person{Garl Vail} stayed where Garl stays, in objects and not in a story: in the hairline of the pot between staple and wire, in the salt-stiff coat with its crease at the left shoulder on the peg behind the door, in the hollow wheel-seat under \place{the Vail wheel-shed} keeping its twenty-year hollow that nobody was allowed to fill, in the clean dark line at the Vail corner with its finer grey beside it called the thaw, and in the unforged collector sentence lying beside the seventh notch at \place{the kindling}, with no name on the man who turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on \lore{the tile debt}. The debt against a firing of tiles went on circling without a name, neither paid nor settled nor done with, and worse for being uncalled. His name had gone unspoken under this roof four hundred and thirteen days, and \lore{the carried fire}'s fifth had gone unpaid the same number, the fire and the man standing side by side without becoming one. He had been a big man with clay in the creases of his hands and a laugh that ended in a cough, and the house was shaped around the place he had put down and not taken up again; the shape was maintenance, not a story, and nobody had made a lesson of it. The proverbs survived him and were very likely better than he had said them.

She carried him up the nineteen steps at the half-pace from every open slate, and the instruction went to the wool, the river wind, the pot, the fire and the banked fire and never to the basket. He was set under the roof and never in \place{the Debt Loft}. She went wide around the seven walked-around places once and not twice, widest at the returning-line place, and put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face. She added no second fold. The keeping was found where put and stayed a finding, not a placing, with no third crust, no name, no gift, no payment and no payer; a habit wants a hand that placed twice and a way wants a third, and both want a payer where none stood. \lore{The widow's mark} hung where it hung at a shelf, a well, a yard, a going of the light and a bed, and was not touched. Seven openings in seven months were seven and not a rule, and no eighth came due on a day that had earned only its nineteenth. The widow's mark was a thumb-length of lead-glazed clay on a cord at her throat with the Vail kiln scratched into it, and her hand went to it before she opened a kiln and at no other hour; an afternoon air less cold was not a kiln, a thaw was not an opening, and a basket was not a door. The tile debt went on circling without a name.

In the small hours \place{the towel} came out at the head of \place{the bed} as instruments come out when a case is done and before the next is called, rough from the wash and smelling of tallow and low smoke. He went over the field by the place of each thing with every gap left a gap. The soft-cuffed clerk's figure, entered before a face was looked at, stayed on its own place beside his inward slate at \place{the out-of-line landing} below \place{the north bridge}, with a finger between it and \person{Tarn}'s degree toward the landing and another between it and the face at the yard's edge filed among the older record-first hands and not counted among them: hand, eye, mouth, and the half-beat in the gap, reported and not witnessed, no man standing under the figure and no room entered. The fifth shaping of \lore{the well-saying} sat on its own tray between \person{Hessa Rale}'s eighth record-first hand up the road and the saying of the road from \place{the Cross} to \place{Greybank}, with a finger between every pair and a third between it and the four ownerless directions; her narrow slate stayed unread up the road. \person{Ilva}'s short strip of notched birch bark stayed in her sleeve unread with her ninth hand nine, and no figure came off the wood. Berr's low saying about kindness being entered stood on a place of its own beside neither the twelve findings nor the new figure, and the belief in it stayed up the road with the box where it had gone. The silence after Weighing's name, the clean voice's missing name, the Long Night story's empty name and the place the Remembering did not fill sat together on the gap tray with a finger between every pair, four absences and not a fifth bearing; the green at the pot board and the shape at the market's edge were sightings with no eighth of either. \person{Tarn}'s sentence, kept back on the eighteenth and spoken again on the nineteenth, stood on a place of its own, not a tenth stopping and not made a habit; \person{Jorra Vail}'s older silence stood on another place beside it, two quiet men under one roof and not a custom. The softening at the river's skin stood on a place of its own beside the clean dark line and the called-gone grey, not a fourth covering and not a sign. None of these repeated, and none was joined to another; a row made even out of separate keepings would want a payer put under every part of it, and none stood.

\place{The boards} kept four nails on the near board, four on the next, and a thumb-width knot where a fifth might have been driven and was not. Under them the carried fire gave four low pulls with the silence where the fifth should have stood, and nobody lifted the floor or supplied the pull; its payer had been unnamed four hundred and thirteen days and Garl's name unspoken the same number, and the two had never become one. The told bell kept its gap in its square beside the heard bell, four, four and a gap, and its gap was not folded into the fire's four and a gap, the boards' four and four and a knot, or the twelve arithmetics behind one unsigned figure beside the first of a fresh count --- three counts in three places staying three, because agreeing any two would want a payer put under both and none stood. Six hearings of \lore{the full note} stood beside and never among, with the seventh place empty and the flat knock from \place{the Drawing house} outside on its own ground; no seventh came on any of the three days. Nine record-first hands stood with no tenth and nine stoppings stood with no tenth. Four rooms kept the same gap with a finger between every pair, and no Court room was brought under the roof as a fifth likeness. \emph{Ilmen 20} stood one morning off, a date and not a day that had earned anything, and he did not bring it under the roof to make the nineteenth a threshold.

The quiet after a figure entered before a face was looked at was kept as a quiet and not made a rule. The boy's exact count ran on under a house that could not say what it counted, and he took a small clean satisfaction in a field taken as found and left as found, and knew it cost nothing, which was how he knew it was not peace. He had once stood at a bay door and sorted twenty people by what they would not survive, and the same hand lay now outside the wool against a fold of a kind nobody under this roof would have thought to keep; the two kinds of knowing stood side by side and neither bought the other. Under them, louder, the wanting went on: the crust lifted two inches, the thread wiped, a hand on the handle of the pot. He wanted the three things without knowing that wanting them was a want, and got none of them. Both were true. The second was louder. Neither won, and no verdict was looked for; the quarrel was the work, and the work went on in silence in a room whose floor does not touch his. Cold had come to cheek, temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back and stopped there as at the end of a line written out for it, with neither an order to go farther nor an order to go away. \lore{The Afterdrop} waited past a warming nobody in this house would perform.

Through the finger's-width door the river kept its note of water over water, a low thing over a lower thing under the winter skin, softer at one edge on this day than on the two days before, found where put on a day that had earned only its nineteenth and not spent on it. The sound came from under the covering and not from the covering; it was not a hearing, not a sign, not a rule and not a payer, and the note ended on its own finishing before any hand came down. The hand outside the wool came up an inch toward the note. The inch was not a reach. He took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole on the note's own finishing under the covering --- neither early nor stayed --- and let it go as the note ended. Then the hand came straight down and laid itself flat against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking.

The clerk's entered figure was kept on its own place with a finger between it and the degree; Tarn's unspoken sentence was kept on its own place and not made a habit; the softening at the river's skin was kept on its own place and not made a sign. The crust's corner stood a hair off the wool and the thread was frozen and unwiped. Four low pulls breathed below the floor with the fifth unsupplied. The first arithmetic of the year was up the clay road in a locked box with no key in his hand. The fifth shaping sat on its own tray with a finger between it and every other kept thing. Nothing had been joined, named, called, paid or ended, and the house went on as a house goes when a pot is set down in its place and the fire kept --- forward, into a morning not yet earned and not brought into this one. Count four hundred and thirteen exact, and no number stood up as a threshold.
