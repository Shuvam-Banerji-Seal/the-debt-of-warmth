\daymark{412}{Ilmen 18, Y.H. 814, Eighthday}

At the head of \place{the bed} \place{the towel} came out as instruments come out when a case is done and before the next is called, rough from the wash and smelling of tallow and low smoke, and \person[Corin]{the boy} took the body in its old order because there was no other work the room allowed him. One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust stood two inches from his ear with its corner standing a hair off the wool, gone past the point where a child would still eat it and hardened through another dry night into old horn. \lore{The keeping at the fold} was found where put and stayed a finding, not a placing, with no second fold, no name spoken over the place, no gift, no payment and no payer. The thread of drool at his mouth had frozen in the dry kiln heat and was unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread. Cold had come to cheek, temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, and stopped there as at the end of a line written out for it --- neither an order to go farther nor an order to go away. \lore{The Afterdrop} waited past a warming nobody in this house would perform.

He knew what the look of him meant and what it did not. A face composed by cold is not composure; the grimace of a cold man wears the shape of a smile and the smile means nothing. Pink returning is the surface still lying warm over a cold core, and a cold patient cannot clot and must be rewarmed from outside and slowly, never hurried, never rubbed, never set too near the fire. A basket set level out of the draught where the heat came to him sideways was the careful rewarming he would have chosen and could not ask for; the asking would have gone to nobody who would have understood what was being asked. He had once stood at a bay door and sorted twenty people by what they would not survive, and that knowing stood side by side with the knowing of the extremities, which give up heat before the core and take it back the same way, and neither bought the other. Under all of it, louder than the register, the wanting went on: the crust lifted two inches, the thread wiped, a hand on the pot handle. He wanted the three things without knowing that wanting them was a want, and he got none of the three. Both were true. The second was louder. Neither won, and no verdict was looked for; the quarrel was the work, and the work went on in silence in a room whose floor does not touch his.

First light came in under \place{the finger's-width door} the way a thin answer comes into a room where nobody has asked the question, and the count went four hundred and eleven to four hundred and twelve exact, six months and two hundred and thirty-one days becoming two hundred and thirty-two, told by nobody who would have known what a number counted. He did not stand four hundred and twelve up as a threshold, a doctrine or a figure. The house kept its days badly, in loaves pushed a finger's width along a board and stopped, in a glance at a clay jar, in the space at the row's end nobody called a number, and his exactness borrowed none of its ways. The twelve arithmetics of Y.H. 813 stood unchanged behind one unsigned figure beside the first of the new count, and he did not lay twelve old findings across the first of the year to make a row come out even, for a row made even by a hand with no key is made even on paper, and paper is not a payer. \emph{Ilmen 20} stood one morning off, a date and not a day that had earned anything, and he did not bring it under the roof. The first arithmetic of the year was up \place{the clay road} in \person[Berr Clay]'s locked box with the key gone up in a coat pocket that never comes back down, and no key lay in his hand.

The soft-cuffed clerk's figure entered on his inward slate before he looked up on \emph{Ilmen 15} stayed on a place of its own, with a finger between it and \person{Tarn}'s degree toward \place{the out-of-line landing} below \place{the north bridge} and another between it and the face at the yard's edge filed among the older record-first hands and not counted among them. It was a report and not a sighting, the next reaching toward the loan that can hear and not the calling of it. The three nothings stayed three, and he did not lay them across one another and call the joining a rule, for a rule is an account that has found a payer and none stood. The fifth shaping of \lore{the well-saying} sat on its own tray between \person{Hessa Rale}'s eighth record-first hand and the saying of the road from \place{the Cross} to \place{Greybank}, with a finger between every pair and a third between it and the four ownerless directions; \person{Ilva}'s strip of notched birch bark stayed in her sleeve unread, her count in wood and her ninth hand nine. He found the shaping where put and made no sixth of it. \lore{The carried fire}'s fifth was unpaid four hundred and twelve days and \person{Garl Vail}'s name was unspoken the same number, the fire and the man standing side by side and never becoming one. Nothing from the seventeenth had crossed under the roof as sign, lesson, mending, cause, payment or payer, and the not-crossing was kept, not celebrated.

At \place{the hearth} \person{Sella Vail} came before the basket, as she had come at the beginning of every day since the boy was brought under the roof. She told the coals it was \lore{the thaw} in the voice another woman would have used for a child who could not hear her, and turned them with the back of a broken pot. The small intake, the hold and the letting-go went toward no opening, and she figured neither of the two gaps: the one between the intake and the hold, the one between the hold and the going-away. Her finger lay along the top of \place{the loaf shelf} so the charcoal tally on its underside stayed unread; her lips moved on a two-gapped row and figured neither gap. One glance went to the clay jar standing cold under \lore{lead glaze} beyond \place{the pot board}, and the hand shut half over nothing, the little-door burn on the back of her left hand --- two straight sides, a lintel and a threshold --- closing on air, and went back to the pot. \emph{The house will keep it} went to the fire and not to the basket, because a thing said aloud becomes a thing the Court can enter.

She lifted the twice-mended pot thumb-first where the tighter wire crossed the crack, and the hairline of the original break showed between the old-brass staple and the wire, a dark thread with a hand's work on either side of it. Garl was in that hairline and in the salt-stiff coat with its crease at the left shoulder on the peg behind the door and in the hollow wheel-seat nobody was allowed to fill, and not in a story anybody told. Both proverbs went to the pot and the fire and never to a person: \emph{Leave the crack showing. People trust a thing they can see has been handled} and \emph{If it's worth mending it's worth mending twice. The second time is the real one}. Repair was not restoration; a restored thing hides the hand that handled it and a mended thing leaves the hand showing, and a surgeon keeps a graft apart from a cure for the same reason a potter leaves a staple in the light. At \place{the rack end} she lifted a corner of the wool and turned the dressed grain piece by piece with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child, setting each down where it had lain, and took no figure from it and no handful for herself. The wool went back over the grain on \place{the drying racks} and the space stayed as it was, covered and unfigured and not knowing what it cost. Then the pot went down on the board again with its rim two inches beyond the reaching hand, and there was a half-beat at the setting and no look toward the bed. The half-beat was the distance between an intention and an arrest; it cost her nothing and bought the boy nothing, and neither of them called it a gift. \lore{The widow's mark} hung where it hung and was not touched: a morning, a hearth, a shelf, a yard and a well are not openings, and seven openings in seven months are seven and not a rule.

At first light she carried him down the nineteen steps at the half-pace from every open slate, out of \place{the Vail kiln-house} in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill}, \place{Grath}, and the instruction went to the wool, the river wind, the pot, the fire and the banked fire and never to the basket; the basket was not told what the day was and was not told to be still. In \place{the Clay Yard} she went once around \place{the seven walked-around places} with a look and a wide step and no number, once and not twice, and widest around \place{the returning-line place} where a hand had gone in on \emph{Seed 15} and had not come back. The wide step was a keeping and not a sign, and a figure set against it would want a payer standing and none stood. At \place{the yard well} a pail went down on its stone where a pail always stood; a rope settled into its own knot with a rasp as stiff as the week's cold; a loaf went a finger's width along a board and stopped with nobody calling the stopping a figure; barley passed from wall to wall in the breath between two women's voices and was never made into a number. At \place{the Vail corner} a neighbour raised a rake at the ridge, looked at the clean dark line with its finer grey beside it called the thaw, and set the rake down with nothing done and nothing said. \lore{Ash} had come up off that line in the dry cold where no broom had gone in two years. He took the small sounds first because they did not lie and the large ones were mostly weather, and the yard's having gone on without him was neither a lesson nor a mending. She set him under the roof and never in \place{the Debt Loft}.

At \place{the Vail wheel-shed} \person{Jorra Vail} came through the finger's-width door with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past his bent little finger and white in a fist-crease where a hand had opened, and it was not the bone-coloured \lore{slip} of the wheel. Three or more men unnamed had gone low into \place{the back room} before first grey and he gave the house no name for them and did not say the room was cold; the broken ones under the lowest rack stayed entered by nobody. He kept dusty forearms and clean ones on one tray and parted them as clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had been broken nor whether it had been a birth-slate, for a question joined to a cause wants a payer standing and none stood. He spoke to the wheel before any person --- \emph{It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady} --- and told a pot \emph{You'll do} in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either. He knew \lore{the full note} that pot would give three turns before it left his hands, with air under it and a want in it, and withheld the knowing from any master come up from \place{High Kiln} who would call it early and sing a pot before its time; the knowing went on a tray of its own beside the hearings and never among them. When Sella had not finished telling him the fire was low his bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper} ahead of her sentence, took the cold out of the fingertip, moved his tongue a hair's breadth and was left standing so the end of her instruction arrived at the pot and the household straightened around the waiting. One piece of iron had two bites, the holding and the taking, and no Grathic word covered both mercies. He brought the finger out without leaving the extra holding of \emph{Ash 26} a second time, gathered once and not made again, for a second would be a habit and a third a way and both want a payer where none stood. \emph{He's not Vail} stayed sealed as a lid nobody had tried to lift in three years; the promise stayed neither given nor withdrawn.

At \place{the kindling} Tarn kept six pieces in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check and the seventh at the lip beyond it, beside Garl's unforged collector sentence with no name on the man who turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on \lore{the tile debt}. The stack kept its own arithmetic of four, four and a space, and the space was not a fifth and was never filled. The small knife lay at the very edge of its sill. His hand went over blade and sill as a man passes a hand over a tool put back where it belongs and came away with no breath held at the top of the reach --- the coming away was the whole of the checking. He cut nothing, counted nothing, spent nothing, and said to the wood in the tone of a man stating the weather, \emph{Six counted. The next I don't count}. He said it on this morning so that a withholding on the morrow would be a change found where put and not a drift; a man can go quiet by degrees and call the quiet a habit before he knows he has been keeping it. Ash was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line, the pale clip-ring at his left wrist caught and gave back what little light there was, and his chest had been tight since the dark. He was helpful when he was frightened and on this morning he was not frightened, and the household read the quiet of him the way it reads the quiet of Sella. He took the broom only after \place{the flagstones} had been given their instruction, said \emph{Yes, house} to the question and to the thing behind it, agreeing so thoroughly the question had nowhere left to put a foot, and went over the stones without a word and without a turn of the head toward the landing.

The morning went on at the grammar of an ordinary Eighthday and gave nobody a figure to carry. Pail, rope, loaf and barley kept their places and their small orders; the rake went up at the ridge and came down with nothing done; the widow's mark hung where it hung at a shelf, a well and a morning, untouched, and no eighth opening came due on a day that had earned only its eighteenth. The boy went around the seven walked-around places once and not twice, with a look and a wide step and no number, widest at the returning-line place, and the wide step stayed a keeping. He kept the day's grammar as the house kept it, without smoothing a joint in it or setting a seam where there was none, so that if a thing came later it would arrive as a thing and not as a custom. Six hearings of the full note stood beside and never among; the fifth shaping sat on its own tray; the clerk's entered figure, the degree toward the landing and the face at the yard's edge stayed on three places with a finger between every pair; nine record-first hands stood with no tenth and nine stoppings with no tenth. None of them was made into a sixth of anything, a seventh hearing, an eleventh arithmetic or a rule. The keeping at the fold was found where put and stayed a finding, with no third crust, no name and no payer. He took a small clean satisfaction in a field taken as found and left as found, and because it cost nothing he knew it was not peace. Under it, louder, the wanting went on, and the two kinds of knowing stood side by side without either buying the other.

Answer three hundred and ninety-nine came out and was filed on the sixth place: exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the twelve readings, the first of the new count, the printed figure, the fifth shaping, the clerk's entered figure, Tarn's degree, the face at the yard's edge, the widow's mark nor the keeping at the fold. Three honest answers stood there now, three hundred and ninety-seven, three hundred and ninety-eight and three hundred and ninety-nine, beginning what the next days would carry on, and not one of them was a doctrine. Count four hundred and twelve exact, six months and two hundred and thirty-two days told by nobody who would have known what a number counted; the carried fire's fifth unpaid the same number and Garl's name unspoken the same number, the fire and the man standing side by side without becoming one. He did not stand four hundred and twelve up as a threshold, a doctrine or a figure, and he borrowed no payer from the arithmetics gone up the road, for paper is not a payer and a row made even on paper still wants a hand under it before it can be called an account.

Through the finger's-width door \place{the river} kept its note of \lore{water over water}, a low thing over a lower thing under \lore{the winter skin}, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming, found where put on a day that had earned only its eighteenth. The sound came from under the covering and not from the covering; it was not a hearing, not a sign, not a fourth of the three coverings, not a rule and not a payer, and the note ended on its own finishing before any hand came down. The hand outside the wool came up an inch toward the note. The inch was not a reach. He took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole on the note's own finishing under the covering --- neither early nor stayed --- and let it go as the note ended. Then the hand came straight down and laid itself flat against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking.

The crust's corner stood a hair off the wool and the thread was stiff and unwiped; cold had come to cheek, temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rides back and stopped there as at the end of a line written out for it, and the Afterdrop waited past a warming nobody here would perform. Four low pulls breathed below the floor with the fifth unsupplied. The first arithmetic of the year was up the clay road in a locked box and no key lay in his hand. The fifth shaping sat on its own tray with a finger between it and every other kept thing. Nothing had been joined, named, called, paid or ended, and the house went on as a house goes when a pot is set down in its place and the fire kept --- forward, into an afternoon not yet earned and not brought into this one.
