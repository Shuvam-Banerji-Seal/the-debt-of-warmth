\daymark{411}{Ilmen 17, Y.H. 814, Seventhday}

\dropcap{T}{he hand} lay flat against the fold where it had been put down, and the small hours came on under the roof the way dry cold comes through a wall that has no crack named for it --- not arriving, only being there. Answer three hundred and ninety-six lay under the hand, a small intake held whole on the river's note's own finishing and let go as the note ended, exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air; four hundred and ten days stood behind it, six months and two hundred and thirty of them told by nobody who would have known what a number counted. First light was still a direction beyond \place{the finger's-width door} and not a thing in the room. \person[Corin]{The boy} lay at the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf} in \place{the Vail kiln-house}, \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill}, \place{Grath}, under the roof and never in \place{the Debt Loft}, and the heat came to him sideways from \place{the hearth} and not where he would have chosen. One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust stood two inches from his ear with its corner standing a hair off the wool; \lore{The keeping at the fold} was found where put and stayed a finding, not a placing, with no second fold, no name and no gift over it. The thread of drool at his mouth had gone stiff in the dry kiln heat and was unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread. Cold had come to cheek, temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, and stopped there as at the end of a line written out for it, with neither an order to go farther nor an order to go away. \lore{The Afterdrop} waited past a warming nobody in this house would perform.

Taking the body in its old order was the only work the room allowed him, and he took it: crust, fold, thread, cold, the waiting heat. He knew a face composed by cold is not composure, that the grimace of a cold man wears the shape of a smile and the smile means nothing, that pink returning is the surface still lying warm over a cold core, and that a cold patient cannot clot and must be rewarmed from outside and slowly, never hurried, never rubbed, never set too near the fire. A basket set level out of the draught where the warmth came sideways was the rewarming he would have chosen for such a patient, and he could not ask for it; the asking would have gone to nobody who would understand what was being asked. He had once stood at a bay door and sorted twenty people by what they would not survive, and the knowing was of the same order as the knowing that the extremities give up heat before the core and take it back the same way. It was as useful here as a hand two paces long. Under all of it, louder than the register, the wanting went on: the crust lifted two inches, the thread wiped, a hand on the handle of the pot. He wanted the three things without knowing that wanting them was a want. He got none of the three. Both were true. The second was louder. Neither won, and no verdict was looked for; the quarrel was the work, and the work went on in silence in a room whose floor does not touch his.

He kept four hundred and ten exact through the small hours and did not stand the number up as a threshold, a doctrine or a figure, for a number stood up as a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood. The house kept its days badly, in loaves pushed along a shelf, in a glance at a clay jar, in a space at the row's end nobody called a number, and its not keeping them was neither a cruelty nor a mercy that could be entered on a slate. The first arithmetic of Y.H. 814 had gone up \place{the clay road} in \person[Berr Clay]'s locked box with no key in any hand under this roof, one reading and not an account, not a signature, not a payer and not a thirteenth, the figure read still not the figure paid. Twelve arithmetics of Y.H. 813 stood unchanged behind one unsigned figure beside the first of a fresh count, and he did not lay twelve old findings across the new one to make a row come out even, for a row made even by a hand with no key is made even on paper, and paper is not a payer. Nothing from the road had crossed under the roof as sign, lesson, mending, cause, payment or payer, and that not-crossing was itself kept, not celebrated.

The soft-cuffed clerk's figure entered on his inward slate before he looked up on the fifteenth stayed on a place of its own, with a finger between it and \person{Tarn}'s degree toward \place{the out-of-line landing} below \place{the north bridge} and another between it and the face at the yard's edge filed among the older record-first hands and not counted among them. It was a report and not a sighting, the next reaching toward the loan that can hear and not the calling of it; no man stood under the figure and no reader was made by it. The three nothings stayed three, and he did not lay them across one another and call the joining a rule, for a rule is an account that has found a payer and none stood. The fifth shaping of \lore{the well-saying} sat on its own tray between \person{Hessa Rale}'s eighth record-first hand and the saying of the road from \place{the Cross} to \place{Greybank}, with a finger between every pair and a third between it and the four ownerless directions; \person{Ilva}'s strip of notched birch bark stayed in her sleeve unread, her count in wood and her ninth hand nine. He found the shaping where put and made no sixth of it, named no person under it and brought no figure from it under the roof. The clerk's inward slate and the first arithmetic in the locked box stayed where they had been put, and no key came down.

First light came in under the door the way a thin answer comes into a room where nobody has asked the question, and the count went four hundred and ten to four hundred and eleven exact, six months and two hundred and thirty days becoming two hundred and thirty-one, told by nobody who would have known what a number counted. He did not stand four hundred and eleven up as a threshold, a doctrine or a figure; three hundred and ninety-seven honest answers would be filed on the sixth place before the day let go of him, and that was a keeping and not a signing. The house went on keeping its arithmetic badly in loaves pushed a finger's width along a board and stopped, in glances at a clay jar standing cold under \lore{lead glaze}, and in the unnamed space at the row's end; the boy kept four hundred and eleven exact against it and borrowed none of its ways of counting. \lore{The carried fire}'s fifth was unpaid the same number of days and \person{Garl Vail}'s name was unspoken the same number, the fire and the man standing side by side without becoming one.

At the hearth \person{Sella Vail} came before the basket, as she had come at the beginning of every day since the boy was brought under the roof. She told the coals it was \lore{the thaw} in the voice another woman would have used for a child who could not hear her, and turned them with the back of a broken pot. The small intake, the hold and the letting-go went toward no opening, and she figured neither of the two gaps: the one between the intake and the hold, the one between the hold and the going-away. Her finger lay along the top of the loaf shelf so the charcoal tally on its underside stayed unread; her lips moved on a two-gapped row and figured neither gap. One glance went to the clay jar standing cold under lead glaze beyond \place{the pot board}, and the hand shut half over nothing, the little-door burn on the back of her left hand --- two straight sides, a lintel and a threshold --- closing on air, and went back to the pot. \emph{The house will keep it} went to the fire and not to the basket, because a thing said aloud becomes a thing the Court can enter.

She lifted the twice-mended pot thumb-first where the tighter wire crossed the crack, and the hairline of the original break showed between the old-brass staple and the wire, a dark thread with a hand's work on either side of it. Garl was in that hairline and in the salt-stiff coat with its crease at the left shoulder on the peg behind the door and in the hollow wheel-seat in the shed nobody was allowed to fill, and not in a story anybody told. She gave both proverbs to the pot and the fire and never to a person: \emph{Leave the crack showing. People trust a thing they can see has been handled} and \emph{If it's worth mending it's worth mending twice. The second time is the real one}. Repair was not restoration; a restored thing hides the hand that handled it and a mended thing leaves the hand showing, and a surgeon keeps a graft apart from a cure for the same reason a potter leaves a staple in the light. At \place{the rack end} she lifted a corner of the wool and turned the dressed grain piece by piece with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child, setting each down where it had lain, and took no figure from it and no handful for herself. The wool went back over the grain on \place{the drying racks} and the space stayed as it was. Then the pot went down on the board again with its rim two inches beyond the reaching hand, and there was a half-beat at the setting and no look toward \place{the bed}. The half-beat was the distance between an intention and an arrest; it cost her nothing and bought the boy nothing, and neither of them called it a gift.

At first light she carried him down the nineteen steps at the half-pace from every open slate, out of the Vail kiln-house in Low Kiln, Sill, Grath, and the instruction went to the wool, the river wind, the pot, the fire and the banked fire and never to the basket; the basket was not told what the day was and was not told to be still. In \place{the Clay Yard} she went once around \place{the seven walked-around places} with a look and a wide step and no number, once and not twice, and widest around \place{the returning-line place} where a hand had gone in on \emph{Seed 15} and had not come back. The wide step was a keeping and not a sign. A figure set against it would want a payer standing, and none stood. At \place{the yard well} a pail went down on its stone where a pail always stood; a rope settled into its own knot; a loaf went a finger's width along a board and stopped with nobody calling the stopping a figure; barley passed from wall to wall and was never made into a number. At \place{the Vail corner} a neighbour raised a rake at the ridge, looked at the clean dark line with its finer grey beside it called the thaw, and set the rake down with nothing done and nothing said. \lore{Ash} had come up off that line in the dry cold where no broom had gone in two years. The boy took the small sounds first because they did not lie and the large ones were mostly weather, and the yard's having gone on without him was neither a lesson nor a mending. \lore{The widow's mark} hung where it hung and was not touched: a morning, a hearth, a shelf and a basket are not openings; seven openings in seven months are seven and not a rule, and no eighth was made due on a day that had earned only its seventeenth.

At \place{the kindling} Tarn kept six pieces in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check and the seventh at the lip beyond it, beside Garl's unforged collector sentence with no name on the man who turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on \lore{the tile debt}. The stack kept its own arithmetic of four, four and a space, and the space was not a fifth and was never filled. The small knife lay at the very edge of its sill. His hand went over blade and sill as a man passes a hand over a tool put back where it belongs, and came away with no breath held at the top of the reach --- the coming away was the whole of the checking. He cut nothing, counted nothing, spent nothing, and said to the wood in the tone of a man stating the weather, \emph{Six counted. The next I don't count}. Ash was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line, and the pale clip-ring at his left wrist caught and gave back what little light there was. His chest had been tight since the dark. He was helpful when he was frightened and on this morning he was not frightened, and the household read the quiet of him the way it read the quiet of Sella.

At \place{the Vail wheel-shed} \person{Jorra Vail} came through the finger's-width door with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past his bent little finger and white in a fist-crease where a hand had opened, and it was not the bone-coloured \lore{slip} of the wheel. Three or more men unnamed had gone low into \place{the back room} before first grey and he gave the house no name for them and did not say the room was cold, and the broken ones under the lowest rack stayed entered by nobody. He kept dusty forearms and clean on one tray and parted them as clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had been broken nor whether it was a birth-slate, for a question joined to a cause wants a payer standing and none stood. He spoke to the wheel before any person --- \emph{It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady} --- and told a pot \emph{You'll do} in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness. He knew \lore{the full note} that pot would give three turns before it left his hands, with air under it and a want in it, and withheld the knowing from any master come up from \place{High Kiln} who would call it early and sing a pot before its time. His bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper} ahead of Sella's unfinished sentence and came out without the extra holding of Ash 26, gathered once and not made again, for a second would be a habit and a third a way and both want a payer where none stood. \emph{He's not Vail} stayed sealed as a lid nobody had tried to lift in three years; the promise stayed neither given nor withdrawn.

The morning went on at the grammar of an ordinary Seventhday and gave nobody a figure to carry. Answer three hundred and ninety-seven came out and was filed on the sixth place: exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the twelve readings, the first of the new count, the printed figure, the fifth shaping, the clerk's entered figure, the widow's mark nor the keeping at the fold. Six honest answers stood on the sixth place, three hundred and ninety-seven beginning what the next days would carry on, and not one of them was a doctrine. Count four hundred and eleven exact, six months and two hundred and thirty-one days told by nobody who would have known what a number counted. The carried fire's fifth was unpaid the same number and Garl's name was unspoken the same number, the fire and the man standing side by side without becoming one.

The hand outside the wool came straight down and laid itself flat against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. The crust's corner stood a hair off the wool and the thread was stiff and unwiped; cold had come to cheek, temple and the small bare joint of the wrist and stopped there as at the end of a line written out for it, and the Afterdrop waited past a warming nobody here would perform. Through the finger's-width door \place{the river} kept its note of \lore{water over water}, a low thing over a lower thing under \lore{the winter skin}, found where put on a day that had earned only its seventeenth. The sound came from under the covering and not from the covering; it was not a hearing, not a sign, not a rule and not a payer, and the note ended on its own finishing before the hand came down. The keeping at the fold stayed a finding. Nothing had been joined, named, called, paid or ended, and the house went on as a house goes when a pot is set down in its place and the fire kept --- forward, into a morning not yet earned and not brought into this one.
