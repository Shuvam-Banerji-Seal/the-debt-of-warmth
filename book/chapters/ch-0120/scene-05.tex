\daymark{413}{Ilmen 19, Y.H. 814, Ninthday}

The small hours came on under the roof the way dry cold comes through a wall that has no crack named for it --- not arriving, only being there --- and \place{the towel} came out at the head of \place{the bed} as instruments come out when a case is done and before the next is called, rough from the wash and smelling of tallow and low smoke. \person[Corin]{The boy} lay at the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf} in \place{the Vail kiln-house}, \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill}, \place{Grath}, under the roof and never in \place{the Debt Loft}, and the heat came to him sideways from \place{the hearth} and not where he would have chosen. One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust, gone harder through another dry night, stood two inches from his ear with its corner standing a hair off the wool; \lore{The keeping at the fold} was found where put and stayed a finding, not a placing, with no second fold, no name, no gift, no payment and no payer. The thread of drool at his mouth was stiff in the dry kiln heat and unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread. Cold had come to cheek, temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, and stopped there as at the end of a line written out for it, with neither an order to go farther nor an order to go away. \lore{The Afterdrop} waited past a warming nobody in this house would perform.

He took the body in the old order because there was no other work the room allowed him: crust, fold, thread, cold, the waiting heat. The crust had set through the dry night into something nearer old horn than bread, and a child would have turned his face from it; he did not turn, having never been asked. The fold lay where a hand had put it and where every morning since had found it, and finding was not placing, for a habit wants a hand that placed twice and a way wants a third, and both want a payer standing where none stood. He knew what the look of him meant and what it did not. A face composed by cold is not composure; the grimace of a cold man wears the shape of a smile, and the smile means nothing. Pink returning is the surface still lying warm over a cold core, and a cold patient cannot clot and must be rewarmed from the outside, slowly, never hurried, never rubbed, never set too near the fire. A basket set level out of the draught where the warmth came sideways was the careful rewarming he would have chosen and could not ask for; the asking would have gone to nobody who would understand what was being asked. He had once stood at a bay door and sorted twenty people by what they would not survive, and that knowing stood side by side with the knowing of the extremities, which give up heat before the core and take it back the same way, and neither bought the other.

Under all of it, louder than the register, the wanting went on: the crust lifted two inches, the thread wiped, a hand on the handle of the pot. He wanted the three things without knowing that wanting them was a want, and he got none of the three. Both were true. The second was louder. Neither won, and no verdict was looked for; the quarrel was the work, and the work went on in silence in a room whose floor does not touch his.

Four hundred and thirteen days stood behind the hand laid flat on the fold, six months and two hundred and thirty-three of them told by nobody who would have known what a number counted. He did not stand the number up as a threshold, a doctrine or a figure. The house kept its days badly, in loaves pushed a finger's width and stopped, in glances at a cold clay jar, in the unnamed space at the row's end where a thing had stood and did not stand now, and his exactness borrowed none of its ways and taught it none. Twelve arithmetics of Y.H. 813 stood unchanged behind one unsigned figure beside the first of a fresh count; he did not lay the twelve across the new one to make a row come out even, for paper is not a payer and a row made even on paper still wants a hand under it before it can be called an account. The first arithmetic of Y.H. 814 was up \place{the clay road} in \person{Berr Clay}'s locked box with the key gone up in a coat pocket that never came back down, and no key lay under any roof and none in his hand. The printed figure stood at one in twelve, unchallenged; the figure read was still not the figure paid, and no signature stood on the space between the two. One reading was one and not an account; a reset was not a signing; the first of a fresh count was not a thirteenth. \emph{Ilmen 20} stood one morning off, a date and not a day that had earned anything, and he did not bring it under the roof.

\person{Sella Vail} came to \place{the hearth} before she came to the basket, as she had come at the beginning of every day since the boy was brought under the roof. She told the coals it was \lore{the thaw} in the voice another woman would have used for a child who could not hear her, and turned them with the back of a broken pot. The small intake, the hold and the letting-go went toward no opening, and she figured neither of the two gaps: the one between the intake and the hold, the one between the hold and the going-away. Her finger lay along the top of the loaf shelf so the charcoal tally on its underside stayed unread; her lips moved on a two-gapped row and figured neither gap. One glance went to the clay jar standing cold under \lore{lead glaze} beyond \place{the pot board}, and the hand shut half over nothing, the little-door burn on the back of her left hand --- two straight sides, a lintel and a threshold --- closing on air, and went back to the pot. \emph{The house will keep it} went to the fire and not to the basket, because a thing said aloud becomes a thing the Court can enter.

She lifted the twice-mended pot thumb-first where the tighter wire crossed the crack, and the hairline of the original break showed between the old-brass staple and the wire, a dark thread with a hand's work on either side of it. Both proverbs went to the pot and the fire and never to a person: \emph{Leave the crack showing. People trust a thing they can see has been handled} and \emph{If it's worth mending it's worth mending twice. The second time is the real one}. Repair was not restoration; a restored thing hides the hand that handled it, and a mended thing leaves the hand showing, and a surgeon keeps a graft apart from a cure for the same reason a potter leaves a staple in the light. At \place{the rack end} she lifted a corner of the wool and turned the dressed grain piece by piece with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child, setting each down where it had lain, and took no figure from it and no handful for herself. The wool went back over the grain on \place{the drying racks} and the space stayed as it was, covered and unfigured and not knowing what it cost. Then the pot went down on the board again with its rim two inches beyond the reaching hand, and there was a half-beat at the setting and no look toward the child. The half-beat was the distance between an intention and an arrest; it cost her nothing and bought the boy nothing, and neither of them called it a gift. \lore{The widow's mark} hung where it hung and was not touched: a morning, a hearth, a shelf, a pot and a carrying are not openings, and seven openings in seven months are seven and not a rule, and no eighth came due on a day that had earned only its nineteenth.

At first light she carried him down the nineteen steps from \place{the Clay Yard} into the kiln-house at the half-pace from every open slate, and the instruction went to the wool, the river wind, the pot, the fire and the banked fire and never to the basket. The basket was not told what the day was and was not told to be still. At \place{the yard well} a pail went down on its stone where a pail always stood, a rope settled into its own knot with a rasp as stiff as the week's cold, a loaf went a finger's width along a board and stopped with nobody calling the stopping a figure, and barley passed from wall to wall in the breath between two women's voices and was never made into a number. She went once around \place{the seven walked-around places} with a look and a wide step and no number, once and not twice, and widest around \place{the returning-line place} where a hand had gone in on \emph{Seed 15} and had not come back. The wide step was a keeping and not a sign, and a figure set against it would want a payer standing and none stood. Beside it \place{the trough} stood dark and bare, and the gone pan kept its shallow round print on a place of its own. At \place{the Vail corner} a neighbour raised a rake at the ridge, looked at the clean dark line and the finer grey beside it that the yard called the thaw, and set the rake down with nothing done and nothing said. \lore{Ash} had come up off that line in the dry cold where no broom had gone in two years, and the sour tang of it lay under the river wind. Out on \place{the worked strips}, \place{the field's empty place} held the shape of a thing taken out, and he did not lay that shape across the loaf's stopping or the left-in-place ridge; a finger stood between them. She set him under the roof and never in the Debt Loft, put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face, and added no second fold. The keeping was found where put and stayed a finding, with no third crust, no name, no gift and no payer. The thread at his mouth was stiff in the dry kiln heat and unwiped.

He took the yard by its small sounds first, because the small sounds do not lie and the large ones are mostly weather, and the yard's having gone on without him was neither a lesson nor a mending. At \place{the kindling} \person{Tarn} kept six pieces in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check and the seventh at the lip beyond it, beside \person{Garl Vail}'s unforged collector sentence with no name on the man who turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on \lore{the tile debt}. The stack kept its own arithmetic of four, four and a space, and the space was not a fifth and had never been filled. The small knife lay at the very edge of its sill. His hand went over blade and sill as a man passes a hand over a tool put back where it belongs, and came away, and the coming away was the whole of the checking. No breath was held at the top of the reach. He said to the wood in the tone of a man stating the weather, \emph{Six counted. The next I don't count}. He had not said it on the eighteenth, and that not-saying was found where put, a change kept on a place of its own and not made a habit on a day that had earned only its nineteenth; a second silence would be a custom and a third a way, and both want a payer where none stood. The sixth was still six, the seventh still at the lip, the \emph{Seed 16} breath still unspent, the loan still uncalled at the very end of a term already ended. He took the broom only after \place{the flagstones} had been given their instruction and said \emph{Yes, house} to the question and to the thing behind it, agreeing so thoroughly that the question had nowhere left to put a foot, and went over the stones without a word and without a turn of the head toward \place{the out-of-line landing} below \place{the north bridge}. His chest had been tight since the dark; Ash was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line, and the pale clip-ring at his left wrist caught and gave back what little light there was. His stroke went over the shallow bright patch without the half-check. He was helpful when he was frightened, and on this morning he was not frightened; the household read the quiet of him the way it read the quiet of Sella, as a weather signal and not a summons.

The row question stood at five askings with one shaping and no witness and no sixth. \lore{The burned-slate word} stood at five, the shut-room tellings at two, and the four ownerless directions --- the Ash 5 joining, the Ash 17, the Ash 23 and the Stone 23 shape --- stood where put, and the fifth shaping of \lore{the well-saying} sat on its own tray between \person{Hessa Rale}'s eighth record-first hand up the road and the saying of the road from \place{the Cross} to \place{Greybank}, with a finger between every pair and a third between it and the four directions; Hessa's narrow slate stayed unread up the road. \person{Ilva}'s short strip of notched birch bark stayed in her sleeve unread with her ninth hand nine, and no figure came off the wood. None of these repeated, and none was joined to another or to the strip, to Berr's thumb, to the soft-cuffed clerk's inward slate at the landing, to the charcoal on the underside of the loaf shelf or to the covered grain at the rack end; a row made even out of separate keepings would want a payer put under every part of it, and none stood. The clerk stayed set below the north bridge with the figure entered before a face was looked at kept on its own place, reported and not witnessed, and the face at the yard's edge was filed among the older record-first hands and not counted among them. No loan was called, no reader made, no tenth hand counted and no witness stood.

Under \place{the Vail wheel-shed} edge \person{Jorra Vail} kept his own silence, which was an older one and not the same. He came through \place{the finger's-width door} with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past his bent little finger and white in a fist-crease where a hand had opened, and it was not the bone-coloured \lore{slip} of the wheel. He parted dusty forearms from clean as clot from vessel, spoke to the wheel before any person and told a pot \emph{You'll do} in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either; he knew the \lore{full note} that pot would give three turns ahead, with air under it and a want in it, and kept the knowing where no master come up from \place{High Kiln} could call it early and sing a pot before its time. His bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper} ahead of an unfinished sentence and came out without leaving the extra holding of \emph{Ash 26} a second time. \emph{He's not Vail} stayed sealed as a lid nobody had tried to lift in three years, and the promise stayed neither given nor withdrawn. The boy kept that silence on its own place too, with a finger between it and the silence at the kindling: two quiet men under one roof were two quiet men and not a custom, and no argument between them had found a foot.

The morning went on at the grammar of an ordinary Ninthday and gave nobody a figure to carry. Pail, rope, loaf and barley kept their places and their small orders; the rake went up at the ridge and came down with nothing done; the widow's mark hung where it hung at a shelf, a well and a morning, untouched, and no eighth opening came due on a day that had earned only its nineteenth. Garl stayed where Garl stays, in objects and not in a story: in the salt-stiff coat with its crease at the left shoulder on the peg behind the door, in the hollow wheel-seat in the shed keeping its twenty-year hollow that nobody was allowed to fill, in the clean dark line at the Vail corner with its finer grey beside it called the thaw, unswept and thickened in the dry cold with the sour tang of Ash where no broom had gone in two years, in the hairline of the pot between staple and wire, and in the unforged collector sentence lying beside the seventh notch at the kindling. The debt against a firing of tiles went on circling without a name, neither paid nor settled nor done with and worse for being uncalled. His name had gone unspoken under this roof four hundred and thirteen days, and \lore{the carried fire}'s fifth had gone unpaid the same number, the fire and the man standing side by side without becoming one. Six hearings of the full note stood beside and never among with the seventh place empty and the flat knock from \place{the Drawing house} outside on its own ground; nine record-first hands stood with no tenth and nine stoppings with no tenth. Four rooms kept the same gap with a finger between every pair and no Court room brought under the roof as a fifth likeness. The heard bell kept its square beside the told, four, four and a gap, its gap not folded into the fire's four and a gap, \place{the boards}' four and four and a thumb-width knot, or the twelve arithmetics behind one unsigned figure beside the first of a fresh count --- three counts in three places staying three, because agreeing any two would want a payer put under both and none stood. He kept the day's grammar as the house kept it, without smoothing a joint in it or setting a seam where there was none, so that if a thing came later it would arrive as a thing and not as a custom. He took a small clean satisfaction in a field taken as found and left as found, and because it cost nothing he knew it was not peace. Under it, louder, the wanting went on, and the two kinds of knowing stood side by side without either buying the other.

Answer four hundred and one came out and was filed on the sixth place: exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the twelve readings, the first of the new count, the printed figure, the fifth shaping, the clerk's entered figure, Tarn's degree, the face at the yard's edge, Jorra's ungiven promise, the withheld sentence of the eighteenth, the widow's mark nor the keeping at the fold. Six honest answers stood there now, three hundred and ninety-seven, three hundred and ninety-eight, three hundred and ninety-nine, four hundred and four hundred and one, beginning what the next days would carry on, and not one of them was a doctrine. Count four hundred and thirteen exact, six months and two hundred and thirty-three days told by nobody who would have known what a number counted; the carried fire's fifth unpaid the same number and Garl's name unspoken the same number, the fire and the man standing side by side without becoming one. He did not stand four hundred and thirteen up as a threshold, a doctrine or a figure, and he borrowed no payer from the arithmetics gone up the road, for paper is not a payer and a row made even on paper still wants a hand under it before it can be called an account. Cold had come to cheek, temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back and stopped there as at the end of a line written out for it; the Afterdrop waited past a warming nobody in this house would perform.

Through the finger's-width door \place{the river} kept its note of \lore{water over water}, a low thing over a lower thing under \lore{the winter skin}, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming, found where put on a day that had earned only its nineteenth. The sound came from under the covering and not from the covering; it was not a hearing, not a sign, not a fourth of \lore{the three coverings}, not a rule and not a payer, and the note ended on its own finishing before any hand came down. The hand outside the wool came up an inch toward the note. The inch was not a reach. He took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole on the note's own finishing under the covering --- neither early nor stayed --- and let it go as the note ended. Then the hand came straight down and laid itself flat against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking.

Nothing had been joined, named, called, paid or ended, and the house went on as a house goes when a pot is set down in its place and the fire kept --- forward, into a morning not yet earned and not brought into this one. The crust's corner stood a hair off the wool and the thread was stiff and unwiped; four low pulls breathed below the floor with the fifth unsupplied; the first arithmetic of the year was up the clay road in a locked box and no key lay in his hand; the fifth shaping sat on its own tray with a finger between it and every other kept thing. Count four hundred and thirteen exact, and no number stood up as a threshold.
