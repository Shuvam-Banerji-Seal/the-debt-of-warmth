## Chronicle

Bone came on without a boundary drawn, and the boy beneath the Vail roof came into his three hundred and sixth, seventh and eighth mornings with nobody to tell him what they counted. The count went from three hundred and five to three hundred and eight; the fire's fifth stayed unpaid with its payer unnamed and Garl Vail's name unspoken. Each morning the body was inventoried in its old order --- crust, frozen thread, cold, the afterdrop waiting --- and the surgeon's knowledge that a cold patient cannot clot bought nothing against the infant's want of the crust.

Sella Vail went to the fire before the basket, told the coals it was the thaw, turned them with a broken pot, said *The house will keep it*, and kept the charcoal unread and the widow's mark untouched, for no hour here was an opening. She lifted the twice-mended pot with her thumb first where the wire crossed the crack and said both proverbs to the pot and fire. At the rack end she turned the dressed grain beneath the wool with no figure; three readings had gone over it in Berr Clay's locked box, the figure read still not the figure paid.

The yard went wide around its seven walked-around places with a look and a step and no number, called the gone frost and the skinned trough weather, and answered a man carrying a hare with the weather. At the well Ilva drew with the pail against her knee, kept her notched strip in her sleeve, and answered the question put rather than the one behind it: she kept the count, not the account. Tarn kept six where the house could check and a seventh at the lip, and stopped his ear-sentence at the word that would call the loan; Jorra parted dusty forearms from clean, withheld the note a pot would give three turns ahead, and set his bent finger in the cold damper ahead of Sella's sentence.

At the going of the light on the eighth, Tarn's broom checked half a beat behind that finger on the flagstones and went on toward the road without a word or a turn of the head --- the old half-check returned, changed, after a hundred and thirty-six days. Neither man named the other's silence. The boy filed it beside three older held changes, four on four places, and forbade them to stand up as a rule, for a rule is an account that has found a payer and none stood; he did not make it again on the ninth. Six times the hand took a small intake from the next room and let it go whole on the river's note as a restraint, not a payment; the answers from the hundred and eighty-seventh to the hundred and ninety-second were worth nothing at two inches of air. At the close the file lay open on the towel with every gap left gap, and Bone went on without a boundary drawn.
