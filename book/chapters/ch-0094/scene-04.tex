The afternoon went on from \place{the boards} without a boundary drawn upon it, the light going thin over \place{the Clay Yard} the way a pot goes slack when it is lifted from the fire and set where the heat can leave it in its own time. Nothing that had been said over the boards crossed down to the well as sign, lesson, mending, cause or payment. \person[Corin]{The boy} kept the joining said there on a place of its own, with a finger between it and everything else, and went on being carried where the household carried him. \person{Sella Vail} set the basket down at the edge of the well work where no slate lay, one hand under the base and one at the fold, and gave the instruction to the wool and the river wind and never to the basket. “Keep it off his ears,” she said. “It's the river wind.” Then she went about her work, and the day went about hers, and the two did not touch.

\person{Ilva} drew at \place{the yard well} with the pail against her knee, the rope stiff in the Ash cold and giving its small dry rasp as the bucket came up, the water in it dark and with a skin at the edge that nobody named. Clay had dried pale in the crescents of her nails and the finer grey dust of the racks was worked into the seam of one thumb, which she did not scrub out. Her hands knew the weight of a pail and the edge of a wet board and had never been soft, and went on knowing these things without reporting on them to anybody. People noticed the pail first and the sleeve not at all, which was the order she preferred. The boy took the yard as a surgeon takes a field before the light, by the small sounds first because the small sounds do not lie and the large ones are mostly weather: the dry rasp of the rope, the knock of a pot set down wrong and then set down right, a broom going around something it did not touch.

A woman with a pot waiting her turn asked what the firings of Ash had taken. Ilva's hand went toward her sleeve and stayed. The short strip of notched birch bark lay where it lay, pale where a thumb had rubbed the notches and dark in the uncut grain between; it was not taken out, read, copied, counted or joined to the open slate lying a pace away on the stone lip, to the charcoal beneath \place{the loaf shelf}, to the cord, to the knot-string or to any saying that had arrived nowhere, and no figure came off the wood. Her eyes went to the asker and her mouth opened after both, with a half-beat between the looking and the word. “Ask the pot what it cost. I keep the count, not the account,” she said. She put the flat of her hand once more on the sleeve and went back to the pail. Nobody turned twice.

The boy took the order of her reaching apart as he took everything apart, by what was there and by what was not. Hand to the record before eyes to the person; eyes before mouth; the arrest at the sleeve measured as the distance between intention and arrest, a small stopping that made no sound and asked no question. He filed it on a place of its own as the ninth record-first hand in the district, with a finger between it and \person{Hessa Rale}'s eighth, and filed no tenth. He made no figure of the open slate's nearness, did not make Ilva a reader of her own keeping, which she was not and had never wanted to be, and joined her to none. A count kept in wood was still a keeping; it stood where no slate could take a figure off it, and the ninth stayed nine.

After that, on the same breath as the price of barley and the weather, a voice with nobody behind it gave the old well-saying a fourth shape. The weather answered first, as the yard always answered what had no owner standing behind it, and the price of barley came after it; the voice said that the runner who had once carried the figure had come back down \place{the clay road} with the writing of the slate turned toward herself, and then went off up \place{the terrace} while the saying stood where it stood and the work went on. There was no room named, no hand at a slate, no name for the person, no figure spoken, no confession and no cause, and nobody standing anywhere to be asked. Nobody turned twice.

The boy took the voice apart as he had taken the other apart, by what was there and by what was not. What was there: a runner, a road, a writing turned inward, said low to the boards and the well-roof and not into any basket. What was not: an owner, a witness in a doorway, a room, a hand at a slate, a date, a figure, a payer, a name under it. He set the fourth shaping on a place of its own beside the first saying and the three earlier shapings, with a finger between every pair, and forbade the four to stand up and call themselves a witness, for a witness stands somewhere and can be asked and none stood. He did not join the voice to Ilva drawing a pace away and did not make her the speaker; did not lay it across the strip in her sleeve, \person{Berr Clay}'s twice-wiped thumb, the soft-cuffed clerk's inward slate or the charcoal under the shelf; did not lay it across the joining said over the boards that morning; did not make Hessa a reader and did not pay the claim. Hessa was not the woman who had spoken the first saying on Frost 14, not the voice of the second on Frost 19, not the voice of the third on Frost 23 and not the voice of this one; she was a runner and not a reader, and the road to \place{Greybank} was a direction and not a confession. Four shapings were still four and no fifth came. The seed grew without being spent.

The hunts and repairs of the yard went on as directions judged by the carrying arm and the weather and never as figures. A man come down from \place{Cold Acre} with a hare across his shoulders shifted the weight when a woman with a pot asked what the carrying had cost, told her to ask the weather, which had carried the snow and could carry the price, and went off toward the clay road with nobody weighing the hare and nobody opening a slate; another with a cracked jar in a sling of rope asked what the mending of the month would want and was told to ask the pot, and the pot, being a pot, said nothing. The boy read each as a chart left blank where the number should stand and entered neither.

The three coverings stayed weather entered nowhere, with a finger between every pair. The white that had gone off the well's north lip on Bone 3 while the yard looked elsewhere was not brought back as a sign; the skin on \place{the trough} broke under a pail's knock and closed again and was called the weather; and the silt-clouded plate of ice stood on \place[the flat stone by the well]{the flat stone} out of the path of feet, lifted there on Frost 9 and left where no foot went. Each was a covering over a depth and the same covering over another depth and a piece of the depth lifted and set where nobody would step, and none of the three was made a fourth likeness or a cause.

At \place{the rack end} the dressed grain lay turned under wool away from the hearth, going on giving up its water in the dry kiln heat. She lifted a corner with a lighter touch than the morning had used and turned it once and gave no figure, and her fingers went in and came out and did not close, so that no handful was lifted. The handful let fall on Bone 22 stayed one object on a place of its own; the empty fingers went beside it as a keeping and not as a second object, and the finger went between them. A number read over covered grain in a month that did not know its cost had not made the grain known, and the boy did not make it known now.

At \place{the kindling} \person{Tarn} kept six where the house could check and the seventh at the lip beside \person{Garl Vail}'s unforged collector sentence, with the small knife at the very edge of its sill where it could be checked and where a hand could also slip it off. He told the wood, “Six counted. The next I don't count,” and brought the sentence about an ear under this roof being worth more than the debt that held it all the way to the word that would call the loan and stopped there, a remembered ninth stopping with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end, the loan uncalled and the Seed 16 breath unspent at the top of the reach. His chest had been tight since the dark. \lore{Ash} was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line and no scrubbing lifted it; the pale clip-ring at his left wrist held what little light there was and gave it back; his stroke went over the shallow bright patch on \place{the flagstones} without the half-check, and the place where it had stopped kept its place. The warm loft stone lay cold in its unmarked pot and was not reconciled by not trying, which was not forgiveness. His thumb's resting on the uncut seventh on Bone 26 was checked as one object and not made again on the fifth of Ash: not a tenth stopping, not the seventh year given, not a payment, not a fifth held change, not a hearing, not a mending and not a rule, with no payer standing. “Yes, house,” he said to the weather and kept the late because a late thaw puts off the paying.

At the shed's edge \person{Jorra Vail} came through the finger's-width door off \place{the Vail wheel-shed} with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bend of his little finger and white in a fist-crease where a hand had opened, and it was not the bone-coloured \lore{slip} of the wheel. Three or more men had gone low into \place{the back room} before first grey and he gave the house no name for them and did not say the room was cold. He parted dusty forearms from clean as a surgeon parts clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had been broken nor whether it was a birth-slate, and spoke to the wheel before he spoke to any person: “It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady.” To a pot he said, “You'll do,” in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either. He knew the \lore{full note} that pot would give three turns before it left his hands, with air under it and a want in it, and kept the knowing where a kiln-master come up from \place{High Kiln} would not be told it and make a song of it. When Sella had not finished telling him the fire was low his bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper} ahead of her sentence, took the cold out of the fingertip, moved the tongue a hair's breadth and left it standing, so that the end of her instruction arrived at the pot. One piece of iron had two bites in it, the holding and the taking, and there was no \lore{Grathic} word for both mercies. “He's not Vail” stayed sealed as a lid nobody had tried to lift in three years, and the promise of the boy's name to the men who burn slates stayed neither given nor withdrawn. The burned slate stood at five with the joining said over the boards held on a place of its own and no sixth telling, and the shut-room tellings stood at two and did not become three.

Garl Vail stayed in objects with his name unspoken three hundred and thirty-four days and his tile debt neither paid nor settled nor done with and worse for being uncalled. The salt-stiff coat kept his broader shape on its peg behind the door with the crease at the left shoulder; the wheel-seat in the shed kept its twenty-year lean and its hollow on that side and nobody was allowed to fill it; the ridge that had been his ash-marker was now a clean dark line with a new finer hairline beside it called \lore{the thaw}, and neither had been swept. His proverbs went to the pot and the fire and never to a person --- “Leave the crack showing. People trust a thing they can see has been handled” and “If it's worth mending it's worth mending twice. The second time is the real one” --- and had improved in the keeping and were very likely not as good as the man had said them. No lesson was made of any of it.

The going of the light came without a bell and without a Cross. Sella carried him up at the half-pace she kept from every open slate, went wide around the seven walked-around places with a look and a wide step and no number, and set him on \place{the bed} under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof and never in \place{the Debt Loft}. She put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face, let her hand rest the half-beat longer than the setting needed before it went away, and left the thread where it lay. There was a sound from above and behind, a foot on the loft stair going up and then still, and no interior was entered and no name given to the sound. The instruction went to the fire, the wool, the pot and the river wind and never to the basket. “The house will keep it,” she said where another woman would have said I. \lore{The widow's mark} hung where it hung at her throat and was not touched, because a going of the light and a bed are not openings; the fired kiln at \place{the kiln mouth} stood cold behind the wall after Ash 2 and nobody went out to look at what it had done. She went to \place{the hearth} before she lifted a cup and told the coals it was the thaw, and turned them with the back of a broken pot, the fire answering four low pulls with the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer unnamed three hundred and thirty-four days. Her finger lay on top of the loaf shelf's front edge so the charcoal tally stayed unread; her lips moved along a row standing with two gaps and figured neither gap; her eye went once to the clay jar standing cold under \lore{lead glaze} and her hand shut half over nothing, the little-door burn closing on air. From \place{the pot board} she lifted the twice-mended pot with her thumb first where the wire crossed the crack, said both proverbs to the pot and the fire and never to a person, and left the hairline of the original break showing between the old-brass staple and the tighter wire. At the rack end of the day's work the dressed grain lay under wool with a lighter touch and no figure and no handful, her fingers going in and coming out without closing; the pot's rim went down two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat at the setting and no look toward the bed.

Under the floor \lore{the carried fire} kept its four and its gap. Overhead the boards kept four nails on the near board, four on the next and a thumb-width knot where a fifth might have been driven and was not. Through the finger's-width door \lore{water over water} went low over lower, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming, and was never numbered among the hearings. Three counts in three places. None rose into another, because agreeing any two would have wanted a payer put under both and none stood to any. Six hearings of the full note stood beside and never among with the seventh place empty, the sixth the small flat knock out of \person{Dessa Silt} as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go and never among the sources; an empty place kept empty was a finding and a finding filled to make a row come out even was a finding forged. The heard bell stayed in its square beside the told bell of \lore{the Long Night}, four, four and a gap with nobody crossing himself, and was not fetched in to make a fourth count or a seventh hearing. The told figure stood beside the paid figure with no signature on the space. The file lay open on \place{the towel} at the head of the bed with every object on its own place and every gap left gap: the staying at the inch, the early letting-go, the handful let fall, the thumb's resting on the uncut seventh, the sixth opening at the kiln mouth, the burned slate at five, the two shut-room tellings, the two bites of the damper, the ninth hand kept in a sleeve and the eighth kept on its own place, the joining said over the boards, and the fourth shaping at the well. Nothing had moved. Six arithmetics of the year rode locked up the clay road in Berr Clay's box toward High Kiln, one in twelve unchallenged, the figure read still not the figure paid, six readings six and not one account. The finding that six are six and not one account stayed on a place of its own with no payer under it and was not laid across the house's not-doings, the covered grain, the five held objects, the handful, the thumb's resting, the two small objects of the hand or his own gaps; the edges came near to touching and the finger went between.

In the small hours, with the river's note low and going lower under the finger's-width door, the hand outside the wool went up an inch toward the sound. It took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole, let the intake go on the same low note, and came straight down and laid itself back against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. The note had done before the hand came down, so the letting-go was neither early again nor stayed, and the hand did not stay; it was the note's own finishing that ended the interval and not any decision of the hand, and that was the whole of the honesty in it. The inch was not a reach and the intake was not a breath kept for warmth; the interval was not a stopping of the river and the note was not his. He filed it as answer two hundred and forty-four: exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment, borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the six arithmetics, the bell's gap, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the stayed object, the early letting-go, the sixth opening, the burned slate at five, the joining said over the boards, the fourth shaping nor his own kept gaps. Two hundred and forty-four honest answers stood on the sixth place, each an object and not a power, and neither the number nor the small hand-movings that had made it stood up and called themselves a doctrine, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood. The surgeon took his small clean satisfaction in a field left as found; the infant underneath took none and went on wanting the crust lifted the two inches, the thread wiped and a hand on the pot handle brought within reach, both true, the second louder, neither winning, the two registers quarrelling without verdict in the dark.

Nothing on the fifth of Ash had been opened, joined by a standing hand, named, called, paid or ended. The joining said over the boards and the fourth shaping at the well were two sayings nobody owned and stood on separate places with a finger between, and neither arrived anywhere. Three hundred and thirty-four stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill}, \place{Grath}; six months and one hundred and fifty-four days were told by nobody; the carried fire's fifth was unpaid the same number of days with its payer unnamed; and the name of the man the salt-stiff coat kept was unspoken the same number. Ash kept its middle days without a boundary drawn and neither habit of naming the months was set right; the keeping of the number was the only honest work there had been to do, bought nothing at two inches of air, was not for sale, and went on because attention is the one thing no institution can tax. The silt-clouded plate of ice stood on its flat stone out of the path of feet; the file lay open on the towel with every gap left gap; the hand lay back against the fold on the two hundred and forty-fourth answer; Ilva's strip stayed in her sleeve with no figure off the wood; and the fourth shaping stood on its own place beside the saying, with the runner not yet seen coming down any road. Ash 5 went on.
