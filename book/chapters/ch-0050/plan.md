## The chapter's one meaningful change

Across the three days the boy asks his hand four times and gets four answers: down at once (201 morning), held for the small intake only (201 small hours), down before the counted breath finished (202 morning), held for the small intake only (203 morning). Then, in the small hours of Day 203, the hand goes up an inch toward the river's note of water over water, stays through the whole of one of the breaths Sella counts at her fire in the room that does not touch his, **does not come down with her breath**, keeps the small height through the silence after her count has ended and comes down on the river's next low note. It is a coordination with a *second* rhythm — the one through the door, which nobody counts and which was never given to him — and not a longer reach, not a stronger hand, not a hearing and not a power. Water over water remains a fifth low thing never counted among the hearings, and the hand remains a physical first filed on its sixth place beside the three held changes, the loft scrape and the pale line, never laid across them and never stood up as a sign. He still cannot close the two inches, lift the crust or speak a number. The river goes on without noticing.

This change is advanced and **not paid**: it settles no long thread and nobody under any roof sees it. The market does not get to read the inch. What presses through the whole chapter is Tarn's seventh year at the lip of the sill — nearer its end than it has ever stood, six counted where the house can check and the seventh where it cannot, the seed-breath unspent, nine stoppings still nine, the ear-sentence on the other side of his teeth, the loan uncalled. Forward, not closed.

## Scene 1 — “The Last Tend Opens Quiet”

- **Day and time.** Day 201, Ember 22, Secondday. Small hours under the roof, then before first grey and into the morning.
- **Place.** Under the Vail kiln-house roof: the bed, the hearth, the loaf shelf's low angle; then the Clay Yard, the yard well's edge and the clay road.
- **Present.** Corin/Dan; Sella; a neighbour at the threshold (walk-on). Three or four men go low into the back room before first grey offstage; Jorra does not appear in this scene. No Berr, Hessa, clerk, green woman or foundling.
- **Beats, in order.**
  1. **No recap.** Open in the small hours with the ordinary, not with a reading of the close of Chapter 48 or 49. The hand is down against the fold. First smell hot clay under tallow and wet wool with the sour mineral breath of the unswept corner; first sound the kiln drawing in fours with a gap, not a bell. The last tend has opened and nobody has drawn a boundary under anything.
  2. **The count carried.** Two hundred exact, complete and unspent, spoken by nobody; the boy two hundred and one days old, six months and twenty-one days, and nobody says so (T071/T072). He has stopped expecting to be told, which is not the same as not wanting it. The one leathery crust lies two inches from his ear with its empty place unnamed and no third; the thread of drool frozen and unwiped because nobody has been given an instruction about the thread; cold stopped at cheek, temple and the bare wrist-joint where the wool rides back, the afterdrop waiting on a warming nobody in this house will perform.
  3. **Three counts.** Carried fire four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have been, payer unnamed for two hundred and one days (T088); river's note of water over water through the finger's-width door, a fifth low thing never counted among the hearings; boards overhead four nails, four and a thumb-width knot. Three counts in three places that do not become one, because a man who wanted them to agree would have to put a payer under all three and none stands to any (T061/T082).
  4. **Sella's morning.** Instruction to the wool, the fire, the pot, the broom, the running water and the river wind and never to the basket; *the house*; half-pace from every open slate; one glance at the clay jar cold under its glaze with the hand starting toward it and shutting half over nothing without a touch; coal-black hair pushed back with the burn-wrist so the grey streak shows pale; gruel from the house's own dish, spoon on her sleeve and his mouth unwiped; pot handle two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat longer at the setting; no look toward the bed. Widow's mark untouched — a morning and a shelf are not openings, and there has been no opening since before the kiln on Ember 1 (T058/T079).
  5. **The cost-question.** The neighbour at the threshold, dry mud-scurf on her hem, asks what the firing will want now the last tend is in. Sella sends it to the pot and the weather; it goes away level, nothing priced, refused or entered (T077). The figure in Berr's locked box stays offstage, printed one in twelve unchallenged, true subtraction hidden (held T015/T041/T047).
  6. **The men.** Three or four men unnamed go low into the finger's-width-open back room before first grey; the house gives them no name and does not say the room is cold. No third telling of the shut counting room (T035/T031/T065/T095 held).
  7. **The asking.** Back under the roof the hand goes up an inch toward the note through the door and comes down at once, with no holding in it. The asking is not always answered. Filed on a sixth place of its own beside the three held changes, the loft scrape and the pale line: not a hearing, not a seventh, not a stopping, not a payment, not one of the three held changes, not a key, not a power, not a rule; no payer named, nothing stood up out of it (T061/T082). He still cannot close the two inches, lift the crust or speak a number.
- **Emotional turn.** From the body's old humiliations — carried, fed by another's dish, wiped by sleeve, temperature stopping at the wrist — to a small unbought fact: the hand was asked and declined to answer. No triumph and no failure in the refusal; it is an object in the file worth exactly nothing.
- **Sensory anchors.** Hot clay under tallow; wet wool; sour Ash breath of the corner; kiln's four-and-a-gap as the first sound, not a bell; leathery crust and its empty place; frozen thread; cold at cheek, temple, wrist; four low pulls and a missing fifth; water over water; four nails, four and a thumb-width knot; dry mud-scurf; clay jar under its glaze; burn-wrist and grey streak; spoon on sleeve, not mouth; half-beat at the setting.
- **Threads:** T071/T072, T088, T061/T082, T058/T079, T077, T035/T031/T065/T095 held, T015/T041/T047 held, T004.
- **Foreshadowing planted / paid off.** The last tend opens without boundary, planting the quiet close of Ember in Chapters 51–52; the knife's absence in this scene plants its return in Scene 2 and the seventh-year pressure; the back-room men plant the burned-slate direction owed in Chapter 51; the pot handle two inches beyond the hand plants every “cannot close two inches” to come. Nothing paid.
- **Exact last image.** The hand comes down at once against the fold; two hundred and one stands exact in the wool, complete and unspent, and the last tend has opened as an ordinary morning with no boundary drawn.

## Scene 2 — “What the Afternoon Kept”

- **Day and time.** Day 201, Ember 22, Secondday. Afternoon into the small hours.
- **Place.** The loaf shelf's low angle, the hearth, the kindling, the wheel-shed edge, the clay road at the going of the light, the bed.
- **Present.** Corin/Dan; Sella; Tarn; Jorra. The men in the back room are offstage before first grey of this day.
- **Beats, in order.**
  1. The afternoon thins without ending; heat goes off the high air first and stays longest under the shelf, behind the pot, in the fold. The underside of the shelf makes a roof that stops at the edge of his reach, and the heat comes down that roof in a slow slant and stops at the same place, as if it too had been given an instruction and come to the end of it.
  2. Sella at the shelf, back to the basket: lips along a row with two unnamed gaps (old space at the row's end, palm's breadth from the night of Seed 6), no figure; one glance at the clay jar and the hand shutting half over nothing; finger kept on top of the front edge so the charcoal stays unread, uncounted, uncopied, and the Day 166 held beat stays where it was left (T100).
  3. At the hearth: told the thaw, coals turned with the back of a broken pot, *The house will keep it*; stands with the back of the pot counting under her breath toward no opening — small intake, hold, letting go; pot rim set out with handle two inches beyond the reaching hand and a half-beat longer at the setting; no look toward the bed (T058/T079). Mark untouched; an afternoon is not an opening.
  4. At the kindling: knife at the edge of its sill, six counted where the house can check and the seventh at the lip where it cannot, Garl's unforged collector sentence beside it. Tarn takes the broom only after it is instructed, tells the wood *Six counted. The next I don't count*, says *Yes, house* to an early thaw and a late while wanting the late because it puts off the paying, sweeps the collector's road without turning; no collector comes. Chest tight since the dark, Ash worked into knuckle creases in the colour of the clean dark line, pale clip-ring; ear-sentence all the way to the word that would call the loan and stops with no figure and no name; Seed 16 breath drawn and not spent; nine stoppings nine objects with no tenth before the seventh year is earned, the seventh year nearer its end than ever and still not given; loan uncalled; stroke over the flagstones past the shallow bright patch without the half-check (T036/T076).
  5. At the wheel-shed: Jorra gives the men no name and does not say the room was cold; fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bent finger and under the nails, not bone-coloured slip; dusty and clean forearms on one tray; asks neither which slate was broken nor whether it was a birth-slate; speaks to the wheel before anyone (*It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady*), tells a pot *You'll do*, knows its full note three turns ahead and withholds it from a man on a wrong wind whose saying about a pot singing early stays a saying about a pot and whose pot is not lost (T035/T031, T061/T082). When Sella's voice goes low and unfinished his bent finger goes into the cold damper and holds until the sentence reaches the pot, then the tongue moves a hair's breadth and the finger is left standing — a remembered repetition, not a change made again, not paired with Day 161, 166 or 171; one iron with two bites, no Grathic word for both mercies, joined to no kiln, entered in no slate (T062). *He's not Vail* sealed; promise ungiven.
  6. At the going of the light: carried home at the half-pace, instruction to the wool and the river wind and never to the basket; woodsmoke in long low bars before her, river wind behind with the taste of water over water, the two passing without taking each other's colour. The arm that has carried him two hundred and one mornings keeps the height of the handle, give of the shoulder, turn of the wrist at a step's end — a record in muscle that remembers weight and not cost. Goes wide around the pan's gone round ring, the bare dark trough called weather, the rack space with its broken birth-slates, the field's empty place and the place the carriers crossed, each with a look and a wide step and no number (T009/T013/T092, T098). Under the roof: bed and not Debt Loft, fold right without a look at his face, thread left frozen, two rooms that do not touch.
  7. Small hours: the file turned out as instruments on a towel, exact, complete and worth nothing. Six hearings beside and never among, sixth out of a person, no seventh invented; three near-full answers advanced and unpaid; told bell beside heard, told figure beside paid; three held changes on three places, loft scrape on a fourth and pale line on a fifth; nine stoppings no tenth; crust's gap, shelf's two gaps, pan's gone ring and field's empty place on trays that do not touch; charcoal unread, stone cold in its loft pot, Seed 16 breath unspent, loan uncalled, cost of the man carried out entered nowhere (T061/T082, T006, T064, T060/T086/T097, T100). The hand goes up and holds for the small intake only and comes down before the letting go — an object on its sixth place, not promoted.
- **Emotional turn.** From the household's ordinary keeping, which is accounts never entered, to his private file, the same kind of keeping with no hand to spend it. He refuses to let six places stand up as one rule; that refusal is the only power he has and is not a power at all.
- **Sensory anchors.** Heat slanting down the shelf's underside; lips moving along two bare boards; back of a broken pot on coals; burn-wrist and pale grey streak; knife's worn thumb-mark and seventh at the lip; Ash in knuckle creases; pale clip-ring; cold iron taking the cold out of a fingertip; woodsmoke in long low bars; river wind with the taste of water; frozen thread; back-of-the-pot count arriving nowhere.
- **Threads:** T058/T079, T100, T036/T076, T035/T031/T062/T065/T095, T009/T013/T092/T098, T061/T082, T006, T064, T071/T072, T088, T004.
- **Foreshadowing planted / paid off.** The knife at the lip and the ninth stopping plant the seventh-year pressure of Chapters 51–53; the withheld full note and the wrong-wind saying plant the small edge owed in Chapter 54; the two shut-room tellings with no third plant the burned-slate direction of Chapter 51; the loft scrape, pale line and record-in-muscle feed the far-off convergence. - **Sensory anchors.** Hot clay under tallow; kiln's four-and-a-gap; clay jar under glaze; spoon on sleeve; half-beat at setting; knife's worn thumb-mark and the seventh at the lip; Ash in knuckles; pale clip-ring; fine grey slate dust ground small as meal; river note through the door; frozen thread.
- **Threads:** T071/T072, T088, T061/T082, T058/T079, T077, T036/T076, T035/T031/T065/T095, T004.
- **Foreshadowing planted / paid off.** The seventh at the lip and the unmade tenth plant the close of the seventh year and the later calling; the men in the back room and the two tellings plant the burned-slate direction of Chapter 51; the small-intake holding plants the different answer of Scene 6 without making a progression. Nothing paid.
- **Exact last image.** Two hundred and three stands exact in the wool, complete and unspent, spoken by nobody under any roof; the hand is down and the count goes forward into the afternoon.

## Scene 6 — “The Hand and the River”

- **Day and time.** Day 203, Ember 24, Fourthday. Afternoon into the small hours — chapter close.
- **Place.** The loaf shelf's low angle, the hearth, the kindling, the wheel-shed edge, the clay road at the going of the light, the bed.
- **Present.** Corin/Dan; Sella; Tarn; Jorra. The wrong-wind man is a walk-on with a saying, no name and no lesson made.
- **Beats, in order.**
  1. The afternoon thins without ending; heat goes off the high air first and stays longest under the shelf, behind the pot, in the fold where the one brown-edged crust lies leathery two inches from the ear with its empty place unnamed and no third; thread of drool frozen and unwiped; cold stopped at cheek, temple and bare wrist joint, the afterdrop waiting.
  2. Sella stands with her back to the basket: lips along the row with two unnamed gaps, no figure; one glance at the clay jar and the hand shutting half over nothing; finger from beneath the shelf so the charcoal stays unread (T100); tells the fire the thaw, turns the coals with the back of a broken pot, says *The house will keep it*, stands with the back of the pot counting under her breath toward no opening — small intake, hold, letting go — pushes coal-black hair back with the burn-wrist so the grey streak shows pale, sets the pot rim out with its handle two inches beyond the reaching hand, lets her hand rest a half-beat longer at the setting, and does not look toward the bed (T058/T079). Widow's mark untouched at a dusk, for a dusk is not an opening.
  3. At the kindling: knife at the very edge of its sill, six counted in the worn thumb-mark where the house can check and the seventh at the lip where it cannot, Garl's unforged collector sentence beside it. Tarn takes the broom only after it is instructed, tells the wood *Six counted. The next I don't count*, says *Yes, house* to an early thaw and a late while wanting the late because it puts off the paying, brings the ear-sentence all the way to the word that would call the loan and stops there with no figure and no name, draws the breath he should have spent and does not spend it. Nine stoppings stay nine objects with no tenth before the seventh year is earned; the seventh year stands nearer its end than ever and has not given, the word on the other side of his teeth; Seed 16 breath unspent; loan uncalled; stroke over the flagstones past the shallow bright patch without the half-check; collector's road swept without turning, no collector comes. Ash worked into knuckles, pale clip-ring holding what light there is, chest tight since the dark (T036/T076).
  4. At the wheel-shed Jorra comes through with fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bent finger and under the nails, not bone-coloured slip; keeps dusty and clean forearms on one tray, asks neither which slate was broken nor whether it was a birth-slate; speaks to the wheel before anyone (*It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady*), tells a pot *You'll do*, knows its full note three turns ahead and withholds it from a man come up on a wrong wind whose saying about a pot singing early stays a saying about a pot and whose pot is not lost (T035/T031, T061/T082). When Sella's voice goes low and unfinished (*The fire's low. It wants---*), his bent finger goes into the cold iron of the damper ahead of his hand and holds until the sentence reaches *feeding* and the end is given to the pot; then the tongue moves a hair's breadth and the finger is left standing. Remembered repetition, not a change made again, not paired with the Day 161, 166 or 171 fractions; one iron with two bites, the holding and the taking, no Grathic word for both, joined to no kiln and entered in no slate; two shut-room tellings at two, no third (T062, T065/T095). *He's not Vail* sealed; promise of the boy's name to the men who burn slates neither given nor withdrawn.
  5. At the going of the light she carries him home up the clay road at the half-pace, instruction to the wool and the river wind and never to the basket; woodsmoke before her in long low bars, river wind behind with the taste of water over water, the two passing without taking each other's colour; the arm that has carried him two hundred and three mornings keeps the height of the handle, give of the shoulder, turn of the wrist at a step's end, a record in muscle that remembers weight and not cost. She goes wide around the pan's gone round ring, the bare dark trough whose water is called weather, the rack space with its broken birth-slates, the field's empty place and the place the carriers crossed, each with a look and a wide step and no number (T009/T013/T092, T098).
  6. Under the roof she sets him on his bed and not in the Debt Loft, puts the fold right without looking at his face and leaves the thread frozen; fire answers four low pulls with the silence where the fifth should have been, payer unnamed for two hundred and three days; river keeps its fifth low note through the finger's-width door; boards keep four nails, four and a thumb-width knot — three counts in three places that do not become one (T088, T061/T082). The two rooms do not touch.
  7. Small hours: the file turned out as instruments on a towel — no towel, no instruments and no case, but the order of the laying the whole of the discipline. Six hearings beside and never among with the sixth out of a person and no seventh invented to fill the empty place; three near-full answers advanced and unpaid; told bell beside heard and told figure beside paid, the second reading gone up the road in Berr Clay's locked box with the true subtraction inside and still not the figure paid, Hessa Rale's slate unread at Greybank with the writing to her sleeve and the soft-cuffed clerk still set at the landing for an uncalled measure; three held changes on three places with the loft scrape on a fourth and the pale line on a fifth; nine stoppings with no tenth; crust's gap, shelf's two gaps, pan's gone ring and field's empty place on trays that do not touch, no third gap, no fourth likeness, no finger drawn; charcoal unread, stone cold in its loft pot, Seed 16 breath unspent, loan uncalled, cost of the man carried out entered nowhere; sixth green still a colour and foundling still a shape (T061/T082, T006, T064, T060/T086/T097, T100, T078, T015/T041/T047, T080, T090, T050/T051, T033/T034).
  8. **The chapter's one meaningful change.** He puts the question to his hand. It goes up an inch toward the note of water over water through the door, stays up through the small intake, the hold and the letting go of one of the breaths Sella counts at her fire in the room that does not touch his, and this time **does not come down with her breath**: it keeps the small height through the little silence after her count has ended and comes down on the river's next low note. A coordination with a second rhythm, the one through the door, which nobody counts and which was never given to him. Not a longer reach, not a stronger hand, not a hearing (water over water is still a fifth low thing never counted among the hearings), not a seventh, not a stopping, not a payment, not one of the three held changes, not a key, not a power, not a rule; no payer named and nothing stood up out of it to call itself a sign. Filed on the sixth place beside the others and never laid across them (T061/T082, advanced and not paid). He still cannot close the two inches to the pot handle, lift the crust two inches to his mouth or speak a number. The body humiliates him and moves by an inch in the same motion, and both are true; the inch is worth exactly what an inch is worth in a district that keeps its accounts in loaves and glances. The river goes on without noticing that an inch rode it for the length of one note.
- **Emotional turn.** From the file laid out as instruments on a towel, exact, complete and worthless, to an answer that arrives wearing a rhythm nobody gave him. Not triumph: the hand is still an inch from a hand that cannot reach a crust, and the new answer is a difference in kind, not a gain in strength. The river does not stop for it, which is the whole of its value.
- **Sensory anchors.** Heat slanting down the shelf's underside; lips along two bare boards; back of a broken pot on coals; burn-wrist and grey streak; knife at the lip of its sill; thumb-worn mark with six; Ash in knuckle creases; pale clip-ring; cold iron taking the cold out of a fingertip; woodsmoke in long low bars; river wind behind with water over water; frozen thread; four pulls and a missing fifth; four nails, four and a knot; the small silence after a count ends before the river's next low note.
- **Threads:** T058/T079, T100, T036/T076, T035/T031/T062/T065/T095, T009/T013/T092/T098, T088, T061/T082, T006, T064, T060/T086/T097, T078, T015/T041/T047, T080, T090, T050/T051, T033/T034, T071/T072, T004.
- **Foreshadowing planted / paid off.** The seventh year at the lip and the nine unadded stoppings plant the household's own keeping of Garl in Chapter 51 and the later calling of the loan; the wrong-wind saying plants the small non-fatal edge owed in Chapter 54; the burned-slate direction and the two tellings plant Chapter 51; the hand riding an uncounted rhythm plants the far-off recognition that attention is the one thing no institution can tax, without making it a hearing, a sign or a power. Nothing paid.
- **Exact last image.** The hand comes down against the fold on the river's note; the river goes on without noticing. Two hundred and three stands exact in the dark, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody. The fire's fifth has had no payer for two hundred and three days; the mark has been touched once, before the kiln on Ember 1, and at no other hour; the stone lies cold in its loft pot; the charcoal lies unread; the Seed 16 breath is unspent; the loan that can hear is uncalled; the seventh year stands nearer its end than ever and has not given. Nothing in the keeping has been joined, named, opened, called, spent or ended, and nothing is now. The fold takes the hand without a word and the count goes forward.

## How the final moment pulls into Chapter 51

The hand's new answer happens in the small hours of Day 203 under a roof no market can enter, and nobody sees it. It goes into the file on its sixth place beside the three held changes, the loft scrape and the pale line, and must not stand up among them as a sign, a hearing or a rule. What carries forward is not the inch but the pressure the chapter has kept on Tarn: the seventh year nearer its end than ever, the knife at the very lip of its sill with six counted where the house can check and the seventh where it cannot, Garl's unforged collector sentence beside it, the ear-sentence on the other side of his teeth, the Seed 16 breath unspent, nine stoppings still nine with no tenth, the loan uncalled and no collector come. Chapter 51 opens on Day 204, Ember 25, Fifthday, with the kiln month's last working week closing in the household's own keeping of Garl — the salt-stiff coat, the hollow seat, the clean dark line, the proverb gone to the pot and the uncalled debt — no boundary drawn, no lesson made, no collector called and no ending. The hand will be asked again as the days ask, still worth an inch. Forward, not closed.
