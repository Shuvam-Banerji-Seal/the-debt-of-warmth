The afternoon thinned without ending. Heat went off the high air first, where there was nothing to hold it, and came down the wall by degrees, and left the top face of the shelf, and stayed longest in the low places --- under the shelf's underside, behind the pot, in the fold of wool where the boy lay at the angle the board makes into a roof. That roof stopped at the edge of his reach. The heat came down its underside in a slow slant, thin as a finger laid along a board, and stopped at the same edge and lay there, going no further, as if it too had been given an instruction and had come to the end of it. He had felt a line hold that way in a body under his own hands, warmth climbing to a landmark and stopping at the landmark, the edge exact and not argued with. He did not make a finding of it. A finding was a thing a man could enter, and nothing under this roof was entered.

\scenebreak

\person{Sella Vail} came to \place{the loaf shelf} with the basket set low at the angle and stood with her back to it, so that the row was in front of her and the boy was behind her and neither was given the other's place. She moved her lips along the row without sound, the way a woman reads a line she knows by heart and does not need to see, and the row stood with two unnamed gaps: the old space at the row's end, and the palm's breadth that had come in on the night of Seed 6. She gave nobody a figure. Her eye went once to the clay jar cold under its glaze on the shelf by the wall, and her hand started toward it and shut half over nothing without a touch, the little-door burn closing on air the way it closes on whatever comes within reach of it, and then the hand went back to the row. She kept her finger on top of the shelf's front edge, plain and unhurried, where any woman in the district could see it lying; whatever lay beneath the board lay unread, uncounted and uncopied, and the beat that had stayed under it on Day 166 stayed where it had been left, not repeated and not paired with the other two changes he kept. She pushed a strand of coal-black hair back with the wrist that carried the burn, so that the grey streak at her temple showed pale in the low light, and did not discuss the streak or the winter it had come in. The household straightened around the quiet of her as cloth straightens when a weight is lifted. Nobody had been told what the grey was, and nobody asked.

At \place{the hearth} she told the fire it was \lore{the thaw}, and turned the coals with the back of a broken pot, and said \emph{The house will keep it} where another woman would have said I. The instruction went to the fire and not to the boy, as all instructions in this house went to the wool, the pot, the broom, the running water and the river wind, and never to the basket; the basket was a pot that had been filled and set down, and a pot set down with no instruction given to it is a pot left where it stands. Then she stood with the back of the pot in her hand and counted under her breath toward no opening --- the small intake, the hold, the letting go --- and the count arrived nowhere, as it had arrived nowhere on every other afternoon of the boy's life, and the house kept its warmth without once saying who had paid for it. She set the pot rim out with its handle two inches beyond the reaching hand and let her hand rest a half-beat longer at the setting than the setting needed, and did not look toward the bed. She never looked toward the bed at the setting. The two inches were not a distance anybody under this roof had been asked to measure, and the hand lying outside the wool on the fold did not close them. \lore{The widow's mark} hung on its cord at her throat where it had hung since the first firing, touched once before the kiln on Ember 1, held one breath, let go, and at no shelf, hearth, well, rack, basket, yard, afternoon or dusk since. An afternoon was not an opening and a shelf was not an opening; there had been no opening since before the kiln on Ember 1, and nobody under any roof had praised what the one opening had mended or seen what it had broken.

\scenebreak

At \place{the kindling} the small knife lay at the very edge of its sill where a man could put a thumb and find it where it should be. Six years stood in the worn thumb-mark, in the place where the house could check; the seventh stood at the lip, where no thumb of the house had gone. \person{Garl Vail}'s unforged collector sentence lay beside it --- the one about a man sent for a collector who turned back at the frozen ford --- kept where the seventh year was kept and entered in nothing. \person[Tarn Vail]{Tarn} came up with the broom and took it only after the broom had been given its instruction, as a man takes up a tool already named. He told the wood what he told it at every going and coming of the light. \emph{Six counted. The next I don't count.} \lore{Ash} was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line, and no scrubbing lifted it; the pale clip-ring at his left wrist held what light there was and gave it back. His chest had been tight since the dark, and at the top of the next reach the breath from Seed 16 stood unspent, with its small interval showing where a breath should have gone in and had not.

He said \emph{Yes, house} to those who wanted the thaw early and to those who wanted it late, agreed so entirely that no argument could find a foot, and went on wanting the late one because a late thaw puts off the paying, though the paying had stood in the ruts since the water went. His stroke went over \place{the flagstones} and past the shallow bright patch without the half-check, and the stones kept the stopped place where the movement had once waited and was not given it again. Then he swept the road a house sends a man down when it wants a collector, over and over, without once turning along it; no collector came up the road and no collector went down it. At the hearth, with the coals banked and the knife set back where it could be checked, the sentence about an ear under this roof being worth more than the debt that holds it came up to the word that would call the loan and stood there with no figure and no name. He did not say it. He drew the breath he should have spent, did not spend it, did not call the loan, and stood no tenth stopping up before the seventh year was earned. The seventh year stood nearer its end than it had ever stood and had not given; nine stoppings stood as nine objects on their tray, with no tenth among them and no payer named to any, and the loan that could hear was still uncalled.

\scenebreak

Through the finger's-width door of \place{the Vail wheel-shed} the wheel went on turning, and \person{Jorra Vail} was at it with his bent little finger leading. Three or four men had gone low into \place{the back room} before first grey and the house gave them no name and did not say the room was cold. Two tellings of a counting room shut in the war stood where they had always stood, one with a date and one with a date, and no third came now to join them to the door off the shed; the boy kept the two apart and supplied nothing from anything he had heard under this roof. He came through with fine grey slate dust ground small as meal into both forearms past the bend of the little finger and under the nails, not the bone-coloured \lore{slip} of the wheel; he parted dust from slip the way a surgeon parts clot from vessel and kept dusty forearms and clean ones on one tray, asking neither which slate had been broken nor whether it was a birth-slate. The broken birth-slates lay under \place[the drying racks]{the lowest rack} where the yard went around them and entered them into nothing. He spoke to the wheel before he spoke to anyone. \emph{It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady.} He told a pot \emph{You'll do} in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either, and knew the \lore{full note} the pot would give three turns before it left his hands. A man come up on a wrong wind said that a pot singing before its time generally sings itself out early; Jorra kept the knowing back and gave him nothing, and the man took the silence for the silence of a man at work and went off up the yard with his saying, which stayed a saying about a pot and was not made true as a lesson. No pot was lost.

When Sella's voice went low and unfinished at the fire --- \emph{The fire's low. It wants---} --- his bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper} ahead of his hand and held there until the sentence reached \emph{feeding} and the end was given to the pot. Then the tongue moved a hair's breadth and the finger was left standing in the iron, cold taking the cold out of the fingertip. It was a remembered repetition, the same holding as on other afternoons, not a change made again, not paired with the other three places; nobody spoke of the waiting and no payer was named to it. He read the iron as one piece with two bites, the holding and the taking, and there was no word in \lore{Grathic} that would carry both mercies in one mouthful; it was joined to no kiln and entered in no slate, a clamp a clamp and not a doctrine. He did not look toward the basket or the bed, did not ask about the ear while Tarn did not ask about the room, and \emph{he's not Vail} stayed sealed as tightly as a lid nobody had tried to lift in three years. The promise of the boy's name to the men who burn slates was neither given nor withdrawn.

\scenebreak

At the going of the light she carried him home up \place{the clay road} at the half-pace, with the instruction going to the wool and the river wind and never to the basket: keep it off his ears, it's the river wind. Woodsmoke went before her in long low bars with the last of the sun in it; the river wind came behind with the taste of \lore{water over water}, and the two passed without taking each other's colour. The arm that had carried him two hundred and one mornings kept the height of the handle, the give of the shoulder, the small turn of the wrist at a step's end --- a record in muscle that remembered weight and not cost, and would not have known a figure if one had been set in front of it. She went wide around the pan's gone round ring, the bare dark \place[the trough]{trough} whose water was called weather, the rack space with its broken birth-slates, the field's empty place and the place the carriers crossed, each with a look and a wide step and no number. The yard made room for these absences the way a body makes room for an old injury, by going around it and never testing it.

Under the roof she set him on \place{the bed} and not in \place{the Debt Loft}, put the fold right without looking at his face and left the thread at his mouth frozen and unwiped. The two rooms did not touch. Through the finger's-width door the river kept its fifth low note; under the floor \lore{the carried fire} gave four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have been, its payer without a name for two hundred and one days; overhead the boards kept four nails, four, and a knot the width of a thumb. Three counts in three places that did not become one, because a man who wanted them to agree would have had to put a payer under all three and no payer stood to any of them.

\scenebreak

In the small hours he turned out the file the way a man lays instruments on a towel --- no towel, no instruments and no case, but the order of the laying the whole of the discipline --- and found it exact, complete and worth nothing. Six hearings of the full note stood beside and never among: five from four sources, and the sixth, the small flat knock out of a person as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go and never among the sources. No seventh had come in two hundred and one days and none was invented to fill the empty place, for an empty place kept empty is a finding and a finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged. Three near-full answers stood advanced and unpaid. The told bell lay beside the heard, four, four and a gap with nobody crossing himself; the told figure lay beside the paid, one in twelve read twice over a basket and still not the figure paid, the box gone up the road locked with the true subtraction inside and the runner's slate unread at Greybank with the writing to her sleeve. There was no hand in this house to spend any of it. A reading of a body at two paces is exact, complete and worth nothing without a hand to carry it; he had made such readings for years and the finding had never once walked out of a room by itself. The file lay under a roof the market could not enter, and whatever was kept there stayed kept.

Three held changes stood on three places with the small dry scrape from the loft on a fourth and the pale line at the gum of the man carried out on a fifth. The crust's gap, the shelf's two gaps, the pan's gone round ring and the field's empty place lay on trays that did not touch --- no third gap, no fourth likeness made a cause, no finger of his drawing the line between them. Nine stoppings stood and no tenth; the Seed 16 breath was unspent and the loan uncalled; the cost of the man carried out was entered nowhere and no cause named for him in Grathic. The charcoal under the shelf's front edge lay unread, and the warm stone lay cold and unreconciled in its unmarked pot in the loft, not forgiving by not trying. He kept the six places where they lay and would not let them stand up together and call themselves a rule, because a rule is an account that has found a payer and no payer stands to any of them. That refusal was the only power he had, and was not a power at all.

He put the question to his hand. The hand outside the wool went up an inch toward the note of water over water through the door, and held for the length of the small intake only, and came down before the letting go. It was an object on its sixth place, not promoted: not a hearing, not a seventh, not a stopping, not a payment, not one of the three held changes, not a key, not a power and not a rule; no payer named to it and nothing stood up out of it to call itself a sign. He still could not close the two inches to the pot handle, lift his chin the two inches to the crust or speak a number that anybody under any roof had been asked to believe. The fold took the hand without a word. The river went on with its note and did not stop for the inch, and two hundred and one stood exact in the wool, complete and unspent, spoken by nobody, and the count went forward into Ember 23.
