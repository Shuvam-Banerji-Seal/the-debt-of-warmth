## Chronicle

Three mornings came up over Low Kiln as the last tend ran on. The boy came into his two hundred and first day, then the second, then the third, and nobody said a number. The carried fire missed its fifth for two hundred and three days; the river kept water over water; the boards kept four nails, four and a thumb-width knot. Three counts in three places, none becoming the others, for agreeing them would have wanted a payer and none stood to any.

Sella Vail gave the instruction to the wool, fire, pot, broom, running water and river wind, never the basket, and said the house where another woman would say I. She fed him from her own dish with the spoon on her sleeve, set the pot handle two inches beyond his reach, moved her lips along a two-gapped row, kept her finger from beneath the charcoal, and counted under her breath toward no opening. Cost-questions went to the pot and the weather and went away level, nothing priced or entered. The widow's mark went untouched; there had been no opening since Ember 1. She carried him home around the pan's gone ring with a wide step and no number.

Tarn told the kindling six counted and the next he did not count, the knife at the lip of its sill and Garl's unforged sentence beside it, and brought the ear-sentence to the word that would call the loan and stopped with no figure and no name. Nine stoppings stood with no tenth before the seventh year was earned, nearer its end than ever; the Seed 16 breath was unspent and the loan uncalled. Jorra parted dust from slip, withheld a note from a wrong wind whose saying stayed a saying and cost no pot, and held his bent finger in the damper until the sentence reached the pot --- remembered, not made again. Men went low into the back room before first grey and got no name; the two tellings stayed two.

Five times he questioned his hand: down at once, one small intake, let go before the breath finished, a whole breath down with the breath, one small intake again. Then in the small hours of Ember 24 the hand went up an inch toward the river's note, stayed through a whole breath Sella counted at her fire in the room that did not touch his --- and did not come down with her breath. It kept the height through the silence after her count ended and came down on the river's next low note: a coordination with a rhythm nobody counted and never gave to him. He filed it on its sixth place, no hearing, seventh, stopping, payment, key, power or rule, no payer named. He still could not close two inches or lift the crust. Two hundred and three stood exact in the wool, unspent, spoken by nobody; the river went on without noticing that an inch had ridden one of its low notes.
