  % come down with the counted breath. It holds through the stop and the
  % little silence after, and comes down on the river's NEXT low note ---
  % a coordination with a second rhythm, one nobody counts and never gave
  % him. Not a reach, not a hearing, filed on a sixth place, worth nothing.

  % past both edges and not stopping for the hand that follows it
