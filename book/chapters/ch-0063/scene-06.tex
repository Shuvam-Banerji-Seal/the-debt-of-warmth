Afternoon thinned after that without ending, the way a fire thins toward the bank when nobody has yet given it the word. Smoke went where the district's months go, without a stroke drawn under it and without anybody setting one name right against the other; a man would have had to stand at the edge of two days and say which was which, and no man stood there. Smoke 10 was seven mornings off, the first Account-day and market of the second firing, and it stood beyond the close of this day and was not brought into a day that had not earned it.

Under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof in \place{Low Kiln} the heat came down out of the light and settled where it was put: behind the pot, under \place{the loaf shelf}'s low angle, into the fold at the ear. One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from \person[Corin]{the boy}'s ear. The empty place beside it kept its name to itself. No third came to lie down there and make the row come out even, because a kindness with a figure on it is the beginning of an account and this house had learned to give without entering. The thread at his mouth had frozen in the dry heat and was unwiped, and would stay unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. Cold had stopped at cheek, at temple, at the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, as though it had come to the end of a line written out for it and had neither an order to go further nor an order to leave. The afterdrop waited on the far side of all three, past a warming nobody in this house would perform. He knew the afterdrop when he saw it. He wanted the crust. Both were true, and the second was louder now in the long thin afternoon, because a child's hunger keeps no hours and the register goes on being exact while the body in which it sits goes on being hungry. The surgeon took the inventory and entered it nowhere.

Behind the wall \lore{the carried fire} drew four low pulls and stopped where the fifth should have stood, its payer without a name for two hundred and forty-two days. Through the finger's-width door the river kept its note of \lore{water over water}, a fifth low thing never counted among the hearings. Overhead \place{the boards} kept four nails on the near board, four on the next, and a thumb-width knot where the fifth nail should have stood. Three counts in three places. A man who wanted them to agree would have to put a payer under all three, and no payer stood to any of them, so he kept them on three trays and let the trays lie where they lay.

The sweet edge of \lore{lead glaze} that the wrong wind had brought off \place{High Kiln} was gone out on the wind that brought it, and one of the three shapings of the pot saying stood again as a saying: that a pot had gone quiet on every row come the thaw. He let it stand where it stood. The sayings were three and they stayed three, and nothing in the going of the edge made a fourth of them; a fourth would have been a likeness standing up and asking to be believed, which is an account with no payer, and no pot had been lost.

\person{Sella Vail} went to the fire before the basket, as she had gone on Smoke 1 and on Smoke 2 and went now on Smoke 3. She told the fire it was \lore{the thaw}, in the tone of a woman naming a thing that would go when it went, turned the coals with the back of a broken pot, and said \emph{The house will keep it} to the fire and not to any ear under this roof. The instruction went out to the wool and the pot and the broom and the running water and the river wind, and never to the basket, because the basket had not been given an instruction and had no need of one. She stood a moment with the back of the pot in her hand, counting something under her breath toward no opening --- small intake, hold, letting-go --- and pushed a strand of coal-black hair back with the wrist that carried the burn, so that the grey streak at her temple showed pale off the coals. The burn across the back of her left hand had the shape of a little door, lintel and threshold and two straight sides, and it closed on nothing now as it closed on nothing when she carried a cup.

\lore{the widow's mark} hung where it hung at her throat, a thumb-length of lead-glazed clay with the Vail kiln scratched into it. It had been touched once before the kiln on Smoke 1, held one breath, let go, and not touched since: not at a shelf, not at a well, not at a rack, not on the clay road, not at a basket, not at an afternoon or a dusk on any of the three days. The hand goes to the mark before the kiln and at no other hour, and the rule held as it had held on the first opening of Tallow and the first opening of Ember, and held now at the going out of Smoke. The one opening lay behind the household and was not looked at again, not as a sign, not as a lesson, not as a payment. Nobody under the roof said what it had mended and nobody made a cause of it.

The dressed grain lay under its fold of wool near \place{the hearth}, covered and unfigured. In the morning she had turned it for damp and spread it thin and gathered it back by the handful and set the fold flat; it had changed state and gained no figure, and now it lay as it lay, counted in glances and handfuls and by the heft of an arm that knew the weight and not the cost. A muscle remembers a load and not what the load was worth; he had spent his life learning to tell the two apart and had no word here in which to enter either. No slate came near the grain and no figure was laid across any of the absences.

\person{Garl Vail} was kept in objects and not in a story: the salt-stiff coat on its peg with the crease at the left shoulder, the wheel-seat in the shed with its twenty-year hollow, the clean dark line where the \lore{Ash} ridge had turned the corner and the finer grey beside it called the thaw, both left unswept. His name had not been spoken under this roof for two hundred and forty-two days. No collector had come up or gone down \place{the clay road} for the tile debt on Smoke 1, 2 or 3, and none had gone down; the keeping of him was maintenance, and nobody had made a story out of it and nobody would. His proverb went to the pot and the fire and never to the basket --- leave the crack showing; people trust a thing they can see has been handled --- and went still to a pot with no crack in it, over a corner where the repair had been rubbed out. A line left where it is is still a line kept.

She came across \place{the flagstones} from the hearth and stood at the loaf shelf with her back to the basket, moving her lips along the two-gapped row. She gave the gaps no figure. The charcoal had gone in beneath the board on the first Account-day of Reed and had stayed there through Grain and into Smoke, a count kept in a hand nobody had been told to read; her finger lay on top of the front edge where the wood was warm from her hand, and nothing could be taken off it. Her eye went once, and only once, to \place{the clay jar} on the wall shelf, cold under its glaze, and her hand shut half over nothing and came away without a touch. When she set the pot rim out she put the handle two inches beyond the reaching hand and let her hand rest a half-beat longer at the setting than the setting required, and did not look toward \place{the bed}. The boy could not close the two inches to the handle, and the half-beat went by without a word from either of them.

At \place{the kindling} \person{Tarn} took the broom only after it had been instructed. He said \emph{Yes, house} to the wood and told it \emph{Six counted. The next I don't count} in the tone of a man stating the weather, and put the small knife back at the very edge of its sill with the flat of his hand and checked it with his eyes: six in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check, the seventh at the lip where it could not. The unforged collector sentence about a man sent for a collector who turned back at the frozen ford lay beside it, with no name on the man and no figure on the debt. He had given no name to that man, though he had been that man.

The ear-sentence came to his teeth as it came every evening at the bank --- that an ear under this roof was worth more than the debt that held it --- and went all the way to the word that would call the loan and stopped there with no figure and no name. It was a remembered stopping, not a new one. Nine stoppings stood, nine objects, and no tenth had been stood up before the seventh year, which stood at its very end and had still not given. He drew the breath he had drawn on Seed 16 at the top of the reach and did not spend it, and the loan stayed uncalled. His chest had been tight since the dark. Ash was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line and no scrubbing lifted it; the pale clip-ring at his left wrist held what light there was and gave it back. His stroke went over the shallow bright patch without the half-check and over the road a house sends a man down when it wants a collector, without once turning along it. Above the shed \place{the Debt Loft} settled, and came to the boy as a direction and nothing more; the warm stone Sella had given him in the winter Garl died lay back in its unmarked clay pot up there, two years gone and cold long since, and he had stopped trying to reconcile it with the debt, which is not forgiving.

At the edge of \place{the Vail wheel-shed} \person{Jorra Vail} came through with the day's slate dust ground small as meal past the bent little finger and under the nails, white in the creases where a fist had opened, not bone-coloured \lore{slip}. He kept dusty forearms and clean ones on one tray and asked neither which slate had been broken nor whether it was a birth-slate. The broken birth-slates lay under \place{the lowest rack} where the yard went around them and entered them into nothing. He spoke to the wheel before he spoke to anyone --- \emph{It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady} --- and told a pot \emph{You'll do} in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either. He knew the \lore{full note} the pot would give three turns before it left his hands, with air under it and a want in it, and withheld the knowing on the near-full tray beside the hearings and never among them, advanced and not paid. The burned-slate word stood at four from across the district and the two tellings of the room shut in the war at two, and he joined neither to the dust, the pot, the men or the wrong-wind glaze; no fifth telling came and no third, and no name was given to what ailed the man carried out on Day 188.

When Sella's sentence went low and unfinished at the hearth his bent finger went into the cold iron of \place{the damper} ahead of it, took the cold out of the fingertip, held until the end of the sentence arrived at the pot, moved a hair's breadth and was left standing --- a remembered repetition and not a change made again, paired with no other fraction. The iron was one piece with two bites and there was no word in \lore{Grathic} for both mercies. \emph{He's not Vail} stayed sealed as tightly as on the hour it was spoken, and the promise of the boy's name to the men who burned slates was neither given nor withdrawn. Three or more men had gone low into \place{the back room} before first grey on Smoke 1, 2 and 3 and had been given no name, and the room had kept its finger's width of open and its cold.

In the small hours no breathing number stood anywhere in the house. The fire behind the wall kept its four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have been, its payer without a name for two hundred and forty-two days; the river kept its note of water over water through the finger's-width door; overhead the boards kept four nails, four and a thumb-width knot. Three counts in three places, and none rose into another. A man who wanted them to agree would have to put a payer under all three, and no payer stood to any, so the three trays lay where they lay and the boy did not lay a finger across them.

He turned out the file the way a man lays instruments on a towel at the end of a long case, and found it exact, complete and worth nothing. There was no towel, no instruments, no case, but the order of the laying was the whole of the discipline: the sharp thing by the sharp thing, the blunt by the blunt, every edge where a hand would look for it first. Six hearings stood beside the full note and never among, the sixth out of a person and filed where a thing out of a person must go, and no seventh had come in two hundred and forty-two days and none had been invented to fill the empty place, for an empty place kept empty is a finding and a finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged. Three near-full answers stood advanced and unpaid. Three shapings of the pot saying stood as sayings, and he took them apart the way he had once taken two pieces of tissue apart under a lens, telling them by what they did when pressed: one was an answer to a wound and one was the wound's own appetite. A saying was what was left when no payer had stood up; a rule was what a likeness became when somebody put a figure under it. The fourth burned-slate telling stood on its own place and the two shut-room tellings on theirs, with no fifth and no third. The told bell stood beside the heard, the told figure beside the paid; Grain's third arithmetic had gone up the clay road in Berr's locked box and was unread under this roof, and the true subtraction stayed locked beyond it, answering nothing for the man carried out on Day 188 and nothing for the hand that went in on Seed 15 and did not come back.

Nine stoppings stood, nine objects with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end and still ungiven. The Seed 16 breath was unspent and the loan uncalled. Three held changes stood on three places --- the fraction of the damper, the held beat over the bright patch, the half-check on the flagstones --- and beside them the small dry scrape from the loft sill on a fourth, the carried man's pale line on a fifth, and the held inch on a sixth. The seventh green was a colour and the seventh shape a shape, gathered at Grain's first market and carried unjoined through the working mornings, with no eighth of either, no second look joined, no name spoken over either, no loaf passed, no slate opened, no sale made and no romance paid; neither was called by a name under this roof. The dressed grain lay covered. The returning-line place had gone around with a look and no number, and the pan's gone ring kept its own tray with no scrape, crumb, print, chip or word.

Over the boards the question stood on its seventh place with four askings and no witness. Four holes in a silence are still not one witness; a witness stands somewhere and can be asked, and a day must bring a place before the question can be put to anybody, and this working day brought none. No fifth asking came and no row was named, and he drew no finger between the empty place beside the crust, the two gaps in the shelf's row, the field's empty place out on \place{the worked strips} and the returning-line place where the hand had gone in, because a row wants a payer standing under it and none stood.

He put the question to his hand the sixth way of the chapter, in the narrow time between the going of the light off the floor and the settling of the house into its small hours. The hand outside the wool went up an inch toward the low note through the finger's-width door. It went through the small intake, the hold and the letting-go of one of the breaths Sella counted at her fire in the room that does not touch his --- a breathing number kept toward no opening and offered to no ear, borrowed by him and not owned --- and it was kept through the little silence after her count had ended, and came down on the river's next low note. Two rhythms crossed in the inch and nowhere else, across a wall that does not touch. It was remembered and not a change made again. He filed it on the sixth place with no payer named. It was not a longer reach, not a miss entered as a finding, not a hearing of the full note, not a seventh, not a tenth stopping, not a payment, not one of the held changes, not a key, not a power and not a rule. Sixty answers stood there now, exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air. The six hearings kept their empty place without a seventh being invented to fill it, and water over water stayed a fifth low thing never counted among them.

Once he had reduced a shoulder in the dark by feel alone and now he could not lift a crust two inches to his mouth; exactness and humiliation were one motion in that small hand and both were true, and neither stood up to call itself a sign. The child wanted the crust. The adult kept the tray. Neither was promoted into a finding. The hand came down against the fold the way a man lays down a tool at the end of work.

Two hundred and forty-two stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in \place{Sarn}, \place{Sill} or \place{Grath}; six months and sixty-two days were told by nobody; the fire's fifth was unpaid the same number of days, its payer without a name; the widow's mark had been touched once before the kiln on Smoke 1 and at no other hour, not at a shelf, a well, a rack, a road, a basket, an afternoon or a dusk on these three days; the seventh year stood at its very end and had still not given. Smoke had gone out without a boundary drawn and neither of the district's two names for the months had been set right or left wrong, and Smoke 10 stood beyond the close, seven mornings off and unbrought.

The hand came down on the river's next low note. The file lay open on the towel, complete and worth nothing, and the count went forward exact and unspent into a day that had not yet earned its heading.
