# Chapter 63 — beat sheet: *The Coal Under the Broken Pot*

Days 240–242, Smoke 1–3, Firstday to Thirdday, Y.H. 813. Normal chapter, three lived days, six scenes, two entries to a day. Every day has its own `\daymark` in the first scene of that day only; each is lived in full in two scenes of well over eight hundred words. No day skipped, poured into another or summarised. Smoke 1 (Day 240) is the first firing of the second firing-month; Smoke 10 (Day 249), the first Account-day and market of Smoke, belongs to Chapter 66 and is not brought early. Check weekdays: 240 = Smoke 1 Firstday, 241 = Smoke 2 Secondday, 242 = Smoke 3 Thirdday (*t* = 336 + *N* − 1).

**Binding shape.** The chapter's one meaningful change is the **first firing of Smoke** in Scene 1: before the kiln, Sella puts her hand to the widow's mark, holds it one breath, lets go, lifts the clay lid with the back of a hand and tips the carried coal under the back of a broken pot; the fire takes, still answering four low pulls with the fifth missing and no payer named. It is an opening performed on a vessel nobody will name, put behind the household without a lesson, a figure, a slate, a bond or a payer --- **T058/T079 and T088 advanced, not paid**. She does not touch the mark again at a shelf, well, rack, road, basket, afternoon or dusk on any of the three days, because the hand goes to the mark before the kiln and at no other hour, the rule holding exactly as on Tallow 1 (Day 150) and Ember 1 (Day 180). Nothing else is settled, paid, joined or named; the ledger stays at 101 threads.

**Counts at the close of each day.** Day 240: 240 exact / six months and sixty days / fire's fifth unpaid 240 days / widow's mark touched once before the kiln after sixty days of going around and at no other hour. Day 241: 241 / six months and sixty-one days / 241 days / mark untouched since the opening. Day 242: 242 / six months and sixty-two days / 242 days / mark untouched since the opening; seventh year at its very end and still ungiven throughout. Hand answers run **fifty-fifth through sixtieth**, one to a scene, each filed on the sixth place as an object and not a power; the sixtieth is a remembered two-rhythm coordination, not a change made again. The held inch is asked a seventeenth way across Days 240–242 with the sixtieth answer; the count of answers stands at sixty.
