## Chronicle

Three days came under the Vail roof, and Smoke opened without a boundary drawn. The kiln drew four low pulls and missed its fifth, its payer unnamed all three days; the river kept its note through the finger's-width door; the boards held four nails, four and a knot. He kept them on three trays; agreeing wanted a payer and none stood to any. He came into his two hundred and fortieth day and the two after. A crust lay two inches from his ear; the thread froze unwiped; cold stopped at cheek, temple and wrist, and the afterdrop waited.

On the morning of Smoke 1 Sella Vail went first to the kiln mouth. The widow's mark hung at her throat, a thumb-length of lead-glazed clay; she put her hand to it, held it one breath and let go, lifted the clay lid with the back of a hand and tipped the carried coal under a broken pot. The fire took, still missing its fifth. Nobody saw what it mended. She did not touch the mark again at shelf, well, rack or basket, for the hand goes to the mark before the kiln and at no other hour.

She turned the dressed grain under wool for damp on the second and third mornings, spread it thin and gathered it by the handful; it gained no figure, counted by an arm that knew weight and not cost. Cost-questions went to pot and weather; a man's number went back into his sleeve. A wrong wind off High Kiln carried the sweet edge of glaze over the boards on Smoke 3, touched the fold and went out on the wind that brought it; one pot saying stood again as a saying, and no fourth likeness was made a cause.

At the kindling Tarn took the broom only after it had been instructed, told the wood six were counted and the next he did not count, set the knife where six could be checked and the seventh could not. The ear-sentence reached the calling word and stopped with no figure and no name; nine stoppings stood, the Seed 16 breath was unspent, no collector came. At the wheel-shed Jorra Vail parted dust from slip, told a pot it would do, knew its note three turns ahead and withheld it; the burned slate stood at four, the shut-room tellings at two, and *he's not Vail* stayed sealed.

Six times the boy put the question to his hand, the fifty-fifth through the sixtieth, each filed with no payer named. At the close the hand went up an inch, rode one of Sella's counted breaths from the room that does not touch his, kept through the little silence after her count and came down on the river's next low note — two rhythms in one inch, remembered, not a change made again. Sixty answers stood, exact and worth nothing. The file lay open. Smoke went out; nothing was joined, named, opened, spent or ended.
