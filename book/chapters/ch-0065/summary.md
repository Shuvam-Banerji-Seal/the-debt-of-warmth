## Chronicle

Three days came under the Vail roof and went without a boundary drawn. Nobody said any number over him; he kept the count like an instrument, exact, complete, unspent, worth nothing at two inches of air. Fire under the floor missed its fifth; river through the door went over water; the boards held four nails, four and a knot. Three counts apart, for agreeing them would want a payer and none stood to any.

One brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from his ear, its empty place unnamed and no third. The thread froze unwiped; cold stopped at cheek, temple and wrist, and the afterdrop waited past a warming nobody would perform. He knew the face of a man going cold and wanted the crust. Both were true; the second was louder.

Sella Vail went to the fire before the basket, told it the thaw, turned the coals with a broken pot, said "The house will keep it" and counted toward no opening. The widow's mark hung where it hung; the hand goes to it before the kiln and at no other hour. Grain came out from under wool, was spread thin and gathered by the handful; it changed state and gained no figure. Costs went to pot and weather; a number went back into a sleeve. The charcoal stayed unread, the pot handle two inches beyond the reaching hand.

At the kindling Tarn put six where the house could check and the seventh where it could not; the ear-sentence stopped at the calling word, nine stoppings with no tenth. Jorra parted dust from slip, told a pot "You'll do" and withheld the note he knew three turns ahead; the burned slate stood at four, the shut-room tellings at two. Garl was kept in objects, his name unspoken two hundred and forty-eight days; no collector came.

On the afternoon of Smoke 8 a voice with no owner, room, hand at the kiln or cause asked over the boards a fifth time: "Which row did the pot go quiet on?" A wrong wind off High Kiln carried the sweet edge of glaze over the boards, touched the fold and went out on that wind; barley's price answered after it; the voice went up the terrace and the row stood. He opened it as a surgeon opens a wound and found no witness. Five askings lay on a seventh place and no row was named. At the well Ilva kept her hand in her sleeve and the ninth hand stayed nine.

Six times he asked his hand, the sixty-seventh through the seventy-second: down at once, down before the letting-go, held to the river's interval, rhythms crossed in the inch, one rhythm held across a silence, and the crossing remembered. None was a reach, a hearing or a power. The hand lay against the fold like a tool put away; the file lay open, exact and worth nothing; under the floor the fire stopped at four and the count went into Smoke 10.
