The afternoon came on the way a fired pot cools, not by a stroke drawn but by a giving over, the heat going out of the oiled paper first, then off the front of the loaf shelf, then back behind the pot where it lay before it went under the low angle and into the fold. \person[Corin]{the boy} lay at the end of it with his ear two inches from the wall of the room that did not touch the room of the fire, and felt the warmth arrive as a hand withdrawn from a basin feels the water move after it. Two hundred and forty-seven stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in \place{Sarn}, \place{Sill} and \place{Grath}; six months and sixty-seven days told by nobody; \lore{the carried fire}'s fifth unpaid the same number of days, its payer without a name. He had ceased to expect a figure spoken over any of it, which was not the same as not wanting one. Through the finger's-width door the river kept its note of \lore{water over water}; under the floor the fire kept four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood; overhead \place{the boards} kept four nails on the near board, four on the next, and a thumb-width knot where the fifth nail should have stood. Three counts in three places, and none rose into another, because agreeing them would want a payer put under all three and no payer stood to any. He left the trays where they lay. It was a discipline and not a belief; a discipline is a thing a hand uses, and his could go up an inch and no further.

One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from his ear on \place{the bed}. The empty place beside it kept its name to itself and no third came to make the row come out even, because a kindness entered in a figure is the beginning of an account and this house had learned to give without entering. The thread of drool at his mouth had frozen in the dry heat and was unwiped; nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread, and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. Cold had come cheek, temple, the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, and stopped there as though the line it followed had been written out to that length and no longer; it neither went further nor went away. The afterdrop waited beyond all three, past a warming nobody in this house would perform. He knew the face a man wears going cold and the face he wears when warmed too fast, knew a cold patient cannot clot, knew the warming must come from the outside and slowly. He knew all of it. He wanted the crust. Both were true and the second was louder here in the afternoon, because a child's hunger keeps no hours while the register goes on being exact inside a hungry body. The surgeon turned out his file as instruments on a towel and found them exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air, and entered neither fact anywhere.

\person{Garl Vail} was kept in objects and not in a story. The salt-stiff coat held a broader man's shape on its peg with the crease at the left shoulder where a man had once leaned to reach a shelf, and nobody straightened it or took the coat down. The wheel-seat kept its twenty-year lean and its hollow on that side and had not been filled. The ridge of \lore{Ash} had turned the corner and become a clean dark line, and beside it a finer hairline of grey had gathered, called \lore{the thaw}, and both were left unswept --- not because a lesson was made of them but because a line left where it is is still a line kept. A proverb of his had gone years ago to a pot with no crack, \emph{leave the crack showing; people trust a thing they can see has been handled}, and was said now over a corner where the repair had been rubbed out. The debt against a firing of tiles circled without a name, neither paid nor settled nor done with, and worse for being uncalled. His name had not been spoken under this roof for two hundred and forty-seven days. No collector had come up \place{the clay road} and none had gone down, not on this Eighthday nor on the Seventhday behind it, and the road went up past \place{the terrace} toward \place{High Kiln} and gave the yard no figure while the yard gave the road none. The keeping of him was maintenance; nobody had made a story out of it and nobody would.

\person{Sella Vail} went to the fire before the basket. She told it it was the thaw, turned the coals with the back of a broken pot, and said \emph{The house will keep it} where another woman would have said \emph{I}. She stood a moment with the back of the pot in her hand and counted under her breath toward no opening --- small intake, hold, letting-go --- a count she had never offered to any ear under this roof, and pushed the coal-black hair back with the wrist that carries the burn so the one grey streak showed pale off the coals. The burn shaped like a little door closed on nothing and opened again to the work. \lore{the widow's mark} hung where it hung on its cord at her throat and she did not touch it, because an afternoon is not an opening and the hand goes to the mark before the kiln and at no other hour; the one opening before Smoke 1 lay behind the household and she did not look at it again, and no lesson was made of it and no payer named. The fire answered with four low pulls behind the wall and the silence where the fifth should have stood, and she went on feeding it as though it had said nothing, because a fire that misses a breath is a fire and not an argument.

At \place{the loaf shelf} she stood with her back to the basket and did not look into it. She moved her lips along the row standing with two gaps and gave nobody a figure, the way a woman runs a thumb along a hem to find the tear and does not say what she found. Her eye went once to \place{the clay jar} cold under its glaze on the wall, and her hand shut half over nothing and came away; she did not touch the jar or say what it held. Her finger stayed on top of the shelf's front edge, not beneath it, so the charcoal tally under the board stayed unread and uncounted; a mark nobody reads is still a mark kept, and a mark read wants a figure put under it, and a figure wants a payer. She set the pot rim out and turned its handle two inches beyond the reaching hand, with a half-beat at the setting that was not a look toward \place{the bed} and was not a look away from it either.

At \place{the kindling} \person{Tarn} took up the broom only after the broom had been given its instruction, the way a man takes up a tool already named. \emph{Yes, house}, he said to the wood, and to the wood again \emph{Six counted. The next I don’t count}, in the tone of a man stating the weather. He set the small knife at the very edge of its sill with six in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check and the seventh at the lip where it could not, and checked it with his eyes after setting it with the flat of his hand, as though the house might have moved it in the interval between the two acts. Beside it lay Garl's unforged collector sentence about the man who turned back at the frozen ford, with no name on the man and no figure on the debt; the two had lain together so long they looked like one tool with two uses, and neither was entered.

The sentence about an ear under this roof being worth more than the debt that held it came to his teeth and went all the way to the word that would call the loan, and stopped there with no figure and no name. It had stopped there before; this was a remembered stopping and not a new one. Nine stoppings stood on Reed 5, 8, 11, 14, 17, 20, 23, 26 and 29, objects and not a rule, with no tenth before the seventh year, which stood at its very end and had still not given. Nine likenesses laid in a row must not stand up and call themselves one, because a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and no payer stood to any of the nine; the pressure toward an answer went around them the way the yard goes around Ash. At the top of each reach he drew the Seed 16 breath and did not spend it, a small interval where the breath should have gone in, and the loan stayed uncalled and no collector came up or went down the clay road on Smoke 7 or 8. His chest had been tight since the dark. Ash was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line and no scrubbing lifted it; the pale clip-ring at his left wrist held what light there was and gave it back; and the warm stone Sella had given him in the winter Garl died lay cold in its unmarked pot in \place{the Debt Loft}, two years gone and unreconciled with the debt by not trying, which is not forgiving.

His stroke went over \place{the flagstones} and across the shallow bright patch without the half-check, and went on over the road a house sends a man down when it wants a collector without once turning along it. Above the shed the loft settled as the evening cooled and came to the boy as a direction and nothing more --- a place he would not enter and had never asked to enter, a sound with no figure in it and no name under it.

At \place{the Vail wheel-shed} \person{Jorra Vail} came through with the day's grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bent little finger and under the nails, white in the creases where a fist had opened, and not bone-coloured \lore{slip}. His quiet went before him into the shed the way a cold air goes before a door, and the household straightened around it without an order. He kept dusty forearms and clean ones on one tray and parted them as a surgeon parts clot from vessel, asked neither which slate had been broken nor whether it had been a birth-slate, and gave the house no name for the men who had gone low into the finger's-width-open \place{the back room} before first grey. He spoke to the wheel before he spoke to any person. \emph{It doesn’t care whose hands. It cares if they’re steady}, he said, and told a pot \emph{You’ll do} in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either. He knew the \lore{full note} that pot would give three turns before it left his hands, with air under it and a want in it, and withheld the knowing on the near-full tray beside the hearings and never among them, advanced and not paid. A knowing kept back is not a lie told, and a note known three turns ahead is not a hearing come early.

The \lore[the burned slate]{burned-slate word} came over the boards from \place{Slate Row}, a quarter league off, a direction a man with a map could have put a finger on even if no clerk could have entered it; the row question had no quarter league behind it, no door, no threshold, no room where a voice had stood to form it, and that was the whole of the difference and enough to keep them on two trays. The word stood at four tellings and the two tellings of the counting room shut in the war stood at two and did not become three. Three shapings of the pot saying stayed three and no fourth likeness was made a cause, because a likeness made a cause wants a payer put under it and none stood to any. No name had been given to what ailed the man carried out on Day 188, and no figure, slate or collector had answered for him. He did not join the dust to the sayings, or the sayings to the room, or the room to the wind that had carried the sweet edge of \lore{lead glaze} over the boards and taken it out again; four tellings were four and two tellings were two, and neither standing up and calling themselves an account would have made a witness stand anywhere.

When Sella's sentence went low and unfinished at \place{the hearth}, his bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper} ahead of it, took the cold out of the fingertip, held while the little silence ran, moved a hair's breadth and was left standing; the end of her instruction arrived at the pot and the household straightened around his silence the way cloth straightens when a weight is lifted. It was a remembered repetition paired with no other fraction, one piece of iron with two bites, the holding and the taking, and no \lore{Grathic} word for both mercies, joined to no kiln and entered in no slate. \emph{He’s not Vail} stayed sealed as tightly as on the hour it was spoken, and the promise of the boy's name to the men who burn slates was neither given nor withdrawn, and he did not look toward the bed or the basket. The two silences stayed side by side through the going of the light and were not joined: the debt's stopped sentence at the kindling, the wheel-keeper's held finger at the iron, each on its own tray with a finger between.

\scenebreak

The afternoon went on at the same small pace, the way a coal goes on under ash with nobody turning it, and the light went off the front of the shelf and came round behind the pot. It was in that flat hour, when the work under the roof had settled into its own noise and no person had raised a voice for a long while, that the question came over the boards.

Not into the basket. Over the boards --- the front board of the loaf shelf where Sella had moved her lips along a row with two gaps --- and low, the way a man speaks to the weather when he has decided already that nobody under the roof will answer him.

“Which row did the pot go quiet on?”

No owner stood behind it. No room, no hand at the kiln, no cause. It did not come from the fire or the pot or the wool or the running water or the river wind, all of which had their instructions and went on keeping them; it did not come into the basket, which had never been given an instruction. It came over the boards and stopped there, and the weather answered it first the way weather answers everything under this roof. A wrong wind got up off High Kiln and carried the sweet edge of glaze over the boards, touched the fold at the boy's ear and went out again on the wind that had brought it, a direction arriving nowhere with nobody behind it to answer for the coming or the going. And after the weather the price of barley answered: a figure come up out of the square and going over the boards on the same small wind, arriving where the glaze had arrived, which was nowhere any house could enter. Then the voice went off up the terrace toward High Kiln with the barley price behind it, and the row stood where it stood. Nobody leaned in to take the answer down; there was nobody under the roof to lean and nothing to have taken down, for the weather had answered in a smell and the barley in a figure with no house attached to it. The sweet edge went out on the wind that brought it and was not a second edge paid, and the green at a silent woman's mouth, gathered at Grain's first market and carried unjoined, was not joined to that wind or that asking.

He had opened this wound four times before and knew the shape of it, which did not make the fifth opening smaller or the finding at the bottom any different. He took it apart as a surgeon takes apart a wound --- not to close it, but to see what lay under it.

First the skin of it, which was what it asked. A row. It did not ask how the pot had gone quiet, or why, or who had carried out the man on Day 188, or what ailed him; it asked on which row, and a row is a place to stand. Under that lay where it stood. Over the boards, in the air, with nothing behind it and nothing under it. A thing that stands nowhere cannot be walked up to and cannot be asked a second question, which is a surgeon's way of saying that it is not a witness. Under that lay what it would want before any man could answer it. Somebody who had been there, who stood somewhere, who had seen which row and could be asked to say so and could be held to the saying. A witness. Under that lay the last layer, the one a man comes to only after he has opened the others and found them empty: who would have to stand under the answer to pay for it. Nobody stood. No owner, no room, no hand at the kiln, no cause, nobody to stand under the saying and nobody to be asked. He had opened it all the way down and found no payer at the bottom, which was a finding and not a disappointment, and he did not fill the empty place to make the layers come out even.

He set the fifth telling on the seventh place beside the first four. The first had come on Grain 21, the second on Grain 23, the third on Grain 26 and the fourth on the afternoon of Grain 29, each a voice with no owner, answered by the weather and the price of barley, gone off up the terrace with the row standing where it stood. This afternoon's had come the same way again. Five askings lay on the seventh place with a finger between each pair. He put a finger between them and the three shapings of the pot saying on the place beside, and a finger between them and the burned-slate word at its fourth telling, and a finger between them and the two tellings of the counting room shut in the war, and a finger between them and the six hearings of \lore{the full note} standing beside and never among, and a finger between them and the count. No seventh hearing had come in two hundred and forty-seven days and none had been invented to fill the empty place, and five askings standing up and calling themselves an account would not have made a witness stand anywhere.

Five askings are not one witness. A witness stands somewhere and can be asked, and none did and none could, so the fifth telling lay beside the first four as an object and not a sixth witness, a seed for the far-off day when a witness might stand somewhere and be asked, not anything that had grown this afternoon. It was not a fourth shaping of the pot saying, because a proverb about pots and a question about a row are different instruments and belong on different trays; a proverb says a thing over the boards and asks nothing, and the voice had asked. It was not a hearing, and not a seventh of the full note; not a fifth telling of the burned-slate word, which stood at four; not a third telling of the counting room shut in the war, which stood at two; not a cause, for nothing had been joined to it; not a name, for no name had been spoken over it; and not a row named, for the row stood where it stood with nobody to say which it was. Three sayings stayed three, the slate word stayed at four, the shut-room tellings stayed at two, and the question kept its five askings on a seventh place of its own with nobody to own them. No sixth asking came this afternoon and none was made under the roof, and he wrote nothing across any of the places. Five likenesses must not stand up and call themselves an account, because an account wants a payer and no payer stood to any of them, and the pressure toward a doctrine went around them the way the yard goes around Ash.

The terrace went quiet behind the voice the way a board goes quiet after a hand has lifted from it. The row stood where it stood, which was the only answer a row could give, and it gave it to nobody.

At the going of the light she carried him up from the yard at the half-pace from every open slate, went wide around the clean dark line, the finer grey called the thaw, the space beneath \place{the lowest rack} with its broken birth-slates, the pan's gone ring, \place[the trough]{the bare dark trough} with its water called weather, the field's empty place out on \place{the worked strips} and the returning-line place where a hand went in on Seed 15 and did not come back, each with a look and a wide step and no number. She set him on his bed under the roof and not in the loft, put the fold right without a look at his face and left the thread frozen; her hand rested a half-beat longer at the setting than the setting needed and then went away. She told the fire it was the thaw, turned the coals with the back of a broken pot, said \emph{The house will keep it} where another woman would have said \emph{I}, stood a moment counting toward no opening, and pushed the coal-black hair back with the burn-wrist so the grey streak showed pale off the coals. There was no market, no \place{the Cross} and no bell on an Eighthday; Smoke 10 (Day 249), the first Account-day and market of the second firing, stood two mornings ahead and had not been brought into a day that had not earned it.

In the small hours there was no breathing number anywhere in the house. The two rooms did not touch. Under the floor the carried fire gave four low pulls and withheld the fifth; through the finger's-width door the river kept its note of water over water; overhead the boards kept four nails, four, and a thumb-width knot. Three counts in three places, and none rose into another, because a man who wanted them to agree would have to put a payer under all three and no payer stood to any. No voice came over the boards in the dark. The question stood where the afternoon had left it, with five askings and nobody to own them, and he did not bring it down among the hearings or the sayings or the count to make a row look fuller. Two hundred and forty-seven stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody; six months and sixty-seven days told by nobody; the fire's fifth unpaid the same number of days; the widow's mark touched once before the kiln on Smoke 1 and at no shelf, well, rack, road, basket, afternoon or dusk on Smoke 7 or 8, because the hand goes to the mark before the kiln and at no other hour; the seventh year at its very end and still ungiven. The child in him wanted the crust. The adult kept the tray. Neither stood up and said what the other was.

He put the question to his hand the fourth way of the chapter. The hand outside the wool went up an inch toward the low note through the finger's-width door and stayed. He kept it there through the small intake, the hold and the letting-go of one of the breaths Sella counted at her fire in the room that does not touch his, borrowing its length from a count never offered to any ear under this roof, and kept it through the little silence after her count had ended. Then he brought it down on the river's next low note. Two rhythms crossed in the inch and nowhere else, across a wall that does not touch: the borrowed count and the river's own interval, meeting only in the fraction of air the hand could hold, and the old coordination was remembered and not a change made again. It was not a longer reach, not a miss entered as a finding, not a hearing of the full note, not a seventh of it, not a tenth stopping, not a payment, not one of the held changes, not a key, a power or a rule, and not a sixth asking of the question over the boards. He filed answer seventy on the sixth place with no payer named, and did not spend it and did not explain it. Seventy answers stood there now, each an object and not a power, exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air. The file lay open on the towel. The hand came down on the river's next low note after the two rhythms crossed in the inch, and answer seventy lay on the sixth place while the row still stood where it stood and the voice was gone up the terrace.
