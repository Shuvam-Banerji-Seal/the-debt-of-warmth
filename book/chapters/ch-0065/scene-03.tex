\daymark{247}{Smoke 8, Eighthday, Y.H. 813}

The day came the way a firing comes, without a stroke drawn across the one behind it, and the count went forward into two hundred and forty-seven with nobody in \place{Sarn}, \place{Sill} or \place{Grath} to say so. Six months and sixty-seven days stood edge to edge where six months and sixty-six had stood, each of them exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody, and \person[Corin]{the boy} kept the count anyway, the way a man keeps an instrument sharp when there is nothing in the room to cut. He had long since stopped asking who would have named a thing like this out loud. The not-asking was not the same as the not-wanting; the wanting went on under it the way a coal goes on under ash, and a child's body carried the whole of it without a hand to spend any part.

Hot clay came first under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof in \place{Low Kiln}, hot clay under tallow and wet wool under that, and under both the sour mineral breath of the unswept corner the house called \lore{the thaw}. The day's first sound was not a bell. Behind the wall \lore{the carried fire} drew in four low pulls and stopped where the fifth should have stood, and the stopping stood where it stood with nobody under any roof to put a name to what stood in it, its payer without a name for two hundred and forty-seven days. There was no market on an Eighthday, no \place[the Cross]{Cross} and no bell. Smoke 10 (Day 249), the first Account-day and market of the second firing, stood two mornings ahead and had not been brought into a day that had not earned it. A day not yet earned keeps its own counsel, and the house kept it.

Through the finger's-width door the river kept its note of \lore{water over water}, a fifth low thing never counted among the hearings; under the floor the fire kept its four and its silence; overhead \place{the boards} kept four nails on the near board, four on the next, and a thumb-width knot where the fifth nail should have stood. Three counts in three places, and none rose into another, because agreeing them would want a payer put under all three and no payer stood to any. He had learned to leave such trays apart. It was a discipline and not a belief; a discipline is a thing a hand uses, and he had only the hand, and it could go up an inch and no further.

One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from his ear on \place{the bed} at the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf}. The empty place beside it kept its name to itself and no third came to lie down and make the row come out even, because a kindness entered in a figure is the beginning of an account and this house had learned to give without entering. The thread of drool at his mouth had frozen in the dry heat and was unwiped, and nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread; a basket set down is a pot set down and left. Cold had come cheek, temple, the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, and had stopped there as though the line it followed had been written out to that length and no longer; it neither went further nor went away. The afterdrop waited beyond all three, past a warming nobody in this house would perform. He knew the quiet face cold wears and the face a man wears when he is warmed too fast, knew a cold patient cannot clot, knew the warming must come from the outside and slowly. He knew all of it and wanted the crust. Both were true and the second was louder, because a child's hunger keeps no hours at all while the register goes on being exact inside a body that goes on being hungry. The surgeon turned out the file as instruments on a towel, found them exact, complete and worth nothing, and entered neither fact anywhere.

\person{Sella Vail} went to the fire before the basket. She told it the thaw, turned the coals with the back of a broken pot, and said \emph{The house will keep it} where another woman would have said \emph{I}. She stood a moment with the back of the pot in her hand counting under her breath toward no opening, and pushed the coal-black hair back with the wrist that carried the burn so the one grey streak showed pale off the coals; the burn shaped like a little door closed on nothing and opened again to the work. \lore{the widow's mark} hung where it hung on its cord at her throat and she did not touch it, because a shelf is not an opening and a rack is not a door and a well is not a kiln; the hand goes to the mark before the firing and at no other hour. The one opening before Smoke 1 lay behind the household and she did not look at it again, and no lesson was made of it and no payer named.

On \place{the drying racks} she lifted the dressed grain from under its wool for the damp and put her fingers into it to the knuckle, spread it thin on the flat stone to finish, turned it, gathered it back by the handful and set the fold flat again. It changed state and gained no figure; she counted it in glances and handfuls and by the weight of the carrying arm, which knows weight and not cost, and entered it on no slate and laid it across none of the absences. The figure read over it on Grain 30 was still not the figure paid, and the true subtraction stayed locked in a box gone up the clay road and answering nothing for the man carried out on Day 188. He watched the work from the edge of it and did not turn the weight into a number. Grain kept its own counsel the way the yard kept the places it went around.

\scenebreak

A neighbour came to the threshold with a pot hitched against her hip and asked what the keeping would take now the grain was under wool. Sella did not price it, refuse it or enter it. She sent the question to the pot and the weather with \emph{Ask the pot what a wet thing costs set down there. The house has said it}, and the woman went off level with her question still on her, carrying it away the way a person carries a thing that has not been given a figure. A thing not priced is not therefore free; it is a thing whose payer has not been named, and a payer not named is not a payer forgiven. The pot sat where it sat and the weather went on being weather and neither took the question off her hands, which was the answer and the whole of it.

A man come down the clay road with dried pale mud-scurf on his coat skirt brought a number up out of a fist toward \place{the yard well}, looked at it, and put it back into his sleeve. The figure arrived nowhere. He was not refusing it and he was not giving it; he was keeping it where no thumb could read it, and a sleeve is no slate. The boy read the shape of the paper without the writing on it --- a chart turned face-down --- and did not call it a missing figure, because a figure kept is not a figure lost and the difference is the whole of the keeping. A fist closed on a number and opened on nothing; a sleeve took it back; the road went on up toward \place{the terrace} and \place{High Kiln} and gave the yard no figure while the yard gave the road none. No collector came up or went down the clay road on an Eighthday.

\person{Garl Vail} was kept in objects and not in a story. His debt against a firing of tiles circled without a name, neither paid nor settled nor done with, and worse for being uncalled; his name had not been spoken under this roof for two hundred and forty-seven days. The salt-stiff coat held a broader man's shape on its peg behind the door with the crease at the left shoulder where a man had once leaned to reach a shelf, and nobody straightened the crease and nobody took the coat down. The wheel-seat in the shed kept its twenty-year lean and its hollow on that side and had not been filled. A proverb of his had gone years ago to a pot with no crack in it, \emph{leave the crack showing; people trust a thing they can see has been handled}, and was said now over a corner where the repair had been rubbed out. The keeping of him was maintenance; nobody had made a story out of it and nobody would.

At the loaf shelf Sella stood with her back to the basket and did not look into it. She moved her lips along the row standing with two gaps and gave nobody a figure, the way a woman runs a thumb along a row of nails without counting them. Her eye went once to the clay jar cold under its glaze on the wall, and her hand shut half over nothing and came away; she did not touch the jar and did not say what it held. Her finger stayed on top of the shelf's front edge, not beneath it, so the charcoal tally under the board stayed unread and uncounted; a mark nobody reads is still a mark kept, and a mark read wants a figure put under it, and a figure wants a payer. She set the pot rim out and turned its handle two inches beyond the reaching hand, with a half-beat at the setting that was not a look toward the bed and was not a look away from it either.

The yard went around its walked-around places with a look and a wide step and no number: the clean dark line at the Vail corner where the ridge of \lore{Ash} had turned, and the finer hairline of grey beside it called the thaw, neither swept; the space beneath \place{the lowest rack} where the broken birth-slates lay; the round ring where the shallow clay pan had stood, gone now with no scrape, no crumb, no print, no chip and no word; \place[the trough]{the bare dark trough} beside the well, with its water called weather; the field's empty place out on \place{the worked strips}; and the returning-line place where a hand went in on Seed 15 and did not come back. Seven places gone around with a look and a wide step, and they were not an account until a payer stood under them, and none stood. He did not draw a finger between the pan's gone ring and the shelf's two gaps, or the fold's one crust and its unnamed gap, or the field's empty place; four absences on four trays with a finger between every pair, and a fourth likeness made a cause would want a payer and none stood to any. The yard gave each thing its own room and went on.

\scenebreak

At the yard well \person{Ilva} drew water with the pail against her knee, clay dried pale in the crescents of her nails and the grey rack dust worked into the seam of one thumb, and she did not scrub the seam out. She was of the yard and not of the house, and nobody in the yard turned to look at her twice. A woman with a pot asked how the firings stood. This time her hand stayed where it was. It did not go into her sleeve; the short strip of birch bark with its notches stayed where it was kept, pale where a thumb had rubbed it and dark in the uncut grain between, and her eyes went to the asker and her mouth answered the question put and not the one behind it: \emph{It's kept where it's kept. Ask the pot what it cost.} She said no figure and gave no name to the thing she kept, and went on with her water, the pail knocking the rim of the well and the water in it called weather.

The boy did not file a tenth hand-shape. The ninth stayed where it had been set, on a place of its own beside the eighth with a finger between it and every other private keeping; nine likenesses are nine and must not stand up and call themselves a rule, for a rule is an account that has found a payer and none stood to any. The order of reaching was a shape and the shape was all he had taken the first time, and he did not take a second shape from a hand that did not reach, because a shape taken from a not-reaching would be a shape made to make the row come out even. He had spent two hundred and forty-seven days learning what a shape is worth when it stands alone, and what it is worth the moment a man puts two of them under one cause. The notched birch stayed in her sleeve, not read, not asked about, not joined to the reed-cutter's cord turned so its knots stood, or the knots, or the six pages, or the tins, or the missing hand, or the pale line where a ring had been worn, or any other private keeping. She was not Marra, not Hessa Rale, not the soft-cuffed clerk set below the north bridge for a measure nobody called, not an officer, and not joined to any of them. The woman went on drawing water, and the count of the firings stayed in wood where no slate could take a figure off it.

Then he put the question to his hand the third way of the chapter. The hand outside the wool went up an inch toward the low note through the finger's-width door and was kept to the river's own low interval, with no count in the house to hold it against and no borrowed rhythm to cross it with; it came down on the next note. It was a fraction of air and not a longer reach, not a miss entered as a finding, not a hearing of \lore{the full note}, not a seventh of it, not a tenth stopping, not a payment, not one of the held changes, not a key, a power or a rule. Answer sixty-nine lay beside sixty-eight on the sixth place with no payer named. The three ways of asking the same inch were three and not one, and none of them was a longer reach; the surgeon took them off the towel as instruments and found them exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air. The thumb stayed inside the sleeve and the woman answered the question put with her mouth and went on with her water, and the ninth hand-shape stayed on its own tray and no tenth was filed.
