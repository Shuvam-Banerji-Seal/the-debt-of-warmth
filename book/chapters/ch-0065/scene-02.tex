The afternoon thinned without ending. It went the way wool goes, a thread at a time, and no thread was the last; the light at the oiled paper lost its yellow first and left a grey that held no warmth, and still the work went on under the roof as though the day had only turned its face away to listen. The heat that had stood full at the kiln mouth in the morning drew back behind the pot, went under the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf}, lay a while in the fold, and left \person[Corin]{the boy} with the edge of it only --- a hand withdrawn from a basin leaves the water moving after it. He took what the afternoon gave in the order it gave it: hot clay going thin under tallow, wet wool going thinner under that, and under both the sour mineral breath of the unswept corner the house called \lore{the thaw}. He had once read a room in the dark by what lay under the hand before what stood behind it; he read this room the same way, and the register ran on without a hand to write with.

One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from his ear. The empty place beside it kept its name to itself, and no third came to lie down and make the row come out even, because a kindness entered in a figure is the beginning of an account and this house had learned to give without entering. The thread of drool at his mouth had frozen in the dry heat and was unwiped; nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread, and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. Cold had come cheek, temple, the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, and had stopped there as though the line it followed had been written out to that length and no longer; it neither went further nor went away. The afterdrop waited beyond all three, past a warming nobody in this house would perform. He knew the face a man wears when he is going cold and the face he wears when he is warmed too fast, knew a cold patient cannot clot, knew the warming must come from the outside and slowly. He knew all of it. He wanted the crust. The two facts stood on one tray and the second was louder, and the surgeon entered neither anywhere.

The house kept its days in loaves pushed along the shelf, in glances at a jar cold under its glaze, in a space at the row's end that nobody called a number; the boy kept his in a count that stood edge to edge against all three and bought him nothing he could spend. Two hundred and forty-six loaves, glances and empty places had gone over this floor and nobody had offered him one of them as a figure, which was not a refusal and not a gift but the household's way of keeping a thing without entering it. He had stopped expecting to be told what the number counted, which is not the same as not wanting it; the wanting went on under the expectation the way a coal goes on under ash.

\person{Garl Vail} was kept in objects and not in a story. The salt-stiff coat held a broader man's shape on its peg behind the door with the crease at the left shoulder where a man had once leaned to reach a shelf, and nobody straightened the crease and nobody took the coat down. The wheel-seat in the shed kept its twenty-year lean and its hollow on that side and had not been filled. The ridge of \lore{Ash} had turned the corner and become a clean dark line, and beside it a finer hairline of grey had gathered, called the thaw, and both were left unswept --- not because a lesson was made of them but because a line left where it is is still a line kept. A proverb of his had gone years ago to a pot with no crack in it, \emph{leave the crack showing; people trust a thing they can see has been handled}, and was said now over a corner where the repair had been rubbed out. The debt against a firing of tiles circled without a name, neither paid nor settled nor done with, and worse for being uncalled. His name had not been spoken under this roof for two hundred and forty-six days. No collector had come up \place{the clay road} for it and none had gone down, not on this Seventhday, and the road went up past \place{the terrace} toward \place{High Kiln} and gave the yard no figure while the yard gave the road none. The keeping of him was maintenance; nobody had made a story out of it and nobody would.

\person{Sella Vail} stood at the shelf with her back to the basket and did not look into it. She moved her lips along the row standing with two gaps and gave nobody a figure, the way a woman runs a thumb along a row of nails without counting them. Her eye went once to the clay jar cold under its glaze on the wall, and her hand shut half over nothing and came away, and she did not touch the jar and did not say what it held. Her finger stayed on top of the shelf's front edge, not beneath it, so the charcoal tally under the board stayed unread and uncounted; a mark nobody reads is still a mark kept, and a mark read wants a figure put under it, and a figure wants a payer. She set the pot rim out and turned its handle two inches beyond the reaching hand, with a half-beat at the setting that was not a look toward \place{the bed} and was not a look away from it either. \lore{the widow's mark} hung where it hung on its cord at her throat and she did not touch it, because a shelf is not an opening and the hand goes to the mark before the kiln and at no other hour.

At \place{the kindling} \person{Tarn} took up the broom only after the broom had been given its instruction, the way a man takes up a tool already named. \emph{Yes, house}, he said to the wood, and to the wood again, \emph{Six counted. The next I don’t count}, in the tone of a man stating the weather. He set the small knife at the very edge of its sill with six in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check and the seventh at the lip where it could not, and he checked it with his eyes after setting it with the flat of his hand, as though the house might have moved it in the interval between the two acts. Beside it lay Garl's unforged collector sentence about the man who turned back at the frozen ford, with no name on the man and no figure on the debt. The sentence had lain beside the knife so long that the two had come to look like one tool with two uses, and neither was entered.

The sentence about an ear under this roof being worth more than the debt that held it came to his teeth and went all the way to the word that would call the loan, and stopped there with no figure and no name. It had stopped there before; this was a remembered stopping and not a new one. Nine stoppings stood on their own places, nine objects and not a rule, with no tenth before the seventh year, which stood at its very end and had still not given. A doctrine is an account that has found a payer, and nine likenesses laid in a row must not stand up and call themselves one; no man had put a payer under any of the nine, so the nine stayed where they were, sharp by sharp and separate, and the pressure toward an answer went around them the way the yard goes around Ash. At the top of each reach he drew the Seed 16 breath and did not spend it, a small interval where the breath should have gone in, and the loan stayed uncalled and no collector came up or went down the clay road on a Seventhday. His chest had been tight since the dark. Ash was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line and no scrubbing lifted it; the pale clip-ring at his left wrist held what light there was and gave it back; and the warm stone Sella had given him in the winter Garl died lay cold in its unmarked pot in \place{the Debt Loft}, two years gone and unreconciled with the debt by not trying, which is not forgiving.

His stroke went over \place{the flagstones} and across the shallow bright patch without the half-check, and over the road a house sends a man down when it wants a collector, without once turning along it. Above the shed the loft settled as the evening cooled and came to the boy as a direction and nothing more --- a place he would not enter and had never asked to enter, a sound with no figure in it and no name under it.

At \place{the Vail wheel-shed} \person{Jorra Vail} came through with the day's grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bent little finger and under the nails, white in the creases where a fist had opened, and not bone-coloured \lore{slip}. He kept dusty forearms and clean ones on one tray and asked neither which slate had been broken nor whether it had been a birth-slate; the broken birth-slates lay under the lowest rack where the yard went around them and entered them into nothing. The boy kept the two states of the one morning on one place of his own and drew no finger between them, because a finger drawn between two likenesses is the beginning of an account and no payer stood under either forearm. He spoke to the wheel before he spoke to any person. \emph{It doesn’t care whose hands. It cares if they’re steady}, he said, and to a pot on the wheel he said \emph{You’ll do} in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either. He knew the \lore{full note} that pot would give three turns before it left his hands, with air under it and a want in it, and withheld the knowing on the near-full tray beside the hearings and never among them, advanced and not paid. A knowing kept back is not a lie told, and a note known three turns ahead is not a hearing come early; he had never said either and nobody had asked him to.

The \lore[the burned slate]{burned-slate word} stood at four out of \place{Slate Row} and the two tellings of the counting room shut in the war stood at two and did not become three, though men unnamed had gone low into \place{the back room} before first grey and the room kept its finger's width of open and its sound of low voices. No name had been given to what ailed the man carried out on Day 188, and no figure, slate or collector had answered for him. Three shapings of the pot saying stayed three and no fourth likeness was made a cause, because a likeness made a cause wants a payer put under it and none stood to any of them. He did not join the dust to the sayings, or the sayings to the room, or the room to the wind that had brought the sweet edge of glaze over \place{the boards} and taken it out again on the wind that brought it; four tellings were four and two tellings were two, and neither standing up and calling themselves an account would have made a witness stand anywhere.

When Sella's sentence went low and unfinished at \place{the hearth}, his bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper} ahead of it, took the cold out of the fingertip, held while the little silence ran, moved a hair's breadth and was left standing; the end of her instruction arrived at the pot and the household straightened around his silence the way cloth straightens when a weight is lifted. It was a remembered repetition paired with no other fraction, one piece of iron with two bites, the holding and the taking, and no \lore{Grathic} word for both mercies, joined to no kiln and entered in no slate. \emph{He’s not Vail} stayed sealed as tightly as on the hour it was spoken, and the promise of the boy's name to the men who burn slates was neither given nor withdrawn. He did not look toward the bed or the basket, and the boy did not take the fraction for a mercy done to him, because a mercy named too early is a debt with a payer's name on it. The two silences under the roof stayed side by side through the going of the light and were not joined: the debt's stopped sentence at the kindling, the wheel-keeper's held finger at the iron, each kept on its own tray with a finger between, and the household straightened around them without an order being given, the way a bed straightens when the weight lifts from it.

\scenebreak

At the going of the light Sella carried him the few steps and set him on his bed under the roof and not in the loft, put the fold right without a look at his face, and left the thread frozen; her hand rested a half-beat longer at the setting than the setting needed and then went away. She told the fire it was the thaw, turned the coals with the back of a broken pot, said \emph{The house will keep it} where another woman would have said \emph{I}, stood a moment counting under her breath toward no opening, and pushed the coal-black hair back with the burn-wrist so the grey streak showed pale off the coals. The burn shaped like a little door closed on nothing and opened again to the work. There was no market, no \place[the Cross]{Cross} and no bell on a Seventhday; Smoke went on without a boundary drawn in the telling and neither of the district's two names for the months was set right or left wrong; Smoke 10 (Day 249), the first Account-day and market of the second firing, stood three mornings ahead and had not been brought into a day that had not earned it.

Two hundred and forty-six stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in \place{Sarn}, \place{Sill} or \place{Grath}; six months and sixty-six days were told by nobody; the fire's fifth was unpaid the same number of days, its payer without a name; the mark had been touched once before the kiln on Smoke 1 and at no shelf, well, rack, road, basket, afternoon or dusk on this Seventhday, because the hand goes to the mark before the kiln and at no other hour; the seventh year stood at its very end and had still not given. Through the finger's-width door the river kept its note of \lore{water over water}, and under the floor \lore{the carried fire} answered four low pulls with the silence where the fifth should have been, and overhead the boards kept four nails on the near board, four on the next, and a thumb-width knot where the fifth nail should have stood: three counts in three places, and none rose into another, because agreeing them would want a payer put under all three and no payer stood to any.

Then he put the question to his hand the second way of the chapter. The hand outside the wool went up an inch toward the low note through the finger's-width door and was held for the small intake only; it came down before the letting-go, on the river's next low note, with no borrow and no crossing. It was a fraction of air and not a longer reach, not a miss entered as a finding, not a hearing of the full note, not a seventh of it, not a tenth stopping, not a payment, not one of the held changes, not a key, a power or a rule. Answer sixty-eight lay beside sixty-seven on the sixth place with no payer named, and the discipline of the sixth place was the whole of it, a thing a hand uses and not a thing a man believes. The first way of the chapter had gone down at once; this way had gone up and held for the small intake only and come down before the letting-go. Two ways of asking the same inch were two and not one, and neither was a longer reach, and the surgeon took both off the towel as instruments and found them exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air. The hand went up an inch, was held for the small intake only and came down before the letting-go; answer sixty-eight lay beside sixty-seven, and the file lay open on the towel, exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air.
