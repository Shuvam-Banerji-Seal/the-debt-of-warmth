# Chapter 65 — The Fifth Asking

Days 246–248, Smoke 7–9, Seventhday to Ninthday, Y.H. 813. Normal chapter: three lived days, six scenes, two entries to a day. Every day has its own `\daymark` in the first scene of that day only — `\daymark{246}{Smoke 7, Seventhday, Y.H. 813}`, `\daymark{247}{Smoke 8, Eighthday, Y.H. 813}`, `\daymark{248}{Smoke 9, Ninthday, Y.H. 813}` — and each is lived in full in two scenes of well over eight hundred words. No day skipped, poured into another or summarised. Weekdays checked against *t* = 336 + *N* − 1 and the tend rule: Smoke 1 (Day 240) = Firstday, so 246 = Smoke 7, Seventhday; 247 = Smoke 8, Eighthday; 248 = Smoke 9, Ninthday; Smoke 10 (Day 249) = Tenthday, the first Account-day and market of the second firing, one morning beyond the close and not brought early. ## The one meaningful change

A voice with no owner, no room, no hand at the kiln and no cause asks the row question over the boards a **fifth** time on the afternoon of Smoke 8: *Which row did the pot go quiet on?* The weather answers first with a wrong wind off High Kiln carrying the sweet edge of lead glaze over the boards and out on the wind that brought it, and the price of barley answers after it; the voice goes off up the terrace while the row stands where it stood and nobody leans in to take the answer down. The boy opens the asking as a surgeon opens a wound and finds no witness at the bottom (T101 advanced, not paid). Five askings stand on a seventh place of its own with a finger between each pair: still not one witness, not a fourth shaping of the pot saying, not a hearing, not a seventh of the full note, not a fifth telling of the burned slate, not a third telling of the shut room, not a cause, not a name and not a row named. The three pot sayings stay three and the sweet edge stays a direction arrived nowhere and not a second edge paid. The one new named character's tally from Chapter 64 is **held, not advanced**: Ilva is seen again at the yard well drawing water and answering the question put, the strip stays in her sleeve and the ninth record-first hand stays on its own tray with a finger between it and the eighth; no tenth hand is filed (T080 held at nine).

**Day and time.** Day 248, Smoke 9, Ninthday. Small hours under the Vail roof, then the ordinary morning at the hearth, the loaf shelf, the drying racks, the Clay Yard, the boards and the terrace. Fifth entry, fifth dated heading, `\daymark{248}{Smoke 9, Ninthday, Y.H. 813}`.

**Place.** The Vail kiln-house and hearth, the loaf shelf and its boards, the drying racks, the Clay Yard and the boards fronting the shelf, the terrace above, the clay road.

**Present.** Corin/Dan, Sella Vail. A neighbour at the threshold with a pot, a man come down the clay road with a number in his fist; both walk-ons, no sheets. Tarn and Jorra are heard only as the ordinary directions of the house in this scene.

**Beats, in order.**
1. New day, new date, no recap: **248** exact, six months and sixty-eight days, nobody told (T071/T072); fire's fifth unpaid two hundred and forty-eight days (T088); widow's mark touched once before the kiln on Smoke 1 and at no shelf, well, rack, road, basket, afternoon or dusk (T058/T079). First smell hot clay under tallow, wet wool under that, sour Ash breath of the thaw; first sound the kiln in fours with its gap, no bell, no market and no Cross. Smoke 10 one morning ahead, unbrought.
2. Dressed grain turned under wool for damp, spread thin to finish, gathered back by the handful and the fold set flat; changed state, gained no figure, counted in glances, handfuls and by the carrying arm's weight that knows weight and not cost, entered on no slate and laid across none of the absences (T077).
3. Neighbour's cost-question answered with the pot and the weather; man with the number in his fist brings it up toward the well, looks at it and puts it back into his sleeve, so the figure arrives nowhere (T015/T041/T047 held). The figure read over the covered grain on Grain 30 is still not the figure paid and the true subtraction stays locked in Berr Clay's box, answering nothing for the man carried out on Day 188.
4. Sella lips along the two-gapped row, glances once at the clay jar cold under its glaze and shuts her hand half over nothing, keeps her finger on top of the shelf's front edge so the charcoal stays unread, sets the pot handle two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat and no look toward the bed (T100). The yard goes around the clean dark line and the finer grey called the thaw, the rack space, the pan's gone ring, the field's empty place and the returning-line place, each with a look and a wide step and no number (T098 carried, T009/T013/T092).
5. Garl in objects, his name unspoken two hundred and forty-eight days, no collector on the clay road (T009/T013/T092).
6. Fifth hand question: hand up an inch toward the low note through the finger's-width door and kept through the little silence after Sella's count has ended in the room that does not touch his, without crossing two rhythms, then down on the river's next low note. Answer **seventy-one** — one rhythm held across the silence and not two crossed — filed with no payer named (T061/T082).

**Emotional turn.** The last working morning before the Account-day settles into ordinary tending; the fifth asking from the day before has not become an answer and the grain under wool has not been given a figure. The boy is at the place he has been for two hundred and forty-eight days: exact, cold, hungry, worth nothing at two inches of air, and still keeping the tray.

**Sensory anchors.** Hot clay under tallow; wet wool; sour Ash; the boards' four nails and thumb-width knot; the leathery crust and its gap; the pot handle two inches out; the river's note through the finger's-width door; the wool over the dressed grain holding its figureless weight.

**Threads advanced.** T071/T072; T088; T058/T079; T077; T015/T041/T047 held; T100; T009/T013/T092; T098 carried; T061/T082 (answer 71); T060/T086.

**Foreshadowing planted or paid off.** T077 keeps the dressed grain gathering interest into Smoke 10, where a figure will be read over it that does not know what it cost. T101's five askings stay five with no witness, held for the day a voice stands somewhere and can be asked. T058/T079 keeps the widow's mark untouched until the first firing of Frost, far off.

**Exact last image.** The hand comes down on the river's next low note after the little silence following Sella's count, and answer seventy-one lies on the sixth place while Smoke 10 stands one morning ahead, unbrought.

## Scene 6 — The Sixth Place

**Day and time.** Day 248, Smoke 9, Ninthday. Afternoon into the small hours. Sixth and final entry, chapter close, no new `\daymark`.

**Place.** The loaf shelf's low angle, the hearth and kiln mouth, the salt coat and hollow wheel-seat, the kindling and flagstones, the wheel-shed edge, the clay road at the going of the light, the bed under the roof.

**Present.** Corin/Dan, Sella Vail, Tarn, Jorra Vail; Garl in objects; unnamed men into the back room before first grey, given no name.

**Beats, in order.**
1. Smoke goes out without a boundary drawn in the narration; neither of the district's two month-names is corrected; Smoke 10 (Day 249), the first Account-day and market of the second firing, stands beyond the close and is not brought into a day that has not earned it. Afternoon thins; heat into the fold; one stiff leathery crust two inches off, its gap unnamed and no third; frozen thread unwiped; cold at cheek--temple--wrist; afterdrop waiting.
2. Garl in objects, his name unspoken two hundred and forty-eight days, no collector up or down the clay road on Smoke 7, 8 or 9; his keeping is maintenance and not a story (T009/T013/T092).
3. Sella gives the fire its instruction before the basket, tells it the thaw, turns the coals with the back of a broken pot, says *The house will keep it* where another woman would say *I*, counts under her breath toward no opening, pushes coal-black hair back with the burn-wrist; the widow's mark is as it was after the one opening before Smoke 1, touched at no shelf, well, rack, road, basket, afternoon or dusk on any of these three days, because the hand goes to the mark before the kiln and at no other hour; the opening is behind the household, no lesson made, no payer named (T058/T079, T100).
4. At the kindling Tarn takes the broom only after it is instructed, tells the wood *Six counted. The next I don't count*, sets the knife where six can be checked and the seventh cannot, brings the ear-sentence to the word that would call the loan and stops there with no figure and no name, a remembered stopping and not a new one. Nine stoppings stand with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end and still ungiven; the Seed 16 breath is unspent and the loan uncalled; chest tight since the dark, Ash in the knuckles, pale clip-ring at the wrist, warm stone cold and unreconciled in its loft pot (T036/T076, T006).
5. At the wheel-shed Jorra comes through with meal-fine dust past the bent finger, parts dust from slip, tells a pot *You'll do*, withholds the note known three turns ahead, keeps the burned slate at four and the shut-room tellings at two, holds his bent finger in the damper ahead of Sella's unfinished sentence as a remembered repetition paired with no other fraction, gives the men unnamed no name, keeps *he's not Vail* sealed and the promise neither given nor withdrawn (T035/T031/T062/T065/T095).
6. The boy turns out the file as instruments on a towel and finds it exact, complete and worth nothing: six hearings beside and never among with no seventh and none invented to fill the empty place; three near-full answers advanced and unpaid; three pot sayings; the fourth burned-slate telling and the two shut-room tellings; the told bell beside the heard and the told figure beside the paid, with Grain's third subtraction locked in Berr Clay's box; nine stoppings with no tenth; the three held changes — the Day 161 fraction, the Day 166 held beat, the Tallow 22 half-check — plus the loft scrape, the pale line and the held inch on six places; the **ninth** record-first hand-shape on a place of its own with a finger between it and the eighth and between it and every other private keeping (T080 held, not paid); the seventh green a colour and the seventh shape a shape with no eighth and no name; the dressed grain covered and unfigured; the pan's gone ring on its own tray; the returning-line place gone around with a look and no number; and the ownerless question on its seventh place with **five askings** and no witness standing (T101 advanced, not paid). He draws no finger between the crust's gap, the shelf's two gaps, the field's empty place and the returning-line place, and no fourth likeness is made a cause (T060/T086, T098).
7. Sixth hand question: hand up an inch toward the low note through the finger's-width door, through the small intake, the hold and the letting-go of one of Sella's counted breaths in the room that does not touch his, kept through the little silence after her count, down on the river's next low note — two rhythms crossing in the inch and nowhere else, across a wall that does not touch, remembered and not a change made again; not a longer reach, miss entered, hearing, seventh, tenth stopping, payment, held change, key, power or rule. Answer **seventy-two** filed with no payer named (T061/T082).
8. Six months and sixty-eight days told by nobody; the fire's fifth unpaid two hundred and forty-eight days, its payer without a name; the seventh year at its very end and still ungiven. The hand lays itself down against the fold like a tool put away. The count goes forward into Smoke 10, a day that has not yet earned its heading.

**Emotional turn.** The count closes at 248 with everything held and nothing settled. The fifth asking has been gathered into the file without being joined, and the two rhythms cross in the inch once more without becoming a sixth sense or a gift. Forward momentum, not resolution.

**Sensory anchors.** Dry heat settling into the fold; the burn-shaped door closing on nothing; the knife's worn mark and the unchecked lip; the damper's double bite; the laid-out order of the file; the river's note through the finger's-width door; the kiln's missing fifth under the floor; the wool over the unfigured grain.

**Threads advanced.** T061/T082 (answer 72 and the unchanged file); T071/T072 (248 exact, six months and sixty-eight days); T088 (fire's fifth unpaid 248 days); T058/T079; T036/T076 and T006; T035/T031/T062/T065/T095; T080 (ninth hand held, advanced from Ch 64 but not paid); T101 (five askings, advanced, not paid); T009/T013/T092; T077; T098, T060/T086, T050/T051, T033/T034 carried at their old counts.

**Foreshadowing planted or paid off.** Nothing is paid and no thread is opened. T101's five askings and T080's ninth hand gather toward the far-off day a witness stands somewhere and the district's private records are laid together by a hand not his; neither is joined now. T036/T076 gathers toward the end of Tarn's seventh year; T035/T031/T062/T065/T095 gathers toward the Broken Slates; T088's unnamed payer and T006's unentered warmth gather toward the far-off naming of the Hearth as a wound. No count is changed and no ending is written.

**Exact last image.** The hand comes down on the river's next low note and lies against the fold like a tool put away; the file lies open on the towel, exact, complete and worth nothing; under the floor the fire stops at four; two hundred and forty-eight stands exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody, and the count goes forward into Smoke 10, a day that has not yet earned its heading.

## Pull into Chapter 66

The chapter closes on Day 248, Smoke 9, Ninthday, with the count at 248 exact, six months and sixty-eight days told by nobody, the fire's fifth unpaid the same number of days, the widow's mark untouched since the opening before Smoke 1, the seventh year at its very end and ungiven, a ninth record-first hand on its own tray, and the row question standing at five askings with no witness and no row named. Chapter 66 opens on Day 249, Smoke 10, Tenthday, in the small hours under the Vail roof with the hand laid down against the fold on the seventy-second answer and the count exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody at 248 — six months and sixty-eight days told by nobody — the first opening behind the household, the grain still covered and unfigured, the five askings still without a witness, and Smoke 10 newly earned by the morning's light. The first Account-day and market of the second firing is that morning's work: Berr Clay comes up short off the clay road with the red wool cord folded square and the green of tasted lead glaze at one mouth-corner, wipes his thumb twice before touching nothing, holds a narrow slate unopened to his forearm, reads *one in twelve, as the notice says* low into the crowd, calls the levy and denies it with *your house*, never *I*, and goes off with the box locked and the true subtraction inside, believing he has done a kindness. The district's arithmetic changes for the first time in Smoke and cannot be undone; the figure read aloud is still not the figure paid and it answers nothing for the man carried out on Day 188 or the hand that went in on Seed 15. Hessa carries the slate up the clay road edge-on and unread, the soft-cuffed clerk stays set below the north bridge for a measure nobody called, the bell goes four, four and a gap with nobody crossing himself, and the heard bell stays beside the told. Sella sets the basket in the moot-post's thin shade where no slate lies and stands half a pace off, then carries him home at the going of the light saying nothing of what was read over him. The seventh green stays a colour and the seventh shape a shape with no eighth, no name, no loaf, no slate and no joining to the read figure. Six more answers, the seventy-third through the seventy-eighth, carry the count to 251. No thread is paid, no new thread owed and no ending written. Forward, not closed.
