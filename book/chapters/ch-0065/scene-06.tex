The afternoon came on without a stroke drawn, the way a coal comes up through its own ash, and \person[Corin]{the boy} lay at the end of it on \place{the bed} at the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf} under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof in \place{Low Kiln}, with the fold at his ear and one stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust two inches from it. Smoke went out the way it had come in, without a boundary drawn in the telling, and neither of the district's two names for the months was set right or left wrong. Smoke 10 (Day 249), the first Account-day and market of the second firing, stood beyond the close and had not been brought into a day that had not yet earned its heading. Two hundred and forty-eight stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in \place{Sarn}, \place{Sill} and \place{Grath}; six months and sixty-eight days told by nobody; \lore{the carried fire}'s fifth unpaid the same number of days, its payer without a name. There was no figure spoken over any of it, and the wanting of one went on under the not-expecting the way a coal goes on under ash.

The heat drew back as the light did. It went out of the oiled paper first, then off the front of the shelf, then behind the pot where it had stood in the morning, and came to rest under the low angle and in the fold, so that he felt the edge of it only, the way a hand withdrawn from a basin feels the water move after it. The smells arrived in their old order, the near before the far: hot clay going thin under tallow, wet wool going thinner under that, and under both the sour mineral breath of the unswept corner the house called \lore{the thaw}. Through the finger's-width door the river kept its note of \lore{water over water}; under the floor the fire kept four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood; overhead \place{the boards} kept four nails on the near board, four on the next, and a thumb-width knot where the fifth nail should have stood. Three counts in three places, and none rose into another, because agreeing them would want a payer put under all three and no payer stood to any. He left the trays where they lay. It was a discipline and not a belief; a discipline is a thing a hand uses, and his could go up an inch and no further.

One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from his ear. The empty place beside it kept its name to itself and no third came to make the row come out even, because a kindness entered in a figure is the beginning of an account and this house had learned to give without entering. The thread of drool at his mouth had frozen in the dry heat and was unwiped; nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread, and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. Cold had come cheek, temple, the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, and stopped there as though the line it followed had been written out to that length and no longer; it neither went further nor went away. The afterdrop waited beyond all three, past a warming nobody in this house would perform. He knew the face a man wears going cold and the face he wears when warmed too fast, knew a cold patient cannot clot, knew the warming must come from the outside and slowly. He knew all of it. He wanted the crust. Both were true and the second was louder in the afternoon, because a child's hunger keeps no hours while the register goes on being exact inside a hungry body. The surgeon took the inventory as he had taken it on the two mornings before and entered neither fact anywhere.

\person{Garl Vail} was kept in objects and not in a story. The salt-stiff coat held a broader man's shape on its peg with the crease at the left shoulder where a man had once leaned to reach a shelf, and nobody straightened it or took it down. The wheel-seat kept its twenty-year lean and its hollow on that side and had not been filled. The ridge of \lore{Ash} had turned the corner and become the clean dark line, and beside it the finer hairline of grey had gathered, called the thaw, and both were left unswept --- not because a lesson was made of them but because a line left where it is is still a line kept. A proverb of his had gone years ago to a pot with no crack, and was said now over a corner where the repair had been rubbed out. The debt against a firing of tiles circled without a name, neither paid nor settled nor done with, and worse for being uncalled. His name had not been spoken under this roof for two hundred and forty-eight days. No collector came up \place{the clay road} on Smoke 7, 8 or 9 and none went down it. The keeping of him was maintenance; nobody had made a story out of it and nobody would, and the boy did not turn the objects into a cause.

\person{Sella Vail} went to \place{the hearth} before the basket, as she went every day, and gave the fire its instruction before she gave one to anything that breathed. She told it it was the thaw, turned the coals with the back of a broken pot at \place{the kiln mouth}, and said \emph{The house will keep it} where another woman would have said \emph{I}. She stood a moment with the back of the pot in her hand and counted under her breath toward no opening --- small intake, hold, letting-go --- a count she had never offered to any ear under this roof, and pushed the coal-black hair back with the wrist that carries the burn so the one grey streak showed pale off the coals. The burn shaped like a little door closed on nothing and opened again to the work. The fire answered with four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood, and she went on feeding it as though it had said nothing, because a fire that misses a breath is a fire and not an argument.

\lore{the widow's mark} hung where it hung on its cord at her throat and she did not touch it in the afternoon or at the going of the light. The hand goes to the mark before the kiln and at no other hour; a shelf, a well, a rack, a road, a basket, an afternoon and a dusk are none of them openings. She had touched it once, before the first firing of Smoke on Smoke 1, held one breath, let go, tipped the carried coal under a broken pot and let the fire take, still missing its fifth. That one opening lay behind the household and she did not look at it again, and no lesson was made of it and no payer named. On Smoke 7, 8 and 9 the mark hung where it hung, untouched at every shelf, well, rack, road, basket, afternoon and dusk, and the boy did not read the rule as a mending performed or a payment made, because a rule that stands up and calls itself a mercy wants a payer under it and none stood.

At the loaf shelf she stood with her back to the basket and did not look into it. She moved her lips along the row standing with two gaps and gave nobody a figure, the way a woman runs a thumb along a hem to find the tear and does not say what she found. Her eye went once to \place{the clay jar} cold under its glaze on the wall, and her hand shut half over nothing and came away; she did not touch the jar or say what it held. Her finger stayed on top of the shelf's front edge, not beneath it, so the charcoal tally under the board stayed unread and uncounted; a mark nobody reads is still a mark kept, and a mark read wants a figure put under it, and a figure wants a payer. She set the pot rim out and turned its handle two inches beyond the reaching hand, with a half-beat at the setting that was not a look toward \place{the bed} and was not a look away from it either. Two inches of air was the whole of the distance and the hand could not close it, and the setting went on being a setting and not a kindness entered.

At \place{the kindling} \person{Tarn} took the broom only after it had been instructed, the instruction going to the tool before it went to the man, and he took it up as a man takes up an instrument already named. “Yes, house,” he said to the wood, and told it “Six counted. The next I don't count” in the tone of a man stating the weather. He set the small knife at the very edge of its sill, six in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check and the seventh at the lip where it could not, and Garl's unforged collector sentence lay beside it, the man sent for a collector who turned back at the frozen ford, with no name on the man and no figure on the debt. He checked the knife with his eyes after setting it with the flat of his hand.

The sentence about an ear under this roof being worth more than the debt that held it came to his teeth and went all the way to the word that would call the loan, and stopped there with no figure and no name. It was a remembered stopping and not a new one. Nine stoppings stood on Reed 5, 8, 11, 14, 17, 20, 23, 26 and 29, nine objects and not a rule, and no tenth stood up before the seventh year, which stood at its very end and had still not given. The Seed 16 breath was drawn at the top of the reach and not spent, and the loan was uncalled; no collector came up or went down the clay road for any of the three days of Smoke 7, 8 and 9. His chest had been tight since the dark. \lore{Ash} was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line and no scrubbing lifted it; the pale clip-ring at his left wrist held what light there was and gave it back. The warm stone Sella had given him in the winter Garl died lay cold in its unmarked pot in \place{the Debt Loft}, two years gone, and he had stopped trying to reconcile it with the debt, which was not forgiving. His stroke went over \place{the flagstones} past the shallow bright patch without the half-check and over the road a house sends a man down when it wants a collector, without once turning along it. Above the shed the loft settled and came to the boy as a direction and nothing more.

At the edge of \place{the Vail wheel-shed} \person{Jorra Vail} came through with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bent little finger and under the nails, white in the creases where a fist had opened, and not bone-coloured \lore{slip}. He kept dusty forearms and clean ones on one tray and parted them as a surgeon parts clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had been broken nor whether it was a birth-slate. Three or more men had gone low into \place{the back room} before first grey on Smoke 7, 8 and 9 and had got no name from him; the room kept its finger's width of open and its sound of low voices, and he did not say it was cold. The broken birth-slates lay under \place{the lowest rack} where the yard went around them and entered them into nothing.

He spoke to the wheel before he spoke to anyone. \emph{It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady}, he said, in the flat voice he used to the clay and the iron and only then to men, and told a pot \emph{You'll do} with no welcome in the sentence and no unkindness either. He knew \lore{the full note} that pot would give three turns before it left his hands, with air under the note and a want in it, and he withheld the knowing, keeping it on the near-full tray beside the hearings and never among them, advanced and not paid. \lore[the burned slate]{The burned-slate word} stood at four from \place{Slate Row}, a direction over the boards arrived nowhere, and the two tellings of \lore{the counting room} shut in the war stood at two and did not become three. Three shapings of the pot saying stayed three and no fourth likeness was made a cause, because a likeness made a cause wants a payer put under it and none stood to any. No name had been given to what ailed the man carried out on Day 188, and no figure, slate or collector had answered for him, and no pot had been lost. Four tellings were four and two tellings were two, and neither standing up and calling themselves an account would have made a witness stand anywhere.

When Sella's sentence went low and unfinished at the hearth, his bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper} ahead of it, took the cold out of the fingertip, held while the little silence ran, moved a hair's breadth and was left standing; the end of her instruction arrived at the pot and the household straightened around his silence the way cloth straightens when a weight is lifted. It was a remembered repetition paired with no other fraction, one piece of iron with two bites, the holding and the taking, and no \lore{Grathic} word for both mercies, joined to no kiln and entered in no slate. \emph{He's not Vail} stayed sealed as tightly as on the hour it was spoken, and the promise of the boy's name to the men who burn slates was neither given nor withdrawn. He did not look toward the bed or the basket, and the two silences under the roof stayed side by side through the going of the light and were not joined: the debt's stopped sentence at the kindling, the wheel-keeper's held finger at the iron, each on its own tray with a finger between.

At the close he turned out the file as a surgeon turns out instruments on a towel, and found it exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air. There was no towel, no instruments and no case, only the order of the laying, sharp by sharp and blunt by blunt, every edge where a hand would look for it first. Six hearings stood beside the full note and never among, with no seventh and none invented to fill the empty place, for a finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged. Three near-full answers stood advanced and unpaid. Three shapings of the pot saying stood as sayings and no fourth likeness had been made a cause. The fourth burned-slate telling stood on its own place and the two tellings of the room shut in the war stood at two and did not become three. The told bell lay beside the heard, the told figure beside the paid, with \person{Berr Clay}'s third subtraction of Grain locked in his box going up and down the clay road and answering nothing for the man carried out on Day 188 or for the hand that went in on Seed 15. Nine stoppings stood with no tenth before the seventh year, which stood at its very end and had still not given.

He laid the held changes out sharp by sharp: the Day 161 fraction, the Day 166 held beat, the Tallow 22 half-check, and beside them the loft-sill scrape on a fourth place, the carried man's pale line on a fifth, and the held inch on a sixth. The ninth record-first hand-shape stayed on a place of its own beside the eighth with a finger between it and that and between it and every other private keeping, the notched bark still in the firing woman's sleeve, unread, unasked about, joined to no reed-cutter's cord, no knot, no slate and no tin; nine likenesses were nine and must not stand up and call themselves a rule, for a rule is an account that has found a payer and none stood to any. The seventh green stood a colour at a silent woman's mouth and the seventh shape a shape in cast-off clothes, with no eighth of either and no name spoken over them; the sweet edge that had come over the boards on the wrong wind had gone out on the wind that brought it, a direction arrived nowhere and not a second edge paid, and he did not join it to the green or make a fourth likeness of the pot saying into a cause. The dressed grain lay covered and unfigured under the wool, changing state and gaining no figure, the figure Smoke 10 would read over it not knowing what it had cost. The pan's gone ring lay on its own tray with no scrape, crumb, print, chip or word. The returning-line place was gone around with a look and no number. He drew no finger between the crust's gap, the shelf's two gaps, the field's empty place out on \place{the worked strips} and the returning-line place; four absences on four trays, and a fourth likeness made a cause would want a payer and none stood to any.

The ownerless question kept its seventh place with five askings and no witness standing. The first four had come on Grain 21, 23, 26 and 29, the fifth on the afternoon of Smoke 8, each a voice with no owner, no room, no hand at the kiln and no cause, answered by the weather and the price of barley, gone off up the terrace with the row standing where it stood. He had gathered the fifth into the file without joining it, and put a finger between it and the three sayings, and a finger between it and the burned-slate word at its fourth telling, and a finger between it and the two shut-room tellings, and a finger between it and the six hearings standing beside and never among, and a finger between it and the count. Five askings are not one witness. A witness stands somewhere and can be asked, and none did and none could. It was not a sixth asking, not a fourth shaping of the pot saying, not a hearing, not a seventh of the full note, not a fifth telling of the burned slate, not a third telling of the shut room, not a cause, not a name and not a row named. The row stood where it stood with nobody to say which it was. Five likenesses must not stand up and call themselves an account, because an account wants a payer and no payer stood to any of them, and the pressure toward a doctrine went around them the way the yard goes around Ash.

He put the sixth question to his hand the sixth way of the chapter. The hand outside the wool went up an inch toward the low note through the finger's-width door and stayed. He kept it there through the small intake, the hold and the letting-go of one of the breaths Sella counted at her fire in the room that does not touch his, and kept it through the little silence after her count had ended. He brought the river's own low note into the inch and crossed it against the borrowed rhythm there and nowhere else, across a wall that does not touch, and then brought the hand down on the river's next low note. Two rhythms crossing in an inch of air were what they had been on the sixth morning of Smoke 6 and on the afternoon of Smoke 8: remembered, and not a change made again. It was not a longer reach, not a miss entered as a finding, not a hearing of the full note, not a seventh, not a tenth stopping, not a payment, not one of the held changes, not a key, not a power and not a rule, and not a sixth asking of the question over the boards. He filed answer seventy-two on the sixth place with no payer named, and did not spend it and did not explain it. Seventy-two answers stood there now, each an object and not a power, exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air.

Six months and sixty-eight days were told by nobody. The fire's fifth was unpaid two hundred and forty-eight days, its payer without a name; the seventh year stood at its very end and had still not given; the widow's mark hung where it hung and the dressed grain lay covered and unfigured; the five askings kept their five with nobody to own them and the ninth hand stayed where it was with a finger between it and the eighth. The hand came down on the river's next low note and laid itself against the fold like a tool put away, where the next hand would find it without looking. The file lay open on the towel, exact, complete and worth nothing. Under the floor the fire stopped at four, and the silence where the fifth should have stood stood where it stood. Two hundred and forty-eight stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in Sarn, Sill and Grath, and the count went forward into Smoke 10, a day that had not yet earned its heading.
