\daymark{246}{Smoke 7, Seventhday, Y.H. 813}

\dropcap{T}{he hand} lay against the fold where it had come to rest on the river's next low note, and \person[Corin]{the boy} let the coordination go the way a surgeon lets an instrument go at the end of a case --- not dropped, not put away with ceremony, but set down where the next hand will find it without looking. It had not been a reach. It had not been a hearing. It was the sixty-sixth answer on the sixth place, the last of two rhythms crossed in one inch of air across a wall that does not touch; now the inch was finished and the hand was a hand again, small, soft-nailed, outside the wool, on \place{the bed} at the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf}, with the fold at his ear and one crust lying two inches from it. He had once reduced a shoulder in the dark by feel alone, and now he could not lift that crust two inches to his mouth. Both were true. Neither stood up to call itself a sign, and the surgeon took the inventory of both and entered it nowhere.

The file lay open on the towel where he had left it, exact, complete and worth nothing. There was no towel, no instruments and no case, only the order of the laying, sharp by sharp and blunt by blunt, every edge where a hand would look for it first. Six hearings stood beside \lore{the full note} and never among, with no seventh and none invented to fill the empty place, for a finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged. Three near-full answers stood advanced and unpaid. Three shapings of the pot saying stood as sayings and no fourth likeness had been made a cause, because a likeness made a cause wants a payer and none stood to any. The fourth burned-slate telling stood on its own place and the two tellings of the room shut in the war stood at two and did not become three. The told bell lay beside the heard, the told figure beside the paid. Nine stoppings stood with no tenth before the seventh year, which stood at its very end and had still not given. The discipline was the whole of it, and a discipline is a thing a hand uses, not a thing a man believes.

He was two hundred and forty-five days old and six months and sixty-five days, and nobody in \place{Sarn}, \place{Sill} or \place{Grath} said either number in his hearing or over his head. The count had come down out of the dark exact and complete and unspent, and it went forward now into two hundred and forty-six, six months and sixty-six days, with no hand to enter it and no voice to say so. He had stopped expecting to be told, which is not the same as not wanting it, and the wanting went on under the expectation the way a coal goes on under ash. Smoke went on the way a firing goes, without a stroke drawn across it, neither of the district's two names for the months set right or left wrong in the telling. There was no market on a Seventhday, no \place[the Cross]{Cross} and no bell. Smoke 10 (Day 249), the first Account-day and market of the second firing, stood three mornings ahead and had not been brought into a day that had not earned it. A day not yet earned keeps its own counsel, and the yard kept it.

Hot clay came first under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof in \place{Low Kiln}, hot clay under tallow and wet wool under that, and under all three the sour \lore{Ash} breath of the unswept corner the house called \lore{the thaw}. The day's first sound was not a bell. Behind the wall the kiln drew in four low pulls and stopped where the fifth should have stood, and the stopping stood where it stood with nobody under any roof to put a name to what stood in it. He took the smells in the order they arrived and the sound in the order it did not, the way he had once taken a room in the dark: what was under the hand first, what was behind it second, and what the ear wanted to hear last. A man who had learned to find the bleeder before the bleed knew that the ear lies and the hand does not, and he had learned it here again in a house where the ear was told nothing and the hand was given everything it could not use.

One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from the ear. The empty place beside it kept its name to itself, and no third came to lie down and make the row come out even, because a kindness with a figure on it is the beginning of an account and this house had learned to give without entering. The thread of drool at his mouth had frozen in the dry heat and was unwiped, and would stay unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. Cold had stopped at cheek, temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, as though it had come to the end of a line written out for it and had neither an order to go further nor an order to go away. It went no further and it did not go away. The afterdrop waited beyond all three, past a warming nobody in this house would perform. He knew the severe face cold wears is a quiet face, knew a cold patient cannot clot, knew a man must be warmed from the outside and slowly. He knew all of it and wanted the crust. Both were true and the second was louder, because a child's hunger keeps no hours at all while the register goes on being exact inside a body that goes on being hungry.

Through the finger's-width door the river kept its note of \lore{water over water}, a fifth low thing never counted among the hearings. Behind the floor \lore{the carried fire} answered four low pulls with the silence where the fifth should have been, and its payer had been without a name for two hundred and forty-six days. Overhead \place{the boards} kept four nails on the near board, four on the next, and a thumb-width knot where the fifth nail should have stood. Fire under the floor, river through the door, nails overhead: three counts in three places, and none rose into another. A man who wanted them to agree would have to put a payer under every one, and no payer stood to any, so the boy left them on three trays and the trays lay where they lay. Three instruments that will not read against one another are still three instruments, and an instrument is a thing a hand uses, not a thing a man believes.

\scenebreak

\person{Sella Vail} went to the fire before she went to the basket. She told it the thaw. She turned the coals with the back of a broken pot, fed them, and said \emph{The house will keep it} where another woman would have said \emph{I}. Then she stood a moment with the back of the pot in her hand and counted under her breath toward no opening, small intake, hold, letting go, a count she kept toward nothing and had never offered to any ear under this roof. She pushed the coal-black hair back with the wrist that carries the burn so the grey streak showed pale off the coals, and the burn across the back of her hand, two straight sides and a lintel and a threshold, closed on nothing and opened again to the work. She did not look toward the bed.

\lore{the widow's mark} hung where it hung on its cord at her throat, a thumb-length of clay under \lore{lead glaze} with the Vail kiln scratched into it, and she did not touch it, because the hand goes to the mark before the kiln and there was no opening before the kiln on a Seventhday, only coals kept and a fire that wanted its fifth. The one opening on Smoke 1 lay behind the household and was not looked at again --- not as a sign, not as a lesson, not as a mending performed and not as a payment; no slate had been entered and no payer named. A shelf, a well, a rack, a road, a basket, an afternoon and a dusk were not openings, and she had touched the mark at none of them.

At \place{the drying racks} the dressed grain came out from under its wool for damp. She turned it under the wool, spread it thin on the flat stone, gathered it back by the handful and set the fold flat again, and the grain changed state and gained no figure. It was counted in glances and handfuls and by a carrying arm that knew weight and not cost; it was entered on no slate and laid across none of the absences. The figure read over the covered grain on Grain 30 was still not the figure paid, and the true subtraction stayed locked in \person{Berr Clay}'s box, going up and down \place{the clay road} and answering nothing for the man carried out on Day 188.

A neighbour at the threshold with a pot under her arm asked what the keeping would take now the grain was under wool, and Sella sent the question to the pot and the weather with nothing priced, refused or entered. \emph{Ask the pot what a wet thing costs set down there. The house has said it}, she said, and carried the unanswered question away level so nothing slopped. A man come down the clay road with dried pale scurf of mud on his coat skirt brought a number up out of a fist toward \place{the yard well}, looked at it, and put it back into his sleeve, so the figure arrived nowhere and was not a missing figure. A sleeve is not a slate, and a thing not priced is not therefore free but a thing whose payer has not been named. The water in \place{the trough} stood bare and dark and was called weather, and nobody took a figure off it.

\person{Garl Vail} was kept in objects and not in a story. The salt-stiff coat held a broader man's shape on its peg behind the door with the crease at the left shoulder where a man had once leaned to reach a shelf, and nobody straightened the crease and nobody took the coat down. The wheel-seat in the shed kept its twenty-year lean and its hollow on that side and no one had filled it. The Ash ridge had turned the corner and become a clean dark line, and beside it a finer hairline of grey had gathered, called the thaw, and both were left unswept, not because a lesson was made of them but because a line left where it is is still a line kept. A proverb of his had gone years ago to a pot with no crack in it, \emph{leave the crack showing; people trust a thing they can see has been handled}, and was said now over a corner where the repair had been rubbed out. The debt against a firing of tiles circled without a name, neither paid nor settled nor done with, and worse for being uncalled. His name had not been spoken under this roof for two hundred and forty-six days. No collector had come up the clay road for it and none had gone down, not on this Seventhday, and the road went up past \place{the terrace} toward \place{High Kiln} and gave the yard no figure while the yard gave the road none. The keeping of him was maintenance, and nobody had made a story out of it and nobody would.

\scenebreak

Before first grey the household came awake in the order it always did: the fire first, the pot second, the broom third, and the boy last, carried because he could not carry himself. Sella set him on the bed at the low angle of the shelf and then, when the light came thin through the oiled paper, carried him the few steps to \place{the kiln mouth} and set his basket where the heat off the brick came to him sideways, not full in the face. She gave the instruction to the heat and not to the basket: \emph{Keep it off his ears. It's the river wind}, she said to the wool, and did not look into the basket to see whether he had taken any of it. The kiln drew in four low pulls and stopped. The heat at the mouth was a thing with an edge to it, and he took its measure the way he took the measure of all heat in this house --- what it gave, what it withheld, and what stood behind it unpaid. At \place{the hearth} the coals held under their ash and gave back a low red that did not reach the far boards; the room that kept the fire and the room that kept the river did not touch, and the boy lay where the two almost met and belonged to neither.

Then he put the question to his hand the first way of the chapter. The hand outside the wool went up an inch toward the low note through the finger's-width door and came down at once on the river's note. It did not hold. It did not borrow a rhythm from the room that does not touch his. It went up and came down, the way a hand goes to a vessel and takes its answer in one small movement, and the answer was filed on the sixth place with no payer named. Sixty-seven answers stood there now, exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air. It was not a longer reach, a miss entered as a finding, a hearing of the full note, a seventh of it, a tenth stopping, a payment, a held change, a key, a power or a rule. He had once crossed a trauma bay at a run with a clamp in his fist, and now two inches of air lay between his hand and a crust he could not lift to his mouth; the two facts stood on one tray and neither was promoted into a finding.

The hand came down on the river's note and lay against the fold like a tool put away. The file lay open on the towel, exact, complete and worth nothing; under the floor the fire stopped at four, and the silence where the fifth should have stood stood where it stood. Two hundred and forty-six stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in Sarn, Sill or Grath; six months and sixty-six days were told by nobody; the fire's fifth was unpaid the same number of days, its payer without a name; the mark had been touched once before the kiln on Smoke 1 and at no shelf, well, rack, road, basket, morning or dusk on this Seventhday, because the hand goes to the mark before the kiln and at no other hour; the seventh year stood at its very end and had still not given. Smoke went on without a boundary drawn in the telling and neither of the district's two names for the months was set right or left wrong; there was no market, no Cross and no bell on a Seventhday, and Smoke 10 (Day 249), the first Account-day and market of the second firing, stood three mornings ahead and had not been brought into a day that had not earned it. The morning came on and found the house at its ordinary work, and the boy at two inches of air with the tray kept.
