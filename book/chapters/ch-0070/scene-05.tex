\daymark{263}{Smoke 24, Fourthday, Y.H. 813}

The small hours of Smoke 24 came on in their own colour, not the colour of the day before and not borrowed from it. \person[Corin]{The boy} lay under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof in \place{Low Kiln} with the hand resting against the fold where the hundredth answer had put it down, the room that does not touch the room of the fire keeping its own air about him. Smoke earned its morning by the light that made it, as a pot earns its name out of the firing and not out of the naming, and was not brought into a day that had not earned it. Two hundred and sixty-three stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in \place{Sarn}, \place{Sill} or \place{Grath}; six months and eighty-three days were told by nobody. He had stopped expecting to be told, which was not the same as not wanting it, and the wanting went on under the expectation the way a coal goes on under ash. No hand had come in to supply the carried warmth, because the house had not been instructed to supply it, and the house did not supply what it had not been told to supply. The third working week was going out as it had come in, on foot, with no bell to speed it and no slate to take its name down.

He took the body in the order the facts presented themselves, as a surgeon takes a field and asks only what is there. One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from his ear, and the empty place beside it kept its name to itself; no third had come in the night to make the row come out even. The thread of drool at his mouth had frozen in the dry heat and was unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. Cold had come to cheek and temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, and had stopped there as if a line had been written out to that length and no farther. The afterdrop waited beyond all three, past a warming nobody in this house would perform. He knew the composed face that is not composure; he knew a cold patient cannot clot and will seem better before he is worse. He wanted the crust lifted the two inches to his mouth. Both were true and the second was louder in a body of two hundred and sixty-three days, and the surgeon entered none of it, not even on the place where he kept the things he did not enter.

The first smell was hot clay under tallow, and under that wet wool sour with the long lodging of a body carried since before first grey, and under that the sour mineral breath of \lore{Ash} in the unswept corner the house called \lore{the thaw}. The first sound was \lore{the carried fire} drawing in fours under the floor with the silence where the fifth should have stood, not a bell. Through the finger's-width door the river kept its note of \lore{water over water}, low over lower; overhead \place{the boards} held four nails on the near board, four on the next, and a thumb-width knot where the fifth nail should have stood. Three counts in three places and none rose into another, because agreeing any two would want a payer set under both and none stood: a knot is not a nail, a river is not a fire, and a fire under a floor is not either of them. Six hearings of \lore{the full note} stood beside and never among, five from four sources and the sixth, the small flat knock out of a woman as the men lifted her; no seventh stood in the empty place and none had been invented to fill it, for a finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged. The fire's fifth was unpaid two hundred and sixty-three days and its payer had no name.

\lore{The widow's mark} hung where it hung, touched once before the first firing of Smoke and at no shelf, well, rack, road, basket, morning, afternoon or going of the light on Smoke 22, 23 or 24, because a yard, a rack, a well and a bed are not openings. Smoke 30 stood six mornings ahead and was not brought into a day that had not earned its light; there was no market, no Cross and no bell, and the first firing of Frost stood far off and unbrought. The second reading had changed the district's arithmetic a second time where two hundred people stood and gone home in a locked box, and the third working week had learned nothing from it, tending the same shelves and the same covered grain as though the square had never opened. The nothing learned was a finding with no payer, and he kept it as such, not as a lesson. Smoke went on without a boundary drawn and neither of the district's two names for the months was set right or left wrong.

\scenebreak

\person{Sella Vail} went to the fire before the basket. She told the coals it was the thaw and turned them with the back of a broken pot, and said \emph{The house will keep it} where another woman would have said \emph{I}. She stood a moment counting under her breath toward no opening --- the small intake, the hold, the letting-go --- and pushed coal-black hair back with the wrist that carries the burn, so that the one grey streak at her temple showed pale off the coals; the burn shaped like a little door closed on nothing. She glanced once at the clay jar cold under its glaze and shut her hand half over nothing. At \place{the loaf shelf} she kept her finger on top of the front edge so the charcoal tally beneath the board stayed unread, and moved her lips along a row standing with two gaps and gave neither gap a figure. She set the pot rim out with its handle two inches beyond the reaching hand, with a half-beat at the setting and no look toward \place{the bed}. Every instruction went to the fire, the pot, the wool and the water, and never to the basket.

The week went out in that grammar. The broom would be given its instruction before it was taken up and would go around the two lines in the corner without lifting either, the clean dark line where the Ash ridge had turned a year ago and the finer grey hairline the yard called the thaw. The charcoal would stay under the shelf's front edge, the fifth pull would stay missing, the loan would stay uncalled, no number would be spoken over the basket, and the crust would lie where it was put. Seeing it again on the week's last morning added nothing, and he kept the nothing as a finding, not a grievance, for a grievance is an account with a man standing under it and none stood. He did not lay the house's omissions across his own kept empty intervals with a finger between them, because a joining would want a payer put under both and none stood to either. The absence of the joining was itself kept, entered nowhere and not spent.

\scenebreak

Out at \place{the drying racks} the day stood warm and dry and went on without asking anything of the grain. The dressed grain lay covered under wool on the cool stone at \place{the rack end}, set away from \place{the hearth} where the heat would find it and take it; she turned it under the wool for damp and laid the fold back the way a hand lays a fold over a sleeping thing. The grain changed state and gained nothing. The arithmetic that would be read over it on Smoke 30 would not know what it had cost, because a figure read over a cover knows the cover and not what lies under it. That reading would be done perfectly, which was the reason it would not know, and the space between the figure and its price stayed unsigned.

Frost had gone out of the stone lip at \place{the yard well} under the bucket and the yard called the whole of it weather; nothing had been paid for the going and the calling joined one morning to no other. A wrong wind off \place{High Kiln} brought the sweet edge of \lore{lead glaze} over the boards, touched the fold at his ear and went out again on the wind that had brought it. It was a direction arrived nowhere: not a second edge paid, not a likeness of the man carried out of the glaze room on Day 188, not a fourth saying about a pot, and not a cause. He kept it on a place of its own and put no name over it. The sweetish taste stayed in the nose after the air went clean, and he made nothing of that either.

At the door a neighbour with a pot asked what the keeping and the storing would take now the grain was under wool and the visits coming, and was told to ask the pot what a wet thing costs set down there, because the house had said it. She went off level with nothing slopped and the question still on her. At the well a man come down \place{the clay road} brought a number up out of a fist toward the water, looked at it, and put it back into his sleeve, so that the figure arrived nowhere. It had not been withheld, only not delivered, and the boy kept the figure read and the figure paid on separate trays, with Smoke's second arithmetic up the road in a locked box and unread under this roof. A fist closed on a number is a chart turned face-down. No collector came up the clay road on Smoke 24 and none went down it.

\scenebreak

At \place{the kindling} the small knife lay at the very lip of its sill. \person{Tarn} had set six in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check and the seventh at the lip where it could not, and \person{Garl Vail}'s unforged collector sentence lay beside it --- the man sent for a collector who turned back at the frozen ford, with no name on the man and no figure on the debt, and the man had been Garl himself, which nobody had entered. Tarn took the broom only after the broom had been instructed. He said \emph{Yes, house} to an early thaw and to a late, agreeing with both so no argument found a foot, and kept the late because a late thaw puts off the paying, though the paying had stood in the ruts since the water went. He told the wood \emph{Six counted. The next I don't count} in the tone of a man stating the weather.

The sentence about an ear under this roof being worth more than the debt that held it came to his teeth and went as far as the word that would call the loan before it stopped, with no figure and no name. It was a remembered ninth stopping and not a new one; no tenth had stood up before the seventh year at its very end, and the seventh year had still not given. He drew the breath that should have gone in at the top of the reach and did not spend it, and his chest was tight as it had been since the dark. Ash was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line and no scrubbing lifted it; the pale clip-ring at his left wrist held what little light there was and gave it back. Up in \place{the Debt Loft}, which came to the boy only as a sound with a direction, the warm stone Sella had given him in the winter Garl died lay in its unmarked clay pot, two years gone and cold long since. He did not try it and did not forgive it; not trying is not forgiving.

At the edge of \place{the Vail wheel-shed} \person{Jorra Vail} came through with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bent little finger and white in the creases of a fist that had opened. He kept dusty forearms and clean ones on one tray and parted them as a surgeon parts clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had been broken nor whether it was a birth-slate. Three or more unnamed men had gone low into \place{the back room} before first grey and got no name from him, and he did not say the room was cold; the room kept its finger's width of open and its sound of low voices, which arrived at the basket as a direction and nothing more.

He spoke to the wheel before he spoke to any person. \emph{It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady.} He told a pot \emph{You'll do} in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either, and knew the full note that pot would give three turns ahead, with air under it and a want in it, and withheld the knowing on the near-full tray beside the hearings and never among them, advanced and not paid. The burned-slate word stood at four from \place{Slate Row}, a direction over the boards and not a fifth telling; the two tellings of the counting room shut in the war stood at two and did not become three; three sayings about a pot stood with the premature note from Smoke 14 on its own place and no fourth likeness made a cause. The promise of the boy's name to the men who burn slates was neither given nor withdrawn, and \emph{he's not Vail} stayed sealed as a lid nobody had tried to lift in three years.

When Sella's sentence went low --- \emph{The fire's low. It wants---} --- his bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper} ahead of it, took the cold out of the fingertip and held until the end arrived at the pot. The boy read the iron as the bite that holds a vessel whole and the bite that can take the hand off a man, mercy and mutilation in one object, joined to no kiln.

\scenebreak

At the going of the light she carried him up at the half-pace, went wide around the places with a look and a wide step, and set him on the bed under the roof and not in the Debt Loft. She put the fold right without a look at his face and left the thread at his mouth frozen and unwiped, her hand resting a half-beat longer than the setting needed. She told the fire it was the thaw, turned the coals with the back of a broken pot, said \emph{The house will keep it} where another woman would have said \emph{I}, counted under her breath toward no opening --- the small intake, the hold, the letting-go --- pushed coal-black hair back with the burn-wrist so the grey streak showed pale, and went to her own bed. The widow's mark hung where it hung, touched once before the kiln on Smoke 1 and at no shelf, well, rack, road, basket, yard, afternoon or going of the light on Smoke 24. The one opening before Smoke 1 lay behind the household, made into no lesson, no sign, no mending and no payment, with no payer named to it.

In the small hours he turned the file out on the towel as a surgeon turns instruments out at the beginning of a case, each thing laid where the next hand will find it without looking, and found it exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air. Six hearings stood beside and never among, five from four sources and the sixth, the small flat knock out of a woman as the men lifted her; the empty seventh place was left empty. Three near-full answers stood advanced and not paid, and beside them three shapings of the pot saying with the premature note from Smoke 14 on its own place and no fourth likeness made a cause. Four low things stood with water over water a fifth, told bell beside heard, told figure beside paid, with Smoke's second arithmetic up the road in a locked box and unread under this roof. Nine stoppings stood with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end; three held changes stood on three places, the loft scrape on a fourth and the pale line of the carried man on a fifth, and the held inch on a sixth with its answers standing on it. Nine record-first hands stood with a finger between every pair, the ninth taken from the firing work, her strip unread in her sleeve and joined to none. The dressed grain lay covered and unfigured at the rack end; the pan's gone ring, the shelf's two gaps and the fold's one crust with its gap lay on trays that do not touch; the ownerless question stood at five askings with no owner, no witness and no row named, and no sixth asking had come. The house's not-doings stood on a place of their own and his own kept gaps on another, with a finger between, and the finding that a not-doing keeps was entered nowhere and not turned into a rule.

He put the question to his hand. There was no ceremony in it and no answer he expected; a man tests a tool before he trusts it. Through the finger's-width door the river went on with its note, low over lower, and under the floor the fire kept its four low pulls with the silence where the fifth should have stood. There was no fire-sitter over the coals to supply a count and no house count to borrow. The hand outside the wool went up an inch toward the river's note and, with the inch held, came down on the river's next low note and not on the kiln's gap, down on the sound and not past it, keeping the three counts in three places that did not become one, for agreeing any two would want a payer set under both and none stood. It was an answer and not an asking. It was one hundred and one, filed on the sixth place beside one hundred with no payer named, exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air: not a longer reach, not a hearing of the full note, not a seventh of it, not a stopping, not a payment, not one of the held changes, not a key, a power or a rule. He made nothing of it. The hand lay back against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking.

Two hundred and sixty-three stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in Sarn, Sill and Grath; six months and eighty-three days were told by nobody. One hundred and one honest answers stood on the sixth place and none of them was a reach. The carried fire stopped at four with its fifth unpaid two hundred and sixty-three days and its payer unnamed; the widow's mark hung where it hung; the dressed grain lay covered and unfigured; the seventh year stood at its very end and had still not given. Smoke went on without a boundary drawn and Smoke 30 stood six mornings ahead and was not reached for, and the first firing of Frost stood far off and unbrought. The towel lay on nothing but the fold, with every tray parted and every gap left gap, and the count went forward into Smoke 25, a day that had not earned its heading. The hand came down on the river's next low note while the kiln under the floor kept its silence where the fifth should stand.
