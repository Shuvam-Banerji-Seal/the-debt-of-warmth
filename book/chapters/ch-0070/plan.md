# Chapter 70 — beat sheet: *What Nobody Tells the Hand*

**Frame.** Days 261–263, Smoke 22–24, Secondday to Fourthday, Y.H. 813. Normal chapter, three lived days, six scenes (two per day), each day opened by its own `\daymark` in the first scene of that day and each lived in full in well over 800 words. No Account-day, no market, no Cross and no bell in these three days; each day earns its own Smoke by its own light. Smoke 30 (Day 269), the third and last Account-day and market of the second firing, stands six mornings ahead and is not reached for. Frost 1 (Day 270) and its first firing (Day 271) stand far off and unbrought. Close third-person past tense; point of view never leaves Dan's eyes; no present-tense Chicago break, no `\memory{}` tag, no Court, collector, Waiting Room or back-room interior, no recap and no ending. The chapter's one meaningful change is small and interior: the boy registers that the house under the Vail roof keeps itself by **not-doings** — the two lines not swept, the mark not touched, the charcoal not read, the fifth pull not supplied, the loan not called, no number spoken over him, the crust not lifted — and keeps the seeing as a finding with no payer, not a grievance, a lesson or a doctrine. He does not lay the house's omissions across his own kept empty intervals, for a joining would want a payer and none stands.

**Counts carried into the first line.** 260 exact, six months and eighty days, told by nobody; ninety-six honest answers on the sixth place; fire's fifth unpaid 260 days, payer unnamed; six hearings beside and never among with no seventh; three near-full answers; three pot sayings with the premature note from Smoke 14 on its own place; burned slate at four; shut-room tellings at two; told bell beside heard, told figure beside paid; nine stoppings with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end and still ungiven; nine record-first hands with Ilva's ninth on its own tray, her strip unread; seventh green a colour and seventh shape a shape with no eighth; five askings of the row question with no witness and no row named; grain covered and unfigured on the cool stone at the rack end; widow's mark touched once before the kiln on Smoke 1 and at no other hour; Smoke's second arithmetic up the clay road in Berr's locked box and unread under this roof; Smoke 30 six mornings ahead.

**Count at close.** 263 exact, six months and eighty-three days, told by nobody; one hundred and two honest answers on the sixth place; fire's fifth unpaid 263 days; all other counts unchanged. Answers: **97, 98** on Day 261; **99, 100** on Day 262; **101, 102** on Day 263. Each is an honest coordination of the held inch, an object on the sixth place and not a reach, hearing, seventh, payment, stopping, held change, key, power or rule. On the 102nd the little silence after the count in the other room is kept **empty** on its own place, no rhythm carried across it, so an empty object is laid beside the held one and apart from it.

## Scene 1 — The Twenty-Second Morning

- **Day & time:** Day 261, Smoke 22, Secondday — small hours under the Vail roof, then before first grey and into the morning.
- **Place:** the Vail kiln-house: the bed at the low angle of the loaf shelf, the hearth, the kiln mouth, the boards, the drying racks, the cool stone at the rack end, the loaf shelf, the yard well.
- **Present:** Corin (carried, awake, silent); Sella Vail; walk-ons at the well and the door (a neighbour with a pot, a man with a number in his fist). Tarn and Jorra offstage until Scene 2. Old life offstage throughout.

**Beats, in order.**
1. First scene of the chapter: the only `\dropcap` and the first `\daymark{261}{...}` (Smoke 22, Secondday, Y.H. 813). Open in the small hours with the hand just laid down against the fold on the **ninety-sixth** answer; set it down as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking, never recalled as a sign, a growth, a reaching, a gift or a power.
2. Carry the count from 260 to **261** (six months and eighty-one days) exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody; the fire's fifth unpaid 261 days, payer unnamed. Do not say the number aloud in the house.
3. Three counts in three places, none rising into another: the kiln's four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should stand under the floor; the river's *water over water* through the finger's-width door; the boards' four nails, four and a thumb-width knot. Agreeing any two would want a payer put under both and none stands to any.
4. Body inventory in the old order: one stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust two inches from the ear, its empty place unnamed and no third; the thread of drool frozen in the dry heat and unwiped, because nobody under any roof has ever been given an instruction about the thread; cold stopped at cheek, temple and the bare wrist-joint where the wool rides back; the afterdrop waiting past a warming nobody in this house will perform. He knows the severe face cold wears, knows a cold patient cannot clot and will seem better before he is worse; he wants the crust. Both true, the second louder in a body of two hundred and sixty-one days, entered nowhere.
5. Sella goes to the fire before the basket: tells it the thaw, turns the coals with the back of a broken pot, says *The house will keep it* where another woman would say *I*, counts under her breath toward no opening (small intake, hold, letting go), pushes coal-black hair back with the burn-wrist so the one grey streak shows pale, glances once at the clay jar cold under its glaze and shuts her hand half over nothing.
6. At the loaf shelf: finger kept on top of the front edge so the charcoal tally stays unread; lips along a two-gapped row without giving either gap a figure; pot rim set out with its handle two inches beyond the reaching hand, a half-beat at the setting and no look toward the bed.
7. The dressed grain lies covered under wool on the cool stone at the rack end away from the hearth. She lifts a corner for damp and lays it back. It changes nothing and gains no figure, so that the arithmetic read over it on Smoke 30 still will not know what it cost. Keep the space between the figure and its price unsigned.
8. Weather: a thin white edge of frost has come on the north stone lip of the well; the bucket knocks it grey and the yard calls it weather. He keeps whatever white was there on a place of its own with no cause — not a payer, not the widow's mark (a well is not an opening), not the first firing of Frost, far off and unbrought.
9. A cost-question at the door goes to the pot and the weather; a man brings a number up out of a fist toward the well, looks at it and puts it back in his sleeve, so the figure arrives nowhere and none is supplied.
10. Smoke 30 stands six mornings ahead and is not brought into a day that has not earned it; no market, Cross or bell. Hand answer **97**: up an inch toward the low note, held for the small intake only, down before the letting-go, borrowing no house count. An object on the sixth place, exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air.

- **Emotional turn:** from the placed-down stillness of the ninety-sixth answer into the stubborn ordinary work of a new day's third working week; the boy registers that the covered grain still waits for a reading that will not know it, and that nobody finds this remarkable. No grievance with a payer, because none stands.
- **Sensory anchors:** hot clay under tallow; wet wool sour with lodging; the sour mineral Ash breath of the unswept corner called the thaw; the kiln drawing in fours with a gap; the river low over lower; a thin white frost edge on cold stone going grey under the bucket; the cold iron of the well rope; the pot handle two inches out with the half-beat gone.
- **Threads advanced:** T071/T072 (261 exact, six months and eighty-one days); T088 (fifth unpaid 261 days); T061/T082 (answer 97, file, three counts apart, six hearings no seventh); T077 (grain covered, unfigured); T058/T079 (mark untouched; no opening at a well); T100 (charcoal unread); T083/T085 (frost called weather, joined to nothing); T015/T041/T047 (figure to sleeve, arrived nowhere); T060/T086 (one crust, unnamed gap, no third).
- **Foreshadowing planted:** Smoke 30's reading stands six mornings ahead; the covered grain waits for a figure that will not know its cost; the white lip is carried toward Frost without pulling Frost into this day. Nothing opened, called or brought early.
- **Exact last image:** the covered grain under wool at the rack end with the corner laid back and no figure on it; the pot handle two inches beyond the reaching hand; the hand resting against the fold.

## Scene 2 — The Places Left Undone
