\daymark{262}{Smoke 23, Thirdday, Y.H. 813}

The small hours came on in their own colour, not the colour of the day before and not a colour borrowed from it. Smoke 23 earned its morning by the light that made it, the way a pot earns its name out of the firing and not out of the naming, and it was not brought back into a day that had not earned its own. \person[Corin]{The boy} lay under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof in \place{Low Kiln} with the hand resting where the ninety-eighth answer had put it down, the room that does not touch the room of the fire keeping its own air about him. Two hundred and sixty-two stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in \place{Sarn}, \place{Sill} or \place{Grath}; six months and eighty-two days were told by nobody. He had stopped expecting to be told, which was not the same as not wanting it, and the wanting went on under the expectation the way a coal goes on under ash, giving out nothing and going out not at all. The carried warmth had not come back in the night and no hand had come in to supply it; the house had not been instructed to supply it, and the house did not supply what it had not been told to supply.

He took the body in the order the facts presented themselves, as a surgeon takes a field and asks only what is there. One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from his ear, gone past the point where a child would have eaten it, and the empty place beside it kept its name to itself; no third had come in the night to make the row come out even. The thread of drool at his mouth had frozen in the dry heat and was unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. Cold had come to cheek and temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, and had stopped there as if a line had been written out to that length and no farther; it neither went forward nor went away. The afterdrop waited beyond all three, past a warming nobody in this house would perform. He knew the composed face that is not composure. He knew a cold patient cannot clot and will seem better before he is worse. He wanted the crust lifted the two inches to his mouth. Both were true and the second was louder in a body of two hundred and sixty-two days, and the surgeon took the inventory and entered none of it, not even on the place where he kept the things he did not enter.

The first smell was hot clay under tallow, and under that wet wool sour with the long lodging of a body carried in it since before first grey, and under that the sour mineral breath of \lore{Ash} in the unswept corner that the house called \lore{the thaw}. The first sound was \lore{the carried fire} drawing in fours under the floor with the silence where the fifth should have stood, not a bell. Through the finger's-width door the river kept its note of \lore{water over water}, low over lower, going on with its own business; overhead \place{the boards} held four nails on the near board, four on the next, and a thumb-width knot where the fifth nail should have stood. Three counts in three places and none rose into another, because agreeing any two would want a payer set under both and no payer stood to any: a knot is not a nail, a river is not a fire, and a fire under a floor is not either of them. Six hearings of \lore{the full note} stood beside and never among, five from four sources and the sixth, the small flat knock out of a woman as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go and never with the things that came from vessels. No seventh stood in the empty place and none had been invented to fill it, for an empty place kept empty is a finding and a finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged. The fire's fifth was unpaid two hundred and sixty-two days and its payer had no name.

\lore{The widow's mark} hung where it hung on its cord at her throat, a thumb-length of lead-glazed clay with the Vail kiln scratched into it, touched once before the first firing of Smoke and at no shelf, well, rack, road, basket, morning, afternoon or going of the light on Smoke 22 or 23. A yard is not an opening and a rack is not an opening and a well is not an opening and a bed is not an opening; the mark had stayed where it stayed for that reason and for no reason anybody had spoken aloud. Smoke 30 stood seven mornings ahead and was not brought into a day that had not earned its light, and there was no market, no Cross and no bell in this one; the first firing of Frost stood far off and unbrought. Smoke went on without a boundary drawn across itself and neither of the district's two names for the months was set right or left wrong.

\scenebreak

\person{Sella Vail} went to the fire before the basket. She told the coals it was the thaw and turned them with the back of a broken pot, and said \emph{The house will keep it} where another woman would have said \emph{I}. She stood a moment with the back of the pot in her hand counting under her breath toward no opening --- the small intake, the hold, the letting-go --- and pushed a strand of coal-black hair back with the wrist that carries the burn, so that the one grey streak at her temple showed pale off the coals; the burn, shaped like a little door with two straight sides and a lintel and a threshold, closed on nothing and was not opened to show anybody. She glanced once at the clay jar cold under its glaze and shut her hand half over nothing. At \place{the loaf shelf} she kept her finger on top of the front edge so that the charcoal tally beneath the board stayed unread, and moved her lips along a row standing with two gaps and gave neither gap a figure. She set the pot rim out with its handle two inches beyond the reaching hand, with a half-beat at the setting and no look toward the bed. Every instruction went to the fire, the pot, the wool and the water, and never to the basket; she carried the basket at the half-pace from every open slate and said the house where another woman would have said I.

It was in the plain work of the morning that he saw it, and it was no revelation, only a thing laid out the way instruments are laid out on a towel --- complete, exact and worth nothing at two inches of air. He had spent two hundred and sixty-two days watching what the house did. Now, in the turning of the coals and the setting of the pot and the going-around of the two lines in the corner, he saw what the house did not do. The broom was taken up in the afternoon and the two lines were left where they lay: the clean dark line where the Ash ridge had turned its corner a year ago, and the finer grey beside it that the house called the thaw, neither swept, and no sweeping given an instruction. The widow's mark hung at her throat and was not touched, because a morning is not an opening. The charcoal tally lay under the shelf's front edge and was not read, because a finger kept on top of a board has not gone beneath it. The fire under the floor gave four pulls and was not supplied a fifth; the debt against a firing of tiles went around the household and was not called; no number was spoken over the basket in a morning or an afternoon or a going of the light; the crust in the fold lay two inches from his ear and was not lifted. The house kept itself by leaving things where they lay. He had thought keeping was a doing. Here it was a not-doing, as exact as a clamp left in place and as hard to put a hand under.

He kept the seeing as a finding with no payer. He did not make a grievance of it, for a grievance is an account with a man standing under it and none stood. He did not make a lesson of it, or a doctrine, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood to any of these. And he did not lay the house's omissions across his own kept empty intervals --- his one crust with its unnamed place, his silent fifth, his loan that had never been called --- with a finger between the two places, because a joining would want a payer put under both and none stood to either. The absence of the joining was itself kept, and entered nowhere, and not spent. A man could notice a whole household's omissions and could not spend one of them; that was the whole of it, and there was no payer to make it more.

\scenebreak

Out at \place{the drying racks} the day stood warm and dry and went on without asking anything of the grain. The dressed grain lay covered under wool on the cool stone at \place{the rack end}, set away from the hearth where the heat would find it and take it; she lifted a corner for damp and put the wool back. The grain changed state and gained nothing. No figure went on it, and the arithmetic that would be read over it on Smoke 30 would not know what the grain had cost, because a figure read over a cover knows the cover and not what lies under it. The work of that reading would be done perfectly, which was the reason it would not know; the space between the figure and its price stayed unsigned.

At \place{the yard well} \person{Ilva} was glimpsed once, drawing with the pail against her knee. Clay was dried pale in the crescents of her nails and the finer grey rack dust was worked into the seam of one thumb, and she did not scrub the seam out. When a woman with a pot asked what the firings of Smoke had cost, her hand went toward her sleeve and stayed there. Her eyes went to the asker and only then did her mouth open, and it answered the question put and not the one behind it.

“Ask the pot what it cost. I keep the count, not the account.”

The notched strip of birch stayed where it was, not taken out, not read, not asked about, not joined to the reed-cutter's cord turned so its knots stood, or to the knots, the six pages, the tins, the charcoal under the shelf or any other private keeping. She went on with her water and nobody turned to look at her twice. The boy filed no tenth record-first hand; the ninth stayed on its own tray with a finger between it and the eighth, nine objects and not a rule, for a rule is an account that has found a payer and none stood. No figure came off the stick and the firings stood in wood where no clerk's thumb could read them. She did not look into the basket and did not know she had been read.

The neighbour with the pot put her cost-question to the pot instead of to the woman, and went off level with nothing slopped, the question still on her and nothing priced, refused or entered. A man come down \place{the clay road} brought a number up out of a fist toward the well, looked at it, and put it back into his sleeve, so that the figure arrived nowhere; it had not been withheld, only not delivered, and the boy kept the two things on separate trays, the figure read and the figure paid, with Smoke's second arithmetic up the road in a locked box and unread under this roof. The white edge that had stood on the north stone lip of the well was gone grey under the bucket, and the yard called the whole of it weather. He kept whatever white had been there on a place of its own with no cause --- not a payer, not the widow's mark, not the first firing of Frost far off and unbrought. Frost had gone out of a well lip under a bucket on other mornings and the yard had called it weather then too, and the calling did not join one morning to another.

\scenebreak

At the going of the light she carried him up at the half-pace, went wide around the places with a look and a step that asked nothing of them, and set him on \place{the bed} under the roof and not in \place{the Debt Loft}. She put the fold right without a look at his face and left the thread at his mouth frozen and unwiped, her hand resting a half-beat longer at the setting than the setting needed before it went away. She told the fire it was the thaw, turned the coals with the back of a broken pot, said \emph{The house will keep it}, stood a moment counting under her breath toward no opening --- the small intake, the hold, the letting-go --- and went to her own bed. The widow's mark hung where it hung, touched once before the kiln on Smoke 1 and at no shelf, well, rack, road, basket, yard, afternoon or going of the light on Smoke 22 or 23. The one opening before Smoke 1 lay behind the household, made into no lesson, no sign, no mending and no payment, with no payer named to it.

In the small hours he turned the file out on the towel as a surgeon turns instruments out at the beginning of a case, each thing laid where the next hand will find it without looking, and found it exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air. Six hearings stood beside and never among, five from four sources and the sixth, the small flat knock out of a woman as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go; the empty seventh place was left empty and none invented to fill it. Three near-full answers stood advanced and not paid, and beside them three shapings of the pot saying with the premature note from Smoke 14 on its own place and no fourth likeness made a cause. The heard bell stood beside the told, and the told figure beside the paid. Nine stoppings stood with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end; three held changes stood on three places, the loft scrape on a fourth and the pale line of the carried man on a fifth, and the held inch on a sixth with its answers standing on it. Nine record-first hands stood with a finger between every pair, the ninth hers, taken from the firing work and not from any house or Court, joined to none. The ownerless question stood at five askings with no owner, no room, no hand at the kiln, no witness and no row named, and no sixth asking had come. The house's not-doings stood on a place of their own, and his own kept gaps stood on another, with a finger between, and the finding that a not-doing keeps as surely as a doing was entered nowhere and not turned into a rule.

He put the question to his hand. There was no ceremony in it and no answer he expected; a man tests a tool before he trusts it, and the testing is the whole of the work. Through the finger's-width door the river went on with its note, low over lower, and the wall between that sound and the bed did not touch anything but itself. There was no fire-sitter over the coals to supply a count and no house count to borrow. The hand outside the wool went up an inch toward the low note and came down before the letting-go of a borrowed count from the room that does not touch, borrowing no stroke and filling nothing to make any row come out even. It was an answer and not an asking. It was ninety-nine, filed on the sixth place beside ninety-eight with no payer named, exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air: not a longer reach, not a miss entered as a finding, not a hearing of the full note, not a seventh of it, not a stopping, not a payment, not one of the held changes, not a key, a power or a rule. He made nothing of it. The hand lay back against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking.

Two hundred and sixty-two stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in \place{Sarn}, \place{Sill} or \place{Grath}; six months and eighty-two days were told by nobody. Ninety-nine honest answers stood on the sixth place and none of them was a reach. The carried fire stopped at four with its fifth unpaid two hundred and sixty-two days and its payer unnamed; the widow's mark hung where it hung; the dressed grain lay covered and unfigured; the seventh year stood at its very end and had still not given. Smoke went on without a boundary drawn and Smoke 30 stood seven mornings ahead and was not reached for, and the first firing of Frost stood far off and unbrought. The towel lay on nothing but the fold, with every tray parted and every gap left gap, and the count went forward into Smoke 24, a day that had not earned its heading. The hand rested against the fold on the ninety-ninth answer and asked to be told nothing.
