## Chronicle

On the twenty-second morning of Smoke the house under the Vail roof took the hand back as a bench takes back a tool. Sella Vail went to the fire before the basket, told the coals it was the thaw, turned them with a broken pot and said the house would keep it where another woman would have said I; she kept her finger above the charcoal and set the pot handle two inches out. The boy came into his two hundred and sixty-first day and nobody said so, the fire's fifth unpaid the same number. Fire, river and boards kept three counts that did not become one, for agreeing any two would want a payer and none stood. Frost wrote white on the well lip and the bucket knocked it grey and the yard called it weather; a fist came up toward the well and went back into a sleeve. The hand held for the small intake (ninety-seven), and in the small hours through one whole interval of the river (ninety-eight).

On the twenty-third the boy saw what the house did not do. The lines were not swept, the mark not touched, the charcoal not read, the fifth pull not supplied, the loan not called, no number spoken over the basket, the crust not lifted. The house kept itself by leaving things where they lay. He kept it as a finding with no payer: no grievance, no lesson, no doctrine, and did not lay the house's omissions across his own kept empty intervals, for a joining would want a payer and none stood. Ilva came once to the well, answering that she kept the count, not the account; the birch stayed unread and no tenth hand was filed. The hand came down before the letting-go of a borrowed count (ninety-nine), then carried one rhythm across the silence after a count in the other room (one hundred).

On the twenty-fourth the week went out having learned nothing from the reading, and the nothing learned was kept as a finding. A wrong wind off High Kiln brought the sweet edge of lead glaze over the boards, touched the fold and went out, arrived nowhere. Sella turned the dressed grain under wool for damp with no figure; the arithmetic read over it would not know its cost. Tarn brought the ear-sentence to the word that would call the loan and stopped, a remembered ninth stopping with no tenth before the seventh year at its end. The hand came down on the river's note and not on the kiln's gap (one hundred and one). On the last answer it left the silence after the count in the other room whole and empty on its own place, an empty object beside the held one, a keeping and not a miss (one hundred and two). Two hundred and sixty-three stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody; the hand lay back against the fold.
