The afternoon of Smoke 24 thinned without ending, the way heat goes out of a pot set off the coals and leaves the pot still warm to the back of the hand. \person[Corin]{The boy} lay where she had set him under the roof of \place{the Vail kiln-house} in \place{Low Kiln}, with the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf} over him and the room that does not touch the room of the fire keeping its own air about him. Smoke went on without a boundary drawn, and neither of the district's two names for the months was set right or left wrong; a day earns its name out of its own light, as a pot earns its name out of the firing and not out of the naming. Heat drew back from the fold and settled behind the pot and into the wool. One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from his ear, and the empty place beside it kept its name to itself and no third came in the afternoon to make the row come out even. The thread of drool at his mouth had frozen in the dry heat and was unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread, and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. Cold had come to cheek and temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, and had stopped there as though a line had been written out for it to that length and no farther. The afterdrop waited beyond all three, past a warming nobody in this house would perform. He knew the composed face that is not composure; he knew a cold patient cannot clot and will seem better before he is worse, and that a man must be warmed from the outside and slowly. He wanted the crust lifted the two inches to his mouth. Both were true and the second was louder in a body of two hundred and sixty-three days, and the surgeon entered none of it, not even on the place where he kept the things he did not enter.

\person{Garl Vail} was kept in objects and not in a story. The salt-stiff coat held a broader man's shape on its peg behind the door with the crease at the left shoulder where the wheel had leaned on him for twenty years; the wheel-seat in the shed kept its hollow on that side and nobody filled it. The proverb about leaving the crack showing had gone years ago to a pot with no crack in it and was said now over a corner where the repair had been rubbed out, a saying about honest repairs said to a thing with none. The debt against a firing of tiles went around the house in a careful circle and got no name, neither paid nor settled nor done with, and worse for being uncalled. His name had not been spoken under this roof in two hundred and sixty-three days. No collector came up \place{the clay road} on Smoke 22, 23 or 24 and none went down it; the keeping of him was maintenance, and a line left where it lay was still a line kept.

\person{Sella Vail} carried the basket at the half-pace from every open slate and went wide around seven things, each with a look and a wide step and no number. The clean dark line at the Vail corner where the \lore{Ash} ridge had turned a year ago; the finer grey hairline beside it that the yard called \lore{the thaw}; the space beneath \place{the lowest rack} where the broken birth-slates lay; the round ring where the shallow clay pan had stood, with no scrape, no crumb, no print and no word to mark it; \place{the yard well} with \place{the trough} bare and dark and its water called weather; the field's empty place out on \place{the worked strips}; and the returning-line place where a hand had gone in on Seed 15 and had not come out. Seven in a row were seven and not an account, because an account wants a payer standing under it and none stood. The hand that had gone in and not come back was carried and not named, not paid and not joined to the shelf's two gaps in the other room or to the one crust in the fold with its gap beside it. She went around the rack space and the gone place as wide as a pail of hot water set where a child might knock it, and gave the instruction to the bucket and the broom and never to the basket.

At the going of the light she carried him up at the half-pace, went wide around the places with a look and a wide step, and set him on \place{the bed} under the roof and not in \place{the Debt Loft}. She put the fold right without a look at his face and left the thread at his mouth frozen and unwiped, her hand resting a half-beat longer than the setting needed before it went away. She told \place{the hearth} it was the thaw and turned the coals with the back of a broken pot, said \emph{The house will keep it} where another woman would have said \emph{I}, and stood a moment counting under her breath toward no opening --- the small intake, the hold, the letting-go --- and pushed coal-black hair back with the wrist that carries the burn so that the one grey streak at her temple showed pale. The burn was shaped like a little door closed on nothing. She went to her own bed. \lore{The widow's mark} hung where it hung, touched once before the kiln on Smoke 1 and at no shelf, well, rack, road, basket, yard, afternoon or going of the light on Smoke 24. The one opening before Smoke 1 lay behind the household, made into no lesson, no mending, no sign and no payment, with no payer named to it. The next real opening stood far off in the first firing of Frost, and was not brought into a day that had not earned its own light. Frost had gone out of the well lip under the bucket and the yard called the whole of it weather.

At \place{the kindling} the small knife lay at the very lip of its sill. \person{Tarn} had set six in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check and the seventh at the lip where it could not, and Garl's unforged collector sentence lay beside it --- the man sent for a collector who turned back at the frozen ford, with no name on the man and no figure on the debt. Tarn took the broom only after the broom had been instructed. He said \emph{Yes, house} to an early thaw and to a late, agreeing with both so thoroughly that no argument found a foot, and kept the late because a late thaw puts off the paying, though the paying had stood in the ruts since the water went. He told the wood \emph{Six counted. The next I don't count} in the tone of a man stating the weather.

The sentence about an ear under this roof being worth more than the debt that held it came to his teeth and went as far as the word that would call the loan before it stopped, with no figure and no name. It was a remembered ninth stopping and not a new one; no tenth had stood up before the seventh year at its very end, and the seventh year had still not given. He drew the breath that should have gone in at the top of the reach and did not spend it, and his chest was tight as it had been since the dark. \lore{Ash} was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line and no scrubbing lifted it; the pale clip-ring at his left wrist held what little light there was and gave it back. Up in the Debt Loft, which came to the boy only as a sound with a direction and never as a room he was taken into, the warm stone Sella had given him in the winter Garl died lay in its unmarked clay pot, two years gone and cold long since. He did not try it and did not forgive it. Not trying is not forgiving. The loan stayed uncalled and no collector came up the clay road for it or went down it.

At the edge of \place{the Vail wheel-shed} \person{Jorra Vail} came through with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bent little finger and white in the creases of a fist that had opened, and not bone-coloured slip. He kept dusty forearms and clean ones on one tray and parted them as a surgeon parts clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had been broken nor whether it was a birth-slate. The broken birth-slates lay beneath the lowest rack where the yard went around them and entered them into nothing. Three or more unnamed men had gone low into \place{the back room} before first grey and got no name from him, and he did not say the room was cold; the room kept its finger's width of open and its sound of low voices, which arrived at the basket as a direction and nothing more.

He spoke to the wheel before he spoke to any person. \emph{It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady.} He told a pot \emph{You'll do} in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either, and knew the \lore{full note} that pot would give three turns ahead, with air under it and a want in it, and withheld the knowing on the near-full tray beside the hearings and never among them, advanced and not paid. The burned-slate word stood at four from \place{Slate Row}, a direction over the boards and not a fifth telling; the two tellings of the counting room shut in the war stood at two and did not become three; three sayings about a pot stood with the premature note from Smoke 14 on its own place and no fourth likeness made a cause. The promise of the boy's name to the men who burn slates was neither given nor withdrawn, and \emph{he's not Vail} stayed sealed as a lid nobody had tried to lift in three years. No pot was lost and no name had been given to what ailed the man carried out of the glaze room on Day 188.

When Sella's sentence went low --- \emph{The fire's low. It wants---} --- his bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper} ahead of it, took the cold out of the fingertip and held until the end arrived at the pot. The boy read the iron as the bite that holds a vessel whole and the bite that can take the hand off a man, mercy and mutilation in one object, with no \lore{Grathic} word for both, joined to no kiln. A wrong wind off \place{High Kiln} had brought the sweet edge of \lore{lead glaze} over the boards earlier and it had gone out on the wind that brought it, a direction arrived nowhere and not a likeness of anything.

In the small hours he turned the file out on the towel as a surgeon turns instruments out at the beginning of a case, each thing laid where the next hand will find it without looking, and found the whole of it exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air. Six hearings stood beside and never among, five from four sources and the sixth, the small flat knock out of a woman as the men lifted her; the empty seventh place was left empty and none was invented to fill it, for a finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged. Three near-full answers stood advanced and not paid, and beside them three shapings of the pot saying with the premature note from Smoke 14 on its own place and no fourth likeness made a cause. Four low things stood with \lore{water over water} a fifth; told bell stood beside heard and told figure beside paid, with Smoke's second arithmetic gone up the road in a locked box and unread under this roof, and the figure read over the covered grain still not the figure paid, the space between them unsigned. Nine stoppings stood with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end; three held changes stood on three places, the loft scrape on a fourth and the pale line of the carried man on a fifth, and the held inch on a sixth with its answers standing on it. Nine record-first hands stood with a finger between every pair, the ninth taken from the firing work, her strip unread in her sleeve and joined to none; the seventh green stood a colour and the seventh shape a shape, with no eighth of either. The dressed grain lay covered and unfigured on the cool stone at \place{the rack end}; the pan's gone ring, the shelf's two gaps and the fold's one crust with its gap lay on trays that do not touch. The ownerless question stood at five askings with no owner, no witness and no row named, and no sixth asking had come. The house's not-doings stood on a place of their own and his own kept gaps on another, with a finger between, and the finding that a not-doing keeps was entered nowhere and not turned into a rule.

He put the question to his hand. There was no ceremony in it and no answer he expected; a man tests a tool before he trusts it. Through the finger's-width door the river went on with its note, low over lower, and under the floor the carried fire kept its four low pulls with the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer unnamed. There was no fire-sitter over the coals to supply a count and no house count to borrow. In the room that does not touch, a count had ended some time before and a little silence had stood after it, and on another day he had carried one rhythm across that empty interval as an object in its own right. This time he did not.

The hand outside the wool went up an inch toward the river's note and, with the inch held for the small intake only, came down on the river's next low note and not on the kiln's gap, down on the sound and not past it, borrowing no stroke and filling nothing to make a row come out even. The little silence after the count in the other room he left where it lay, whole and empty on a place of its own beside the held one and apart from it, no rhythm carried across it and nothing supplied to make a stroke in a row that had none. An empty object laid beside the held one and apart from it. A keeping and not a miss, not a longer reach, not a hearing of the full note, not a seventh of it, not a stopping, not a payment, not one of the held changes, not a key, a power or a rule. He made nothing of the likeness and would not let the two stand up as a rule between them, because a rule is an account that has found a payer and none stood to either.

It was one hundred and two, filed on the sixth place beside one hundred and one with no payer named, exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air. The hand lay back against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking.

Two hundred and sixty-three stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in \place{Sarn}, \place{Sill} and \place{Grath}; six months and eighty-three days were told by nobody. Ninety-six honest answers had become one hundred and two and none of them was a reach. The carried fire stopped at four with its fifth unpaid two hundred and sixty-three days and its payer unnamed; the widow's mark hung where it hung; the dressed grain lay covered and unfigured; the seventh year stood at its very end and had still not given. Smoke went on without a boundary drawn and Smoke 30 stood six mornings ahead and was not reached for, and the first firing of Frost stood far off and unbrought. The towel lay on nothing but the fold, with every tray parted and every gap left gap. The little silence in the other room kept its emptiness and the file kept its honest objects, and the count went forward into Smoke 25, a day that had not earned its heading. The hand came to rest against the fold on the hundred and second answer while the kiln under the floor kept its silence where the fifth should stand.
