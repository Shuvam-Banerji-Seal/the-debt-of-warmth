Afternoon went out of the yard the way heat goes out of a pot set off the coals --- not in an instant, not with a sound, but by degrees, the warmth drawing back from the lip and settling into the belly of the thing and staying there a long while after the hand had done with it. The light thinned without ending. It drew no boundary across itself and called the next hour by another name; neither of the district's two names for the months was set right or left wrong, and Smoke went on the way smoke goes on, without a seam in it for a man to put his finger under. Under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof the heat that had stood all morning behind the pot came round into the fold and lay there, and the wool smelled sour with the long lodging of a body carried in it since before first grey. \person[Corin]{the boy} lay with the hand resting where the ninety-seventh answer had put it down, and the room took the hand back the way a bench takes back a tool, without comment and without expecting it to have learned anything.

He took the body in the order the facts presented themselves, as a surgeon takes a field and asks only what is there. One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from his ear, gone past the point where a child would have eaten it, and the empty place beside it kept its name to itself; no third had come in the afternoon to make the row come out even. The thread of drool at his mouth had frozen in the dry heat and was unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. Cold had come to cheek and temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, and had stopped there as if a line had been written out to that length and no farther; it neither went forward nor went away. The afterdrop waited beyond all three, past a warming nobody in this house would perform. He knew the composed face that is not composure. He knew a cold patient cannot clot and will seem better before he is worse. He wanted the crust lifted the two inches to his mouth. Both were true and the second was louder in a body of two hundred and sixty-one days, and the surgeon took the inventory and entered none of it, not even on the place where he kept the things he did not enter.

Through the finger's-width door the river kept its note of \lore{water over water}, low over lower, going on with its own business. Under the floor \lore{the carried fire} gave four low pulls and kept the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer without a name for two hundred and sixty-one days. Overhead \place{the boards} held four nails on the near board, four on the next, and a thumb-width knot where the fifth nail should have stood. Three counts in three places and none rose into another, because agreeing any two would want a payer set under both and no payer stood to any; a knot is not a nail and a river is not a fire and a fire under a floor is not either of them. He did not make the day's thinned light a fourth thing to set beside them. Six hearings of \lore{the full note} stood beside and never among, five from four sources and the sixth, the small flat knock out of a woman as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go and never with the things that came from vessels. No seventh stood in the empty place and none had been invented to fill it, for an empty place kept empty is a finding and a finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged.

\person{Garl Vail} was kept in objects and not in a story. His salt-stiff coat held a broader shoulder's outline on the peg behind the door with the crease at the left shoulder still where a man had set his weight against a bench to reach a high shelf; the wheel-seat in the shed kept its twenty-year lean and its hollow on that side, which no weight of another man had ever filled. His proverb about leaving the crack showing had gone years ago to a pot with no crack in it and was said still over a corner where the repair had been rubbed out, a saying about honest repairs outliving the thing it was true about. The debt against a firing of tiles went around the household's talk as a woman goes around \lore{Ash} left in a corner, circled without a name and worse for being uncalled, neither paid nor settled nor done with. No collector had come up \place{the clay road} on Smoke 22 and none had gone down it, and his name had not been spoken under this roof in two hundred and sixty-one days. The keeping of him was maintenance. A line left where it is is still a line kept.

\person{Sella Vail} carried the basket at the half-pace from every open slate, and she gave the instruction to the wool and the water and never to the basket. Out in \place{the Clay Yard} she went wide around seven things with a look and a wide step and no number spoken over any of them. She went around the clean dark line where the Ash ridge had turned its corner a year ago and the new finer hairline beside it that the house called \lore{the thaw}, and she swept neither, because a line left where a house has left it is a keeping. She went around the space beneath \place{the lowest rack} where the broken birth-slates lay, gone around as wide as a pail of hot water set where a child might knock it, and the yard entered those slates into nothing. She went around the round ring where the shallow clay pan had once stood, with no scrape, crumb, print, chip or word left in it to say what had gone. She went around \place{the trough} at \place{the yard well}, bare and dark, with its water called weather by everybody who drew from it, and around the field's empty place out on \place{the worked strips}, where hands had gone in a month ago and the strip stood as it stood. Last she went around the place in the returning line where a hand had gone in on Seed 15 and had not come out, and she gave it the look and the wide step she gave the others and no figure and no name.

Seven in a row are seven. They are not an account until a payer stands under them, and none stood. He kept them as seven places on seven trays with a finger between every pair and did not lay the pan's gone ring across the shelf's two gaps or the fold's one crust, did not lay the field's empty place across the empty place at his ear, did not lay the hand that had not come back across any of them. Seven absences in the yard are seven absences and make no eighth, and a row of them standing up and calling itself a debt would want somebody to answer for it, and nobody here had been told there was a debt to answer.

\scenebreak

The afternoon came to \place{the kindling} as a voice before it came to a person. \person{Tarn} took the broom only after it had been instructed, the way a man takes up a tool already named, and said \emph{Yes, house} to an early thaw and a late, agreeing with both so thoroughly that no argument could find a foot to stand on, and keeping the late because it puts off the paying, though the paying had stood in the ruts since the water went. He told the wood \emph{Six counted. The next I don't count} in the tone of a man stating the weather. The small knife lay at the very edge of its sill, six counted in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check and the seventh at the lip where it could not, and Garl's unforged collector sentence lay beside it --- the sentence about a man sent for a collector who turned back at the frozen ford, with no name on the man who had turned back and no figure on the debt. Garl had been that man and had given no name to himself in the telling. There was no collector's name on the knife and no figure anywhere on the sill, and Tarn put the knife back where it could be checked and told the empty yard where he had put it, unasked, as he had told it on other afternoons.

The sentence about an ear under this roof being worth more than the debt that held it came to his teeth and went as far as the word that would call the loan, and stopped there. It stopped with no figure and no name, a remembered ninth stopping and not a tenth stood up to make the nine into a rule, for a rule wants a payer under it and none stood to any of the nine. The seventh year stood at its very end and had still not given. He drew the breath that should have gone in at the top of the reach and did not spend it, a small interval showing where it should have stood, and his chest had been tight as it had been since the dark. Ash was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line and no scrubbing lifted it. The pale clip-ring at his left wrist, in place since Y.H. 806, held what light there was and gave it back. Up in \place{the Debt Loft}, which reached the basket only as a sound with a direction and never as a room he was taken into, the warm stone Sella had given him in the winter Garl died lay in its unmarked clay pot, two years gone and cold long since; not tried is not forgiven, and not forgiven is not entered. His stroke went over the shallow bright patch on \place{the flagstones} and over the road a house sends a man down when it wants a collector, without the half-check and without a turn of the head toward either, and no collector came.

At the edge of \place{the Vail wheel-shed} \person{Jorra Vail} had come through with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bent little finger and white in the creases where a fist had opened, and not bone-coloured slip. He kept dusty forearms and clean ones on one tray and parted them as a surgeon parts clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had been broken nor whether it had been a birth-slate. The broken birth-slates lay under the lowest rack, where the yard went around them and entered them into nothing. Three or more men with their collars high had gone low into \place{the back room} before first grey and given no name to anybody, and he had not said the room was cold; it kept its finger's width of open and its sound of low voices, which came to the basket as a direction and nothing more, a direction with no speaker standing under it.

He spoke to the wheel before he spoke to any person. \emph{It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady}, he said, and told a pot \emph{You'll do} in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either. He knew the \lore{full note} that pot would give three turns before it left his hands, with air under it and a wanting in it, and withheld the knowing on the near-full tray beside the hearings and never among them, advanced and not paid. The burned-slate word stood at four from \place{Slate Row}, a direction over the boards arrived nowhere and not a cause and not a fifth telling. The two tellings of the counting room shut in the war stood at two and had not become three, though \lore{the damper} in the shed held its double bite under one piece of iron and there was no word in \lore{Grathic} for both mercies. Three sayings about a pot stood as sayings, with the premature note from Smoke 14 on its own place and no fourth likeness made a cause; no pot had been lost and no name had been given to what ailed the man carried out on Day 188. The promise of the boy's name to the men who burn slates was neither given nor withdrawn, and \emph{he's not Vail} stayed sealed as tightly as a lid nobody had tried to lift in three years.

When her sentence went low at \place{the hearth} --- \emph{The fire's low. It wants---} --- his bent finger went into the cold iron of the damper ahead of it, took the cold out of the fingertip and held until the end of the sentence arrived at the pot and the household straightened around his silence. The boy read the iron again as the bite that holds a vessel whole and the bite that can take the hand off a man, mercy and mutilation in one piece, filed as an object joined to no kiln. Neither man named the other's silence, and the two silences kept their old equality under the roof: two ears, two held breaths, a loan that can hear and has never been called.

\scenebreak

At the going of the light she carried him up at the half-pace, went wide around the seven places with a look and a step that asked nothing of them, and set him on \place{the bed} under the roof and not in the Debt Loft. She put the fold right without a look at his face and left the thread at his mouth frozen and unwiped, her hand resting a half-beat longer at the setting than the setting needed before it went away. She told the fire it was the thaw, turned the coals with the back of a broken pot, said \emph{The house will keep it} where another woman would have said \emph{I}, stood a moment counting under her breath toward no opening --- the small intake, the hold, the letting-go --- and went to her own bed. \lore{The widow's mark} hung where it hung on its cord at her throat, a thumb-length of lead-glazed clay with the Vail kiln scratched into it, touched once before the first firing of Smoke and at no shelf, well, rack, road, basket, yard, afternoon or going of the light on Smoke 22. The one opening before Smoke 1 lay behind the household and was not looked at again, made into no lesson, no sign, no mending and no payment, with no payer named to any of it. The next real opening stood far off in the first firing of Frost and was not brought into a day that had not earned its own light.

In the small hours he turned the file out as a surgeon turns instruments out on a towel at the beginning of a case, each thing laid where the next hand will find it without looking, and found it exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air. There was no hand to enter any of it and no slate within reach, and the towel lay on nothing but the fold. Six hearings stood beside and never among, five from four sources and the sixth, the small flat knock out of a woman as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go; the empty seventh place was left empty and none invented to fill it. Three near-full answers stood advanced and not paid, and beside them three shapings of the pot saying with the premature note from Smoke 14 on its own place and no fourth likeness made a cause. Four low things stood with \emph{water over water} a fifth, and the heard bell beside the told, and the told figure beside the paid, with the district's second arithmetic up the road in a locked box and unread under this roof. Nine stoppings stood with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end, and the Seed 16 breath lay unspent and the loan uncalled.

The Day 161 fraction lay on its own place, the Day 166 held beat on a second, the flagstone half-check of Tallow 22 on a third; the small dry scrape of a pot's foot from the loft sill lay on a fourth, the pale line of the carried man on a fifth, and the held inch on a sixth with its answers standing on it. Nine record-first hands stood with a finger between every pair, and the ninth was \person{Ilva}'s, taken from the firing work and not from any house or Court; her notched strip of birch stayed in her sleeve, unread, unasked about, not taken out and not joined to the reed-cutter's cord turned so its knots stood, and no tenth hand was filed from a hand that did not reach. The dressed grain lay covered and unfigured on the cool stone at \place{the rack end}. The pan's gone ring, the shelf's two gaps and the fold's one crust with its empty place lay on trays that did not touch. The ownerless question stood at five askings on a place of its own with no owner, no room, no hand at the kiln, no cause, no witness and no row named; five askings are not one witness and do not make a sixth. He kept the whole of it as a finding with no payer, and did not turn the finding into a lesson, for a lesson is an account that has found somebody to stand under it and nobody stood.

He put the question to his hand. There was no ceremony in it and no answer he expected; a man tests a tool before he trusts it, and the testing is the whole of the work. Through the finger's-width door the river went on with its note, low over lower, and the wall between that sound and the bed did not touch anything but itself. There was no house count to borrow and no fire-sitter over the coals to supply one. The hand outside the wool went up an inch toward the low note and held through one whole interval of the river's own going, from the note to the one following it, and came down on the river's next low note, down on the sound and not past it. It borrowed no stroke from the room that does not touch and filled nothing to make any row come out even. It was an answer and not an asking. It was ninety-eight, filed on the sixth place beside ninety-seven with no payer named, an object among objects, exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air: not a longer reach, not a miss entered as a finding, not a hearing of the full note, not a seventh of it, not a tenth stopping, not a payment, not one of the held changes, not a key, a power or a rule. He made nothing of it. The hand came down and lay back against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking.

Two hundred and sixty-one stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in \place{Sarn}, \place{Sill} or \place{Grath}; six months and eighty-one days were told by nobody. Ninety-eight honest answers stood on the sixth place and none of them was a reach. The carried fire stopped at four with its fifth unpaid two hundred and sixty-one days and its payer unnamed; the widow's mark hung where it hung; the dressed grain lay covered and unfigured; the seventh year stood at its very end and had still not given. Smoke went on without a boundary drawn and Smoke 30 stood eight mornings ahead and was not reached for, and the first firing of Frost stood far off and unbrought. The towel lay on nothing but the fold, with every tray parted and every gap left gap, and the count went forward into Smoke 23, a day that had not earned its heading. The hand rested against the fold on the ninety-eighth answer and asked to be told nothing.
