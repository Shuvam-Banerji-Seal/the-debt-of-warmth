The afternoon went out of the light the way heat goes out of a pot set off the coals, not all at once and not with a word said over it, but drawing back from the edge of the day and settling behind the broken pot where the coals lay. \person[Corin]{The boy} lay under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof in \place{Low Kiln} with the hand resting against the fold where the ninety-ninth answer had put it down, and the room that does not touch the room of the fire kept its own air about him as it had kept it through the morning. Smoke went on without a boundary drawn across itself and neither of the district's two names for the months was set right or left wrong; an afternoon is an afternoon and does not become a boundary. Two hundred and sixty-two stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in \place{Sarn}, \place{Sill} or \place{Grath}; six months and eighty-two days were told by nobody. The carried warmth had not come back in the thinning light and no hand had come in to supply it, because no hand under any roof had been given an instruction to supply it. \lore{the carried fire} kept its four low pulls under the floor with the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer without a name for two hundred and sixty-two days, and through the finger's-width door the river kept \lore{water over water}, low over lower, and overhead \place{the boards} kept four nails on the near board, four on the next, and a thumb-width knot where the fifth nail should have stood. Three counts in three places and none rose into another, because agreeing any two would want a payer set under both and none stood to any: a knot is not a nail, a river is not a fire, and a fire under a floor is not either of them.

He took the body in the order the facts presented themselves, as a surgeon takes a field and asks only what is there. One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from his ear, gone past the point where a child would have eaten it, and the empty place beside it kept its name to itself; no third had come in the afternoon to make the row come out even. The thread of drool at his mouth had frozen in the dry heat and was unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. Cold had come to cheek and temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, and had stopped there as though a line had been written out to that length and no farther; it neither went forward nor went away. The afterdrop waited beyond all three, past a warming nobody in this house would perform. He knew the composed face that is not composure. He knew a cold patient cannot clot and will seem better before he is worse. He wanted the crust lifted the two inches to his mouth. Both were true and the second was louder in a body of two hundred and sixty-two days, and the surgeon took the inventory and entered none of it, not even on the place where he kept the things he did not enter.

The first smell was hot clay under tallow, and under that wet wool sour with the long lodging of a body carried in it since before first grey, and under that the sour mineral breath of \lore{Ash} in the unswept corner that the house called \lore{the thaw}. Out in \place{the Clay Yard} the two lines lay where they had lain since the ridge turned its corner a year ago --- the clean dark line where the Ash had turned, and the finer grey beside it that the yard called the thaw --- and the broom when it came would be given its instruction before it was taken up and would go around both without lifting either. A line left where it is, is still a line kept; he had entered that once and had not entered it again.

\person{Garl Vail} was kept in objects in the afternoon as he had been kept in the morning, a keeping and not a story. The salt-stiff coat held a broader man's shape on its peg with the crease settled into the left shoulder, and the wheel-seat kept its twenty-year hollow on that side, and the proverb about leaving the crack showing had gone years ago to a pot with no crack in it, said now over a corner where the repair had been rubbed out. The debt against a firing of tiles went around the household and was not called, circled without a name, neither paid nor settled nor done with. His name had not been spoken under this roof for two hundred and sixty-two days. No collector came up \place{the clay road} in the afternoon and none went down it; the road kept its ruts and gave back nothing. The ridge that had been his ash-marker was a clean dark line with a finer hairline beside it, and neither had been swept, and the boy made no lesson of it, because a lesson is an account that has found a payer and none stood to a line in a corner.

\person{Sella Vail} carried the basket at the half-pace from every open slate and went wide around seven things, each with a look and a wide step and no number. She went around the clean dark line and the finer grey called the thaw; around the space beneath \place{the lowest rack} where the broken birth-slates lay and the yard entered them into nothing; around the round ring where the shallow clay pan had stood, with no scrape, crumb, print, chip or word to say where it had gone; around \place{the trough} beside \place{the yard well}, bare and dark, with its water called weather; around the field's empty place out on \place{the worked strips}; and around the returning-line place where a hand had gone in on Seed 15 and had not come out, carried and not named, not paid and not joined to the shelf's two gaps or to the one crust in the fold. Seven in a row are seven. They do not become an account until a payer stands under them and none stood. She went around the place where the hand had not come back as wide as a pail of hot water set where a child might knock it, and gave the instruction to the broom and the bucket and the wool and never to the basket, and said the house where another woman would have said I.

The boy held the house's omissions on one place and his own kept empty intervals on another, with a finger between them. The finding that a not-doing keeps as surely as a doing was entered nowhere, and the absence of a joining was itself kept, and not spent, because a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood to any of these. He made no third gap out of the shelf's two and the fold's one; he made no fourth likeness of three pot sayings; he put no name over the returned line and no rule over the returning. A man could notice a whole household kept by what it refused to do and could not spend one of its refusals, and there was no payer to make it more than that.

At \place{the kindling} the small knife lay at the very lip of its sill. \person{Tarn} had set six in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check and the seventh at the lip where it could not, and Garl Vail's unforged collector sentence lay beside it --- the man who had been sent for a collector and had turned back at the frozen ford, with no name on the man and no figure on the debt, and the man had been Garl himself, which nobody had entered. Tarn took the broom only after the broom had been instructed. He said \emph{Yes, house} to an early thaw and to a late, agreeing with both so thoroughly that no argument could find a foot, and kept the late because a late thaw puts off the paying, though the paying had stood in the ruts since the water went. He told the wood \emph{Six counted. The next I don't count} in the tone of a man stating the weather.

The sentence about an ear under this roof being worth more than the debt that held it came to his teeth in the thinning light and went as far as the word that would call the loan before it stopped, with no figure and no name. It was a remembered ninth stopping and not a new one; no tenth had stood up before the seventh year at its very end, and the seventh year had still not given. He drew the breath that should have gone in at the top of the reach and did not spend it, and his chest was tight as it had been since the dark. Ash was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line and no scrubbing lifted it; the pale clip-ring at his left wrist, in place since Y.H. 806, held what little light there was and gave it back. Up in \place{the Debt Loft}, which came to the boy only as a sound with a direction and never as a room he was taken into, the warm stone Sella had given him in the winter Garl died lay in its unmarked clay pot, two years gone and cold long since. He did not try it and did not forgive it; not trying is not forgiving, and the stone lay where it lay.

His stroke went on over \place{the flagstones} past the shallow bright patch without the half-check, the stones still keeping the place where it had stopped, and neither man named the other's silence.

At the edge of \place{the Vail wheel-shed} \person{Jorra Vail} came through with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bent little finger and white in the creases of a fist that had opened. He kept dusty forearms and clean ones on one tray and parted them as a surgeon parts clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had been broken nor whether it was a birth-slate; the broken birth-slates lay under the lowest rack where the yard went around them and entered them into nothing. Three or more unnamed men had gone low into \place{the back room} before first grey and got no name from him, and he did not say the room was cold; the room kept its finger's width of open and its sound of low voices, which arrived at the basket as a direction and nothing more.

He spoke to the wheel before he spoke to any person. \emph{It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady.} He told a pot \emph{You'll do} in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either, and knew \lore{the full note} that pot would give three turns ahead, with air under it and a want in it, and withheld the knowing on the near-full tray beside the hearings and never among them, advanced and not paid. The burned-slate word stood at four from \place{Slate Row}, a direction over the boards arrived nowhere and not a fifth telling; the two tellings of the counting room shut in the war stood at two and did not become three; three sayings about a pot stood as sayings with the premature note from Smoke 14 on its own place and no fourth likeness made a cause. The promise of the boy's name to the men who burn slates was neither given nor withdrawn, and \emph{he's not Vail} stayed sealed as tightly as a lid nobody had tried to lift in three years.

When Sella's sentence went low --- \emph{The fire's low. It wants---} --- his bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper} ahead of it, took the cold out of the fingertip and held until the end arrived at the pot. The boy read the iron again as the bite that holds a vessel whole and the bite that can take the hand off a man, mercy and mutilation in one object, with no \lore{Grathic} word for both, joined to no kiln.

At the going of the light she carried him up at the half-pace, went wide around the seven places with a look and a step that asked nothing of them, and set him on \place{the bed} under the roof and not in the Debt Loft. She put the fold right without a look at his face and left the thread at his mouth frozen and unwiped, her hand resting a half-beat longer at the setting than the setting needed before it went away. She told the fire it was the thaw, turned the coals with the back of a broken pot, said \emph{The house will keep it} where another woman would have said \emph{I}, stood a moment counting under her breath toward no opening --- the small intake, the hold, the letting-go --- pushed coal-black hair back with the wrist that carries the burn so the one grey streak showed pale, and went to her own bed. \lore{the widow's mark} hung where it hung at her throat, a thumb-length of lead-glazed clay with the Vail kiln scratched into it, touched once before the kiln on Smoke 1 and at no shelf, well, rack, road, basket, yard, afternoon or going of the light on Smoke 23. The one opening before Smoke 1 lay behind the household, made into no lesson, no sign, no mending and no payment, with no payer named to it.

In the small hours he turned the file out on the towel as a surgeon turns instruments out at the beginning of a case, each thing laid where the next hand will find it without looking, and found it exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air. Six hearings stood beside and never among, five from four sources and the sixth, the small flat knock out of a woman as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go; the empty seventh place was left empty and none invented to fill it. Three near-full answers stood advanced and not paid, and beside them three shapings of the pot saying with the premature note from Smoke 14 on its own place and no fourth likeness made a cause. Four low things stood with water over water a fifth, and told bell stood beside heard, and told figure beside paid, with Smoke's second arithmetic up the road in a locked box and unread under this roof. Nine stoppings stood with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end; three held changes stood on three places, the loft scrape on a fourth and the pale line of the carried man on a fifth, and the held inch on a sixth with its answers standing on it. Nine record-first hands stood with a finger between every pair, the ninth taken from the firing work and not from any house or Court, her strip unread in her sleeve and joined to none. The dressed grain lay covered and unfigured at \place{the rack end}; the pan's gone ring, the shelf's two gaps and the fold's one crust with its gap lay on trays that do not touch; the ownerless question stood at five askings with no owner, no witness and no row named, and no sixth asking had come. The house's not-doings stood on a place of their own, and his own kept gaps stood on another, with a finger between, and the finding that a not-doing keeps as surely as a doing was entered nowhere and not turned into a rule.

He put the question to his hand. There was no ceremony in it and no answer he expected; a man tests a tool before he trusts it, and the testing is the whole of the work. Through the finger's-width door the river went on with its note, low over lower, and the wall between that sound and the bed did not touch anything but itself. There was no fire-sitter over the coals to supply a count and no house count to borrow. The hand outside the wool went up an inch toward the low note and, with the inch held, took the little silence after a count that had ended in the room that does not touch and carried one rhythm across that empty interval as an object in its own right --- crossing no second count and filling nothing to make a stroke in a row that had none, laying the silence beside the held inch and not across it --- and came down on the river's next low note, down on the sound and not past it. It was an answer and not an asking. It was one hundred, filed on the sixth place beside ninety-nine with no payer named, exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air: not a hearing, not a seventh of the full note, not a stopping, not a payment, not one of the held changes, not a key, a power or a rule. He made nothing of it. The hand lay back against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking.

Two hundred and sixty-two stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in Sarn, Sill or Grath; six months and eighty-two days were told by nobody. One hundred honest answers stood on the sixth place and none of them was a reach. The carried fire stopped at four with its fifth unpaid two hundred and sixty-two days and its payer unnamed; the widow's mark hung where it hung; the dressed grain lay covered and unfigured at the rack end; the seventh year stood at its very end and had still not given. Smoke went on without a boundary drawn and Smoke 30 stood seven mornings ahead and was not reached for, and the first firing of Frost stood far off and unbrought. The towel lay on nothing but the fold, with every tray parted and every gap left gap, and the count went forward into Smoke 24, a day that had not earned its heading. The hand came down on the river's next low note with the little silence behind it kept whole and uncrossed.
