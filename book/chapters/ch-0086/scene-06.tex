The light went off \place{the Clay Yard} the way it goes off a yard nobody has ever measured, from the edge inward, leaving the cool stone at \place{the rack end} lit longest and \place{the trough} dark first, and \place{Bone} went on without a boundary drawn around it. Neither of the district's two habits of naming its months was set right or left wrong: the older women called it by the weather and the slatemen by the number of Account-days, and the two names stood side by side on no slate anybody showed, with a finger's width between them and no third name made to agree them. A month that ends without a boundary does not end. It thins. The work goes on in the thin part, and the child, carried past with his cheek to the fold, had long ago stopped expecting a door to shut anywhere and had made the not-shutting into a place he could live in.

\person{Sella Vail} carried him down at the half-pace she kept from every open slate, the fold over his ears against the river wind and the instruction going to the wool and the wind and never to the basket. She went wide around the seven walked-around places with a look and a wide step and no number: the clean dark line at the Vail corner and the finer grey hairline beside it called \lore{the thaw}, unswept both; the space beneath \place{the lowest rack} at \place{the drying racks} where the broken birth-slates lay; the round ring where the shallow pan once stood, with no scrape, crumb, print, chip or word in it; the water in the trough; the field's empty place out on \place{the worked strips}; and the returning-line place where a hand went in on Seed 15 and did not come out. Seven places kept by going around them, and she made no eighth likeness stand up as a row and put no figure to any of them. \person[Corin]{The boy} kept the seven on seven places of his own and did not lay them across the house's \lore{not-doings} or across the small gaps in his own file; the edges came near to touching, and the finger went between.

The month's hunts and repairs went on as directions judged by the carrying arm and the weather, never as figures. A man come down from \place{Cold Acre} with a hare across his shoulders shifted the weight to the other collarbone when a woman with a pot asked what the carrying had cost, and said \emph{Ask the weather. It carried the snow; it can carry the price}, and went off toward \place{the north gate road}. Nobody weighed the hare, nobody opened a slate, and the boy filed the answer as a direction and not a figure, as he had filed the weather's other answers on other days, and made no row of them. Another man with a barrow asked what the month's mending would want now the light went early and was told to ask the pot; the pot, being a pot, said nothing, and the yard went on with its work. No man priced a repair and stood up in the yard to be asked. He had learned in a better-lit room that a number put to a thing does not tell you who paid for it, and the yard's refusal to put a number to anything was a kind of care and not a kind of knowledge; he kept the care on its own place and did not make a doctrine of it, because a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood.

At \place{the yard well} the stayed frost had gone off the north lip while the yard looked elsewhere, and the skin on the trough and the silt-clouded plate of ice standing on \place{the flat stone by the well} out of the path of feet stayed three coverings in three places. The yard called all of them weather and entered none of them; a finger went between the gone lip and the trough's skin, and between the skin and the clouded plate, and no fourth likeness was stood up as a cause and no payer named to any of the three. He read them the way he had once read a covering laid over a depth --- by what lay under it and by what the hand that laid it had chosen to leave showing --- and put no name to the depth and no figure to the covering, and asked nothing of any of them to mean.

At the rack end the dressed grain lay covered under wool away from \place{the hearth}, turned earlier in the day with a progressively lighter touch and given no figure; the reading that had gone over it went up \place{the clay road} and was not brought back now as a lesson or a mending or a cause, and the number that had read it still did not know what it cost. He kept that on its own place too.

At the going of the light she carried him up at the half-pace and set him under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof and never in \place{the Debt Loft}, and put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face. The instruction had gone to the wool and the river wind and the pot and the broom and the running water, and never to the basket. The thread at his mouth had frozen in the dry heat and was left where it lay, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread and a basket set down is a pot set down and left.

Out on \place{the flagstones} the shallow bright patch held the going light a moment after the rest of the yard had gone dark, and \person{Tarn}'s stroke went over it without the half-check, as it had gone on the seventh and the ninth, and the stone kept the place where it had stopped on the eighth without anybody's naming it. The returned half-check of the eighth of Bone stayed on the flagstone's own place with the three older held changes beside it --- the Day 161 damper fraction, the Day 166 shelf beat, the Frost 5 returned beat --- four held changes on four places and not a rule. No fifth likeness was made a cause and no payer was named to any of them. A thing kept by not being made again is still kept, and the boy kept it by not making it again, and asked nothing of the four to stand up and agree.

At \place{the kindling} Tarn had the small knife at the very edge of its sill, where it could be checked and where it could slip, six years standing in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check and the seventh at the lip beyond it where it could not, and beside it \person{Garl Vail}'s unforged collector sentence with no name on the man who turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on the debt. \lore{Ash} was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line and no scrubbing lifted it; the pale clip-ring at his left wrist, in place since Y.H. 806, held what little light there was and gave it back, and he held the wrist up a moment to the grey as though to see whether the light had come to anything. His chest had been tight since the dark and the breath from Seed 16 was still unspent, a small empty place at the top of each reach where the breath should have gone in. He told the wood \emph{Six counted. The next I don't count} in the tone of a man stating the weather and put the knife back where it could be checked, and announced it to nobody because the yard had already been told. \emph{Yes, house}, he said to an early thaw and a late, wanting the late because it puts off the paying, though the paying had stood in the ruts since the water went. The sentence about an ear under this roof being worth more than the debt that held it came to the word that would call the loan and stopped there, with no figure and no name --- a remembered ninth stopping, with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end and still ungiven. In the loft above the shed the warm stone she had given him in the winter Garl died lay cold in its unmarked pot, and he did not reconcile it with the debt and did not try, which is not forgiveness. The loan stayed uncalled and no collector came up the clay road or went down it.

\person{Jorra Vail} came through the finger's-width door off \place{the Vail wheel-shed} with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bent little finger and under the nails, white in the creases where a fist had opened, not bone-coloured \lore{slip}. He kept dusty forearms and clean ones on one tray and parted them as a surgeon parts clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had broken nor whether it was a birth-slate. He spoke to the wheel before he spoke to any person --- \emph{It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady} --- and told a pot \emph{You'll do} in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either. He knew the note that pot would give three turns before it left his hands, with air under it and a want in it, and kept the knowing on the near-full tray beside the hearings and never among them, and would not have said it before a kiln-master from \place{High Kiln} who would call it early and make something of it. Before anyone had finished telling him the fire was low his bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper}, moved the tongue a hair's breadth and left it standing, reading one piece of iron with two bites, the bite that holds a vessel whole and the bite that takes the hand off a man, with no \lore{Grathic} word for both mercies and no slate entered. Men unnamed had gone low into \place{the back room} before first grey and he gave the house no name for them and did not say the room was cold.

He grew quieter as the saying about the burned slate went around, the way he always did, and the household straightened around the quiet without an order. The fifth telling of \lore{the burned slate} stood at five with a finger between the fifth and the four, no witness standing, no hand named at any flame, no sixth due on a day that had not earned one, and the seed waiting on the far-off calling-in of his promise, not a confession and not a payment. \emph{He's not Vail} stayed sealed as tightly as a lid nobody had tried to lift in three years.

Garl stayed in objects and not in a story. The salt-stiff coat with the crease at the left shoulder kept his broader shape on its peg behind the door; the wheel-seat in the shed kept its twenty-year lean and the hollow on that side, and Sella would let no one fill it. The clean dark line at the Vail corner and the finer grey beside it called the thaw were unswept both, and the ridge measured by a thumb against the death-house ridge stayed where it was. His proverb went to the pot and the fire and never to a person --- \emph{Leave the crack showing. People trust a thing they can see has been handled}, and \emph{If it's worth mending it's worth mending twice. The second time is the real one} --- and it had improved in the keeping and was very likely not as good a sentence as the man had said it. The debt against a firing of tiles circled without a name, neither paid nor settled nor done with, and was worse for being uncalled; his name had not been spoken under this roof for three hundred and eleven days, and the keeping of him was maintenance and not a lesson, and made no rule.

In the small hours the finding he had come by on the morning of the first reading was not set beside anything to make it mean. Four arithmetics of the year stood behind one printed figure, the figure stood at one in twelve unchallenged, the figure read was still not the figure paid, and no signature stood on the space between; the four true subtractions rode locked up the clay road in \person{Berr Clay}'s box toward High Kiln, and nobody under this roof had seen the face of them since. Four readings were four and not one account, and he kept the finding on a place of its own with no payer under it --- not a lesson, not a grievance, not a mending, not a doctrine and not a rule --- and did not lay it across the house's not-doings, or the dressed grain lying covered and unfigured under wool at the rack end, or the four held changes standing on four places, or the small gaps he kept in his own file. The edges of these things came near to touching; the finger went between them and did not spend itself joining them. A finding with no payer can be carried a long way without becoming a rule, and this one was carried, light, on its own place, and asked nothing of any of the others to stand up and mean.

Under the floor \lore{the carried fire} gave its four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood, and nobody lifted the floor and nobody supplied the pull; its payer had been unnamed three hundred and eleven days. Through the finger's-width door the river kept its low note of \lore{water over water}, a low thing over a lower thing, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming, and put no number to itself. Overhead \place{the boards} kept four nails on the near board, four on the next and a thumb-width knot where a fifth nail might have been driven and was not. Four and a gap in three different materials, and agreeing any two of them would have wanted a payer put under both, and none stood to either.

\lore{The widow's mark} hung where it hung on its cord at her throat, a thumb-length of lead-glazed clay with the Vail kiln scratched into it, and she did not touch it at a well, a rack, a yard, a going of the light or a bed, because none of these was an opening and the hand goes to the mark before the kiln and at no other hour. Five openings in five months were five and not a rule, and the next stood far off in a later month's first firing, not hoped for and not counted toward. The clay jar stood cold under \lore{lead glaze} where she had set it, and her finger lay on top of the loaf shelf's front edge a breath and came away with the charcoal tally unread beneath it, and her lips moved along a row standing with two gaps and figured neither of them. The burn shaped like a little door on the back of her left hand shut on nothing and shut on the pot's weight, and the boy, who could not have named the shape, kept the two shuttings on separate places and made nothing between them.

He turned the file out as he had once turned instruments out on a towel at the end of a list, laying each thing on its own place and asking nothing of any of them. Six hearings of \lore{the full note} stood beside and never among, with the seventh place empty and none invented to fill it; the bell he had heard in the square stayed in its square beside the told bell of \lore{the Long Night}; the told figure stood beside the paid figure with no signature on the space; nine record-first hands stood with \person[Ilva]{Ilva}'s strip in a sleeve and no tenth; the row question stood at five askings with one shaping and no witness; the well-saying stood at three shapings; the shut-room tellings stood at two; the pot sayings stood at three with the Smoke 14 note on its own place apart; nine stoppings stood with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end; seven green stood a colour and seven shape a shape with no eighth. Nothing was spent to make a row come out even, nothing stood up as a reach, a hearing, a seventh, a stopping, a payment, a held change, a key, a power, a freedom, a gift, a doctrine or a rule, and no payer was named to any of it. The file lay open on \place{the towel} at the head of \place{the bed} with every gap left gap, every tray parted by a finger's width and nothing that had moved.

At the very end of the day the hand outside the wool came up an inch toward the river's note, took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, and let it go whole on the same low note --- a restraint and not a payment, borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, carried into neither the carried fire's missing fifth, the bell's gap left in the square, nor the river's going low over lower. Nothing was promoted into a reach, a hearing, a seventh, a stopping, a payment, a held change, a gift, a doctrine or a rule; no payer was named. It was the hundred and ninety-eighth answer, exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air, and it stood up as none of the things it refused.

The older man took a clean satisfaction in the keeping of the number, the satisfaction of a man who has laid an instrument down without pretending it had healed anybody; the infant wanted the crust lifted the two inches to his mouth and the thread wiped from his lip, and \lore{the Afterdrop} waited past a warming nobody in this house would perform. Both were true and the second was louder and neither won, and the argument was the work. He had once read such a face in a bay with fluorescent light in it and gone past the face to the note and signed past the note himself, and the knowledge had never once since made a warm place for anybody, and he did not ask it to now.

The hand came down on the river's note and laid itself back against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. Three hundred and eleven stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill} or \place{Grath}; six months and one hundred and thirty-one days were told by nobody; the carried fire's fifth was unpaid the same number of days with its payer unnamed; Garl's name had not been spoken under the roof the same number. One hundred and ninety-eight honest answers stood on the sixth place, each an object and not a power, and neither the number nor the small hand-movings that made it stood up and called themselves a doctrine, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood. Nothing on the twelfth of Bone was opened, joined, named, called, paid or ended.

The river's low note went quiet under the finger's-width door while the silt-clouded plate of ice stood on its flat stone out of the path of feet, the file lay open on the towel with every gap left gap, and Bone went on without a boundary drawn.
