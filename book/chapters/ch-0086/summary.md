## Chronicle

Bone came on without a boundary drawn, and the boy beneath the Vail roof came into his three hundred and ninth, tenth and eleventh mornings with nobody to tell him what they counted. The count went from three hundred and eight to three hundred and eleven; the fire's fifth stayed unpaid, and Garl Vail's name unspoken the same number of days. Each morning the body was taken in its old order --- crust two inches off, thread frozen, cold at cheek, temple and wrist, the afterdrop waiting past a warming nobody would perform --- and the surgeon's knowing bought nothing against the infant's want of the crust. Both were true; the second was louder; neither won.

On the tenth of Bone, the month's first Account-day, the reading came to the Cross. Berr Clay wiped his thumb twice on his apron before touching nothing, passed a finger over a slate held writing-inward, and read *One in twelve, as the notice says*. He called the levy and denied it --- *It is not called by me*; *your house*, never *I* --- taking himself out of his own sentence as a surgeon leaves a field. He went up the road with the box locked, Bone's first true subtraction riding beside the three of Frost. The figure read was not the figure paid, and no signature stood on the space between.

Four arithmetics of the year now stood behind that one printed figure. Four were four and not one account; a rule made from them would want a payer put under all four, and none stood. The boy kept the finding on a place of its own, not a lesson, grievance, mending or doctrine, and did not lay it across the house's not-doings, the covered grain or his own gaps --- the edges came near to touching and the finger went between. The bell went four, four and a gap. Hessa Rale carried Berr's slate up the road unread: *I don't read it. I carry it.* A green at a woman's lip and a small shape in cast-offs were a seventh of each and no eighth.

On the eleventh and twelfth the house went back to its tending. Sella went to the fire before the basket, said *The house will keep it*, lifted the twice-mended pot with her thumb where the wire crossed the crack, and did not touch the widow's mark; five openings in five months were five and not a rule. She went wide around the seven walked-around places. Tarn kept six and a seventh at the lip; Jorra set his bent finger in the cold damper and grew quieter at the fifth burned-slate telling. Six answers, from the hundred and ninety-third to the hundred and ninety-eighth, went whole on the river's note, worth nothing at two inches of air. At the close the file lay open with every gap left gap, three hundred and eleven stood exact and spoken by nobody, and Bone went on without a boundary drawn.
