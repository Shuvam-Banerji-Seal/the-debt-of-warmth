## Scene 6 — Six Behind One Figure, Held

- **Day and time.** Day 347, Ash 18, Eighthday. Afternoon through the going of the light and the bed; small hours at the very end of the day. Chapter close. No new dated heading.
- **Place.** The Clay Yard's seven places; the yard well and flat stone; the rack end; the kindling; the flagstones; the wheel-shed edge and damper; then the hearth, pot board, loaf shelf, bed, boards and towel.
- **Present.** Corin/Dan, Sella Vail, Tarn, Jorra Vail; Garl in objects. Walk-ons: the Cold Acre hare-carrier, the cracked-jar man, a woman with a basket of kindling, two fence-menders. No Ilva, no Berr, no Hessa, no runner on any road, no green-mouthed woman or foundling sighting, no collector, no market, Cross or bell.
- **Beats, in order.**
  1. The yard goes around its seven places with a look and a wide step and no number; the three coverings stay weather entered nowhere with a finger between every pair and no fourth likeness made a cause; the dressed grain lies covered and unfigured with no handful lifted. Hunts and repairs stay directions judged by the carrying arm and the weather; Tarn says *Yes, house* to early and late and keeps the late.
  2. Tarn keeps six where the house can check and the seventh at the lip beside Garl's unforged collector sentence; checks the Ash 14 held breath and does not make it again; tells the wood *Six counted. The next I don't count*; brings the ear-sentence to the calling word and stops — nine stoppings and no tenth before the seventh year at its very end, the Seed 16 breath unspent, the loan uncalled. Jorra parts dusty forearms from clean, speaks to the wheel and a pot before any person, withholds the note known three turns ahead, puts his bent finger into the cold damper ahead of Sella's unfinished sentence, one iron with two bites and no Grathic word for both mercies, *he's not Vail* sealed and the promise neither given nor withdrawn.
  3. At the going of the light Sella carries him up at the half-pace wide around the seven places, sets him on the bed under the roof and never in the Debt Loft, fold righted without a look at his face, half-beat at setting, thread left frozen. Widow's mark untouched; six openings in six months not a rule and no seventh made due. Twice-mended pot thumb-first at the wire with the hairline showing and both proverbs to pot and fire; dressed grain turned once under wool with fingers going in and coming out without closing. Pot rim two inches beyond the reaching hand.
  4. The whole tray is checked and left as it lay: six hearings of the full note beside and never among with the seventh place empty, the sixth the small flat knock out of a woman as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go; heard bell in its square beside the told bell of the Long Night, four, four and a gap with nobody crossing himself; told figure beside the paid with no signature on the space; seven arithmetics seven and not one account; nine record-first hands with no tenth; nine stoppings with no tenth; the row at five askings with one shaping and no sixth; the burned slate at five, the shut-room tellings at two, and the Ash 5 joining, the Ash 17 direction, the fourth shaping and the Ash 6 turning each on a place of its own with a finger between every pair, none spent, named, joined, promoted into a witness, a sixth telling, a third shut-room telling, a sixth asking, a fifth shaping, a cause, a payment or a rule — for a rule is an account that has found a payer and none stood. The far-off hand that will one day lay the district's private keepings together is still far off and is not his.
  5. Garl stays in objects and not in a story: salt-stiff coat with the left-shoulder crease, hollow wheel-seat, clean dark line with its finer grey, twice-mended pot with the hairline showing, unforged collector sentence beside the knife, tile debt uncalled, name unspoken three hundred and forty-seven days.
  6. Small hours: the hand outside the wool comes up an inch toward the river's note, does not reach, takes a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, holds the interval whole, lets it go on the note's own finishing, comes straight down and lays itself back against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. The note had done before the hand came down, so the letting-go was neither early again nor stayed, and the hand did not stay. Answer **270**.
  7. **Two hundred and seventy honest answers** stand on the sixth place, each an object and not a power, not one standing up and calling itself a doctrine, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood. Count closes at **347 exact**, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody; six months and one hundred and sixty-seven days told by nobody; fire's fifth unpaid the same number with its payer unnamed; Garl's name unspoken the same. Ash 20 stood two mornings ahead and was not brought into a day that had not earned it.
- **Emotional turn.** The whole file is reviewed and deliberately left unclosed: the day's emotion is the relief and cost of an honest field left as found. ## Pull into Chapter 99

The chapter closes on answer **270**, the count exact at **347**, six months and one hundred and sixty-seven days told by nobody, the carried fire's fifth unpaid and Garl's name unspoken the same number. Nothing has been opened, joined by a standing hand, named, called, paid or ended; the Ash 17 direction stands on its own place beside the Ash 5 joining, still gathering and still unspent, and the promise under the wheel-shed is still neither given nor withdrawn. Ash 20 (Day 349) stands **two mornings ahead**, named only as a date and never as a promise. Chapter 99 opens on Day 348, Ash 19, Ninthday, with the hand laid down on answer 270 and the count at 347 exact; on Ash 20 the dark month's second Account-day and market comes to the Cross, Berr reads *one in twelve, as the notice says* twice over the basket and goes off with the box locked and Ash's second true subtraction inside, and **eight arithmetics of the year stand behind one unsigned figure — eight are eight and not one account** — while the foundling carries forward 270 honest answers, six hearings with the seventh place empty, nine hands with no tenth, nine stoppings with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end, and a row at five askings with no witness. No answer becomes a power, no reading answers a held question, and the hand goes on being laid down where the next hand will find it. Forward, not closed.
