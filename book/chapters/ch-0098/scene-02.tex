In the afternoon the dry kiln heat went on pressing at the roof the way a hand presses at a seam and finds it holding, and \person{Sella Vail} carried the basket down under the finger's-width door into \place{the Clay Yard}. The instruction went to the wool and the river wind and never to the basket. “Keep it off his ears,” she told the wind, and set the fold right under his chin without a look at his face. She carried him level at the half-pace she kept from every open slate, the way a woman carries a pot filled to the brim and does not slop it, and the yard came up around \person[Corin]{the boy} as a field comes up under the eye before the dressing goes on, by the small sounds first, because the small sounds do not lie and the large ones are mostly weather: the dry rasp of rope at the well, the settle of clay going on cooling behind a wall, the far small knock of a pot set down somewhere out of sight. Three hundred and forty-five stood exact behind him, six months and one hundred and sixty-five days told by nobody in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill}, \place{Grath}. Under the floor of the house \lore{the carried fire} had given four low pulls in the morning and kept the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer unnamed three hundred and forty-five days; on its peg the salt-stiff coat kept a broader man's shape the same number of days with no name spoken over it. Nothing from the morning crossed the yard as sign, lesson, mending, cause or payment. The answer taken in the small hours had gone where answers go and had bought nothing at two inches of air, and the yard was not its echo.

The crust kept its two inches from his ear in the dry cold, gone leathery and brown-edged, its corner standing a hair off the wool, and the empty place beside it keeping its name to itself; no third had come since the morning to make the row come out even. The thread at his mouth had frozen and would stay frozen, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread, and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. Cold had come to cheek and temple and the small bare joint of his wrist where the wool rode back, and had stopped there as at the end of a line written out for it; \lore{the Afterdrop} waited past the stopping for a warming nobody in this house would perform. He wanted the crust lifted two inches, the thread wiped away, and a hand that would close on a pot handle set within reach. Both were true, the second was louder, and neither won. The surgeon's register took the yard in its old order while the infant underneath went on being cold, and the two quarrelled without a verdict; the argument was the work. He had once stood in a room that smelled of disinfectant and cold metal and decided which of twenty bodies would be opened first, and here he could not lift a barley crust to his own mouth. The joke cost him nothing and was not funny.

She went around the yard the way the yard went around itself, with a look and a wide step and no number. At \place{the Vail corner} the clean dark line lay where it had lain and the finer grey hairline beside it that the district called \lore{the thaw} lay beside it unswept, and she went wide of both and swept neither. Beneath \place{the lowest rack} the broken birth-slates lay where they lay and she stepped around them, entering nothing; the round empty ring where the shallow clay pan had once stood kept its place with no scrape, no crumb, no print, no chip and no word, and a man stepping over it did not look down. At \place{the yard well} \place{the trough} kept its skin, and on \place{the flat stone by the well} the silt-clouded plate of ice stood out of the path of feet; out on \place{the worked strips} the field kept its empty place; and in the returning line, where a hand had gone in on Seed 15 and had not come out, she went around the place the way everybody went around it, with no name spoken. Seven places, and no eighth likeness and no cause across two of them and no payer under any. He entered none, because a thing entered is a thing that wants a payer and none stood.

The three coverings stayed weather and were entered nowhere. The frost that had written white on the north lip of the well in the dark month had gone off while the yard looked elsewhere, and it was not a sign now and had not been a sign then; the skin on the trough broke under the knock of a pail and closed again over what it had covered, and nobody lifted a finger to it; the silt-clouded plate stood where no foot went. A covering over a depth, the same covering over another depth, a piece of the depth lifted and set where no foot would go---three things on three places with a finger between every pair, called weather, and no fourth likeness made a cause. The figure in the box up the road had not entered any of them and was not made to.

At \place{the rack end} the dressed grain lay turned under wool away from \place{the hearth}, and Sella lifted a corner with a touch still lighter than the morning's and turned it once and gave no figure. Her fingers went in and came out and did not close, so that no handful was lifted. The boy filed the empty fingers on a place of their own beside the one object that was the handful let fall on Bone 22---a keeping, not a second handful, not a sixth held change, not a rule, with no payer under it. Wool over turning grain, the dry rasp of it, the smell of the yard coming up through it: the small sounds and the small smells, taken first. Whatever figure the sixteenth might put upon a heap of grain would not know what the heap had cost, and the space between that figure and its price would have no signature set into it.

The hunts and repairs of the afternoon stayed directions judged by the carrying arm and the weather, never figures. A man come down from \place{Cold Acre} shifted a hare across his shoulders at the well and, to a woman who asked what the carrying had cost, told her to ask the weather, which had carried the snow and could carry the price; he went off toward \place{the clay road} with the hare unweighed and nobody opening a slate. Another with a cracked jar in a sling of rope asked what the month's mending would want and was told to ask the pot, and the pot, being a pot, said nothing. A woman with a basket of kindling asked what the seventh year would want when it came, and two men mending a strip of fence said the thaw would be early and late. \person{Tarn}, bent at the shoulders from six years in the slip pit, with \place{Ash} worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line, said “Yes, house” to each of them, and kept the late because a late thaw put off the paying, agreeing so thoroughly that there was nowhere for an argument to find a foot. The boy read each as a chart left blank where the number should stand and entered none of the blanks; a cost-question answered with the weather was a direction arrived nowhere, and the arriving was the whole of it.

At \place{the kindling} Tarn kept his account in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check and in the lip beyond it where it could not: six standing, the seventh at the lip beside the unforged collector sentence that \person{Garl Vail} had left there with no name on the man who turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on the debt. The small knife lay at the very edge of its sill, where a man could check it and where a man's hand could also slip it off. “Six counted. The next I don't count,” he told the wood, in the tone of a man stating the weather, and cut nothing and counted nothing and called no loan.

He did the small checking a house could see and no more. He set the knife where it could be checked and passed his hand over it the way a man passes a hand over a tool put back where it belongs, the fingers going flat over the blade and the wooden sill, the pale clip-ring at his left wrist catching what little light there was and giving it back. That much the house could have checked and would have found in order. The hand went away when the checking was done. No breath stayed at the top of the reach. The held breath he had left at the sill on the fourteenth was where it had been put: checked on the fifteenth and found, and now checked again and found, and not made again on the sixteenth. He filed it where it had stood since the fourteenth, on a place of its own beside the five held objects and the pot-crack resting, with a finger between every pair, expressly not a sixth held object, not a seventh of anything, not a tenth stopping, not the seventh year given, not the calling of the loan, not a habit, a way, a hearing, a mending, a sign, a cause or a payment, with no payer standing under it. He cut nothing. He counted nothing. He did not spend the Seed 16 breath, and the thumb's resting on the uncut seventh of Bone 26 he checked and found where it had been laid and did not make again.

The boy had been looking at the kindling without appearing to look at it, as he had looked at a hundred knife-sills, taking the field by the small sounds first. He took this one apart by what was there and by what was not. What was there was a hand that had gone up to check and come down from the checking, nothing held at the top of the reach. What was not there was a cut, a count, a word that called the loan, a spent breath, a thumb laid down where a thumb had been laid before. He had watched a surgeon draw a hand from a field before the dressing went on and known the difference between the hand that works and the hand that only rests; this was the hand that checks and goes away, and the difference was exact.

To the woman with the kindling and to the two fence-menders Tarn said “Yes, house” to the early thaw and the late, and kept the late because it puts off the paying. At \place{the flagstones} his stroke went over the shallow bright patch without the half-check, and the returned half-check of Bone 8 stayed on its own place with the Day 161 fraction, the Day 166 shelf beat and the Frost 5 returned beat, four held changes on four places and not a rule. The warm loft stone lay cold in its unmarked pot above the shed, unreconciled by not trying, which is not forgiveness. He brought the ear-sentence all the way to the word that would call the loan and stopped there, the ninth stopping with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end, the Seed 16 breath unspent at the top of the reach and the loan uncalled. He did not say the sentence. The nine stoppings were nine and not a rule, and no tenth came due on a day that had not earned one.

\person[Jorra Vail]{Jorra} came through the finger's-width door off \place{the Vail wheel-shed} with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bent little finger and white in the creases where a fist had opened, and it was not the bone-coloured \lore{slip} of the wheel. He kept his dusty forearms and his clean ones on one tray and parted them as a surgeon parts clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had been broken nor whether it was a birth-slate. Three or more men had gone low into \place{the back room} before first grey and he gave the house no name for them and did not say the room was cold. To the wheel he spoke before he spoke to any person: “It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady.” To a pot he said, “You'll do,” in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either, and knew the \lore{full note} it would give three turns before it left his hands, with air under it and a want in it, and kept the knowing where nobody could make a song of it, withheld from a kiln-master come up from \place{High Kiln} who would call it early and make a song of it.

Sella had not finished telling him the fire was low when his bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper}, took the cold out of the fingertip, moved the tongue a hair's breadth and was left standing; the end of her instruction arrived at the pot and the household straightened around his silence the way cloth straightens when a weight is lifted. One piece of iron had two bites, the holding and the taking, and there was no \lore{Grathic} word for both mercies. “He's not Vail” stayed sealed as a lid nobody had tried to lift in three years, and the promise of the boy's name to the men who burn slates was neither given nor withdrawn. The burned-slate word stood at five with a finger between the fifth and the four and no witness standing; the joining said over \place{the boards} on the fifth of Ash stood beside it on a place of its own, not a sixth telling, not a third telling of the shut room, not a cause, not a confession and not a rule. The shut-room tellings stood at two and the row of askings at five with no witness and no sixth, and no sixth was due on a day that had not earned one. No ownerless voice came over the boards that afternoon, and the boy did not look for one standing beside the five.

Two men under one roof had that afternoon kept each a small thing no house could check: the one at the kindling had brought his hand to the sill and away again with nothing held at the top of the reach, and the one at the damper stood with a finger in cold iron ahead of a sentence unfinished. The boy set the two on one tray and parted them as he parted clot from vessel, and the finger he put between them was his own, going between and joining nothing. It was the nearest thing to an equality the yard had given him all day, and he refused to turn it into a cause: a nearly-touching of two silences is not a touching, and a touching is not a payment, and no payer stood under either. The surgeon took the exactness of it; the infant underneath took none and went on wanting the crust lifted, the thread wiped and a hand on the pot handle.

At the going of the light Sella carried him up at the half-pace and wide around the seven walked-around places, and the returning-line place where the hand had gone in and not come out was gone around with a look and no figure, as it had been gone around going down. She set him on \place{the bed} under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof and never in \place{the Debt Loft}, put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face, let her hand rest the half-beat longer than the setting needed before it went away, and left the thread where it lay. A sound came from above and behind and no interior was entered and no name was given to the sound; the instruction went to the fire, the wool, the pot and the river wind and never to the basket. She said “The house will keep it” where another woman would have said I. \lore{The widow's mark} hung where it hung and was not touched, because a going of the light and a bed are not openings; six openings in six months were six and not a rule, and no seventh was made due on a day that had not earned one. The hand at the sill had not been named under the roof and bought no lesson here.

She went to the hearth before the basket, told the coals it was the thaw and turned them with the back of a broken pot, not with the poker, because a pot's back knows how much a coal will bear. The fire answered four low pulls with the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer unnamed three hundred and forty-five days, and she did not supply the pull. Her finger lay on top of \place{the loaf shelf}'s front edge so the charcoal tally stayed unread; her lips moved along a two-gapped row and figured neither gap; and her eye went once to the clay jar standing cold under \lore{lead glaze} with the hand half over nothing and the little-door burn on the back of her left hand closing on air. From \place{the pot board} she lifted the twice-mended pot with her thumb first where the wire crossed the crack and only then let the weight come on the handle; both proverbs went to the pot and the fire and never to a person, and the hairline of the original break stood showing between the old-brass staple and the tighter wire. Repair stayed apart from restoration: a restored thing hides the hand that handled it, and a mended thing leaves that hand showing, and here the hand was left showing in the dark hairline between brass and wire. At the rack end the dressed grain lay turned under wool; she lifted a corner with a lighter touch and turned it once and gave no figure, and her fingers went in and came out without closing, so that no handful was lifted. The pot's rim went down two inches beyond the reaching hand, with a half-beat at the setting and no look toward the bed. The boy took the half-beat apart by what was there and by what was not: the distance between the hand's intention and its arrest, a small exact interval that bought nothing at two inches of air and was not a gift, for a gift wants a giver and nobody had given.

Garl stayed in objects and not in a story. His salt-stiff coat held a broader man's crease at the left shoulder on its peg behind the door; the wheel-seat in the shed kept its twenty-year hollow on that side and nobody was allowed to fill it; the ridge that had been his ash-marker was now the clean dark line at the Vail corner with the finer grey beside it unswept; and the unforged collector sentence lay beside the knife at the kindling sill with no name on the man who turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on the debt. The twice-mended pot on the pot board carried the hairline of the original break between the old-brass staple and the tighter wire, and both proverbs had gone to the pot and the fire and never to a person---improved in the keeping and very likely not as good as the man had said them. The debt against a firing of tiles circled without being called, neither paid nor settled nor done with and worse for being uncalled; his name had not been spoken under this roof three hundred and forty-five days, and no lesson had been made of any of it. The keeping of him was maintenance and not a story, and the held breath at the sill belonged to a living man and was not laid across any of his objects.

Under the floor the carried fire kept its four and its gap; overhead the boards kept four nails on the near board, four on the next and a thumb-width knot where a fifth might have been driven and was not; through the finger's-width door \lore{water over water} went low over lower, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming, and was never numbered among the hearings, for a hearing wants a source standing and none stood. Three counts in three places and none rose into another, because agreeing any two would have wanted a payer put under both and none stood to any. Six hearings of the full note stood beside and never among with the seventh place empty, the sixth the small flat knock out of \person{Dessa Silt} as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go and never among the things that had come out of vessels; an empty place kept empty was a finding and a finding filled to make a row come out even was a finding forged. The heard bell stayed in its square beside the told bell of \lore{the Long Night}, four, four and a gap with nobody crossing himself, and was not fetched in to make a fourth count or a seventh hearing. The told figure stood beside the paid with no signature on the space; one in twelve was printed, read and unchallenged, and had not been paid by anybody the boy could see. Seven arithmetics of the year rode locked up the clay road in \person{Berr Clay}'s box toward High Kiln, figure read still not figure paid, seven readings seven and not one account, the finding kept on a place of its own with no payer under it. It was not laid across the house's \lore{not-doings}, the dressed grain covered under wool and read over by a number that did not know its cost, the five held objects, the handful, the thumb's resting, the pot-crack resting, the new breath at the sill or his own kept gaps. The edges came near to touching, so near that a less careful hand would have called the touching a cause and the cause a payment; the finger went between. No doctrine stood up out of any of it, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood. Ash's first had gone up the road in a locked box and answered nothing under this roof; Ash's second stood two mornings ahead, named only as a date and not brought into a day that had not earned it.

In the small hours, with the river's note low and going lower under the finger's-width door, the hand outside the wool went up an inch toward the sound. It did not reach; the inch was not a reach and he had long ago stopped calling it one. It was the distance a hand may move in a field without disturbing the dressing, and he took it the way he had once taken a hand into a cavity, slowly, with the whole of the attention and the knowledge that most of what a hand does in a field is wait. The hand took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole, let the intake go on the note's own finishing, and came straight down and laid itself back against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. The note had done before the hand came down, so the letting-go was neither early again nor stayed, and the hand did not stay; it was the note's own finishing that ended the interval and not any decision of the hand, and that was the whole of the honesty in it. The intake was not a breath kept for warmth, the interval not a stopping of the river, the note not his.

He filed it as answer two hundred and sixty-six: exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment, borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the seven arithmetics, the bell's gap, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the held breath at the sill, the Ash 5 joining, the fourth shaping, the turned writing, the notched wood nor his own kept gaps. Two hundred and sixty-six honest answers stood on the sixth place, each an object and not a power, and not one stood up and called itself a doctrine, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood. The surgeon took his small clean satisfaction in a field left as found and in an answer entered where nobody could see it; the infant underneath took none of it and went on wanting the crust lifted the two inches to his mouth, the thread wiped and a hand on the pot handle. Both were true, the second louder, neither winning, the two registers quarrelling without verdict in the ordinary small hours of the sixteenth.

Three hundred and forty-five stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in Low Kiln, Sill and Grath; six months and one hundred and sixty-five days were told by nobody; the carried fire's fifth was unpaid the same number with its payer unnamed; and the name of the man the salt-stiff coat kept was unspoken the same number. Nothing on the sixteenth had been opened, joined by a standing hand, named, called, paid or ended. No runner had been seen on any road and no collector come up or down the clay road, and there was no market, no Cross and no bell. The keeping of the number was the only honest work there had been to do, bought nothing at two inches of air, was not for sale, and went on because attention is the one thing no institution can tax. The file lay open on \place{the towel} at the head of the bed with every gap left gap; the twice-mended pot stood on the pot board with the hairline of the break showing between the old-brass staple and the tighter wire; one stiff brown-edged crust lay two inches from his ear with its empty place unnamed and no third, the thread at his mouth frozen and unwiped; and under the floor the carried fire kept four and a gap with its payer unnamed three hundred and forty-five days. Forward, not closed.
