The afternoon came on as afternoons come in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill}, \place{Grath}, by the light thinning off the terrace and the clay cooling behind the wall, and \person{Sella Vail} carried the basket down at the half-pace she keeps from every open slate, the instruction going to the wool and the river wind and never to the basket. \person[Corin]{The boy} lay with his face turned a little to the air, the way she set him so the wind went past his ears and not into them, and he took the yard as it arrived, by the small sounds first, because the small sounds do not lie and the large ones are mostly weather: the dry rasp of rope at \place{the yard well}, the far small knock of a pot set down out of sight, the scrape of a broom going around something it did not enter. Three hundred and forty-seven stood behind him, exact and unspent and spoken by nobody, and under the floor of the house he had left \lore{the carried fire} kept its four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer without a name for the same number of days. Nothing from the morning crossed into the afternoon as sign, lesson, mending, cause or payment. A day lived is a day set down, and he did not go back into it to fetch a meaning out of it.

She went around the seven walked-around places with a look and a wide step and no number. At \place{the Vail corner} the clean dark line where the Ash ridge had turned lay with the finer grey hairline called \lore{the thaw} beside it, unswept both, and her look went over them the way a hand goes over a sleeping animal while her step went wide around them without a word. Beneath \place{the lowest rack} the broken birth-slates lay where they had lain, entered by nobody and asked about by nobody. The gone pan's round ring held no scrape, crumb, chip, print or word, only the shape the thing had left when it was taken away. The skin on \place{the trough} had broken under a pail earlier and closed again, and \place{the flat stone by the well} kept its plate of silt-clouded ice stood out of the path of feet. In the field's empty place on \place{the worked strips} the returning line had a gap in it where a hand had gone in on Seed 15 and had not come out, and the yard went around that place as it went around the others, with a look and a wide step and no number, no name, no cause, no body, no figure and no payer. Seven places. She made no eighth of them and set no figure on any.

The three coverings stayed weather entered nowhere, with a finger between every pair and no fourth likeness made a cause: the gone white frost on the north lip of the well, the skin that breaks under a pail and closes, the silt-clouded plate stood out of the path of feet. The yard had called all three weather and entered them in no slate, and no reading had paid them and no opening under any roof had answered for them. He kept them on three places that did not touch. A fourth would have wanted a payer put under all four, and none stood.

At \place{the rack end} the dressed grain lay turned under wool away from the hearth. She lifted a corner with a touch lighter than the morning's, turned the heap once and gave no figure, and her fingers went in and came out without closing, so that no handful was lifted. Whatever arithmetic \place{Ash}'s second would put upon the heap in two mornings' time would not know what the heap had cost, and the space between that figure and its price would have no signature set into it. The empty fingers went beside the one object of Bone 22 as a keeping and not a second handful, for two would be a habit and three a way, and both want a payer.

The month's hunts and repairs went around the yard as directions judged by the carrying arm and the weather, never figures. A man come down from \place{Cold Acre} shifted a hare across his shoulders at the well and, to a woman who asked what the carrying had cost, told her to ask the weather, which had carried the snow and could carry the price, and went off toward \place{the clay road} with the hare unweighed and nobody opening a slate. Another with a cracked jar in a sling of rope asked what the month's mending would want and was told to ask the pot, and the pot, being a pot, said nothing. A woman with a basket of kindling asked what the seventh year would want when it came, and two men mending a strip of fence said the thaw would be early and late. \person{Tarn} said *Yes, house* to the early and to the late and kept the late, because a late thaw puts off the paying, agreeing so thoroughly that no argument could find a foot.

At \place{the kindling} Tarn kept six in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check and the seventh at the lip beyond it where it could not, and beside the seventh lay \person[Garl Vail]'s unforged collector sentence with no name on the man who had turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on the debt. The small knife lay at the very edge of its sill where it could be checked and where a hand could also slip it off. He passed his hand over the knife the way a man passes a hand over a tool put back where it belongs, and the hand went away when the checking was done; the breath that had stayed a breath longer than the check required on Ash 14 was where it had been set, and he did not make it again. *Six counted. The next I don't count,* he told the wood, in the tone of a man stating the weather. The sentence about an ear under this roof being worth more than the debt that held it came up to the word that would call the loan and stopped there, the ninth stopping with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end, the Seed 16 breath unspent at the top of the reach and the loan uncalled. He cut nothing and counted nothing into the seventh.

\person{Jorra Vail} came through the finger's-width door off \place{the Vail wheel-shed} with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bent little finger and white in the creases where a fist had opened, and it was not the bone-coloured \lore{slip} of the wheel. He kept his dusty forearms and his clean ones on one tray and parted them as a surgeon parts clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had been broken nor whether it was a birth-slate, and the three or more men who had gone low into \place{the back room} before first grey got no name from him and no word that the room was cold. He spoke to the wheel before he spoke to any person. *It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady.* To a pot he said, *You'll do,* in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either, and he knew \lore{the full note} it would give three turns before it left his hands, with air under it and a want in it, and kept the knowing where no kiln-master come up from \place{High Kiln} could call it early and make a song of it.

When Sella had not finished telling him the fire was low his bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper} ahead of her sentence, took the cold out of the fingertip, moved the tongue a hair's breadth and was left standing, so that the end of her instruction arrived at the pot and the household straightened around the waiting the way cloth straightens when a weight is lifted. One piece of iron had two bites, the holding and the taking, with no \place{Grathic} word for both mercies.

He grew quieter when the direction went around \place{the boards} and up \place{the terrace}, with nobody standing anywhere to ask it anything, and the household straightened around the quiet without an order from anybody. \lore{The burned-slate word} stood at five with a finger between the fifth and the four, the shut-room tellings at two and the row at five askings with no sixth, and the claim went on gathering interest against the promise he had neither given nor withdrawn. *He's not Vail* stayed sealed as a lid nobody had tried to lift in three years.

Tarn's stroke went over the shallow bright patch on \place{the flagstones} without the half-check, and the warm loft stone lay cold in its unmarked pot in \place{the Debt Loft}, unreconciled by not trying, which is not forgiveness. The returned half-check of Bone 8 stayed on the flagstone's own place beside the Day 161 fraction, the Day 166 shelf beat and the Frost 5 returned beat, four held changes on four places and not a rule; neither the handful nor the thumb's resting nor the pot-crack resting nor the held breath at the sill had been made a fifth.

At the going of the light she carried him up at the half-pace, wide around the seven walked-around places, and set him on \place{the bed} under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof and never in the Debt Loft. She righted the fold at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face, let her hand rest the half-beat longer than the setting needed before it went away, and left the thread at his mouth frozen and unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread. The crust lay two inches from his ear, gone stiffer in the dry kiln heat, its corner standing a hair off the wool and the empty place beside it keeping its name to itself; cold had stopped at cheek and temple and the bare joint of his wrist where the wool rode back, and \lore{the Afterdrop} waited past the stopping, beyond a warming nobody in this house would perform.

\lore{The widow's mark} hung where it hung and was not touched. A morning, a hearth, a going of the light, a yard and a bed are not openings; the hand goes to the mark before the kiln and at no other hour, and six openings in six months --- Tallow, Ember, Smoke, Frost, Bone and Ash --- are six and not a rule, with the sixth held behind at \place{the kiln mouth} on Ash 2 and no seventh made due on a day that had not earned one. The mark answered nothing this afternoon for the crust at two inches, the empty place beside it, the fire's missing fifth, the hand that had gone in on Seed 15 and not come out, or the woman carried out of the brook clay-heavy and uncollected; none of those stood under it asking to be answered, and she did not ask on their behalf.

At \place{the hearth} she told the coals it was the thaw and turned them with the back of a broken pot, not with the poker, because a pot's back knows how much a coal will bear. Her finger lay on top of \place{the loaf shelf}'s front edge so the charcoal tally underneath stayed unread; her lips moved along a two-gapped row and figured neither gap; her eye went once to the clay jar standing cold under \lore{lead glaze} and her hand shut half over nothing, the little-door burn on the back of her left hand closing on air, and nothing in the jar was named and no figure came off the glaze.

At \place{the pot board} she lifted the twice-mended pot with her thumb first where the wire crossed the crack, felt what the thumb had to feel, and only then let the weight come on the handle. *If it's worth mending it's worth mending twice. The second time is the real one,* she said to the pot; and to the fire, *Leave the crack showing. People trust a thing they can see has been handled.* The sayings went to a pot and a fire and never to a person, improved in the keeping and very likely not as good as the man who had first said them, which did not stop them being true. The hairline of the original break stood dark between the old-brass staple and the tighter wire and she left it showing, for repair stays apart from restoration: a restored thing hides the hand that handled it, and a mended thing leaves that hand showing. At the rack end the grain lay turned once under wool, her fingers going in and coming out without closing, and the pot's rim went down two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat at the setting and no look toward the bed. He took the half-beat apart by what was there and by what was not: the distance between the hand's intention and its arrest, a small exact interval that bought nothing at two inches of air and was not a gift, for a gift wants a giver and nobody had given. He wanted the rim under his fingers and got the space where it would have been, and the space was not entered.

The whole tray was checked and left as it lay, as a surgeon checks a field before the light and sets down every instrument where it was found. Six stood behind the one unsigned figure and stayed held behind it, not spent to make the figure come out even, and the figure did not know what any of them cost: six hearings of the full note beside and never among with the seventh place empty, six openings in six months with no seventh made due, six where the house could check and the seventh at the lip. An empty place kept empty is a finding, while a finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged. The sixth hearing was the small flat knock out of \person{Dessa Silt} as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go and never among the things that had come out of vessels. The heard bell stood in its square beside the told bell of \lore{the Long Night}, four, four and a gap with nobody crossing himself, and was not fetched in to make a seventh of the hearings or a fourth of the three counts. The told figure stood beside the paid with no signature on the space, and seven arithmetics of the year --- the three of Frost, the first, second and third of Bone and Ash's first --- stood behind one printed figure, seven and not one account, Ash's first locked and unread up the clay road in \person{Berr Clay}'s box, Ash's second two mornings ahead and named only as a date.

Nine record-first hands stood on nine places with no tenth --- the wiped thumb, the turned wrist, the soft-cuffed clerk's inward slate, the assessor's entered number, the midwife's slate before the fold, the hand to the sleeve before eyes and mouth, and the others --- nine were nine and not a rule, the ninth sitting with a finger between it and the eighth. Nine stoppings stood with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end, the loan uncalled, the Seed 16 breath unspent at the top of each reach. Five held objects sat each on its own place, with the pot-crack resting beside them and the Ash 14 hand-at-the-sill breath beside that, another finger between every pair, and none a sixth. The row stood at five askings with one shaping and no sixth; the burned-slate word stood at five with a finger between the fifth and the four; \lore{the counting room} shut in the war stood at two tellings and not three; and the Ash 5 joining, the Ash 17 direction that had come from the direction of \place{Slate Row} and gone up the terrace, the fourth shaping at the well and the Ash 6 turning of the runner's writing stood each on a place of its own with a finger between every pair, none spent, named, joined, promoted into a witness, a sixth telling, a third shut-room telling, a sixth asking, a fifth shaping, a cause, a payment or a rule --- for a rule is an account that has found a payer and none stood. The edges came near to touching, so near that a less careful hand would have called the touching a cause and the cause a debt, and the finger went between. The finding that a house keeps by \lore{the not-doings} stood on a place of its own beside his own gaps with a finger between, and was not laid across the absence of any man.

Garl stayed in objects and not in a story. The salt-stiff coat with the crease at the left shoulder kept his broader shape on its peg behind the door; the wheel-seat in the shed kept its twenty-year hollow on that side and nobody was allowed to fill it; the ridge that had been his ash-marker was now the clean dark line at the Vail corner with the finer grey beside it called the thaw, unswept both; the twice-mended pot stood on the pot board with the hairline of the original break showing between the old-brass staple and the tighter wire; and the unforged collector sentence lay beside the knife at the kindling sill with no name on the man who had turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on the debt. The debt against a firing of tiles circled without being called, neither paid nor settled nor done with and worse for being uncalled. His name had not been spoken under this roof three hundred and forty-seven days, and no lesson had been made of any of it. The keeping of him was maintenance and not a story.

Under the floor the carried fire kept its four and its gap; overhead the boards kept four nails on the near one, four on the next and a thumb-width knot where a fifth might have been driven and was not; through \place{the finger's-width door} \lore{water over water} went low over lower, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming, and was never numbered among the hearings, for a hearing wants a source standing and none stood. Three counts in three places, and none rose into another, because agreeing any two would have wanted a payer put under both and none stood to any. The bell's gap stayed in its square and was not folded into any of them.

In the small hours the hand outside the wool went up an inch toward the river's note under the finger's-width door, the way a hand goes up in a field it has learned not to disturb. It did not reach, and the inch was not a reach; he had long ago stopped calling it one. Most of what a hand does in a field is wait, and he had once taken a hand into a cavity slowly, with the whole of the attention, and knew the difference between the moving and the waiting. The note went water over water under the finger's-width door, a low thing over a lower thing, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming, never numbered among the hearings, for a hearing wants a source standing and none stood. The hand took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole, let the intake go on the note's own finishing, and came straight down and laid itself back against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. The note had done before the hand came down, so the letting-go was neither early again nor stayed, and the hand did not stay. It was the note's own finishing that ended the interval and not any decision of the hand, and that was the whole of the honesty in it. The intake was not a breath kept for warmth, the interval not a stopping of the river, the note not his.

He filed it as answer two hundred and seventy: exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the seven arithmetics, the bell's gap, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the held breath at the sill, the staying at the inch, the early letting-go, the sixth opening, the pot-crack resting, the Ash 5 joining, the word that stood in no doorway, the fourth shaping, the turned writing, the strip in the sleeve nor his own kept gaps. Two hundred and seventy honest answers stood on the sixth place, each an object and not a power, and not one stood up and called itself a doctrine, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood. The surgeon took his small clean satisfaction in a field left as found and in an answer entered where nobody could see it; the infant underneath took none of it and went on wanting the crust lifted the two inches to his mouth, the thread wiped and a hand on the pot handle. Both were true, the second louder, neither winning, the two registers quarrelling without verdict in the ordinary small hours of the eighteenth.

Three hundred and forty-seven stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody under the Vail roof; six months and one hundred and sixty-seven days were told by nobody; the carried fire's fifth was unpaid the same number with its payer unnamed; and the name of the man the salt-stiff coat kept was unspoken the same number. Nothing on the eighteenth had been opened, joined by a standing hand, named, called, paid or ended. No runner had been seen on any road and no collector had come up or down the clay road, and there was no market, no Cross and no bell. Ash kept its eighteenth day without a boundary drawn and neither of the district's two habits of naming its months --- the older women by the weather, the slatemen by the number of Account-days --- was set right or left wrong in so many words; the month carried both names the way a pot carries two handles and is none the worse. The keeping of the number was the only honest work there had been to do, bought nothing at two inches of air, was not for sale, and went on because attention is the one thing no institution can tax. The file lay open on \place{the towel} at the head of the bed with every gap left gap; the twice-mended pot stood on the pot board with the hairline of the break showing between the old-brass staple and the tighter wire; one stiff brown-edged crust lay two inches from his ear with its empty place unnamed and no third, the thread at his mouth frozen and unwiped; and through the finger's-width door the river went water over water, low over lower, while the boards overhead kept four nails on the near one, four on the next and a thumb-width knot where a fifth might have been driven and was not. Ash 20 stood two mornings ahead and was named only as a date and not brought into a day that had not earned it. Forward, not closed.
