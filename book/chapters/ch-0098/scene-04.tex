In the afternoon the dry kiln heat pressed at the Vail roof the way a hand presses at a seam and finds it holding, and \person{Sella Vail} carried the basket down under the finger's-width door into \place{the Clay Yard}. The instruction went to the wool and the river wind and never to the basket. “Keep it off his ears,” she told the wind, and set the fold right under his chin without a look at his face. She carried him at the half-pace she kept from every open slate, the way a woman carries a pot filled and does not slop it, and the yard came up around \person[Corin]{the boy} by the small sounds first, because the small sounds do not lie and the large ones are mostly weather: the dry rasp of rope at the well, the settle of clay cooling behind a wall, the far knock of a pot set down out of sight. Three hundred and forty-six stood exact behind him, six months and one hundred and sixty-six days told by nobody in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill}, \place{Grath}. Under the floor \lore{the carried fire} kept four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer unnamed three hundred and forty-six days; on its peg the salt-stiff coat kept a broader man's shape the same number with no name spoken. Nothing from the morning crossed the yard as sign, lesson, mending, cause or payment.

The crust kept its two inches from his ear, leathery and brown-edged, its corner a hair off the wool, the empty place beside it keeping its name to itself; no third had come to make the row come out even. The thread at his mouth had frozen and would stay frozen, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread, and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. Cold had come to cheek, temple and the small bare joint of his wrist where the wool rode back, and had stopped there as at the end of a line written out for it; \lore{the Afterdrop} waited past the stopping for a warming nobody here would perform. He knew the composed face that is not composure and the grimace of deep cold wearing the look of a smile, and a cold patient who will not clot and will seem better before he is worse. He wanted the crust lifted two inches, the thread wiped, and a hand that would close on a pot handle set within reach. Both were true, the second was louder, neither won. A man who had once decided which of twenty bodies would be opened first could not now lift a barley crust to his own mouth. The joke cost him nothing and was not funny.

She went around the yard the way the yard went around itself, with a look and a wide step and no number. At \place{the Vail corner} the clean dark line lay where it had lain and the finer grey hairline the district called \lore{the thaw} lay beside it unswept, and she went wide of both and swept neither. Beneath \place{the lowest rack} the broken birth-slates lay where they lay and she stepped around them, entering nothing; the round ring where the shallow pan had once stood kept its place with no scrape, crumb, print, chip or word. At \place{the yard well} \place{the trough} kept its skin, and on \place{the flat stone by the well} the silt-clouded plate of ice stood out of the path of feet; out on \place{the worked strips} the field kept its empty place; and in the returning line, where a hand had gone in on Seed 15 and not come out, she went around with a look and no name. Seven places, no eighth likeness, no cause across two of them, no payer under any. He entered none, because a thing entered wants a payer and none stood.

The three coverings stayed weather and were entered nowhere: the frost gone off the well's north lip while the yard looked elsewhere, neither a sign now nor a sign then; the skin on the trough breaking under a pail and closing over what it covered; the silt-clouded plate standing where no foot went. Three things on three places with a finger between every pair, and no fourth likeness made a cause.

At \place{the rack end} the dressed grain lay turned under wool away from \place{the hearth}, and Sella lifted a corner with a touch still lighter than a woman would use on a thing she meant to count, turned it once and gave no figure. Her fingers went in and came out without closing, so no handful was lifted. The boy filed the empty fingers beside the one object that was the handful let fall on Bone 22---a keeping, not a second handful, not a sixth held change, not a rule, with no payer under it. Whatever figure the seventeenth put on that heap would not know what it cost, and the space between figure and price would have no signature.

The hunts and repairs stayed directions judged by the carrying arm and the weather. A man come down from \place{Cold Acre} shifted a hare across his shoulders at the well and, asked what the carrying had cost, told the asker to ask the weather, which had carried the snow and could carry the price; he went off toward \place{the clay road} with the hare unweighed and nobody opening a slate. Another with a cracked jar in a rope sling asked what the month's mending would want and was told to ask the pot, and the pot said nothing. A woman with kindling asked what the seventh year would want, and two fence-menders said the thaw would be early and late. \person{Tarn}, bent at the shoulders from six years in the slip pit, with \place{Ash} worked into his knuckle creases in the colour of the clean dark line, said “Yes, house” to each and kept the late because a late thaw put off the paying, agreeing so thoroughly that no argument could find a foot. The boy read each as a chart blank where the number should stand and entered none of the blanks.

It was into this ordinary going-on, with the boards already talking and the yard going around its places, that the word came. It came over \place{the boards} from the direction of \place{Slate Row}, spoken over the boards and not into the basket, on the same breath as the price of barley and the weather. A voice with no owner said that a room had been shut with the damper closed, and that a slate had been burned over a man whose name nobody said. It gave no room named, no hand at any flame, no figure, no date standing, and no man standing in any doorway to be asked. It was a direction and not a visit. The voice went along the boards where the neighbours stood with their baskets the way a man speaks to boards, and did not look into the basket and did not lower itself for the child in it; a word spoken over a thing is not a word spoken to it, and this one had come a quarter league and would go back the way it came.

The weather answered first. A wrong wind came up off \place{High Kiln} and carried the sweet edge of \lore{lead glaze} over the boards, where the boy lay with the fold against his ear, and touched the fold and went out on the wind that brought it, a direction arrived nowhere and not a second edge paid. The price of barley answered after it, going over the boards and off up the yard, and the voice went with it up \place{the terrace} and was gone. The boards lay where they had laid, four nails on the near one, four on the next and a thumb-width knot where a fifth might have been driven and was not. Nobody leaned in to take the answer down. Nobody turned twice.

The boy took the word apart as a surgeon takes a wound apart, refusing from the first cut to let it close on itself and call itself whole. What was there was a direction, a voice with no owner, a room shut with the damper closed, a slate burned, the price of barley, the weather. What was not there was a room named, a hand at a flame, a figure, a date, a name for the man, a man standing anywhere to be asked. A voice from a direction was not a witness; a word going around a yard was not a hand at a flame, for a witness stands somewhere and can be asked, and this voice stood a quarter league away and went on to the price of barley.

He found no witness at the bottom of it. He filed the word on a place of its own beside the joining said over these boards on the fifth of Ash, when an ownerless voice first joined the burned slate to the room with the damper shut, and put a finger between the two: a further shaping of that joining and an advancement of the finding that a place wants a witness and a witness is a day somebody stands somewhere and can be asked, but not the witness and not standing. \lore{The burned-slate word} stayed at five with a finger between the fifth and the four, not a sixth telling; \lore{the counting room} shut in the war at two, not three; the row at five askings with no sixth; the well-saying at four shapings with no fifth. Not a hearing of \lore{the full note}, not a seventh, not a cause, not a confession, not a payment, not a reader, not a name, not a witness. No payer stood under it. He set it there and made nothing of its being there, and looked for no sixth beside it, because a finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged.

At the yard well \person{Ilva} drew with the pail against her knee, the rope stiff in the Ash cold and giving its small dry rasp as the bucket came up, clay dried pale in the crescents of her nails and the finer grey dust of \place{the drying racks} worked into the seam of one thumb she did not scrub out. Her hands knew the weight of a pail and the edge of a wet board and had never been soft. People noticed the pail first and the sleeve not at all.

A woman waiting her turn, with a pot on her hip and her shawl tight against the river wind, asked what Ash's firings had taken. Ilva's hand went toward her sleeve and stayed. The strip of notched birch bark, pale where a thumb had rubbed the notches, was not taken out, read, copied, counted or joined to the open slate a pace away, to the charcoal beneath \place{the loaf shelf}, to the cord, the knot-string or any saying that arrived nowhere; no figure came off the wood. Her eyes went to the asker and her mouth opened after both with a half-beat. “Ask the pot what it cost. I keep the count, not the account,” she said, answering the question put and not the one behind it. Flat hand once more on the sleeve, back to the pail, nobody turning twice.

The boy took the order of her reaching apart as a surgeon takes a field apart: hand to the record before eyes to the person, eyes before mouth, the half-beat in the gap where a lesser observer would put an intention and find nothing. He filed it as the ninth record-first hand in the district, with a finger between it and \person{Hessa Rale}'s eighth, and filed no tenth. He made no figure of the slate's nearness, made her no reader of her own keeping, joined her to none. She was not the voice that went over the boards; her well gave no fifth shaping and no witness; the turning of the runner's writing toward herself on Ash 6 stayed a sighting. He did not make her the speaker of a direction that arrived nowhere.

At \place{the kindling} Tarn kept six in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check and the seventh at the lip beside the unforged collector sentence \person{Garl Vail} had left there, with no name on the man who turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on the debt. The small knife lay at the edge of its sill, where it could be checked and where a hand could also slip it off. “Six counted. The next I don't count,” he told the wood, and cut nothing, counted nothing, called no loan.

He did the small checking a house could see. He set the knife where it could be checked, passed his hand over blade and sill as a man passes a hand over a tool put back where it belongs, the pale clip-ring at his left wrist catching what little light there was and giving it back, and went away; no breath stayed at the top of the reach. The breath left at the sill on the fourteenth was where it had been put: checked on the fifteenth and sixteenth, checked a third time now and found, not made again on the seventeenth, filed beside the five objects and the pot-crack resting with a finger between every pair, not a sixth, not a seventh, not a tenth stopping, not the calling of the loan, with no payer under it. He cut nothing. He counted nothing. He did not spend the Seed 16 breath, and the thumb's resting of Bone 26 he checked and did not make again.

At \place{the flagstones} his stroke went over the shallow bright patch without the half-check; the returned half-check of Bone 8 stayed on its own place with the Day 161 fraction, the Day 166 shelf beat and the Frost 5 beat, four held changes on four places and not a rule. The warm loft stone lay cold in its unmarked pot, unreconciled by not trying, which is not forgiveness. He brought the ear-sentence to the word that would call the loan and stopped, the ninth stopping with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end, the loan uncalled.

\person[Jorra Vail]{Jorra} came through the finger's-width door off \place{the Vail wheel-shed} with meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bent little finger, white in the creases of a fist that had opened, not the bone-coloured \lore{slip} of the wheel. He kept dusty forearms and clean ones on one tray and parted them as a surgeon parts clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had been broken nor whether it was a birth-slate. Men unnamed had gone low into \place{the back room} before first grey and he gave the house no name for them and did not say the room was cold. To the wheel he spoke before any person: “It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady.” To a pot, “You'll do,” with no welcome and no unkindness, knowing the full note it would give three turns ahead and withholding it from a kiln-master come up from High Kiln who would call it early and make a song of it.

Sella had not finished telling him the fire was low when his bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper}, took the cold out of the fingertip, moved the tongue a hair's breadth and was left standing; the end of her instruction arrived at the pot and the household straightened around his silence the way cloth straightens when a weight is lifted. One iron had two bites, the holding and the taking, and no \lore{Grathic} word for both mercies. “He's not Vail” stayed sealed as a lid nobody had tried to lift in three years, and the promise of the boy's name to the men who burn slates was neither given nor withdrawn. The burned-slate word stood at five; the word that had gone over the boards stood beside the Ash 5 joining on a place of its own with a finger between, not a sixth telling, not a third telling of the shut room, not a cause, not a confession, not a rule. The shut-room tellings stood at two and the row at five askings with no witness.

He grew quieter when the direction went around the boards and the household straightened around the quiet without an order. Nobody told anybody to straighten. Sella gave the fire its instruction and Tarn gave the broom its, and the two went on as if the quiet were weather they had agreed to work in. The claim on the boy gathered interest against the promise he had neither given nor withdrawn; he did not pay it and did not refuse it, which in this district is a way of keeping a thing alive. He did not say that a slate burns in four minutes and had once timed it. The two tellings of the counting room shut in the war stood where they stood, unjoined to the word over the boards, and the back room kept its finger's width of open and its men unnamed.

Under one roof two men had kept each a small thing no house could check, and the boy set them on one tray and parted them as clot from vessel, his finger going between and joining nothing. A nearly-touching of two silences is not a touching, and a touching is not a payment.

At the going of the light Sella carried him up at the half-pace and wide around the seven walked-around places, the returning-line place gone around with a look and no figure as it had been going down. She set him on \place{the bed} under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof and never in \place{the Debt Loft}, put the fold right without a look at his face, let her hand rest the half-beat longer than the setting needed, and left the thread where it lay. A sound came from above and behind and no interior was entered; the instruction went to the fire, the wool, the pot and the river wind and never to the basket. She said “The house will keep it” where another woman would have said I. \lore{The widow's mark} hung where it hung and was not touched, because a going of the light and a bed are not openings; six openings in six months were six and not a rule, no seventh made due. The word that had gone up the terrace bought no opening here.

She went to the hearth before the basket, told the coals it was the thaw and turned them with the back of a broken pot, not the poker, because a pot's back knows how much a coal will bear. The fire answered four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer unnamed three hundred and forty-six days, and she did not supply the pull. Her finger lay on top of the loaf shelf's front edge so the charcoal tally stayed unread; her lips moved along a two-gapped row and figured neither gap; her eye went once to the clay jar cold under lead glaze with the hand half over nothing. From \place{the pot board} she lifted the twice-mended pot with her thumb first where the wire crossed the crack, both proverbs to the pot and the fire and never to a person, the hairline showing between old-brass staple and tighter wire, repair kept apart from restoration. At the rack end her fingers went in and came out without closing, so no handful was lifted. The pot's rim went down two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat and no look toward the bed. He took the half-beat apart: the distance between intention and arrest, bought at two inches of air, not a gift, for a gift wants a giver and nobody had given.

Garl stayed in objects and not in a story: the salt-stiff coat with its left-shoulder crease on its peg, the wheel-seat keeping its twenty-year hollow that nobody filled, the clean dark line at the Vail corner with its finer grey unswept, the twice-mended pot with the hairline showing between old-brass staple and tighter wire, and the unforged collector sentence beside the knife with no name on the man who turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on the debt. The tile debt circled uncalled, worse for being uncalled; his name had not been spoken under this roof three hundred and forty-six days, and the keeping of him was maintenance and not a story.

Under the floor the carried fire kept its four and its gap; overhead the boards kept four nails, four and a thumb-width knot; through the finger's-width door \lore{water over water} went low over lower, never numbered among the hearings, for a hearing wants a source standing and none stood. Six hearings of the full note kept the seventh place empty, the sixth the small flat knock out of \person{Dessa Silt} as the men lifted her. Seven arithmetics rode locked in \person{Berr Clay}'s box up the clay road, figure read still not figure paid, seven readings and not one account. Nine hands with no tenth, nine stoppings with no tenth before the seventh year at its end. Ash 20 stood two mornings ahead, named only as a date.

In the small hours the hand outside the wool went up an inch toward the river's note under the finger's-width door. It did not reach; the inch was not a reach and he had long stopped calling it one. It was the distance a hand may move in a field without disturbing the dressing, and he took it as he had once taken a hand into a cavity, slowly, knowing most of what a hand does in a field is wait. He took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole, let it go on the note's own finishing, and came straight down and laid itself back against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. The note had done before the hand came down, so the letting-go was neither early again nor stayed; it was the note's own finishing that ended the interval, and that was the whole of the honesty in it.

He filed it as answer two hundred and sixty-eight: exact, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the seven arithmetics, the bell's gap, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the held breath at the sill, the joining of the fifth of Ash, the word that stood in no doorway, the fourth shaping, the turned writing, Ilva's wood nor his own kept gaps. Two hundred and sixty-eight honest answers stood on the sixth place, each an object and not a power, not one standing up and calling itself a doctrine, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood. The surgeon took his small clean satisfaction in a field left as found; the infant took none and went on wanting the crust lifted, the thread wiped and a hand on the pot handle.

Three hundred and forty-six stood exact, unspent and spoken by nobody; six months and one hundred and sixty-six days were told by nobody; the carried fire's fifth was unpaid the same number, the name in the coat unspoken the same. Nothing on the seventeenth was opened, named, called, paid or ended; no runner on any road, no collector on the clay road, no market, Cross or bell. The file lay open on \place{the towel} with every gap left gap; the twice-mended pot stood with the hairline showing; one crust lay two inches from his ear with its empty place unnamed and no third, the thread frozen and unwiped. The voice had gone up the terrace with nobody having stood in any doorway to ask it anything. Forward, not closed.
