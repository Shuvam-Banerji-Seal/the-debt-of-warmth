\daymark{346}{Ash 17, Seventhday, Y.H. 813}

The hand lay against the fold where it had been laid down on the two hundred and sixty-sixth answer, as a tool is put back where the next hand will find it without looking. It was a small soft hand with milky nails and no strength in the wrist worth the naming; it had once reduced a shoulder in a room that smelled of disinfectant and cold metal, and had since learned the exact weight of a barley crust at two inches. In the small hours under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof \person[Corin]{the boy} lay awake on \place{the bed} at the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf}, and took the room as a surgeon takes a field before the light, by the small sounds first, because the small sounds do not lie and the large ones are mostly weather: the dry tick of clay cooling behind the wall, the settle of a coal under its ash, the river going low over lower under the finger's-width door. The room smelled of wet wool and tallow and, under them, the faint sour mineral tang of the Ash ridge along the skirting board, which had not been swept in two years and would not be swept this morning. Three hundred and forty-six stood exact behind him, six months and one hundred and sixty-six days told by nobody in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill}, \place{Grath}. Under the floor \lore{the carried fire} had given four low pulls and kept the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer unnamed three hundred and forty-six days; on its peg behind the door the salt-stiff coat kept the broader shape of \person[Garl Vail]{a man} whose name had not been spoken under this roof the same number. Nothing from the sixteenth crossed the dark toward him as sign, lesson, mending, cause or payment. A day lived is a day set down; he did not go back into it to fetch a meaning out.

\place{Ash} kept its seventeenth day without a boundary drawn. The older women of the district named the months by the weather and the slatemen by the number of Account-days gone into them, and neither habit was set right or left wrong in so many words under this roof; the month carried both names the way a pot carries two handles and is none the worse. There would be no market, no Cross and no bell on the seventeenth, and no collector up or down \place{the clay road}; the going of the light would come as an ordinary going of the light, measured in loaves and glances and the space where a loaf should have stood. Three hundred and forty-six was not round and not a threshold, only one more in a row nobody counted aloud, and he had long since stopped expecting to be told what a number counted, which was not the same as not wanting it.

The crust lay two inches from his ear in the dry kiln heat, gone stiffer and browner than it had been the day before, its corner standing a hair off the wool and the empty place beside it keeping its name to itself; no third had come in the days behind to make the row come out even. The thread of drool at his mouth had frozen in the dry heat and would stay frozen, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread, and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. Cold had come to cheek and temple and the small bare joint of his wrist where the wool rode back, and had stopped there as if it had come to the end of a line written out for it, with neither an order to go farther nor an order to go away; \lore{the Afterdrop} waited past the stopping, beyond a warming nobody in this house would perform.

He knew the composed face that is not composure and the ease that is no ease, the grimace of deep cold that wears the look of a smile. A cold patient cannot clot, will seem better before he is worse, and must be warmed from the outside and slowly, never hurried, never rubbed, never set too near the fire. He knew the order in which a body takes heat back, the small joint first and the core last, and the order in which it gives it up, the reverse, which comes sooner than anybody likes; and that pink returning to a face is the surface still cold and not the core. He wanted the crust lifted the two inches to his mouth, the thread wiped away, and a hand that would close on a pot handle set within reach. Both were true, the second was louder, and neither won. The argument went on under the wool without a verdict, the surgeon's register taking the small hours in its old order while the infant underneath went on being cold. It was a small humiliation and an exact one: a man who had once decided which of twenty bodies would be opened first could not lift a barley crust two inches, could not wipe his own mouth, could not keep a fold of wool at his own chin. The joke cost him nothing and was not funny.

\scenebreak

\person{Sella Vail} came to \place{the hearth} before the basket. She was a broad woman with forearms the colour of dried slip and a burn across the back of her left hand shaped like a little door --- two straight sides, a lintel and a threshold, the skin inside it puckered and pale --- and she smelled of woodsmoke and wet wool. One grey streak at her temple showed pale in the low light as she pushed the coal-black hair back with the burn-wrist. She told the coals it was \lore{the thaw} and turned them with the back of a broken pot, not with the poker, because a pot's back knows how much a coal will bear. The fire answered four low pulls with the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer unnamed three hundred and forty-six days, and she did not supply the pull. “The house will keep it,” she said where another woman would have said I. Then she stood a moment counting a small intake, a hold and a letting-go toward no opening, and gave neither gap a figure.

Her finger lay on top of the loaf shelf's front edge so the charcoal tally underneath stayed unread. Her lips moved along a two-gapped row and figured neither gap. Her eye went once to the clay jar standing cold under \lore{lead glaze} and her hand shut half over nothing, the little-door burn on the back of her left hand closing on air; nothing in the jar was named and no figure came off the glaze. When her hands had nothing to do they counted spoons, nails, the breaths of a sleeping child, the days in loaves and glances and unnamed spaces, never in a number anybody else would have recognised as a number.

From \place{the pot board} she lifted the twice-mended pot with her thumb first where the wire crossed the crack, felt what the thumb had to feel, and only then let the weight come on the handle. “If it's worth mending it's worth mending twice. The second time is the real one,” she said to the pot; and to the fire, “Leave the crack showing. People trust a thing they can see has been handled.” The sayings went to a pot and a fire and never to a person. They had improved in the keeping and were very likely not as good as the man who had first said them, which did not stop them being true. The hairline of the original break stood dark between the old-brass staple and the tighter wire, and she left it showing. Repair stayed apart from restoration: a restored thing hides the hand that handled it, and a mended thing leaves that hand showing. He read the mending as a wound closed in a place the hand would not have chosen, by what the hand had done and by what it had left undone, and kept the two readings apart, for a seam that holds is not a seam that never broke.

At \place{the rack end} the dressed grain lay turned under wool away from the hearth. She lifted a corner with a touch still lighter than the morning before, turned it once and gave no figure, and her fingers went in and came out without closing, so that no handful was lifted. Whatever arithmetic the seventeenth might put upon a heap of grain would not know what the heap had cost, and the space between that figure and its price would have no signature set into it.

The pot's rim went down two inches beyond the reaching hand, with a half-beat at the setting and no look toward the bed. He took the half-beat apart by what was there and by what was not: the distance between the hand's intention and its arrest, a small exact interval that bought nothing at two inches of air and was not a gift, for a gift wants a giver and nobody had given. He wanted the rim under his fingers and got the space where it would have been, and the space was not entered.

\lore{The widow's mark} hung where it hung and was not touched. A morning and a hearth are not openings, and the hand goes to the mark before the kiln and at no other hour; the last touch had been at \place{the kiln mouth} on Ash 2, before the dark month's first firing. Six openings in six months --- Tallow, Ember, Smoke, Frost, Bone and Ash --- are six and not a rule. The sixth stood behind at Ash 2, held without a name and not spent, and no seventh was made due on a day that had not earned one. The mark answered nothing this morning for the crust at two inches, the empty place beside it, the fire's missing fifth, the hand that had gone in on Seed 15 and not come out, or the woman carried out of the brook clay-heavy and uncollected; none of those stood under it asking to be answered, and she did not ask on their behalf.

\scenebreak

The whole tray was checked and left as it lay, as a surgeon checks a field before the light and sets down every instrument where it was found. Seven arithmetics of the year --- the three of Frost, the first, second and third of Bone, and Ash's first --- stood behind one printed figure, and seven were seven and not one account. The figure read was still not the figure paid, and no signature stood on the space between. The finding sat where findings sat, on a place of its own with no payer under it, and was not laid across the house's \lore{not-doings}, the dressed grain covered under wool, the five held objects, the handful let fall at the rack end on Bone 22, the resting of a thumb on the uncut seventh at \place{the kindling} on Bone 26, the pot-crack resting beside the five, the held breath at the knife-sill of Ash 14, the two small objects of the hand, the sixth opening at the kiln mouth, the Ash 5 joining, the fourth shaping at the well, the Ash 6 turning of the runner's writing, \person{Ilva}'s wood nor his own kept gaps. The edges came near to touching, so near that a less careful hand would have called the touching a cause and the cause a payment; the finger went between. A finding with no payer can be carried a long way without becoming a rule, and no doctrine stood up out of seven. Ash's first rode locked and unread up the clay road in \person{Berr Clay}'s box toward \place{High Kiln}; Ash's second stood offstage on Ash 20, named only as a date and not brought into a day that had not earned it.

Six hearings of \lore{the full note} stood beside and never among, with the seventh place empty, and an empty place kept empty is a finding while a finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged. The sixth was the small flat knock out of \person{Dessa Silt} as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go and never among the things that had come out of vessels. The heard bell stood in its square beside the told bell of \lore{the Long Night}, four, four and a gap with nobody crossing himself, and was not fetched in to make a seventh of the hearings or a fourth of the three counts. The told figure stood beside the paid with no signature on the space. Nine record-first hands stood on nine places with no tenth --- Berr's wiped thumb, \person{Hessa Rale}'s turned wrist, the soft-cuffed clerk's inward slate, the assessor's entered number, the midwife's slate before the fold, Ilva's hand to her sleeve before eyes and mouth, and the others --- and nine were nine and not a rule, the ninth sitting with a finger between it and the eighth. Nine stoppings stood with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end, the loan uncalled, the Seed 16 breath unspent at the top of the reach. Five held objects sat each on its own place, with the pot-crack resting beside them and the Ash 14 hand-at-the-sill breath beside that, another finger between every pair, and none was a sixth. He set down what was there and looked for nothing to fill an empty place, because a finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged. The surgeon took his small clean satisfaction in the refusal itself, plain and unadorned and worth nothing; the infant underneath took none of it and went on wanting.

Exactly no voice came over \place{the boards} in the small hours. The Ash 5 joining of the burned slate to the room shut with the damper closed, the fourth shaping at the well and the Ash 6 turning of the runner's writing stayed each on its own place, unspent and unpromoted, with a finger between every pair. No sixth telling of the slate came due, no third telling of the shut room, no sixth asking of the row, no fifth shaping of the well-saying, no hearing, no seventh of the full note, no witness standing in any doorway to be asked, no cause and no payment. The boards lay where they lay, four nails on the near one, four on the next and the thumb-width knot where a fifth might have been driven and was not. The far-off hand that would one day lay the district's private keepings together was still far off, and he did not look for it.

Garl stayed in objects and not in a story. The salt-stiff coat with the crease at the left shoulder kept his broader shape on its peg behind the door; the wheel-seat in the shed kept its twenty-year hollow on that side and nobody was allowed to fill it; the ridge that had been his ash-marker was now the clean dark line at \place{the Vail corner} with the finer grey beside it called the thaw, unswept both; the twice-mended pot stood on the pot board with the hairline of the original break showing between the old-brass staple and the tighter wire; and the unforged collector sentence lay beside the knife at the kindling sill with no name on the man who had turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on the debt. The debt against a firing of tiles circled without being called, neither paid nor settled nor done with and worse for being uncalled. His name had not been spoken under this roof three hundred and forty-six days, and no lesson had been made of any of it. The keeping of him was maintenance and not a story.

Under the floor the carried fire kept its four and its gap; overhead the boards kept four nails on the near one, four on the next and a thumb-width knot where a fifth might have been driven and was not; through the finger's-width door \lore{water over water} went low over lower, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming, and was never numbered among the hearings, for a hearing wants a source standing and none stood. Three counts in three places, and none rose into another, because agreeing any two would have wanted a payer put under both and none stood to any. The bell's gap stayed in its square and was not folded into any of them, and the heard bell was not made a seventh of the full note.

First light came through the finger's-width door and the room took its shape by degrees, the pot board first, then the shelf's low edge, then the fold at his ear. The morning started anyway, indifferent and welcome, the way a pot is set down where a pot belongs and no speech made over it. The boy lay small and cold under the wool and filed what there was to file, which was all of it and none of it spendable.

\scenebreak

In the small hours the hand outside the wool went up an inch toward the river's note, the way a hand goes up in a field it has learned not to disturb. It did not reach, and the inch was not a reach; he had long ago stopped calling it one. Most of what a hand does in a field is wait, and he had once taken a hand into a cavity slowly, with the whole of the attention, and knew the difference between the moving and the waiting. The note went water over water under the finger's-width door, a low thing over a lower thing, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming, never numbered among the hearings, for a hearing wants a source standing and none stood. The hand took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole, let the intake go on the note's own finishing, and came straight down and laid itself back against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. The note had done before the hand came down, so the letting-go was neither early again nor stayed, and the hand did not stay. It was the note's own finishing that ended the interval and not any decision of the hand, and that was the whole of the honesty in it. The intake was not a breath kept for warmth, the interval not a stopping of the river, the note not his.

He filed it as answer two hundred and sixty-seven: exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the seven arithmetics, the bell's gap, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the held breath at the sill, the staying at the inch, the early letting-go, the sixth opening, the pot-crack resting, the Ash 5 joining, the fourth shaping, the turned writing, Ilva's wood nor his own kept gaps. Two hundred and sixty-seven honest answers stood on the sixth place, each an object and not a power, and not one stood up and called itself a doctrine, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood. The surgeon took his small clean satisfaction in a field left as found and in an answer entered where nobody could see it; the infant underneath took none of it and went on wanting the crust lifted the two inches to his mouth, the thread wiped and a hand on the pot handle. Both were true, the second louder, neither winning, the two registers quarrelling without verdict in the ordinary small hours of the seventeenth.

Three hundred and forty-six stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in Low Kiln, Sill and Grath; six months and one hundred and sixty-six days were told by nobody; the carried fire's fifth was unpaid the same number with its payer unnamed; and the name of the man the salt-stiff coat kept was unspoken the same number. Nothing on the seventeenth had been opened, joined by a standing hand, named, called, paid or ended. No runner had been seen on any road and no collector had come up or down the clay road, and there was no market, no Cross and no bell. The keeping of the number was the only honest work there had been to do, bought nothing at two inches of air, was not for sale, and went on because attention is the one thing no institution can tax. The file lay open on \place{the towel} at the head of the bed with every gap left gap; the twice-mended pot stood on the pot board with the hairline of the break showing between the old-brass staple and the tighter wire; one stiff brown-edged crust lay two inches from his ear with its empty place unnamed and no third, the thread at his mouth frozen and unwiped; and through the finger's-width door the river went water over water, low over lower, while the boards overhead kept four nails on the near one, four on the next and a thumb-width knot where a fifth might have been driven and was not. Forward, not closed.
