## Chronicle

Those three days of Ash went by the way a pot goes around a shelf — without speech, and with a place kept for what was absent. Sella Vail went to the hearth before the basket, told the coals it was the thaw, turned them with the back of a broken pot, and said *The house will keep it* where another woman would have said I. The charcoal tally stayed unread under her finger, and the mended pot came up thumb-first with its hairline showing. In the Clay Yard she went wide around seven walked-around places with a look and no number and turned the grain once under wool without lifting a handful. Tarn kept six where the house could check and the seventh at the lip, said *Six counted. The next I don't count*, and stopped the ear-sentence short of the calling word. Jorra spoke to the wheel before any person, withheld the note he knew three turns ahead, and put his bent finger into the cold damper ahead of a sentence unfinished.

On the seventeenth a voice with no owner came over the boards from the direction of Slate Row — a word of a room shut with the damper closed and a slate burned over a man nobody named, with no room named, no hand at the flame, no figure, no date and nobody standing in any doorway to be asked. The weather answered first and the price of barley after it, and the voice went up the terrace. The boy filed it beside the joining of the fifth of Ash, a further shaping and not a witness, not a sixth telling, not a payment; no payer stood under it. Jorra grew quieter and the household straightened around the quiet without an order. At the well Ilva answered *Ask the pot what it cost. I keep the count, not the account*, her hand on her sleeve and the notched wood unread.

Each night the hand outside the wool went up an inch, took a small intake, held it whole on the river's finishing and came straight down — answers 265 through 270, exact and worth nothing at two inches of air. Two hundred and seventy honest answers stood on the sixth place, each an object and not a power. Three hundred and forty-seven days stood exact and spoken by nobody; the carried fire kept four pulls and a gap, the boards four nails, four and a thumb-width knot, and nothing under that roof was opened, joined by a standing hand, named, called, paid or ended. What the three days cost was attention, which no institution can tax, and the keeping of a number nobody would speak.
