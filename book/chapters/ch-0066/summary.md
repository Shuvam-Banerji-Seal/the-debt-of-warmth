## Chronicle

Three days came and went under the Vail roof. Nobody spoke a number over the boy; he kept it exact, complete, unspent, worth nothing at two inches of air. Fire under the floor drew four pulls and missed its fifth; river through the door went over water; the boards held four nails, four and a knot. Three counts in three places, for agreeing any two would want a payer and none stood to any.

On the tenth of Smoke Sella set the basket in the moot-post's thin shade. Eight pigs went over the wet boards and two hundred leaned in. Berr Clay came up short off the clay road; he wiped his thumb twice, passed a finger over a figure and read one in twelve, as the notice says, the figure going twice over the basket. He called the levy, denied it with your house, never I, and went off with the box locked and the true subtraction inside, believing he had done a kindness. The district's arithmetic changed for the first time in Smoke and could not be changed back.

The printed figure stood unchallenged, the figure read still not the figure paid, the space between the figure over the covered grain and its cost unsigned. The bell went four, four and a gap; nobody crossed himself. Hessa Rale carried the slate up unread. Sella brought him home at dusk and said nothing of the reading.

The next two mornings went back to the house's grammar. The dressed grain came out from under wool, was spread thin, gathered by the handful and set flat, and gained no figure. A cost went to the pot, and a number in a fist came up toward the well and went back into a sleeve, so the figure arrived nowhere. Sella went to the fire first, said The house will keep it, kept her finger above the charcoal. At the well Ilva kept her hand in her sleeve: I keep the count, not the account.

At the kindling Tarn set six where the house could check and the seventh where it could not; the ear-sentence stopped at the word that would call the loan, nine stoppings with no tenth. Jorra parted dust from slip, told a pot You'll do and withheld a note; the burned slate stood at four, the shut-room tellings at two, and he's not Vail stayed sealed. Garl was kept in objects, his name unspoken two hundred and fifty-one days; no collector came.

Six times the boy asked his hand, the seventy-third through the seventy-eighth: down at once; held through the bell's missing stroke; held for the small intake; kept to the river's own interval; one rhythm across a borrowed silence; two rhythms crossing a wall that does not touch, remembered. None was a reach, a hearing or a power. At the close the file lay exact, complete and worth nothing; the hand laid itself against the fold like a tool put away, and the fire stopped at four.
