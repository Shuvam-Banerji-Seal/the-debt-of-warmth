The afternoon thinned without ending. It went the way slip goes when a hand keeps working water into it --- the same clay, gone lighter, spread further, holding less of itself in any one place --- and the light came to lie flat along the floorboards instead of standing up in the door. The heat that had all morning lived at \place{the kiln mouth} backed away behind the pot, went under the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf}, and settled at last into the fold where \person[Corin]{the boy} lay. It was a dry heat, the heat of a room that has been given a great deal and returns very little, and he knew it for the wrong heat. A man may be warm to the back of his hand and still be losing from the middle of him, and the loss does not show on the skin.

One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from his ear, gone hard in the dry warmth, and the empty place beside it kept its name to itself. No third had come to lie down and make the row come out even; a third crust entering a fold where two had stood would be the beginning of an account, and this house gave without entering. The thread of drool at his mouth had frozen hours ago and was unwiped still, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread, and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. Cold had come cheek, temple, the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, and had stopped there as though a line had been written out for it to that length and no longer. The afterdrop waited beyond all three, past a warming nobody in this house would perform. He knew the severe face cold wears and the face a man wears when warmth is brought back too fast; he knew a cold patient cannot clot and that the warming, if it ever came, must come from the outside and slowly. He wanted the crust. Both were true and the afternoon made the second no quieter. The surgeon took the facts in the order they arrived --- crust, gap, frozen thread, cold, the waiting afterdrop --- and entered none of them, not even in the place where he kept the count.

Two hundred and fifty stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in \place{Sarn}, \place{Sill} or \place{Grath}; six months and seventy days stood where six months and sixty-nine had stood the day before, and nobody under any of those roofs had put a voice to either number. Under the floor \lore{the carried fire} drew four low pulls and kept the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer without a name for two hundred and fifty days. Through the finger's-width door the river kept its note of \lore{water over water}, a low thing going over a lower thing, and overhead \place{the boards} kept four nails on the near board, four on the next, and a thumb-width knot where the fifth nail should have stood. Three counts in three places, and none rose into another, because agreeing any two would want a payer put under both and no payer stood to any. The bell's four, four and a gap from the day before stayed where it had sounded, beside the told bell and not folded into the three; he had not made a seventh of the gap in the square and did not make one now, alone in a room whose low angle kept the light.

The room that held him and the room of the fire shared a wall and did not touch. Heat came through as a fact and not as a message, and sound came through as a direction and not as an instruction; Sella's voice went to the pot and the broom and the wool and the river wind in the next room, and none of it was ever sent to the basket, because a basket set down is a pot set down and left. He had spent two hundred and fifty days learning the difference between a wall and a door, and the wall had acquired no door. The half-beat at the pot's setting, the little silence after a count that had ended somewhere else, the river's note through the finger's-width gap --- these came to him as intervals with their own edges and not as a summons. A thing that does not touch is still a thing kept, and he kept the two rooms apart the way a surgeon keeps a field clean, without congratulating himself on it.

\person{Garl Vail} was kept in objects again this afternoon, which is a keeping and not a story. The \place{the salt-stiff coat} hung on its peg behind the door with the crease at the left shoulder where a broader man had leaned for twenty years, and the \place{the hollow wheel-seat} in the shed kept its hollow on that side and would not be filled. Along the skirting board the clean dark line where the Ash ridge had turned lay unswept, and beside it the new finer grey called \lore{the thaw} lay unswept too, though the broom had stood all afternoon within reach of both. His proverb had gone years ago to a pot with no crack in it, said now over a corner where a repair had been rubbed out: \emph{Leave the crack showing. People trust a thing they can see has been handled.} The debt against a firing of tiles was neither paid nor settled nor done with; it circled the house without a name on it and was worse for being uncalled. His name had not been spoken under this roof for two hundred and fifty days, and the keeping of him was maintenance, the way a pot is kept clean between firings, with no lesson made of it. No collector had come up \place{the clay road} on Smoke 10 or Smoke 11 and none had gone down it.

At \place{the kindling} \person{Tarn} took up the broom only after it had been given its instruction. The instruction went to the broom and not to him, as all instructions in this house went to the thing before the person: \emph{There. Go over it.} Tarn said \emph{Yes, house} to the wood and to the broom and to whatever else in the yard was willing to be told, and he said it in the flat tone a man uses to state the weather. \emph{Six counted. The next I don't count}, he told the kindling, in that same tone, so that the sentence sounded like a fact about the light and not like a man keeping a thing back. He set the small knife at the very edge of its sill, with six in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check and the seventh at the lip where it could not, and he looked at it after he had set it, the way a man looks at a tool he has put where somebody else will find it. Beside it lay \person{Garl Vail}'s unforged collector sentence about the man who had turned back at the frozen ford, with no name on the man and no figure on the debt.

The sentence about an ear under this roof being worth more than the debt that held it came to Tarn's teeth as he stood there. The boy had watched it come nine times and knew its shape the way he knew the shape of an instrument laid out on a towel: it went all the way to the word that would call the loan, and there it stopped, with no figure under it and no name on the man who would have paid. It was a remembered stopping and not a new one. Nine stoppings stood on the interval tray, nine objects and not a rule, with no tenth raised before the seventh year, which had come to its very end and had still not given. Nine likenesses are nine and must not stand up and call themselves a doctrine, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood to any. At the top of his reach Tarn drew the breath that should have gone in on Seed 16 and did not spend it; the small interval showed where it should have gone, and the loan stayed uncalled, and no collector came up the road or went down it on this day either. His chest had been tight since the dark. \lore{Ash} was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line, and no scrubbing lifted it; the pale clip-ring at his left wrist held what light there was and gave it back. Up above the shed, in an unmarked clay pot on a shelf of \place{the Debt Loft}, lay the warm stone \person{Sella Vail} had given him in the winter Garl died, two years gone and cold long since, unreconciled with the debt by not trying, which is not forgiving.

His stroke went over \place{the flagstones} and past \place{the shallow bright patch} without the half-check, the way a man sweeps over a place where he once stopped and does not stop again; the stones kept the place where the stroke had stopped and gave it to nobody. It went over the road a house sends a man down when it wants a collector, over and over, and Tarn did not once turn along it. That road had carried no collector for two hundred and fifty days, and the debt against the tiles had gone on standing in the ruts without a number. Above the shed the Debt Loft settled in the afternoon the way a loft settles when nobody is in it, a small sound with a direction, and the boy took the direction and nothing more. He did not bring the stone down or put a figure to it; a place a man sleeps is not the same as the man, and a pot on a shelf is not the warmth that went out of the stone. The keeping was a maintenance and a keeping, and he let it stand as one.

At \place{the Vail wheel-shed} \person{Jorra Vail} came through with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bent little finger and under the nails, white in the creases where a fist had opened, and not the bone-coloured \lore{slip} of the wheel. He kept dusty forearms and clean ones on one tray and parted them the way a surgeon parts clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had been broken nor whether it was a birth-slate. The broken birth-slates lay under \place{the lowest rack} where the yard went around them and entered them into nothing, and the boy had long ago stopped expecting anyone to lift them.

Jorra spoke to the wheel before he spoke to any person. \emph{It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady}, he told it, in the flat voice that did not finish sentences and did not need to. To a pot on the bench he said \emph{You'll do}, with no welcome in it and no unkindness either, the way a man might pass a tool to another man and not look up. The boy knew the \lore{full note} that pot would give three turns ahead, with air under it and a want in it, and Jorra knew it too and withheld the knowing, setting it on the near-full tray beside the hearings and never among them, advanced and not paid. It was a knowing and not a power: a man can know what a pot will say before it says it and still be unable to make it say anything else. Three or more men had gone low into \place{the back room} before first grey and he had given the house no name for them, and the room kept its finger's width of open and its sound of low voices that did not resolve into words. He did not say it was cold, and the boy did not say it either.

He turned the hearings out beside the file and found six, with no seventh and none invented to fill the empty place. An empty place kept empty is a finding, and a finding filled to make the row come out even is a finding forged. Three near-full answers stood advanced and unpaid beside them, each on its own place, and the pot's saying stood as a saying and not as an answer to anything the house had asked. It was the surgeon's habit and not a gift: open a thing, look, close it again, and write no figure over what was found.

The burned-slate word stood at four, told from \place{Slate Row} and arriving as a direction over the boards, and the two tellings of the counting room shut in the war stood at two and did not become three, though men had gone low into the room before the light and spoken in voices too low to carry. No name had been given to what ailed the man carried out on Day 188, and the men belonged to \lore{the Broken Slates} without being named. Three shapings of the pot saying stayed three and no fourth likeness was made a cause, for a likeness made a cause wants a payer and none stood to any. The first reading's figure, gone up the clay road in a locked box, stayed beside the figure paid and answered nothing for the carried man or the missing hand; the space between the figure read over the covered grain and what that grain had cost had no signature and was left apart, not averaged.

When Sella's sentence went low and unfinished at the hearth --- \emph{The fire's low. It wants---} --- Jorra's bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper} ahead of it, took the cold out of the fingertip, held until the end of the sentence arrived at the pot, moved a hair's breadth and was left standing. It was a remembered repetition paired with no other fraction: one piece of iron with two bites, the bite that holds a vessel whole and the bite that can take the hand off a man, with no \lore{Grathic} word for both mercies, joined to no kiln and entered in no slate. He had once held a clamp like that in a room with a hard white light, and the iron had been the same iron and the mercy the same mercy; here it was a damper and a pot and a sentence that finished somewhere else. The household straightened around the silence without an order being given, the way cloth straightens when a weight is lifted, and nobody said what had been done with the iron. \emph{He's not Vail} stayed sealed where it had been sealed on the hour it was spoken, and the promise of the boy's name to the men who burn slates was neither given nor withdrawn.

The three held changes stayed on their three places --- the Day 161 fraction at the iron, the Day 166 beat beneath the shelf, the Tallow 22 half-check on the flagstones --- beside the loft scrape on a fourth, the carried man's pale line on a fifth and the held inch on a sixth, with a finger between every pair and no fourth likeness standing up and calling itself a cause. Smoke's first arithmetic had gone up the road and changed the district's numbers where two hundred people stood, and none of the six places under this roof had moved to meet it; the reading in the square had not reached the house and the house had not gone out to meet the reading. The two silences under the roof stayed side by side and were not joined: the silence of the ear that kept a sound and the silence of the room that kept its low voices, each with its own direction and no payer standing under either.

He put the fourth question of the chapter to his hand as the room went quiet around the fire. The hand outside the wool went up an inch and was kept to the river's own low interval, with no count in the house to hold it against and no borrowed silence from the room that does not touch his. It came down on the next note. It was a fraction of air and not a longer reach, not a miss entered as a finding, not a hearing of the full note, not a seventh of it, not a tenth stopping, not a payment, not one of the held changes, not a key, a power or a rule. Answer seventy-six lay on the sixth place beside seventy-five with no payer named, and the surgeon turned out the file as instruments on a towel and found it exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air.

Under the floor the fire stopped at four and the silence where the fifth should have stood stood where it stood, its payer unnamed two hundred and fifty days. Two hundred and fifty stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in \place{Sarn}, \place{Sill} and \place{Grath}, and the count went forward into Smoke 12, a day that had not earned its heading. The file lay open on the towel.
