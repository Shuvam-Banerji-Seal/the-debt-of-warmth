The afternoon thinned the way broth thins at the edge of a fire, not going out but losing its body, so that what had been thick at noon was a skin and a small warmth by the going of the light. Heat settled into the fold at the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf}, where a shelf seen from below becomes a roof, and stayed there with the patience of a thing that has nowhere else to be. Smoke went on without a boundary drawn through it, and neither of the district's two names for the months was set right or left wrong under this roof; a house that keeps its days in loaves and glances has no use for a name, and a name, like a figure, wants a payer standing under it. Smoke 13 (Day 252) stood beyond the close and was not brought into a day that had not yet earned its heading; Smoke 20 (Day 259), the second Account-day and market of the second firing, stood eight mornings beyond this day and was not reached for early. A day that has not come does not answer to a hand that reaches toward it, and a figure not yet read is not a figure owed.

\person[Corin]{the boy} lay where the morning had left him, on \place{the bed} under the roof in \place{Low Kiln}, in the room that did not touch the room of the fire, and the inventory arrived in its old order. One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from his ear, gone hard in the dry warmth, and the empty place beside it kept its name to itself; no third had come to lie down and make the row come out even, for a third crust entering a fold where two had stood would be the beginning of an account, and this house gave without entering. The thread of drool at his mouth had frozen in the heat and was unwiped still, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread, and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. Cold had come to cheek, temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, and had stopped there as if a line had been written out for it to that length and no farther; it neither went further nor went away. The afterdrop waited beyond all three, past a warming nobody in this house would perform. He knew the face of a man going cold and knew that a cold patient cannot clot, knew the grimace severe cold wears and that the warming, if it ever came, must come from outside and slowly. He knew all of it and wanted the crust. Both were true, and the afternoon made the second no quieter. The surgeon took the facts in the order they arrived and entered none of them, not even in the place where he kept the count.

Through the finger's-width door the river kept its note of \lore{water over water}, a low thing going over a lower thing and never counted among the hearings; under the floor \lore{the carried fire} gave four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer without a name for two hundred and fifty-one days; overhead \place{the boards} kept four nails on the near board, four on the next and a thumb-width knot where the fifth nail should have stood. Three counts in three places, and none rose into another, for agreeing any two of them would want a payer put under both and no payer stood to any. The bell's four, four and a gap from the market stayed where it had sounded two days before, beside the told bell of the Long Night and not folded into the three; he had not made a seventh of its gap in the square and did not make one now in a quiet room. The figure read over the basket stayed beside the figure paid, and Smoke's first arithmetic had gone up \place{the clay road} in a locked box and answered nothing for the man carried out on Day 188 or for the hand that went in on Seed 15. He kept the trays apart without congratulating himself on it. A discipline is a thing a hand uses; a belief is a thing a man stands under, and he had only the hand, and it could go up an inch and no further.

\person{Garl Vail} was kept in objects through the afternoon, which is a keeping and not a story. The salt-stiff coat hung on its peg behind the door with the crease at the left shoulder where a broader man had leaned for twenty years, and the hollow wheel-seat out at the shed kept its hollow on that side and would not be filled by any weight of a son's. His proverb had gone years ago to a pot with no crack in it, said now over a corner where a repair had been rubbed out: \emph{Leave the crack showing. People trust a thing they can see has been handled.} The debt against a firing of tiles was neither paid nor settled nor done with; it circled the house without a name on it and was worse for being uncalled, as an untreated wound is worse for being left open in a warm room. His name had not been spoken under this roof for two hundred and fifty-one days, and the keeping of him was maintenance, the way a pot is kept clean between firings, with no lesson made of it and no payer named. No collector came up or went down the clay road on Smoke 10, 11 or 12, and the debt stood in the ruts with no figure on it. Smoke's first reading had changed the district's arithmetic where two hundred people stood and had not moved this house to meet it; the house had not gone out to the reading and the reading had not come back under the roof.

\person{Sella Vail} went to \place{the hearth} before she went to the basket. She told the fire \lore{the thaw}, turned the coals with the back of a broken pot and said \emph{The house will keep it} where another woman would have said \emph{I}. She stood a moment with the back of the pot in her hand and counted under her breath toward no opening --- the small intake, the hold, the letting-go --- and pushed the coal-black hair back with the wrist that carried the burn, so the one grey streak showed pale off the coals and the burn shaped like a little door closed on nothing and opened again to the work. The fire answered four low pulls and kept its fifth where it had always kept it, and she fed it as if it had given her no reason to count what it lacked. The hand went to \lore{the widow's mark} before the kiln and at no other hour; here, at the hearth on Smoke 12, it went to the coals and the pot and the work, and the mark hung where it hung on its cord at her throat, a thumb-length of lead-glazed clay with the Vail kiln scratched into it. She touched it at no shelf, no well, no rack, no road, no basket, no afternoon and no dusk on any of the three days of this tend. The one opening before Smoke 1 lay behind the household; she did not look at it again and made no lesson of it and named no payer under it. A thing that does not touch is still a thing kept, and the two rooms stayed apart the way a surgeon keeps a field clean, without congratulating herself on that either.

At \place{the kiln mouth} the dry heat came up in a small breathing that was not the fire's count, and she set a pot rim out with its handle two inches beyond the reaching hand, with a half-beat at the setting that was neither a look toward the bed nor a look away from it. Two inches of air was the whole distance and the hand could not close it; the setting went on being a setting and not a kindness entered. She moved her lips along the row at the loaf shelf with its two gaps and gave nobody a figure, the way a woman runs a thumb along a hem to find the tear and does not say what she found. Her finger stayed on top of the shelf's front edge, not beneath it, so the charcoal tally under the board stayed unread and uncounted, a mark kept where nobody thinks to look above a row kept badly on top. A mark nobody reads is still a mark kept, and a mark read wants a figure put under it, and a figure wants a payer. Her eye went once to the clay jar cold under its glaze on the wall and her hand shut half over nothing and came away; she did not touch the jar and did not say what it held.

At \place{the kindling} \person{Tarn} took up the broom only after it had been given its instruction, and he took it the way a man takes up a tool already named. \emph{There. Go over it}, the house said to the broom, and he said \emph{Yes, house} to the wood and to whatever else in the yard was willing to be told, in the flat tone another man uses to state the weather. \emph{Six counted. The next I don't count}, he told the kindling, so that the sentence sounded like a fact about the light and not like a man keeping a thing back. He set the small knife at the very edge of its sill, with six in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check and the seventh at the lip where it could not, and looked at it after setting it the way a man looks at a tool he has put where somebody else will find it. Beside it lay Garl's unforged collector sentence about the man who turned back at the frozen ford, with no name on the man and no figure on the debt.

The ear-sentence came to his teeth and went all the way to the word that would call the loan and stopped there, with no figure and no name, a remembered stopping and not a new one. Nine stoppings stood on their places with no tenth before the seventh year, which stood at its very end and had still not given. He drew the breath from Seed 16 at the top of the reach and did not spend it, and the loan stayed uncalled and no collector came. His chest had been tight since the dark; \lore{Ash} was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line and no scrubbing lifted it; the pale clip-ring at his left wrist held what light there was and gave it back. Above the shed \place{the Debt Loft} settled and came to the boy as a direction and nothing more, and the warm stone Sella had given him in the winter Garl died lay in its unmarked clay pot up there, two years gone, cold and unreconciled with the debt by not trying, which is not forgiving. His stroke went over the shallow bright patch on \place{the flagstones} without the half-check and over the road a house sends a man down when it wants a collector, without once turning along it.

At \place{the Vail wheel-shed} \person{Jorra Vail} came through with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bent little finger and under the nails, white in the creases where a fist had opened and not bone-coloured \lore{slip}. He kept dusty forearms and clean ones on one tray and parted them as a surgeon parts clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had been broken nor whether it was a birth-slate. Men unnamed had gone low into \place{the back room} before first grey, and the room kept its finger's width of open and its sound of low voices that did not resolve into words; he gave the house no name for the men and did not say the room was cold. He spoke to the wheel before he spoke to any person. \emph{It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady}, he told it, in the flat voice that did not finish sentences, and to a pot on the bench he said \emph{You'll do}, with no welcome in it and no unkindness. He knew the \lore{full note} that pot would give three turns ahead, with air under it and a want in it, and withheld the knowing on the near-full tray beside the hearings and never among them, advanced and not paid.

The burned-slate word stood at four from \place{Slate Row} and the two tellings of the counting room shut in the war stood at two and did not become three; the three shapings of the pot saying stood at three and no fourth likeness was made a cause, for a likeness made a cause wants a payer and none stood to any. When Sella's sentence went low and unfinished at the hearth, his bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper} ahead of it, took the cold out of the fingertip, held until the end of the sentence arrived at the pot, moved a hair's breadth and was left standing --- a remembered repetition paired with no other fraction, one piece of iron with two bites and no \lore{Grathic} word for both mercies, joined to no kiln and entered in no slate. \emph{He's not Vail} stayed sealed and the promise of the boy's name to the men who burn slates was neither given nor withdrawn. He did not look toward the bed or into the basket, and the boy kept one piece of iron and two silences on trays that did not touch.

Then the boy turned out the file as a surgeon turns out instruments on a towel, and found it exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air.

Six hearings lay beside and never among, five from four sources and the sixth, the small flat knock out of a woman as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go and never among the sources. No seventh had come in three days and none had been invented to fill the empty place, for an empty place kept empty is a finding and a finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged.
