\daymark{251}{Smoke 12, Secondday, Y.H. 813}

The small hours came under the Vail roof the way water comes into a pot set out for the rain --- quietly, and to a level the pot did not choose. Two hundred and fifty-one stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in \place{Sarn}, \place{Sill} or \place{Grath}; six months and seventy-one days stood where six months and seventy had stood the day before, and no voice under any of those roofs had put a name to either. \person[Corin]{the boy} kept the number the way a man keeps a blade oiled through a winter when there is nothing in the room to cut. It was a habit of the hands and not a belief. He had long ago stopped asking who would have spoken it, which is not the same as not wanting it, and the wanting went on under the not-asking the way a coal goes on under ash, which a house can go around without ever sweeping.

The inventory arrived in its old order and he took it as it came. One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from his ear on \place{the bed} at the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf}, gone hard in the dry warmth, and the empty place beside it kept its name to itself. No third had come to lie down and make the row come out even; a third crust entering a fold where two had stood would be the beginning of an account, and this house gave without entering. The thread of drool at his mouth had frozen in the dry heat and was unwiped still, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread, and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. Cold had come cheek, temple, the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, and had stopped there as though a line had been written out for it to that length and no longer; it neither went further nor went away. The afterdrop waited beyond all three, past a warming nobody in this house would perform. He knew the severe face cold wears and the face a man wears when warmth is brought back too fast; he knew a cold patient cannot clot and that the warming, if it ever came, must come from the outside and slowly. He knew all of it and wanted the crust. Both were true and the small hours made the second no quieter, because a child's hunger keeps no hours at all while the register goes on being exact inside a body that cannot lift a crust two inches to its mouth. The surgeon took the facts in the order they arrived and entered none of them, not even in the place where he kept the count.

The first smell was hot clay under tallow, wet wool under that, and under both the sour mineral breath of the unswept corner the house called \lore{the thaw}. The first sound was not a bell. Behind the wall \lore{the carried fire} drew in four low pulls and stopped where the fifth should have stood, and the stopping stood there as it had stood for two hundred and fifty-one days with nobody under any roof to put a name to what stood in it, its payer without a name. There was no market on a Secondday, no \place{the Cross} and no bell; the square and its pigs and its two hundred leaners belonged to Smoke 10 and had gone home with it. Smoke 20 (Day 259), the second Account-day and market of the second firing, stood eight mornings beyond the close and was not brought into a morning that had not earned it. A day not yet arrived does not answer to a hand that reaches early for it, and a figure not yet read is not a figure owed.

Through the finger's-width door the river kept its note of \lore{water over water}, a low thing going over a lower thing and never counted among the hearings; under the floor the fire kept its four and its silence; overhead \place{the boards} kept four nails on the near board, four on the next and a thumb-width knot where the fifth nail should have stood. Three counts in three places, and none rose into another, for agreeing any two of them would want a payer put under both and no payer stood to any. The bell's four, four and a gap from the market stayed where it had sounded, beside the told bell and not folded into the three, and he had not made a seventh of the gap then and did not make one now alone in a room whose low angle kept the light. The figure read over the basket stayed beside the figure paid, and Smoke's first arithmetic went up \place{the clay road} in a locked box answering nothing for the man carried out on Day 188 or the hand that went in on Seed 15. He kept the trays apart without congratulating himself on it. A discipline is a thing a hand uses; a belief is a thing a man stands under, and he had only the hand, and it could go up an inch and no further.

The room that held him and the room of the fire shared a wall and did not touch. Heat came through as a fact and not as a message, and sound came through as a direction and not as an instruction; words went to the pot, the broom, the wool and the river wind, and none were ever sent to the basket, because a basket set down is a pot set down and left. He had spent two hundred and fifty-one days learning the difference between a wall and a door, and the wall had acquired no door. The half-beat at a pot's setting, the little silence after a count that had ended somewhere else, the river's note through the finger's-width gap --- these came to him as intervals with their own edges and not as a summons. \lore{the widow's mark} hung where it hung on its cord at a throat he could not see, touched once before the kiln on Smoke 1, held one breath, let go, and touched at no shelf, no well, no rack, no road, no basket, no afternoon and no dusk on any of the three days of this tend. The one opening before Smoke 1 lay behind the household; he did not look at it again and made no lesson of it and named no payer under it. A thing that does not touch is still a thing kept, and he kept the two rooms apart the way a surgeon keeps a field clean, without congratulating himself on that either.

\scenebreak

\person{Sella Vail} went to the fire before the basket. She told it the thaw, turned the coals with the back of a broken pot and said \emph{The house will keep it} where another woman would have said \emph{I}. She stood a moment with the back of the pot in her hand counting under her breath toward no opening --- the small intake, the hold, the letting-go --- and pushed the coal-black hair back with the wrist that carried the burn so the one grey streak showed pale off the coals; the burn shaped like a little door closed on nothing and opened again to the work. The fire answered four low pulls and kept its fifth where it had always kept it, and she fed it as if it had given her no reason to count what it lacked. Her hand went to the mark before the kiln on Smoke 1 and at no other hour; here, at \place{the hearth} on Smoke 12, it went to the coals and the pot and the work, and the mark hung where it hung.

On \place{the drying racks} she lifted the dressed grain out from under its wool for the damp and put her fingers into it to the knuckle, spread it thin on the flat stone, turned it, gathered it back by the handful and set the fold flat again. It changed state and gained no figure; she counted it in glances and handfuls and by the weight of the carrying arm, which knows weight and not cost, and entered it on no slate and laid it across none of the absences. The figure spoken over it two days before went up the road with the box and did not know what the grain had cost, and the boy did not turn the weight of the fold into a number to make the two agree. A thing counted by handfuls is a thing kept by handfuls, and a handful is not a measure any clerk has thought to enter.

\scenebreak

A neighbour came to the threshold with a pot hitched against her hip and asked what the keeping would take now the grain was under wool. Sella answered the question put and not the one behind it, and sent both to the hearth. \emph{Ask the pot what a wet thing costs set down there. The house has said it.} The woman went off level with her question still on her, carrying it the way a person carries a thing that has not been given a figure. A thing not priced is not therefore free; it is a thing whose payer has not been named, and a payer not named is not a payer forgiven. The pot sat where it sat and the weather went on being weather, and neither took the question off her hands, which was the answer and the whole of it.

A man come down the clay road with dried pale mud-scurf on his coat skirt brought a number up out of a fist toward \place{the yard well}, looked at it, and put it back into his sleeve. The figure arrived nowhere. He was not refusing it and he was not giving it; he was keeping it where no thumb could read it, and a sleeve is no slate. The boy read the shape of the paper without the writing on it, a chart turned face-down, and did not call it a missing figure, because a figure kept is not a figure lost and the difference is the whole of the keeping. A fist closed on a number and opened on nothing; a sleeve took it back; the road went on up toward \place{the terrace} and \place{High Kiln} and gave the yard no figure while the yard gave the road none. No collector came up or went down the clay road on Smoke 10, 11 or 12, and the debt against the tiles stood in the ruts without a number.

At \place{the loaf shelf} Sella stood with her back to the basket and did not look into it. She moved her lips along the row standing with two gaps and gave nobody a figure, the way a woman runs a thumb along a hem to find the tear and does not say what she found. Her eye went once to \place{the clay jar} cold under its glaze on the wall, and her hand shut half over nothing and came away; she did not touch the jar and did not say what it held. Her finger stayed on top of the shelf's front edge, not beneath it, so the charcoal tally under the board stayed unread and uncounted; a mark nobody reads is still a mark kept, and a mark read wants a figure put under it, and a figure wants a payer. She set the pot rim out and turned its handle two inches beyond the reaching hand, with a half-beat at the setting that was neither a look toward the bed nor a look away from it. Two inches of air was the whole of the distance, and the hand could not close it, and the setting went on being a setting and not a kindness entered.

The yard went around its walked-around places with a look and a wide step and no number: the clean dark line at the Vail corner where the ridge of \lore{Ash} had turned, and the finer hairline of grey beside it called the thaw, neither swept; the space beneath \place{the lowest rack} where the broken birth-slates lay; the round ring where the shallow clay pan had stood, gone now with no scrape, no crumb, no print, no chip and no word; \place[the trough]{the bare dark trough} beside the well with its water called weather; the field's empty place out on \place{the worked strips}; and the returning-line place where a hand went in on Seed 15 and did not come back. Seven places gone around with a look and a wide step, and they were not an account until a payer stood under them, and none stood. He did not draw a finger between the pan's gone ring and the shelf's two gaps, or the fold's one crust and its unnamed gap, or the field's empty place; four absences on four trays with a finger between every pair, and a fourth likeness made a cause would want a payer and none stood to any. The question over the boards stood at five askings with no witness and no row named, held for the day a voice stands somewhere and can be asked; it did not become a sixth asking and it did not become a hearing. The yard gave each thing its own room and went on.

\person{Garl Vail} was kept in objects again this morning, which is a keeping and not a story. The salt-stiff coat hung on its peg behind the door with the crease at the left shoulder where a broader man had leaned for twenty years, and the hollow wheel-seat in the shed kept its hollow on that side and would not be filled. His proverb had gone years ago to a pot with no crack in it, said now over a corner where a repair had been rubbed out: \emph{Leave the crack showing. People trust a thing they can see has been handled.} The debt against a firing of tiles was neither paid nor settled nor done with; it circled the house without a name on it and was worse for being uncalled. His name had not been spoken under this roof for two hundred and fifty-one days, and the keeping of him was maintenance, the way a pot is kept clean between firings, with no lesson made of it. Smoke's first reading had gone up the road in a locked box and changed the district's numbers where two hundred people stood, and none of this had moved to meet it. The house had not gone out to meet the reading, and the reading had not reached the house.

\scenebreak

He put the fifth question of the chapter to his hand as the room went quiet around the fire. The hand outside the wool went up an inch toward the low note through the finger's-width door and was kept through the little silence after Sella's count had ended in the room that does not touch his. He did not cross two rhythms to hold it: it was one rhythm carried across a borrowed silence, an interval held against the end of a count that had not been sent to him, and not a second sound taken up beside the river's own. It came down on the river's next low note. It was a fraction of air and not a longer reach, not a miss entered as a finding, not a hearing of \lore{the full note}, not a seventh of it, not a tenth stopping, not a payment, not one of the held changes, not a key, a power or a rule. Answer seventy-seven lay on the sixth place beside seventy-six with no payer named, and the surgeon turned out the file as instruments on a towel and found it exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air.

Under the floor the fire stopped at four and the silence where the fifth should have stood stood where it stood, its payer unnamed two hundred and fifty-one days. Two hundred and fifty-one stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in \place{Sarn}, \place{Sill} and \place{Grath}, and the boy was where he had been for all of them: exact, cold, hungry, worth nothing at two inches of air, and still keeping the tray. The hand came down on the river's next low note after the little silence following a count, answer seventy-seven lay on the sixth place, and Smoke 20 stood eight mornings ahead and was not brought into a day that had not earned its heading. The count went forward into Smoke 12, a day that had earned its morning and not yet its evening. The file lay open on the towel.
