## Chronicle

On Ilmen 2 a woman at the well said the water tasted different, and a man from the Ilm said the ice was honeyed under the mud. Nobody said the word. Corin, thirty-one days old, kept the shape of what a district would not name. Mira Silt answered a question with a question, her thumb to a grey cord, a second twisting twice beneath it. Neither was counted.

On Ilmen 3 the boy was a month old and nothing marked it. Tarn set the small knife on its sill, six years of seven counted and the last held back, and wanted the late thaw, which puts off the paying. Sella fed him from the house's own dish, said the house where another woman would say I, and gave the instruction to the fire and the wool.

On Ilmen 4 Jorra Vail came from the back room with grey slate dust on his forearms and said nothing; the space under the rack where slate should have stood went around by everybody and entered by nobody. On Ilmen 5 Alda Reed laid the birth-slates on the bench before the fold, crossed two fingers at the birth-flower without knowing it, and covered the sign with a date nobody asked for. Warm and breathing, she said, which was exact and not true.

On Ilmen 6 a pot set down out of a woman's shadow answered nearly full under nobody's knuckle: not the flat note kept five times from four sources, but an answer with air under it and a want in it. He filed it on its own tray, advanced and not paid, the first answer to an interval carried since Stone 13. Sella touched the widow's mark before she opened the kiln and at no other hour.

On Ilmen 7 a hairline showed in the Ilm's ice, running in and stopping a thumb's breadth short of the edge. A man came back with a figure in his fist and thought better of giving it; Berr Clay wiped his thumb twice before touching nothing and went off with the box still locked. On Ilmen 8 Dessa Silt stood at the end of nineteen steps, her cough worse and still called weather, put the nineteen into her thumb and did not look in or say the number or the name. Mira passed her sister without looking. When Dessa's thumb went along the wicker, an old hand in a grey sleeve reached for a pocket and found wool.

At dusk the boy was carried up the yard, thirty-seven days old, the two crusts in the fold and no third. He had kept the count exactly through seven days while the household kept its number badly in fists and mouths and weather, and the keeping had bought him a thumb on wicker he could not spend and the knowledge of a woman going thin he could not treat. His hand outside the wool went up an inch toward the warmth and came down.

## Continuity

- **Exact final moment.** The boy lies on his bed under the Vail roof, thirty-seven days old, the fold at his ear and the two crusts in it, the carried fire still under blind ash behind him keeping four, four and a gap. His hand outside the wool goes up an inch toward the warmth and comes down. He has just been carried up the Clay Yard at dusk from the doorway at the end of nineteen steps below House Silt, where Dessa Silt's hand went to the wicker of the basket for the space of one breath and let go, and neither woman looked at the other. The Slip Brook runs clay-heavy a little higher than it should behind the racks. Nobody has spoken to the basket; nothing has been joined, paid or explained.
- **Day and time at the end.** Day 37, Ilmen 8, Y.H. 813, Eighthday, from before the smoke cleared of the morning fires to dusk and the banked coals. Chapter 9 ends here; Chapter 10 opens on Day 38, Ilmen 9, Ninthday.
- **Seven lived days, one dated entry each.** Day 31 Ilmen 2, Day 32 Ilmen 3, Day 33 Ilmen 4, Day 34 Ilmen 5, Day 35 Ilmen 6, Day 36 Ilmen 7, Day 37 Ilmen 8. No day poured into another, no recap, no experiment, no clamp, no heresy, no rank change. The old life surfaces once, on Day 37, in past tense and untagged: the shape of a hand on a basket handle reaching for a small cold rectangle in a pocket, a boat on cold water nine promises old, the name not looked at, nothing crossing back.
- **Where everyone is now.** Corin: on his bed under the roof, carried everywhere, mute, drool unwiped, blanket not staying on, cold at cheek–temple–wrist, hand up an inch and down. Sella Vail: at the fire and the bed, having carried him three doors down and set him in the shade of the nineteen steps, fed him from the house's own dish, moved the damper after Jorra, touched the widow's mark only before the kiln opening on Day 35, and banked the coals at dusk without touching it; she has said nothing about the payer of any warmth. Tarn: knife back on its sill and announced to the empty yard, six years counted and the seventh held back, Ash in the knuckles that no scrubbing lifts; his head went a degree toward the nearly full pot on Day 35 and he swept on without naming it, and he still wants a late thaw because a late thaw puts off the paying. Jorra Vail: in and out of the wheel-shed with grey slate dust in the creases past his bent little finger on Day 33 and bone-coloured slip on the other mornings, telling pots they will do and moving the damper before Sella has finished; the full note is in a Vail pot before it leaves the wheel and he would not say so before a kiln-master; he has not once looked toward the basket. Alda Reed: came under the roof on Day 34 with soap and warm milk and birth-slates, wrote a fourth hidden note and went home to a house the boy cannot name. Berr Clay: glimpsed coming back from the river on Day 36 with his red cord folded under his coat and green at one corner of his mouth, wiped his thumb twice, had a number he did not care to give, and went off with the box in his house still locked. Mira Silt: came up from the eels on Days 31 and 37, answered a silence with her own answer, passed her sister without looking, touched her grey cord at the figure of the loaves and showed the second cord twisted twice beneath it, brought no crust and did not look into the basket. Dessa Silt: alive at the end of nineteen steps, visibly going thin, her cough worse than on Stone 28 and still called weather; she does not look into the basket and has not said the number or the name. Garl Vail: absent; coat on its peg, hollow seat on the left, clean dark line where the ridge was burned, uncalled tile debt, both proverbs said to the pot. Walk-ons at the well, the racks and the Slip Brook carry figures and withheld numbers; none is Marra, and the collector's clerk at the Fishstairs is present to nobody this chapter.
- **Items gained, lost or used.** New: the district's word *honeyed* for the ice under the mud and the small sour taste at the well; the honeyed ice and the hairline that runs in and stops a thumb's breadth short of the edge, carried forward from the shard held to tallow on Stone 19; a figure shut in a fist and passed around as a shape that nobody gives out; Berr's gesture of wiping his thumb twice before touching nothing, filed with the record-first hands but not called the same hand; the Singing Sill's low note coming early and called weather; the fourth hidden note Alda wrote in the second hand and turned face-down toward a floorboard; a fourth date laid over the birth-flower sign; the two not-lookings of Dessa and Mira, kept on separate boards; the Slip Brook running clay-heavy and a little higher than it should; the nearly full note from the pot set down out of a woman's shadow, filed on its own tray as an answer to the Stone 13 interval, advanced and not paid; the shape of a thumb along the wicker, kept where things with no hour are kept. Unchanged: both crusts (one stiff since Stone 15, one still giving a thin breath of barley smoke when the wind turns), neither eaten, lifted or named, and no third; the small pale river stone in the fold on the abandoned bed in House Silt, unmentioned; the bench knife on its sill; the charred slate in the kiln ash; the basket and wool lent without a word; Alda's tin shut in her bodice; Sella's private roll unshown; Berr's box locked; Mira's two cords with knots uncounted; the clean dark line where the Ash ridge turned its corner; the carried fire under ash and the back of a broken pot.
- **Money and figures.** No money reaches the boy. One in twelve stays printed and unchallenged and the true subtraction is spoken by nobody; the number of the ice's crack goes from a fist to a mouth to a shoulder-shrug and is never given out; Berr sends a neighbour to a slate at the watch and says a number goes into a slate, not into a well; the levy falls in the thaw, which is why half the yard wants it late; Dessa's four loaves on the seventh of Stone in eight hundred and twelve, three to the levy, one eaten with her feet up and bits to the woman with a basket, and nineteen out and nineteen back, all lie in her thumb. Every figure goes past him shut, unspendable, uncorrectable, unreturnable.
- **Powers and cost.** No usable power, no rank change, no experiment, no clamp, no heresy. He is cracked, low-capacity, unranked, below Thin, twelve to fifteen embers at best, with a flaw nobody has named and a rate of loss nobody has measured. Counting is an old-life habit, not a gift. The ear holds five hearings of the full note from four sources and still no sixth: a cup across the birth room, a pot in the Vail wheel-shed through the wall, the district's slate twice at the Cross, and a man giving at the Drawing. The nearly full note of Day 35 is a third sound, neither the flat answer nor a silence, and is filed as an answer to an interval, not added to the five; there is no Grathic word for nearly and no hand to tap the pot and ask it to say the thing again. Five sightings of the small pause in the neat dark hand stand unchanged and unjoined; the Drawing collector's ordinary hand with no pause stays filed apart. Five pauses lie side by side and do not join. The trays do not touch: intervals, gaps, told bell beside but never on the heard bell, and the three objects of Day 35 (the missing breath, the empty fifth, the nearly full note) are kept in separate places. Keeping has bought him nothing he can lift a hand to.
- **Facts, lies, promises, debts.** The thaw comes on in small things and the district calls it a warm spell, then the weather; the Singing Sill's low note, the honeyed ice, the hairline and the higher brook are all entered nowhere. Sella's hand goes to the widow's mark before an opening and at no other hour, a rule held at the bank, at Alda's visit and across seven days. Tarn's seventh year remains the one entry in the house the house cannot check; Garl's debt against the firing of tiles remains neither paid nor settled nor done with, and his proverb goes on being said to the pot after the crack is gone. Lies and coverings: dates are laid where a wonder or a name should stand; Alda says warm and breathing, which is exact and not true; Dessa calls her cough the pits and the weather, worse than Stone 28 and given the same name; Mira says a basket is a basket and came from a warm house; Berr takes himself out of his own sentence and says your house and the slate and the watch. A kindness which cannot be answered is still a kindness that cannot be refused. No new promise is made; the boat on cold water is remembered untagged, the name under the lit rectangle is not looked at, and the hand comes back with wool.
- **Threads advanced.** T085 and T083 (thaw in honeyed ice and hairline, Singing Sill early, worked toward the Breaking), T077 (number withheld in fists and mouths and by Berr, now a district habit; one in twelve unchallenged), T025 and T059 (Mira's grey cord and second cord twisted twice, knots uncounted), T060 and T086 (two crusts still in the fold, neither eaten or named, no third), T071/T072 (bucket's draw, broom's half-check, nearly full note, nineteen into the thumb, three objects kept on three places, count carried into seven days), T009 and T013 (coat, hollow seat, clean dark line, proverb said to the pot), T036 (six counted, seventh held, late thaw wanted), T076 (two ears and two silences, Tarn's head going a degree), T035 and T031 (grey dust from the back room, the space under the rack where whole slate should have stood, the two forearms filed apart), T061 (count stays five from four sources; nearly is not a sixth), T082 (first earthly answer to the Stone 13 interval, advanced not paid), T079 (mark before the opening only), T023 (fourth hidden note under a floorboard, tin unopened), T007/T008 and T073 (unaware sign covered with a date, exact lie, name and number unsaid, cough called weather), T044 and T004 (pocket-place, boat promise, unsent sentence in past tense untagged), T058 (instruction to the fire and the wool, never to the basket), T065 (kiln's four, four and a gap; fire still under ash), T015, T047, T078, T041, T087 (held toward Day 38). Nothing is paid and nothing is joined.
- **Foreshadowing planted.** The honeyed ice and the hairline stopping short of the edge toward the first Breaking and the day the river puts the Fishstairs back out of line; the withheld number and one in twelve toward the printed deduction against the true figure; Berr's quiet passing with the box locked toward the box opened years hence; Jorra's grey dust and the space under the rack toward the day broken birth-slates are named and a boy's name is handed to men who burn slates; the nearly full note toward the third thing, a vessel that holds nearly full and never quite; Alda's fourth note and the covered sign toward the tin read after she is gone and the Second Want record; the thumb going over the sieve-wire scar and the nineteen into the thumb toward the count that keeps a larger thing out of the room; the higher clay-heavy brook toward the day it takes Dessa uncollected; Mira's two cords toward the knot-string read years hence; the two crusts toward the day one is gone and nobody says who took it. The sixth hearing and the paying of the hand-on-the-basket detail are held for Day 38, not paid here.
- **The next chapter must not forget.** Day 38, Ilmen 9, Ninthday, Y.H. 813: Dessa drowns in the clay-heavy rise of the Slip Brook, uncollected, and stays dead. The boy is thirty-eight days old, held on the bank, and hears the small flat knock of a body cracked all the way through --- the sixth hearing and the second from a person, the payoff of the plant from Stone 25. The collector's clerk is at the Fishstairs; Alda arrives after the vessel has given. He cannot speak, move or look away, and will remember the exact shape of her hand on the basket handle, the detail he is given to keep and never to spend. No visit to the bank may become a quest. Keep the two crusts, the empty fifth, the told bell beside but not on the heard bell, the silence where a name should be, the clean dark line where the Ash ridge was, the charred slate, five hearings from four sources, five unjoined pauses, the Drawing collector's separate hand, the nearly full note on its own tray, and the small pale stone in House Silt unmentioned. Neither Sella's roll, Berr's box, Marra's name, the knots, one in three, the word forty nor a joining of the pauses may appear. No joining, no explanation, no ending.
