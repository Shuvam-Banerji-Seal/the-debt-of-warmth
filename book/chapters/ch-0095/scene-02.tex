In the afternoon \person{Sella Vail} took the basket down and carried \person[Corin]{the boy} out under the finger's-width door into \place{the Clay Yard}, and the instruction went to the wool and the river wind and never to the basket. “Keep it off his ears,” she told the wind, in the tone she used on the pot and the fire and the broom, and set the fold right under his chin without a look at his face. She carried him level, at the half-pace she kept from every open slate, the way a woman carries a pot filled to the brim and does not slop it. The crust lay two inches from his ear in the dry cold, stiff and leathery, its corner standing a hair off the wool and the empty place beside it keeping its name to itself; the thread at his mouth had frozen and would stay frozen, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread. Cold had come to cheek and temple and the small bare joint of his wrist and had stopped there as at the end of a line written out for it, and \lore{the Afterdrop} waited past the stopping for a warming nobody in this house would perform. He wanted the crust lifted two inches to his mouth, the thread wiped away, and a hand that would close on the pot handle brought within reach. Both were true and the second was louder and neither won, and the argument went on under the wool without a verdict, which was how it had gone on every afternoon of his life under this roof.

She went around the yard as the yard went around itself, with a look and a wide step and no number. At the Vail corner the clean dark line lay where it had lain and the finer grey hairline beside it that the district called \lore{the thaw} lay beside it unswept, and she went wide of both and swept neither. Beneath \place{the lowest rack} the broken birth-slates lay where they lay and she gave them the look and stepped around, entering nothing; the round ring where the shallow clay pan had once stood kept its place with no scrape, no crumb, no print, no chip and no word on it, and a man stepping over it did not look down, so the ring stayed what it was. At \place{the yard well} the \place{trough} kept its skin, and on \place{the flat stone by the well} the silt-clouded plate of ice stood out of the path of feet; out on \place{the worked strips} the field kept its empty place; and in the returning line, where a hand had gone in on Seed 15 and had not come out, she went around the place the way everybody went around it, with the look and the wide step and no name spoken. Seven places, and no eighth likeness was made among them, and no cause was laid across two of them, and no payer stood under any. The boy took the circuit as a field taken in the order it offered itself, not counting the places because she did not count them, and entered nothing, because a thing entered is a thing that wants a payer and none stood.

\scenebreak

The three coverings stayed weather and were entered nowhere. The \lore{frost-bloom} had gone off the well's north lip on Bone 3 while the yard looked elsewhere, and it was not a sign now and had not been a sign then; the skin on the trough broke under the knock of a pail and closed again over what it had covered, and nobody lifted a finger to it; the silt-clouded plate on the flat stone stood where no foot went. A covering over a depth, the same covering over another depth, a piece of the depth lifted and set where no foot would go --- three things on three places with a finger between every pair, called weather, and no fourth likeness made a cause. He had learned in this yard that things were covered and the covering was called weather, and that to name the covering was to ask who had put it there, and nobody asked; he had learned it the way a man learns the shape of a wound by not opening it.

A man come down from \place{Cold Acre} shifted a hare across his shoulders at the well, and a woman with a pot asked what the carrying had cost. He judged her by the arm and not by a figure, looked at the weather, and told her to ask the weather, which had carried the snow and could carry the price. He went off toward \place{the clay road} with the hare unweighed and no slate opened, and nobody followed him with a question. Another man with a cracked jar in a sling of rope asked what the month's mending would want, and Sella told him to ask the pot, and the pot, being a pot, said nothing. A woman with a cost-question got the weather for hers and went away with it as she had come. Each was a direction judged by the carrying arm and the weather, and the boy read each as a chart left blank where the number should stand; he entered none of the blanks and supplied no payer for any of them.

A woman with a basket of kindling asked what the seventh year would want when it came, and two men mending a strip of fence said the thaw would be early and the thaw would be late. \person{Tarn} said “Yes, house” to the woman and “Yes, house” to each of the men, and kept the late, because a late thaw put off the paying. He agreed so thoroughly that there was nowhere for an argument to find a foot. The boy took the four answers --- the hare sent to the weather, the jar sent to the pot, the cost sent to the weather, the year sent to a thaw that had not done what it promised --- and laid them out as a surgeon lays out a field before the dressing goes on, by what was there and by what was not: four directions, four blank places where a figure would have stood, no hand to enter them and no payer standing to any.

\scenebreak

At \place{the rack end} the dressed grain lay turned under wool away from the hearth, and Sella lifted a corner with a touch lighter than the day before and turned it once and gave no figure. Her fingers went in and came out and did not close, so that no handful was lifted. The boy watched the empty fingers come away and filed them on a place of their own beside the one object that was the handful let fall on Bone 22 --- a keeping, not a second handful, not a fifth held change, not a habit and not a rule, with no payer under it. Wool over turning grain, the dry rasp of it, the smell of the yard coming up through it: these were the small sounds and the small smells, and he took them first, because the small ones do not lie and the large ones are mostly weather.

At \place{the kindling} Tarn kept his account in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check and in the lip beyond it where it could not: six standing, the seventh at the lip beside the unforged collector sentence that \person{Garl Vail} had left there with no name on the man who turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on the debt. The small knife lay at the very edge of its sill, where a man could check it and where a man's hand could also slip it off. “Six counted. The next I don't count,” he told the wood, in the tone of a man stating the weather, and cut nothing and counted nothing and called no loan. Ash was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line, and the pale clip-ring at his left wrist held what little light there was and gave it back.

The sentence about an ear under this roof being worth more than the debt that held it came up to the word that would call the loan and stopped there, as it had stopped on a ninth morning and would stop again; there were nine stoppings and no tenth before the seventh year at its very end, and the loan was uncalled. The breath that should have gone in at the top of the reach stayed unspent, a small interval at the top of every reach, and his chest had been tight since the dark. At \place{the flagstones} his stroke went over the shallow bright patch without the half-check, and the returned half-check of Bone 8 stayed on its own place with the Day 161 fraction, the Day 166 shelf beat and the Frost 5 returned beat, four held changes on four places and not a rule, and no fifth was made of the thumb's resting. The warm loft stone lay cold in its unmarked pot above the shed, unreconciled by not trying, which is not forgiveness.

The boy checked the resting of a thumb on the uncut seventh of Bone 26 and found it where it had been laid, and did not make it again on the seventh of \place{Ash}. It was one object on one place with a finger between it and the nine stoppings and the four held changes and the handful and his own gaps: not a tenth stopping, not the seventh year given, not a payment, not a sixth held change, not a hearing, not a mending and not a rule, and no payer standing under it. He had learned to look at a kept thing without asking what it was for; asking what it was for was how a thing got entered, and entering was how it got a payer.

\person[Jorra Vail]{Jorra} came through the finger's-width door off \place{the Vail wheel-shed} with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bent little finger and white in the creases where a fist had opened, and it was not the bone-coloured \lore{slip} of the wheel. He kept his dusty forearms and his clean ones on one tray and parted them as a surgeon parts clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had been broken nor whether it was a birth-slate. Three or more men had gone low into \place{the back room} before first grey, and he gave the house no name for them and did not say the room was cold. To the wheel he spoke before he spoke to any person: “It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady.” To a pot he said, “You'll do,” in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either, and knew the full note it would give three turns before it left his hands, with air under it and a want in it, and kept the knowing where nobody could make a song of it, withheld from a kiln-master come up from \place{High Kiln} who would call it early and make a song of it.

Sella had not finished telling him the fire was low when his bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper}, took the cold out of the fingertip, moved the tongue a hair's breadth and was left standing; the end of her instruction arrived at the pot and the household straightened around his silence the way cloth straightens when a weight is lifted. One piece of iron had two bites, the holding and the taking, and there was no Grathic word for both mercies. “He's not Vail” stayed sealed as a lid nobody had tried to lift in three years, and the promise of the boy's name to the men who burn slates was neither given nor withdrawn. The burned-slate word stood at five with a finger between the fifth and the four and no witness standing; the joining said over the boards on the fifth of Ash stood beside it on a place of its own, not a sixth telling, not a third telling of the shut room, not a cause, not a confession and not a rule, and no sixth was due on a day that had not earned one. The boy set the five down where they lay and did not look for a sixth standing beside them.

The afternoon went around him and did not stop for him. Nobody under the roof was made wiser by the circuit of the yard, and nobody was poorer for it either; the keeping of it meant nothing to anyone except the boy, who could not have told a soul what he had noticed and would not have been believed if he had. He took that as the ordinary condition of his days and not as a grievance, for a grievance is an account with a payer standing under it and none stood.

Garl stayed in objects and not in a story. His salt-stiff coat held a broader man's crease at the left shoulder on its peg behind the door; the wheel-seat in the shed kept its twenty-year hollow on that side and nobody was allowed to fill it; the ridge that had been his ash-marker was now the clean dark line at the Vail corner with the finer grey beside it unswept; the twice-mended pot stood on \place{the pot board} with the hairline of the original break showing between the old-brass staple and the tighter wire, and both proverbs had gone to the pot and the fire and never to a person --- “Leave the crack showing. People trust a thing they can see has been handled,” and “If it's worth mending it's worth mending twice. The second time is the real one,” improved in the keeping and very likely not as good as the man had said them. Repair stayed apart from restoration because a restored thing hides the hand that handled it and a mended thing leaves that hand showing. The debt against a firing of tiles circled without being called, neither paid nor settled nor done with and worse for being uncalled; his name had not been spoken under this roof three hundred and thirty-six days, and no lesson had been made of any of it.

At the going of the light Sella carried him up at the half-pace and set him on \place{the bed} under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof and never in \place{the Debt Loft}. She put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face, let her hand rest the half-beat longer than the setting needed before it went away, and left the thread where it lay. A sound came from above and behind --- a foot on the loft stair, a pot set down, a small scrape of something moved and not explained --- and no interior was entered and no name was given to the sound; the instruction went to the fire, the wool, the pot and the river wind and never to the basket. She said “The house will keep it” where another woman would have said I. \lore{The widow's mark} hung where it hung and was not touched, because a going of the light and a bed are not openings; six openings in six months were six and not a rule, and no seventh was made due on a day that had not earned one.

She went to \place{the hearth} before the basket, told the coals it was the thaw and turned them with the back of a broken pot. The fire answered four low pulls with the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer unnamed three hundred and thirty-six days, and she did not supply the pull. Her finger lay on top of \place{the loaf shelf}'s front edge so the charcoal tally stayed unread, her lips moved along a two-gapped row and figured neither gap, and her eye went once to the clay jar standing cold under \lore{lead glaze} with the hand half over nothing and the little-door burn closing on air. Under the floor the carried fire kept its four and its gap; overhead \place{the boards} kept four nails on the near board, four on the next and a thumb-width knot where a fifth might have been driven and was not; through the finger's-width door \lore{water over water} went low over lower, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming, and was never numbered among the hearings. Three counts in three places and none rose into another, because agreeing any two would have wanted a payer put under both and none stood to any.

Six hearings of \lore{the full note} stood beside and never among, with the seventh place empty, the sixth the small flat knock out of \person[Dessa Silt] as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go; an empty place kept empty was a finding and a finding filled to make a row come out even was a finding forged. The heard bell stayed in its square beside the told bell of \lore{the Long Night}, and the told figure stood beside the paid with no signature on the space; six arithmetics of the year rode locked up the clay road in \person[Berr Clay]'s box toward High Kiln, figure read not figure paid, six readings six and not one account, the finding on a place of its own with no payer, not laid across the house's \lore{not-doings}, the covered grain, the five held objects, the handful, the thumb's resting or his own kept gaps. The edges came near to touching and the finger went between, and no doctrine stood up, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood.

In the small hours, with the river's note low and going lower under the finger's-width door, the hand outside the wool went up an inch toward the sound. It took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole, let the intake go on the same low note and came straight down and laid itself back against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. The note had done before the hand came down, so the letting-go was neither early again nor stayed and the hand did not stay; the inch was not a reach, the intake not a breath kept for warmth, the interval not a stopping of the river and the note not his. He filed it as answer two hundred and forty-eight: exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment, borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the six arithmetics, the bell's gap, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the stayed object, the early letting-go, the sixth opening, the joining over the boards, the fourth shaping, the turned writing nor his own kept gaps. Two hundred and forty-eight honest answers stood on the sixth place, each an object and not a power, and neither the number nor the small hand-movings that had made it stood up and called themselves a doctrine, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood.

The surgeon took his small clean satisfaction in a field left as found, and the infant underneath took none of it and went on wanting the crust lifted and the thread wiped and a hand on the pot handle, both true, the second louder, neither winning, the two registers quarrelling without verdict in the dark. Three hundred and thirty-six stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill}, \place{Grath}; six months and one hundred and fifty-six days were told by nobody; the carried fire's fifth was unpaid the same number with its payer unnamed, and the name of the man the salt-stiff coat kept was unspoken the same number. Nothing on the seventh of Ash had been opened, joined by a standing hand, named, called, paid or ended. The keeping of the number was the only honest work there was to do, bought nothing at two inches of air, was not for sale, and went on because attention is the one thing no institution can tax. The file lay open on \place{the towel} at the head of the bed with every gap left gap; the burned slate still stood at five on its place with no sixth standing beside it; the silt-clouded plate of ice stood on its flat stone out of the path of feet; and the river's low note went quiet under the finger's-width door. Ash 10 stood three mornings ahead and was not brought into a day that had not earned it. Forward, not closed.
