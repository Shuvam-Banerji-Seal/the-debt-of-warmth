## Chronicle

Three days of the dark month went by in the Vail house with no boundary drawn and nothing opened, named, paid or ended. On the seventh he came into his three hundred and thirty-sixth day with one crust two inches off, the thread frozen, cold stopped at cheek, temple and wrist, and the afterdrop waiting past a warming nobody would perform. Sella Vail went to the hearth before the basket, told the coals it was the thaw, turned them with a broken pot, and said *The house will keep it* where another woman would have said I. The fire answered four low pulls and kept the silence where the fifth should have stood. In the afternoon she carried him wide around seven places of the yard, the hare going to the weather and the cracked jar to a pot that said nothing. Tarn kept six where the house could check and the seventh at the lip: *Six counted. The next I don't count.*

On the eighth Ilva drew at the well, the rope stiff in the cold. To a woman who asked what the firings of Ash had taken, her hand went toward her sleeve and stayed; the notched strip did not come out and no figure came off the wood. *Ask the pot what it cost. I keep the count, not the account.* The boy filed her reaching as the ninth hand beside Hessa Rale's eighth, with no tenth. No ownerless saying came and no fifth shaping came. Jorra put his bent finger into the cold damper ahead of her sentence.

Each morning and close the hand went up an inch toward the river's note, took a small intake, held it whole and let it go on the same low note. Six answers were entered, two hundred and forty-seven through two hundred and fifty-two; two hundred and fifty-two honest answers stood on the sixth place, each an object and not a power. Three hundred and thirty-eight stood exact and spoken by nobody, six months and one hundred and fifty-eight days told by nobody, the carried fire's fifth unpaid and Garl's name unspoken the same number. The surgeon took the inventory and the infant wanted the crust lifted; both were true, the second was louder and neither won.

At the going of the light on the ninth, Sella lifted the twice-mended pot and her thumb stayed at the wire crossing the crack one breath longer than the lift required. She said both proverbs to pot and fire and never to a person, and left the hairline of the break showing, for a mended thing leaves the hand that handled it showing. The boy set the held interval beside the five held objects --- expressly not a sixth, not a habit, bought at nothing and with no payer standing. The file lay open on the towel with every gap left gap, the twice-mended pot stood on its board and the river's low note went quiet under the finger's-width door.
