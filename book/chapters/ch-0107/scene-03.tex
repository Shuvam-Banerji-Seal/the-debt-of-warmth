\daymark{373}{Stone 14, Fourthday, Y.H. 813}

In the small hours \person[Corin]{the boy} laid his hand flat on the fold on answer three hundred and twenty and let the count stand where it stood. Three hundred and seventy-two exact, six months and one hundred and ninety-two days, carried out of the thirteenth and not spent. Nothing from the day behind crossed under the roof as sign, lesson, mending, cause, payment or payer; the fire kept its own silence under the floor and the dark in the corner kept its own shape. He took the room the way a trauma bay is taken, by the small sounds first, because the small sounds do not lie and the large ones are mostly weather: a coal settling under ash with a dry rasp, \place{the loaf shelf} ticking as the clay cooled along it, the low note of \lore{water over water} coming under the river's winter skin through \place{the finger's-width door}. The hand outside the wool went still on the fold. It had done its inch and its small intake and its letting-go the night before, and the note had finished under the ice before the hand came down, and there was nothing to add.

The surgeon took the body in the old order, and the order had a different rhythm this morning, as if a line written out for it had been copied out again in a stricter hand. One leathery brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from the ear, gone harder in the dry cold, its corner standing a hair off the wool. Beside it the empty place kept its name to itself and no third came to make the row come out even. The thread of drool at the mouth had frozen in the dry kiln heat and was unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread. Cold had come to cheek and temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, and had stopped there as at the end of a line written out for it with neither an order to go farther nor an order to go away. \lore[the Afterdrop]{The Afterdrop} waited past all three, beyond a warming nobody in this house would perform. He knew the composed face that was not composure; he knew a cold patient cannot clot and will seem better before he is worse, and that pink returning to a face is the surface still cold and not the core. He knew that the order in which a body gives heat up is the reverse of the order in which it takes it back. Knowing it changed nothing here, and the knowing was the work as much as the wanting was.

The infant wanted the crust lifted the two inches to his mouth, the thread wiped, a hand closed on the pot handle set where the fingers could reach. Both were true. The second was louder. Neither won, and the quarrel between them was the work.

Then the morning came in under the roof and \person{Sella Vail} went to \place{the hearth} before she went to the basket, as she had gone for three years. She told the coals it was \lore{the thaw} and turned them with the back of a broken pot. “It's the thaw,” she said, and then, “The house will keep it,” where another woman would have said I. She stood a moment counting a small intake, a hold and a letting-go toward no opening, and gave neither gap a figure. Her finger lay along the top of the loaf shelf's front edge so that the charcoal tally beneath stayed unread; her lips moved along a two-gapped row and figured neither gap. She glanced once at the clay jar standing cold under \lore{lead glaze}, and shut her hand half over nothing, the little-door burn on the back of her left hand closing on air. The cord of her \lore{widow's mark} was oiled where a working thumb had touched it ten thousand times, and the mark hung where it hung, untouched, because a morning and a hearth are not openings, seven openings in seven months are seven and not a rule, and no eighth was made due.

On \place{the pot board} the twice-mended pot kept the hairline of the original break showing between the old-brass staple and the tighter wire. Her thumb went first where the wire crossed the crack before the weight came on the handle, and the proverbs went to the pot and the fire and never to a person: “Leave the crack showing. People trust a thing they can see has been handled.” And: “If it's worth mending it's worth mending twice. The second time is the real one.” They were improved in the keeping and very likely not as good as \person{Garl Vail} had said them. Repair stayed apart from restoration: a restored thing hides the hand that handled it, a mended thing leaves that hand showing. She turned the dressed grain under wool with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child and lifted no figure and no handful, and she set the pot rim two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat and no look toward \place{the bed}. The half-beat was the distance between intention and arrest, bought at nothing and not a gift, for a gift wants a giver and nobody gave. Garl's name had gone unspoken under that roof three hundred and seventy-three days and \lore{the carried fire} had gone as long unpaid, its fifth pull unanswered and its payer without a name.

When she carried him down she kept the half-pace she kept from every open slate, level and out of the draught, as a pot is carried that must not be slopped. The instruction went to the wool and the river wind and to the fire she would bank against their coming back, and never to the basket. “Keep it off his ears,” she told the wool. “It's the river wind.” That was all. She did not look into the basket and she did not speak to what lay in it.

\place{Low Kiln} lay under \place{Sill} in \place{Grath} with the dry cold on it, and the yard stood white where the frost had been and brown where feet had gone. In \place{the Clay Yard} she went around the seven walked-around places with a look and a wide step and no number. At \place{the Vail corner} the clean dark line kept its finer grey hairline beside it, the thing the house called the thaw, unswept and thickened in the month's dry cold, with the faint sour tang of \lore{Ash} coming up off it where it had not been swept in two years. Beneath \place{the lowest rack} the broken \lore{birth-slates} lay where they had been put and were entered by nobody, and on \place{the flagstones} by the rack end the round ring where the shallow pan had once stood kept no scrape, no crumb, no print, no chip and no word. \place{the yard well} stood with its rope stiff in the cold and \place{the trough} skinned over from edge to edge; on \place{the flat stone by the well} the silt-clouded plate of ice sat a little out of the path of feet, which was where it had been put and where it stayed. Farther out, on \place{the worked strips}, the field's empty place kept its own shape in the stubble, and nearer in, where the returning line came back on itself, lay the place where a hand had gone in on Seed 15 and had not come out; she went around that one widest, by nothing measurable, and went around it once and not twice, because a second going-around would have made a figure of once.

A line left where it is is still a line kept. A sign is a thing that points to a payer, and no payer stands under that ground.

The gone white frost off the well's north lip, the skin on the trough and the clouded plate on the flat stone stayed weather entered nowhere, with a finger between every pair, not a fourth covering and not a sixth low thing. Through the finger's-width door the river kept its note of water over water under the winter skin that the cold had put on and the cold would take off, found where put on the first day of the month and not made again, not spent on any date and not made to buy a lesson under any roof. The winter skin sat on its own place beside the low note and not upon it, a covering put on by the weather and entered nowhere; the boy entered it nowhere and kept a finger between it and the note it muffled. The empty place beside the crust was left as it had been left on the morning before, and on the morning before that: no hand at it, no third, no name spoken over it.

In the afternoon \person{Hessa Rale} came down \place{the clay road} in the boy's sight out of the direction of \place{Greybank}, with \person{Berr Clay}'s narrow slate held edge-on to her own forearm and the writing turned toward her sleeve. The second strap was let out for the long road and the dried pale scurf of road-mud stood unbrushed on the skirt she had shortened for it. The small flat correction of the strap came first and the turn of the wrist after it, bringing the edge round, and only then did her eyes go to the yard; her hand had gone to the slate before either. The thumb lay along the grain of the frame and nothing pressed where the writing was, not concealment but the manner of carrying a slate so the writing was not rubbed and no goer-by took a figure off it. She went on at the pace of a person who has made a bargain with distance and keeps it without thinking. She gave no figure, read nothing aloud and did not look into the basket.

From the low angle of the basket the boy watched the writing go down the road turned toward her sleeve. He took the order of her reaching apart as a surgeon takes a field apart: the hand to the record before the eyes to the yard, the eyes before the mouth, the small flat correction of the strap sitting in the gap where a lesser observer would have put an intention. He filed the turning of the writing toward herself on a place of its own beside the Ash 6 sighting, with a finger between. A runner coming back down the road with the writing turned toward herself was not a voice standing in a doorway; a sighting was a sighting and a saying was a saying, and nothing in the one paid the other. He made no fifth shaping of the Frost 14 well-saying, no witness, no reader, no payment, and joined her neither to \person{Ilva}'s strip in her sleeve, nor to Berr's thumb passed twice over a figure, nor to the charcoal beneath the loaf shelf, nor to the soft-cuffed clerk's inward slate at the landing, nor to any other private keeping. Her eighth record-first hand stayed eight with no tenth. No ownerless word came over the boards on that afternoon, and the Ash 5 joining, the Ash 17 direction and the Ash 23 direction stood where they had been put, did not repeat and bought no lesson; the burned slate stood at five, the shut-room tellings at two, the row at five askings with no witness, the well-saying at four shapings, and no sixth asking, fifth shaping, third telling or witness was supplied to make a row come out even.

\scenebreak

The four rooms with the same gap in their count stayed where they had been set: the birth room in \place{House Silt}, \place{the Vail kiln-house}, the room where the slate had been read on market-day, and \place{the Drawing house} seen two doors down. The boy held them on a place of their own with a finger between every pair, four likenesses and not one account. An account wants a hand to lay four rooms together and a payer under all four, and no such hand stood and no such payer stood. The likeness gathered interest where it lay and was not spent, and he did not lay it across the house's not-doings or across any of the private keepings he had kept apart that day. Six hearings of \lore{the full note} stood beside and never among with the seventh place empty; the flat knock of \lore[the Hearth Drawing]{the Drawing} stood on its own place outside them, not a seventh and not a filling of the empty place, for a hearing wants a source standing and that room had been seen from a basket and not entered. Ten arithmetics stood behind one unsigned figure, the figure read still not the figure paid, no signature on the space and no doctrine come out of ten. He borrowed none of them and supplied no payer.

At the going of the light Sella carried him up the nineteen steps under the roof and never into \place{the Debt Loft}, set him on his bed at the low angle of the loaf shelf and put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face. She let her hand rest the half-beat longer than the setting needed before it went away, left the thread frozen and unwiped where it lay, and said nothing of where they had been. The empty place beside the crust was as it had been all day: no hand at it, no third, no name. The file came out on \place{the towel} at the head of the bed as instruments come out when a case is done and before the next is called, and went over by the place of each thing with every gap left gap.

The hand outside the wool came up an inch toward the river's note under the finger's-width door, the way a hand goes up in a field it has learned not to disturb. It did not reach, and the inch was not a reach. He took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole on the note's own finishing under the winter skin, let the intake go as the note ended and came straight down and laid itself back against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. The note had done before the hand came down, so the letting-go was neither early nor stayed and the hand did not stay; the note's own finishing under the covering ended the interval and not any decision of the hand. The intake was not a breath kept for warmth, the interval not a stopping of the river, the note not his. He filed it as answer three hundred and twenty-one: exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the ten readings, the bell's gap, the flat knock, the child's grey fingertips, the mother's cup, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the held breath at the sill, the two bites of iron, the river's winter skin, the widow's mark nor his own kept gaps. Three hundred and twenty-one honest answers stood on the sixth place and not one was a doctrine.

The surgeon took his small clean satisfaction in a field taken as found and left as found, and it cost him nothing, which was how he knew it was not peace. The infant wanted the crust lifted two inches, the thread wiped and a hand on the pot handle. Both were true. The second was louder. Neither won, and the quarrel between them was the work. The crust kept its hair-lifted corner and its empty place with no third; cold stopped at cheek, temple and the bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back; the Afterdrop waited past all three, beyond a warming nobody in this house would perform. Count three hundred and seventy-three exact, six months and one hundred and ninety-three days told by nobody, the carried fire's fifth unpaid and Garl's name unspoken the same number. Stone kept its fourteenth day without a boundary drawn, the deep-winter month held, and the count went on into the fifteenth.
