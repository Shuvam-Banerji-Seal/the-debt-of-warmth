\daymark{372}{Stone 13, Thirdday, Y.H. 813}

\dropcap{T}{he hand} outside the wool lay flat on the fold where it had been put at the close of the day before, and the count stood at three hundred and seventy-one exact. \person[Corin]{The boy} did not carry it forward by any decision; he carried it the way he carried the room's small sounds and the crust's hair-lifted corner, because it was there and because a man's attention goes where his field is. He lay on \place{the bed} at the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf} under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill}, \place{Grath}, in the small hour before light, and this morning had a date that no roof under that sky would speak aloud: \place{Stone} 13, the thirteenth day of the deep-winter month. Three hundred and seventy-one days, six months and one hundred and ninety-one days, told by nobody who would have known what a number counted. \lore{The carried fire}'s fifth stood unpaid the same number with its payer unnamed, and \person{Garl Vail}'s name had not been spoken under this roof the same number. \lore[The Hearth Drawing]{The Drawing} stood behind the household on its own morning and bought no lesson. Nothing from the tenth, the eleventh or the twelfth of the month had crossed under the roof as sign, lesson, mending, cause, payment or payer; the file lay open on \place{the towel} at the head of the bed with every gap left gap, and the boy lay under the roof and not in \place{the Debt Loft}, as he had been set at every going of the light.

The surgeon took the body in the old order, as a man takes a field before the light is good enough to work in it. One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from the ear, gone harder and browner in the dry cold, its corner standing a hair off the wool; the empty place beside it kept its name to itself and no third had come to make the row come out even. The thread of drool at the mouth had frozen in the dry kiln heat and lay unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread, and a basket set down was a pot set down and left. Cold had come to cheek, temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, and stopped there as though it had reached the end of a line written out for it, with neither an order to go farther nor an order to go away. \lore{The Afterdrop} waited past all three, beyond a warming nobody in this house would perform. He knew a face composed by cold from a face composed by choice. He knew a cold patient cannot clot and will seem better before he is worse, and that the order in which a body gives heat up is the reverse of the order in which it takes it back. The knowledge was exact and useless at two inches of air.

\scenebreak

He wanted the crust lifted the two inches to his mouth, the thread wiped, and a hand that would close on the pot handle set where the fingers could reach it. The surgeon knew the basket set level out of the draught where the heat came sideways was the careful rewarming he would choose, slow and from the outside, never hurried and never rubbed, never set too near the fire, and could not ask for it. Both were true. The second was louder. Neither won, and the quarrel between them was the work.

\person{Sella Vail} came to \place{the hearth} before the basket, as she had come to it at every morning since the boy came under the roof. “It's \lore{the thaw},” she told the coals, and turned them with the back of a broken pot. “The house will keep it,” she said, where another woman would have said I, and stood a moment counting a small intake, a hold and a letting-go toward no opening, giving neither gap a figure. The instruction went to the wool and the river wind and to the fire she would bank against their coming back, and never to the basket. “Keep it off his ears,” she told the wool. “It's the river wind.” She did not look at the basket when she said it, and she did not look at the boy when she carried him. The cord of her \lore{widow's mark} lay oiled where a working thumb had touched it ten thousand times, the little-door burn on the back of her left hand shutting on nothing, and the mark hung where it hung. A morning and a hearth were not openings; seven openings in seven months were seven and not a rule, and no eighth was made due on a day the district had not named.

At \place{the pot board} the twice-mended pot kept the hairline of the original break showing between the old-brass staple and the tighter wire. She put her thumb first where the wire crossed the crack before she let the weight come on the handle, and said both proverbs to the pot and the fire and never to a person: *Leave the crack showing. People trust a thing they can see has been handled* and *If it's worth mending it's worth mending twice. The second time is the real one*, improved in the keeping and very likely not as good as the man said them. Repair stayed apart from restoration, because a restored thing hides the hand that handled it and a mended thing leaves that hand showing, and the hairline was the hand.

The dressed grain lay covered and unfigured, turned under wool with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child, her fingers going in and coming out without closing, no figure lifted and no handful. The empty fingers stood beside the one handful let fall at the rack end on Bone 22 as a keeping and not a second handful, for a second would be a habit and a third a way, and both want a payer and none stood. Her finger lay on top of the loaf shelf so the charcoal tally underneath stayed unread; her lips moved along a two-gapped row and figured neither gap. She glanced once at the clay jar standing cold under \lore{lead glaze} and shut her hand half over nothing, the little-door burn closing on air.

She set the pot rim two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat and no look toward the bed. The half-beat was the distance between intention and arrest, bought at nothing and not a gift; a gift wants a giver and nobody gave. The infant wanted a hand on the pot handle. The surgeon knew the heat came sideways in that corner and that a thing set level there was a thing being warmed carefully and slowly, and he could not ask for it and could not thank anybody for it. Both were true. The second was louder. Neither won.

The room went on around the basket in the way a room goes on around a thing put away. Coal settled in the hearth with a small dry sound, and clay ticked along the shelf as the wall gave up the night's heat by degrees; woodsmoke and wet wool lay over everything, and under them the faint sour tang of the unswept ridge, which nobody in this house had ever swept before a firing and nobody would sweep today. These were the small sounds, and the small sounds do not lie; the large ones are mostly weather. The morning came on without a boundary drawn anywhere, and the household went about its tending as a surgeon goes about a field taken as found and left as found: not gladly, not reluctantly, but with the whole of the attention on what was there.

\scenebreak

The salt-stiff coat kept its crease at the left shoulder on its peg behind the door; the wheel-seat in \place{the Vail wheel-shed} kept its twenty-year hollow on that side and nobody was allowed to fill it; the clean dark line at \place{the Vail corner} with its finer grey beside it called the thaw lay unswept and thickened in the month's dry cold, and the faint sour tang of the \lore{Ash} ridge came up from the skirting board where it had not been swept in two years. Garl Vail's unforged collector sentence lay beside the seventh notch at \place{the kindling} with no name on the man who turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on the debt, and the debt against a firing of tiles lay where it lay, neither paid nor settled nor done with and worse for being uncalled. His name had gone unspoken under the roof three hundred and seventy-two days and the carried fire's fifth had gone unpaid the same number with its payer unnamed. His was a keeping and not a story, and the house kept its shape around him the way a pot keeps its shape around a flaw in the wall: by leaving the flaw showing and going on holding what it held.

The surgeon checked the held things without standing up, as a man checks a field by its small sounds before he lets the light into it. Six hearings of \lore{the full note} stood beside and never among, the sixth the small flat knock out of \person[Dessa Silt] as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go and never among the sources; the seventh place stayed empty and was not dug for or filled. The Drawing's small flat knock stayed on its own place outside them, not a seventh and not a filling of the empty place, for a hearing wants a source standing and that room was seen from a basket and not entered. \place{the boards} overhead kept four nails on the near one, four on the next and a thumb-width knot where a fifth might have been driven and was not; under the floor the carried fire gave four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood; through \place{the finger's-width door} the river went low over lower under its skin. Three counts in three places and none rose into another, because agreeing any two would have wanted a payer put under both and no payer stood. The bell's gap stayed in its square beside the told bell of \lore{the Long Night} and was not folded into any of them.

Four rooms kept the same gap in their count --- the birth room in \place{House Silt}, the Vail kiln-house, the room where the slate was read on market-day and \place{the Drawing house} seen two doors down --- held apart on their own places with a finger between every pair, four likenesses and not one account, an account wanting a hand to lay them together and a payer under all four, and no such hand standing and no such payer. Ten arithmetics of the year stood behind one unsigned figure, the figure read still not the figure paid and no signature on the space between, ten readings ten and not one account, and no doctrine come out of ten. Their true subtractions rode in a box up \place{the clay road} locked, the key gone up in a coat pocket that did not come back down, and the boy had no key and made no figure of the lock. Nine record-first hands kept no tenth, and nine stoppings kept no tenth before the seventh year at its very end; the row stood at five askings with no witness, the burned slate at five, the shut-room tellings at two, the well-saying at four shapings. The Ash 5 joining, the Ash 17 direction and the Ash 23 direction stood where they had been put, did not repeat and bought no lesson. The second chill kept its cause withheld and the cold foot stayed uncured and unnamed, held where they had been put and not entered. No ownerless word came over the boards in the small hours, and none was supplied to make a row come out even; a finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged. The finding that ten are not one account sat on a place of its own with a finger between it and every other kept thing and was not a grievance, a sign, a power or a lesson. He had once read a pale line where a ring had been on the back of a strange hand and had filed it among the hands that know what a face lies about, and that hand too stood on a place of its own and had never been made to stand up and call itself a rule.

\scenebreak

Through the finger's-width door the river kept its note of \lore{water over water}, a low thing over a lower thing, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming, muffled as a voice is muffled by a shut door. Over it from bank to bank lay a winter skin, a covering put on by the cold and taken off by the cold and entered nowhere, found where put on the first day of the month and found again this morning and not made again, sitting on its own place beside the low note and not upon it, not spent on the reading behind or the Drawing behind and not made to buy a lesson under any roof. It was weather entered nowhere, and the boy entered it nowhere, keeping a finger between it and the note it covered as between every other pair of things under this roof.

The hand outside the wool came up an inch toward the note, the way a hand goes up in a field it has learned not to disturb. It did not reach, and the inch was not a reach. He took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole on the note's own finishing under the winter skin, let the intake go as the note ended, and came straight down and laid itself flat against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. The note had done before the hand came down, so the letting-go was neither early nor stayed, and the hand did not stay; the note's own finishing under the covering ended the interval and not any decision of the hand. The intake was not a breath kept for warmth, the interval not a stopping of the river, the note not his.

He filed it as answer three hundred and nineteen: exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the ten readings, the bell's gap, the flat knock, the child's grey fingertips, the mother's cup, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the held breath at the sill, the two bites of iron, the river's winter skin, the widow's mark nor his own kept gaps. Three hundred and nineteen honest answers stood on the sixth place and not one was a doctrine.

The surgeon took a small clean satisfaction in a field taken as found and left as found, and it cost him nothing, which was how he knew it was not peace. The infant wanted the crust lifted two inches, the thread wiped and a hand on the pot handle. Both were true. The second was louder. Neither won, and the quarrel between them was the work.

The crust kept its hair-lifted corner and its empty place with no third; cold stopped at cheek, temple and the bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back; the Afterdrop waited past all three, beyond a warming nobody in this house would perform. Overhead the boards kept four nails on the near one, four on the next and a thumb-width knot where a fifth might have been driven and was not; under the floor the carried fire gave four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood; through the finger's-width door the river went low over lower under its winter skin, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming. Count three hundred and seventy-two exact, six months and one hundred and ninety-two days told by nobody, the carried fire's fifth unpaid and Garl's name unspoken the same number. Stone kept its thirteenth day without a boundary drawn, the deep-winter month held, and the count went on.
