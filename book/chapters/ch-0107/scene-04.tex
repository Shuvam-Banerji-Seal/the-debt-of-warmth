In the afternoon the light thinned off the clay without any boundary drawn to mark the hour, and \person{Sella Vail} carried \person[Corin]{the boy} down out of \place{the Vail kiln-house} at the half-pace she kept from every open slate, level and out of the draught, as a pot is carried that must not be slopped. \place{Low Kiln} lay under \place{Sill} in \place{Grath} with the dry cold on it, and the yard stood white where the frost had been and brown where feet had gone. The instruction went to the wool and the river wind and to the fire she would bank against their coming back, and never to the basket. “Keep it off his ears,” she told the wool. “It's the river wind.” That was all. She did not look into the basket and she did not speak to what lay in it.

From the low angle of the basket the boy took the afternoon as a field is taken before the light goes, by the small sounds first, because the small sounds do not lie and the large ones are mostly weather: clay ticking where it cooled along the shelf behind him, a coal settling under ash, the river's low note coming under its winter skin through \place{the finger's-width door}. The surgeon's register took the body in its old order without being asked to — the brown-edged barley crust two inches off with its corner lifted a hair from the wool, the empty place beside it still without a name and with no third come to make the row even, the thread frozen in the dry heat, cold stopped at cheek and temple and the bare joint of the wrist, \lore[the Afterdrop]{the Afterdrop} waiting past all three. The infant wanted the crust lifted, the thread wiped, a hand on the pot handle. Both were true; the second was louder; neither won, and the quarrel was the work. He had stopped expecting the quarrel to end, which was not the same as being used to it.

She went around \place{the Clay Yard} as the yard went around its seven walked-around places, with a look and a wide step and no number. At \place{the Vail corner} the clean dark line kept its finer grey hairline beside it, the thing the house called \lore{the thaw}, unswept and thickened in the month's dry cold, with the faint sour tang of \lore{Ash} coming up off it where it had not been swept in two years. Beneath \place{the lowest rack} the broken \lore{birth-slates} lay where they had been put and were entered by nobody, and on \place{the flagstones} by the rack end the round ring where the shallow pan had once stood kept no scrape, no crumb, no print, no chip and no word. \place{the yard well} stood with its rope stiff in the cold and \place{the trough} skinned over from edge to edge; on \place{the flat stone by the well} the silt-clouded plate of ice sat a little out of the path of feet, which was where it had been put and where it stayed. Farther out, on \place{the worked strips}, the field's empty place kept its own shape in the stubble, and nearer in, where the returning line came back on itself, lay the place where a hand had gone in on Seed 15 and had not come out; she went around that one widest, by nothing measurable, and went around it once and not twice, because a second going-around would have made a figure of once.

A line left where it lay was still a line kept. A sign was a thing that pointed to a payer, and no payer stood under that ground.

The gone white frost off the well's north lip, the skin on the trough and the clouded plate on the flat stone stayed weather entered nowhere, with a finger between every pair, not a fourth covering and not a sixth low thing. Through the finger's-width door the river kept its note of \lore{water over water} under the winter skin that the cold had put on and the cold would take off, found where put on the first day of the month and not made again, not spent on this afternoon and not made to buy a lesson under any roof. The winter skin sat on its own place beside the low note and not upon it, a covering put on by the weather and entered nowhere; the boy entered it nowhere and kept a finger between it and the note it muffled. The empty place beside the crust was left as it had been left on the two mornings before: no hand at it, no third, no name spoken over it. It was kept, and a keeping was not a sign, and a kept thing did not become a payer by being kept.

The day's hunts and repairs went by as directions judged by the carrying arm and the weather, never as figures. A man come down from \place{Cold Acre} shifted a hare across his shoulders at the well and, to a woman who asked what the carrying had cost, said, “Ask the weather. It carried the snow. It can carry the price,” and went off toward \place{the clay road} with the hare unweighed and nobody opening a slate. Another man with a cracked jar in a sling of rope asked what the month's mending would want and was told to ask the pot; the pot, being a pot, said nothing. A woman with a basket of kindling asked what the seventh year would want when it came, and two men mending a strip of fence said the thaw would be early and the thaw would be late. \person{Tarn} said “Yes, house” to each of them, and kept the late because a late thaw puts off the paying, though the paying had stood in the ruts since the water went. He agreed so thoroughly that there was nowhere for an argument to find a foot.

The boy filed the answering as he had filed the week's other answers — not as a figure taken off the hare or the jar or the fence, but as the shape of a man declining to enter anything. A direction was not a number and a number was not a debt, and nobody in the yard had brought the two together under a roof where a hand could be laid across them.

At \place{the kindling} the account stood in its old exactness. Six pieces lay in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check and the seventh lay at the lip beyond it where it could not, and beside the seventh lay \person{Garl Vail}'s unforged collector sentence, with no name on the man who turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on the debt; the debt against a firing of tiles lay where it lay, neither paid nor settled nor done with and worse for being uncalled. His name had gone unspoken under the roof three hundred and seventy-three days and \lore{the carried fire}'s fifth had gone unpaid the same number with its payer unnamed. The small knife lay at the very edge of its sill, where it could be checked and where a hand could also slip it off. Tarn's hand passed over blade and sill as a man passes over a tool put back where it belongs and went away when the checking was done, and no breath stayed at the top of the reach.

“Six counted,” he told the wood. “The next I don't count.” He said it in the tone of a man stating the weather.

The held breath he had left at the sill on Ash 14 was where it had been put, and the thumb's resting of Bone 26 was where that had been put, found and not made again; the pale clip-ring at his left wrist caught what little light there was and gave it back. Each stood on a place of its own beside the five objects and the pot-crack resting, with a finger between every pair: not a sixth held object, not a seventh of anything, not a tenth stopping, not the calling of the loan, no payer standing under any of them. His chest had been tight since the dark and Ash was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line at the corner; his stroke went over the flagstones' shallow bright patch without the half-check, the Bone 8 returned half-check staying where it had been put and not made a fifth of anything.

He brought the ear-sentence all the way to the word that would call the loan and stopped there, as a man stops at a remembered ninth stopping with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end. The breath he drew did not go in; the Seed 16 breath stayed unspent at the top of the reach and the loan stayed uncalled, and he did not look along the road. The equality under the roof held where it had moved to: two ears, two silences, and a loan that could hear and had never been called, with nobody to enter it and nobody to call it. He had once put a sentence into that room about a foundling and a debt and asked them not to be put in the same sentence; the house had not answered him then and he had not repeated it, which in that house was a way of keeping it.

\person{Jorra Vail} came through the finger's-width door off \place{the Vail wheel-shed} with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bent little finger and white in a fist-crease where a hand had opened, and it was not the bone-coloured \lore{slip} of the wheel. Three or more men unnamed had gone low into \place{the back room} before first grey, and he gave the house no name for them and no word that the room was cold. He kept his dusty forearms and his clean ones on one tray and parted them as a surgeon parts clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had been broken nor whether it was a birth-slate while the broken ones lay under the lowest rack and were entered by nobody.

He spoke to the wheel before he spoke to any person. “It doesn't care whose hands,” he said. “It cares if they're steady.” To a pot on the bench he said, “You'll do,” in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either, and he knew \lore{the full note} that pot would give three turns before it left his hands, with air under it and a want in it, and kept the knowing where nobody could make a song of it, withheld from any master come up from \place{High Kiln} who would call it early and sing a pot before its time. The boy kept the knowing on a tray of its own beside the hearings and never among them, and no seventh hearing stood up in the empty place because a pot in a shed had given its note before its time.

When Sella had not finished telling him the fire was low, his bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper} ahead of her sentence, took the cold out of the fingertip, moved the tongue a hair's breadth and was left standing there, so that the end of her instruction arrived at the pot and the household straightened around the waiting as cloth straightens when a weight is lifted. It was one piece of iron with two bites, the holding and the taking, and there was no Grathic word covering both mercies. He took the finger out without leaving the extra holding of Ash 26 a second time: gathered once on a place of its own with no payer standing, not a sixth or seventh or eighth held object, not a habit, a way, a sign, a hearing, a mending, a cause, a payment or a rule. A second would have made it a habit, and a third a way, and both want a payer and none stood.

“He's not Vail” stayed sealed as a lid nobody had tried to lift in three years, and the promise of the boy's name to the men who burn slates was neither given nor withdrawn, gathering interest against the burned slate at five and the shut-room tellings at two. Neither man asked the other about the ear or the room; the two silences stayed unjoined where they had been put, and the loan went on being carried.

At the going of the light Sella carried him up the nineteen steps under the roof and never into \place{the Debt Loft}, set him on \place{the bed} at the low angle of the loaf shelf and put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face. She let her hand rest the half-beat longer than the setting needed before it went away and left the thread where it had lain, frozen in the dry heat and unwiped, and said nothing of where they had been. The empty place beside the crust was as it had been all day: no hand at it, no third, no name.

The hearth grammar came after, as it came at both ends of every day, and the boy lay with his ear to the low angle and took it in the order it was given. She went to \place{the hearth} before she went to the basket and told the coals it was the thaw, turning them with the back of a broken pot. “It's the thaw,” she said, and then, “The house will keep it,” where another woman would have said I. She stood a moment counting a small intake, a hold and a letting-go toward no opening, and gave neither gap a figure. On \place{the pot board} the twice-mended pot kept the hairline of the original break showing between the old-brass staple and the tighter wire; her thumb went first where the wire crossed the crack before the weight came on the handle, and the proverbs went to the pot and the fire and never to a person: “Leave the crack showing. People trust a thing they can see has been handled.” And: “If it's worth mending it's worth mending twice. The second time is the real one.” They were improved in the keeping and very likely not as good as Garl had said them, who had said them to a pot and never to a person. Repair stayed apart from restoration: a restored thing hides the hand that handled it, a mended thing leaves that hand showing.

She turned the dressed grain under wool with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child and lifted no figure and no handful, and set the pot rim two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat and no look toward the bed. Her finger lay along the top of \place{the loaf shelf}'s front edge so that the charcoal tally beneath stayed unread; her lips moved along a two-gapped row and figured neither gap. She glanced once at the clay jar standing cold under \lore{lead glaze}, and shut her hand half over nothing, the little-door burn on the back of her left hand closing on air. The cord of her \lore{widow's mark} was oiled where a working thumb had touched it ten thousand times, and the mark hung where it hung, untouched, because an afternoon and a going of the light and a bed are not openings, seven openings in seven months are seven and not a rule, and no eighth was made due. The carried fire kept four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer unnamed three hundred and seventy-three days. Overhead \place{the boards} kept four nails on the near one, four on the next and a thumb-width knot where a fifth might have been driven and was not; through the finger's-width door the river went low over lower under its winter skin, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming. Three counts in three places, and none rose into another, because agreeing any two would have wanted a payer put under both and no payer stood.

The four rooms with the same gap in their count stayed where they had been set: the birth room in \place{House Silt}, the Vail kiln-house, the room where the slate had been read on market-day, and \place{the Drawing house} seen two doors down. The boy held them on a place of their own with a finger between every pair, four likenesses and not one account. An account wants a hand to lay four rooms together and a payer under all four, and no such hand stood and no such payer stood. The likeness gathered interest where it lay and was not spent, and he did not lay it across the house's not-doings or across any of the private keepings he had kept apart that day — not across the covered grain, not across the five objects and the pot-crack resting, not across the held breath at the sill or the holding in the damper, not across the burned slate at five, the shut-room tellings at two, the row at five askings with no witness, the well-saying at four shapings, or the Ash 5 joining and the Ash 17 and Ash 23 directions standing where they had been put. Four likenesses were four and wanted nothing; a fifth likeness made to make a row come out even would have wanted a payer, and none stood.

Six hearings of the full note stood beside and never among with the seventh place empty; the flat knock of \lore[the Hearth Drawing]{the Drawing} stood on its own place outside them, not a seventh and not a filling of the empty place, for a hearing wants a source standing and that room had been seen from a basket and not entered. Six answers stood on the sixth place and not one called itself a doctrine. The heard bell stayed in its square beside the told bell, four, four and a gap with nobody crossing himself; the told figure stayed beside the paid figure with ten readings behind it and no signature on the space between. He borrowed none of them and supplied no payer.

The file came out on \place{the towel} at the head of the bed as instruments come out on a towel when a case is done and before the next is called, and went over by the place of each thing with every gap left gap: the six hearings standing beside and never among with the seventh place empty, and the Drawing's flat knock on its own place outside them; the nine record-first hands with no tenth and the nine stoppings with no tenth; the burned slate at five, the shut-room tellings at two, the row at five askings with no witness, the well-saying at four shapings; the five objects with the pot-crack resting, the hand-at-sill breath and the holding in the cold damper on their own places beside them; the river's winter skin found where put beside water-over-water and not spent on any date; the promise under the wheel-shed neither given nor withdrawn and the loan uncalled; ten arithmetics behind one unsigned figure, the figure read still not the figure paid and no doctrine out of ten. No sixth hearing, seventh opening, sixth held object, sixth telling, third shut-room telling, sixth asking, fifth shaping, tenth hand or tenth stopping was supplied to make a row come out even, for a finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged. The house kept itself by not-doings, and the warmth of it was maintenance and not mercy, and the boy knew the difference by what it cost him: nothing, and therefore not peace.

The hand outside the wool came up an inch toward the river's note under the finger's-width door, the way a hand goes up in a field it has learned not to disturb. It did not reach, and the inch was not a reach. He took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole on the note's own finishing under the winter skin, let the intake go as the note ended and came straight down and laid itself back against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. The note had done before the hand came down, so the letting-go was neither early nor stayed and the hand did not stay; the note's own finishing under the covering ended the interval and not any decision of the hand. The intake was not a breath kept for warmth, the interval not a stopping of the river, the note not his. He filed it as answer three hundred and twenty-two: exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the ten readings, the bell's gap, the flat knock, the child's grey fingertips, the mother's cup, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the held breath at the sill, the two bites of iron, the river's winter skin, the widow's mark nor his own kept gaps. Three hundred and twenty-two honest answers stood on the sixth place and not one was a doctrine.

The surgeon took his small clean satisfaction in a field taken as found and left as found, and it cost him nothing, which was how he knew it was not peace. The infant wanted the crust lifted two inches, the thread wiped and a hand on the pot handle. Both were true. The second was louder. Neither won, and the quarrel between them was the work.

Count three hundred and seventy-three exact, six months and one hundred and ninety-three days told by nobody, the carried fire's fifth unpaid and Garl's name unspoken the same number. The file lay open on the towel at the head of the bed with every gap left gap; the hand lay flat on the fold on answer three hundred and twenty-two; the crust kept its hair-lifted corner and its empty place with no third, and cold stopped at cheek, temple and the bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back. Stone kept its fourteenth day without a boundary drawn, the deep-winter month held, and the count went on.
