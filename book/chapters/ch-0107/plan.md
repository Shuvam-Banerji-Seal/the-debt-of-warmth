## Scene 1 — The Morning After the Drawing

- **Beats, in order.**
  1. First `\dropcap` and first `\daymark{372}{Stone 13, Thirdday, Y.H. 813}`. The hand is flat on the fold on answer 318 and the count stands at 371 exact. He carries it forward without deciding to; nobody under any roof speaks the number.
  2. The surgeon takes the body in the old order: the stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust two inches from the ear, corner standing a hair off the wool, empty place beside it unnamed and no third; the drool thread frozen in the dry kiln heat and unwiped, nobody under any roof ever given an instruction about the thread, a basket set down a pot set down and left; cold at cheek, temple and the bare wrist-joint where the wool rides back, as at the end of a line written out for it; the afterdrop waiting past a warming nobody here will perform.   3. The quarrel: infant wants the crust lifted two inches, the thread wiped, a hand on the pot handle; surgeon knows the basket set level out of the draught where heat comes sideways is the careful rewarming he would choose and cannot ask for. Both true, the second louder, neither wins.
  4.   5.   6. ## Scene 2 — The Count in Wood

- **Day and time.** Day 372, Stone 13, Thirdday. Afternoon in the Clay Yard and at the yard well and kindling, then the going of the light and the small hours at the close of the day.
- **Place.** Clay Yard (seven walked-around places, well, trough, flat stone by the well, worked strips), kindling and flagstones, wheel-shed edge and cold damper, then back under the Vail roof (bed, fold, hearth, pot board, towel).
- **Present.** Corin/Dan, Sella Vail, Ilva (at the well), Tarn (at the kindling), Jorra Vail (wheel-shed edge and damper); Garl in objects. Walk-ons only: the Cold Acre hare-carrier, the cracked-jar man in a rope sling, a woman with a basket of kindling, two fence-menders. No Hessa, Berr, collector, green-mouthed woman or foundling; no ownerless voice.
- **Beats, in order.**
  1. Sella carries him down at the half-pace she keeps from every open slate, level and out of the draught; instruction to the wool, the river wind and the fire she will bank against their coming back, never to the basket.
  2. The yard goes around its seven walked-around places with a look and a wide step and no number: the clean dark line at the Vail corner with its finer grey hairline called the thaw, unswept and thickened in the dry cold; the space beneath the lowest rack where the broken birth-slates lie and are entered by nobody; the round ring where the shallow pan once stood with no scrape, crumb, print, chip or word; the yard well with rope stiff in the cold and the trough skinned over from edge to edge; the silt-clouded plate of ice on the flat stone, a little out of the path of feet; the field's empty place out on the worked strips; and the returning-line place where a hand went in on Seed 15 and did not come out, gone around widest by nothing measurable and not gone around twice to make a figure of once. A line left where it is is still a line kept, and a sign is a thing that points to a payer and no payer stands under that ground.
  3. The gone white frost off the well's north lip, the skin on the trough and the clouded plate of ice stay weather entered nowhere with a finger between every pair, not a fourth covering and not a sixth low thing; through the finger's-width door the river keeps water-over-water under its winter skin and is not spent on the reading behind or the Drawing behind.
  4. At the well Ilva draws with the pail against her knee, the rope stiff in the Stone cold giving its small dry rasp as the bucket comes up, the water dark and moving without a sound. Clay dried pale in the crescents of her nails, finer grey rack dust in the seam of one thumb she does not scrub out; her hands know the weight of a pail and the edge of a wet board and have never been soft; people notice the pail first and the sleeve not at all.
  5. A woman waiting her turn asks what Stone's firings have taken. Ilva's hand goes toward her sleeve and stays; the short strip of notched birch, pale where a thumb has rubbed the notches and dark in the uncut grain between, is not taken out, read, copied, counted or joined to the open slate a pace away, to the charcoal under the shelf, to the cord, the knot-string or any saying that arrived nowhere; no figure comes off the wood. Her eyes go to the asker and her mouth opens after both with a half-beat; she answers the question put and not the one behind it: *Ask the pot what it cost. I keep the count, not the account.* She puts the flat of her hand once more on the sleeve and goes back to the pail, and nobody turns twice.
  6. The boy takes the order of reaching apart as a surgeon takes a field apart — hand to the record before eyes to the person, eyes before mouth, the half-beat in the gap where a lesser observer would put an intention — and files it as the **ninth** record-first hand on a place of its own with a finger between it and Hessa Rale's eighth; he files no tenth, makes no reader of her keeping, joins her to none and makes no figure of the slate's nearness. She is expressly not the speaker of any voice; no ownerless word came over the boards.
  7. The day's hunts and repairs stay directions judged by the carrying arm and the weather, never figures: the Cold Acre hare-carrier answers the price with the weather that carried the snow and can carry the price and goes off toward the clay road with the hare unweighed; the cracked jar in its rope sling is sent to a pot, and the pot, being a pot, says nothing; to the woman with kindling asking what the seventh year will want and the two fence-menders saying the thaw will be early and the thaw will be late, Tarn says *Yes, house* to each and keeps the late because it puts off the paying, agreeing so thoroughly that no argument can find a foot.
  8. At the kindling the account stands in its old exactness: six pieces in the worn thumb-mark where the house can check and the seventh at the lip beyond it where it cannot, Garl's unforged collector sentence beside the seventh with no name on the man who turned back at the frozen ford. The small knife lies at the very edge of its sill, where it can be checked and where a hand could also slip it off; his hand passes over blade and sill as a man passes over a tool put back where it belongs and goes away when the checking is done, no breath held at the top of the reach. *Six counted. The next I don't count*, in the tone of a man stating the weather.
  9. The Ash 14 held breath and the Bone 26 thumb's resting are found where they were put and not made again; the pale clip-ring at the left wrist catches what little light there is and gives it back. Each stands on a place of its own beside the five objects and the pot-crack resting with a finger between every pair — not a sixth held object, not a seventh of anything, not a tenth stopping, not the calling of the loan, no payer standing. His chest has been tight since the dark, Ash worked into the knuckle creases in the colour of the clean dark line; his stroke goes over the flagstones' shallow bright patch without the half-check, the Bone 8 returned half-check staying where it was put and not made a fifth of anything. The equality under the roof holds where it moved to: two ears, two silences, a loan that can hear and has never been called.
  10. Jorra comes through the finger's-width door off the wheel-shed with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bent little finger and white in a fist-crease where a hand had opened, and it is not the bone-coloured slip of the wheel. Three or more men unnamed have gone low into the back room before first grey and get no name from him and no word that the room is cold. He keeps dusty forearms and clean ones on one tray and parts them as a surgeon parts clot from vessel, asking neither which slate was broken nor whether it was a birth-slate while the broken ones lie under the lowest rack and are entered by nobody. He speaks to the wheel before any person — *It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady* — and tells a pot *You'll do* in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either, knowing the full note it will give three turns before it leaves his hands, with air under it and a want in it, and keeping the knowing where nobody can make a song of it, withheld from any master come up from High Kiln who would call it early.
  11. When Sella has not finished telling him the fire is low his bent finger goes into the cold iron of the damper ahead of her sentence, takes the cold out of the fingertip, moves the tongue a hair's breadth and is left standing so the end of her instruction arrives at the pot and the household straightens around the waiting as cloth straightens when a weight is lifted — one piece of iron with two bites, the holding and the taking, with no Grathic word covering both mercies. The finger comes out without leaving the extra holding of Ash 26 a second time, gathered once on a place of its own with no payer standing, not a sixth or seventh or eighth held object, not a habit, a way, a sign, a hearing, a mending, a cause, a payment or a rule. *He's not Vail* stays sealed as a lid nobody has tried to lift in three years, and the promise of the boy's name to the men who burn slates is neither given nor withdrawn, gathering interest against the burned slate at five and the shut-room tellings at two.
  12. At the going of the light Sella carries him up the nineteen steps under the roof and never in the Debt Loft, puts the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face, lets her hand rest the half-beat longer than the setting needed and leaves the thread where it lay; she says nothing of where they have been. The fire keeps four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer unnamed 372 days. The file comes out on the towel with every gap left gap. The hand comes up an inch, takes a small intake, holds it whole on the river's note's own finishing under the ice, lets it go and lays itself back on answer **320**, exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air.
- **Emotional turn.** The district's withholdings are all on stage and none of them is made to stand up: the pail before the person, the seventh piece at the lip beyond the check, the second bite of iron, the count in wood. The turn is toward keeping rather than resolution — the boy's exact reading of Ilva's reaching is an act of attention that buys him nothing, and the surgeon's satisfaction in a field found and left as found is not peace.
- **Sensory anchors.** The rope's small dry rasp in the Stone cold; stiff rope; skinned trough; silt-clouded plate of ice set out of the path of feet; pale clay in nail crescents and grey rack dust in a thumb seam; woodsmoke, wet wool and tallow; Ash worked into knuckle creases; the pale clip-ring catching the little light; meal-fine grey slate dust white in a fist-crease; the cold iron of the damper taking the heat out of a fingertip; the river's low note under its skin.
