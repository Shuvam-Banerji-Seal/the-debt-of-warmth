\person[Corin]{The boy} took the afternoon in through the small sounds first, as he took every room, because the small sounds do not lie and the large ones are mostly weather: the dry tick of cooling clay along \place{the loaf shelf}, the rasp of a coal settling under the ash at \place{the hearth}, the low note of the river coming under \lore{the winter skin} through \place{the finger's-width door}. The light had gone off the low angle where he lay and left the fold and the crust's hair-lifted corner in the shade, and the count stood where it stood, three hundred and seventy-four exact, carried out of the morning and not spent and told by nobody who would have known what a number counted. \place{Stone} kept its fifteenth day without a boundary drawn to mark the turning of the light; the deep-winter month held both its names, the older women calling it by the weather and the slatemen by the count of Account-days, neither habit set right or left. He did not stand the number up as a threshold for anybody's foot to cross. A day was a small vessel and would not be poured into the next.

\person{Sella Vail} came to the basket after the fire, as she came to everything, and gave the instruction to the wool, the river wind and the fire she would bank against their coming back, never to the basket. “Keep it off his ears,” she told the wool. “It's the river wind.” She lifted him level and out of the draught at the half-pace she kept from every open slate, and carried him down the nineteen steps into \place{the Clay Yard} with his cheek against the post's cold and her whole attention on the basket's level, as a pot is carried that must not be slopped.

The yard went around its seven walked-around places with a look and a wide step and no number. At \place{the Vail corner} the clean dark line kept its finer grey hairline beside it, the thing the house called \lore{the thaw}, unswept and thickened in the month's dry cold, with the faint sour tang of \lore{Ash} coming up off it where it had not been swept in two years. Beneath \place{the lowest rack} the broken \lore{birth-slates} lay where they had been put and were entered by nobody, and on \place{the flagstones} by the rack end the round ring where the shallow pan had once stood kept no scrape, no crumb, no print, no chip and no word. \place{the yard well} stood with its rope stiff in the cold and \place{the trough} skinned over from edge to edge; on \place{the flat stone by the well} the silt-clouded plate of ice sat a little out of the path of feet, which was where it had been put and where it stayed. Farther out, on \place{the worked strips}, the field's empty place kept its own shape in the stubble, and nearer in, where the returning line came back on itself, lay the place where a hand had gone in on Seed 15 and had not come out; she went around that one widest, by nothing measurable, and went around it once and not twice, because a second going-around would have made a figure of once.

The gone white frost off the well's north lip, the skin on the trough and the clouded plate on the flat stone stayed weather entered nowhere, with a finger between every pair, not a fourth covering and not a sixth low thing. Through the finger's-width door the river kept its note of \lore{water over water}, a low thing over a lower thing, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming; over it from bank to bank lay the winter skin the cold had put on and the cold would take off, found where put on the first day of the month and found again this afternoon and not made again, not spent on the day's work or the going of the light and not made to buy a lesson under any roof. The skin sat on its own place beside the low note and not upon it, a covering put on by the weather and entered nowhere, and he entered it nowhere and kept a finger between it and the note it muffled. A line left where it is is still a line kept, and a sign is a thing that points to a payer, and no payer stood under that ground.

At \place{the kindling} the account stood in its old exactness. Six pieces lay in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check and the seventh lay at the lip beyond it where it could not, and beside the seventh lay \person{Garl Vail}'s unforged collector sentence, with no name on the man who turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on the debt; the debt against a firing of tiles lay where it lay, neither paid nor settled nor done with and worse for being uncalled. His name had gone unspoken under the roof three hundred and seventy-four days and \lore{the carried fire}'s fifth had gone unpaid the same number with its payer unnamed. The small knife lay at the very edge of its sill, where it could be checked and where a hand could also slip it off.

\person{Tarn} passed his hand over blade and sill as a man passes over a tool put back where it belongs, and the hand went away when the checking was done and no breath stayed at the top of the reach. “Six counted,” he said to the wood. “The next I don't count.” The held breath he had left at the sill on Ash 14 and the thumb's resting of Bone 26 were found where they had been put and not made again, set where they could be checked and passed over as a man passes over a tool put back where it belongs, beside the five objects and the pot-crack resting with a finger between every pair — not a sixth held object, not a seventh of anything, not a tenth stopping, not the calling of the loan and with no payer standing. The pale clip-ring at his left wrist caught what little light there was and gave it back. His chest had been tight since the dark and the Ash was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line, and his stroke went over the flagstones' shallow bright patch without the half-check, the Bone 8 returned half-check staying where it had been put.

He brought the ear-sentence to the word that would call the loan and stopped there as a remembered ninth stopping with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end, the Seed 16 breath unspent at the top of the reach and the loan uncalled, and he did not look along the road. The equality under the roof held where it had moved to: two ears, two silences, a loan that can hear and has never been called, with nobody to enter it and nobody to call it. The boy took the stopping apart by what was there and by what was not and filed it on the interval tray among the nine with a finger between, no tenth standing up in the empty place, for a tenth stopping would have been a habit and a habit wants a payer and none stood.

\person{Jorra Vail} came through the finger's-width door off \place{the Vail wheel-shed} with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bent little finger and white in a fist-crease where a hand had opened, and it was not the bone-coloured \lore{slip} of the wheel. Three or more men unnamed had gone low into \place{the back room} before first grey, and he gave the house no name for them and no word that the room was cold. He kept his dusty forearms and his clean ones on one tray and parted them as clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had been broken nor whether it was a birth-slate while the broken ones lay under the lowest rack and were entered by nobody. He spoke to the wheel before he spoke to any person. “It doesn't care whose hands,” he said. “It cares if they're steady.” To a pot on the bench he said, “You'll do,” in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either, and he knew \lore{the full note} that pot would give three turns before it left his hands, with air under it and a want in it, and kept the knowing where nobody could make a song of it, withheld from any master come up from \place{High Kiln} who would call it early and sing a pot before its time.

When Sella had not finished telling him the fire was low his bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper} ahead of her sentence, took the cold out of the fingertip, moved the tongue a hair's breadth and was left standing so the end of her instruction arrived at the pot and the household straightened around the waiting as cloth straightens when a weight is lifted — one piece of iron with two bites, the holding and the taking, with no Grathic word covering both mercies. The finger came out without leaving the extra holding of Ash 26 a second time, gathered once on a place of its own with no payer standing, not a sixth or seventh or eighth held object, not a habit, a way, a sign, a hearing, a mending, a cause, a payment or a rule. “He's not Vail” stayed sealed as a lid nobody had tried to lift in three years, and the promise of the boy's name to the men who burn slates was neither given nor withdrawn, gathering interest against the burned slate at five and the three Ash directions, with nobody standing to be asked.

\scenebreak

At the going of the light Sella carried him up the nineteen steps under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof and never into \place{the Debt Loft}, level and out of the draught at the half-pace from every open slate, and set him on \place{the bed} at the low angle of the loaf shelf. She put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face, righting the wool where it had ridden back from the cold; and then her hand, doing what hands do at a bed, tucked the wool into the empty place beside the crust. The wool went in as a hand goes in where a thing used to be and is not — not to fill the place, for it kept its shape and its emptiness both, but to leave the warmth of a hand where the cold had been.

It was a small keeping. No word went with it. No third crust was put there and no name spoken over the place and no look toward his face and no instruction given to the basket. The crust kept its hair-lifted corner two inches from the ear and the place beside it kept its name to itself, and now the wool lay in it, folded once, the way a woman tucks a blanket against a child's shoulder in a room that is not quite warm. Her hand rested the half-beat longer than the setting needed before it went away, the little-door burn on the back of her left hand closing on nothing, and the thread of drool at his mouth was left frozen in the dry kiln heat and unwiped where it lay. She said nothing of where they had been. No ownerless voice came over the boards on this day of all days, and the Ash 5 joining and the Ash 17 and Ash 23 directions stood where they had been put and did not repeat.

\scenebreak

The boy took the interval apart by what was there and by what was not, as a surgeon takes a wound apart by the tissue that is present and the tissue that is missing. There had been a hand at the fold's gap, and it had come once, and there had been no word spoken over it and no giver standing to be thanked or called. He filed it on a place of its own beside the crust's own gap, the loaf shelf's two gaps in the room whose floor did not touch this one, the round ring where the shallow pan had once stood on the flagstones, and the field's empty place out on the worked strips, with a finger between it and every other, and he kept a finger between it and the keeping at the damper and the held breath at the sill and the thumb's resting and the pot-crack resting, so that no row came out even by his doing.

It was expressly not a joining of rooms, though the wool lay now in a place that had kept its name to itself for three hundred and seventy-four days and the shelf's two gaps lay in another room and the likeness between them was plain enough to make a man reach across with his finger. He did not reach. It was not a third crust, for no crust had been put there; not a gift, for a gift wants a giver and nobody gave; not a payment, for no payer stood under it; not a sixth held object and not a seventh or eighth; not a habit, for a habit wants a second, and a second would be a keeping made again and a third a way, and both want a payer where none stood; not a sign, not a hearing, not a mending, not a cause, not a rule. It was a small keeping found where it was put, gathered once and not made again, going the way of the stayed inch and the early letting-go and the extra holding in the cold damper, each on a place of its own with a finger between.

The far-off finger that would one day lay shelf and fold together and call the joining an account was still far off and was not his; it had not yet come. The account wanted a hand to lay the places together and a payer under all of them, and no hand stood and no payer stood. The keeping gathered interest where it lay and was not paid and did not stand up and call itself a want. He had learned to tell the difference between a line drawn by weather and a line drawn by a hand, and this one had been drawn by a hand that had not looked at his face and had not said what it was doing, which was the whole of what made it a keeping and not a sign.

The surgeon read the movement the way he read a hand at the edge of a wound that closes nothing: exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a warmth laid against the cold side of a vessel that would not hold it. He knew that a face composed by cold is not composure and that pink returning to a face is the surface still cold and not the core, and he knew the order in which heat comes back is the reverse of the order in which it goes, and that a cold patient cannot clot and will seem better before he is worse. There was a small clean satisfaction in seeing a hand do the one careful thing available to it in a room where nothing careful could be asked for, and the satisfaction cost him nothing, which was how he knew it was not peace.

The infant wanted the crust lifted two inches to his mouth, the thread frozen at his lip wiped, and a hand that would close on the pot handle set where the fingers could reach it. The wool in the empty place was not any of these. Both were true. The second was louder. Neither won, and the quarrel between them was the work; he had stopped expecting the quarrel to end, which was not the same as being used to it.

Under the roof Sella went to the hearth before she went to the basket, as she had gone at both ends of every day for three years. “It's the thaw,” she told the coals, and turned them with the back of a broken pot, and then, “The house will keep it,” where another woman would have said I. She stood a moment with her hands gone still, counting a small intake, a hold and a letting-go toward no opening, and gave neither gap a figure, so that the count went into the room and not onto a slate.

On \place{the pot board} the twice-mended pot kept the hairline of the original break showing between the old-brass staple and the tighter wire. Her thumb went first where the wire crossed the crack before the weight came on the handle, and the proverbs went to the pot and the fire and never to a person. “Leave the crack showing. People trust a thing they can see has been handled.” And: “If it's worth mending it's worth mending twice. The second time is the real one.” They were improved in the keeping and very likely not as good as the man had said them, who had said them to a pot and never to a person; he was present in the house in objects and not in a story, in the salt-stiff coat on its peg behind the door, in the hollow of the wheel-seat nobody filled, in the clean dark line at the corner with its finer grey unswept and thickened in the month's dry cold. Repair stayed apart from restoration: a restored thing hides the hand that handled it, and a mended thing leaves that hand showing. She set the pot rim two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat and no look toward the bed, the half-beat the distance between intention and arrest, bought at nothing and not a gift, for a gift wants a giver and nobody gave.

She turned the dressed grain under wool with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child and lifted no figure and no handful. Her finger lay along the top of the loaf shelf's front edge so the charcoal tally underneath stayed unread; her lips moved along a two-gapped row and figured neither gap. She glanced once at the clay jar standing cold under \lore{lead glaze}, and shut her hand half over nothing, the little-door burn on the back of her left hand closing on air. The cord of her \lore{widow's mark} was oiled where a working thumb had touched it ten thousand times, and the mark hung where it hung, untouched, because a going of the light and a bed are not openings, seven openings in seven months are seven and not a rule, and no eighth was made due. Under the floor the carried fire kept four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer without a name three hundred and seventy-four days and nobody lifting the floor to supply the pull. Overhead \place{the boards} kept four nails on the near one, four on the next, and a thumb-width knot where a fifth might have been driven and was not. Three counts in three places, and none rose into another, because agreeing any two would have wanted a payer put under both and no payer stood.

The held things were checked without standing up, as a man checks a field by its small sounds before he lets the light into it. Six hearings of the full note stood beside and never among, the sixth the small flat knock out of \person[Dessa Silt] as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go and never among the sources; the seventh place stayed empty and was not dug for or filled. The flat knock of \lore[the Hearth Drawing]{the Drawing} stood on its own place outside them, not a seventh and not a filling of the empty place, for a hearing wants a source standing and that room had been seen from a basket and not entered. The heard bell stayed in its square beside the told bell of \lore{the Long Night} and was not folded into any of them.

Nine record-first hands stood with no tenth: \person{Hessa Rale}'s eighth at \place{the Cross}, \person{Ilva}'s ninth at the well, the collector's ordinary hand on its own tray joined to none, and no hand stood to lay them together and call them a rule, for a rule is an account that has found a payer and none stood. Nine stoppings kept no tenth before the seventh year at its very end, the loan uncalled and the Seed 16 breath unspent. Ten arithmetics stood behind one unsigned figure — the three of \place{Frost}, the first, second and third of \place{Bone}, and the first, second and third of \place{Ash} and Stone's first — the figure read still not the figure paid, no signature on the space and no doctrine come out of ten; their ten true subtractions rode in \person{Berr Clay}'s box up \place{the clay road} locked, unread under this roof, the key gone up in a coat pocket and not coming back down. No sixth hearing, seventh opening, sixth held object, sixth telling, third shut-room telling, sixth asking, fifth shaping, tenth hand or tenth stopping was supplied to make a row come out even, for a finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged. The burned slate stood at five, the shut-room tellings at two, the row at five askings with no witness and the well-saying at four shapings, and none was made a sixth of on a day that had not earned one.

In the small hours \place{the towel} came out at the head of the bed as instruments come out on a towel when a case is done and before the next is called, and the file went over by the place of each thing with every gap left gap: three hundred and twenty-three honest answers standing on the sixth place and the three hundred and twenty-fourth to come; the five objects with the pot-crack resting, the hand-at-sill breath and the holding in the damper beside them; the new keeping at the fold beside the crust's gap with a finger between it and every other; the ninth record-first hand with no tenth; the nine stoppings with no tenth; the burned slate at five, the shut-room tellings at two, the well-saying at four shapings, the row at five askings with no witness; the four rooms' gap on a place of its own; the river's winter skin beside water over water, found where put and not spent; the promise under the wheel-shed neither given nor withdrawn and the loan uncalled. The hand outside the wool came up an inch toward the river's note under the finger's-width door, the way a hand goes up in a field it has learned not to disturb. It did not reach, and the inch was not a reach. He took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole on the note's own finishing under the winter skin, let the intake go as the note ended and came straight down and laid itself flat against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. The note had done before the hand came down, so the letting-go was neither early nor stayed and the hand did not stay; the note's own finishing under the covering ended the interval and not any decision of the hand. The intake was not a breath kept for warmth, the interval not a stopping of the river, the note not his.

He filed it as answer three hundred and twenty-four: exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the ten readings, the bell's gap, the flat knock, the child's grey fingertips, the mother's cup, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the held breath at the sill, the two bites of iron, the river's winter skin, the widow's mark, the new keeping at the fold nor his own kept gaps. Three hundred and twenty-four honest answers stood on the sixth place and not one was a doctrine, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood. The surgeon took his small clean satisfaction in a field taken as found and left as found, and it cost him nothing, which was how he knew it was not peace. The infant wanted the crust lifted two inches, the thread wiped, a hand on the pot handle. Both were true. The second was louder. Neither won, and the quarrel between them was the work.

Overhead the boards kept four nails on the near one, four on the next and a thumb-width knot where a fifth might have been driven and was not; under the floor the carried fire gave four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood; through the finger's-width door the river went low over lower under its winter skin, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming.
