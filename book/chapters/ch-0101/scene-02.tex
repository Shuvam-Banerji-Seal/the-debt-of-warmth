The afternoon came on without a market, a Cross or a bell, the sky the colour of wet ash and the pots standing bellies full of short light. \person{Sella Vail} carried \person[Corin]{the boy} down from under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof at the half-pace she keeps from every open slate, the wicker level in her two hands the way a pot filled is carried that must not be slopped. The instruction went ahead of her to the wool, the river wind and the fire --- keep it off his ears; it's the river wind; bank it against their coming back --- and never to the basket. She did not look into it. She did not look toward the yard's seven places until she was among them, and then she went around them with a look and a wide step and no number, the way a woman goes around a pail of hot water set where a child might knock it.

There was the clean dark line at \place{the Vail corner} where the Ash ridge turned, and the finer grey beside it that the yard called \lore{the thaw}, unswept both. There was the space beneath \place{the lowest rack} where the broken birth-slates lay, and nobody put a hand among them. There was the round ring where the shallow clay pan had once stood, with no scrape, no crumb, no print, no chip and no word left in it; a place named from the shape it had left. There was the skin on \place{the trough}. There was the field's empty place out on \place{the worked strips}, and the returning-line place where a hand had gone in on \place{Seed} 15 and had not come out. Seven places, gone around and entered none, for a thing entered wants a payer standing and none stood. The boy took them in as a field is taken in before the dressing, by what was there and by what was not: a line, a space, a ring, a skin, an absence in a strip, an absence in a line of carriers going home, and over all of it the wide step that would not set a foot inside. He made no eighth of the seven and no lesson of the going around.

Three coverings stayed weather entered nowhere, with a finger between every pair and no fourth likeness made a cause. The gone white frost on the north lip of \place{the yard well} had not come back; the skin on the trough broke under a pail and closed behind it; the plate of ice clouded with silt lifted its face a little out of the path of feet on \place{the flat stone by the well}, where Sella had told the weather where to stand. Three things that the cold had laid on three different objects, and between the three a finger, because likeness without a payer is only a likeness and not a cause. Nobody swept the lip, broke the plate or counted the coverings, and the boy filed them where they lay and left them there.

At \place{the rack end} she turned the dressed grain once under wool with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child, her fingers going in and coming out without closing, no figure and no handful. The empty fingers went on a place of their own beside the one object of \place{Bone} 22 --- the handful lifted and let fall back through the fingers instead of spread --- as a keeping and not a second handful, for two would be a habit and three a way, and both want a payer and none stood. The grain lay covered and unfigured under a number that did not know what it cost.

The month's hunts and repairs went about the seven places as directions judged by the carrying arm and the weather, never figures. A man come down from \place{Cold Acre} shifted a hare across his shoulders at the well and, to a woman who asked what the carrying had cost, told her to ask the weather, which had carried the snow and could carry the price; he went off toward \place{the clay road} with the hare unweighed and nobody opening a slate. Another with a cracked jar in a sling of rope asked what the month's mending would want and was told to ask the pot, and the pot, being a pot, said nothing. A woman with a basket of kindling asked what the seventh year would want when it came. Two men mending a strip of fence said the thaw would be early and late. To each of them \person{Tarn} said \emph{Yes, house}, agreeing so thoroughly that no argument could find a foot, and he kept the late because a late thaw puts off the paying, though the paying had stood in the ruts since the water went. He did not weigh the hare, number the jar, figure the kindling or settle the fence. The yard took what the arm told it and asked for nothing on a slate.

Tarn took up his station at \place{the kindling} with the broom only after it had been given its instruction. Six of the seven years stood in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check, and the seventh stood at the lip beyond it, beside \person[Garl Vail]'s unforged collector sentence with no name on the man who had turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on the debt. The small knife lay at the very edge of its sill, where it could be checked and where a hand could also slip it off. He passed his hand over blade and sill as a man passes a hand over a tool put back where it belongs, the pale clip-ring at his left wrist catching what little light there was and giving it back, and the hand went away when the checking was done with no breath held at the top of the reach.

“Six counted. The next I don't count,” he told the wood, in the tone of a man stating the weather.

His chest had been tight since the dark, and \place{Ash} was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line, and no scrubbing lifted it. He checked the thumb's resting of \place{Bone} 26 and found it where it had been put and did not make it again. He checked the held breath at the knife-sill of Ash 14 --- hand to the sill, over the knife as the house checks, away when the checking was done, no breath held at the top of the reach --- and found it where it had been put and did not make it again. Not a sixth held object, not a seventh, not a tenth stopping, not the seventh year given, not the calling of the loan, not joined to the handful, the thumb's resting or the pot-crack resting, with no payer standing under any of them.

The ear-sentence came up to the word that would call the loan and stopped there as a remembered ninth stopping, with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end, the Seed 16 breath unspent and the loan uncalled. He did not look along \place{the clay road}. His stroke went over the shallow bright patch on \place{the flagstones} without the half-check; the returned half-check of Bone 8 stayed where it had stopped with the Day 161 fraction, the Day 166 beat and the \place{Frost} 5 beat, four held changes on four places and not a rule; and neither the handful, the thumb's resting, the pot-crack resting nor the held breath at the sill was made a fifth. The warm loft stone lay cold in its unmarked pot, unreconciled by not trying, which was not forgiveness. Two ears had kept the same silence under one roof for years, and the equality had moved five times and none of the changes had stood up and called itself a rule.

\person{Jorra Vail} came through \place{the finger's-width door} off \place{the Vail wheel-shed} with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bent little finger and white in a fist-crease where a hand had opened, and it was not the bone-coloured \lore{slip} of the wheel. His dusty forearms and his clean ones he kept on one tray and parted as a surgeon parts clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had been broken nor whether it was a birth-slate; the broken birth-slates under the lowest rack were entered by nobody. Men unnamed had gone low into \place{the back room} before first grey, and he gave the house no name for them and did not say the room was cold.

He spoke to the wheel before he spoke to any person. “It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady,” he said, and to a pot he told \emph{You'll do} in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either. He knew the \lore{full note} that pot would give three turns before it left his hands, with air under it and a want in it, and he kept the knowing where nobody could make a song of it, withheld from any kiln-master come up from \place{High Kiln} who would call it early and sing a pot before its time.

When Sella had not finished telling him the fire was low, his bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper} ahead of her sentence, took the cold out of the fingertip, moved the tongue a hair's breadth and left it standing so that the end of her instruction arrived at the pot and the household straightened around the waiting as cloth straightens when a weight is lifted. One piece of iron had two bites, the holding and the taking, and there was no \lore{Grathic} word for both mercies. \emph{He's not Vail} stayed sealed, and the promise of the boy's name to the men who burn slates was neither given nor withdrawn, while the claim went on gathering interest against it. The extra holding that would come on the morrow was not present this afternoon and was not hoped for.

\scenebreak

The boy turned out the whole file as a surgeon turns out instruments on \place{the towel}, each thing in its place and nothing laid across anything else, checked and left as it lay. Six hearings of the full note stood beside and never among, the sixth the small flat knock out of a woman as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go and never among the sources, and the seventh place stayed empty and none was invented to fill it. The heard bell --- four, four and a gap, heard at \place{the Cross} with nobody crossing himself --- stayed in its square beside the told bell of \lore{the Long Night}, not folded into the three counts and not heard as a seventh of the full note. The told figure stood beside the paid with no signature on the space between. Eight arithmetics of the year --- three of Frost, the first, second and third of Bone, and the first two of \place{Ash} --- were eight and not one account, both of the dark month's subtractions locked and unread up the clay road in \person{Berr Clay}'s box, the finding on a place of its own with no payer under it. Nine record-first hands stood with no tenth, \person{Ilva}'s ninth on its own place with a finger between it and \person{Hessa Rale}'s eighth; nine stoppings with no tenth; the row at five askings, the burned slate at five, the shut-room tellings at two, the well-saying at four shapings; and the Ash 5 joining, the Ash 17 direction and the Ash 23 direction held apart with a finger between every pair.

No voice came over \place{the boards} this afternoon to make a sixth telling, a third shut-room telling, a sixth asking, a fifth shaping, a tenth hand or a tenth stopping, and none was supplied to make a row come out even; nothing from the twenty-third had crossed as sign, lesson, mending, cause or payment. The far-off hand that would one day lay the district's private keepings together was still far off and was not his. \place[Ash]{Ash 30} stood five mornings ahead and was named only as a date, never as a promise, and was not brought into a day that had not earned it; no ninth arithmetic was supplied early, no seventh opening made due, no sixth hearing, sixth held object, sixth telling, third shut-room telling, sixth asking, fifth shaping, tenth hand or tenth stopping invented to make the row come out even.

At the going of the light Sella carried him up at the half-pace and went wide around the seven places again with a look and a step and no number. She set him on his bed under the roof and never in \place{the Debt Loft}, righted the fold at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face, let her hand rest the half-beat longer than the setting needed before it went away, and left the thread where it lay, frozen and unwiped. To the coals she gave their instruction and told them it was the thaw, and said \emph{The house will keep it} where another woman would have said I; she kept her finger on top of \place{the loaf shelf} so the charcoal tally stayed unread, and lifted the twice-mended pot at \place{the pot board} thumb-first where the wire crossed the crack, with both proverbs gone to the pot and the fire and never to a person and the hairline left showing between old-brass staple and tighter wire. She set the pot rim two inches beyond the reaching hand, with a half-beat and no look toward the bed. \lore{The widow's mark} hung where it hung and was not touched --- a going of the light and a bed are not openings, six openings in six months are six and not a rule, and no seventh was made due.

In the small hours the hand outside the wool came up an inch toward the river's note under the finger's-width door, the way a hand goes up in a field it has learned not to disturb. It did not reach, and the inch was not a reach. The note went \lore{water over water}, a low thing over a lower thing, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming, never numbered among the hearings, for a hearing wants a source standing and none stood. The hand took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole on the note's own finishing, let the intake go, and came straight down and laid itself back against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. The note had done before the hand came down, so the letting-go was neither early again nor stayed, and the hand did not stay. He filed it as answer two hundred and eighty-four: exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the eight arithmetics, the bell's gap, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the pot-crack resting, the held breath at the sill, the staying at the inch, the early letting-go, the sixth opening, the Ash 5 joining, the Ash 17 direction, the Ash 23 direction, the word that stood in no doorway, the fourth shaping, the turned writing, the strip in the sleeve nor his own kept gaps.

The surgeon took his small clean satisfaction in a field left as found and it cost him nothing, which was how he knew it was not the same as peace. Underneath, the infant went on wanting the crust lifted the two inches to his mouth, the thread wiped and a hand on the pot handle; both were true, the second was louder, neither won, and the two registers quarrelled without verdict. Three hundred and fifty-four stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody under the Vail roof; six months and one hundred and seventy-four days were told by nobody; \lore{the carried fire}'s fifth was unpaid the same number with its payer unnamed; and the man whose coat hung salt-stiff on its peg behind the door had not been named under that roof the same number of days. Nothing on the twenty-fifth had been opened, joined by a standing hand, named, called, paid or ended.

The hand lay back against the fold on answer two hundred and eighty-four; the file lay open on the towel at the head of the bed with every gap left gap; the twice-mended pot stood on the pot board with the hairline showing between old-brass staple and tighter wire; one stiff brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from his ear with its empty place unnamed and no third, and the thread at his mouth was frozen and unwiped. Through the finger's-width door the river went water over water, low over lower, while the boards overhead kept four nails on the near one, four on the next and a thumb-width knot where a fifth might have been driven and was not. On its peg behind the door the salt-stiff coat kept the broader shape of a man whose name had not been spoken under this roof three hundred and fifty-four days. Forward, not closed.
