## Chronicle

Ash kept its last three tending days without a boundary drawn, no market, no Cross or bell and no collector on the clay road. Under the Vail roof Sella Vail went to the hearth before the basket, told the coals it was the thaw, turned them with a broken pot's back and said *The house will keep it* where another woman would have said I. She went wide around the yard's seven walked-around places with a look and no number, turned the dressed grain under wool with no figure or handful, lifted the twice-mended pot thumb-first where the wire crossed the crack and left the hairline showing, and set the rim two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat and no look toward the bed. The trough's skin grew thick enough to hold a spoon and the river's note sat lower under the door.

Tarn kept six in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check and the seventh at the lip beside Garl's unforged collector sentence, said *Six counted. The next I don't count*, checked the thumb's resting of Bone 26 and the held breath of Ash 14 and found each where put and not made again, and brought the ear-sentence to the word that would call the loan and stopped. Jorra Vail spoke to the wheel before any person, parted dusty from clean as clot from vessel, and put his bent finger into the cold damper ahead of Sella's unfinished sentence --- one iron with two bites, the holding and the taking.

On the twenty-sixth, after the sentence was finished and the household had straightened, that finger stayed in the cold iron a breath longer than the work required. The boy filed it on a place of its own with a finger between it and every other keeping: gathered once and not spent, not a sixth held object, not a habit, not a way, not a rule, with no payer under it. On the twenty-seventh Jorra took his finger out and left the interval where it had been put, for a second would be a habit and a third a way. At the well Ilva, her hand to her sleeve before eyes to the asker and mouth after both, answered *Ask the pot what it cost. I keep the count, not the account*, the notched wood unread; it was the ninth record-first hand, with no tenth, and she was the speaker of no voice.

Each night the hand went up an inch toward the river's note, took a small intake, held it whole on the note's own finishing and came straight down --- answers 283 through 288, exact and worth nothing at two inches of air. Three hundred and fifty-six days stood exact and spoken by nobody, six months and one hundred and seventy-six days; the carried fire kept four pulls and a gap, the boards four, four and a knot, the river low over lower. What the days cost was attention, which no institution can tax.
