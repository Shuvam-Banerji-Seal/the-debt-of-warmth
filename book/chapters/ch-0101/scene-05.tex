\daymark{356}{Ash 27, Seventhday, Y.H. 813}

The hand outside the wool lay back against the fold on answer two hundred and eighty-six, as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. It had come up an inch a moment before toward the river's note under \place{the finger's-width door}, done its small work and gone, and now it rested with the backs of the fingers against the wool and the thumb a little apart, the way a hand rests after a probe has been set down in its tray and let go. In the small hours under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof nothing had changed hands. Two hundred and eighty-six honest answers stood on the sixth place, each an object and not a power, and the two hundred and eighty-seventh had not yet been entered, because the note under the door had not yet finished. \person[Corin]{The boy} lay on \place{the bed} at the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf} and listened to a house keeping itself by its not-doings. The room had its night sounds: coals settling under their ash, the wool breathing at his ear, a dry tick from cooling clay, and under all of them the low river. The large sounds were mostly weather, and the small ones did not lie, so he read the room as a trauma bay is read before the light, by the small sounds first. Overhead \place{the boards} kept four nails on the near board, four on the next and a thumb-width knot where a fifth might have been driven and was not. Under the floor \lore{the carried fire} kept four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer without a name. Through the finger's-width door the river went \lore{water over water}, a low thing over a lower thing, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming. Three counts in three places, and none rose into another, because agreeing any two would have wanted a payer put under both and none stood.

Nothing from the days behind had crossed under this roof as sign, lesson, mending, cause or payment, and the day before this one had been kept exactly and left exactly. Eight arithmetics of the year stood behind one unsigned figure --- the three of \place{Frost}, the first, second and third of \place{Bone}, and the first two of \place{Ash} --- and both of the dark month's subtractions rode locked and unread up \place{the clay road} in \person{Berr Clay}'s box, the key gone up in a coat pocket and not coming back down. One in twelve stood printed and unchallenged; the figure read was still not the figure paid, and no signature stood on the space between. Eight were eight and not one account. The finding sat on a place of its own with no payer under it and no doctrine standing up out of it, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood. \lore{The widow's mark} had not been touched since the sixth opening at \place{the kiln mouth} on the second of Ash; six openings in six months were six and not a rule, and no seventh was made due. Six hearings of \lore{the full note} stood beside and never among, the seventh place empty; the heard bell four, four and a gap in its square beside the told bell of \lore{the Long Night}; nine record-first hands with no tenth; nine stoppings with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end and the loan uncalled. The burned slate stood at five, the shut-room tellings at two, the row at five askings with no witness, the well-saying at four shapings, \person{Hessa Rale}'s writing still turned toward herself and unread, \person{Ilva}'s notched strip still in her sleeve, and the breath held at the knife-sill still where it had been set and not made again. Five held objects sat with the pot-crack resting beside them and the hand-at-sill breath beside that, none a sixth and none a seventh. The Ash 5 joining, the Ash 17 direction and the Ash 23 direction lay each on a place of its own with a finger between every pair, advanced and not paid. And the holding of the twenty-sixth --- the extra breath in the cold iron after the work was done --- lay on its own place with a finger between it and every other object, gathered once and not spent. This morning he found it where it had been put and did not make it again. A second would be a habit and a third a way, and both want a payer, and none stood; it was not a sixth held object, not a seventh, not an eighth of anything, not a mending, not a hearing, not a near-full answer, not a cause and not a rule, and it gathered toward the far-off hand that is still far off and is not his.

Three hundred and fifty-five had stood exact at the going of the light yesterday; three hundred and fifty-six stood exact now, in the small hours of the twenty-seventh, and told by nobody under this roof. Six months and one hundred and seventy-five days had become six months and one hundred and seventy-six. The carried fire's fifth was unpaid three hundred and fifty-six days and its payer had no name. The salt-stiff coat on its peg behind the door kept the broader shape of \person[Garl Vail]{a man} whose name had not been spoken under this roof three hundred and fifty-six days, and the debt against a firing of tiles circled without a name, neither paid nor settled nor done with and worse for being uncalled. Three hundred and fifty-six was not a round number and not a threshold anybody under this roof would speak. It was one more in a row nobody counted aloud. He had long ago stopped expecting to be told what a number counted, which was not the same as not wanting it.

The surgeon's register took the body in its old order, as a wound is taken in the order the layers lie. The crust came first: one stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust two inches from his ear, gone harder and browner in the dry kiln heat, its corner standing a hair off the wool. Beside it the empty place kept its name to itself and no third had come in these days to make the row come out even. The thread of drool at his mouth had frozen in the dry heat and was unwiped, because nobody under any roof in \place{Low Kiln}, in \place{Sill}, in \place{Grath}, had ever been given an instruction about the thread. A basket set down was a pot set down and left. Cold had come cheek, temple, the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, and stopped there as though it had reached the end of a line written out for it, with neither an order to go farther nor an order to go away. \lore{The Afterdrop} waited beyond all three, past a warming nobody in this house would perform.

He knew the composed face that was not composure. He knew the grimace of deep cold that looks like a smile, and the pink that returns to a surface while the core is still cold; he knew a cold patient cannot clot and must be warmed from the outside and slowly, never hurried, never rubbed, never set too near the fire. He knew the order in which a body takes heat back, the small joint first and the core last, and the order in which it gives it up, the reverse, which comes sooner than anybody likes. He knew all of it, and he could not lift his own head.

Under the knowing the infant wanted, and the wanting was louder. He wanted the crust lifted the two inches to his mouth. He wanted the thread wiped. He wanted a hand that would close on the pot handle set two inches beyond the reaching fingers. Both were true, the second louder, neither won, and the argument between them was the work. The body humiliated him in its exact small ways: the drool frozen with no thumb to wipe it, the head useless on the fold, the crust leathery past the point any child would eat it, the pot handle two inches beyond the hand that would have closed on it in another life. He had reduced a thousand shoulders in the dark and he could not turn over. He had once held pressure in a cavity with both hands and now he could not keep a blanket on. The dry joke lived in that and cost him nothing and was not, in the end, funny.

Before first light \person{Sella Vail} came to \place{the hearth}, and the fire came before the basket. She did not look toward the bed. Her forearms were the colour of dried slip, and the grey streak at her temple showed where the lamplight touched it; she smelled of woodsmoke and wet wool, and under that the faint sour mineral tang of the Ash ridge along the skirting board, which she had not swept in two years and did not intend to sweep. Her hand went to the coals first. She told them it was \lore{the thaw} and turned them with the back of a broken pot. “The house will keep it,” she said, where another woman would have said I. The instruction went to the wool and the wind and the fire and never to the basket: “Keep it off his ears,” she told the wool; “it's the river wind,” to the wind. To the pot she gave another practical sentence and to the coals another, and to the thing in the wicker she gave nothing at all.

She stood a moment over the coals counting a small intake, a hold and a letting-go toward no opening, giving neither gap a figure. Her finger lay on top of the loaf shelf so the charcoal tally underneath stayed unread. Her lips moved along a two-gapped row and figured neither gap. Her eye went once to \place{the clay jar} standing cold under \lore{lead glaze} and her hand shut half over nothing, the little-door burn on the back of her left hand closing on air, the cord of her widow's mark oiled where a working thumb had touched it ten thousand times. At \place{the pot board} she lifted the twice-mended pot with her thumb first where the wire crossed the crack, and held the weight a half-beat before she let it come on the handle, the hairline of the original break showing between old-brass staple and tighter wire. She said both proverbs to the pot and to the fire and never to a person. “Leave the crack showing. People trust a thing they can see has been handled,” she told the pot. “If it's worth mending it's worth mending twice. The second time is the real one,” she told the fire, and neither saying was improved in the keeping and neither was wasted. She left the hairline showing, so that repair stayed apart from restoration, for a restored thing hides the hand that handled it and a mended thing leaves that hand showing. Near the hearth she turned the dressed grain once under wool with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child, her fingers going in and coming out without closing, no figure and no handful. Then she set the pot rim two inches beyond the reaching hand, with a half-beat and no look toward the bed. The half-beat was the distance between intention and arrest, bought at nothing and not a gift, for a gift wants a giver and nobody gave.

The boy took that setting apart by what was there and by what was not: a hand, a pot, a rim, a pause; no figure spoken, no look toward the bed, no instruction to the basket. He filed the pause where it belonged and made no more of it. A morning was not an opening and a hearth was not an opening, and the widow's mark hung where it hung and was not touched. The hand goes to the mark before the kiln and at no other hour; six openings in six months were six and not a rule, the sixth held behind at the kiln mouth on the second of Ash without a name and not spent, and no seventh was made due on a day that had not earned one. The mark answered nothing for the crust at two inches, the empty place beside it, the fire's missing fifth, the hand that had gone in on \place{Seed} 15 and not come out, or the woman carried out of the brook clay-heavy and uncollected. None of those stood under it asking to be answered, and she did not ask on their behalf.

The dark month was at its last tend, and the cold had a harder edge at the finger's-width door than it had had a morning ago. Out in the yard the skin on \place{the trough} had thickened until it would have held a poured spoon; the silt-clouded plate of ice stood lifted a little out of the path of feet, and the gone white frost on the well's north lip had not come back. Under the door the river's note sat lower, and under the floor the carried fire answered four low pulls with the silence where the fifth should have stood. The kiln stood cold behind the wall and nobody went out to look at it. Three coverings the cold had laid on three different objects, and between the three a finger, because likeness without a payer is only a likeness and not a cause; and the trough's skin and the plate and the gone frost were filed where they lay and left there. \place[Ash]{Ash 30} stood three mornings ahead and was named only as a date, never as a promise, and its nearness was not brought into a morning that had not earned it. No ninth arithmetic was supplied early, and no seventh opening, sixth hearing, sixth held object, sixth telling, third shut-room telling, sixth asking, fifth shaping, tenth hand or tenth stopping was invented to make the row come out even.

In the small hours the hand outside the wool came up an inch toward the river's note under the finger's-width door, the way a hand goes up in a field it has learned not to disturb. It did not reach, and the inch was not a reach; he had long ago stopped calling it one. Most of what a hand does in a field is wait, and he had once taken a hand into a cavity slowly, with the whole of the attention, and knew the difference between the moving and the waiting. The note went water over water under the door, a low thing over a lower thing, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming, never numbered among the hearings, for a hearing wants a source standing and none stood. The hand took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole on the note's own finishing, let the intake go, and came straight down and laid itself back against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. The note had done before the hand came down, so the letting-go was neither early again nor stayed, and the hand did not stay. It was the note's own finishing that ended the interval and not any decision of the hand, and that was the whole of the honesty in it. The intake was not a breath kept for warmth, the interval not a stopping of the river, the note not his.

He filed it as answer two hundred and eighty-seven: exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the eight arithmetics, the bell's gap, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the pot-crack resting, the held breath at the sill, the staying at the inch, the early letting-go, the sixth opening, the Ash 5 joining, the Ash 17 direction, the Ash 23 direction, the word that stood in no doorway, the fourth shaping, the turned writing, the strip in the sleeve nor his own kept gaps. Two hundred and eighty-seven honest answers stood on the sixth place, each an object and not a power, and not one stood up and called itself a doctrine, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood.

The surgeon took his small clean satisfaction in a field left as found and it cost him nothing, which was how he knew it was not the same as peace. The last clean morning of Ash before its last tend had come with nothing due and nothing answered; the keeping went on as the only honest work there was, and attention was the one thing no institution here had ever thought to tax. The infant underneath took none of the satisfaction and went on wanting the crust lifted the two inches to his mouth, the thread wiped and a hand on the pot handle. Both were true, the second was louder, neither won, and the two registers quarrelled without verdict in the ordinary small hours of the twenty-seventh. He did not spend any of it on himself or make a lesson of it. Nothing on this day had yet been opened, joined by a standing hand, named, called, paid or ended.

Three hundred and fifty-six stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody under the Vail roof; six months and one hundred and seventy-six days were told by nobody; the carried fire's fifth was unpaid the same number with its payer unnamed; and the name of the man the salt-stiff coat kept on its peg was unspoken the same number. Ash kept its twenty-seventh day without a boundary drawn and neither of the district's two habits of naming its months --- the older women by the weather, the slatemen by the number of Account-days --- was set right or left wrong in so many words; the month carried both names the way a pot carries two handles and is none the worse. Ash 30 stood three mornings ahead and was named only as a date, never as a promise, and was not brought into a day that had not earned it. The keeping of the number was the only honest work there had been to do, bought nothing at two inches of air, not for sale, and went on because attention is the one thing no institution can tax.

The hand lay back against the fold on answer two hundred and eighty-seven; the file lay open on \place{the towel} at the head of the bed with every gap left gap; the twice-mended pot stood on the pot board with the hairline showing between old-brass staple and tighter wire; one stiff brown-edged crust lay two inches from his ear with its empty place unnamed and no third, the thread at his mouth frozen and unwiped; and the extra interval in the iron stayed where nobody would enter it, a keeping gathered once and not made again. Through the finger's-width door the river went water over water, low over lower, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming, while the boards overhead kept four nails on the near one, four on the next and a thumb-width knot where a fifth might have been driven and was not. Forward, not closed.
