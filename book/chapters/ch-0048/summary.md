## Chronicle

Three mornings came up over Low Kiln; the kiln month's second working week ran on without a boundary drawn; Tallow was the firing month and Ember the kiln month. The boy woke to hot clay under tallow and wet wool under that; the carried fire answered four low pulls and kept the silence where the fifth should have been, its payer unnamed for a hundred and ninety-seven days, and the river kept its note of water over water. Nobody told him his days: a hundred and ninety-five, then ninety-six, then ninety-seven. He had stopped expecting to be told, which was not the same as not wanting it.

Sella Vail gave the morning its instruction to the wool, the fire, the pot, the broom and the running water, never the basket, and said *the house* where another woman would say *I*. She set the pot handle two inches beyond the reaching hand, fed him gruel from the house's own dish with the spoon on her sleeve, counted under her breath toward no opening, and kept her finger from the shelf's front edge so the charcoal stayed unread. Cost-questions sent to the pot and the weather went away level, nothing priced or entered. She did not touch the widow's mark; there had been no opening since Ember 1, and carried him home around the yard's absences.

Tarn took the broom only after it was told its work, told the wood six counted and the next he did not count, and brought the ear-sentence to the word that would call the loan and stopped; nine stoppings stayed nine, and no collector came. Jorra kept dusty and clean forearms on one tray, told a pot it would do, withheld the note he knew three turns ahead from a man on a wrong wind, and held his bent finger in the cold damper until Sella's sentence reached the pot --- a remembered holding, not a change made again. The sweet edge of lead glaze came off High Kiln on a wrong wind and went.

Three times in three days the boy questioned his own hand. On the first morning it stayed for one of the breaths Sella counted at her fire in the room that does not touch his; on the second it came down before the breath was done; on the third morning it came down at once. In the small hours of the third day it stayed through the whole breath and into the letting go, coming down with the breath rather than before it --- the longest holding, a coordination, not a reach. He filed each answer on a place of its own, not a hearing, not a seventh, not a stopping or a payment, not one of the three held changes, with no payer named. He still could not close two inches to the pot handle, lift the crust or speak a number; the body humiliated him and moved by an inch, and the inch was worth nothing.
