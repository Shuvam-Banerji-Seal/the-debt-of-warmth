## The chapter's one meaningful change

The held inch is **asked again three times in three days** and is held three different ways: on Day 195 it holds for one of Sella's counted breaths (as on Day 194); on Day 196 it comes down before the breath has finished — a shorter holding, the body keeping its own time; on Day 197, in the small hours, it stays up through the whole of the counted breath and **on into the letting go**, and comes down with the breath rather than before it. The longest holding yet, and still a physical first worth nothing. An inch held three times starts to look like a reaching; that is the whole of what this chapter may make of it. It is filed on its own place (a sixth beside the three held changes, the loft scrape and the pale line), never laid across them, never promoted into a hearing, a seventh, a stopping, a payment, a key, a power or a rule, with no payer named and no doctrine stood up out of it. He still cannot close the two inches, lift his chin to the crust or speak a number. Forward, not closed.

## Scene 1 — "The Hand Asked Again"

- **Day and time.** Day 195, Ember 16, Sixthday. Small hours under the roof, then before first grey and into the morning.
- **Place.** Under the Vail kiln-house roof: the bed, the hearth, the loaf shelf's low angle; then out into the Clay Yard.
- **Present.** Corin/Dan; Sella; a neighbour at the threshold with dry scurf of mud on her hem (walk-on).
- **Beats, in order.**
  1. **No recap.** Open directly on the hand outside the wool going up an inch toward the note of water over water through the finger's-width door and coming down as it had each morning of his life. The count of one hundred and ninety-four is carried into the new morning, exact, complete and unspent (T071/T072); he is one hundred and ninety-five days old, six months and fifteen days, and nobody says so.
  2. **First things.** First smell hot clay under tallow and wet wool, with the sour mineral breath of the unswept corner; first sound the kiln drawing in fours with a gap, not a bell. The crust lies leathery two inches from his ear, empty place unnamed, no third; the thread of drool frozen and unwiped; cold stopped at cheek, temple and the bare wrist joint, afterdrop waiting on a warming nobody will perform.
  3. **Three counts.** The carried fire under the floor gives four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have been, its payer unnamed for a hundred and ninety-five days (T088); the river keeps its note of water over water, a fifth low thing never counted among the hearings; overhead the boards keep four nails, four, and a knot the width of a thumb. Three counts in three places that do not become one, because a man who wanted them to agree would have to put a payer under them and no payer stands to any of them (T061/T082).
  4. **Sella's morning.** Instruction to the wool, fire, pot, broom and running water, never the basket; *the house*; half-pace; one glance at the clay jar cold under its glaze without a touch; coal-black hair pushed back with the burn-wrist so the grey streak shows pale; pot handle set two inches beyond the reaching hand with her hand resting a half-beat longer at the setting; barley gruel from the house's own dish with two fingers behind his ear, spoon wiped on her sleeve and not his mouth; the fire told it is the thaw and *The house will keep it* (T058/T079). The widow's mark hangs where it hung — a morning and a shelf are not openings, and there has been no opening since before the kiln on Ember 1.
  5. **The yard's arithmetic.** A neighbour with dry mud-scurf on her hem asks what the firing will want now the account has gone up the road; Sella sends the cost to the pot and the weather, and the question goes away level, nothing priced, refused or entered (T077; the locked box and the printed one in twelve stay offstage, T015/T041/T047 held). The sweet edge comes up off High Kiln on a wrong wind and is answered as a direction, not a second edge paid (T064 held).
  6. **The asking.** Back under the roof, at the note through the door, the hand goes up an inch and is asked to stay. It holds for the length of one of the breaths Sella counts at her fire in the room that does not touch his — small intake, hold, letting go — and comes down against the fold. The surgeon who once held pressure in a cavity until the vessel took holds nothing now but one inch of a small soft hand. Filed on a place of its own: not a hearing, not a seventh, not a stopping, not a payment, not one of the three held changes, not a rule; no payer named, nothing promoted (T061/T082). He still cannot close two inches, lift the crust or speak a number.
- **Emotional turn.** From the old, humiliating morning order — carried, fed, wiped by sleeve, temperature stopped at the wrist — to the small, unbought fact that the inch answered when asked. The body humiliates him and moves by an inch at the same time; both are true. No triumph: the holding is worth nothing and nobody sees it.
- **Sensory anchors.** Hot clay under tallow; wet wool; sour Ash breath; frozen thread of drool; the five low note of water over water; the kiln's four-and-a-gap; four pulls and a missing fifth under the floor; four nails, four, a thumb-width knot overhead; the half-beat at the setting; spoon on sleeve, not mouth; the sweet edge on the wrong wind.
- **Threads:** T071/T072, T088, T061/T082, T058/T079, T077, T015/T041/T047 held, T064 held, T004.
- **Foreshadowing.** The inch's first asking of the chapter plants the second and third askings and the long holding of Scene 6; the pot handle two inches beyond the hand plants the final "cannot close two inches"; the figure gone up the road plants Chapter 49's second reading without putting it on stage. Nothing paid.
- **Exact last image.** The hand outside the wool comes down against the fold, and the fold takes it without a word; one hundred and ninety-five stands exact in the dark, spoken by nobody.

## Scene 2 — "The File Laid Out Again"

Six hearings beside and never among, no seventh, the sixth from a person; three near-full answers advanced and unpaid; nine stoppings as objects and not a rule; three held changes on three places; loft scrape on a fourth; the carried man's pale line on a fifth; and now the held inch on a sixth place of its own.
