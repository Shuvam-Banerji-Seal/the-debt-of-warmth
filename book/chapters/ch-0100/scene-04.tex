The afternoon came on without a boundary drawn, the sky the colour of wet ash, and \person{Sella Vail} carried the basket down from under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof at the half-pace she kept from every open slate. The instruction went to the wool and the river wind and never to the basket. “Keep it off his ears,” she told the wind, and put the fold right under his chin without a look at his face. \place[the Clay Yard]{The yard} came up around \person[Corin]{the boy} by the small sounds first --- the dry rasp of rope at the well, the settle of clay cooling behind a wall, a pot set down out of sight giving its flat note and then nothing --- because the small sounds do not lie and the large ones are mostly weather. Three hundred and fifty-two stood exact behind him, six months and one hundred and seventy-two days told by nobody in \place{Low Kiln}, in \place{Sill}, in \place{Grath}. Under the floor \lore{the carried fire} gave four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer unnamed these three hundred and fifty-two days; on its peg behind the door the salt-stiff coat kept the broader shape of \person{Garl Vail} the same number of days, and his name had not been spoken under this roof in all of them. Nothing from the morning crossed the yard as sign, lesson, mending, cause or payment.

The crust kept its two inches from his ear, gone stiffer and browner in the dry kiln heat, its corner standing a hair off the wool, the empty place beside it keeping its name to itself and no third come in these three days to make the row come out even. The thread of drool at his mouth had frozen and would stay frozen, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread, and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. Cold had come to cheek, temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, and had stopped there as at the end of a line written out for it, with neither an order to go farther nor an order to go away; \lore{the Afterdrop} waited beyond the stopping for a warming nobody in this house would perform. He knew the composed face that is not composure, and the grimace of deep cold wearing the look of a smile, and that a cold patient cannot clot and will seem better before he is worse and must be warmed from outside and slowly, never rubbed, never set too near the fire. He wanted the crust lifted two inches, the thread wiped, a hand that would close on a pot handle set within reach. Both were true, the second louder, neither won. He had once held pressure in a cavity with both hands and now he could not keep the wool on his own shoulder. The joke cost him nothing and was not funny.

She went around the yard the way the yard went around itself, with a look and a wide step and no number. At \place{the Vail corner} the clean dark line lay where it had lain and the finer grey hairline the district called \lore{the thaw} lay beside it, unswept both; beneath \place{the lowest rack} the broken birth-slates lay where they lay and she stepped around them, entering nothing; the round ring where the shallow pan had once stood kept its place with no scrape, crumb, print, chip or word. At \place{the yard well} \place{the trough} kept its skin, thickened now to hold a poured spoon, and on \place{the flat stone by the well} the silt-clouded plate of ice stood a little out of the path of feet; out on \place{the worked strips} the field kept its empty place; and in the returning line, where a hand had gone in on Seed 15 and not come out, she went around with a look and no name. Seven places, no eighth likeness, no cause across two of them, no payer under any. He entered none, because a thing entered wants a payer standing and none stood.

The three coverings stayed weather and were entered nowhere: the gone white frost on the well's north lip, neither a sign now nor a sign then; the skin on the trough breaking under a pail and closing over what it covered; the silt-clouded plate standing where no foot went. Three things on three places with a finger between every pair, and no fourth likeness made a cause. At \place{the rack end} the dressed grain lay turned under wool away from \place{the hearth}, and Sella lifted a corner with a touch still lighter than a woman would use on a thing she meant to count, turned it once and gave no figure. Her fingers went in and came out without closing, so no handful was lifted. The boy filed the empty fingers beside the one object that was the handful let fall on Bone 22 --- a keeping, not a second handful, not a sixth held change, not a rule, with no payer under it. Any figure read over that heap would not know what it cost, and the space between figure and price would have no signature.

The hunts and repairs stayed directions judged by the carrying arm and the weather. A man come down from \place{Cold Acre} shifted a hare across his shoulders at the well and, asked what the carrying had cost, told the asker to ask the weather, which had carried the snow and could carry the price; he went off toward \place{the clay road} with the hare unweighed and nobody opening a slate. Another with a cracked jar in a rope sling asked what the month's mending would want and was told to ask the pot, and the pot, being a pot, said nothing. A woman with a basket of kindling asked what the seventh year would want when it came, and two men mending a strip of fence said the thaw would be early and late. \person{Tarn}, bent at the shoulders from six years in the slip pit, with \place{Ash} worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line, said “Yes, house” to each and kept the late because a late thaw put off the paying, agreeing so thoroughly that no argument could find a foot. The boy read each as a chart blank where the number should stand and entered none of the blanks.

It was into this ordinary going-on, with the boards already talking and the yard going around its places, that the word came. It came over \place{the boards} from the direction of \place{Slate Row}, spoken over the boards and not into the basket, on the same breath as the price of barley and the weather. A voice with no owner said that a room had been shut with the damper closed, and that a slate had been burned over a man whose name nobody said; and under that, lower, it put a direction: the room had been shut against the cold, the slate burned so that a count would not go up. It gave no room named, no hand at any flame, no figure, no date standing, and no man standing in any doorway to be asked. \lore{The damper} was not named as any room's, no flame was given a hand, no count was given a figure. It was a direction and not a visit, a reason spoken by nobody and not a reason given to him. The voice went along the boards where the neighbours stood with their baskets the way a man speaks to boards, and did not look into the basket and did not lower itself for the child in it; a word spoken over a thing is not a word spoken to it, and this one had come a quarter league and would go back the way it came.

The weather answered first. A wrong wind came up off \place{High Kiln} and carried the sweet edge of \lore{lead glaze} over the boards, where the boy lay with the fold against his ear, and touched the fold and went out on the wind that brought it, a direction arrived nowhere and not a second edge paid. The price of barley answered after it, going over the boards and off up the yard, and the voice went with it up \place{the terrace} and was gone. Nobody leaned in to take the answer down. Nobody turned twice. The boards lay where they had laid, four nails on the near one, four on the next and a thumb-width knot where a fifth might have been driven and was not; they had carried the word and kept their own count through it, and did not give the word a place among the nails.

The boy took the word apart as a surgeon takes a wound apart, refusing from the first cut to let it close on itself and call itself whole. What was there was a direction, a voice with no owner, a room shut against the cold, a slate burned over a man nobody named, a reason for the shutting and a reason for the burning, the price of barley and the weather. What was not there was a room named, a hand at any flame, a figure, a date, a name for the man, an account that had gone up or been kept down, and a man standing anywhere to be asked. A reason spoken by nobody is not a reason given; a direction is not a witness; a word going around a yard is not a hand at a flame, for a witness stands somewhere and can be asked, and this voice stood a quarter league away and went on to the price of barley. He had once been sent a chart with its note turned down and a hand going past it to a signature, and he knew the difference between a space left unsigned and a space filled by a man who wanted the row to come out even. This space had been left as it was left.

He found no witness at the bottom of it. He filed the word on a place of its own beside the joining said over these boards on the fifth of Ash and the direction that had come on the seventeenth, and put a finger between the new place and each of the two, and a finger between the two older ones as they had stood: three places in a row and none of them touching. It was a further shaping of that joining and an advancement of the finding that a place wants a witness and a witness is a day somebody stands somewhere and can be asked, but not the witness and not standing. \lore{The burned-slate word} stayed at five with a finger between the fifth and the four, not a sixth telling; \lore{the counting room} shut in the war at two, not three; the row at five askings with no sixth; the well-saying at four shapings with no fifth. Not a hearing of \lore{the full note}, not a seventh, not a cause, not a confession, not a reader, not a name, not a witness, not a payment. No payer stood under it. The edges came near to touching --- near to \person{Ilva}'s unread strip, to \person{Hessa Rale}'s turned writing, to \person{Berr Clay}'s locked box, to the soft-cuffed clerk's inward slate, to the charcoal beneath \place{the loaf shelf}, to the covered grain, to the handful let fall at the rack end --- and the finger went between every pair. He did not let the findings stand up and call themselves a doctrine, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood. The far-off hand that will one day lay the district's private keepings together was still far off and was not his.

At \place{the kindling} Tarn kept six in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check and the seventh at the lip beside the unforged collector sentence Garl Vail had left there, with no name on the man who turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on the debt. The small knife lay at the edge of its sill, where it could be checked and where a hand could also slip it off. “Six counted. The next I don't count,” he told the wood, and cut nothing, counted nothing, called no loan.

He did the small checking a house could see. He set the knife where it could be checked, passed his hand over blade and sill as a man passes a hand over a tool put back where it belongs, the pale clip-ring at his left wrist catching what little light there was and giving it back, and went away; no breath stayed at the top of the reach. The breath left at the sill on the fourteenth was where it had been put: checked on the nineteenth, the twentieth, the twenty-first and the twenty-second, checked a fifth time now and found, not made again on the twenty-third, filed beside the five objects and the pot-crack resting with a finger between every pair, not a sixth held object, not a seventh, not a tenth stopping, not the calling of the loan, with no payer under it. The thumb's resting on the uncut seventh of Bone 26 was likewise found where it had been put and not made again. He cut nothing. He counted nothing. He did not spend the Seed 16 breath. He brought the ear-sentence to the word that would call the loan and stopped there as a remembered ninth stopping with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end, the loan uncalled, and did not look along the road.

At \place{the flagstones} his stroke went over the shallow bright patch without the half-check; the returned half-check of Bone 8 stayed on its own place with the Day 161 fraction, the Day 166 beat and the Frost 5 beat, four held changes on four places and not a rule. The warm loft stone lay cold in its unmarked pot, unreconciled by not trying, which is not forgiveness.

\person{Jorra Vail} came through \place{the finger's-width door} off \place{the Vail wheel-shed} with meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bent little finger, white in the creases of a fist that had opened, not the bone-coloured \lore{slip} of the wheel. He kept dusty forearms and clean ones on one tray and parted them as a surgeon parts clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had been broken nor whether it was a birth-slate. Men unnamed had gone low into \place{the back room} before first grey and he gave the house no name for them and did not say the room was cold. To the wheel he spoke before any person: “It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady.” To a pot, “You'll do,” in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either, knowing the full note it would give three turns ahead, with air under it and a want in it, and withholding the knowing from a kiln-master come up from High Kiln who would call it early and make a song of it.

Sella had not finished telling him the fire was low when his bent finger went into the cold iron of the damper, took the cold out of the fingertip, moved the tongue a hair's breadth and was left standing; the end of her instruction arrived at the pot and the household straightened around his silence the way cloth straightens when a weight is lifted. One iron had two bites, the holding and the taking, and no \lore{Grathic} word for both mercies. “He's not Vail” stayed sealed as a lid nobody had tried to lift in three years, and the promise of the boy's name to the men who burn slates was neither given nor withdrawn while the claim went on gathering interest against it. He did not say that a slate burns in four minutes and had once timed it.

He grew quieter when the direction went around the boards and up the terrace, quieter than the ordinary quiet he kept, and the household straightened around it without an order from anybody. Nobody told anybody to straighten. Sella gave the fire its instruction and Tarn gave the broom its, and the two went on as if the quiet were weather they had agreed to work in. She gave the voice no name, no figure and no lesson, and did not answer on behalf of the man nobody had named. The boy set the two silences under one roof on one tray and parted them as clot from vessel, his finger going between: the ear that had stopped at the calling word and the mouth that had not said the room was cold. A nearly-touching of two silences is not a touching, and a touching is not a payment.

At the going of the light Sella carried him up at the half-pace and wide around the seven walked-around places, the returning-line place gone around with a look and no figure as it had been going down. She set him on \place{the bed} under the Vail roof and never in \place{the Debt Loft}, put the fold right without a look at his face, let her hand rest the half-beat longer than the setting needed, and left the thread where it lay. A sound came from above and behind and no interior was entered; the instruction went to the fire, the wool, the pot and the river wind and never to the basket. She said “The house will keep it” where another woman would have said I. \lore{The widow's mark} hung where it hung and was not touched, because a going of the light and a bed are not openings; six openings in six months were six and not a rule, no seventh made due. The word that had gone up the terrace bought no opening here.

She went to the hearth before the basket, told the coals it was the thaw and turned them with the back of a broken pot, not the poker, because a pot's back knows how much a coal will bear. The fire answered four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer unnamed three hundred and fifty-two days, and she did not supply the pull. Her finger lay on top of the loaf shelf's front edge so the charcoal tally stayed unread; her lips moved along a two-gapped row and figured neither gap; her eye went once to the clay jar cold under lead glaze with the hand half over nothing, the little-door burn on the back of her left hand closing on air. From \place{the pot board} she lifted the twice-mended pot with her thumb first where the wire crossed the crack, both proverbs to the pot and the fire and never to a person, the hairline showing between old-brass staple and tighter wire, repair kept apart from restoration. At the rack end her fingers went in and came out without closing, so no handful was lifted. The pot's rim went down two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat and no look toward the bed. He took the half-beat apart: the distance between intention and arrest, bought at two inches of air, not a gift, for a gift wants a giver and nobody had given.

Garl stayed in objects and not in a story: the salt-stiff coat with its left-shoulder crease on its peg, the wheel-seat keeping its twenty-year hollow that nobody filled, the clean dark line at the Vail corner with its finer grey unswept, the twice-mended pot with the hairline showing between old-brass staple and tighter wire, and the unforged collector sentence beside the knife with no name on the man who turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on the debt. The tile debt circled uncalled, worse for being uncalled; his name had not been spoken under this roof three hundred and fifty-two days, and the keeping of him was maintenance and not a story.

Under the floor the carried fire kept its four and its gap; overhead the boards kept four nails, four and a thumb-width knot; through the finger's-width door \lore{water over water} went low over lower, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming, never numbered among the hearings, for a hearing wants a source standing and none stood. Six hearings of the full note kept the seventh place empty, the sixth the small flat knock out of \person{Dessa Silt} as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go. Eight arithmetics of the year stood behind one unsigned figure, eight and not one account, both of Ash's subtractions locked and unread up the clay road in Berr Clay's box, the figure read still not the figure paid and no signature on the space between. Nine hands with no tenth, nine stoppings with no tenth before the seventh year at its end. The file lay open on \place{the towel} at the head of the bed with every gap left gap. No runner had been seen on any road and no collector had come up or gone down the clay road; there was no market, no \place{the Cross} and no bell. Ash kept its twenty-third day without a boundary drawn, and neither of the district's two habits of naming its months --- the older women by the weather, the slatemen by the number of Account-days --- was set right or left wrong in so many words; the month carried both names the way a pot carries two handles and is none the worse.

In the small hours the hand outside the wool went up an inch toward the river's note under the finger's-width door. It did not reach; the inch was not a reach and he had long stopped calling it one. It was the distance a hand may move in a field without disturbing the dressing, and he took it as he had once taken a hand into a cavity, slowly, knowing most of what a hand does in a field is wait. He took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole on the note's own finishing, let the intake go, and came straight down and laid itself back against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. The note had done before the hand came down, so the letting-go was neither early again nor stayed; it was the note's own finishing that ended the interval, and that was the whole of the honesty in it. The intake was not a breath kept for warmth, the interval not a stopping of the river, the note not his.

He filed it as answer two hundred and eighty: exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the eight arithmetics, the bell's gap, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the held breath at the sill, the staying at the inch, the early letting-go, the sixth opening, the pot-crack resting, the Ash 5 joining, the Ash 17 direction, the Ash 23 direction, the word that stood in no doorway, the fourth shaping, the turned writing, the strip in the sleeve nor his own kept gaps. Two hundred and eighty honest answers stood on the sixth place, each an object and not a power, not one standing up and calling itself a doctrine, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood. The surgeon took his small clean satisfaction in a field left as found; the infant took none and went on wanting the crust lifted, the thread wiped and a hand on the pot handle.

Three hundred and fifty-two stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody under the Vail roof; six months and one hundred and seventy-two days were told by nobody; the carried fire's fifth was unpaid the same number with its payer unnamed; and on its peg behind the door the salt-stiff coat kept the broader shape of a man whose name had not been spoken under this roof the same number, while the debt against a firing of tiles circled without a name, neither paid nor settled nor done with and worse for being uncalled. The Ash 23 direction stood on its own place beside the Ash 5 joining and the Ash 17 direction with a finger between every pair, still gathering and still unspent, with no witness, no sixth telling, no third shut-room telling, no sixth asking and no fifth shaping. Nothing on the twenty-third had been opened, joined by a standing hand, named, called, paid or ended; no runner on any road, no collector on the clay road, no market, Cross or bell. The hand lay back against the fold on answer two hundred and eighty; the file lay open on the towel with every gap left gap; the twice-mended pot stood on the pot board with the hairline showing; one crust lay two inches from his ear with its empty place unnamed and no third; the thread at his mouth frozen and unwiped. The word that had come over the boards stood nowhere to be asked and bought no lesson under this roof. Forward, not closed.
