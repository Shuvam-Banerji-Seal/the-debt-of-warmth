In the afternoon \person{Sella Vail} carried \person[Corin]{the boy} down to \place{the Clay Yard} with the basket held level and the instruction going where it always went --- to the wool, to the river wind, to the fire that would be banked against their coming back --- and never to the basket. “Keep it off his ears,” she said to the wool; “it’s the river wind,” to the wind, and there was no I in either sentence and no instruction for the thing in the wicker. The half-pace she kept from every open slate was a measurement and not slowness, a basket balanced the way a tray is balanced across a room where somebody might have set down a slate and expected a figure off it. He went under her arm with his cheek out of the wind. The cold arrived in its known order, cheek, temple, the small bare joint where the wool rode back, and stopped there as at the end of a line written out for it, with neither an order to go farther nor an order to go away. Under the floor of \place{the Vail kiln-house} \lore{the carried fire} kept its four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer without a name for three hundred and fifty-three days. Three hundred and fifty-three stood behind him exact and unspent, six months and one hundred and seventy-three days told by nobody in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill}, \place{Grath}, and would stand exact at the going of the light. On its peg behind the door the salt-stiff coat kept the broader shape of \person[Garl Vail]{a man} whose name had not been spoken under this roof the same number of days.

The yard lay under a sky the colour of wet ash, and the short afternoon light lay across the rows of pots standing with their bellies full of it, each holding its small private cold until a hand came to take it. The air had the smell of wet clay in it and the last woodsmoke of the day going flat in the chill. She went around the place as she went around it every day, with a look and a wide step and no number. At \place{the Vail corner} the clean dark line of the old \place{Ash} ridge lay where it lay, and beside it the finer grey hairline that somebody had called \lore{the thaw} lay where that lay too, unswept both --- a line left where it is being a line kept. Under \place{the lowest rack} the broken birth-slates lay in their own dark and were entered by nobody, and she went around them as wide as a pail of hot water set where a child might knock it. The gone pan had left a round ring in the dust with no scrape in it, no crumb, no print, no chip and no word. \place{the trough} had a skin on it that would break under a pail and close again. Out on \place{the worked strips} was the field’s empty place, a space in the row where nothing had been set and nobody had supplied a thing to set there. On \place{the flat stone by the well} the silt-clouded plate of ice stood out of the path of feet. In the returning line, where a hand had gone in on Seed 15 and not come out, the yard went around a place that had no figure and no name and never acquired one. Seven places, gone around with a look and a wide step. He entered none of them, because a thing entered wants a payer standing under it and none stood.

Three coverings stayed weather, entered nowhere, with a finger between every pair and no fourth likeness made a cause: the gone white frost on the north lip of \place{the yard well}, the skin on the trough that broke under a pail and closed, the plate of ice lifting its clouded face a little out of the path. Each was a covering and each was weather, and the three were three. If a man had made them one thing he would have had to put a name under it, and no name stood.

At the rack end the dressed grain lay under wool away from \place{the hearth}. She lifted a corner with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child, turned the heap once and gave no figure. Her fingers went in and came out without closing, so that no handful was lifted. The empty fingers went on a place of their own beside the one object of Bone 22 as a keeping and not a second handful, for two would be a habit and three a way, and both want a payer. Whatever arithmetic a later reading would lay upon the heap would not know what it cost; the dressed grain would stand under a figure that had never felt the weight of it, and the space would have no signature. The boy took the going-in and the coming-out apart by what was there and by what was not: fingers, wool, a heap turned once; no closing, no lifting, no figure spoken over it. It was a small clean piece of work and nobody in the yard knew it had been done.

The yard’s hunts and repairs came and went as directions judged by the carrying arm and the weather, and never as figures. A man down from \place{Cold Acre} shifted a hare across his shoulders at the well and, to a woman who asked what the carrying had cost, told her to ask the weather, which had carried the snow and could carry the price; he went off toward \place{the clay road} with the hare unweighed and nobody opening a slate. Another with a cracked jar in a sling of rope asked what the month’s mending would want and was told to ask the pot, and the pot, being a pot, said nothing. A woman with a basket of kindling asked what the seventh year would want when it came. Two men mending a strip of fence said the thaw would be early, and said it would be late, and \person{Tarn} said “Yes, house” to the one and “Yes, house” to the other, agreeing so thoroughly that no argument could find a foot, and kept the late because a late thaw puts off the paying, though the paying had stood in the ruts since the water went. No runner came on any road this afternoon and no collector came up or down the clay road; there was no market, no Cross and no bell, and no ownerless voice came over \place{the boards} to make a sixth of anything. The direction that had come over the boards on the twenty-third stood where it had been put and did not repeat, and bought no lesson under this roof.

At \place{the kindling} Tarn kept six in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check and the seventh at the lip beyond it where it could not, beside Garl’s unforged collector sentence with no name on the man who had turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on the debt. The small knife lay at the very edge of its sill, where it could be checked and where a hand could also slip it off. He passed his hand over blade and sill as a man passes a hand over a tool put back where it belongs, and told the wood, “Six counted. The next I don’t count.” His chest had been tight since the dark, and Ash was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line, and no scrubbing lifted it; the pale clip-ring at his left wrist caught what little light there was and gave it back.

He checked the thumb’s resting of \place{Bone} 26 and found it where it had been put and did not make it again. He checked the held breath he had left at the sill on Ash 14 and found it where it had been put too: hand to the sill, over the knife as the house checks, away when the checking was done, no breath held at the top of the reach. It was not a tenth stopping, not the seventh year given, not a sixth held object, not made again, not joined to the handful, the thumb’s resting, the pot-crack resting or any other keeping, and there was no payer standing under it. The ear-sentence came up to the word that would call the loan and stopped there --- a remembered ninth stopping with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end, the Seed 16 breath unspent at the top of the reach, the loan uncalled. He did not make a tenth of the stopping or a call of the loan, and he did not look along the road a house sends a man down when it wants a collector.

His stroke went over the shallow bright patch on \place{the flagstones} without the half-check. The returned half-check of Bone 8 stayed on the flagstone’s own place with the Day 161 fraction, the Day 166 beat and the Frost 5 beat, four held changes on four places and not a rule; neither the handful nor the thumb’s resting nor the pot-crack resting nor the held breath at the sill had been made a fifth. The warm loft stone lay cold in its unmarked pot, unreconciled by not trying, which is not forgiveness. Two ears had kept the same silence under one roof for years, his and the boy’s, a loan that could hear and had never been called; the equality had moved five times and none of the changes had stood up and called itself a rule. The five held objects with the pot-crack resting beside them and the hand-at-the-sill breath beside that stayed each on its own place with a finger between every pair, none made a sixth, a seventh, a habit or a rule, and no payer standing under any of them. He said nothing of it to the yard and the yard asked nothing of him.

\person{Jorra Vail} came through \place{the finger's-width door} off \place{the Vail wheel-shed} with the day’s meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bent little finger and white in the creases where a fist had opened, and it was not the bone-coloured \lore{slip} of the wheel. He kept his dusty forearms and his clean ones on one tray and parted them as a surgeon parts clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had been broken nor whether it was a birth-slate. The broken birth-slates under the lowest rack stayed entered by nobody. Men unnamed had gone low into \place{the back room} before first grey and he gave the house no name for them and did not say the room was cold.

He spoke to the wheel before he spoke to any person. “It doesn’t care whose hands. It cares if they’re steady,” he said, and told a pot “You’ll do” in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either. He knew the \lore{full note} that the pot would give three turns before it left his hands, with air under it and a want in it, and withheld the knowing from any \place{High Kiln} master who would call it early and make a song of it; the knowing stayed on the near-full tray beside the hearings and never among them. Sella had not finished telling him the fire was low when his bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper} ahead of her sentence, took the cold out of the fingertip, moved the tongue a hair’s breadth and was left standing, so that the end of her instruction arrived at the pot and the household straightened around the waiting the way cloth straightens when a weight is lifted. One piece of iron had two bites, the holding and the taking, with no \lore{Grathic} word for both mercies. \emph{“He’s not Vail”} stayed sealed as a lid nobody had tried to lift in three years, and the promise of the boy’s name to the men who burn slates was neither given nor withdrawn; the claim went on gathering interest against it.

The burned slate stood at five with a finger between the fifth and the four and no witness standing. The shut-room tellings stood at two and the row at five askings with no sixth, and the Ash 5 joining, the Ash 17 direction and the Ash 23 direction stayed held apart on places of their own with a finger between every pair, advanced and not paid. No voice came over the boards this afternoon to make a sixth telling, a third shut-room telling, a sixth asking, a fifth shaping, a witness, a name, a reader, a cause or a payment; the direction of the twenty-third stood where it had been put, and the fourth shaping and the Ash 6 turning of the writing stayed where they had been put, and no sixth was made due on a day that had not earned one. The far-off hand that would one day lay the district’s private keepings together was still far off and was not his.

At the going of the light she carried him up at the half-pace, wide around the seven places, with the instruction going to the wool and the river wind and never to the basket. She set him on \place{the bed} under the roof and never in \place{the Debt Loft}, put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face, let her hand rest the half-beat longer than the setting needed before it went away, and left the thread frozen where nobody had told anybody to wipe it. At \place{the hearth} she told the coals it was the thaw and turned them with the back of a broken pot, said “The house will keep it” where another woman would have said I, and stood a moment counting a small intake, a hold and a letting-go toward no opening, giving neither gap a figure. Her finger lay on top of \place{the loaf shelf} so the charcoal tally underneath stayed unread; her lips moved along a two-gapped row and figured neither gap; her eye went once to \place{the clay jar} standing cold under \lore{lead glaze} and her hand shut half over nothing, the little-door burn on the back of her left hand closing on air. At \place{the pot board} she lifted the twice-mended pot with her thumb first where the wire crossed the crack, said both proverbs to the pot and to the fire and to nobody else, and left the hairline of the original break showing between old-brass staple and tighter wire. Near the hearth she turned the dressed grain once under wool with fingers going in and coming out without closing, and set the pot rim two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat and no look toward the bed.

\lore{The widow’s mark} hung where it hung and was not touched. A going of the light and a bed are not openings; the hand goes to the mark before the kiln and at no other hour, and six openings in six months --- Tallow, Ember, Smoke, \place{Frost}, Bone and Ash --- are six and not a rule, with the sixth held behind at \place{the kiln mouth} on the second of Ash without a name and no seventh made due on a day that had not earned one. The mark answered nothing this evening for the crust at two inches, the empty place beside it, the fire’s missing fifth, the hand that had gone in on Seed 15 and not come out, or the woman carried out of the brook clay-heavy and uncollected; none of those stood under it asking to be answered, and she did not ask on their behalf.

He turned out the whole file as a surgeon turns out instruments on \place{the towel}, each thing in its place and nothing laid across anything else, checked and left as it lay. Six hearings of the full note stood beside and never among, the sixth the small flat knock out of a woman as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go and never among the sources, and the seventh place stayed empty and none was invented to fill it, for an empty place kept empty is a finding and a finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged. The heard bell --- four, four and a gap, heard at \place{the Cross} with nobody crossing himself --- stayed in its square beside the told bell of \lore{the Long Night}, not folded into the three counts and not heard as a seventh of the full note. The told figure stood beside the paid with no signature on the space between. Eight arithmetics of the year --- the three of Frost, the first, second and third of Bone, Ash’s first and Ash’s second --- stood behind one unsigned figure, eight and not one account; the figure read was still not the figure paid, both subtractions of Ash rode locked and unread up the clay road in \person{Berr Clay}’s box, and the finding sat on a place of its own with no payer and no doctrine standing up out of eight.

Nine record-first hands stood with no tenth: \person{Ilva}’s ninth unread in her sleeve and \person{Hessa Rale}’s eighth turned toward herself on the road, and Ilva was not under this roof this day and had not been the speaker of any voice. Nine stoppings stood with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end; the row stood at five askings, the burned slate at five, the shut-room tellings at two, and the Ash 5 joining, the Ash 17 direction and the Ash 23 direction lay each on a place of its own with a finger between every pair, advanced and not paid, none spent, named, joined, promoted into a witness, a sixth telling, a third shut-room telling, a sixth asking, a fifth shaping, a reader, a cause or a payment, for a rule is an account that has found a payer and none stood. The edges came near to touching --- near to the strip in the sleeve, to the turned writing, to the locked box, to the charcoal beneath the loaf shelf, to the covered grain, to the handful let fall at the rack end --- and the finger went between every pair. No voice came over the boards on the twenty-fourth. The finding that a house keeps by its \lore{not-doings} stood on a place of its own beside his own gaps with a finger between and was not laid across the absence of any man. The far-off hand that would one day lay the district’s private keepings together was still far off and was not his.

Under the floor the carried fire kept four and a gap; overhead \place{the boards} kept four nails on the near one, four on the next and a thumb-width knot where a fifth might have been driven and was not; through \place{the finger's-width door} \lore{water over water} went low over lower, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming, never numbered among the hearings, for a hearing wants a source standing and none stood. Three counts in three places, and none rose into another, because agreeing any two would have wanted a payer put under both and none stood to any. The bell’s gap stayed in its square and was not folded into any of them. Nothing in the day had answered the hand that went in on Seed 15, the man carried out on Day 188 with the cost entered nowhere, the dressed grain read over by a figure that did not know what it cost, the carried fire’s missing fifth, the word that had gone up \place{the terrace}, or the room shut with the damper closed. Eight readings had changed the district where two hundred people stood and could not be changed back, and had answered none of those, and no doctrine stood up out of them, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood.

Garl stayed in objects and not in a story: the salt-stiff coat with the left-shoulder crease on its peg behind the door, the hollow wheel-seat in the shed keeping its twenty-year hollow that nobody filled, the clean dark line at the Vail corner with its finer grey called the thaw unswept both, the twice-mended pot on the pot board with the hairline of the original break showing between old-brass staple and tighter wire, the unforged collector sentence lying beside the knife at the kindling sill, and the debt against a firing of tiles neither paid nor settled nor done with and worse for being uncalled. His name had not been spoken under this roof three hundred and fifty-three days and no lesson had been made of any of it; the keeping of him was maintenance and not a story. The held breath at the sill had belonged to a living man and was not laid across his objects. The surgeon took his small clean satisfaction in a field left as found and in every entry set down on its own place and not averaged into one account. It was a cold satisfaction and it cost him nothing, which was how he knew it was not the same as peace. The infant underneath took none of it and went on wanting the crust lifted the two inches to his mouth, the thread wiped and a hand on the pot handle. Both were true, the second was louder, neither won, and the two registers quarrelled without verdict in the ordinary going of the light on the twenty-fourth. He did not spend the eight readings on himself or make them a lesson, a grievance, a sign or a power. They waited.

In the small hours the hand outside the wool came up an inch toward the river’s note under the finger’s-width door, the way a hand goes up in a field it has learned not to disturb. It did not reach, and the inch was not a reach; he had long ago stopped calling it one. Most of what a hand does in a field is wait, and he had once taken a hand into a cavity slowly, with the whole of the attention, and knew the difference between the moving and the waiting. The note went water over water under the door, a low thing over a lower thing, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming, never numbered among the hearings, for a hearing wants a source standing and none stood. The hand took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole, let the intake go on the note’s own finishing, and came straight down and laid itself back against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. The note had done before the hand came down, so the letting-go was neither early again nor stayed, and the hand did not stay. It was the note’s own finishing that ended the interval and not any decision of the hand, and that was the whole of the honesty in it. The intake was not a breath kept for warmth, the interval not a stopping of the river, the note not his.

He filed it as answer two hundred and eighty-two: exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the eight arithmetics, the bell’s gap, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb’s resting, the held breath at the sill, the staying at the inch, the early letting-go, the sixth opening, the pot-crack resting, the Ash 5 joining, the Ash 17 direction, the Ash 23 direction, the word that stood in no doorway, the fourth shaping, the turned writing, the strip in the sleeve nor his own kept gaps. Two hundred and eighty-two honest answers stood on the sixth place, each an object and not a power, and not one stood up and called itself a doctrine, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood.

Three hundred and fifty-three stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody under the Vail roof; six months and one hundred and seventy-three days were told by nobody; the carried fire’s fifth was unpaid the same number with its payer unnamed; and the name of the man the salt-stiff coat kept on its peg was unspoken the same number. Nothing on the twenty-fourth had been opened, joined by a standing hand, named, called, paid or ended. No runner had been seen on any road and no collector had come up or down the clay road, and there was no market, no Cross and no bell. Ash kept its twenty-fourth day without a boundary drawn and neither of the district’s two habits of naming its months --- the older women by the weather, the slatemen by the number of Account-days --- was set right or left wrong in so many words; the month carried both names the way a pot carries two handles and is none the worse. Ash 30 stood six mornings ahead and was named only as a date, never as a promise, and was not brought into a day that had not earned it; no ninth arithmetic was supplied early, no seventh opening made due, no sixth hearing, sixth held object, sixth telling, third shut-room telling, sixth asking, fifth shaping, tenth hand or tenth stopping invented to make the row come out even. The keeping of the number was the only honest work there had been to do, bought nothing at two inches of air, was not for sale, and went on because attention is the one thing no institution can tax.

The hand lay back against the fold on answer two hundred and eighty-two; the file lay open on the towel at the head of the bed with every gap left gap; the twice-mended pot stood on the pot board with the hairline showing between old-brass staple and tighter wire; one stiff brown-edged crust lay two inches from his ear with its empty place unnamed and no third, the thread at his mouth frozen and unwiped; and through the finger’s-width door the river went water over water, low over lower, while the boards overhead kept four nails on the near one, four on the next and a thumb-width knot where a fifth might have been driven and was not. Ash 30 stood six mornings ahead and was named only as a date. Forward, not closed.
