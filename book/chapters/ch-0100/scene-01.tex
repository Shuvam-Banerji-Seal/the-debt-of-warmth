\daymark{351}{Ash 22, Secondday, Y.H. 813}

\dropcap{T}{he hand} outside the wool lay back against the fold as a man lays down a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. It had come up an inch a moment before, done its small work and gone, and now it rested with the backs of the fingers against the wool and the thumb a little apart, the way a hand rests after it has put a probe into a tray and let go. In the small hours under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof nothing had changed hands. Two hundred and seventy-six honest answers stood on the sixth place, each an object and not a power, and the two hundred and seventy-seventh had not yet been entered, because the note under the door had not yet finished. \person[Corin]{The boy} lay on \place{the bed} at the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf} and listened to a house keeping itself by its \lore{not-doings}. The room had its night sounds: coals settling under their ash, the wool breathing at his ear, a dry tick from cooling clay, and under all of them the low river. The large sounds were mostly weather, and the small ones did not lie, so he read the room as a trauma bay is read before the light, by the small sounds first. Overhead \place{the boards} kept four nails on the near board, four on the next and a thumb-width knot where a fifth might have been driven and was not. Under the floor \lore{the carried fire} kept four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer without a name. Through \place{the finger's-width door} the river went \lore{water over water}, a low thing over a lower thing, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming. Three counts in three places, and none rose into another, because agreeing any two would have wanted a payer put under both and none stood.

Nothing from the nineteenth, the twentieth or the twenty-first had crossed under this roof as sign, lesson, mending, cause or payment. The reading on the twentieth had bought no lesson here. Eight arithmetics of the year stood behind one unsigned figure --- the three of the first frost-month, the first, second and third of the hunting month, and the first two of the dark month --- and both of the dark month's subtractions rode locked and unread up \place{the clay road} in \person{Berr Clay}'s box, the key gone up in a coat pocket and not coming back down. One in twelve stood printed and unchallenged; the figure read was still not the figure paid, and no signature stood on the space between. Eight were eight and not one account. The finding sat on a place of its own with no payer under it and no doctrine standing up out of it, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood. \lore{The widow's mark} had not been touched since the sixth opening at \place{the kiln mouth} on the second of \place{Ash}; six openings in six months were six and not a rule, and no seventh was made due. The burned slate stood at five with a finger between the fifth and the four, the shut-room tellings at two, the row at five askings with no witness, the well-saying at four shapings, \person{Hessa Rale}'s writing still turned toward herself and unread, \person{Ilva}'s notched strip still in her sleeve with no tenth, the breath held at the knife-sill still where it had been set and not made again. Nine stoppings stood with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end, and the loan was uncalled. Five held objects lay on their own places, the pot-crack resting beside them and the hand-at-the-sill breath beside that, with a finger between every pair and no sixth. \place[Ash]{Ash 30} stood offstage as a date only, six mornings ahead, and he did not bring it into a day that had not earned it.

Three hundred and fifty had stood exact at the going of the light yesterday; three hundred and fifty-one stood exact now, in the small hours of the twenty-second, and told by nobody under this roof. Six months and one hundred and seventy days had become six months and one hundred and seventy-one. The carried fire's fifth was unpaid three hundred and fifty-one days and its payer had no name. The salt-stiff coat on its peg behind the door kept the broader shape of \person[Garl Vail]{a man} whose name had not been spoken under this roof three hundred and fifty-one days, and the debt against a firing of tiles circled without a name, neither paid nor settled nor done with and worse for being uncalled. Three hundred and fifty-one was not a round number and not a threshold. It was one more in a row nobody counted aloud. He had long ago stopped expecting to be told what a number counted, which was not the same as not wanting it.

The surgeon's register took the body in its old order, as a wound is taken in the order the layers lie. The crust came first: one stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust two inches from his ear, gone harder in the dry kiln heat, its corner standing a hair off the wool. Beside it the empty place kept its name to itself and no third had come in three days to make the row come out even. The thread of drool at his mouth had frozen in the dry heat and was unwiped, because nobody under any roof in \place{Low Kiln}, in \place{Sill}, in \place{Grath}, had ever been given an instruction about the thread. A basket set down was a pot set down and left. Cold had come cheek, temple, the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, and stopped there as though it had reached the end of a line written out for it, with neither an order to go farther nor an order to go away. \lore{The Afterdrop} waited beyond all three, past a warming nobody in this house would perform.

He knew the composed face that was not composure. He knew the grimace of deep cold that looks like a smile, and the pink that returns to a surface while the core is still cold; he knew a cold patient cannot clot and must be warmed from the outside and slowly, never hurried, never rubbed, never set too near the fire. He knew the order in which a body takes heat back, the small joint first and the core last, and the order in which it gives it up, the reverse, which comes sooner than anybody likes. He knew all of it, and he could not lift his own head.

Under the knowing the infant wanted, and the wanting was louder. He wanted the crust lifted the two inches to his mouth. He wanted the thread wiped. He wanted a hand that would close on the pot handle set two inches beyond the reaching fingers. Both were true, the second louder, neither won, and the argument between them was the work. The body humiliated him in its exact small ways: the drool frozen with no thumb to wipe it, the head useless on the fold, the crust leathery past the point any child would eat it, the pot handle two inches beyond the hand that would have closed on it in another life. He had reduced a thousand shoulders in the dark and he could not turn over. He had once held pressure in a cavity with both hands and now he could not keep a blanket on. The dry joke lived in that and cost him nothing and was not, in the end, funny.

Before first light \person{Sella Vail} came to \place{the hearth}, and the fire came before the basket. She did not look toward the bed. Her forearms were the colour of dried slip, and the grey streak at her temple showed where the lamplight touched it; she smelled of woodsmoke and wet wool, and under that the faint sour mineral tang of the Ash ridge along the skirting board, which she had not swept in two years and did not intend to sweep. Her hand went to the coals first. She told them it was \lore{the thaw} and turned them with the back of a broken pot. “The house will keep it,” she said, where another woman would have said I. The instruction went to the wool and the wind and the fire and never to the basket: “Keep it off his ears,” she told the wool; “it's the river wind,” to the wind. To the pot she gave another practical sentence and to the coals another, and to the thing in the wicker she gave nothing at all.

She stood a moment over the coals counting a small intake, a hold and a letting-go toward no opening, giving neither gap a figure. Her finger lay on top of the loaf shelf so the charcoal tally underneath stayed unread. Her lips moved along a two-gapped row and figured neither gap. Her eye went once to \place{the clay jar} standing cold under \lore{lead glaze} and her hand shut half over nothing, the little-door burn on the back of her left hand closing on air, the cord of her widow's mark oiled where a working thumb had touched it ten thousand times. At \place{the pot board} she lifted the twice-mended pot with her thumb first where the wire crossed the crack, and held the weight a half-beat before she let it come on the handle, the hairline of the original break showing between old-brass staple and tighter wire. She said both proverbs to the pot and to the fire and never to a person. “Leave the crack showing. People trust a thing they can see has been handled,” she told the pot. “If it's worth mending it's worth mending twice. The second time is the real one,” she told the fire, and neither saying was improved in the keeping and neither was wasted. She left the hairline showing, so that repair stayed apart from restoration, for a restored thing hides the hand that handled it and a mended thing leaves that hand showing. Near the hearth she turned the dressed grain once under wool with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child, her fingers going in and coming out without closing, no figure and no handful. Then she set the pot rim two inches beyond the reaching hand, with a half-beat and no look toward the bed. The half-beat was the distance between intention and arrest, bought at nothing and not a gift, for a gift wants a giver and nobody gave.

The boy took that setting apart by what was there and by what was not: a hand, a pot, a rim, a pause; no figure spoken, no look toward the bed, no instruction to the basket. He filed the pause where it belonged and made no more of it. A morning was not an opening and a hearth was not an opening, and the widow's mark hung where it hung and was not touched. The hand goes to the mark before the kiln and at no other hour; six openings in six months were six and not a rule, the sixth held behind at the kiln mouth on the second of Ash without a name and not spent, and no seventh was made due on a day that had not earned one. The mark answered nothing for the crust at two inches, the empty place beside it, the fire's missing fifth, the hand that had gone in on Seed 15 and not come out, or the woman carried out of the brook clay-heavy and uncollected. None of those stood under it asking to be answered, and she did not ask on their behalf.

He turned out the whole file as a surgeon turns out instruments on \place{the towel}, each thing in its place and nothing laid across anything else, checked and left as it lay. Six hearings of \lore{the full note} stood beside and never among, the sixth the small flat knock out of a woman as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go and never among the sources, and the seventh place stayed empty and none was invented to fill it, for an empty place kept empty is a finding and a finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged. The heard bell --- four, four and a gap, heard at \place{the Cross} with nobody crossing himself --- stayed in its square beside the told bell of \lore{the Long Night}, not folded into the three counts and not heard as a seventh of the full note. The told figure stood beside the paid with no signature on the space between. Nine record-first hands stood with no tenth; nine stoppings with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end; the row at five askings with one shaping and no sixth; the burned slate at five, the shut-room tellings at two, and the Ash 5 joining, the Ash 17 direction, the fourth shaping and the Ash 6 turning each on a place of its own with a finger between every pair. None was spent, named, joined, promoted into a witness, a sixth telling, a third telling of the shut room, a sixth asking, a fifth shaping, a hearing, a cause, a payment or a rule, for a rule is an account that has found a payer and none stood. The finding that a house keeps by its not-doings stood on a place of its own beside his own gaps with a finger between and was not laid across the absence of any man. The far-off hand that would one day lay the district's private keepings together was still far off and was not his. Nothing in the file had answered the hand that went in on Seed 15, the man carried out on Day 188 with the cost entered nowhere, the dressed grain read over by a figure that did not know what it cost, the fire's missing fifth, the word that had gone up the terrace, or the room shut with the damper closed. Eight readings had changed the district where two hundred people stood and could not be changed back, and had answered none of those, and no doctrine stood up out of them, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood.

In the small hours the hand outside the wool came up an inch toward the river's note under the finger's-width door, the way a hand goes up in a field it has learned not to disturb. It did not reach, and the inch was not a reach; he had long ago stopped calling it one. Most of what a hand does in a field is wait, and he had once taken a hand into a cavity slowly, with the whole of the attention, and knew the difference between the moving and the waiting. The note went water over water under the door, a low thing over a lower thing, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming, never numbered among the hearings, for a hearing wants a source standing and none stood. The hand took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole, let the intake go on the note's own finishing, and came straight down and laid itself back against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. The note had done before the hand came down, so the letting-go was neither early again nor stayed, and the hand did not stay. It was the note's own finishing that ended the interval and not any decision of the hand, and that was the whole of the honesty in it. The intake was not a breath kept for warmth, the interval not a stopping of the river, the note not his.

He filed it as answer two hundred and seventy-seven: exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the eight arithmetics, the bell's gap, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the held breath at the sill, the staying at the inch, the early letting-go, the sixth opening, the pot-crack resting, the Ash 5 joining, the Ash 17 direction, the word that stood in no doorway, the fourth shaping, the turned writing, the strip in the sleeve nor his own kept gaps. Two hundred and seventy-seven honest answers stood on the sixth place, each an object and not a power, and not one stood up and called itself a doctrine, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood.

The surgeon took his small clean satisfaction in a field taken as found and left as found, and it cost him nothing, which was how he knew it was not the same as peace. The infant underneath took none of it and went on wanting the crust lifted the two inches to his mouth, the thread wiped and a hand on the pot handle. Both were true, the second was louder, neither won, and the two registers quarrelled without verdict in the ordinary small hours of the twenty-second. He did not spend any of it on himself or make a lesson of it. Nothing on this day had yet been opened, joined by a standing hand, named, called, paid or ended.

Three hundred and fifty-one stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody under the Vail roof; six months and one hundred and seventy-one days were told by nobody; the carried fire's fifth was unpaid the same number with its payer unnamed; and the name of the man the salt-stiff coat kept on its peg was unspoken the same number. Ash 30 stood six mornings ahead and was named only as a date, never as a promise, and was not brought into a day that had not earned it; no ninth arithmetic was supplied early, no seventh opening made due, no sixth hearing and no sixth held object invented to make the row come out even. The keeping of the number was the only honest work there had been to do, bought nothing at two inches of air, not for sale, and went on because attention is the one thing no institution can tax.

The hand lay back against the fold on answer two hundred and seventy-seven; the file lay open on the towel at the head of the bed with every gap left gap; the twice-mended pot stood on the pot board with the hairline showing between old-brass staple and tighter wire; one stiff brown-edged crust lay two inches from his ear with its empty place unnamed and no third, the thread at his mouth frozen and unwiped; and through the finger's-width door the river went water over water, low over lower, while the boards overhead kept four nails on the near one, four on the next and a thumb-width knot where a fifth might have been driven and was not. Ash 30 stood six mornings ahead and was named only as a date. Forward, not closed.
