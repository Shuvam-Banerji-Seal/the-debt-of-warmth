## Scene 6 — What the Ash Kept

- **Day and time.** Day 353, Ash 24, Fourthday; afternoon through the going of the light and the small hours at the very end of the day. Chapter close. No new dated heading.
- **Place.** The Clay Yard's seven places, yard well and flat stone, rack end, kindling, flagstones, wheel-shed edge and damper; then the hearth, pot board, loaf shelf, bed, boards and towel.
- **Present.** Corin/Dan, Sella Vail, Tarn, Jorra Vail; Garl in objects. Walk-ons only: Cold Acre hare-carrier, cracked-jar man, woman with kindling, two fence-menders. No Ilva, no Berr, no Hessa, no green-mouthed woman or foundling sighting, no collector, no market, Cross or bell; **no ownerless voice on the twenty-fourth** — the direction of the twenty-third does not repeat.
- **Beats, in order.**
  1. The yard goes around its seven places with a look and a wide step and no number; three coverings stay weather entered nowhere with a finger between every pair and no fourth likeness made a cause; dressed grain covered and unfigured with no handful; hunts and repairs stay directions judged by the carrying arm and the weather. Tarn says *Yes, house* to early and late and keeps the late because it puts off the paying. No runner on any road, no collector up or down the clay road (T009/T013/T092, T083/T085, T077, T098).
  2. Tarn at the kindling: six in the worn thumb-mark where the house can check and the seventh at the lip beside Garl's unforged collector sentence, knife at the very edge of its sill, *Six counted. The next I don't count*; checks the Bone 26 thumb's resting and the Ash 14 held breath, finds each where it was put and does not make either again — not a sixth held object, not a seventh, not a tenth stopping, not the calling of the loan, with no payer standing; brings the ear-sentence to the word that would call the loan and stops as a remembered ninth stopping with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end, the Seed 16 breath unspent and the loan uncalled; stroke over the shallow bright patch without the half-check; the five held objects with the pot-crack resting beside them and the hand-at-the-sill breath beside that kept on their own places with a finger between every pair and none made a sixth, a seventh, a habit or a rule (T036/T076/T006).
  3.   4. The whole file turned out as instruments on a towel and checked and left as it lay: six hearings of the full note beside and never among with the seventh place empty (the sixth the small flat knock out of a woman as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go); the heard bell four, four and a gap in its square beside the told bell of the Long Night; the told figure beside the paid with no signature; **eight arithmetics of the year — three of Frost, first, second and third of Bone, Ash's first and second — behind one unsigned figure, eight are eight and not one account**, both Ash subtractions locked and unread up the clay road in Berr's box, the finding on a place of its own with no payer and no doctrine standing up out of eight; nine record-first hands with no tenth (Ilva's ninth, Hessa's eighth); nine stoppings with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end and the loan uncalled; the row at five askings, the burned slate at five, the shut-room tellings at two, and the Ash 5 joining, the Ash 17 direction and the Ash 23 direction held apart with a finger between every pair, advanced and not paid, none spent, named, joined, promoted into a witness, a sixth telling, a third shut-room telling, a sixth asking, a fifth shaping, a reader, a cause or a payment (T015/T041/T047, T061/T082, T078, T080, T035/T031/T065/T095 and T101 held). The far-off hand that will one day lay the district's private keepings together is still far off and is not his.
  5. Going of the light: Sella carries him up at the half-pace wide around the seven places, sets him on the bed under the roof and never in the Debt Loft, fold righted without a look at his face, hand resting the half-beat longer than the setting needed, thread left frozen; she tells the coals it is the thaw and *The house will keep it*, leaves the charcoal unread beneath her finger, lifts the twice-mended pot thumb-first where the wire crosses the crack with both proverbs to pot and fire and the hairline showing, and sets the pot rim two inches beyond the reaching hand. The widow's mark hangs where it hangs and is not touched — a going of the light and a bed are not openings, six openings in six months are six and not a rule, no seventh made due (T058/T079/T100).
  6. Small hours at the very end of the day: the hand outside the wool comes up an inch toward the river's note under the finger's-width door the way a hand goes up in a field it has learned not to disturb; it does not reach and the inch is not a reach; most of what a hand does in a field is wait. It takes a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, holds the interval whole on the note's own finishing, comes straight down and lays itself back against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. The note had done before the hand came down, so the letting-go is neither early again nor stayed and the hand does not stay; it is the note's own finishing that ends the interval, and that is the whole of the honesty in it. **Answer 282**: exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the eight arithmetics, the bell's gap, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the held breath at the sill, the staying at the inch, the early letting-go, the sixth opening, the pot-crack resting, the Ash 5 joining, the Ash 17 direction, the Ash 23 direction, the word that stood in no doorway, the fourth shaping, the turned writing, the strip in the sleeve nor his own kept gaps (T061/T082).
  7. **353 exact**, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody under the Vail roof; six months and one hundred and seventy-three days told by nobody; the carried fire's fifth unpaid the same number with its payer unnamed; Garl's name unspoken the same number. Nothing on the twenty-fourth has been opened, joined by a standing hand, named, called, paid or ended. Ash keeps its twenty-fourth day without a boundary drawn and neither of the district's two habits of naming its months — the older women by the weather, the slatemen by the number of Account-days — is set right or left wrong in so many words; the month carries both names the way a pot carries two handles and is none the worse. Ash 30 (Day 359) stands six mornings ahead and is named only as a date, never as a promise, and is not brought into a day that has not earned it; no ninth arithmetic is supplied early, no seventh opening made due, no sixth hearing, sixth held object, sixth telling, third shut-room telling, sixth asking, fifth shaping, tenth hand or tenth stopping invented to make a row come out even. Three counts in three places — fire four and a gap, boards four, four and a knot, river low over lower — do not become one, because agreeing any two would want a payer put under both and none stands; the bell's gap stays in its square and is not folded into any of them. The keeping of the number is the only honest work there has been to do, bought nothing at two inches of air, not for sale, and goes on because attention is the one thing no institution can tax.
