\daymark{230}{Grain 21, Y.H. 813 (Firstday)}

A day came under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof and nobody marked its coming. In the small hours the first smell was hot clay under tallow, and wet wool under that, and under the wool the sour mineral breath of the unswept corner the house called \lore{the thaw}; the first sound was the kiln drawing in fours with a gap, four low pulls and the silence where a fifth should have stood, and no bell went with it and no market opened, because a Firstday has no reading and the district's arithmetic could stand where it had stood. \person[Corin]{The boy} lay at the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf} in a body of two hundred and thirty days, six months and fifty days, and nobody under any roof had told him a number had been reached. He had stopped expecting to be told, which was not the same as not wanting it; the wanting went on under the expectation the way a coal goes on under ash.

The count stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in \place{Sarn}, \place{Sill} or \place{Grath}. Under the floor \lore{the carried fire} gave four low pulls and withheld the fifth, its payer without a name for two hundred and thirty days; through the finger's-width door the river kept its note of \lore{water over water}, a fifth low thing never counted among the hearings; overhead the boards kept four nails on the near board, four on the next, and a thumb-width knot where the fifth nail should have stood. Three counts in three places, and none rose into another, because a man who wanted them to agree would have to put a payer under all three and no payer stood to any of them.

One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from his ear. The place beside it kept its namelessness; no third had come to stand there and make the row come out even, and an empty place kept empty is a finding while a finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged. The thread of drool at his mouth had frozen in the dry heat and was unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. Cold had stopped at cheek and temple and the small bare joint at the wrist where the wool rode back, as if it had reached the end of an order written out for it; it went no farther and it did not go away, and the afterdrop waited on the far side of a warming nobody in this house would perform. He knew the afterdrop when he saw it and he wanted the crust. Both were true, and in a body two hundred and thirty days old the child's wanting was the louder.

Morning came, and with it the first ordinary tending after a reading. \person{Sella Vail} went to the fire before she went to the basket, the back first, the face withheld, the hand going where it had gone ten thousand times before. Every instruction in the room went to the wool, the fire, the pot, the broom, the running water and the river wind, and never to the basket. \emph{The house}, she said, where another woman would have said \emph{I}. She turned the coals with the back of a broken pot, told the fire it was the thaw, and stood a moment with the back of the pot in her hand counting under her breath toward no opening --- small intake, hold, letting go --- the count arriving nowhere as it had arrived nowhere on every day of this boy's life. “The house will keep it,” she said. She pushed a strand of coal-black hair back with the wrist that carried the burn, and the grey streak at her temple showed pale off the coals, and she did not discuss the streak or the winter it had come in.

The dressed grain lay under its fold near \place{the hearth}. She counted it in glances and handfuls and by the weight her carrying arm remembered, a record in muscle that knew weight and not cost; it had gone into the covering cut, beaten out, winnowed, spread thin and gathered back, and nothing in those postures had given it a figure. It had been read over yesterday by a number that did not know what it had cost, and it answered nothing for the hand that had gone in on Seed 15 and had not come back with the rest. A keeping is a keeping by being covered or by being spread, and the two postures of one grain do not make a number. The surgeon's register set it down where it belonged, a thing that had happened above him and not to him, and found it exact, complete and worth nothing at two paces.

In \place{the Clay Yard} the morning went around what it had gone around before. It went around the clean dark line where the ridge had turned and the new finer hairline of grey beside it called the thaw, neither swept; around the rack space beneath the lowest of \place{the drying racks} where the broken birth-slates lay; around the round ring where the shallow clay pan had stood, with no scrape, no crumb, no print, no chip and no word; around \place{the trough}, bare and dark, whose water was called weather; around the field's empty place out on \place{the worked strips}; and around the place in the returning line where a hand had gone in on Seed 15 and had not come back with the rest. Each got its look and its wide step and no number --- no name, no cause, no accident, no body, no figure and no payment. Seven walked-around things standing in a row are still seven and not an account until somebody finds a payer to stand under them, and none did. The absence went on gathering its interest on a place of its own, advanced and unpaid, and he did not lay it across the covered grain or the crust's empty place.

A wrong wind came up off \place{High Kiln} before the light was fully on \place{the flagstones}. It carried the sweet edge of \lore{lead glaze} over the boards, the sweetness that is the taste of a mouth gone wrong, and the edge rode over the fold, touched it and went out again on the wind that brought it --- a direction arrived nowhere and not a second edge paid. The seventh green stayed a colour with no eighth to stand behind it, and no name was spoken over the colour. A voice from off the terrace let one of the three shapings of the pot saying stand again over the boards: a pot gone quiet on every row come the thaw. The boy took the saying apart as a surgeon takes apart a wound --- what it said, where it had come from, what it asked, who had paid --- and set it back where it had stood, a saying and not a fourth likeness made a cause. No lesson came out of it. No name was given to what ailed the man carried out on Day 188, and no pot was lost in this yard. The voice went on about the weather and went off up the terrace, and the saying stood where it stood.

At the threshold a woman with a pot hitched against her hip asked what the storing would take now the grain was under wool. Sella did not turn from the coals. “Ask the pot what a wet thing costs set down there,” she said. “The house has said it.” The woman went away level, with nothing priced, nothing refused and nothing entered. A man come down \place{the clay road} stood a moment at \place{the yard well} with a number brought up out of a fist toward the water; he looked at it as a man looks at a thing he has not decided to keep, and put it back into his sleeve, so that the figure arrived nowhere. Cost questions went to the pot and the weather and away level; nothing was entered against the covered fold.

\scenebreak

With her back to the basket she moved her lips along the loaf row, and the row stood with its two unnamed gaps and she gave nobody a figure. Her eye went once to the clay jar cold under its glaze on the wall shelf, and her hand started toward it and shut half over nothing without a touch, the little-door burn closing on air the way it closes on everything that comes within reach of it. Her finger lay on top of the shelf's front edge where any woman in the district could see it lying, and the charcoal tally under the board stayed unread, uncounted and uncopied, because a woman's back turned is not the same thing as a finger beneath it and the reason stands between the two, unspoken. \lore{The widow's mark} hung where it hung on its cord at her throat, touched once before the kiln on Ember 1 and at no hour since, and there had been no opening in fifty days, because a morning and a shelf and a basket are not openings. She set the pot rim out with its handle two inches beyond the reaching hand and let her hand rest a half-beat longer at the setting than the setting required, and did not look toward \place{the bed}.

At \place{the kindling} \person{Tarn} took the broom only after it had been instructed, the instruction going to the tool before it went to the man, and he took it up as a man takes up an instrument already named. “Yes, house,” he said. To the wood he said, “Six counted. The next I don't count,” in the tone of a man stating the weather. He set the small knife at the very edge of its sill, six in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check and the seventh at the lip where it could not, the one entry in this house that the house could not check. Beside it lay the unforged collector sentence of \person{Garl Vail} --- a man sent for a collector who turned back at the frozen ford --- and Tarn gave no name to the man who had turned back, though he had been that man.

Then the ear-sentence came to his teeth as it had come on other mornings: an ear under this roof being worth more than the debt that held it. It went all the way to the word that would call the loan and stopped there, with no figure and no name; a remembered stopping and not a new one. He drew the breath he should have spent at the top of the reach and did not spend it, and the ninth stopping stood at nine with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end, and the seventh year at its very end had still not given. The loan was not called. No collector came up the clay road and no collector went down it on Grain 21; the district's arithmetic had changed twice in Grain and the road stood where it had stood, with the ruts holding what the ruts held. His chest had been tight since the dark; \lore{Ash} was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line and no scrubbing lifted it; the pale clip-ring at his left wrist held what light there was and gave it back. Above the shed, in \place{the Debt Loft} that reached him only as a sound with a direction, an unmarked clay pot held the warm stone Sella had given him in the winter Garl died, two years gone and cold, unreconciled with the debt by not trying, which is not forgiving.

He said “Yes, house” to those who wanted the thaw early and to those who wanted it late, agreeing so entirely that no argument could find a foot, and went on wanting the late because a late thaw puts off the paying, though the paying had stood in the ruts since the water went. His stroke went over the flagstones past the shallow bright patch without the half-check and over the road a house sends a man down when it wants a collector, over and over, without once turning along it. He did not ask about the room and nobody asked about the ear, and the two silences stayed unjoined under the one roof.

The boy kept the stopping where it lay, an object on a tray and not a rule. The ear under this roof was worth more than the debt, and the sentence had come to the word and stopped, and the stopping was an interval and not a payment, a thing the house had done and not a thing the house had said. It stood beside the other eight on their own places with a hand between it and every one of them, and no tenth stood up to call itself a doctrine. He did not join it to the nearly full answer under the shed wall or to the saying about a pot gone quiet on every row come the thaw, which was a saying and stayed a saying. Nine objects are nine objects and not an account until somebody finds a payer to stand under them, and no payer stood.

At the edge of \place{the Vail wheel-shed} \person{Jorra Vail} came through with the morning's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bent little finger and under the nails, white in the creases where a fist had opened, and not bone-coloured \lore{slip}. Three or more men had gone low into the finger's-width-open \place{the back room} before first grey and got no name from him, and he did not say the room was cold. He kept dusty forearms and clean ones on one tray and parted them as a surgeon parts clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had been broken nor whether it was a birth-slate; the broken birth-slates lay under the lowest rack where the yard went around them and entered them into nothing.

He spoke to the wheel before he spoke to anyone. “It doesn't care whose hands,” he said. “It cares if they're steady.” To a pot on the wheel he said, “You'll do,” in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either. He knew the \lore{full note} that pot would give three turns before it left his hands, and the knowing had air under it and a want in it, and he kept it back on the near-full tray beside the hearings and never among them. Three shapings of the pot saying stood on their own place and stayed three sayings; none was promoted into the hearings, no cause stood up, no lesson was made, and \lore{the burned-slate word} stood at a fourth telling, a direction come over the boards from \place{Slate Row} and arrived nowhere. The two tellings of the counting room shut in the war stayed two with no third, and he joined them neither to the dust nor to the pot nor to the men.

He grew quieter as the burned-slate word went around, and the household straightened around the quiet without an order, the way cloth straightens when a weight is lifted off one corner; the quiet went before him into the shed the way a cold air goes before a door. \emph{He's not Vail} stayed sealed as tightly as the hour it was spoken, and the promise of the boy's name to the men who burn slates was neither given nor withdrawn. When Sella's voice went low and unfinished at the hearth, his bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper} ahead of her sentence and stood there. The iron took the cold out of the fingertip and gave nothing back. He held until the sentence reached the pot, then moved the tongue a hair's breadth and left the finger standing. It was a remembered repetition, not a change made again, joined to no kiln and entered in no slate; one iron with two bites, the bite that holds a vessel whole and the bite that can take the hand off a man, with no word in \lore{Grathic} for both mercies. Three places were three places and not a rule with a payer standing to any of them.

\scenebreak

In the middle of the morning, with the wrong wind going off the terrace and the covered grain lying where it lay, he put the fifth question of the chapter to his hand. The conditions were the conditions that had sometimes answered: an inch of air between the hand and the note of water over water through the finger's-width door, a morning with no count anywhere in the house to borrow, and a voice over the boards that had gone off about the price of barley without asking the thing that stood at the edge of his notice. The hand went up an inch toward the note and came down at once. It did not stay for the small intake, did not wait through the hold, did not carry a breath counted in the room that does not touch his, did not keep the river's own low interval and come down on the next note. It came down at once, the way it had come down on other mornings under conditions that had once answered, and lay against the fold.

It was the fifth way of the chapter and the thirty-fifth answer, filed on the sixth place with no payer named to it --- not a longer reach, not a miss entered, not a hearing, not a seventh of the full note, not a tenth stopping, not a payment, not one of the three held changes, not a key, not a power, not a rule. Thirty-five answers stood on that place, each exact and worth nothing. The surgeon's register laid the whole of it down at two paces and asked the dark what it was for; the dark gave back the river's low note and nothing that could be entered.

The plain question stood at the edge of the chapter's notice and was not brought into the morning. It had no owner yet, no room, no hand at the kiln and no cause; it was a direction arrived nowhere, waiting for the hour when somebody would ask it over the boards. Grain 30, the third and last Account-day and market, stood nine mornings off and was not brought early into this day. The dressed grain lay under its covering with no figure over it, the returning-line place went around with its look and no number, six hearings stood beside and never among with no seventh, three sayings stayed three, four tellings stayed four and two tellings stayed two, nine stoppings stood at nine, and the count stood exact, complete and unspent and spoken by nobody.

A kiln-hand's voice went over the boards with the price of barley behind it, and the boy's hand came down at once under conditions that had once answered.
