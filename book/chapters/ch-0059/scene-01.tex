\daymark{228}{Grain 19, Ninthday, Y.H. 813}

\dropcap{H}{ot clay} came first under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof, hot clay under tallow and wet wool under that, and under all three the sour \lore{Ash} breath of the unswept corner the house called \lore{the thaw}. \person[Corin]{The boy} lay on \place{the bed} at the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf} with the hand outside the wool lying down again against the fold. It had come down in the small hours just gone on the river's next low note, after being carried through the small intake, the hold and the letting go of a breath counted in a room that does not touch this one, kept through the little silence after that count had ended; two rhythms held against each other across a wall, remembered, not a change made again. Then the hand lay down. He set the coordination down where a man sets a tool on a bench at the end of the work --- not watched, not named, not asked to stand up and say what it was. It was not a sign, not a growth, not a reaching, not a gift, not a power. The room that warmed him and the room he could not enter kept their old wall between them, and the fire went on in the one and the river in the other, and neither stopped for the ear under the roof.

He was two hundred and twenty-eight days old and six months and forty-eight days, and nobody said either number in his hearing or over his head; a house that kept its days in loaves and glances and the space at a row's end had no use for a figure belonging to a child it had taken in without a slate. The count had gone forward in the dark from two hundred and twenty-seven to two hundred and twenty-eight with no hand to enter it and no voice to say so, exact, complete, unspent. The day's first sound was not a bell. Behind the wall the kiln drew in four low pulls and stopped where the fifth should have stood, and the stopping stood where it stood, with nobody under any roof to put a name to what stood in it. Under the floor \lore{the carried fire} gave four low pulls and withheld the fifth, its payer unnamed for two hundred and twenty-eight days. Through the finger's-width door the river kept its note of \lore{water over water}, a fifth low thing never counted among the hearings; overhead the boards kept four nails on the near board, four on the next, and a thumb-width knot where the fifth nail should have stood. Fire under the floor, river through the door, nails overhead: three counts in three places, and none rose into another, because a man who wanted them to agree would have to put a payer under all three and no payer stood to any of them. He kept them on three trays and let the trays lie where they lay.

One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from his ear and kept the empty place beside it unnamed, and no third came to make the row come out even. An empty place kept empty is a finding; a finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged, and he had set that down on other days without its needing to be said again. The thread of drool at his mouth had frozen in the dry heat and was unwiped, because nobody under this roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread, and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. Cold had stopped at cheek and temple and the small bare joint at the wrist where the wool rode back, as if it had reached the end of an order written out for it; it went no further and it did not go away. The afterdrop waited on the far side of a warming nobody in this house would perform. He knew the order in which a body takes heat back, the small joint first and the core last, and he knew that a vessel which has cooled cannot clot and must not be rubbed too fast; neither knowing bought him the use of his own hands. He knew the afterdrop when he saw it. He wanted the crust. Both were true, and in a body of two hundred and twenty-eight days the wanting was the louder. He could lift his chin two inches and lift the crust two inches and put the one to the other, and he could do neither. Once he had reduced a shoulder in the dark by feel alone; now the whole reach of him was two inches of air and the fold's warm, yeasty smell.

\scenebreak

With the light he turned out the file as a man lays instruments on a towel --- no towel, no instruments, no case, but the order of the laying the whole of the discipline --- and found it exact, complete and worth nothing. Six hearings of \lore{the full note} stood beside and never among: five from four sources, a cup across a birth room, a pot in \place{the Vail wheel-shed} through a finger's width of door, the district's slate twice at \place{the Cross}, and the sixth, the small flat knock out of \person[Dessa Silt]{Dessa} as the men lifted her, from a person, filed where a thing out of a person must go and never among the sources. Each sat on its own square of the towel with its object beside it --- the cup's thin lip, the pot's side under the knuckle, the slate's upper line, the small flat sound out of a body lifted wet --- and no object had grown a handle that a child could close two inches on. No seventh had come in two hundred and twenty-eight days and none was invented to fill the empty place, for an empty place kept empty is a finding and a finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged. Three near-full answers stood on their own tray, advanced and unpaid: a pot under the shed wall that answered nearly full with air under the note and a want in it, a rack pot lifted a hand's breadth too high and set down again, and the knowing the wheel-keeper kept back from a man come up on a wrong wind. Beside them stood three shapings of the pot saying, all sayings and no rule: a pot that sings before its time mostly sings itself out early; the pot that sang early last month had not been heard from since; and a pot gone quiet on every row come the thaw, which wrong-wind voices had let stand again on the last morning. None had been promoted into the hearings, no cause stood up, no lesson was made, no name had been given to what ailed the man carried out on Day 188, no pot had been lost in this yard.

The burned-slate word stood on a place of its own with a hand between the three sayings and the two tellings of the counting room shut in the war, which stayed two and did not become three. The told bell lay beside the heard, four, four and a gap with nobody crossing himself; the told figure lay beside the paid, one in twelve read twice over a basket on Grain 10 and still not the figure paid, with the true subtraction locked in \person{Berr Clay}'s box going up toward \place{High Kiln} and \person{Hessa Rale}'s narrow slate gone up unread toward \place{Greybank}, the writing to her sleeve, and the soft-cuffed clerk still set at \place{the out-of-line landing} below \place{the north bridge} for a measure nobody called. Dessa's cost stood unentered and the cost of the man carried out on Day 188 stood where it had stood, entered nowhere. Grain 20 stood two mornings ahead with the second Account-day and the market and was not brought early into a day that had its own work. The nine stoppings stood where they had stood, on Reed 5, 8, 11, 14, 17, 20, 23, 26 and 29, as objects and not a rule, with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end and the seventh year at its very end and still ungiven. The Seed 16 breath was unspent and the loan uncalled. Three held changes stood on three places --- the Day 161 fraction at \lore{the damper}, the Day 166 beat beneath the shelf, the Tallow 22 half-check on \place{the flagstones} --- with the loft-sill scrape on a fourth, the carried man's pale line on a fifth and the held inch on a sixth. No payer was named to any of them, and nothing stood up out of them to call itself a reach, a hearing, a seventh, a stopping, a payment, a key, a power or a rule.

He put the question to his hand the first way of this chapter. It went up an inch toward the note of water over water through the finger's-width door and stayed a moment, then came down at once under conditions that had sometimes answered --- a plain coming-down, neither held past the intake nor carried through the counted breath, only the inch and the letting-go. Not a longer reach, not a miss entered, not a hearing, not a seventh of the full note, not a tenth stopping, not a payment, not one of the three held changes, not a key, not a power, not a rule. He filed it on the sixth place with no payer named and did not spend it and did not explain it. Exactness and humiliation are one motion in a small hand and both are true and neither is a sign; once he had reduced a shoulder in the dark by feel alone and now he could not close the two inches to the pot handle or lift a crust to his mouth or speak a number that anybody under any roof would be asked to believe. Thirty-one answers stood on the sixth place, each an object and not a power. The hand lay down again against the fold.

\scenebreak

\person{Sella Vail} came to the fire before the basket. She gave the instruction to the wool and the river wind and the pot and the broom and the running water and the fire, and to no basket: \emph{Keep it off his ears. It's the river wind.} It was the same instruction she had given on the first morning and had not varied in two hundred and twenty-eight days, and she said \emph{the house} where another woman would have said \emph{I}. At \place{the loaf shelf} she stood with her back to the basket and moved her lips along the row the way a woman moves her lips over the days of a week without saying them. The row stood with two gaps: the old space at the end and the palm's breadth that had come in years back without a scrape, a crumb, a print or a word. She gave nobody a figure. When her hands had nothing to do they counted: spoons, nails, the breaths of a sleeping child, and here the row's two gaps took the place of spoons. Her eye went once to the clay jar cold under its glaze on the wall shelf, and her hand started toward it, the little-door burn opening, and shut half over nothing without a touch, the burn closing on air. Her finger lay on top of the shelf's front edge where any woman in the district could see it lying, and the charcoal tally beneath the board stayed unread, uncounted and uncopied, because a woman's back turned is not the same thing as a finger beneath it and the reason stood between the two, unspoken.

The dressed grain lay under its covering near \place{the hearth}, dressed and stored, and the work of it was finished three days gone. It had come off the strips as cut stalks, had gone under wool as a bundle, had been beaten out on the flagstones and winnowed on the river wind, had lain thin to finish drying, and now lay covered again with the fold set flat; three changes of state and no figure over it anywhere. He took it apart in the order he took everything within two paces: what it was, grain; where it had stood, in \place{the worked strips} east of \place{Low Kiln}; who had paid a figure for it, nobody under any roof; what it asked of him, nothing. The carrying arm was a record in muscle that remembered the weight and not what the grain would have cost to buy back, and the yard had counted it in glances and handfuls and in the space a hand's scoop left in the fold, and no slate had been opened and no hand entered it. When a woman at the door asked what the storing would take now the grain was under wool, Sella sent the question to the pot and the weather: \emph{Ask the pot what a wet thing costs set down there. The house has said it}, and carried the answer away level, nothing priced, nothing refused, nothing entered. Three changes of posture make no number, and a keeping is a keeping by being covered or by being spread, and the two postures of one grain do not add to a figure. That the grain had been out of its covering and back again three times and was not a figure still stood, and the surgeon's register set the finding down without triumph: the bundle had been a keeping, the spread grain a keeping, the covered grain a keeping, and keeping is not the same act as counting and has no figure to enter.

The yard would go around its places as it had gone around them every morning since the grain came in. It would go around the clean dark line where the Ash ridge had turned and the finer hairline of grey beside it that the house called the thaw, neither swept; around the rack space beneath \place[the drying racks]{the lowest rack} with its broken birth-slates; around the round ring where the shallow clay pan had stood; around the bare dark \place{the trough} whose water was called \lore{weather}; around the field's empty place; and around the place in the returning line where a hand had gone in on Seed 15 and had not come out with the rest. Each would get its look and its wide step and no number: no name, no cause, no accident, no body, no identification of which hand, no figure and no payment. Seven walked-around things standing in a row are still seven and not an account until a payer stands under them, and none did. The absence gathered interest the way a pot gathers heat, by being left where it was, and he kept it on the place where it lay and did not lay it across the dressed grain or the shelf's two gaps or the pan's gone ring. The dressed grain and the returning-line place were two keepings, and he drew no finger between them. Grain 20 stood two mornings ahead and was not brought early into a day that had its own keeping to do.

At the hearth she told the fire it was the thaw and turned the coals with the back of a broken pot. \emph{The house will keep it}, she said, where another woman would have said \emph{I}, and stood a moment with the back of the pot in her hand counting under her breath toward no opening --- small intake, hold, letting go --- the count arriving nowhere as it had arrived nowhere on every day of this boy's life. She pushed a strand of coal-black hair back with the wrist that carried the burn and the grey streak at her temple showed pale off the coals, and she did not discuss the streak or the winter it had come in. Then she set the pot rim out with its handle two inches beyond the reaching hand and let her hand rest a half-beat longer at the setting than the setting required, and did not look toward \place{the bed}. He lay two inches short of the handle and watched the half-beat go by as a man watches a hand come out of a field before the dressing goes on: a thing done and a thing withheld in the same motion, an interval and an interval kept, not a promise, not a key, not an opening. The fire went on in the room that warmed him and the river in the room he could not enter, and the day's ordinary work began over the covered grain with no figure anywhere under the roof.
