## Pull into Chapter 60
The final moment hands the new question forward rather than closing it. Chapter 60 opens on Day 231, Grain 22, Secondday, with the count at **230** exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody, the second Account-day's changed arithmetic standing unread under the Vail roof, and the Grain 21 question (T101) on its new place as a direction arrived nowhere with no owner, room, hand at the kiln or cause. The hand lies down again after the thirty-sixth answer; the three pot sayings, four burned-slate tellings and two shut-room tellings hold at their old counts; the ninth stopping stands at nine before the seventh year at its very end; the dressed grain stays covered and unfigured and the returning-line place goes around with a look and no number. Every engine keeps turning toward Grain 30 (Day 239), the third and last Account-day and market. Forward, not closed.
