Back under the roof the day went on doing what it had done before the market, thinning without ending. The light lost its edge against the floorboards and lay there shallow as water on a flat stone, going nowhere, and the heat that had stood all afternoon in the yard came down and settled under the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf}, behind the pot, into the fold where \person[Corin]{the boy} lay with his ear two inches from a stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust. The place beside it kept its namelessness. No third had come to stand there and make the row come out even, and an empty place kept empty is a finding while a finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged; he had set that down on other days and did not need to set it down again. The thread of drool at his mouth had frozen in the dry heat and was unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. Cold had stopped at cheek and temple and the small bare joint at the wrist where the wool rode back, as if it had reached the end of an order written out for it; it went no farther and it did not go away, and the afterdrop waited on the far side of a warming nobody in this house would perform. He knew the afterdrop when he saw it and he wanted the crust. Both were true, and in a body of two hundred and twenty-nine days the child's wanting was the louder.

The district's arithmetic had changed for the second time in Grain where two hundred people stood, and it could not be changed back, and under this roof it went into no slate. He kept the figure read over the basket on one tray and the dressed grain lying covered near \place{the hearth} on another, with a hand's breadth of air between them that no finger of his drew across. One in twelve had been read twice over a basket and was still not the figure paid; the true subtraction had gone up the clay road in a locked box and answered nothing for the man carried out on Day 188, and the reading had not reached back under the roof to touch the hand that had gone in on Seed 15 and had not come back with the rest. The dressed grain had changed state three times since the strips and lay now under its fold with no figure over it, counted in glances and handfuls and by the weight a carrying arm remembered, entered nowhere. A keeping is a keeping by being covered or by being spread, and the two postures of one grain do not make a number. The surgeon's register set the morning where it belonged, a thing that had happened above him and not to him, and found it exact, complete and worth nothing at two paces.

\person{Sella Vail} came to the fire before she came to the basket, the way she came to everything that wanted keeping: the back first, the face withheld, the hand going where it had gone ten thousand times before. Every instruction in the room went to the wool, the fire, the pot, the broom, the running water and the river wind, and never to the basket. \emph{The house}, she said, where another woman would have said \emph{I}, and told the fire it was \lore{the thaw}. She turned the coals with the back of a broken pot and the fire answered four low pulls and withheld the fifth, and the space where the fifth should have stood had gone on being a space for two hundred and twenty-nine days with no payer named to it. “The house will keep it,” she said, and stood a moment with the back of the pot in her hand counting under her breath toward no opening --- small intake, hold, letting go --- the count arriving nowhere as it had arrived nowhere on every day of this boy's life. She pushed a strand of coal-black hair back with the wrist that carried the burn, and the grey streak at her temple showed pale off the coals, and she did not discuss the streak or the winter it had come in. The little-door burn opened on the pot and shut on nothing when the pot was set down.

A neighbour come to the threshold with a pot hitched against her hip asked what the storing would take now the grain was under wool. Sella did not turn from the coals. “Ask the pot what a wet thing costs set down there,” she said. “The house has said it.” The woman went away level, with nothing priced, nothing refused and nothing entered, carrying the question out into the yard as a man carries a pail he has decided not to set down. A man come down \place{the clay road} stood a moment at \place{the yard well} with a number brought up out of a fist toward the water; he looked at it as a man looks at a thing he has not decided to keep, and put it back into his sleeve, so that the figure arrived nowhere. Nothing was priced by it, nothing bought, nothing entered against the dressed grain; a number that goes from a fist to a sleeve is a number returned to the place it came from, and he filed it on its own place and did not lay it across the covered fold or the empty place at his ear.

With her back to the basket she moved her lips along the loaf row, and the row stood with two unnamed gaps and she gave nobody a figure. Her eye went once to the clay jar cold under its glaze on the wall shelf and her hand started toward it and shut half over nothing without a touch, the little-door burn closing on air the way it closed on everything that came within reach of it. Her finger lay on top of the shelf's front edge where any woman in the district could see it lying, and the charcoal tally under the board stayed unread, uncounted and uncopied, because a woman's back turned was not the same thing as a finger beneath it and the reason stood between the two, unspoken. \lore{The widow's mark} hung where it hung on its cord at her throat, touched once before the kiln on Ember 1 and at no hour since, and there had been no opening in forty-nine days, because an afternoon and a shelf and a basket are not openings. She set the pot rim out with its handle two inches beyond the reaching hand and let her hand rest a half-beat longer at the setting than the setting required, and did not look toward the bed.

At \place{the kindling} \person{Tarn} took the broom only after it had been instructed, the instruction going to the tool before it went to the man, and he took it up as a man takes up an instrument already named. “Yes, house,” he said. To the wood he said, “Six counted. The next I don't count,” in the tone of a man stating the weather. He set the small knife at the very edge of its sill, six in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check and the seventh at the lip where it could not, the one entry in this house that the house could not check. Beside it lay the unforged collector sentence --- a man sent for a collector who turned back at the frozen ford --- and Tarn gave no name to the man who had turned back, though he had been that man, and his own name was not spoken with the sentence either.

Then the ear-sentence came to his teeth as it had come on other afternoons: an ear under this roof being worth more than the debt that held it. It went all the way to the word that would call the loan and stopped there, with no figure and no name; a remembered stopping and not a new one. He drew the breath he should have spent at the top of the reach and did not spend it, and the ninth stopping stood at nine with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end, and the seventh year at its very end had still not given. The loan was not called. No collector came up the clay road and no collector went down it on Grain 20; the district's arithmetic had changed twice in Grain and the road stood where it had stood, with the ruts holding what the ruts held. His chest had been tight since the dark; \lore{Ash} was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line where the ridge had turned, and no scrubbing lifted it; the pale clip-ring at his left wrist held what light there was and gave it back. Above the shed, in \place{the Debt Loft} that reached him only as a sound with a direction, an unmarked clay pot held the warm stone Sella had given him in the winter \person{Garl Vail} died, two years gone and cold, unreconciled with the debt by not trying, which is not forgiving.

He said “Yes, house” to those who wanted the thaw early and to those who wanted it late, agreeing so entirely that no argument could find a foot, and went on wanting the late because a late thaw puts off the paying, though the paying had stood in the ruts since the water went. His stroke went over \place{the flagstones} past the shallow bright patch without the half-check and over the road a house sends a man down when it wants a collector, over and over, without once turning along it. He did not ask about the room and nobody asked about the ear, and the two silences stayed unjoined under the one roof.

The boy kept the stopping where it lay, an object on a tray and not a rule. The ear under this roof was worth more than the debt, and the sentence had come to the word and stopped, and the stopping was an interval and not a payment, a thing the house had done and not a thing the house had said. It stood beside the other eight on their own places with a hand between it and every one of them, and no tenth stood up to join them and call itself a doctrine. He did not join it to the two ears keeping the same silence, or to the nearly full answer under the shed wall, or to the saying about a pot gone quiet on every row come the thaw, which was a saying and stayed a saying. Nine objects are nine objects and not an account until somebody finds a payer to stand under them, and no payer stood. The loan that could hear stayed uncalled, and something must give at the end of the seventh year, and it had not given here.

At the edge of \place{the Vail wheel-shed} \person{Jorra Vail} came through with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bent little finger and under the nails, white in the creases where a fist had opened, and not bone-coloured \lore{slip}. Three or more men had gone low into the finger's-width-open \place{the back room} before first grey and got no name from him, and he did not say the room was cold. He kept dusty forearms and clean ones on one tray and parted them as a surgeon parts clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had been broken nor whether it was a birth-slate; the broken birth-slates lay under \place[the drying racks]{the lowest rack} where the yard went around them and entered them into nothing.

He spoke to the wheel before he spoke to anyone. “It doesn't care whose hands,” he said. “It cares if they're steady.” To a pot on the wheel he said, “You'll do,” in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either. He knew the \lore{full note} that pot would give three turns before it left his hands, and the knowing had air under it and a want in it, and he kept it back on the near-full tray beside the hearings and never among them. Three shapings of the pot saying stood on their own place and stayed three sayings about a pot: a pot that sings before its time mostly sings itself out early; the pot that sang early last month had not been heard from since; a pot gone quiet on every row come the thaw. None was promoted into the hearings, no cause stood up, no lesson was made, no name was given to what ailed the man carried out on Day 188, and no pot was lost in this yard. \lore{The burned-slate word} stood at a fourth telling, a direction come over the boards from \place{Slate Row} and arrived nowhere, and the two tellings of the counting room shut in the war stayed two with no third, and he did not join them to the dust or the pot or the men. The seventh green stayed a colour and the seventh shape a shape where they had been laid, gathered and unjoined, and no eighth of either came to stand beside them in the shed.

He grew quieter as the burned-slate word went around, and the household straightened around the quiet without an order, the way cloth straightens when a weight is lifted off one corner; the quiet went before him into the shed the way a cold air goes before a door. \emph{He's not Vail} stayed sealed as tightly as the hour it was spoken, and the promise of the boy's name to the men who burn slates was neither given nor withdrawn. He did not look toward the bed and did not ask about the ear, and Tarn did not ask about the room.

When Sella's voice went low and unfinished at the hearth, his bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper} ahead of her sentence and stood there. The iron took the cold out of the fingertip and gave nothing back. He held until the sentence reached the pot, then moved the tongue a hair's breadth and left the finger standing. It was a remembered repetition, not a change made again, joined to no kiln and entered in no slate; the boy read it as he had read it before, one iron with two bites, the bite that holds a vessel whole and the bite that can take the hand off a man, with no word in \lore{Grathic} for both mercies. He did not pair it with the fraction held on an older day or the beat beneath the shelf or the half-check on the flagstones. Three places were three places and not a rule with a payer standing to any of them.

At the going of the light she lifted the basket level and carried him up the clay road at the half-pace from every open slate, woodsmoke before her in long low bars and river wind behind with the taste of \lore{water over water}. The carrying arm was a record in muscle that knew weight and not cost, and it had carried him up and down this yard for two hundred and twenty-nine days. In \place{the Clay Yard} she went wide around the clean dark line where the ridge had turned and the new finer hairline of grey beside it called the thaw, neither swept; around the rack space beneath the lowest rack with its broken birth-slates; around the round ring where the shallow clay pan had stood, with no scrape, no crumb, no print, no chip and no word; around the bare dark \place{the trough} whose water was called weather; around the field's empty place; and around the place in the returning line where a hand had gone in on Seed 15 and had not come back with the rest. Each got its look and its wide step and no number --- no name, no cause, no accident, no body, no figure and no payment --- and seven walked-around things standing in a row are still seven and not an account until a payer stands under them, and none did. The absence went on gathering its interest in a place of its own, advanced and unpaid, and he did not lay it across the covered grain or the figure read over the basket or the crust's empty place. Grain 21 stood one morning ahead with its ordinary tending and its boards, and nothing that morning held was brought early into this yard.

She set him on his bed under the roof and not in the Debt Loft, put the fold right without a look at his face and left the thread frozen and unwiped, because a basket set down is a pot set down and left. The dressed grain stayed covered near the hearth with its figure nowhere, read over in the square by a number that did not know what it had cost and entered on no slate under this roof. From above came the small sound of the loft settling, a direction and nothing more, and the warm stone in its unmarked pot stayed cold.

In the small hours there was no breathing number anywhere in the house. Sella had gone to her own bed before the dark was old and left the coals to keep themselves; there was nothing in his room to hold a rhythm against, and he borrowed none from the room that did not touch his. Through the finger's-width door the river kept its note of water over water, a fifth low thing never counted among the hearings; under the floor \lore{the carried fire} gave four low pulls and withheld the fifth, its payer unnamed for two hundred and twenty-nine days; overhead the boards kept four nails on the near board, four on the next, and a thumb-width knot where the fifth nail should have stood. Three counts in three places, and none rose into another, because a man who wanted them to agree would have to put a payer under all three and no payer stood to any of them.

He turned out the file as a man lays instruments on a towel --- no towel, no instruments and no case, but the order of the laying the whole of the discipline --- and found it exact, complete and worth nothing. Six hearings of the full note stood beside and never among: five from four sources, a cup across a birth room, a pot in the wheel-shed through a finger's width of door, the district's slate twice at the moot, and the sixth, the small flat knock out of a person as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go and never among the sources. No seventh had come in two hundred and twenty-nine days and none would be invented to fill the empty place. Three near-full answers stood on their own tray, advanced and unpaid; three shapings of the pot saying stood as sayings and no rule, and a fourth likeness had not stood up among them to call itself a cause; the burned-slate word stood at a fourth telling beside two tellings of the counting room shut in the war, with a hand between; the told bell lay beside the heard, four, four and a gap with nobody crossing himself; the told figure lay beside the paid, read twice over a basket and still not the figure paid, with the true subtraction gone up the road in a locked box and answering nothing. Nine stoppings stood with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end, which had come and had still not given; the Seed 16 breath was unspent and the loan uncalled. Three held changes stood on three places, the loft-sill scrape on a fourth, the pale line on a fifth and the held inch on a sixth with thirty-three answers on it, each filed on a place of its own and none standing up out of its place to call itself a payer. Nothing had been joined, named, opened, called, spent or ended.

He put the fourth question of the chapter to his hand. There was no breathing number anywhere in the house to hold it against --- no count at the fire in the room that did not touch his, no count in the yard, no count of his own to borrow and call a measure. The conditions were the river's own keeping: the note of water over water going low through the finger's-width door with nobody in the district counting it, and the space between one low note and the next standing open with nothing in it to make a row come out even. The hand went up an inch toward that note and stayed; he kept it there for the length of the river's own low interval, with no small intake to carry it, no counted breath to borrow, no hold of anybody's to stand under it, and brought it down on the next low note. It was the fourth way of the chapter: not down at once under conditions that had sometimes answered, not held to the small intake only and down before the letting-go, not down before the counted breath with the hold still ahead, not carried through a breath counted in another room and kept past the end of it. It went on the sixth place as the thirty-fourth answer, an object with no payer named to it --- not a longer reach, not a miss entered, not a hearing, not a seventh of the full note, not a tenth stopping, not a payment, not one of the three held changes, not a key, not a power, not a rule. Thirty-four answers stood on that place, each exact and worth nothing, and the surgeon's register laid the whole of it down at two paces and asked the dark what it was for; the dark gave back the river's low note and nothing that could be entered.

The hand went down on the river's next low note with no count in the house to hold it against, and the dressed grain lay covered with no figure over it.
