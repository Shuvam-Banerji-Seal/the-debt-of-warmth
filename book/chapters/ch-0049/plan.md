## The chapter's one meaningful change

Ember's **second Account-day** is read over the basket on Day 199. Berr Clay comes up short off the clay road, red office cord folded under his coat, glaze green at one mouth-corner, wipes his thumb twice before touching nothing, passes a finger over a narrow slate held unopened to his forearm, reads *One in twelve, as the notice says* low into the top of the crowd — the figure going twice over the basket, once for the moot and once for the households that come late — calls the levy and denies it with *The levy is called. It is not called by me* and *your house*, never *I*, and goes off with the box locked and the true subtraction inside, believing he has done a kindness. The district's arithmetic for the kiln month changes for the second time and cannot be changed back; the printed figure stands unchallenged and a figure read aloud is still not the figure paid. Hessa carries the slate up the road unread; the clerk stays set for an uncalled measure.

Crucially this reading **does not answer** the man carried out on Day 188: no figure, collector or slate knows what he cost, no collector is called, no cause is named in Grathic and the pale line stays on its own place. This change is advanced and **not paid**: it settles no long thread. The hand's inch is asked again on all three days and held three ways (down before the breath finished, down at once, and held through the whole breath and into the letting go at the close) and stays a physical first filed on a sixth place of its own — never a hearing, seventh, stopping, payment, key, power or rule. Forward, not closed.

## Scene 1 — “The Hand before the Reading”

- **Day and time.** Day 198, Ember 19, Ninthday. Small hours under the roof, then before first grey and into the morning.
- **Place.** Under the Vail kiln-house roof: the bed, the hearth, the loaf shelf's low angle; then out into the Clay Yard, the yard well and the clay road's edge.
- **Present.** Corin/Dan; Sella; a neighbour at the threshold with dry scurf of mud on her hem (walk-on). No Berr, Hessa, clerk, green woman or foundling today.
- **Beats, in order.**
  1. **No recap.** Open directly, in the small hours, on the hand outside the wool going up an inch toward the note of water over water through the finger's-width door and coming down. Do not re-read Chapter 48's longest holding as a sign or a promise. The count at one hundred and ninety-seven is carried into the new morning, exact, complete and unspent (T071/T072); he is one hundred and ninety-eight days old and six months and eighteen days, and nobody says so. He has stopped expecting to be told, which is not the same as not wanting it.
  2. **First things.** First smell hot clay under tallow and wet wool with the sour mineral breath of the unswept corner; first sound the kiln drawing in fours with a gap, not a bell. The one brown-edged crust lies leathery two inches from his ear with its empty place unnamed and no third; the thread of drool at his mouth is frozen and unwiped, because nobody under any roof has been given an instruction about the thread; cold has stopped at cheek, temple and the small bare joint at the wrist where the wool rides back, and the afterdrop waits on a warming nobody in this house will perform.
  3. **Three counts.** The carried fire under the floor gives four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have been, its payer unnamed for a hundred and ninety-eight days (T088); through the finger's-width door the river keeps its note of water over water, a fifth low thing never counted among the hearings; overhead the boards keep four nails, four, and a knot the width of a thumb. Three counts in three places that do not become one, because a man who wanted them to agree would have to put a payer under them and no payer stands to any (T061/T082).
  4. **Sella's morning.** Instruction to the wool, fire, pot, broom and running water, never the basket; *the house*; half-pace from every open slate; one glance at the clay jar cold under its glaze with the hand starting toward it and shutting half over nothing without a touch; coal-black hair pushed back with the burn-wrist so the grey streak shows pale off the coals; pot handle set two inches beyond the reaching hand with her hand resting a half-beat longer at the setting; gruel from the house's own dish, spoon on her sleeve and not his mouth; fire told it is the thaw and *The house will keep it* (T058/T079). Widow's mark untouched — a morning and a shelf are not openings, and there has been no opening since before the kiln on Ember 1.
  5. **The cost-question.** A neighbour at the threshold with dry mud-scurf on her hem asks what the firing will want now the second reading stands a day off. Sella sends the question to the pot and the weather; it goes away level, nothing priced, refused or entered (T077). The figure up the road in Berr's locked box stays offstage; the printed one in twelve unchallenged, the true subtraction hidden (T015/T041/T047 held). The sweet edge of lead glaze comes up off High Kiln on a wrong wind and is answered as a direction, not a second edge paid (T064 held).
  6. **The asking.** Back under the roof, at the note through the door, the hand goes up an inch and is asked to stay. It comes down before the counted breath has finished — a shorter holding, the body keeping its own time, an inch let go early no more a failure than an inch that stays. Filed on a place of its own beside the others: not a hearing, not a seventh, not a stopping, not a payment, not one of the three held changes, not a key, not a power and not a rule; no payer named, nothing promoted (T061/T082). He still cannot close the two inches, lift the crust or speak a number.
  7. **Forward.** Ember's second Account-day and market stand one day off on Day 199. The market does not get to read the inch. No boundary drawn; no recap of the reading to come.
- **Emotional turn.** From the old humiliating order — carried, fed, wiped by sleeve, temperature stopping at the wrist — to the small unbought fact that the inch answered when asked, and then answered differently. The body humiliates him and moves by an inch in the same motion; both are true. No triumph: the holding is worth nothing and nobody sees it.
- **Sensory anchors.** Hot clay under tallow; wet wool; sour Ash breath; frozen thread of drool; the fifth low note of water over water; kiln's four-and-a-gap; four pulls and a missing fifth under the floor; four nails, four and a thumb-width knot overhead; dry mud-scurf on a hem; sweet edge on a wrong wind; half-beat at the setting; spoon on sleeve, not mouth.
- **Threads:** T071/T072, T088, T061/T082, T058/T079, T077, T015/T041/T047 held, T064 held, T004.
- **Foreshadowing planted / paid off.** The day-off phrase plants Day 199's reading without putting it on stage; the pot handle two inches beyond the hand plants every “cannot close two inches” to come; the hand's shorter holding plants the miss of Scene 4 and the long holding of Scene 6 without making a progression. Nothing paid.
- **Exact last image.** The hand outside the wool comes down before the breath is done, and the fold takes it without a word; one hundred and ninety-eight stands exact in the dark, spoken by nobody.

## Scene 2 — “The Figure Kept Out of the File”

- **Day and time.** Day 198, Ember 19, Ninthday. Afternoon into the small hours.
- **Place.** The loaf shelf's low angle, the hearth, the kindling, the wheel-shed edge, the clay road at the going of the light, the bed.
- **Present.** Corin/Dan; Sella; Tarn; Jorra. Three or four men go into the back room before first grey of the *next* morning only — offstage here; this afternoon keeps dust and damper without a new telling.
- **Beats, in order.**
  1. The afternoon thins without ending; heat goes off the high air first and stays longest under the shelf, behind the pot, in the fold of wool. The underside of the shelf makes a roof that stops at the edge of his reach, and the heat comes down that roof in a slow slant and stops at the same place, as if it too had been given an instruction and come to the end of it.
  2. Sella at the shelf, back to the basket: lips along a row with two unnamed gaps (the old space at the row's end and the palm's breadth from the night of Seed 6), no figure; one glance at the clay jar, hand shutting half over nothing; finger kept from beneath the front edge so the charcoal tally stays unread, uncounted, uncopied, and the Day 166 held beat stays where it was left (T100).
  3. At the hearth: told the thaw, coals turned with the back of a broken pot, *The house will keep it*; stands with the back of the pot counting under her breath toward no opening — small intake, hold, letting go; pot rim set out with handle two inches beyond the reaching hand; hand a half-beat longer at the setting; no look toward the bed (T058/T079). The mark hangs where it hung; an afternoon is not an opening.
  4. At the kindling: knife at the edge of its sill, six counted in the worn thumb-mark where the house can check and the seventh at the lip where it cannot, Garl's unforged collector sentence beside it. Tarn takes the broom only after it has been instructed, tells the wood *Six counted. The next I don't count*, says *Yes, house* to an early thaw and a late while wanting the late because it puts off the paying, sweeps the collector's road without turning; no collector comes. Chest tight since the dark; Ash worked into the knuckle creases in the colour of the clean dark line; pale clip-ring holding what light there is. Ear-sentence to the word that would call the loan and stops there with no figure and no name; Seed 16 breath drawn and not spent; nine stoppings nine objects with no tenth before the seventh year is earned; seventh year nearer its end than ever and still has not given; loan uncalled; stroke over the flagstones past the shallow bright patch without the half-check (T036/T076).
  5. In the wheel-shed: Jorra gives the men no name and does not say the room was cold; dusty and clean forearms on one tray; asks neither which slate was broken nor whether it was a birth-slate; speaks to the wheel before anyone (*It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady*), tells a pot *You'll do*, knows the full note three turns ahead and withholds it from a man on a wrong wind whose saying about a pot singing before its time stays a saying about a pot, not made true as a lesson, no pot lost (T035/T031/T062, T065/T095). When Sella's voice goes low and unfinished, his bent finger goes into the cold damper and holds until the sentence reaches the pot, then the tongue moves a hair's breadth and the finger is left standing — a remembered repetition, not a change made again, not paired with Day 161, 166 or 171. The iron is one piece with two bites, the holding and the taking, with no word in Grathic for both mercies, joined to no kiln and entered in no slate (T062). *He's not Vail* sealed; promise of the boy's name to the slate-burners ungiven.
  6. At the going of the light: carried home up the clay road, instruction to wool and river wind and never to the basket; woodsmoke in long low bars before her, river wind behind with the taste of water over water, the two passing without taking each other's colour. The arm that has carried him a hundred and ninety-eight mornings keeps the height of the handle, give of the shoulder, turn of the wrist at a step's end — a record in muscle that remembers weight and not cost. Goes wide around the pan's gone round ring, the bare dark trough whose water is called weather, the rack space with its broken birth-slates, the field's empty place and the place the carriers crossed, each with a look and a wide step and no number (T009/T013/T092, T098). Under the roof: bed, not Debt Loft; fold right without a look at his face; thread left frozen; pot handle two inches beyond the reaching hand; the two rooms do not touch.
  7. Small hours: the file laid out as instruments on a towel. Six hearings beside and never among, the sixth out of a person, no seventh invented to fill the empty place; three near-full answers advanced and unpaid; told bell beside heard and told figure beside paid; three held changes on three places, loft scrape on a fourth, pale line on a fifth, held inch on a sixth; crust's gap, shelf's two gaps, pan's gone ring and field's empty place on trays that do not touch; no third gap, no fourth likeness, no finger drawn; stone cold in its loft pot; charcoal unread; Seed 16 breath unspent; loan uncalled (T061/T082, T006, T064, T060/T086/T097, T100). The market's figure, read or to be read, is not entered in any of it: a figure read aloud is not the figure paid, and the read figure lies beside the paid and never becomes one.
- **Emotional turn.** From the household's ordinary keeping, shown to be accounts never entered, to his private file, the same kind of keeping with no hand to spend it. He refuses to let six places stand up as one rule, which is the only power he has and is not a power at all.
- **Sensory anchors.** Heat slanting down the shelf's underside; lips moving along two bare boards; back of a broken pot on coals; burn-wrist and pale grey streak; knife's worn thumb-mark and seventh at the lip; Ash in knuckle creases; pale clip-ring; cold iron taking the cold out of a fingertip; woodsmoke in long low bars; river wind with the taste of water; frozen thread; the back-of-the-pot count arriving nowhere.
- **Threads:** T058/T079, T100, T036/T076, T035/T031/T062/T065/T095, T009/T013/T092/T098, T061/T082, T006, T064, T060/T086/T097, T071/T072, T088, T004.
- **Foreshadowing planted / paid off.** The read figure kept out of the file plants Scene 4's finding that the market changed nothing; loft scrape, pale line and record-in-muscle kept on their separate places feed the far-off convergence; the six places plant the seventh-sounding that must never come. Nothing paid.
- **Exact last image.** The file taken up again in the same order, exact, complete and worth nothing; the hand stays down against the fold and the count goes on.

## Scene 3 — “The Second Reading over the Basket”

- **Day and time.** Day 199, Ember 20, Tenthday. Before noon through the morning; the second Account-day and market-day of the kiln month.
- **Place.** The Cross: the reading block, the moot-post's finger's width of shade, the pot board across the square, the stalls, the out-of-line landing below the north bridge, and the clay road up toward Greybank.

- **Beats, in order.**

  3. Two hundred lean to the reading block; eight pigs go over the wet boards; the bell goes four, four and a gap with nobody crossing himself, the heard kept beside the told (T078). No fifth pull, no crossing, no lesson made of the gap.
  4. **Berr's reading.** Comes up short off the clay road, red office cord folded square under his coat, glaze green at one mouth-corner; wipes thumb twice before touching nothing; holds a narrow slate unopened to his forearm; passes a finger over the figure; reads *One in twelve, as the notice says* low into the top of the crowd, the figure going twice over the basket — once for the moot, once for the households that come late.   6. **Hessa.** Takes the narrow slate up the road before noon, edge-on to her forearm, writing to her sleeve; to the women who ask, *I don't read it. I carry it. He is not Marra, not the Drawing collector, not the Court-table clerk, not the foundling.
  8. **The green and the shape.** Across the square at the pot board, a silent woman with clay under her nails in small crescents, a pale line across the back of one hand and the sweetish green of lead glaze in the crease beside her lip turns a cup a quarter, sets it back exactly where it stood, says nothing and goes off with the light — the sixth green still a colour joined to no ear, never Fenna in narration, no seventh green (T050/T051 held). ## Scene 5 — “The Day after the Reading”

- **Day and time.** Day 200, Ember 21, Firstday. Before first grey through the morning.
- **Place.** Under the Vail roof; the yard well; the drying racks and the space beneath the lowest rack; the wheel-shed edge; the kindling; up and down the clay road.
- **Present.** Corin/Dan; Sella; Jorra; Tarn; a rack woman with slip dried pale in her wrists and a man come off the clay road (walk-ons); three or four unnamed men going low into the back room before first grey (walk-ons).
- **Beats, in order.**
  1. Fifth dated heading. The month's last tend opens on the morrow but no boundary is drawn here; Tallow firing month / Ember kiln month, neither corrected. He is two hundred days old and six months and twenty days, nobody says so (T071/T072); fire's payer unnamed for two hundred days (T088); three counts in three places that do not become one (T061/T082).
  2. First smell hot clay under tallow, wet wool under that, sour Ash breath; first sound the kiln drawing in fours with a gap. Sella's instruction to wool, fire, pot, broom, running water, never basket; *the house*; half-pace; jar-glance without a touch; gruel from the house's own dish, spoon on her sleeve and not his mouth; burn-wrist pushing hair back so the grey streak shows pale; pot handle two inches beyond the reaching hand, half-beat at the setting (T058/T079). Mark untouched — a morning and a shelf are not openings.
  3. At the well: rack woman with slip dried pale in her wrists asks what the pots will want before the kiln month is out; told to ask the pot and the weather. A man come off the clay road asks what the kiln will want now the figure has gone up twice; answered with the house and the weather. Both go away level, nothing slopped, priced, refused or entered (T077).
  4. Sweet edge of lead glaze comes up off High Kiln on a wrong wind and goes out on the same wind — a direction, answered with pot and weather; no second edge paid so soon after the carrying-out, sickness not named in Grathic, no collector called, no lesson or account made (T064 held).
  5. Yard goes around the clean dark line, the new finer grey called the thaw, the rack space with broken birth-slates, pan's gone round ring with no scrape or chip, bare dark trough with water called weather, field's empty place where a hand went in on Seed 15 and did not come out, place the carriers crossed — look, wide step, no number; no third gap, no fourth likeness made a cause (T009/T013/T092, T098, T060/T086/T097).
  6. Before first grey three or four men unnamed go low into the finger's-width-open back room, get no name from house or Jorra; two shut-room tellings stay two with no third (T065/T095). Jorra comes through with grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bent finger, parted from slip as a surgeon parts clot from vessel; dusty and clean forearms on one tray; asks neither which slate broken nor whether birth-slate; tells wheel it does not care whose hands and pot *You'll do*; knows full note three turns ahead, withholds it from a man on a wrong wind whose saying stays a saying about a pot, no pot lost (T035/T031/T062). His bent finger goes into the cold damper while Sella's sentence stays unfinished, holds until the sentence reaches the pot, then moves a hair's breadth and is left standing — remembered repetition, not a change made again (T062).
  7. At the kindling: knife at edge of sill, six counted where house can check and seventh at lip where it cannot, Garl's unforged sentence beside it; Tarn takes broom only after instruction, *Six counted. The next I don't count*, sweeps collector's road without turning, no collector comes; ear-sentence to the word that would call the loan and stops, Seed 16 breath unspent, nine stoppings nine objects with no tenth before the seventh year is earned, loan uncalled, stroke over the bright patch without the half-check (T036/T076).
  8. Under the roof in the morning: hand goes up an inch toward the note of water over water, holds for the length of the small intake only, comes down before the letting go — shorter than the longest, the body keeping its own time — filed on its sixth place, not promoted into hearing, seventh, stopping, payment, key, power or rule, no payer named (T061/T082). The reading is a day behind, gone up the road in a locked box with the printed one in twelve unchallenged and the true subtraction inside. Market does not get to read the inch.
- **Emotional turn.** The day after the reading is ordinary work and keeps its shape; the account has gone up the road and cannot be reached back. The boy's count reaches two hundred exact against a house that keeps its days in loaves and glances, and nobody marks the number. Turn from the working morning to the closing night.
- **Sensory anchors.** Hot clay under tallow; wet wool; sour Ash; kiln's four-and-a-gap; clay dust dried pale in wrist creases; sweet edge on wrong wind; clean dark line and finer grey; trough bare and dark; grey slate dust ground small as meal; pale clip-ring; knife at the sill's edge; fifth low note of water over water; pot handle two inches beyond the hand.
- **Threads:** T071/T072, T088, T061/T082, T058/T079, T077, T064 held, T009/T013/T092/T098, T060/T086/T097, T065/T095, T035/T031/T062, T036/T076, T004.
- **Foreshadowing planted / paid off.** The last tend's eve plants Chapter 50's opening without a boundary; the two-hundredth day plants the long count without a celebration or a word; the remembered damper holding and the unspent breath keep Tarn's seventh year pressing toward its end. Nothing paid.
- **Exact last image.** The pot rim set out with its handle two inches beyond the reaching hand, her hand a half-beat longer at the setting, and the boy's small hand coming down before the letting go; the figure a day behind and gone up the road.

## Scene 6 — “The Count Goes Forward”

- **Day and time.** Day 200, Ember 21, Firstday. Afternoon into the small hours; chapter close.
- **Place.** The loaf shelf's low angle, the hearth, the kindling, the wheel-shed edge, the clay road at the going of the light, the bed.
- **Present.** Corin/Dan; Sella; Tarn; Jorra. No one else; the reader never leaves the boy's eyes.
- **Beats, in order.**
  1. Afternoon thins without ending; heat off the high air first, staying longest under the shelf, behind the pot, in the fold of wool. One leathery crust two inches from his ear, empty place unnamed, no third; frozen thread unwiped; cold stopped at cheek, temple, bare wrist joint, afterdrop waiting on a warming nobody will perform.
  2. Sella at the shelf, back to the basket: lips along row with two unnamed gaps, no figure; clay jar glanced at and hand shut half over nothing; finger from beneath the front edge, charcoal unread; told the thaw, coals turned with back of broken pot, *The house will keep it*; count under breath toward no opening — small intake, hold, letting go; burn-wrist pushing hair back, grey streak pale; pot rim set out with handle two inches beyond reaching hand, half-beat longer at setting, no look toward the bed — she never looks toward the bed at the setting (T058/T079, T100). Mark touched once before the kiln on Ember 1 and at no other hour; dusk is not an opening.
  3. At the kindling: knife at edge of sill, six in worn thumb-mark and seventh at lip, Garl's sentence beside it; Tarn takes broom only after instruction, *Six counted. The next I don't count*, ear-sentence to the threshold of the word that would call the loan and stops there, breath drawn and not spent, *Yes, house* to the fire, goes on; nine stoppings nine objects with no tenth before seventh year is earned, seventh year nearer its end than ever and has not given, Seed 16 breath unspent, loan uncalled, stroke over bright patch without half-check (T036/T076).
  4. In the wheel-shed: Jorra gives men no name, dusty and clean forearms on one tray, tells wheel and pot their sayings, withholds note known three turns ahead from a wrong wind, leaves bent finger standing in cold damper after Sella's sentence reaches the pot — remembered repetition, not stood up with the other fractions. Iron one piece with two bites, no word in Grathic for both mercies, joined to no kiln, entered in no slate; *he's not Vail* sealed, promise ungiven (T035/T031/T062, T065/T095).
  5. At the going of the light: carried home up the clay road, instruction to wool and river wind and never to basket; woodsmoke before in long low bars, river wind behind with taste of water over water; carrying arm a record in muscle that remembers weight and not cost; wide around pan's gone ring, bare dark trough with water called weather, rack space with broken birth-slates, field's empty place and place the carriers crossed, each with a look and a wide step and no number (T009/T013/T092, T098). Under roof: bed, not Debt Loft; fold right without a look at his face; thread left frozen; coals banked with back of broken pot; fire four low pulls with fifth missing, no payer for two hundred days; river's fifth note, boards' four nails four and thumb-width knot — three counts in three places, none rising up and becoming the others because a man who wanted them to agree would have to put a payer under all three and none stands to any (T088, T061/T082).
  6. Small hours: file turned out as instruments on a towel. Six hearings beside and never among, sixth from a person, no seventh invented; three near-full answers advanced and unpaid; told bell beside heard, told figure beside paid; box gone up the road locked with true subtraction inside, Hessa's slate unread at Greybank, clerk still set for uncalled measure; three held changes on three places, loft scrape fourth, pale line fifth; crust's gap, shelf's two gaps, pan's gone ring, field's empty place on trays that do not touch; no third gap, no fourth likeness, no finger drawn; stone cold in its loft pot, charcoal unread, Seed 16 breath unspent, loan uncalled, cost of man carried out entered nowhere (T061/T082, T006, T064, T060/T086/T097, T100, T015/T041/T047).
  7. **The hand, final asking.** Goes up an inch toward the note of water over water through the finger's-width door, stays up through the whole of one of the breaths Sella counts at her fire in the room that does not touch his — small intake, hold, on into the letting go — and comes down with the breath rather than before it, against the fold. Not a greater reach, not a stronger hand, not a power: a coordination with a rhythm in a room he cannot enter, and the rhythm goes on without him and does not stop to notice. Filed on its sixth place beside the others, never laid across them; not reaching, hearing, seventh, stopping, payment, one of the three held changes, key, power or rule; no payer named, no doctrine stood up. Still cannot close two inches, lift chin to crust, speak a number. Body humiliates him and moves by an inch in the same motion; both true (T061/T082).
  8. **Close, forward.** Two hundred stands exact in the dark of the wool, complete and unspent, spoken by nobody under any roof. Fire's fifth unpaid; mark touched once before the kiln; stone cold in loft pot; charcoal unread; Seed 16 breath unspent; loan uncalled; carried man's cost entered nowhere. River's fifth note, fire's missing fifth, boards' four nails four and knot — three counts that do not become one. Nothing joined, named, opened, called, spent or ended. The count goes forward into Day 201, Ember 22, Secondday.
- **Emotional turn.** From the afternoon's ordinary keeping and the night's inventory to the last small coordination: the longest holding is not a compensation for anything the body cannot do. The count reaches two hundred unspent and nobody in any room knows there is a number to reach. Forward, not closed.
- **Sensory anchors.** Heat slanting down shelf's underside; leathery crust and frozen thread; back of broken pot on coals; burn-wrist and grey streak; knife's worn mark and seventh at lip; pale clip-ring; cold damper iron; woodsmoke and river wind; banked coals; fifth low note of water over water; four nails, four and a thumb-width knot; the small hand coming down with the breath.
- **Threads:** T058/T079, T100, T036/T076, T035/T031/T062/T065/T095, T009/T013/T092/T098, T088, T061/T082, T006, T064, T060/T086/T097, T015/T041/T047, T071/T072, T004.
- **Foreshadowing planted / paid off.** The long holding is the sibling of Chapter 48's close, not its promotion; it plants later askings that will be held or refused as the days ask, never a power. The two-hundredth day and the uncalled loan press toward the seventh year's end and Chapter 51's near-saying. The pale line, the loft scrape, the charcoal and the cold stone go on gathering interest for the far-off hand that lays private records together. Nothing paid.
- **Exact last image.** The hand comes down with the breath against the fold, the fold takes it without a word, and two hundred stands exact in the dark of the wool while the river, the fire and the boards keep three counts in three places that do not become one. The count goes forward.

## How the last moment pulls into Chapter 50

The chapter closes in the small hours of Day 200 with the hand held through a whole counted breath and let go with it — an inch, a coordination, worth nothing — and with two hundred exact and unspent in the wool, spoken by nobody. Every holding is carried across untouched: six hearings with no seventh, three near-full answers unpaid, nine stoppings with no tenth, three held changes on three places, loft scrape on a fourth, pale line on a fifth, held inch on a sixth; crust, two shelf gaps, pan's ring and field's empty place on separate trays; stone cold and unreconciled, charcoal unread, mark touched once before the kiln, Seed 16 breath unspent, loan uncalled, cost of the carried man entered nowhere. Ember's second reading has gone up the road in Berr's locked box with the printed one in twelve unchallenged and the true subtraction inside; Hessa's slate is unread at Greybank and the clerk still set for his uncalled measure. No boundary is drawn. Chapter 50 opens on Day 201, Ember 22, Secondday, the kiln month's last tend beginning as an ordinary morning: the same household arithmetic in loaves and glances, the same three counts in three places, the same hand asked again and held or not held as the days ask, and Tarn's seventh year pressing nearer its end with the word still on the other side of his teeth. Nothing has been settled; the account goes on.
