## Chronicle

Three mornings came up over Low Kiln, the kiln month unmarked, Tallow the firing month and Ember the kiln month uncorrected. The boy came into his ninety-eighth day, then his ninety-ninth, then his two-hundredth, and nobody said a number. The carried fire missed its fifth under the floor, unnamed for two hundred days; the river kept its note of water over water, the boards four nails, four and a thumb-width knot. Three counts in three places, none became the others, for agreeing them would have wanted a payer and none stood to any.

Sella Vail gave the instruction to the wool, fire, pot, broom and running water, never the basket, and said *the house* where another woman would say *I*. She fed him from the house's own dish, set the pot handle two inches beyond his reach, moved her lips along a two-gapped loaf row without a figure, and kept her finger from beneath the charcoal. Cost-questions went to the pot and the weather. The widow's mark went untouched; there had been no opening since Ember 1. She carried him home around the pan's gone ring and the yard's absences, giving each a wide step and no number.

On the twentieth of Ember the district's arithmetic changed again. Two hundred leaned to the block; the bell went four, four and a gap with nobody crossing. Berr Clay wiped his thumb twice, passed a finger over the slate and read *One in twelve, as the notice says* low into the crowd --- the figure read twice over the basket, once for the moot and once for those who came late. He called the levy, denied it with *your house*, never *I*, and went off with the box locked and the subtraction inside. Hessa's slate went up the road unread; the clerk stayed set for an uncalled measure. It did not answer the man carried out breathing: no figure, no cause, his pale line where it was.

Tarn told the kindling six counted and the next he did not count, brought the ear-sentence to the word that would call the loan and stopped; nine stoppings stood with no tenth before the seventh year was earned. Jorra parted dust from slip, withheld a note known three turns ahead from a wrong wind, and held his bent finger in the damper until the sentence reached the pot --- remembered, not made again.

Four times he questioned his hand: down before the breath was done, down at once, held for the small intake, and in the small hours held through a whole breath at her fire, coming down with the breath. He filed each on its own place, not a hearing, seventh, stopping, payment, key, power or rule, no payer named. He still could not close two inches to the pot handle, lift the crust or speak a number. The body humiliated him and moved by an inch, and the inch was worth nothing. Two hundred stood exact in the wool, unspent, spoken by nobody.

## Continuity

- **Exact final moment.** Day 200, Ember 21, Firstday, Y.H. 813, the small hours, under the Vail kiln-house roof in Low Kiln. Corin/Dan lies on his bed with the fold at his ear, one leathery brown-edged crust two inches from that ear and its empty place unnamed, no third, the thread of drool frozen and unwiped. He has just laid out the file exact, complete and worth nothing; then his hand outside the wool went up an inch toward the note of water over water through the finger's-width door, stayed up through the whole of one of the breaths Sella counts at her fire in the room that does not touch his (small intake, hold, letting go), and came down with the breath rather than before it, against the fold. The hand is down at the close; the holding is the longest yet, a coordination and not a reach. Present: the boy only, awake and silent. Sella is asleep in her own room after setting him on his bed; Tarn and Jorra are offstage at their beds after the day's work. The count goes forward into Day 201, Ember 22, Secondday, when the month's last tend opens.
- **Day and time at end.** Day 200 of the new life; six months and twenty days old; Ember 21, Firstday, the small hours; the kiln month's last working week, no boundary drawn. The district's second reading was a day before, gone up the road in a locked box; nothing was joined, named, opened, called, spent or ended.
- **The chapter's one meaningful change.** Ember's second Account-day and market were read over the basket on Day 199. Berr Clay came up short off the clay road, red office cord folded square under his coat, glaze green at one mouth-corner, wiped his thumb twice before touching nothing, held a narrow slate unopened to his forearm, passed a finger over the figure, and read *One in twelve, as the notice says* low into the top of the crowd --- the figure going twice over the basket, once for the moot and once for the households that come late. He said *The levy is called. It is not called by me* and *your house*, never *I*, and went off toward High Kiln with the box locked and the true subtraction inside, believing he had done a kindness. The district's arithmetic for the kiln month changed for the second time and cannot be changed back; the printed figure stands unchallenged and a figure read aloud is still not the figure paid. Advanced and not paid: it settles no long thread, and it does not answer the man carried out on Day 188 --- no figure, collector or slate knows what he cost, no cause is named in Grathic and the pale line stays on its own place.
- **The held inch.** Asked on all three days and held four ways: Day 198, up an inch and down before the counted breath had finished (a shorter holding, the body keeping its own time); Day 199 small hours, up and down at once with no holding (the asking is not always answered); Day 200 morning, held for the length of the small intake only, down before the letting go; Day 200 small hours, up through the whole counted breath and on into the letting go, down with the breath. Each is filed on the sixth place beside the others and never laid across them: not a reaching, hearing, seventh, stopping, payment, one of the three held changes, key, power or rule; no payer named, no doctrine stood up. Three like holdings are not a trend and not a power; he still cannot close two inches, lift the crust or speak a number.
- **On-stage characters and condition.** Corin: carried, unable to roll, stand, speak, read Grathic, reach a cup, close the two inches to the pot handle or lift his chin to the crust; cold stopped at cheek, temple and the bare wrist-joint, afterdrop waiting on a warming nobody will perform; humiliated and moved by an inch in the same motion. Sella: half-pace from open slates, instruction to wool, fire, pot, broom, running water and river wind, never the basket; burn-wrist pushing coal-black hair back so the grey streak shows pale, jar glanced at without a touch, lips along a two-gapped row with no figure, finger kept from beneath the shelf, pot handle two inches beyond the hand with a half-beat at the setting, count under her breath toward no opening, mark untouched; gone to her own bed. Tarn: chest tight since the dark, Ash worked into knuckles, pale clip-ring at left wrist, knife at the sill's edge with six checked and the seventh at the lip, ear-sentence stopped at the word that would call the loan, Seed 16 breath unspent, stroke over the bright patch without the half-check, no collector came. Jorra: grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bent finger and under the nails, dusty and clean forearms on one tray, note withheld from the wrong wind, bent finger left standing in the cold damper as a remembered repetition, *he's not Vail* sealed. Berr Clay and Hessa Rale were on stage on Day 199 at the Cross; the soft-cuffed clerk was on stage at the out-of-line landing; the green-mouthed woman and the foundling shape appeared once each at the pot board and the market's edge.
- **Items, money, powers.** Nothing usable, no money, no purchase, sale, loaf or slate within reach on any of the three days. Printed figure one in twelve unchallenged; true subtraction locked in Berr's box up the clay road toward High Kiln; Hessa's slate unread at Greybank; clerk still set at the landing for an uncalled measure. The warm stone lies cold in its unmarked pot in the Debt Loft. The one brown-edged crust stays stiff and leathery in the fold. The loaf shelf keeps its two unnamed gaps; the charcoal beneath its front edge stays unread, uncounted, uncopied, and Sella's finger went beneath it on no day. Widow's mark touched once before the kiln on Ember 1 and at no hour since. No usable power, no rank change, no experiment, no clamp; counting is an old-life habit, not a gift.
- **Facts, lies, promises, debts.** Cost-questions answered as directions to the pot and the weather, nothing priced, refused or entered (T077 advanced, unspent). The account went up the road in the locked box and cannot be reached back; the box went toward High Kiln with the reading denied by *your house*, never *I*. No collector called for the man carried out on Day 188, no figure given, no sickness named in Grathic, no slate opened; his carrying cost entered nowhere; the narration invented no offstage fate for him, a breath kept not an ending. High Kiln's saying stayed a saying, no pot lost, no second edge paid so soon after the carrying-out. Hessa carried and did not read; the clerk entered a measure nobody called; both records go inward and neither was opened. *He's not Vail* stays sealed; promise of the boy's name to the men who burn slates stays ungiven. No payer named to any holding.
