The afternoon thinned without ending. Heat went off the high air first and settled where the room kept what it had no use for just then: under the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf}, behind the pot on its board, into the fold where one stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from the ear of \person[Corin]{the boy} and kept its empty place unnamed. No third came to lie beside it. An empty place kept empty is a finding; a finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged. He had set that rule down on other days and it held on this one without his having to touch it. The thread at his mouth had frozen in the dry heat and was unwiped, because nobody under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread, and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. Cold had come to cheek and temple and the small bare joint at the wrist where the wool rode back, and stopped there as if it had reached the end of an order written out for it; it went no further and it did not go away. The afterdrop waited on the far side of a warming nobody in this house would perform. He knew the afterdrop when he saw it. He wanted the crust. Both were true, and the second was louder in a body of two hundred and twenty-four days.

Under the floor \lore{the carried fire} gave four low pulls and kept the fifth where it had kept it from the first, its payer without a name for two hundred and twenty-four days. Through the finger's-width door the river held its note of \lore{water over water}, a fifth low thing never counted among the hearings. Overhead the boards kept four nails on the near board, four on the next, and a thumb-width knot where the fifth nail should have stood. The kiln drew in fours with a gap, not a bell; no market stood under this roof and no slate lay open within two paces of the basket. Three counts in three places and none rose into another, because a man who wanted them to agree would have to put a payer under all three and no payer stood to any of them. He kept them on three trays. The trays did not touch. In the unswept corner the sour \lore{Ash} breath went on, the thing the house called \lore{the thaw}, and the name did not make it go.

The first grain of the year lay where it had been set after the morning's cutting, near \place{the hearth} under a fold of wool, covered so that a man might step around it without seeing what it was. Nobody had opened a slate over it. The hands that cut it had read its weight in their arms and let their arms keep the weight, a record in muscle that knows what a thing weighs and not what it would take to buy it back; the yard counted it in glances and handfuls the way it counted loaves, and the count arrived nowhere and was entered by nobody. A bundle set down is a pot set down and covered. He took it apart as he took apart everything that came within two paces: what was it, grain; where had it stood, in \place{the worked strips} east of \place{Low Kiln}; who had paid a figure for it, nobody under any roof; what did it ask of him, nothing. He filed it on a place of its own and laid nothing across it, not across the two unnamed gaps in the row, not across the one crust in the fold, not across the field's empty place. A harvest housed under a roof is a harvest housed; it is not an account until somebody finds a payer to stand beneath it.

And with the returning hands the yard had gone around the empty place in the returning line once more --- the place where a hand had gone in on Seed 15 and had not come out with the rest --- with a look and a wide step and no number: no name, no cause, no accident, no body, no identification of which hand, no figure and no payment. He had let it go around and had not gone after it. A man can go around a place for a whole season and never once turn it into a cause; the yard had been doing it for years with the clean dark line and the rack space and the round ring where the shallow pan had stood, and the going-around was a way of keeping, not a way of counting. No third gap came. No fourth likeness stood up to make a cause of the ring, the rack space, the bare trough, the field's empty place and the place in the line; five walked-around things stood in a row and a row is not an account until a payer stands under it, and none did.

\scenebreak

\person{Sella Vail} came to the shelf with her back to the basket and did not look toward \place{the bed}. The instruction had gone first to the wool, the fire, the pot, the broom, the running water and the river wind, and never to the basket; she said \emph{the house} where another woman would have said \emph{I}, and the boy under the low angle was not addressed at all. Her lips moved along the row as a woman's lips move over the days of a week, and the row stood with two gaps --- the old space at the end and the palm's breadth that had come in years back without a scrape, a crumb, a print or a word --- and she gave nobody a figure, though the first bundle lay covered behind her near the fire. Her eye went once to the clay jar cold under its glaze on the wall shelf and her hand started toward it, the little-door burn opening, and shut half over nothing without a touch, the burn closing on air. Her finger lay on top of the shelf's front edge where any woman in the district could see it lying, and the charcoal tally under the board stayed unread, uncounted and uncopied, because a woman's back turned is not the same thing as a finger beneath it and the reason stood between the two, unspoken.

She told the fire it was the thaw and turned the coals with the back of a broken pot, and the fire answered four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have been. “The house will keep it,” she said, and stood a moment with the back of the pot in her hand counting under her breath toward no opening --- small intake, hold, letting go --- the count arriving nowhere as it had arrived nowhere on every day of his life. She pushed a strand of coal-black hair back with the wrist that carried the burn, and the grey streak at her temple showed pale off the coals, and she did not discuss the streak or the winter it had come in. Then she set the pot rim out with its handle two inches beyond the reaching hand and let her hand rest a half-beat longer at the setting than the setting required, and did not look toward the bed.

He lay two inches short of the handle and watched the half-beat go by as a man watches a hand come out of a field before the dressing goes on: a thing done and a thing withheld in one motion. He put it where it belonged, laid nothing across it and named no payer to it. A half-beat is an interval, and an interval kept is an interval; it is not a promise, not a key and not an opening. At an afternoon and a shelf she did not touch \lore{the widow's mark}; it hung on its cord at her throat where it had hung since the first firing, touched once before the kiln on Ember 1 and at no hour since, and there had been no opening in forty-four days, because a shelf and an afternoon are not openings. Her private roll stayed unshown and \person{Dessa Silt}'s cost was unentered, and the cost of the man carried out on Day 188 stood where it had stood, entered nowhere.

\scenebreak

\person{Garl Vail} was kept in objects and not in a story. The salt-stiff coat held his broader shape on its peg with the crease at the left shoulder where he had leaned for twenty years; the seat at the fire kept its hollow and its leftward lean; the Ash ridge had turned into a clean dark line with a new, finer hairline of grey beside it called the thaw, and neither had been swept. His proverb had gone years ago to a pot with no crack in it, said now over a corner where the repair had been rubbed out, \emph{leave the crack showing; people trust a thing they can see has been handled}; the other, \emph{feed the fire before you feed yourself}, went to the fire and came back as heat that nobody named as his. The debt against a firing of tiles lay where he had left it, neither paid nor settled nor named, circled without a figure and worse for being uncalled; no collector had come up \place{the clay road} on Grain 15 and no lesson had been made of any of it. His name had not been spoken under this roof in two hundred and twenty-four days. The keeping of him was maintenance, and a line left where it is is still a line kept.

At \place{the kindling} \person{Tarn} took the broom only after it had been instructed, the instruction going to the tool before it went to the man, and he took it up as a man takes up an instrument already named. “Yes, house,” he said, and told the wood “Six counted. The next I don't count.” He set the small knife at the very edge of its sill, six in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check and the seventh at the lip where it could not, and Garl's unforged collector sentence lay beside it where it had lain. The sentence about an ear under this roof being worth more than the debt that held it came to his teeth and went all the way to the word that would call the loan and stopped there with no figure and no name; he filed the stopping as a remembered stopping and not a new one, because he had brought it to that word on other days and the stopping was the same object each time. He counted nine behind it with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end, and the seventh year stood at its very end and had still not given. He drew the breath from Seed 16 at the top of the reach and did not spend it, and kept the loan uncalled. No collector came up the clay road on Grain 15.

His chest had been tight since the dark; Ash was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line and no scrubbing lifted it, and the pale clip-ring at his left wrist held what light there was and gave it back. His stroke went over \place{the flagstones} past the shallow bright patch kept since Seed 22 without the half-check and over the road a house sends a man down when it wants a collector without once turning along it; the stones kept the place where the stroke had stopped, and nothing stood up out of the keeping to call itself a rule. Above the shed the warm stone Sella had given him in the winter Garl died lay back in its unmarked clay pot in \place{the Debt Loft}, two years gone and cold, unreconciled with the debt by not trying, which was not forgiving.

\scenebreak

At the edge of \place{the Vail wheel-shed} \person[Jorra Vail]{Jorra} came through with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bent little finger and under the nails, white in the creases where a fist had opened, and not bone-coloured \lore{slip}. He kept dusty forearms and clean ones on one tray and parted them as a surgeon parts clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had been broken nor whether it was a birth-slate. The broken birth-slates lay under the lowest rack where the yard went around them and entered them into nothing. Three or more men had gone low into the finger's-width-open \place{the back room} before first grey and he gave them no name and did not say the room was cold; the two tellings of the room shut in the war stayed two and no third came. He spoke to the wheel before he spoke to anyone. “It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady,” he said, and told a pot “You'll do” in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either. He knew \lore{the full note} the pot would give three turns before it left his hands and the knowing had air under it and a want in it, and he kept it back, on the near-full tray beside the hearings and never among them.

The burned-slate word had come over the boards four times out of \place{Slate Row} and arrived nowhere --- a direction with no hand at the flame and no figure at its foot --- and he grew quieter whenever it went around; the household straightened around the quiet without an order, the quiet going before him into the shed the way a cold air goes before a door. He let the three shapings of the pot saying stand as sayings about a pot with no lesson made in them and gave no name to what ailed the man carried out on Day 188: a pot that sings before its time mostly sings itself out early; the pot that sang early last month had not been heard from since; a pot gone quiet on every row come the thaw, which a wrong-wind voice had let stand again in the morning. Three sayings and a fourth telling on their own places, four directions arrived nowhere, and no pot lost in this yard. When her voice went low at the hearth his bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper} ahead of her hand and held until the sentence reached the pot, then moved a hair's breadth and was left standing --- a remembered repetition, not a change made again. The iron was one piece with two bites, and he had no word in \lore{Grathic} for both mercies, and joined it to no kiln and entered it in no slate. \emph{He's not Vail} stayed sealed as tightly as on the hour it was spoken, and the promise of the boy's name to the men who burn slates was neither given nor withdrawn. He did not look toward the bed and did not ask about the ear, and Tarn did not ask about the room.

\scenebreak

At the going of the light Sella carried him home at the half-pace, woodsmoke before her in long low bars and river wind behind with the taste of water over water. The carrying arm was a record in muscle that remembered weight and not cost; it had carried him up and down this yard for two hundred and twenty-four days and had never once asked what it carried was worth. In \place{the Clay Yard} she went wide around the clean dark line and the finer hairline called the thaw, around the round ring where the shallow clay pan had stood, around the bare dark \place{the trough} whose water was called \lore{weather} by people who did not taste it, around the rack space beneath the lowest rack with its broken birth-slates, around the field's empty place and the place the carriers crossed and the place in the returning line where the hand had gone in and not come back. Each had its look and its wide step and its no number; the walked-around things stood in a row and a row is not an account until a payer stands under it, and none did. She set the basket on the bed under the roof and not in the Debt Loft, put the fold right without a look at his face and left the thread frozen; the first grain lay covered near the fire and the two did not become one keeping.

\scenebreak

In the small hours Sella had gone to her own bed and left the coals to keep themselves, and the two rooms did not touch. He turned out the file as a man lays instruments on a towel --- no towel, no instruments, no case, but the order of the laying the whole of the discipline --- and found it exact, complete and worth nothing. Six hearings of \lore{the full note} stood beside and never among: five from four sources, a cup across a birth room, a pot in the Vail wheel-shed through a finger's width of door, the district's slate twice at \place{the Cross}, and the sixth, the small flat knock out of Dessa Silt as the men lifted her, from a person, filed where a thing out of a person must go and never among the sources. No seventh came in two hundred and twenty-four days and none was invented to fill the empty place, for an empty place kept empty is a finding and a finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged. Three near-full answers stood on their own tray, advanced and unpaid: a pot under the shed wall that answered nearly full with air under the note and a want in it, a rack pot lifted a hand's breadth too high and set down again, and the knowing the wheel-keeper kept back from a man come up on a wrong wind. Beside them stood three shapings of the pot saying, all sayings and no rule, advanced and unpaid; beside the hearings but never among them the seventh green was a colour and the seventh shape a shape, gathered three working mornings ago on places that did not touch, with no eighth of either, no second look joined, no name spoken over either, no loaf, no slate and no sale. Six greens behind a seventh make a row of greens and six shapes behind a seventh make a row of shapes, and a row is not an account until somebody finds a payer to stand under it, and none stood under either.

The told bell lay beside the heard, four, four and a gap with nobody crossing himself. The told figure lay beside the paid, one in twelve read twice over a basket on Grain 10 and still not the figure paid, the true subtraction locked in \person{Berr Clay}'s box going up toward \place{High Kiln}, \person{Hessa Rale}'s narrow slate gone up unread toward \place{Greybank}, the soft-cuffed clerk still set at the out-of-line landing below \place{the north bridge} for a measure nobody called. A figure read twice is still not the figure paid, and a district can change its arithmetic and go on not knowing what it changed; none of it reached back under the roof to be opened, copied or paid. The nine stoppings stood on Reed 5, 8, 11, 14, 17, 20, 23, 26 and 29 as objects and not a rule, with no payer named to any and no tenth before the seventh year at its very end, and the seventh year stood at its very end and had not given. The Seed 16 breath was unspent and the loan uncalled. Three held changes stood on three places --- the Day 161 fraction at the damper, the Day 166 beat beneath the shelf, the Tallow 22 half-check on the flagstones --- with the loft-sill scrape on a fourth, the pale line on the carried man on a fifth and the held inch on a sixth. Beside the dust and the two shut-room tellings, with a hand between the three, the burned-slate word stood on its own place: four tellings out of Slate Row, a direction arrived nowhere, not a cause, not a hearing, not a stopping, not a payment. No third gap had come and no fourth likeness had stood up; nothing had been filled to make a row come out even.

He put the question to his hand. It went up an inch toward the note of water over water through the finger's-width door, and stayed. In the other room her breaths had gone small intake, hold, letting go at her fire, a count she kept toward no opening and had never offered to any ear under this roof; the wall stood between that count and his ear and he did not hear it and was not asked to. He took the hand through the small intake, the hold and the letting go of one of those counted breaths, kept it through the little silence after her count had ended and brought it down on the river's next low note. Two rhythms, one held against the other across a wall that did not touch, remembered, not a change made again. It was not a longer reach, not a miss entered, not a hearing, not a seventh of the full note, not a tenth stopping before the seventh year at its very end, not a payment, not one of the three held changes, not a key, not a power and not a rule. He filed it on the sixth place with no payer named and did not spend it and did not explain it. Exactness and humiliation are one motion in a small hand and both are true and neither is a sign; once he reduced a shoulder in the dark by feel alone and now he cannot close the two inches to the pot handle, lift his chin two inches to the crust or speak a number that anybody under any roof would be asked to believe.

Two hundred and twenty-four stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in \place{Sarn}, \place{Sill} or \place{Grath}. The carried fire's fifth was unpaid for two hundred and twenty-four days; the seventh year stood at its very end and had not given; the seventh green was a colour and the seventh shape a shape, gathered and unjoined. Grain 20 stood five mornings ahead, the second Account-day and the market, and was not brought early into these small hours. The crust lay two inches from his ear with its empty place unnamed and no third, the thread at his mouth was frozen and unwiped, and the first grain lay covered near the fire with the place in the returning line still gone around and still without a number. Fire went on in the room that warmed him and the river in the room he could not enter, and neither stopped for the ear under the roof; the hand came down on the river's next low note, and the count went forward exact and unspent into Day 225, Grain 16, Sixthday.
