## Chronicle

Grain's middle tend came under the Vail roof with no bell, only the kiln drawing in fours and stopping where the fifth should have stood. Hot clay came first under tallow, wet wool under that, and under all three the sour breath of the corner the house called the thaw. The boy lay at the loaf shelf's low angle, two hundred and twenty-four days old by a reckoning no slate in Sarn, Sill or Grath had entered. Fire under the floor, river through the door and the boards' four nails, four and a knot, kept three counts in three places; none rose into another, for agreeing them would want a payer and none stood to any.

On the third morning the strips gave back what the fist would not count. Hands came out of the worked strips with the year's first grain; Sella Vail set it near the hearth and covered it with wool. Nobody gave a figure and no slate was opened. The arms knew weight and not cost; the yard counted it in glances and handfuls. With the returning hands the yard went around an empty place in the line, where a hand had gone in on Seed 15 and had not come out, with a look, a wide step and no number. No name, no cause, no figure. He kept bundle and absence on separate trays and would not let them stand up as an account.

Sella sent cost-questions to the pot and the weather, kept her finger above the charcoal, set the pot handle two inches beyond the hand, with a half-beat and no look toward the bed. Garl was kept in objects: salt-stiff coat, hollow seat, the unswept line and finer grey, the proverb gone to a pot with no crack, the tile debt circling without a name. Tarn counted six where the house could check, kept the seventh at the lip, brought the ear-sentence to the word that would call the loan and stopped. Jorra parted dust from slip as clot from vessel, told a pot *You'll do*, withheld the note and grew quieter whenever the burned-slate word went around.

In the small hours the boy asked his hand again. It went up an inch toward the river's note and came down, at once, or before the breath finished, or to the river's low interval. On the last night it went through a breath counted in a room that did not touch his, kept past the silence after the count, and came down on the river's next low note. The file was exact, complete and worth nothing: six hearings with no seventh, told bell beside heard, told figure beside paid, nine stoppings with no tenth, the loan uncalled. He could not lift the crust two inches, close on the pot handle or speak a believable number. The first grain lay covered near the fire, and the place in the returning line went around still without a figure.
