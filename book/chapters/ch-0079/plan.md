**Days:** 288–290 (Frost 19, Ninthday → Frost 21, Firstday, Y.H. 813). **Mode:** normal, three lived days, six scenes, two scenes per day, no day skipped or poured into another. ## Scene 1 — The Nineteenth Morning

- **Day / time:** Day 288, Frost 19, Ninthday; small hours under the roof into first light. Ordinary Ninthday: **no market, no Cross, no bell**.

- **Foreshadowing planted / paid off:** Plants the second Account-day as one morning ahead, explicitly unbrought; keeps the empty seventh place, the two-gapped row, the charcoal unread and the Frost 14 saying's single shaping waiting. Nothing paid.
- **Exact last image:** The hand comes down from the 151st intake against the fold, the pot rim standing two inches out, the river's low note going quiet, with Frost 20 one morning ahead and the file open on the towel.

## Scene 3 — The Twentieth Morning

  4.   7. ## The pull into Chapter 80

The final image is deliberately small and unclosed: 290 exact, six months and one hundred and ten days told by nobody; the fire's fifth unpaid the same number of days; the hand laid on the 156th answer; Frost 30 nine mornings ahead and explicitly not brought into this day. Chapter 80 opens on **Day 291, Frost 22, Secondday**, in the small hours under the same roof with the hand laid down on the 156th answer and the count still at 290, the first and second arithmetics locked up the clay road, the covered grain still read over by numbers that do not know its cost, the two shapings of the well-saying held beside Hessa's eighth hand with a finger between, the ninth stopping ungiven and the loan uncalled, Garl's name unspoken 291 days, and the month's last working week gathering toward the third reading without any of it being called a sign, lesson, mending or payment. The forward pressure is the arithmetic itself: two readings of Frost are behind and cannot be changed back, the third stands on Frost 30, and the boy is the only person in Low Kiln keeping the days exactly while the house keeps its number in loaves, glances and unnamed spaces. Nothing in this chapter opens, joins, names, pays or ends; the hand simply goes down on the next answer and the river goes quiet, which is how the next chapter must begin.
