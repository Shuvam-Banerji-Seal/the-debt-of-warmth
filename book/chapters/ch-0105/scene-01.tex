\daymark{366}{Stone 7, Seventhday, Y.H. 813}

\dropcap{T}{he hand} outside the wool had been laid back against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking, and it lay there in the small hours under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof at \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill}, \place{Grath}, flat and quiet on answer three hundred and six. \person[Corin]{The boy} was on \place{the bed} at the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf}, awake and silent, and the file lay open on \place{the towel} at the head of the bed with every gap left gap, the way instruments lie on a towel when a case is done and before the next is called. Nothing had crossed under the roof in the inch the hand had spent; no sign, no lesson, no mending, no cause, no payment and no payer stood anywhere in the room. The count had gone three hundred and sixty-five to three hundred and sixty-six in the dark, six months and one hundred and eighty-five days becoming six months and one hundred and eighty-six, told by nobody. He kept the number exactly, as he kept all of them, and did not stand it up as a line for anybody's foot to cross.

Three hundred and sixty-six was a whole year stood edge to edge in the only count that was exact. The month he had been born in had come round again under this roof, the deep-winter month, and no party had been made of it, no cake baked, no figure entered, no recognition given, no threshold crossed and nobody under any roof who would have known what a number counted if they had been told. He had long since stopped expecting to be told, which was not the same thing as not wanting it. The house kept its days in loaves, in glances, in the space where a loaf had stood and in the shape a room took when a weight was lifted out of it, and it could not say that a year had turned. Nothing out of the days behind had crossed as lesson or mending or cause to make the turning into one. Under the floor \lore{the carried fire} gave four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer without a name for three hundred and sixty-six days; the name of the man whose salt-stiff coat kept a broad shape on its peg behind the door had not been spoken under this roof the same number. Two counts stood exact and unspent and nobody joined them, for agreeing any two would want a payer put under both and none stood.

The body took itself in the surgeon's old order, and the old order did not flatter it at the turning of a year. One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from his ear, its corner standing a hair off the wool, its empty place beside it unnamed and no third come to make the row come out even. The thread of drool at his mouth had frozen in the dry kiln heat and was unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. Cold had stopped at cheek, temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, as though it had come to the end of a line written out for it with neither an order to go farther nor an order to go away. \lore{The Afterdrop} waited past all three, past a warming nobody in this house would perform. He knew the composed face that was not composure, knew a cold patient cannot clot and will seem better before he is worse, knew the order in which a body takes heat back, the small joint first and the core last, and the order in which it gives it up, the reverse, which comes sooner than anybody likes, and that pink returning to a face is the surface still cold and not the core. He had once taken a core temperature with a glass under a tongue and read the number off it in a white room. Now he could not lift a crust two inches to his own mouth. That was the joke, and it was not a good one, and he had learned to find it funny without laughing, which is a way of laughing that costs less and keeps the same blade in it. The infant wanted the crust lifted, the thread wiped and a hand that would close on the pot handle set where the fingers could reach it. Both were true, the second was louder, neither won, and the quarrel between them was the work.

\person{Sella Vail} came to \place{the hearth} before she came to the basket, fire before basket, as she had come at both ends of every day for three years. She told the coals it was \lore{the thaw}, and turned them with the back of a broken pot, the way a woman turns a thing that has been left to keep overnight and must not be woken all at once. “The house will keep it,” she said, where another woman would have said I, and the sentence went to the fire and not to the wicker. She stood a moment counting a small intake, a hold and a letting-go toward no opening, giving neither gap a figure, and the two gaps stayed where they were, for a finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged. Her finger lay on top of \place{the loaf shelf}'s front edge so the charcoal tally underneath stayed unread, and her lips moved along a two-gapped row with neither gap figured. She did not look into the basket, and the boy was part of the load she carried and not a person she was addressing.

Her eye went once to \place{the clay jar} standing cold under \lore{lead glaze}, and her hand shut half over nothing, the little-door burn on the back of her left hand closing on air; the cord of \lore{the widow's mark} at her throat was oiled where a working thumb had touched it ten thousand times, and the mark hung where it hung and was not touched, because a morning and a hearth are not openings. Seven openings in seven months were seven and not a rule, the sixth held behind at \place{Ash} 2 without a name, and no eighth was made due. At \place{the pot board} she lifted the twice-mended pot with her thumb first where the wire crossed the crack, held the weight a half-beat before she let it come on the handle, and left the hairline showing between old-brass staple and tighter wire. “Leave the crack showing. People trust a thing they can see has been handled,” she told the pot. “If it's worth mending it's worth mending twice. The second time is the real one,” she told the fire. Both sayings went to the pot and the fire and never to a person, improved in the keeping and very likely not as good as the man had said them, and repair stayed apart from restoration, for a restored thing hides the hand that handled it and a mended thing leaves that hand showing. She turned the dressed grain under wool with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child, her fingers going in and coming out without closing, no figure lifted off it and no handful, and set the pot rim two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat at the setting and no look toward the bed. The half-beat was the distance between intention and arrest, bought at nothing and not a gift, for a gift wants a giver standing and none stood; he had once known a surgeon who could not resist closing that distance in a patient and had learned, by closing it, what it cost. The rim stayed where it was set. The hand could not close two inches on it and did not try.

\scenebreak

The file came out on the towel as instruments come out on a towel, and he went over it by the place of each thing and not by its name. Three hundred and six honest answers stood on the sixth place, each an object and not a power, and not one had stood up and called itself a doctrine, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood. Six hearings of \lore{the full note} stood beside and never among, the sixth the small flat knock out of \person[Dessa Silt] as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go and never among the sources, and the seventh place stayed empty and none was invented to fill it. The heard bell, four, four and a gap, heard at \place{the Cross} with nobody crossing himself, stayed in its square beside the told bell of \lore{the Long Night}; the told figure stood beside the paid figure with no signature on the space between and no doctrine standing up out of nine. Nine record-first hands kept no tenth, with \person{Ilva}'s ninth on a place of its own and \person{Hessa Rale}'s eighth a finger's width away, the two women offstage and neither promoted by absence; nine stoppings kept no tenth before the seventh year at its very end, and the loan stood uncalled.

Five held objects sat each on its place with a finger between every pair, the staying at the inch, the early letting-go, the handful let fall at the rack end, the thumb's resting on the uncut seventh, the sixth opening at the kiln mouth; the pot-crack resting lay beside them, the hand-at-the-sill breath beside that, the extra holding Jorra left in the cold iron beside all, and the setting-down-wrong-and-swept-around stood beside the premature note of Smoke 14, each found where put and not made again, none a sixth, a seventh or an eighth, no payer standing under any. The burned slate stood at five, the shut-room tellings at two, the well-saying at four shapings, the row at five askings with no witness and expressly no sixth. The Ash 5 joining, the Ash 17 direction and the Ash 23 direction stood each where they had been put, held apart with a finger between every pair, and bought no lesson. The river's winter skin lay on a place of its own beside \lore{water over water}, found where put and not made again, not a fourth covering, not a sixth low thing, not a hearing, not spent on this day or on a date three mornings off. Nine arithmetics of the year stood behind one unsigned figure, all three subtractions of Ash locked and unread in \person{Berr Clay}'s box up \place{the clay road} with the key gone up in a coat pocket that did not come back down; the figure read was still not the figure paid, no signature on the space, and no doctrine stood up out of nine. The promise under \place{the Vail wheel-shed} was neither given nor withdrawn, and \emph{he's not Vail} stayed sealed as a lid nobody had tried to lift in three years. Three counts in three places did not become one: the fire's four low pulls with its fifth unpaid, \place{the boards}' four nails on the near one and four on the next with a thumb-width knot where a fifth might have been driven and was not overhead, and the river's low over lower under its skin through \place{the finger's-width door}; agreeing any two would want a payer put under both and none stood, and the bell's gap stayed in its square. Attention was the one thing no institution in Grath had thought to tax, and it was the whole of what he had spent, and it cost nobody anything and bought nothing that could be entered on a slate.

\scenebreak

The hand goes to the mark before the kiln and at no other hour, and a morning is not an opening. The mark hung untouched this day, and no hand took it up for a birthday or for anything else; seven openings in seven months were seven and not a rule, the sixth stood behind at Ash 2 without a name, the seventh stood behind the household from \place{Stone} 5 without a lesson anybody named, and no eighth was made due and the empty place where one might have stood was not dug for or filled. The first firing put behind the household was not brought forward as sign or mending; the setting-down-wrong stayed gathered once on its own place beside the premature note of Smoke 14; nothing crossed out of any of it into this morning to make a cause or a payment. \place{Stone} 10's Account-day and market stood one morning beyond the last of the three days he was living, and \lore{the Hearth Drawing} of Stone 12 stood three mornings further, both named only as dates inside his exact keeping and brought into no day that had not earned them, for nothing is made due by a year finished.

In the small hours the hand outside the wool came up an inch toward the river's note under the finger's-width door, the way a hand goes up in a field it has learned not to disturb. Most of what a hand does in a field is wait, and he had once taken a hand into a cavity slowly, with the whole of the attention, and knew the difference between the moving and the waiting. The hand did not reach, and the inch was not a reach. It took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole on the note's own finishing under the ice, let the intake go, and came straight down and laid itself back against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. The note had done before the hand came down, so the letting-go was neither early nor stayed, and the hand did not stay; it was the note's own finishing under the covering that ended the interval and not any decision of the hand. The intake was not a breath kept for warmth, the interval not a stopping of the river, the note not his. Nothing crossed under the roof in that inch as sign, lesson, mending, cause, payment or payer, and no sixth hearing, seventh opening, sixth held object, sixth telling, third shut-room telling, sixth asking, fifth shaping, tenth hand or tenth stopping was supplied to make a row come out even.

He filed it as answer three hundred and seven: exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the nine arithmetics, the bell's gap, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the held breath at the sill, the staying at the inch, the early letting-go, the seventh opening, the pot-crack resting, the river's winter skin, the setting-down-wrong, the ninth hand at the well, the Ash 5 joining, the Ash 17 direction, the Ash 23 direction, the turned writing, the strip in the sleeve nor his own kept gaps. Three hundred and seven honest answers stood on the sixth place, each an object and not a power, and not one stood up and called itself a doctrine. Underneath the exactness the infant went on wanting the crust lifted two inches to his mouth, the thread wiped and a hand that would close on the pot handle; both were true, the second was louder, neither won, the two registers quarrelling without verdict in the ordinary small hours of the first morning of a second year.

Stone kept its seventh day without a boundary drawn and neither of the district's two habits of naming its months was set right or left in so many words; the month carried both names the way a pot carries two handles and is none the worse for having them. The deep-winter month in which he was born held, come round again and not recognised. The hand lay back against the fold on answer three hundred and seven; the file lay open on the towel at the head of the bed with every gap left gap; the twice-mended pot stood on the pot board with the hairline of the original break showing between old-brass staple and tighter wire; one stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from his ear with its empty place unnamed and no third, the thread frozen and unwiped; nine arithmetics rode locked and unread up the clay road in Berr Clay's box with the key gone up in a coat pocket and not coming back down; and through the finger's-width door the river, \place{the Ilm}, went under its winter skin, low over lower, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming, while the boards overhead kept four nails on the near one, four on the next and a thumb-width knot where a fifth might have been driven and was not. Cold had stopped at cheek, temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, as at the end of a line written out for it with neither an order to go farther nor an order to go away, and the afterdrop waited past all three. Three hundred and sixty-six stood exact where nobody could see it, a whole year finished and named by no roof. Stone 7 was done and the deep-winter month held. Forward, not closed.
