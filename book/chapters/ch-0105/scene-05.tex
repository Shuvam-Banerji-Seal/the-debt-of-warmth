\daymark{368}{Stone 9, Ninthday, Y.H. 813}

The hand lay flat against the fold on answer three hundred and ten, laid down where the next hand would find it without looking, and it lay there in the small hours under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof at \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill}, \place{Grath}. The room had not moved in the inch the hand had spent. The count went three hundred and sixty-seven to three hundred and sixty-eight in the dark, six months and one hundred and eighty-seven days becoming six months and one hundred and eighty-eight, told by nobody. He kept the number exactly as he kept all of them and did not stand it up as a line for anybody's foot to cross. There was no loaf set aside, no figure entered, no word said over a basket that would have told a person a number had finished. The house woke to the grammar it had woken to for three years and went about its small work without looking at the space where a celebration would have stood, because nobody under any roof had put one there. A whole year stood edge to edge behind him and nobody under any roof could say what a year would count.

\person[Corin]{The boy} lay on \place{the bed} at the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf}, awake and silent. The file lay open on \place{the towel} at the head of the bed with every gap left gap, the way instruments lie on a towel when a case is done and before the next is called. One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from his ear, gone harder and browner in the dry kiln heat, its corner standing a hair off the wool, its empty place unnamed and no third come in the night to make the row come out even. The thread of drool at his mouth had frozen and was unwiped, because nobody under any roof has ever been given an instruction about the thread and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. Cold had stopped at cheek, temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rides back, as at the end of a line written out for it with neither an order to go farther nor an order to go away, and \lore{the Afterdrop} waited past all three, past a warming nobody in this house would perform.

He knew the composed face that was not composure. He knew a cold patient cannot clot and will seem better before he is worse, and must be warmed from the outside and slowly, never rubbed and never set too near the fire, and that pink returning to a face is the surface still cold and not the core; he knew the order in which a body takes heat back, the small joint first and the core last, and the order in which it gives it up, the reverse, which comes sooner than anybody likes. A trauma surgeon who could reduce a shoulder in the dark could not lift a crust two inches to his own mouth. That was the joke and it cost him nothing and was not funny enough to spend. The infant wanted the crust lifted, the thread wiped and a hand that would close on the pot handle set two inches beyond the reaching hand. Both were true, the second was louder, neither won, and the quarrel was the work.

The register took the room in its old order, by the small sounds first because the small sounds do not lie and the large ones are mostly weather: the dry rasp of a coal settling under ash, the small tick of cooling clay along the loaf shelf, the low note of the river under its winter skin through \place{the finger's-width door}, muffled as a voice is muffled by a shut door. Under the floor \lore{the carried fire} gave four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer without a name three hundred and sixty-eight days. Overhead \place{the boards} kept four nails on the near one, four on the next and a thumb-width knot where a fifth might have been driven and was not. He took the three counts in and kept them where they were and did not join them, for a finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged, and agreement between any two would have wanted a payer put under both and none stood. Of all that had honestly grown in three hundred and sixty-eight days the number was the only thing, and the number was worth nothing to the body that kept it.

\scenebreak

First light came round to the high side of the wall and \person{Sella Vail} came to \place{the hearth} before the basket, fire before basket, as she had come at both ends of every day for three years. “It's the thaw,” she told the coals, and turned them with the back of a broken pot. “The house will keep it,” she said, where another woman would have said I. Beneath the woodsmoke came the faint sour mineral tang of \place{Ash} along the skirting board, the ridge unswept and thickened in the month's dry cold, and she had not swept it in two winters and was not sweeping it now. She stood a moment counting a small intake, a hold and a letting-go toward no opening, giving neither gap a figure. Her finger lay on top of the loaf shelf's front edge so the charcoal tally underneath stayed unread, and her lips moved along a two-gapped row and figured neither gap. A man who had filled the row to make it come out even would have wanted a payer under it and no payer stood.

One glance went to \place{the clay jar} standing cold under \lore{lead glaze}, and her hand shut half over nothing, the little-door burn on the back of her left hand closing on air. The cord of \lore{the widow's mark} at her throat was oiled where a working thumb had touched it ten thousand times, and the mark hung where it hung and was not touched, because a morning and a hearth are not openings. Seven openings in seven months were seven and not a rule, the sixth stood behind at Ash 2 without a name, the seventh stood behind the household from \place{Stone} 5 without a lesson anybody named, and no eighth was made due and the empty place where one might have stood was not dug for or filled. The day before had wanted no hand on the mark and had got none; this day wanted none and got none, and the two mornings did not join into a row.

At \place{the pot board} she lifted the twice-mended pot with her thumb first where the wire crossed the crack, held the weight a half-beat before she let it come on the handle, and left the hairline showing between old-brass staple and tighter wire. “Leave the crack showing. People trust a thing they can see has been handled,” she told the pot. “If it's worth mending it's worth mending twice. The second time is the real one,” she told the fire. Both sayings went to the pot and the fire and never to a person, improved in the keeping and very likely not as good as the man had said them, and repair stayed apart from restoration, for a restored thing hides the hand that handled it and a mended thing leaves that hand showing. The man whose proverbs those were had died in \lore{the Long Cold}; the salt-stiff coat with its left-shoulder crease kept \person{Garl Vail}'s broader shape on its peg behind the door, and nobody took it down. His name had not been spoken under this roof three hundred and sixty-eight days, and the debt against a firing of tiles stood uncalled the same number, circled without a name and worse for being uncalled.

She turned the dressed grain under wool with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child, her fingers going in and coming out without closing, no figure lifted off it and no handful. The pot rim went down two inches beyond the reaching hand, with a half-beat at the setting and no look toward the bed. The half-beat was the distance between intention and arrest, bought at nothing and not a gift, for a gift wants a giver standing and none stood. Instruction went to the wool and the river wind and the fire she would bank against their coming back and never to the wicker. “Keep it off his ears,” she told the wool. “It's the river wind.” The hand under the fold lay still. The crust kept its hair-lifted corner and its empty place, the thread kept its frozen place at his mouth, and the morning went by in its own order without a boundary drawn anywhere.

He took the whole of it as maintenance on a vessel and not as a sign. A pot set level out of a draught, a fire told the weather, a crack left showing, a rim set two inches beyond a hand that could not reach it: each was a thing done to keep a room live, and none had been done for him and none had been withheld from him, which was the difference between a household and an account. He had once kept a field clean by standing at its edge and letting no hand come in that did not know what it was doing, and here he stood at the edge of a room where the hands knew exactly what they were doing and had never once been told what the doing was for. The surgeon read the grammar and found it sound. The infant under the wool wanted the crust lifted and the thread wiped, and the reading did not feed him.

\scenebreak

The file came out on the towel as instruments come out on a towel, and he went over it by the place of each thing. Three hundred and ten honest answers stood on the sixth place, each an object and not a power and not one standing up and calling itself a doctrine, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood. Six hearings of \lore{the full note} stood beside and never among, the sixth the small flat knock out of \person[Dessa Silt] as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go, and the seventh place stayed empty and was not dug for or filled. The heard bell, four, four and a gap, had gone at the Cross on Ash 30 with nobody crossing himself and stayed in its square beside the told bell of \lore{the Long Night}; \person{Berr Clay}'s told figure stood beside the paid figure with no signature on the space and no doctrine out of nine, the nine arithmetics of the year behind it and all three Ash subtractions locked and unread in his box up \place{the clay road}, the key gone up in a coat pocket and not coming back down. Nine record-first hands kept no tenth, \person{Ilva}'s notched strip still in her sleeve offstage and \person{Hessa Rale}'s writing still unread offstage; nine stoppings kept no tenth before the seventh year at its very end and the loan stood uncalled.

Five held objects kept their places with the pot-crack resting beside them and the held breath at the sill beside that, the staying at the inch and the early letting-go and the handful and the thumb's resting each on a place of its own with a finger between every pair, and the setting-down-wrong gathered once on its own place beside the premature note of \place{Smoke} 14. The burned slate stood at five, the shut-room tellings at two, the well-saying at four shapings and the row at five askings with no witness. Ash 5's joining, the Ash 17 direction and the Ash 23 direction stood apart with a finger between every pair. The river's winter skin sat on its own place beside \lore{water over water}, not a fourth covering, not a hearing, not a sign and not spent on the birthday two days behind, this morning or the Drawing; over \place{the Ilm} it lay low over lower under a covering put on by the cold and taken off by the cold, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming. \place{the Vail wheel-shed} kept its promise ungiven and \emph{he's not Vail} stayed sealed as a lid nobody had tried to lift in three years. Three counts in three places did not become one and the bell's gap stayed in its square: the carried fire under the floor, the boards overhead and the river through the finger's-width door. No sixth hearing, seventh opening, sixth held object, sixth telling, third shut-room telling, sixth asking, fifth shaping, tenth hand or tenth stopping was supplied to make any row come out even.

Two dates stood where dates stand and were not brought into a day that had not earned them. Stone 10, the month's first Account-day and market, stood one morning beyond the close, with its reading and its bell and its figure going twice over a basket; the \lore{Hearth Drawing} of Stone 12 stood three mornings further, with its cold iron tube and its rekindled child worse on the third day and a reason that had yet to be given. Both were named only inside the boy's exact keeping, and neither was made due by a year finished. The month carried both its names the way a pot carries two handles, the older women naming it by the weather and the slatemen by the count of Account-days, and no hand under any roof set either name right or left on this morning. A whole year could end without a boundary drawn and no account in the district would alter by one mark on that account, which was the truest thing he knew about the number he kept and the least useful. He had stopped expecting the question, which was not the same as not wanting it.

The morning gave way to the middle of the day without a boundary drawn, the way a banked coal gives way from one degree of heat to the next and is the same coal throughout. No market came, no Cross, no bell; no collector went up the clay road and no runner came down it, and no ownerless voice came over the boards to put a sixth telling where five stood or a third shaping where four stood. The count of loaves went on in the woman's lips and the count of days went on in his, and the two counts did not meet and were not one, because a house keeps its days in loaves and glances and in the space where a loaf has stood, and a boy keeps them in a number nobody asks to see.

\scenebreak

In the small hours the hand outside the wool came up an inch toward the river's note under the finger's-width door, the way a hand goes up in a field it has learned not to disturb. Most of what a hand does in a field is wait, and he had once taken a hand into a cavity slowly, with the whole of the attention, and knew the difference between the moving and the waiting. The hand did not reach, and the inch was not a reach. It took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole on the note's own finishing under the ice, let the intake go, and came straight down and laid itself back against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. The note had done before the hand came down, so the letting-go was neither early nor stayed, and the hand did not stay; it was the note's own finishing under the covering that ended the interval and not any decision of the hand. The intake was not a breath kept for warmth, the interval not a stopping of the river, the note not his.

He filed it as answer three hundred and eleven: exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the nine arithmetics, the bell's gap, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the held breath at the sill, the staying at the inch, the early letting-go, the seventh opening, the pot-crack resting, the river's winter skin, the setting-down-wrong, the ninth hand, the Ash 5 joining, the Ash 17 direction, the Ash 23 direction, the turned writing, the strip in the sleeve nor his own kept gaps. Three hundred and eleven honest answers stood on the sixth place, each an object and not a power, and not one stood up and called itself a doctrine. Underneath the exactness the infant went on wanting the crust lifted two inches to his mouth, the thread wiped and a hand that would close on the pot handle; both were true, the second was louder, neither won, and the quarrel was the work.

The boards overhead kept four nails on the near one, four on the next and a thumb-width knot where a fifth might have been driven and was not, and the small hand lay flat against the fold on answer three hundred and eleven. Three hundred and sixty-eight stood exact, six months and one hundred and eighty-eight days told by nobody, a whole year finished and named by no roof, and one morning between it and Stone 10. The twice-mended pot stood on the pot board with the hairline of its first break showing between old-brass staple and tighter wire, and its rim stood two inches beyond the reaching hand. His name had not been spoken under this roof three hundred and sixty-eight days, and fire's fifth stood unpaid the same number with no payer standing to it. The file lay open on the towel with every gap left gap, and through the finger's-width door the river went low over lower under its winter skin, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming. Stone kept its ninth day without a boundary drawn. Forward, not closed.
