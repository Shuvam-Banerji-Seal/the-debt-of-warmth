## How the final moment pulls into the next chapter

The chapter closes on the hand laid back against the fold on answer **312** and the count exact at **three hundred and sixty-eight** — six months and one hundred and eighty-eight days told by nobody, a whole year finished and named by no roof. Nothing in the close is spent: the fire's fifth stays unpaid and Garl's name unspoken 368 days, the river stays under its winter skin on its own place beside water-over-water, seven openings in seven months stay seven and not a rule with no eighth made due, nine arithmetics stand behind one unsigned figure with all three Ash subtractions locked and unread in Berr's box up the clay road, the promise under the wheel-shed is neither given nor withdrawn and the loan stands uncalled.

Day 369, Stone 10, Tenthday, stands **one morning** beyond this close. It earns its own Account-day and market: Berr Clay will come up short off the clay road, wipe his thumb twice, read one in twelve twice over the basket, call the levy and deny it with *your house*, never I, and go off with the box locked and the month's true subtraction inside, believing he has done a kindness — **ten readings are ten and not one account**, the figure read still not the figure paid, no signature on the space and no doctrine out of ten. Sella will set the basket in the moot-post's finger's width of shade and stand half a pace off; Hessa will carry the slate up the road edge-on and unread; the bell will go four, four and a gap with nobody crossing himself. And Day 371, Stone 12, the Hearth Drawing of Y.H. 813, stands three mornings beyond the close: the cold iron tube, a vessel's small flat knock kept among the six hearings and not made a seventh, a rekindled child worse on the third day, the collector's grey fingertips and a reason, all seen from a basket at the edge of the light. The boy will take the tenth morning and the Drawing as they come, field taken as found and left as found, and the hand will go on being laid down where the next hand will find it without looking. The count will go on. Forward, not closed.
