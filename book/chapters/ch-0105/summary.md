## Chronicle

Under the Vail kiln-house roof the deep-winter month came round to the day a year before when a boy had been carried in and given a number nobody spoke. Stone 7 passed as an ordinary tending day: no loaf set aside, no figure entered, no word over the basket to tell a person that a year had finished. The older women named the month by the weather and the slatemen by the count of Account-days, and neither habit was set right or left; a house kept its days in loaves and glances and in the space where a loaf had stood, and could not say that a year had turned. Only the boy kept three hundred and sixty-six exactly, stood edge to edge in the only count that was exact, and did not stand it up as a line for anybody's foot to cross.

Sella Vail came to the hearth before the basket at both ends of each day, told the coals it was the thaw, turned them with the back of a broken pot, said *The house will keep it* where another woman would have said I, and set the pot rim two inches beyond a hand that could not close on it. In the yard she went wide around seven walked-around places, widest of all around the returning-line place where a hand had gone in on Seed 15 and had not come out, and gave no number to any of them. The frost off the well's north lip, the skin on the trough and the clouded plate of ice on the flat stone stayed weather entered nowhere, with a finger between every pair; the river went low over lower under its winter skin and was not spent on a day nobody named. Tarn kept six where the house could check and the seventh at the lip beside an unforged collector sentence, told the wood *Six counted. The next I don't count*, brought the ear-sentence to the word that would call the loan and stopped, a ninth stopping with no tenth. Jorra parted dusty forearms from clean as clot from vessel, spoke to the wheel before any person, and put a bent finger into the cold damper ahead of a sentence unfinished, one iron with two bites, taking it out without a second interval.

On Stone 8 the house woke to the same grammar and no mark of the day before; on Stone 9 a whole year stood finished and named by no roof. Six small intakes came and went on the river's note under the door, answers 307 through 312, each exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment. At the close the hand lay flat against the fold where the next hand would find it without looking, the file open on the towel with every gap left gap, and the count at three hundred and sixty-eight, six months and one hundred and eighty-eight days told by nobody.
