The afternoon came on without a boundary drawn, the way light goes off a banked fire by degrees and is the same fire throughout. \person{Sella Vail} carried \person[Corin]{the boy} down from under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof at the half-pace she kept from every open slate, the whole of her attention on the level of the basket and none of it on the face inside it, and the instruction went to the wool, to the river wind, to the fire she would bank against their coming back, and never to the wicker. “Keep it off his ears,” she told the wool. “It's the river wind.” Out in \place{the Clay Yard} the dry cold lay over the ground like a sheet laid down and not tucked, and the day went around its places as it had gone around them for years, with a look and a wide step and no number.

She went widest around \place[the worked strips]{the returning-line place}, where a hand had gone in on Seed 15 and had not come out, giving it the room a woman gives a pail of hot water set where a child might knock it; and around the other six without dwelling. The clean dark line at \place{the Vail corner} with the finer grey beside it called \lore{the thaw} lay unswept and thickened in the month's dry cold. The space beneath \place[the drying racks]{the lowest rack} kept its broken birth-slates, entered by nobody. The round ring where the shallow clay pan had once stood kept no crumb, chip, print or word. \place{the yard well} had its rope stiff in the cold, \place{the trough} its skin drawn edge to edge, and the silt-clouded plate of ice on \place{the flat stone by the well} stood a little out of the path of feet. Seven places gone around with a look and a step, and the seven was her own keeping and was not said over the basket.

Three coverings stayed weather entered nowhere, with a finger between every pair: the white frost gone off the well's north lip, the skin on the trough, the clouded plate on its flat stone. Each was put on by the cold and would be taken off by the cold, and she had given none of them a name and was not giving one now. Out beyond the door, over \place{the Ilm}, \lore{water over water} went low over lower under a winter skin that had lain there since the afternoon of Stone 1, a covering put on and taken off by the cold and entered nowhere, found where it was put on every morning since and not made again. It was not a fourth covering. It was not a sixth low thing, not a hearing, not a sign, and it was not spent on a birthday nobody had named or on a Drawing still three mornings off. The boy found it where it lay and left it lie. Dressed grain lay covered under wool at \place{the rack end}; her fingers went in and came out without closing, and no figure came off the heap and no handful was lifted, the empty fingers a keeping beside the one object of Bone 22 and not a second handful.

The month's hunts and repairs went by as directions judged by the carrying arm and the weather, never as figures. A man come down from \place{Cold Acre} shifted a hare across his shoulders at the well and, to a woman who asked what the carrying had cost, said, “Ask the weather. It carried the snow. It can carry the price,” and went off toward \place{the clay road} with the hare unweighed and nobody opening a slate. Another with a cracked jar in a sling of rope asked what the month's mending would want and was told to ask the pot; and the pot, being a pot, said nothing. A woman with a basket of kindling asked what the seventh year would want when it came, and two men mending a strip of fence said the thaw would be early and the thaw would be late. To each of them \person{Tarn} said, “Yes, house,” and kept the late, because a late thaw puts off the paying. He agreed so thoroughly that there was nowhere for an argument to find a foot. No collector came up the clay road and no runner came down it; there was no market, no Cross and no bell, and no ownerless voice came over \place{the boards} to put a sixth telling where five stood or a fifth shaping where four stood.

At \place{the kindling} Tarn kept six where the house could check and the seventh at the lip beyond, where it could not, and \person{Garl Vail}'s unforged collector sentence lay beside the seventh with no name on the man who turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on the debt. The small knife lay at the very edge of its sill, where a hand could be checked and where a hand could also slip it off. He passed his hand over blade and sill as a man passes a hand over a tool put back where it belongs, and told the wood, “Six counted. The next I don't count,” cutting nothing and counting nothing. The pale clip-ring at his left wrist caught what little light there was and gave it back; the Ash was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line, which no scrubbing lifted and which he had long ago stopped trying to lift.

The held breath he had left at the sill on Ash 14 was where it had been put. He passed over it as a man passes over a tool returned to its place, found it, and did not make it again. The thumb's resting on the uncut seventh of Bone 26 was likewise found where it lay and not made again. Neither became a sixth held object, a seventh of anything, a tenth stopping, the seventh year given, the calling of the loan, a habit, a way, a sign, a payment or a rule, with no payer standing under either.

Then he brought the ear-sentence up. It was the sentence about an ear under this roof being worth more than the debt that held it going, and he brought it all the way to the word that would call the loan and stopped there, with no figure after it and no name. He stopped as a man stops at the edge of a field and does not step in: the ninth stopping, with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end. The breath he drew did not go in. The Seed 16 breath stayed unspent at the top of the reach, the loan stayed uncalled, and he did not look along the road. The equality under the roof held at the place it had moved to --- two ears, two silences, a loan that could hear and had never been called --- with no payer named under it and nobody to say what the equality was worth.

The boy watched the hand go up to the knife and away again, and took it apart as he took all hands apart. It was neither the hand that worked nor the hand that only rested, but the hand that checks and goes away: the movement of a surgeon drawing his own hand out of a field before the dressing goes on, which is the same movement as a man's hand leaving a thing he is not permitted to own. Tarn's chest had been tight since the dark. His stroke over \place{the flagstones} went on past the shallow bright patch without the half-check, and the returned half-check of Bone 8 stayed where it had lain on the stones, a place kept and not a rule, joined to no cause and no ear.

\person{Jorra Vail} came through \place{the finger's-width door} off \place{the Vail wheel-shed} with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bent little finger and white in a fist-crease where a hand had opened. It was not the bone-coloured \lore{slip} of the wheel. Men unnamed had gone low into \place{the back room} before first grey, and he gave the house no name for them and did not say the room was cold. He kept his dusty forearms and his clean ones on one tray and parted them as a surgeon parts clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had been broken nor whether it was a birth-slate, while the broken ones lay under the lowest rack and were entered by nobody.

He spoke to the wheel before he spoke to any person. “It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady,” he told it. To a pot he said, “You'll do,” in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either. He knew the \lore{full note} that pot would give three turns before it left his hands, with air under it and a want in it, and kept the knowing where nobody could make a song of it, withheld from any master come up from \place{High Kiln} who would call it early and sing a pot before its time. That knowing sat on a tray of its own beside the hearings and never among them, and no seventh hearing stood up in the empty place.

Sella had not finished telling him the fire was low. His bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper} ahead of her sentence, took the cold out of the fingertip, moved the tongue a hair's breadth, and was left standing so that the end of her instruction arrived at the pot and the household straightened around the waiting as cloth straightens when a weight is lifted. One piece of iron had two bites, the holding and the taking, and there was no Grathic word for both mercies. The boy read the two bites as anatomy under pressure. He had put a clamp across a vessel a thousand times and knew the line where the heat stopped, the flow held and the flow stopped in the same iron, mercy and mutilation in one bite, with a price that had to be named somewhere or it would be paid by somebody who had not agreed to pay it. Here the bite stood in a damper and did its work and went on standing in the room, an object among objects, and he could enter neither the holding nor the stopped flow on any slate within reach of the basket.

Presently Jorra took the finger out. He did not leave the extra holding of Ash 26 a second time. The first interval of that holding stayed gathered once on a place of its own, found where put and not made again, for a second would be a habit and a third a way, and both want a payer and none stood. \emph{he's not Vail} stayed sealed as a lid nobody had tried to lift in three years, and the promise of the boy's name to the men who burn slates was neither given nor withdrawn. It went on gathering interest against the burned slate at five, the shut-room tellings at two, the row at five askings with no witness, and the Ash 5 joining, the Ash 17 direction and the Ash 23 direction held apart with a finger between every pair. No voice came over the boards to supply a sixth telling, a third shut-room telling, a sixth asking, a fifth shaping, a witness or a name. The boy filed the dust, the two bites and the unspent breath on three separate places and wrote no cause across any of them, because a cause is an account that has found a payer and none stood.

The going of the light came on without a boundary drawn, and the hearth grammar renewed in its old order. Sella went to \place{the hearth} before the basket, fire before basket, and told the coals it was \lore{the thaw}, turning them with the back of a broken pot. “The house will keep it,” she said, where another woman would have said I. She stood a moment counting a small intake, a hold and a letting-go toward no opening, giving neither gap a figure. Her finger lay on top of \place{the loaf shelf}'s front edge so the charcoal tally underneath stayed unread, and her lips moved along a two-gapped row and figured neither gap. One glance went to \place{the clay jar} standing cold under \lore{lead glaze}, and her hand shut half over nothing, the little-door burn on the back of her left hand closing on air. \lore{The widow's mark} at her throat was oiled where a working thumb had touched it ten thousand times, and it hung where it hung and was not touched, because an afternoon and a going of the light are not openings; seven openings in seven months were seven and not a rule, the sixth behind at Ash 2 without a name and the seventh behind the household from \place{Stone} 5 without a lesson, and no eighth was made due.

At \place{the pot board} she lifted the twice-mended pot with her thumb first where the wire crossed the crack, held the weight a half-beat before she let it come on the handle, and left the hairline showing between old-brass staple and tighter wire. “Leave the crack showing. People trust a thing they can see has been handled,” she told the pot. “If it's worth mending it's worth mending twice. The second time is the real one,” she told the fire. Both sayings went to the pot and the fire and never to a person, improved in the keeping and very likely not as good as the man had said them, and repair stayed apart from restoration, for a restored thing hides the hand that handled it and a mended thing leaves that hand showing. The dressed grain went under wool with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child, and the pot rim went down two inches beyond the reaching hand, with a half-beat at the setting and no look toward \place{the bed}.

She carried him up at the half-pace from every open slate and set him on his bed under the roof and never in \place{the Debt Loft}. She put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face, let her hand rest the half-beat longer than the setting needed before it went away, and left the thread of drool frozen and unwiped, because nobody under any roof has ever been given an instruction about the thread. She said nothing of where they had been, and that was not a refusal to tell a basket what a keeping was worth, for nobody under any roof tells a basket that. The crust kept its hair-lifted corner and its empty place unnamed, and no third came in to make the row come out even. Under the floor \lore{the carried fire} gave four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer without a name three hundred and sixty-seven days; \person{Garl Vail}'s name had not been spoken under this roof the same number. The count stood at three hundred and sixty-seven exact, six months and one hundred and eighty-seven days told by nobody, and the year that had finished stood behind the boy without a line drawn under it or a figure anybody could be asked about.

In the small hours the file came out on \place{the towel} as instruments come out on a towel, and he went over it by the place of each thing. Three hundred and ten honest answers would stand on the sixth place when this one was laid down, each an object and not a power and not one standing up and calling itself a doctrine, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood. Six hearings of the full note stood beside and never among, the sixth the small flat knock out of \person[Dessa Silt] as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go, and the seventh place stayed empty. The heard bell, four, four and a gap, stayed in its square beside the told bell of \lore{the Long Night}; \person{Berr Clay}'s told figure stood beside the paid figure with no signature on the space and no doctrine out of nine, the nine arithmetics of the year behind it and all three Ash subtractions locked and unread in his box up the clay road, the key gone up in a coat pocket and not coming back down. Nine record-first hands kept no tenth, \person{Ilva}'s strip still in her sleeve offstage and \person{Hessa Rale}'s writing still unread offstage; nine stoppings kept no tenth before the seventh year at its very end and the loan stood uncalled. Five held objects kept their places with the pot-crack resting beside them and the held breath at the sill beside that, the setting-down-wrong gathered once beside the premature note of \place{Smoke} 14; the burned slate stood at five, the shut-room tellings at two, the well-saying at four shapings, the row at five askings with no witness. Three counts in three places did not become one and the bell's gap stayed in its square. No sixth hearing, seventh opening, sixth held object, sixth telling, sixth asking, fifth shaping, tenth hand or tenth stopping was supplied to make any row come out even.

The hand outside the wool came up an inch toward the river's note under the finger's-width door, the way a hand goes up in a field it has learned not to disturb. Most of what a hand does in a field is wait, and he had once taken a hand into a cavity slowly, with the whole of the attention, and knew the difference between the moving and the waiting. The hand did not reach, and the inch was not a reach. It took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole on the note's own finishing under the ice, let the intake go, and came straight down and laid itself back against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. The note had done before the hand came down, so the letting-go was neither early nor stayed, and the hand did not stay; it was the note's own finishing under the covering that ended the interval and not any decision of the hand. He filed it as answer three hundred and ten: exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the nine arithmetics, the bell's gap, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the held breath at the sill, the staying at the inch, the early letting-go, the seventh opening, the pot-crack resting, the river's winter skin, the setting-down-wrong, the ninth hand, the Ash 5 joining, the Ash 17 direction, the Ash 23 direction, the two bites, the held dust, the turned writing, the strip in the sleeve nor his own kept gaps. Underneath the exactness the infant went on wanting the crust lifted two inches to his mouth, the thread wiped and a hand that would close on the pot handle; both were true, the second was louder, neither won, and the quarrel was the work.

The bent finger had been taken out of the cold damper without a second interval, and the flagstones kept the shallow bright patch where Tarn's stroke had gone on without the half-check, and the hand lay flat again against the fold on answer three hundred and ten with the promise under the wheel-shed neither given nor withdrawn. Through the finger's-width door the river went low over lower under its winter skin, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming. Stone kept its eighth day without a boundary drawn. Forward, not closed.
