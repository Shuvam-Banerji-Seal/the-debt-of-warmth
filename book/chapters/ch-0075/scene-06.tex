Frost went out of the yard without anybody drawing a line under it. The light thinned as heat goes out of a pot set off the coals and left standing on a cold stone --- not ending, only drawing back by degrees, first the glare on the well lip, then the warmth in the flat \place{the flagstones}, then the last thin brightness along \place{the rack end} where the dressed grain lay under wool. Neither of the district's two habits of naming the months was set right that afternoon, and neither was left wrong in so many words; the yard called the days what it had always called them and left the weather to answer for both. No lesson was made of four openings. The first firing of Frost stood behind the household with its hands washed and its face turned away, and the next stood in the first firing of a later month, far off, not hoped for and not counted toward. \person[Corin]{The boy} lay under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill}, \place{Grath}, carried there at the going of the morning and not yet carried up, and took the afternoon as it came.

He took the body as it presented itself, in the order the facts arrived, because that was the only order he had ever found trustworthy. One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from his ear, gone past the point where a child would eat it, and the empty place beside it kept its name to itself; no third had come in the afternoon to make the row come out even. The thread of drool at his mouth had frozen in the dry heat and was unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. Cold had come to cheek and temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, and stopped there as at the end of a line written out for it, with neither an order to go forward nor an order to go away. The afterdrop waited beyond all three, past a warming nobody in this house would perform. He knew the composed face that is not composure; he knew a cold patient cannot clot and will seem better before he is worse, and that a man must be warmed from the outside and slowly. He wanted the crust lifted the two inches to his mouth. Both were true, and in a body two hundred and seventy-eight days old the second was louder; the surgeon took the inventory in its old order --- crust, gap, thread, cold, the waiting --- and entered none of it, not even on the place where he kept the things he did not enter.

Through the finger's-width door the river kept its note of \lore{water over water}, a low thing over a lower thing, never numbered among the hearings; under the floor \lore{the carried fire} gave four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer without a name for two hundred and seventy-eight days, and nobody lifted the floor and nobody supplied the pull. Overhead \place{the boards} kept four nails on the near board, four on the next, and a thumb-width knot where the fifth nail should have stood. Three counts in three places, and none rose into another, because agreeing any two would want a payer put under both and no payer stood to either. Six hearings of \lore{the full note} stood beside and never among, five from four sources and the sixth, the small flat knock out of a woman as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go and never with the things that came from vessels; the empty place kept its emptiness and none was invented to fill it. The heard bell's four, four and a gap stayed in the square beside the told bell of \lore{the Long Night}, not folded into the three counts and not heard as a seventh of the full note.

Out on \place{the Clay Yard} the stayed frost lay on the north lip of \place{the yard well} past the going of the light, white where the yard had left it white, and the yard went on calling it weather. Women drew with the pail against the knee and set the rope down stiff, and went around \place{the trough} with a look and a wide step, and the plate of ice that \person{Sella Vail} had lifted in the morning stood on its flat stone where she had set it, out of the path of feet, clouded where the silt had risen into it. Nobody gave it a figure and nobody carried it away; the finding stayed entered nowhere, and the surgeon's reading of a skin laid over a depth stayed where he had laid it that morning, beside the stayed white and the gone white of Frost 4, with a finger between. He made no fourth likeness of the three and no cause of any two.

Then \person{Sella Vail} came up the yard with the basket at the half-pace from every open slate and set him on \place{the bed} under the roof and not in \place{the Debt Loft}. She put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face, and left the thread frozen where it lay. She went to the fire before she went to anything else and told the coals they were \lore{the thaw}. \emph{You'll go when you go}, she told them, and turned them with the back of a broken pot, and said \emph{The house will keep it} where another woman would have said \emph{I}. Her finger stayed on top of \place{the loaf shelf}'s front edge so the charcoal on the underside remained unread; her lips moved along a row standing with two gaps and gave neither gap a figure; she glanced once at the clay jar standing cold under its \lore{lead glaze} and shut her hand half over nothing. She stood the pot rim two inches beyond the reaching hand, with a half-beat at the setting and no look toward the bed. At no hour of that day did she give an instruction to the basket. \lore{The widow's mark} hung where it hung on its cord at her throat, and her hand did not go to it at a going of the light, because a going of the light is not an opening and the hand goes to the mark before the kiln and at no other hour.

At \place{the kindling} \person{Tarn} set the small knife at the very edge of its sill, six counted in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check and the seventh at the lip where it could not, with \person{Garl Vail}'s unforged collector sentence lying beside it and no name on the man who had turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on the debt. \emph{Six counted. The next I don't count}, he told the wood, in the tone of a man stating the weather. The sentence about an ear under this roof being worth more than the debt that held it came to his teeth and went as far as the word that would call the loan before stopping, with no figure and no name --- a remembered ninth stopping, and no tenth before the seventh year at its very end and still ungiven. He drew the breath that should have gone in at the top of the reach and did not spend it, his chest tight as it had been since the dark. \lore{Ash} was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line and no scrubbing lifted it, and the pale clip-ring at his left wrist held what light there was and gave it back. To those who wanted the thaw early and those who wanted it late he said \emph{Yes, house} to both, agreeing so thoroughly that no argument could find a foot, and kept wanting the late because it puts off the paying though the paying stood in the ruts. His stroke went over the flagstones past the shallow bright patch without the half-check, the stones still keeping the place where it had stopped. Up in \place{the Debt Loft}, which came to the boy only as a sound with a direction and never as a room he was taken into, the warm stone in its unmarked pot was cold and unreconciled by not trying, which is not forgiveness. No collector came up \place{the clay road} on Frost 7, 8 or 9 and none went down it.

\person{Jorra Vail} stood a moment at the edge of \place{the Vail wheel-shed} with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bend of his little finger, not bone-coloured slip, and parted dusty forearms from clean ones as a surgeon parts clot from vessel. \emph{It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady}, he told the wheel before he told any person, and told a pot \emph{You'll do} in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either. He knew the full note that pot would give three turns before it left his hands, with air under it and a want in it, and withheld the knowing on the near-full tray beside the hearings and never among them. His bent finger stood in the cold iron of \lore{the damper} ahead of \person{Sella Vail}'s unfinished sentence, one iron with two bites and no \lore{Grathic} word for both mercies. \place{the back room} kept its finger's width of open and its sound of low voices, and the house gave the men inside no name and did not say the room was cold; to the boy it was a direction and a sound. The burned-slate word stood at four from \place{Slate Row}, a direction over the boards and not a fifth telling; the two tellings of the counting room shut in the war stood at two and had not become three; three sayings about a pot stood as sayings, with the premature Smoke 14 note on its own place and no fourth likeness made a cause; the row question stood at five askings with one further shaping and no witness under any and no row named. He kept each where it lay and asked nothing that would join the dust on his forearms to a cause. The promise of the boy's name to the men who burned slates was neither given nor withdrawn, and \emph{he's not Vail} stayed sealed.

In the small hours, when the house had gone quiet into the shape it keeps when it is doing nothing anybody could enter, \person{Sella Vail} came once more to the fire, gave it its instruction and went away without looking toward the bed. \place{the towel} lay at the head of the bed where it had been laid, and the boy turned the file out on it as a man turns out instruments on a towel before a night he will not be called to: each thing in its own place with room between, each thing known by its weight before the eye fell on it, exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air.

Six hearings of the full note went down first, five from four sources and the sixth, the small flat knock out of a woman as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go and never with the things that came from vessels. The seventh place beside them was kept empty and was not filled to make a row come out even; an empty place kept empty is a finding, and a finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged. Beside them, in the square where it had stood since the last market, the heard bell kept its four, four and a gap next to the told bell of the Long Night, not folded into the three counts under the floor and not heard as a seventh of the note. The three sayings about a pot lay together on their own tray, and the premature note from Smoke 14 lay on a place apart, not a fourth likeness and not a cause; no pot was lost and no name had been given to what ailed the man carried out on Day 188. The burned-slate word stood at four, a direction over \place{the boards} and not a fifth telling; the two tellings of the counting room shut in the war stood at two and had not become three. The ownerless question lay on a seventh place with five askings and one further shaping, a finger between every pair, no witness under any and no row named; five askings are not one witness, and a sixth was not invented to make the number comfortable.

The nine stoppings went down one after another with a finger between; \person{Tarn}'s came to the ninth in the small hours of this same day and no tenth stood up before the seventh year at its very end and still ungiven, and a thing held nine times is still owed. The nine record-first hands went down on nine places with a finger between every pair, and \person{Ilva}'s ninth stayed where it lay in her sleeve, not read, not asked about, not taken a figure from, not joined to the charcoal under the shelf or the cord or the knots or the pages or the tins or the missing hand or the pale line, and she did not know she had been read. The seventh green was a colour and the seventh shape a shape, with no eighth of either and no name over either; a colour is not a hearing and a shape is not a seventh of the note. Smoke's third arithmetic stayed locked in \person[Berr Clay]{Berr}'s box going up \place{the clay road} toward \place{High Kiln}, and the printed one in twelve stood unchallenged while the figure read was still not the figure paid, with no signature on the space between. The dressed grain lay covered and unfigured under wool on the cool stone at the rack end, turned twice that morning for damp with a lighter touch each time, and the arithmetic that would read over it on the morrow did not know what it cost.

He set the small dry scrape of a pot's foot on a place of its own, and the carried man's pale line on another, and did not lay either across the other. \person{Garl Vail} stayed in objects and not in a story, his name unspoken two hundred and seventy-eight days: the salt-stiff coat with the crease at the left shoulder, the wheel-seat keeping its twenty-year lean and its hollow, the clean dark line with the finer grey beside it unswept, the debt against a firing of tiles neither paid nor settled nor done with, and worse for being uncalled. The finding that a house keeps itself by \lore{not-doings} stayed on its own place with the boy's own gaps on another and a finger between --- the two gaps above the shelf, the one crust in the fold and its unnamed empty place, the pan's gone ring, the field's empty place out on \place{the worked strips}, the returning-line place where a hand went in and did not come out --- and no third gap came and no fourth likeness stood up as a cause. The week's small movements stayed each where it had been laid: the frost that would not go, the plate of ice out of the path of feet, the grain turned twice beneath the wool, the held interval and the empty interval side by side with a finger between, the two intakes held apart and the early ending kept whole. Three are three and four are four, and no doctrine was made of them, because a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and no payer stood to any of these.

The hand went up an inch toward the river's note. It took the small intake of the river's own going and the hold of one of the counted breaths from the room whose floor does not touch his, set them side by side with a finger between, borrowed no house count and supplied no fifth to the kiln, took nothing from the two-gapped row, the charcoal under the shelf or the grain under the wool, and came down on the river's next low note. Neither interval was promoted into a reach, a hearing, a gift or a keeping. On the sixth place the small intake and the hold came down beside one hundred and thirty-one, and one hundred and thirty-two was filed there, exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air. The surgeon felt the clean small correctness of two things kept apart and answered in their own order; the boy wanted the crust lifted two inches to his mouth; both were true, and the wanting was louder, and the checking was the work. He made nothing of it and would not let it stand up as a gift, because a gift wants a taker and nobody under this roof had taken anything.

The hand laid back against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. Two hundred and seventy-eight stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in Sarn, Sill or Grath; six months and ninety-eight days were told by nobody; the fire's fifth was unpaid the same number of days with its payer unnamed. Nothing was opened, named, called, paid or ended; the file lay open on the towel with every gap left gap, every tray parted from its neighbour and nothing that had moved. The river's low note went quiet after the hand came down. Beyond the finger's-width door the frost lay white on the well lip where the yard had left it, called weather and entered nowhere, and Frost 10 stood one morning ahead and was not brought into this day. The crust lay two inches from his ear with its empty place beside it unnamed, and there was no third.
