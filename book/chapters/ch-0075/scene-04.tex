The afternoon thinned without ending. It went as heat goes out of a pot set off the coals, drawing back by degrees and sitting behind the pot where a hand might still find it, and nobody drew a line under the going. \person{Sella Vail} had set the basket at the edge of the work in \place{the Clay Yard} where no slate lay, in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill}, \place{Grath}, and \person[Corin]{the boy} lay under the wool with the light coming down thin through the rime, awake, carried, silent, and told by nobody that the day had a number. Under both the clay smell and the wool there was the faint sour mineral breath of the unswept corner called \lore{the thaw}, which had followed him out from the room whose floor does not touch his.

He took the body as it presented itself, in the order the facts arrived. One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from his ear, gone past the point where a child would eat it, and the empty place beside it kept its name to itself; no third had come in the afternoon to make the row come out even. The thread of drool at his mouth had frozen in the dry heat and was unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. Cold had come to cheek and temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, and had stopped there as at the end of a line written out for it, with neither an order to go forward nor an order to go away. The afterdrop waited beyond all three, past a warming nobody in this house would perform. He knew the composed face that is not composure; he knew a cold patient cannot clot and will seem better before he is worse, and that a man must be warmed from the outside and slowly. He wanted the crust lifted the two inches to his mouth. Both were true, and in a body two hundred and seventy-seven days old the second was louder; the surgeon took the inventory in its old order --- crust, gap, thread, cold, the waiting --- and entered none of it, not even on the place where he kept the things he did not enter.

Under the floor of \place{the Vail kiln-house} \lore{the carried fire} gave four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer without a name for two hundred and seventy-seven days, and nobody lifted the floor and nobody supplied the pull. Through the finger's-width door the river kept its note of \lore{water over water}, a low thing over a lower thing, never numbered among the hearings. Overhead \place{the boards} kept four nails on the near board, four on the next, and a thumb-width knot where the fifth nail should have stood. Three counts in three places, and none rose into another, because agreeing any two would want a payer put under both and no payer stood to either. Six hearings of \lore{the full note} stood beside and never among, five from four sources and the sixth, the small flat knock out of a woman as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go and never with the things that came from vessels; the empty place kept its emptiness and none was invented to fill it.

At \place{the yard well} the women drew with a rope that had stiffened in the cold until it would not lie against the stone, and one of them made two pulls of one pull and set the pail down against her knee. The white frost had written on the lip in the small hours was still there in the afternoon, fine and stacked like the little branches of a burnt tree on the north side where the Warm had lately gone out of the stone. A bucket knocked the edge of one frond into grey meal; the rest stood. Every woman at the well called it weather. Nobody who would have had to pay for it called it anything at all, and nobody put a figure on the drawing of a bucket through it. Beside the well \place{the trough} had taken a skin at its edges, thin as the film at the rim of a pot set off the coals and left to stand, and the water underneath kept the yard's name of weather. The yard went around the trough with a look and a wide step and gave the skin no name. The boy read a vessel with a covering laid over a depth, and set the reading on a place of its own beside the stayed white and the yard's word for it, with a finger between each pair. He wrote no cause across any two. The reading bought nothing, and the wanting was louder.

\person{Ilva} came for water with the pail against her knee, as the yard's women came, and went about it without looking to see who had noticed her. Clay had dried pale in the crescents of her nails, and the finer grey dust of \place{the drying racks} was worked into the seam of one thumb, which she did not scrub out. Her hands knew the weight of a pail and the edge of a wet board and were not soft. The rope took the two pulls, the bucket came up, and she set it down level.

A woman with a pot, come up from the racks with an unanswered question still standing in her mouth, asked what the month's firings had taken. The boy watched the order of it come into his reach the way he had once watched an instrument laid out on a towel: hand first, toward the sleeve, and there it stayed; eyes next, to the woman who had asked; mouth last. The short strip of birch bark with its notches was not taken out, read, asked about, taken a figure from or joined to the charcoal under the shelf, the cord, the knots, the pages, the tins, the missing hand or the pale line where a ring had been worn. The hand went back into the sleeve with the flat of the palm, the way a tool is put away where the next hand will find it without looking.

\emph{Ask the pot what it cost. I keep the count, not the account.} Her eyes had gone to the asker before her mouth opened, and her mouth answered the question put and not the one behind it. She said no figure and gave no name to the thing she kept. The woman with the pot went off level with her answer, and nobody in the yard turned to look at either of them twice.

He wanted the stick turned over. The wanting was small and animal and went toward the notches before the surgeon could put a name on what they were; a count kept in wood was a record, and a record wanted reading, and his hands were the hands of a child who could not lift the fold from his own ear. He did not read the strip, did not ask her about it, did not take a figure off it. He filed no tenth hand-shape. The ninth stayed on its own place beside the eighth with a finger between, nine objects and not a rule, for a rule is an account that has found a payer and none stood to this one. The count stayed in wood where no slate could take a figure off it, and she went on with her water not knowing she had been read. The check was not envy and not virtue. It was only the exact order of another person's reaching, filed apart, and the filing was the work, done with neither hand.

On \place{the flagstones} before the kiln-house the shallow bright patch lay where it had lain since Seed 22, and the broom went over it without the half-check, the stones keeping the place where the stroke had stopped. The small dry scrape from \place{the Debt Loft} sill lay on a place of its own, and the carried man's pale line lay on another, and neither was laid across the bright patch. The finding that a house keeps itself by \lore{not-doings} had a third place of its own, with a finger between it and his own kept gaps; he did not lay it across the stayed white, the unread charcoal, the fifth pull missing under the floor, the loan uncalled in the ruts or the one crust in the fold. A joining would want a payer and none stood, and the not-saying was itself a keeping and not a doctrine, because a doctrine is an account that has found a payer.

\person{Jorra Vail} stood at the edge of \place{the Vail wheel-shed} with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal into both forearms past the bend of his little finger, not bone-coloured slip; he kept dusty forearms and clean ones on one tray and asked neither which slate had been broken nor whether it was a birth-slate. He spoke to the wheel before he spoke to any person --- \emph{It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady} --- and told a pot \emph{You'll do} in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either. He knew the full note that pot would give three turns before it left his hands, with air under it and a want in it, and withheld the knowing on the near-full tray beside the hearings and never among them. He asked no question that would join the dust on his forearms to a cause. The \lore{damper} stood cold where his bent finger had left it, one iron with two bites and no Grathic word for both mercies, joined to no kiln and entered in no slate. \place{the back room} kept its finger's width of open and its sound of low voices, and the house gave the men inside no name and did not say the room was cold; to the boy it was a direction and a sound. The burned-slate word stood at four from Slate Row, a direction over the boards and not a fifth telling; the two tellings of the counting room shut in the war stood at two and had not become three; three sayings about a pot stood as sayings, with the premature Smoke 14 note on its own place and no fourth likeness made a cause. He kept each where it lay, with a finger between, and did not ask the wheel to explain any of them.

No sixth asking came over the boards that afternoon. The silence stood where the five askings stood and the one further shaping beside them, with no witness to be asked and no row named; it was a keeping, not a sixth witness, for a witness would want a row under it. The heard bell's four, four and a gap stayed in the square beside the told bell of \lore{the Long Night}, not folded into the three counts and not heard as a seventh of the full note. Frost 10 stood two mornings ahead with its reading and its market and its visits, and was not brought into a day that had not earned it.

At the going of the light \person{Sella Vail} came for the basket. She carried at the half-pace from every open slate and went wide around the places the yard went around with a look and no number; she gave the instruction to the fire, the wool, the pot, the broom and the running water and never to the basket, which was a keeping and not a conversation. She set him on \place{the bed} at the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf} under the Vail roof and not in the Debt Loft, put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face and left the thread frozen where it lay. She told the fire it was the thaw and turned the coals with the back of a broken pot, banked an hour earlier than on the days before with nobody giving the hour a name, and said \emph{The house will keep it} where another woman would have said \emph{I}. Her finger stayed on top of the shelf's front edge so the charcoal tally on the underside remained unread; her lips moved along a row standing with two gaps and gave neither gap a figure. She glanced once at the clay jar standing cold under its \lore{lead glaze} and shut her hand half over nothing. \lore{The widow's mark} hung where it hung on its cord at her throat, and her hand did not go to it at a well, a rack, an afternoon or a going of the light, because none of these was an opening and the hand goes to the mark before the kiln and at no other hour. She stood the pot rim with its handle two inches beyond the reaching hand, with a half-beat at the setting and no look toward the bed, and went to her own bed without explaining the count.

In the small hours the woman's low counting came through the floor that does not touch his --- the small intake, the hold, the letting-go --- a sound with a direction and no name. \place{the towel} lay where it had been laid at the head of the bed, and the thread stayed frozen above it, unwiped. Through the finger's-width door the river kept its low note over its lower note. Outside, on the north lip of the well where the yard had left it, the frost lay where it had lain since morning, called weather and entered nowhere; the skin on the trough stood where the skin had stood; Ilva's hand had gone back into her sleeve and nobody had turned twice.

The hand went up an inch toward the river's note. It took the small intake of one counted breath from the room whose floor does not touch his, took the hold of the next breath after it, and bridged the two over the river's low note, neither made to agree with the fifth missing under the floor and no house count borrowed for the bridge. The bridge came down on the sixth place beside one hundred and twenty-nine as an object among objects, and one hundred and thirty was filed there, exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air: not a reach, not a hearing, not a seventh, not a stopping, not a payment, not a freedom, not a key, a power, a doctrine or a rule. The surgeon felt the clean small correctness of a span laid between two points and a stroke set down at the right moment, and the boy wanted the crust lifted two inches to his mouth; both were true, and the wanting was louder, and the checking was the work. He made nothing of the answer and would not let it stand up as a gift, because a gift wants a taker and nobody under this roof had taken anything.

Out at the well Ilva went on with her water, her hand in her sleeve and nobody turning twice. Under the roof the boy's hand bridged the two counted breaths over the river's note and laid the bridge down as an object on the sixth place. It laid back against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking, and the river went on with its low note while the frost lay on the lip where the yard had left it, called weather and entered nowhere.
