## Ground rules held in every scene

- **Body inventory, in the old order every time:** one stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust two inches from the ear, its empty place unnamed and no third; the thread of drool frozen in the dry heat and unwiped, because nobody under any roof has ever been given an instruction about the thread and a basket set down is a pot set down and left; cold stopped at cheek, temple and the bare wrist-joint where the wool rides back, as at the end of a line written out for it; the afterdrop waiting past a warming nobody in this house will perform. He knows the composed face that is not composure, that a cold patient cannot clot and will seem better before he is worse, and that a man must be warmed from the outside and slowly. He wants the crust lifted two inches. Both are true; in this body the second is louder; he enters neither, not even on the place where he keeps the things he does not enter (T060/T086).
- **Three counts in three places:** the carried fire's four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should stand under the floor, its payer unnamed; the river's *water over water* at the finger's-width door; the boards' four nails, four and a thumb-width knot. No fire-sitter supplies the fifth and no house count is borrowed; agreeing any two would want a payer put under both and none stands (T088, T061/T082). The heard bell's four, four and a gap stays in the square beside the told bell of the Long Night, not folded into the three and not a seventh of the full note (T078).
- **Six hearings of the full note** stand beside and never among, five from four sources and the sixth, the small flat knock out of Dessa Silt as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go and never with the things that came from vessels. No seventh comes and none is invented to fill the empty place, for an empty place kept empty is a finding and a finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged.
- **The household grammar:** fire before basket; instruction to the fire, the wool, the pot, the broom and the running water and never to the basket; *the house* and *The house will keep it* where another woman would say *I*; half-pace from every open slate; pot handle two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat and no look toward the bed; fold righted without a look at his face; finger on top of the shelf's front edge so the charcoal stays unread; lips moved along the two-gapped row without a figure; one glance at the clay jar under its lead glaze and the hand shut half over nothing; the bed under the roof and never the Debt Loft (T058/T079, T100).
- **The widow's mark:** touched once before the kiln on Frost 2 and at no shelf, well, rack, road, basket, yard, morning, hearth, afternoon, dusk or going of the light. A shelf, a strip, a rack, a well, a bed, a basket, a market and a going of the light are not openings. The first firing of Frost stands behind the household with no lesson, sign, mending or payer made of it; four openings are four and not a rule; the next stands in a later month's first firing, far off and not hoped for (T058/T079).
- **Held, not advanced:** five askings of the row with one shaping and no witness and no row named (T101); nine stoppings with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end and still ungiven (T036/T076); nine record-first hands with Ilva's ninth unread and no tenth (T080); three pot sayings with the premature Smoke 14 note on its own place and no fourth likeness made a cause; burned slate at four from Slate Row, a direction over the boards and not a fifth telling; two tellings of the counting room shut in the war, not three (T065/T095); three near-full answers advanced and unpaid; seventh green a colour and seventh shape a shape with no eighth; one crust, two shelf gaps, the pan's gone ring and the field's empty place on separate trays with no third gap and no fourth likeness (T060/T086, T097 held). Nothing is joined, named, opened, called, paid or ended.
- **The past life stays offstage:** no present-tense Chicago break, no `\memory{}` tag, no departure from the boy's eyes. The surgeon's register colours the noticing only. No Court, collector, Waiting Room or back-room interior. No new named character with a real role.

## How the final moment pulls into Chapter 76

The chapter closes with the hand put away for the next finding, the count at **278 exact** and one hundred and thirty-two honest answers on the sixth place, the file open with every gap left gap, and the frost lying on the well lip past the going of the light. Nothing is settled, so everything remains in place to be read: the dressed grain lies covered and unfigured under wool at the rack end with the arithmetic that will read over it tomorrow still not knowing what it cost; Smoke's three true subtractions sit locked in Berr's box up the clay road and the printed figure still says one in twelve; the heard bell stays in the square beside the told; Hessa's inward slate, the soft-cuffed clerk's landing and the moot-post's finger's width of shade are all where Frost 10 will want them. Chapter 76 opens on Day 279, Frost 10, Tenthday, in the small hours under the same roof, with the hand laid down on the **one hundred and thirty-second** answer and the count at **278** — six months and ninety-eight days told by nobody — the fire's fifth unpaid the same number of days with its payer unnamed. The month's first reading is earned by that morning's own light and is not anticipated here. The boy carries the stayed frost, the broken plate of ice, the held and empty intervals and every parted tray into the Account-day unchanged, and nobody under the roof has yet said what a number would count.
