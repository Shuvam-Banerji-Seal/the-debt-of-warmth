## Scene 1 — *Water Over Water*

- **Day and time.** Day 61, Silt 2, Y.H. 813, Secondday; first light at the drying racks, a short stretch at the Fishstairs in the middle of the day, dusk under the Vail roof. One `\daymark{61}{Silt 2, Y.H. 813}` opens the scene; one `\scenebreak` before the Fishstairs stretch and one before the dusk return, if the rhythm wants them.
- **Place.** The drying racks at the eastern edge of the Clay Yard, with the Slip Brook running behind them; the Fishstairs below the north bridge; the bed under the Vail kiln-house roof.
- **Present.** \person{Corin} (carried in Sella's basket under the fold); \person{Sella Vail}; \person{Mira Silt} (at the eels); \person{Berr Clay} (short); the soft-cuffed \place{the Fishstairs} clerk glimpsed and silent; rack-women and a weir woman as walk-ons. \person{Garl Vail} and \person{Dessa Silt} present only as the coat, the seat, the clean dark line, the one crust and the empty place.
- **Beats, in order.**
  1. Open forward, with no recap, on the racks: the brook runs higher behind them and the clay comes down heavier than it did in Ilmen. The boy, sixty-one days old, carried level as a pot filled, hears a new sound --- water going over water, the river's own note, distinct from the early Singing Sill note and from the told and heard bells --- and his hand outside the wool goes up an inch toward it and comes down. He keeps the number of days exactly and cannot spend it. (T025, T071/T072)
  2. Sella sets the basket at the rack's edge out of the draught and gives every instruction to the fire, the wool and the bucket, never to the basket; says *the house* where another woman says *I*; tells the fire it is the thaw; keeps her half-pace from every open slate and touches \lore{the widow's mark} only before an opening and at no other hour. (T058, T079, T085)
  3. A figure is spoken at the Fishstairs --- the eel price, the levy, the rising water, any one, never given as a number the boy can spend. Mira works with pale slime dried on her sleeves, silt in the hollow of her neck, her head held right where the top of her left ear was gone; answers a question with a question and her own answer; moves eel-bones against her thumb while deciding whether to lie and puts one away when the deciding is done; when the figure is spoken her thumb goes under her apron to the grey cord and stays, and shifting a basket shows the second cord twisted twice beneath it, never carried upriver to \place{Nine Pots}, never lifted at a door, knots on neither counted. Her board lies beside Orra's knot-string and does not touch it. (T025, T059, T024 as presence)
  4. Berr comes up short at the edge: red wool cord folded under his coat, green of tasted glaze at one corner of his mouth, hot clay over the smell of nothing. He wipes his thumb twice before touching nothing, holds a narrow slate unopened, says a number goes into a slate and not into a well, takes himself out of his own sentence with *there'll be a slate at the watch*, and never looks into the basket. His box stays locked. (T041, T047, T077) The soft-cuffed clerk is glimpsed with his hand to his slate before his eyes to the stalls and does not speak; file him apart from Marra, the Drawing collector and the Court-table clerk. (T090, T080)
  5. The boy files water-over-water on a place of its own, beside but never on the early note, the told bell, the heard bell and the kiln's four-four-and-a-gap; he writes no cause across any of them. (T078, T065, T061/T082, T071/T072)
  6. Dusk: Sella lifts the basket level, carries him up at a half-pace from every open slate, sets him on his bed under the roof and not in \place{the Debt Loft}, puts the fold right without looking at his face. The one crust lies stiff where it has lain; the empty place at his ear goes through another day without a name. (T086, T060)
- **Emotional turn.** From the hum of ordinary work to the first felt pressure of the flood. The adults treat the higher water as one more thing to name and go home over; the boy's interior registers a new sound nobody says aloud, and the day's only event is something he cannot spend.
- **Sensory anchors.** Clay-heavy water going over water; wet wool at the ear; tallow smoke; eel slime dried pale, silt in the neck; cold at cheek, temple, wrist; the twice-wiped thumb; the river wind Sella warns the wool about.
- **Threads advanced.** T025, T059, T071/T072, T058, T079, T085, T083, T041, T047, T077, T090, T080, T078, T065, T061/T082, T086, T060. T024 carried as presence only.
- **Foreshadowing planted/paid.** Plants water-over-water and the heavier clay toward the year's flood in Chapter 15; carries Mira's second cord toward the thin child upriver in Nine Pots and Berr's withheld figure toward the true deduction; keeps the record-first hands on separate trays.
- **Exact last image.** On his bed under the roof with one crust and the empty place beside it; the door a finger's width open; from outside, the low new sound of the brook going over itself, and his hand outside the wool going up an inch toward it and coming down.

## Scene 2 — *The Seventh Year Held*

- **Day and time.** Day 62, Silt 3, Y.H. 813, Thirdday; before light through dusk. One `\daymark{62}{Silt 3, Y.H. 813}`.
- **Place.** The Clay Yard and the Vail kiln-house, chiefly the place near the racks where a pot cracked on Stone 25 and was swept around; the bed under the roof at dusk.
- **Present.** \person{Corin}; \person{Tarn} (central); \person{Sella Vail}; yard voices; \person{Jorra Vail} only as the damper and the forearms if a beat needs him. Garl as the coat, the hollow seat, the clean dark line and the proverb.
- **Beats, in order.**
  1. Before light Tarn sets the small bench knife on its sill and tells the empty yard where it is, unasked --- *It's back. You can check* --- the way he has announced it for six years. Ash is worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line; the pale clip-ring at his left wrist holds what light there is. Six years counted; the seventh is now nearly gone and still held back, the one entry in this house the house cannot check. (T036)
  2. The yard argues the thaw early and late; he says *Yes, house* to both, agrees so thoroughly there is nowhere for an argument to put its foot, and goes on wanting the late one, because a late thaw puts off the paying. This day the wanting has an edge: the water is up and the paying comes with it. (T036, T085)
  3. His broom checks at the place where the cracked pot stood on Stone 25 and went unswept. He does not turn his head, does not name the sound, says nothing. The boy keeps the third interval as an object on its own tray, not a hearing; the six hearings stay six, five from four sources and one from a person, and no seventh comes in this house. (T031, T082, T061)
  4. A vessel gives its note past the yard door and his head does not turn; the boy does not call it a seventh hearing. The two ears under one roof keep their equality and the loan that can hear stays uncalled --- the sentence neither man can say lying under the day. (T076)
  5. Sella tells the fire it is the thaw, feeds it from the house's dish, keeps Garl's salt coat on its peg and his hollow seat empty, and leaves the clean dark line where it turned the corner with the new grey beside it unswept. Garl's proverb about leaving the crack showing goes to a pot with no crack in it. (T009, T013, T092, T058)
  6. The body goes on with its small humiliations while he keeps the count; Sella sets him on his bed at dusk with one crust and the empty place. (T071/T072, T086, T060)
- **Emotional turn.** Tarn's held seventh year presses against the rising water: the man who has spent six years putting off the paying can feel it coming anyway, and his small check of the broom is the only thing in the yard that notices the old crack. The boy keeps that check as a fact.
- **Sensory anchors.** Broom bristles over trodden ash; the small knife on its sill; Ash ground into knuckle creases; the pale clip-ring catching light; cold iron at the cheek as he is carried; the fire's missing fourth breath.
- **Threads advanced.** T036, T031, T082, T061, T076, T071/T072, T009, T013, T092, T085, T058.
- **Foreshadowing planted/paid.** Carries Tarn's seventh year toward the end of the term and the later refusal of the whole return; revisits the cracked-pot interval toward the third thing; carries the higher water toward the flood. No accident, no naming.
- **Exact last image.** Tarn's broom checking half a stroke at the empty place where the pot had cracked and then going on; under the roof, the boy's hand outside the wool going up an inch toward the warmth and coming down.

## Scene 3 — *The Back Room's Cold*

- **Day and time.** Day 63, Silt 4, Y.H. 813, Fourthday; morning into afternoon. One `\daymark{63}{Silt 4, Y.H. 813}`.
- **Place.** The Vail wheel-shed, the room off it (not entered), the drying racks outside it.
- **Present.** \person{Corin}; \person{Jorra Vail} (central); \person{Sella Vail}; \person{Tarn} briefly at the shed end; unnamed men present only as the low talk behind the door. \person{Garl Vail} as the wheel with its hollow seat.
- **Beats, in order.**
  1. Jorra comes in from the room off the wheel-shed with fine grey slate dust worked into the creases of his forearms past the bend of his little finger --- not the bone-coloured slip of the wheel --- and says nothing about where he has been. The men who came into that room are not named. (T035)
  2. The broken birth-slates stay under the drying rack where the yard goes around them and enters them into nothing. The boy keeps the dusty forearms and the clean bone-coloured forearms on the same tray as two states of one morning and asks nothing: which slate was broken, whether it was a birth-slate. (T035, T031)
  3. Jorra speaks to the wheel before he speaks to anyone --- it does not care whose hands, only if they are steady --- and tells a pot it will do, in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either. He knows the full note of a Vail pot before it leaves the wheel and would not say so before a kiln-master, who would call it early and make something of it. The boy keeps that knowing beside his own and says nothing. (T035, T061)
  4. Sella has not finished telling him the fire is low; his bent finger goes in ahead of his hand at the damper and moves it, so that the end of her sentence arrives at the pot instead of at him. The household straightens around his silence without an order. (T035)
  5. He does not say the back room is cold, does not look toward the bed or the basket. The promise of Corin's name to the people who burn slates stays where it has always stayed, ungiven and not withdrawn; *he's not Vail* stays sealed as tightly as a lid, and the foundling goes on being carried under the roof unwritten. (T057, T058, T084)
  6. Dusk; the one crust and the empty place; the count exact. (T071/T072, T086)
- **Emotional turn.** The house's quiet anger has a room the boy cannot enter and a record no slate will read. Jorra's dust is a second keeping under the same roof as the boy's own; the nearer the wheel's hollow seat is filled, the more sealed the sentence about the foundling becomes.
- **Sensory anchors.** Grey slate dust in forearm creases; damp clay and the sweetish edge of lead glaze on a bad wind; tallow; the bend of the little finger; the wheel's steady rhythm; damper iron going in ahead of the hand.
- **Threads advanced.** T035, T031, T061, T057, T058, T084, T071/T072.
- **Foreshadowing planted/paid.** Carries the broken birth-slates and Jorra's promise of the name toward the day he hands it to the people who burn slates; carries his knowing of the full note toward the kiln-masters' discovery; nothing entered, nothing withdrawn.
- **Exact last image.** Jorra's bent finger going in ahead of his hand at the damper and moving it before Sella has finished, the end of her instruction arriving at the pot; the boy's hand outside the wool going up an inch and coming down.

## Scene 4 — *The Sixth Note*

- **Day and time.** Day 64, Silt 5, Y.H. 813, Fifthday; mid-morning three doors down, afternoon and dusk under the Vail roof. One `\daymark{64}{Silt 5, Y.H. 813}`.
- **Place.** The front room of \place{House Silt} (the death-house room with the pewter tin above the empty bed), then the bed under the Vail roof.
- **Present.** \person{Corin}; \person{Sella Vail}; \person{Alda Reed} (central); the neighbour scrubbing in the back of House Silt as a silent walk-on if needed.
- **Beats, in order.**
  1. Sella carries him three doors down and sets the basket just inside the finger's-width-open door, at the edge of the light and away from every open slate; she opens no slate and turns no errand into a walk to Slate Row, and stands a half-pace off where the writing lies. (T058, T084)
  2. From the low angle of the basket the round pewter tin above the empty bed keeps its pale fold at the seam and a thumb's bright rub on the lid --- birth-cloth kept three years, unopened --- and the little river stone in the fold of grey wool on the abandoned board-bed stays unmentioned. (T089, T074, T081)
  3. Alda comes short with soap-and-milk gone cold under river silt and lye sour under the soap, the birth-slates under her arm; she lays the writing-down on the bench before she turns to the fold and presses the fold flat at his shoulder a breath longer than a fold requires. (T023, T060)
  4. She touches the little tin inside her bodice once and does not open it; makes the two-finger sign at the red edge of the birth-flower before she knows she has done it, and covers the sign with the same date she gave Dessa --- Stone seven, the account up on the eighth --- then goes on with the cooper's seventh in Mud End and a potter's third child in Stonemouth after there is no question left to answer. She says *warm and breathing*, exact and not true; does not say the woman's name or the third day. (T007, T008, T073)
  5. **The payoff, here and nowhere earlier.** On a narrow slate she writes in the second hand what she will not leave lying, and turns it face-down into her shawl toward a floorboard in a house the boy cannot name. The hidden record goes from five pages to six. It is read by nobody and buys no recognition: he cannot read Grathic, cannot ask which house holds the floorboard, cannot hand the gesture back. (T023 paid)
  6. Two private records in two materials stand in one room on separate trays and are read by nobody; the mercy-lie now curdles all the way through to a woman twenty-six mornings dead that no living person can correct. (T089, T008)
  7. Dusk: Sella carries him home, puts the fold right without a look at his face, sets him on his bed under the roof with one crust and the empty place. (T086, T060)
- **Emotional turn.** The sixth note is written and the room holds two shut records, and the writing changes nothing for either woman or for him. The hidden truth grows by a page in a house he will never enter, and the sign at his own flower is covered again with a date.
- **Sensory anchors.** Soap-and-milk gone cold under river silt; lye sour under the soap; birth-slates laid writing-down on the bench; the thumb's bright rub on the pewter lid; the two-finger sign; a narrow slate going face-down under a grey shawl.
- **Threads advanced.** T023 (paid), T089, T074, T081, T007, T008, T073, T058, T084, T060, T086.
- **Foreshadowing planted/paid.** Pays the sixth note withheld through Chapter 13 and carries the tin under the floorboard toward the day Dan finds it after Alda is gone; carries the covered sign and the mercy-lie forward; two private records still unopened.

(If the scene runs through dusk, close instead on the bed under the roof with one crust and the empty place, the count going on.)

## Scene 5 — *Nearly Full*

- **Day and time.** Day 65, Silt 6, Sixthday; morning through afternoon. One `\daymark{65}{Silt 6, Y.H. 813}`.
- **Place.** The drying racks in the Clay Yard; the Vail kiln-house under the roof.
- **Present.** \person{Corin}; \person{Sella Vail}; \person{Tarn} (brief); a rack-woman who sets the pot down (walk-on, not the Stone 25 woman, not the Ilmen 6 or Ilmen 23 women); yard voices.
- **Beats, in order.**
  1. At the racks a woman sets a pot down out of her shadow and, under nobody's knuckle, it answers nearly full --- air under the note and a want in it. It is a third such answer, to be filed beside Ilmen 6 and Ilmen 23, advanced and not paid. (T082)
  2. The boy files it on the near-full tray: nearly and full are still two sounds, he has no hand to tap the pot and ask it to speak again and no Grathic word for nearly. It is not a hearing. The six hearings stay six --- five from four sources and one out of a person --- and no seventh comes. (T082, T061)
  3. The three intervals from Stone 13, Stone 17 and Stone 25 stay objects on their own tray, kept apart from the near-full tray and the hearing tray; he does not let one tray touch another. (T082)
  4. Tarn's head goes a degree toward the note and he goes back to his broom without naming it or looking toward the basket; the boy keeps his turning beside his own hearing and does not join them. (T076, T061)
  5. Sella tells the fire it is the thaw, leaves the clean dark line and the new grey beside it unswept, and keeps the carried fire under blind ash and the back of a broken pot, warming the boards by degrees and missing one breath in every four. (T009, T013, T092, T065, T088)

## Scene 6 — *The Note Under the Boards*

- **Day and time.** Day 66, Silt 7, Seventhday; before light through dusk. One `\daymark{66}{Silt 7, Y.H. 813}`.
- **Place.** The edge of the Ilm beyond the racks, the Clay Yard, the bed under the Vail roof.

- **Beats, in order.**
  1. The Singing Sill gives its low note earlier than it should, felt in the teeth before it is placed in the ear, and the yard calls it weather; a woman at the tubs says the water is running fast under the ice and then says weather and goes home warm on it. (T085, T078)
  2. The boy sets this early note on its own place beside the told bell of the Long Night, the heard bell at \place{the Cross} and \lore{the kiln's breath} under his own roof --- four low sounds in four places, kept apart for two months with no cause written across them. (T078, T065, T061/T082, T071/T072)
  3. Word comes up from \place{the Weir} that the weak board in \place{the plank walk} is still there and trodden by nobody, and that the soft-cuffed clerk is still set at the Fishstairs with a slate for a measure nobody called. The board goes beside the missing Fishstairs step and the stopping hairlines; the clerk's order of reaching goes on its own tray beside the other record-first hands, not joined to any of them. (T083, T090, T085, T092, T080)
  4. He keeps the count exactly: six hearings from four sources and one from a person, no seventh; three near-full answers advanced and not paid; the word on its own tray with the readable shape of the double turn beside it and the weight of a thumb apart from both. (T086, T060, T071/T072, T082)

- **Sensory anchors.** The low note felt in the teeth before the ear places it; cold off the water; wet wool; woodsmoke and tallow; the board's note coming up through the floor under the basket.
- **Threads advanced.** T085, T078, T065, T061/T082, T071/T072, T083, T090, T092, T024, T058, T079, T088, T086, T060.
- **Foreshadowing planted/paid.** Carries the early note and the stopping hairlines toward the Breaking; the weak board toward Chapter 15, where the water may put it out of line --- but no accident made here; the four low sounds toward the day they are finally joined or refused.
- **Exact last image.** The low note coming up through the boards beneath his basket, called nothing, while through the floor the carried fire keeps four, four and a gap.

## Scene 7 — *Honeyed All Through*

- **Beats, in order.**
  1. The hairlines in the Ilm's ice have widened; a man come back from looking says the ice is honeyed all through, not only at the edge, and says it to the boards, so nobody joins it to any house or any flood. (T085, T083)
  2. At the well the stone lip is wet and the mud has got into the rope; the women speak of the skin on the bucket water to the bucket and not to the boy; a man says the month came early and a woman says it came as it always came, and neither gives a figure. He lies in the well's thin shade and keeps the number exactly; cold comes cheek, temple, wrist and the wool does less than the wind wants; he wants the bucket and the crust and can reach neither. (T085, T077, T071/T072, T086, T060)
  3. Beyond the racks the Ilm runs under a skin so thin the dark current shows through it in veins; more hairlines have run into the ice and stopped a thumb's breadth short of the edge; the new grey in the Vail corner lies beside the old clean dark line, unswept because the house says it is the thaw. (T083, T085, T092, T013)
  4. He sets the widening hairlines, the honeyed-all-through saying, the new grey, the early note, the still-set clerk and the weak board in places that do not touch, and writes no cause across any of them; the hearings stay six and no seventh comes; the crust stays one and the empty place stays unnamed. (T058, T086, T060, T065, T071/T072, T078)
  6. Last lines of the chapter: the count goes on into Silt 9 unspent; nothing joined, paid, explained or ended; his hand outside the wool goes up an inch toward the water and comes down. (T071/T072, T086, T061/T082)

- **Sensory anchors.** Wet rope and the skin on the bucket water; clay-heavy current under thinning ice; the dark veins in the ice; the new grey hairline beside the clean dark line; cold at cheek, temple, wrist; the river's low note through the door gap.
- **Threads advanced.** T085, T083, T092, T013, T082, T061/T082, T090, T071/T072, T086, T060, T058, T065, T077.

## The pull into Chapter 15

The final image --- hand up an inch toward the sound of the river and coming down, the river's low note through the door gap, the count crossing into Silt 9 unspent --- is the exact posture Chapter 15 takes up and completes. On Day 68, Silt 9, Ninthday, before light, the boy will be carried down to the Fishstairs edge; the water will be over the lower steps and coming up the road in a thin brown sheet, and he will hear the note of a whole district under strain --- a vessel with an enormous capacity and a slow, certain failure --- and will have no word for it and no hand to point with. The water-over-water of Day 61, the widening hairlines and the honeyed-all-through of Day 67, the still-set clerk, the weak board trodden by nobody and the new grey called thaw all arrive at the flood's edge still filed on separate trays, still joined by nobody. Nothing has been explained, paid or ended. Forward, not closed.
