## Scene 6 — *What the File Kept of Grain*

**Day and time.** Day 227, Grain 18, Eighthday, chapter close. Afternoon into the small hours. No new `\daymark`; no drop cap.

**Place.** The loaf shelf's low angle and the hearth under the Vail roof; the salt coat and hollow seat; the kindling; the wheel-shed edge; the clay road at the going of the light; the Clay Yard; the bed.

**Present.** Corin/Dan; Sella; Tarn; Jorra; Garl in objects. No other character on stage in the small hours. Grain 20 two mornings off; no market, slate, collector or reading under this roof.

**Beats, in order.**
1. The afternoon thins without ending; heat settles under the shelf, behind the pot and into the fold; one stiff brown-edged crust two inches from the ear, empty place unnamed, no third; thread frozen and unwiped because nobody was ever instructed about the thread and a basket set down is a pot set down and left; cold stopped at cheek, temple and bare wrist-joint as if it had reached the end of an order; afterdrop waiting past a warming nobody performs.
2.
