The afternoon thinned without ending. It did not go out the way a lamp goes out; it wore away at the edges, the light losing its heat before it lost its colour, so that the low angle under \place{the loaf shelf} held a little warmth longer than the yard did, the way a cup keeps a last warmth in the thick of its base after the surface has gone cold. Heat settled under the low angle, went behind the pot, came into the fold by degrees and lay there against \person[Corin]{the boy}'s ear with one stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust two inches from it. The empty place beside the crust kept its name to itself, and there was no third to make the row come out even. The thread at his mouth had frozen in the dry heat and was unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread, and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. Cold had stopped at cheek, at temple, at the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, as though it had come to the end of a sentence written out for it and had neither an order to go further nor an order to leave. The afterdrop waited on the far side of a warming nobody in this house would perform.

\person{Garl Vail} was kept in objects and not in a story. The salt-stiff coat held a broader man's shape on its peg with the crease at the left shoulder where the wheel had pulled him for twenty years, and the seat in the shed kept its twenty-year lean to the left and its hollow on that side, which nobody had ever filled and nobody now tried to fill. Along the skirting board the clean dark line where the Ash ridge had turned its corner lay unswept, and beside it the new finer hairline of grey that the house called \lore{the thaw} lay unswept too, and neither was going anywhere because a line left where it is is still a line kept. His proverb about leaving the crack showing had gone years ago to a pot with no crack in it and was still said over that pot, a saying about honest repair said over a corner where the repair had been rubbed out, and cost the more now there was nobody to say it in his own voice. The debt against a firing of tiles circled without a name, neither paid nor settled nor done with, worse for being uncalled. His name had not been spoken under this roof in two hundred and thirty-three days. No collector came up \place{the clay road} or went down it on Grain 22, on Grain 23 or on Grain 24, and the keeping of him went on as maintenance and not as a lesson, because a thing left where it is is still a thing kept.

\scenebreak

\person{Sella Vail} gave the fire its instruction before she gave it to the pot and before she gave anything to the basket, which had never been given an instruction and had no need of one. She told the fire it was the thaw. She turned the coals with the back of a broken pot and fed them, and said \emph{The house will keep it} where another woman would have said \emph{I}. Then she stood a moment with the back of the pot in her hand counting under her breath toward no opening --- small intake, hold, letting go --- and the count went nowhere that any slate could read. She pushed a strand of coal-black hair back with the wrist that carried the little-door burn, so that the grey streak at her temple showed pale off the coals, and set the pot rim out with its handle two inches beyond the reaching hand, and let her hand rest a half-beat longer at the setting than the setting required, and did not look toward \place{the bed}. \lore{The widow's mark} hung where it hung on its cord at her throat, touched once before the kiln on Ember 1 and at no hour since; there had been no opening in fifty-three days, because an afternoon, a shelf and a pot are not openings. The dressed grain lay covered under its fold near \place{the hearth}, counting in glances and handfuls and by the carrying arm's weight that knew weight and not cost, and nothing was entered against it. A cost-question that came to the door went to the pot and the weather and away level, with nothing priced, refused or entered; the figure was a direction and the direction arrived nowhere.

The hand was asked nothing by any of this, and the file waited where it had been laid.

\scenebreak

At the kindling \person{Tarn} took up the broom only after the broom had been given its instruction; a man who owns nothing knows the difference between a thing spoken to and a thing taken, and the house had said sweep, so he swept. \emph{Six counted. The next I don't count}, he told the wood, in the tone of a man stating the weather, and set the small knife at the very edge of its sill: six in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check, the seventh at the lip where it could not. Beside them lay Garl Vail's unforged collector sentence, the man sent for a collector who turned back at the frozen ford, entered nowhere and named for nobody, though he had been that man. He said \emph{Yes, house} to those who wanted the thaw early and to those who wanted it late, agreed so entirely that no argument could find a foot, and went on wanting the late one, because a late thaw puts off the paying, though the paying had stood in the ruts since the water went.

The sentence about an ear under this roof being worth more than the debt that held it came up to his teeth as he worked. It went all the way to the word that would call the loan and stopped there, with no figure and no name, and he filed the stopping as a remembered stopping and not a new one. Nine stoppings stood on Reed 5, 8, 11, 14, 17, 20, 23, 26 and 29, objects and not a rule, with no tenth stood up before the seventh year was earned and now at its very end and still ungiven. He drew the breath he should have spent at the top of the reach, the Seed 16 breath, and did not spend it; a small interval showed where it should have gone in. The loan stayed uncalled and no collector came down the clay road on Grain 24. His chest had been tight since the dark. \lore{Ash} was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line and no scrubbing lifted it, and the pale clip-ring at his left wrist held what light there was and gave it back. Above the shed the warm stone Sella had given him in the winter Garl died lay cold in its unmarked clay pot in \place{the Debt Loft}, two years gone and unreconciled with the debt by not trying, which is not forgiving. The loft came to him as a sound with a direction and nothing more, and he did not turn his head toward it. His stroke went over \place{the flagstones} past the shallow bright patch without the half-check and over the road a house sends a man down when it wants a collector without once turning along it.

At the edge of \place{the Vail wheel-shed} \person{Jorra Vail} came through with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bent little finger and under the nails, white in the creases where a fist had opened, and not bone-coloured \lore{slip}. Three or more men had gone low and quiet into the finger's-width-open \place{the back room} before first grey, and he gave the house no name for them and did not say the room was cold. He kept dusty forearms and clean ones on one tray, parting dust from slip as a surgeon parts clot from vessel, and asked neither which slate had been broken nor whether it had been a birth-slate; a thing ground is a thing that got there by being broken, and a thing mixed was put together on purpose, and he gave neither a cause.

He spoke to the wheel before he spoke to anyone. \emph{It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady}, he said, and told a pot \emph{You'll do} in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either. He knew the \lore{full note} that pot would give three turns before it left his hands, with air under the note and a want in it, and withheld the knowing, setting it on the near-full tray beside the hearings and never among them. The burned-slate word stood at four and the two tellings of the counting room shut in the war stood at two, with a hand between the three sayings and the tellings and no fifth and no third. When Sella's voice went low and unfinished at the hearth his bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper} ahead of her sentence, took the cold out of the fingertip and gave nothing back, held until the sentence reached the pot, moved a hair's breadth and was left standing --- a remembered repetition and not a change made again, joined to no kiln and paired with no other fraction. \emph{He's not Vail} stayed sealed as tightly as a lid nobody had tried to lift in three years, and the promise of the foundling's name to the men who burn slates was neither given nor withdrawn, and he did not look toward the bed or the basket.

\scenebreak

At the going of the light she carried him home at the half-pace, woodsmoke before her in long low bars and river wind behind with the taste of \lore{water over water}. She went wide at every open slate. At the Vail corner the yard went around the clean dark line and the finer grey beside it called the thaw, and swept neither; under the lowest rack the rack space kept its broken birth-slates and got its look and its wide step and no number; the shallow clay pan that had stood mouth-up through Reed was gone these many days and its round empty ring lay where the ring was with no scrape, no crumb, no print, no chip and no word, kept on a tray of its own and not laid across the shelf's two gaps or the fold's one crust; at \place{the yard well} \place{the trough} stood bare and dark and its water was called weather; out on \place{the worked strips} east of \place{Low Kiln} the field kept its empty place, and in the returning line the place where a hand had gone in on Seed 15 and had not come out with the rest kept its own look and its own wide step. Each of the seven got what it got and none got a figure: no name, no cause, no body, no identification of which hand, no payment. Seven walked-around things standing in a row are still seven and not an account until somebody finds a payer to stand under them, and none did. A thing gone around is still a thing kept.

She set him on his bed under the roof and not in the Debt Loft, put the fold right without a look at his face and left the thread frozen, and went to her own bed before the small hours and left the coals to keep themselves. Grain 30, the third and last Account-day and market, stood six mornings ahead and was not brought into a day that had not earned it.

In the small hours no breathing number sounded anywhere in the house, and Sella was gone to her own bed and the coals were keeping themselves. The two rooms did not touch. Under the floor \lore{the carried fire} kept its four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have been, its payer without a name for two hundred and thirty-three days; through the finger's-width door the river kept its fifth low note of water over water, never counted among the hearings; overhead the boards kept four nails on the near board, four on the next, and a thumb-width knot where the fifth nail should have stood. Three counts in three places, and none rose up into the others, because a man who wanted them to agree would have to put a payer under all three and no payer stood to any. Nothing in his own room held a count for him; the only count in the house was one Sella kept at her fire in the room that does not touch his --- small intake, hold, letting-go --- toward no opening, never offered to any ear under this roof, with a wall standing between it and his. He took that count for his own the way a man takes a rhythm he has heard a thousand times and can keep without the sound.

He turned out the file as a man lays instruments on a towel and found it exact, complete and worth nothing. Six hearings of the full note stood beside and never among: five from four sources, a cup across a birth room, a pot in the shed through a finger's width of door, the district's slate twice at the Cross, and the sixth, the small flat knock out of a woman as the men lifted her, from a person, filed where a thing out of a person must go and never among the sources. No seventh had come in all these days and none was invented to fill the empty place, for a finding kept empty is a finding and a finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged. Three near-full answers stood on their own tray, advanced and unpaid; beside them stood three shapings of the pot saying, all sayings and no rule, with the burned-slate word on a place of its own at four and the two tellings of the counting room shut in the war standing at two with a hand between them and the sayings. The told bell lay beside the heard, four, four and a gap with nobody crossing himself; the told figure lay beside the paid, one in twelve read low twice over a covered basket and still not the figure paid, while the true subtraction stayed locked in a box going up toward \place{High Kiln} and answered nothing for the man carried out on Day 188. Nine stoppings stood with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end and still ungiven; the Seed 16 breath was unspent and the loan uncalled. Three held changes stood on three places, the loft-sill scrape on a fourth, the carried man's pale line on a fifth and the held inch with its answers on a sixth. Beside the hearings but never among them the seventh green was a colour and the seventh shape was a shape, gathered at a market and carried unjoined through the working mornings since, with no eighth of either, no name spoken over either, no loaf, no slate and no sale. The dressed grain lay covered and unfigured; the returning-line place lay gone around with no number; the charcoal under the shelf's front board lay unread beneath a finger kept on top of the board where any woman in the district could see it lying. And on a seventh place of its own, with a finger between it and the hearings and the sayings and the tellings and the count, the question stood with two askings and nobody to own it: a voice with no owner, no room, no hand at the kiln and no cause, answered by the weather and by the price of barley, gone off up the terrace with the row standing where it stood. Two askings were not one witness, and a witness stood somewhere and could be asked, and none stood and none could be asked. It was a seed and not a hearing, not a cause, not a name, not a fifth witness, held for the far-off day a witness stands somewhere and can be asked. Nothing was joined, named, opened, called, spent or ended.

He put the question to his hand the sixth way of this chapter. The hand outside the wool went up an inch toward the low note through the finger's-width door and stayed; he took it through the small intake, through the hold and into the letting-go of one of the counted breaths Sella kept at her fire in the room that does not touch his, kept it through the little silence after her count had ended and brought it down on the river's next low note. Two rhythms held one against the other across a wall that does not touch, remembered, not a change made again. Not a longer reach, not a miss entered, not a hearing, not a seventh of the full note, not a tenth stopping, not a payment, not one of the three held changes, not a key, not a power, not a rule. He filed it on the sixth place with no payer named and did not spend it and did not explain it. Forty-two answers stood there now, each an object and not a power, exact, complete and worth nothing at the length of two inches of air.

Two hundred and thirty-three stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in \place{Sarn}, \place{Sill} or \place{Grath}; six months and fifty-three days told by nobody; the carried fire's fifth unpaid for two hundred and thirty-three days; the seventh year at its very end and still ungiven; the seventh green a colour and the seventh shape a shape with no eighth of either; the dressed grain covered with no figure over it; the returning-line place gone around with no number; the widow's mark hung where it hung with no opening in fifty-three days. The question stood over the boards with two askings and nobody to own it, and the count went forward exact and unspent into a day that had not yet earned its heading. Grain 30 stood six mornings ahead and was not brought early. The hand came down on the river's next low note, and the file lay open on the towel, complete, and worth nothing.
