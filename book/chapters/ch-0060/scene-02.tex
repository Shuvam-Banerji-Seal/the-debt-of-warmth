The afternoon thinned without ending. It went out of the yard by degrees, the way heat goes out of a pot lifted off the coals --- not cut at a stroke but given over inch by inch to the air that came off the river, and under the low angle at the end of \place{the loaf shelf} the warmth that had gathered since morning stayed longer than it had any right to. It pooled behind the pot and ran down into the fold, so that the wool at \person[Corin]{the boy}'s ear was warm while the bare joint at his wrist was cold: two temperatures in one small body, with a wall of wool between them he could not shift. He lay where she had set him and let the two come on as they would. The surgeon's register had a name for losing heat by degrees and a name for holding it, and neither name bought him the use of a hand to move his own covering. Cold had stopped where it had stopped in the small hours, at cheek, at temple, at the wrist-joint where the wool rode back, as if it had come to the end of a sentence written out for it and had neither an instruction to go further nor an instruction to leave. The afterdrop waited on the far side of a warming nobody in this house would perform.

One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from his ear. It had lain there since the morning and would lie there into the dark, and the empty place beside it kept its name to itself and had no third to make the row come out even. The thread at his mouth had frozen in the dry heat and was unwiped, because nobody under any roof in Sarn had ever been given an instruction about the thread, and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. He wanted the crust. He could not lift his chin two inches to it or lift the crust two inches to his mouth, and the wanting went on under the not-reaching the way a coal goes on under ash, giving no light and not going out. He knew the order in which a body takes heat back, the small joint first and the core last, and he knew that a vessel which has cooled cannot clot and must not be rubbed too fast; the knowing was exact, complete, and worth nothing at the length of two inches of air.

\person{Garl Vail} was kept in the house as a man is kept in a room he has left: by the things that hold his shape and are not him. The salt-stiff coat hung on its peg behind the door with the crease at the left shoulder where a broad man had leaned for twenty years, and nobody took it down. The wheel-seat in the shed kept its hollow on that same side, worn by a man who had arrived slowly and early, which in a kiln-house is the same thing, and nobody filled it. Along the skirting board the clean dark line where the \lore{Ash} ridge had turned its corner lay unswept, and beside it the new, finer hairline of grey that the house called \lore{the thaw} lay also unswept, and the two were kept apart by a look and a wide step and no word between them. His proverb had gone years ago to a pot with no crack in it and was still said over a corner where the repair had been rubbed out: leave the crack showing; people trust a thing they can see has been handled. The saying outlived the thing it was true about and cost more now there was nobody to say it in his own voice. The debt against a firing of tiles went around the house and around the talk at the door, circled and never named, worse for being uncalled, for a debt uncalled can neither be paid nor settled nor done with. His name had not been spoken under this roof in two hundred and thirty-one days. No collector had come up \place{the clay road} for the tiles on Grain 22, and the keeping of him was maintenance and not a story, and no lesson had been made of any of it. A line left where it is is still a line kept.

\scenebreak

At \place{the kindling} \person{Tarn} took up the broom only after it had been given its instruction. He did not take tools that had not been named; a man who owns nothing knows the difference between a thing spoken to and a thing taken, and the house had said sweep, so he swept. \emph{Six counted. The next I don't count}, he told the wood, in the tone of a man stating the weather, and set the small knife at the very edge of its sill: six in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check, the seventh at the lip where it could not, and Garl's unforged collector sentence lying beside them both, the man sent for a collector who had turned back at the frozen ford, entered nowhere and named for nobody, though he had been that man.

The sentence about an ear under this roof being worth more than the debt that held it came up to his teeth as he worked. It went all the way to the word that would call the loan and stopped there, with no figure and no name, and he filed the stopping as a remembered stopping and not a new one. Nine stoppings stood on Reed 5, 8, 11, 14, 17, 20, 23, 26 and 29, objects and not a rule, with no tenth stood up before the seventh year was earned and now at its very end and still ungiven. He drew the breath he should have spent at the top of the reach, the Seed 16 breath, and did not spend it; a small interval showed where it should have gone in. The loan stayed uncalled. His chest had been tight since the dark. Ash was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line, and no scrubbing lifted it, and the pale clip-ring at his left wrist held what light there was and gave it back. Above the shed the warm stone \person{Sella Vail} had given him in the winter Garl died lay cold in its unmarked clay pot in \place{the Debt Loft}, two years gone and unreconciled with the debt by not trying, which is not forgiving. The loft came to him as a sound with a direction and nothing more, and he did not turn his head toward it.

His stroke went over \place{the flagstones} and past the shallow bright patch without the half-check, and went over the road a house sends a man down when it wants a collector without once turning along it. The stones kept the place where the stroke had stopped on an earlier day and did not give it back. No collector came up the clay road on Grain 22, and he did not say that what had not happened had a name. He agreed so entirely with those who wanted the thaw early and those who wanted it late that no argument could find a foot to stand on, and he went on wanting the late one, because a late thaw puts off the paying, though the paying had stood in the ruts since the water went. When the boy's hand went up an inch toward a note through the door and came down, Tarn's head did not turn, and he said nothing about either ear. The equality under the roof kept its shape without a word passing between them.

\scenebreak

At the edge of \place{the Vail wheel-shed} \person{Jorra Vail} came through with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bent little finger and under the nails, white in the creases where a fist had opened, and not bone-coloured \lore{slip}. Three or more men had gone low and quiet into the finger's-width-open \place{the back room} before first grey, and he gave the house no name for them and did not say the room was cold. He kept dusty forearms and clean ones on one tray, and asked neither which slate had been broken nor whether it had been a birth-slate; the broken birth-slates lay under \place[the drying racks]{the lowest rack} where the yard went around them and entered them into nothing. A thing ground is a thing that got there by being broken; a thing mixed was put together on purpose. He parted one from the other as a surgeon parts clot from vessel and gave neither a cause.

He spoke to the wheel before he spoke to anyone. \emph{It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady}, he said, and told a pot \emph{You'll do} in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either. He knew the \lore{full note} that pot would give three turns before it left his hands, with air under the note and a want in it, and he withheld the knowing, setting it on the near-full tray beside the hearings and never among them. The three shapings of the pot saying stayed sayings: a pot that sings before its time mostly sings itself out early; the pot that sang early last month has not been heard from since; a pot gone quiet on every row come the thaw. No fourth likeness stood up to make a cause, no name was given to what ailed the man carried out on Day 188, and no pot was lost in the yard. The burned-slate word stood at four and the two tellings of the counting room shut in the war stood at two, with a hand between the three sayings and the tellings and no fifth and no third. On its own seventh place, with a finger between it and the hearings and the sayings and the tellings and the count, the question from Grain 21 waited with no owner, no room, no hand at the kiln, no cause and no witness standing anywhere to be asked. Two askings were not yet two on this day, and the second was held for a day not yet come, and he did not bring it early into the afternoon.

He grew quieter whenever the burned-slate word went around, and the household straightened around the quiet without an order, the way cloth straightens when a weight is lifted off it; the quiet went before him into the shed the way a cold air goes before a door. When Sella's voice went low and unfinished at the hearth his bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper} ahead of her sentence, took the cold out of the fingertip and gave nothing back, held while the sentence reached the pot, moved a hair's breadth and was left standing. It was a remembered repetition and not a change made again, joined to no kiln and paired with no other fraction. The iron was one piece with two bites, the bite that holds a vessel whole and the bite that can take the hand off a man, with no word in Grathic for both mercies, and he read it as an object worth nothing and entered it in no slate. \emph{He's not Vail} stayed sealed as tightly as a lid nobody had tried to lift in three years, and the promise of the foundling's name to the men who burn slates was neither given nor withdrawn, and he did not look toward the bed or the basket.

\scenebreak

Sella came to the fire before the basket. She told it the thaw and turned the coals with the back of a broken pot. \emph{The house will keep it}, she said, where another woman would have said \emph{I}, and stood a moment with the back of the pot in her hand counting under her breath toward no opening --- small intake, hold, letting go --- the count arriving nowhere as it had arrived nowhere on every day of this boy's life. She pushed a strand of coal-black hair back with the wrist that carried the little-door burn, and the grey streak at her temple showed pale off the coals, and she did not discuss the streak or the winter it had come in. \lore{The widow's mark} hung where it hung on its cord at her throat, touched once before the kiln on Ember 1 and at no hour since; there had been no opening in fifty-one days, because an afternoon and a shelf and a basket are not openings. Her eye went once to the clay jar cold under its glaze on the wall shelf, and her hand started toward it and shut half over nothing without a touch, the burn closing on air. Her finger lay on top of the shelf's front edge where any woman in the district could see it lying, and the charcoal tally beneath the board stayed unread, uncounted and uncopied. She gave nobody a figure. A cost-question that came to the door went to the pot and the weather and away level, nothing priced, nothing refused, nothing entered, and she did not look toward \place{the bed}.

At the going of the light she carried him up the clay road at the half-pace, woodsmoke before her in long low bars and river wind behind with the taste of water over water. The carrying arm was a record in muscle that remembered the weight of him and not what he would have cost to keep; it had carried him up and down this yard for two hundred and thirty-one days and had carried the dressed grain that she counted in glances and handfuls. At every open slate she kept the half-pace. She went wide around the clean dark line and the finer grey called the thaw; around the rack space beneath the lowest rack with its broken birth-slates; around the round empty ring where the shallow clay pan had stood, with no scrape, no crumb, no print, no chip and no word; around \place{the trough} at \place{the yard well}, bare and dark, whose water was called weather; around the field's empty place out on \place{the worked strips} east of \place{Low Kiln}; and around the place in the returning line where a hand had gone in on Seed 15 and had not come out with the rest. Each got its look and its wide step and no number --- no name, no cause, no body, no identification of which hand, no figure and no payment. Seven walked-around things standing in a row are still seven and not an account until a payer stands under them, and none did. She set him on his bed under the roof and not in the Debt Loft, put the fold right without a look at his face, and left the thread frozen.

\scenebreak

In the small hours there was no breathing number anywhere in the house. Sella had gone to her own bed before them and left the coals to keep themselves, and the two rooms under the roof did not touch. Under the floor \lore{the carried fire} gave four low pulls and withheld the fifth, its payer without a name for two hundred and thirty-one days. Through the finger's-width door the river kept its note of \lore{water over water}, a fifth low thing never counted among the hearings; overhead the boards kept four nails on the near board, four on the next, and a thumb-width knot where the fifth nail should have stood. Fire under the floor, river through the door, nails overhead: three counts in three places, and none rose into another, because a man who wanted them to agree would have to put a payer under all three and no payer stood to any of them. Two hundred and thirty-one stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in \place{Sarn}, \place{Sill} or \place{Grath}, six months and fifty-one days told by nobody, and the seven-year at its very end had still not given.

He put the question to his hand the second way of this chapter. The hand outside the wool went up an inch toward the low note through the door and was held to the small intake only, the first drawing of a breath and no more, and came down before the letting-go --- a held fraction shorter than the plain coming-down of the morning and shorter than the whole counted breath, not carried through the intake and the hold and the release, not kept past the end of a count, not matched to the river's own interval. Not a longer reach, not a miss entered, not a hearing, not a seventh of the full note, not a tenth stopping, not a payment, not one of the three held changes, not a key, not a power, not a rule. He filed it on the sixth place with no payer named and did not spend it and did not explain it. Thirty-eight answers stood there now, each an object and not a power. The hand came down before the letting-go and lay against the fold while the fire went on in the room that warmed him and the river in the room he could not enter, and neither stopped for the ear under the roof.
