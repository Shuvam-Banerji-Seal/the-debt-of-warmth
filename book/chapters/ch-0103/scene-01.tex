\daymark{360}{Stone 1, Firstday, Y.H. 813}

\dropcap{T}{he hand} outside the wool lay back against the fold on answer two hundred and ninety-four as a man lays down a tool where the next hand will find it without looking, and the room went on around it the way a room goes on around a thing put away for the night. \person[Corin]{The boy} lay on \place{the bed} at the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf} under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof, in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill}, \place{Grath}, in the small hour before light, and the dark had a weight and a smell: wet wool, tallow smoke, clay dust gone cold, and under them the faint sour mineral tang of the ridge along the skirting board that had not been swept in two years and would not be swept on a morning that was no opening. Through the floor \lore{the carried fire} kept four low pulls with the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer unnamed three hundred and fifty-nine days; through \place{the finger's-width door} the river kept its note of \lore{water over water}, a low thing over a lower thing, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming. Above him \place{the boards} kept four nails on the near one, four on the next and a thumb-width knot where a fifth might have been driven and was not. Three counts in three places, and none rose into another, for agreeing any two would want a payer put under both and none stood; the bell's gap stayed in its square and was not folded into them.

Three hundred and fifty-nine had stood exact behind the day; three hundred and sixty stood now, one more in a row nobody counted aloud, not round, not a threshold, only a day. Six months and one hundred and seventy-nine days had become one hundred and eighty, and nobody under the roof said so. He had long ago stopped expecting to be told what the number counted, which was not the same as not wanting it. The fire's fifth was unpaid the same number with its payer unnamed; the name of the man whose salt-stiff coat kept his shape on the peg behind the door had not been spoken under this roof the same number. \place{Stone} had come on in the dark beyond the door while the house slept, and it had come on the way water comes over a stone, without a boundary drawn and without a word.

The surgeon's register took the body in its old order before the light came, because the light would not wait and the body would. One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from his ear, gone harder and browner through the long cold, its corner standing a hair off the wool; the empty place beside it kept its name to itself and no third had come in the three days behind to make the row come out even. A thread of drool at his mouth had frozen in the dry kiln heat and was unwiped, because nobody under any roof in Sill, in Grath, had ever been given an instruction about the thread, and a basket set down was a pot set down and left. Cold had stopped at cheek, temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, as though it had come to the end of a line written out for it, with neither an order to go farther nor an order to go away. \lore{The Afterdrop} waited beyond all three, past a warming nobody in this house would perform.

He knew the composed face that was not composure, knew it the way he had once known it across a table in a white room: a cold patient cannot clot, will seem better before he is worse, must be warmed from the outside and slowly, never hurried, never rubbed, never set too near the fire; pink returning to a face is the surface still cold and not the core, and the core gives heat up in the reverse order to the one in which it takes it back, which comes sooner than anybody likes. He knew all of it exactly and it was worth nothing at two inches of air. The want underneath was louder and would not be argued down: lift the crust the two inches to his mouth, wipe the thread, set a hand that would close on the pot handle where the fingers could reach it. Both were true, the second louder, neither won, and the quarrel between them was the work. The body humiliated him in the exactness and not in any large way: the drool frozen, the head useless, the crust gone past the point where any child would eat it, the pot handle two inches beyond the reaching hand, a hand that could not lift itself to its own chin. He had reduced a thousand shoulders in a room that smelled of disinfectant, and had once found a joke funny in a car park with a man who made jokes instead of asking; here he could not keep a fold of wool over one ear. He could still find the joke funny without laughing, which was a way of laughing that cost less and kept the same blade in it.

The file had come out on \place{the towel} at the head of the bed and been checked and left as it lay, instruments on a towel. Two hundred and ninety-four honest answers stood on the sixth place, each an object and not a power, and not one of them stood up and called itself a doctrine. Six hearings of \lore{the full note} stood beside and never among, the seventh place empty, the sixth the small flat knock out of \person{Dessa Silt} as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go and never among the sources. The heard bell stood four, four and a gap in its square beside the told bell of \lore{the Long Night}, not folded into the three counts and not made a seventh of the full note. Nine record-first hands stood with no tenth, \person{Ilva}'s ninth on a place of its own with a finger between it and \person{Hessa Rale}'s eighth, and nine stoppings stood with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end, the loan uncalled.

Nine arithmetics of the year --- the three of \place{Frost}, the first, second and third of \place{Bone}, and \place{Ash}'s first, second and third --- stood behind one unsigned figure: one in twelve printed and unchallenged, the figure read still not the figure paid, no signature on the space between, nine readings nine and not one account. All three of Ash's subtractions rode locked and unread up \place{the clay road} in \person{Berr Clay}'s box toward \place{High Kiln}, the key gone up in a coat pocket and not coming back down. The finding sat on a place of its own with no payer under it and no doctrine standing out of nine, and nothing from the twenty-eighth, twenty-ninth or thirtieth of Ash had crossed under this roof as sign, lesson, mending, cause or payment; the ninth reading had bought no lesson here and answered none of the held questions.

Beside the five held objects, with a finger between every pair, sat the pot-crack resting of Ash 9; beside that, with a finger between, the held breath at the sill of Ash 14; beside all of them, with a finger between it and each, the holding \person{Jorra Vail} had left in the cold iron on the twenty-sixth --- gathered once, found where it had been put on every day since and not made again, for a second would be a habit and a third a way, and both want a payer standing and none stood. The staying at the inch, the early letting-go, the handful let fall at the rack end, the resting of a thumb on the uncut seventh and the sixth opening at \place{the kiln mouth} on Ash 2 sat each on a place of its own with a finger between every pair; six openings in six months were six and not a rule, the sixth held without a name and no seventh made due. The burned-slate word stood at five with a finger between the fifth and the four, the shut-room tellings at two, the well-saying at four shapings, the row at five askings with no witness and expressly no sixth. The Ash 5 joining, the Ash 17 direction and the Ash 23 direction lay each on a place of its own with a finger between every pair, still gathering and unspent, nowhere to be asked and buying no lesson. No tenth hand, tenth stopping, sixth hearing, seventh opening, sixth held object, sixth telling, third shut-room telling, sixth asking or fifth shaping was supplied to make a row come out even.

The man the salt-stiff coat kept on its peg behind the door had not been named under this roof for three hundred and sixty days. \person{Garl Vail} was kept in objects and not in a story: the coat with the crease at the left shoulder, the hollow wheel-seat in \place{the Vail wheel-shed} that nobody was allowed to fill, the clean dark line at the corner with the finer grey beside it that the house called \lore{the thaw}, unswept both, the twice-mended pot on \place{the pot board} with the hairline of the original break showing between old-brass staple and tighter wire, and the unforged collector sentence lying beside a knife at \place{the kindling} with no name on the man who had turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on the debt. The debt against a firing of tiles remained neither paid nor settled nor done with, circled without a name and worse for being uncalled. The keeping of him was maintenance and not a story, and no lesson had been made of any of it.

In the first grey \person{Sella Vail} came to \place{the hearth} before she came to the basket, the way she had come to it at the going of the light and at every morning for three years. Fire before basket; the instruction to the wool, the pot and the river wind and never to the basket. “It's the thaw,” she told the coals, and turned them with the back of a broken pot. “The house will keep it,” she said, where another woman would have said I. She stood a moment counting a small intake, a hold and a letting-go toward no opening, giving neither gap a figure; her finger lay on top of the loaf shelf so the charcoal tally underneath stayed unread, and her lips moved along a two-gapped row and figured neither gap. Her eye went once to \place{the clay jar} standing cold under \lore{lead glaze}, and her hand shut half over nothing, the little-door burn on the back of her left hand closing on air. At the pot board she lifted the twice-mended pot with her thumb first where the wire crossed the crack, held the weight a half-beat before she let it come on the handle, and left the hairline showing. “Leave the crack showing. People trust a thing they can see has been handled,” she told the pot. “If it's worth mending it's worth mending twice. The second time is the real one,” she told the fire, and neither saying was improved in the keeping and neither was wasted, and repair stayed apart from restoration, for a restored thing hides the hand that handled it and a mended thing leaves that hand showing.

She turned the dressed grain under wool with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child, her fingers going in and coming out without closing, no figure lifted off it and no handful. Then she set the pot rim two inches beyond the reaching hand, with a half-beat at the setting and no look toward the bed. \lore{The widow's mark} hung where it hung on the cord at her throat, the little-door burn half-closing on nothing; it was not touched on the first of Stone, because a morning and a hearth are not openings, and six openings in six months were six and not a rule, the sixth held at the kiln mouth on Ash 2 without a name and no seventh made due. It was an ordinary tending day with no market, no Cross, no bell and no collector on the clay road, and no runner came on any road; the directions of the fifth, the seventeenth and the twenty-third stood where they had been put and did not repeat. The boy took the morning apart by what was there and what was not, as he took a field apart before the light, by the small sounds first because the small sounds do not lie and the large ones are mostly weather: the rasp of the pot's wire, the tick of cooling clay, the low river under all of it. Nothing in the room had changed hands since the day before.

The ridge along the skirting board had thickened in the month's cold. It ran near two thumbs wide where it turned the corner, gone the colour of the clean dark line and a shade beyond it, and the sour mineral tang of it came through the wool to where his head lay, so that he tasted the Ash before he saw the light. He set the thickness on a place of its own as weather entered nowhere --- not an eighth opening, not a sign, not a mending, not a cause and not a payment, with no payer standing under it. There had been a thickening in Bone and a thickening in Ash, and neither had been laid across the other; a man who laid them together and called them a rule would have wanted a payer, and none stood. The house had not swept it and would not sweep it before the Last Day, and the boy had long ago stopped expecting anyone to tell him what the ridge kept count of, which was not the same as not wanting to be told.

\scenebreak

In the small hours the surgeon took his small clean satisfaction in a field taken as found and left as found, and it cost him nothing, which was how he knew it was not the same as peace. Underneath it the infant went on wanting the crust lifted the two inches to his mouth, the thread wiped and a hand that would close on the pot handle set beyond the reaching fingers. Both were true, the second was louder, neither won, and the two registers quarrelled without verdict in the ordinary small hours of the first of Stone. He did not spend any of it on himself or make a lesson of it. Three hundred and sixty stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody; six months stood exactly at the close of this day and nobody under the roof called the number or marked a threshold. The keeping of it, bought at two inches of air and not for sale, went on because attention is the one thing no institution here has thought to tax.

The hand outside the wool came up an inch toward the river's note under the finger's-width door, the way a hand goes up in a field it has learned not to disturb. It did not reach, and the inch was not a reach; he had long ago stopped calling it one. Most of what a hand does in a field is wait, and he had once taken a hand into a cavity slowly, with the whole of the attention, and knew the difference between the moving and the waiting. The note went water over water under the door, a low thing over a lower thing, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming, never numbered among the hearings, for a hearing wants a source standing and none stood. The hand took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole on the note's own finishing, let the intake go, and came straight down and laid itself back against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. The note had done before the hand came down, so the letting-go was neither early again nor stayed, and the hand did not stay; it was the note's own finishing that ended the interval and not any decision of the hand, and that was the whole of the honesty in it. The intake was not a breath kept for warmth, the interval not a stopping of the river, the note not his.

He filed it as answer two hundred and ninety-five: exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the nine arithmetics, the bell's gap, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the holding in the iron, the held breath at the sill, the staying at the inch, the early letting-go, the sixth opening, the pot-crack resting, the Ash 5 joining, the Ash 17 direction, the Ash 23 direction, the word that stood in no doorway, the fourth shaping, the turned writing, the strip in the sleeve nor his own kept gaps. Two hundred and ninety-five honest answers stood on the sixth place, each an object and not a power, and not one stood up and called itself a doctrine, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood.

Stone came on without a boundary drawn. Neither of the district's two habits of naming its months --- the older women by the weather, the slatemen by the number of Account-days --- was set right or left wrong in so many words; the month carried both names the way a pot carries two handles and is none the worse for having them. The kiln's first firing stood four mornings ahead on Stone 5, named only as a date; the boy's first birthday on Stone 7 stood beyond that and \lore{the Hearth Drawing} on Stone 12 further off still, and none was named to him under any roof. No ninth arithmetic was supplied early, no seventh hearing, sixth held object, seventh opening, sixth telling, third shut-room telling, sixth asking, fifth shaping, tenth hand or tenth stopping invented to make a row come out even. The far-off hand that would one day lay the district's private keepings together was still far off and was not his.

The hand lay back against the fold on answer two hundred and ninety-five; the file lay open on the towel at the head of the bed with every gap left gap; the twice-mended pot stood on the pot board with the hairline of the original break showing between old-brass staple and tighter wire; one stiff brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from his ear with its empty place unnamed and no third, the thread frozen and unwiped; and through the finger's-width door the river went water over water, low over lower, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming, while the boards overhead kept four nails on the near one, four on the next and a thumb-width knot where a fifth might have been driven and was not. Forward, not closed.
