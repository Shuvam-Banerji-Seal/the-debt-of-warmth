\daymark{362}{Stone 3, Thirdday, Y.H. 813}

The hand outside the wool lay back against the fold on answer two hundred and ninety-eight as a man lays down a tool where the next hand will come and find it without looking, and the room went on around it the way a room goes on around a thing put by for the night. \person[Corin]{The boy} lay on \place{the bed} at the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf} under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof, in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill}, \place{Grath}, in the small hour before light, and the dark had a weight and a smell the way a cloth has a weight and a smell: wet wool, tallow smoke gone cold, clay dust settled into the seams, and under them the sour mineral tang of the ridge along the skirting board that had not been swept in two years and would not be swept on a morning that was no opening. Through the floor \lore{the carried fire} kept four low pulls with the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer unnamed three hundred and sixty-two days. Through \place{the finger's-width door} \place[the Ilm]{the river} kept its note low under its winter skin, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming, the covering laid on two days before and not laid on again. Above him \place{the boards} kept four nails on the near one, four on the next and a thumb-width knot where a fifth might have been driven and was not. Three counts in three places, and none rose into another, for agreeing any two would want a payer put under both and none stood; the bell's gap stayed in its square and was not folded into them.

Three hundred and sixty-one had stood exact behind the day; three hundred and sixty-two stood now, one more in a row nobody counted aloud, not round, not a threshold, only a day. Six months and one hundred and eighty-one days had become one hundred and eighty-two, and nobody under the roof said so. He had long ago stopped expecting to be told what the number counted, which was not the same as not wanting it. The fire's fifth was unpaid the same number with its payer unnamed; the name of the man whose salt-stiff coat kept his shape on the peg behind the door had not been spoken under this roof the same number. \place{Stone} had come on two days before without a boundary drawn and kept its third day the same way, the way water goes on over a stone and gives no account of itself. Neither of the district's two habits of naming its months --- the older women by the weather, the slatemen by the number of Account-days --- was set right or left wrong in so many words; the month carried both names the way a pot carries two handles and is none the worse for having them. The kiln's first firing stood two mornings ahead as a date and was named to nobody, and the boy's first birthday on Stone 7 and \lore{the Hearth Drawing} on Stone 12 stood further off still and were not brought into a day that had not earned them.

The surgeon's register took the body in its old order. One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from his ear, its corner standing a hair off the wool, gone harder and browner through the long cold, its empty place unnamed and no third come in to make the row come out even. The thread of drool at his mouth had frozen in the dry kiln heat and was unwiped, because nobody under any roof has ever been given an instruction about the thread and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. Cold had stopped at cheek, temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, as though it had come to the end of a line written out for it with neither an order to go farther nor an order to go away, and \lore{the Afterdrop} waited beyond all three, past a warming nobody in this house would perform. He knew the composed face that was not composure, knew that a cold patient cannot clot and will seem better before he is worse and must be warmed from the outside and slowly, never hurried, never rubbed, never set too near the fire, and that pink returning to a face is the surface still cold and not the core. He knew it exactly and it was worth nothing at two inches of air.

Underneath the exactness the infant went on wanting the crust lifted the two inches to his mouth, the thread wiped, and a hand that would close on the pot handle set where the fingers could reach it. Both were true, the second was louder, neither won, and the quarrel between them was the work. The body humiliated him in the exactness and not in any grand way: drool frozen, a head that could not be held up, a crust gone leathery past the point where any child would eat it, the pot handle two inches beyond the reaching hand, a hand that could not lift itself to its own chin. He had once reduced a thousand shoulders in the dark and he could not keep a fold of wool on his ear. The joke lived in the exactness and cost him nothing and was not funny to anybody else.

\person{Sella Vail} came to \place{the hearth} before the basket, fire before basket, as she had come at every morning for three years, and the instruction went to the wool, to the river wind and to the fire she would bank against their coming back, never to the basket. “Keep it off his ears,” she told the wool. “It's the river wind.” She told the coals it was the thaw and turned them with the back of a broken pot. “The house will keep it,” she said, where another woman would have said I. She stood a moment counting a small intake, a hold and a letting-go toward no opening, giving neither gap a figure; her finger lay on top of the loaf shelf so the charcoal tally underneath stayed unread, and her lips moved along a two-gapped row and figured neither gap. Her eye went once to \place{the clay jar} standing cold under \lore{lead glaze}, and her hand shut half over nothing, the little-door burn on the back of her left hand closing on air; the cord of \lore{the widow's mark} at her throat was oiled where a working thumb had touched it ten thousand times, and the mark hung where it hung and was not touched, because a morning and a hearth are not openings and six openings in six months are six and not a rule, the sixth held at \place{the kiln mouth} on the second of Ash without a name and no seventh made due.

At \place{the pot board} she lifted the twice-mended pot with her thumb first where the wire crossed the crack, held the weight a half-beat before she let it come on the handle, and left the hairline showing between old-brass staple and tighter wire. “Leave the crack showing. People trust a thing they can see has been handled,” she told the pot. “If it's worth mending it's worth mending twice. The second time is the real one,” she told the fire, and both sayings went to the pot and the fire and never to a person, and neither was improved in the keeping and neither was wasted, and repair stayed apart from restoration, for a restored thing hides the hand that handled it and a mended thing leaves that hand showing. She turned the dressed grain under wool with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child, her fingers going in and coming out without closing, no figure lifted off it and no handful. She set the pot rim two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat at the setting and no look toward the bed.

The boy checked the river's note under the finger's-width door and found the skin where it had been put on the first of Stone, not made again, not added to the three coverings and not made a sixth low thing standing up as a rule. It sat on a place of its own beside \lore{water over water}, with a finger between, a covering put on by the cold and taken off by the cold and entered nowhere, not a hearing, not a seventh of the full note, not a sign, a lesson, a cause, a payment or a payer. Three coverings stayed weather entered nowhere, each on its own place with a finger between every pair: the gone white frost off \place{the yard well}'s north lip, the skin on \place{the trough} that had broken under a hand and closed again, the plate of ice on \place{the flat stone by the well} lifted a little out of the path of feet. A man who laid the river's skin among them and called them a rule would have wanted a payer under all four and none stood.

The file went on where it had been left. Two hundred and ninety-eight honest answers stood on the sixth place, each an object and not a power, and the hand would set the two hundred and ninety-ninth beside them at the end of the dark. Nine arithmetics of the year --- the three of Frost, the first, second and third of Bone, and Ash's first, second and third --- stood behind one unsigned figure; nine were nine and not one account, one in twelve was printed and unchallenged, the figure read was still not the figure paid, and no signature stood on the space between. All three Ash subtractions rode locked and unread up \place{the clay road} in \person{Berr Clay}'s box, the key gone up in a coat pocket and not coming back down. The finding sat on a place of its own with no payer under it, and no doctrine stood up out of nine, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood, and the ninth reading had bought no lesson under this roof. Six hearings of \lore{the full note} stood beside and never among, the seventh place empty, the sixth the small flat knock out of a woman as the men lifted her filed where a thing out of a person must go. The heard bell stood in its square beside the told bell, four, four and a gap, and nobody crossed himself. Nine record-first hands kept no tenth, \person{Ilva}'s ninth on its own place with a finger between it and \person{Hessa Rale}'s eighth, and nine stoppings kept no tenth before the seventh year at its very end with the loan uncalled. Five held objects kept the pot-crack resting beside them, the hand-at-the-sill breath beside that and the holding of the twenty-sixth of Ash beside all, none a sixth, seventh or eighth, with a finger between every pair. The burned slate stood at five, the shut-room tellings at two, the well-saying at four shapings and the row at five askings; the \place{Ash} 5 joining, the Ash 17 direction and the Ash 23 direction stood apart with a finger between every pair, still gathering and unspent. The cold foot below the sill stayed uncured and unnamed and the tins stayed shut. \person{Jorra Vail}'s promise of the boy's name to the men who burn slates was neither given nor withdrawn, and \emph{he's not Vail} stayed sealed. No sixth hearing, seventh opening, sixth held object, sixth telling, third shut-room telling, sixth asking, fifth shaping, tenth hand or tenth stopping was supplied to make a row come out even; a finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged.

In the small hours the surgeon took his small clean satisfaction in a field taken as found and left as found, and it cost him nothing, which was how he knew it was not the same as peace. The hand outside the wool came up an inch toward the river's note under the finger's-width door. It was low under its skin. The hand did not reach, and the inch was not a reach; he had long ago stopped calling it one. Most of what a hand does in a field is wait, and he had once taken a hand into a cavity slowly, with the whole of the attention, and knew the difference between the moving and the waiting. It took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole on the note's own finishing under the ice, let the intake go, and came straight down and laid itself back against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. The note had done before the hand came down, so the letting-go was neither early again nor stayed, and the hand did not stay; it was the note's own finishing under the covering that ended the interval and not any decision of the hand, and that was the whole of the honesty in it. The intake was not a breath kept for warmth, the interval not a stopping of the river, the note not his. A man had held a breath at a knife-sill a hand's breadth beyond the reach of this one on the fourteenth of Ash, and the two intervals did not join; one was spent into a sill and one into a sound, and neither had a payer standing under it.

He filed it as answer two hundred and ninety-nine: exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the nine arithmetics, the bell's gap, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the holding in the iron, the held breath at the sill, the staying at the inch, the early letting-go, the sixth opening, the pot-crack resting, the river's skin, the ninth hand at the well, the Ash 5 joining, the Ash 17 direction, the Ash 23 direction, the word that stood in no doorway, the fourth shaping, the turned writing, the strip in the sleeve nor his own kept gaps. Two hundred and ninety-nine honest answers stood on the sixth place, each an object and not a power, and not one stood up and called itself a doctrine, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood. Three hundred stood close ahead of them now, a round number that nobody under any roof would speak or mark, and he kept it exactly without expecting to be told what it counted, which was not the same as not wanting it. The number would not stand up and call itself anything either.

Stone kept its third day without a boundary drawn, the way water goes on under a skin and gives no account of the cold. Neither of the district's two habits of naming its months was set right or left wrong in so many words; the month carried both names the way a pot carries two handles and is none the worse for having them. No ninth arithmetic was supplied early, no seventh hearing, sixth held object, sixth telling, third shut-room telling, sixth asking, fifth shaping, tenth hand, tenth stopping or fourth likeness invented to make a row come out even. The kiln's first firing on the fifth of Stone stood two mornings ahead as a date and was named to nobody; the boy's first birthday on Stone 7 and \lore{the Hearth Drawing} on Stone 12 stood further off still and were not brought into a day that had not earned them. The far-off hand that would one day lay the district's private keepings together was still far off and was not his.

The hand lay back against the fold on answer two hundred and ninety-nine; the file stayed where it was carried with every gap left gap; the twice-mended pot stood on the pot board with the hairline of the original break showing between old-brass staple and tighter wire; one stiff brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from his ear with its empty place unnamed and no third, the thread frozen and unwiped; and through the finger's-width door the river went under its skin, low over lower, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming, while the boards overhead kept four nails on the near one, four on the next and a thumb-width knot where a fifth might have been driven and was not. Forward, not closed.
