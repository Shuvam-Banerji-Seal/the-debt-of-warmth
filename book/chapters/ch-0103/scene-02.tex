The morning went its length without a boundary drawn and gave way to the afternoon the way a pot gives up heat, by degrees that nobody marked. In the afternoon \person{Sella Vail} carried \person[Corin]{the boy} down. She took the basket level in both hands and went at the half-pace she keeps from every open slate, the pace of a woman carrying a pot filled that must not be slopped, and the instruction went to the wool, to the river wind and to the fire she would bank against their coming back, never to the basket. “Keep it off his ears,” she told the wool. “It's the river wind.” The kiln stood cold behind its wall and would stand cold through four more mornings; she banked the coals under blind ash before she lifted him, with the back of a broken pot, and said nothing about the hour or the number. Under the fold his hand lay back against the crust as a tool lies where the next hand will find it, and the note under \place{the finger's-width door} went still \lore{water over water}, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming, and nothing in the room had changed hands since the light.

She went wide around the seven walked-around places in \place{the Clay Yard}, with a look and a wide step and no number, and a thing entered wants a payer standing and none stood. The clean dark line at \place{the Vail corner} lay where the ridge had turned, with the finer grey beside it that the house called \lore{the thaw}, unswept both. The space beneath \place[the drying racks]{the lowest rack} held its broken birth-slates and was entered by nobody. The shallow clay pan's gone place kept its round ring, with no scrape, no crumb, no print, no chip and no word. \place{the trough} at \place{the yard well} had skinned over from edge to edge, and the silt-clouded plate of ice stood on \place{the flat stone by the well} a little out of the path of feet. The field's empty place lay out on \place{the worked strips}, and \place[the worked strips]{the returning-line place}, where a hand had gone in on \place{Seed} 15 and had not come out, was gone around widest of all, wider than the rest by the width of the step that would have put a foot where the foot had been. Seven places entered none. She did not count them and she did not look twice, and the boy kept the seven where they were and made no eighth of them.

Three coverings stayed weather entered nowhere, with a finger between every pair and no fourth likeness made a cause: the gone white frost off the well's north lip, the skin on the trough that had broken under a hand and closed again, the plate of ice that lifted its clouded face a little out of the path of feet. Each sat on a place of its own. A man who laid them together and called them a rule would have wanted a payer under all three and none stood. At \place{the rack end} she turned the dressed grain once under wool with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child, her fingers going in and coming out without closing, no figure lifted off it and no handful. The empty fingers went on a place of its own beside the one object of \place{Bone} 22, a keeping and not a second handful; the two did not join, and a second handful made to match the first would have been a finding forged.

Then the river's note under the door came low under a skin.

It was not louder. It was not a new sound. A man standing in the yard with his back to the wall might have taken it for the same water he had heard at morning, but the boy had taken the note apart by its small sounds before, and the small sounds did not lie. The water over water had gone. A covering had been put on from bank to bank, and the note came through it low and held, a thing under a lid, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming still but muffled in the going, as a voice is muffled by a door shut between the room and the speaker. \place{The Ilm} had taken its winter ice in the afternoon, under a sky the yard called weather, and had given no account of the cold.

A man come down from \place{Cold Acre} shifted a hare across his shoulders at the well and stood a moment with the weight settling into the small of his back. “It's gone under,” he said, to nobody in particular, and nobody made the sentence a sign.

A woman with her pail against her knee asked what the ice would cost.

“Ask the weather,” the man said. “It laid it on. It'll take it off.” He moved the hare up a hand's breadth and went toward \place{the clay road} with it unweighed, and nobody opened a slate and no figure came. The carrying had been judged by the carrying arm and the weather and had gone where such things go, into a direction and not an account.

The boy took the change apart by what was there and by what was not. There was a skin on the river and there was no hand that had laid it. There was cold and there was no payer standing under it. There was a note low under the covering and there was no source raised up to be asked, and a hearing wants a source standing. He filed the new note on a place of its own beside water over water, which stayed where it had been put and was not displaced by it. Ice over water was a covering put on by the cold and taken off by the cold, entered nowhere; it was expressly not a sixth low thing standing up as a rule, not a hearing, not a seventh of \lore{the full note}, not a fourth of the three coverings, which kept their finger's width of air between every pair, not a sign, a lesson, a cause, a payment or a payer. It gathered toward the deep winter and the kiln's first firing on \place{Stone} 5 and the boy's first birthday on Stone 7 and \lore{the Hearth Drawing} on Stone 12, and it paid none of them and joined none of them, for a thing joined to a date wants a hand to join it and none had done it. The field was left as found and the finding cost nothing, which was how he knew it was not the same as peace.

The afternoon's hunts and repairs went by as directions judged by the carrying arm and the weather, never as figures. A man with a cracked jar in a rope sling asked what the month's mending would want, and was told to ask the pot; the pot, being a pot, said nothing. A woman with a basket of kindling asked what the seventh year would want when it came, and two men mending a strip of fence said the thaw would be early and the thaw would be late. \person{Tarn} said “Yes, house” to each and kept the late, because a late thaw puts off the paying, and he agreed so thoroughly that no argument could find a foot to stand on. The \place{Ash} 5 joining, the Ash 17 direction and the Ash 23 direction stood where they had been put and did not repeat and bought no lesson, and no ownerless voice came over \place{the boards} to make a sixth telling of the burned slate, a third telling of the shut room or a sixth asking of the row. The hare, the jar, the kindling and the mending went off into their several directions and no slate was opened for any of them and no number crossed under any roof.

At the kindling Tarn kept six in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check and the seventh at the lip beyond it where it could not, and \person{Garl Vail}'s unforged collector sentence lay beside the seventh with no name on the man who had turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on the debt. The small knife lay at the very edge of its sill, where a hand could be checked and where a hand could also slip it off, and he passed his hand over blade and sill as a man passes a hand over a tool put back where it belongs. The pale clip-ring at his left wrist caught what little light there was and gave it back. “Six counted. The next I don't count,” he told the wood, in the tone of a man stating the weather, and cut nothing and counted nothing and spent nothing.

He checked the Bone 26 thumb's resting and the Ash 14 held breath at the sill and found each where it had been put and not made again, beside the five held objects and the pot-crack resting with a finger between every pair. The held breath was not a sixth held object and the thumb's resting was not a seventh of anything and neither was a tenth stopping, and no payer stood under either. He brought the ear-sentence up to the word that would call the loan and stopped there, as a surgeon draws a hand out of a field before the dressing goes on — a remembered ninth stopping with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end, the Seed 16 breath unspent at the top of the reach and the loan uncalled. His chest had been tight since the dark, and the Ash was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line, and his stroke went over the shallow bright patch on \place{the flagstones} without the half-check. He did not look along the road.

\person{Jorra Vail} came through the finger's-width door off \place{the Vail wheel-shed} with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bent little finger and white in a fist-crease where a hand had opened, and it was not the bone-coloured \lore{slip} of the wheel. Men unnamed had gone low into \place{the back room} before first grey; he gave the house no name for them and did not say the room was cold. He kept his dusty forearms and his clean ones on one tray and parted them as a surgeon parts clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had been broken nor whether it was a birth-slate while the broken ones lay under the lowest rack and were entered by nobody.

He spoke to the wheel before he spoke to any person. “It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady,” he said, and told a pot “You'll do” in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either. He knew the full note that pot would give three turns before it left his hands, with air under it and a want in it, and kept the knowing where nobody could make a song of it, withheld from any master come up from \place{High Kiln} who would call it early and sing a pot before its time.

Sella had not finished telling him the fire was low. His bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper} ahead of her sentence, took the cold out of the fingertip, moved the tongue a hair's breadth and left it standing, so that the end of her instruction arrived at the pot and the household straightened around the waiting as cloth straightens when a weight is lifted. One piece of iron had two bites, the holding and the taking, and there was no \place{Grathic} word for both mercies. He did not leave the extra interval of \place{Ash} 26 a second time. The holding stayed where nobody would enter it, gathered once and not made again, for a second would be a habit and a third a way, and both want a payer and none stood. “He's not Vail” stayed sealed and the promise of the boy's name to the men who burn slates was neither given nor withdrawn.

At the going of the light Sella lifted the basket out of the yard with the whole of her attention on its level, as a potter lifts a thing that is still soft, and went back around the seven places with a look and a wide step and no number, widest around the returning-line place. The instruction went to the wool and the river wind and never to the basket. She said nothing of where they had been and nothing of what the afternoon had laid on the river, and that was not a refusal to tell him, for nobody under any roof tells a basket what a covering means and she was not the woman who would say a thing to please the speaker.

Under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof she came to \place{the hearth} before the basket, fire before basket, as she had come at every going of the light for three years. “It's the thaw,” she told the coals, and turned them with the back of a broken pot. “The house will keep it,” she said, where another woman would have said I. She stood a moment counting a small intake, a hold and a letting-go toward no opening, giving neither gap a figure, and her finger lay on top of \place{the loaf shelf} so the charcoal tally underneath stayed unread. Her lips moved along a two-gapped row and figured neither gap. Her eye went once to \place{the clay jar} standing cold under \lore{lead glaze}, and her hand shut half over nothing, the little-door burn on the back of her left hand closing on air; the cord of \lore{the widow's mark} at her throat was oiled where a working thumb had touched it ten thousand times, and the mark hung where it hung and was not touched, because an afternoon and a going of the light are not openings and six openings in six months are six and not a rule, the sixth held at \place{the kiln mouth} on Ash 2 without a name and no seventh made due.

At \place{the pot board} she lifted the twice-mended pot with her thumb first where the wire crossed the crack, held the weight a half-beat before she let it come on the handle, and left the hairline showing between old-brass staple and tighter wire. “Leave the crack showing. People trust a thing they can see has been handled,” she told the pot. “If it's worth mending it's worth mending twice. The second time is the real one,” she told the fire, and both sayings went to the pot and the fire and never to a person, and neither was improved in the keeping and neither was wasted, and repair stayed apart from restoration, for a restored thing hides the hand that handled it and a mended thing leaves that hand showing. She turned the dressed grain under wool with no figure lifted and no handful, and set the pot rim two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat at the setting and no look toward the bed.

Then she set him on \place{the bed} under the roof and never in \place{the Debt Loft}, put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face, let her hand rest the half-beat longer than the setting needed before it went away, and left the thread frozen and unwiped, because nobody under any roof has ever been given an instruction about the thread. The crust lay two inches from his ear, stiff and leathery, its corner standing a hair off the wool, its empty place unnamed and no third. Cold had stopped at cheek, temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, as at the end of a line written out for it with neither an order to go farther nor an order to go away, and \lore{the Afterdrop} waited past a warming nobody in this house would perform. He knew all of it exactly and wanted the crust lifted, the thread wiped and a hand that would close on the pot handle set where the fingers could reach it. Both were true, the second was louder, neither won, and the quarrel between them was the work.

In the small hours the hand outside the wool came up an inch toward the river's note under the finger's-width door. It was low under its skin now. The hand did not reach, and the inch was not a reach; he had long ago stopped calling it one. Most of what a hand does in a field is wait, and he had once taken a hand into a cavity slowly, with the whole of the attention, and knew the difference between the moving and the waiting. It took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole on the note's own finishing under the ice, let the intake go, and came straight down and laid itself back against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. The note had done before the hand came down, so the letting-go was neither early again nor stayed, and the hand did not stay; it was the note's own finishing under the covering that ended the interval and not any decision of the hand, and that was the whole of the honesty in it. The intake was not a breath kept for warmth, the interval not a stopping of the river, the note not his.

He filed it as answer two hundred and ninety-six: exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the nine arithmetics, the bell's gap, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the holding in the iron, the held breath at the sill, the staying at the inch, the early letting-go, the sixth opening, the pot-crack resting, the river's skin, the Ash 5 joining, the Ash 17 direction, the Ash 23 direction, the word that stood in no doorway, the fourth shaping, the turned writing, the strip in the sleeve nor his own kept gaps. Two hundred and ninety-six honest answers stood on the sixth place, each an object and not a power, and not one stood up and called itself a doctrine, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood. \lore{The carried fire} kept four low pulls with the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer unnamed three hundred and sixty days; the name of the man whose salt-stiff coat kept his shape on the peg behind the door had not been spoken under this roof the same number.

The hand lay back against the fold on answer two hundred and ninety-six; the file lay open on \place{the towel} at the head of the bed with every gap left gap; the twice-mended pot stood on the pot board with the hairline of the original break showing between old-brass staple and tighter wire; one stiff brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from his ear with its empty place unnamed and no third, the thread frozen and unwiped; and through the finger's-width door the river went low under its winter skin, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming, while the boards overhead kept four nails on the near one, four on the next and a thumb-width knot where a fifth might have been driven and was not. Forward, not closed.
