# Chapter 56 — beat sheet: "The Seventh of the Green"

**Days:** 219–221, Grain 10–12, Tenthday to Secondday, Y.H. 813. Normal chapter, three lived days, six scenes of two per day, each day opened by its own `\daymark` in that day's first scene only (Scene 1 = `\daymark{219}{Grain 10, Tenthday, Y.H. 813}`, Scene 3 = `\daymark{220}{Grain 11, Firstday, Y.H. 813}`, Scene 5 = `\daymark{221}{Grain 12, Secondday, Y.H. 813}`). Scenes 2, 4 and 6 carry no new heading. Day 219 is the first Account-day and market of Grain. No day is skipped, poured into another or summarised; each is lived for well over 800 words across its two scenes.

**Voice before every scene:** `design/STYLE-REFERENCE.md` and `bible/voice.md` open beside `memory/state.md` and this sheet. Close third-person past tense inside Dan/Corin; the present-tense seam belongs only to Chicago breaking through and earns no appearance here; `\memory{}` tags none. The surgeon's register colours the noticing without explaining the world. No recap at any scene opening --- a day begins as a smell and a gap in sound, not as a reminder of the day before. Body humiliation exact: drool frozen and unwiped, the useless head, the crust two inches off, the pot handle two inches beyond the hand, cold stopped at cheek--temple--wrist with the afterdrop waiting past a warming nobody performs. Power: none. No rank, no experiment, no clamp, no seventh hearing, no tenth stopping, no payer named.

**The chapter's one meaningful change:** at Grain's first market a **seventh green** comes to a silent woman's mouth at the pot board and a **seventh sighting of the foundling** comes at the market's edge. Both are paid as a colour and a shape on places that do not touch, filed beside the six earlier greens and six earlier shapes, never joined, never Fenna or Ulda in the narration, never promoted into a hearing, a seventh of the full note, a tenth stopping, a cause, a name or a romance. The loves that cannot be booked stay unbooked; Fenna's ear and Ulda's sale to the Waiting Room clerk stay years away. The market's arithmetic changes for Grain and cannot be changed back, while the printed figure stands unchallenged and the figure read aloud is still not the figure paid.

## Pull into the next chapter

The final moment hands Chapter 57 a district that has had its first Account-day of Grain and a house that has not read any of it. The seventh green and the seventh shape are now facts on separate trays --- a colour and a shape, gathered and not joined --- and Grain's middle tend opens with nothing to prove them and no eighth to add; the arts of the market have changed the district's arithmetic for Grain, but the printed figure stands unchallenged, the true subtraction stays in Berr's locked box and a figure read aloud is still not the figure paid.
