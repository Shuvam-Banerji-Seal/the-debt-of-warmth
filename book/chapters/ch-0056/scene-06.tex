In \place{Low Kiln} the afternoon thinned without ending. Heat left the high air first, the way water leaves a stone that has stood long in the sun, and settled into the low places: under \place{the loaf shelf}, behind the pot, into the fold at the boy's ear. There one stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from him and kept its empty place unnamed, and no third came to make the row come out even. The room smelled of hot clay under tallow and wet wool under that, and beneath both the sour \lore{Ash} breath of the unswept corner the house called \lore{the thaw}. Under the floor \lore{the carried fire} drew in four low pulls and withheld the fifth, and the space where the fifth should have stood went on being a space with no payer named to it. Fire in the room that warmed him, river in the room he could not enter; neither stopped for the ear under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof.

Cold had stopped at cheek and temple and the small bare joint at the wrist where the wool rode back, as if it had reached the end of an order written out for it. It went no further and it did not go away. The afterdrop waited on the far side of a warming nobody in this house would perform; he knew the afterdrop when he saw it and he wanted the crust, and the child's wanting was the louder of the two, as it had been on every day of his life. The thread of drool at his mouth had frozen in the dry heat and was unwiped, because nobody under any roof had been given an instruction about the thread and a basket set down was a pot set down and left. He could not lift his chin two inches to the crust, close his hand two inches on the rim of the basket that held him, or speak a number that anybody would believe. He had learned to hold the humiliation and the small capacity on separate trays and let neither stand up as a lesson about the other.

\scenebreak

\person{Sella Vail} came to the shelf with her back to the basket, the back first and the face withheld, as she had come to it ten thousand afternoons. She moved her lips along the loaf row, and the row stood with two unnamed gaps, the old space at the row's end and the palm's breadth that had come in on a night years back with no scrape, no crumb, no print and no word. She gave nobody a figure. Her eye went once to the clay jar cold under its glaze on the wall shelf, and her hand started toward it and shut half over nothing without a touch, the little-door burn closing on air the way it closed on everything that came within reach of it. Her finger lay on the top of the shelf's front edge where any woman in the district could see it lying, and the charcoal tally under the board stayed unread, uncounted and uncopied, because a woman's back turned was not the same thing as a finger beneath it and the reason stood between the two, unspoken. A hand that knows a board by heart and a hand that has been told not to look leave the same mark on a room.

At the hearth she told the fire it was the thaw, and turned the coals with the back of a broken pot, and the fire answered four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have been. “The house will keep it,” she said, where another woman would have said \emph{I}, and stood a moment with the back of the pot in her hand counting under her breath toward no opening --- small intake, hold, letting go --- the count arriving nowhere as it had arrived nowhere on every day of his life. She pushed a strand of coal-black hair back with the wrist that carried the burn and the grey streak at her temple showed pale off the coals, and she did not discuss the streak or the winter it had come in.

She set the pot rim out with its handle two inches beyond the reaching hand and let her hand rest a half-beat longer at the setting than the setting required, and did not look toward \place{the bed}. At an afternoon and a shelf she did not touch \lore{the widow's mark}; it hung on its cord at her throat where it had hung since the first firing, touched once before the kiln on Ember 1 and at no hour since, and there had been no opening in forty-one days, because a shelf, an afternoon and a bed are not openings. The boy lay two inches short of the handle and watched the half-beat go by as a man watches a hand come out of a field before the dressing goes on. It was a thing done and a thing withheld in one motion, and he put it where it belonged and laid nothing across it and named no payer to it. Her private roll stayed unshown and \person[Dessa Silt]{Dessa}'s cost stayed where it had been put down and never entered.

\scenebreak

\person[Garl Vail]{Garl} was kept to the afternoon without a story, as he had been kept to every afternoon since the water went. The salt-stiff coat hung on its peg behind the door with the crease at the left shoulder holding a broader man's shape, and the seat in the shed kept its twenty-year hollow and its lean to the left and nobody had filled either. In the corner the clean dark line where Ash had turned kept its place, and the new finer hairline of grey beside it, called the thaw, had not been swept; the two lines lay in the returning order and no broom had been given an instruction about either. His proverb had gone years ago to a pot with no crack in it, said now over a corner where the repair had been rubbed out: leave the crack showing; people trust a thing they can see has been handled. The debt against a firing of tiles went on circling without a name, neither paid nor settled nor done with, and worse for being uncalled. No lesson came out of it and no collector came up \place{the clay road} for it; his name had not been spoken under this roof for two hundred and twenty-one days, and the keeping of him was maintenance and not a story.

\scenebreak

At \place{the kindling} \person{Tarn} took the broom only after it had been instructed, the instruction going to the tool before it went to the man, and he took it up as a man takes up an instrument already named. “Yes, house,” he said, and told the wood “Six counted. The next I don't count.” He set the small knife at the very edge of its sill, six in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check and the seventh at the lip where it could not, and Garl's unforged collector sentence lay beside it, the man sent for a collector who turned back at the frozen ford, neither entered. He gave no name to the man who had turned back, though he had been that man.

He said \emph{Yes, house} to those who wanted the thaw early and to those who wanted it late, agreed so entirely that no argument could find a foot, and went on wanting the late because a late thaw puts off the paying, though the paying had stood in the ruts since the water went. His chest had been tight since the dark; Ash was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line and no scrubbing lifted it, and the pale clip-ring at his left wrist held what light there was and gave it back.

The sentence about an ear under this roof being worth more than the debt that held it came to his teeth and went all the way to the word that would call the loan before it stopped, with no figure and no name; he filed the stopping as a remembered stopping and not a new one, and counted nine behind it with no tenth standing before the seventh year at its very end, which had come and had still not given. The breath from Seed 16 drew at the top of each reach and was not spent, a small interval where the cost should have gone in, and the loan went on being uncalled. The warm stone Sella gave him in the winter Garl died lay in its unmarked clay pot in \place{the Debt Loft}, two years gone and cold long since, and he could no more reconcile it with the debt than the house's kindness with the account he kept of it; he had stopped trying, which is not forgiving.

His stroke went over \place{the flagstones} past the shallow bright patch without the half-check and over the road a house sends a man down when it wants a collector without once turning along it, and no collector came up or went down the clay road on Grain 12.

\scenebreak

At the edge of \place{the Vail wheel-shed} \person{Jorra Vail} came through with the afternoon's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bent little finger and under the nails, white in the creases where a fist had opened, and not bone-coloured \lore{slip}. He kept dusty forearms and clean ones on one tray and parted them as a surgeon parts clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had been broken nor whether it was a birth-slate. Three or more men had gone low into the finger's-width-open \place{the back room} before first grey and he gave them no name and did not say the room was cold; the two tellings of the shut counting room stood at two with no third, and \lore{the burned-slate word} stood where it had stood, four times told and arrived nowhere from \place{Slate Row}.

He spoke to the wheel before he spoke to anyone. “It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady,” he said, and told a pot “You'll do” in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either. He knew \lore{the full note} that the pot would give three turns before it left his hands, and the knowing had air under it and a want in it, and he kept it back, on the near-full tray beside the hearings and never among them. The three shapings of the pot saying stood as sayings about a pot with no lesson made and no pot lost, and a fourth likeness did not stand up among them and call itself a rule. He gave no name to what ailed the man carried out on Day 188. \emph{He's not Vail} stayed sealed as tightly as on the hour it was spoken, and the promise of the boy's name to the men who burn slates was neither given nor withdrawn.

When Sella's voice went low and unfinished at the hearth his bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper} ahead of her hand, and held until the sentence reached the pot, then moved a hair's breadth and was left standing --- a remembered repetition, not a change made again; the boy read it as one iron with two bites, the bite that holds a vessel whole and the bite that can take the hand off a man, with no word in \lore{Grathic} for both mercies, an object worth nothing joined to no kiln.

\scenebreak

Sella carried him out at the going of the light, and \place{the Clay Yard} went around what it had lost the way water goes around a stone that lies where a stone lies. Woodsmoke went before her in long low bars and river wind came behind with \lore{water over water}, and the two smells did not mix. The clean dark line and the new finer grey beside it kept their places; under \place{the lowest rack} the broken birth-slates lay where the yard entered them into nothing, and she went wide around the space; the round empty ring where the shallow clay pan had stood kept no scrape, no crumb, no print, no chip and no word, and she went wide around that too; at \place{the yard well} \place{the trough} stood bare and dark with its water called weather, and beyond it the field's empty place and the place the carriers crossed had a look and a wide step each and no number. No third gap came in the returning line and no fourth likeness stood up to make a cause of any two absences, and no finger of his drew the line between them. The carrying arm was a record in muscle that remembered weight and not cost, and it had carried him up and down this yard for two hundred and twenty-one days without once being asked what the weight was worth.

Under the roof she set him on his bed and not in the Debt Loft, put the fold right without a look at his face and left the thread frozen; told the fire it was the thaw, turned the coals with the back of a broken pot, and went to her own bed before the small hours.

\scenebreak

In the small hours, with the house gone quiet, he turned out the file as a man lays instruments on a towel --- no towel, no instruments, no case, but the order of the laying the whole of the discipline --- and found it exact, complete and worth nothing.

Six hearings of the full note stand beside and never among: five from four sources, a cup across a birth room, a pot in the wheel-shed through a finger's width of door, the district's slate twice at \place{the Cross}, and the sixth, the small flat knock out of Dessa as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go and never among the sources. No seventh came in two hundred and twenty-one days and none was invented to fill the empty place, for an empty place kept empty is a finding and a finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged.

Three near-full answers stand on their own tray, advanced and unpaid: a pot under the shed wall that answered nearly full with air under the note and a want in it, a rack pot lifted a hand's breadth too high and set down again, and the knowing the wheel-keeper keeps back from a man come up on a wrong wind. Beside them stand three shapings of the pot saying, all sayings and no rule: a pot that sings before its time mostly sings itself out early; the pot that sang early last month has not been heard from since; and a pot gone quiet on every row come the thaw. None was promoted into the hearings, no cause stands up, no lesson was made, no name was given to what ailed the man carried out on Day 188, and no pot was lost in the yard.

The told bell lies beside the heard, four, four and a gap with nobody crossing himself; the told figure lies beside the paid, one in twelve read three times over a basket and still not the figure paid, the three readings of Ember and the third reading gone up the clay road in \person{Berr Clay}'s locked box with the true subtraction inside, answering nothing for the glaze-room hand carried out on Day 188; and the printed figure stands unchallenged where it has always stood.

Nine stoppings stand on Reed 5, 8, 11, 14, 17, 20, 23, 26 and 29 as objects and not a rule, with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end, which has come and has still not given, and no payer named to any of them. The Seed 16 breath is unspent and the loan uncalled.

Three held changes stand on three places. The fraction from the morning of Day 161 --- the hand that waited at the iron for a sentence to finish --- lies where it was left, held and never spoken, no payer named to it and no slate entered. Beneath the loaf shelf the beat that stayed under the board on Day 166 stands on a place of its own, the reason standing between knowing the board by heart and being told not to look; Sella kept her finger on top of the shelf's front edge through the afternoon and did not go beneath it. The flagstone half-check made on Tallow 22 stands on a third place, a place in a yard as the others are places in a wall and in a room that does not touch either; it was not repeated. A fourth place holds the small dry scrape of a pot's foot set down on stone in the Debt Loft on Day 183. A fifth place holds the pale line at the gum of the glaze-room hand, read at the well on Day 187 and on the carried body on Day 188, exact, complete and worth nothing at two paces. A sixth place holds the held inch, with each answer put there one at a time and no payer named to any. Three held changes, not a pair and not a doctrine, with a fourth, fifth and sixth place beside them and no word spoken over any and no payer named; he will not let them stand up together and call themselves a rule, because a rule is an account that has found a payer.

Beside the hearings but never among them, the seventh green is a colour and the seventh shape is a shape, two days gathered now and gathered nothing in the gathering. The green came to the mouth of a silent woman at \place{the pot board} --- clay in the small crescents under the nails, the sweetish \lore{lead glaze} in the crease beside the lip --- and the shape came at the market's edge, a green sliver on a string that caught the light and went dull, a head that turned toward a set-down pot a half-beat late and stopped in the middle of the turn. He filed the one as a colour and the other as a shape on places that do not touch, with a finger between each and the hearings, and no name spoken over either. No eighth of either had come, no second look had been joined to the first, and neither had been promoted into a hearing, a seventh of the note, a tenth stopping, a cause, a name or a romance. Six greens behind a seventh green made a row of greens; six shapes behind a seventh shape made a row of shapes; a row is not an account until somebody finds a payer to stand under it, and no payer stood under either row. The woman had no name in the narration and the shape had none, and the sale to a clerk of \place{the Waiting Room} stayed years ahead of the basket and was not brought early into a day that had no market in it.

And beside the dust and the two shut-room tellings, with a hand between the three, the burned-slate word stood where it had stood: a direction arrived nowhere, four times told, no cause, no hearing, no seventh, no stopping, no payment, nothing a clerk could chart, advanced and not paid. Four tellings of a burning are not four witnesses to one fire, and a word about a slate is not a word about a pot. No slate burned under any roof in these three days and no new fire was lit at the kiln mouth: a fire would have cost something and the telling cost nothing, and the boy kept the two apart. No third gap came in the returning line, no fourth likeness made a cause, and nothing was filled to make a row come out even.

Then he put the question to his hand. Sella was gone to her bed and there was no breathing number anywhere in the house --- nothing to hold against but the rhythm that came through the finger's-width door, the low note of water over water drawing out and slackening, and the next low note coming when it came. In the other room her breaths had gone small intake, hold, letting go at her fire, a count she keeps toward no opening and has never offered to any ear under this roof. The two rooms do not touch and neither do the two rhythms. The hand outside the wool went up an inch toward the note and stayed; he took it through one of those counted breaths and kept it through the little silence after her count had ended and brought it down on the river's next low note. The two-rhythm coordination was remembered, not a change made again. It was a holding and a holding is a holding, set down like a tool: not a longer reach, not a miss, not a hearing, not a seventh, not a stopping, not a payment, not one of the three held changes, not a key, not a power and not a rule, filed on its sixth place with no payer named to it. He still could not close the two inches to the pot handle Sella had set out, lift his chin two inches to the crust, or speak a number that anybody under any roof had been asked to believe. Exactness and humiliation were one motion in that small hand and both were true, and neither stood up to call itself a sign.

Two hundred and twenty-one stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in \place{Sarn}, in \place{Sill} or in \place{Grath}. The carried fire gave four low pulls and withheld its fifth, its payer unnamed for two hundred and twenty-one days; the river kept its fifth low note through the finger's-width door; overhead the boards kept four nails, four and a thumb-width knot. Three counts in three places, and none rose up and became another, because a man who wanted them to agree would have to put a payer under all three and no payer stood to any of them. The seventh year stood at its very end and had still not given; the seventh green was a colour and the seventh shape a shape, gathered and unjoined; Grain 20, eight mornings off, waited for the second Account-day and market and was not brought early into the small hours. The widow's mark hung where it hung with no opening in forty-one days; the shelf kept its two gaps and the fold its one crust with the empty place beside it still unnamed; the charcoal stayed unread and the stone cold in its loft pot. Nothing was joined, named, opened, called, spent or ended.

The fire went on in the room that warmed him and the river in the room he could not enter, and neither stopped for the ear under the roof. The hand came down on the river's next low note after the house's count and its little silence, the thread frozen and unwiped at his mouth, one stiff brown-edged crust two inches from his ear with its empty place still unnamed, and the count went forward exact and unspent into Day 222, Grain 13, with nothing settled and nothing ended.
