The afternoon thinned without ending. It went off the high air first, the thin heat that had lain all day along the top of \place{the loaf shelf}, and settled by degrees into the low places --- under the shelf, behind the pot, into the fold of grey wool at \person[Corin]{the boy}'s ear, where one stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from him and kept the empty place beside it unnamed. No third came to make the row come out even. The house had kept one crust through two hundred and twenty days and would go on keeping one through all the days to come, and keeping was not the same thing as adding; a man who filled an empty place to make a row stand level would be entering a thing nobody had handed him. The thread of drool at his mouth had frozen in the dry heat and was unwiped, because nobody under any roof had been given an instruction about the thread and a basket set down was a pot set down and left. Cold had come to cheek and temple and the small bare joint at the wrist where the wool rode back, and had stopped there as if it had reached the end of an order written out for it; it went no further and it did not go away, and the afterdrop waited on the far side of a warming nobody in this house would perform. He knew the afterdrop when he saw it and he wanted the crust. Both were true, and the child's wanting was the louder of the two, as it had been on every afternoon of his life.

Under the floor \lore{the carried fire} gave four low pulls and withheld the fifth, and the space where the fifth should have stood went on being a space with no payer named to it for two hundred and twenty days. Through the finger's-width door the river kept its note of \lore{water over water}, a fifth low thing never counted among the hearings; overhead the boards kept four nails on the near board, four on the next, and a thumb-width knot where the fifth nail should have stood. Three counts in three places, and none rose up and became another, because a man who wanted them to agree would have to put a payer under all three and no payer stood to any of them. The fire went on in the room that warmed him and the river in the room he could not enter, and neither stopped for the ear under the roof. He took the room's smell as the day's last report --- hot clay under tallow, wet wool under that, and under both the sour mineral breath of the unswept corner the house called \lore{the thaw} --- and filed it without an entry, the way a man files a thing he will never need to spend.

\scenebreak

\person{Sella Vail} came to the fire before she came to the basket at the going of the afternoon, the way she came to everything that wanted keeping: the back first, the face withheld, the hand going where it had gone ten thousand times before. “It's the thaw,” she told the fire, and turned the coals with the back of a broken pot, and the fire answered four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have been. She did not look toward \place{the bed} to see whether the boy had heard the gap. Every instruction in the room went to the wool, the fire, the pot, the broom, the running water and the river wind, and never to the basket. “The house,” she said, where another woman would have said \emph{I}, and “The house will keep it,” and stood a moment with the back of the pot in her hand counting under her breath toward no opening --- small intake, hold, letting go --- the count arriving nowhere as it had arrived nowhere on every day of his life.

At \place{the loaf shelf} she stood with her back to the basket and moved her lips along the loaf row, and the row stood with two unnamed gaps, the old space at the row's end and the palm's breadth that had come in on a night years back with no scrape, crumb, print or word. She gave nobody a figure. Her eye went once to the clay jar cold under its glaze on the wall shelf, and her hand started toward it and shut half over nothing without a touch, the little-door burn closing on air the way it closed on everything that came within reach of it. Her finger lay on the top of the shelf's front edge where any woman in the district could see it lying, and the charcoal tally under the board stayed unread, uncounted and uncopied, because a woman's back turned was not the same thing as a finger beneath it and the reason stood between the two, unspoken. A hand that knows a board by heart and a hand that has been told not to look leave the same mark on a room. She pushed a strand of coal-black hair back with the wrist that carried the burn and the grey streak at her temple showed pale off the coals, and she did not discuss the streak or the winter it had come in.

She set the pot rim out with its handle two inches beyond the reaching hand and let her hand rest a half-beat longer at the setting than the setting required, and did not look toward the bed. At an afternoon and at a dusk she did not touch \lore{the widow's mark}; it hung on its cord at her throat where it had hung since the first firing, touched once before the kiln on Ember 1 and at no hour since, and there had been no opening in forty days, because a shelf, an afternoon and a bed are not openings. The boy lay two inches short of the handle and watched the half-beat go by as a man watches a hand come out of a field before the dressing goes on. It was a thing done and a thing withheld in one motion, and he put it where it belonged and laid nothing across it and named no payer to it. Her private roll stayed unshown and \person[Dessa Silt]{Dessa}'s cost stayed where it had been put down and never entered.

\scenebreak

\person{Garl Vail} was kept to the day without a story, as he had been kept for two hundred and twenty days. The salt-stiff coat hung on its peg behind the door with the crease at the left shoulder where a broader man had turned toward the wheel, and Sella did not take it down and Jorra had twice reached for it in the dark and stopped. The seat at the wheel kept its twenty-year hollow and its lean to the left, and nobody filled either. In the corner the clean dark line where \lore{Ash} had turned kept its place, and the new finer hairline of grey beside it, called the thaw, had not been swept. A line left where it is is still a line kept. His sayings lived in the house's mouth: \emph{feed the fire before you feed yourself}, and \emph{leave the crack showing; people trust a thing they can see has been handled} --- the proverb gone years ago to a pot with no crack in it, said now over a corner where the repair had been rubbed out. The debt against a firing of tiles went around the house without a name, circled and circled and worse for being uncalled; it was neither paid nor settled nor done with, and no collector came up \place{the clay road} for it on Grain 11. His name had not been spoken under this roof in two hundred and twenty days. The keeping of him was maintenance, not a lesson and not a story, and no account stood up out of the objects to call itself a grief.

The seventh green and the seventh shape were two days gathered now and had gathered nothing in the gathering. The green was a colour at the mouth of a silent woman at \place{the pot board}, clay in the small crescents under the nails and the sweetish \lore{lead glaze} in the crease beside the lip; the shape was a shape at the market's edge, a green sliver on a string that caught the light and went dull, a head that turned toward a set-down pot a half-beat late and stopped in the middle of the turn. He had filed the one as a colour and the other as a shape on places that do not touch, with a finger between each and the hearings of \lore{the full note}, and no name spoken over either. No eighth of either had come to stand beside them, no second look had been joined to the first, and neither had been promoted into a hearing, a seventh of the note, a tenth stopping, a cause, a name or a romance. Six greens behind a seventh green made a row of greens; six shapes behind a seventh shape made a row of shapes; a row is not an account until somebody finds a payer to stand under it, and no payer stood under either row. The woman had no name in the narration and the shape had none, and the sale to a clerk of \place{the Waiting Room} stayed years ahead of the basket and was not brought early into an afternoon that had no market in it.

\lore[the burned-slate word]{The burned-slate word} stood where it had stood, four times told and still a direction arrived nowhere from \place{Slate Row}, not a cause, not a hearing, not a seventh, not a stopping, not a payment, nothing a clerk could chart. Four tellings of a burning are not four witnesses to one fire, and a word about a slate is not a word about a pot. He took it apart as a surgeon takes apart a wound and set it on its own place beside the wheel-keeper's dust and the two shut-room tellings with a hand between the three, and he let it stand there, advanced and unpaid. No slate burned under any roof this afternoon and no new fire was lit at the kiln mouth; a fire would have cost something and the telling cost nothing, and he kept the two apart. The district's changed arithmetic lay outside the roof like weather, and weather is a thing a child in a basket can lie under without once being asked to read it.

\scenebreak

At \place{the kindling} \person{Tarn} took the broom only after it had been instructed, the instruction going to the tool before it went to the man, and he took it up as a man takes up an instrument already named. “Yes, house,” he said, and told the wood “Six counted. The next I don't count.” He set the small knife at the very edge of its sill, six in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check and the seventh at the lip where it could not, and Garl's unforged collector sentence lay beside it, the man sent for a collector who turned back at the frozen ford, neither entered. He gave no name to the man who had turned back, though he had been that man. He said \emph{Yes, house} to those who wanted the thaw early and to those who wanted it late, agreed so entirely that no argument could find a foot, and went on wanting the late because a late thaw puts off the paying, though the paying had stood in the ruts since the water went. His stroke went over \place{the flagstones} past the shallow bright patch without the half-check and over the road a house sends a man down when it wants a collector without once turning along it, and no collector came up or went down the clay road on Grain 11. \lore{Ash} was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line and no scrubbing lifted it, and the pale clip-ring at his left wrist held what light there was and gave it back. His chest had been tight since the dark.

The sentence about an ear under this roof being worth more than the debt that held it came to his teeth and went all the way to the word that would call the loan before it stopped, with no figure and no name; he filed the stopping as a remembered stopping and not a new one, and counted nine behind it with no tenth standing before the seventh year at its very end, which had come and had still not given. The breath from Seed 16 drew at the top of each reach and was not spent, a small interval where the cost should have gone in, and the loan went on being uncalled. The warm stone Sella gave him in the winter Garl died lay in its unmarked clay pot in \place{the Debt Loft}, two years gone and cold long since, and he could no more reconcile it with the debt than he could the house's kindness with the account he kept of it; he had stopped trying, which is not forgiving. He did not ask about the room and nobody asked about the ear, and the two silences stayed unjoined under the one roof. Above the shed, in the loft pot with no mark on it, the stone kept its cold and asked nothing of anybody.

At the edge of \place{the Vail wheel-shed} \person{Jorra Vail} came through with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bent little finger and under the nails, white in the creases where a fist had opened, and not bone-coloured \lore{slip}. He kept dusty forearms and clean ones on one tray and parted them as a surgeon parts clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had been broken nor whether it was a birth-slate. Three or more men had gone low into the finger's-width-open \place{the back room} before first grey and he gave them no name and did not say the room was cold; the two tellings of the shut counting room stood at two with no third, and the burned-slate word stood where it had stood, four times told and arrived nowhere. He spoke to the wheel before he spoke to anyone. “It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady,” he said, and told a pot “You'll do” in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either.

He knew the full note that the pot would give three turns before it left his hands, and the knowing had air under it and a want in it, and he kept it back, on the near-full tray beside the hearings and never among them. The three shapings of the pot saying stood as sayings about a pot with no lesson made and no pot lost, and a fourth likeness did not stand up among them and call itself a rule, because a rule is an account that has found a payer. He gave no name to what ailed the man carried out on Day 188. \emph{He's not Vail} stayed sealed as tightly as on the hour it was spoken, and the promise of the boy's name to the men who burn slates was neither given nor withdrawn. When Sella's voice went low and unfinished at the hearth his bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper} ahead of her hand, and held until the sentence reached the pot, then moved a hair's breadth and was left standing --- a remembered repetition, not a change made again; the boy read it as one iron with two bites, the bite that holds a vessel whole and the bite that can take the hand off a man, with no word in \lore{Grathic} for both mercies, an object worth nothing joined to no kiln.

\scenebreak

Sella carried him home at the going of the light at the half-pace, woodsmoke before her in long low bars and river wind behind with the taste of water over water, and the carrying arm was a record in muscle that remembered weight and not cost. She went around \place{the Clay Yard} the way she went around a pail of hot water set where a child might knock it. The clean dark line kept its place and the finer hairline called the thaw had not been swept; under \place{the lowest rack} the broken birth-slates lay where the yard entered them into nothing, and she went wide around the space; the round empty ring where the shallow clay pan had stood kept no scrape, no crumb, no print, no chip and no word, and she went wide around that too; at \place{the yard well} \place{the trough} stood bare and dark with its water called weather, and beyond it the field's empty place and the place the carriers crossed had a look and a wide step each and no number. No third gap came in the returning line and no fourth likeness stood up to make a cause of any two absences, and no finger of his drew the line between them. The yard went around what it had lost the way water goes around a stone that lies where a stone lies, and the boy kept the wide steps as directions and gave them no figure.

Under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof she set him on his bed and not in the Debt Loft, put the fold right without a look at his face and left the thread frozen, and then went to her own bed before the small hours, leaving the coals to keep themselves. The two rooms did not touch. There was no breathing number anywhere in the house --- nothing to hold the hand against but the rhythm that came through the finger's-width door, the low note of water over water drawing out and slackening, and the next low note coming when it came. The hand outside the wool went up an inch toward the note and stayed. He kept it to the river's own low interval: the draw, the slack, the small pause, and down on the next low note. It was the one-rhythm coordination remembered, not a change made again, not a longer reach, a hearing, a seventh, a stopping, a payment, one of the three held changes, a key, a power or a rule. He filed it on the sixth place beside the others and laid nothing across them and named no payer to any of them. Exactness and humiliation were one motion in that small hand and both were true, and neither was a sign.

Two hundred and twenty stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in \place{Sarn}, \place{Sill} or \place{Grath}; the carried fire gave four low pulls and withheld its fifth, its payer unnamed for two hundred and twenty days; the widow's mark hung where it hung with no opening in forty days; the shelf kept its two gaps and the fold its one crust with the empty place beside it still unnamed. The hand came down on the river's next low note with no count anywhere in the house to hold it against. The fire went on in the room that warmed him and the river in the room he could not enter, and neither stopped for the ear under the roof, and the count went forward with nothing settled and nothing ended.
