\daymark{221}{Grain 12, Y.H. 813 (Secondday)}

The small hours came to the child before they came to anybody else under the roof. He lay at the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf} with the fold at his ear, and the day arrived first as a smell and afterward as a gap in a sound: hot clay under tallow, wet wool under that, and beneath both the sour mineral breath of the unswept corner the house called \lore{the thaw}. Then \lore{the carried fire} drew in four low pulls under the floor and withheld the fifth, and the space where the fifth should have stood went on being a space with no payer named to it for two hundred and twenty-one days. He was two hundred and twenty-one days old in \place{Low Kiln}, six months and forty-one days, and nobody who could have known what either number counted said a word about it; there was no slate anywhere in \place{Sarn}, in \place{Sill} or in \place{Grath}, with a place to enter either. Through the finger's-width door the river kept its note of \lore{water over water}, a fifth low thing never counted among the hearings; overhead the boards kept four nails on the near board, four on the next, and a thumb-width knot where the fifth nail should have stood. Three counts in three places, and none rose up and became another, because a man who wanted them to agree would have to put a payer under all three and no payer stood to any of them. The fire went on in the room that warmed him and the river in the room he could not enter, and neither stopped for the ear under the roof.

Cold had come to cheek and temple and the small bare joint at the wrist where the wool rode back, and had stopped there as if it had reached the end of an order written out for it; it went no further and it did not go away. The afterdrop waited on the far side of a warming nobody in this house would perform; he knew the afterdrop when he saw it and he wanted the crust, and the child's wanting was the louder of the two, as it had been on every morning of his life. One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from his ear and kept the empty place beside it unnamed, and no third came to make the row come out even. The thread of drool at his mouth had frozen in the dry heat and was unwiped, because nobody under any roof had been given an instruction about the thread and a basket set down was a pot set down and left. He could not lift his chin two inches to the crust, close his hand two inches on the rim of the basket that held him, or speak a number that anybody would believe. The body humiliated him and moved an inch at the same time, and both were true and neither was a sign.

\scenebreak

Grain's first Account-day lay two mornings behind and the second, Grain 20, lay eight mornings ahead, and neither was brought early into a day that had no market in it. The district's arithmetic had changed on the tenth and could not be changed back, but under this roof no figure had been entered and no slate opened. The printed \emph{one in twelve} stood unchallenged where it had always stood; the figure read over a basket was still not the figure paid; \person{Berr Clay}'s box had gone up \place{the clay road} toward \place{High Kiln} with the true subtraction locked inside it, and \person{Hessa Rale}'s narrow slate lay unread at \place{Greybank} with the writing turned to her sleeve, and the soft-cuffed clerk stayed set at \place{the out-of-line landing} below \place{the north bridge} for a measure nobody had called. All of it lay offstage in the boy's file and none of it opened or reached back, and he laid nothing across the pieces and named no payer to any of them. A district may change its arithmetic and go on not knowing what it has changed; a house may keep its days in loaves, glances and unnamed spaces and never put a number to the difference. The changed figure lay outside the roof like weather, and weather is a thing a child in a basket can lie under without once being asked to read it.

The seventh green and the seventh shape were two days gathered now and had gathered nothing in the gathering. The green was a colour at the mouth of a silent woman at \place{the pot board} --- clay in the small crescents under the nails, the sweetish \lore{lead glaze} in the crease beside the lip --- and the shape was a shape at the market's edge, a green sliver on a string that caught the light and went dull, a head that had turned toward a set-down pot a half-beat late and stopped in the middle of the turn. He had filed the one as a colour and the other as a shape on places that do not touch, with a finger between each and the hearings of \lore{the full note}, and no name spoken over either. No eighth of either had come to stand beside them, no second look had been joined to the first, and neither had been promoted into a hearing, a seventh of the note, a tenth stopping, a cause, a name or a romance. Six greens behind a seventh green made a row of greens; six shapes behind a seventh shape made a row of shapes; a row is not an account until somebody finds a payer to stand under it, and no payer stood under either row. The woman had no name in the narration and the shape had none, and the sale to a clerk of \place{the Waiting Room} stayed years ahead of the basket and was not brought early into a morning that had no market in it.

\scenebreak

A wrong wind came up off High Kiln before first grey and went over the boards where the neighbours stood with their baskets, and with it came the sweet edge of lead glaze, a direction and not a second edge paid. It carried a voice. The voice let one of the three shapings of the pot saying stand --- that a pot gone quiet on every row come the thaw --- and went on to the price of barley and the weather, with no owner named, no room, no hand at the foot of it and no reason. Dust lay in the hollows of the pot board remembered at a distance, the board that had stood across the square two mornings back, and the saying came off that distance the way a smell comes off a cold hearth, belonging to no mouth that could be questioned. The boy took it apart as a surgeon takes apart a wound. A report of a silence is not a second hearing of the same pot; three likenesses standing on one tray are not three witnesses to one death; a word about a pot is not a word about a slate, and a fourth likeness does not stand up among three and call itself a rule, because a rule is an account that has found a payer and no payer stood to this. He left the saying where it stood, a saying about a pot with no pot lost, no lesson made and no name given to what ailed the man carried out on Day 188, and he laid nothing across it and let it stand advanced and unpaid. Nothing was joined, named, opened, called, spent or ended.

\scenebreak

A neighbour at the threshold asked what the month's firing would want now that Grain's middle tend had set in, and the question went to the pot and the weather and away level, with nothing priced, refused or entered. The boy watched the question go as he watched all such questions go, with the surgeon's attention to where a thing was put down: not into the basket, not into any slate, but onto the pot and the air, where it lay with the weight of a direction and no figure under it. It was the same answer he had given a hundred times in a house where no instruction was ever addressed to him, and it arrived nowhere he could reach.

Under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof \person{Sella Vail} came to the fire before she came to the basket, the back first and the face withheld, the hand going where it had gone ten thousand times before. “It's the thaw,” she told the fire, and turned the coals with the back of a broken pot, and the fire answered four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have been. Every instruction in the room went to the wool, the fire, the pot, the broom, the running water and the river wind, and never to the basket. “The house,” she said, where another woman would have said \emph{I}, and “The house will keep it,” and stood a moment with the back of the pot in her hand counting under her breath toward no opening --- small intake, hold, letting go --- the count arriving nowhere as it had arrived nowhere on every day of his life.

At the loaf shelf she stood with her back to the basket and moved her lips along the loaf row, and the row stood with two unnamed gaps, the old space at the row's end and the palm's breadth that had come in on a night years back with no scrape, crumb, print or word. She gave nobody a figure. Her eye went once to the clay jar cold under its glaze on the wall shelf, and her hand started toward it and shut half over nothing without a touch, the little-door burn closing on air the way it closed on everything that came within reach of it. Her finger lay on the top of the shelf's front edge where any woman in the district could see it lying, and the charcoal tally under the board stayed unread, uncounted and uncopied, because a woman's back turned was not the same thing as a finger beneath it and the reason stood between the two, unspoken. A hand that knows a board by heart and a hand that has been told not to look leave the same mark on a room. She pushed a strand of coal-black hair back with the wrist that carried the burn and the grey streak at her temple showed pale off the coals, and she did not discuss the streak or the winter it had come in.

She set the pot rim out with its handle two inches beyond the reaching hand and let her hand rest a half-beat longer at the setting than the setting required, and did not look toward \place{the bed}. At a morning and a shelf she did not touch \lore{the widow's mark}; it hung on its cord at her throat where it had hung since the first firing, touched once before the kiln on Ember 1 and at no hour since, and there had been no opening in forty-one days, because a shelf, a morning and a bed are not openings. The boy lay two inches short of the handle and watched the half-beat go by as a man watches a hand come out of a field before the dressing goes on. It was a thing done and a thing withheld in one motion, and he put it where it belonged and laid nothing across it and named no payer to it. Her private roll stayed unshown and \person[Dessa Silt]{Dessa}'s cost stayed where it had been put down and never entered.

Sella carried him out into \place{the Clay Yard} before the morning was old, and the yard went around what it had lost the way water goes around a stone that lies where a stone lies. The clean dark line where \lore{Ash} had turned kept its place and the new finer hairline of grey beside it, called the thaw, had not been swept; under \place{the lowest rack} the broken birth-slates lay where the yard entered them into nothing, and she went wide around the space; the round empty ring where the shallow clay pan had stood kept no scrape, no crumb, no print, no chip and no word, and she went wide around that too; at \place{the yard well} \place{the trough} stood bare and dark with its water called weather, and beyond it the field's empty place and the place the carriers crossed had a look and a wide step each and no number. No third gap came in the returning line and no fourth likeness stood up to make a cause of any two absences, and no finger of his drew the line between them. The carrying arm was a record in muscle that remembered weight and not cost, and it had carried him up and down this yard for two hundred and twenty-one days without once being asked what the weight was worth.

\scenebreak

At \place{the kindling} \person{Tarn} took the broom only after it had been instructed, the instruction going to the tool before it went to the man, and he took it up as a man takes up an instrument already named. “Yes, house,” he said, and told the wood “Six counted. The next I don't count.” He set the small knife at the very edge of its sill, six in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check and the seventh at the lip where it could not, and \person[Garl Vail]{Garl}'s unforged collector sentence lay beside it, the man sent for a collector who turned back at the frozen ford, neither entered. He gave no name to the man who had turned back, though he had been that man. He said \emph{Yes, house} to those who wanted the thaw early and to those who wanted it late, agreed so entirely that no argument could find a foot, and went on wanting the late because a late thaw puts off the paying, though the paying had stood in the ruts since the water went. His chest had been tight since the dark; Ash was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line and no scrubbing lifted it, and the pale clip-ring at his left wrist held what light there was and gave it back. His stroke went over \place{the flagstones} past the shallow bright patch without the half-check and over the road a house sends a man down when it wants a collector without once turning along it, and no collector came up or went down the clay road on Grain 12.

The sentence about an ear under this roof being worth more than the debt that held it came to his teeth and went all the way to the word that would call the loan before it stopped, with no figure and no name; he filed the stopping as a remembered stopping and not a new one, and counted nine behind it with no tenth standing before the seventh year at its very end, which had come and had still not given. The breath from Seed 16 drew at the top of each reach and was not spent, a small interval where the cost should have gone in, and the loan went on being uncalled. The warm stone Sella gave him in the winter Garl died lay in its unmarked clay pot in \place{the Debt Loft}, two years gone and cold long since, and he could no more reconcile it with the debt than he could the house's kindness with the account he kept of it; he had stopped trying, which is not forgiving. He did not ask about the room and nobody asked about the ear, and the two silences stayed unjoined under the one roof.

At the edge of \place{the Vail wheel-shed} \person{Jorra Vail} came through with the morning's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bent little finger and under the nails, white in the creases where a fist had opened, and not bone-coloured \lore{slip}. He kept dusty forearms and clean ones on one tray and parted them as a surgeon parts clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had been broken nor whether it was a birth-slate. Three or more men had gone low into the finger's-width-open \place{the back room} before first grey and he gave them no name and did not say the room was cold; the two tellings of the shut counting room stood at two with no third, and \lore[the burned-slate word]{the burned-slate word} stood where it had stood, four times told and arrived nowhere from \place{Slate Row}. He spoke to the wheel before he spoke to anyone. “It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady,” he said, and told a pot “You'll do” in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either.

He knew the full note that the pot would give three turns before it left his hands, and the knowing had air under it and a want in it, and he kept it back, on the near-full tray beside the hearings and never among them. The three shapings of the pot saying stood as sayings about a pot with no lesson made and no pot lost, and a fourth likeness did not stand up among them and call itself a rule. He gave no name to what ailed the man carried out on Day 188. \emph{He's not Vail} stayed sealed as tightly as on the hour it was spoken, and the promise of the boy's name to the men who burn slates was neither given nor withdrawn. When Sella's voice went low and unfinished at the hearth his bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper} ahead of her hand, and held until the sentence reached the pot, then moved a hair's breadth and was left standing --- a remembered repetition, not a change made again; the boy read it as one iron with two bites, the bite that holds a vessel whole and the bite that can take the hand off a man, with no word in \lore{Grathic} for both mercies, an object worth nothing joined to no kiln.

\scenebreak

Asked in the morning, the hand went up an inch toward the note and came down at once against the fold. It came down under conditions that had sometimes answered, and it was a miss, and there was no failure hidden under the miss. He filed it on the sixth place beside the others and laid nothing across them and named no payer to any of them. Nothing stood up out of it to call itself a longer reach, a hearing, a seventh, a stopping, a payment, one of the three held changes, a key, a power or a rule. Two hundred and twenty-one stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in Sarn, Sill or Grath; the carried fire gave four low pulls and withheld its fifth, its payer unnamed for two hundred and twenty-one days; the widow's mark hung where it hung with no opening in forty-one days; the shelf kept its two gaps and the fold its one crust with the empty place beside it still unnamed. The fire went on in the room that warmed him and the river in the room he could not enter, and neither stopped for the ear under the roof. The hand came down at once against the fold under conditions that had sometimes answered, a miss, the crust still two inches off, the thread frozen and unwiped, and the seventh green and the seventh shape only a colour and a shape now two days gathered, and the count went forward with nothing settled and nothing ended.
