# Chapter 46 — beat sheet: "The Figure over the Basket"

**Days 189–191, Ember 10–12, Y.H. 813, Tenthday to Secondday.** A **normal three-day chapter**, three dated `\daymark` headings, two scenes per day, every day lived in full, none skipped, poured into another or summarised. Chapter 45 closed on Day 188, Ember 9, Ninthday, in the small hours; that day is done and is not revisited. The binding calendar rule is *t* = 336 + *N* − 1: Day 189 = Ember 10, Tenthday (the first **Account-day and market-day** of Ember); Day 190 = Ember 11, Firstday; Day 191 = Ember 12, Secondday. All three weekdays are checked against `bible/calendar.md`. The district keeps calling Tallow the firing month and Ember the kiln month and neither is corrected in narration.

**The chapter's one meaningful change.** The first Account-day and market-day of Ember is read over the basket: Berr Clay reads *one in twelve, as the notice says* for the first time in the kiln month, calls the levy for Ember and denies it with *The levy is called. It is not called by me* and *your house*, never *I*, and goes off up the clay road with the box locked and the true subtraction inside, believing he has done a kindness. The district's arithmetic changes for the month and cannot be undone; the printed figure stands unchallenged and a figure read aloud is still not the figure paid. This is the hub of the chapter, advanced and **not paid**: it is a real change to the district's account, not the closing of any long thread. Crucially the reading **does not answer the man carried out on Day 188** — no figure, no collector and no slate knows what he cost, and the pale line at his gum stays on a place of its own.

**What is gathered, not resolved.** Hessa Rale's road and inward slate (T080, advanced and not paid); the soft-cuffed clerk set at the out-of-line landing for an uncalled measure (T090); the sixth green at a mouth, still a colour and never Fenna in narration (T050/T051 held); the foundling as a held shape, **not counted as a seventh sighting** and given no loaf, slate, sale, half-beat or dialogue (T033/T034 held); the charcoal under the loaf shelf still unread (T100); the stone cold and unreconciled in the loft (T006); nine stoppings still nine with no tenth before the seventh year is earned (T036/T076); the widow's mark touched once before the kiln on Ember 1 and at no other hour.

**Hard guards for the author.** No recap of Chapter 45's close; the carried man went up the road breathing and no offstage fate is invented. No new hearing, no seventh, no tenth stopping, no third gap, no fourth likeness made a cause, no finger of his drawing the line, no calling of the loan, no payer named, no private record opened or read. No present-tense Chicago break, no `\memory{}` tag, no departure from the boy's eyes, no Court or back-room interior. No silt-leach, cause or cure named for any cold. Dessa dead and unnamed to the boy; Marra out of Sill; *he's not Vail* sealed; the foundling's name promise ungiven; the old life offstage. ## Pull into Chapter 47

The final image — the hand up an inch toward the river's note and down against the fold with the count at one hundred and ninety-one — carries directly into the first morning of Day 192, Ember 13. The account of Ember's first reading has gone up the clay road in Berr's locked box and cannot be undone; the printed figure still says one in twelve and the true subtraction is still in the box. Chapter 47 opens on the quiet days after the market with the household going around the two unswept lines, the rack space, the pan's gone ring, the field's empty place and the place the carriers crossed, with a look and a wide step and no number, while the boy's file grows heavier by three days without any likeness standing up and calling itself a cause: six hearings with no seventh, three near-full answers unpaid, nine stoppings with no tenth, three held changes on three places, the loft scrape on a fourth, the pale line on a fifth, the charcoal still unread and the mark untouched. The loan stays uncalled, the seventh year presses nearer its end and has not given, the fire's fifth has had no payer for a hundred and ninety-two days, and the foundling's loaf goes on gathering interest in an account the child cannot read. Forward, not closed.
