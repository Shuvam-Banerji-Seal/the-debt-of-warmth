## Chronicle

On Ember's first Account-day and market-day, Sella Vail carried the boy to the Cross at the half-pace from every open slate and set him in the moot-post's shade where no slate lay. Two hundred leaned to the reading block; eight pigs went over the wet boards; the bell went four, four and a gap, and nobody crossed himself. Berr Clay came up short off the road, office cord folded under his coat, glaze green at one mouth-corner, wiped his thumb twice before touching nothing, passed a finger over a figure and read *one in twelve, as the notice says* low into the crowd. *The levy is called. It is not called by me* --- *your house*, never *I* --- and he went off toward High Kiln with the box locked and the true subtraction inside, believing he had done a kindness. The month's arithmetic changed and could not be changed back; a figure read aloud is not the figure paid.

Hessa Rale took the slate up the road to Greybank before noon, edge-on to her forearm: *I don't read it. I carry it. There's a difference, and the road doesn't care which.* A soft-cuffed clerk stayed set below the bridge for a measure nobody called, the market selling over him as water over a stone. A woman at the pot board turned a cup a quarter and set it back, green at her mouth; at the market's edge a small shape in cast-offs caught the light on a green sliver and went dull. The boy gave the colour no ear, the shape no name.

The reading did not answer the man carried out the day before: no figure, collector or slate knew what he cost, and the pale line at his gum lay on a place of its own. On the two mornings after, the household went around the pan's gone ring, the bare trough, the broken birth-slates, the field's empty place and the carriers' place, with a look and a wide step and no number. Cost-questions went to the pot and weather; the sweet edge on a wrong wind was answered as a direction, and the pot that sang early was swept around, not made an omen. Jorra withheld the note he knew from a man on the wrong wind; Tarn held the seventh at the lip and the ear-sentence short of the word that would call the loan. The charcoal waited unread and the mark hung untouched.

In the small hours he laid out the file as a man lays instruments on a towel: six hearings with no seventh, three near-full answers unpaid, nine stoppings with no tenth, three held changes on three places, the loft scrape on a fourth, the pale line on a fifth. He wanted the crust, the breast, the pot handle and a number nobody would speak, and got none. One hundred and ninety-one stood exact, complete and unspent in the wool, and the cost of the man carried out was entered nowhere.
