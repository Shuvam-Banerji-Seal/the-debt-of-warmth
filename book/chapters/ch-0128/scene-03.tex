\daymark{436}{Silt 12, Y.H. 814, Secondday}

The small hours of the twelfth came down under the Vail roof without a sound of their coming, the way a second film of silt comes over a floor already filmed with the first: quietly, and heaviest where nothing had stirred it. The hand outside the wool lay flat on the fold where the going of the light had left it, on answer four hundred and forty-six, flat and patient as a tool put away where a next hand would find it without looking. There was no next hand in the room. Four hundred and thirty-five exact, and six months and two hundred and fifty-five days told by nobody who would have known what a number counts. Under \place{the boards} \lore{the carried fire} kept four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer without a name for four hundred and thirty-five days; \person[Garl Vail]{Garl}'s name had gone unspoken the same four hundred and thirty-five, the man and the fire standing side by side without becoming one. Four readings of the year stood behind one unsigned figure up \place{the clay road}, four being four and not one account, the first not made a thirteenth by the three that followed, the second supplying no signature, the third no doctrine, the fourth no payer, the figure read still not the figure paid; all four rode locked in \person{Berr Clay}'s box with the key gone up in a coat pocket that never came back down. None of it had crossed under the roof in the night as sign or lesson, mending or cause, payment or payer. Through \place{the finger's-width door} \place{the river} kept the low note of \lore{water over water} under \lore{the winter skin}, ending on its own finishing, and nobody in the room gave the softness at one edge a name. A twelfth morning was only a twelfth morning.

\person[Corin]{The boy} lay on \place{the bed} at the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf} in \place{the Vail kiln-house}, \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill}, \place{Grath}, under the roof and never in \place{the Debt Loft}, held where the heat came to him sideways and not where he would have chosen. The room kept its heat the way a cup keeps water, with care, and with one crack nobody had mentioned. He took the body in the old order, because the order was the only instrument he had. One stiff brown-edged barley crust stood two inches from his ear, gone past leather into old horn in the dry nights, with its corner a hair off the wool; a child under this roof would have tried to eat it six nights ago and would not try now. He could not lift it. He could not have lifted it when it was soft. Beside it the empty place held \lore{the keeping at the fold}, found where put on the mornings and goings of the light of the days behind, still a finding and not a placing, with no second fold, no third crust, no name, no gift, no payment and no payer. It had been put there on the fifteenth by a hand that had not come back to place it again, and a hand that finds is not a hand that places. The thread of drool at his mouth had gone stiff in the dry kiln heat and was unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread. Cold had come to cheek and temple and the small bare joint at the wrist where the wool rode back, and had stopped there as at the end of a line written out for it; a face composed by cold is not composure, and a cold patient cannot clot. \lore{The Afterdrop} waited past a warming nobody in this house would perform. He knew a basket set level out of the draught, with the heat coming to it sideways, was the careful rewarming he would choose, and could not ask for it; the knowing was a finding like any other, with no hand under it to make it a claim on anybody. He had learned the shape of that warming in another life: from the outside, slowly, never rubbed, with the cold core watched past the pink returning at the surface, because the surface lies. There was nobody here to watch a core and nobody to perform the warming, and the knowing bought nothing under this roof.

The three wants came up in the old quarrel and neither won it. He wanted the crust lifted two inches from the wool, he wanted the thread wiped from his mouth, he wanted a hand on the pot handle that stood beyond him; the surgeon's register said the crust was old horn and the thread a thing nobody had been told to wipe and the hand a thing that would not come, and the infant said all three again without hearing the register. Both were true. The second was louder. Neither bought the other, and the file went on with the case open. He read the house by what it would not say and not by what it would not survive. There had been rooms in the other life where every instrument was named before it touched a body and named again when it came away, so that the naming stood between the thing and the man; he had worked in a warm room once where the room insisted a man was warm because the room was warm, while the fingers below the wrist went the colour of old rope. That room was past, and the reading of it was past tense and untagged, and paid nothing here. He had held pressure in cavities where the field was kept clean of everything but the hands, and had learned to ask which of two numbers was the finding and which the telling; here there was no chart and no nurse and no second number, only a house that keeps its days in loaves and glances and unnamed spaces and will not say what it will not survive. He did not stand the number up as a threshold or a doctrine; a number counted is a number with air around it, and air buys no warmth.

First grey came the way it had come on the eleven mornings behind, finding the edges of things before it found the middle. \person{Sella Vail} carried him down \place{the nineteen steps} at the half-pace from every open slate, the instruction going to the wool and the river wind and the pot and the fire and the banked fire and never to the basket. The basket was not told what the day was or the hour, and was not told to be still. She set him where the heat came sideways and went to \place{the hearth} before the basket, as though the fire had been waiting for her and the boy had not. The coals were told it was \lore{the thaw} and not told it was a child. She turned them with the back of a broken pot, a dry scrape of iron over stone, and gave the small intake, the hold and the letting-go toward no opening, figuring neither of the two gaps. Her finger lay along the top of the loaf shelf so the charcoal on its underside stayed unread; no hand went beneath it and no mark was copied. Her lips moved on a two-gapped row. One glance went to the clay jar standing cold under \lore{lead glaze} beyond \place{the pot board}, the hand shut half over nothing, the little-door burn on the back of her left hand closing on air.

The twice-mended pot came up thumb-first where the tighter wire crossed the crack, the hairline of the original break showing between the older brass staple and the wire. \emph{Leave the crack showing. People trust a thing they can see has been handled}, she said to the pot; \emph{If it's worth mending it's worth mending twice. The second time is the real one}, to the fire, and never to a person. Repair was not restoration, and she kept them apart the way a surgeon keeps a graft apart from a cure. At \place{the rack end} the dressed grain was lifted for damp and turned piece by piece, the touch lighter each time a hand went in and came out, laid back where it lay and covered with the flat of a palm; no figure went over it and no handful was counted, though a fist of barley weighed in the hand knows what it weighs. She read the damp with the palm and not with a figure, and where the wool had held overnight warmth the grain came up cool and went back cool, and the touch lightened as the hand learned what the grain would bear. The pot rim stood two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat and no look toward \place{the bed}. The half-beat was the distance between the intention and the arrest; she did not measure it and did not name it, for a named gap wants a payer put under it and none stood.

\lore{The widow's mark} hung where it hung and she did not touch it. She touched it before she opened \place{the kiln mouth} and at no other hour, and the kiln mouth was not open. A Secondday morning, a hearth, a shelf, a pot and basket are not openings; seven openings in seven months are seven and not a rule, and a twelfth tending day was not made an eighth by the reading that had gone up the road on the tenth. Her hands were never still; when they had nothing to do they counted spoons, nails and the breaths of a child nobody numbers and came back to the hairline at the wire. The one grey streak at her temple said winter and she said nothing about the winter. The clean dark line at \place{the Vail corner} kept its finer grey hairline called the thaw, thickened in the dry cold with the faint sour tang of \lore{Ash} coming up where no broom had gone in two years, unswept and called weather. Nobody looked twice. She put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face and added no second fold, and the keeping was found where put and stayed a finding.

\person{Tarn} was at \place{the kindling} and \place{the flagstones} with the morning already deep in him, six pieces standing in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check and the seventh at the lip beside \person[Garl Vail]{Garl}'s unforged collector sentence, with no name on the man who turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on \lore{the tile debt}. The stack kept its own arithmetic of four, four and a space, and the space was not a fifth and was not filled. The small knife lay at the very edge of its sill. His hand went over blade and sill as a man passes a hand over a tool put back where it belongs, and came away with no breath held at the top of the reach; the coming away was the whole of the checking. \emph{Six counted. The next I don't count}, he told the wood in the tone of a man stating the weather, and the sentence was found where put and not made a habit, a tenth stopping, a payment, a hearing or a rule, for a second would be a custom and a third a way and both want a payer where none stood. The sentence about an ear under this roof being worth more than the debt that held it came all the way to the word that would call the loan and stopped there, a remembered ninth stopping with no tenth, the Seed 16 breath unspent, the loan uncalled in a term already ended. His valuable ear was exposure and not safety. The fear of being entered again for vagrancy was still his without a figure, and so was the want he did not voice for a slate with his own name and no mark against it. Ash had worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line, and the pale clip-ring at his left wrist caught what little light there was and gave it back. He took the broom only after it had been given its instruction, said \emph{Yes, house} to the fire and went on over the bright flagstones without turning his head toward the landing. The boy filed the sentence on a place of its own beside the unforged collector sentence and \person[Jorra Vail]{Jorra}'s silence, two quiet men under one roof and not a compact, and laid none of them across the reading gone up the road, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood.

Jorra came through the finger's-width door to \place{the Vail wheel-shed} edge with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small past his bent little finger and white in a fist-crease that was not opened twice, and it was not the bone-coloured slip of the wheel. He kept his dusty forearms and his clean ones on one tray and parted them as clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had been broken nor whether it was a birth-slate. Men unnamed had gone low into \place{the back room} before first grey and he gave the house no name for them and no word that the room was cold; the broken ones under the lowest rack stayed entered by nobody. He spoke to the wheel before any person, \emph{It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady}, and told a pot \emph{You'll do} in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either. He knew the \lore{full note} that the pot would give three turns before it left his hands, with air under it and a want in it, and withheld the knowing from any master come up from \place{High Kiln} who would call it early and sing a pot before its time. Before Sella had finished telling him the fire was low, his bent finger went into the cold iron of \place{the damper} ahead of her unfinished sentence. One bite of the iron held and one took; there was no Grathic word covering both mercies. He took the cold out of the fingertip, moved his tongue a hair's breadth and came out without leaving the extra holding of Ash 26 a second time, gathered once and not made again, for a second would be a habit and a third a way and both want a payer where none stood. \emph{He's not Vail} stayed sealed as a lid nobody had tried to lift in three years; the promise to the men who burn slates was neither given nor withdrawn against \lore{the burned slate} standing at five, gathering toward a calling that was not made on a morning that had earned only its twelfth. The household straightened around the waiting without a word from anybody.

The morning went on at the work of a morning, and the boy added none of it together. Beside him on places of their own with a finger between every pair stood the offered figure and its return, \person{Hessa Rale}'s reported second slate, the hair-thin thaw, the neighbour's near-reaching at the shelf, the clerk's entered figure brought up the yard as a report and not a sighting, the fifth shaping of \lore{the well-saying}, the three likenesses of \place{Slate Row}, and the short strip of notched birch that \person{Ilva} kept in her sleeve offstage, pale where a thumb had rubbed the notches and dark in the uncut grain between. None was a reader, a witness, a tenth hand, a custom, a payment or a payer; none was joined to another, and the asking that would one day come at \place{the yard well} was not anticipated by a word or a look from him on a day that had earned only its twelfth. The hand that will one day lay charcoal and cord and knot-string and inward slate and strip of birch together and call them an account is far off and is not his, and he did not bring it under the roof to answer any of the places waiting there. Six hearings of the full note stood beside and never among, with the seventh place empty and \place{the Drawing house}'s flat knock outside on its own place. The heard bell stood beside the told bell with both gaps in their own squares, and three counts in three places stayed three, for agreeing any two would want a payer put under both and none stood. Nine record-first hands stood with nine stoppings and no tenth. Nothing from the eleventh had crossed under the roof as sign, lesson, mending, cause, payment or payer, and an eleventh day had earned only its eleventh.

He kept four hundred and thirty-six exact, six months and two hundred and fifty-six days, told by nobody who would have known what a number counts. The carried fire's fifth went unpaid and Garl's name went unspoken the same four hundred and thirty-six, the man and the fire standing side by side without becoming one. Answer four hundred and forty-seven was filed on the sixth place, exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment and not a doctrine. The hand outside the wool took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole, let it go and came straight down and laid the answer beside the others with a finger between every pair. The seventh place stayed empty. There was no next hand in the room. He did not wipe the thread and did not lift the crust. Forward, into the afternoon of the twelfth not lived and not brought into this one.
