\daymark{437}{Silt 13, Y.H. 814, Thirdday}

The small hours of the thirteenth came down under the Vail roof the way a fine ash settles on a pot left cooling --- not in a fall but in a laying, thickest where the air had been stillest and thin along the edges where somebody had stirred. The hand outside the wool lay flat on the fold where the going of the light had left it, on answer four hundred and forty-eight, flat and patient as a tool put away where a next hand would find it without looking. There was no next hand in the room. Four hundred and thirty-six exact, and six months and two hundred and fifty-six days told by nobody who would have known what a number counts. The count went on under the dark the way a banked fire goes on under ash, with nobody turning it and nobody supplying what it lacked. Under \place{the boards} \lore{the carried fire} kept four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer without a name for four hundred and thirty-six days; \person[Garl Vail]{Garl}'s name had gone unspoken the same four hundred and thirty-six, the man and the fire standing side by side without becoming one. Four readings of the year stood behind one unsigned figure up \place{the clay road}, four being four and not one account, the first not made a thirteenth by the three that followed, the second supplying no signature, the third no doctrine, the fourth no payer, the figure read still not the figure paid, all four locked in \person{Berr Clay}'s box with the key gone up in a coat pocket that never came back down. The asking at the well of the day before had not crossed under the roof in the night as sign or lesson, mending or cause, payment or payer; it lay where it had been put on its own place, and would not be told again on a morning that had earned only its thirteenth. Through \place{the finger's-width door} \place{the river} kept the low note of \lore{water over water} under \lore{the winter skin}, ending on its own finishing, and nobody in the room gave the softness at one edge a name. A thirteenth morning was only a thirteenth morning.

\person[Corin]{The boy} lay on \place{the bed} at the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf} in \place{the Vail kiln-house}, \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill}, \place{Grath}, under the roof and never in \place{the Debt Loft}, held where the heat came to him sideways and not where he would have chosen. The room kept its heat the way a cup keeps water, with care, and with one crack nobody had mentioned. He took the body in the old order, because the order was the only instrument he had, and the surgeon's register read the order of its small failures and put no figure on any of them. One stiff brown-edged barley crust stood two inches from his ear, gone past leather into old horn in the dry nights, with its corner a hair off the wool; a child under this roof would have tried to eat it six nights ago and would not try now, and a thing past eating is a thing past wanting even if the hand that wants it is a child's. He could not lift it. He could not have lifted it when it was soft. Beside it the empty place held \lore{the keeping at the fold}, found where put on the mornings and goings of the light of the days behind, still a finding and not a placing, with no second fold, no third crust, no name, no gift, no payment and no payer. It had been put there on the fifteenth by a hand that had not come back to place it again, and a hand that finds is not a hand that places. The thread of drool at his mouth had gone stiff in the dry kiln heat and was unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread. Cold had come to cheek and temple and the small bare joint at the wrist where the wool rode back, and had stopped there as at the end of a line written out for it; a face composed by cold is not composure, and a cold patient cannot clot. \lore{The Afterdrop} waited past a warming nobody in this house would perform. He knew a basket set level out of the draught, with the heat coming to it sideways, was the careful rewarming he would choose, and could not ask for it; the knowing was a finding like any other, with no hand under it to make it a claim on anybody. He had learned the shape of that warming in another life, from the outside and slowly, with the surface lying pink over a cold core that would not be hurried; that knowledge was past tense, untagged, and paid nothing under this roof.

Three things found as they had been left, and none the better for the finding. The infant under the same skin wanted the crust lifted two inches to his mouth, wanted the thread wiped from his lip, wanted a hand on the handle of the pot that would bring a cup within reach; the surgeon knew all three wants were useless and filed them without verdict, and the infant wanted them all the same. Both were true. The second was louder. Neither bought the other, and the quarrel went on without a settlement, because a settlement wants a hand to enter it and the only hand in the case was the small one outside the wool that could not close two inches of air. He had once stood at the head of a table and laid a tray of instruments in the order they would be wanted, and had known the difference between a finding and a want without having to think which was which; that was another room and another pair of hands, and it paid nothing here. The wanting was not a weakness in the register and the register was not a denial of the wanting; they were two entries on the same page, and neither had been signed.

\scenebreak

First grey came into the room the way silt comes into a pail of water left standing --- along the rim first and down one side, making the water-line before it made the water. \person{Sella Vail} came to him and carried him down \place{the nineteen steps} at the half-pace from every open slate, the instruction going to the wool, the river wind, the pot, the fire and the banked fire and never to the basket. She did not tell the basket what the day was and did not tell it to be still. A basket carried down is a pot carried down and set where the heat comes to it, and no woman instructs the pot about the hour. She set him under the roof and not in the loft, at the low angle of the shelf where the sideways heat found him, and went to \place{the hearth} before the basket, as though the fire had been waiting for her and the boy had not.

She told the coals it was \lore{the thaw}, in the voice another woman would have used for a child who could not hear her, and did not tell it to the basket. She turned them with the back of a broken pot, a dry scrape of iron over stone that had done this work before and expected to do it again. She gave the small intake, the hold and the letting-go toward no opening, figuring neither of the two gaps, and moved her lips on a two-gapped row that was neither a prayer nor a count and wanted no answer. Her finger lay along the top of the loaf shelf so the charcoal on its underside stayed unread; no hand went beneath, no mark was copied, no row was numbered. One glance went to the clay jar standing cold under \lore{lead glaze} beyond \place{the pot board}; the hand shut half over nothing, and the little-door burn on the back of her left hand --- two straight sides with a lintel and a threshold --- closed on air and went back to the pot.

She lifted the twice-mended pot thumb-first where the tighter wire crossed the crack, the hairline of the original break showing between the older brass staple and the wire, a dark thread with a hand's work on either side. \emph{Leave the crack showing. People trust a thing they can see has been handled}, she said to the pot; \emph{If it's worth mending it's worth mending twice. The second time is the real one}, to the fire, and never to a person. Repair stayed apart from restoration, as a surgeon keeps a graft apart from a cure. At \place{the rack end} she lifted the dressed grain for damp and turned it piece by piece with a touch that lightened as the hand went in and came out, laid it back where it lay and drew the wool over with the flat of her palm; no figure went over it and no handful was counted, though a fist of barley weighed in the hand knows what it weighs. The pot rim stood two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat and no look toward \place{the bed}. The half-beat was the distance between the intention and the arrest; she did not measure it and did not name it, for a named gap wants a payer put under it and none stood.

\lore{The widow's mark} hung where it hung and she did not touch it. She touched it before she opened \place{the kiln mouth} and at no other hour, and the kiln mouth was not open. A morning, a shelf, a pot, a hearth and a going of the light are not openings; seven openings in seven months are seven and not a rule, and a Thirdday tending morning was not made an eighth by the reading that had gone up the road on the tenth or by the asking of the day before. Her hands were never still; when they had nothing to do they counted spoons, nails and the breaths of a child nobody numbers and came back to the hairline at the wire. The one grey streak at her temple said winter and she said nothing about the winter. Under \place{the Vail corner} the clean dark line kept its finer grey beside it, thickened in the dry cold with the faint sour tang of \lore{Ash} coming up where no broom had gone in two years, unswept and called weather, and the boy lay with his eyes at the level of it and looked once and not twice. She put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face and added no second fold, and the keeping was found where put and stayed a finding.

\scenebreak

\person{Tarn} was at \place{the kindling} and \place{the flagstones} with the morning's quiet already worked into him, six pieces standing in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check and the seventh at the lip beside \person[Garl Vail]{Garl}'s unforged collector sentence, with no name on the man who turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on \lore{the tile debt} circling against a firing of tiles that would never come. The stack kept its own arithmetic of four, four and a space, and the space was not a fifth and was not filled. The small knife lay at the very edge of its sill, and his hand went over blade and sill as a man passes a hand over a tool put back where it belongs and came away with no breath held at the top of the reach; the coming away was the whole of the checking. \emph{Six counted. The next I don't count}, he told the wood in the tone of a man stating the weather, cutting and counting and spending nothing. The sentence was found where put and not made a habit, a tenth stopping, a payment, a hearing or a rule; a second would be a custom and a third a way, and both want a payer where none stands.

The other sentence, about an ear under this roof being worth more than the debt that held it, came all the way to the word that would call the loan and stopped there, a remembered stopping that was not made a tenth. The Seed 16 breath stayed unspent, the loan uncalled in a term already ended, his valuable ear exposure and not safety. The fear of being entered again for vagrancy was still his without a figure, and so was the want he did not voice for a slate with his own name and no mark against it. His chest had been tight since the dark. Ash had worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line, and the pale clip-ring at his left wrist caught what little light there was and gave it back. He took the broom only after it had been given its instruction, said \emph{Yes, house} to the fire and went on over the flagstones without turning his head toward the landing. A man who owns nothing knows where every small thing is; a man who has counted six years knows what a seventh is worth when nobody comes to call it. The boy took the stopping apart by the place of each part --- what was there, what was not, and no payer standing under either --- and filed the silence on a place of its own. It was an object and not a doctrine; a doctrine is an account that has found a payer, and none stood.

At \place{the Vail wheel-shed} edge \person{Jorra Vail} came through the finger's-width door with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past his bent little finger and white in a fist-crease where a hand had opened, and it was not the bone-coloured \lore{slip} of the wheel. He kept dusty forearms and clean on one tray and parted them as clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had been broken nor whether it had been a birth-slate. Three or more men unnamed had gone low into \place{the back room} before first grey and got no name from him and no word that the room was cold, while the broken ones under the lowest rack stayed entered by nobody. He spoke to the wheel before any person --- \emph{It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady} --- and told a pot \emph{You'll do} in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness. He knew the \lore{full note} that pot would give three turns ahead, with air under it and a want in it, and withheld the knowing from any master come up from \place{High Kiln} who would call it early and sing a pot before its time.

Before Sella had finished telling him the fire was low, his bent finger went into the cold iron of \place{the damper} ahead of her unfinished sentence. One bite of the iron held and one took; there was no Grathic word covering both mercies. He took the cold out of the fingertip, moved his tongue a hair's breadth and came out without leaving the extra holding of Ash 26 a second time, gathered once and not made again, for a second would be a habit and a third a way and both want a payer where none stood. The household straightened around the waiting without a word from anybody. The boy lay under the shelf and kept \emph{he's not Vail} sealed as a lid nobody had tried to lift in three years; the promise to the men who burn slates was neither given nor withdrawn against \lore{the burned slate} standing at five, gathering toward a calling that was not made on a morning that had earned only its thirteenth. The two quiet men under one roof kept their places on trays of their own, their silences maintenance and not a compact, and the boy made no joining of what he saw.

\scenebreak

The morning went on at the work of a morning, and the boy added none of it together, for a surgeon who adds findings before the last is in has already made up the answer he wanted before he cut. Beside him on places of their own with a finger between every pair stood the offered figure and its return, \person{Hessa Rale}'s reported second slate, the hair-thin thaw, the neighbour's near-reaching at the shelf, the clerk's entered figure brought up the yard as a report and not a sighting, the fifth shaping of \lore{the well-saying}, the three likenesses of \place{Slate Row}, and the short strip of notched birch that \person{Ilva} kept in her sleeve offstage, pale where a thumb had rubbed the notches and dark in the uncut grain between. The asking of the day before stood on its own beside them, unjoined to any of them and not told again on an unearned morning; none of them was a reader, a witness, a tenth hand, a custom, a payment or a payer, and the hand that will one day lay charcoal and cord and knot-string and inward slate and strip of birch together and call them an account is far off and is not his. He did not bring it under the roof to answer any of the places waiting there, and he made no cause of what the afternoon might carry and did not look for one. Six hearings of the full note stood beside and never among, with the seventh place empty and \place{the Drawing house}'s flat knock outside on its own place. The heard bell stood beside the told bell with both gaps in their own squares, and three counts in three places stayed three, for agreeing any two would want a payer put under both and none stood. Nine record-first hands stood with nine stoppings and no tenth. Nothing from the tenth's reading or the twelfth's asking had crossed under the roof as sign, lesson, mending, cause, payment or payer, and a thirteenth day had earned only its thirteenth.

He kept four hundred and thirty-seven exact, six months and two hundred and fifty-seven days, told by nobody who would have known what a number counts. The carried fire's fifth went unpaid and Garl's name went unspoken the same four hundred and thirty-seven, the man and the fire standing side by side without becoming one, for a number counted is a number with air around it and air buys no warmth. Answer four hundred and forty-nine was filed on the sixth place, exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment and not a doctrine. The hand outside the wool took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole, let it go and came straight down and laid itself flat against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. There was no next hand in the room. He did not wipe the thread and did not lift the crust. Under the boards the four low pulls kept their silence where the fifth should have stood, and nobody lifted the floor and nobody supplied the pull. Forward, into the afternoon of the thirteenth not lived and not brought into this one.
