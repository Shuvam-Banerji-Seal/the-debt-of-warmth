## Scene 1 — The Eleventh Morning of Silt

- **Day and time:** Day 435, Silt 11, Y.H. 814, Firstday; small hours opening into first grey and the morning's work.
- **Place:** Vail kiln-house under the loaf shelf's low angle; the hearth, pot board, rack end; the nineteen steps down; the kindling and flagstones; the wheel-shed edge and cold damper.
- **Present:** Corin/Dan (carried), Sella Vail, Tarn, Jorra Vail; Garl Vail in objects only. Ilva, Hessa, Berr and the soft-cuffed clerk offstage with their keepings found where put. No market, Cross, bell, Account-day, collector or runner.

**Beats in order**
1. The chapter's only `\dropcap`, then the first `\daymark{435}{Silt 11, Y.H. 814, Firstday}`; the small hours open on the hand flat on the fold on **answer 444**, count **434 exact**, six months and 254 days told by nobody, the fire's fifth unpaid and Garl's name unspoken the same number, four readings behind one unsigned figure up the clay road in Berr's locked box with the key gone up in a coat pocket, and **no next hand in the room**. Do not recap the fourth reading or make it a lesson on the morning after.
2. The surgeon takes the body in the old order: crust gone past leather into old horn two inches from the ear with its corner a hair off the wool; the keeping at the fold found where put in the empty place, still a finding and not a placing, no third crust, no name, no payer; the thread stiff in the dry kiln heat and unwiped, because nobody under any roof has ever been given an instruction about the thread; cold stopped at cheek, temple and the bare wrist-joint where the wool rides back as at the end of a written line; a face composed by cold is not composure and a cold patient cannot clot; the Afterdrop waiting past a warming nobody here will perform, with the level basket and sideways heat being the rewarming he would choose and cannot ask for.
3. The infant's three wants — crust lifted, thread wiped, a hand on the pot handle — quarrel with the surgeon's register; both true, the second louder, neither buying the other, the quarrel the work and no verdict.
4. First grey: Sella carries him down the nineteen steps at the half-pace from every open slate, instruction to wool, river wind, pot and fire and never to the basket, basket not told the day or the hour or told to be still; goes to the hearth before the basket; tells the coals it is the thaw and turns them with the back of a broken pot; gives the small intake, hold and letting-go toward no opening with neither gap figured; finger along the shelf's top so the charcoal stays unread; lips on a two-gapped row; one glance at the cold clay jar with the hand shut half over nothing and the little-door burn closing on air.
5. Twice-mended pot lifted thumb-first where the wire crosses the crack, hairline showing between older brass staple and tighter wire, both proverbs to pot and fire and never to a person; dressed grain turned under wool unfigured; pot rim two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat and no look toward the bed; widow's mark untouched — a Firstday morning is not an opening and seven openings in seven months are seven and not a rule.
6. Tarn at the kindling and flagstones: six where the house can check, seventh at the lip beside Garl's unforged collector sentence with no name on the man who turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on the tile debt; small knife at the edge of its sill, hand over blade and sill and away with no breath held at the top of the reach, the coming away the whole of the checking; *Six counted. The next I don't count* told to the wood in the tone of a man stating the weather, found where put and not made a habit, a tenth stopping, a payment or a rule; ear-sentence stopped at the word that would call the loan, term ended, loan uncalled; Ash in the knuckle creases, pale clip-ring at the left wrist.
7. Jorra at the wheel-shed edge: meal-fine grey slate dust ground small past the bent little finger and white in a fist-crease; dusty forearms parted from clean as clot from vessel; wheel and pot spoken to before any person; full note known three turns ahead and withheld from any High Kiln master who would call it early; bent finger into the cold damper ahead of the unfinished sentence, one bite holding and one taking, no Grathic word covering both, coming out without a second holding of Ash 26; *he's not Vail* sealed, promise neither given nor withdrawn against the burned slate at five.
8. The boy keeps **435 exact**, six months and 255 days, and files **answer 445** on the sixth place, exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment.

- **Emotional turn:** the house takes up its grammar as though nothing had turned; the surgeon's register and the infant's want argue without a verdict, and the wanting goes on. No lesson is made of Silt 10.
- **Sensory anchors:** tallow, woodsmoke, wet wool; dry scrape of a pot's back over coals; barley wall to wall without a number; the pale clip-ring catching thin light; fine grey dust white in a fist-crease; the cold bite of the damper iron.
- **Threads advanced, none paid:** T071/T072 (435 exact; answer 445); T060/T086/T097 (body inventory, three wants quarrelling, keeping found where put); T058/T079/T100 (whole hearth grammar, charcoal unread, mark untouched, pot rim two inches beyond); T036/T076/T006 (six and seventh, ear-sentence stopped, loan uncalled); T035/T031/T062 (dust, damper, full note withheld, promise ungiven); T088 (fifth unpaid, name unspoken the same number); T015/T041/T047 (four behind one unsigned figure, box locked, not brought under the roof).
- **Foreshadowing planted / paid off:** nothing paid. The kept things stay on their own places with a finger between: the offered figure and its return, the reported second slate of Silt 6, the hair-thin thaw of Silt 7, the neighbour's near-reaching, the fifth shaping, the clerk's entered figure, the two market sightings on separate trays with no eighth of either. ## Scene 2 — What the Eleventh Left

- **Day and time:** Day 435, Silt 11, Firstday; afternoon, going of the light, small hours at the close.

**Beats in order**
1. The boy takes the yard's small sounds first because they do not lie and the large ones are mostly weather: pail down on the flat stone with its flat knock, rope settling into its knot with the cold-stiffened rasp, barley wall to wall without a number, a loaf pushed a finger's width and stopped with nobody calling the stopping a figure. Takes each apart by fist, eye, mouth and the half-beat in the gap; gives the gap no intention, for an intention wants a payer where none stands.
2. One wide circuit of the seven walked-around places with a look and a wide step and no number, once and not twice, widest at the returning-line place where a hand went in on Seed 15 and did not come back — no name, no body, no figure, no accident, no payer (T098). 3. No slate opened within reach of the basket, no figure entered, no money at any point.
4. Sella carries him home at the going of the light at the half-pace, says nothing to the basket of what the day was; the whole grammar returns at both ends of the day — fire before basket, coals told the thaw and not the basket, charcoal unread under her finger, lips on a two-gapped row, one glance at the cold clay jar with the hand shut half over nothing, twice-mended pot thumb-first with hairline showing and both proverbs to pot and fire, dressed grain turned under wool unfigured, pot rim two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat and no look toward the bed, widow's mark untouched, fold righted without a look at his face and no second fold (T058/T079/T100, T060/T086/T097).
5. The file in the small hours: towel out at the head of the bed as instruments come out when a case is done; he goes over the day by the place of each thing with every gap a gap and does not add them, for a surgeon who adds findings before the last is in has already made up his answer. Six hearings of the full note beside and never among, seventh place empty, Drawing house's flat knock outside on its own place; heard bell beside told with both gaps in their squares and three counts in three places staying three; nine record-first hands with nine stoppings and no tenth; seven openings in seven months not a rule. The offered figure and return, well-asking answered with weather, second man's turned slate and direction, clerk's entered figure as a report, fifth shaping, three likenesses of Slate Row, neighbour's near-reaching, reported second slate, hair-thin thaw, burned slate at five and four ownerless directions each on its own place with a finger between every pair — none a rule, witness, tenth hand, sixth shaping, doctrine or payer, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood (T061/T082, T078, T080/T090/T102, T097, T065/T095, T101 held).
6. **Answer 446** filed on the sixth place; count holds at **435 exact**, six months and 255 days, told by nobody; fire's fifth unpaid and Garl's name unspoken the same number, the two counts side by side and not one. Silt 20 (Day 444) stands nine mornings off as a date and is not brought.

- **Emotional turn:** the yard's ordinary arithmetic stays unentered and the boy's file stays exact and worth nothing; the day closes without a verdict and without a payer.
- **Sensory anchors:** flat knock of a pail on stone, cold-stiffened rope, sour tang of Ash in the unswept corner, the half-beat at the pot's setting, woodsmoke and tallow on the fold, the rough wash-towel.
- **Threads advanced, none paid:** T061/T082 and T078 (small sounds taken apart; hearings, bells and counts kept in their squares); T098/T009/T013/T092 (seven walked-around places once, widest at the returning-line place; trough's round print; two unswept lines called weather); T083/T085/T092 (thaw and softer river edge found where put, not made again); T077 (costs as directions arriving nowhere); T058/T079/T100 (whole grammar and unread charcoal); T071/T072 (answer 446, count 435); T088.
- **Foreshadowing planted / paid off:** nothing paid. The fourth reading is not made to answer the near-reaching, the second slate, the thaw or any kept thing; no eighth opening, no eleventh arithmetic, no tenth hand, no three counts made one. The two market sightings stay on separate trays, never named, never Fenna or Ulda, no eighth of either.
- **Exact last image:** the river keeps its low note of water over water under the winter skin, softer at one edge; the hand comes up an inch toward it (the inch not a reach), takes a small intake, holds the interval whole on the note's finishing, lets it go and comes straight down flat on the fold on answer 446; there is no next hand in the room.

2. Ilva's hand goes flat over the fold in her sleeve **before** her eyes go to the asker and her mouth after both, the half-beat lying in the gap where a lesser observer would put an intention. The boy takes the order of reaching apart by the place of each part — hand to sleeve, eyes to asker, mouth after both, the half-beat in the gap — and files the **second asking** on a place of its own beside Ilva's offered figure and its return, her ninth record-first hand and the fifth shaping of the well-saying, with a finger between every pair: **not a second offer, not a custom, not a tenth hand, not a witness, not a sale, not the calling of the loan**, no figure under it and no payer, and expressly unjoined to the clerk's entered figure, the reported second slate of Silt 6, the neighbour's near-reaching at the shelf, the well-asking answered with weather and the three likenesses of Slate Row. ## Scene 6 — What the Thirteenth Left

- **Day and time:** Day 437, Silt 13, Thirdday; afternoon at the yard's edge, going of the light, small hours closing the chapter.
- **Place:** the Clay Yard's edge and the path along it; under the Vail kiln-house roof at the loaf shelf and bed; the finger's-width door and the river beyond.
- **Present:** Corin/Dan, Sella Vail; **the man from Slate Row** at the yard's edge (a seen shape only — no sheet, no name, no interior, no errand, no standing as a witness); Tarn and Jorra offstage; Garl Vail in objects; Ilva offstage with her count in wood and her fear unpaid.

**Beats in order**
1. In the afternoon a man from Slate Row whose name is on no roll stands at the yard's edge with his hands loose and the patient face of a man who can wait in a doorway. Nobody asks him what he wants and he does not say; no loaf passes, no slate is shown, no word carries and no threshold is crossed. He is not entered and does not enter.
2. The boy takes the face apart by the place of each part — loose hands, patient face, the space where a question would stand and does not — and files it on a place of its own beside the older record-first hands, the watcher's late turn and the first man's face at the edge, with a finger between it and Ilva's second asking of Silt 12 and another between it and the Slate Row likenesses. **Not a tenth hand, a reader, a witness, a sale, a payment or a payer**; not joined to the clerk's entered figure, the reported second slate, the neighbour's near-reaching or the fourth reading; not brought under the roof as a lesson. The household's exposure goes on gathering toward the far-off calling and is not called here (T090/T080 with T036/T076 and T035/T031).
3. Berr's kindness and *your house* remain believed and not owed; the Kindling collector's reason, the clean voice's missing name and the Long Night story's empty name stay on their own trays; no payer stands to any of them (T065/T095).
4. Sella carries him home at the going of the light at the half-pace from every open slate and says nothing to the basket of the face at the edge or of what the day was. Under the roof the whole grammar comes back as though nothing had turned; she puts the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face and adds no second fold, the keeping found where put and still a finding (T058/T079, T060/T086/T097).
5. Small hours: the towel comes out at the head of the bed as instruments come out when a case is done, rough from the wash and smelling of tallow and low smoke. He goes over the day by the place of each thing with every gap a gap and does not add them. Six hearings beside and never among with the seventh place empty and the Drawing's flat knock outside; heard bell beside told with both gaps in squares and three counts in three places staying three; nine record-first hands with nine stoppings and no tenth; seven openings in seven months and not a rule. Four readings of the year stand behind one unsigned figure — four being four and not one account, the first not a thirteenth, the second no signature, the third no doctrine, the fourth no payer, the twelve of Y.H. 813 not laid across the four, the figure read still not the figure paid — and the fourth reading is not made to answer any kept thing under this roof (T015/T041/T047, T078/T061/T082). The unsent call to Nina stays where it was put and is not paid again (T020).
6. **Answer 450** filed on the sixth place, exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment; six honest answers **445–450** stand there, two to each of the three days, not one a doctrine. Count **437 exact**, six months and 257 days told by nobody; fire's fifth unpaid and Garl's name unspoken the same number, the man and the fire standing side by side without becoming one, for a number counted is a number with air around it and air buys no warmth.
7. Through the finger's-width door the river keeps its low note of water over water under the winter skin, softer at one edge where the low note runs nearer and the tang thinner, the note coming from under the covering and ending on its own finishing. The hand outside the wool comes up an inch toward it — the inch not a reach, not a wanting made into a claim, only the body's small assent to a sound — takes a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, holds the interval whole on the note's finishing, lets it go and comes straight down and lays itself flat against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. There is no next hand in the room. He does not wipe the thread and does not lift the crust. Under the boards the carried fire keeps four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood, and nobody lifts the floor and nobody supplies the pull.
