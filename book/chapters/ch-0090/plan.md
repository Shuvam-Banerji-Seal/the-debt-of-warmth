## Pull into Chapter 91

The last image is deliberately not an ending: the count has reached **three hundred and twenty-three exact**, six months and one hundred and forty-three days told by nobody, and Bone 30 (Day 329), the month's third and last Account-day, now stands **six mornings** ahead. The handful at the rack end is held as one object and not spent; the burned slate still waits at five with no sixth; Ilva's strip is still in her sleeve; Hessa's slate is still unread up the road; Berr's box still holds five true subtractions under one unsigned figure. Chapter 91 opens on Day 324, Bone 25, Fifthday, with the hand coming up for the two hundred and twenty-third answer and the count going to three hundred and twenty-four, in the last days before the third reading — no sign carried forward, no lesson made, nothing joined, and forward motion only.
