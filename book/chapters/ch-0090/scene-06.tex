\person{Sella Vail} carried him down in the afternoon at the half-pace she kept from every open slate, with the instruction going to the wool and the river wind and never to the basket. The yard stood in its dry light as a field stands after the harvest, with nothing left in it that a number could take hold of. \person[Corin]{The boy} came into it under her arm with his fold at his ear and the one stiff crust two inches from it, and took the place by its small sounds first, because the small sounds do not lie. The rope at \place{the yard well} gave its dry rasp as a bucket went down and came up; a pot set down somewhere out of sight answered flat and then went quiet; the river kept \lore{water over water} under everything, a low thing over a lower thing, and was not numbered among the hearings. There was no market, no \place{the Cross} and no bell on the twenty-fourth of Bone. Bone went on without a boundary drawn, and neither of the district's two habits of naming its months was set right or left wrong in so many words: the older women called it by the weather that had not come and the slatemen by their count of Account-days, and the month carried both names the way a pot carries two handles and is none the worse for it.

She went wide around the seven places with a look and a wide step and no number. The clean dark line at the corner of the house lay unswept and the finer grey hairline beside it called \lore{the thaw} lay unswept too, two lines that a man with a broom might have made into one cause if a payer had stood under it; no payer stood. Beneath \place[the drying racks]{the lowest rack} the broken birth-slates kept their place in the dust, and she went around the whole space as wide as a pail of hot water set where a child might knock it. The shallow clay pan's round ring had no scrape, no crumb, no print, no chip and no word in it, and the boy kept the ring on a place of its own rather than make a fifth likeness stand up out of three empty ones. \place{the trough} held its skin of ice, grey and whole where a pail had broken and closed it through the morning. Out on \place{the worked strips} the field kept its empty place with the same look it had kept on the twenty-second and the twenty-third, and the returning-line place where a hand had gone in on Seed 15 and not come out was gone around with a look and no figure. Seven places, a look and a wide step and no number. No eighth likeness stood up and no cause was made across two, because two likenesses are not a cause and do not call themselves a rule without a payer, and none stood.

Three coverings stayed weather entered nowhere. The frost that had gone off the well's north lip on Bone 3 while the yard looked elsewhere was a going and not a sign; the skin on the trough had broken under a pail's knock and closed again and was a skin and nothing more; the silt-clouded plate of ice stood on \place{the flat stone by the well} out of the path of feet, where Sella had told the weather to stand on Frost 9, and the yard had gone around it ever since without lifting it or naming it. A finger went between every pair. A fourth likeness was not made a cause, and no figure, sign, lesson, mending or payment came off any of the three. The surgeon in him read the three as a covering laid over a depth, the same covering over another depth, and a piece of the depth lifted and set where no foot goes, and made no depth of the three together, because a depth made out of three coverings would have wanted a payer under it and none stood.

The month's hunts and repairs went past as directions judged by the carrying arm and the weather and never as figures. A man come down from \place{Cold Acre} with a hare across his shoulders shifted the weight when a woman at the well asked what the carrying had cost, and told her to ask the weather, which had carried the snow and could carry the price, and went off toward \place{the clay road} with nobody weighing the hare and nobody opening a slate. Another with a cracked jar in a sling of rope asked what the mending of the month would want and was told to ask the pot; the pot, being a pot, said nothing. The boy read both as a chart left blank where the number should have stood, and entered neither. A cost question was a direction and a direction arrived nowhere and wanted no payer.

\scenebreak

At \place{the rack end} the dressed grain lay under wool on its cool stone away from \place{the hearth}, giving up its water and going on giving it up with a small dry rasp that did not change from morning to afternoon. She turned it beneath the wool with the lighter touch and gave no figure to it. Her fingers went in and came out and did not close. They did not lift a small handful and let it fall back grain by grain and then all at once, the way they had done on the twenty-second. The wool came down over the place where no handful had fallen, and she went on to the next thing as though there had been no place to notice.

The boy checked the holding as a surgeon checks a tray before the dressing goes on. The handful let fall back through her fingers on Bone 22 sat on a place of its own, one object lifted and tested and replaced, kept because it was true and not because it bought anything. It was not made again on the twenty-third or the twenty-fourth, and the empty fingers of those two mornings went beside it as a keeping and not as a second. He put a finger between the keeping and the object kept, and a finger between both and the four held changes on their four places. It was not a fifth held change, not a sign, not a lesson, not a mending, not a cause, not a sixth arithmetic and not a rule. Two would have been a habit and a habit would have wanted a payer put under it; three would have been a way and a way would have wanted one too. No payer stood. The surgeon in him took a clean satisfaction in a thing done once and left alone, in a field not disturbed twice. The infant underneath took none of it: the infant wanted the crust lifted the two inches to his mouth, the thread wiped, a hand on the pot handle set beyond his reach, and a field left as found was nothing to him and bought him nothing at two inches of air. Both were true and the second was louder and neither won; the argument went on without a verdict, and he kept the small grief of it on its own place and did not spend it, because an absence kept empty is a finding and an absence filled with a feeling to make a row come out even is a finding forged.

The staying of Bone 15 and the early letting-go of Bone 17 sat each on its own place, two objects on two places with a finger between them and between them and everything else there, neither made again on any of these three days, neither a habit nor a way nor a fifth held change, both wanting a payer who did not stand. Five arithmetics of the year stood behind one printed figure: one in twelve printed and unchallenged, the figure read still not the figure paid, no signature on the space between, five readings five and not one account. Their true subtractions rode locked up the clay road in \person{Berr Clay}'s box toward \place{High Kiln}, unread under this roof and not brought back as an answer to anything. The finding sat on a place of its own with no payer under it, not a lesson, not a grievance, not a mending, not a doctrine, not a rule. It was not laid across the house's \lore{not-doings}, or across the dressed grain under wool, or across the four held changes, or across the handful, or across the two small objects of the hand, or across his own kept gaps. The edges came near to touching, so near that a less careful hand would have pushed them together and called the touching a cause; the finger went between. The surgeon knew what an institution would have done with five subtractions under one unsigned figure: laid them together, called the agreement an account and put a man under it to pay. He had spent a life watching that done and had signed one of those accounts himself. Keeping the finding where no slate could take it was the nearest honest work he had left, and it bought nothing.

\scenebreak

He took the whole holding tray as he took the body, in its old order, asking nothing of any of it. The burned-slate word stood at five with a finger between the fifth and the four and no sixth; five tellings were five and not five witnesses to one fire, and the claim went on gathering interest against a promise nobody had given. The row stood at five askings with one shaping and no witness and expressly no sixth asking. The well-saying stood at three shapings, with \person[Hessa Rale]'s eighth hand kept on its own place beside it and a finger between. Nine record-first hands stood with \person{Ilva}'s ninth in a sleeve where the notched birch strip stayed unread, and no tenth stood up; she was offstage on the twenty-fourth and her keeping stayed in wood where no slate could take a figure off it. Nine stoppings stood with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end and the loan uncalled, and the warm loft stone lay cold in its unmarked pot, unreconciled by not trying, which was not forgiveness. Six hearings of \lore{the full note} stood beside and never among, the sixth the small flat knock out of a woman as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go and never among the things that came out of vessels, and the seventh place stayed empty and none was invented to fill it, for an empty place kept empty is a finding and a finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged. The heard bell stayed in its square beside the told bell of \lore{the Long Night}; the told figure stood beside the paid with no signature on the space. Two shut-room tellings stood at two and not three; three pot sayings stood with the Smoke 14 note apart; five openings in five months were five and not a rule with the next far off; seven green a colour and seven shape a shape with no eighth; \lore{the widow's mark} hung where it hung and was touched at no shelf, well, rack, road, basket, yard, afternoon, going of the light or bed, because none of these is an opening. No collector came up or went down the clay road.

At \place{the kindling} \person{Tarn} kept six where the house could check and the seventh at the lip beside \person{Garl Vail}'s unforged collector sentence, where the house could not check. The small knife lay at the very edge of its sill where a man's hand could slip it off and where it could also be checked, and he told the wood in the tone of a man stating the weather, \emph{Six counted. The next I don't count}. He put the knife back where it could be checked and announced it to nobody, because the yard had already been told, and when a woman with a basket of kindling asked what the seventh year would want when it came he said \emph{Yes, house} to her and to the man who said the thaw would be early and again to the one who said it would be late, and kept the late because a late thaw puts off the paying. The sentence about an ear under this roof being worth more than the debt that held it came up to the word that would call the loan and stopped there, with no figure and no name, a ninth stopping with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end and still ungiven. He drew the breath that should have gone in at the top of the reach and did not spend it, and his chest had been tight since the dark. \lore{Ash} was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line and no scrubbing lifted it, and the pale clip-ring at his left wrist held what little light there was and gave it back. His stroke went over the shallow bright patch on \place{the flagstones} without the half-check, and the stones kept the place where it had stopped.

The boy kept the equality under the roof as he had found it: two ears, two silences, a loan that could hear and had never been called. He filed the stopping on the interval tray as an interval and not a payment, with no payer named to it and no tenth standing up out of nine likenesses to call the nine a rule. He did not join Tarn's held breath to his own small intake, though both were a breath not spent; a finger went between them, because two empty places kept by two men under one roof are two empty places and not one finding. To Sella the clay-bound man said nothing that was not a statement of fact, and she gave the instruction to the pot and the fire and did not ask about his seventh year or his ear.

\person{Jorra Vail} came through the finger's-width door off \place{the Vail wheel-shed} with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bent little finger and white in the creases where a fist had opened, and it was not the bone-coloured \lore{slip} of the wheel. Three or more men had gone low into \place{the back room} before first grey and he gave the house no name for them and did not say the room was cold. He kept dusty forearms and clean ones on one tray and parted them as a surgeon parts clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had been broken nor whether it was a birth-slate. Before he spoke to any person he spoke to the wheel: \emph{It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady}. To a pot on the bench he said \emph{You'll do} in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either, and he knew the full note that pot would give three turns before it left his hands, with air under it and a want in it, and kept the knowing on the near-full tray beside the hearings and never among them, and would not say it before a kiln-master come up from High Kiln who would call it early and make something of it, singing before its time and singing itself out early. When Sella had not finished telling him the fire was low his bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper}, took the cold out of the fingertip, moved the tongue a hair's breadth and left it standing, so that the end of her instruction arrived at the pot and the household straightened around his silence. He did not look toward the basket.

The boy read the iron again as one piece with two bites: the bite that holds a vessel whole and the bite that can take the hand off a man, with no word in Grathic for both mercies. He filed it as an object joined to no kiln, with the two shut-room tellings beside it and no third, and did not make the dusty forearms a cause of anything or the bent finger a promise. \emph{He's not Vail} stayed sealed as tightly as a lid nobody had tried to lift in three years, and the promise of the boy's name to the men who burn slates was neither given nor withdrawn while the burned slate stood at five with a finger between the fifth and the four.

Garl stayed in objects and not in a story. The salt-stiff coat with the crease at the left shoulder kept a broader man's shape on its peg behind the door and the wheel-seat in the shed kept its twenty-year lean and its hollow on that side, which Sella let no one fill. His proverbs went to the pot and the fire and never to a person, and had improved in the keeping and were very likely not as good as the man had said them. His name had not been spoken under the roof for three hundred and twenty-three days, and the debt against a firing of tiles circled without a name and was worse for being uncalled, and no lesson was made of any of it.

At the going of the light Sella carried him up the yard at the half-pace from every open slate and set him on \place{the bed} under the roof in \place{the Vail kiln-house}, never in \place{the Debt Loft}. The loft was a sound above and behind --- a small dry scrape on \place{the boards} from a room nobody entered --- and she did not go up and no interior opened. She put the fold right at his ear without a look at his face, her hand resting a half-beat longer than the setting needed before it went away, and left the thread of drool at his mouth frozen and unwiped because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. One stiff brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from his ear, gone leathery in the dry kiln heat with its corner standing a hair off the wool; the empty place beside it kept its name to itself and no third came to make the row come out even. Cold had stopped at cheek, temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, as at the end of a line written out for it, and \lore{the Afterdrop} waited beyond all three past a warming nobody in this house would perform. He knew the composed face was not composure, that a cold vessel cannot clot and will seem better before it is worse and must be warmed from the outside and slowly, never rubbed and never set too near the fire, and the knowing bought him nothing at two inches of air. Both were true and the second was louder and neither won, and the argument went on in the dark without a verdict and without a witness.

Under the floor \lore{the carried fire} gave four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer unnamed for three hundred and twenty-three days; overhead the boards kept four nails on the near board, four on the next and a thumb-width knot where a fifth might have been driven and was not; through the finger's-width door the river kept water over water, a low thing over a lower thing, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming, and was not numbered among the hearings. Three counts in three places, and none rose into another, because agreeing any two would have wanted a payer put under both and no payer stood to any. The bell's gap left behind in the square was not fetched in to make a fourth, and the heard bell stayed in its square beside the told; the file lay open on \place{the towel} at the head of the bed with every object on its own place and every gap left gap. The twice-mended pot stood where Sella had set it with the hairline of the original break showing between the old-brass staple and the tighter wire, repair kept apart from a whole vessel and neither called the other by a name.

At the very end of the day, with the yard quiet and the river's note at its lowest under the finger's-width door, the hand outside the wool went up an inch toward the sound. It took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole, let the intake go on the same low note, and came straight down and laid itself back against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. The note had done before the hand came down, so the letting-go was neither early again nor stayed, and the hand did not stay. The inch was not a reach and the intake was not a breath held for warmth; the interval was not a stopping of the river and the note was not his. He filed it as answer two hundred and twenty-two: exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment, borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the five arithmetics, the bell's gap, the covered grain, the handful, the stayed object, the early letting-go nor his own kept gaps. Two hundred and twenty-two honest answers stood on the sixth place, each an object and not a power, and neither the number nor the small hand-movings that had made it stood up and called themselves a doctrine, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood.

The surgeon took a clean satisfaction in the field left as it was found, and the infant underneath wanted the crust lifted two inches and the thread wiped and got neither, and both were true and the second was louder and neither won. Three hundred and twenty-three stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill} and \place{Grath}; six months and one hundred and forty-three days were told by nobody; the carried fire's fifth was unpaid the same number of days with its payer unnamed; and the name of the man the coat kept was not spoken under the roof the same number. Nothing on the twenty-fourth had been opened, joined, named, called, paid or ended. The five arithmetics stood behind one unsigned figure; the burned slate waited at five with no sixth; the strip stayed in the sleeve; the grain lay covered and unfigured; the handful stayed one object on its own place. The keeping of the number was the only honest work there had been to do, bought nothing at two inches of air, and went on because attention is the one thing no institution can tax. The file lay open on the towel with every gap left gap, one stiff brown-edged crust lay two inches from his ear with its empty place still unnamed and no third, and the hand lay back against the fold while the river's low note went quiet under the finger's-width door and Bone went on without a boundary drawn, six mornings short of the month's last reading and not brought into a day that had not earned it.
