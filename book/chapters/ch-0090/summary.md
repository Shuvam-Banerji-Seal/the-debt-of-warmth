## Chronicle

Three days went over the Vail kiln-house in the last tend of Bone and left the house as they found it. The boy came into his three hundred and twenty-first morning with the hand laid down on answer two hundred and sixteen, a tool put away, and went out of the twenty-fourth with answer two hundred and twenty-two laid down in the same way. Between them six small intakes were taken from the room whose floor does not touch his and let go whole on the river's note, six honest answers entered where nobody could read them, each an object and not a power, exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air.

Nothing was carried across as a sign. The reading of Bone 20 was not read again; one in twelve stood unchallenged, the figure read still not the figure paid, and five arithmetics of the year rode up the clay road in a locked box under a space nobody had signed. The boy kept the finding on a place of its own.

Sella kept the grammar of the house: fire before basket, *The house will keep it* where another woman would have said I, the twice-mended pot lifted on the thumb at the crack, the crack left showing, the pot rim set two inches beyond the reaching hand, the widow's mark untouched because a morning and a hearth are not openings. On the twenty-second, at the rack end, she lifted the wool, took a small handful of dressed grain and let it fall back through her fingers instead of spreading it. She said nothing, gave no figure, and did not do it again. The surgeon knew the lift of a dressing's edge and the letting of it fall; the infant wanted the crust lifted two inches and got no lifting of it, and both were true and the second was louder and neither won.

In the yard she went wide around the seven places with a look and a wide step, and the three coverings at the well stayed weather entered nowhere. At the well on the twenty-third Ilva drew with the pail against her knee, and when a woman asked what the firings of Bone had taken, her hand went toward her sleeve and stayed; the notched strip stayed in wood, and she answered, *Ask the pot what it cost. I keep the count, not the account.* The boy filed her reaching as a ninth hand and no tenth. Tarn kept six where the house could check and the seventh at the lip, his ear-sentence stopped at the word that would call the loan. Jorra put his bent finger into the cold damper ahead of Sella's unfinished sentence. Nobody opened a slate.

Nothing was opened, joined, named, called, paid or ended. The handful stayed one object, the stayed inch and the early letting-go stayed two, and three hundred and twenty-three stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody while Bone went on without a boundary drawn.
