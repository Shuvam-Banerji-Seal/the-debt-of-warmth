\person{Sella Vail} carried him down into \place{the Clay Yard} in the afternoon at the half-pace she kept from every open slate, the way a vessel is carried that is filled to the lip and must not be slopped. The instruction went to the wool and the river wind and never to the basket. \emph{Keep it off his ears. It's the river wind}, she told the fold, and told the wind nothing, because the wind was the one thing in the yard that had never asked to be told anything. The small jolts of her walking came up through the straw into \person[Corin]{the boy}'s ribs, and the crust two inches from his ear had gone leathery in the dry heat with its corner standing a hair off the wool; the thread at his mouth had frozen again and was unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread. Cold had come to cheek and temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, and had stopped there as though it had come to the end of a line written out for it. \lore{The Afterdrop} waited past the stopping, past a warming nobody here would perform. He took the inventory in its old order, by the small sounds first because the small sounds do not lie, and the infant underneath wanted the crust lifted two inches and the thread wiped and went on wanting them while the surgeon took the field. Both were true and the second was louder and neither won; the argument was the work.

Three hundred and twenty-two stood exact behind him and before him, six months and one hundred and forty-two days told by nobody under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill}, \place{Grath}. The carried fire's fifth was unpaid the same number of days with its payer unnamed, and the name of the man the coat kept had not been spoken the same number. Nothing from the morning's file had come down into the yard as a sign, a lesson, a mending, a cause or a payment. The handful let fall through the fingers at the rack end on the day behind this one stayed one object on its own place and was not made again; this morning's empty fingers had been a keeping beside it and not a second, and two would have been a habit and a habit would have wanted a payer, and none stood. Bone went on without a boundary drawn. The older women of the district named the month by the weather that had not come and the slatemen by their count of Account-days, and neither habit was set right or left wrong under the roof; the month carried both names the way a pot carries two handles and is none the worse for it.

She went around the seven places with a look and a wide step and no number. The clean dark line at the house's corner where the \lore{Ash} ridge turned lay unswept, and the finer grey hairline beside it that the yard called \lore{the thaw} lay unswept beside it, and she carried the basket wide of both as though a pail of hot water stood there that a child might knock. Under \place[the drying racks]{the lowest rack} the broken birth-slates lay where no broom went and no hand entered. On the stone lip by the well the shallow clay pan's gone place kept its round ring with no scrape, no crumb, no print, no chip and no word. The water in \place{the trough} had a skin on it from edge to edge. Out on \place{the worked strips} the field had its empty place, and in the returning line the place where a hand had gone in on Seed 15 and had not come out was gone around with a look and a wide step and no figure spoken over the going. Seven places, each with a look and a wide step; no eighth likeness stood up among them as a row, and no cause was laid across any two, because a cause wants a payer put under it and no payer stood.

Three coverings at the well stayed weather and were entered nowhere. The frost that had stayed on the well's north lip on Bone 3 while the yard looked elsewhere was gone now, and its going was not a sign. The skin on the trough broke under a pail's knock and closed again over the water without a word spoken to it. The plate of ice clouded with silt stood on \place{the flat stone by the well} out of the path of feet where she had set it on Frost 9 and told the weather where to stand. There was a finger between every pair of them. A fourth likeness would have stood up as a rule, and a rule wanted a payer, and none stood. The boy kept the three where they were, not a lesson written on the air and not a mending anybody had performed.

At \place{the rack end} the dressed grain lay under wool on the cool stone away from the hearth. Sella turned it with a touch lighter than the last and gave no figure to it. The dry rasp of the grain came up small and unmistakable, the sound a thing makes when it has given up its water and is going on giving it up. Her fingers went in and moved through the heap and came out again. They did not close. They did not take a small handful and let it fall back grain by grain and then all at once, the way they had done on the day behind this one. The wool came down over the place where no handful had fallen, and she went on to the next thing as though there had been no place to notice. The boy watched the not-doing as carefully as another boy would have watched a doing. One object lifted and tested and replaced was one object; an empty hand going in and coming out was a keeping beside it and not a second. He put a finger between the keeping and the object kept and a finger between both and the four held changes on their four places, and forbade the two to stand up together and call themselves a rule, quietly and with the exactness of a man setting a tool back on a tray where the next hand will find it without looking.

At the edge of the yard stood \place{the yard well}, its stone lip worn smooth by pails and ropes and the years of hands that had drawn there. \person{Ilva} was drawing when Sella carried him past, and the boy took in the small sounds of the drawing first because the small sounds do not lie. The rope was stiff in the dry cold and gave its small dry rasp as the bucket came up, a sound like a hand dragged over dry boards, and the pail was set against her knee while she got the rope's weight. Clay had dried pale in the crescents of her nails, and the finer grey dust of the drying racks was worked into the seam of one thumb, which she did not scrub out. Her hands knew the weight of a pail and the edge of a wet board and had never been soft, and went on knowing these things without reporting on them to anybody. She was of the firing work and not of a house, of the yard in the way the rack and the trough were of the yard, and the district would not have turned its head twice for her. People noticed the pail first and the sleeve not at all, which was the order she preferred.

A woman with a pot came and asked what the firings of Bone had taken. The question was put in the level tone of a person asking the price of a thing and expecting a figure to come back. Ilva's hand went toward her sleeve and stayed. The short strip of notched birch bark lay where it lay; it was pale where a thumb had rubbed the notches and dark in the uncut grain between, and it did not come out, was not taken out, read, copied, counted or joined to the charcoal beneath \place{the loaf shelf}, the cord, the open slate lying on the stone lip a pace away, the knot-string or the ownerless saying. No figure came off the wood. The boy watched the small motion arrest itself at the sleeve the way he had once watched a hand stop short of a field it had been about to enter, before the dressing went on and while there was still time to keep the field clean.

Her eyes went to the asker and her mouth opened after both, with a half-beat between the looking and the word --- the hand to the record before the eyes to the person, the eyes before the mouth, an order as exact as the order in which a field is opened and the instrument laid into it. “Ask the pot what it cost,” she said. “I keep the count, not the account.” There was no proverb in it and no instruction to a pot or a fire; that belonged to the houses. She answered the question put and not the one behind it, gave no name to the thing she kept, and would not have known what there was to explain. She put the flat of her hand once more on the sleeve and went back to the pail, and nobody turned twice. The woman with the pot went off with her answer level, and the yard went on about its work.

\scenebreak

The boy took the order of her reaching apart as a surgeon takes a chart apart, by what was there and by what was not, and filed it. It was the ninth such hand in the district, and it stayed nine. He put a finger between it and \person{Hessa Rale}'s eighth, and a finger between both and every other hand on the tray, and would not let the nine stand up and call themselves a rule, for a rule is an account that has found a payer and none stood to this one. Nine objects were nine objects, and the count of them was a thing he kept and not a thing he could spend. He did not make her a reader of her own keeping, which she was not and had never wanted to be; a person can carry a record for years and never be its reader, and the wood stays wood and the notches stay notches whether anybody reads them or not. He did not take a figure off the strip, did not make the slate lying a pace away into a cause, and did not join the nearness of the two into anything that wanted a witness.

She was not \person{Marra}, not the soft-handed man, not the soft-cuffed clerk set below the north bridge for a measure nobody called, not Hessa, not the green-mouthed woman at the pot board, not the woman whose voice had come over the boards, and not the Drawing collector, and she was joined to none of them. Each of those had been filed on a place of its own with a finger between it and the next, and the finger went between now. The old exact pleasure in noticing rose in him, the pleasure of a man who has found a thing where nobody thought a thing was kept, and it was checked at once by the rule that two likenesses are not a cause. A second person in the district kept a figure where no slate could take it. That was all it was. To join the strip in her sleeve to the handful under the wool, or either of them to the charcoal beneath the shelf, would have wanted a payer put under both and none stood, and the edges of the things nearly touched and the finger went between.

His attention had moved from the handful under the wool to the strip in the sleeve without joining them, and it went back. The one hand had tested grain and let it fall; the other had gone to wood and stayed there. Testing and keeping are two acts and neither pays for the other. The surgeon in him took a clean satisfaction in the two acts kept distinct, in a field not disturbed twice and a record not taken off its wood. The infant underneath took none of it. The infant wanted the crust lifted two inches and the thread wiped, and a clean field and a true count bought him nothing at two inches of air. Both were true and the second was louder and neither won; the argument went on without a verdict and without a witness, and the satisfaction and the grief of it sat side by side on the tray with a finger between.

At \place{the kindling} \person{Tarn} kept his six where the house could check and the seventh at the lip beyond, beside a collector sentence that had never been forged and lay there with no name on the man who had turned back at the frozen ford. He set the small knife at the very edge of its sill, where a man's hand could check it and where a man's hand could also slip it off, and told the wood \emph{Six counted. The next I don't count} in the tone of a man stating the weather. The announcement went to nobody, because the yard had already been told. When the sentence about an ear under this roof being worth more than the debt that held it came up in him, it came all the way to the word that would call the loan and stopped there, with no figure and no name, a ninth stopping with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end. He drew the breath from Seed 16 that should have gone in at the top of the reach and did not spend it, and his chest had been tight since the dark. \lore{Ash} was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line, and no scrubbing lifted it; the pale clip-ring at his left wrist held what little light there was and gave it back. No collector came up \place{the clay road} or went down it, and the debt that circled the firing of tiles kept its shape without a name and was worse for being uncalled.

At the shed's edge \person{Jorra Vail} came through the finger's-width door off \place{the Vail wheel-shed} with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bent little finger and white in the creases where a fist had opened; it was not the bone-coloured \lore{slip} of the wheel. He kept dusty forearms and clean ones on one tray and parted them as a surgeon parts clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had been broken nor whether it was a birth-slate. Three or more men had gone low into \place{the back room} before first grey and he gave the house no name for them and did not say the room was cold. He spoke to the wheel before he spoke to any person. \emph{It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady}, he said to the wheel, and to a pot \emph{You'll do}, in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either. He knew the full note that pot would give three turns before it left his hands, with air under it and a want in it, and kept the knowing on the near-full tray beside the hearings and never among them; he would not say it before a kiln-master come up from \place{High Kiln} who would call it early and make something of it, singing before its time and singing itself out early. Before Sella had finished telling him the fire was low his bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper}, took the cold out of the fingertip, moved the tongue a hair's breadth and left it standing so that the end of her instruction arrived at the pot. One piece of iron had two bites in it, the holding and the taking, and there was no word in the district's speech for both mercies. He did not look toward the basket. \emph{He's not Vail} stayed sealed as tightly as a lid nobody had tried to lift in three years, and the promise of a name to the men who burned slates was neither given nor withdrawn.

The holding tray was checked and found unchanged. The burned-slate word stood at five with a finger between the fifth and the four, no sixth, no witness, no hand at the flame and no payer, the claim going on gathering interest against Jorra's ungiven promise. The row stood at five askings with one shaping and no witness and expressly no sixth asking; the well-saying at three shapings; the shut-room tellings at two; the pot sayings at three with the Smoke 14 note on its own place and no fourth likeness made a cause. Six hearings of \lore{the full note} stood beside and never among with the seventh place empty, the sixth the small flat knock out of a woman as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go and never among the things that came out of vessels; an empty place kept empty is a finding and a finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged. Nine stoppings stood with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end, the loan uncalled. The returned half-check of Bone 8 kept \place{the flagstones}' own place with the Day 161 damper fraction, the Day 166 shelf beat and the Frost 5 returned beat, four held changes on four places and not a rule; the handful at the rack end was not made a fifth, and the staying of Bone 15 and the early letting-go of Bone 17 sat each on its own place and were not made again. Five arithmetics of the year stood behind one printed figure up the road: one in twelve unchallenged, the figure read still not the figure paid, no signature on the space, five readings five and not one account, their true subtractions riding locked in \person{Berr Clay}'s box toward \place{High Kiln}. The finding sat on a place of its own with no payer and was not laid across the house's \lore{not-doings}, the covered grain, the four held changes, the handful, the two small objects of the hand, the strip in the sleeve or the boy's own gaps. The edges came near to touching, so near that a less careful hand would have pushed them together and called the touching a cause. The finger went between.

\lore{The widow's mark} hung where it hung at the rack, the well, the kindling, the shed and the bed, because none of these was an opening; five openings in five months were five and not a rule, and the next stood far off in a later month's first firing, not hoped for and not counted toward. The heard bell stayed in its square beside the told bell of \lore{the Long Night}, and seven green was a colour and seven shape was a shape with no eighth of either. No collector came up the clay road or went down it. The man whose absence kept the house in its shape stayed in objects and not in a story: the salt-stiff coat on its peg with the crease at the left shoulder, the hollow wheel-seat that nobody filled, the clean dark line and the finer grey unswept, both proverbs gone to the pot and the fire and never to a person, the debt against a firing of tiles circling without a name and worse for being uncalled. \person{Garl Vail}'s name had not been spoken under the roof three hundred and twenty-two days, and the keeping of him was maintenance and not a lesson, and no doctrine was raised out of it.

At the going of the light Sella carried him up at the half-pace from every open slate and never once went into \place{the Debt Loft}. She set him on \place{the bed} under the roof, put the fold right at his ear without a look at his face, her hand resting the half-beat longer than the setting needed before it went away, and left the thread where it lay. Three counts stood in three places and did not become one. Under the floor \lore{the carried fire} gave four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer unnamed three hundred and twenty-two days; overhead \place{the boards} kept four nails on the near board, four on the next, and a thumb-width knot where a fifth might have been driven and was not; through the finger's-width door the river kept \lore{water over water}, a low thing over a lower thing, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming and not numbered among the hearings. Agreeing any two would have wanted a payer put under both, and no payer stood to any of them, so the three stayed side by side and not joined.

In the small hours, with the yard quiet and the river's note at its lowest under the finger's-width door, the hand outside the wool went up an inch toward the sound. It took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole, let the intake go on the same low note, and came straight down and laid itself back against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. The note had done before the hand came down, so the letting-go was neither early again nor stayed, and the hand did not stay. The inch was not a reach and the intake was not a breath kept for warmth; the interval was not a stopping of the river and the note was not his. He filed it as answer two hundred and twenty: exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment, borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the five arithmetics, the bell's gap, the covered grain, the handful, the stayed object, the early letting-go, the strip in the sleeve nor his own kept gaps. Two hundred and twenty honest answers stood on the sixth place, and neither the number nor the small hand-movings that had made it stood up and called themselves a doctrine, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood.

One stiff brown-edged crust lay two inches from his ear with its empty place unnamed and no third, and the surgeon took his clean satisfaction in the field left as found while the infant underneath wanted the crust lifted and the thread wiped and got neither, and both were true and the second was louder and neither won. Three hundred and twenty-two stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in Low Kiln, Sill and Grath; six months and one hundred and forty-two days were told by nobody; the carried fire's fifth was unpaid the same number of days with its payer unnamed; and the name of the man the coat kept was not spoken under the roof the same number. Nothing on the twenty-third had been opened, joined, named, called, paid or ended. The file lay open on \place{the towel} at the head of the bed with every gap left gap, the hand lay back against the fold, and the river's low note came under the finger's-width door while Bone went on without a boundary drawn.
