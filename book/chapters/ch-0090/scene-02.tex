\person{Sella Vail} carried him down into \place{the Clay Yard} in the afternoon at the half-pace she kept from every open slate, the way a pot is carried that is filled to the lip and must not be slopped. The instruction went to the wool and the river wind and never to the basket. \emph{Keep it off his ears. It's the river wind}, she told the fold, and told the wind nothing, because the wind was the one thing in the yard that had never asked to be told anything. \person[Corin]{The boy} felt the small jolts of her walking come up through the straw into his ribs, and the crust two inches from his ear had gone leathery in the dry heat of the day with its corner standing a hair off the wool, and the thread at his mouth had frozen again and was unwiped, and cold had come to cheek and temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back and had stopped there as though it had reached the end of a line written out for it. \lore{The Afterdrop} waited past the stopping, past a warming nobody under any roof here would perform. He took the inventory in its old order without choosing to, as a man reads a bay before he touches the patient, by the small sounds first because the small sounds do not lie, and the infant underneath wanted the crust lifted two inches and the thread wiped and went on wanting them while the surgeon took the field. Both were true and the second was louder and neither won; the argument was the work.

She went around the seven places with a look and a wide step and no number. The clean dark line at the house's corner where the \lore{Ash} ridge turned lay unswept, and the finer grey hairline beside it that the yard called \lore{the thaw} lay unswept beside it, and she carried the basket wide of both as though a pail of hot water stood there that a child might knock. Under \place[the drying racks]{the lowest rack} the broken birth-slates lay where no broom went and no hand entered, and on the stone lip by \place{the yard well} the shallow clay pan's gone place kept its round ring with no scrape, no crumb, no print, no chip and no word. The water in \place{the trough} had a skin on it from edge to edge. Out on \place{the worked strips} the field had its empty place, and in the returning line the place where a hand had gone in on Seed 15 and had not come out was gone around with a look and a wide step and no figure spoken over the going. Seven places, each with a look and a wide step; no eighth likeness stood up among them as a row, and no cause was laid across any two, because a cause wants a payer put under it and no payer stood.

Three coverings at the well stayed weather and were entered nowhere. The frost that had stayed on the well's north lip on Bone 3 while the yard looked elsewhere was gone now, and its going was not a sign; the skin on the trough broke under a pail's knock and closed again over the water without a word spoken to it; and the plate of ice clouded with silt stood on \place{the flat stone by the well} out of the path of feet where she had set it on Frost 9 and told the weather where to stand. There was a finger between every pair of them. A fourth likeness would have stood up as a rule, and a rule wanted a payer, and none stood. The boy kept the three where they were, not a lesson written on the air and not a mending anybody had performed.

A man come down from \place{Cold Acre} with a hare across his shoulders shifted the weight on his neck when a woman with a pot asked what the carrying had cost. “Ask the weather,” he said. “It carried the snow. It'll carry the price.” He went off toward \place{the clay road} with the hare still across him and nobody weighing it and nobody opening a slate, and the boy entered neither the hare nor the price. Another man with a cracked jar in a sling of rope asked what the mending of the month would want and was told to ask the pot, and the pot, being a pot, said nothing. These were the month's hunts and repairs, directions judged by the carrying arm and the weather, never figures; the boy took them apart the way he took apart a voice coming over the boards, by what was there and by what was not, and filed the blanks where a number should have stood and went on.

At \place{the rack end} the dressed grain lay under wool on the cool stone away from the hearth. Sella had turned it earlier with a touch lighter than the last time and no figure, and the boy had noted the place and the touch and put them where he put things not yet asked to mean anything. Now she lifted a corner of the wool, and the dry rasp of the grain came up small and unmistakable, the sound a thing makes when it has given up its water and is going on giving it up. She put her fingers into the heap and took a small handful. The boy watched the fingers close. She did not spread the handful to finish the turning, as she had spread grain on other days; she let it fall back through her fingers onto the heap, grain by grain and then all at once, and drew her hand away, and said nothing, and gave no figure to anything. The wool came down again over the place where the handful had fallen.

The surgeon in him knew that movement. It was the lifting of a dressing's edge to see what lay under it, and the letting of it fall again when there was nothing under it that wanted a hand; a careful lift, a small testing, a replacing exactly where it had been. It had not exposed anything anybody would have named. The infant underneath saw only a hand that had tested a thing and kept it, and wanted the crust lifted two inches and got no lifting of it.

He filed the handful on a place of its own. He put a finger between it and the four held changes on their four places --- the damper's fraction, the shelf's returned beat, the returned half-check of Bone 8, the Frost 5 beat returned --- and a finger between it and his own kept gaps, and forbade it, quietly and with the exactness of a man setting a tool back on a tray, to stand up as a fifth held change, a sign, a lesson, a mending, a cause, a sixth arithmetic or a rule. Two likenesses would have been a habit and a habit would have wanted a payer; three would have been a way and a way would have wanted one too. There was no payer standing to this handful. Nobody under the roof had spoken a number over it and nobody had looked toward the basket when it fell. It was kept because it was true and not because it bought anything, which was the only kind of keeping that survived under that roof.

She did not lift a handful again that afternoon and did not look at the place where she had let it fall. He noted the not-doing as carefully as the doing, because a thing done once and then left alone is one object and two would be a habit. The grain went on giving up its water under the wool, and the afternoon went on about its work, and nothing in the yard turned its head toward the basket.

At \place{the kindling} \person{Tarn} kept his six where the house could check and the seventh at the lip beyond, beside a collector sentence that had never been forged and lay there without a name on the man who had turned back at the frozen ford. He set the small knife at the very edge of its sill, where a man's hand could check it and where a man's hand could also slip it off, and told the wood \emph{Six counted. The next I don't count} in the tone of a man stating the weather. The announcement went to nobody, because the yard had already been told. When the sentence about an ear under this roof being worth more than the debt that held it came up in him it came all the way to the word that would call the loan and stopped there, with no figure and no name, a ninth stopping with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end. He drew the breath from Seed 16 that should have gone in at the top of the reach and did not spend it, and his chest had been tight since the dark. \lore{Ash} was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line, and no scrubbing lifted it; the pale clip-ring at his left wrist held what little light there was and gave it back, and he lifted the wrist a moment toward the grey as though to see whether the light had come to anything. No collector came up \place{the clay road} or went down it, and the debt that circled the firing of tiles kept its shape without a name and was worse for being uncalled.

The boy did not join the stopping to Sella's handful. The two lay near each other on the tray and a finger went between them. One was a hand that had tested and replaced; the other was a breath drawn and not spent. Five of one thing and nine of another were not a cause, and a rule made out of them would have wanted a payer put under both. He had spent a life watching institutions make one account out of several hands and had signed one of those accounts himself, and the small exact keeping of things apart was the nearest honest work he had left.

At the shed's edge \person{Jorra Vail} came through the finger's-width door off \place{the Vail wheel-shed} with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bent little finger and white in the creases where a fist had opened; it was not the bone-coloured \lore{slip} of the wheel. He kept dusty forearms and clean ones on one tray and parted them as a surgeon parts clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had been broken nor whether it was a birth-slate. Three or more men had gone low into \place{the back room} before first grey and he gave the house no name for them and did not say the room was cold. He spoke to the wheel before he spoke to any person. \emph{It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady}, he said to the wheel, and to a pot \emph{You'll do}, in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either. He knew the full note that pot would give three turns before it left his hands, with air under it and a want in it, and kept the knowing on the near-full tray beside the hearings and never among them; he would not say it before a kiln-master come up from \place{High Kiln} who would call it early and make something of it, singing before its time and singing itself out early.

Before Sella had finished telling him the fire was low his bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper}, took the cold out of the fingertip, moved the tongue a hair's breadth and left it standing so that the end of her instruction arrived at the pot. One piece of iron had two bites in it, the holding and the taking, and there was no word in the district's speech for both mercies, so the house had made do with one word and gone on. He did not look toward the basket. \emph{He's not Vail} stayed sealed as tightly as a lid nobody had tried to lift in three years, and the promise of a name to the men who burned slates was neither given nor withdrawn. The burned-slate word kept its five with a finger between the fifth and the four, and no sixth was due on a day that had not earned one; the shut-room tellings stayed at two, the pot sayings at three with the Smoke 14 note on its own place, the row at five askings with one shaping and no witness. Five arithmetics of the year stood behind one printed figure up the road, one in twelve unchallenged, the figure read still not the figure paid, no signature on the space, five readings five and not one account; the finding sat on a place of its own with no payer and was not laid across this house's \lore{not-doings}, the covered grain, the four held changes, the handful, the two small objects of the hand or his own gaps. The edges came near to touching. The finger went between.

\lore{The widow's mark} hung where it hung at the rack, the well, the kindling, the shed and the bed, because none of these was an opening; five openings in five months were five and not a rule. The man whose absence kept the house in its shape stayed in objects and not in a story: the salt-stiff coat on its peg with the crease at the left shoulder, the hollow wheel-seat that nobody filled, the clean dark line and the finer grey unswept, both proverbs gone to the pot and the fire and never to a person, the debt against a firing of tiles circling without a name and worse for being uncalled. His name had not been spoken under the roof three hundred and twenty-one days, and the keeping of him was maintenance and not a lesson, and no doctrine was raised out of it.

At the going of the light Sella carried him up at the half-pace from every open slate and never once went into \place{the Debt Loft}. She set him on \place{the bed} under the roof, put the fold right at his ear without a look at his face, her hand resting the half-beat longer than the setting needed before it went away, and left the thread where it lay. Under the floor \lore{the carried fire} gave four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer unnamed three hundred and twenty-one days; overhead \place{the boards} kept four nails on the near board, four on the next and a thumb-width knot where a fifth might have been driven and was not; through the finger's-width door the river kept \lore{water over water}, a low thing over a lower thing, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming and not numbered among the hearings. Three counts in three places; agreeing any two would have wanted a payer put under both, and none stood. Six hearings of \lore{the full note} stayed beside and never among with the seventh place empty, the heard bell in its square beside the told bell of \lore{the Long Night}, the told figure beside the paid. Two hundred and seventeen honest answers stood on the sixth place, each an object and not a power, and the number was spoken by nobody.

In the small hours, with the yard quiet and the river's note at its lowest under the finger's-width door, the hand outside the wool went up an inch toward the sound. It took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole, let the intake go on the same low note, and came straight down and laid itself back against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. The note had done before the hand came down, so the letting-go was neither early again nor stayed, and the hand did not stay. The inch was not a reach and the intake was not a breath kept for warmth; the interval was not a stopping of the river and the note was not his. He filed it as answer two hundred and eighteen: exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment, borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the five arithmetics, the bell's gap, the covered grain, the handful, the stayed object, the early letting-go nor his own kept gaps. Two hundred and eighteen honest answers stood on the sixth place, and neither the number nor the small hand-movings that had made it stood up and called themselves a doctrine, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood.

One stiff brown-edged crust lay two inches from his ear with its empty place unnamed and no third, and the surgeon took his clean satisfaction in the field left as found while the infant underneath wanted the crust lifted and the thread wiped and got neither, and both were true and the second was louder and neither won. Three hundred and twenty-one stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill}, \place{Grath}; six months and one hundred and forty-one days were told by nobody; the carried fire's fifth was unpaid the same number of days with its payer unnamed; and the name of the man the coat kept was not spoken under the roof the same number. Nothing on the twenty-second had been opened, joined, named, called, paid or ended. The hand lay back against the fold, the file lay open on \place{the towel} at the head of the bed with every gap left gap, and the river's low note came under the finger's-width door while Bone went on without a boundary drawn.
