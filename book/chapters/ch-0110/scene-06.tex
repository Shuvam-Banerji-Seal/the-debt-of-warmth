The afternoon came down thin through the finger's-width door and went on without a boundary drawn, the way a week goes out under that roof: not at a stroke but by the slow subtraction of light from the boards. \person[Corin]{The boy} lay where the morning had left him under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill}, \place{Grath}, with the crust's corner standing its hair off the wool and the thread frozen at his lip, and the small sounds of the house coming to him in the order a field comes to a surgeon before the light --- the couch of the fire under the floor, the dry rasp of a pail, a foot on the top step before the foot was on the second. The small sounds do not lie. The large ones are mostly weather.

\person{Sella Vail} came for him at the going-down of the afternoon and carried him down the nineteen steps at the half-pace she keeps from every open slate. The instruction went to the wool, to the river wind, to the fire she would bank against their coming back, and never to the basket; she corrected the tilt at every turn of the landing before it had time to settle into the wicker, so that he felt the small correction before he felt the stair. He took the order as a field taken at rest. Inside the wool the infant wanted the correction to go the other way, toward the crust two inches off and the thread at his lip, and got none of it.

Out in \place{the Clay Yard} the afternoon stood in the dry cold and the yard went around its seven walked-around places with a look and a wide step and no number. It went widest around the returning-line place, where a hand had gone in on Seed 15 and had not come out, so wide that her elbow cut the corner and the basket canted the other way to balance it and was righted without a look down. Once, and not twice. A second going-around would have made a figure of once, and a figure wants a payer standing under it and none stood. The keeping cost a step each time and nobody entered the steps and he did not enter them either.

The clean dark line at \place{the Vail corner} kept its finer grey hairline called \lore{the thaw}, unswept and thickened in the month's dry cold with the faint sour tang of \lore{Ash} coming up off it where no broom had crossed in two years. Beneath \place{the lowest rack} the broken birth-slates lay and were entered by nobody. At \place{the rack end} the round ring where the shallow pan once stood kept no scrape, no crumb, no print, no chip and no word. The gone white frost off the north lip of \place{the yard well}, the skin drawn edge to edge across \place{the trough}, and the silt-clouded plate of ice on \place{the flat stone by the well} set a little out of the path of feet stayed three coverings entered nowhere, with a finger between every pair. Out on \place{the worked strips} the field kept its empty place and did not fill it. And \place{the river} kept \lore{the winter skin} on its own place beside water over water, found where put on the first day of the month and found again this afternoon and not made again, not spent on the week's work or on the going of the light and not made to buy a lesson under any roof. No hand had laid the skin, no payer stood under it and no source stood to be asked. A sign points to a payer; a covering left where it is is weather and not a promise.

Through \place{the finger's-width door} the river went on with its note of \lore{water over water}, a low thing over a lower thing, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming, muffled under its covering as a voice is muffled by a shut door. He had long since learned to keep the covering on a place of its own beside the note and to put a finger between the two, so that no row came out even by his doing. A covering is not a hearing and a note is not a witness, and there was nobody standing in the doorway to be asked which one had paid for the other.

At \place{the kindling} the account stood in its old exactness. Six pieces lay in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check, and the seventh lay at the lip beyond it beside \person{Garl Vail}'s unforged collector sentence, with no name on the man who turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on the debt. The small knife lay at the very edge of its sill. \person{Tarn} passed his hand over blade and sill and away with no breath held at the top of the reach, and the coming away was the whole of the checking.

“Six counted. The next I don't count,” he told the wood.

He cut nothing, counted nothing, spent nothing. The Ash 14 held breath was where it had been put and the Bone 26 thumb's resting was where it had been put, and he made neither again; they stood beside the five held objects and the pot-crack resting with a finger between every pair, not a sixth held object, not a seventh of anything, not a tenth stopping. Then he brought the ear-sentence up to the word that would call the loan and stopped there, as a remembered ninth stopping with no tenth before the seventh year now at its very end. The Seed 16 breath stayed unspent at the top of the reach and the loan stayed uncalled. Two ears, two silences, and a loan that can hear and has never been called, with nobody standing to enter it. A term ending was not a payment, a forgiveness or a freedom. A man with no house stood where the road ran, and the fear that he would be entered again for vagrancy stayed his, kept without a figure and without asking anybody to carry it.

His chest had been tight since the dark. The Ash was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line, the pale clip-ring at his left wrist caught what little light there was and gave it back, and his stroke went over the shallow bright patch of \place{the flagstones} without the half-check. He did not look along \place{the clay road}. The fourth direction over the boards called no loan and supplied no tenth stopping, and nothing in the afternoon's yard came under the roof as a sign or a payer.

\person{Jorra Vail} came through the finger's-width door off \place{the Vail wheel-shed} with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bent little finger and white in a fist-crease where a hand had opened, and it was not the bone-coloured \lore{slip} of the wheel. Three or more men unnamed had gone low into \place{the back room} before first grey and he gave the house no name for them and no word that the room was cold. He kept his dusty forearms and his clean ones on one tray and parted them as clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had been broken nor whether it had been a birth-slate, while the broken ones under the lowest rack stayed entered by nobody. He spoke to the wheel before he spoke to any person.

“It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady,” he said to the wheel.

“You'll do,” he told a pot going past on a bench, in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either. He knew the \lore{full note} that pot would give three turns before it left his hands, with air under it and a want in it, and he withheld the knowing from any master come up from \place{High Kiln} who would call it early and sing a pot before its time. The boy kept that knowing on a tray of its own beside the hearings and never among them. A pot's note known before its time was not a hearing and no source stood under it.

When Sella had not finished telling him the fire was low, his bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper} ahead of her sentence, took the cold out of the fingertip, moved the tongue a hair's breadth and was left standing so that the end of her instruction arrived at the pot and the household straightened around the waiting as cloth straightens when a weight is lifted. One piece of iron had two bites, the holding and the taking, and no \lore{Grathic} word covered both mercies. The finger came out without leaving the extra holding of Ash 26 a second time; that holding had been gathered once and stood where nobody entered it. Neither looked at the other and neither said what the iron had been asked to carry, because a thing said aloud becomes a thing the Court can enter.

\scenebreak

*He's not Vail* stayed sealed as a lid nobody had tried to lift in three years. The promise of the boy's name to the men who burn slates was neither given nor withdrawn, gathering interest against \lore{the burned-slate word} at five, the shut-room tellings at two, the row at five askings with no witness, the Ash 5 joining of the slate to \lore{the counting room} shut in the war, the Ash 17 direction, the Ash 23 direction and the fourth direction of the day before --- all of them standing where put, none repeating, none buying a lesson under this roof. No collector had come up or gone down the clay road on any of the three days, and the tile debt against a firing of tiles circled without a name and was worse for being uncalled.

At the going of the light Sella carried him up the nineteen steps under the roof and never into \place{the Debt Loft}, set him on \place{the bed} at the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf}, said nothing of where they had been, put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face and added no second fold to the empty place. The thread stayed frozen and unwiped and the wool was found where put again and was not made again. His hand went toward the crust two inches off and came back inside the wool, and the refusal was not spoken because there was nobody to speak it to.

Under the roof she went to \place{the hearth} before the basket, as she had at both ends of every day for three years. She told the coals it was the thaw and turned them with the back of a broken pot.

“The house will keep it,” she said to the coals, where another woman would have said I. She stood a moment on a small intake, a hold and a letting-go toward no opening, and figured neither of the two gaps. Her finger lay along the top of the loaf shelf so the charcoal tally beneath stayed unread. Her lips moved along a two-gapped row of the shelf's front edge and gave neither gap a number. She glanced once at \place{the clay jar} standing cold under \lore{lead glaze} and shut her hand half over nothing, the little-door burn on the back of her left hand closing on air; the cord of her \lore{widow's mark} hung oiled where a working thumb had touched it ten thousand times, and she did not touch it now, for a going of the light and a bed are no openings and seven openings in seven months are seven and not a rule.

On \place{the pot board} the twice-mended pot stood with the hairline of the original break showing between the old-brass staple and the tighter wire. She lifted it thumb-first where the wire crossed the crack, the thumb reaching the fault before the weight came on the handle.

“Leave the crack showing,” she told the pot. “People trust a thing they can see has been handled.”

“If it's worth mending it's worth mending twice. The second time is the real one,” she told the fire. The proverbs had been improved in the keeping and were very likely not as good as the man had thought them when he first said them, and they went to the pot and the fire and never to a person. Repair stayed apart from restoration: a restored thing hides the hand that handled it and a mended thing leaves that hand showing. The dressed grain she turned under wool with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child, turning it once and lifting no figure and no handful. Then she set the pot rim two inches beyond the reaching hand, with a half-beat and no look toward the bed. Inside the wool his hand went toward the rim and stopped where the half-beat had stopped it, and that interval was bought at nothing and was not a gift. Under the floor \lore{the carried fire} gave four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer without a name for three hundred and eighty-three days. Nobody lifted the floor to supply the pull and nobody named the payer.

In the small hours the \place{the towel} came out at the head of the bed as instruments come out when a case is done and before the next is called, with every gap left gap. Three hundred and forty-one honest answers stood on the sixth place and the three hundred and forty-second was to come. The five held objects stood with the pot-crack resting and the hand-at-the-sill breath and the holding in the damper beside them, each on a place of its own with a finger between every pair, and the keeping at the fold stood beside all of them and was expressly not a ninth held object. It had been found where put on the mornings and at the goings of the light of Stone 22, 23 and 24 --- a seventh, an eighth and a ninth finding --- and nine findings from a hand that only read had added no ninth placing to the first. A second placing would make a habit and a third a way, and both want a payer where none stands. No name had been spoken over the place, no third crust stood against the two gaps to make a row come out even, and the far-off finger that would one day lay shelf and fold together and call the joining an account was still far off, was not his, and had not come.

Nine record-first hands kept no tenth; nine stoppings kept no tenth before the seventh year at its very end; the burned-slate word stood at five, the shut-room tellings at two, the row at five askings with no witness and expressly no sixth, the well-saying at four shapings with no fifth. Four rooms kept the same gap in their count --- the birth room in House Silt, the Vail kiln-house, the room where the slate was read on market-day and the Drawing house seen two doors down --- held apart with a finger between every pair, four likenesses and not one account, an account wanting a hand to lay them together and a payer under all four, and no such hand standing and no such payer. The fourth direction of Stone 23 stood on its own place beside the Ash 5 joining and the Ash 17 and Ash 23 directions, with a finger between every pair. No sixth hearing, seventh opening, sixth held object, sixth telling, third shut-room telling, sixth asking, fifth shaping, tenth hand, tenth stopping or twelfth arithmetic was supplied to make a row come out even, for a finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged. The finding that eleven are not one account sat on a place of its own with a finger between it and every other kept thing, and the keeping at the fold and the fourth direction sat beside all of these and were not laid across any of them.

Eleven arithmetics stood behind one unsigned figure: the three of \place{Frost}, the first, second and third of \place{Bone}, the first, second and third of Ash, Stone's first and Stone's second. The figure had been read and was still not the figure paid; no signature stood on the space between, and no doctrine had come out of eleven. Their eleven true subtractions rode locked in \person{Berr Clay}'s box up the clay road toward High Kiln, the key gone up in a coat pocket that did not come back down and the boy having no key. Six hearings of the full note stood beside and never among, the sixth the small flat knock out of \person[Dessa Silt] as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go and never among the sources; the seventh place stayed empty and was not dug for. \lore[The Hearth Drawing]{The Drawing}'s small flat knock out of the neighbour on the pallet stayed on its own place outside them, not a seventh and not a filling of the empty place, for a hearing wants a source standing and that room had been seen from a basket and not entered. The heard bell of \lore{the Long Night} stayed in its square beside the told, four, four and a gap, and was not folded into any of the counts.

The surgeon took his small clean satisfaction in a field taken as found and left as found, and it cost him nothing, which was how he knew it was not peace. Under it, louder, the infant wanted the crust lifted two inches, the thread wiped and a hand on the pot handle. Both were true. The second was louder. Neither won. The quarrel was the work and he had stopped looking for a verdict in it, which was not the same as having found one. \place{Stone} kept its twenty-second, twenty-third and twenty-fourth days without a boundary drawn, the deep-winter month holding both its names, older women by weather and slatemen by Account-days, neither set right nor left in so many words. The count stood at three hundred and eighty-three exact, six months and two hundred and three days told by nobody who would have known what a number counted, the carried fire's fifth unpaid the same number and Garl's name unspoken the same number. The wool lay folded once in the crust's empty place beside the crust gone harder and browner in the dry cold, its corner a hair off the wool, with no second fold, no third crust and no name spoken over the place, and the thread was frozen and unwiped. Eleven arithmetics stood behind one unsigned figure with no signature on the space and no doctrine out of eleven, and the keeping at the fold was not one of them and had not been laid across any of them. \lore{The Five} stood beyond, and the year's last reading stood six mornings beyond the week and was not brought into a day that had not earned it.

Through the finger's-width door the river kept its low note under its winter skin. The hand outside the wool came up an inch toward it, as a hand goes up in a field it has learned not to disturb. It did not reach, and the inch was not a reach. He took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole on the note's own finishing under the winter skin, let the intake go as the note ended and came straight down and laid itself flat against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. The note had done before the hand came down, so the letting-go was neither early nor stayed, and the hand did not stay.

He filed it as answer three hundred and forty-two: exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the eleven readings, the bell's gap, the flat knock, the child's grey fingertips, the mother's cup, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the held breath, the two bites of iron, the river's skin, the widow's mark, the fourth direction nor the keeping at the fold. The surgeon's exactness was not comfort. The infant wanted the crust lifted, the thread wiped and a hand on the pot handle, and got none of the three. The hand lay flat on the fold with the crust's corner standing a hair off the wool, and the count went on under the roof in Low Kiln, Sill, Grath.
