## Chronicle

Three days the house under the Vail roof kept its shape, and the boy kept a count no roof could read. Stone twenty-two, twenty-three and twenty-four came without a boundary drawn, the last full working week of the year. Each morning the surgeon took the body in its old order: barley crust two inches from the ear, corner a hair off the wool; wool folded in the empty place where a hand put it on the fifteenth; thread frozen and unwiped; cold stopping at cheek, temple and bare wrist-joint; the afterdrop waiting past a warming nobody would perform. The infant wanted the crust lifted, the thread wiped, a hand on the pot handle. Both true, the second louder, neither winning; the quarrel was the work.

On the twenty-second the fold was found where put a seventh time and still not placed. Sella Vail went to the hearth before the basket, told the coals it was the thaw, said the house would keep it where another woman would say I, carried him down the nineteen steps, and set the pot rim two inches beyond his reach with a half-beat. Ilva at the well answered: Ask the pot what it cost. I keep the count, not the account — ninth record-first hand, no tenth. Tarn told the wood Six counted. The next I don't count; Jorra's bent finger went into the cold damper ahead of an unfinished sentence. Answers 337 and 338; the count stood at 381.

On the twenty-third the fold was found an eighth time. Hessa Rale went up the clay road before noon with Berr Clay's narrow slate edge-on and the writing turned to her own sleeve, telling the woman who asked that she carried the slate and did not read it. In the afternoon a voice with nobody standing under it came over the boards from Slate Row: a room shut against the cold, a slate burned so a count would not go up, the cold and the count gone out through the same gap. The boy opened it as a wound and filed it beside the older joinings — a fourth direction, not a sixth telling, not a witness, not a rule — gathering toward a hand far off and not his. Answers 339 and 340; the count stood at 382.

On the twenty-fourth the fold was found a ninth time, and nine findings added no ninth placing. The yard went around its seven places, widest around the returning-line place, once and not twice; Tarn's ear-sentence stopped at the calling word, the seventh year at its end; Jorra's finger came out of the iron without the extra holding. Answers 341 and 342; the count stood at 383 exact, six months and two hundred and three days told by nobody, the fire's fifth unpaid and Garl's name unspoken the same number. The surgeon's satisfaction in a field taken as found cost nothing, which was how he knew it was not peace.

- **Where everyone is at the close.** Sella Vail — under the roof, having carried him up the nineteen steps at the going of the light, set him on the bed, put the fold at his ear and under his chin with no look at his face and no second fold, renewed the hearth grammar and left the twice-mended pot, dressed grain and unread charcoal as found; wants the house kept by not-doings and gives no instruction to the basket. Berr Clay — offstage, gone up toward High Kiln with the eleven true subtractions locked in his box, the key gone up in a coat pocket that does not come back down.
