The afternoon came on as a field taken at rest, and a field taken at rest is read by its small sounds first, because the small sounds do not lie and the large ones are mostly weather. \person[Corin]{The boy} was carried down the nineteen steps at the half-pace \person{Sella Vail} keeps from every open slate, the tilt corrected at each turn of the landing before it had time to settle into the wicker, the instruction going to the wool and the river wind and the fire she would bank against their coming back and never to the basket. She set \place{the basket} level out of the draught at the edge of \place{the Clay Yard}, where the heat would come to it sideways, and went about her work without a look toward it. No runner came up \place{the clay road} that afternoon; no collector went up it or down it; there was no market, no Cross and no bell. Nothing from the eleventh reading, nothing from the keeping at the fold, nothing from the road taken before noon crossed into the afternoon as sign, lesson, mending, cause, payment or payer. A house keeps its days in loaves and glances and in the space where a loaf has stood; a yard keeps its work in hands and weights and weather; and the two keepings did not meet and were not one, because a figure raised between them would want a payer standing under it and none stood.

\place{The boards} went on behind him the way boards go on, talk arriving along their length and going off it without a taker. A woman set a pot down and told the pot what it had cost rather than telling anybody standing near. Two men came up off the clay road with a number held shut between them and let it out only as far as the weather. The price of barley went over the boards on the same breath as everything else, and after the price of barley came the weather, and after the weather came nothing at all. It was the grammar of the district and had been for as long as anyone under the fold could remember: nothing in it required a taker, and nothing in it supplied one. The boy took it in as a surgeon takes a field before the light, by the part that can be checked first, and left the rest lying where it lay.

The yard went around its seven walked-around places with a look and a wide step and no number. At \place{the Vail corner} the clean dark line kept its finer grey hairline beside it, the hairline called \lore{the thaw}, unswept and thickened in the dry cold with the faint sour tang of \place{Ash} coming up off it where no broom had crossed in two years. Under \place{the lowest rack} the broken birth-slates lay and were entered by nobody. At \place{the rack end} the round ring where the shallow clay pan had once stood kept its emptiness without a scrape, a crumb, a print, a chip or a word. Out on the worked strips the field's empty place lay where it had lain since the hands came in and one hand did not come back with the rest. \place{The trough} held its skin, and the silt-clouded plate of ice stood on \place[the flat stone by the well]{its flat stone} a little out of the path of feet. Widest of all was the returning-line place, gone around so wide that the elbow cut the corner and the basket tilted the other way to balance it, righted without looking down. Once and not twice. A second going-around would have made a figure of once, and a figure wants a payer standing under it and none stood, so the keeping cost one step and no more and nobody entered the step and he did not enter it either.

The three coverings stayed weather entered nowhere, with a finger between every pair: the white frost gone off the north lip of \place{the yard well}, the skin drawn edge to edge across the trough, the clouded plate lifted up and set where no foot would go. \place{The river} kept \lore{water over water} below them, a low thing over a lower thing, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming, and over both, bank to bank since the afternoon of \place{Stone} 1, lay \lore{the winter skin}, found where put and not made again, not spent on the week's work or the going of the light and not made to buy a lesson under any roof. No hand had laid the skin, no payer stood under it, no source stood to be asked. A sign points to a payer; a covering left where it is is weather and not a promise. He kept the covering on its own place beside the note and put a finger between the two so that no row came out even by his doing.

\scenebreak

It was into this ordinary going-on, with the boards already talking and the yard going around its places, that the word came.

It came up from the direction of \place{Slate Row}, which the yard spoke of as a direction and not as a place anybody meant to go that afternoon. There was no owner standing under it. It went along the boards where the neighbours stood with their baskets, spoken over the boards and not into any basket, on the same breath as the price of barley and the weather behind that. A voice with nobody to it said that a room had been shut against the cold, and that a slate had been burned so a count would not go up, and that the cold and the count had gone out together through the same gap. It named no room. It put no hand at any flame. It gave no figure, no date, and no name to the man it would have been about, and there was nobody standing in any doorway to be asked. It was a direction and not a visit. It went on to the price of barley as a man goes on to the price of barley when he has said something else entirely, and then to the weather, and then it went off up the yard and was gone while the work went on around it. Nobody leaned in to take the answer down. Nobody turned twice.

The boy opened it as a surgeon opens a wound, by what was there and by what was not. What was there: a room shut, a slate burned, a count kept down, a gap that let cold and figure out by the same opening. What was not: a witness standing somewhere and able to be asked, a room named, a hand at the flame, a date, a figure, a payer, a name under it. He laid what was there beside what was not and found no seam between them that any clerk could close. The voice had arrived from a quarter league off and had not lowered itself for the child in the basket; a word spoken over a thing is not a word spoken to it. It had said a shape of things and not a fact about them. He set the shape on a place of its own beside the Ash 5 joining of \lore{the burned-slate word} to \lore{the counting room} shut in the war, and beside the Ash 17 direction from Slate Row, and beside the Ash 23 direction one shape further, and put a finger between it and each of the three, so that the four should not touch and make a row come out even.

It was a fourth direction and he would not let it stand up as anything more. It was expressly not a sixth telling of the burned slate, which stood at five with a finger between the fifth and the four. It was not a third telling of the shut room, which stood at two. It was not a fifth shaping of the Frost 14 well-saying, which stood at four, and not a sixth asking of the row, which stood at five with no witness. It was not a hearing of \lore{the full note}; the six hearings stood beside and never among and the seventh place stayed empty and was not dug for. It was not a seventh of anything, not a witness, not a cause, not a confession, not a reader, not a joining to the promise neither given nor withdrawn, and not a joining to the keeping at the fold, which lay where it had been put and had gathered no hand to place it a second time. It was not a payment and not a rule, for a rule is an account that has found a payer and none stood to this. He made nothing of its being there and looked for no fifth beside it, because a finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged.

The shape went on gathering interest as dust gathers in a corner nobody sweeps, and a corner is kept by nobody's hand all the same. It gathered toward the far-off hand that would one day lay the district's private keepings together and call the joining an account; that hand was not his and had not come. An account wants a hand to lay the places together and a payer standing under all of them, and no hand stood and no payer stood. The yard's barley price went on over the boards as though nothing had been said into it, which was the truth of the matter, and the boards lay where they had laid, four nails on the near one, four on the next, and a thumb-width knot where a fifth might have been driven and was not.

\scenebreak

At \place{the kindling} the account stood in its old exactness. Six pieces lay in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check them; the seventh lay at the lip beyond it, beside \person{Garl Vail}'s unforged collector sentence, with no name on the man who had turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on the debt. The small knife lay at the very edge of its sill, where a hand could be checked on it and where a hand could also slip it off. \person{Tarn} passed his hand over blade and sill as a man passes over a tool put back where it belongs, and the hand went up and came away with no breath held at the top of the reach. The coming away was the whole of the checking.

“Six counted. The next I don't count,” he told the wood, and cut nothing, counted nothing, spent nothing.

The Ash 14 held breath and the Bone 26 thumb's resting were found where they had been put and were not made again, each on a place of its own beside the five held objects and the pot-crack resting, with a finger between every pair; not a sixth held object, not a seventh, not a tenth stopping, and with no payer standing under either. His chest had been tight since the dark. The Ash was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line at the corner, and the pale clip-ring at his left wrist caught what little light there was and gave it back. His stroke went over the shallow bright patch of \place{the flagstones} without the half-check that had once stood there and had gone back to where it belonged. He brought the ear-sentence up to the word that would call the loan and stopped there, as a remembered ninth stopping with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end, the Seed 16 breath unspent at the top of the reach and the loan uncalled. He did not look along the road. Two ears, two silences, a loan that can hear and has never been called, with nobody standing to enter it. A term ending is not a payment, a forgiveness or a freedom; a man with no house stands where the road runs, and the fear that he will be entered again for vagrancy stays his, kept without a figure and without asking anybody to carry it. The fourth direction over the boards called no loan and supplied no tenth stopping, and the boy did not carry it down to the sill.

The day's hunts and repairs went by as directions judged by the carrying arm and the weather, never as figures. A man come down from \place{Cold Acre} shifted a hare across his shoulders and, to a woman who asked what the carrying had cost, said, “Ask the weather. It carried the snow. It can carry the price,” and went off toward the clay road with the hare unweighed and nobody opening a slate. Another with a cracked jar in a rope sling asked what the month's mending would want and was told to ask the pot; the pot, being a pot, said nothing. A woman with a basket of kindling asked what the seventh year would want when it came, and two men mending a strip of fence said the thaw would be early and the thaw would be late. To each of them Tarn said, “Yes, house,” and kept the late, because a late thaw puts off the paying. He agreed so thoroughly that there was nowhere for an argument to find a foot. No sixth asking of the row came over the boards that afternoon, no tenth record-first hand stood up in the empty place, and the fourth direction stood where it had been put and did not repeat and bought no lesson.

\person{Jorra Vail} came through \place{the finger's-width door} off \place{the Vail wheel-shed} with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bent little finger and white in a fist-crease where a hand had opened, and it was not the bone-coloured \lore{slip} of the wheel. Three or more men unnamed had gone low into \place{the back room} before first grey; he gave the house no name for them and said nothing about the room being cold. He kept his dusty forearms and his clean ones on one tray and parted them as clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had been broken nor whether it had been a birth-slate, while the broken ones under the lowest rack stayed entered by nobody.

He spoke to the wheel before he spoke to any person. “It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady,” he said to it, and told a pot going past on a bench, “You'll do,” in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either. He knew the full note that pot would give three turns before it left his hands, with air under it and a want in it, and withheld the knowing from any master come up from \place{High Kiln} who would call it early and sing a pot before its time. The boy kept that knowing on a tray of its own beside the hearings and never among them; a pot's note known before its time is not a hearing, and no source stands under it. The burned slate stood at five and was not made six by a voice over the boards; the fourth direction stood on its own place beside the three older ones and was not joined to the promise or laid across the dust.

When Sella had not finished telling him the fire was low, his bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper} ahead of her sentence, took the cold out of the fingertip, moved the tongue a hair's breadth and was left standing, so that the end of her instruction arrived at the pot and the household straightened around the waiting as cloth straightens when a weight is lifted. One piece of iron had two bites, the holding and the taking, and there was no \lore{Grathic} word covering both mercies. He did not look at her and she did not look at him; neither said what the iron had been asked to carry, because a thing said aloud becomes a thing the Court can enter. Presently he took the finger out. He did not leave the extra holding of Ash 26 a second time, for that had been gathered once and stayed where nobody would enter it, found where put and not made again. \emph{He's not Vail} stayed sealed as a lid nobody had tried to lift in three years, and the promise of the boy's name to the men who burn slates was neither given nor withdrawn. The claim went on gathering interest against that ungiven promise, and neither the voice over the boards nor the boy's filing of it paid the one or refused the other, which in this district is a way of keeping a thing alive.

He grew quieter as the afternoon went on, the way he did when a direction went around the boards, and the household straightened around the quiet without an order from anybody. Sella gave the fire its instruction and Tarn gave the broom its, and the two went on as if the quiet were weather they had agreed to work in. The boy did not take the quiet for an answer. A silence that follows a saying is a silence and not a witness; the gap between the voice and the weather's answer to it had been kept empty and was not filled to make a row come out even.

\scenebreak

At the going of the light Sella carried him up the nineteen steps and under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof, never into \place{the Debt Loft}, and set him on \place{the bed} at the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf}. She put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face, and added no second fold to the crust's empty place. The thread at his lip had frozen in the dry heat and was left unwiped, because nobody under any roof has ever been given an instruction about the thread; the wool lay folded once where it had been put on the fifteenth, found where put again and not made again. She said nothing of where they had been and nothing over the keeping, and her hand did only what the setting needed before it went away.

The hearth grammar was renewed at both ends of the day as it had been for three years. She came to \place{the hearth} before the basket, told the coals it was the thaw and turned them with the back of a broken pot, said, “The house will keep it,” where another woman would have said I, and stood a moment on a small intake, a hold and a letting-go toward no opening, figuring neither of the two gaps. Her finger lay along the top of the loaf shelf so the charcoal tally beneath stayed unread; her lips moved along a two-gapped row and gave neither gap a number. She glanced once at \place{the clay jar} standing cold under \lore{lead glaze} and shut her hand half over nothing, the little-door burn on the back of her left hand closing on air. On \place{the pot board} the twice-mended pot was lifted thumb-first where the wire crossed the crack, the hairline of the original break showing between the old-brass staple and the tighter wire. “Leave the crack showing. People trust a thing they can see has been handled,” she told the pot. “If it's worth mending it's worth mending twice. The second time is the real one,” she told the fire; the sayings went to the pot and the fire and never to a person. Repair stayed apart from restoration in her hands. The dressed grain was turned under wool with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child, with no figure lifted and no handful. \lore{The widow's mark} hung untouched at her throat where a working thumb had oiled the cord ten thousand times, because a hearth and a going of the light are not openings, seven openings in seven months are seven and not a rule, and no eighth was made due. Under the floor \lore{the carried fire} gave four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer without a name for three hundred and eighty-two days, and nobody lifted the floor to supply the pull.

In the small hours the file came out on \place{the towel} at the head of the bed as instruments come out when a case is done and before the next is called, with every gap left gap. Three hundred and thirty-nine honest answers stood on the sixth place and the three hundred and fortieth was to come. The five held objects stood with the pot-crack resting beside them, and the hand-at-the-sill breath of Ash 14 and the holding in the damper of Ash 26 stood beside those, each on a place of its own with a finger between every pair. The keeping at the fold stood beside the crust's gap, the two gaps in the shelf in the room whose floor does not touch this one, the gone pan's round ring and the field's empty place, and was not laid across any of them and was not a ninth held object. The four rooms with the same gap were held apart with a finger between every pair, four likenesses and not one account, for an account wants a hand to lay them together and a payer under all four and none stood. Nine record-first hands stood with no tenth, and nine stoppings stood with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end; the eighth and the ninth stayed where they had been put and neither stood up in the empty place to call itself a rule. Eleven arithmetics stood behind one unsigned figure, the figure read still not the figure paid, no signature on the space and no doctrine out of eleven, and the finding that eleven are not one account sat on a place of its own with a finger between it and everything else. Six hearings of the full note stood beside and never among, the sixth the small flat knock out of \person[Dessa Silt] as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go and never among the sources; the seventh place stayed empty and was not dug for, and \lore{the Long Night}'s heard bell stayed in its square beside the told, four, four and a gap, not folded into any of the counts. The fourth direction stood on its own place beside the Ash 5 joining and the Ash 17 and Ash 23 directions, with a finger between it and each of the three. Nothing had moved. No sixth hearing, seventh opening, sixth held object, tenth hand, tenth stopping, twelfth arithmetic, witness, reader or payment had been supplied to make a row come out even, and the year's last reading stood beyond the week and was not brought into a day that had not earned it.

Through the finger's-width door the river kept its note of water over water under its winter skin, muffled as a voice is muffled by a shut door, found where put and not spent on the day's work or the going of the light. The hand outside the wool came up an inch toward the note, as a hand goes up in a field it has learned not to disturb. It did not reach, and the inch was not a reach. He took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole on the note's own finishing under the winter skin, let the intake go as the note ended and came straight down and laid itself flat against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. The note had done before the hand came down, so the letting-go was neither early nor stayed, and the hand did not stay.

He filed it as answer three hundred and forty: exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the eleven readings, the bell's gap, the flat knock, the child's grey fingertips, the mother's cup, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the held breath, the two bites of iron, the river's skin, the widow's mark, the fourth direction, the keeping at the fold nor his own kept gaps. The count stood at three hundred and eighty-two exact, six months and two hundred and two days told by nobody who would have known what a number counted, the carried fire's fifth unpaid the same number and Garl's name unspoken the same number. The twenty-third came on without a boundary drawn, the deep-winter month holding both its names, older women by weather and slatemen by Account-days, neither set right nor left in so many words. The surgeon took his small clean satisfaction in a field found as left and left as found, and the infant underneath took none of it and went on wanting the crust lifted, the thread wiped and a hand on the pot handle, both true, the second louder, neither winning, the two registers quarrelling without verdict in the dark. The hand lay flat on the fold on the three hundred and fortieth answer, with the fourth direction standing on its own place beside the three older ones and nobody standing anywhere to be asked. The count went on under the roof in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill}, \place{Grath}.
