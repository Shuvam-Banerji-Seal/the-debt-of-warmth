\daymark{264}{Smoke 25, Fifthday, Y.H. 813}

\dropcap{T}{he hand} lay against the fold where the hundred and second answer had put it down, and the room under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof took it back the way a bench takes back a tool at the end of a shift. \person[Corin]{The boy} had not asked it to remember what it had done. An inch was an inch, a low note was a low note, and the two intervals he had laid apart on the sixth place were two objects and not one likeness; he put the hand away with the same hand that had put away the others, where the next hand would find it without looking. No reach in it, no hearing, no gift, no rule for a morning to obey. Two hundred and sixty-three had stood exact at the going of the dark. In the dark it became two hundred and sixty-four; six months and eighty-three days became six months and eighty-four; and nobody under any roof in \place{Sarn}, \place{Sill} or \place{Grath} said so, or knew there was a number to say. He had stopped expecting anybody to tell him, which was not the same as not wanting it; the wanting went on under the expectation the way a coal goes on under ash, giving out nothing and going out not at all. Under the floor \lore{the carried fire} went on giving its fifth unpaid day to the payer it had given all the others, a payer without a name for two hundred and sixty-four days. Smoke 25 would earn its own morning out of its own light, when there was light enough to earn it; Smoke 30, the third and last reading of the second firing, stood five mornings ahead and was not reached for. There would be no market, no \place[the Cross]{Cross} and no bell on a Fifthday, and the district's two names for the months were not set right or left wrong.

The first smell was hot clay under tallow, the dry smell of a kiln fed and left to work. Under it came wet wool, sour with the long lodging of a body carried in it since before first grey. Under that, finer and more persistent, came the sour mineral breath of the unswept corner the house called \lore{the thaw} --- \lore{Ash} so long in the air that it had ceased to be an event and become a condition of the room, the way cold in a stone is not noticed until a hand goes to it. The first sound came up through \place{the kiln mouth} behind the wall: the kiln drawing in fours, four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood. It was not a bell. Nothing in this house struck four, four and a gap and called it a summons; the heard bell stayed where it had sounded, beside the told bell, and was not folded into the morning's work. Told figure stood beside paid figure, with Smoke's second arithmetic gone up the road in a locked box and unread under this roof.

Through the finger's-width door the river kept its note, \lore{water over water}, a low thing over a lower thing, going on while he listened and going on when he stopped. Above him \place{the boards} kept four nails on the near board, four on the next, and a thumb-width knot where the fifth nail should have stood. Three counts in three places. The kiln's four low pulls and the silence under the floor; the river's water over water; the boards' four nails, four and a knot. None rose into another, because agreeing any two would want a payer put under both and no payer stood to any. Six hearings of \lore{the full note} stood beside and never among, five from four sources and the sixth, the small flat knock out of a woman as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go; the seventh place was left empty and none was invented to fill it, for a finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged. The boy kept the three counts as he kept everything else the house could not use: exact, complete, and worth nothing at two inches of air.

He took the body in the order the facts presented themselves, as a surgeon takes a field. One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from his ear, gone past the point where a child would have eaten it, and the empty place beside it kept its name to itself; no third had come in the night to make the row come out even. The thread of drool at his mouth had frozen in the dry heat and was unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread, and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. Cold had come to cheek and temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, and had stopped there as though a line had been written out for it to that length and no farther; it neither went forward nor went away. The afterdrop waited beyond all three, past a warming nobody in this house would perform. He knew the composed face that is not composure. He knew a cold patient cannot clot and will seem better before he is worse, and that a man must be warmed from the outside and slowly. He wanted the crust lifted the two inches to his mouth. Both were true and the second was louder in a body of two hundred and sixty-four days; the surgeon entered none of it, not even on the place where he kept the things he did not enter.

\scenebreak

Before first grey \person{Sella Vail} went to \place{the hearth} before the basket. She told the coals it was the thaw and turned them with the back of a broken pot, and said \emph{The house will keep it} where another woman would have said \emph{I}. She stood a moment with the back of the pot in her hand, counting under her breath toward no opening --- the small intake, the hold, the letting-go --- and pushed coal-black hair back with the wrist that carries the burn, so that the one grey streak at her temple showed pale off the coals. The burn was shaped like a little door closed on nothing. She glanced once at the clay jar standing cold under its \lore{lead glaze} and shut her hand half over nothing. \lore{The widow's mark} hung where it hung, touched once before the kiln on Smoke 1 and at no shelf, well, rack, road, basket, morning or dusk on Smoke 25, because a morning and a hearth are not openings. The one opening before Smoke 1 lay behind the household, made into no lesson, no mending, no sign and no payment; the next real opening stood far off in the first firing of Frost and was not brought into a day that had not earned its own light.

At \place{the loaf shelf} she kept her finger on top of the front edge, so the charcoal tally on the underside stayed unread; she had not looked beneath it in three years and was not going to begin on a Fifthday. Her lips moved along a row standing with two gaps, and neither gap was given a figure. Then she set the pot rim out, its handle two inches beyond the reaching hand, with a half-beat at the setting and no look toward \place{the bed}. The half-beat went where the look would have gone. No instruction came to the basket at any hour; the instruction went to the fire, the wool, the pot and the water. The boy, who had once reduced a thousand shoulders in the dark and could not close two inches on a rim, lay under the low angle of the shelf and let the distance be a distance. The shelf's two gaps and the fold's one crust with its empty place beside it stood on trays that did not touch; nobody had asked him to make them agree, and no payer stood to do it.

Out in \place{the Clay Yard}, at \place{the drying racks}, the dressed grain lay covered under wool on the cool stone at \place{the rack end}, away from the hearth. She lifted a corner for damp, felt what the air under the wool had to say, and laid the corner back smooth, changing nothing and gaining nothing; no figure went onto it and no word was spoken over it. The arithmetic that would be read over it on Smoke 30 would know the cover and not the grain, and would do its work perfectly, which was precisely why it would not know what the grain had cost. The space between the figure and its price stayed unsigned. The boy watched the laying-back with a surgeon's attention to a thing handled for its own good and entered nowhere, and kept the watching where he kept the rest, without making a finding of it yet and without laying it across the house's other not-doings, for a joining would want a payer and none stood.

At \place{the yard well} the white that frost had written on the north stone lip was grey now under the bucket's knock, and the yard called the whole of it weather. He kept whatever white had been there on a place of its own with no cause attached: not a payer, not the widow's mark, for a well is not an opening, and not the first firing of Frost, which stood far off. A neighbour with a pot hitched against her hip asked what the keeping and the storing would take now the grain was under wool and the visits coming. Sella did not answer from herself; she sent the question where it belonged, with \emph{Ask the pot what a wet thing costs set down there. The house has said it}. The neighbour went off level with nothing slopped, nothing priced, nothing refused and nothing entered. A man come down \place{the clay road} with dried pale scurf of mud on his coat skirt brought a number up out of a fist toward the well, his thumb tucked over it as over a thing that might be read, looked at it, and put it back into his sleeve. The figure arrived nowhere. It was not a missing figure and it had not been withheld; it was a chart turned face-down, read as read and entered nowhere. The bucket went on knocking the grey lip, and the rope was cold iron under a hand that had long since stopped noticing that it was cold.

\scenebreak

Back under the roof, in the small hours, he put the question to his hand. There was no ceremony in it and no answer he expected; a man tests a tool before he trusts it. Through the finger's-width door the river went on with its low note; under the floor the fire kept four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood; over his head the boards kept four, four and a knot. No fire-sitter stood over the coals to supply a count and no house count was there to borrow. In the room that does not touch, a count had ended some time before and a little silence stood after it, and on other days he had left that interval whole and empty on a place of its own beside the held one, an object and not a miss, never crossed to make a row come out even.

The hand outside the wool went up an inch toward the low note and, with the inch held for the small intake only, came down on the river's next low note and not on the kiln's gap, borrowing no stroke, borrowing no house count, filling nothing. The little silence after the count in the other room he left where it lay, whole and empty on its own place beside the held one and apart from it, no rhythm carried across and nothing supplied to make a stroke in a row that had none. An empty object laid beside a held one. A keeping and not a miss; not a longer reach, not a hearing of the full note, not a seventh of it, not a stopping, not a payment, not one of the held changes, not a key, a power or a rule. He made nothing of the likeness and would not let the two stand up as a rule between them, because a rule is an account that has found a payer and none stood to either.

It was one hundred and three, filed on the sixth place beside one hundred and two with no payer named, exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air. The hand lay back against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking.

Two hundred and sixty-four stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in Sarn, Sill and Grath; six months and eighty-four days were told by nobody. One hundred and two honest answers had become one hundred and three and none of them was a reach. The carried fire stopped at four with its fifth unpaid two hundred and sixty-four days and its payer unnamed; the widow's mark hung where it hung; the dressed grain lay covered and unfigured with the corner laid back and no figure on it; the pot handle rested two inches beyond the reaching hand with the half-beat gone; the pan's gone ring lay on its own tray, and the ownerless question stood at five askings with no witness and no row named. Smoke went on without a boundary drawn and Smoke 30 stood five mornings ahead and was not reached for, and the first firing of Frost stood far off and unbrought. The count went forward into Smoke 26, a day that had not earned its heading. The file lay open with every tray parted and every gap left gap, and the hand rested against the fold on the hundred and third answer, an object and not a power.
