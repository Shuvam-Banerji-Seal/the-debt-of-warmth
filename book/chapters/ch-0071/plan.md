# Chapter 71 — beat sheet: *What the Grain Does Not Say*

**Frame.** Days 264–266, Smoke 25–27, Fifthday to Seventhday, Y.H. 813. Normal chapter, three lived days, six scenes (two per day), each day opened by its own `\daymark` in the first scene of that day and each lived in full in two scenes of well over eight hundred words. No Account-day, no market, no Cross and no bell in these three days; each day earns its own Smoke by its own light. Smoke 30 (Day 269), the third and last Account-day and market of the second firing, stands three mornings ahead of the close and is not reached for. Frost 1 (Day 270) and its first firing (Day 271) stand far off and unbrought. Close third-person past tense; point of view never leaves Dan's eyes; no present-tense Chicago break, no `\memory{}` tag, no Court, collector, Waiting Room or back-room interior, no recap and no ending.

**Counts carried into the first line.** 263 exact, six months and eighty-three days, told by nobody; one hundred and two honest answers on the sixth place; fire's fifth unpaid 263 days, payer unnamed; six hearings beside and never among with no seventh; three near-full answers; three pot sayings with the premature Smoke 14 note on its own place; burned slate at four; shut-room tellings at two; told bell beside heard, told figure beside paid; nine stoppings with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end and still ungiven; nine record-first hands with Ilva's ninth on its own tray, her strip unread; seventh green a colour and seventh shape a shape with no eighth; five askings of the row question with no witness and no row named; grain covered and unfigured on the cool stone at the rack end; widow's mark touched once before the kiln on Smoke 1 and at no other hour; Smoke's second arithmetic up the clay road in Berr's locked box and unread under this roof; Smoke 30 five mornings ahead of the opening line.

**Count at close.** 266 exact, six months and eighty-six days, told by nobody; one hundred and eight honest answers on the sixth place; fire's fifth unpaid 266 days; all other counts unchanged, except that T101 gains one further shaping of the row question — a report of an asking, not a sixth asking — with the askings still at five and no witness. Answers: **103, 104** on Day 264; **105, 106** on Day 265; **107, 108** on Day 266. Each is an honest coordination of the held inch, an object on the sixth place and not a reach, hearing, seventh, stopping, payment, held change, key, power or rule. On the 108th the hand goes on past the river's next low note to the one after it, holding across two of the river's intervals with the little silence between kept empty and no second house count borrowed for the second; the house's count, the river's two notes and the empty interval stay on three separate places and nothing is supplied to agree any two.

**The chapter's one meaningful change, advanced and not paid.** Two domestic movements and one physical one, all held with no payer and nothing joined: (1) on Smoke 26 the covered grain is taken wholly out of the wool and turned piece by piece in the flat of a hand for the last time before the reading, examined closely and still entered nowhere, which the boy keeps as the finding that a thing can be handled and still carry no figure — not a lesson, a grievance or a doctrine, and not laid across the house's other not-doings or his own kept gaps; (2) the ownerless row question gains a further shaping over the boards, a report of an asking rather than a fresh asking, so that T101 advances while the askings still stand at five with no witness standing and no row named; (3) on the last answer the hand holds across two of the river's intervals without borrowing a second house count, keeping the silence between them empty. No thread is opened, paid or closed; nothing is joined, named, opened, called, spent or ended.

## Scene 3 — The Grain Under the Hand
