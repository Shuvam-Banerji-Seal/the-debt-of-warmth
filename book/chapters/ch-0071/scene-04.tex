In \place{Low Kiln} the afternoon thinned without ending, the way heat goes out of a pot set off the coals and sits a while in the pot before it consents to be gone. \person[Corin]{The boy} felt it come back off the stone and into the wool at his ear, a small giving-back of the morning's warmth that was not the morning and would not last. Nothing in the district drew a line across Smoke and said it ended here. Neither of the town's two names for the months was set right or left wrong; the firing went on because the fire went on, and the day kept its own shape without asking anybody to put a figure on it. Under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof \lore{the carried fire} gave four low pulls beneath the floor and the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer without a name for two hundred and sixty-five days; through the finger's-width door the river kept its note of \lore{water over water}, a low thing over a lower thing; above \place{the bed} \place{the boards} kept four nails, four, and a thumb-width knot. Three counts in three places, and none rose into another, because agreeing any two would want a payer put under both and none stood to either.

He took the body as it came, the way a man takes a field before he cuts into it. One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from his ear; the empty place beside it kept its name to itself and no third had come in the afternoon to make the row come out even. The thread at his mouth had frozen in the dry heat and was unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread, and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. Cold had come to cheek and temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, and had stopped there as at the end of a line written out for it, neither going forward nor going away; the afterdrop waited beyond all three, past a warming nobody in this house would perform. He knew the composed face that is not composure, knew that a cold patient cannot clot and will seem better before he is worse, and that a man must be warmed from the outside and slowly. He wanted the crust lifted the two inches. Both were true and the second was louder in a body of two hundred and sixty-five days; the surgeon entered neither, not even on the place where he kept the things he did not enter.

\scenebreak

\person{Sella Vail} carried him down at the half-pace from every open slate and went around the walked-around places the way water goes around a stone that lies where a stone lies. In \place{the Clay Yard} she gave each a look and a wide step and no number: the clean dark line at the Vail corner, the finer grey beside it called \lore{the thaw}, the space beneath \place{the lowest rack} where the broken birth-slates lay, the round ring where the shallow pan had stood with no scrape or crumb or word to say what had gone, \place{the yard well} with \place{the trough} bare and dark beside it and its water called weather, the field's empty place out on \place{the worked strips}, and the returning-line place where a hand had gone in on Seed 15 and had not come out. Seven in a row were seven and not an account, because an account wants a payer standing under it and none stood. The hand in the returning line was carried and not named, not paid, not joined to the shelf's two gaps in the other room or to the one crust in the fold, and the boy drew no finger between the keepings. \lore{The widow's mark} hung where it hung, touched once before the kiln on Smoke 1 and at no shelf, well, rack, road, basket, yard, afternoon or going of the light on Smoke 25 or 26, because an afternoon and a yard are not openings.

Below the sill a child's foot that had stayed cold after the flood was wrapped in a woman's wool and called the cold by her and weather by the yard. The boy had watched it be wrapped and had watched the yard go on over it, and kept the two names apart without setting either right. The extremity goes before the core; the afterdrop waits past a warming nobody here would perform. There was no word in \lore{Grathic} for the thing the foot was doing, and nobody in the yard had asked for one, so it remained a wool-wrapped foot and the weather, and the surgeon's reading of it worked perfectly and bought nothing. He named no cause and no silt-leach and no cure, and put no hand two paces out, and the not-putting was entered nowhere.

\scenebreak

A wrong wind came up off \place{High Kiln} and carried the sweet edge of \lore{lead glaze} over the boards, where the boy lay at the edge of the work with the fold against his ear. It touched the fold and went out on the wind that brought it, a direction arrived nowhere. It was not a second edge paid, not joined to the man carried out of the kiln on Day 188, not joined to any saying about a pot or to the pale line he kept on a place of its own. The sweetness went and the yard went on with its hands, and nobody made a lesson of the air.

Mid-afternoon, over the boards and not into the basket, a voice with no owner and no room and no hand at the kiln and no cause said the row question again in a new form. It was not a fresh asking. Somebody, the voice said, had been asking which row it was that went quiet. The weather answered first and the price of barley after it; the voice went off up \place{the terrace} and the row stood where it stood with nobody leaning in to take an answer down. The boy took the saying apart as a surgeon takes a wound apart: what it asked, where it stood, what it would want before any man could answer it, and who would have to stand under the answer to pay for it. He found no witness at the bottom of it. Five askings stood on a seventh place with a finger between each pair, and this shaping lay beside them with a finger between it and the nearest. No sixth asking had come. No witness stood anywhere to be asked and no row was named. The shaping was not a hearing, not a seventh of \lore{the full note}, not a fifth telling of the burned slate, which stood at four, not a third telling of the counting room shut in the war, which stood at two, not a fourth likeness of the pot sayings, which stood at three with the premature Smoke 14 note on its own place, not a cause, not a name and not a payment. He set it there and made nothing of its being there.

\scenebreak

At \place{the kindling} the small knife lay at the very edge of its sill with six counted in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check and the seventh at the lip where it could not, and \person{Garl Vail}'s unforged collector sentence beside it, with no name on the man who had turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on the debt. \person{Tarn} took the broom only after it had been given its instruction, said \emph{Yes, house} to an early thaw and a late and agreed with both so thoroughly that no argument could find a foot, and kept wanting the late because a late thaw puts off the paying. \lore{Ash} was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line and no scrubbing lifted it; the pale clip-ring at his left wrist held what light there was and gave it back; his chest was tight as it had been since the dark and the breath from Seed 16 lay unspent at the top of each reach. He told the wood \emph{Six counted. The next I don't count} in the tone of a man stating the weather, and his stroke went on over \place{the flagstones} without the half-check, the stones keeping the shallow bright patch where it had stopped. Then the sentence about an ear under this roof being worth more than the debt that held it came to his teeth and went as far as the word that would call the loan and stopped there. It was a remembered stopping, the ninth, with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end and still ungiven. He would not stand a tenth up to make a rule of the nine, because a rule is an account that has found a payer and none stood. The loan stayed uncalled, and no collector came up \place{the clay road} on Smoke 25 or 26 and none went down it. Up in \place{the Debt Loft}, which reached the boy only as a sound with a direction and never as a room he was taken into, the warm stone a woman had given him in the winter Garl Vail died lay in its unmarked clay pot, two years gone and cold long since, not tried and not forgiven, and not trying is not forgiveness.

At the edge of \place{the Vail wheel-shed} \person{Jorra Vail} came through with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bent little finger and white in the creases of a fist that had opened, not bone-coloured slip. He kept dusty forearms and clean ones on one tray and parted them as a surgeon parts clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had been broken nor whether it was a birth-slate; the broken birth-slates lay beneath the lowest rack and the yard entered them into nothing. He spoke to the wheel before he spoke to any person --- \emph{It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady} --- and told a pot \emph{You'll do} in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either. He knew the full note that pot would give three turns before it left his hands, with air under it and a want in it, and withheld the knowing on the near-full tray beside the hearings and never among them, advanced and not paid. Three or more unnamed men had gone low into \place{the back room} before first grey and got no name from him, and he did not say the room was cold; it kept its finger's width of open and its sound of low voices, which arrived at the basket as a direction and nothing more. When Sella's sentence went low, his bent finger stood in the cold iron of \place{the damper} ahead of it, took the cold out of the fingertip and held until the end arrived at the pot; the boy read one piece of iron with two bites, mercy and mutilation, with no Grathic word for both, joined to no kiln. The burned-slate word stood at four from \place{Slate Row}, a direction over the boards and not a fifth telling; the two shut-room tellings stood at two and did not become three; the promise of the boy's name to the men who burn slates was neither given nor withdrawn and \emph{he's not Vail} stayed sealed as tightly as a lid nobody had tried to lift in three years. No pot was lost and no name had been given to what ailed the man carried out on Day 188.

\scenebreak

At the going of the light Sella carried him up at the half-pace from every open slate, went wide around the seven places and set him on the bed under the roof and not in the Debt Loft. She put the fold right without a look at his face, told the fire it was the thaw, turned the coals with the back of a broken pot, said \emph{The house will keep it}, counted under her breath toward no opening, and went to her own bed. The thread stayed frozen. Smoke went on without a boundary drawn; Smoke 30, the third and last reading of the firing, stood four mornings ahead and was not brought into a day that had not earned it, and the first firing of Frost stood far off and unbrought.

In the small hours he turned the file out on \place{the towel} and found it, as he found it every time, exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air. Six hearings of the full note stood beside and never among, with a seventh place kept empty and none invented to fill it; three near-full answers stood advanced and unpaid, and three pot sayings stood on a place of their own with the premature Smoke 14 note apart and no fourth likeness made a cause; told bell stood beside heard and told figure beside paid; nine stoppings stood with no tenth; three held changes stood on three places, the loft scrape on a fourth and the pale line of the carried man on a fifth, and the held inch on a sixth with a hundred and five answers; nine record-first hands stood with the ninth on its own tray and the strip unread; the new shaping of the row question lay apart from the five askings with no witness under any of them; and the house's \lore{not-doings} --- the unswept line, the untouched mark, the unread charcoal, the missing fifth pull, the uncalled loan, no number spoken over the basket, the crust not lifted --- stood on a place of its own apart from his own kept gaps with a finger between, as they had stood the day before and would stand again, a finding and not a grievance, not a lesson and not a doctrine. Every tray was parted from its neighbour and every gap left gap. He made nothing of any of it, and would not let the finding of the morning about the handled grain lie across anything else.

Then he put the question to his hand. Through the finger's-width door the river kept its low note; under the floor the fire kept four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood; above him the boards kept their nails and their knot. Three counts in three places and none rose into another. He took the low note as he took the others, as an object and nothing more.

The hand outside the wool went up an inch toward the note and held for the small intake and let it go, one rhythm complete. Then a second rhythm went up across the little silence after a count that had ended in the room that does not touch --- not borrowed, not filled, the little silence left whole and empty on its own place beside the held one --- and came down on the river's next low note. The two intervals lay apart and not across one another; nothing was supplied to make a stroke where there was none and no second house count was borrowed for the river's going. It was one hundred and six, filed on the sixth place beside one hundred and five with no payer named, exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air: not a longer reach, not a hearing, not a seventh of the full note, not a stopping, not a payment, not one of the held changes, not a key, a power or a rule. He made nothing of the likeness and would not let it stand up as a gift.

Two hundred and sixty-five stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in \place{Sarn}, \place{Sill} or \place{Grath}; six months and eighty-five days were told by nobody; one hundred and five honest answers had become one hundred and six and none was a reach. The carried fire stopped at four with its fifth unpaid two hundred and sixty-five days and its payer unnamed; the widow's mark hung where it hung; the dressed grain lay covered and unfigured at \place{the rack end} with the arithmetic of Smoke 30 four mornings off and not knowing what it cost; the figure that had come up out of a fist had gone back into a sleeve and no figure had been supplied; the report of the asking lay on its own tray with nobody standing under it. Smoke went on without a boundary drawn. The file lay open on the towel with every tray parted and every gap left gap, and the hand came down on the river's next low note with the little silence behind it kept whole and uncrossed, and lay back against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking.
