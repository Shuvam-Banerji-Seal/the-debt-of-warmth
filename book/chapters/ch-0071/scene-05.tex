\daymark{266}{Smoke 27, Seventhday, Y.H. 813}

Under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof in \place{Low Kiln} the small hours came on the way heat comes off a stone and sits a while in the wool, with nowhere to go and no instruction to go anywhere. \person[Corin]{The boy} lay at the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf} with \place{the bed} under him and \place{the boards} overhead, awake and carried and silent, and the room kept him the way a shelf keeps a pot set upon it, without once noticing the weight. Two hundred and sixty-five had stood exact at the going of the dark; in the dark it became two hundred and sixty-six, and six months and eighty-five days became six months and eighty-six. Nobody under any roof in \place{Sarn}, \place{Sill} or \place{Grath} said so, or knew there was a number to be said. He had stopped expecting to be told, which was not the same as not wanting it; the wanting went on under the expectation the way a coal goes on under ash, giving out nothing and going out not at all. Under the floor \lore{the carried fire} gave its fifth unpaid day to the payer it had given all the others, a payer without a name for two hundred and sixty-six days. Smoke 27 would earn its own morning out of its own light, when there was light enough to earn it; Smoke 30, the third and last Account-day and reading of the second firing, stood three mornings ahead and was not reached for. There would be no market, no Cross and no bell on a Seventhday, and neither of the district's two names for the months was set right or left wrong.

The first smell was hot clay under tallow, the dry breath of a kiln fed and left to work; under it wet wool sour with a body carried in it since before first grey; under that, finer and more lasting, the sour mineral breath of the unswept corner the house called \lore{the thaw} --- \lore{Ash} so long in the air that it had ceased to be an event and become a condition of the room. The first sound came up through \place{the kiln mouth} behind the wall: the kiln drawing in fours, four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood. It was not a bell. Through the finger's-width door the river kept its note of \lore{water over water}, a low thing over a lower thing; above him the boards kept four nails on the near board, four on the next, and a thumb-width knot where the fifth nail should have stood. Three counts in three places, and none rose into another, because agreeing any two would want a payer put under both and no payer stood to either. Six hearings of \lore{the full note} stood beside and never among, with the seventh place kept empty and none invented to fill it; told figure stood beside paid figure, with Smoke's second arithmetic gone up the road in a locked box and unread under this roof.

He took the body in the order the facts presented themselves. One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from his ear, gone past the point where a child would have eaten it, and the empty place beside it kept its name to itself; no third had come in the night to make the row come out even. The thread of drool at his mouth had frozen in the dry heat and was unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread, and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. Cold had come to cheek and temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, and had stopped there as at the end of a line written out for it, neither going forward nor going away. The afterdrop waited beyond all three, past a warming nobody in this house would perform. He knew the composed face that is not composure; he knew a cold patient cannot clot and will seem better before he is worse, and that a man must be warmed from the outside and slowly. He wanted the crust lifted the two inches to his mouth. Both were true and the second was louder in a body of two hundred and sixty-six days; the surgeon entered neither, not even on the place where he kept the things he did not enter.

\scenebreak

Before first grey \person{Sella Vail} went to \place{the hearth} before the basket. She told the coals it was the thaw and turned them with the back of a broken pot, and said \emph{The house will keep it} where another woman would have said \emph{I}. She stood a moment with the back of the pot in her hand and counted under her breath toward no opening --- the small intake, the hold, the letting-go --- then pushed coal-black hair back with the wrist that carries the burn, so that the one grey streak at her temple showed pale off the coals. The burn was shaped like a little door closed on nothing; her hand shut half over nothing when she glanced at the clay jar standing cold under its \lore{lead glaze}. \lore{The widow's mark} hung where it hung, touched once before the kiln on Smoke 1 and at no shelf, well, rack, road, basket, morning or dusk on Smoke 25, 26 or 27, because a morning and a hearth are not openings. The one opening before Smoke 1 lay behind the household, made into no lesson, no mending, no sign and no payment; the next real opening stood far off in the first firing of Frost.

At the loaf shelf she kept her finger on top of the front edge, so the charcoal tally on the underside stayed unread; she had not looked beneath it in three years and was not going to begin on the week's last morning. Her lips moved along a row standing with two gaps, and neither gap was given a figure. Then she set the pot rim out, its handle two inches beyond the reaching hand, with a half-beat at the setting and no look toward the bed. The half-beat went where the look would have gone. No instruction came to the basket at any hour; the instruction went to the fire, the wool, the pot and the water. The boy, who had once put a shoulder back in the dark and could not close two inches on a rim, lay under the low angle of the shelf and let the distance be a distance. The shelf's two gaps and the fold's one crust with its empty place beside it stood on trays that did not touch, and nobody had asked him to make them agree, because a payer would have been wanted and none stood.

Out in \place{the Clay Yard}, at \place{the drying racks}, the dressed grain lay covered under wool on the cool stone at \place{the rack end}, away from the hearth. She drew the wool back only far enough to feel for damp, turned the grain once in the flat of a hand and laid it where it had lain, and drew the wool over it again. She did not handle it as she had handled it the day before; the week's last morning went over it with a lighter touch and learned nothing from the second reading. It changed state and gained no figure. No word was spoken over it and nothing was entered. The arithmetic that would be read over it on Smoke 30 would know the cover and not the grain and would do its work perfectly, which was precisely why it would not know what the grain had cost; the space between the figure and its price stayed unsigned. The boy kept the not-learning as a finding with no payer, not a lesson and not a grievance, and did not lay it across the house's \lore{not-doings} or across his own kept gaps, for a joining would want a payer and none stood.

\scenebreak

At \place{the yard well} the white frost had written on the north stone lip was gone under the bucket's knock, and the yard called the whole of it weather. He kept whatever white had been there on a place of its own with no cause attached: not a payer, not the widow's mark, for a well is not an opening, and not the first firing of Frost, which stood far off. Beside the well \place{the trough} stood bare and dark, its water called weather as the lip was. A neighbour with a pot hitched against her hip asked what the keeping and the storing would take now the grain was under wool and the visits coming. Sella did not answer from herself; she sent the question where it belonged, with \emph{Ask the pot what a wet thing costs set down there. The house has said it}. The neighbour went off level with nothing slopped, nothing priced, nothing refused and nothing entered. A man come down \place{the clay road} with dried pale scurf of mud on his coat skirt brought a number up out of a fist toward the well, his thumb tucked over it as over a thing that might be read, looked at it and put it back into his sleeve. The figure arrived nowhere. It was not a missing figure and it had not been withheld; it was a chart turned face-down, read as read and entered nowhere. The bucket went on knocking the grey lip, and the rope was cold iron under a hand that had long since stopped noticing that it was cold.

A wrong wind came up off \place{High Kiln} and carried the sweet edge of lead glaze over the boards, where the boy lay at the edge of the work with the fold against his ear. It touched the fold and went out on the wind that brought it, a direction arrived nowhere. It was not a second edge paid, not joined to the man carried out of the kiln on Day 188, not joined to any saying about a pot or to the pale line he kept on a place of its own. The sweetness went and the yard went on with its hands, and nobody made a lesson of the air.

\scenebreak

At \place{the kindling} the small knife lay at the very edge of its sill with six counted in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check and the seventh at the lip where it could not, and \person{Garl Vail}'s unforged collector sentence beside it, with no name on the man who had turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on the debt. \person{Tarn} took the broom only after it had been given its instruction, said \emph{Yes, house} to an early thaw and a late and agreed with both so thoroughly that no argument could find a foot, and kept wanting the late because a late thaw puts off the paying. Ash was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line and no scrubbing lifted it; the pale clip-ring at his left wrist held what light there was and gave it back; his chest was tight as it had been since the dark and the breath from Seed 16 lay unspent at the top of each reach. He told the wood \emph{Six counted. The next I don't count} in the tone of a man stating the weather, and his stroke went on over \place{the flagstones} without the half-check, the stones keeping the shallow bright patch where it had stopped. Then the sentence about an ear under this roof being worth more than the debt that held it came to his teeth and went as far as the word that would call the loan and stopped there. It was a remembered stopping, the ninth, with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end and still ungiven. He would not stand a tenth up to make a rule of the nine, because a rule is an account that has found a payer and none stood. The loan stayed uncalled, and no collector came up the clay road on Smoke 25, 26 or 27 and none went down it.

At the edge of \place{the Vail wheel-shed} \person{Jorra Vail} came through with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bent little finger and white in the creases of a fist that had opened, not bone-coloured slip. He kept dusty forearms and clean ones on one tray and parted them as a surgeon parts clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had been broken nor whether it was a birth-slate; the broken birth-slates lay beneath the lowest rack and the yard entered them into nothing. He spoke to the wheel before he spoke to any person --- \emph{It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady} --- and told a pot \emph{You'll do} in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either. He knew the full note that pot would give three turns before it left his hands, with air under it and a want in it, and withheld the knowing on the near-full tray beside the hearings and never among them, advanced and not paid. Three or more unnamed men had gone low into \place{the back room} before first grey and got no name from him, and he did not say the room was cold; it kept its finger's width of open and its sound of low voices, which arrived at the basket as a direction and nothing more. When Sella's sentence went low, his bent finger stood in the cold iron of \lore{the damper} ahead of it, took the cold out of the fingertip and held until the end arrived at the pot; the boy read one piece of iron with two bites, mercy and mutilation, with no word in \lore{Grathic} for both, joined to no kiln. The burned-slate word stood at four from \place{Slate Row}, a direction over the boards and not a fifth telling; the two shut-room tellings stood at two and did not become three; the three sayings about a pot stood with the premature Smoke 14 note on its own place and no fourth likeness made a cause; the five askings of the row question stood with the one shaping beside them and no witness under any and no row named. The promise of the boy's name to the men who burn slates was neither given nor withdrawn and \emph{he's not Vail} stayed sealed as tightly as a lid nobody had tried to lift in three years. No pot was lost and no name had been given to what ailed the man carried out on Day 188.

\scenebreak

Back under the roof, in the small hours, he put the question to his hand. Through the finger's-width door the river kept its low note; under the floor the fire kept four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood; over his head the boards kept four, four and a knot. No fire-sitter stood over the coals to supply a count and no house count was there to borrow. In the room that does not touch, a count had ended some time before and a little silence stood after it.

The hand outside the wool went up an inch toward the low note. It held for the small intake and for the hold of one of the breaths the woman counted in the room that does not touch, and came down before the letting-go, borrowing no stroke and supplying nothing to make a row come out even. It did not follow her breath to its end and did not cross the little silence after her count; that silence lay where it lay, whole and empty on a place of its own beside the held one, an object and not a miss. No second house count was borrowed for the river's going. It was one hundred and seven, filed on the sixth place beside one hundred and six with no payer named, exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air: not a longer reach, not a hearing, not a seventh of the full note, not a stopping, not a payment, not one of the held changes, not a key, a power or a rule. The hand lay back against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking.

Two hundred and sixty-six stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in Sarn, Sill and Grath; six months and eighty-six days were told by nobody. One hundred and six honest answers had become one hundred and seven and none was a reach. The carried fire stopped at four with its fifth unpaid two hundred and sixty-six days and its payer unnamed; the widow's mark hung where it hung; the dressed grain lay covered and unfigured on the cool stone at the rack end with the arithmetic of Smoke 30 three mornings off and not knowing what it cost; the pot handle stood two inches beyond the reaching hand with the half-beat gone; the fist that had come up toward the well had gone back into its sleeve and no figure had been supplied; the report of the asking lay on its own tray with nobody standing under it. Smoke went on without a boundary drawn; Smoke 30 stood three mornings ahead and was not reached for, and the first firing of Frost stood far off and unbrought. The third working week went out as it had come in, an ordinary tending that had learned nothing from the reading, and the nothing learned was kept as a finding with no payer. The file lay open with every tray parted and every gap left gap, and the hand lay back against the fold on the hundred and seventh answer, an object and not a power, with the pot handle still two inches beyond the reaching hand and the little silence behind it left whole on its own place.
