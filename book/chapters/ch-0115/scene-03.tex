\daymark{397}{Ilmen 3, Y.H. 814, Thirdday}

The small hours came on under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof the way a coal comes on under ash, without a flare and without a hand at it, and \person[Corin]{the boy} lay at the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf} in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill}, \place{Grath}, where the dry heat arrived sideways and went no farther, and where nobody under any roof had ever thought to give an instruction to the basket. Three hundred and ninety-six days stood behind him exact, six months and two hundred and sixteen of them told by nobody who would have known what a number counted; answer three hundred and sixty-eight had been laid flat on the fold at the going of the light and had bought nothing with its exactness, and the new morning asked nothing of him in return. Under the floor \lore{the carried fire} breathed four low pulls and kept the fifth where it had always been kept, and \place{the bed} held him level out of the draught in the place \person{Sella Vail} had chosen and not in the one he would have chosen. Outside, the dark went about its ordinary work along \place{the Clay Yard}, and no door was opened for it and no name given to what crossed.

The surgeon took the body in the order it arrived, because a field read out of order is a field read wrong. One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust stood two inches from his ear with its corner standing a hair off the wool; beside it the empty place held the fold put there on the fifteenth, found where put again in the dark and not made again, with no second fold, no third crust and no name spoken over the place. A keeping found is a keeping still however many hands find it, and a hand that finds is not a hand that places; a habit wants a hand that placed twice and a way wants a third, and both want a payer where none stood. The thread at his mouth had gone stiff in the dry kiln heat and was unwiped, because nobody had ever been told about the thread. Cold had come to cheek, temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, and stopped there as though it had reached the end of a line written out for it; \lore{the Afterdrop} waited past a warming nobody in this house would perform. A face composed by cold is not composure. A cold patient cannot clot, and pink returning is the surface still lying over a cold core, and the rewarming he would choose is a basket set level out of the draught where the heat comes sideways, slow and from the outside, never hurried and never rubbed, and he could not ask for it. He knew the order in which a body gives up heat, the extremities before the core, the surface warm while the middle is still cold, and the knowledge was of the same order as the knowledge of a chart signed past a note nobody read, and as useless here, for there was no hand two paces long to put either to work. Under the exactness the infant wanted the crust lifted, the thread wiped and a hand on the pot handle. Both were true. The second was louder. Neither won, and no verdict was looked for; the quarrel was the work.

Sella came to \place{the hearth} before the basket and gave the coals their instruction first. “It's the thaw,” she said, in the voice another woman would have used for a child who could not hear her, and turned them with the back of a broken pot. She gave the small intake, the hold and the letting-go toward no opening, with neither gap figured and neither made to stand for a number, and kept her finger along the top of the loaf shelf so the charcoal tally underneath stayed unread. Her lips moved on the two gaps in the loaf row and gave nobody a figure. One glance went to the clay jar cold under \lore{lead glaze} beyond \place{the pot board}, and her hand started toward it and shut half over nothing, the little-door burn on the back of her left hand closing on air. \person{Garl Vail} was in the room as he was in every room of the house, in the salt-stiff coat with its crease at the left shoulder on the peg behind the door, in the hollow wheel-seat nobody was allowed to fill, in the clean dark line at \place{the Vail corner} with its finer grey beside it called \lore{the thaw}, thickened in the dry cold with the faint sour tang of \lore{Ash} where no broom had gone in two years. His name was unspoken three hundred and ninety-six days and the fire's fifth was unpaid the same number, and the two stood side by side in the house's keeping without becoming one keeping. She lifted the twice-mended pot thumb-first where the tighter wire crossed the crack, left the hairline showing between the old-brass staple and the new binding, and said both proverbs to the pot and the fire and never to a person. Repair was kept apart from restoration as a surgeon keeps a graft apart from a cure: a restored thing hides the hand that handled it, and a mended thing leaves that hand showing. She turned the dressed grain under wool with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child and with no figure over it and no handful lifted, and set the pot rim two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat and no look toward the bed. The instruction went to the wool and the pot and the fire and the banked fire and the river wind, and never to the basket. The \lore[the widow's mark]{widow's mark} hung where it hung and she did not touch it; a morning, a shelf and a basket were not openings, and nothing under this roof made a threshold of a Thirdday.

She carried him down the nineteen steps at the half-pace she keeps from every open slate, went wide around the seven walked-around places with a look and a wide step and no number, once and not twice, widest around \place{the returning-line place} where a hand had gone in on Seed 15 and had not come out, and set him under the roof and never in \place{the Debt Loft}. The fold went right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face and without a second fold. Out in the yard the morning went about its grammar: a woman set a pail where she always set it, a man counted kindling into a stack and did not count the stack, and the price of barley passed from one end of the wall to the other without standing up as a figure. Then a wind came up wrong off \place{High Kiln}, the way a wind comes that has taken the wrong side of the terrace, and brought the sweet edge of lead glaze over \place{the boards} and past the pot board, where the clay jar stood cold under its glaze. It was a direction and not a second edge paid; it came across the yard, touched the wool at his ear and went out again on the wind that brought it. Sella's hand went toward the jar and shut half over nothing, the little-door burn closing on air, and she said nothing to the woman at the pail and nothing to the pot. The sweetish edge went and came and went, and the jar stood where it stood, and the boy filed the edge as weather and gave it no source, for a source is an account that has found a payer and none stood.

\scenebreak

A man come down \place{the clay road} from High Kiln stood at \place{the yard well} a while later with the sweetish green of tasted lead glaze at one corner of his mouth, and spoke to the weather about the pots. “They'll take a glaze this weather if you let them,” he said, and wiped the back of his wrist across his mouth, and went off toward \place{the drying racks} with the green still at the corner of it. He was a glaze-hand and nothing more under this roof: no name went with him, no slate, no figure, no room entered and no errand that brought him down the road. The boy filed the green as a colour on a place of its own, beside \person{Berr Clay}'s green at the reading and the greens that had come to mouths at past markets, with a finger between every pair and no joining. Berr's green was a colour and those were colours and the colour at this man's mouth was a colour; greens were greens and not one account, and no eighth of them was made due. He did not call the colour by the name of any woman, and no woman was given a name under this roof. It was not a hearing, not a cause, not a name and not a payer; it gathered where it stood and was not spent.

Costs came to the yard as directions and went away as weather. A man brought a number up out of a fist toward the pail at the well, looked at it where it stood in the air between his knuckles and the water, and put it back into his sleeve; it arrived nowhere and entered nothing. A woman with clay pale in her nail crescents asked what the day's work had cost and was sent to the pot with “the house has said it,” and the pot, being a pot, said nothing at all. The boy filed the order of reaching as he had filed it before --- hand to the record before eye to the person, mouth after both --- and made no tenth hand of the man with the fist, the woman at the pail, the glaze-hand at the racks or any hand that had gone before them. Nine hands stood with no tenth and the place where a tenth might have stood was left empty. He gave no figure to the number in the fist and no source to the silence of the pot; a cost that arrives nowhere has no payer under it, and he did not put one there.

Word came up the yard before noon that a runner had gone up the clay road earlier with the writing turned to her own sleeve and unread. It was a shape reported in a place, not a sighting from the basket and not a room entered; the boy took it as he took the weather, and gave no interior to the voice that carried it. \person[Hessa Rale]{Hessa}'s eighth hand stayed eighth on its own tray, and she was not made a reader by the carrying of a slate, for a runner is not a reader of what she carries and no figure came off the narrow slate going up the road. \person[Ilva]{Ilva}'s notched strip of birch bark stayed in her sleeve with the count kept in wood and no figure coming off the wood, and her ninth hand stayed ninth, not a reader and not a tenth. Berr Clay's twelve true subtractions stayed locked in his box up the road with the key gone up in a coat pocket that never came back down, and the boy had no key. Twelve arithmetics stood behind one unsigned figure with no signature on the space and no doctrine out of twelve, and the reset of the tend had supplied no payer to any of them. No tenth hand was filed and no reader stood under any roof; the word went up the road and arrived where words go when nobody asks them to stop.

\scenebreak

The kept things were found where put. The crust stood two inches off with its corner standing a hair up from the wool, and beside it the empty place held the fold put there on the fifteenth; \lore{the keeping at the fold} was found where put and was still a finding, not a placing, with no second fold and no name spoken over the place. Out in the yard the gone white frost on the north lip of the well, the skin on the trough and the silt-clouded plate of ice set out on its flat stone beyond the path of feet stood as three coverings with a finger between every pair, called weather and entered nowhere; no fourth likeness was made a cause of them. Through \place{the finger's-width door} \place{the river} kept its note of \lore{water over water} under \lore{the winter skin}, a low thing over a lower thing, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming. The winter skin was a covering put on by the cold and taken off by the cold, found where put and not a fourth of the three, not a hearing, not a sign and not spent on a tend-day. The neighbour's called-gone grey stayed on its own place beside the left-in-place ridge at the Vail corner, and a thing called finished was not finished for being called so.

The four rooms with the same gap stayed four with a finger between every pair, and no Court room was brought under the roof as a fifth; the cold foot below the sill stayed uncured and unnamed, and \lore{the second chill} kept its withheld cause, carried and not spent, with no silt-leach, cure, recognition or treatment named. The boy held the four rooms, the cold foot and the withheld cause on three places with a finger between, and did not lay a doctrine across them, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood. Six hearings of \lore{the full note} stood beside and never among with the seventh place empty and not dug for, and \place{the Drawing house}'s flat knock stayed outside them on its own place. The heard bell stayed in its square beside the told bell, four, four and a gap, its gap not folded into the fire's four and a gap under the floor, the boards' four and four and a thumb-width knot or the twelve arithmetics behind one unsigned figure; three counts in three places stayed three because agreeing any two would want a payer put under both and none stood.

When the morning's work was done she carried him up the nineteen steps at the half-pace from every open slate and set him on the bed under the Vail kiln-house roof, and put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face and without a second fold. The thread was left frozen and unwiped, because nobody had given an instruction about the thread.

Ilmen 10 stood seven mornings off and was not brought into a morning that had not earned it. The year's first arithmetic waited where it had been left, a reset and not a signing, and the twelve readings behind the unsigned figure bought no teaching under this roof. Nothing that had come up the clay road or gone along the yard crossed under the roof as sign, lesson, mending, cause, payment or payer; no slate had been opened within reach of the basket and no figure had been given for the boy. The morning had gone the way mornings go when a pot is set down in its place and the fire kept, with every kept thing left where it lay.

The file came out on \place{the towel} at the head of the bed as instruments come out between cases and before the next is called, and he went over the field by the place of each thing and left every gap a gap. The crust's gap with its corner standing a hair off the wool; the shelf's two gaps; the gone pan's round ring; \place{the field's empty place}; the keeping found where put and still a finding; the told bell's gap in its square beside the heard bell; the silence after the name from \place{Weighing} on \lore{the gap tray} beside the clean voice's missing name and the empty name the neighbours had left in the story of \place{the Remembering}; the called-gone grey on its own place beside the left-in-place ridge; the colour at the glaze-hand's mouth on a place of its own; the number that had risen out of a fist and arrived nowhere; the notched strip unread in a sleeve and the narrow slate gone up the road unread. Each lay where the morning had left it with a finger between, advanced and not paid, and none stood up and called itself a doctrine.

Answer three hundred and sixty-nine was filed exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the twelve readings, the bell's gap, the flat knock, the child's grey fingertips, the mother's cup, the covered grain, \lore{the handful}, \lore{the thumb's resting}, \lore{the held breath}, \lore{the two bites of iron}, the river's skin, the widow's mark, the fourth direction nor the keeping at the fold. Six honest answers stood on the sixth place from the days behind and three hundred and sixty-nine stood since the first, not one a doctrine. The count came to three hundred and ninety-seven exact, six months and two hundred and seventeen days told by nobody who would have known what a number counted. The carried fire's fifth was unpaid the same number and its payer unnamed the same number; Garl's name was unspoken the same number, and the two stood side by side without becoming one. The surgeon took his small clean satisfaction in a field taken as found and left as found, every object in its place and nothing laid across anything else, and it cost him nothing, which was how he knew it was not peace. Under it, louder, the infant wanted the crust lifted two inches, the thread at his mouth wiped and a hand on the pot board's handle, and got none of the three, and the wanting and the having-not went on being the work the way a wound goes on being a wound under a dressing that does not close it.

Sella's hand went toward the clay jar beyond the pot board and shut half over nothing, the little-door burn closing on air, while the sweetish edge of glaze came and went on the wrong wind and gave nobody a name for what it was. The hand outside the wool came straight down and laid itself flat against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. Answer three hundred and sixty-nine lay flat with the crust's corner a hair off the wool and the thread frozen and unwiped, the green kept as a colour on a place of its own and no eighth made of it, the count standing at three hundred and ninety-seven exact and told by nobody, and four low pulls breathing below the floor with the fifth unsupplied. Nothing had been opened, joined, named, called, paid, forgiven or ended; no witness stood, no reader stood, no payer stood, and the house went on as a house goes when a pot is set down in its place and the fire kept, forward.
