The afternoon went over \place{the Clay Yard} the way dry heat goes out of a kiln after the last firing, by degrees nobody called and with no door opened for it. \person[Corin]{The boy} lay in the finger's width of shade \person{Sella Vail} had chosen for him and not in the one he would have chosen, and the instruction went past him to the wool, the pot and the fire and never to the basket. She had carried him down the nineteen steps at the half-pace she keeps from every open slate and set him level out of the draught where the heat came sideways, and she had put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face and without a second fold. The crust stood two inches off with its corner standing a hair up from the wool; the thread at his mouth had gone stiffer in the dry cold and was unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread. The surgeon took the field as he found it. He had learned long ago that a body left in the position it was put is a body somebody has chosen not to move, and that the choice is a fact of the room and not an argument; he had once stood under a different roof and counted twenty such choices in a single morning and entered none of them, and the counting had kept him honest then as it kept him honest now, at two inches of air from everything he wanted.

The yard went around its seven walked-around places with a look and a wide step and no number, once and not twice. It went around the clean dark line at \place{the Vail corner} and the finer grey beside it called \lore{the thaw}, thickened in the dry cold with the faint sour tang of \lore{Ash} coming up off it where no broom had gone in two years; it went around the space under the lowest rack where the broken birth-slates lay, and the gone pan's round ring on the board, and the bare dark trough, and \place{the field's empty place} out beyond the last house. Widest of all it went around \place{the returning-line place}, where a hand had gone in on Seed 15 and had not come out, and the look stayed the length of a step and then went off the place as a man takes his eye off a thing he has no business examining. Nobody swept, named, counted or entered any of them. The boy kept the wide step as a keeping and not as a sign. A sign wants somebody standing at the end of it to read it, and no one stood. No cause, no accident, no body, no figure and no payment were put under the step; it was a thing the yard did the way a woman turns a loaf that is burning on one side, and the turning was the whole of it.

\person{Tarn} stood at \place{the kindling} at the very end of a term already ended. Six pieces stood in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check, and the seventh lay at the lip beyond it, where the house could not check and where it could be taken, beside \person{Garl Vail}'s unforged collector sentence with no name on the man who turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on \lore{the tile debt}. The small knife lay at the very edge of its sill. His hand went over blade and sill and away with no breath held at the top of the reach, the coming away the whole of the checking, and he said to the wood in the tone of a man stating the weather, “Six counted. The next I don't count.” He cut nothing. He counted nothing. He spent nothing. A woman come for an armful of kindling asked what the house would want of him now his time was out, and he said “Yes, house” to the question and to the thing behind the question, agreeing so thoroughly that the question had nowhere left to put a foot, and went on stacking with the small tools of the house going into his pocket one after another and coming out again where they belonged. He had owned nothing for six years and he knew where every small thing was.

The sentence about an ear under this roof being worth more than the debt that holds it was found where it had stopped, at the word that would call the loan. It had come all the way to that word and no farther: a remembered ninth stopping with no tenth stood up, no figure on it and no name on the man. The breath drawn on Seed 16 was still unspent at the top of the reach, standing as a small interval where it should have gone in and had not, and the loan that could hear had not been called. The fear of being entered again for vagrancy stayed his without a figure, and so did the want he had not voiced — a slate with his own name on it and no mark against it, a thing a man could hold up and be called by. He did not look along \place{the clay road}. The boy read the stopping as a surgeon reads a clamp left on a vessel: the instrument did not close anything, and the not-closing was a state of the field and not an omission by any hand. A term ending was not a payment, a forgiveness or a freedom. It left a man more exposed than it had found him, because a man with no house has no wall between his body and the people who would measure it, and the exposure was carried on this afternoon and not spent. Two ears, two silences, a loan that could hear and had never been called; the boy set the three on one place with a finger between each and no payer standing under any of them.

The held breath of Ash 14 lay at the knife-sill where it had been put, and the thumb's resting of Bone 26 lay where that had been put, found where put and not made again, beside the five held objects and the pot-crack resting with a finger between every pair. Not a sixth held object, not a tenth stopping, not a hearing, not a rule; no payer stood under any of them and none of them repeated. His chest had been tight since the dark. Ash was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line at the Vail corner, and no scrubbing lifted it; the pale clip-ring at his left wrist caught what little light the afternoon had and gave it back. His stroke went on over the shallow bright patch of \place{the flagstones} without the half-check, the stones keeping the place where an earlier stroke had stopped, and went over the road a house sends a man down when it wants a collector without once turning along it. No collector came up and no runner came down. The patch kept its own place on the stones and was joined to no cause and no ear.

\person[Jorra Vail] came through \place{the finger's-width door} off \place{the Vail wheel-shed} with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past his bent little finger and white in a fist-crease where a hand had opened, and it was not the bone-coloured \lore{slip} of the wheel. Dust ground small is a thing that got there by being broken; slip is a thing somebody mixed on purpose. He kept the two states apart the way the boy kept clot apart from vessel, on one tray with a finger between, and asked neither which slate had been broken nor whether it had been a birth-slate. Three or more men unnamed had gone low into \place{the back room} before first grey and got no name from him and no word that the room was cold. He parted his dusty forearms from his clean ones without a word passing between the two states, and the household went on about its work as cloth goes on holding its shape after a weight has been lifted off it.

He spoke to the wheel before he spoke to any person. “It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady.” To a pot on the bench he said, “You'll do,” in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either. He knew the \lore{full note} that pot would give three turns ahead, with air under it and a want in it, and he withheld the knowing from any master come up from \place{High Kiln} who would call it early and sing a pot before its time. A full note called before its time is a note spent before it is owed, and the knowing of it was a thing he held where nobody could make a song of it. The boy filed the knowing on a place of its own, beside the hearings and never among them; no seventh hearing stood up in the empty place to take it.

Sella had not finished telling him the fire was low when his bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper} ahead of her sentence. He took the cold out of the fingertip, moved his tongue a hair's breadth and was left standing so that the end of her instruction arrived at the pot and the household straightened around the waiting. One piece of iron had two bites in it, the holding and the taking, and no word in \lore{Grathic} covered both mercies in one breath; the boy read it as a surgeon reads a clamp that is mercy and mutilation in the same bite, and gave it no name that would make the two into one account. He came out without leaving the extra holding of \emph{Ash 26} a second time. A second would be a habit and a third a way, and both want a payer where none stood. The finger went in and the finger came out, and neither of the two looked at the other or said what the iron had been asked to carry.

“He's not Vail” stayed sealed as a lid nobody had tried to lift in three years, and the promise of the boy's name under the wheel-shed was neither given nor withdrawn, going on gathering interest against the burned slate at five. \lore{The burned-slate word} stood at five with a finger between the fifth and the four and no witness standing; the shut-room tellings stood at two; the row stood at five askings with one shaping and no witness; the well-saying stood at four shapings. The Ash 5 joining lay on its own place, and the Ash 17, Ash 23 and Stone 23 directions out of \place{Slate Row} lay each on a place of its own with a finger between every pair. None of them repeated on this afternoon and none bought a lesson. The boy kept Tarn's loan and Jorra's promise on two trays and did not lay them together to make a cause, for two likenesses stood up as one would want a payer and none stood. The man at the kindling had not asked for a slate in words; the boy in the basket had not asked to be named in words. Two nothings, held apart, and the space between them kept as a space.

The light went the way it went every day, from tallow to the colour of a banked coal and then to nothing over the yard, and nobody made an hour of it. Sella came to \place{the hearth} before the basket and gave the coals their instruction first. “It's the thaw,” she said, in the voice another woman would have used for a child who could not hear her, and turned them with the back of a broken pot. She gave the small intake, the hold and the letting-go toward no opening, with neither gap figured and neither made to stand for a number, and kept her finger along the top of \place{the loaf shelf} so the charcoal tally underneath stayed unread. Her lips moved on the two gaps in the loaf row and gave nobody a figure. One glance went to the clay jar cold under \lore{lead glaze} beyond \place{the pot board}, and her hand started toward it and shut half over nothing, the little-door burn on the back of her left hand closing on air. She lifted the twice-mended pot thumb-first where the tighter wire crossed the crack, left the hairline showing between the old-brass staple and the new binding, and said both proverbs to the pot and the fire and never to a person. Repair was kept apart from restoration as a surgeon keeps a graft apart from a cure: a restored thing hides the hand that handled it, and a mended thing leaves that hand showing. She turned the dressed grain under wool with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child and with no figure over it and no handful lifted, and set the pot rim two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat and no look toward the bed. The instruction went to the wool and the pot and the fire and the banked fire and the river wind, and never to the basket.

Through the finger's-width door \place{the river} kept its note of \lore{water over water} under \lore{the winter skin}, a low thing over a lower thing, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming, a covering put on by the cold and taken off by the cold, found where put and not spent on an ordinary afternoon. Under the floor \lore{the carried fire} gave four low pulls with the silence where the fifth should have stood, its giver without a name and its fifth unpaid three hundred and ninety-six days, and nobody lifted the floor to supply what was wanting below it. Garl's name was unspoken the same number, and the fire and the man stood side by side in the house's keeping without becoming one keeping. The \lore[the widow's mark]{widow's mark} hung where it hung and she did not touch it; a shelf and a basket and a going of the light were not openings, and no eighth was made due on a Secondday. Six hearings of the full note stood beside and never among with the seventh place empty and not dug for, and \place{the Drawing house}'s flat knock outside them on its own place. The four rooms with the same gap stayed four with no Court room brought under the roof as a fifth, and \lore{the second chill} kept its withheld cause while the cold foot below the sill stayed uncured and unnamed. Green stayed a colour and the foundling a shape, with no eighth of either and no name given under this roof.

The file came out on \place{the towel} at the head of the bed as instruments come out between cases and before the next is called, and he went over the field by the place of each thing and left every gap a gap. The crust's gap with its corner standing a hair off the wool; the shelf's two gaps; the gone pan's round ring; the field's empty place; \lore[the keeping at the fold]{the keeping at the fold} found where put and still a finding; the told bell's gap in its square beside the heard bell, four, four and a gap in two places and a gap in a third, three counts in three places staying three because agreeing any two would want a payer put under both and none stood; the silence after the name from \place{Weighing} on \lore{the gap tray} beside the clean voice's missing name and the empty name the neighbours had left in the story of \place{the Remembering}, each with a finger between; the neighbour's called-gone grey on its own place beside the left-in-place ridge at the Vail corner; the stopped ear-sentence at the word that would call the loan; the promise under the wheel-shed neither given nor withdrawn; Garl's unforged sentence beside the seventh notch; the held breath at the knife-sill and the thumb's resting found where put. Each lay where the afternoon had left it with a finger between, advanced and not paid, and none stood up and called itself a doctrine.

Nine record-first hands stood with no tenth, and the clean-voice speaker stayed taken out of his own sentences on a place of his own, for a clean voice is not a hand. Nine stoppings stood with no tenth and no new stopping made on this tend-day. Everything the afternoon had carried was entered nowhere. The surgeon took his small clean satisfaction in a field taken as found and left as found, every object in its place and nothing laid across anything else, and it cost him nothing, which was how he knew it was not peace. Under it, louder, the infant wanted the crust lifted two inches, the thread at his mouth wiped and a hand on the pot board's handle, and got none of the three, and the wanting and the having-not went on being the work the way a wound goes on being a wound under a dressing that does not close it.

Answer three hundred and sixty-eight was filed exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the twelve readings, the bell's gap, the flat knock, the child's grey fingertips, the mother's cup, the covered grain, \lore{the handful}, \lore{the thumb's resting}, \lore{the held breath}, \lore{the two bites of iron}, the river's skin, the widow's mark, the fourth direction nor the keeping at the fold. Six honest answers stood on the sixth place from the days behind and three hundred and sixty-eight stood since the first, not one a doctrine. The count came to three hundred and ninety-six exact, six months and two hundred and sixteen days told by nobody who would have known what a number counted. The carried fire's fifth was unpaid the same number and its payer unnamed the same number; Garl's name was unspoken the same number, and the two stood side by side without becoming one. Nothing had been opened, joined, named, called, paid, forgiven or ended; no slate had been opened within reach of the basket, no figure given for the boy, no door drawn across the tend, no payer set under any of the kept things, and the year's first arithmetic waited eight mornings off where it had been left.

The hand outside the wool came straight down and laid itself flat against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. Tarn's hand going over blade and sill and away with no breath held at the top of the reach stood on its own place where the afternoon had left it, the knife where it could be checked and the seventh piece at the lip where it could be taken, and the loan that could hear was not called. Answer three hundred and sixty-eight lay flat on the fold with the crust's corner a hair off the wool and the thread frozen and unwiped, the count standing at three hundred and ninety-six exact and told by nobody, and four low pulls breathing below the floor with the fifth unsupplied.
