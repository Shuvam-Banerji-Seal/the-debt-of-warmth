The afternoon came down off the dry heat the way warmth comes off a banked wall, by degrees nobody called. \person[Corin]{The boy} lay in the finger's width of shade \person{Sella Vail} had chosen, at the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf} under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill}, \place{Grath}, where the heat came sideways and stopped at his cheek as at the end of a written line. Three hundred and ninety-seven days stood behind him exact, six months and two hundred and seventeen told by nobody. Answer three hundred and sixty-nine had gone down at the last light and bought nothing with its exactness. The instruction went past the basket to the wool, the pot and the fire, and never to the basket; a man may spend an afternoon being spoken over by people who have never thought to speak to him. Under the floor \lore{the carried fire} breathed four low pulls and kept the fifth where it had always been kept.

The surgeon read the afternoon as a field at rest, by the small sounds first, because the small sounds do not lie and the large ones are mostly weather. The yard went around its seven walked-around places with a look and a wide step and no number, once and not twice: the clean dark line at \place{the Vail corner} with the finer grey beside it called \lore{the thaw}, thickened in the dry cold with the faint sour tang of \lore{Ash} where no broom had gone in two years; the space beneath the lowest rack where the broken birth-slates lay; the gone pan's round ring; the bare dark trough; \place{the field's empty place} beyond the last house. Widest it went around \place{the returning-line place}, where a hand went in on Seed 15 and did not come out, and the look stayed one step and went off. Nobody swept, named, counted or entered any of them. The boy kept the step as a keeping and not as a sign, for a sign wants a reader standing at the end of it and none stood; the yard turned around the places the way a woman turns a scorching loaf, and the turning was the whole of it.

\person{Tarn} stood at \place{the kindling} at the very end of a term already ended. Six pieces stood in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check, and the seventh lay at the lip beyond it, where the house could not check and a hand could take it, beside \person{Garl Vail}'s unforged collector sentence with no name on the man who turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on \lore{the tile debt}. The small knife lay at the very edge of its sill. His hand went over blade and sill and away with no breath held at the top of the reach, the coming away the whole of the checking, and he said to the wood in the tone of a man stating the weather, “Six counted. The next I don't count.” He cut nothing. He counted nothing. He spent nothing. The seventh lay at the lip and was not lifted.

The sentence about an ear under this roof being worth more than the debt that holds it was found where it had stopped, at the word that would call the loan: a remembered ninth stopping with no tenth stood up, no figure on it and no name on the man. The breath drawn on Seed 16 was still unspent at the top of the reach, and the loan that could hear had not been called. The fear of being entered again for vagrancy stayed his without a figure, and so did the want he had not voiced: a slate with his own name on it and no mark against it. He did not look along \place{the clay road}. The boy read the stopping as a surgeon reads a clamp left on a vessel: the instrument closed nothing, and the not-closing was a state of the field and not an omission by any hand. A term ending is not a payment, a forgiveness or a freedom. It leaves a man more exposed than it found him, for a man with no house has no wall between his body and the people who would measure it. Two ears, two silences, a loan that could hear and had never been called: the boy set the three on one place with a finger between and no payer under any.

Ash was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line at the Vail corner, and no scrubbing lifted it; the pale clip-ring at his left wrist gave back what little light there was, and his chest had been tight since the dark. The held breath of Ash 14 lay at the knife-sill and the thumb's resting of Bone 26 where that had been put, found where put and not made again, beside the five held objects and the pot-crack resting with a finger between every pair. His stroke went on over the shallow bright patch of \place{the flagstones} without the half-check, the stones keeping the place where an earlier stroke had stopped. He did not take up the broom until the broom had been given its instruction, and when he did the stroke went where it was told and no farther.

Word came up the yard from a neighbour come up from below \place{the north bridge}, passed from a pail to a latch the way a coal is passed in a dish, and reached the shade where the basket stood. The soft-cuffed clerk was still set, the neighbour said, at \place{the out-of-line landing} below the bridge, with his slate turned inward for a measure nobody had called. The cuff was soft from washing and folded back twice and gone grey from washing; the hand went to the slate before the eyes went to the landing, every time. He called nothing. He wrote no name. No face under any roof had seen him, and the word came up as a shape in a place and no more than that. The boy took it as he took the weather: a report carried by a voice he gave no interior to, not a sighting from the basket, not a room entered, not a witness standing, not a cause. A witness is a man who has seen a thing and can be asked; nobody under the roof had seen this man and nobody asked. The landing kept its own place at the end of the word and did not become a door because a neighbour had spoken its name; nobody under any roof named it one.

The boy filed the order of reaching on a place of its own, apart from every other record-first hand. Hand to the record before eye to the person, mouth after both: the clerk's reported hand had gone to the slate before his eyes went to the landing, and that order was filed where the morning's fist at the pail and the woman sent to the pot had not been filed, with a finger between. Nine hands stood and nine hands stayed nine; no tenth stood up in the empty place.

The neat dark hand at the Court table, \person{Marra}'s hand precise about other people's warmth, the finger beneath the shelf, \person{Berr Clay}'s thumb wiped twice before it touched nothing, \person[Hessa Rale]{Hessa}'s eighth going up unread, and \person[Ilva]{Ilva}'s ninth with the strip unread in her sleeve sat each on a tray of its own with a finger between every pair. The Drawing collector's ordinary hand and the Kindling collector's ordinary hand sat each on its own, for two men using the same flat words are not one hand and do not become one account. The clerk's reported hand sat on the ninth tray, reported and not witnessed. The clean-voice speaker stayed taken out of his own sentences on a place of his own and was not made a tenth hand of, for a clean voice is not a hand. Sella's, \person[Alda Reed]'s and \person[Mira Silt]'s hands sat on another tray and were not made a cause of anything.

It was Tarn's head that moved first, and moved least. As the word of the landing went along the yard his head went a degree toward it and came back to the broom without a word. He took up the broom only after it had been given its instruction, and the stroke went on over the shallow bright flagstones without another turn of the head and without the half-check, the stones keeping the place where an earlier stroke had stopped. Nothing was said under any roof. Nobody was arrested, nobody was called, no slate was opened within reach of the basket; the danger came as a piece of news and one small movement. This was the first movement that joined an ended term and a valuable ear to the people who would measure it, and the boy filed it on the place where it stood and left it there. It was not a payment, not a hearing, not a seventh, not the calling of the loan, not a tenth stopping, not a rule and not an argument heard under any roof; the turn was not repeated as a habit and did not become a way, for a habit wants a second and a way wants a third and both want a payer where none stood. The surgeon read the movement as a man being measured without a slate being opened: exact, small, worth nothing at two inches of air, filed and left where it lay. What landed was that a term ending had left the man more exposed, not less, and that the exposure had begun to gather a shape. The boy kept the degree-turn on its own place with the clerk's inward slate beside it and a finger between the two, and did not lay them together to make a cause.

\person[Jorra Vail] came through \place{the finger's-width door} off \place{the Vail wheel-shed} with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past his bent little finger and white in a fist-crease where a hand had opened, and it was not the bone-coloured \lore{slip} of the wheel. Dust ground small is a thing that got there by being broken; slip is a thing somebody mixed on purpose. He kept the two states apart the way the boy kept clot apart from vessel, on one tray with a finger between, and asked neither which slate had been broken nor whether it had been a birth-slate. Three or more men unnamed had gone low into \place{the back room} before first grey and got no name from him and no word that the room was cold. He parted his dusty forearms from his clean ones without a word passing between the two states, and the household went on about its work.

He spoke to the wheel before he spoke to any person. “It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady.” To a pot on the bench he said, “You'll do,” in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either. He knew the \lore{full note} that pot would give three turns ahead, with air under it and a want in it, and he withheld the knowing from any master come up from \place{High Kiln} who would call it early and sing a pot before its time. A full note called early is a note spent before it is owed. The boy filed the knowing on a place of its own, beside the hearings and never among them; no seventh hearing stood up in the empty place to take it.

Sella had not finished telling him the fire was low when his bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper} ahead of her sentence. He took the cold out of the fingertip, moved his tongue a hair's breadth and was left standing so that the end of her instruction arrived at the pot and the household straightened around the waiting. One piece of iron had two bites in it, the holding and the taking, and no word in \lore{Grathic} covered both mercies in one breath; the boy read it as a surgeon reads a clamp that is mercy and mutilation in the same bite, and gave it no name that would make the two into one account. He came out without leaving the extra holding of \emph{Ash 26} a second time. A second would be a habit and a third a way, and both want a payer where none stood. The finger went in and the finger came out, and neither of the two looked at the other or said what the iron had been asked to carry.

“He's not Vail” stayed sealed as a lid nobody had tried to lift in three years, and the promise of the boy's name under the wheel-shed was neither given nor withdrawn, going on gathering interest against the burned slate at five. \lore{The burned-slate word} stood at five with a finger between the fifth and the four and no witness standing; the shut-room tellings stood at two; the row stood at five askings with one shaping and no witness; the well-saying stood at four shapings. The Ash 5 joining lay on its own place, and the Ash 17, Ash 23 and Stone 23 directions out of \place{Slate Row} lay each on a place of its own with a finger between every pair. None of them repeated on this afternoon and none bought a lesson. The boy kept Tarn's degree-turn and Jorra's promise on two places with a finger between and did not lay them together to make a cause. The man at the kindling had not asked for a slate in words; the boy in the basket had not asked to be named in words; the clerk at the landing had not called a measure in words. Three nothings, held apart, and the space between them kept as a space.

The light went the way it went every day, from tallow to the colour of a banked coal and then to nothing over the yard, and nobody made an hour of it. Sella came to \place{the hearth} before the basket and gave the coals their instruction first. “It's the thaw,” she said, in the voice another woman would have used for a child who could not hear her, and turned them with the back of a broken pot. She gave the small intake, the hold and the letting-go toward no opening, with neither gap figured, and kept her finger along the top of the loaf shelf so the charcoal tally stayed unread; her lips moved on the two gaps in the loaf row and gave nobody a figure. One glance went to the clay jar cold under \lore{lead glaze} beyond \place{the pot board}, and her hand started toward it and shut half over nothing, the little-door burn on the back of her left hand closing on air. She lifted the twice-mended pot thumb-first where the tighter wire crossed the crack, left the hairline showing between the old-brass staple and the new binding, and said both proverbs to the pot and the fire and never to a person. Repair was kept apart from restoration as a surgeon keeps a graft apart from a cure: a restored thing hides the hand that handled it, and a mended thing leaves that hand showing. She turned the dressed grain under wool with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child and with no figure over it and no handful lifted, and set the pot rim two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat and no look toward the bed. The instruction went to the wool and the pot and the fire and the banked fire and the river wind, and never to the basket.

Through the finger's-width door \place{the river} kept its note of \lore{water over water} under \lore{the winter skin}, a low thing over a lower thing, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming, a covering put on by the cold and taken off by the cold, found where put and not spent on an ordinary afternoon. Under the floor the carried fire gave four low pulls with the silence where the fifth should have stood, its giver without a name and its fifth unpaid three hundred and ninety-seven days, and nobody lifted the floor to supply what was wanting below it. Garl's name was unspoken the same number, and the fire and the man stood side by side without becoming one keeping. The \lore[the widow's mark]{widow's mark} hung where it hung and she did not touch it; a shelf and a basket and a going of the light were not openings, and no eighth was made due on a Thirdday. Six hearings of the full note stood beside and never among with the seventh place empty and not dug for, and \place{the Drawing house}'s flat knock outside them on its own place. The year's first arithmetic waited seven mornings off, a reset and not a signing, and was not brought into a day that had not earned it.

The file came out on \place{the towel} at the head of the bed as instruments come out between cases and before the next is called, and he went over the field by the place of each thing and left every gap a gap. The crust's gap with its corner standing a hair off the wool; the shelf's two gaps; the gone pan's round ring; the field's empty place; \lore[the keeping at the fold]{the keeping at the fold} found where put and still a finding; the told bell's gap in its square beside the heard bell, four, four and a gap in two places and a gap in a third, three counts in three places staying three because agreeing any two would want a payer put under both and none stood; the silence after the name from \place{Weighing} on \lore{the gap tray} beside the clean voice's missing name and the empty name the neighbours had left in the story of \place{the Remembering}, each with a finger between; the neighbour's called-gone grey beside the left-in-place ridge at the Vail corner; the stopped ear-sentence at the word that would call the loan; the promise under the wheel-shed neither given nor withdrawn; Garl's unforged sentence beside the seventh notch; the held breath at the knife-sill and the thumb's resting found where put; the clerk's inward slate at the landing and the degree the head had gone toward it and back to the broom without a word. Nine record-first hands stood with no tenth, the clerk at the landing a reported shape and not a tenth hand and no witness, the clean-voice speaker taken out of his own sentences and not made a tenth hand of. The surgeon took his small clean satisfaction in a field taken as found and left as found, every object in its place and nothing laid across anything else, and it cost him nothing, which was how he knew it was not peace. Under it, louder, the infant wanted the crust lifted two inches, the thread at his mouth wiped and a hand on the pot board's handle, and got none of the three, and the wanting and the having-not went on being the work the way a wound goes on being a wound under a dressing that does not close it.

Answer three hundred and seventy was filed exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the twelve readings, the bell's gap, the flat knock, the child's grey fingertips, the mother's cup, the covered grain, \lore{the handful}, \lore{the thumb's resting}, \lore{the held breath}, \lore{the two bites of iron}, the river's skin, the widow's mark, the fourth direction nor the keeping at the fold.
