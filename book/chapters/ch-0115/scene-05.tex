\daymark{398}{Ilmen 4, Y.H. 814, Fourthday}

The small hours came down dry under the Vail roof, with the cold settling into \place{the boards} the way silt settles in a still pail, a little at a time and all of it at last. \person[Corin]{The boy} lay at the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf} in \place{the Vail kiln-house}, \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill}, \place{Grath}, held where the heat came sideways and stopped at his cheek as at the end of a line written out for it. Three hundred and ninety-seven days stood behind him exact, six months and two hundred and seventeen told by nobody who would have known what a number counted. Answer three hundred and seventy had gone down at the last light and bought nothing with its exactness. No slate lay open within reach of the basket, and this morning had not earned a figure. The instruction went past the basket to the wool, the pot and the fire, and never to the basket; a man may lie a whole tend under a roof that speaks over him and never once to him. Under the floor \lore{the carried fire} breathed four low pulls and kept the fifth where it had always been kept.

The surgeon took the body in its old order, because the old order was the only instrument he had been left. One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust stood two inches from his ear, gone harder through the dry nights, its corner standing a hair off the wool. Beside it the empty place held the fold put there on the fifteenth, found where put and still a finding: a keeping found where put is a keeping however many hands find it, and a finding is not a placing, for a placing wants a hand that puts and a second hand that puts again, and both want a payer where none stood. The thread of drool at his mouth had frozen stiff in the dry kiln heat and was unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread. Cold had come to cheek, temple and the bare joint at the wrist where the wool rode back, and stopped there as at the end of its own sentence, with neither an order to go farther nor an order to go away. \lore{The Afterdrop} waited past a warming nobody in this house would perform. He knew a basket set level out of the draught where the heat came sideways was the rewarming he would choose — slow, from the outside, never hurried and never rubbed, with pink returning the surface still lying over a cold core and a face composed by cold no composure at all — and he could not ask for it. A cold patient cannot clot. He knew that too, and had nothing to clamp with and no patient but himself.

The infant wanted the crust lifted two inches, the thread at his mouth wiped, and a hand on \place{the pot board}'s handle. Both were true and the second was louder, and neither won, and the quarrel went on being the work the way a wound goes on being a wound under a dressing that does not close it. He had once reduced a thousand shoulders and could not lift his own hand to his own chin. The two registers stood side by side and he judged neither, which is a kind of triage and not a peace.

The house kept its days in loaves pushed along the shelf, in glances at the clay jar standing cold under \lore{lead glaze} beyond the pot board, and in a space at the end of the loaf row that nobody called a number. Sella moved a loaf a finger's width and did not say what the moving counted. Out at the wall a woman with a basket pushed it along with her knee and said to nobody that the batch had come out light, and a man going past with a pail glanced at the jar and away, and the space at the row's end stayed a space. Three hundred and ninety-eight he kept against all of it, exact and edge to edge, and did not stand the number up as a threshold, a doctrine or a figure. Three hundred and ninety-eight days are three hundred and ninety-eight days and not a door. A man may count his own breaths and learn nothing from the count but the counting. Ilmen 10 stood six mornings off with the year's first arithmetic waiting there, a reset and not a signing, and was not brought into a morning that had not earned it.

\scenebreak

\person{Sella Vail} came to \place{the hearth} before she came to the basket. “It's the thaw,” she said to the coals, in the voice another woman would have used for a child who could not hear her, and turned them with the back of a broken pot so the small red eye under the ash showed and shut and showed again. She stood a moment with her hands gone still, counting a small intake, a hold and a letting-go toward no opening, with neither gap figured, the way a woman stands over a wound she has dressed and will not look at twice. “The house will keep it,” she said to the pot, where another woman would have said I. She did not look toward the bed.

Her finger lay along the top edge of the loaf shelf so the charcoal tally underneath stayed unread; her lips moved on the two gaps in the loaf row and gave nobody a figure. One glance went to the clay jar under glaze beyond the board, and her hand started toward it and shut half over nothing, the little-door burn on the back of her left hand closing on air. The burn was shaped like a door with a lintel and a threshold, and it shut on nothing the way it always did when there was nothing for it to hold. She lifted the twice-mended pot thumb-first where the tighter wire crossed the crack, and left the hairline of the original break showing dark between the old-brass staple and the new binding. “Leave the crack showing,” she said to the pot. “People trust a thing they can see has been handled.” To the fire she said, “If it's worth mending it's worth mending twice. The second time is the real one.” Both proverbs went to the pot and the fire and never to a person. Repair was kept apart from restoration as a surgeon keeps a graft apart from a cure: a restored thing hides the hand that handled it, and a mended thing leaves that hand showing for anybody who cares to look.

She turned the dressed grain under wool with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child, and no figure went over it and no handful was lifted, and then she set the pot rim two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat and no look toward the bed. The half-beat was the distance between the hand's intention and the hand's arrest, bought at nothing and not a gift. The instruction went to the wool, the river wind, the pot, the fire and the banked fire, and never to the basket.

\scenebreak

\lore[The widow's mark]{The widow's mark} hung where it hung against the cord at her throat, a thumb-length of lead-glazed clay with the Vail kiln scratched into it, oiled where a working thumb had touched it ten thousand times. A morning was not an opening; a shelf was not an opening; a bed and a basket and a going of the light were not openings. Seven openings in seven months were seven and not a rule, and no eighth was made due on a Fourthday. The twelve arithmetics of Y.H. 813 stood unchanged behind one unsigned figure, twelve and not one account: the three of Frost, the first, second and third of Bone, the first, second and third of Ash, and Stone's first, second and last, all carried across the turning of the year with nothing signed. The printed figure stood unchallenged over the basket, one in twelve, and the figure read was still not the figure paid, and no signature stood on the space between. Their twelve true subtractions rode locked in a box gone up \place{the clay road}, the key gone up in a coat pocket that never came back down and the boy having no key. Twelve are twelve, and a doctrine out of twelve wants a payer standing where none stood. Ilmen 10 was not brought in to settle any of it.

\scenebreak

Under the floor the carried fire answered four low pulls and kept the silence where the fifth should have stood, the way a man answers a roll-call with four names and leaves the space for a fifth nobody will fill. Its giver had been without a name for three hundred and ninety-eight days, and nobody lifted the floor to supply what was wanting below it, and nobody asked who had first kept it. \person{Garl Vail}'s name had gone unspoken under this roof the same number, and the fire and the man stood side by side without becoming one keeping, for a fifth pull and an unspoken name are two gaps on two places with a finger between, and agreeing them would want a payer put under both and none stood. The salt-stiff coat kept its crease at the left shoulder on the peg behind the door; the hollow wheel-seat in the shed kept the twenty years of sitting worn into it and nobody filled it; the clean dark line at \place{the Vail corner} kept its finer grey hairline beside it, called \lore{the thaw}, thickened in the dry cold with the faint sour tang of \lore{Ash} coming up off it where no broom had gone in two years. The twice-mended pot kept its crack showing on the board. The unforged collector sentence lay at \place{the kindling} beside the seventh notch with no name on the man who turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on \lore{the tile debt}, and the debt went on circling without a name and was worse for being uncalled. None of these things was a story and none of them had been paid.

\lore[The keeping at the fold]{The keeping at the fold} was found where put and stayed a finding: the wool folded once in the empty place beside the crust, no second fold, no third crust, no name spoken over the place, no gift, no payment and no payer. It was no more a habit for being found again than a stone is a road for being stepped over; a habit wants a hand that places twice and a way wants a third, and both want a payer where none stood. The three coverings stayed where put with a finger between every pair and no fourth likeness made a cause: the gone white frost off the north lip of \place{the yard well}, the skin on the trough, the silt-clouded plate of ice set out of the path of feet. \place{The river} through \place{the finger's-width door} kept its note of \lore{water over water}, a low thing over a lower thing, under \lore{the winter skin}, a covering put on by the cold and taken off by the cold and entered nowhere, not a hearing, not a sign, not a fourth of the three and not spent on a tend-day. The four rooms that hold the same four things with a finger between each stayed four with a finger between every pair and no Court room brought under the roof as a fifth; the gap they held was neither filled nor named, the cold foot below the sill stayed uncured and unnamed, and the cause of the second chill kept its withheld cause, carried and not spent.

Six hearings of \lore{the full note} stood beside and never among, the seventh place empty and not dug for or filled, and \place{the Drawing house}'s small flat knock outside them on its own place. The heard bell stayed in its square beside the told bell, four, four and a gap, and its gap was not folded into the fire's four and a gap under the floor, \place{the boards}' four and four and the thumb-width knot where a fifth nail might have been driven and was not, or the twelve arithmetics behind one unsigned figure. Three counts in three places stayed three, because agreeing any two would want a payer put under both and none stood. The flat knock was not made a seventh hearing; the names that went up the yard on \place{the Remembering} were sounds in a yard and not rooms entered, and a story's gap was the shape of a vessel's interval and not a source; the silence after the name from \place{Weighing}, the empty name in the story of \place{the Long Night} and the place the Remembering did not fill sat on \lore{the gap tray} with a finger between every pair. Nine record-first hands stood with no tenth and nine stoppings with no tenth, and the small flat knock out of \person{Dessa Silt} stayed where a thing out of a person must go and was not made a seventh of anything. Each thing lay where it lay with a finger between, advanced and not paid, and none stood up and called itself a rule.

He knew what the field was worth, which was nothing, and that the nothing was exact. The house's arithmetic of loaves, glances and an unnamed space at the row's end did not agree with his arithmetic of days and did not need to; two accounts that do not touch are two accounts and not one dispute. The field taken as found and left as found went on gathering toward a hand not his and not yet come, a hand that would one day lay a district's private keepings together and call the joining an account. He did not look for that hand and did not give it a name, for a name here would want a payer standing and none stood; what gathered gathered without him and went on gathering.

\scenebreak

At the going of the light the file came out on \place{the towel} at the head of the bed as instruments come out when a case is done and before the next is called. He went over the field by the place of each thing and left every gap a gap. The crust's gap with its corner standing a hair off the wool; the shelf's two gaps; the gone pan's round ring; \place{the field's empty place}; the keeping at the fold found where put and still a finding; the told bell's gap in its square beside the heard bell; the silence after Weighing's name on the gap tray beside the clean voice's missing name; the Long Night story's empty name and the place the Remembering did not fill, each with a finger between; the neighbour's called-gone grey beside the left-in-place ridge at the Vail corner; the held breath at the knife-sill and the thumb's resting found where put; the pot-crack resting with a finger between it and the five held objects; the two bites of iron; the river's winter skin; the widow's mark hanging where it hung; the fourth direction standing where put; the twelve arithmetics behind one unsigned figure with no signature on the space. None had been opened, joined, named, called, paid, forgiven or ended, and no slate had been opened within reach of the basket and no figure given for the boy.

Answer three hundred and seventy-one came out and the hand came down. It was exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment, borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the twelve readings, the bell's gap, the flat knock, the child's grey fingertips, the mother's cup, the covered grain, \lore{the handful}, \lore{the thumb's resting}, \lore{the held breath}, \lore{the two bites of iron}, the river's skin, the widow's mark, the fourth direction nor the keeping at the fold. Six honest answers stood on the sixth place from the days behind, and three hundred and seventy-one stood since the first, and not one of them was a doctrine. The count came to three hundred and ninety-eight exact, six months and two hundred and eighteen days told by nobody who would have known what a number counted. The carried fire's fifth was unpaid the same number and its payer unnamed the same number; Garl's name was unspoken the same number, and the two stood side by side without becoming one. The surgeon took his small clean satisfaction in a field taken as found and left as found, every object in its place and nothing laid across anything else, and it cost him nothing, which was how he knew it was not peace. Under it, louder, the infant wanted the crust lifted two inches, the thread at his mouth wiped and a hand on the pot board's handle, and got none of the three.

The dressed grain lay covered under wool with no figure over it. The pot rim stood two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat and no look toward the bed. Answer three hundred and seventy-one lay flat on the fold with the crust's corner a hair off the wool, the thread frozen and unwiped, and the count at three hundred and ninety-eight exact, and four low pulls breathed below the floor with the fifth unsupplied. The house went on as a house goes when a pot is set down in its place and the fire kept, forward.
