## Chronicle

Three tending days came under the Vail roof after the turning of the year — Ilmen 2, 3 and 4 — and each morning the surgeon took the body in its old order: the crust two inches from the ear, the fold in the empty place found where put, the thread frozen and unwiped, cold stopping at cheek, temple and wrist, the afterdrop waiting past a warming nobody would perform. The infant wanted the crust lifted, the thread wiped, a hand on the pot handle. Both true; the second louder; neither winning.

On Ilmen 2 the house went back to its grammar as though nothing had turned. Sella told the coals it was the thaw, kept the charcoal unread and set the twice-mended pot's rim two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat and no look toward the bed. At the kindling Tarn stood at the end of an ended term, six pieces where the house could check and the seventh at the lip; his hand went over blade and sill and away with no breath held, and he told the wood, *Six counted. The next I don't count.* The ear-sentence stopped at the word that would call the loan.

On Ilmen 3 a wrong wind off High Kiln brought the sweet edge of lead glaze over the boards. A glaze-hand down the clay road had the green at one corner of his mouth; Sella's hand went toward the cold jar and shut half over nothing. Costs came and went as weather: a number rose out of a fist and went back into a sleeve, and a woman sent to the pot got no figure from a pot. Word came up that a runner had gone up the road with the writing turned to her sleeve and unread.

Then word came up that the soft-cuffed clerk was still set at the out-of-line landing, his slate turned inward for a measure nobody had called. He was a shape seen by nobody under the roof, and no witness stood. The boy filed the order of reaching on a place of its own and made no tenth hand of him. Tarn's head went a degree toward the landing and back to the broom without a word, and the stroke went on over the flagstones. The danger came as news and one small movement, and gathered interest without being called.

Six answers were filed, 367 through 372. The count stood at 398 exact, six months and two hundred and eighteen days told by nobody; the carried fire's fifth was unpaid and Garl's name unspoken the same number. Twelve arithmetics stood behind one unsigned figure; the year's first waited six mornings off, unbrought. His satisfaction in a field taken as found and left as found cost nothing, which was how he knew it was not peace. The infant wanted the crust lifted, the thread wiped and a hand on the pot handle, and got none of the three.
