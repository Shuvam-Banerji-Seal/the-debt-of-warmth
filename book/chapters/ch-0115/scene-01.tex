\daymark{396}{Ilmen 2, Y.H. 814, Secondday}

\dropcap{T}{he hand} lay flat on the fold where the dark had left it, and the small hours came on under the roof the way weather comes, with no door opened for them and nobody saying what they had crossed. Three hundred and ninety-five days stood behind that hand exact and unspent, six months and two hundred and fifteen of them told by nobody who would have known what a number counted; the day had a working name, Secondday, and nothing under the roof made a threshold of it or a signing. Under the floor \lore{the carried fire} gave four low pulls and kept the fifth where it had always been kept, and \person[Corin]{the boy} lay at the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf} in \place{the Vail kiln-house}, in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill}, \place{Grath}, with the dry kiln heat coming to him sideways and the smell of woodsmoke and wet wool in the fold and, under that, the faint sour tang of \lore{Ash} lifting out of \place{the Vail corner} where no broom had gone in two years. The room held its heat the way a cup holds water, with a crack in it nobody mentioned. Outside, the dark was going about its ordinary work along \place{the Clay Yard}, and nobody under any roof was asking it to mean anything.

The surgeon took the body in the order it arrived, because a field read out of order is a field read wrong. One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust stood two inches from his ear with its corner a hair off the wool; beside it the empty place held the fold put there on the fifteenth, found where put again in the dark and not made again, with no second fold, no third crust and no name spoken over the place. A keeping found is a keeping still however many hands find it, and a hand that finds is not a hand that places; a habit wants a hand that placed twice and a way wants a third, and both want a payer where none stood. The thread of drool at his mouth had gone stiff in the dry kiln heat and was unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread. Cold had come to cheek, temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, and stopped there as though it had reached the end of a line written out for it; \lore{the Afterdrop} waited past a warming nobody in this house would perform. A face composed by cold is not composure. Pink returning is the surface still lying over a cold core, and a cold patient cannot clot, and the rewarming he would choose is a basket set level out of the draught where the heat comes sideways, slow and from the outside, never hurried and never rubbed, and he could not ask for it. He knew the order in which a body gives up heat and the order in which it takes it back, the extremities before the core, the surface warm while the middle is still cold; he had once stood at a bay door and sorted twenty people by what they would not survive, and the knowledge was of the same order and as useless here, for there was no hand two paces long to put any of it to work. Under the exactness the infant wanted the crust lifted, the thread wiped and a hand on the pot handle. Both were true. The second was louder. Neither won, and no verdict was looked for; the quarrel was the work.

\person{Sella Vail} came to \place{the hearth} before she came to the basket, as she had come to it every morning of his life, and gave the coals their instruction first. “It's \lore{the thaw},” she said, in the voice another woman would have used for a child who could not hear her, and the low red eye under the back of the broken pot answered her with a small breathing that was not an answer to anything anybody had asked. She turned the coals with the back of the pot and stood a moment with her hands not still, counting a small intake, a hold and a letting-go toward no opening, the count arriving nowhere as it had arrived nowhere every morning of his life, with neither gap figured and neither made to stand for a number. Her finger lay along the top of the loaf shelf where any woman in the district could see it lying, and the charcoal tally underneath stayed unread; her lips moved along the two gaps in the loaf row and gave nobody a figure. Her eye went once to the clay jar cold under \lore{lead glaze} beyond \place{the pot board}, with the sweetish edge of a wrong wind coming and going over it, and her hand started toward it and shut half over nothing, the little-door burn on the back of her left hand closing on air. She lifted the twice-mended pot thumb-first where the tighter wire crossed the crack, and the hairline of the original break showed between the old-brass staple and the new binding, and she said both proverbs to the pot and the fire and never to a person: \emph{Leave the crack showing. People trust a thing they can see has been handled}, and the other about a thing worth mending being worth mending twice, the second time being the real one, improved in the keeping and very likely not as good as the man had said it. She kept repair apart from restoration as a surgeon keeps a graft apart from a cure; a restored thing hides the hand that handled it and a mended thing leaves that hand showing. The pot gave back a small flat note as she set it down --- not the full clear note of a sound vessel and not the dead note of one cracked through, but the note of a thing that carries its break and goes on holding --- and she had heard it every morning of his life and had never once called it mended. A strand of coal-black hair with one grey streak at the temple came loose and she did not push it back. She turned the dressed grain under wool with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child, with no figure over it and no handful lifted, and set the pot rim two inches beyond the reaching hand. There was a half-beat at the setting and no look toward \place{the bed}. He filed the half-beat as the distance between intention and arrest, bought at nothing and not a gift; it was the same interval he had once bought with a clamp and called by another name, and the other name bought nothing here either.

\scenebreak

The house went back to its grammar as though nothing had been asked of it. The instruction went to the wool, to the pot, to the fire and the banked fire and to the river wind, and never to the basket. What she said low went to the pot and the fire, and when she spoke of the boy it was \emph{the boy}, and when she spoke of what she meant to do it was \emph{the house}, a grammar that had outlasted a turning of the year without losing a word. \lore[The widow's mark]{The widow's mark} hung where it hung on its cord of working thumbs and she did not touch it; a morning, a shelf and a basket were not openings, seven openings in seven months were seven and not a rule, and no eighth was made due on a Secondday. A rule is an account that has found a payer and none stood. Under the floor the carried fire breathed four low pulls with the silence where the fifth should have stood, its giver without a name and its fifth unpaid three hundred and ninety-six days, and nobody lifted the floor to supply what was wanting below it. \person{Garl Vail}'s salt-stiff coat kept its crease at the left shoulder on the peg behind the door, the wheel-seat in the shed kept the hollow twenty years had worn on that side and nobody filled it, the clean dark line at the Vail corner kept its finer grey beside it called the thaw, thickened in the dry cold with the faint sour tang of Ash coming up off it where no broom had gone in two years, the twice-mended pot kept the hairline showing between staple and wire, his unforged collector sentence lay at \place{the kindling} with no name on the man who turned back at the frozen ford, and \lore{the tile debt} against a firing of tiles circled without a name and was worse for being uncalled. His name was not spoken under this roof on this morning, and the fire and the man stood side by side in the house's keeping without becoming one keeping. No collector came up \place{the clay road} and no runner came down it; no market and no bell, and no reading at \place{the Cross}. The Ash 5 joining of \lore{the burned-slate word} to \lore{the counting room} shut in the war and the Ash 17, Ash 23 and Stone 23 directions from the direction of \place{Slate Row} stood where they had been put, did not repeat and bought no lesson, and the boy gave no fifth shaping to the four.

He carried twelve arithmetics across into this morning and found them unchanged. The three of \place{Frost}, the first, second and third of \place{Bone}, the first, second and third of \place{Ash}, and \place{Stone}'s first, second and last: twelve readings of one small printed figure, twelve and not one account. The printed figure stood at one in twelve, unchallenged, and twelve true changes to the district's figures had not moved that one mark. The figure read was still not the figure paid; the space between them had no signature on it and no doctrine came out of twelve, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood. Their twelve true subtractions rode locked in \person{Berr Clay}'s box up the clay road toward \place{High Kiln}, the key gone up in a coat pocket that never came back down and the boy with no key to anything. The reset of the tend had supplied no payer to the old twelve and none to a new one, and it was a fact of the Court's counting and not an act of anybody under this roof. He did not lay his twelve old findings across a new figure to make a row come out even. \place{Ilmen 10} stood eight mornings off with the year's first arithmetic waiting there, a reset and not a signing, and he did not bring it into a morning that had not earned it. No thirteenth reading stood behind the figure and no signature stood on the space where the year had gone over like a pot set down in the same place on a board, the board not changed under it.

Sella carried him down the nineteen steps at the half-pace she keeps from every open slate, and the instruction went to the wool and the river wind and never to the basket. She went wide around the seven walked-around places with a look and a wide step and no number, once and not twice, and widest around \place{the returning-line place} where a hand had gone in on Seed 15 and had not come out; the look stayed the length of a step and then went off the place. She set him under the roof and never in \place{the Debt Loft}, put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face, and added no second fold. The three coverings stood weather entered nowhere with a finger between every pair, and \place{the river} kept its note of \lore{water over water} under \lore{the winter skin} beyond \place{the finger's-width door}, a covering put on by the cold and taken off by the cold, found where put and not spent on an ordinary morning. The thread at his mouth was frozen and unwiped because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread.

Out in the yard a woman set a pail down where she always set it, and a man counted kindling into a stack and did not count the stack, and two voices passed the price of barley from one end of the wall to the other without either voice making a figure of it. Nothing in the yard arrived anywhere as a number and no hand came under the roof to enter what had not been entered. The keeping at the fold was found where put on the morning and was still a finding, not a placing; he did not give the place a name or the finding a second hand, and the warmth of it, such as it was, went on being kept at cost to nobody who could have been asked.

\scenebreak

He turned out the file in the hush after the yard's ordinary sounds had gone past, as instruments come out on \place{the towel} between cases and before the next is called, and went over the field by the place of each thing. It was not put away for good; another case would come and the towel would come out again and the hand would find the place of every instrument without looking. The crust's gap first: one stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust two inches from his ear with its corner standing a hair off the wool, the empty place beside it holding the fold found where put and not made again, and \lore[the keeping at the fold]{the keeping at the fold} a finding still. The shelf's two gaps stayed where the loaves had not been set; the gone pan's round ring stayed on the board where a pan had stood and stood no longer; \place{the field's empty place} kept its shape out beyond the door. \lore[the told bell]{The told bell}'s gap sat in its square beside \lore{the heard bell} from the Cross, four, four and a gap in two places and a gap in a third, three counts in three places staying three because agreeing any two would want a payer put under both and none stood. The silence after the name from \place{Weighing} lay on \lore{the gap tray} with the clean voice's missing name and the empty name the neighbours had left in the story of \place{the Remembering}, each with a finger between, none filled, none made a witness, a reader or a payer. The neighbour's called-gone grey lay on its own place beside the left-in-place ridge at the Vail corner, and a thing called finished was not finished for being called so. The stopped ear-sentence stood at the word that would call the loan, at the very end of a term already ended, with no figure on it and no name on the man who turned back at the frozen ford; the promise under \place{the Vail wheel-shed} stood neither given nor withdrawn; Garl's unforged collector sentence lay beside the seventh notch. Each lay on its own place with a finger between and none stood up to call itself a rule.

Nine record-first hands stood with no tenth, each on a tray of its own, and he did not make a tenth of the hand that had gone to the coals before the basket or of any hand that would come up the road before noon to measure what nobody had called. Six hearings of \lore{the full note} stood beside and never among with the seventh place empty and not dug for, and \place{the Drawing house}'s flat knock outside them on its own place. \lore[The four directions]{The four directions} stayed four with a finger between every pair; the coverings and the river's skin stayed found where put and unspent; the four rooms with the same gap stayed four with no Court room brought under the roof as a fifth; \lore{the second chill} kept its withheld cause and the cold foot below the sill stayed uncured and unnamed; green was a colour and the foundling a shape with no eighth of either. Every one advanced and none paid, and no hand under the roof drew a boundary across the morning to make them stand as a doctrine.

Answer three hundred and sixty-seven was filed exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the twelve readings, the bell's gap, the flat knock, the child's grey fingertips, the mother's cup, the covered grain, \lore{the handful}, \lore{the thumb's resting}, \lore{the held breath}, \lore{the two bites of iron}, the river's skin, the widow's mark, the fourth direction nor the keeping at the fold. Six honest answers stood on the sixth place from the days behind and three hundred and sixty-seven stood since the first, not one a doctrine.

The count came to three hundred and ninety-six exact, six months and two hundred and sixteen days told by nobody who would have known what a number counted. The carried fire's fifth was unpaid the same number and its payer unnamed the same number; Garl's name was unspoken the same number, and the two stood side by side without becoming one. The surgeon took his small clean satisfaction in a field taken as found and left as found, every object in its place and nothing laid across anything else, and it cost him nothing, which was how he knew it was not peace. Peace in the old life had always come with something spent --- a shift ended, hands washed, a chart signed; this cost nothing because nobody had been asked to pay. Under it, louder, the infant wanted the crust lifted two inches, the thread at his mouth wiped and a hand on the pot board's handle, and got none of the three, and the wanting and the having-not went on being the work the way a wound goes on being a wound under a dressing that does not close it.

Nothing had been opened, joined, named, called, paid, forgiven or ended. No slate had been opened within reach of the basket, no figure given for the boy, no door drawn across the tend, no payer set under any of the kept things, and the year's first arithmetic waited eight mornings off where it had been left. Beyond the finger's-width door the river went on under its skin, water over water, and the hand outside the wool came straight down and laid itself flat against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. The crust's corner stood a hair off the wool, the thread was frozen and unwiped, the count stood at three hundred and ninety-six exact and told by nobody, and four low pulls breathed below the floor with the fifth unsupplied. The house went on as a house goes when a pot is set down in its place and the fire kept, forward.
