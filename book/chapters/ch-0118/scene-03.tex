\daymark{406}{Ilmen 12, Y.H. 814, Secondday}

The small hours came on under the roof without being let in, the way cold comes into a room that has no door for it --- not arriving, only being there, the boards giving up their last thin warmth and the dark settling into the wool as though it had been asked and had answered. \person[Corin]{The boy} lay at the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf} in \place{the Vail kiln-house} in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill}, \place{Grath}, where the heat came to him sideways and not where he would have chosen, and the hand outside the wool lay flat where it had been laid, four hundred and five exact days behind it and answer three hundred and eighty-six under it, a restraint put down as a man puts down a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. Secondday was the working name of this morning, and nothing under the roof made a threshold of it or a signing. Dry kiln heat had gone into the wool with elm-bark smoke and tallow, and under that the faint sour tang of \lore{Ash} lifting out of \place{the Vail corner} where the clean dark line kept its finer grey hairline beside it, called \lore{the thaw}, thickened in the dry cold and left unswept two years. Under \place{the boards} \lore{the carried fire} gave four low pulls and kept the silence where the fifth should have stood, and nobody lifted the floor to supply what was wanting below it. Through \place{the finger's-width door} \place{the river} kept its note of \lore{water over water}, low under \lore{the winter skin}, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming, found where put and not spent on a tend-day. Outside, the dark went about its ordinary work along \place{the Clay Yard}, and nobody under any roof was asking it to mean anything.

The surgeon took the body in its old order, because an order is the one thing a field will give a man who cannot move in it. The crust stood two inches from the ear with its corner a hair off the wool, leathery and brown-edged and harder than it had been, hardened again through the dry nights, gone past the point where a child would still have eaten it. Beside it the empty place held the fold put there on the fifteenth, found where put and still a finding, not a placing; no second fold lay over it and no name had been spoken above it. The thread of drool at the mouth had gone stiff in the dry kiln heat and was unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread. Cold had come to cheek and temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, and stopped there as at the end of a line written out for it, with neither an order to go farther nor an order to go away. \lore{The Afterdrop} waited past a warming nobody here would perform.

He knew what the surface was doing. A face composed by cold is not composure; the grimace of severe cold wears the shape of a smile, and pink returning is the surface still lying warm over a cold core, and a cold patient cannot clot and must be warmed from outside and slowly, never hurried, never rubbed, never set too near the fire. A basket set level out of the draught where the heat came to him sideways was the careful rewarming he would have chosen and could not ask for. Under the exactness the infant wanted the crust lifted two inches, the thread wiped and a hand on the pot board's handle; both were true, the second was louder, neither won, and the quarrel was the work. He had once stood at a bay door and sorted twenty people by what they would not survive, and he could not lift his own hand to his own chin.

He kept four hundred and six exact, six months and two hundred and twenty-six days told by nobody who would have known what a number counted, and did not stand the number up as a threshold, a doctrine or a figure, because a number stood up as a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood. The house kept its days in loaves and glances and in the unnamed space at the row's end where a thing had stood and stood no longer, and could not have told a man the day's name if a man had asked, which nobody did. The first arithmetic of the year had gone up \place{the clay road} in \person[Berr Clay]{Berr}'s locked box, the key gone up in a coat pocket that did not come back down and no key in his hand, and he did not bring it down again or lay twelve old findings across it to make a row come out even, for paper is not a payer and a row made even by a hand with no key is a row made even on paper. The printed figure stood at one in twelve, unchallenged; the figure read was still not the figure paid, and the space between them had no signature on it and he set none against it. Nothing from the day behind crossed under the roof as sign, lesson, mending, cause, payment or payer.

\person{Sella Vail} came to \place{the hearth} before the basket, as she had come at both ends of every day since he came under this roof, and told the coals it was the thaw in the voice another woman would have used for a child who could not hear her, and turned them with the back of a broken pot. She gave the small intake, the hold and the letting-go toward no opening, and figured neither of the two gaps; a small thing done toward nothing, the way a woman breathes before a lift she has already decided not to make. Her finger lay along the top of the loaf shelf so the charcoal tally on its underside stayed unread, and her lips moved on a two-gapped row and figured neither gap. One glance went to the clay jar standing cold under \lore{lead glaze} beyond the board, and the hand shut half over nothing, the little-door burn on the back of her left hand closing on air, and went back to the pot. At her throat \lore{the widow's mark} hung where it hung and was not touched: a morning, a hearth, a shelf and a basket are not openings, seven openings in seven months are seven and not a rule, and no eighth was made due on a morning that had earned only its twelfth.

She lifted the twice-mended pot from \place{the pot board} thumb-first where the tighter wire crossed the crack, and the hairline of the original break showed between the old-brass staple and the wire, a dark thread with a hand's work on either side of it. Garl was in that hairline, and in the salt-stiff coat with its left-shoulder crease on its peg behind the door, and in the hollow wheel-seat in the shed that nobody was allowed to fill, and not in a story anybody told; \person{Garl Vail}'s name had not been spoken under the roof for four hundred and six days, and the two counts --- his name and the fire's fifth --- stood side by side without becoming one. She said both proverbs to the pot and the fire and never to a person: \emph{Leave the crack showing. People trust a thing they can see has been handled} and \emph{If it's worth mending it's worth mending twice. The second time is the real one}. Repair was not restoration; a restored thing hides the hand that handled it and a mended thing leaves the hand showing, and a surgeon keeps a graft apart from a cure for the same reason a potter leaves a staple in the light. \lore{The tile debt} against a firing of tiles went on circling without a name and worse for being uncalled, and no collector came up the road on a Secondday to call it.

At \place{the rack end} the dressed grain lay covered under wool on the cool stone away from the hearth. She lifted a corner and turned what was under it with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child, piece by piece, setting each down where it had lain, and took no figure from it and no handful for herself. The arithmetic read over grain covered at home did not know what the grain cost, and the space between the figure and the price had no signature on it; she, who could not enter a figure anywhere and had no hand to lift a crust two inches, set none against it. The wool went back over the grain with the same light touch and the space stayed as it was. Then the pot went down on the board again with its rim two inches beyond the reaching hand, and there was a half-beat at the setting and no look toward \place{the bed}. The half-beat was the distance between an intention and an arrest: her hand had meant to set the pot nearer and had stopped where it stopped, and the distance cost her nothing and bought the boy nothing, and neither of them called it a gift. Under the roof nobody instructs a basket, and nobody instructs a pot about a hand.

She carried him down the nineteen steps at the half-pace she keeps from every open slate, correcting the tilt at every turn of the landing before it had time to settle into the wicker, and the instruction went to the wool, the river wind, the pot, the fire and the banked fire and never to the basket. \place{The seven walked-around places} were gone around with a look from the basket's low angle and a wide step that belonged to a hand larger than his, once and not twice, widest around \place{the returning-line place} where a hand had gone in on \emph{Seed 15} and had not come back; the wide step was a keeping and not a sign, and a figure set against it would want a payer standing under it and none stood. He was set under the roof and never into \place{the Debt Loft}. She put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face and added no second fold to the crust's empty place, and \lore{the keeping at the fold} was found where put and still a finding, with no name over it and no payer under it. The ridge at the corner kept its clean dark line and its finer grey called the thaw, thickened in the dry cold with the sour tang of Ash where no broom had gone in two years, and she did not sweep it and did not say why.

At \place{the kindling} \person{Tarn} kept six pieces in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check and the seventh at the lip beyond it, beside Garl's unforged collector sentence with no name on the man who turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on the tile debt. The small knife lay at the very edge of its sill, and his hand went over blade and sill as a man passes a hand over a tool put back where it belongs, and came away with no breath held at the top of the reach --- the coming away the whole of the checking. He cut nothing, counted nothing, spent nothing, and told the wood in the tone of a man stating the weather, \emph{Six counted. The next I don't count}. He took the broom only after \place{the flagstones} had been given their instruction, said \emph{Yes, house} to the question and to the thing behind it, agreeing so thoroughly that the question had nowhere left to put a foot, and went over the stones without a word and without a turn of the head toward \place{the out-of-line landing}. The degree toward the landing was not repeated; it stayed a shape found where put on a place of its own. The ear-sentence about an ear under this roof being worth more than the debt that holds it came all the way to the word that would call the loan and stopped there, a remembered ninth stopping with no tenth, an object and not a rule, an interval and not a payment; the \emph{Seed 16} breath stayed unspent and the loan uncalled at the very end of a term already ended, and a term ending is not a payment, a forgiveness or a freedom but leaves a man more exposed than it found him. Ash worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line, the pale clip-ring at his left wrist catching and giving back what little light there was. He owned nothing and knew where every small thing was, and the fear of being entered again for vagrancy stayed his without a figure.

\person{Jorra Vail} came through the finger's-width door from \place{the Vail wheel-shed} with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past his bent little finger and white in a fist-crease where a hand had opened, and it was not the bone-coloured \lore{slip} of the wheel. Three or more men unnamed had gone low into \place{the back room} before first grey and got no name from him and no word that the room was cold, and the broken ones under the lowest rack stayed entered by nobody. He kept dusty forearms and clean on one tray and parted them as clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had been broken nor whether it was a birth-slate, for a question joined to a cause wants a payer standing and none stood. He spoke to the wheel before any person --- \emph{It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady} --- and told a pot \emph{You'll do} in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either, knowing the \lore{full note} that pot would give three turns before it left his hands, with air under it and a want in it, and withholding the knowing from any master come up from \place{High Kiln} who would call it early and sing a pot before its time. Sella had not finished telling him the fire was low when his bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper} ahead of her sentence; he took the cold out of the fingertip, moved his tongue a hair's breadth and was left standing so the end of her instruction arrived at the pot and the household straightened around the waiting as cloth straightens when a weight is lifted. One piece of iron had two bites, the holding and the taking, and no Grathic word covered both mercies; he took the finger out without leaving the extra holding of \emph{Ash 26} a second time, gathered once and not made again, for a second would be a habit and a third a way and both want a payer where none stood. \emph{He's not Vail} stayed sealed as a lid nobody had tried to lift in three years, and the promise of the boy's name to the slate-burners stayed neither given nor withdrawn, gathering interest against \lore{the burned-slate word} at five and the four ownerless directions with a finger between every pair.

The afternoon had not yet come down off the walls when the hand outside the wool came up an inch toward the river's note under the winter skin. The inch was not a reach. He took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole on the note's own finishing under the covering --- neither early nor stayed --- and let it go as the note ended. Then the hand came straight down and laid itself flat against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking.

Answer three hundred and eighty-seven came out and the hand came down. It was exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the twelve readings, the first of the new count, the printed figure, the bell's gap, the flat knock, the child's grey fingertips, the mother's cup, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the held breath, the two bites of iron, the river's skin, the widow's mark, the seventh notch, the clerk's inward slate, Berr's low saying nor the keeping at the fold. Six honest answers stood on the sixth place, three hundred and eighty-two through three hundred and eighty-seven, and not one of them was a doctrine. The count came to four hundred and six exact, six months and two hundred and twenty-six days told by nobody who would have known what a number counted. The carried fire's fifth was unpaid the same number and its payer unnamed the same number; Garl's name was unspoken the same number, and the fire and the man stood side by side without becoming one. The four shapings of \lore{the well-saying} stood where put and did not repeat and bought no fifth; the four ownerless directions stood where put and bought no lesson; no tenth hand stood and no reader was made. No slate lay open within reach of the basket, no figure was given for the boy, and the month's second Account-day stood eight mornings beyond a day that had earned only its twelfth and was not brought into it. Nothing was opened, joined, named, called, paid, forgiven or ended; no door was drawn across the tend, and the surgeon's small clean satisfaction in a field taken as found and left as found cost him nothing, which was how he knew it was not peace. Under it, louder, the infant wanted the crust lifted, the thread wiped and a hand on the pot board's handle, and got none of the three. The crust's corner stood a hair off the wool and the thread was frozen and unwiped; four low pulls breathed below the floor with the fifth unsupplied; the keeping at the fold was found where put with no second fold; the first arithmetic of the year was up the clay road in Berr's locked box with no key in his hand; and the house went on as a house goes when a pot is set down in its place and the fire kept.
