Afternoon came down off the yard walls by small gains that nobody entered, the way warmth comes back to a room from a banked fire: not all at once, not by anybody's leave, but degree by degree, the boards giving back what the coals had given them and the cold held at the door without being asked in and without being told away. \person[Corin]{The boy} lay at the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf} in \place{the Vail kiln-house} in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill}, \place{Grath}, where the heat came to him sideways and not where he would have chosen, and the house kept what warmth it had the way the twice-mended pot on \place{the pot board} kept water --- with care, and with one hairline of a crack that nobody mentioned, the care the whole of the keeping and the hairline the whole of the cost, and neither entered on any slate under this roof. Under \place{the boards} \lore{the carried fire} gave four low pulls and kept the silence where the fifth should have stood, and nobody lifted the floor to supply what was wanting below it. Through \place{the finger's-width door} \place{the river} kept its note of \lore{water over water}, low under \lore{the winter skin}, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming, found where put and not spent on a tend-day. Behind the small sounds the large ones went on being weather: the kiln breathing in fours behind the wall, the wind going where the wind went, a rope settling. He let them be. Three counts in three places stayed three, because agreeing any two would have wanted a payer put under both and none stood.

From the basket's low angle he took the small sounds first, because the small sounds do not lie and the large ones are mostly weather. In \place{the Clay Yard} a pail went down on a stone where a pail always stood and did not look down to see whether it stood where it had stood before. The ridge lay where it lay. There was a word in the yard for grey put down and another for grey gone, and the two words were kept on two places with a finger between them, for a thing called gone is a thing somebody has agreed to stop looking at, and no such agreement had been made here.
