The afternoon came down off the yard walls by gains nobody entered, the way a pot cools --- not all at once but inch by inch, the shoulder giving up its heat before the belly, the belly before the foot. From the low angle of the basket \person[Corin]{the boy} took the small sounds first, because the small sounds do not lie and the large ones are mostly weather: the small dry rasp of a pail set where the pail always stood, a rope settling on the well's stone lip, a loaf pushed a finger's width along a board and stopped there with nobody calling the stopping a figure. \place{The yard well} had a bucket-round about it, the sort of gathering that is not a meeting and has no name, and \place{the trough} beside it held water that moved dark and made no sound. On \place{the flat stone by the well} a cup had stood and left its pale round place, which the yard called weather when the frost went out of it and called nothing at all the rest of the day. \place{The Clay Yard} went on in the grammar it had before he came under the roof and would go on in after he was gone from it, and he took that grammar as a field at rest, entered by its small signs and left as he found it.

\person{Ilva} drew with the pail against her knee. The rope, stiff in the dry cold, gave its small rasp as the bucket came up, and clay had dried pale in the crescents of her nails while the finer grey of the racks was worked into the seam of one thumb, which she did not scrub out. Her hands knew the weight of a pail and the edge of a wet board and had never been soft; people noticed the pail first and the sleeve not at all, which was the order she preferred. A woman waiting her turn with a pot against her hip, and another with her shawl drawn tight against the river wind, stood in the bucket-round and talked of the ordinary things --- barley, a knee in weather, the price of a hurdle --- and the talk went along the rope from one hand to the next without ever becoming a figure.

One of them said it low, into the space between her pot and the next woman's shoulder, in the tone in which a yard says the price of barley or the state of a knee: that \person[Hessa Rale]{the woman who carries the slate} had once carried a figure that named a person she knew. No owner stood under the word. No room came with it, no hand at a slate, no name for the person, no figure, no date, and nobody to ask. It stopped on the stone lip a moment, as a word will stop on a hard place, and then went off into the yard's ordinary talk as smoke goes off a chimney and is gone into the whole air. The bucket-round went on with its turns, and the wind got no answer because it had not wanted one.

The boy took the word apart the way he had taken the first arithmetic apart on the tenth: by the order of its parts and by what each part wanted. A shape is a shape, and the yard is full of shapes; the district's trouble had always been that a shape stood up and wanted a man put under it. There had been four of this one standing where they were put since the Frost well, four and not a witness, and here a fifth came up out of a woman's talk at a well and stopped on the stone lip, advanced and not paid. He gave it a place of its own on the tray where he kept such things, with a finger between it and the runner's eighth record-first hand and another finger between it and the saying of the road from \place{the Cross} to \place{Greybank}. A third finger went between it and the four ownerless directions --- the Ash 5 joining of \lore{the burned-slate word} to \lore{the counting room} shut in the war, and the directions that came afterward from \place{Slate Row} --- so that none of them should touch and make a column come out even.

It was not a witness. A witness stands somewhere and can be asked; this word stood nowhere and there was nobody standing to ask. It was not a confession, for no mouth had owned the figure and no ear had taken it into an account; it was not a reader made, for no slate had been opened within reach of the basket and no figure had come off any wood into a hand; and it was not a sixth shaping, and no sixth was made due by the coming of a fifth. A fifth is a fifth. \lore{The well-saying} went on gathering interest toward a hand not his that would one day lay the district's private keepings together and call the joining an account, but that hand was far off and no part of it stood in this yard this afternoon.

The woman with the pot asked what Ilmen's firings had taken. Ilva's hand went toward her sleeve before her eyes went to the asker, and her mouth opened after both, with the half-beat lying in the gap where a lesser observer would have put an intention. The short strip of birch bark lay in the fold where it always lay, pale where a thumb had rubbed the notches and dark in the uncut grain between. It was not taken out, read, copied, counted or joined to the word that had gone off into the yard, and no figure came off the wood. She answered the question put and not the one behind it.

“Ask the pot what it cost. I keep the count, not the account,” she said, and put the flat of her hand once more on the sleeve and went back to the pail, and nobody turned twice.

Her ninth record-first hand stayed nine. She was expressly not the speaker of the well-saying: the well had given up a word and the woman at the well had given a count, and the two things went on separate places with a finger between. He made no tenth hand of her and no reader of her keeping, and the notches stayed as they were, a count in wood for the far-off hand not his. A woman who keeps a count in wood refuses to make an account of a word, and a boy who cannot reach a cup keeps the two refusals apart because they are not the same refusal and do not want the same payer.

He did not lay the fifth shaping across \person{Tarn}'s ended term, or across the degree toward the landing, or across \person{Jorra Vail}'s promise neither given nor withdrawn, or across the face of the man from Slate Row filed on its tray, or across the first arithmetic gone up \place{the clay road} in \person[Berr Clay]{Berr}'s locked box. Likenesses stood up want a man put under every one of them, and no man stood. A word at a well and a figure in a box were two things with a finger between, and the finger cost nothing and bought nothing, which was how he knew it was not an account.

At the going of the light \person{Sella Vail} came to \place{the hearth} before the basket, as she had come at both ends of every day since he came under this roof, and told the coals it was \lore{the thaw} in the voice another woman would have used for a child who could not hear her. She turned them with the back of a broken pot. She gave the small intake, the hold and the letting-go toward no opening, and figured neither of the two gaps; a small thing done toward nothing, the way a woman breathes before a lift she has already decided not to make. Her finger lay along the top of \place{the loaf shelf} so the charcoal tally on its underside stayed unread, and her lips moved on a two-gapped row and figured neither gap. One glance went to the clay jar standing cold under \lore{lead glaze} beyond the board, and the hand shut half over nothing, the little-door burn on the back of her left hand closing on air, and went back to the pot. At her throat \lore{the widow's mark} hung where it hung and was not touched: a hearth, a shelf, a well, a going of the light and a basket are not openings, seven openings in seven months are seven and not a rule, and no eighth was made due on a day that had earned only its twelfth.

She lifted the twice-mended pot from \place{the pot board} thumb-first where the tighter wire crossed the crack, and the hairline of the original break showed between the old-brass staple and the wire, a dark thread with a hand's work on either side of it. \person{Garl Vail}{Garl} was in that hairline, and in the salt-stiff coat with its left-shoulder crease on its peg behind the door, and in the hollow wheel-seat in the shed that nobody was allowed to fill, and not in a story anybody told. His name had not been spoken under the roof for four hundred and six days, and the two counts --- his name and the fire's fifth --- stood side by side without becoming one. She said both proverbs to the pot and the fire and never to a person: \emph{Leave the crack showing. People trust a thing they can see has been handled} and \emph{If it's worth mending it's worth mending twice. The second time is the real one}. Repair was not restoration; a restored thing hides the hand that handled it and a mended thing leaves the hand showing, and a surgeon keeps a graft apart from a cure for the same reason a potter leaves a staple in the light. \lore{The tile debt} against a firing of tiles went on circling without a name and worse for being uncalled, and no collector came up the road on a Secondday to call it.

At \place{the rack end} the dressed grain lay covered under wool on the cool stone away from the hearth. She lifted a corner and turned what was under it with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child, piece by piece, setting each down where it had lain, and took no figure from it and no handful for herself. The arithmetic read over grain covered at home did not know what the grain cost, and the space between the figure and the price had no signature on it; she, who could not enter a figure anywhere and had no hand to lift a crust two inches, set none against it. The wool went back over the grain with the same light touch and the space stayed as it was. Then the pot went down on the board again with its rim two inches beyond the reaching hand, and there was a half-beat at the setting and no look toward \place{the bed}. The half-beat was the distance between an intention and an arrest: her hand had meant to set the pot nearer and had stopped where it stopped, and the distance cost her nothing and bought the boy nothing, and neither of them called it a gift. Under the roof nobody instructs a basket, and nobody instructs a pot about a hand.

She carried him up the nineteen steps at the half-pace she keeps from every open slate, correcting the tilt at every turn of the landing before it had time to settle into the wicker, and the instruction went to the wool, the river wind, the pot, the fire and the banked fire and never to the basket. \place{The seven walked-around places} were gone around with a look from the basket's low angle and a wide step that belonged to a hand larger than his, once and not twice, widest around \place{the returning-line place} where a hand had gone in on \emph{Seed 15} and had not come back; the wide step was a keeping and not a sign, and a figure set against it would want a payer standing under it and none stood. He was set under the roof and never into \place{the Debt Loft}. She put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face and added no second fold to the crust's empty place, and \lore{the keeping at the fold} was found where put and still a finding, with no name over it and no payer under it. The ridge at \place{the Vail corner} kept its clean dark line and its finer grey called the thaw, thickened in the dry cold with the sour tang of \lore{Ash} where no broom had gone in two years, and she did not sweep it and did not say why.

At \place{the kindling} Tarn kept six pieces in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check and the seventh at the lip beyond it, beside Garl's unforged collector sentence with no name on the man who turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on the tile debt. The small knife lay at the very edge of its sill, and his hand went over blade and sill as a man passes a hand over a tool put back where it belongs, and came away with no breath held at the top of the reach --- the coming away the whole of the checking. He cut nothing, counted nothing, spent nothing, and told the wood in the tone of a man stating the weather, \emph{Six counted. The next I don't count}. He took the broom only after \place{the flagstones} had been given their instruction, said \emph{Yes, house} to the question and to the thing behind it, agreeing so thoroughly that the question had nowhere left to put a foot, and went over the stones without a word and without a turn of the head toward \place{the out-of-line landing}. The degree toward the landing was not repeated; it stayed a shape found where put on a place of its own.

The ear-sentence about an ear under this roof being worth more than the debt that holds it came all the way to the word that would call the loan and stopped there, a remembered ninth stopping with no tenth, an object and not a rule, an interval and not a payment; the \emph{Seed 16} breath stayed unspent and the loan uncalled at the very end of a term already ended, and a term ending is not a payment, a forgiveness or a freedom but leaves a man more exposed than it found him. Ash worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line, the pale clip-ring at his left wrist catching and giving back what little light there was. He owned nothing and knew where every small thing was, and the fear of being entered again for vagrancy stayed his without a figure. He did not look along the road, and the word that had come up at the well did not come to the kindling to be made a cause.

Jorra came through \place{the finger's-width door} from \place{the Vail wheel-shed} with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past his bent little finger and white in a fist-crease where a hand had opened, and it was not the bone-coloured \lore{slip} of the wheel. Three or more men unnamed had gone low into \place{the back room} before first grey and got no name from him and no word that the room was cold, and the broken ones under the lowest rack stayed entered by nobody. He kept dusty forearms and clean on one tray and parted them as clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had been broken nor whether it was a birth-slate, for a question joined to a cause wants a payer standing and none stood. He spoke to the wheel before any person --- \emph{It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady} --- and told a pot \emph{You'll do} in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either, knowing the \lore{full note} that pot would give three turns before it left his hands, with air under it and a want in it, and withholding the knowing from any master come up from \place{High Kiln} who would call it early and sing a pot before its time.

Sella had not finished telling him the fire was low when his bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper} ahead of her sentence; he took the cold out of the fingertip, moved his tongue a hair's breadth and was left standing so the end of her instruction arrived at the pot and the household straightened around the waiting as cloth straightens when a weight is lifted. One piece of iron had two bites, the holding and the taking, and no Grathic word covered both mercies; he took the finger out without leaving the extra holding of \emph{Ash 26} a second time, gathered once and not made again, for a second would be a habit and a third a way and both want a payer where none stood. \emph{He's not Vail} stayed sealed as a lid nobody had tried to lift in three years, and the promise of the boy's name lying unspoken under the shed stayed neither given nor withdrawn. He gave the well-word no nod and the runner no name, and the two things went on their separate trays with a finger between.

In the small hours \place{the towel} came out at the head of the bed as instruments come out when a case is done and before the next is called, rough from the wash and smelling of tallow and low smoke, and he went over the field by the place of each thing with every gap left a gap. The fifth shaping sat on its own tray between the eighth record-first hand and the Cross-to-Greybank road saying, with a finger between it and each and a third finger between it and the four ownerless directions, found where put and not made again and not made due. The first arithmetic of the year stood gone up the road in Berr's box with no key in any hand under this roof; twelve old findings stood behind one unsigned figure beside the first of the fresh count, and the new figure was not laid across the twelve to make a row come out even, for paper is not a payer. The keeping at the fold was found where put and still a finding, with no second fold, no name over it and no payer under it. Six hearings of the full note stood beside and never among with the seventh place empty and not dug for or filled, and \place{the Drawing house}'s small flat knock stayed outside them on its own place. Nine record-first hands stood with no tenth and nine stoppings stood with no tenth; the clean-voice speaker was taken out of his own sentences on a place of his own, and the face at the edge was filed beside the hands and not counted among them. Four rooms kept the same gap with a finger between every pair and no Court room came under the roof as a fifth. The green at the pot board and the shape at the market's edge stayed a colour and a shape with no eighth of either; the clerk's inward slate stayed at the landing reported and not witnessed; Tarn's degree stayed a shape found where put; Garl's unforged collector sentence lay beside the seventh notch; the man from Slate Row's face stayed on its own tray. The fifth shaping bought no witness, no reader, no sixth and no payment; the row at five askings, the burned-slate word at five and the shut-room tellings at two stood found where put and were not repeated on a day that had earned only its twelfth.

Answer three hundred and eighty-eight came out and the hand came down. It was exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the twelve readings, the first of the new count, the printed figure, the fifth shaping, the bell's gap, the flat knock, the child's grey fingertips, the mother's cup, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the held breath, the two bites of iron, the river's skin, the widow's mark, the seventh notch, the clerk's inward slate, Berr's low saying nor the keeping at the fold. Six honest answers stood on the sixth place, three hundred and eighty-three through three hundred and eighty-eight, and not one of them was a doctrine. The count came to four hundred and six exact, six months and two hundred and twenty-six days told by nobody who would have known what a number counted. \lore{The carried fire}'s fifth was unpaid the same number and its payer unnamed the same number; Garl's name was unspoken the same number, and the fire and the man stood side by side without becoming one. No slate lay open within reach of the basket, no figure was given for the boy, and the month's second Account-day stood eight mornings beyond a day that had earned only its twelfth and was not brought into it. Nothing was opened, joined, named, called, paid, forgiven or ended; no door was drawn across the tend, and the surgeon's satisfaction in a field taken as found and left as found cost him nothing, which was how he knew it was not peace. Under it, louder, the infant wanted the crust lifted, the thread wiped and a hand on the pot board's handle, and got none of the three.

The hand outside the wool came up an inch toward \place{the river}'s note of \lore{water over water}, low under \lore{the winter skin} through the finger's-width door. The inch was not a reach. He took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole on the note's own finishing under the covering --- neither early nor stayed --- and let it go as the note ended. Then the hand came straight down and laid itself flat against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. The fifth shaping sat on its own tray with a finger between it and the eighth hand and another between it and the road saying; the crust's corner stood a hair off the wool and the thread was frozen and unwiped; four low pulls breathed below the floor with the fifth unsupplied; and the word that had come up from a well in the afternoon was gone into the small hours with nothing under the roof changed hands.
