# Chapter 118 — beat sheet: A Word at the Well

Days 405–407 · Ilmen 11–13, Y.H. 814, Firstday to Thirdday. Three lived days, six scenes, two full scenes to each day, every day opened by its own dated `\daymark` and never skipped, poured into another or summed up. Normal mode. Answers **385–390**; count **405 exact** at the opening to **407 exact** at the close — six months and two hundred and twenty-five to two hundred and twenty-seven days told by nobody. Ilmen 20 (Day 414), the month's second Account-day, stands seven mornings beyond the close and is not brought forward. No present-tense break and no `\memory{}` tag; the white room, the page over the speakers and the hands that stayed in the field go on gathering interest from Chapter 113 and are not spent. Point of view never leaves Dan/Corin; no Court room entered, no collection interior, no Debt Loft entered, no back room entered, and the well is a stone lip and a bucket-round, not a room with an owner.

**The chapter's one meaningful change (advanced, NOT paid):** on Ilmen 12 a word comes up once at the yard well that the woman who carries the slate once carried a figure that named a person she knew — a **fifth shaping** of the well-saying (T080), filed on its own tray between Hessa Rale's eighth hand and the Cross-to-Greybank road saying with a finger between every pair. No owner stands, no name is spoken over the figure, no reader is made, and no sixth shaping is made due; the four ownerless Ash and Slate directions are not made a sixth shaping and are not joined to it. Ilva draws at the well, keeps her count in wood and is expressly not the speaker of any voice.

## The pull into Chapter 119

The chapter closes with the hand laid down on answer **390**, the count exact at **407** — six months and two hundred and twenty-seven days told by nobody — the carried fire's fifth unpaid and Garl's name unspoken the same number, the first arithmetic of Y.H. 814 gone up the clay road in Berr's locked box with no key in any hand here, and the fifth shaping of the well-saying held on its own tray with a finger between it and Hessa's eighth hand and the road saying. Nothing was joined, named, called, paid or ended: the shaping did not become a witness, the figure did not become the price, the keeping at the fold stayed a finding, and the boy's three wants were not granted. That held quiet is exactly what Chapter 119 opens on — Day 408, Ilmen 14, Fourthday, the hand laid down on answer 390 and the count at 407 — and the interest gathered here goes forward with it: the fifth shaping and the unread slate toward the far-off hand that will lay the private keepings together, Tarn's valuable ear and ended term toward the clerk who will enter a figure before he looks up on Ilmen 15, Jorra's ungiven promise toward the burned slate at five and the four directions, and Berr's kindness toward the day it is remembered against him. Ilmen 20's second reading waits seven mornings beyond this close, unbrought and not made a boundary; the story moves forward on the ordinary grammar of the house, a pot set down in its place and the fire kept.
