## Chronicle

Bone came on without a boundary drawn; beneath the Vail roof the boy came into his three hundred and eighteenth, nineteenth and twentieth mornings, untold. The count went from three hundred and seventeen to three hundred and twenty; the fire's fifth stayed unpaid, Garl's name unspoken. Each morning the body was inventoried in its old order --- crust, unnamed gap, frozen thread, cold, the waiting afterdrop --- and the surgeon's knowing bought nothing at two inches of air. The infant wanted the crust lifted and the thread wiped. Both were true, the second louder; neither won, and the argument was the work.

Sella went to the fire before the basket, told the coals it was the thaw, and said *The house will keep it* where another woman would have said I. She lifted the twice-mended pot on the thumb where the wire crossed the crack, left the crack showing, turned the dressed grain beneath the wool with no figure, and set the pot rim two inches beyond the reaching hand. She did not touch the widow's mark: five openings in five months are five and not a rule. Garl stayed in objects, his name not spoken.

In the yard she went around seven walked-around places; the three coverings at the well stayed weather and entered nowhere. Tarn kept six where the house could check and left the Seed 16 breath unspent. Jorra put his bent finger into the cold damper ahead of Sella's unfinished sentence. Ilva drew at the well on Bone 20; her hand went to her sleeve, the notched strip did not come out, and she answered *Ask the pot what it cost. I keep the count, not the account.* The boy filed her reaching as the ninth record-first hand, no tenth.

On Bone 20 the district's second reading came to the Cross. Berr Clay read *One in twelve, as the notice says*, sent the figure twice over the basket, called the levy and denied it with *your house*, never I, and went off up the clay road with the box locked, believing it a kindness. Five arithmetics now stand behind one printed figure: five and not one account, the figure read still not the figure paid, no signature on the space. Hessa carried his slate up unread; the bell went four, four and a gap with nobody crossing himself, and nothing under the roof was paid by any of it.

Six answers went whole on the river's note --- the two hundred and eleventh through the two hundred and sixteenth --- each worth nothing at two inches of air, no payer named, none repeating the stayed inch or the early letting-go. At the close the file lay open on the towel with every gap left gap, the two small objects of the hand on separate places, two hundred and sixteen honest answers standing on the sixth, and three hundred and twenty exact and spoken by nobody. Bone went on without a boundary drawn.
