\daymark{320}{Bone 21, Firstday, Y.H. 813}

The file lay open on \place{the towel} at the head of \place{the bed} with every gap left gap, every tray parted by a finger's width and nothing moved. The hand lay back against the fold from the two hundred and fourteenth answer as a tool is put away where the next hand will find it without looking. It had gone up an inch toward the low note under the finger's-width door, taken its small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, let it go whole on that same note and come straight down; the note had done before the hand came down, so the letting-go was neither early again nor stayed. There it lay, put away in the dark, exact and complete and worth nothing at two inches of air. The small hours went on the way water goes on under ice, from the inside, by a change in the air that no hand had made and nobody had marked.

The twenty-first came on the way a vessel comes to the top of a kiln. The older women of the district named their months by the weather and the slatemen named them by the count of Account-days, and neither habit was set right or left wrong under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof; the month carried both names the way a pot carries two handles and is none the worse for it. \person[Corin]{The boy} came into his three hundred and twentieth day with nobody saying so, and six months and one hundred and forty days were told by nobody in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill} and \place{Grath}. The count went from three hundred and nineteen to three hundred and twenty exact, complete, unspent, spoken by no man under any roof. \lore{The carried fire}'s fifth stood unpaid the same number of days with its payer unnamed, and \person{Garl Vail}'s name had not been spoken under the roof the same number. No market, no Cross, no bell on a Firstday, and nothing lifted a hand in the dark to mark the turning. Nothing that had gone over the basket on the twentieth came back under this roof as a sign, a lesson, a mending, a cause or a payment, and the figure was not read again. He had stopped expecting anybody to tell him what the number counted, which was not the same as not wanting it, and the wanting went on under the not-asking the way a coal goes on under ash.

He read the room by its small sounds first, because the small sounds do not lie. The coals breathed under blind ash with the dry, sour smell of hot clay gone cold, and tallow and wet wool lay over it, and under everything the faint mineral breath of the \lore{Ash} ridge along the skirting board, which nobody had swept in two years and nobody had named. Through the finger's-width door the river kept its note of \lore{water over water}, a low thing over a lower thing, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming, never numbered among the hearings. The little door of the fire's warmth stood shut on air. He took the inventory in its old order, the way a man reads the numbers at the edge of a chart before the writing in the middle, and entered it nowhere, not even on the place where he kept the things he did not enter.

One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from his ear, gone stiffer in the dry heat of the kiln, its corner standing a hair off the wool. The empty place beside it kept its name to itself and no third had come in to make the row come out even. The thread at his mouth had frozen in the dry heat and was unwiped, for nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread, and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. Cold had stopped at cheek, temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, as though it had come to the end of a line written out for it with neither an order to go farther nor an order to go away. \lore{The Afterdrop} waited beyond all three, past a warming nobody in this house would perform.

The surgeon in him took the room as he had once taken a field, by the small sounds first, then the shape of the loss, then the question of what could be done and how little of it there was; the infant in him took the same room as a warmth that was not where he wanted it. The two readings were of one room and agreed on nothing, and both were true. He knew the ease a cold face puts on for what it is not, the composed face that is not composure; he knew a cold patient cannot clot and will seem better before he is worse and must be warmed from the outside and slowly, never rubbed, never set too near the fire. The knowing bought nothing at two inches of air. The infant underneath wanted the crust lifted to his mouth, the thread at his mouth wiped and a hand that would close on the pot handle set beyond his reach. Both were true and the second was louder and neither won, and the two voices argued without a word between them the way a man argues in his sleep, and the argument was the work.

\scenebreak

Before the basket came the fire. \person{Sella Vail} crossed to \place{the hearth} in the dark with her little-door burn shut on nothing, told the coals it was the thaw and turned them with the back of a broken pot, the way a woman turns a sleeping animal without waking it. She stood a moment with the back of the pot in her hand counting small intake, hold and letting-go under her breath toward no opening, and gave neither gap a figure. \emph{The house will keep it}, she said to the fire, where another woman would have said \emph{I}; the words went to the coals and stopped there. The dry heat had gone the crust leathery and gone the hem of the wool brittle, and the room smelled of tallow, clay dust and the old sour mineral, and she moved through it as a woman moves through a room she has arranged so that nothing will fall.

At \place{the loaf shelf} her finger lay on top of the front edge so that the charcoal beneath stayed unread, and her lips moved along a row standing with two gaps, and she figured neither gap. She glanced once at the clay jar standing cold under \lore{lead glaze} by the wall and shut her hand half over nothing, the burn closing on air, and gave the jar no word. In the yard the morning would go around what the yard went around, but here under the roof nothing had been brought in to explain the two gaps or the cold jar or the space where a loaf was not; a figure laid across any of them would have wanted a payer put under it, and none stood.

She lifted the twice-mended pot from \place{the pot board} with her thumb first where the wire crossed the crack, felt what the thumb had to feel, and only then let the weight come on the handle. The hairline of the original break showed between the old-brass staple and the tighter wire, and she left it showing. \emph{If it's worth mending it's worth mending twice. The second time is the real one}, she said to the pot, and \emph{Leave the crack showing. People trust a thing they can see has been handled}, she said to the fire, and to no person. The sayings had improved in the keeping and were very likely not as good as the man had said them; they had been said so often to a pot and a fire that they had gone past being about either. The pot answered with a small flat sound when she set it down, the sound a vessel makes that has been closed in a place the hand would not have chosen, and the boy kept that sound in its own place and did not make a hearing of it.

She turned the dressed grain beneath the wool at \place{the rack end} with a lighter touch than she had used the day before, lifting a corner, turning what lay hidden, setting the fold back and giving no figure to anything. The arithmetic that had gone over that grain had not known what it cost, and had gone up \place{the clay road} and had not come back as a lesson or a mending, and the space between its figure and the price of the grain had no signature. He read the turn of the wool as a surgeon turns a wound to see the part that lies hidden, and asked nothing of it.

Then she set the pot rim two inches beyond the reaching hand, handle toward him, with a half-beat and no look toward the bed, and gave the instruction to the wool and to the river wind and never to the basket: \emph{Keep it off his ears. It's the river wind}. She put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without looking at his face, her hand resting a half-beat longer than the setting needed before it went away, and left the thread where it lay. \lore{The widow's mark} hung where it hung on its cord at her throat, a thumb-length of lead-glazed clay with the Vail kiln scratched into it, and she did not touch it; a morning is not an opening, and five openings in five months were five and not a rule, with the next far off. The boy watched the order of it --- the hand to the fire before the hand to the basket, the finger to the shelf before the eyes to the row, the thumb to the crack before the weight to the handle --- and filed none of it as a cause, because an order of reaching is an order of reaching and not a message sent.

\scenebreak

Under the floor the carried fire gave four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer without a name for three hundred and twenty days, and nobody lifted the floor and nobody supplied the pull. \place{The boards} overhead kept four nails on the near board, four on the next and a thumb-width knot where a fifth might have been driven and was not. The river kept its water over water under the door. Three counts in three places did not become one, and agreeing any two of them would have wanted a payer put under both and none stood; the bell's gap left behind in the square was not fetched in to make a fourth, and the heard bell stayed in its own square beside the told bell of \lore{the Long Night}. Six hearings of \lore{the full note} stood beside and never among, the sixth the small flat knock out of a woman as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go, and the seventh place stayed empty and was not filled to make the row come out even, for an empty place kept empty is a finding and a finding filled is a finding forged.

Five arithmetics of the year stood behind one printed figure: the four that had gone before and the fifth beside them. One in twelve stood unchallenged in every notice, the figure read was still not the figure paid, and no signature stood on the space between. Five were five and not one account. A rule made out of five would have wanted a payer put under all five and no payer stood under any of them, and the boy kept the finding on a place of its own with no payer under it, not a lesson, not a grievance, not a mending, not a doctrine. The five true subtractions rode locked up the clay road in \person{Berr Clay}'s box toward \place{High Kiln}, unread under this roof and not brought back as an answer to anything. He did not lay the finding across the house's \lore{not-doings}, or across the dressed grain under its wool, or across the four held changes on their four places --- the returned half-check on \place{the flagstones}, the Day 161 fraction at \lore{the damper}, the Day 166 beat beneath the shelf and the Frost 5 returned beat --- or across the stayed-at-the-inch object, or across the early letting-go, or across his own kept gaps. The edges came near to touching, so near that a less careful hand would have pushed them together and called the touching a cause, and the finger went between.

The staying stayed one object and the early letting-go stayed one object, each on its own place with a finger between them and between them and every other object on the sixth. Neither was made again in the morning, neither stood up as a habit or a way, neither became a fifth held change or a doctrine with a payer. The burned slate kept its five tellings with a finger between the fifth and the four and no sixth due on a day that had not earned one; the row kept five askings with one shaping and no witness; the well-saying kept three shapings and the shut-room tellings two; nine hands stood with no tenth and nine stoppings with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end; the seventh green was a colour and the seventh shape a shape with no eighth. None of these paid anything here and none of them was laid across the other, for a row of likenesses is a payer's tool and there was no payer standing to be asked.

He turned the file out on the towel at the head of the bed the way he had once turned instruments out on a towel at the end of a list, laying each thing on its own place with a finger's width between, asking nothing of any of it and answering none of its questions. Two hundred and fourteen honest answers stood on the sixth place, each an object and not a power, and neither the number nor the small hand-movings that had made it stood up and called themselves a doctrine, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood. The file lay open with every gap left gap and nothing that had been entered that morning asked to be joined to anything else.

In the morning the hand outside the wool went up an inch toward the river's note. It took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole, let the intake go on the same low note, and came straight down and laid itself back against the fold; the note had done before the hand came down, so the letting-go was not early a second time and the hand did not stay. He filed it as answer two hundred and fifteen: exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment, borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the five arithmetics nor the bell's gap nor the covered grain nor his own kept gaps. Two hundred and fifteen honest answers stood on the sixth place and the older man took a clean satisfaction in the keeping, the satisfaction of a field left as it was found, while the infant underneath wanted the crust lifted the two inches to his mouth and the thread at his mouth wiped. Both were true and the second was louder and neither won, and they went on arguing without a word as the light came up past the finger's-width door.

Three hundred and twenty stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in Low Kiln, Sill and Grath; six months and one hundred and forty days were told by nobody; the carried fire's fifth was unpaid the same number of days with its payer unnamed; and Garl's name had not been spoken under the roof the same number. Nothing on the twenty-first had been opened, joined, named, called, paid or ended. The keeping of the number was the only honest work there was to do and bought nothing at two inches of air, and the count went on the way the river goes, water over water, a low thing over a lower thing, with the file lying open on the towel and every gap left gap.
