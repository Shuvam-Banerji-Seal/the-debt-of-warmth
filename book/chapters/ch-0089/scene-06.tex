The light went out of \place{the Clay Yard} the way a pot goes cold, from the rim in, by degrees that no hand hastened and no hand stayed. \person{Sella Vail} came for the basket at the half-pace she had kept from every open slate all month, gave the instruction to the wool and the river wind and never to the basket, and lifted \person[Corin]{the boy} as a woman lifts a pot that is full and must not be slopped. Nothing had been said over him at the going of the light and nothing was said now. The month went on without a boundary drawn. The older women of the district named it by the weather that had not come and the slatemen named it by its count of Account-days, and neither habit was set right or left wrong under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof; the month carried both names the way a pot carries two handles and is none the worse for it. Bone 30 stood nine mornings ahead and was not brought into a day that had not earned it.

She went wide around the places the yard goes around, with a look and a wide step and no number. At the Vail corner the clean dark line where the \lore{Ash} ridge had turned lay where it lay, and the finer grey hairline beside it that the older women called \lore{the thaw} lay beside it unswept, the old dark under the new fine as a seam lies under a later stitching; a broom went around the two together and took neither. Beneath \place[the drying racks]{the lowest rack} the broken birth-slates kept their own shadow, their edges paler than the rack dust sifted over them, and the sweeping went as wide of the space as a pail of hot water set where a child might knock it. On the stone beside \place{the yard well} a round ring held the place where a shallow clay pan had once stood, with no scrape, no crumb, no print, no chip and no word on it, only the darker circle where the pan's bottom had kept the dust off. \place{the trough} had a skin drawn from edge to edge. Out on \place{the worked strips} the field kept its empty place, a direction and not a thing standing; and in the returning line the place where a hand had gone in on Seed 15 and had not come out with the last hands kept its own emptiness, which the yard went around wider than all the rest. Seven places, gone around with a look and a wide step and no figure spoken over any of them; no eighth likeness was stood up, no cause laid across two of them, and no payer named to the going-around. A thing a household walks around is a thing it still keeps, and the keeping was maintenance and not a story.

The month's hunts and repairs went past as directions judged by the carrying arm and the weather and never as figures. A man come down from \place{High Kiln} with a hare across his shoulders shifted the weight when a woman at the well asked what the carrying had cost, and told her to ask the weather, which had carried the snow and could carry the price; he went off toward \place{the clay road} with nobody weighing the hare and nobody opening a slate. Another with a cracked jar in a sling of rope asked what the mending of the month would want, and was told to ask the pot; and the pot, being a pot, said nothing. The boy read the two answers as a surgeon reads a chart that has been left blank in the space where the number should stand: a direction arrived nowhere, a question put to a vessel that cannot answer, and no figure coming off either. He entered neither. The yard's ordinary work went on being done by arms that knew what they carried, and the knowing was in the arm and not on any slate.

\scenebreak

At \place{the yard well} the three coverings stayed called weather and entered nowhere. The frost that had stayed had gone off the north lip on Bone 3 while the yard was looking elsewhere, gone in the way a thing goes when no hand takes it and no word is spent on its going, and the north lip was bare; the place where it had been was not a fourth likeness to set beside the two that remained. The trough kept its skin, a grey membrane drawn tight, and when a pail knocked it the skin broke and closed again and was called weather. On \place{the flat stone by the well}, out of the path of feet, the plate of ice clouded with silt stood where it had been set, a piece of a depth lifted up and carried three paces and stood where nobody would step. The boy kept the gone white, the skin on the water and the lifted plate on three places with a finger between every pair, and made no cause of the going or the staying. A covering laid over a depth, the same covering over another depth, and a piece of the depth taken up and set aside: three acts of three hands, and a habit would have wanted a payer under all three, and none stood. No fourth likeness was made a cause and no figure came off any of them, and the yard went on drawing its buckets and calling it weather.

The four held changes kept their four places. On \place{the flagstones} the returned half-check of Bone 8 lay where it had been returned, the shallow bright patch that Tarn's stroke had once stopped short of and now went over without the check, the stone keeping the place where the stroke had stopped as a thing nobody entered. At \lore{the damper} the Day 161 fraction stood; beneath \place{the loaf shelf} the Day 166 beat; and the Frost 5 returned beat kept where it had been returned. Four held changes on four places and not a rule. A rule made out of four would have wanted a payer put under all four and none stood to any of them. The half-check had not been made again on the twenty-first, and a thing kept by not being made again is still kept: it had not gone because nobody had brought it out, the way the white had gone off the well lip while the yard looked elsewhere, and the keeping of it was a maintenance as exact as the keeping of a fire. The boy set the four on their four places with a finger between every pair and did not let the fifth likeness stand up, because a fifth likeness would have made a row and a row would have asked who stood under it.

\scenebreak

The staying stayed one object and the early letting-go stayed one object, each on its own place on the sixth with a finger between them and between them and every other object there. The staying was the inch at which the hand had held after the note had done, and the early letting-go was the intake released before the note was done, which the surgeon had read as a clamp taken off before the field had dried and the infant as a hand that gave up before it was done wanting. Both were true, neither had won, and neither had been made again on the twenty-first. They did not stand up as a habit, a way, a fifth held change or a doctrine with a payer; nobody under any roof had seen either hand-movement and nobody had spoken a number over either. The finger went between them as it went between all the other objects, and the space between was not empty but kept.

Five arithmetics of the year stood behind one printed figure. One in twelve was unchallenged in every notice, the figure read was still not the figure paid, and no signature stood on the space between; five readings were five and not one account. The five true subtractions rode locked up the clay road in \person{Berr Clay}'s box toward \place{High Kiln}, unread under this roof and not brought back as an answer to anything. A rule made out of five would have wanted a payer put under all five and no payer stood under any of them, and the boy kept the finding on a place of its own with no payer under it, not a lesson, not a grievance, not a mending, not a doctrine. He did not lay it across the house's \lore{not-doings}, or across the dressed grain covered under wool at \place{the rack end} and read over by a number that did not know its cost, or across the four held changes on their four places, or across the two small objects of the hand, or across his own kept gaps. The edges came near to touching, so near that a less careful hand would have pushed them together and called the touching a cause, and the finger went between. The finding had cost him nothing anybody could have entered and bought nothing at two inches of air, and he carried it because it was true and not because it paid.

The whole holding tray came out unchanged. The burned slate kept its five tellings with a finger between the fifth and the four, no sixth due on a day that had not earned one, no witness standing and no hand named at any flame; the claim went on gathering interest against a promise neither given nor withdrawn and paid nothing here. The shut-room tellings stood at two and not three. The row kept five askings with one shaping and no witness, and expressly no sixth asking; the well-saying kept three shapings; the pot sayings three with the Smoke 14 note on a place of its own; nine record-first hands stood with \person{Ilva}'s strip unread in her sleeve and no tenth; nine stoppings stood with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end and the loan uncalled. Six hearings of \lore{the full note} stood beside and never among, the sixth the small flat knock out of a woman as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go and never among the sources; the seventh place stayed empty and was not filled to make the row come out even, for an empty place kept empty is a finding and a finding filled is a finding forged. Five openings in five months stood where they stood, the fifth an act that had mended nothing anybody named, with \lore{the widow's mark} hanging where it hung and the next opening far off; the seventh green was a colour and the seventh shape a shape with no eighth. None of these paid anything here and none was laid across any other, for a row of likenesses is a payer's tool and there was no payer standing to be asked.

\scenebreak

At \place{the kindling} \person{Tarn} kept six where the house could check and the seventh at the lip beyond, where it could not, with \person{Garl Vail}'s unforged collector sentence lying beside the seventh, no name on the man who had turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on the debt. He told the wood \emph{Six counted. The next I don't count} in the tone of a man stating the weather, and put the little knife back at the very edge of its sill where it could be checked and where a man's hand could also slip it off; he announced it to nobody, because the yard had already been told. The sentence about an ear under this roof being worth more than the debt that held it came up to the word that would call the loan and stopped there, with no figure and no name, a ninth stopping with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end. The Seed 16 breath stayed unspent at the top of every reach and his chest had been tight since the dark. Ash was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line and no scrubbing lifted it, and the pale clip-ring at his left wrist held what little light there was and gave it back. No collector came up the clay road or went down it, and the tile debt circled without a name and was worse for being uncalled.

At the shed's edge \person{Jorra Vail} parted dusty forearms from clean as a surgeon parts clot from vessel, meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bent little finger and white in the creases where a fist had opened, not bone-coloured slip. Three or more men had gone low into \place{the back room} before first grey and he gave the house no name for them and did not say the room was cold. He spoke to the wheel before he spoke to any person, told a pot \emph{You'll do} in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either, and knew the full note that pot would give three turns before it left his hands, with air under it and a want in it, and withheld the knowing on the near-full tray from a kiln-master out of High Kiln who would have called it early and made something of it; a pot that sings early generally sings itself out early. Before she had finished telling him the fire was low his bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper}, took the cold out of the fingertip, moved the tongue a hair's breadth and left it standing, so that the end of her instruction arrived at the pot. The boy read one piece of iron with two bites, the holding and the taking, with no Grathic word for both mercies, joined to no kiln and entered in no slate. The dust and the damper and the men unnamed went unasked about, and \emph{he's not Vail} stayed sealed as tightly as a lid nobody had tried to lift in three years.

Garl stayed where he had always stayed, in objects and not in a story. The salt-stiff coat with its crease at the left shoulder kept a broader man's shape on its peg behind the door, the wheel-seat in the shed kept its twenty-year lean and its hollow on that side, and Sella let no one fill it. His sayings went to the pot and the fire and never to a person, \emph{Leave the crack showing. People trust a thing they can see has been handled} and \emph{If it's worth mending it's worth mending twice. The second time is the real one}; they had improved in the keeping and were very likely not as good as the man had said them. His name had not been spoken under the roof for three hundred and twenty days, and keeping him was maintenance and not a story, and no lesson was made of any of it. The finding of five arithmetics behind one printed figure was not laid across this absence, and the carried fire still missed its fifth with its payer unnamed, and no payer stood to any of it.

\scenebreak

Sella carried him up at the going of the light at the half-pace and set him on \place{the bed} under the roof, never in \place{the Debt Loft}. She said nothing about where they had been or about the questions that had gone unanswered at the well; the instruction went to the wool and the river wind and to the fire before it went to the basket, and a basket set down under a roof is a pot set down and left. She put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face, her hand resting a half-beat longer than the setting needed before it went away, and left the thread where it lay. Under \place{the boards} four nails stood on the near board and four on the next and a thumb-width knot where a fifth might have been driven and was not; under the floor \lore{the carried fire} gave four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer without a name for three hundred and twenty days; at the finger's-width door the river kept \lore{water over water}, a low thing over a lower thing, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming, and was not numbered among the hearings. Three counts in three places that did not become one, because agreeing any two would have wanted a payer put under both and none stood. The heard bell stayed in its square beside the told bell of \lore{the Long Night}, and the told figure stood beside the paid with the space between unsigned.

The file lay open on \place{the towel} at the head of the bed with every object on its own place and every gap left gap, and the boy turned it over in his mind the way he had once turned instruments out on a towel at the end of a list, asking nothing of any of it. Two hundred and fifteen honest answers stood on the sixth place when the light went; the last of the day was still his to keep. The surgeon took a clean satisfaction in the keeping, the satisfaction of a field left as it was found, and the infant underneath wanted the crust lifted two inches to his mouth and the thread at his mouth wiped, and both were true and the second was louder and neither won, and the argument between them was the work.

At the very end of the day, when the yard had gone quiet and the river's note had come down to its lowest under the finger's-width door, the hand outside the wool went up an inch toward the sound. It took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole, let the intake go on the same low note, and came straight down and laid itself back against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. The note had done before the hand came down, so the letting-go was neither early again nor stayed. He filed it as answer two hundred and sixteen: exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment, borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the five arithmetics nor the bell's gap nor the covered grain nor the stayed object nor the early letting-go nor his own kept gaps. Two hundred and sixteen honest answers stood on the sixth place, each an object and not a power, and neither the number nor the small hand-movings that had made it stood up and called themselves a doctrine, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood.

Three hundred and twenty stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill} and \place{Grath}; six months and one hundred and forty days were told by nobody; the carried fire's fifth was unpaid the same number of days with its payer unnamed; and Garl's name had not been spoken under the roof the same number. Nothing on the twenty-first had been opened, joined, named, called, paid or ended. The keeping of the number was the only honest work there was to do and bought nothing at two inches of air, and he went on keeping it because attention was the one thing no institution could tax. The river's low note went quiet under the finger's-width door while the silt-clouded plate of ice stood on its flat stone out of the path of feet, the file lay open on the towel with every gap left gap, and Bone went on without a boundary drawn.
