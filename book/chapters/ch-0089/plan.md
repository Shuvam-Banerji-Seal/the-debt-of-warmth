## How the final moment pulls into the next chapter

The last image is deliberately not an ending: the count stands at **three hundred and twenty exact**, the hand laid down on the **two hundred and sixteenth** answer, the file open with every gap left gap, and Bone going on without a boundary drawn. Chapter 90 opens on Day 321, Bone 22, Secondday, with the last tend of Bone's month in front of it and Bone 30 (Day 329), the month's third and last reading, nine mornings ahead. Everything this chapter held is carried into those days unchanged and unspent: the stayed-at-the-inch object and the early letting-go as two separate objects on the sixth and not a habit; the four held changes on four places and not a rule; five arithmetics in Berr's locked box with one in twelve printed and unchallenged, the figure read still not the figure paid and no signature on the space, five readings five and not one account; the burned slate at five with no sixth; nine hands and nine stoppings with no tenth; six hearings with the seventh place empty; Ilva's strip unread; the grain covered under wool and unfigured. Nothing in this chapter reaches the last tend as sign, lesson, mending, cause, payment or payer, and no payer stands to any of it. The forward pull is the month's third accounting still to come over a basket that cannot answer, and the boy's private count going on toward a number nobody will speak.
