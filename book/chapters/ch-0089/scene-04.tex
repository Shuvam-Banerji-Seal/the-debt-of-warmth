The going of the light came into the market the way ash comes into a room, without being carried and without anybody's permission, and \person{Sella Vail} lifted the basket out of \place{the moot-post}'s finger's width of shade as if lifting a pot from a shelf where it had never been hot. She gave no account of where they had been and no word of the figure that had gone twice over him while the pig complained and the bell kept its gap in the square; a basket set down in shade and lifted again is a pot set down and lifted, and nothing had been said to it and nothing said to him. She gave the instruction to the wool and to the river wind and never to the basket: \emph{Keep it off his ears. It's the river wind}. Then she carried him down \place{the clay road} at the half-pace she used from every open slate, his face turned to her shoulder, the little-door burn shut on nothing, going around the slates the way a woman goes around a sleeping animal, with her eyes on the ground ahead of her feet.

\person[Corin]{The boy} went out of the twentieth of Bone with nobody saying so. Six months and one hundred and thirty-nine days were told by nobody in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill} and \place{Grath}; the count stood at three hundred and nineteen exact, complete, unspent and spoken by no man under any roof, and the going of the light added nothing to it and took nothing from it. \lore{The carried fire}'s fifth stood unpaid the same number of days with its payer unnamed, and \person{Garl Vail}'s name had not been spoken under this roof the same number. He had stopped expecting anybody to tell him what the number counted, which was not the same as not wanting it, and the wanting went on under the not-asking the way a coal goes on under ash.

Under the fold the crust kept its two inches from the ear, stiff and leathery in the dry cold, its browned corner standing a hair off the wool, its empty place keeping its name to itself and no third coming to make the row come out even. The thread at his mouth had frozen and was unwiped, for nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread. Cold had stopped at cheek, temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, as though it had come to the end of a line written out for it with neither an order to go farther nor an order to go away; \lore{the Afterdrop} waited beyond all three, past a warming nobody here would perform. He knew the composed face for what it was, the ease a cold face puts on that is not ease, and he knew a cold patient cannot clot and will seem better before he is worse and must be warmed from the outside and slowly, never rubbed, never set too near the fire. The knowing bought nothing at two inches of air. The infant underneath wanted the crust lifted to his mouth and the thread at his mouth wiped, and both were true and the second was louder and neither won, and the two voices argued without a word between them as men argue in their sleep, and the argument was the work.

The \place{Clay Yard} received them the way a yard receives a pot carried across it, by making room and saying nothing. Sella went wide around the places the yard went around, with a look and a wide step and no number. At the Vail corner the clean dark line lay where the \lore{Ash} ridge had turned for a year and two days, and beside it the finer grey hairline called \lore{the thaw} lay finer still, and neither had been swept; she carried him past both as wide as a pail of hot water set where a child might knock it. Under \place[the drying racks]{the lowest rack} the broken birth-slates lay where they had lain, entered by nobody. On the stone lip stood the round ring where the shallow clay pan had once stood, with no scrape, no crumb, no print, no chip and no word to say what had lifted it. \place{The trough} kept its water. Out on \place{the worked strips} the field's empty place kept its own shape. In the returning line the place where a hand went in on Seed 15 and did not come out was gone around with a look and no figure spoken over the going. Seven places, each gone around, and no eighth likeness stood up as a row and no number was laid on any of them; a row of eight would have wanted a payer put under it and none stood.

At \place{the yard well} three coverings stood and were called weather and entered nowhere. The stayed frost had gone off the north lip while the yard looked elsewhere, gone as a thing goes when nobody stands to watch it go. The trough kept its skin. On \place{the flat stone by the well}, out of the path of feet, the plate of ice lifted from the water stood clouded with silt, thin as a dish and holding the light badly. Sella set the basket down a hand's breadth from the stone and went around it. The boy kept a finger between every pair of the three, and between the trough's skin and the trough as a walked-around place, and did not make a fourth likeness into a cause. A covering laid over a depth and the same covering over another depth and a piece of the depth lifted and set where no foot goes are three things and not one thing, and a cause would have wanted a payer standing under it, and none stood.

At \place{the rack end} the dressed grain lay under wool on the cool stone, away from the hearth. She lifted a corner and turned what lay beneath with a still lighter touch than she had used in the morning, and set the fold back and gave no figure to anything. The arithmetic that had gone over it this morning had not known what it cost, and had gone up the road and had not come back as a lesson or a mending; the space between that figure and the price of the grain had no signature and nobody under any roof supplied one. He read the turn of the wool as a surgeon turns a wound to see the part that lies hidden, and asked nothing of it.

\person{Ilva} was at the well drawing with the pail against her knee. The rope was stiff in the dry cold and gave its small dry rasp as the bucket came up, and clay had dried pale in the crescents of her nails and the finer grey dust of \place{the drying racks} was worked into the seam of one thumb, which she did not scrub out. Her hands knew the weight of a pail and the edge of a wet board, and had never been soft, and went on knowing these things without reporting on them to anybody. A woman with a pot asked what the firings of Bone had taken.

Her hand went toward her sleeve and stayed. The short strip of notched birch bark, pale where a thumb had rubbed the notches and dark in the uncut grain between, did not come out, was not taken out, read, copied, counted or joined to the charcoal beneath \place{the loaf shelf}, the cord, the open slate lying on the stone lip a pace away, the knot-string or the ownerless saying, and no figure came off the wood. Her eyes went to the asker and her mouth opened after both, with a half-beat between the looking and the word --- the hand to the record before the eyes to the person, the eyes before the mouth, an order as exact as the order in which a field is opened and the instrument laid into it. \emph{Ask the pot what it cost. I keep the count, not the account}, she said, answering the question put and not the one behind it. She put the flat of her hand once more on the sleeve and went back to the pail, and nobody turned twice.

On the stone lip where the pail had stood the fresh scurf of road-mud had come in on a coat gone up the road with the reading before noon, and it lay there a direction arrived nowhere, brushed by nobody and given no figure. The boy took the order of her reaching apart with the exactness he had once used on a chart laid out on a desk, by what was there and by what was not, and filed it on a place of its own as the ninth record-first hand in the district, with a finger between it and \person{Hessa Rale}'s eighth. He filed no tenth. He made no figure of the open slate a pace away, and did not make her a reader of her own keeping, which she was not and had never wanted to be. She was not \person{Marra}, not the soft-handed man, not the soft-cuffed clerk set below the bridge, not Hessa with her edge-on slate, and not the silent woman who had turned a cup at the pot board in the square. The fifth arithmetic that had gone up the road this morning had brought no reading to her well, and the count stayed in wood where no slate could take a figure off it. A count kept in wood is still a keeping, and she did not know she had been read.

The tray came out at the going of the light and every part of it was where it had been left. The \lore{burned slate} stood at five tellings with no sixth and no witness and no hand named at any flame, the claim on it gathering interest against a promise neither given nor withdrawn and paying nothing here. The row stood at five askings with one shaping and no witness, and no sixth asking had been put. The well-saying stood at three shapings, the shut-room tellings at two, the pot sayings at three with the Smoke 14 note on its own place apart; five openings in five months were five and not a rule, with the next far off. Nine record-first hands stood with Ilva's ninth among them and no tenth, and the nine stoppings stood at nine with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end. Six hearings of \lore{the full note} stood beside and never among, the sixth the small flat knock out of a person as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go, and the seventh place stayed empty and was not filled to make the row come out even, for an empty place kept empty is a finding and a finding filled is a finding forged. The heard bell stayed in its own square beside the told bell of \lore{the Long Night}, and its gap was not fetched in to make a seventh of the hearings or a payer for the silence. The seventh green was a colour and the seventh shape a shape, and no eighth of either came into the yard on the twentieth of Bone.

Four held changes stood on four places and did not stand up and call themselves a rule: the returned half-check of Bone 8 on \place{the flagstones}, the Day 161 fraction at \lore{the damper}, the Day 166 beat beneath the shelf, and the Frost 5 returned beat. Tarn's stroke went over the shallow bright patch without the half-check again at the going of the light, and the stone kept the place where it had stopped; a thing kept by not being made again is still kept, and the boy did not spend it to make a fifth. The stayed-at-the-inch object and the early letting-go of Bone 17 sat each on its own place with a finger between them and between them and every other object on the sixth; neither was made again, neither stood up as a habit or a way, and the letting-go this evening would be an ordinary letting-go and not a second early one.

Five arithmetics of the year now stood behind one printed figure: the four that had gone before and Bone's second, read twice over the basket that morning. One in twelve stood unchallenged in every notice, the figure read was still not the figure paid, and no signature stood on the space between. Five were five and not one account. A rule made out of five would have wanted a payer put under all five and no payer stood under any of them, and the boy kept the finding on a place of its own with no payer under it, not a lesson, not a grievance, not a mending, not a doctrine. He did not lay it across the house's \lore{not-doings}, or across the dressed grain under its wool, or across the four held changes on their four places, or across the stayed-at-the-inch object, or across the early letting-go, or across his own kept gaps. The edges came near to touching, so near that a less careful hand would have pushed them together and called the touching a cause, and the finger went between. The reading answered nothing for the hare that had gone into the weather, nothing for the hand that had gone in on Seed 15 and had not come out, nothing for the man carried out on Day 188 whom no figure had ever known the cost of, nothing for the grain turned under wool by a number that did not know what it cost. The five true subtractions rode locked up the clay road in \person{Berr Clay}'s box toward \place{High Kiln}, and the space where the price should have stood kept its emptiness without a signature.

At \place{the kindling} \person{Tarn} kept the account he kept where the house could not check it. The small knife lay at the very edge of its sill, where it could be checked and where it could slip, and he had put it back and told the empty yard where it was, unasked, in the tone of a man stating the weather. Six years stood in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check; the seventh stood at the lip beyond it, beside the unforged collector sentence that Garl Vail had never spoken in his own voice, with no name on the man who turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on the debt. \emph{Six counted. The next I don't count}, he told the wood, and went on with the kindling.

The sentence about an ear under this roof being worth more than the debt that held it came up to the word that would call the loan and stopped there. He did not say the word. He drew the breath that should have gone in at the top of the reach and did not spend it, and the small interval stood at the top of every reach after that as it had stood since Seed 16, and his chest had been tight since the dark. It was a remembered ninth stopping with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end, and the loan was uncalled and no collector had come up the clay road or gone down it on this day. Ash was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line, and no scrubbing lifted it; the pale clip-ring at his left wrist held what little light the going of the light had left and gave it back, and he lifted the wrist a moment to the grey as though to see whether the light had come to anything. The boy kept the stopping beside the remembered finger on one tray without laying a finger across them, and did not call either a hearing or a payment.

At the edge of \place{the Vail wheel-shed} \person{Jorra Vail} came through with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bent little finger and under the nails, white in the creases where a fist had opened, and it was not the bone-coloured \lore{slip} of the wheel. Three or more men had gone low into the finger's-width-open \place{the back room} before first grey and he gave the house no name for them and did not say the room was cold. He had taken the wheel at thirteen when his father did not come back out of the Long Cold, and had spent three years trying to fill a seat worn hollow on the left by twenty years of another man's weight; he did not say this, and the hollow kept its shape. He kept dusty forearms and clean ones on one tray and parted them as a surgeon parts clot from vessel, and asked neither which slate had been broken nor whether it was a birth-slate. He spoke to the wheel before he spoke to any person --- \emph{It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady} --- and told a pot \emph{You'll do} in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either.

He knew the full note that pot would give three turns before it left his hands, with air under it and a want in it, and kept the knowing on the near-full tray beside the hearings and never among them; he would not say it before a kiln-master come up from \place{High Kiln} who would call it early and make something of it, singing before its time and singing itself out early. Before Sella had finished telling him the fire was low his bent finger went into the cold iron of the damper, took the cold out of the fingertip, moved the tongue a hair's breadth and left it standing so that the end of her instruction arrived at the pot. He did not look toward the basket, and \emph{he's not Vail} stayed sealed as tightly as a lid nobody had tried to lift in three years. The fifth arithmetic that had changed the district in the square brought no word under the wheel-shed and did not loosen the promise; it was neither given nor withdrawn, and the burned slate kept its five with a finger between the fifth and the four and no sixth due on a day that had not earned one. The boy read one piece of iron with two bites, the bite that holds a vessel whole and the bite that can take the hand off a man, and there was no \place{Grathic} word for both mercies, and he filed it joined to no kiln and entered in no slate.

The light went off the yard without a line drawn across it, and Sella lifted the basket and carried him up at the half-pace from every open slate, going wide around the seven places once more with a look and a wide step and no number. She said nothing of where they had been or of what had been read over him in the square, and gave the wool and the river wind their instruction and the basket none. She set him on \place{the bed} under the roof and never in \place{the Debt Loft}, with \place{the boards} overhead keeping four nails on the near board, four on the next, and a thumb-width knot where a fifth might have been driven and was not. She put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face, her hand resting a half-beat longer than the setting needed before it went away, and left the thread where it lay. The twice-mended pot stood on \place{the pot board} with the hairline of the original break showing between the old-brass staple and the tighter wire, and the crack was left showing.

Under the floor \lore{the carried fire} gave four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer unnamed three hundred and nineteen days. Through the finger's-width door the river kept its note of \lore{water over water}, a low thing over a lower thing, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming, never numbered among the hearings. The boards kept their four and four and knot. Three counts in three places did not become one, and agreeing any two of them would have wanted a payer put under both and none stood; the bell's gap left behind in the square was not fetched in to make a fourth, and the heard bell stayed in its square beside the told bell of the Long Night. Five arithmetics behind one printed figure, five and not one account, sat on their own place with a finger between them and the three counts, and the finding sat on a place of its own with no payer under it.

In the small hours the hand outside the wool went up an inch toward the river's note. It took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole, let the intake go on the same low note, and came straight down and laid itself back against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. The note had done before the hand came down, so the letting-go was not early a second time and the hand did not stay; the stayed-at-the-inch object and the early letting-go of Bone 17 sat where they sat, two objects on two places, and the staying was not made again and the early letting-go was not made again. He filed it as answer two hundred and fourteen: exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment, borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the five arithmetics nor the bell's gap nor the covered grain nor his own kept gaps. Two hundred and fourteen honest answers stood on the sixth place, each an object and not a power, and neither the number nor the small hand-movings that made it stood up and called themselves a doctrine, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood.

The file lay open on \place{the towel} at the head of the bed with every gap left gap, every tray parted by a finger's width and nothing that had moved. Three hundred and nineteen stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in Low Kiln, Sill and Grath; six months and one hundred and thirty-nine days were told by nobody; the carried fire's fifth was unpaid the same number of days with its payer unnamed; and Garl's name had not been spoken under the roof the same number. Nothing on the twentieth had been opened, joined, named, called, paid or ended. The second reading had changed the district's arithmetic and had not changed anything under this roof, and the keeping of the number was the only honest work there was to do and bought nothing at two inches of air. The hand came straight down on the two hundred and fourteenth answer with the letting-go not early a second time, and the five arithmetics stayed on a place of their own with no signature on the space and every gap left gap.
