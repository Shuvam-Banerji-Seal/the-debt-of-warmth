The afternoon came on without a boundary drawn, as a pot fills from a tap nobody watches, and \person[Corin]{the boy} was carried down at the half-pace from every open slate. \person{Sella Vail} had been to \place{the hearth} once since the light came, and now she lifted the basket and gave the instruction to the wool and to the river wind and never to the basket: \emph{Keep it off his ears. It's the river wind}. She said it to the fold and to the wind and to the wool, and set him level the way a pot is set level on a board, and went with him down \place{the clay road} into \place{the Clay Yard} with her burn shut on nothing and his face turned to her shoulder. The yard smelled of hot dry clay given up to the sun, of tallow and wet wool, and under all of it the faint sour mineral breath of the Ash ridge along the skirting board at home, which had followed him out under the fold. The river wind came through the finger's-width gap and put its cold against the wool at his ear, and the wool kept it off. Nothing in the carrying was said to him. He had been carried so many days that being carried had become a kind of weather, and the surgeon in him took the jolt of each step as a man takes a field before the light, by the small sounds first, because the small sounds do not lie.

Under the fold the crust lay two inches from his ear, gone leathery in the dry kiln heat, its corner standing a hair off the wool, its empty place unnamed and no third coming to make the row come out even. The thread at his mouth had frozen and was unwiped, for nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread. Cold had stopped at cheek, temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, as though it had come to the end of a line written out for it; \lore{the Afterdrop} waited beyond all three, past a warming nobody here would perform. He knew the composed face for what it was, the ease a cold face puts on that is not ease, and he knew a cold patient cannot clot and will seem better before he is worse, and must be warmed from the outside and slowly, never rubbed, never set near the fire. The knowing bought nothing at two inches of air. The infant underneath wanted the crust lifted the two inches to his mouth and the thread at his mouth wiped, and both were true and the second was louder and neither won, and the two voices argued without a word between them as men argue in their sleep, and the argument was the work.

She went wide around the places the yard went around, with a look and a wide step and no number. At the Vail corner the clean dark line lay where the \lore{Ash} ridge had turned for a year and two days, and beside it the finer grey hairline that the house called \lore{the thaw} lay finer still, and neither had been swept; she carried him past both as wide as a pail of hot water set where a child might knock it. Under \place[the drying racks]{the lowest rack} the broken birth-slates lay where they had lain, entered by nobody. On the stone lip stood the round ring where the shallow clay pan had once stood, with no scrape, no crumb, no print, no chip and no word to say what had lifted it. \place{The trough} kept its water. Out on \place{the worked strips} the field's empty place kept its own shape. In the returning line the place where a hand went in on Seed 15 and did not come out was gone around with a look and no figure spoken over the going. Seven places, each gone around, and no eighth likeness stood up as a row and no number was laid on any of them; a row of eight would have wanted a payer put under it and none stood.

At \place{the yard well} three coverings stood and were called weather and entered nowhere. The stayed frost had gone off the north lip while the yard looked elsewhere, gone as a thing goes when nobody stands to watch it go. The trough kept its skin. On \place{the flat stone by the well}, out of the path of feet, the plate of ice lifted from the water stood clouded with silt, thin as a dish and holding the light badly. Sella set the basket down a hand's breadth from the stone and went around it. The boy kept a finger between every pair of the three, and between the trough's skin and the trough as a walked-around place, and did not make a fourth likeness into a cause. A covering laid over a depth and the same covering over another depth and a piece of the depth lifted and set where no foot goes are three things and not one thing, and a cause would have wanted a payer standing under it, and none stood.

At \place{the rack end} the dressed grain lay under wool on the cool stone, away from the hearth. She lifted a corner of the wool and turned what lay beneath with a lighter touch than she had used in the morning, and set the fold back and gave no figure to anything. The arithmetic that had gone over it on Bone 10 had not known what it cost, and had gone up the road and not come back as a lesson or a mending; the space between that figure and the price of the grain had no signature and nobody under any roof supplied one. He read the turn of the wool as a surgeon turns a wound to see the part that lies hidden, and asked nothing of it.

Bone's hunts and repairs were directions judged by the carrying arm and the weather, and came into the yard as questions and went out again without figures. A man come down from \place{Cold Acre} with a hare across his shoulders shifted the weight from one collarbone to the other when a woman with a pot asked what the carrying had cost. \emph{Ask the weather. It carried the snow; it can carry the price}, he said, and went off toward \place{the clay road} with the hare still on his shoulder and nobody weighing it and nobody opening a slate. Another came with a cracked jar in a sling of rope and asked what the mending of the month would want, and was told to ask the pot; and the pot, being a pot, said nothing. No entry came of either, and no number went over the basket, and the yard went on with its work.

At \place{the kindling} \person{Tarn} kept the account he kept where the house could not check it. The small knife lay at the very edge of its sill, where it could be checked and where it could slip, and he had put it back and told the empty yard where it was, unasked, in the tone of a man stating the weather. Six years stood in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check; the seventh stood at the lip beyond it, beside the unforged collector sentence that \person{Garl Vail} had never spoken in his own voice, with no name on the man who turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on the debt. \emph{Six counted. The next I don't count}, he told the wood, and went on with the kindling.

The sentence about an ear under this roof being worth more than the debt that held it came up to the word that would call the loan and stopped there. He did not say the word. He drew the breath that should have gone in at the top of the reach and did not spend it, and the small interval stood at the top of every reach after that as it had stood since Seed 16, and his chest had been tight since the dark. It was a remembered ninth stopping with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end, and the loan was uncalled and no collector had come up \place{the clay road} or gone down it on this day. Ash was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line, and no scrubbing lifted it; the pale clip-ring at his left wrist held what little light there was and gave it back, and he lifted the wrist a moment to the grey as though to see whether the light had come to anything. His stroke went over the shallow bright patch on \place{the flagstones} without the half-check, and the stones kept the place where it had stopped. The boy set the stopping beside the remembered finger on one tray without laying a finger across them, and did not call either a hearing or a payment.

A woman with a basket of kindling asked him what the seventh year would want when it came, and Tarn said \emph{Yes, house} to her and to the man beside her who said the thaw would be early, and \emph{Yes, house} again to the one who said it would be late; and he kept the late, because a late thaw puts off the paying, though the paying had stood in the ruts since the water went. He agreed so thoroughly that there was nowhere for an argument to find a foot. The boy kept the exact account of six and the seventh on its own place and made no figure of it, and did not join the woman's question to the loan that could hear.

At the edge of \place{the Vail wheel-shed} \person{Jorra Vail} came through with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bent little finger and under the nails, white in the creases where a fist had opened, and it was not the bone-coloured slip of the wheel. Three or more men had gone low into the finger's-width-open back room before first grey and he gave the house no name for them and did not say the room was cold. He had taken the wheel at thirteen when his father did not come back out of the Long Cold, and had spent three years trying to fill a seat worn hollow on the left by twenty years of another man's weight; he did not say this, and the hollow kept its shape. He kept dusty forearms and clean ones on one tray and parted them as a surgeon parts clot from vessel, and asked neither which slate had been broken nor whether it was a birth-slate. He spoke to the wheel before he spoke to any person --- \emph{It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady} --- and told a pot \emph{You'll do} in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either.

He knew the \lore{full note} that pot would give three turns before it left his hands, with air under it and a want in it, and kept the knowing on the near-full tray beside the hearings and never among them; he would not say it before a kiln-master come up from \place{High Kiln} who would call it early and make something of it, singing before its time and singing itself out early. Before Sella had finished telling him the fire was low his bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper}, took the cold out of the fingertip, moved the tongue a hair's breadth and left it standing so that the end of her instruction arrived at the pot. He did not look toward the basket, and \emph{he's not Vail} stayed sealed as tightly as a lid nobody had tried to lift in three years. The boy read one piece of iron with two bites, the bite that holds a vessel whole and the bite that can take the hand off a man, and there was no \place{Grathic} word for both mercies, and he filed it joined to no kiln and entered in no slate.

The tray came out at the going of the light and every part of it was where it had been left. The \lore{burned slate} stood at five tellings with no sixth and no witness and no hand named at any flame, the claim on it gathering interest against a promise neither given nor withdrawn and paying nothing here. The row stood at five askings with one shaping and no witness, and no sixth asking had been put. The well-saying stood at three shapings, the shut-room tellings at two, the pot sayings at three with the Smoke 14 note on its own place apart; five openings in five months were five and not a rule, with the next far off. \person{Ilva}'s notched strip stayed in her sleeve with her ninth record-first hand on its own place and no tenth, and no figure came off the wood. The nine stoppings stood at nine with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end. The heard bell stayed in its square beside the told bell of \lore{the Long Night}, and its gap was not fetched into the three counts under the roof. The seventh green was a colour and the seventh shape a shape with no eighth of either. Four arithmetics of the year stood behind one printed figure in \person{Berr Clay}'s locked box up the road, one in twelve unchallenged, the figure read still not the figure paid, no signature on the space; four readings were four and not one account. The debt against a firing of tiles circled without a name, neither paid nor settled nor done with, and worse for being uncalled, and no collector had come up or gone down the clay road on the nineteenth. The finding about the four stayed on a place of its own with no payer under it, not a lesson, grievance, mending, doctrine or rule, and was not laid across the house's \lore{not-doings}, or across the covered grain, or across the four held changes on four places, or across the stayed-at-the-inch object, or across the early letting-go of Bone 17, or across his own kept gaps. The edges came near to touching, so near that a less careful hand would have pushed them together and called the touching a cause; the finger went between them.

The light went off the yard without a line drawn across it, and Sella carried him up at the half-pace from every open slate, going wide around the seven places once more with a look and a wide step and no number. She said nothing of where they had been or what had been asked of the weather and the pot, and set him on \place{the bed} under the roof and never in \place{the Debt Loft}, with \place{the boards} overhead keeping four nails on the near board, four on the next, and a thumb-width knot where a fifth might have been driven and was not. She put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face, her hand resting a half-beat longer than the setting needed before it went away, and left the thread where it lay. \lore{The carried fire} under the floor gave four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer unnamed three hundred and eighteen days, and through the finger's-width door the river kept its note of \lore{water over water}, a low thing over a lower thing, and the two counts did not become one, and the bell's gap in the square was not fetched in to make a third.

In the small hours the hand outside the wool went up an inch toward the river's note. It took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, let the intake go whole on the same low note, and came straight down and laid itself back against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. The note had done before the hand came down, so the letting-go was not early a second time and the hand did not stay; the stayed-at-the-inch object and the early letting-go of Bone 17 sat where they sat, two objects on two places, and the staying was not made again and the early letting-go was not made again. He filed it as answer two hundred and twelve: exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment, borrowing no house count and supplying no payer. Two hundred and twelve honest answers stood on the sixth place, each an object and not a power, and neither the number nor the small hand-movings that made it stood up and called themselves a doctrine, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood.

The file lay open on \place{the towel} at the head of the bed with every gap left gap and nothing moved. Three hundred and eighteen stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill} and \place{Grath}; six months and one hundred and thirty-eight days were told by nobody; the carried fire's fifth was unpaid the same number of days with its payer unnamed; and the name under the roof had not been spoken the same number. Nothing on the nineteenth had been opened, joined, named, called, paid or ended, and the keeping of the number was the only honest work there was to do and it bought nothing at two inches of air. Bone 20, the month's second Account-day and market, stood one morning ahead in a day that had not earned it, and was not brought into this one as sign, lesson, mending, cause or payment.
