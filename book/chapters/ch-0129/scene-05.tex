\daymark{440}{Silt 16, Y.H. 814, Sixthday}

The small hours of the sixteenth came down under the Vail roof without a boundary drawn, the way frost comes through the join of a window and settles on the inside of the glass before anybody thinks to look at it --- not poured but arriving, finding the cold place first and going no further than it must. \person[Corin]{The boy} lay on \place{the bed} at the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf} in \place{the Vail kiln-house}, \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill}, \place{Grath}, under the roof and never in \place{the Debt Loft}, held where the heat came to him sideways and not where he would have chosen. The hand outside the wool lay flat on the fold where the going of the light had left it, on answer four hundred and fifty-four, flat and patient as a tool put away where the next hand would find it without looking. There was no next hand in the room. Four hundred and thirty-nine exact, and six months and two hundred and fifty-nine days told by nobody who would have known what a number counts. Under \place{the boards} \lore{the carried fire} kept four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer without a name for four hundred and thirty-nine days; \person[Garl Vail]{Garl}'s name had gone unspoken the same four hundred and thirty-nine, the man and the fire standing side by side without becoming one. A sixth morning of Silt was a sixth morning of Silt and nothing more. Nothing from the fifteenth's withheld word had crossed this threshold in the night as sign, lesson, mending, cause, payment or payer; the house had taken up its grammar as though nothing had turned, and the quiet under the roof was maintenance and not an answer.

He took the body in its old order, because the order was the only instrument he had and a surgeon who takes a field in the order he wishes it were in has made his answer before he cuts. One stiff brown-edged barley crust stood two inches from his ear, gone past leather into old horn in the dry nights, with its corner a hair off the wool; a child under this roof would have tried to eat it weeks ago and would not try now, and he could not lift it, and could not have lifted it when it was soft. Beside it the empty place held \lore{the keeping at the fold}, found where put on the mornings and goings of the light behind, still a finding and not a placing, with no second fold, no third crust, no name, no gift, no payment and no payer. It had been put there on the fifteenth by a hand that had not come back to place it again, and a hand that finds is not a hand that places. The thread of drool at his mouth had gone stiff in the dry kiln heat and was unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread. Cold had come to cheek and temple and the small bare joint at the wrist where the wool rode back, and had stopped there as at the end of a line written out for it; a face composed by cold is not composure, and a cold patient cannot clot. \lore{The Afterdrop} waited past a warming nobody in this house would perform. He knew a basket set level out of the draught, with the heat coming to it sideways, was the careful rewarming he would choose, and could not ask for it; the knowing was a finding like any other, with no hand under it to make it a claim on anybody.

The room insisted he was warm. It was a warm room and the heat came into it sideways off the banked coals, and a less careful reader would have stopped there: a warm room, a covered child, a fire kept. He had once stood at the foot of a table and read a man bleeding out under a blanket and a lamp, and known the room was lying, because the fingers had gone the colour of old rope while the face kept its composed pink. Pink returning is the surface still lying over a cold core. He had learned that in a white room with a page over a speaker and had carried it here without once using the words of that room, past tense and untagged, paying nothing. A warm room insisting a body is warm while the fingers go the colour of old rope is a thing to read past the name given it; he read it by the order of its small failures and not by the story it told about himself. He had come to read this house the same way --- not by what it would not survive, which was the loud question and wanted a rescuer standing under it, but by what it would not say, which was the quiet one and wanted only an observer. The grain would not say what it had cost. The pot would not say who had mended it twice. The corner would not say why no broom had gone there in two years. Three refusals, three places, and no payer among them. He put no figure on any, for a surgeon who names the silence before the last finding is in has already decided what he will find.

Three things found as they had been left, and none the better for the finding. The surgeon read them in their order and put no figure on them. The infant under the same skin wanted the crust lifted two inches to his mouth, wanted the thread wiped from his lip, wanted a hand on the handle of the pot that would bring a cup within reach. Both registers were true. The second was louder. The infant wanted and the surgeon filed, and neither bought the other, the quarrel going on without a settlement because a settlement wants a hand to enter it and the only hand in the case was the small one outside the wool that could not close two inches of air. He had once laid a tray of instruments in the order they would be wanted and known the difference between a finding and a want without having to think which was which. That was another room and another pair of hands. It paid nothing here. The wanting was not a weakness in the register and the register was not a denial of the wanting; they were two entries on the same page and neither had been signed. In not lifting the crust and not wiping the thread he did not make a doctrine of the not-doing.

\scenebreak

First grey came up the clay road and into the yard without asking, and \person{Sella Vail} came to the basket with the dark still in her sleeves. She carried him down \place{the nineteen steps} at the half-pace from every open slate, the instruction going to the wool, the wind off \place{the river}, the pot and the fire and never to the basket; the basket was not told what the day was, or what the hour was, or told to be still. A basket carried down is a pot carried down and set where the heat comes to it, and no woman explains the hour to a pot. \place{The Clay Yard} was wet ash and old brick dust under the thin light, with barley wall to wall and no number on it; a woman went around what she went around with a look and a wide step and no count, and a sixth morning of Silt was not an Account-day and brought no slate into the yard. He was set at the edge of \place{the kindling} where the coming and going of feet did not touch the wicker, and the light lay flat on \place{the flagstones} and had no height in it yet.

Under the roof she went to \place{the hearth} before the basket. She told the coals it was \lore{the thaw}, in the voice another woman would have used to a child who could not hear her, and turned them with the back of a broken pot, a dry scrape of iron over stone that went into the boy's ear ahead of the warmth. She gave the small intake, the hold and the letting-go toward no opening, and figured neither of the two gaps; they were gaps and wanted no figure put under them, for a figured gap wants a payer standing in it and none stood. Her finger went along the top of the loaf shelf so that the charcoal tally on its underside stayed unread; no hand went beneath, no mark was copied, no row was numbered. Her lips moved on a two-gapped row that was neither prayer nor count and wanted no answer. One glance went to the clay jar standing cold under \lore{lead glaze} beyond \place{the pot board}; the hand shut half over nothing, and the little-door burn on the back of her left hand --- two straight sides, a lintel and a threshold --- closed on air and went back to the pot.

She lifted the twice-mended pot thumb-first where the tighter wire crossed the crack, the hairline of the original break showing between the older brass staple and the wire, a dark thread with a hand's work on either side. \emph{Leave the crack showing. People trust a thing they can see has been handled}, she said to the pot. \emph{If it's worth mending it's worth mending twice. The second time is the real one}, to the fire, and never to a person. Repair stayed apart from restoration, as a surgeon keeps a graft apart from a cure; the pot was mended and was not made whole, and the crack was left where a man could see it had been handled. The boy watched the thumb go to the crossing of the wire and understood that a thing is held less heavily the better it is known. He had handled a thousand fields with a light hand and had never once called it gentleness.

At \place{the rack end} she drew back the wool from the dressed grain to turn it for damp. This was the work of the morning and she gave it the whole of her attention, which was a way of giving none of it to the basket. She lifted a corner of the cloth, laid her palm flat on the grain and let the cool come up into it, the cool of a thing that had taken the night's damp and kept it; the palm told her what the eye could not and what no figure read over the grain at a reading would ever know. Piece by piece she turned it, lighter each time, laying each back under the wool in the order it had been lifted, until the whole lay covered and unfigured and the damp was shut in again. She gave it no figure and no handful and no name for the damp, and the arithmetic that would be read over grain laid like this at the month's next reading would not know what it cost, and the ink-neat space between that figure and its price would carry no signature, for paper is not a payer and no key came under this roof. The pot rim stood two inches beyond his reaching hand, with a half-beat and no look toward the bed; the half-beat was the distance between the intention and the arrest, and she did not measure it and did not name it.

\lore{The widow's mark} hung where it hung. She touched it before she opened \place{the kiln mouth} and at no other hour, and the kiln mouth was not open; a Sixthday morning was not an opening, and seven openings in seven months were seven and not a rule, and no eighth came due on a day that had earned only its sixteenth. Under \place{the Vail corner} the clean dark line kept its finer grey beside it, thickened in the dry cold with the faint sour tang of \lore{Ash} coming up where no broom had gone in two years, unswept and called weather. She put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face and added no second fold. The keeping at the fold was found where put and stayed a finding. Nothing under the roof had been mended that morning by anything that had stood on its own place through the night, and nothing had been made a lesson of it.

\scenebreak

At the kindling \person{Tarn} kept the six where the house could check and the seventh at the lip beside Garl's unforged collector sentence, with no name under the man who had turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on \lore{the tile debt} circling against a firing of tiles that would never come. The stack kept its own arithmetic of four, four and a space, and the space was not a fifth and was not filled. The small knife lay at the very edge of its sill, and his hand went over blade and sill as a man passes a hand over a tool put back where it belongs, and came away with no breath held at the top of the reach; the coming away was the whole of the checking. He cut nothing, counted nothing and spent nothing.

And in the tone of a man stating the weather he said the sentence again. \emph{Six counted. The next I don't count}, he said to the wood.

The word that had gone to the kindling on the fourteenth, and had not gone to it on the fifteenth, went to it now. The silence of the yesterday was found where it had been put and was not made a habit, for a second silence would have been a custom and a third a way, and a custom and a way both want a payer standing under them and none stood. The boy took the finding by the place of it --- six in the worn thumb-mark, seven at the lip, the knife set back, the hand away with the breath let go unspent, the word standing where the word had stood before --- and did not add the three mornings together. Three sentences were three sentences and not one rule. He filed the return on a place of its own beside the older stopped sentence and Garl's unforged collector sentence and the withheld word of the day behind, with a finger between every pair. It was exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, not a tenth stopping, not a payment, not a hearing, not a custom, with no payer named and no slate entered. He did not lay it across the second asking of the twelfth, or the face from \place{Slate Row} at the yard's edge on the thirteenth, or the reported second slate of the sixth, or the clerk's entered figure brought up the yard as a report and never a sighting, or the promise Jorra had not given against \lore{the burned slate} standing at five, or the four readings of the year behind one unsigned figure up \place{the clay road}. A joining would have wanted a payer, and there was nobody under any roof to pay.

The loan stayed uncalled in a term already ended. The ear-sentence came all the way to the word that would call the loan and stopped there, a remembered ninth stopping with no tenth; the Seed 16 breath stayed unspent, his valuable ear exposure and not safety, for a term ending is not a payment, a forgiveness or a freedom and leaves a man more exposed than it found him. The fear of being entered again for vagrancy was still his without a figure, and so was the want he did not voice for a slate with his own name and no mark against it. His chest had been tight since the dark. Ash was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line, and the pale clip-ring at his left wrist caught what little light there was and gave it back. He took the broom only after it had been given its instruction. \emph{Yes, house}, he said to the fire, and went on over the flagstones without turning his head toward \place{the out-of-line landing} below \place{the north bridge}, where the soft-cuffed clerk was reported still set with his slate turned inward for a measure nobody had called, a report brought up the yard and never a sighting, a witness and a tenth hand to nobody. A man who owns nothing knows where every small thing is; a man who has counted six years knows what a seventh is worth when nobody comes to call it.

At \place{the Vail wheel-shed} edge \person{Jorra Vail} came through \place{the finger's-width door} with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small past his bent little finger and white in a fist-crease where a hand had opened, and it was not the bone-coloured \lore{slip} of the wheel. He kept dusty forearms and clean on one tray and parted them as clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had been broken nor whether it had been a birth-slate. Three or more men unnamed had gone low into \place{the back room} before first grey and got no name from him and no word that the room was cold, while the broken ones under the lowest rack stayed entered by nobody; the room was a shape and a dust from the basket's place and no more than that. He spoke to the wheel before any person --- \emph{It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady} --- and told a pot \emph{You'll do} in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness. He knew the \lore{full note} that pot would give three turns ahead, with air under it and a want in it, and withheld the knowing from any master come up from \place{High Kiln} who would call it early and sing a pot before its time.

Before Sella had finished telling him the fire was low, his bent finger went into the cold iron of \place{the damper} ahead of her unfinished sentence. One bite of the iron held and one took; there was no \lore{Grathic} word covering both mercies. He took the cold out of the fingertip, moved his tongue a hair's breadth and came out without leaving the extra holding of Ash 26 a second time, gathered once and not made again, for a second would be a habit and a third a way and both want a payer where none stood. The household straightened around the waiting without a word from anybody. The boy lay where he had been set and kept \emph{he's not Vail} sealed as a lid nobody had tried to lift in three years; the promise to the men who burn slates was neither given nor withdrawn against the burned slate standing at five, and the two quiet men under one roof kept their places on trays of their own, their silences maintenance and not a compact. Nothing from the twelfth's asking, the thirteenth's face or the fifteenth's withheld word had come under the shed as sign, mending, cause, payment or payer, and the boy made no joining of what he saw.

The morning went on at the work of a morning. Sella's hands were never still; when they had nothing to do they counted spoons, nails and the breaths of a child nobody numbers, and came back to the hairline at the wire. The barley lay wall to wall without a number. Tarn's seven stood where the house could check and could not. Jorra's dust stayed white in a fist-crease that was not opened twice. The clean dark line under the corner kept its finer grey and was called weather, and the air off the river was as cold as it had been at the going of the light on the days behind, with nothing in it today that had not been in it yesterday, and he made nothing of either. Each thing kept its own place with a finger between it and the next, and the boy added none of them together, for a surgeon who adds findings before the last is in has already made up the answer he wanted before he cut. A likeness was not a cause. A hand going flat over a sleeve was not a reader. A number counted was a number with air around it, and air bought no warmth.

The sixth place held four hundred and fifty-three and four hundred and fifty-four on its own row, and the seventh place was empty. Six hearings of the full note stood beside and never among with the seventh empty and \place{the Drawing house}'s flat knock outside on its own place; the heard bell stood beside the told bell with both gaps in their squares, and three counts in three places stayed three --- the kiln's four-and-a-gap behind the wall, the boards' four-and-four-and-a-knot, and the carried fire's four low pulls with the fifth missing. Nine record-first hands stood with nine stoppings and no tenth. Seven openings in seven months were seven and not a rule. The offered figure and its return, the second asking of the twelfth, the face from Slate Row, the reported second slate of the sixth, \person{Ilva}'s fear kept in wood, \person{Hessa Rale}'s slate unread up the clay road, the neighbour's near-reaching at the shelf, the hair-thin thaw of the seventh, the clerk's entered figure brought up the yard as a report and never a sighting, the fifth shaping of \lore{the well-saying}, the three likenesses of Slate Row, the withheld word of the fifteenth, the burned slate at five and the four ownerless directions stood each on its own place with a finger between every pair. None was a reader, a witness, a tenth hand, a custom, a payment or a payer, and none was made to answer another. Four readings of the year stood behind one unsigned figure up the clay road, four being four and not one account, the first not made a thirteenth by the three that followed, the second supplying no signature, the third no doctrine, the fourth no payer, the figure read still not the figure paid; \person{Berr Clay}'s box stayed locked and the key went up in a coat pocket that never came back down. None of the four was made to answer any kept thing under this roof. The unsent call to \person{Nina} stood where it had been put and was not paid again.

He kept four hundred and forty exact, six months and two hundred and sixty days told by nobody who would have known what a number counts. The carried fire's fifth went unpaid and Garl's name went unspoken the same four hundred and forty, the man and the fire standing side by side without becoming one. Answer four hundred and fifty-five was filed on the sixth place beside the others with a finger between every pair, exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment and not a doctrine; the seventh place stayed empty. Through the finger's-width door the river kept \lore{water over water}, a low thing over a lower thing under \lore{the winter skin}, the note coming from under the covering and not from the covering and ending on its own finishing. The hand outside the wool came up an inch toward it --- the inch not a reach, not a wanting made into a claim, only the body's small assent to a sound --- took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole on the note's finishing, let it go and came straight down and laid itself flat against the fold on answer four hundred and fifty-five as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. There was no next hand in the room. He did not wipe the thread and did not lift the crust. Under the boards the four low pulls kept their silence where the fifth should have stood, and nobody lifted the floor and nobody supplied the pull. Forward, into the afternoon of the sixteenth not lived and not brought into this one.
