## Chronicle

Three mornings came down under the Vail roof like Ash settling where no broom had gone, and the house took them up as though nothing had turned. On Silt 14, 15 and 16 Sella Vail went to the hearth before the basket, told the coals it was the thaw, turned them with a broken pot's back, lifted the twice-mended pot thumb-first with both proverbs to pot and fire, turned the dressed grain under wool, and set the pot rim two inches beyond his reach. The widow's mark hung where it hung; seven openings in seven months were seven, not a rule. The boy took the body in its old order — crust gone into old horn, the keeping found where put, the thread stiff and unwiped, cold stopped at cheek and wrist-joint, the Afterdrop waiting past a warming nobody would perform — and the three wants quarrelled without verdict, both true, the second louder.

At the kindling Tarn set six in the worn thumb-mark and the seventh at the lip beside Garl Vail's unforged sentence. On the fourteenth he told the wood, *Six counted. The next I don't count*. On the fifteenth he cut and counted, said no word, left that space a space; the boy filed it beside the older stopped sentence — a hand withdrawn from a field before the dressing goes on, worth nothing at two inches. On the sixteenth the word went back to the wood and the silence was found where put, not made a habit. The loan stayed uncalled in a term already ended.

Jorra Vail parted dusty forearms from clean as clot from vessel, led at the cold damper, knew the full note three turns ahead and withheld it from any High Kiln master. *He's not Vail* stayed sealed; the promise to the slate-burners stayed neither given nor withdrawn against the burned slate at five. Garl stayed in objects: coat, wheel-seat, visible crack, unforged sentence, the fifth pull nobody supplied.

In the afternoon of the sixteenth the clean dark line at the Vail corner kept its finer grey called the thaw a hair less distinct along one unswept stretch where no broom had gone in two years, thickened in the dry cold with the faint sour tang of Ash; through the finger's-width door the river's air was less cold at one edge where its low note ran nearer under the winter skin. He found both where put, did not look twice, called them weather, and supplied no payer.

Six honest answers, 451 through 456, stood on the sixth place, two to each day, not one a doctrine. The count was four hundred and forty exact, six months and two hundred and sixty days told by nobody. The hand came up an inch toward the river, took a small intake, held the interval whole and came down flat on the fold on answer 456. There was no next hand in the room. He did not wipe the thread and did not lift the crust.

## Continuity

The hand outside the wool came up an inch toward the river's note (the inch not a reach), took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole on the note's finishing, let it go and came straight down flat on the fold on answer 456, as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. No next hand in the room. He did not wipe the thread and did not lift the crust; four low pulls under the boards kept their silence where the fifth should have stood and nobody supplied the pull.
