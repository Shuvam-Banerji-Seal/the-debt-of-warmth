## Scene 1 — The Fourteenth Morning of Silt

- **Day and time:** Day 438, Silt 14, Y.H. 814, Fourthday; small hours opening into first grey and the morning's work.
- **Place:** Vail kiln-house under the loaf shelf's low angle; the hearth, pot board, rack end; the nineteen steps down; the kindling and flagstones; the wheel-shed edge and cold damper.
- **Present:** Corin/Dan (carried), Sella Vail, Tarn, Jorra Vail; Garl Vail in objects only. Ilva offstage with her count in wood and her fear unpaid; Hessa's slate unread up the road, Berr up the clay road with the box locked and the key gone up in a coat pocket, the soft-cuffed clerk reported at the out-of-line landing and never a sighting. No market, Cross, bell, Account-day, collector or runner.

**Beats in order**
1. The chapter's only `\dropcap`, then the first `\daymark{438}{Silt 14, Y.H. 814, Fourthday}`; small hours open on the hand flat on the fold on **answer 450**, count **437 exact**, six months and 257 days told by nobody, the carried fire's fifth unpaid and Garl's name unspoken the same number, four readings of the year behind one unsigned figure up the clay road in Berr's locked box behind the twelve of Y.H. 813, four being four and not one account, the figure read still not the figure paid and no signature on the space, and **no next hand in the room**. The second asking of Silt 12 and the Slate Row face of Silt 13 stand each on its own place with a finger between and are **not** told again on a morning that earned only its fourteenth; do not recap them, do not make a lesson of the fourth reading.
2. The surgeon takes the body in its old order: crust gone past leather into old horn two inches from the ear with its corner a hair off the wool; keeping at the fold found where put in the empty place, still a finding and not a placing, no third crust, no name, no payer; thread stiff in the dry kiln heat and unwiped, because nobody under any roof has ever been given an instruction about the thread; cold stopped at cheek, temple and the bare wrist-joint where the wool rides back as at the end of a written line; a face composed by cold is not composure and a cold patient cannot clot; the Afterdrop waiting past a warming nobody here will perform, with the level basket and sideways heat being the rewarming he would choose and cannot ask for.
3. The infant's three wants — crust lifted two inches, thread wiped, a hand on the pot handle — quarrel with the surgeon's register; both true, the second louder, neither buying the other, the quarrel the work and no verdict.
4. First grey: Sella carries him down the nineteen steps at the half-pace from every open slate, instruction to wool, river wind, pot and fire and never to the basket, basket not told the day or the hour or told to be still; goes to the hearth before the basket; tells the coals it is the thaw and turns them with the back of a broken pot, a dry scrape of iron over stone; gives the small intake, hold and letting-go toward no opening with neither gap figured; finger along the shelf's top so the charcoal stays unread; lips on a two-gapped row; one glance at the cold clay jar with the hand shut half over nothing and the little-door burn closing on air.
5. Twice-mended pot lifted thumb-first where the tighter wire crosses the crack, hairline showing between older brass staple and wire, both proverbs to pot and fire and never to a person; dressed grain turned under wool unfigured; pot rim two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat and no look toward the bed; widow's mark untouched — a Fourthday morning is not an opening and seven openings in seven months are seven and not a rule.
6. Tarn at the kindling and flagstones: six where the house can check, seventh at the lip beside Garl's unforged collector sentence with no name on the man who turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on the tile debt; small knife at the edge of its sill, hand over blade and sill and away with no breath held at the top of the reach, the coming away the whole of the checking; in the tone of a man stating the weather he tells the wood *Six counted. The next I don't count* — found where put and not made a habit, a tenth stopping, a payment or a rule; ear-sentence stopped at the word that would call the loan, term ended, loan uncalled; Ash in the knuckle creases, pale clip-ring at the left wrist, *Yes, house* to the fire.
7. Jorra at the wheel-shed edge: meal-fine grey slate dust ground small past the bent little finger and white in a fist-crease; dusty forearms parted from clean as clot from vessel; wheel and pot spoken to before any person; full note known three turns ahead and withheld from any High Kiln master who would call it early; bent finger into the cold damper ahead of Sella's unfinished sentence, one bite holding and one taking, no Grathic word covering both, coming out without a second holding of Ash 26; *he's not Vail* sealed, promise neither given nor withdrawn against the burned slate at five.
8. The boy keeps **438 exact**, six months and 258 days, and files **answer 451** on the sixth place, exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment.
