The afternoon of the fifteenth came down off the clay road the way the mornings came, without a servant and without a knock, finding the yard where the yard stood and taking the low places first. \person[Corin]{The boy} was carried out after the grain was turned and set at the edge of \place{the kindling} where the coming and going of feet did not touch the basket; the instruction went to the wool, the river wind, the pot and the fire and never to the basket, and the basket was not told what the hour was or told to be still. A basket set down is a pot set down and no woman explains the afternoon to a pot. The light lay flat on \place{the flagstones} and had no height in it, and the yard went about its small work the way a mouth goes on with its chewing while the mind is elsewhere, which is to say honestly and without attention to him.

\person{Tarn} was at the kindling with the quiet already worked into him. Six pieces stood in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check, and the seventh lay at the lip beside \person[Garl Vail]{Garl}'s unforged collector sentence, with no name under the man who had turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on \lore{the tile debt} circling against a firing of tiles that would never come. The stack kept its own arithmetic of four, four and a space, and the space was not a fifth and was not filled. The small knife lay at the very edge of its sill, and his hand went over blade and sill as a man passes a hand over a tool put back where it belongs, and came away with no breath held at the top of the reach; the coming away was the whole of the checking. He cut nothing, counted nothing and spent nothing.

And he said no word to the wood.

This was the afternoon's whole event, and it stood no higher than a held breath and made no sound. The word that had gone to the kindling on the fourteenth, and again that morning in the tone of a man stating the weather, did not go to it now. The space where the sentence would have stood was left as a space, as a place at a table is left when the hand that would have set the cup there does not come. A withholding is a change when a word was there yesterday and is not there today; it is not a drift when the hand that withholds is the same hand that gave and gives again when the day has earned the giving. He would say it on the sixteenth so that the silence would be found where put and not made a habit, for a second silence would be a custom and a third a way, and a custom and a way both want a payer standing under them and none stood.

The boy took the absence apart by the place of what was there and what was not. Six in the worn thumb-mark. Seven at the lip. The knife set back at the edge of its sill. The hand gone away from blade and sill with the breath let go unspent. And the space where the sentence would have stood, which was a space and nothing more, with no voice under it and no intention in it, for an intention wants a payer where none stands. He had once watched a surgeon take his hand out of a field before the dressing went on --- out and clean and complete, the hand coming away the whole of the care --- and had known the value of the thing by where the hand stopped and not by what the hand said afterwards. That was another room and another pair of hands. It paid nothing here. But the shape was the shape, and he had it by the place of it: a hand withdrawn from a field before the dressing goes on, exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air.

He filed it on a place of its own beside the older stopped sentence and Garl's unforged collector sentence, with a finger between every pair. It was a finding like any other, an object and not a doctrine, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood. It was expressly not a tenth stopping, not a payment, not a hearing, not a rule, not a custom; no payer was named under it and no slate was entered. He did not lay it across the second asking of the twelfth, or the face from \place{Slate Row} at the yard's edge on the thirteenth, or the reported second slate of the sixth, or the soft-cuffed clerk's entered figure brought up the yard as a report and never a sighting, or the promise \person[Jorra Vail]{Jorra} had not given against \lore{the burned slate} standing at five, or the four readings of the year behind one unsigned figure up \place{the clay road}. Two silences under one roof were two silences, and four readings were four and not one account, and none of them had found a hand to stand under it and enter it. A joining would have wanted a payer, and there was nobody under any roof to pay. The caregiver's hand that had gone to the wood and had not put the sentence into it was a finding like any other, with no hand under it to make it a claim on anybody.

The loan stayed uncalled in a term already ended. The Seed 16 breath stayed unspent, his valuable ear exposure and not safety; a term ending is not a payment, a forgiveness or a freedom, and leaves a man more exposed than it found him. The sentence about an ear under this roof being worth more than the debt that held it came all the way to the word that would call the loan and stopped there, a remembered ninth stopping with no tenth, and did not go further on an afternoon that had earned only its fifteenth. The fear of being entered again for vagrancy was still his without a figure, and so was the want he did not voice for a slate with his own name and no mark against it. His chest had been tight since the dark. \lore{Ash} was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line, and the pale clip-ring at his left wrist caught what little light there was and gave it back.

He took the broom only after it had been given its instruction. \emph{Yes, house}, he said to the fire, and went on over the flagstones without turning his head toward \place{the out-of-line landing} below \place{the north bridge}, where the soft-cuffed clerk was reported still set with his slate turned inward for a measure nobody had called, a report brought up the yard and never a sighting, a witness and a tenth hand to nobody. The stroke went over the shallow bright patch where the half-check had once stopped and went on past it without the half-check, the stones keeping the place where it had stopped, joined to no cause and to no ear. A man who owns nothing knows where every small thing is; a man who has counted six years knows what a seventh is worth when nobody comes to call it. The small sounds came first because they do not lie and the large ones are mostly weather: the broom's dry rasp, the knife's small settling on stone, a pail set down on \place{the flat stone by the well} with its flat knock, barley wall to wall without a number. Costs came as directions and went away as weather. A fist came up out of a sleeve toward the water, looked at the light, shut and went back into the sleeve, arriving nowhere; no figure came off the wood in any sleeve in the yard, and no slate was opened within reach of the basket.

The light went off the flagstones the way water goes off a stone lip, thinning at one edge first and then leaving all at once, and \person{Sella Vail} came for the basket at the going of it. She carried him up \place{the nineteen steps} at the half-pace from every open slate, the instruction going to the wool and the banked fire and never to the basket, and said nothing to the basket of what the kindling had withheld or of what the afternoon had been. A woman carries a pot home and does not give the pot the day's news. Under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof, \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill}, \place{Grath}, the whole grammar came back as though nothing had turned.

She went to \place{the hearth} before the basket. She told the coals it was \lore{the thaw} in the voice another woman would have used to a child who could not hear her, and turned them with the back of a broken pot, a dry scrape of iron over stone that went into the boy's ear ahead of the warmth. She gave the small intake, the hold and the letting-go toward no opening, and figured neither of the two gaps; they were gaps and wanted no figure put under them, for a figured gap wants a payer standing in it and none stood. Her finger went along the top of \place{the loaf shelf} so the charcoal tally on its underside stayed unread; no hand went beneath, no mark was copied, no row was numbered. Her lips moved on a two-gapped row that was neither prayer nor count and wanted no answer. One glance went to the clay jar standing cold under \lore{lead glaze} beyond \place{the pot board}; the hand shut half over nothing, and the little-door burn on the back of her left hand --- two straight sides, a lintel and a threshold --- closed on air and went back to the pot.

She lifted the twice-mended pot thumb-first where the tighter wire crossed the crack, the hairline of the original break showing between the older brass staple and the wire, a dark thread with a hand's work on either side. \emph{Leave the crack showing. People trust a thing they can see has been handled}, she said to the pot. \emph{If it's worth mending it's worth mending twice. The second time is the real one}, to the fire, and never to a person. Repair stayed apart from restoration, as a surgeon keeps a graft apart from a cure; the pot was mended and was not made whole, and the crack was left where a man could see it had been handled. The boy watched the thumb go to the crossing of the wire and understood, as he had understood on the morning, that a thing is held less heavily the better it is known. He had handled a thousand fields with a light hand and had never once called it gentleness.

At \place{the rack end} she drew back the wool from the dressed grain and turned it for damp, the work of the ending of a day given the whole of her attention, which was a way of giving none of it to the basket. She lifted a corner, laid her palm flat on the grain and let the cool come up into it, the cool of a thing that had taken the night's damp and kept it. Piece by piece she turned it, lighter each time, laying each back under the wool in the order it had been lifted, until the whole lay covered and unfigured and the damp was shut in again. She gave it no figure and no handful and no name for the damp. The arithmetic that would be read over grain laid like this at the month's next reading would not know what it cost, and the ink-neat space between that figure and its price would carry no signature, for paper is not a payer and no key came under this roof.

The pot rim stood two inches beyond his reaching hand, with a half-beat at the setting and no look toward \place{the bed}; the half-beat was the distance between the intention and the arrest, and she did not measure it and did not name it. \lore{The widow's mark} hung where it hung. She touched it before she opened \place{the kiln mouth} and at no other hour, and the kiln mouth was not open; a going of the light on a Fifthday was not an opening, and seven openings in seven months were seven and not a rule, and no eighth came due on a day that had earned only its fifteenth. At \place{the Vail corner} the clean dark line kept its finer grey beside it, thickened in the dry cold with the faint sour tang of Ash coming up where no broom had gone in two years, unswept and called weather. She put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face and added no second fold. \lore{The keeping at the fold} was found where put and stayed a finding. Through \place{the finger's-width door} \place{the river} kept \lore{water over water}, a low thing over a lower thing under \lore{the winter skin}, the note coming from under the covering and not from the covering and ending on its own finishing; the air off the river was as cold as it had been at the going of the light on the days behind, and there was nothing in it today that had not been in it yesterday, and he made nothing of it. Nothing under the roof had been mended that evening by anything that had stood on its own place through the afternoon, and nothing had been made a lesson of it. The keeper of the fire fed the fire and the pot and the grain and said nothing at all to the basket about the wood.

The small hours came down under the roof without a boundary drawn, the way water comes into a dry cup when nobody is watching. \place{The towel} came out at the head of the bed as instruments come out when a case is done and before the next is called, rough from the wash and smelling of tallow and low smoke. The boy lay at the low angle of the loaf shelf with the fold at his ear; one stiff brown-edged barley crust stood two inches from his ear, gone past leather into old horn in the dry nights, and the empty place beside it held the keeping found where put at the going of the light, still a finding and not a placing, with no second fold, no third crust, no name, no gift, no payment and no payer. The thread of drool at his mouth had gone stiff in the dry kiln heat and was unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread. Cold had stopped at cheek and temple and the small bare joint at the wrist where the wool rode back, and had stopped there as at the end of a line written out for it; a face composed by cold is not composure, and a cold patient cannot clot. \lore{The Afterdrop} waited past a warming nobody in this house would perform. He knew a basket set level out of the draught, with the heat coming to it sideways, was the careful rewarming he would choose, and could not ask for it; the knowing was a finding like any other, with no hand under it to make it a claim on anybody. The infant under the same skin wanted the crust lifted two inches to his mouth, wanted the thread wiped from his lip, wanted a hand on the handle of the pot that would bring a cup within reach. Both registers were true. The second was louder. Neither bought the other, and the quarrel went on without a settlement because a settlement wants a hand to enter it and the only hand in the case was the small one outside the wool that could not close two inches of air.

He went over the day by the place of each thing with every gap left a gap and did not add them, for a surgeon who adds findings before the last is in has already made up the answer he wanted before he cut. Six in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check and could not. The seventh at the lip beside the unforged collector sentence. The knife set back. The hand away. The space where the word should have stood and was a space. He set them out on the towel in the order they would be wanted and found them exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air. Costs had come as directions and gone away as weather; a fist toward the water had arrived nowhere; a pot had been given a proverb and not a price; a number read over covered grain had not known what the grain cost and the space between figure and price carried no signature. He entered none of them.

Six hearings of the \lore{full note} stood beside and never among with the seventh place empty and \place{the Drawing house}'s flat knock outside on its own place; the heard bell stood beside the told bell with both gaps in their squares, and three counts in three places stayed three --- the kiln's four-and-a-gap behind the wall, the boards' four-and-four-and-a-knot, and \lore{the carried fire}'s four low pulls with the fifth missing. Nine record-first hands stood with nine stoppings and no tenth. Seven openings in seven months were seven and not a rule. The withheld word of the afternoon stood on a place of its own beside the older stopped sentence and the unforged collector sentence, not a tenth stopping, not a payment, not a hearing, not a rule and not a custom, with no payer under any of the three and no joining between them. The offered figure and its return, the second asking at the well on the twelfth, the face from Slate Row on the thirteenth, the reported second slate of the sixth, the neighbour's near-reaching at the shelf, the hair-thin thaw of the seventh, the clerk's entered figure brought up the yard as a report and never a sighting, the fifth shaping of \lore{the well-saying}, the three likenesses of Slate Row and the two market sightings on separate trays --- a colour and a shape, no loaf, slate, sale or name passing between them --- stood each on its own place with a finger between every pair. None was a reader, a witness, a tenth hand, a custom, a payment or a payer, and none was made to answer the word the wood had not been given. A likeness was not a cause. A hand going flat over a sleeve was not a reader. A silence under one roof was not a sentence spoken under another.

Four readings of the year stood behind one unsigned figure up the clay road, four being four and not one account, the first not made a thirteenth by the three that followed, the second supplying no signature, the third no doctrine, the fourth no payer, the figure read still not the figure paid; the twelve of Y.H. 813 were not laid across the four to make a row come out even, and \person{Berr Clay}'s box stayed locked with the key gone up in a coat pocket that never came back down. None of the four was made to answer any kept thing under this roof, and the month's second reading and the year's fifth stood beyond the close as a date and were not brought. The unsent call to \person{Nina} stood where it had been put and was not paid again.

He kept four hundred and thirty-nine exact, six months and two hundred and fifty-nine days told by nobody who would have known what a number counts. The carried fire's fifth went unpaid and Garl's name went unspoken the same four hundred and thirty-nine, the man and the fire standing side by side without becoming one, for a number counted is a number with air around it and air buys no warmth. Answer four hundred and fifty-four was filed on the sixth place beside the others with a finger between every pair, exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment and not a doctrine; the seventh place stayed empty. Through the finger's-width door the river kept its low note of water over water under the winter skin, coming from under the covering and ending on its own finishing. The hand outside the wool came up an inch toward it --- the inch not a reach, not a wanting made into a claim, only the body's small assent to a sound --- took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole on the note's finishing, let it go and came straight down and laid itself flat against the fold on answer four hundred and fifty-four as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. There was no next hand in the room. Six stood in the worn thumb-mark and the seventh at the lip where the house could check and could not, and the word the wood had been told on the fourteenth was not given to it on the fifteenth. He did not wipe the thread and did not lift the crust. Under the boards the four low pulls kept their silence where the fifth should have stood, and nobody lifted the floor and nobody supplied the pull. Forward, into the sixteenth day not lived and not brought into this one.
