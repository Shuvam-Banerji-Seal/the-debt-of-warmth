The afternoon of the sixteenth came on without a bell and without a hand to call it, the way a draught comes under a door in a warm room --- not at the door and not all at once, but along the floor first, and up the skirting, and only afterward at the back of the neck. \person{Sella Vail} went to \place{the hearth} before she went to the basket, and the household straightened around the order of it as a table straightens when a hand lifts a weight off one corner. She carried \person[Corin]{the boy} down \place{the nineteen steps} at the half-pace from every open slate, taking the tilt out of the turn at the landing before the wicker had settled, and the instruction went to the wool, the wind off \place{the river}, the pot and the fire and never to the basket. The basket was not told what the hour was or what the day had earned. In \place{the Clay Yard}, \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill}, \place{Grath}, she set him where the coming and going of feet would not touch the wicker, out of the straight draught where she could keep an eye on him between her pail and her broom, and went back to the small honest work of an ordinary Sixthday.

He took the small sounds first because they did not lie and the large ones were mostly weather. A pail went down on \place{the flat stone by the well} with a flat knock that had no note in it and wanted none; the knock was the whole of the event and there was no message folded inside it. The rope at \place{the yard well} settled into its knot with the cold-stiffened rasp, complaining while the weight came on it and going quiet when the weight was taken, and the knot held what the knot was given and asked nothing back. Barley lay wall to wall without a number. A loaf was pushed a finger's width along a board and stopped, and nobody called the stopping a figure. He took each apart by fist and eye and mouth and the half-beat in the gap: the fist was the part that arrived, the eye the part that checked, the mouth the part that said or held its peace, and between them stood the half-beat, a place and not a person, an interval with no intention in it. An intention wants a payer standing under it and none stood.

One wide circuit went around \place{the seven walked-around places}, once and not twice, with a look and a wide step and no number; widest around \place{the returning-line place}, where a hand had gone in on \emph{Seed 15} and had not come back out. No name went with the wide step, no body, no figure for what any month had cost, no accident written across the space. The step was a keeping and not a sign. At \place{the trough} the bare dark water kept the shallow round print of the clay pan lifted away, a ring on a place of its own, not laid across the loaf's stopping and not laid across the ridge under the corner. The water-line stood level in a vessel nobody had calibrated; level is not honest and a level is not a promise. He went around the places as a man goes around a table with a sleeping child under it, not looking under and not forgetting what is there.

Costs came as directions and went away as weather and left no entry. A woman waiting her turn opened a shut fist toward the water, looked at what was in it, shut the fist again and put it back into a sleeve; the number arrived nowhere. A woman sent to ask what a pot cost was sent to the pot and got a note and not a price. The figure read over covered grain at home would not know what the grain had cost, and the ink-neat space between figure and price would carry no signature, for paper is not a payer and no key came under this roof. He had worked in a room where a price had to be said aloud before it could be entered, and a number arrived at a desk with the hand that had held it gone clean out of the sentence; here the numbers went fist to sleeve and figure to weather, and nobody turned them into a row. Walk-ons came and went at the well and the rail with pails and pots, going about the ordinary grammar of a Sixthday, and none looked into the wicker or gave the basket a name, an hour or an instruction. He took their not-seeing as findings and not as slights; a finding is what a surgeon takes when he cannot ask and nobody in the room can answer. \person{Ilva} was offstage with her short strip of notched birch in its fold and her fear unpaid, her ninth hand nine and no figure off the wood; a second neighbour had asked on the twelfth and the answer had been the pot and the weather, and the asking stood where it had been put and was not told again on a day that earned only its sixteenth.

There was no market, no \place{Cross}, no bell, no Account-day, no called collector and no runner on the sixteenth. Nothing from the fifteenth's withheld word, or from the twelfth's asking, or from the face at the yard's edge on the thirteenth, had come into the yard as sign, lesson, mending, cause, payment or payer, and the house took up its grammar as though nothing had turned.

At \place{the Vail corner} the clean dark line kept its finer grey called \lore{the thaw} a hair less distinct along one stretch where no broom had gone in two years. The grey had thickened in the dry cold and carried the faint sour tang of \lore{Ash} coming up off it, the mineral smell of a place where something had been left too long and nobody had swept it into an account. Beside it the called-gone grey of the ridge lay left in place with a finger between; a thing left in place is not a thing forgiven. He had seen the hair-thin grey of this same line on the seventh, and had seen it lie a hair less distinct then too, and the finding now was not the older finding made again: the line had not been made to answer the month's turn or the reading that had gone up the road, and nobody had passed a finger along the stretch or swept it into a row. It lay where it lay, and he found it where put and did not look twice, for looking twice is how a finding becomes a habit and a habit wants a payer standing under it.

Through \place{the finger's-width door} the air off the river was less cold at one edge, where the low note of \lore{water over water} ran nearer under \lore{the winter skin} and the tang came thinner. The note came from under the covering and not from the covering; the covering had been laid on by cold and would be taken off by cold, and neither had been asked who would pay for the taking off. He called both findings weather, and weather is the name a thing is given when no hand stands under it to own it.

Not a sign. Not a fourth covering. Not a fourth likeness. Not a sixth shaping. Not a payment. There was no payer under either grey and he did not supply one, and he did not make the softer air again on a day that had earned only its sixteenth. The two greys at the corner kept their finger between them, the called-gone beside the left-in-place, and he did not lay one across the other to make a row come out even. In the other life he had stood at a ward window and watched frost leave the glass in two lines that met at a corner, and a nurse had called it the weather and gone on with her count; he had learned to read such lines by the place where they stopped and not by the name anybody gave them. These lines stopped where they stopped. He wrote nothing down that any Court could read. The nurse's count had been a count of something else, and the frost had not been entered in it, and the two had stood side by side without becoming one, which was the whole of what he carried out of that room and the reason he carried it past tense and untagged, paying nothing.

The word that had gone to \place{the kindling} on the fourteenth and had not gone to it on the fifteenth had gone back to it in the morning of this day, said in the tone of a man stating the weather, found where put and not made a habit. It stood on its own place in the file beside \person{Garl Vail}'s unforged collector sentence and the older stopped sentence, with a finger between every pair, and he did not bring it under the roof as a lesson or lay it across the greys at the corner. A silence kept where put and a grey lying a hair less distinct kept where put were two findings and not one doctrine, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood. Nothing from either crossed the threshold in the afternoon as sign, lesson, mending, cause, payment or payer, and the house went on with its grammar.

The going of the light came without anyone calling it. Sella came to the hearth before she came to the basket, and the household straightened around the order as a table straightens when a weight is set back where it belongs.

\emph{It's the thaw}, she told the coals, and did not tell the basket. She said it in the voice another woman would have used for a child who could not hear her, and turned the coals with the back of a broken pot, the dry scrape of iron over stone going into the boy's ear ahead of the warmth and leaving the blind ash where it lay. She gave the small intake, the hold and the letting-go toward no opening, figuring neither of the two gaps; a figured gap wants a payer standing in it and none stood. Her finger lay along the top of \place{the loaf shelf} so the charcoal tally beneath stayed unread; no hand went beneath, no mark was copied, no row was numbered. Her lips moved on a two-gapped row that was neither prayer nor count and wanted no answer. One glance went to the clay jar standing cold under \lore{lead glaze} beyond \place{the pot board}; the hand shut half over nothing, and the little-door burn on the back of her left hand, two straight sides with a lintel and a threshold, closed on air and went back to the pot.

Her hands were never still. When they had nothing to do they counted --- spoons, nails, the breaths of a child nobody numbered --- and came back to the hairline at the wire. He had worked in a room where the instrument count was called aloud before a case and again after, and the count was the only thing in that room that could not lie, because a sponge is a sponge and a needle is a needle and neither has a reason to flatter you. Here a woman counted spoons and the breaths of a child nobody numbered and came back to the hairline, and the count came back right, and nobody looked under anything. He read the work and not the woman, which was the whole of what he was allowed. One grey streak at her temple said winter, and she said nothing about the winter. She smelled of tallow and woodsmoke and wet wool, and under it the faint sour tang of the corner nobody had swept, and she carried the smell the way she carried the mark, without comment.

\lore{The widow's mark} hung where it hung and she did not touch it; a going of the light is not an opening, a Sixthday is not an opening, and seven openings in seven months are seven and not a rule. She touched the mark before she opened \place{the kiln mouth} and at no other hour, and the kiln mouth was not open. She lifted the twice-mended pot thumb-first where the tighter wire crossed the crack, the hairline of the original break showing between the older brass staple and the wire, a dark thread with a hand's work on either side, and both proverbs went to the pot and the fire and never to a person: \emph{Leave the crack showing. People trust a thing they can see has been handled}; \emph{If it's worth mending it's worth mending twice. The second time is the real one}. Repair was not restoration; a restored thing hides the hand that handled it and a mended thing leaves the hand showing, so that a vessel carrying its break can be read by the next woman who takes it up. Garl Vail's laugh had ended in a cough and his proverbs had survived him improved by use, and neither the hairline nor the sayings bought the absence a payment, a name or a mending.

At \place{the rack end} she turned the dressed grain under wool with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child, set each piece where it had lain and drew the wool back with the flat of her hand; no figure went over it and no handful was counted. Then she set the pot rim two inches beyond the reaching hand, with a half-beat and no look toward \place{the bed}. The half-beat was the distance between the intention and the arrest; she did not measure it and did not name it, for a named gap wants a payer put under it and none stood. Two inches of air bought no warmth and she knew it as well as he did, and neither of them said so.

She carried him up the nineteen steps at the half-pace and set him under the roof at the low angle of the loaf shelf, at the edge of the light where the heat came sideways, never in \place{the Debt Loft}. She put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face and added no second fold. \lore{The keeping at the fold} in its empty place was found where put and stayed a finding, with no third crust, no name, no gift, no payment and no payer. The crust stood unmoved two inches from his ear, gone into old horn in the dry nights with its corner a hair off the wool. The thread at his mouth stayed stiff and unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread. Cold had come to cheek and temple and the small bare joint at the wrist where the wool rode back, and had stopped there as at the end of a line written out for it; a face composed by cold is not composure and a cold patient cannot clot. \lore{The Afterdrop} waited past a warming nobody under this roof would perform. He could not speak a word of \lore{Grathic} and had no way to ask for any of the three things the body wanted, and the wanting was complete and therefore useless. Under \place{the boards} \lore{the carried fire} kept four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer without a name for four hundred and forty days; nobody lifted the floor and nobody supplied the pull. The kiln behind the wall breathed in fours with its gap, and the river under the door kept its low over lower under a skin, three counts in three places that did not become one, for agreeing any two would want a payer put under both and none stood.

In the small hours \place{the towel} came out at the head of the bed as instruments come out when a case is done and before the next is called, rough from the wash and smelling of tallow and low smoke. He went over the day by the place of each thing with every gap left a gap and did not add them: the pail's flat knock on the stone, the rope's cold-stiffened rasp, barley wall to wall without a number, the loaf stopped a finger's width; the seven walked-around places once and not twice, widest at the returning-line place; the trough's round print on its own place; the two unswept lines at the corner with a finger between; the hair-thin thaw found where put and called weather; the softer air through the door found where put and called weather; the fist arriving nowhere, the note that was not a price, the figure that did not know what the covered grain cost with the ink-neat space unsigned. The file turned out exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer.

Six hearings stood beside and never among, with the seventh place empty and \place{the Drawing}'s flat knock outside; the heard bell stood beside the told bell with both gaps in their squares, and three counts in three places stayed three, for agreeing any two would want a payer put under both and none stood. Nine record-first hands stood with nine stoppings and no tenth. Seven openings in seven months were seven and not a rule. The withheld word of the fifteenth stood on its own place beside Garl's older stopped sentence and the unforged collector sentence at the kindling, a hand withdrawn from a field before the dressing goes on, exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, not a tenth stopping, not a payment, not a hearing, not a rule and not a custom, with no payer named and no slate entered. It did not join the line at the corner or the softer note under the door; a joining would want a payer and none stood.

Beside all these, on places of their own with a finger between every pair, stood the things kept but not spent: the offered figure that had come out of a shut fist, looked at the light and gone back into its sleeve, and its return; the second asking of the twelfth; the face from \place{Slate Row} at the yard's edge on the thirteenth; the reported second slate of the sixth; the neighbour's near-reaching at the shelf; the hair-thin thaw of the seventh; this sixteenth day's hair-thin thaw; the clerk's entered figure brought up the yard from \place{the out-of-line landing} below \place{the north bridge} as a report and never as a sighting; the fifth shaping of \lore{the well-saying}; the three likenesses of Slate Row; \lore{the burned slate} standing at five; the four ownerless directions; and the two market sightings on separate trays with no eighth of either. None was a reader, a witness, a tenth hand, a custom, a payment or a payer, and none was made to answer another. \person{Tarn} was offstage at the kindling with six where the house could check and the seventh at the lip beside \lore{the tile debt} circling against a firing of tiles that would never come; \person{Jorra Vail} was offstage at \place{the Vail wheel-shed}, his bent finger's cold taken out of \place{the damper} ahead of a sentence nobody finished, \lore{the full note} known three turns ahead and withheld. Neither was made a compact, and neither promise was called. The hand that will one day lay the district's private keepings together and call the joining an account is far off and is not his.

Four readings of the year stood behind one unsigned figure up \place{the clay road}, four being four and not one account: the first not made a thirteenth by the three that followed, the second supplying no signature, the third no doctrine, the fourth no payer, the figure read still not the figure paid. The twelve arithmetics of Y.H. 813 were not laid across the four to make a row come out even, for paper is not a payer and no key had come under any roof, and \person{Berr Clay} was up the road toward \place{High Kiln} with the box locked and the key gone up in a coat pocket that never came back down; his \emph{your house}, never \emph{I}, and his low saying about kindness kept their own places with a finger between, believed and not owed. None of the four was made to answer any kept thing under this roof. Silt 20, the month's second reading and the year's fifth, stood four mornings off as a date and was not brought into a day that had earned only its sixteenth. \person{Hessa Rale}'s narrow slate stayed unread up the road, her eighth hand eighth and joined to none. The surgeon's unsent call to \person{Nina} stood where it had been put and was not paid again, a small thing with a name on it that he did not look at.

The surgeon's exactness and the infant's want stood side by side, both true, the second louder, neither winning, the quarrel the work. He wanted the crust lifted two inches to his mouth, the thread wiped from his lip, a hand on the pot handle where the warmth lived; he knew the crust was old horn and would not nourish, the thread a small dry nothing, the pot handle a hand arriving at two inches of air and stopping. He had finished everything on his plate in another country and could not reach this one.

Answer four hundred and fifty-six came out and was filed on the sixth place. Six honest answers, four hundred and fifty-one through four hundred and fifty-six, stood on the sixth place, two to each of the three days, and not one was a doctrine. The answer was exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment laid across neither the grey at the corner nor the softer note under the door, neither the withheld word nor the offered figure and its return, neither the second asking nor the face at the yard's edge, neither the clerk's entered figure nor the four readings behind one unsigned figure, neither the keeping at the fold nor the carried fire's missing pull, nor the widow's mark.

Four hundred and forty exact, six months and two hundred and sixty days told by nobody who would have known what a number counted. The carried fire's fifth went unpaid and Garl's name went unspoken the same four hundred and forty, the man and the fire standing side by side without becoming one; his name had not crossed a threshold under this roof in four hundred and forty days and no account had gone up the road to call his debt. He did not stand the number up as a threshold, a doctrine or a figure, for a number counted is a number with air around it and air buys no warmth, and a number stood up as a doctrine is an account that has found a payer where none stood.

Through the finger's-width door the river kept its low note of water over water, a low thing over a lower thing under the winter skin, softer at one edge where the note ran nearer under the covering, the tang thinner there. The note came from under the covering and not from the covering and ended on its own finishing. The hand outside the wool came up an inch toward it --- the inch not a reach, not a wanting made into a claim, only the body's small assent to a sound --- took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole on the note's finishing, let it go as the note ended, and came straight down and laid itself flat against the fold on answer four hundred and fifty-six as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. There was no next hand in the room. He did not wipe the thread and did not lift the crust. Under the boards the four low pulls kept their silence where the fifth should have stood, and nobody lifted the floor and nobody supplied the pull. Forward, into a seventeenth day not lived and not brought.
