# Beat sheet — Chapter 55, *What the Week Gathered*

Days 216–218, Grain 7–9, Seventhday to Ninthday, Y.H. 813. Normal chapter, three lived days, two scenes per day, six scenes, about 17,600 words. Each day opens with its own `\daymark` in its first scene only; the second scene of a day has no new dated heading. Point of view never leaves Daniel Marchetti (Corin). The old life stays offstage: no present-tense Chicago break, no `\memory{}` tag. There is no market and no Account-day in these three days; Grain 10 (Day 219) is the first, held three mornings off. The one meaningful change is a saying that turns back toward the proverb it came from, gathered and **not paid**; a fourth telling of the burned slate arrives nowhere. Everything else advances without being spent. The count reaches two hundred and eighteen exact and unspent, spoken by nobody. Forward, not closed.

## Scene 1 — The Week Opens Without a Boundary

- **Day and time:** Day 216, Grain 7, Seventhday. Small hours under the roof, then before first grey and into the morning.
- **Place:** The bed at the loaf shelf's low angle; the hearth; the loaf shelf; the edge of the Clay Yard; the Vail threshold; the clay road at mid-morning.
- **Present:** Corin (alone in the small hours), Sella Vail (fire, shelf, door), Tarn and Jorra at the morning work, one neighbour at the door and a neighbour going up the clay road with boards (walk-ons). Garl held in objects.
- **Only drop cap of the chapter; first `\daymark{216}{Grain 7, Y.H. 813 (Seventhday)}`.**

**Beats, in order**

1. The hand lies down against the fold after the letting-go of the night before. Do not recall that coordination as a sign, growth, reaching or promise: a holding is a holding, set down like a tool. The boy is two hundred and sixteen days old and six months and thirty-six days, told by nobody (T071/T072).
2. The day arrives first as smell, then as a gap in sound: hot clay under tallow, wet wool under that, the sour Ash breath of the unswept corner the house calls the thaw; the kiln drawing in fours with a gap, not a bell. Carried fire four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have been, payer unnamed for two hundred and sixteen days (T088). River through the finger's-width door, boards' four nails, four and a knot: three counts in three places that do not become one, because agreeing them would want a payer and none stands to any (T061/T082).
3. The body: one leathery brown-edged crust two inches from the ear, empty place unnamed, no third; frozen drool thread unwiped; cold stopped at cheek, temple, bare wrist-joint where the wool rides back, afterdrop waiting past a warming nobody will perform. Surgeon's register takes the reading and buys nothing at two paces.
4. The file turned out as instruments on a towel and found exact, complete and worth nothing: six hearings beside and never among, sixth out of a person, no seventh; three near-full answers advanced and unpaid, now holding the pot that sang early and is not heard from; told bell beside heard; told figure beside paid with Ember's third reading gone up the clay road in Berr's locked box and answering nothing for the man carried out on Day 188; nine stoppings with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end and still ungiven; Seed 16 breath unspent; three held changes on three places, loft scrape fourth, pale line fifth, held inch sixth; burned-slate word told three times on its own place beside Jorra's dust and the two shut-room tellings with a hand between the three (T061/T082, T036/T076, T006, T035/T031/T065/T095). An empty place kept empty is a finding; a finding filled to make a row even is forged.
5. Word comes up the clay road that stalls are being set at the Cross against Grain 10; a neighbour goes up with boards on a barrow. No market and no Account-day in these three days. The district's arithmetic stays in loaves, glances, arms and the weather, never a figure (T077). Grain 10 is a direction with a date and no slate yet opened.
6. Sella comes to the fire before the basket. Instruction to wool, fire, pot, broom, running water, river wind --- never the basket. "The house." Half-pace from every open slate; glance at the clay jar cold under its glaze and hand shut half over nothing without a touch; lips along a row with two unnamed gaps and no figure; finger on top of the shelf's front edge so the charcoal stays unread; pot rim set out with handle two inches beyond the reaching hand, a half-beat longer at the setting, no look toward the bed. Widow's mark untouched --- no opening in thirty-six days (T058/T079, T100).
7. A neighbour's cost-question at the door goes to the pot and the weather, goes away level, nothing priced, refused or entered (T077). The yard goes around the clean dark line, the finer grey called the thaw, the rack space with broken birth-slates, the pan's gone round ring, the bare dark trough whose water is called weather, the field's empty place --- each with a look and a wide step and no number (T009/T013/T092/T098).
8. Morning hand: up an inch toward the note, **down at once** against the fold. A miss, no failure in it. Filed on the sixth place, laid across nothing, no payer named (T061/T082).

- **Emotional turn:** A working week opens without a boundary being drawn, while the world beyond the yard begins to make ready for a market the boy cannot enter. The oldest man in the room is the least use in it and the only one keeping the count.
- **Sensory anchors:** Hot clay under tallow; wet wool; sour Ash breath called thaw; kiln in fours with a gap; river water over water through a finger's-width door; stiff crust two inches off; frozen thread; grey streak pale off the coals; boards going up the clay road; the walnut-dark shelf and the neat charcoal hidden under its front edge.
- **Threads advanced:** T071/T072, T088, T061/T082, T058/T079, T100, T077, T009/T013/T092/T098, T036/T076, T006, T035/T031/T065/T095.
- **Foreshadowing planted / held:** Boards going toward the Cross for the tenth; Grain 10 as the day the seventh green and seventh shape are owed; pot handle two inches beyond his hand; the count nobody can enter; Berr's box and Hessa's slate stay up the road, unopened and unread.
- **Exact last image:** A neighbour's boards going up the clay road toward the Cross, and the boy's hand back down against the fold with the pot handle still two inches beyond it.

## Scene 2 — What the Fire Kept

- **Day and time:** Day 216, Grain 7. Afternoon into the small hours.
- **Place:** Loaf shelf's low angle; hearth; salt coat and hollow seat; kindling; edge of the wheel-shed; clay road at the going of the light; bed.
- **Present:** Corin, Sella, Tarn, Jorra; three or four unnamed men going low into the back room before first grey; Garl as the house's shape.

**Beats, in order**

1. Afternoon thins without ending; heat goes off the high air first, stays longest under the shelf, behind the pot and in the fold. Underside of the shelf becomes a roof that stops where his hand stops; one crust keeps its empty place unnamed, no third comes. Cold at cheek, temple, bare wrist-joint.
2. Garl kept in objects, not in a story: salt-stiff coat with the crease at the left shoulder, hollow seat and leftward lean, clean dark line and finer grey called the thaw unswept, proverb about leaving the crack showing gone to a pot with no crack, debt against the tiles circled without a name and worse for being uncalled. No lesson, no collector, name unspoken for two hundred and sixteen days (T009/T013/T092).
3. Sella at the shelf with her back to the basket: lips along a two-gapped row, no figure; jar-glance and hand half-shut; finger on top of the front edge; fire told the thaw; coals turned with the back of a broken pot; "The house will keep it"; count under the breath toward no opening; mark untouched at an afternoon and a dusk, because neither is an opening (T058/T079, T100).
4. Tarn at the kindling: broom only after instructed; "Six counted. The next I don't count"; knife at the very edge of its sill with six checkable and the seventh at the lip; Garl's unforged collector sentence beside it. Ear-sentence comes all the way to the word that would call the loan and stops with no figure and no name --- paid, if paid at all, as an interval and not as a calling: breath drawn at the top of the reach and not spent. Nine stoppings still nine, no tenth before the seventh year at its very end, still ungiven; Seed 16 breath unspent; loan uncalled. "Yes, house"; broom over the bright patch without the half-check and over the collector road without a turn; no collector came on Grain 7; stone cold in its unmarked loft pot, loft scrape on its fourth place (T036/T076, T061/T082, T006, T062).
5. Jorra at the wheel-shed edge: meal-fine grey slate dust past the bent finger and under the nails, white in a knuckle where a fist opened, not bone-coloured slip; dusty and clean forearms on one tray, parted as clot from vessel; men unnamed before first grey, room not called cold, two shut-room tellings still two, burned-slate word still a direction arrived nowhere; wheel told it cares if hands are steady, pot told "You'll do"; full note known three turns ahead and withheld; bent finger into the cold damper until Sella's unfinished sentence reaches the pot, then a hair's breadth and left standing --- a remembered repetition, one iron with two bites and no Grathic word for both mercies; "he's not Vail" sealed, promise ungiven (T035/T031/T062/T065/T095).
6. Carry home at the going of the light at the half-pace, woodsmoke before in long low bars, river wind behind with the taste of water over water; wide around the pan's gone ring, bare trough, rack space with broken birth-slates, field's empty place, carriers' place, each with a look and no number; carrying arm a record in muscle that remembers weight and not cost; bed, not loft; fold right, thread frozen (T058/T079, T098).
7. Small hours: hand up an inch toward the note, **held for the small intake only, down before the letting go**. An honest fraction on its sixth place; no hearing, seventh, stopping, payment, held change, key, power, rule; no payer. Count 216 exact, unspent, spoken by nobody; fire's fifth unpaid 216 days.

- **Emotional turn:** The household's maintenance goes on without an entry; the day's ordinary keeping settles into a small honest fraction of a hand --- a partial answer that is neither a miss nor a holding made twice.
- **Sensory anchors:** Afternoon heat slanting under the shelf; salt-stiff wool; fine grey beside the clean dark line; Ash worked into knuckle creases; pale clip-ring holding what light there is; cold iron of the damper; woodsmoke and river wind; frozen drool thread.
- **Threads advanced:** T009/T013/T092, T058/T079, T100, T036/T076, T061/T082, T006, T062, T035/T031/T065/T095, T098.
- **Foreshadowing planted / held:** Garl's unforged collector sentence; the knife's uncheckable seventh; the cold stone and its loft scrape; the two shut-room tellings held at two; no third telling; Grain 10 held.
- **Exact last image:** The hand coming down before the letting go of a breath nobody under the roof has counted, the thread frozen at his mouth and the one stiff crust two inches from his ear.

## Scene 3 — The Saying Turns Back

- **Day and time:** Day 217, Grain 8, Eighthday. Small hours, then before first grey and into the morning and midday.
- **Place:** Bed; hearth; loaf shelf; Clay Yard; Vail wheel-shed edge; yard well.
- **Present:** Corin, Sella, Tarn, Jorra; a kiln-hand come up from High Kiln on a wrong wind with a barrow (walk-on); a rack woman at the well and a man off the clay road with a number in his fist (walk-ons).
- **Second `\daymark{217}{Grain 8, Y.H. 813 (Eighthday)}`.**

**Beats, in order**

1. New day, no recap. Count 217, six months and thirty-seven days, nobody told (T071/T072); fire's fifth unpaid 217 days (T088); fire, river, boards three counts in three places (T061/T082). First smell hot clay under tallow; first sound the kiln in fours with a gap. Grain's second working week has no market and no Account-day until Day 219.
2. **The chapter's one meaningful change, gathered and not paid:** a kiln-hand come up from High Kiln with a barrow says over the boards, on a wrong wind, that there is a pot gone quiet on every row come the thaw. The singular report of one pot's silence bends back toward the old general proverb. Now three shapings stand on one tray --- "mostly sings itself out early," "has not been heard from since," "gone quiet on every row" --- and the boy takes them apart as a surgeon takes apart a wound, refusing to let three likenesses stand up and call themselves a rule, for a rule is an account that has found a payer and no payer stands to any shaping (T061/T082 near-full tray advanced and unpaid, T064, T035/T031).
3. The sweet edge of lead glaze rides up off High Kiln on the wrong wind and goes out on it --- a direction, not a second edge paid, no second green at a mouth, no seventh of anything (T064 held).
4. Jorra, who knew that pot's full note three turns before it left his hands and holds the nearly-full answer with air under it and a want in it, lets the saying stand as a saying. He gives no name to what ailed the man carried out on Day 188, makes no lesson of the green on the wind, and no pot is lost. The knowing stays on the near-full tray beside the hearings and never among them; no seventh hearing comes, no cause stands up, nothing is spent (T061/T082 advanced and unpaid, T035/T031).
5. At the yard well a rack woman with slip dried pale in her wrists asks what the month's pots will want; a man off the clay road keeps a number shut in his fist. Both go to the pot and the weather, arrive nowhere, nothing priced, refused or entered; the printed one in twelve stands unchallenged and the true subtraction stays locked in Berr's box up the road (T077, T015/T041/T047 held).
6. Sella's instruction grammar, the house, half-pace, jar-glance and hand half-shut, lips along the two-gapped row, finger above the charcoal, pot handle two inches beyond the hand with a half-beat and no look toward the bed; widow's mark untouched, no opening in thirty-seven days (T058/T079, T100).
7. Morning hand: up an inch, **down before the counted breath has finished** --- an honest miss filed on its sixth place and laid across nothing (T061/T082).

- **Emotional turn:** A report of one pot's silence bends back into a proverb about all pots, and the thing gathers interest without becoming a lesson. The district's arithmetic stays in loaves, glances and the weather, while a saying nobody owns takes a third shape on a tray nobody can read.
- **Sensory anchors:** Wrong-wind sweetness at the back of the throat; a barrow's boards going up the road; meal-fine slate dust white in a knuckle; the nearly-full note with air under it; bare dark trough and its water called weather; a number closing in a fist; grey streak pale off the coals.
- **Threads advanced:** T071/T072, T088, T061/T082 (near-full answer advanced), T064, T077, T058/T079, T100, T035/T031; T015/T041/T047 held offstage.
- **Foreshadowing planted / held:** The pot not heard from now has three shapings and still no cause, no owner, no name and no pot lost; do not join it to the carried man on Day 188 or name what ailed him; no seventh green or seventh sighting before Grain 10; no eighth likeness made a cause.
- **Exact last image:** Three shapings of one saying standing on a tray with no hand laying them together, and the wrong wind going out of the yard with the sweet edge behind it and nothing paid.

- **Foreshadowing planted / held:** The ear-sentence still short of the calling word; no tenth stopping; no collector; the collector sentence beside the knife; the pot sayings still sayings; Grain 10 held.

- **Threads advanced:** T035/T031/T065/T095, T077, T058/T079, T100, T036/T076, T062, T061/T082, T071/T072, T088; T050/T051, T033/T034, T015/T041/T047, T080, T090 held.

## Pull into Chapter 56

The final fraction is held and let go, not spent and not explained; nothing stands up out of it as a power, a rule or a payer. The pot that sang early and was not heard from has taken a third shaping and bent back toward the old proverb, with no pot lost, no cause and no name for what ailed the man carried out on Day 188; the burned-slate word has come over the boards a fourth time and arrived nowhere, still not a third telling of the counting room shut in the war; Tarn's ninth stopping still stands with the seventh year at its very end and the Seed 16 breath unspent; the widow's mark has not been touched since before the kiln on Ember 1; no opening, no collector, no third gap and no fourth likeness made a cause. The stalls are going up and the reading block is being made ready, but the market has not come and the boy has not been carried to the Cross. Two hundred and eighteen goes forward exact and unspent into Day 219, Grain 10, Tenthday, the count carried by the only person who would have known what it counted and spoken by nobody. Chapter 56 opens on the first Account-day and first market of Grain, where the seventh green is owed at a silent woman's mouth and the seventh sighting of the foundling at the market's edge --- both to be paid as a colour and a shape on places that do not touch, no loaf, no slate, no sale, never Fenna or Ulda in the narration and never promoted into a hearing, a cause or a romance. The district's arithmetic will change again and still not be the figure paid, and all the long threads stay open. Forward, not closed.
