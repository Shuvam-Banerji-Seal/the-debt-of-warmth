## Chronicle

Three days went over the Vail roof in the second working week of Grain, and the week opened without a boundary drawn. The boy at the loaf shelf's low angle was two hundred and sixteen days old on the seventh of the month and two hundred and eighteen on the ninth; nobody said either number. Each day came as a smell --- hot clay under tallow, wet wool, the sour corner --- and then as a gap in sound, the kiln drawing in fours and stopping, not a bell. Three counts in three places did not become one, for agreeing them would have wanted a payer. The crust lay two inches from his ear, its empty place unnamed and no third; the thread froze unwiped; cold stopped at cheek, temple and wrist, the afterdrop waiting past a warming nobody would perform.

Sella came to the fire before the basket, instructed wool, fire, pot and river wind, said the house where another woman would say I, kept her finger above the charcoal, and set the pot handle two inches beyond the hand with a half-beat and no look toward the bed. She did not touch the widow's mark; no opening in thirty-eight days. Garl was kept in objects --- salt coat, hollow seat, unswept line, a proverb gone to a pot, a tile debt circled without a name --- no lesson, no collector. Cost-questions went to the pot and the weather and away level.

On the eighth a kiln-hand from High Kiln on a wrong wind let fall a third shaping of the old saying: a pot gone quiet on every row come the thaw. The boy laid three likenesses on the near-full tray with a finger between them and would not let them stand up and call themselves a rule, for a rule wants a payer and none stood. Glaze's sweet edge went out on the wind that brought it. On the ninth the burned-slate word came over the boards a fourth time from Slate Row, on the breath of barley price and weather: a fourth telling of a slate, still not a third telling of the room shut in the war, and no new fire at the kiln mouth. Jorra grew quieter and the household straightened around the quiet without an order.

Tarn kept six where the house could check and the seventh at the lip, brought the ear-sentence to the word that would call the loan and stopped; no collector came. Six times the hand went up an inch and came down: at once, before a counted breath, twice more short of the count, down on the river's note, at once under conditions that had sometimes answered, and at the close through Sella's breath past the end of her count and down on the river's next note. Remembered, not a reach or a power; he still could not close two inches. Two hundred and eighteen went forward exact and unspent, spoken by nobody.

## Continuity

- **Exact final moment:** Small hours of Day 218, Grain 9, Ninthday. Corin on his bed at the low angle of the loaf shelf under the Vail roof, alone; Sella gone to her own bed before the small hours, the coals left to keep themselves, the two rooms not touching. With no breathing number in the house, he put the question to his hand: it went up an inch, was taken through the small intake, the hold and the letting go of one of the breaths Sella counts at her fire, kept through the little silence after her count had ended, and came down on the river's next low note --- a two-rhythm coordination, remembered, not a change made again, filed on the sixth place with nothing laid across it and no payer named. Thread frozen and unwiped; one stiff brown-edged crust two inches from the ear, its empty place unnamed, no third. Fire in the room that warms him, river in the room he cannot enter, neither stopping for the ear. Forward, not closed.
- **Day and time at the end:** Day 218, Grain 9, Y.H. 813 (Ninthday), small hours. Count two hundred and eighteen exact, complete, unspent, spoken by nobody; six months and thirty-eight days. Fire's fifth unpaid 218 days. Seventh year at its very end and still ungiven.
- **Where everyone is now:** Corin on his bed under the roof. Sella Vail in her own bed; she carried him home at the going of the light on all three days at the half-pace, set him on his bed and not in the loft, put the fold right without a look at his face and left the thread frozen. Tarn at the kindling/sill earlier, knife at the very edge with six counted where the house can check and the seventh at the lip, ear-sentence stopped short of the calling word as a remembered stopping, no collector on Grain 7, 8 or 9, warm stone cold in its loft pot. Jorra Vail at the wheel-shed with meal-fine dust, his quiet going before him into the shed the way a cold air goes before a door. Garl held in objects: salt coat, hollow seat, clean dark line and finer grey called the thaw unswept, proverb gone to the pot, tile debt neither paid nor settled nor named, name unspoken for 218 days. Three or more unnamed men went low into the back room before first grey on all three mornings, given no name.
- **Items, money, powers:** Nothing gained, lost or used within reach; no money, no purchase, no sale, no loaf passed, no slate opened. Pot handle stays two inches beyond the hand. Powers: none usable, no rank change, no experiment, no clamp; the six answers of this chapter (two on each day) filed as objects on the sixth place, not powers. Seed 16 breath unspent; loan uncalled.
- **Facts, says, promises, debts:** The pot saying took a third shaping on Day 217 --- a pot gone quiet on every row come the thaw --- so that three likenesses stood on the near-full tray, taken apart as a surgeon takes apart a wound and not allowed to stand up and call themselves a rule; no cause, no owner, no name for the man carried out on Day 188, no lesson and no pot lost. The sweet edge of lead glaze came up off High Kiln on a wrong wind on Day 217, touched the fold and went out on the wind that brought it: a direction, not a second edge paid. The burned-slate word came over the boards a fourth time on Day 218 from Slate Row on the breath of barley price and weather; four tellings of a slate are still not a third telling of the counting room shut in the war, and no slate burned under any roof and no fire was lit at the kiln mouth. Widow's mark untouched; no opening in thirty-eight days. *He's not Vail* sealed; promise of the boy's name neither given nor withdrawn. Sella's private roll unshown and Dessa's cost unentered. No collector called for Garl's tile debt or for the carried man.
- **Threads advanced:** T071/T072 (218 exact, nobody told), T088 (fifth unpaid), T061/T082 (near-full tray advanced, three shapings of the pot saying; six more hand answers filed, no seventh), T058/T079, T100, T077 (four cost-questions answered with pot and weather), T009/T013/T092/T098, T036/T076, T006, T062, T035/T031/T065/T095 (fourth telling of the burned slate, still two shut-room tellings; Jorra's claim gathers interest unpaid), T064 (wrong-wind sweetness a direction); T050/T051 and T033/T034 held (no seventh green, no seventh sighting before Grain 10), T015/T041/T047, T080, T090 held offstage. No seventh hearing, no tenth stopping, no third gap, no fourth likeness made a cause, no opening.
- **What Chapter 56 must not forget:** Opens on Day 219, Grain 10, Tenthday, the first Account-day and first market of Grain, one morning on from the close. Count goes forward at two hundred and eighteen, exact, unspent and spoken by nobody; the seventh green is owed at a silent woman's mouth as a colour and the seventh sighting of the foundling at the market's edge as a shape, places that do not touch --- both paid as sightings only, never promoted into hearings, a cause, a name or a romance, and neither named Fenna or Ulda in the narration. Keep the three shapings of the pot saying and the fourth burned-slate telling as directions arrived nowhere; keep the two shut-room tellings at two, the nine stoppings with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end, the Seed 16 breath unspent, the loan uncalled, the mark untouched, no collector, no pot lost and no third gap. The final fraction was held and let go, not spent and not explained; ask the hand again under the conditions that have sometimes answered.
