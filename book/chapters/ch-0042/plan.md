# Chapter 42 — beat sheet: *The Last Reading of Tallow*

**Days covered.** Days 173–179, Tallow 24–30, Y.H. 813, Fourthday to Tenthday. A **passage chapter at the seven-day cap**: seven lived days, each opened by its own dated `\daymark` on the first scene of that day and each lived in full for at least eight hundred words, none skipped, poured into another or summarised. Day 173 in Scene 1, 174 in Scene 2, 175 in Scene 3, 176 in Scene 4, 177 in Scene 5, 178 in Scene 6, 179 in Scenes 7–8. Day 179, Tallow 30, is the third Account-day of Tallow and the last market of the firing month. Check every weekday against `bible/calendar.md` (*t* = 336 + *N* − 1): Day 173 = Tallow 24 = Fourthday, 174 = Tallow 25 = Fifthday, 175 = Tallow 26 = Sixthday, 176 = Tallow 27 = Seventhday, 177 = Tallow 28 = Eighthday, 178 = Tallow 29 = Ninthday, 179 = Tallow 30 = Tenthday. Day 172, Tallow 23, is done and is not revisited, recapped or explained; Day 180 is Ember 1 and belongs to Chapter 43. Ember, not Smoke, is the eighth month; the district keeps calling Tallow the firing month and the narration never corrects it. No character speaks the date of the Account-day in advance.

**Arc.** Arc 1, *The Cold Stone* — "a warm stone, already going cold." Nothing here reaches Thin, opens a way, clamps, grafts, speaks Grathic or changes rank. The count is an old-life habit, not a gift. The surgeon's register colours the noticing and never becomes a power.

**The chapter's one meaningful change.** The month's last Account-day is lived and read over the boy. Berr Clay reads *one in twelve, as the notice says* for the third time in Tallow, wipes his thumb twice, calls the levy and denies it with *your house*, never *I*, and goes off up the clay road with the box locked and the true subtraction inside, believing he has done a kindness; the figure read aloud is still not the figure paid. Sella sets the basket in the moot-post's finger's width of shade where no slate lies and stands a half-pace off, a displacement made with no word and no reason given to the basket, and carries him home at the going of the light saying nothing. This is a real forward movement — a dated event that changes the district's arithmetic for the month and cannot be undone — but it settles nothing: no payer is named, no slate opened, no bond changed hands, no debt paid. The count reaches one hundred and seventy-nine exact and unspent and goes forward into Ember with the fire's fifth still missing.

**The discipline of the chapter.** Advance, never pay. All seven days carry the count exactly and the fire's payer unnamed. No calling of the loan, no tenth stopping before the seventh year is earned, no spending of the Seed 16 breath, no giving or cutting of the seventh year, no seventh hearing of the full note, no fourth near-full answer, no payer named to any keeping, no third gap, no fourth likeness made a cause, no finger of his drawing a line. The three held changes — the Day 161 damper fraction, the Day 166 held beat beneath the loaf shelf, the Day 171 flagstone half-check — stay on three separate places through all seven days; none is repeated as a new change, paired, tripled into a doctrine or allowed to stand up as a rule, for a rule is an account that has found a payer. The two shut-room tellings stay two; the told bell stays beside the heard; the widow's mark is touched at no shelf, well, rack, market, road, basket, bed or dusk, because none of them is an opening.

**What is withheld.** Marra stays out of Sill; Alda, Mira, Orra and the reed-cutter stay offstage; Dessa stays dead and unnamed to the boy, her tin unopened and her number unsaid; the foundling stays offstage with the sixth glimpse unspent, the green never Fenna in narration and no loaf, slate or sale near the basket; *he's not Vail* stays sealed and the promise of the foundling's name to the men who burn slates stays ungiven; the charcoal under the shelf stays unread, uncounted and uncopied; the two shelf gaps, the fold's crust and its gap, the pan's gone ring and the field's empty place stay on separate trays; the cord, knot-string, second twist, six pages, both tins, the locked box, the edge-on slate and the clerk's inward slate stay unread; no silt-leach, cause or cure is named for any cold. Point of view never leaves Dan's eyes: no Court, collector, back-room interior or Hearthseat scene. No present-tense Chicago break and no `\memory{}` tag (T004).

## Scene 1 — "The Last Tend Opens"
**Day and time.** Day 173, Tallow 24, Fourthday, before first grey through mid-morning. First `\daymark{173}{Tallow 24, Y.H. 813 (Fourthday)}` and the chapter's drop capital. No recap of Chapter 41.
**Place.** Under the Vail kiln-house roof (hearth, bed, loaf shelf's low angle), the kiln mouth, the drying racks and the yard well in the Clay Yard.
**Present.** Corin (in the basket, carried), Sella Vail, Tarn; rack and well neighbours as texture. Jorra is not on stage.

**Beats, in order.**
1. Drop capital into the smell of hot clay under tallow and wet wool; the first sound is the kiln's breath in fours with a gap, not a bell. He is one hundred and seventy-three days old and five months and twenty-three days and nobody under any roof says so (T071/T072). The crust lies stiff and leathery two inches from his ear with its empty place unnamed and no third; the thread of drool is frozen and unwiped; his hand outside the wool goes up an inch toward the note of water over water and comes down against the fold.
2. Sella gives every instruction to the wool, the fire, the pot, the broom and the running water and never to the basket; she says *the house* where another woman would say *I*, keeps the half-pace, turns the coals with the back of a broken pot, tells the fire it was the thaw, says *The house will keep it*, counts under her breath toward no opening, glances once at the clay jar cold under its glaze without touching it, pushes coal-black hair back with the burn-wrist so the grey streak shows pale, and sets the pot rim out with its handle two inches beyond the reaching hand, her hand resting a half-beat longer than the setting needs. The widow's mark hangs where it hung: a morning and a shelf are not openings (T058/T079).
3. The carried fire under the broken pot answers four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have been, its payer unnamed for a hundred and seventy-three days (T088). The river's note of water over water comes through the finger's-width door as a fifth low thing never counted among the hearings; overhead the boards keep four nails, four, and a knot the width of a thumb. Three counts in three places, and none becomes the others (T061/T082).
4. The yard goes around the clean dark line, the finer grey called the thaw, the rack space with its broken birth-slates and the empty place in the returning line where a hand went in on Seed 15 and did not come out, with a look and a wide step and no number (T009/T013/T092, T098 carried). The trough stands bare and dark and the water is called weather; the shallow pan's gone place keeps its round ring on a tray of its own.
5. Tarn takes the broom only after it is instructed, says *Yes, house* to an early thaw and a late while going on wanting the late because it puts off the paying, and sweeps over the road a house sends a man down when it wants a collector without once turning along it; no collector comes. Six are counted in the worn thumb-mark where the house can check and the seventh is held at the lip of the sill where it cannot, the knife at the very edge with Garl's unforged collector sentence beside it, Ash worked into the knuckle creases in the colour of the clean dark line, the pale clip-ring holding what light there is and giving it back, his chest tight since the dark (T036/T076).
6. Nine stoppings stay nine objects and not a rule with no payer named to any, the tenth withheld, the Seed 16 breath unspent at the top of each reach and the loan uncalled (T061/T082). The Day 161 fraction, the Day 166 beat under the board and the Day 171 flagstone half-check stand on three separate places and are not repeated, paired or joined (T062, T100, T036/T076).
7. The month's danger gathers in the dust ground small as meal on the wrong wind, the sweetish lead edge off High Kiln and a pot giving its thin note before its time; the boy files the sound as a danger and **not** as a fourth near-full answer. He reads the firing as a theatre he cannot enter — heat kept and counted behind a screen, a gate of two bites, a cost paid by a man whose name is not spoken — and his file is exact and worthless, an inventory that is not a key and a list that is not a hand. No tagged memory, no Chicago break (T004).

**Emotional turn.** From the domestic order of the opening to the recognition that the month's heat has a payer nobody will name. The certainty is not triumph: the file is complete and useless, and he is the only adult in the room and the least use in it.

**Sensory anchors.** Hot clay under tallow, woodsmoke, wet wool, grey Ash in knuckles, the cold glaze of the clay jar, the high thin note of a pot singing early, the pale clip-ring catching what light there is.

**Threads advanced, none paid.** T071/T072 (173 exact), T088 (payer unnamed 173 days), T058/T079, T009/T013/T092, T098 (carried), T036/T076, T061/T082 (nine stoppings, no tenth, three counts), T062/T100 (separate places), T077, T004 (offstage).

**Foreshadowing planted / paid off.** Gathered only: the sweetish lead edge, the dust ground small as meal and the early note feed the non-fatal edge owed in Chapter 45; the held ninth stopping and the seventh year at the sill feed a later giving; the damper's double bite feeds the shut-room convergence around ~650; the pan's gone ring and the two shelf gaps feed the far-off hand that will lay the private records together. Nothing paid.

**Exact last image.** Tarn's broom going over the collector's road without once turning along it while the kiln breathes in fours with its gap and the river keeps its fifth note through the door; the boy's hand goes up an inch toward the note of water and comes down against the fold with one hundred and seventy-three standing exact and unspent.

## Scene 2 — "The Dust Off the Back Room"
**Day and time.** Day 174, Tallow 25, Fifthday, before first grey into the morning. Second dated heading.
**Place.** The Vail wheel-shed, the cold damper, the flagstones, the drying racks and the yard well.
**Present.** Corin, Sella, Tarn, Jorra; a High Kiln kiln-master and unnamed well neighbours as walk-ons. Three or four unnamed men enter the back room before first grey and are not followed or entered.

**Beats, in order.**
1. The two tellings of a counting room shut in the war stand side by side with no third (T065/T095).
2. Jorra comes through with fine grey slate dust ground small as meal into both forearms past the bent little finger and under the nails, not bone-coloured slip; he keeps dusty forearms and clean ones on one tray and asks neither which slate was broken nor whether it was a birth-slate. 3. He speaks to the wheel before anyone (*It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady*), tells a pot *You'll do*, knows its full note three turns before it leaves the wheel and withholds the knowing from a High Kiln kiln-master come up on the wrong wind who says a pot singing before its time generally sings itself out early. The knowing goes to the near-full tray, beside the hearings and never among them, advanced and not paid; no pot is lost in the yard (T061/T082).
4. When Sella's instruction is low and unfinished, Jorra's bent finger goes to the cold iron of the damper and waits for the sentence, then moves the tongue a hair's breadth and leaves it standing. 5. Tarn's stroke goes on over the flagstones past the shallow bright patch without the half-check, and neither man names the other's silence; the three held changes stay three and are not paired or made a rule (T036/T076). The damper is read once more as one piece of iron with two bites, the holding and the taking, with no word in Grathic that would carry both mercies in one mouthful, filed as an object joined to no kiln.
6. Through the finger's-width door the river keeps its fifth low note, never counted among the hearings and never invented to stand up as a seventh (T061/T082). At the well a woman asks what the kilns will want now the last tend is in and is given the weather, the pot and the river, never a figure (T077).
7. The carried fire misses its fifth under the floor with its payer unnamed for a hundred and seventy-four days (T088); the boy is one hundred and seventy-four days old and nobody says so (T071/T072).

**Emotional turn.** From the men's anonymous passage into the room to the household's ordinary straightening around a sentence allowed to finish. The danger is present in the dust and the withheld note, and knowing it gives nobody a hand to act on it.

**Sensory anchors.** Fine grey slate dust under the nails and in knuckle whites, damp clay and tallow, the cold of the iron taking the warmth out of a fingertip, the sweetish edge of glaze on the wrong wind, wet rope on the well rim.

**Threads advanced, none paid.** T071/T072 (174), T088 (unnamed 174 days), T035/T031/T062 (dust, withheld note, held fraction), T061/T082 (near-full answer unjoined, no seventh), T036/T076 (unchecking stroke, three places), T065/T095 (two tellings, no third), T077, T058/T079.

**Foreshadowing planted / paid off.** Gathered only: the slate dust and the singing pot feed the Chapter 45 edge; the back room and the two shut-room tellings feed ~650; the withheld knowing joins the near-full tray toward a far-off ear that will be able to use it. Nothing paid.

**Exact last image.** The river's note coming through the finger's-width door as a fifth low thing while the fire misses its fifth under the floor and the boy is one hundred and seventy-four days old with nobody saying so.

## Scene 3 — "The Cost Sent to the Pot"
**Day and time.** Day 175, Tallow 26, Sixthday, midday into the going of the light. Third dated heading; the day's second half.

## Scene 7 — "One in Twelve, as the Notice Says"

## Scene 8 — "Four, Four and a Gap into Ember"
**Day and time.** Day 179, Tallow 30, Tenthday, the going of the light down the clay road and the small hours under the Vail kiln-house roof. Chapter close; no new dated heading.
**Place.** The clay road, the Vail kiln-house (bed, hearth, loaf shelf's low angle), under the roof in the small hours.
**Present.** Corin, Sella; no other on-stage characters. Every private record stays offstage and unread.

**Beats, in order.**
1. 2. ## How the final moment pulls into Chapter 43

The last image is held open, not closed. The hand goes up an inch and comes down; the count stands at one hundred and seventy-nine; the fire misses its fifth with no payer; the charcoal waits. Chapter 43 opens on Day 180, Ember 1, Firstday, with the boy one hundred and eighty days old and six months, and nobody saying so. The month turns without a boundary being drawn in the narration: the district keeps calling Tallow the firing month and Ember the kiln month and neither is corrected. The first firing of Ember under the Vail roof may bring a real opening, and if it does the widow's mark is touched before the kiln and at no other hour, exactly as on Tallow 1 (T058/T079), with nobody to praise it and nobody to see it broken. The count crosses into Ember exact and unspent, the fire's payer still unnamed, the ninth stopping still nine with no tenth, the Seed 16 breath still held, the three held changes still on three places and the old life still offstage. Nothing is joined, named, opened, called, spent or ended. Forward, not closed.
