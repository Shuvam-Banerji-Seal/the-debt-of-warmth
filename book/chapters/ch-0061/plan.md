## How the final moment pulls into Chapter 62

The chapter closes on Day 236, Grain 27, Seventhday, in the small hours, the count at **two hundred and thirty-six** exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody, the fourth answer of the chapter's close a remembered two-rhythm coordination filed like a tool and not a power. That held state is exactly what Chapter 62 takes up. Days 237–239 (Grain 28–30) carry the household into the third and last Account-day and market of Grain: the district's arithmetic changes for the third time and cannot be changed back, Berr comes up short with the red wool cord folded square and the green of tasted lead glaze at one mouth-corner, wipes his thumb twice, reads *one in twelve, as the notice says* low into the crowd, calls the levy and denies it with *your house*, never *I*, and goes off with the box locked and the true subtraction inside, believing he has done a kindness. The boy carries 237, 238 and 239 exactly into the month's close; the hand goes on answering as the body answers. Forward into the last market, not closed.
