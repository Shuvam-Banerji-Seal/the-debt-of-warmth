\daymark{235}{Grain 26, Sixthday, Y.H. 813}

The hand lay against the fold where it had come down, and the day went over in the dark the way water goes over a stone that lies where a stone lies --- without asking the stone whether it had meant to stand there, and without moving it. Two hundred and thirty-five came where two hundred and thirty-four had stood, exact, complete, unspent, and six months and fifty-five days went along with it, and nobody in \place{Sarn}, \place{Sill} or \place{Grath} said either number over \person[Corin]{the boy}'s head or into his ear. He had stopped expecting to be told, which is not the same thing as not wanting it; the wanting went on underneath the expectation the way a coal goes on underneath ash, and the ash lay where it lay and was not swept.

He was at the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf} on \place{the bed} that did not feel like a bed so much as the shadow a shelf throws, and \place{the Vail kiln-house} kept him the way a cup keeps a little water in the bottom --- carefully, and with one flaw nobody had named. Hot clay came first under the roof, hot clay under tallow and wet wool under that, and beneath all three the sour \lore{Ash} breath of the unswept corner the house called \lore{the thaw}. The day's first sound was not a bell. Behind the wall \lore{the carried fire} drew in four low pulls and stopped where the fifth should have stood, and its payer had had no name for two hundred and thirty-five days; the fifth was not invented to make the row come out even. Through the finger's-width door the river kept its note of \lore{water over water}, a fifth low thing that had never counted among the hearings, and overhead the boards held four nails on the near one, four on the next, and a thumb-width knot where a fifth nail should have been. Three counts in three places, and none of them rose into another, because a man who wanted them to agree would have had to put a payer under all three and no payer stood to any of them.

The crust lay two inches from his ear, leathery and stiff and brown at the edges, the last warmth that had crossed \place{House Silt} before he was taken, gone cold now and keeping the shape of what it had been. The empty place beside it kept its name to itself. No third came to make the row come out even, and he did not want one, because a third crust laid beside two absences would be a kindness with a figure on it and a kindness with a figure on it is the beginning of an account. The thread at his mouth had frozen in the dry heat and was unwiped; nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread, and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. Cold had stopped at his cheek, at his temple, at the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, as though it had come to the end of a line written out for it and had neither an order to go further nor an order to leave. The afterdrop waited on the far side of all three, past a warming nobody in this house would perform. He knew the afterdrop when he saw it. He wanted the crust. Both were true, and the second was louder here in the dark, because a child's hunger does not keep the hours an adult keeps, and the adult's register went on being exact while the child in him went on being hungry. They argued without a word between them and had argued so for two hundred and thirty-five days.

He turned out the file as a man lays instruments on a towel and found it exact, complete and worth nothing. Six hearings of \lore{the full note} stood beside and never among, five from four sources and the sixth out of \person[Dessa Silt] as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go and never among the sources; no seventh had come in two hundred and thirty-five days and none was invented to fill the empty place, for a finding kept empty is a finding and a finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged. Three near-full answers stood on their own tray. Three shapings of the pot saying stood as sayings, and \lore{The burned-slate word} stood at four on a place of its own, and the two tellings of the counting room shut in the war stood at two and did not become three. The told bell lay beside the heard, four, four and a gap with nobody crossing himself; the told figure lay beside the paid, and \person[Berr Clay]'s twice-wiped thumb and his finger over a figure before his eyes found the crowd lay on a tray of their own, joined to nothing. Nine stoppings stood with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end and still ungiven, and the Seed 16 breath stood unspent at the top of every reach, and the loan that could hear stayed uncalled. The seventh green was a colour and the seventh shape was a shape, carried unjoined through the working mornings, with no eighth of either; and on a seventh place of its own, with a finger between it and the hearings, the question kept its two askings and no witness stood anywhere to be asked. The true subtraction stayed locked in Berr's box going up \place{the clay road} toward \place{High Kiln} and answered nothing for the man carried out on Day 188. Nothing had been joined, named, opened, called, spent or ended.

\scenebreak

Morning came without a bell and without a market, because a Sixthday has no reading, and the day took up its ordinary work where the dark had left it. Sella came to the fire before she came to the basket, as she had come to it every morning of his life under this roof. Every instruction went out ahead of her --- to the wool, the fire, the pot, the broom, the running water and the river wind, and never to the basket, which had never been given an instruction and had no need of one. “Keep it off his ears. It's the river wind,” she told the wool, and told the fire it was the thaw, and turned the coals with the back of a broken pot. “The house will keep it,” she said where another woman would have said I. The fire answered the telling of the thaw with four low pulls behind the wall and the silence where the fifth should have stood, and she went on feeding it as though it had said nothing at all, because a fire that misses a breath is a fire and not an argument.

The dressed grain lay near \place{the hearth} under a fold of wool. It had come off \place{the worked strips} east of \place{Low Kiln}, been beaten out on \place{the flagstones} with a flail of ash-wood and leather, winnowed so the chaff went up on the river wind, spread thin to finish and gathered in by the handful. Now she turned it for damp. The wool was cool and dry under her hand and under his, and the grain under it had the small chaff-dry smell of a thing that has given its water up to the air and asks to be left alone afterwards. She put her fingers into it for damp, found it dry to her knuckle, spread it thin on the flat stone to finish and gathered it back by the handful and poured it into its cover, and set the fold flat. It changed state three times in the space of one morning --- covered, spread, covered --- and gained no figure in any of them. She counted it in glances and in handfuls, and by the weight of the carrying arm, which is a record in muscle and knows weight and not cost. That arm had carried him up and down the clay road at the going of the light for two hundred and thirty-five days at the half-pace and had carried the grain in a dish across \place{the Clay Yard} past \place{the drying racks} on the same mornings; it knew the weight of both and the cost of neither, because the cost of the one had never been entered and the cost of the other was locked in a box going up toward High Kiln. Nothing went onto a slate. Nothing was laid across the empty place at his ear or the empty place on the row or the round ring where the shallow clay pan had stood or the place in the returning line where a hand had gone in on Seed 15 and had not come out. A keeping is a keeping by being covered or by being spread, and the grain lay back under its fold with no figure over it.

\scenebreak

A neighbour came to the threshold with a pot hitched against her hip and asked what the storing would take now the grain was under wool. Sella's hand went toward the shelf and came away without touching the clay jar, and she said “Ask the pot. The weather knows the rest.” The question went off level and the neighbour went off with it, and nothing was priced, refused or entered, because a cost sent to a pot is a cost that has arrived nowhere.

A man come down the clay road brought a number up out of a fist toward \place{the yard well}, looked at it as though he had forgotten what it was for, and put it back into his sleeve. He did not give it to the water and the water did not take it; the figure arrived nowhere. The \place{the trough} at the well stood bare and dark, and its water was called weather, and the weather answered nothing that any clerk could take down. On Grain's second Account-day the figure had been read over the covered basket twice, once for the moot and once for the households that came late, and \lore{one in twelve} stood printed and unchallenged; the district's arithmetic had changed twice in the month already and could not be changed back, and the change stood unread under this roof. But the figure read over a basket is not the figure paid and knows nothing of what the covered grain cost to bring in, and the true subtraction sat in Berr's box on its way up toward High Kiln answering nothing for the man carried out on Day 188 or for the hand that had gone in on Seed 15. The twice-wiped thumb and the finger passed over a figure before the eyes found the crowd lay on a tray of their own, joined to no man and to no morning's work, and the boy left them there. He did not lay the number in the fist beside the number over the basket and call them the same number with two faces; a row of such hands will call itself an account if a man leaves it standing long enough to want an answer, and he wanted none.

\scenebreak

In the middle of the morning a wrong wind came up off High Kiln and brought the sweet edge of \lore{lead glaze} over the boards. It rode low, the way a smell rides on a day that has no better use for it, went across the shelf's front and touched the fold and went out on the wind that brought it. It was a direction arrived nowhere and not a second edge paid; the boy filed it where the other wrong-wind mornings stood and gave it no figure and no payer. The seventh green remained a colour and the seventh shape remained a shape, gathered at Grain's first market and carried unjoined through the working mornings since, with no eighth of either and no second look joined to the first, no name spoken over either --- neither the woman at the pot board nor the shape at the market's edge was given one here --- no loaf passed, no slate come near, no sale made, no promotion into a hearing of the full note, a cause or a romance. A colour is a colour and a shape is a shape until somebody stands under them and calls them an account, and nobody did.

One of the three shapings of the pot saying stood again in the sweet air: a pot gone quiet on every row come the thaw. It stood as a saying and not as a fourth likeness made a cause. No lesson came out of it, no name was given to what ailed the man carried out on Day 188, and no pot was lost in the yard. The three shapings stayed three, the burned-slate word stayed at four, the two tellings of the counting room shut in the war stayed two, and a finger stood between each place and the next, because a man who lets two things touch will find by morning that they have stood up together and called themselves a rule, and a rule is an account that has found a payer.

\scenebreak

At the loaf shelf Sella moved her lips along the two-gapped row without a figure, the way a woman runs a thumb along a hem to find the tear and does not say what she found. She glanced once at the clay jar cold under its glaze on its wall shelf and shut her hand half over nothing without a touch, and then she kept her finger on top of the shelf's front edge so the charcoal tally beneath it stayed unread. \lore{The widow's mark} hung where it hung on its cord at her throat, touched once before the kiln on Ember 1 and at no hour since. There had been no opening in fifty-five days, because a morning, a shelf and a yard are not openings, and she set the pot rim out with its handle two inches beyond the reaching hand and let her hand rest a half-beat longer at the setting than the setting required, and did not look toward the bed.

At \place{the kindling} \person{Tarn} took the broom only after it had been given its instruction, and said “Yes, house” to the wood and to nobody. He set the small knife at the very edge of its sill, six counted in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check and the seventh at the lip where it could not, and the sentence about a man sent for a collector who turned back at the frozen ford lay beside it where it had lain for six years, with no name in it and never one. His chest had been tight since the dark; Ash was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line and no scrubbing lifted it, and the pale clip-ring at his left wrist held what light there was and gave it back. The other sentence came to his teeth and went all the way to the word that would call the loan and stopped there with no figure and no name, a remembered stopping and not a new one. Nine stoppings stood with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end, the Seed 16 breath was drawn and not spent, and no collector came up the clay road on Grain 26 and none went down it. His stroke went over \place{the flagstones} past the shallow bright patch without the half-check and went on over the road a house sends a man down when it wants a collector without once turning along it.

At the edge of \place{the Vail wheel-shed} \person{Jorra Vail} came through with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bent little finger and under the nails, white in the creases where a fist had opened, and not bone-coloured \lore{slip}. He kept dusty forearms and clean ones on one tray and parted them as a surgeon parts clot from vessel, asked neither which slate had been broken nor whether it was a birth-slate, and gave the house no name for the three or more men who had gone low into the finger's-width-open \place{the back room} before first grey. He spoke to the wheel before anyone. “It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady,” he said, and told a pot “You'll do” in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either. He knew the note the pot would give three turns ahead, with air under it and a want in it, and withheld the knowing, keeping it on the near-full tray beside the hearings and never among them. The three sayings stayed three, the burned-slate word stayed at four, the two tellings of the counting room shut in the war stayed two, and he did not look toward the bed or the basket. His quiet went before him into the shed the way a cold air goes before a door, and Tarn did not ask about the room, so the two silences stayed unjoined under the one roof.

\scenebreak

The morning thinned without ending, wearing away the way the edge of a thumb-mark wears, a little at a time, so that a man who looked up from his work could not have said when the light had turned. Heat came under the low angle where the boy lay, taking its route behind the pot first and into the fold afterwards, and settled there and went no further. The wool fold was cool and dry where his hand lay against it, and the grain lay back under its fold with no figure over it, and the river kept its note of water over water through the finger's-width door. The question with its two askings stood over the boards and the morning had not asked it; the air held the expectation of it the way a held breath holds a sound not yet spent, but no voice came and he did not make one, because the afternoon had not yet earned the asking and there was no payer to put under it. Grain 30, the third and last Account-day and market, stood four mornings ahead and was not brought into a day that had not earned it.

In the fire room Sella stood with the back of the pot in her hand and counted under her breath toward no opening --- small intake, hold, letting go --- a count she kept toward nothing and had never offered to any ear under this roof. The wall stood between that count and his. It was not a count given to him, and he took its length only as a man takes the length of a shift he has worked a thousand times, or the weight of a basket he has carried every morning; the room did not touch his and nothing crossed from it that could be entered.

He put the third question of the chapter to the hand. The hand outside the wool went up an inch toward the river's low note through the finger's-width door and stayed a moment --- not the instant of the old conditions, not the intake alone, but a small interval held between the two with no figure in it --- and came down before the count in the other room had reached its letting-go. It was an answer and it was filed on the sixth place with no payer named, the forty-fifth, not a longer reach, not a hearing, not a seventh of the full note, not a tenth stopping, not a payment, not one of the held changes, not a key, not a power and not a rule. Exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air. The child in him wanted the crust. The adult kept the tray. Neither stood up and said what the other was.

Two hundred and thirty-five stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody; six months and fifty-five days had been told by nobody under any roof; the fire's fifth had gone unpaid the same number of days; the widow's mark had not been touched and there had been no opening in fifty-five days. Nothing had been joined, named, opened, called, spent or ended. The hand came down before the letting-go and lay against the fold while the grain lay back under its wool with no figure over it, and the question stood over the boards with two askings and nobody to own them, and the day went on toward an afternoon that had not yet asked it.
