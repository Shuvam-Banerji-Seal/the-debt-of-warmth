## Chronicle

Three days came under the Vail roof. The house kept its time in the kiln's four pulls and the silence where the fifth should stand, in the river's note through the finger's-width door, in the boards' four nails, four and a knot. The boy kept the three counts on separate trays, for agreeing them would have wanted a payer and none stood to any. He came into his two hundred and thirty-fourth day, then the fifth and the sixth, six months and fifty-four days to fifty-six.

Each morning Sella Vail went to the fire before the basket, giving her instructions to everything but the basket. She turned the dressed grain under its wool for damp, spread it thin and gathered it back by the handful; it changed state and gained no figure, counted in glances and by an arm that knew weight and not cost. Cost-questions went to the pot and the weather; a fist's number went back into its sleeve. Her finger kept the charcoal unread, her hand shut half over the clay jar, and the widow's mark hung untouched for fifty-four, then fifty-five, then fifty-six days.

At the kindling Tarn took the broom only after it was instructed and told the wood that six were counted and the next he did not count, the knife at the lip beside Garl's unforged collector sentence. The ear-sentence stopped at the word that would call the loan; nine stoppings stood, the Seed 16 breath was unspent, and no collector came on Grain 25, 26 or 27. Jorra Vail parted dust from slip, withheld the note he knew three turns ahead, kept the burned-slate word at four and the shut-room tellings at two, and held his bent finger in the damper until Sella's sentence reached the pot.

In the second afternoon the ownerless voice came a third time over the boards: which row had the pot gone quiet on? A wrong wind off High Kiln answered first with the sweet edge of lead glaze, the price of barley after it, and the voice went up the terrace while the row stood. He opened it as a surgeon opens a wound --- what it asked, where it stood, what it wanted, who would stand under the answer to pay --- and found no witness at the bottom. Three askings are not one witness. He set the third telling on the seventh place beside the first two, a finger between each pair and between them and the sayings, tellings, hearings and count.

Six times the hand answered, the forty-third through the forty-eighth: the fifth a miss entered without shame, the last a two-rhythm coordination held across the wall --- through one of Sella's counted breaths, past the silence after her count, and down on the river's next low note. Each was filed on the sixth place with no payer named. At the close the file lay open on the towel, complete and worth nothing; nothing had been joined, named, opened, called, spent or ended.
