The afternoon thinned the way thin gruel thins, not toward nothing but toward the colour of the dish it sat in. Heat that had stood all morning in the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf} went nowhere in a hurry; it settled behind the pot, went into the fold at \person[Corin]{the boy}'s ear and lay there with the stubbornness of a thing set down and never instructed to rise. One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from that ear. The empty place beside it kept its name to itself. No third came to make the row come out even, because a kindness with a figure on it is the beginning of an account, and this house had learned to give without entering. The thread at his mouth had frozen in the dry heat and was unwiped. Nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread, and a basket set down is a pot set down and left.

Cold had stopped at cheek, at temple, at the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back. It had come to the end of an order written out for it and had neither a command to go further nor a command to leave. The afterdrop waited on the far side of all three, past a warming nobody in this house would perform. He knew the afterdrop when he saw it. He wanted the crust. Both were true, and in the afternoon the second was quieter than it had been in the dark, because the light set the eye other things to count, and a body with a child's appetite cannot keep one hunger louder than another through a whole tend. The adult who had once stood at a warming bed and known the number by the patient's skin took the same reading now from the inside of his own wrist and entered it nowhere. There was no hand to carry the finding to a slate, and no slate under this roof would have known what it counted.

\scenebreak

The house kept a man in objects and not in a story. The salt-stiff coat hung on its peg with the crease at the left shoulder where a broader man had leaned, and the peg did not bend under it. The seat at the table kept its twenty-year leftward lean and its hollow on that side, and nobody straightened the cushion into the hollow, because a hollow left where it was is a hollow kept. At the Vail corner the clean dark line where the ridge had turned lay unswept, and beside it the finer hairline of grey the house called \lore{the thaw} lay unswept too, the two held apart by the width of a thumb and neither swept into the other. A proverb had gone years ago to a pot with no crack in it, which was a saying about leaving the repair showing said over a corner where the repair had been rubbed out; the proverb went on being said, and the crack it spoke of was not in the pot. The debt against a firing of tiles circled the house without a name put on it, neither paid nor settled nor done with, worse for being uncalled. His name had not been spoken under this roof in two hundred and thirty-six days. No collector had come up \place{the clay road} or gone down it on Grain 25, Grain 26 or Grain 27, and the clay-bound hand's broom had gone over the road three times without once turning along it.

A coat on a peg is a coat kept. A line left where it lay is a line kept. He had known men who kept a thing by writing it down and men who kept it by refusing to write, and he had learned here that a house could keep by neither, which is harder to enter than either. He turned the three keepings over as a man turns instruments on a towel and found them exact, complete and worth nothing to anybody who wanted a figure. The man who had left them had fallen in \lore{the Long Cold} when the river was iced and no collector could cross, and the household had arranged itself around his absence with the same care another house would have given a presence, without ever saying what it was arranging itself around.

\person{Sella Vail} came to the fire before the basket. She gave the instruction to the wool, the fire, the pot, the broom, the running water and the river wind, and never to the basket, which had never been given an instruction and had no need of one; she said the house where another woman would have said I, told the fire it was \lore{the thaw} and turned the coals with the back of a broken pot. “The house will keep it,” she said. The fire answered four low pulls behind the wall and the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer without a name for two hundred and thirty-six days, and she went on feeding it as though it had said nothing at all, because a fire that misses a breath is a fire and not an argument. She stood a moment with the back of the pot in her hand and counted under her breath toward no opening --- small intake, hold, letting go --- a count she kept toward nothing and had never offered to any ear under this roof. The wall stood between that count and his, and he took its length only as a man takes the length of a shift he has worked a thousand times; nothing crossed from that room that could be entered.

She pushed a strand of coal-black hair back with the wrist that carries the burn, and the grey streak at her temple showed pale off the coals. At \place{the loaf shelf} she moved her lips along the two-gapped row without a figure, the way a woman runs a thumb along a hem to find the tear and does not say what she found. Her eye went once to \place{the clay jar} cold under its glaze on the wall shelf, and her hand started toward it and shut half over nothing without a touch. Then she kept her finger on top of the shelf's front edge so the charcoal tally beneath the board stayed unread, uncounted and uncopied, and set the pot rim out with its handle two inches beyond the reaching hand, letting her hand rest a half-beat longer at the setting than the setting required and not looking toward \place{the bed}. \lore{The widow's mark} hung where it hung on its cord at her throat, touched once before the kiln on Ember 1 and at no hour since. There had been no opening in fifty-six days, because an afternoon, a shelf, a yard, a basket and a pot are not openings.

\scenebreak

At \place{the kindling} \person{Tarn} took the broom only after it had been given its instruction, as a man takes up a tool already named, and told the wood “Six counted. The next I don't count” in the tone of a man stating the weather. He set the small knife at the very edge of its sill with six in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check and the seventh at the lip where it could not, and the unforged collector sentence about a man sent for a collector who turned back at the frozen ford lay beside it, entered nowhere. He said \emph{Yes, house} to those who wanted the thaw early and to those who wanted it late, agreed so entirely that no argument could find a foot to stand on, and went on wanting the late one, because a late thaw puts off the paying, though the paying had stood in the ruts since the water went.

His chest had been tight since the dark. \lore{Ash} was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line and no scrubbing lifted it, and the pale clip-ring at his left wrist held what light there was and gave it back. The sentence about an ear under this roof being worth more than the debt that held it came to his teeth, went all the way to the word that would call the loan and stopped there with no figure and no name. He filed the stopping as a remembered stopping and not a new one. Nine stoppings stood on Reed 5, 8, 11, 14, 17, 20, 23, 26 and 29, nine objects and not a rule, with no tenth before the seventh year, which stood at its very end and had still not given. The breath he should have spent at the top of each reach went in and was not spent, the Seed 16 breath held against nothing, and the loan stayed uncalled. He drew the broom over \place{the flagstones} past the shallow bright patch without the half-check and on over the road a house sends a man down when it wants a collector, without once turning along it. No collector came up or went down the clay road on Grain 25, 26 or 27. Above the shed the loft came to him as a sound with a direction and nothing more, and the warm stone lying cold in its unmarked pot in \place{the Debt Loft} was not reconciled with the debt by not trying, which is not forgiving.

At the edge of \place{the Vail wheel-shed} \person{Jorra Vail} came through with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bent little finger and under the nails, white in the creases where a fist had opened, and not bone-coloured \lore{slip}. His quiet went before him into the shed the way a cold air goes before a door, and the household straightened around it without an order. He gave the house no name for the men who had gone low into \place{the back room} before first grey; the two tellings of the room shut in the war stayed two and did not become three. He kept dusty forearms and clean ones on one tray and asked neither which slate had been broken nor whether it was a birth-slate. The broken birth-slates lay under \place[the drying racks]{the lowest rack} where the yard went around them and entered them into nothing.

He spoke to the wheel before anyone. “It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady,” he said, and told a pot “You'll do” in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either. He knew the \lore{full note} that the pot would give three turns before it left his hands, with air under it and a want in it, and withheld the knowing, keeping it on the near-full tray beside the hearings and never among them. The burned-slate word stood at four from \place{Slate Row}, a direction over the boards arrived nowhere, and no fifth telling came to join it; the three shapings of the pot saying stayed sayings, and no fourth likeness stood up and called itself a cause. He grew quieter whenever the word went around, and nobody gave the quiet a name.

When Sella's voice went low and unfinished at the hearth his bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper} ahead of her sentence, took the cold out of the fingertip and gave nothing back, held until the sentence reached the pot, moved a hair's breadth and was left standing --- a remembered repetition and not a change made again, joined to no kiln and paired with no other fraction. The iron was one piece with two bites and no word in \lore{Grathic} for both mercies: the bite that holds a vessel whole and the bite that can take a hand off a man. He kept \emph{he's not Vail} sealed as tightly as the hour it was spoken, and the promise of the boy's name to the men who burn slates was neither given nor withdrawn. He did not look toward the bed or the basket.

\scenebreak

At the going of the light she carried him home at the half-pace, woodsmoke before her in long low bars and river wind behind with the taste of \lore{water over water}. The carrying arm was a record in muscle that knew weight and not cost: it had carried him up and down the clay road for two hundred and thirty-six days and had carried the dressed grain past the racks on the same mornings, and knew the weight of both and the price of neither. She went wide around the clean dark line and the finer grey called the thaw beside it, around the rack space with its broken birth-slates, around the round ring where the shallow clay pan had stood, around \place{the yard well} where \place{the trough} stood bare and dark and its water was called weather, out on \place{the worked strips} east of \place{Low Kiln} where the field kept its empty place, and around the place in the returning line where a hand had gone in on Seed 15 and had not come out with the rest. Each got its look and its wide step and none got a figure. Seven walked-around things standing in a row are still seven and not an account until a payer stands under them, and none did. No third gap came, no fourth likeness stood up and called itself a cause, and nothing had been joined, named, opened, called, spent or ended.

Under the Vail roof she set him on his bed and not in the Debt Loft, put the fold right without a look at his face and left the thread frozen, and went to her own bed before the small hours and left the coals to keep themselves. The two rooms did not touch.

In the small hours there was no breathing number anywhere in the house. Fire, river and boards kept their three counts in three places that did not become one: under the floor \lore{the carried fire} gave four low pulls and withheld the fifth; through the finger's-width door the river kept its note of water over water; overhead the boards kept four nails on the near board, four on the next, and a thumb-width knot where the fifth nail should have stood. A man who wanted the three to agree would have to put a payer under all of them, and none stood to any. He turned out the file as a man lays instruments on a towel and found it exact, complete and worth nothing.

Six hearings of the full note stood beside and never among, five from four sources and the sixth out of a person, filed where a thing out of a person must go, with no seventh in two hundred and thirty-six days and none invented to fill the empty place, for an empty place kept empty is a finding and a finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged. Three near-full answers stood on their own tray, advanced and unpaid. Beside them stood three shapings of the pot saying, all sayings and no rule; the fourth telling of the burned slate stood on a place of its own, and the two tellings of the counting room shut in the war stayed two and did not become three. The told bell lay beside the heard, four, four and a gap with nobody crossing himself; the told figure lay beside the paid, and the figure read over the covered grain was still not the figure paid, the true subtraction locked beyond this roof and answering nothing for the man carried out on Day 188. Nine stoppings stood with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end, and still ungiven; the Seed 16 breath was unspent and the loan uncalled. Three held changes stood on three places, the loft-sill scrape on a fourth, the carried man's pale line on a fifth and the held inch on a sixth. The seventh green was a colour and the seventh shape a shape with no eighth of either, no second look joined, no name spoken over either, no loaf, slate or sale. The dressed grain lay covered and unfigured; the returning-line place had gone around with a look and no number. On a seventh place of its own, with a finger between each pair and a finger between them and the sayings, the tellings, the hearings and the count, the question stood with three askings and nobody to own them. Three askings are not one witness; a witness stands somewhere and can be asked, and none did and none could.

He put the question to his hand the sixth way of this chapter. The hand outside the wool went up an inch toward the low note through the finger's-width door and stayed. He took it through the small intake, the hold and the letting go of one of the breaths Sella counted at her fire in the room that did not touch his, kept it through the little silence after her count had ended and brought it down on the river's next low note. Two rhythms, one held against the other across a wall that does not touch, remembered, not a change made again. He filed the coordination on the sixth place with no payer named, and it was not spent or explained: not a longer reach, not a miss entered, not a hearing, not a seventh of the full note, not a tenth stopping, not a payment, not one of the held changes, not a key, not a power, not a rule. Forty-eight answers stood on that place now, exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air.

Two hundred and thirty-six stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in \place{Sarn}, \place{Sill} or \place{Grath}; six months and fifty-six days told by nobody; the fire's fifth unpaid the same number of days; the widow's mark untouched with no opening in fifty-six days; the seventh year at its very end and still ungiven. Grain 30, the third and last Account-day and market, stood three mornings ahead and was not brought into a day that had not earned it. The question stood over the boards with three askings and no owner. The hand came down on the river's next low note, the file lay open on the towel complete and worth nothing, and the count went forward exact and unspent into a day that had not yet earned its heading.
