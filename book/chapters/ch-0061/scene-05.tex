\daymark{236}{Grain 27, Seventhday, Y.H. 813}

The hand lay against the fold where the river's next low note had left it, and the day came over the house the way a coal comes up through ash: slowly, and with no sound that a man could have put a name to. Two hundred and thirty-six came where two hundred and thirty-five had stood, exact, complete, unspent, and six months and fifty-six days went along with it, and nobody in \place{Sarn}, \place{Sill} or \place{Grath} said either number over \person[Corin]{the boy}'s head or into his ear. He had stopped expecting to be told, which is not the same as not wanting it; the wanting went on under the expectation the way a fire goes on under ash, and neither the wanting nor the fire had a payer named to them. Under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof the first smell was hot clay under tallow and wet wool under that, and under the wool the sour breath of the corner the house called \lore{the thaw}; the first sound was \lore{the carried fire} drawing in fours behind the wall with the gap where the fifth should have stood, and no bell went with it, because a Seventhday has no reading to mark the hour. There was no market and no Cross and no voice over the boards in the small hours. The question that had gathered a third asking the day before stood over the boards with nobody to own it, and he did not bring it down into his room to make the dark seem fuller than it was.

Under the floor the fire gave four low pulls and withheld the fifth; through the finger's-width door the river kept its note of \lore{water over water}; overhead the boards kept four nails on the near board, four on the next, and a thumb-width knot where the fifth nail should have stood. Three counts in three places, and none rose into another, because a man who wanted them to agree would have to put a payer under all three and no payer stood to any. He had laid three instruments on a towel before and had known better than to reach for a fourth to make the set look whole.

One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from his ear; the empty place beside it kept its name to itself, and no third came to make the row come out even, because a kindness with a figure on it is the beginning of an account. The thread at his mouth had frozen in the dry heat and was unwiped; nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread, and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. Cold had stopped at cheek, at temple, at the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, as though it had come to the end of an order written out for it and had neither a command to go further nor a command to leave. The afterdrop waited on the far side of all three, past a warming nobody in this house would perform. He knew the afterdrop when he saw it. He wanted the crust. Both were true, and the second was louder in the dark, because a child's hunger does not keep the hours an adult keeps, and the adult's register went on being exact while the child in him went on being hungry.

\scenebreak

\person{Sella Vail} came to the fire before the basket. She gave the instruction to the wool, the fire, the pot, the broom, the running water and the river wind, and never to the basket, which had never been given an instruction and had no need of one; she said the house where another woman would have said I, told the fire it was the thaw and turned the coals with the back of a broken pot. “The house will keep it,” she said. The fire answered four low pulls behind the wall and the silence where the fifth should have stood, and she went on feeding it as though it had said nothing at all, because a fire that misses a breath is a fire and not an argument. She stood a moment with the back of the pot in her hand and counted under her breath toward no opening --- small intake, hold, letting go --- a count she kept toward nothing and had never offered to any ear under this roof. The wall stood between that count and the boy, and he took its length only as a man takes the length of a shift he has worked a thousand times, and nothing crossed from that room that could be entered. She pushed a strand of coal-black hair back with the wrist that carries the burn, and the grey streak at her temple showed pale off the coals.

The dressed grain lay near \place{the hearth} under its fold of wool. In the morning she turned it for damp, put her fingers into it, spread it thin on the flat stone to finish, gathered it back by the handful and set the fold flat; it had changed state again and gained no figure in any of it. She counted it in glances and handfuls and by the weight of the carrying arm, which is a record in muscle and knows weight and not cost. That arm had carried him up and down \place{the clay road} at the half-pace for two hundred and thirty-six days and had carried the grain across the yard past the racks on the same mornings, and knew the weight of both and the cost of neither, because the cost of the one had never been entered and the cost of the other lay in a locked box beyond this roof and answered nothing for the man carried out on Day 188. Nothing went onto a slate, and nothing was laid across the empty place at his ear or the empty place on the row or the round ring where the shallow clay pan had stood. A keeping is a keeping by being covered or by being spread, and the grain lay back under its fold with no figure over it.

A neighbour at the threshold hitched her pot against her hip and asked what the storing would take now the grain was under wool. “Ask the pot what a wet thing costs set down there,” Sella said. “The house has said it.” The woman went off with her question unanswered and her pot level, and nothing was priced, refused or entered. At \place{the yard well} a woman with wet sleeves asked the same thing of the weather and was given the river in place of a number; the water there was called weather by people who did not taste it, and weather does not put a figure on a basket. A man come down the clay road brought a number up out of a fist toward the well, looked at it, and put it back into his sleeve, so that the figure arrived nowhere. The figure read over the covered basket on Grain's second Account-day was still not the figure paid, and a number that goes back into a sleeve has not been spent even though a man's fist has held it and his shoulder has carried it up the terrace. At \place{the loaf shelf} another neighbour wanted to know what the storing would take; Sella sent her to the pot and carried the question away level so that nothing slopped. He had once read a chart that said one thing while a room had done another, and the space between the two had been closed with a signature so that the two would agree. Here the space between the figure read and the figure paid had no signature on it and no name over it; it went up the terrace in a locked box and stayed there, answering nothing for a body carried out of the glaze room with no figure entered against it. A surgeon who sees two accounts that do not agree does not average them. He leaves them apart and writes down that they are apart, which is the whole of what the house had done without ever writing it.

In the middle of the morning a wrong wind got up off \place{High Kiln} and brought the sweet edge of \lore{lead glaze} over the boards. It touched the fold at the boy's ear and went out again on the wind that had brought it, a direction arriving nowhere with nobody behind it to answer for the coming or the going, and not a second edge paid. The seventh green was a colour and the seventh shape was a shape, gathered at Grain's first market and carried unjoined through the working mornings since; no eighth of either came, no second look was joined to the first, no name was spoken over either, no loaf passed, no slate came near, no sale was made. Neither was promoted into a hearing of \lore{the full note}, a seventh of it, a cause or a romance. He parted the colour from the hearing and the shape from the seventh as a surgeon parts clot from vessel, and neither bled into the other when he did it. Six greens behind a seventh green make a row of greens and six shapes behind a seventh shape make a row of shapes, and a row is not an account until somebody finds a payer to stand under it; he had put none under either row, and the wrong wind left nothing behind but the sweet edge going out where it had come in. A colour stayed a colour and a shape stayed a shape because nobody stood under either to pay for the joining.

At the loaf shelf she moved her lips along the two-gapped row without a figure, the way a woman runs a thumb along a hem to find the tear and does not say what she found. Her eye went once to \place{the clay jar} cold under its glaze on the wall shelf and her hand started toward it and shut half over nothing without a touch. Then she kept her finger on top of the shelf's front edge so the charcoal tally beneath the board stayed unread, uncounted and uncopied, and set the pot rim out with its handle two inches beyond the reaching hand, letting her hand rest a half-beat longer at the setting than the setting required and not looking toward \place{the bed}. \lore{The widow's mark} hung where it hung on its cord at her throat, touched once before the kiln on Ember 1 and at no hour since. There had been no opening in fifty-six days, because a morning, a shelf, a yard and a basket are not openings.

At \place{the kindling} \person{Tarn} took the broom only after it had been given its instruction, and told the wood “Six counted. The next I don't count” in the tone of a man stating the weather. He set the small knife at the very edge of its sill with six in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check and the seventh at the lip where it could not, and his stroke went over \place{the flagstones} past the shallow bright patch without the half-check and on over the road a house sends a man down when it wants a collector, without once turning along it. No collector came up or went down the clay road on Grain 25, 26 or 27. His chest had been tight since the dark; \lore{Ash} was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line and no scrubbing lifted it, and the pale clip-ring at his left wrist held what light there was and gave it back. Above the shed the loft came to him as a sound with a direction and nothing more, and the warm stone lying cold in its unmarked pot in \place{the Debt Loft} was not reconciled with the debt by not trying, which is not forgiving.

At the edge of \place{the Vail wheel-shed} \person{Jorra Vail} came through with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bent little finger and under the nails, white in the creases where a fist had opened, and not bone-coloured \lore{slip}. His quiet went before him into the shed the way a cold air goes before a door, and the household straightened around it without an order. He gave the house no name for the men who had gone low into \place{the back room} before first grey. He spoke to the wheel before anyone. “It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady,” he said, and told a pot “You'll do” in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either. When Sella's voice went low and unfinished at the hearth his bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper} ahead of her sentence, took the cold out of the fingertip and gave nothing back, held until the sentence reached the pot, moved a hair's breadth and was left standing --- a remembered repetition and not a change made again, joined to no kiln and paired with no other fraction. He kept dusty forearms and clean ones on one tray and did not look toward the bed or the basket.

Then the yard went around its seven walked-around places with a look and a wide step and no number. It went around the clean dark line at the Vail corner and the finer grey called the thaw beside it, unswept and kept apart; around the rack space beneath \place[the drying racks]{the lowest rack} where the broken birth-slates lay; around the round ring where the shallow clay pan had stood, with no scrape, no crumb, no print, no chip and no word; around the yard well where \place{the trough} stood bare and dark and its water was called weather; out on \place{the worked strips} east of \place{Low Kiln}, where the field kept its empty place; and around the place in the returning line where a hand had gone in on Seed 15 and had not come out with the rest. Each got its look and its wide step and none got a figure: no name, no cause, no body, no identification of which hand, no payment. Seven walked-around things standing in a row are still seven and not an account until a payer stands under them, and none did. No third gap came to join the pan's gone ring to the shelf's two gaps or the one at his ear, and no fourth likeness stood up and called itself a cause. Nothing had been joined, named, opened, called, spent or ended.

\scenebreak

In the small hours there was no breathing number anywhere in the house. The two rooms did not touch. Fire, river and boards kept their three counts in three places that did not become one. The question kept its three askings on a seventh place of its own, with a finger between each pair and a finger between them and the sayings, the tellings, the six hearings and the count. Three askings are not one witness; a witness stands somewhere and can be asked, and none did and none could. No voice came over the boards in the dark to make a fourth of it, and he did not make one himself, because a voice made under this roof would have an owner and the question stood on the fact of having none. He put the question to his hand the fifth way of this chapter. Under conditions that had once answered --- the note through the finger's-width door, a room warming at a fire that did not touch his, no number borrowed from any breath under this roof --- the hand outside the wool went up an inch toward the note, and came down at once. It did not stay. He entered the miss and filed it on the sixth place with no payer named, beside the answers that had held and the answers that had not, an object laid with objects. It was not a longer reach, not a hearing, not a seventh of the full note, not a tenth stopping, not a payment, not one of the held changes, not a key, not a power, not a rule. Forty-seven answers stood on that place now, exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air, and the miss was filed without shame and without promotion, because a miss entered honestly is a finding and a finding filled in to make a row come out even is a finding forged.

Two hundred and thirty-six stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in Sarn, Sill or Grath; six months and fifty-six days told by nobody; the fire's fifth unpaid the same number of days; the widow's mark untouched with no opening in fifty-six days; the question standing over the boards with three askings and nobody to own them. The seventh year stood at its very end and had still not given. Grain 30, the third and last Account-day and market, stood three mornings ahead and was not brought into a day that had not earned it. The hand came down at once under conditions that had once answered, the grain lay back under its fold with no figure over it, and the yard went around its seven places with a look and a wide step and no number.
