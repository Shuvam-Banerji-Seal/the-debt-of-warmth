## Chronicle

On Kindling, before first light, a clay dish came up the Clay Yard with a coal under ash, passed from hearth to hearth. Sella Vail touched the widow's mark, held it one breath, tipped the coal into the kiln, and touched it at no other hour. Corin, twenty-five days old at the threshold, counted the firelight: four, and four, and a place where a fifth would be. A collector took the year's first measure; no figure reached the roof.

On Weighing an officer read the measures in a clean voice, the Cross's grammar in a temple's clothes. A whole return was announced for someone absent; a name was read that the household would not repeat, and the boy filed the silence after it among the gaps. Berr Clay wiped his thumb and said nothing. The Court's room came as a voice through a doorway: bench, pot, draught and missing line. He set it beside three other rooms and wrote no cause.

The Long Night came with no fire after dusk. The house sat in the dark telling of the Return: a child who knew warmth before a face, a man returned with somebody else's hands, a hearth-maid giving dates where wonder should stand. In one telling the bell went four, four and a gap, and he kept the told bell beside the heard bell without letting them touch. Cold came at cheek, temple and wrist; neither the breast nor the crust was given. Tarn held his seventh year back.

On the Remembering names passed door to door, but under the Vail roof Garl's name was not spoken; Sella went around it as around ash left in a corner. Alda Reed touched her tin unopened, crossed two fingers at the birth-flower without knowing it, and covered the sign with a date. In the old register a hand found wool where a lit rectangle with a name had lain; the name went unlooked-at. The charred slate lay in the kiln ash.

On the Last Day bread was baked, and no third crust came into the fold. Mira Silt answered a question with a question and her own answer, touched the grey cord under her apron at a figure, and showed a second cord twisted twice beneath it; the knots went uncounted. Sella would not let the ash be swept early, measured both ridges with one thumb and left them where they lay, then banked the coals without touching the mark.

In the morning the grey was burned and called dealt with. No opening came, so the mark stayed untouched and the Kindling fire still lay under ash. Slate Row a direction, one in twelve printed and unchallenged, and the district entered a new year without coming to the kiln. At thirty days old he carried his count into it: five hearings from four sources and no sixth, the empty fifth, the unspoken name, five pauses unjoined. His hand outside the wool went up an inch toward the warmth and came down.

## Continuity

- **Exact final moment.** The boy lies on his bed under the Vail roof, thirty days old, the fold at his ear and the two crusts in it, the fire behind him keeping four, four and a gap. His hand outside the wool goes up an inch toward the warmth and comes down. Nobody has spoken to the basket, nothing has been joined, and nothing has been explained. Sella, Tarn and Jorra are under the roof; Garl is a coat on a peg, a hollow seat and a proverb said to the pot. The ridge is gone from the corner, burned and called dealt with.
- **Day and time at the end.** Day 30, Ilmen 1, Firstday, Y.H. 813, the Opening of the Slates, from before light into night. The era turned on this day; the count begins again at Firstday. Chapter 9 opens on Day 31.
- **Six lived days, one dated entry each.** Day 25 Kindling, Day 26 Weighing, Day 27 the Long Night, Day 28 the Remembering, Day 29 the Last Day, Day 30 Ilmen 1. No day poured into another, no recap, no present-tense Chicago break; the pocket-place and the boat promise came in past tense on Day 28, untagged.
- **Where everyone is now.** Corin: on his bed under the roof, carried, mute, drool unwiped, blanket not staying on, cold at cheek–temple–wrist. Sella Vail: at the fire and the bed, having burned no opening and touched no mark since Kindling; she fed him from the house's own dish and said nothing about the payer of the warmth. Tarn: knife back on its sill, announced to the empty yard, six years counted and the seventh held back again, Ash in the knuckles that no scrubbing lifts; he has kept silence about the flat note all five days. Jorra Vail: in and out of the wheel-shed with bone-coloured slip dried to old bone up both forearms and the bent little finger leading at the damper, which he moves before Sella has finished; no slate dust since the Last Day, the back room never mentioned, no look toward the bed. Alda Reed: came under the roof on Days 27 and 28, told dates where wonder should stand, touched her tin once unopened, and went home to a house the boy cannot name; she is offstage on Days 29–30. Mira Silt: came up from the eels on Day 29, answered a question with a question and her own answer, touched one cord, showed another, went back down the yard, and said nothing about knowing the child. Berr Clay: glimpsed once at the Weighing with his red cord folded under his coat and green at one corner of his mouth, wiped his thumb, passed a finger over his slate, and went off toward his own house with the box still locked. Garl Vail: absent; the ridge burned, the coat and hollow seat kept, the uncalled tile debt still uncalled. Dessa Silt: offstage, alive, cough still weather; she dies on Day 38 and must stay dead. Collectors, clerk and officer remain walk-ons; none is Marra.
- **Items gained, lost or used.** New: the year's fire came into the house in a clay dish from hands along the yard, its source and cost unspoken; an empty shelf where a fifth of the kindling light would be, filed beside the bench's interval; the silence after a name read at the Weighing and the silence where Garl's name should stand, both filed as gaps; three told gaps from the Long Night (question to answer, a name left out, a story stopped short and finished with a date); the told bell kept beside the heard bell in two places; the Court's room set down as a fourth likeness beside the birth room, the kiln-house and the death-house; the missing closing line in the officer's figures filed as an interval; the grey ridge measured by thumb on Day 29 and burned on Day 30, its place now a clean dark line. Lost or changed: the Ash is gone and called dealt with; the ridge no longer turns its corner. Unchanged: both crusts (one stiff since Stone 15, one given Day 22, still smelling faintly of barley smoke), the charred slate in the kiln ash, the bench knife on its sill, the small pale river stone in the fold on the abandoned bed in House Silt (never mentioned), basket and wool lent by Sella, Alda's tin shut in her bodice and her narrow slate turned toward a floorboard, Sella's private roll unshown, Berr's box locked, Mira's two cords with knots still uncounted.
- **Money and figures.** No money reaches the boy. The year's first measure was taken offstage and entered without a figure spoken in the house; a whole return was read out for somebody absent and the household did not repeat the name; one in twelve stays printed and unchallenged and the true subtraction is spoken by nobody; the levy for the feast and Garl's uncalled tile debt remain as they were. Every figure goes past him shut, unspendable, uncorrectable, unreturnable.
- **Powers and cost.** No usable power, no rank change, no experiment, no clamp, no heresy. He is cracked, low-capacity, unranked, below Thin, twelve to fifteen embers at best, with a flaw nobody has named. Counting is an old-life habit, not a gift. The ear still holds five hearings of the full note from four sources and no sixth was added by any of the five days; no Grathic word for it, no hand to tap a vessel. The five sightings of the neat dark hand are unchanged and unjoined; the Drawing collector's ordinary hand with no pause stays filed apart. Five pauses lie side by side and do not join. The hot trace that carried him is in him still and has no name.
- **Facts, lies, promises, debts.** The year's fire is carried from hearth to hearth and nobody says where it was first lit or what it cost; Sella's hand goes to the mark before an opening and at no other hour, a rule that holds across the turn of the year. The Court's borrowed grammar can be trained into a clean institutional voice; a whole return can be announced for a person not present. A house can remember by keeping objects and withholding one word. A thing left in place and a thing burned and called dealt with are two ways of keeping the same room. Lies and coverings: the grey is called gone and dealt with; dates are laid over signs and over names; Sella says *the house* where another woman would say *I*. No new promise is made; the boat on cold water is remembered untagged, the name under the lit rectangle is not looked at. Tarn's seventh year is still held back; Garl's debt is still uncalled.

- **Foreshadowing planted.** The carried fire toward a payer beyond the yard; the hypothetical fifth toward a note nearly full and never quite; the clean voice and the Court's room toward Derren's grammar, the Weighing Hall and Ledgerhand; the whole return for an absent person toward the private purchase; the unspoken name toward Jorra's later weapon and Dessa's death; the told gaps toward the third thing; the ridge called gone toward the Ash that remembers and the later heresy that a house left where it is is still kept; the two cords toward the knot-string years hence; the crusts toward the day one is gone and nobody says who took it; the count carried into a new year toward the Breaking.
- **The next chapter must not forget.** Day 31, Ilmen 2, Y.H. 813, Secondday, at the yard well: a woman lets the bucket down and says the water tastes different; a man come up from the Ilm says the ice is honeyed under the mud; nobody says the word *the Breaking*. The boy is a month old on Day 32 and still carried, mute, cracked, unranked. Keep the two crusts, the empty fifth, the told and heard bells apart, the silence where a name should be, the ridges, the charred slate, five hearings from four sources, five unjoined pauses, the Drawing collector's separate hand and the pale stone in House Silt unmentioned. Days 31–44 get fourteen dated entries; Dessa dies on Day 38, uncollected, in the Slip Brook, and stays dead; Day 38 is the sixth hearing and the second from a person. No joining, no explanation, no ending.
