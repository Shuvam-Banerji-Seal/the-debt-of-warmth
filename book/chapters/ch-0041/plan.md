# Chapter 41 — beat sheet: *Where the Stroke Stopped*

**Days covered.** Days 170–172, Tallow 21–23, Y.H. 813, Firstday to Thirdday. A normal chapter of three lived days, each opened by its own dated `\daymark` on the first scene of that day and lived in full: Day 170 in Scenes 1–2, Day 171 in Scenes 3–4, Day 172 in Scenes 5–6. Day 169, Tallow 20, the second Account-day, is done and is not revisited, recapped or explained. Check every weekday against `bible/calendar.md` (*t* = 336 + *N* − 1): Day 170 = Tallow 21 = Firstday, Day 171 = Tallow 22 = Secondday, Day 172 = Tallow 23 = Thirdday. Day 179 is Tallow 30, the third Account-day and last market of Tallow, and belongs to Chapter 42; Day 180 is Ember 1.

**Arc.** Arc 1, *The Cold Stone* — "a warm stone, already going cold." Nothing here reaches Thin, opens a way, clamps, grafts, speaks Grathic or changes rank. The count is an old-life habit, not a gift. The surgeon's register colours the noticing and never becomes a power.

**The chapter's one meaningful change.** A third movement of the equality under the Vail roof, held and unspoken. On Day 171, while Jorra's bent finger waits in the cold damper until Sella's unfinished sentence has finished, Tarn's broom checks for half a beat behind the standing finger on the flagstones and goes on without a word or a turn of the head. The Seed 22 half-check returns after a hundred and fifty days and comes *changed*: this time it happens in the same breath as a sentence allowed to finish and a finger standing in the iron, with both men looking elsewhere. It is not a reconciliation, a hearing, a stopping, a payment or a reconciliation dressed as mercy; no payer is named, no slate is entered, the loan is uncalled and no tenth stopping stands up. The boy files it on a place of its own **beside** the Day 161 fraction at the damper and the Day 166 held beat beneath the loaf shelf, and forbids the three to stand up and call themselves a rule, for a rule is an account that has found a payer.

**The discipline of the chapter.** Advance, never pay. No calling of the loan, no tenth stopping before the seventh year is earned, no spending of the Seed 16 breath, no giving or cutting of the seventh year, no seventh hearing of the full note, no fourth near-full answer, no payer named to any keeping, no third gap, no fourth likeness made a cause, no finger of his drawing a line. The Day 161 fraction and Day 166 held beat stay on separate places through Days 170 and 172 and are not repeated. The pan's gone place, the two shelf gaps, the fold's crust and its gap and the field's empty place stay on their own trays. The charcoal is never read, copied or entered. The widow's mark is touched at no shelf, well, rack, basket, bed or dusk, because none is an opening. Marra stays out of Sill; Alda, Mira, Orra, Berr, Hessa, the soft-cuffed clerk and the foundling stay offstage; Dessa stays dead and unnamed to the boy; the green is never Fenna in narration; *he's not Vail* stays sealed and the promise of the foundling's name stays ungiven. No present-tense Chicago break and no `\memory{}` tag (T004). Point of view never leaves Dan's eyes: no Court, collector, back-room interior or Hearthseat scene.

## Scene 1 — "The Last Working Week Opens"
**Day and time.** Day 170, Tallow 21, Firstday, before first grey through mid-morning. First `\daymark{170}{Tallow 21, Y.H. 813 (Firstday)}` and the chapter's drop capital.
**Place.** Under the Vail kiln-house roof (hearth, bed, loaf shelf's low angle), the kiln mouth, the drying racks and the yard well in the Clay Yard.
**Present.** Corin (in the basket, carried), Sella Vail, Tarn; unnamed rack and well neighbours as texture. Jorra is not on stage.

**Beats, in order.**
1. Drop capital into hot clay under tallow; the first sound is the kiln's breath in fours with a gap, not a bell. He is one hundred and seventy days old and five months and twenty days and nobody under any roof says so (T071/T072). The crust lies stiff two inches from his ear, its empty place unnamed and no third; the thread of drool is frozen and unwiped; his hand outside the wool goes up an inch toward the note of water over water and comes down against the fold.
2. Sella gives every instruction to the wool, the fire, the pot, the broom and the running water and never to the basket; she says *the house*, keeps the half-pace from every open slate, turns the coals with the back of a broken pot, tells the fire it was the thaw, says *The house will keep it*, counts under her breath toward no opening, pushes coal-black hair back with the burn-wrist (grey streak pale off the coals), and sets the pot rim out with its handle two inches beyond the reaching hand, her hand resting a half-beat longer than the setting needs. The widow's mark hangs where it hung: a morning and a shelf are not openings (T058/T079).
3. The carried fire under the broken pot answers four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have been, its payer unnamed for a hundred and seventy days (T088). The kiln breathes behind the wall in fours with its own gap. The river's note of water over water comes through the finger's-width door as a fifth low thing never counted among the hearings.
4. The yard goes around the clean dark line, the new finer grey called the thaw, the rack space with its broken birth-slates and the empty place in the returning line where a hand went in on Seed 15 and did not come out, with a look and a wide step and no number (T009/T013/T092, T098 carried). The trough stands bare and dark and the water is called weather; the clay jar stands cold under its glaze, glanced at once and not touched; the shallow pan's gone place keeps its round ring on its own tray.
5. Tarn takes the broom only after it is instructed, says *Yes, house* to an early thaw and a late while going on wanting the late because it puts off the paying, and sweeps over the road a house sends a man down when it wants a collector without once turning along it; no collector comes. Six are counted in the worn thumb-mark where the house can check and the seventh is held at the lip of the sill where it cannot; the small knife is back at the very edge with Garl's unforged collector sentence beside it; Ash is worked into the knuckle creases in the colour of the clean dark line; the pale clip-ring holds what light there is and gives it back; his chest has been tight since the dark; the warm stone in the clay pot in the loft is cold and unreconciled by not trying (T036/T076, T006 held). Nine stoppings stay nine objects and not a rule with no payer named to any, the tenth withheld, the Seed 16 breath unspent and the loan uncalled (T061/T082).
6. The danger of the firing month gathers in the dust ground small as meal on the wrong wind and the sweetish lead edge from High Kiln: a pot set down at the racks gives a high thin note before its time and the woman who set it down does not hear it. The boy files the sound as a danger, **not** as a fourth near-full answer; no room is changed and no pot is lost. He reads the firing as a theatre he cannot enter — heat kept and counted behind a screen, a gate of two bites, a cost paid by a man whose name is not spoken — and his file is exact and worthless, an inventory that is not a key and a list that is not a hand.
7. The Day 161 fraction at the damper and the Day 166 held beat beneath the loaf shelf stand on separate places and are not repeated or joined; no fourth likeness is made a cause. Three counts stay in three places — the kiln behind the wall, the river through the door, the boards' four nails, four and a knot the width of a thumb overhead — and none becomes the others. No tagged memory, no Chicago break (T004).

**Emotional turn.** From the domestic order of the week's opening to the recognition that the month's heat has a payer nobody will name. The boy's certainty is not triumph: the file is complete and useless, and he is the only adult in the room and the least use in it.

**Sensory anchors.** Hot clay under tallow, woodsmoke, wet wool, grey Ash in knuckles, cold iron at the cheek, river wind meeting smoke on the stair, the high thin note of a pot singing early, the pale clip-ring catching what light there is.

**Threads advanced, none paid.** T071/T072 (170 exact), T088 (payer unnamed 170 days), T058/T079, T009/T013/T092, T098 (carried), T036/T076 and T006 (six counted, seventh held, warm stone cold), T061/T082 (nine stoppings, no tenth), T077, T004 (offstage).

**Foreshadowing planted / paid off.** Planted and gathered: the sweetish lead edge and the pot singing out of time feed the non-fatal edge owed in Chapter 45; the held ninth stopping and the seventh year at the sill feed the later giving; the damper's double bite feeds the shut-room convergence around ~650; the pan's gone place and the two shelf gaps feed the far-off hand that will lay them together. Nothing paid.

**Exact last image.** Tarn's broom going over the collector's road without once turning along it while the kiln breathes in fours with its gap and the river keeps its fifth note through the door; the boy's hand goes up an inch toward the note of water and comes down against the fold with one hundred and seventy standing exact and unspent.

## Scene 2 — "The Cost Sent to the Pot"
**Day and time.** Day 170, midday into the going of the light. Continuation of the same day; no new `\daymark`.
**Place.** The yard well, the drying racks, the loaf shelf and under the Vail roof.
**Present.** Corin, Sella, Tarn briefly; a neighbour with a wet pot, rack women, a kiln-hand as walk-ons.

**Beats, in order.**
1. At the well a neighbour with a wet pot asks what a firing costs set down in the Vail house. Sella glances once at the clay jar cold under its glaze, does not touch it, and sends the woman to the pot with *Ask the pot what a wet thing costs set down there. The house has said it* (T077). The question is carried away level and nothing slops; nothing is priced and nothing is refused.
2. At the racks the yard goes around the clean dark line, the finer grey called the thaw, the rack space with its broken birth-slates and the field's empty place, with a look and a wide step and no number (T009/T013/T092, T098 carried).
3. Sella moves her lips along her own loaf row standing with two gaps and gives nobody a figure. The two unnamed gaps (the old space at the row's end and the palm's breadth that came in on the night of Seed 6), the one stiff brown-edged crust in the fold and its empty place, the shallow pan's gone place on its own tray and the field's empty place stay in rooms and on trays that do not touch. No third gap, no fourth likeness made a cause (T060/T086, T097).
4. From the basket's low angle the underside of the loaf shelf becomes a roof and the charcoal marks wait under its front edge. Sella does not reach beneath the board this day and the boy does not read, count or copy the marks, which wait for a hand that is not his and a day that has not come (T100 carried, not paid).
5. At the going of the light she carries him home up the clay road, woodsmoke before her and the river wind behind, going wide around the pan's gone place, the bare dark trough and the rack space without explaining the turns to the basket. She sets him on his bed and not in the Debt Loft, puts the fold right without looking at his face, leaves the thread frozen, lets her hand rest a half-beat longer than the setting, tells the fire it was the thaw, turns the coals with the back of a broken pot, says *The house will keep it* where another woman would say *I*, counts under her breath toward no opening, pushes coal-black hair back with the burn-wrist and sets the pot rim out with its handle two inches beyond the reaching hand (T058/T079).
6. The widow's mark is not touched, because a dusk is not an opening and the hand goes to the mark before the kiln and at no other hour. The fire answers four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have been, its payer unnamed for a hundred and seventy days (T088). The boy's count stands at one hundred and seventy, exact, complete and unspent, spoken by nobody (T071/T072).

**Emotional turn.** The day's costs are sent as directions to pots, weather and rivers and leave no entry; the danger of the firing month gathers without a lesson. The boy's reading of the cost is perfect and buys nothing.

**Sensory anchors.** A pot carried level so nothing slops, wet rope on the well rim, dry slip dust in the rack shadows, tallow smoke going before her on the stair, the cold glaze of the clay jar, the leathery crust two inches from his ear.

**Threads advanced, none paid.** T077 (cost as direction), T058/T079, T009/T013/T092, T098 (carried), T060/T086/T097, T100 (carried, unread), T088, T071/T072.

**Foreshadowing planted / paid off.** Gathered only: the unread charcoal under the board, the question sent to the pot, the two shelf gaps and the pan's ring, the fire's unnamed fifth. Nothing paid.

**Exact last image.** The pot rim set out with its handle standing two inches beyond the reaching hand while the fire misses its fifth under the floor and the two rooms do not touch.

## Scene 3 — "The Finger in the Iron"
**Day and time.** Day 171, Tallow 22, Secondday, before first grey into the morning. Second `\daymark{171}{Tallow 22, Y.H. 813 (Secondday)}`.
**Place.** The Vail wheel-shed, the flagstones, the cold damper, the drying racks, the finger's-width-open back room (not entered).
**Present.** Corin, Jorra Vail, Sella Vail, Tarn; three or four unnamed men who go into the back room and a High Kiln kiln-master as walk-ons.

**Beats, in order.**
1. Before first grey three or four men go low into the finger's-width-open back room and get no name; Sella carries past at the half-pace, turns no head and does not say the room is cold. The two shut-room tellings stand side by side with no third (T065/T095).
2. Jorra comes through with fine grey slate dust ground small as meal into both forearms past the bent little finger and under the nails, not bone-coloured slip; he asks neither which slate was broken nor whether it was a birth-slate, and keeps dusty forearms and clean ones on one tray. The broken birth-slates stay under the lowest rack, entered by nobody (T035/T031).
3. He speaks to the wheel before anyone (*It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady*), tells a pot *You'll do*, knows its full note before it leaves the wheel and withholds the knowing from a High Kiln kiln-master come up on the wrong wind who says a pot singing before its time generally sings itself out early. The knowing goes to the near-full tray, beside the hearings and never among them, advanced and not paid; no pot is lost in the yard (T061/T082).
4. When Sella's instruction is low and unfinished (*The fire's low. It wants---*), Jorra's bent finger goes to the cold iron of the damper and stops, as on Tallow 12 and Tallow 15; he holds the iron while she finishes *It wants feeding*, then moves the tongue a hair's breadth and leaves it standing. The fraction at the iron is remembered in the holding and not in a word; nobody speaks of the waiting and no payer is named (T062).
5. **The change:** while the finger still stands in the iron and the sentence has finished, Tarn, come up the yard with the broom, checks his stroke for half a beat behind the standing finger on the flagstones and goes on toward the road without a word or a turn of the head. The stones keep the shallow bright patch where the stroke stopped; neither man names the other's silence. This is the third movement of the equality under the roof, held and unspoken: no reconciliation, no hearing, no payment, no slate entered, the loan uncalled and the nine stoppings still nine (T036/T076, T062).
6. The boy files this movement on a place of its own **beside** the Day 161 fraction at the damper and the Day 166 held beat beneath the loaf shelf and refuses to let three fragments stand up and call themselves a rule, for a rule is an account that has found a payer and no payer stands to any of the three (T061/T082, T100 kept apart). He does not call the loan and does not invent a tenth stopping or a seventh hearing.
7. Tarn sweeps over the road a house sends a man down when it wants a collector, without once turning along it, and no collector comes; six are counted where the house can check and the seventh held where it cannot, the knife at the very edge of its sill, the Seed 16 breath unspent, no tenth stood up before the seventh year is earned (T036/T076, T061/T082). Through the finger's-width door the river keeps its note of water over water, a fifth low thing never counted among the hearings and never invented to stand up as a seventh; the damper's double bite is read once more as one iron holding and taking, filed as an object joined to no kiln with no Grathic word for both mercies, and the two shut-room tellings stay beside it with no third.

**Emotional turn.** From the yard's danger and the men's separate silences to a sudden, tiny synchronization of two kept things — and the immediate refusal to make anything of it. The movement is felt as a widening of what can be held, not a settling.

**Sensory anchors.** Grey dust ground small as meal worked past a bent finger; cold iron taking the warmth out of a fingertip; damp clay on the wheel's edge; the sweetish lead edge on a wrong wind; the shallow bright patch on the flagstones where one stroke stopped.

**Threads advanced, none paid.** T065/T095 (two tellings, no third), T035/T031 (men unnamed, grey dust, birth-slates unentered), T061/T082 (full note known and withheld, near-full tray, three fractions kept apart), T062 (the fraction at the iron, and the third movement held), T036/T076 (equality moved a third time, sixth year counted and seventh held, loan uncalled), T100 (kept apart), T088, T009/T013/T092.

**Foreshadowing planted / paid off.** Gathered: Jorra's dust and the unnameable back room toward the Broken Slates; the damper's double bite and the two shut-room tellings toward ~650; the sweetish lead and the pot singing early toward Chapter 45's small edge. The half-check returns changed from Seed 22 and is held, not paid.

**Exact last image.** The two strokes going on in one room without a word, the flagstones keeping the shallow bright patch where one stopped, and the boy setting three held changes on three places and drawing no line between them.

## Scene 4 — "Three Places and No Rule"

## Scene 5 — "Six Counted, the Next I Don't Count"
**Day and time.** Day 172, Tallow 23, Thirdday, morning through the afternoon. Third `\daymark{172}{Tallow 23, Y.H. 813 (Thirdday)}`.
**Place.** The Clay Yard, the yard well and the kindling under the Vail roof.
**Present.** Corin, Sella, Tarn; well neighbours as walk-ons. Jorra is not on stage.

**Beats, in order.**
1. The firing month's last tend is in its third day with the kilns at their heat; the danger gathers in the dust ground small as meal, the sweetish lead edge on a wrong wind and the kiln's breath in fours with a gap, and no room is changed and no pot lost.
2. Tarn takes the broom only after it has been given its instruction and tells the kindling *Six counted. The next I don't count*, with the small knife at the very edge of its sill, six counted in the worn thumb-mark where the house can check and the seventh held at the lip where it cannot, and Garl's unforged collector sentence beside it (T036/T076).
3. 4. He says *Yes, house* to an early thaw and a late, agrees so entirely that no argument can find a foot, and goes on wanting the late because a late thaw puts off the paying, though the paying has stood in the ruts since the water went; his broom goes over the collector's road without once turning along it and no collector comes.
5. **The near-saying held:** the sentence about an ear under this roof being worth more than the debt that holds it comes to his teeth and no further; he draws the breath he should have spent, does not say the word that would call the loan, does not spend the Seed 16 breath and does not stand a tenth stopping up to join the nine, because the seventh year has not yet been earned (T036/T076, T061/T082). 6. At the well a woman asks what the kilns will want now the last week is in and is given the weather, the pot and the river, never a figure; the yard goes around the clean dark line, the finer grey called the thaw, the rack space with its broken birth-slates and the field's empty place, with a look and a wide step and no number (T009/T013/T092, T098 carried, T077).

8. The surgeon's register reads the held breath as a clamp left too long and the near-saying stopped at the word as a chart closed over its finding with the neat margin showing: the reading is exact and worth nothing at two paces. The carried fire answers four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have been, its payer unnamed for a hundred and seventy-two days (T088), and the boy's count stands at one hundred and seventy-two, exact, complete and unspent, spoken by nobody (T071/T072). No tagged memory and no Chicago break (T004).

**Emotional turn.** The pressure of the seventh year at the very edge of the sill; the debt's almost-sentence held at the word. The boy recognises that something must give at the end of the seventh year and knows it is not here. The reading works perfectly and buys nothing.

**Sensory anchors.** Banked coals turning under a broken pot, cold iron of the clip-ring, grey Ash in knuckle creases, wet wool, splintered kindling, hot clay smell on the wrong wind, a thumb-worn mark on a sill.

**Threads advanced, none paid.** T036/T076 (six counted, seventh held, near-saying stopped, no tenth), T061/T082, T006 (held), T058/T079, T009/T013/T092, T098 (carried), T077, T088 (172 days), T071/T072 (172 exact), T004 (offstage).

**Foreshadowing planted / paid off.** Gathered: the seventh year's approaching end (to be paid only after the year is earned, beyond Chapter 45); the carried fire's unnamed payer toward Sella's second roll (~330) and the Hearth as a wound; the sweetish lead and pot-singing danger toward the small non-fatal edge in Chapter 45. Nothing paid.

**Exact last image.** The small knife set back at the very edge of its sill with the seventh year held where the house cannot check, and the fire missing its fifth under the floor with its payer unnamed for a hundred and seventy-two days.

Three counts in three places, and none rises up and becomes the others. Cold arrives in its known order, cheek, temple, the small bare joint at the wrist where the wool rides back, and stops as if it had reached the end of its instruction.
6. He wants the crust, the breast, the pot handle two inches beyond his hand and the number spoken by somebody who would have known what a number was, and gets none; he has stopped expecting to be told what the number counts, which is not the same as not wanting it. One hundred and seventy-two stands exact, complete and unspent in the dark of the wool, spoken by nobody under any roof (T071/T072). His hand outside the wool goes up an inch toward the note of water and comes down against the fold. The payer under the floor has had no name for a hundred and seventy-two days (T088) and the charcoal waits under the shelf for a hand that is not his and a day that has not come.

**Emotional turn.** The exact and worthless file; three held fractions growing heavier without becoming a rule. The chapter closes on forward motion, not settlement: the weight of nine stoppings and the approaching end of the seventh year are carried into the month's last tend.

**Sensory anchors.** Tallow smoke and river wind passing on the stair, the fold at his ear, the frozen thread, grey Ash in a corner, the leathery crust, charcoal dust on the underside of a board, the cold of cheek--temple--wrist.

**Threads advanced, none paid.** T071/T072 (170–172 exact), T088 (payer unnamed through 172), T058/T079, T061/T082 (six hearings with no seventh, three near-full answers, nine stoppings as objects, no tenth), T036/T076 and T062/T100 (three held changes on separate places), T065/T095, T078 (carried), T060/T086/T097, T098 (carried), T004 (offstage). T050/T051 and T033/T034 held offstage; nothing spent.

**Foreshadowing planted / paid off.** Gathered only: the unread charcoal awaits the far-off hand that will lay the district's private records together; the three held fractions await the day a rule will try to stand up out of likenesses; the seventh year and the ninth stopping await the end of the term; the fire's unnamed payer awaits Sella's second roll; Tallow 30's Account-day awaits Chapter 42. Nothing paid, named or joined.

**Exact last image.** His hand outside the wool goes up an inch toward the note of water over water and comes down against the fold, while one hundred and seventy-two stands exact, complete and unspent, the payer under the floor has had no name for a hundred and seventy-two days, and the charcoal waits under the shelf for a hand that is not his and a day that has not come.

## How the final moment pulls into Chapter 42

The last tend of Tallow has three days left before its Account-day and last market on Day 179 (Tallow 30), and the month goes out without a boundary being drawn. The count stands at one hundred and seventy-two, exact and unspent and spoken by nobody; the fire's fifth has no payer and the charcoal has no reader; three held changes sit on three places and may not stand up as a rule; nine stoppings are still nine and the seventh year has not given. Chapter 42 must open on Day 173, Tallow 24, Fourthday, with no recap of this close, and carry the district's arithmetic through to the third Account-day without settling any of it: the figure read will still not be the figure paid, Berr's box will go up the clay road still locked, Hessa's slate edge-on and unread if she appears, the soft-cuffed clerk still set for his uncalled measure, the green still a colour and the foundling still a shape, and the tenth stopping still withheld until the seventh year is earned. Forward, not closed.
