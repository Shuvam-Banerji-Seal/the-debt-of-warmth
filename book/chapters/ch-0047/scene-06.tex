The rest of that day came on in its own time, the afternoon thinning without ending, going off the high air first the way water draws off a stone that has held the sun and staying longest in the low places: under \place{the loaf shelf}, behind the pot, in the fold of wool where \person[Corin]{the boy} lay. The smell of hot clay lay under tallow and wet wool lay under that, and beneath both the sour mineral breath of the corner nobody had swept in two years. The underside of the shelf made a roof that stopped at the edge of his reach, and the heat came down that roof in a slow slant and stopped at the same place, as if it too had been given an instruction and had come to the end of it. Nobody in the house said what the instruction was. Through the floor \lore{the carried fire} answered four low pulls and kept the silence where the fifth should have been, its payer without a name for a hundred and ninety-four days; through the finger's-width door the river went on with its note of \lore{water over water}, a fifth low thing never counted among the hearings; overhead the boards kept four nails, four, and a knot the width of a thumb. Three counts in three places, and none of them rose up and became the others, because a man who wanted them to agree would have to put a payer under them and no payer stood to any of them.

\person{Sella Vail} came to the shelf and stood with her back to the basket at the angle where the board's underside became a roof, and moved her lips along the row without sound. The row stood with two unnamed gaps, the old space at the end and the palm's breadth that had come in on the night of Seed 6, and her lips went over them the way a thumb goes over a worn notch in a handle, knowing the shape and refusing the figure. Her eye went once to the clay jar cold under its glaze on the wall and her hand started toward it and shut half over nothing without touching it. She gave nobody a number. Her finger did not go beneath the front edge where the charcoal tally waited, so the marks lay unread, uncounted, uncopied, and the beat that had stayed under the board since Day 166 stayed where it had been left, not repeated and not paired with the fraction at the damper or the half-check on the stones. A hand that keeps from under a board either knows it by heart or has been told not to look, and the reason stood between the two.

She went to the hearth and told the fire it was the thaw, and turned the coals with the back of a broken pot, and said \emph{The house will keep it} where another woman would have said \emph{I}. Then she stood a moment with the back of the pot in her hand and counted under her breath toward no opening --- the small intake at the top, the hold, the letting go --- the way she had counted at every going of the light, and never once toward a thing that came. She pushed a strand of coal-black hair back with the wrist that carries the little-door burn so that the grey streak at her temple showed pale off the coals; it had come in the winter Garl died and she would say no more. She set the pot rim out on the board with its handle standing two inches beyond the reaching hand, and her hand rested a half-beat longer at the setting than the setting needed before it went away, and she did not look toward \place{the bed}. \lore[the widow's mark]{The widow's mark} hung on its cord at her throat where it had hung since the first firing, touched once before the kiln on Ember 1, held one breath, let go, and at no shelf, well, rack, road, basket, afternoon or dusk since. An afternoon and a shelf were not openings. There had been no opening since before the kiln on Ember 1.

\scenebreak

At \place{the kindling} under the wall the small knife lay at the edge of its sill, six counted in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check and the seventh at the lip where it could not, and Garl's unforged collector sentence beside it, the sentence of the man who turned back at the frozen ford. \person[Tarn Vail]{Tarn} took up the broom only after the broom had been told what it was wanted for.

“Six counted. The next I don't count,” he told the wood.

He said \emph{Yes, house} to an early thaw and a late, agreed with both, and went on wanting the late because a late thaw puts off the paying, though the paying had stood in the ruts since the water went. His chest had been tight since the dark. \lore{Ash} was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line and no scrubbing lifted it, and the pale clip-ring at his left wrist held what light there was and gave it back. The sentence about an ear under this roof being worth more than the debt that held it came up to his teeth and all the way to the word that would call the loan, and stopped there with no figure and no name. He drew the breath he should have spent at the top of the reach and did not spend it, the Seed 16 breath standing where the breath should have gone in. Nine stoppings stood as objects with no tenth before the seventh year was earned; the seventh year stood nearer its end than it had ever stood and had not given, and the loan that can hear stayed uncalled. He said \emph{Yes, house} to the fire and went on with the broom. His stroke went over \place{the flagstones} past the shallow bright patch and back without the half-check. He swept the road a house sends a man down when it wants a collector without once turning along it, and no collector came up \place{the clay road} and no collector went down it.

\scenebreak

Out in \place{the Vail wheel-shed} \person{Jorra Vail} came through from the finger's-width-open \place{the back room} with fine grey slate dust ground small as meal into both forearms past the bent little finger and under the nails, white in the creases where a fist had opened. It was not bone-coloured \lore{slip}. Three or four men with their collars high had gone low into that room before first grey and he gave the house no name for them and did not say the room was cold; the two tellings of the shut counting room stayed two with no third. He kept dusty forearms and clean ones on one tray and asked neither which slate had been broken nor whether it was a birth-slate. He spoke to the wheel before he spoke to anyone.

“It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady,” he said, and told a pot “You'll do” in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either. He knew \lore{the full note} that the pot would give three turns before it left his hands and withheld the knowing from a man come up on a wrong wind who said a pot singing before its time generally sings itself out early; the knowing went to the near-full tray beside the hearings and never among them, advanced and not paid. No pot was lost. \emph{He's not Vail} stayed sealed and the promise of the boy's name to the men who burn slates stayed ungiven and not withdrawn. He did not look toward the basket.

When Sella's voice went low and unfinished at the hearth, his bent finger went to the cold iron of \lore{the damper} and held while she finished the sentence and gave its end to the pot. He moved the tongue a hair's breadth and left the finger standing. It was a remembered repetition, not a change made again, not stood up with Day 161, 166 or 171; the household straightened around the silence, and nobody spoke of the waiting. The iron stayed one piece with two bites, the holding and the taking, with no word in \lore{Grathic} that would carry both mercies in one mouthful, joined to no kiln and entered in no slate.

The stone lay back in its unmarked clay pot in \place{the Debt Loft}, two years gone and cold and not looked at in a year, unreconciled with the debt by not trying, which is not the same as forgiving. From below the boy kept only the remembering of the small dry scrape of a pot's foot set down on stone, filed on a place of its own and not joined to the three held changes or the loft-sill scrape or the carried man's pale line, and not made into a cause, for a fourth likeness standing up would call itself a cause and a cause would want a payer under it.

At the going of the light she carried him home up the clay road with the instruction going to the wool and the river wind and never to the basket. Woodsmoke went before her in long low bars out of the chimneys and the river wind came up behind with the taste of water over water, the two passing on the stair of the evening as they had passed every evening of his life without taking each other's colour or spending a word on either. The arm that had carried him a hundred and ninety-four mornings kept the height of the handle, the give of the shoulder and the turn of the wrist at a step's end; it was a record in muscle that remembered weight and not cost.

She went wide around the pan's gone place on the wall and the bare dark \place{the trough} at \place[the yard well]{the well} whose water was called weather, wide around the space beneath \place[the drying racks]{the lowest rack} with its broken birth-slates entered by nobody, wide around the empty place in the returning line where a hand had gone in on Seed 15 and had not come out, and wide around the place the two carriers had crossed with the glaze-room hand between them. Each got a look that asked for nothing and found nothing, a wide step and no number and no explanation given to the basket. Under the roof she set him on \place{the bed} and not in the Debt Loft, base first and the weight settled the way a woman sets a vessel that must not rock, put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without once looking at his face, left the thread of drool frozen and unwiped, and let her hand rest a half-beat longer at the setting than the setting needed before it went away. She told the carried fire it was the thaw, banked the coals with the back of a broken pot and did not touch the widow's mark, because a dusk is not an opening and there had been no opening since before the kiln on Ember 1. The two rooms did not touch: hers held a pot, a fire and a woman counting under her breath toward nothing entered, and his held a crust two inches from an ear and an empty place beside it with no name.

\scenebreak

In the small hours he turned out the file the way a man lays instruments on a towel before a case begins, each thing going into its own hollow and nothing resting across anything else. He had no towel and no instruments and no case, and laid them out anyway in the dark with hands that could not have lifted the least of them. Six hearings of the full note lay beside and never among --- five from four sources, a cup across a birth room, a pot in the Vail wheel-shed through a finger's width of door, the district's slate twice at \place{the Cross}, and a man giving at \lore[the Hearth Drawing]{the Drawing} --- and the sixth, the small flat knock out of a woman as the men lifted her, from a person and filed where a thing out of a person must go, never among the sources. No seventh came in a hundred and ninety-four days and none was invented to fill the empty place, for a finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged. Three near-full answers stood on their own tray, advanced and not paid: a pot under the shed wall that answered nearly full with air under the note and a want in it; a rack pot lifted a hand's breadth too high and set down again; and the knowing the wheel-keeper kept back from a man come up on a wrong wind. None was promoted into the hearings and no pot was lost in the yard.

On the widened tray nine stoppings stood --- Reed 5, 8, 11, 14, 17, 20, 23, 26 and 29 --- objects and not a rule, intervals and not payments, held apart by hand because a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and no payer stood to any of them. A tenth had not stood up before the seventh year was earned. The seventh year stood nearer its end than it had ever stood and had not given; the loan that can hear was uncalled; the Seed 16 breath stood unspent at the top of every reach with the small interval where a breath should have gone in and had not. The damper lay beside them as one piece of iron with two bites and no word for both, joined to no kiln and entered in no slate. Three held changes stood on three places: the fraction from the morning of Day 161 at the cold iron, the beat that stayed under the board on Day 166, and the half-check on the flagstones on Day 171, with the loft-sill scrape on a fourth place and the carried man's pale line at the gum on a fifth. The told bell stood beside the heard, four, four and a gap with nobody crossing himself, and the told figure stood beside the paid, one in twelve printed and unchallenged while the true subtraction went up the road in a locked box. The crust's gap, the shelf's two gaps, the pan's gone round ring and the field's empty place in the returning line lay on trays that did not touch. No third gap came, no fourth likeness was made a cause and no finger of his drew the line, because three likenesses standing up together will call themselves a truth if a man leaves them standing long enough to want an answer.

Then his hand outside the wool went up an inch toward the note of water over water through the finger's-width door.

It did not come down at once.

It stayed up. It stayed for the length of one of the breaths Sella counted at her fire in the room that did not touch this one --- the small intake at the top, the hold, the letting go --- and the surgeon who had once held pressure in a cavity until the vessel took could hold nothing now but this: one inch of a small soft hand lifted toward a sound and left there, counted against a breath he could not hear and a fire he could not see. Then the hand came down against the fold, as hands come down, and the fold took it without a word. It was a change held and unspoken, filed on a place of its own beside the others and never laid across them; it was not a hearing, not a seventh, not a stopping, not a payment and not one of the three held changes, and no payer was named to it and no doctrine was allowed to stand up out of it. He still could not close the two inches to the pot handle, lift his chin to the crust or speak a number anybody would have known what a number counted. The body humiliated him and moved by an inch at the same time, and both were true.

One hundred and ninety-four stood exact, complete and unspent in the dark of the wool, spoken by nobody under any roof. Cold had stopped at cheek, temple and the small bare joint at the wrist where the wool rode back, and the afterdrop waited on the far side of a warming nobody in this house would perform. The nine stoppings were still nine with no tenth; the Seed 16 breath was still held; the charcoal still waited under the shelf for a hand that was not hers and a day that had not come; the mark had been touched once before the kiln and at no other hour; and the cost of the man carried out stood entered nowhere. Through the finger's-width door the river kept its note of water over water, a fifth low thing never counted among the hearings; through the floor the carried fire gave four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have been; overhead the boards kept four nails, four, and a knot the width of a thumb. Three counts in three places, and none rose up and became the others. Nothing had been joined, named, opened, called, spent or ended. The hand had gone up an inch and held and come down, and the count went forward into the next day by that much and by no more.
