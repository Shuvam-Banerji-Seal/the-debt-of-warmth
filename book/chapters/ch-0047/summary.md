## Chronicle

Three mornings came up over Low Kiln in the kiln month, and nobody drew a boundary under the first. Tallow was the firing month and Ember the kiln month. The boy woke to hot clay under tallow and wet wool under that; the kiln drew in fours with a gap, the carried fire under the floor answered four low pulls and kept the silence where the fifth should have been, its payer unnamed for a hundred and ninety-four days, and the river kept its note of water over water. Nobody told him how old he was; he had stopped expecting to be told, which was not the same as not wanting it.

Sella Vail gave the morning its instruction before she gave it to any person --- to the wool, the fire, the pot, the broom, the running water, the river wind, never the basket --- and said *the house* where another woman would have said *I*. She fed him barley gruel, set the pot handle two inches beyond his hand, and let her hand rest a half-beat longer at the setting. Costs brought to her were sent to the pot and the weather and went away level, nothing priced or entered. She kept her finger from the charcoal and did not touch the widow's mark, for there had been no opening since Ember 1, and at dusk carried him home, going wide around the yard's absences.

Tarn took the broom only after it had been told what it was wanted for, told the wood six counted and the next he did not count, and brought the ear-sentence to the word that would call the loan and stopped there. He drew the breath he should have spent and did not spend it; nine stoppings stayed nine with no tenth before the seventh year was earned, and no collector came. Jorra kept dusty and clean forearms on one tray, told a pot it would do, withheld the note he knew three turns ahead from a wrong wind, and held his bent finger in the cold damper until Sella's sentence reached the pot --- a remembered holding, not a change made again.

In the small hours the boy laid out the file: six hearings beside and never among, no seventh invented, nine stoppings with no tenth, three held changes on three places and two more on a fourth and a fifth. Then his hand outside the wool went up an inch toward the river's note and did not come down at once; it stayed for the length of one of the breaths Sella counted at her fire in the room that did not touch this one, and then came down against the fold. It was an inch held and let go, filed on a place of its own, worth nothing. He still could not close two inches on the pot handle or speak a number. The body humiliated him and moved by an inch at the same time; both were true.

## Continuity

- **Exact final moment.** Day 194, Ember 15, Fifthday, Y.H. 813, the small hours, under the Vail kiln-house roof in Low Kiln. Corin/Dan lies on his bed with the fold at his ear, one leathery crust two inches from that ear and its empty place unnamed, the thread of drool frozen and unwiped. In the night inventory he has laid out the file, exact, complete and worthless; then his hand outside the wool went up an inch toward the note of water over water through the finger's-width door, **stayed up for the length of one of the breaths Sella counts at her fire in the room that does not touch this one** (small intake, hold, letting go), and came down against the fold. The hand is down at the close. Present: the boy only, awake and silent. Sella is asleep in her own room; Tarn and Jorra are offstage at their beds after the day's work. The count goes forward into Day 195, Ember 16.

- **What Chapter 48 must not forget.** Open on Day 195, Ember 16, Sixthday, in the kiln month's third working week, with the count at one hundred and ninety-five, the fire's fifth still missing with no payer, the nine stoppings still nine, the charcoal unread, the mark untouched, the stone cold and the loan uncalled. The hand that stayed up for one breath will be asked to stay up again; an inch held twice starts to look like a reaching, but the saga's method is to keep it a physical first and not a power or a rule. Do not join the held inch to the three held changes, the loft scrape, the pale line, the absences, the sweet wind, the dust or the early note. Forward, not closed.
