The afternoon went off the high air the way water goes off a stone that has held the sun. It did not end; it lost its edges. The heat left the upper boards first and came down in a slow slant under \place{the loaf shelf}, behind the pot, into the fold of wool where \person[Corin]{the boy} lay, and stopped at the edge of his reach as if it too had been given an instruction and had come to the end of it. He lay at the low angle where the underside of the shelf made a roof, and the roof came down toward him and did not arrive. Two inches stood between his hand and the pot handle. The world was full of two inches. Hot clay lay under tallow under wet wool, and below all three came the sour mineral breath of the corner that no broom in \place{Low Kiln} had been given an instruction about in two years; the household went around that corner and gave the broom no word about it.

\person{Sella Vail} stood at the shelf with her back to the basket and moved her lips along the row above him. The row stood with two unnamed gaps: the old space at the row's end, and the palm's breadth that had come in on the night of Seed 6. She gave nobody a figure. Her eye went once to the clay jar cold under its glaze on the wall, and her hand started toward it and shut half over nothing without touching it. She kept her finger from beneath the front edge of the shelf, so the charcoal tally lay where it lay, unread, uncounted, uncopied, and the beat that had stayed under the board on the night of Day 166 stayed where it had been left. An afternoon was not that night. A woman's back turned was not the same as a finger held under a board for a counted breath; he had spent a hundred and ninety-two days taking likenesses down one at a time, and he did not let this one stand up and call itself a return of that one. She kept her half-pace and said \emph{the house} where another woman would have said \emph{I}, and a neighbour come to the threshold with a cost in her hands went away with the cost still in her hands and nothing priced, refused or entered.

At \place{the hearth} she told \lore{the carried fire} it was \lore{the thaw} and turned the coals with the back of a broken pot. “The house will keep it,” she said where another woman would have said \emph{I}, and stood a moment with the back of the pot in her hand counting under her breath toward no opening --- the small intake at the top, the hold, the letting go. She pushed a strand of coal-black hair back with the wrist that carries the little-door burn so the grey streak at her temple showed pale off the coals, set the pot rim out on the board with its handle standing two inches beyond the reaching hand, and let her hand rest a half-beat longer at the setting than the setting needed. Then she went away and did not look toward \place{the bed}. The two rooms did not touch: hers held a pot and a fire and a woman counting toward nothing entered, and his held a crust and an empty place beside it with no name.

At \place{the kindling} the small knife lay at the edge of its sill with six counted in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check and the seventh at the lip where it could not, and \person[Garl Vail]{Garl}'s unforged collector sentence beside it, the sentence of the man who turned back at the frozen ford. \person[Tarn Vail]{Tarn} took the broom only after the broom had been told what it was wanted for. “Six counted. The next I don't count,” he told the wood. To a neighbour wanting the thaw early and another wanting it late he said “Yes, house” to both, agreed so entirely that no argument could find a foot, and went on wanting the late because a late thaw puts off the paying, though the paying had stood in the ruts since the water went. His chest had been tight since the dark. \lore{Ash} was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line and no scrubbing lifted it, and the pale clip-ring at his left wrist held what light there was and gave it back. He swept the road a house sends a man down when it wants a collector without once turning along it. No collector came up \place{the clay road} and no collector went down it.

The breath from Seed 16 stood unspent at the top of every reach, with the small interval where a breath should have gone in and had not. The sentence about an ear under this roof being worth more than the debt that held it came to his teeth and to the threshold of the word that would call the loan, and stopped there with no figure and no name. He drew the breath he should have spent and did not spend it. Nine stoppings stood as objects with no tenth before the seventh year was earned; the seventh year stood nearer its end than it had ever stood and had not given, and the loan that can hear stayed uncalled. His stroke went over \place{the flagstones} past the shallow bright patch and back without the half-check, and the stones kept the place where it had stopped, joined to no cause and to no ear.

Beyond \place{the Vail wheel-shed} the wheel turned. Three or four men unnamed had gone low into the finger's-width-open \place{the back room} before first grey and had got no name from the house, and what passed beyond the door the boy did not see. \person{Jorra Vail} came through in the afternoon with fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bent finger and under the nails, white in the creases where a fist had opened, not bone-coloured \lore{slip}. He kept dusty forearms and clean ones on one tray and asked neither which slate had been broken nor whether it was a birth-slate; the broken birth-slates lay beneath \place[the drying racks]{the lowest rack} and were entered by nobody. He spoke to the wheel before he spoke to anyone. “It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady,” he said, and told a pot “You'll do” in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either. He knew the full note that pot would give three turns before it left his hands and withheld the knowing from a man come up on a wrong wind who said a pot singing before its time generally sings itself out early. The knowing went to the near-full tray beside the hearings and never among them, advanced and not paid, and no pot was lost in the yard.

\scenebreak

When Sella's instruction went low and unfinished at the hearth --- “The fire's low. It wants---” --- Jorra's bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper} and stood there until the end of the sentence arrived at the pot; then he moved the tongue a hair's breadth and left the finger standing. It was a remembered holding, not a change made again, and lay on neither the place of the Day 161 fraction nor the Day 166 beat beneath the shelf nor the Day 171 half-check on the stones. He gave nobody a word about the waiting, did not look toward the boy, and did not say the room was cold. The two shut-room tellings stayed two with no third. The damper lay as one piece of iron with two bites, the holding and the taking, with no word in \lore{Grathic} that would carry both mercies in one mouthful, joined to no kiln and entered in no slate. A clamp is a clamp and not a doctrine, set where it is needed and not asked to mean more. \emph{He's not Vail} stayed sealed and the promise of the boy's name to the men who burn slates stayed ungiven.

The sweet edge of \lore{lead glaze} came up off \place{High Kiln} on a wrong wind, was taken as a direction and not a name and not a cause, and went out on the same wind. The pot that had sung itself out early stood swept around where it stood and was not made an omen and not made true as a lesson. Nobody named what had ailed the man carried out of the yard in the kiln month, no collector was called, no figure was given and no slate was opened; his pale line lay where it had been laid, on a fifth place across nothing, and the cost of his carrying stood entered nowhere. He had gone out of the yard breathing and had gone on up the clay road, and a breath kept is not an ending.

Above the shed in \place{the Debt Loft} the warm stone lay back in its unmarked clay pot, two years gone and cold, unreconciled with the debt by not trying, which is not forgiving. From below the boy kept only the remembering of the small dry scrape of a pot's foot set down on stone, a sound from a room he could not enter, filed on a place of its own and not a holding made again, not joined to the three held changes and not made a cause, for a fourth likeness standing up would call itself a cause and a cause would want a payer under it.

At the going of the light Sella carried him home up the clay road, woodsmoke going before her in long low bars out of the chimneys and the river wind coming up behind with the taste of \lore{water over water}, the two passing on the stair of the evening as they had passed every evening of his life without taking each other's colour or spending a word on either. The instruction went to the wool and the river wind and never to the basket. The arm that had carried him a hundred and ninety-one mornings kept the height of the handle, the give of the shoulder and the turn of the wrist at a step's end --- a record in muscle that remembered weight and gave no account of cost. It would have said, if a man could ask it, that the weight was the same on this morning as on the first, which is true, and that the cost was not the same, which is also true and entered nowhere.

She went around the pan's gone round place on the wall with no scrape, crumb, print or chip in the dust; around \place{the trough} at the well, bare and dark, whose water is called weather; around the space beneath the lowest rack where the broken birth-slates lay, entered by nobody; around the empty place in the returning line where a hand had gone in on Seed 15 and had not come out; and around the place the two carriers had crossed with the glaze-room hand between them. Each got a look that asked for nothing and found nothing, a wide step and no number and no explanation to the basket. Under the roof she set him on his bed and not in the Debt Loft, base first and the weight settled the way a woman sets a vessel that must not rock, put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without once looking at his face, left the thread of drool frozen and unwiped, and let her hand rest a half-beat longer at the setting than the setting needed. She told the fire it was the thaw, banked the coals with the back of a broken pot, and did not touch \lore{the widow's mark}, because a dusk is not an opening and there had been no opening since before the kiln on Ember 1. She went to her own bed. The two rooms did not touch.

In the small hours he turned out the file the way a man lays instruments on a towel before a case begins, each thing going into its own hollow and nothing resting across anything else, the order of the laying being the whole of the discipline. Six hearings of \lore{the full note} lay beside and never among: five from four sources --- a cup across a birth room, a pot in the Vail wheel-shed through a finger's width of door, the district's slate twice at \place{the Cross}, and a man giving at \lore[the Hearth Drawing]{the Drawing} --- and the sixth, a small flat knock out of a person as the men lifted, filed where a thing out of a person must go and never among the sources. No seventh came in a hundred and ninety-two days and none was invented to fill the empty place, for an empty place kept empty is a finding and a finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged. Three near-full answers stood on their own tray, advanced and not paid: a pot under the shed wall that answered nearly full with air under the note and a want in it, a rack pot lifted a hand's breadth too high and set down again, and the knowing the wheel-keeper kept back from the wrong wind. None was promoted into the hearings and no pot was lost. The older intervals lay at Stone 13, Stone 17 and Stone 25 with the half-beat behind a standing finger, the unchecking stroke that went on over the bright patch, his own held breath from a market morning, and nine stoppings on Reed 5, 8, 11, 14, 17, 20, 23, 26 and 29, objects and not a rule, intervals and not payments, with no payer named to any of them and no tenth stood up before the seventh year was earned.

Three held changes stood on three places --- the Day 161 fraction at the iron, the Day 166 beat beneath the shelf, the Day 171 half-check on the stones --- with the loft-sill scrape on a fourth and the carried man's pale line on a fifth. The told bell stood beside the heard, four, four and a gap, and the told figure stood beside the paid, one in twelve against a subtraction in a locked box up the road. The shelf's two gaps, the fold's crust and its gap, the pan's gone ring, the field's empty place, the sixth green and the foundling's sliver lay on trays that did not touch; he did not lay the sweet wind, the grey dust or the pot that sang early across any of them, and no finger of his drew the line. He wanted all of it to mean something and would not let it stand up and mean something, because a rule is an account that has found a payer and no payer stood to any of it. The reading had changed the district's arithmetic and changed nothing in this room. The file came back exact, complete and worth nothing, and the one cost the square could not know was still the only thing he was holding for the man who had been carried out.

He wanted the crust, wanted the breast, wanted the pot handle standing two inches beyond his hand, wanted a number spoken by somebody who would have known what a number counted. He got none of them and had stopped expecting to be told, which was not the same as not wanting it. Cold had stopped at cheek, temple and the small bare joint at the wrist where the wool rides back, and the afterdrop waited on the far side of a warming nobody in this house would perform. Through the finger's-width door the river kept its note of water over water, a fifth low thing never counted among the hearings; through the floor the carried fire gave four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have been, its payer without a name for a hundred and ninety-two days; overhead the boards kept four nails, four, and a knot the width of a thumb. Three counts in three places, and none of them rose up and became the others, because a man who wanted them to agree would have to put a payer under them and no payer stood to any of them. Nothing had been joined, named, opened, called, spent or ended. One hundred and ninety-two stood exact, complete and unspent in the dark of the wool, spoken by nobody under any roof, and the pale line lay on its fifth place across nothing. His hand outside the wool went up an inch toward the note of water over water through the finger's-width door and came down against the fold, as it had every night of his life, and the count went forward.
