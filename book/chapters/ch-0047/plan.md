# Chapter 47 — beat sheet: "Where the Figure Went"

**Days 192–194, Ember 13–15, Y.H. 813, Thirdday to Fifthday.** A **normal three-day chapter**, three dated `\daymark` headings, two scenes per day, every day lived in full, none skipped, poured into another or summarised. Chapter 46 closed on Day 191, Ember 12, Secondday, in the small hours; that day is done and is not revisited. The binding calendar rule is *t* = 336 + *N* − 1: Day 192 = Ember 13, Thirdday; Day 193 = Ember 14, Fourthday; Day 194 = Ember 15, Fifthday. All three weekdays are checked against `bible/calendar.md`. The district keeps calling Tallow the firing month and Ember the kiln month and neither is corrected in narration.

**The chapter's one meaningful change.** In the small hours of Day 194, the boy's hand outside the wool goes up an inch toward the note of water over water and, for the first time, does not come down at once: it stays up for the length of one of the breaths Sella counts at her fire in the room that does not touch his, then comes down against the fold. It is a change held and unspoken, filed on a place of its own beside the others and never laid across them — not a hearing, not a seventh, not a stopping, not a payment, not one of the three held changes, with no payer named and no doctrine allowed to stand up out of it. He still cannot close the two inches to the pot handle, lift his chin to the crust or speak a number; the body humiliates him and moves by an inch at the same time. **T071/T072 and T061/T082 advanced, not paid.** The chapter is otherwise the quiet after the reading: the account has gone away up the road in Berr's locked box and nothing in these three days changes hands.

## Scene 1 — "The Figure Gone Up the Road"

**Day 192, Ember 13, Thirdday, before first grey through the morning.** Place: under the Vail kiln-house roof (the bed, the hearth, the loaf shelf's low angle, the kiln mouth), then out into the Clay Yard (the trough at the yard well, the drying racks, the lowest rack, the wall where the shallow pan stood, the place in the returning line, the place the carriers crossed) and a step toward the clay road. Present: Corin/Dan (carried), Sella Vail; Tarn and Jorra present only as the household's ordinary morning requires, given no named dialogue here. Neighbours are walk-ons with no dialogue and no sheet.

**Beats, in order.**
1. Open with the drop capital — `\dropcap{T}{he ...}` — on the hand outside the wool going up an inch toward the note of water over water through the finger's-width door and coming down against the fold, with the count at one hundred and ninety-one exact, complete and unspent carried into the new day. **No recap of the reading.** The boy is one hundred and ninety-two days old and six months and twelve days and nobody under any roof says so (T071/T072).
2. First smell hot clay under tallow and wet wool with the sour mineral breath of the unswept corner; first sound the kiln drawing breath in fours with a gap, **not a bell** (T061/T082). The carried fire under the floor answers four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have been, its payer unnamed for a hundred and ninety-two days (T088). The river keeps its note of water over water, a fifth low thing never counted among the hearings; overhead the boards keep four nails, four, and a knot the width of a thumb — three counts in three places that do not become one.
3. The body's humiliation stated exactly: the crust gone leathery two inches from his ear with its empty place unnamed and no third, the thread of drool frozen and unwiped, cold stopped at cheek, temple and the small bare joint at the wrist where the wool rides back, the afterdrop waiting on the far side of a warming nobody in this house will perform. He wants the crust, the breast, the pot handle and a number somebody would have known what a number counted, and gets none; he has stopped expecting to be told, which is not the same as not wanting it.
4. Sella's morning grammar (T058/T079): instruction to the wool, the fire, the pot, the broom and the running water and never to the basket; *the house* where another woman says *I*; half-pace; one glance at the clay jar cold under its glaze without a touch; coal-black hair pushed back with the burn-wrist so the grey streak shows pale off the coals; pot rim set out with the handle two inches beyond the reaching hand and her hand resting a half-beat longer at the setting; *The house will keep it* told to the carried fire.
5. The widow's mark hangs where it hung, touched once before the kiln on Ember 1 and at no shelf, well, rack, road, basket, yard, afternoon or dusk since, because a quiet morning after a reading is not an opening and nobody praised what it mended and nobody saw it broken (T058/T079 held).
6. A neighbour asking what the firing will want now the account has gone up the road is sent to the pot and the weather with *Ask the pot what a wet thing costs set down there. The house has said it*; the question goes away level, nothing priced, refused or entered (T077). The printed figure still says one in twelve and the true subtraction sits in Berr's box up the road; Hessa's slate is delivered unread at Greybank and the clerk is still set at the landing — held, named only as what has gone away, and not brought on stage (T015/T041/T047, T080, T090 held).
7. The yard goes around its places with a look and a wide step and no number: the clean dark line where the Ash turned the corner and the finer grey called the thaw beside it, neither swept; the space beneath the lowest rack with its broken birth-slates entered by nobody; the pan's gone round ring with no scrape, crumb, print or chip; the trough bare and dark whose water is called weather; the empty place in the returning line where a hand went in on Seed 15 and did not come out; and the place the carriers crossed with the glaze-room hand between them (T009/T013/T092/T098 carried).

**Emotional turn.** From the narrow humiliating warmth of the bed into the broad, ordinary work of the yard: the public arithmetic has gone away and left the household with the same objects and the same unnamed spaces. The turn is not relief but attention — the adult's register noting that the yard's arithmetic is still carried in glances and wide steps, while the infant's body wants the breast and the crust.

**Sensory anchors.** Hot clay under tallow; wet wool under that; sour mineral Ash breath; the kiln's four-and-a-gap under the floor; water over water through the finger's-width door; the four nails and the knot; the burn-wrist pushing back coal-black hair; dry scurf of mud on a neighbour's hem; the bare dark trough; woodsmoke and river wind in the yard.

**Threads advanced.** T071/T072 (192 exact, six months and twelve days, told by nobody); T088 (payer unnamed for 192 days); T061/T082 (three counts in three places, no seventh); T058/T079 (instruction, *the house*, half-pace, jar-glance without touch, half-beat at setting, mark untouched); T077 (cost asked as a direction, answered with pot and weather); T009/T013/T092/T098 (unswept lines, rack space, pan's gone ring, field's empty place, carriers' place gone around).

**Foreshadowing planted / paid off.** Held from Chapter 46 and not paid: Berr's locked box and unchallenged one in twelve, Hessa's unread slate, the clerk's uncalled measure, the mark waiting on an opening. Planted toward Scene 4: the hand going up toward the note and coming down as it always has, so that its hesitation later can be read as a fraction and not a healed wound.

**Exact last image.** The boy at the low angle under the loaf shelf, his hand going up an inch toward the river's note and coming down against the fold while Sella, half a pace from an open slate, sends another cost-question to the pot and the weather and the yard goes wide around the place the carriers crossed without a number.

## Scene 2 — "Six Hearings and a Fifth Place"

**Day 192, Ember 13, Thirdday, afternoon into the small hours.** Place: the Vail kiln-house (the loaf shelf and its low angle, the hearth, the kindling, the bed), the wheel-shed edge, the yard well and the clay road at the going of the light. Present: Sella Vail, Tarn, Jorra Vail, Corin/Dan; unnamed men before first grey (never entered by the narration); the man on the wrong wind (walk-on, no name).

**Beats, in order.**
1. The afternoon thins without ending, losing its edges the way water goes off a stone that has held the sun, heat going first out of the high air and staying longest in the low places under the shelf, behind the pot, in the fold of wool where the boy lies.
2. Sella at the loaf shelf with her back to the basket moves her lips along a row standing with two unnamed gaps, the old space at the row's end and the palm's breadth that came in on the night of Seed 6, gives nobody a figure, glances once at the clay jar without touching it, and keeps her finger from beneath the front edge, so the charcoal tally stays unread, uncounted, uncopied, and the Day 166 held beat stays where it was left — an afternoon and a woman's back turned not the same as that night (T100).
3. She tells the fire it is the thaw, turns the coals with the back of a broken pot, says *The house will keep it*, stands with the back of the pot in her hand counting under her breath toward no opening — the small intake at the top, the hold, the letting go — sets the pot rim out with the handle two inches beyond the reaching hand and does not look toward the bed (T058/T079).
4. At the kindling the small knife lies at the edge of its sill with six counted where the house can check and the seventh at the lip where it cannot, Garl's unforged collector sentence beside it; Tarn takes the broom only after it has been given its instruction, tells the wood *Six counted. The next I don't count*, says *Yes, house* to an early thaw and a late while wanting the late because it puts off the paying, and sweeps the collector's road without turning; no collector comes (T036/T076).
5. His chest is tight since the dark; the breath from Seed 16 stands unspent at the top of every reach with the small interval where a breath should have gone in and had not; the ear-sentence comes to the threshold of the word that would call the loan and stops there with no figure and no name, drawn and not spent; the nine stoppings stay nine objects with no tenth before the seventh year is earned and the loan remains uncalled (T036/T076). His stroke goes over the flagstones and the shallow bright patch without the half-check and the stones keep the place where it stopped.
6. In the wheel-shed Jorra gives the men unnamed before first grey no name, comes through with grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bent finger and under the nails, white in the creases where a fist opened, not bone-coloured slip; keeps dusty forearms and clean ones on one tray, asks neither which slate was broken nor whether it was a birth-slate; tells the wheel it does not care whose hands, tells a pot *You'll do*, knows its note three turns ahead and withholds it from a man on a wrong wind who says a pot singing before its time generally sings itself out early — the knowing goes to the near-full tray, advanced and not paid, and no pot is lost (T035/T031, T061/T082). When Sella's sentence goes low and unfinished his bent finger stands in the cold damper until the end reaches the pot, then moves the tongue a hair's breadth and is left standing: a **remembered** holding, not a new change, not paired with Day 161, Day 166 or Day 171 (T062). The two shut-room tellings stay two with no third (T065/T095). *He's not Vail* stays sealed and the promise of the boy's name to the slate-burners stays ungiven.
7. The sweet edge of lead glaze comes up off \place{High Kiln} on a wrong wind and is answered as a direction and goes out on the same wind; the pot that sang itself out early stands swept around and is not made an omen or a lesson; no name is given for what ailed the man carried out and no collector is called (T064 held, T061/T082).
8. The stone lies back in its unmarked clay pot in the Debt Loft, two years gone and cold, unreconciled with the debt by not trying, which is not forgiving; from below the boy keeps only the remembering of the small dry scrape of a pot's foot on stone, filed on a place of its own and not a holding made again (T006).
9. At the going of the light Sella carries him home up the clay road, woodsmoke before her in long low bars and the river wind behind with the taste of water over water, going wide around the pan's gone place, the bare trough, the rack space, the field's empty place and the place the carriers crossed, each with a look and a wide step and no number. Under the roof she sets him on his bed and not in the Debt Loft, puts the fold right without looking at his face, leaves the thread frozen, lets her hand rest a half-beat longer at the setting, tells the fire it is the thaw, banks the coals and does not touch the mark; the two rooms do not touch (T058/T079, T009/T013/T092/T098).
10. In the small hours he turns out the file as a man lays instruments on a towel: six hearings beside and never among with the sixth out of a person and no seventh; three near-full answers advanced and unpaid; nine stoppings as objects with no tenth; three held changes on three places, the loft-sill scrape on a fourth and the carried man's pale line on a fifth, exact, complete and worth nothing, not joined to the sweet wind, the dust, the pot that sang early, the sixth green, the foundling's sliver, the pan's ring or any absence (T060/T086/T097/T100/T006/T064). He wants the crust, the breast, the pot handle and a number nobody will speak, and gets none.

**Emotional turn.** The boy registers that the reading has changed the district's arithmetic and changed nothing in this room: the same file comes back exact and worthless, and the one cost the square could not know is still the only thing he is holding for the man who was carried out.

**Sensory anchors.** Water going off a sun-warmed stone; the heat staying under the shelf and behind the pot; the knife at the edge of the sill and the worn thumb-mark; Ash in the knuckle creases and the pale clip-ring holding light; grey slate dust ground small as meal; cold iron with two bites and no Grathic word for both; the sweet edge on a wrong wind; the small dry scrape remembered from a room he cannot enter; woodsmoke and river wind on the stair of the evening.

**Threads advanced.** T061/T082 (six hearings, no seventh, three near-full answers, three counts); T058/T079 (shelf, fire, fold, half-beat, mark untouched, two rooms); T036/T076 (knife, seven held, breath unspent, ear-sentence short, nine with no tenth, unchecking stroke); T035/T031/T062/T065/T095 (men unnamed, dust, withheld note, remembered damper holding, tellings at two); T006 (stone cold, remembering filed as remembering); T100 (charcoal unread); T064 (pale line on a fifth place); T009/T013/T092/T098 (wide turns); T071/T072; T088.

**Foreshadowing planted / paid off.** Held, not paid: the box, the unread slate, the clerk's measure, the green, the foundling, the mark, the charcoal, the stone, the loan. Planted toward Scene 4: Tarn's held breath at the top of every reach, read against the boy's hand going up and coming down.

**Exact last image.** One hundred and ninety-two standing exact, complete and unspent in the dark of the wool, the pale line held on a fifth place across nothing, and the boy's hand going up an inch toward the note of water over water and coming down against the fold as it has every night of his life.

## Scene 4 — "A Fraction at the Reach"

**Emotional turn.** The household's two held silences and the boy's own small hesitation are each filed on their own places and not allowed to stand up together. The turn is from watching adults hold their breath to finding his own body holding something back — still worth nothing, still exact, and still the only thing in the room nobody can enter.

**Sensory anchors.** Sun-heat going off a stone; the shelf's low roof; the worn thumb-mark and the knife at the lip of the sill; Ash-grey knuckles and the pale clip-ring; cold iron with two bites; the river's fifth low note; the pot handle two inches beyond the hand; the frozen thread of drool.

7. Three or four men unnamed go low into the back room before first grey and get no name; the two shut-room tellings stay two with no third (T035/T031/T065/T095). Jorra's dusty forearms and clean ones stay on one tray, the full note known three turns ahead and withheld from a man on the wrong wind, the knowing advanced and not paid, *he's not Vail* sealed and the promise to the slate-burners ungiven (T035/T031).
8. Tarn's knife stays at the edge of its sill with six where the house can check and the seventh where it cannot, the Seed 16 breath unspent, the ear-sentence short of the word that would call the loan and the nine still nine (T036/T076). The charcoal waits unread under the shelf (T100); the stone lies cold and unreconciled in its loft pot (T006); the crust stays leathery two inches from his ear with its empty place unnamed and no third (T060/T086).

**Emotional turn.** The third quiet morning is the fullest yet: the yard has learned to carry every absence with a look and a wide step, and the boy has learned to read the space a hand does not go into as carefully as the space it does. The turn is from the yard's endless accounting-by-avoidance back to the one space inside his own reach that a hand might enter — the inch toward the river's note.

**Sensory anchors.** Dry kiln heat on wet slip; the sour corner; barley and the spoon on a sleeve; the widow's mark on its cord; the pan's gone round ring in the dust; the trough's dark water; grey dust on a wheel-keeper's forearms; the knife at the edge of the sill; the pot handle two inches beyond the hand.

## Scene 6 — "One Breath Held at the Reach"

**Day 194, Ember 15, Fifthday, afternoon into the small hours.** Place: the Vail kiln-house (the loaf shelf's low angle, the hearth, the kindling, the bed) and the clay road at the going of the light. Present: Sella Vail, Tarn, Jorra Vail, Corin/Dan; unnamed men before first grey (never entered by the narration).

**Beats, in order.**
1. The afternoon thins without ending; heat goes first out of the high air and stays longest under the shelf, behind the pot, in the fold of wool where the boy lies, and the afternoon comes down the shelf's roof in a slow slant and stops at the edge of his reach.
2. Sella stands with her back to the basket, moves her lips along the row with two unnamed gaps and gives nobody a figure, glances once at the clay jar without touching it, keeps her finger from beneath the front edge so the charcoal stays unread, tells the fire it is the thaw, turns the coals with the back of a broken pot, says *The house will keep it* where another woman would say *I*, stands with the back of the pot in her hand counting under her breath toward no opening — the small intake at the top, the hold, the letting go — pushes coal-black hair back with the burn-wrist so the grey streak shows pale off the coals, sets the pot rim out on the board with its handle standing two inches beyond the reaching hand and does not look toward the bed (T058/T079, T100).
3. At the kindling the knife lies at the edge of its sill with six counted in the worn thumb-mark where the house can check and the seventh at the lip where it cannot, Garl's unforged collector sentence beside it; Tarn takes the broom only after it is instructed, tells the wood *Six counted. The next I don't count*, brings the ear-sentence to the threshold of the word that would call the loan and stops there with no figure and no name, draws the breath and does not spend it, says *Yes, house* to the fire and goes on; the nine stoppings stand as objects with no tenth before the seventh year is earned, the loan uncalled, his stroke over the flagstones and the shallow bright patch without the half-check (T036/T076).
4. In the wheel-shed Jorra gives the men no name, keeps dusty and clean forearms on one tray, tells the wheel it does not care whose hands, tells a pot *You'll do*, withholds its note from the wrong wind, and holds his bent finger in the cold damper until Sella's unfinished sentence reaches the pot as a remembered repetition, not a new change (T035/T031/T062). The damper stays one iron with two bites and no word for both, joined to no kiln (T062/T035).
5. The stone lies back in its unmarked pot in the loft, two years gone and cold, unreconciled by not trying, which is not forgiving; from below the boy keeps only the remembering of the dry scrape on a place of its own, not joined to the three held changes and not made a cause (T006).
6. At the going of the light she carries him home up the clay road, woodsmoke going before her in long low bars out of the chimneys and the river wind coming up behind with the taste of water over water, the two passing on the stair of the evening as they have passed every evening of his life without taking each other's colour or spending a word on either; the instruction goes to the wool and the river wind and never to the basket, and the arm that has carried him a hundred and ninety-four mornings keeps the height of the handle, the give of the shoulder and the turn of the wrist at a step's end, a record in muscle that remembers weight and not cost (T058/T079).
7. She goes wide around the pan's gone place, the bare trough, the rack space with its broken birth-slates, the field's empty place and the place the carriers crossed, each with a look and a wide step and no number; under the roof she sets him on his bed and not in the Debt Loft, base first and the weight settled the way a woman sets a vessel that must not rock, puts the fold right at his ear and under his chin without once looking at his face, leaves the thread of drool frozen and unwiped, lets her hand rest a half-beat longer at the setting than the setting needed, tells the fire it is the thaw, banks the coals with the back of a broken pot and does not touch the mark, because a dusk is not an opening and there has been no opening since before the kiln on Ember 1. The two rooms do not touch: hers holds a pot, a fire and a woman counting under her breath toward nothing entered, and his holds a crust two inches from an ear and an empty place beside it with no name.
8. **The chapter's one meaningful change.** In the small hours he turns out the file as a man lays instruments on a towel and finds it exact, complete and worthless — six hearings beside and never among with the sixth from a person and no seventh, three near-full answers advanced and unpaid, nine stoppings as objects with no tenth, three held changes on three places with the loft scrape on a fourth and the carried man's pale line on a fifth, the told bell beside the heard and the told figure beside the paid, the crust's gap, the shelf's two gaps, the pan's ring and the field's empty place on trays that do not touch, no third gap, no fourth likeness made a cause and no finger of his drawing the line. Then his hand outside the wool goes up an inch toward the note of water over water through the finger's-width door and **does not come down at once**: it stays there for the length of one of the breaths Sella counts at her fire in the room that does not touch this one — a small intake at the top, a hold, a letting go — and only then comes down against the fold. It is a change held and unspoken, filed on a place of its own beside the others and never laid across them, not a hearing, not a seventh, not a stopping, not a payment, not one of the three held changes, with no payer named and no doctrine allowed to stand up out of it. He still cannot close the two inches to the pot handle, lift his chin to the crust or speak a number; the body humiliates him and moves by an inch at the same time (T071/T072, T061/T082).
9. Close on the held inventory: one hundred and ninety-four stands exact, complete and unspent in the dark of the wool, spoken by nobody under any roof; cold has stopped at cheek, temple and the small bare joint at the wrist where the wool rides back, and the afterdrop waits on the far side of a warming nobody in this house will perform. The nine stoppings are still nine with no tenth, the Seed 16 breath is still held, the charcoal still waits under the shelf for a hand that is not hers and a day that has not come, the mark was touched once before the kiln and at no other hour, and the cost of the man carried out stands entered nowhere. Through the finger's-width door the river keeps its note of water over water, a fifth low thing never counted among the hearings; through the floor the carried fire gives four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have been; overhead the boards keep four nails, four, and a knot the width of a thumb — three counts in three places, and none of them rises up and becomes the others. Nothing has been joined, named, opened, called, spent or ended.

**Emotional turn.** The boy has spent three days watching other people hold their breath at thresholds they will not cross, and at the close his own body holds something back for the length of one counted breath and lets it go without a word. The turn is from the file being exact and worthless to the file having gained one small physical object that it refuses to promote into a rule — the first honest inch of reaching, still worth nothing and still forward.

**Sensory anchors.** The shelf's low roof and the slant of thinning light; the burn-wrist at the fire and the grey streak pale off the coals; the knife at the lip of the sill; the damper's two bites; the loft remembered only as a dry scrape; woodsmoke before and river wind behind on the stair of the evening; the half-beat at setting; the two rooms that do not touch; the three counts of river, fire and boards.

**Threads advanced.** T071/T072 and T061/T082 (the held inch, another exact day); T058/T079 (fire, fold, half-beat, carry home, mark untouched, two rooms); T036/T076 (knife, seven held, ear-sentence short, nine with no tenth, unchecking stroke); T035/T031/T062 (men unnamed, withheld note, remembered damper holding, two bites of iron); T006 (stone cold and unreconciled, remembering filed as remembering); T100 (charcoal unread); T060/T086/T097 (gaps and crust on separate trays, no third gap, no fourth likeness); T064 (pale line on a fifth place); T009/T013/T092/T098 (wide turns); T088 (payer unnamed for 194 days).

**Foreshadowing planted / paid off.** Paid off in kind: the hand going up and coming down in Scene 1, hesitating in Scene 4, held for one counted breath here; the remembered damper holding; the ninth stopping held again. Planted and held for later chapters: the hand's inch toward the water is a physical first, not a power, not a hearing and not a key — it will be recognized only long after a hand could close the two inches to a pot handle. Still held and unpaid: the box and its true subtraction, the unread slate at Greybank, the clerk's uncalled measure, the green as a colour, the foundling as a shape with no seventh sighting, the charcoal, the stone, the mark, the loan, the Seed 16 breath, the ninth stopping with no tenth, the broken birth-slates, the pan's ring, the two shelf gaps, the crust's empty place, the field's empty place and the missing hand's place.

**Exact last image.** One hundred and ninety-four stands exact, complete and unspent in the dark of the wool; his hand outside the wool goes up an inch toward the note of water over water through the finger's-width door, stays there for the length of one breath counted at a fire in a room that does not touch this one, and comes down against the fold; the count goes forward into Day 195, Ember 16.

## How the final moment pulls into Chapter 48

The held inch is a beginning and not a breakthrough: the boy can lift a hand toward a sound and hold it there, but he still cannot close two inches on a pot handle, lift his chin to the crust or speak a number, and nothing in the file has been promoted, paid or joined. Chapter 48 opens on Day 195, Ember 16, Sixthday, in the kiln month's third working week, with the count at one hundred and ninety-five, the fire's fifth still missing with no payer, the nine stoppings still nine, the charcoal unread, the mark untouched, the stone cold and the loan uncalled. The hand that stayed up for one breath will be asked to stay up again — and the saga's whole method is what happens when an inch held twice starts to look like a reaching. Forward, not closed.
