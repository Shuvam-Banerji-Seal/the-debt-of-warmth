## Chronicle
Frost went out as it had come in, without a boundary drawn. Its three mornings came each on its own, with no market, no reading at the Cross and no bell, taking the house one board at a time. Nobody under any roof told the boy that his days had come to two hundred and ninety-six. Under the floor the carried fire kept its fifth unpaid the same number of days with its payer unnamed.

He took the body: a stiff brown-edged crust two inches from his ear, its empty place unnamed and no third to even the row; the thread frozen in the dry heat and unwiped; cold stopping at cheek, temple and the bare wrist-joint, with the afterdrop waiting past a warming nobody would perform. He wanted the crust lifted and could not close two inches on the pot handle. Both were true.

Sella Vail went to the fire before the basket, told the coals it was the thaw, turned them with the back of a broken pot and said The house will keep it where another woman would have said I. The grain turned beneath the wool with a lighter touch each time and named no cost; the pot rim stood two inches beyond the reaching hand. She did not touch the widow's mark, for a morning is not an opening. The yard went wide around its seven places.

On the twenty-sixth Ilva drew at the yard well, clay pale in her nails. Asked what the month's firings had taken, her hand went toward her sleeve and stayed there; the notched birch strip was not taken out, read or joined to the open slate lying near. Ask the pot what it cost. I keep the count, not the account. The boy laid the order of the reaching --- hand to record, eyes to asker, mouth after both --- on the ninth hand's own place; he filed no tenth.

Tarn at the kindling kept six where the house could check and a seventh at the lip, stopped the ear-sentence at the word that would call the loan as a remembered ninth stopping with no tenth; the loan stayed uncalled and no collector came on the clay road. Jorra Vail spoke to the wheel before any person, put his bent finger into the cold damper and withheld the note he knew three turns ahead; he's not Vail stayed sealed. Garl stayed in objects, his name unspoken two hundred and ninety-six days.

The file lay out on the towel at the close: six hearings with the seventh place empty, the told figure beside the paid, nine hands and nine stoppings with no tenth. The hand filed the hundred and sixty-third through the hundred and sixty-eighth answers, each worth nothing at two inches of air. Nothing was opened, joined, named, called, paid or ended. The river's low note went quiet under the door, and the plate of ice stood clouded with silt.
