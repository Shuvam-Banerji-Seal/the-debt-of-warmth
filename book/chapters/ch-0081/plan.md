# Chapter 81 — beat sheet: *The Last Tend of Frost*

**Days:** 294–296 (Frost 25–27, Fifthday to Seventhday, Y.H. 813). **Mode:** normal, six scenes, two per day, every day opened by its own dated `\daymark` in the first scene of that day and lived for well over eight hundred words. **Opening state:** the hand laid back against the fold on the **one hundred and sixty-second** answer, count at **293 exact** (six months and one hundred and thirteen days told by nobody), carried fire's fifth unpaid 293 days with its payer unnamed, file open on the towel with the held and empty intervals parted by a finger and nothing moved. **Closing state:** **one hundred and sixty-eight** answers on the sixth place, count **296 exact** (six months and one hundred and sixteen days told by nobody), fire's fifth unpaid 296 days; nothing opened, joined, named, called, paid or ended. Frost 30 (Day 299), third and last Account-day, stands **three mornings** ahead at the close and is not brought in.

**Whole-chapter throughline:** Frost's last working tend goes out as it came in, without a boundary drawn. The one movement, advanced and **not paid**, is Ilva's ninth record-first hand seen once more at the yard well on Frost 26 with the notched birch strip unread in her sleeve and no figure coming off the stick — held for the far-off hand that will lay the district's private keepings together (T080). No new thread, no payoff, no promotion of likeness into rule, witness, gift or doctrine.

## Pull into Chapter 82

The chapter ends with the hand laid down on the **one hundred and sixty-eighth** answer and the count at **296 exact**. Chapter 82 opens on Day 297, Frost 28, Eighthday, in the small hours under the same roof with that hand still on the fold, the file still open with every gap left gap, the fire's fifth unpaid 297 days, and Frost 30 (Day 299) standing two mornings ahead. The third reading waits in Berr Clay's locked box with the printed one in twelve unchallenged and the figure read still not the figure paid; the covered grain at the rack end will be read over by a number that does not know its cost. Nothing this chapter saw or held — not Ilva's strip, not the three shapings, not the stayed frost, not the ninth stopping — may go into Frost 28 or 29 as a sign, lesson, mending, cause or payment; each must reach the third reading unchanged, so that when Berr reads the figure a third time the district's arithmetic changes once more and nothing under the Vail roof is settled by it.
