The afternoon thinned without ending. It went the way heat goes out of a pot lifted off the coals and set down on a cold stone --- not in one giving-up, and not with any word said over it, but by small degrees a hand could follow on the outside of the pot longer than an eye could follow it on the stone. Heat drew back from the fold. The light through the finger's-width door fell in one narrow bar and did not move, and the pot rim stood where she had set it two inches beyond the reaching hand with the handle turned toward it, and the half-beat had gone out of the room with her.

This was the last tend of Frost, and it went out as it had come in, with no boundary drawn under it and no line set beside it. The older women of the district named their months by the weather and the slatemen named theirs by the number of Account-days, and neither habit was set right or left wrong under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof; a saying in a kitchen is not a correction made in a counting room, and nobody in \place{Low Kiln} had asked either to be. Frost's first arithmetic and Frost's second rode locked up \place{the clay road} in \person[Berr Clay]'s box toward \place{High Kiln}, with the printed one in twelve unchallenged and the figure read still not the figure paid; no lesson came out of the box and no payer stood under either arithmetic. Frost 30 (Day 299), the month's third and last Account-day and market, stood three mornings ahead and was not brought into a day that had not earned it and was not made a sign of anything.

\person[Corin]{The boy} lay on \place{the bed} at the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf}, and the inventory renewed itself in the order the facts arrived, because that order had never lied to him and no other had promised to. One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from his ear, gone past the point where a child would eat it, and the empty place beside it kept its name to itself; no third had come in the afternoon to make the row come out even. The thread of drool at his mouth had frozen in the dry heat and was unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. Cold had come to cheek and temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, and stopped there as at the end of a line written out for it, with neither an order to go forward nor an order to go away. The afterdrop waited beyond all three, past a warming nobody in this house would perform. Under the sour mineral breath of the unswept corner called \lore{the thaw} he knew what the ease of cold means: a cold patient cannot clot, and will seem better before he is worse, and the ease a man sees on a face at that stage is not ease. A man must be warmed from the outside and slowly, never hurried, never rubbed. He wanted the crust lifted the two inches to his mouth. Both were true, and in a body six months and one hundred and sixteen days old the second was louder; the surgeon took the facts in their old order --- crust, gap, thread, cold, the waiting --- and entered none of them, not even on the place where he kept the things he did not enter. The knowing bought him nothing at two inches of air. He had once reduced a shoulder in the dark by feel alone and now could not close two inches on the pot handle set beyond his reach; the thought came and went without bitterness, which was its own small betrayal.

Two men lay under the roof and only one of them had a body that could be warmed. The older kept the inventory, the younger kept the wanting, and the older's exactness had no authority over the younger's cold. He had once signed a chart with a note turned down in the margin and had not read the note; in this country everybody turned notes face-down toward floorboards, and he read them all and entered none. The thread had gone stiff as a wire drawn through tallow. He could not lift a hand to wipe it, and no instruction had ever been given to anybody to wipe it, and the two facts stood side by side without either of them becoming a complaint.

In the afternoon \person{Sella Vail} carried him down at the half-pace she keeps from every open slate, out of the Vail kiln-house and into \place{the Clay Yard}, as a woman carries a pot filled that must not be slopped. She gave the instruction to the bucket and the broom and the running water and never to the basket, and said \emph{the house} where another woman would have said \emph{I}. The yard went about its last work of the month without making anything of the month. Women passed with buckets wanting the thaw early and women passed with buckets wanting it late, and the yard went around the places it goes around with a look and a wide step and no number, the way a foot goes around a sleeping animal.

At the Vail corner the clean dark line where an Ash ridge had once turned lay beside the finer grey hairline the house calls the thaw, and neither had been swept. Beneath \place{the lowest rack} the broken birth-slates lay where the yard had put them and entered them into nothing. On the stone lip of \place{the yard well} was the round ring where the shallow pan had stood, with no scrape, no crumb, no print, no chip and no word to say what had been set there. \place{the trough} beside it had a skin over its water from edge to edge. Out on \place{the worked strips} the field kept an empty place the planting had not filled; and in the returning line, where a hand had gone in on Seed 15 and had not come out, the yard went wide and did not look twice. Seven places were seven and not an account; the missing hand was carried and not named, not paid and not joined to the one crust in the fold, or to the dressed grain under wool, or to the man carried out on Day 188. A place left alone is kept, and kept is not paid or explained.

At the well the white fronds still stood on the north lip, stiff where the bucket had not knocked them; the rope gave its dry note; the water in the trough was skinned edge to edge; and on \place{the flat stone by the well}, set out of the path of feet, the lifted plate of ice stood clouded with silt. Three coverings in three places, and the boy kept them with a finger between every pair. He made no fourth likeness of them and no cause; a cause wants a payer standing under it and nobody stood. The stayed frost, the skinned water and the plate of ice were weather, entered nowhere, and no figure came off any of them. Frost would go when it goes, as the house had told the fire, and telling a thing when it goes is not entering it.

At \place{the rack end}, on the cool stone away from \place{the hearth}, the dressed grain lay under wool. She lifted one corner to feel for damp and laid it back with a touch lighter than the one before, and turned it once more beneath the wool, and gave no figure to it, so that the reading still ahead would pass over a thing whose cost it did not know. The wool went down again over the grain the way a fold goes down over a face that is not to be looked at. No number came off the grain and no cost was named to it.

\scenebreak

\person{Tarn} was at \place{the kindling} with the small knife at the very edge of its sill. Six stood in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check and the seventh stood at the lip where it could not; \person{Garl Vail}'s unforged collector sentence lay beside it, with no name on the man who had turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on the debt. He told the wood \emph{Six counted. The next I don't count} in the tone of a man stating the weather, and to a woman who wanted the thaw early and another who wanted it late he said \emph{Yes, house} to each so thoroughly that no argument could find a foot, and went on wanting the late because it puts off the paying, though the paying stood in the ruts of the clay road. His chest had been tight since the dark. The sentence about an ear under this roof being worth more than the debt that held it came up to the word that would call the loan and stopped there with no figure and no name --- a remembered ninth stopping, and he did not make a tenth of it before the seventh year at its very end and still ungiven. He drew the breath that should have gone in at the top of the reach and did not spend it. \lore{Ash} was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line, and no scrubbing lifted it; the pale clip-ring at his left wrist, in place since Y.H. 806, held what light there was and gave it back. His stroke went on over \place{the flagstones} past the shallow bright patch without the half-check, the stones keeping the place where it had stopped. From above came a sound with a direction out of \place{the Debt Loft}, where the warm stone in its unmarked pot was cold and unreconciled by not trying, which is not forgiveness. He took the broom only after it had been given its instruction. The loan stayed uncalled; no collector came up the clay road and none went down it, and the boy kept the seventh where Tarn had set it and entered nothing, for a stopping kept whole is not a payment made early.

\person{Jorra Vail} came through the finger's-width-open door at the edge of \place{the Vail wheel-shed} with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal into both forearms past the bend of his little finger and under the nails, not bone-coloured \lore{slip}. He kept dusty forearms and clean ones on one tray and parted them as a surgeon parts clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had broken nor whether it was a birth-slate. He spoke to the wheel before he spoke to any person: \emph{It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady}. To a pot on the wheel he said \emph{You'll do} in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either. He knew the \lore{full note} that pot would give three turns before it left his hands, with air under it and a want in it, and withheld the knowing on the near-full tray beside the hearings and never among them, advanced and not paid. Men unnamed had gone low into \place{the back room} before first grey and he gave the house no name for them and did not say the room was cold; to the boy it was a direction and a sound, and no more a room than the loft was. When Sella's sentence came low and unfinished his bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper} ahead of the end of it, took the cold out of the fingertip, moved the tongue a hair's breadth and left it standing, so the end of her instruction arrived at the pot and the household straightened around his silence. There is no \lore{Grathic} word for the two bites of that one iron, the holding and the taking, and the boy set the iron on its own place joined to no kiln and entered in no slate; a clamp is a clamp. The burned-slate word stood at four from \place{Slate Row}, a direction over \place{the boards} and not a fifth telling; the two tellings of the counting room shut in the war stood at two and had not become three; three sayings about a pot stood together with the premature Smoke 14 note on its own place and no fourth likeness made a cause; the row question stood at five askings with one shaping and no witness. \emph{He's not Vail} stayed sealed as tightly as a lid nobody had tried to lift in three years, and the promise of the boy's name to the men who burn slates was neither given nor withdrawn.

Garl stayed in objects and not in a story. His salt-stiff coat with the crease at the left shoulder kept a broader man's shape on its peg behind the door, and the wheel-seat in the shed kept its twenty-year lean and its hollow on that side, and Sella let no one fill it. The clean dark line at the corner kept its finer grey beside it, unswept; the proverb had gone years ago to a pot and the fire and never to a person --- feed the fire before you feed yourself, leave the crack showing, people trust a thing they can see has been handled --- and the debt against a firing of tiles stood neither paid nor settled nor done with, and was worse for being uncalled. His name had not been spoken under this roof in two hundred and ninety-six days. No collector came up the clay road and none went down it; the keeping of him by \lore{not-doings} stayed on its own place, maintenance and not a story, not joined to any shaping that had come over the boards and not made to answer for a name a district had spent. The house went around his absence the way it went around the line on the floor, and there was no lesson in it for anybody who cared to learn one.

\scenebreak

At the going of the light Sella carried him up at the half-pace from every open slate and set him on the bed under the roof and never in the Debt Loft. She put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face, and left the thread where it lay. She told the fire it was the thaw and turned the coals with the back of a broken pot, and said \emph{The house will keep it} where another woman would have said \emph{I}. When she came to the loaf shelf her finger stayed on top of the front edge so the charcoal tally beneath was unread, and her lips moved along a row standing with two gaps and gave neither gap a figure; she glanced once at the clay jar standing cold under its \lore{lead glaze} and shut her hand half over nothing. She pushed coal-black hair back with the wrist that carries the burn, and the one grey streak at her temple showed pale off the coals, and stood a moment with the back of the pot in her hand counting something under her breath toward no opening. \lore{The widow's mark} hung where it hung on its cord at her throat, a thumb-length of lead-glazed clay with the Vail kiln scratched into it, and she did not touch it, because a going of the light is not an opening and the hand goes to the mark before the kiln and at no other hour. The next opening stands in a later month's first firing, far off, not hoped for and not counted toward; four openings in four months are four and not a rule. When she set the pot down on \place{the pot board} she set the rim two inches beyond the reaching hand with the handle turned toward it, and there was a half-beat between the setting and her going away, and she did not look toward the bed.

The boy watched the going of her burn-wrist the way he had learned to watch a surgeon's hand leave a field --- for what it did not touch --- and filed the not-touching on its own place, maintenance and not a message. The half-beat was not a gift and the handle turned toward the hand was not an instruction to take; a gift wants a taker and he was not given the means to close two inches of air. He wanted the thread wiped and the crust lifted and a number spoken by somebody who would have known what a number counted, and he got none of the three and could not ask for any of them. There was no bitterness in the not-asking either, which was the second small betrayal of the day, and he did not give it a place to stand.

\scenebreak

Under the floor \lore{the carried fire} kept its fifth unpaid two hundred and ninety-six days with its payer unnamed, and nobody lifted the floor and nobody supplied the pull. In the small hours he turned the file out on \place{the towel} at the head of the bed as a surgeon turns instruments out on a tray, each in its place, none of them a cure. Six hearings of the full note stood beside and never among, five from four sources and the sixth, the small flat knock out of \person[Dessa Silt] as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go and never with the things that came from vessels; the seventh place kept its emptiness and none was invented to fill it, for an empty place kept empty is a finding and a finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged. The heard bell stood beside the told with its four, four and a gap, and no bell had come on this day and none was lent an ear it had not earned. The told figure stood beside the paid with the space between them unsigned; Frost's two arithmetics were locked up the road and the printed one in twelve stood unchallenged, and two sayings were two sayings and not one account.

The three shapings of the Frost 14 saying stood on a place of their own beside \person{Hessa Rale}'s eighth record-first hand, with a finger between every pair. Not a witness, not a cause, not a confession, not a payment, and not joined to \person{Ilva}'s strip in a sleeve that was a pace off yesterday and is not under this roof tonight, nor to Berr's twice-wiped thumb, nor to the soft-cuffed clerk's inward slate at \place{the out-of-line landing}, nor to the charcoal beneath the shelf. A finding with no throat is not a witness however often it is said, and a shaping is not an opening of a slate nor a figure put in a runner's hand. Ilva's ninth hand stayed nine with the notched birch strip unread and no figure coming off the stick, and he filed no tenth hand; the count stayed in wood where no slate could take a figure off it. Nine stoppings stood with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end. The row question stood at five askings with one shaping and no witness; the burned-slate word at four, the shut-room tellings at two. The seventh green was a colour and the seventh shape was a shape, and no eighth came to either. The house's not-doings stayed on a place apart from his own gaps with a finger between them, for want of a payer under either. Nothing stood up and called itself a rule.

Outside the wool the hand went up an inch toward the next room. At the very end of the day a small intake came from the room whose floor does not touch his --- the breath of a woman counting what she will not say aloud --- and he took it at its top and let it go on the same note, whole and unjoined. He did not carry it into the carried fire's missing fifth, did not cross the room's letting-go with the river's low note of \lore{water over water}, did not borrow a house count, did not supply a pull under the floor. The hand came down on the river's note as it always did, and the gap stayed a gap. The hundred and sixty-eighth answer was filed exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air, promoted into no reach, no hearing, no seventh, no stopping, no payment, no held change, no key, no power, no freedom, no gift, no doctrine and no rule. The clean satisfaction the surgeon felt at the exact coordination was checked by the infant's plain want of the crust two inches off, and the checking was the work and he did not call it a virtue.

The hand laid itself back against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. One hundred and sixty-eight honest answers stood on the sixth place, each an object and not a power, exact, complete and worth nothing. Two hundred and ninety-six stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill} and \place{Grath}; six months and one hundred and sixteen days were told by nobody; the fire's fifth was unpaid the same number of days with its payer unnamed. Nothing was opened, joined, named, called, paid or ended; no thread was opened and none was closed. The file lay open on the towel with every gap left gap, every tray parted by a finger's width and nothing that had moved. The river's low note went quiet under the finger's-width door after the hand came down. The crust lay two inches off with its empty place unnamed, and out on the flat stone by the well, out of the path of feet, the plate of ice stood clouded with silt, and Frost went on without a boundary drawn.
