Three hundred and forty-three stood exact behind him, six months and one hundred and sixty-three days told by nobody in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill}, \place{Grath}. Under the floor of the house \lore{the carried fire} had given four low pulls that morning and kept the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer unnamed three hundred and forty-three days; on its peg the salt-stiff coat kept a man's shape the same number of days without a name spoken over it. Nothing from the fourteenth crossed the yard with them as sign, lesson, mending, cause or payment. The morning's answer had gone where answers go and had bought nothing at two inches of air, and the yard was not its echo.

The crust kept its two inches from his ear in the dry kiln cold, gone leathery and brown-edged, its corner standing a hair off the wool, and the empty place beside it keeping its name to itself; no third had come since the morning to make the row come out even. The thread at his mouth had frozen and would stay frozen, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread. Cold had come to cheek and temple and the small bare joint of his wrist where the wool rode back, and had stopped there as at the end of a line written out for it; \lore{the Afterdrop} waited past the stopping for a warming nobody in this house would perform. He wanted the crust lifted two inches, the thread wiped away, and a hand on the pot handle brought within reach. Both were true, the second was louder, and neither won. The surgeon's register took the yard in its old order while the infant underneath went on being cold, and the two quarrelled under the wool without a verdict; the argument was the work. He had once stood in a room that smelled of disinfectant and cold metal and decided which of twenty bodies would be opened first, and here he could not lift a barley crust to his own mouth. The joke cost him nothing and was not funny.

She went around the yard as the yard went around itself, with a look and a wide step and no number. At \place{the Vail corner} the clean dark line lay where it had lain and the finer grey hairline beside it that the district called \lore{the thaw} lay beside it unswept, and she went wide of both and swept neither. Beneath \place{the lowest rack} the broken birth-slates lay where they lay and she stepped around them, entering nothing; the round empty ring where the shallow clay pan had once stood kept its place with no scrape, no crumb, no print, no chip and no word, and a man stepping over it did not look down. At \place{the yard well} the \place{trough} kept its skin, and on \place{the flat stone by the well} the silt-clouded plate of ice stood out of the path of feet; out on \place{the worked strips} the field kept its empty place; and in the returning line, where a hand had gone in on Seed 15 and had not come out, she went around the place the way everybody went around it, with no name spoken. Seven places, and no eighth likeness and no cause across two of them and no payer under any. He entered none, because a thing entered is a thing that wants a payer and none stood.

The three coverings stayed weather and were entered nowhere. The frost that had written white on the north lip of the well in the dark month had gone off while the yard looked elsewhere, and it was not a sign now and had not been a sign then; the skin on the trough broke under the knock of a pail and closed again over what it had covered, and nobody lifted a finger to it; the silt-clouded plate stood where no foot went. A covering over a depth, the same covering over another depth, a piece of the depth lifted and set where no foot would go---three things on three places with a finger between every pair, called weather, and no fourth likeness made a cause. The figure in the box up the road had not entered any of them and was not made to.

The hunts and repairs of the afternoon stayed directions judged by the carrying arm and the weather, never figures. A man come down from \place{Cold Acre} shifted a hare across his shoulders at the well and, to a woman who asked what the carrying had cost, told her to ask the weather, which had carried the snow and could carry the price; he went off toward \place{the clay road} with the hare unweighed and nobody opening a slate. Another with a cracked jar in a sling of rope asked what the month's mending would want and was told to ask the pot, and the pot, being a pot, said nothing. A woman with a basket of kindling asked what the seventh year would want when it came, and two men mending a strip of fence said the thaw would be early and late. \person{Tarn}, bent at the shoulders from six years in the slip pit, with \place{Ash} worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line, said “Yes, house” to each of them, and kept the late because a late thaw put off the paying, agreeing so thoroughly that there was nowhere for an argument to find a foot. The boy read each as a chart left blank where the number should stand and entered none of the blanks; a cost-question answered with the weather was a direction arrived nowhere, and the arriving was the whole of it.

At \place{the rack end} the dressed grain lay turned under wool away from \place{the hearth}, and Sella lifted a corner with a touch lighter than she had used in the morning and turned it once and gave no figure. Her fingers went in and came out and did not close, so that no handful was lifted. The boy filed the empty fingers on a place of their own beside the one object that was the handful let fall on Bone 22---a keeping, not a second handful, not a sixth held change, not a rule, with no payer under it. Wool over turning grain, the dry rasp of it, the smell of the yard coming up through it: the small sounds and the small smells, taken first. Whatever figure the fourteenth might put upon a heap of grain would not know what the heap had cost, and the space between that figure and its price would have no signature set into it.

At \place{the kindling} Tarn kept his account in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check and in the lip beyond it where it could not: six standing, the seventh at the lip beside the unforged collector sentence that \person{Garl Vail} had left there with no name on the man who turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on the debt. The small knife lay at the very edge of its sill, where a man could check it and where a man's hand could also slip it off. “Six counted. The next I don't count,” he told the wood, in the tone of a man stating the weather, and cut nothing and counted nothing and called no loan.

Then he did a thing the house could not check.

He set the knife where it could be checked. He passed his hand over it the way a man passes a hand over a tool put back where it belongs, the fingers going flat over the blade and the wooden sill, the pale clip-ring at his left wrist catching what little light there was and giving it back. That much the house could have checked and would have found in order. But the hand did not go away when the checking was done. It stayed at the sill a breath longer than the check required---not a long breath, not a breath a woman at the hearth would have turned her head for, but a breath with a shape to it, held at the top of the reach where the other breath had stayed unspent since the dark. His chest had been tight since the dark, and the breath that should have gone in at the top of every reach stayed where it was, a small interval with nothing spent under it. Then he took his hand away. He cut nothing. He counted nothing. He did not bring the ear-sentence to the word that would call the loan, and the loan stayed uncalled; he did not spend the Seed 16 breath; and the resting of a thumb on the uncut seventh of Bone 26 he checked and found where it had been laid, and did not make again on the fourteenth of \place{Ash}---not a tenth stopping, not the seventh year given, not a payment, not a sixth held change, not a hearing, not a mending and not a rule, with no payer standing under it.

The boy had been looking at the kindling without appearing to look at it, as he had looked at a hundred knife-sills, taking the field by the small sounds first. He took this one apart as he took everything apart, by what was there and by what was not. What was there was a hand that stayed. What was not there was a cut, a count, a word that called the loan, a spent breath, a thumb laid down where a thumb had been laid before. The hand had gone up to check and come down from something else, and the something else had no name spoken over it and no payer standing under it. He had watched a surgeon draw a hand from a field before the dressing went on and known the difference between the hand that works and the hand that only rests; this was neither, and it was exact.

He filed the held breath at the sill on a place of its own. It went beside the staying at the inch of Bone 15, the early letting-go of Bone 17, the handful let fall at the rack end on Bone 22, Tarn's thumb's resting on the uncut seventh of Bone 26, the sixth opening at \place{the kiln mouth} on the second of \place{Ash}, and the pot-crack resting of the ninth, each on its own place with a finger between every pair. It was expressly not a sixth held object, not a seventh of anything, not a tenth stopping, not the seventh year given, not a habit, a way, a hearing, a near-full answer, a sign, a mending, a cause, a payment or a rule. It was not made again, and he did not join it to the charcoal beneath the shelf, to the notched strip kept in a sleeve, to the Ash 5 joining of the burned slate to the shut room, to the fourth shaping at the well, to the turning of a runner's writing, to the dressed grain under wool or to any of his own kept gaps. No payer stood under it and none was supplied. It gathered, quietly and without his consent, toward a far-off hand that would one day lay the district's private keepings together---a hand that was not his, and was still far off. He did not follow it there. A finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged.

To the woman with the kindling and to the two fence-menders Tarn said “Yes, house” to the early thaw and the late, and kept the late because it puts off the paying. At \place{the flagstones} his stroke went over the shallow bright patch without the half-check, and the returned half-check of Bone 8 stayed on its own place with the Day 161 fraction, the Day 166 shelf beat and the Frost 5 returned beat, four held changes on four places and not a rule. The warm loft stone lay cold in its unmarked pot above the shed, unreconciled by not trying, which is not forgiveness. Nothing further came of the hand at the sill; no second breath was held there, and the knife lay where a man could check it or slip it off.

\person[Jorra Vail]{Jorra} came through the finger's-width door off \place{the Vail wheel-shed} with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bent little finger and white in the creases where a fist had opened, and it was not the bone-coloured \lore{slip} of the wheel. It cares if they're steady.” To a pot he said, “You'll do,” in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either, and knew the \lore{full note} it would give three turns before it left his hands, with air under it and a want in it, and kept the knowing where nobody could make a song of it, withheld from a kiln-master come up from \place{High Kiln} who would call it early and make a song of it.

Garl stayed in objects and not in a story. In the small hours, with the river's note low and going lower under the finger's-width door, the hand outside the wool went up an inch toward the sound. It did not reach; the inch was not a reach and he had long ago stopped calling it one. It was the distance a hand may move in a field without disturbing the dressing, and he took it the way he had once taken a hand into a cavity, slowly, with the whole of the attention and the knowledge that most of what a hand does in a field is wait. The hand took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole, let the intake go on the note's own finishing, and came straight down and laid itself back against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. The note had done before the hand came down, so the letting-go was neither early again nor stayed, and the hand did not stay; it was the note's own finishing that ended the interval and not any decision of the hand, and that was the whole of the honesty in it. The intake was not a breath kept for warmth, the interval not a stopping of the river, the note not his. A man had held a breath at a knife-sill that afternoon a hand's breadth beyond the reach of this one, and the two intervals did not join; one had been spent into a sill and one into a sound, and neither had a payer standing under it.

He filed it as answer two hundred and sixty-two: exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment, borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the seven arithmetics, the bell's gap, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the stayed object, the early letting-go, the sixth opening, the pot-crack resting, the new breath at the sill, the Ash 5 joining, the fourth shaping, the turned writing, the notched wood nor his own kept gaps. Two hundred and sixty-two honest answers stood on the sixth place, each an object and not a power, and neither the number nor the small hand-movings that had made it stood up and called themselves a doctrine, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood.

The surgeon took his small clean satisfaction in a field left as found, and the infant underneath took none of it and went on wanting the crust lifted, the thread wiped and a hand on the pot handle, both true, the second louder, neither winning, the two registers quarrelling without verdict in the small hours.
