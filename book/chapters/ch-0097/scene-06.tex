The afternoon came on without a bell, as the fifteenth had come without a market or a Cross, and \person{Sella Vail} carried the basket down under the finger's-width door and took \person[Corin]{the boy} out into \place{the Clay Yard}. The instruction went to the wool and the river wind and never to the basket. “Keep it off his ears,” she told the wind, and set the fold right under his chin without a look at his face. She carried him level at the half-pace she kept from every open slate, the way a woman carries a pot filled to the brim and does not slop it, and the yard came up around him as a field comes up under the eye before the dressing goes on, by the small sounds first, because the small sounds do not lie and the large ones are mostly weather: the dry rasp of rope at the well, the settle of clay going on cooling behind a wall, the far small knock of a pot set down somewhere out of sight. Three hundred and forty-four stood exact behind him, six months and one hundred and sixty-four days told by nobody in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill}, \place{Grath}. Under the floor of the house \lore{the carried fire} had given four low pulls that morning and kept the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer unnamed three hundred and forty-four days; on its peg the salt-stiff coat kept the broader shape of \person[Garl Vail]{a man} whose name had not been spoken under that roof the same number. Nothing from the fourteenth crossed the yard with them as sign, lesson, mending, cause or payment. A man had held a breath at a knife-sill the day before where no house could check it, and the breath had stayed where it was put and bought no lesson; the answer of the morning had gone where answers go, and the yard was not its echo.

The crust kept its two inches from his ear in the dry kiln cold, gone leathery and brown-edged, its corner standing a hair off the wool, the empty place beside it keeping its name to itself; no third had come since the morning to make the row come out even. The thread at his mouth had frozen and would stay frozen, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread. Cold had come to cheek and temple and the small bare joint of his wrist and stopped there as at the end of a line written out for it; \lore{the Afterdrop} waited past the stopping for a warming nobody in this house would perform. He wanted the crust lifted the two inches to his mouth, the thread wiped away, and a hand that would close on the pot handle brought within reach. Both were true, the second was louder, and neither won; the surgeon's register took the yard in its old order while the infant underneath went on being cold, and the two quarrelled under the wool without a verdict. The argument was the work.

She went around the yard as the yard went around itself, with a look and a wide step and no number. At \place{the Vail corner} the clean dark line lay where it had lain and the finer grey hairline beside it that the district called \lore{the thaw} lay beside it unswept, and she went wide of both and swept neither. Beneath \place{the lowest rack} the broken birth-slates lay where they lay and she stepped around them, entering nothing; the round empty ring where the shallow clay pan had once stood kept its place with no scrape, no crumb, no print, no chip and no word, and a man stepping over it did not look down. At \place{the yard well} the \place{trough} kept its skin, and on \place{the flat stone by the well} the silt-clouded plate of ice stood out of the path of feet; out on \place{the worked strips} the field kept its empty place; and in the returning line, where a hand had gone in on Seed 15 and had not come out, she went around the place the way everybody went around it, with no name spoken. Seven places, and no eighth likeness and no cause across two of them and no payer under any. The three coverings stayed weather entered nowhere, with a finger between every pair; no fourth likeness was made a cause. He entered none, because a thing entered is a thing that wants a payer and none stood.

\person{Ilva} drew at the yard well with the pail against her knee, the rope stiff in the \place{Ash} cold and giving its small dry rasp as the bucket came up. She was a woman of the firing work and not of a house, past her first youth and not old enough to be spoken of as old, with the yard's pale clay dried in the crescents of her nails and the finer grey dust of \place{the drying racks} worked into the seam of one thumb, which she did not scrub out. Her hands knew the weight of a pail and the edge of a wet board and had never been soft, and people noticed the pail first and the sleeve not at all, which was the order she preferred. A woman waiting her turn, with a pot balanced on her hip and her shawl drawn tight against the river wind, asked what Ash's firings had taken. Ilva's hand went toward her sleeve and stayed. The short strip of notched birch bark lay where it lay, pale where a thumb had rubbed the notches and dark in the uncut grain between; it was not taken out, read, copied, counted or joined to the open slate a pace away on the stone lip, to the charcoal beneath \place{the loaf shelf}, to the cord, to the knot-string or to any saying that had arrived nowhere, and no figure came off the wood. Her eyes went to the asker and her mouth opened after both with a half-beat between the looking and the word.

“Ask the pot what it cost. I keep the count, not the account,” she said --- the question put and not the one behind it. She put the flat of her hand once more on the sleeve and went back to the pail, and nobody turned twice. The asker shifted her pot from one hip to the other and let it stand there, as a cost-question is let stand when the weather has been offered instead of a price.

The boy took the order of her reaching apart as a surgeon takes a field apart, by what was there and by what was not: hand to the record before eyes to the person, eyes before mouth, the half-beat in the gap where a lesser observer would have put an intention. He filed it on a place of its own as the ninth record-first hand in the district, with a finger between it and \person{Hessa Rale}'s eighth. He filed no tenth. He made no figure of the slate's nearness, made her no reader of her own keeping and joined her to none --- not to the runner with the edge-on slate, not to the clerk set at his landing, not to the charcoal, not to the figure that had gone up the road and not to any voice standing in no doorway to be asked. There was no ownerless saying at this well on the fifteenth; she was expressly not the speaker of any voice, no fifth shaping came, and the turning of the runner's writing stayed a sighting. The count stayed in wood where no slate could take a figure off it, and the ninth hand stayed nine.

The hunts and repairs of the afternoon stayed directions judged by the carrying arm and the weather, never figures. A man come down from \place{Cold Acre} shifted a hare across his shoulders at the well and, to a woman who asked what the carrying had cost, told her to ask the weather, which had carried the snow and could carry the price; he went off toward \place{the clay road} with the hare unweighed and nobody opening a slate. Another with a cracked jar in a sling of rope asked what the month's mending would want and was told to ask the pot, and the pot, being a pot, said nothing. A woman with a basket of kindling asked what the seventh year would want when it came, and two men mending a strip of fence said the thaw would be early and late. \person{Tarn}, bent at the shoulders from six years in the slip pit, with Ash worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line, said “Yes, house” to each of them and kept the late because a late thaw put off the paying, agreeing so thoroughly that there was nowhere for an argument to find a foot. The boy read each as a chart left blank where the number should stand and entered none of the blanks; a cost-question answered with the weather was a direction arrived nowhere, and the arriving was the whole of it.

At \place{the rack end} the dressed grain lay turned under wool away from \place{the hearth}, and Sella lifted a corner with a touch still lighter than she had used in the morning and turned it once and gave no figure. Her fingers went in and came out and did not close, so that no handful was lifted. The empty fingers went on a place of their own beside the one object that was the handful let fall on Bone 22---a keeping, not a second handful, not a sixth held change, not a rule, with no payer under it. Wool over turning grain, the dry rasp of it, the smell of the yard coming up through it: the small sounds and the small smells, taken first. Whatever arithmetic the fifteenth might put upon a heap of grain would not know what the heap had cost, and the space between that figure and its price would have no signature set into it.

At \place{the kindling} Tarn kept his account in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check and in the lip beyond it where it could not: six standing, the seventh at the lip beside the unforged collector sentence that Garl had left there with no name on the man who turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on the debt. The small knife lay at the very edge of its sill, where a man could check it and where a man's hand could also slip it off. “Six counted. The next I don't count,” he told the wood, in the tone of a man stating the weather, and cut nothing and counted nothing and called no loan. The boy checked the resting of a thumb on the uncut seventh of Bone 26 and found it where it had been laid, and did not make it again on the fifteenth: not a tenth stopping, not the seventh year given, not a payment, not a sixth held change, not a hearing, not a mending and not a rule, with no payer standing under it. The held breath at the knife-sill from the day before he checked also, and found it where it had been put; the hand went to the sill, passed over the knife as the house would check it, and went away when the checking was done, and no breath stayed at the top of the reach. It was not made again and it was not joined to any other keeping.

The sentence about an ear under this roof being worth more than the debt that held it came up to the word that would call the loan and stopped there, as it had stopped on a ninth morning and would stop again; there were nine stoppings and no tenth before the seventh year at its very end, and the loan was uncalled. The breath that should have gone in at the top of the reach stayed unspent, a small interval at the top of every reach, and his chest had been tight since the dark. The pale clip-ring at his left wrist, in place since Y.H. 806, held what little light there was and gave it back. At \place{the flagstones} his stroke went over the shallow bright patch without the half-check, and the returned half-check of Bone 8 stayed on its own place with the Day 161 fraction, the Day 166 shelf beat and the Frost 5 returned beat, four held changes on four places and not a rule. The warm loft stone lay cold in its unmarked pot above the shed, unreconciled by not trying, which is not forgiveness. Nothing under the roof made a sixth of his one silk-thin keeping or promoted it into a habit, a way, a sign, a cause or a payment, and no payer stood under it.

\person{Jorra Vail} came through the finger's-width door off \place{the Vail wheel-shed} with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bent little finger and white in the creases where a fist had opened, and it was not the bone-coloured \lore{slip} of the wheel. He kept his dusty forearms and his clean ones on one tray and parted them as a surgeon parts clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had been broken nor whether it was a birth-slate. Three or more men had gone low into \place{the back room} before first grey and he gave the house no name for them and did not say the room was cold. To the wheel he spoke before he spoke to any person: “It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady.” To a pot he said, “You'll do,” in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either, and knew the \lore{the full note} it would give three turns before it left his hands, with air under it and a want in it, and kept the knowing where nobody could make a song of it, withheld from a kiln-master come up from \place{High Kiln} who would call it early and make a song of it.

Sella had not finished telling him the fire was low when his bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper}, took the cold out of the fingertip, moved the tongue a hair's breadth and was left standing; the end of her instruction arrived at the pot and the household straightened around his silence the way cloth straightens when a weight is lifted. One piece of iron had two bites, the holding and the taking, and there was no \lore{Grathic} word for both mercies. “He's not Vail” stayed sealed as a lid nobody had tried to lift in three years, and the promise of the boy's name to the men who burn slates was neither given nor withdrawn. The burned-slate word stood at five with a finger between the fifth and the four and no witness standing; the Ash 5 joining said over \place{the boards} stood beside it on a place of its own, not a sixth telling, not a third telling of the shut room, not a cause, not a confession and not a rule. The shut-room tellings stood at two and the row of askings at five with no witness and no sixth, and no sixth was due on a day that had not earned one. The boy set the five down where they lay and did not look for a sixth standing beside them, and he did not lay the new breath at the sill across any of them.

Back under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof the whole tray was checked and left as it lay. Six hearings of the full note stood beside and never among, with the seventh place empty, the sixth the small flat knock out of \person[Dessa Silt] as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go and never among the things that had come out of vessels; an empty place kept empty was a finding while a finding filled to make a row come out even was a finding forged. The heard bell stayed in its square beside the told bell of \lore{the Long Night}, four, four and a gap with nobody crossing himself, and was not fetched in to make a fourth count or a seventh hearing. The told figure stood beside the paid with no signature on the space: one in twelve printed, read and unchallenged, and not paid by anybody the boy could see. Seven arithmetics of the year --- the three of Frost, the first, second and third of Bone, and Ash's first --- stood behind one unsigned figure, and seven were seven and not one account. Ash's first rode locked and unread up the clay road in \person{Berr Clay}'s box toward High Kiln; Ash's second stood five mornings ahead on a day that had not earned it and was not brought early. The edges came near to touching, so near that a less careful hand would have called the touching a cause and the cause a payment; the finger went between. The finding sat on a place of its own with no payer under it and was not laid across the house's \lore{not-doings}, the dressed grain covered under wool and read over by a number that did not know its cost, the five held objects, the handful, the thumb's resting, the two small objects of the hand, the sixth opening, the three movements of the fifth and sixth of Ash, the pot-crack resting, the new breath at the sill or his own kept gaps. No doctrine stood up out of any of it, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood.

Nine record-first hands stood with no tenth, the runner's eighth and the well-woman's ninth each on a place of its own with a finger between. Nine stoppings stood with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end, and the loan stayed uncalled. The row stood at five askings with one shaping and no sixth and no witness. The burned slate stood at five with a finger between the fifth and the four; the shut-room tellings stood at two; the Ash 5 joining of the burned slate to the room with the damper shut, the fourth shaping of the Frost 14 well-saying and the Ash 6 turning of the runner's writing stood each on a place of its own with a finger between every pair, none spent, paid, named or promoted into a sixth telling, a third shut-room telling, a fifth shaping, a witness, a cause or a rule, for a rule is an account that has found a payer and none stood. No ownerless saying had come under the roof on the fifteenth. The far-off hand that would one day lay the district's private keepings together was still far off, and it was not his; he did not follow it there. A finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged.

Garl stayed in objects and not in a story. The salt-stiff coat with the crease at the left shoulder kept his broader shape on its peg behind the door; the wheel-seat in the shed kept its twenty-year hollow on that side and nobody was allowed to fill it; the ridge that had been his ash-marker was now the clean dark line with the finer grey beside it called the thaw, unswept both; the twice-mended pot stood on \place{the pot board} with the hairline of the original break showing between the old-brass staple and the tighter wire; and the unforged collector sentence lay beside the knife at the kindling sill with no name on the man who turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on the debt. Both proverbs had gone to the pot and the fire and never to a person, improved in the keeping and very likely not as good as the man who had first said them. The debt against a firing of tiles circled without being called, neither paid nor settled nor done with and worse for being uncalled. His name had not been spoken under this roof three hundred and forty-four days, and no lesson had been made of any of it. The keeping of him was maintenance and not a story.

At the going of the light Sella carried him up at the half-pace and wide around the seven walked-around places, and the returning-line place where the hand had gone in and not come out was gone around with a look and no figure, as it had been gone around going down. She set him on \place{the bed} under the Vail roof and never in \place{the Debt Loft}, put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face, let her hand rest the half-beat longer than the setting needed before it went away, and left the thread where it lay. A sound came from above and behind and no interior was entered and no name was given to the sound; the instruction went to the fire, the wool, the pot and the river wind and never to the basket. She said “The house will keep it” where another woman would have said I. \lore{The widow's mark} hung where it hung and was not touched, because a going of the light and a bed are not openings; six openings in six months were six and not a rule, the sixth held behind at \place{the kiln mouth} on the second of Ash without a name, and no seventh was made due on a day that had not earned one. The mark answered nothing for the crust at two inches, the empty place beside it, the fire's missing fifth, the hand that had gone in on Seed 15 and not come out, or the man carried out in the clay-heavy rise of the brook; none of those stood under it asking to be answered, and she did not ask on their behalf.

She went to the hearth before the basket, told the coals it was the thaw and turned them with the back of a broken pot, not with the poker, because a pot's back knows how much a coal will bear. The fire answered four low pulls with the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer unnamed three hundred and forty-four days, and she did not supply the pull. Her finger lay on top of the loaf shelf's front edge so the charcoal tally stayed unread; her lips moved along a two-gapped row and figured neither gap; and her eye went once to the clay jar standing cold under \lore{lead glaze} with the hand half over nothing and the little-door burn on the back of her left hand closing on air. From the pot board she lifted the twice-mended pot with her thumb first where the wire crossed the crack and only then let the weight come on the handle; both proverbs went to the pot and the fire and never to a person, and the crack was left showing. At the rack end the dressed grain lay turned under wool away from the hearth; she lifted a corner with a lighter touch and turned it once and gave no figure, and her fingers went in and came out without closing, so that no handful was lifted. The pot's rim went down two inches beyond the reaching hand, with a half-beat at the setting and no look toward the bed. He took the half-beat apart by what was there and by what was not: the distance between the hand's intention and its arrest, a small exact interval that bought nothing at two inches of air and was not a gift, for a gift wants a giver and nobody had given. He wanted the rim under his fingers and got the space where it would have been, and the space was not entered.

Under the floor the carried fire kept its four and its gap; overhead the boards kept four nails on the near one, four on the next and a thumb-width knot where a fifth might have been driven and was not; through the finger's-width door \lore{water over water} went low over lower, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming, and was never numbered among the hearings, for a hearing wants a source standing and none stood. Three counts in three places, and none rose into another, because agreeing any two would have wanted a payer put under both and none stood to any. The bell's gap stayed in its square and was not folded into any of them, and the heard bell was not made a seventh of the full note.

In the small hours the hand outside the wool came up an inch toward the river's note. It did not reach; the inch was not a reach and he had long ago stopped calling it one. It was the distance a hand may move in a field without disturbing the dressing, and he took it the way he had once taken a hand into a cavity, slowly, with the whole of the attention and the knowledge that most of what a hand does in a field is wait. The note under the finger's-width door went water over water, a low thing over a lower thing, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming, and was never numbered among the hearings. The hand took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole, and let the intake go on the note's own finishing. It came straight down and laid itself back against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. The note had done before the hand came down, so the letting-go was neither early again nor stayed, and the hand did not stay; it was the note's own finishing that ended the interval and not any decision of the hand, and that was the whole of the honesty in it. The intake was not a breath kept for warmth, the interval not a stopping of the river, the note not his. He filed it as answer two hundred and sixty-four: exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the seven arithmetics, the bell's gap, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the stayed object, the early letting-go, the sixth opening, the pot-crack resting, the breath at the sill, the Ash 5 joining, the fourth shaping, the turned writing, the notched wood nor his own kept gaps.

Two hundred and sixty-four honest answers stood on the sixth place, each an object and not a power, and not one of them stood up and called itself a doctrine, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood. The breath held at the knife-sill stayed where it was put and was not made again. The surgeon took his small clean satisfaction in a field left as found; the infant underneath took none of it and went on wanting the crust lifted, the thread wiped and a hand on the pot handle, both true, the second louder, neither winning. Three hundred and forty-four stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in Low Kiln, Sill and Grath; six months and one hundred and sixty-four days were told by nobody; the carried fire's fifth was unpaid the same number with its payer unnamed; and the name of the man the salt-stiff coat kept was unspoken the same number. Ash kept its fifteenth day without a boundary drawn and neither of the district's two habits of naming its months was set right or left wrong in so many words. Nothing on the fifteenth had been opened, joined by a standing hand, named, called, paid or ended. No runner had been seen on any road and no collector come up or down the clay road, and there was no market, no Cross and no bell. The keeping of the number was the only honest work there had been to do, bought nothing at two inches of air, was not for sale, and went on because attention is the one thing no institution can tax. The file lay open on \place{the towel} at the head of the bed with every gap left gap; the twice-mended pot stood on the pot board with the hairline of the break showing between the old-brass staple and the tighter wire; one stiff brown-edged crust lay two inches from his ear with its empty place unnamed and no third, the thread at his mouth frozen and unwiped. Ash 20 stood five mornings ahead and was not brought into a day that had not earned it. Forward, not closed.
