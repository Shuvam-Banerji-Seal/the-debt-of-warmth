## Chronicle

Bone came on without a boundary drawn, and beneath the Vail roof the boy came into his three hundred and twelfth, thirteenth and fourteenth mornings, untold. The count went from three hundred and eleven to three hundred and fourteen; the fire's fifth stayed unpaid, Garl's name unspoken likewise. Each morning the body was taken in its old order --- crust two inches off, leathery, its empty place unnamed, thread frozen, cold at cheek, temple and wrist, the afterdrop unattended. His knowledge that a cold patient cannot clot bought nothing at two inches of air. The infant wanted the crust lifted and the thread wiped. Both were true, the second louder; neither won, and the argument was the work.

On Bone 13 and 14 Sella carried him down at the half-pace from every open slate, the instruction to the wool, never the basket. She went around seven places with a look and a wide step: the clean dark line, the birth-slates, the pan's gone ring, the trough, the field's empty place, the returning-line place where a hand went in on Seed 15 and did not come out. Three coverings at the well were called weather and entered nowhere; the dressed grain lay under wool, turned with a lighter touch and given no figure. Hunts and repairs were directions, not figures: a hare's price was the weather; a cost sent to the pot, which said nothing, left no entry.

On Bone 14 Ilva drew at the well, rack dust in her thumb seam. Asked what the firings had taken, her hand went toward her sleeve and stayed; the notched strip did not come out and no figure came off the wood. *Ask the pot what it cost. I keep the count, not the account*, she said. The boy filed the order of her reaching as a ninth record-first hand beside Hessa's eighth, and filed no tenth.

Six answers went whole on the river's note --- the hundred and ninety-ninth through the two hundred and fourth --- each exact and worth nothing at two inches of air, no payer named. On Bone 15 the hand, having let its intake go, did not come straight down: it stayed at the inch for the space of the note's fading, held and not reaching, the two inches unclosed. The surgeon read a clamp left after bleeding; the infant, a hand still wanting. Both were true; neither won. He filed the staying as answer two hundred and three and did not make it again, for one staying is a keeping and two a habit, and a habit wants a payer.

At the close the file lay open on the towel with every gap left gap, three hundred and fourteen stood exact and spoken by nobody, and Bone went on without a boundary drawn.
