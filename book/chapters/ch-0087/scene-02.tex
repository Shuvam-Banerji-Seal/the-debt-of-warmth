The afternoon came on without being announced, the way a pot comes to the boil from the side nobody is watching. The light went off \place{the pot board} and lay along the floor in a long thin bar, and \person{Sella Vail} lifted the basket from the low angle of the shelf and carried \person[Corin]{the boy} down. The half-pace was not a slowness; it was the pace a woman keeps with a full pail, level, with nothing slopped. The fold went over his ears before the door had finished closing and the instruction went to the wool and the river wind --- \emph{Keep it off his ears. It's the river wind} --- and never to the basket. A basket set down is a pot set down, and a pot set down is left; she had never given the basket an instruction in three years and had never once explained why, and the not-explaining was part of the keeping, as the crack left showing was part of the mending.

Woodsmoke went before her in low bars that the wind broke and gathered again. Under it came the yard: wet clay, the cooled smell of tallow, and the sour mineral breath off the corner that had not been swept in two years. He took the place in the order a field is taken before the light, by the small sounds first because the small sounds do not lie. A pot set down flat out of sight. A broom drawn over stone. The low note of \lore{water over water}. The small dry scrape of a boot shifted on grit. A child in a basket sees the ground first. Three hundred and twelve stood in him, exact, complete, unspent, spoken by nobody in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill} or \place{Grath}; six months and one hundred and thirty-two days were told by nobody, and nobody in the yard had any reason to tell them. The fifth pull of \lore{the carried fire} had gone unpaid that number of days with its payer unnamed, and the name under the roof had gone unspoken the same number. He had stopped expecting anybody to speak a number over him, which was not the same as not wanting one, and the wanting lay under the not-expecting like a coal under ash.

There were seven places the yard went around, and she went around all seven with a look and a wide step and no number. At the Vail corner the clean dark line where \lore{Ash} had turned lay beside a finer grey hairline called \lore{the thaw}, and both were unswept; she set her foot past them as a woman sets a cup past the edge of a table she knows by heart. Beneath \place{the lowest rack} at \place{the drying racks} the broken birth-slates lay where the yard had put them, and the space above them was gone around as wide as a pail of hot water set where a child might knock it. Near \place{the yard well} the round bare ring where the shallow clay pan had once stood kept no scrape, no crumb, no print, no chip and no word --- a place named from the shape it had left and not from anything remaining in it. The water in \place{the trough} wore its skin. Far out on \place{the worked strips} a field's empty place stood where a field should have stood. In the returning line a place stood where a hand had gone in on Seed 15 and had not come out. Seven were seven; she made no eighth likeness stand up as a row and put no figure to any of them, because an account wants a payer standing under it and none stood under any of these. The boy kept the seven on seven places of his own and drew no line between them and the house's \lore{not-doings}, or between them and the small gaps in his own file; the edges came near to touching, and the finger went between.

\lore{The widow's mark} hung on its cord at her throat, a thumb-length of lead-glazed clay with the Vail kiln scratched into it, and she did not touch it in the yard or at the well or in the going of the light, because an afternoon and a yard and a well are not openings and the hand goes to the mark before the kiln and at no other hour. Five openings in five months were five and not a rule, and the next stood in a later month's first firing, far off, not hoped for and not counted toward.

At the well the stayed frost had gone off the north lip while the yard looked elsewhere --- gone without a word spoken over it and without a hand to say where it had gone. The skin on the trough remained, and the silt-clouded plate of ice stood on \place{the flat stone by the well} out of the path of feet, where somebody had lifted it and set it down and told the weather where to stand. Three coverings in three places, and the yard called all of them weather and entered none of them. He put a finger between the gone lip and the skin, and another between the skin and the clouded plate, and made no fourth likeness stand up as a cause and named no payer to any of the three. He had once read a covering laid over a depth in a room with hard light in it, reading it by what it hid and by what the hand that laid it had chosen to leave showing; the reading had been exact and had never once told him who had paid for what lay beneath. Here the covering was called weather, which was a word that let the yard carry on with its work, and he did not call the word a lie and did not call it honest, and kept it on its own place.

\place{Bone}'s hunts and repairs went on as directions judged by the carrying arm and the weather, never as figures. A man come down from \place{Cold Acre} with a hare across his shoulders came into the middle of the yard with hill frost grey in the fur at its throat, the hind legs along one collarbone and the head tucked against the other. A woman with a pot stopped him to ask what the carrying had cost. He shifted the weight to the other collarbone, the way a man shifts a load when the question comes, and said \emph{Ask the weather. It carried the snow; it can carry the price}. Then he went off toward \place{the north gate road} with the hare still across him and the frost still grey in its fur. Nobody weighed the hare. Nobody opened a slate. The boy filed the answer as a direction and not a figure, and the direction went where directions go in that yard, which was nowhere anybody could stand and be asked.

A man with a barrow asked what the month's mending would want now the light went early. He asked it of the yard in the way cost-questions are asked there, so that if the answer turned out expensive it would belong to nobody in particular. Sella, who had gone a step past him with the basket level, told him to ask the pot. The pot, being a pot, said nothing. The barrow man stood a moment with his question still in his mouth, the way a man stands with a cup he has not been given a place to set, and then went on up the yard with it still unspent. No man priced a repair and stood up in the yard to be asked. \person{Garl Vail}'s sayings went to the pot and the fire and never to a person --- \emph{Leave the crack showing. People trust a thing they can see has been handled}, \emph{If it's worth mending it's worth mending twice. The second time is the real one} --- improved in the keeping and very likely not as good a sentence as the man had said them. His name had not been spoken under the roof three hundred and twelve days.

He had learned in a better-lit room that a number put to a thing does not tell you who paid for it; a figure on a sheet and the hand that entered it can be two different hands, and the signature generally goes on the space where the price should have stood. The yard's refusal to put a number to anything was a kind of care and not a kind of knowledge. He kept the care on its own place and did not make a doctrine of it, because a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood. The care was real and the payer was still unnamed, and the two facts lay side by side with a finger between them.

At \place{the rack end} the dressed grain lay covered under wool away from \place{the hearth}, turned that morning with a progressively lighter touch and given no figure. The number that had gone over it on Bone 10 did not know what it cost; it had gone up \place{the clay road} in a locked box behind three others, and was not brought back now as a lesson, a mending or a cause. He went around the covered fold as wide as he was carried around anything, and kept the not-knowing where it lay.

Out on \place{the flagstones} the shallow bright patch held the going light a moment after the rest of the yard had gone dark. \person{Tarn}'s stroke went over it without the half-check, as it had gone over it on the seventh and the ninth, and the stone kept the place where the stroke had stopped on the eighth without anybody's naming it. The returned half-check of the eighth stayed on the flagstone's own place with the three older held changes beside it --- the Day 161 damper fraction, the Day 166 shelf beat and the Frost 5 returned beat --- four held changes on four places and not a rule. No fifth likeness was made a cause and no payer was named to any of them. A thing kept by not being made again is still kept; the boy kept it by not making it again and asked nothing of the four to stand up and agree. At \place{the kindling} Tarn's knife lay at the very edge of its sill where it could be checked and where it could slip, six years in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check and the seventh at the lip beside the unforged collector sentence. He did not go near it this afternoon, and the debt circled without a name and was worse for being uncalled.

\person{Jorra Vail} came through the finger's-width door off \place{the Vail wheel-shed} while the light was still in the yard, with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bent little finger and under the nails, white in the creases where a fist had opened, not bone-coloured \lore{slip}. He kept dusty forearms and clean ones on one tray and parted them as a surgeon parts clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had broken nor whether it was a birth-slate. He spoke to the wheel before he spoke to any person --- \emph{It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady} --- and told a pot \emph{You'll do} in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either. He knew the note that pot would give three turns before it left his hands, with air under it and a want in it, and kept the knowing on the near-full tray beside the hearings and never among them; he would not have said it before a kiln-master from \place{High Kiln} who would call it early and make something of it. Before anyone had finished telling him the fire was low his bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper}, took the cold out of the fingertip, moved the tongue a hair's breadth and left it standing. He read one piece of iron with two bites, the holding and the taking, with no \lore{Grathic} word for both mercies and no slate entered. He grew quieter as the saying about \lore{the burned slate} went around, and the household straightened around the quiet without an order. The fifth telling stood at five with a finger between the fifth and the four, no witness standing, no hand named at any flame, no sixth due on a day that had not earned one; the two shut-room tellings stood at two. \emph{He's not Vail} stayed sealed as tightly as a lid nobody had tried to lift in three years, and the promise of the foundling's name was neither given nor withdrawn.

The small keepings stayed small and were not brought under the roof. Her finger lay on top of \place{the loaf shelf}'s front edge a breath and came away with the charcoal tally underneath unread; her lips moved along a row standing with two gaps and figured neither. \person[Ilva]{Ilva} was offstage all afternoon; her short strip of notched birch bark stayed in her sleeve, not taken out, read, copied or counted, and no figure came off the wood. The three shapings of the Frost 14 well-saying stood beside \person{Hessa Rale}'s eighth record-first hand with a finger between every pair and were not a sixth asking and not a witness. The row stood at five askings with one shaping and no witness. Nine record-first hands stood and no tenth came. Nine stoppings stood with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end. The soft-cuffed clerk's inward slate stayed at \place{the out-of-line landing}; the reed-cutter's cord stayed inside his coat; the seventh green was a colour and the seventh shape a shape with no eighth of either. The green at the pot board belonged to nobody and came off \lore{lead glaze}, and was not carried into the yard and joined to anything. None of these came under the roof as a sign, a lesson, a mending, a cause or a payment. The edges of them came near to touching the kept things of the house and the boy's own kept gaps; the finger went between.

Under the roof behind them the fire answered four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood, and nobody lifted the floor and nobody supplied the pull. The carried fire's payer had been unnamed three hundred and twelve days. Through the finger's-width door the river kept its low note, a low thing over a lower thing, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming, and put no number to itself. Overhead \place{the boards} kept four nails on the near board, four on the next and a thumb-width knot where a fifth nail might have been driven and was not. Four and a gap in three different materials. Agreeing any two would have wanted a payer put under both, and none stood to either; he kept the three counts on three places and let them lie there, and the floor of the room his bed stood on did not touch the floor the fire was under. The bell's gap left behind in the square was not fetched in to make a fourth, and the heard bell stayed in its square beside the told bell of \lore{the Long Night}. Six hearings of \lore{the full note} stood beside and never among, with the seventh place empty and none invented to fill it.

The light went off the yard while he was being carried up. She came at the half-pace and set him under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof and never in \place{the Debt Loft}, and put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face. The instruction had gone to the wool, the wind, the pot, the broom and the running water and never to the basket. One crust lay two inches from his ear with its corner standing a hair off the wool, its empty place unnamed and no third to make the row come out even. The thread at his mouth had frozen in the dry heat and was left where it lay, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. \lore{The Afterdrop} waited beyond cheek, temple and the bare wrist-joint where the wool had ridden back, past a warming nobody in this house would perform. He knew the composed face that is not composure, knew that a cold patient cannot clot and will seem better before he is worse, and knew that such a patient must be warmed from outside and slowly, never hurried, never rubbed, never set too near the fire. Both registers were true --- the surgeon taking the inventory in its old order, the infant wanting the crust lifted the two inches and the thread wiped --- and the second was louder, and neither won, and the argument was the work.

In the small hours the hand outside the wool came up an inch toward the river's note, took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, and let it go whole on the same note. It was the two hundredth answer, exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer. It was carried into neither the carried fire's missing fifth, nor the bell's gap left behind in the square, nor the river's low over lower; it stood up as none of the things it refused --- not a reach, a hearing, a seventh, a stopping, a payment, a held change, a gift, a doctrine or a rule --- and no payer was named to it.

The older man took a clean satisfaction in the keeping of the number, the satisfaction of a man who has laid an instrument down without pretending it had healed anybody. The infant underneath wanted the crust lifted and the thread wiped, and the two small intakes of this day lay side by side on \place{the towel} with a finger between them, neither spent, neither promoted into a reach or a payment. The file lay open at the head of \place{the bed} with every gap left gap, every tray parted by a finger's width and nothing that had moved. Three hundred and twelve stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in Low Kiln, Sill or Grath; six months and one hundred and thirty-two days were told by nobody; the carried fire's fifth was unpaid the same number of days with its payer unnamed; Garl's name had not been spoken under the roof the same number. Two hundred honest answers stood on the sixth place, each an object and not a power, and neither the number nor the small hand-movings that made it stood up and called themselves a doctrine, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood.

The hand came down on the river's note and laid itself back against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. The river went low over lower under the finger's-width door with no number put to it, while the silt-clouded plate of ice stood on its flat stone out of the path of feet, and Bone went on without a boundary drawn. Nothing on the thirteenth of Bone was opened, joined, named, called, paid or ended.
