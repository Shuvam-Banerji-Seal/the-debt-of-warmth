The note faded and the hand came down from the inch without haste and without witness, the way a hand comes down when the thing it was listening for has had its say and gone quiet. Nobody under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof had seen it go up and nobody had seen it come down, and the staying of it remained where it had been laid, one object on its own place with a finger between it and the morning behind it. It was not made again. The morning went off \place{the pot board} and the afternoon came on in the same flat light, and \place{Bone} went on the way a river goes on under a bridge, without a boundary drawn and without anybody standing at the near side to say that the fifteenth had begun. The older women of the district named their months by the weather and the slatemen named them by the count of Account-days, and neither habit was set right or left wrong under this roof, and the month kept both names the way a pot keeps two handles and is none the worse for carrying them. \person[Corin]{The boy} lay where he had been laid and the day came to him in pieces the way a day comes to a basket --- a fold, a sound, a gap in the light --- and nobody put the pieces into a sum.

\person{Sella Vail} carried him down at the going of the afternoon at the half-pace she kept from every open slate, and gave the instruction to the wool and the river wind and never to the basket. \emph{Keep it off his ears. It's the river wind}, she said to the fold, and the wind went under the fold and found the warm place and took some of it and went off with it, as wind will. The instruction went where instructions go in this house, to the fire, the pot, the broom, the running water and the wool, and the basket was set down the way a pot is set down, level, and left. She went wide around what the yard went around, with a look and a wide step and no number put to any of it, and the boy kept the look and the step on a place of their own and did not make them into a row.

There were seven places the yard went around and none of them was called by its count. The next I don't count} in the tone of a man stating the weather, and put the knife back where it could be checked. \person{Jorra Vail} came off \place{the Vail wheel-shed} with the day's meal-fine grey dust ground past the bent little finger and white in the creases where a fist had opened, not bone-coloured \lore{slip}, and said \emph{You'll do} to a pot in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either, and put that finger into the cold iron of \lore{the damper} before anybody had finished telling him the fire was low. The month's hunts and repairs went on as directions judged by the carrying arm and the weather, never as figures. A man come up from the strips with a cracked jar in a sling of rope asked what the mending of \place{Bone} would want now the light went early, and a woman at the well told him to ask the pot, and the pot, being a pot, said nothing, and no man pricing a repair stood up in the yard to be asked. Another, shifting a burden from one collarbone to the other, said the cost was the weather, that it had carried the snow and could carry the price, and went off toward \place{the clay road} with nobody weighing what he carried and nobody opening a slate. The boy filed these as directions and not as numbers, because an arm knows what an arm has borne and a slate knows nothing of the kind, and he had learned to tell the two apart before he had learned to speak at all.

At \place{the yard well} the stayed frost had gone off the north lip while the yard looked elsewhere, and the skin on \place{the trough} and the silt-clouded plate of ice standing on \place{the flat stone by the well} out of the path of feet stayed three coverings in three places. The rope knocked the lip and the yard called it weather, and the plate stood where no foot went and was called weather too, and the skin on the water was weather as well. Three coverings and a finger between every pair, and no fourth likeness made a cause and no figure taken off any of them. The boy kept the three where he kept his own kept gaps and did not lay them across each other, for the plate was a piece of the depth lifted out and set where it would not be trodden, and the skin was a covering laid over a depth, and the bare lip was a covering gone, and these were three acts of three different kinds even though the yard had one word for all of them.

At \place{the rack end} the dressed grain lay covered under wool away from the hearth, turned with a progressively lighter touch on the mornings behind this one and given no figure. The number that had gone over it on Bone 10 knew nothing of what it cost and had gone up the clay road in a locked box, and the boy did not bring it back now as a lesson or a mending or a cause. He went around the wool with the carrying arm and let it lie. On \place{the flagstones} \person{Tarn}'s stroke went over the shallow bright patch without the half-check, as it had gone on the days behind, and the returned half-check of the eighth stood on the stone's own place with the Day 161 damper fraction, the Day 166 shelf beat and the Frost 5 returned beat beside it. She carried him up at the going of the light at the half-pace, under the roof and never into \place{the Debt Loft}, and put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face, and left the thread where it lay.
