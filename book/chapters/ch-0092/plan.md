**Place:** Clay Yard; yard well and flat stone; rack end; kindling; flagstones; wheel-shed edge and damper; bed, boards, towel. **Present:** Corin/Dan, Sella Vail, Tarn, Jorra Vail; walk-ons (Cold Acre hare-carrier, cracked-jar man, woman with a basket of kindling, two men arguing the thaw); Garl in objects. No Ilva, no Berr, no Hessa, no clerk, no green or shape at the close; no collector.

**Beats, in order:**
1. Bone goes out without a boundary drawn and neither of the district's two habits of naming its months is corrected. Yard goes around its seven walked-around places with a look and a wide step and no number; hunts and repairs remain directions judged by the arm and the weather, never figures; three coverings weather entered nowhere with a finger between every pair (T009/T013/T092, T098 carried, T083/T085, T077).
2. Hunts and repairs as directions: a man down from Cold Acre answers the price of his hare with the weather; a man with a cracked jar in a rope sling is told to ask the pot and the pot says nothing; a woman with a basket of kindling asks what the seventh year will want when it comes and two men say the thaw will be early and late; Tarn says *Yes, house* to each and keeps the late because a late thaw puts off the paying, agreeing so thoroughly that no argument can find a foot; no collector comes up or down the clay road (T036/T076, T077).
3. The held objects are checked and left on their own places: the thumb's resting on the seventh notch is one object, not made again on Bone 28, 29 or 30, not a tenth stopping, not the seventh year given, not a payment, not a fifth held change, not a hearing, not a mending and not a rule, with a finger between it and the nine stoppings, the four held changes, the handful and his own gaps — a touch that took nothing and paid nothing, kept because it was true and not because it bought anything (T036/T076/T006); the handful let fall at the rack end on Bone 22 stays one object and is not made again; the staying of Bone 15 and the early letting-go of Bone 17 stay two objects and are not made again; four held changes on four places and not a rule.
4. Six arithmetics with their true subtractions ride locked in Berr's box toward High Kiln, one in twelve unchallenged, figure read not figure paid, no signature, six are six and not one account, finding on a place of its own with no payer and not laid across the not-doings, covered grain, four held changes, handful, thumb's resting, two small objects or his gaps — edges nearly touch, finger goes between; no doctrine stands up, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood (T015/T041/T047). The reading answered nothing for the hand that went in, the carried man, the grain or the missing fifth.
5. Whole holding tray checked unchanged: burned slate five with no sixth, no witness and no hand at the flame; row five askings with one shaping and no witness; well-saying three shapings with Hessa's eighth hand on its own place and a finger between; shut-room tellings two; three pot sayings with the Smoke 14 note apart; nine record-first hands with Ilva's ninth in a sleeve and no tenth, the wood keeping its count where no slate can take a figure off it; nine stoppings with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end and the loan uncalled; six hearings beside and never among with the seventh place empty, the sixth the small flat knock out of a woman as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go; five openings in five months with the sixth far off at Ash 2 (Day 331) and not brought near; seven green a colour and seven shape a shape with no eighth; heard bell in its square beside the told; no collector up or down the clay road (T080, T101, T035/T031/T065/T095, T061/T082, T036/T076, T006, T078, T050/T051, T033/T034, T058/T079).
6. Garl in objects, name unspoken 329 days, tile debt uncalled, no lesson made; salt coat, hollow wheel-seat, clean dark line and finer grey, proverbs to pot and fire never a person (T009/T013/T092). Jorra at the shed's edge and the damper: dusty forearms parted from clean, spoken to the wheel and a pot before any person, full note withheld, bent finger into the cold damper ahead of Sella's unfinished sentence, one iron with two bites and no Grathic word for both mercies, *he's not Vail* sealed, promise neither given nor withdrawn (T035/T031/T062).
7. Sella sets him on his bed under the roof at the going of the light, half-pace, fold righted without a look at his face, thread left, never the Debt Loft; a sound from above and behind only and no interior entered. Fire four and a gap; boards four, four and a knot; river water over water — three counts in three places and none rises into another, because agreeing any two would want a payer put under both and none stands.
8. At the very end of the day answer **234**: hand up an inch, small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, interval held whole, let go on the same low note, straight down and laid back against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. The note had done before the hand came down, so neither early again nor stayed. Exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment, borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the six arithmetics, the bell's gap, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the stayed object, the early letting-go nor his own gaps. **Two hundred and thirty-four honest answers** stand on the sixth, each an object and not a power, and neither the number nor the small hand-movings stood up and called themselves a doctrine, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood (T061/T082).
9. Close on the count: 329 exact, complete, unspent, spoken by nobody; six months and 149 days told by nobody; the fire's fifth unpaid the same number with its payer unnamed; Garl's name unspoken the same; nothing opened, joined, named, called, paid or ended; the keeping of the number the only honest work there was to do, bought nothing, going on because attention is the one thing no institution can tax. Bone goes out without a boundary drawn; Ash 1 (Day 330) one morning ahead and Ash 2 (Day 331), the sixth opening, two mornings ahead, neither brought into a day that has not earned it (T071/T072, T088).

**Emotional turn:** a quiet, earned certainty that nothing settled here can be spent, followed by the smallest possible forward motion — one more answer laid down and one more day kept exact, the month gone out with six readings behind one figure and no payer named. The ending is not restful; it is a held breath before Ash comes on.

**Sensory anchors:** unswept lines in the corner; flat stone with its silt-clouded plate; wool folded over grain; knife at the edge of its sill; the damper's cold iron; the river's note going quiet; the half-beat before the hand comes down; the fold against the ear.

**Threads advanced:** T009/T013/T092, T098 (carried), T083/T085, T077, T060/T086, T061/T082, T036/T076/T006, T015/T041/T047, T080 (held), T101 (held), T035/T031/T065/T095 (held), T035/T031/T062, T078 (held), T050/T051 and T033/T034 (held), T088, T058/T079. **Foreshadowing planted/paid off:** none paid; the strip, the charcoal, the thumb's resting, the handful, Hessa's slate and Berr's box all stay closed and unjoined for the far-off hand. **Nothing paid.**

**Exact last image:** the silt-clouded plate of ice standing on its flat stone out of the path of feet, the file open on the towel with every gap left gap, the hand laid back against the fold on answer 234, and the river's low note going quiet under the finger's-width door while Bone goes out without a boundary drawn.

## Pull into Chapter 93

The last image is deliberately not an ending. The count has reached **three hundred and twenty-nine exact**, six months and one hundred and forty-nine days told by nobody, and Bone has gone out into Ash without a boundary drawn. Six arithmetics of the year now stand behind one unsigned figure, six readings six and not one account, the finding kept on a place of its own with no payer; the handful at the rack end, the thumb's resting on the seventh notch, the staying at the inch and the early letting-go are each held as one object and not spent; Ilva's strip is still in her sleeve, Hessa's slate still unread up the road, the clerk's inward measure still at the landing, and Berr's box still holds six true subtractions. **Ash 1 (Day 330) stands one morning ahead, and Ash 2 (Day 331), the first firing of the dark month and the sixth opening, stands two mornings ahead** — close now, but not brought into a day that has not earned it. Chapter 93 opens on Day 330, Ash 1, Firstday, with the hand coming up for the two hundred and thirty-fifth answer and the count going to three hundred and thirty — an ordinary tending day first, the opening earned by its own morning, no sign carried forward, no lesson made, nothing joined, and forward motion only.
