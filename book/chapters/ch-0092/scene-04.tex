In the afternoon \person{Sella Vail} carried him down into \place{the Clay Yard} at the half-pace she kept from every open slate, the way a pot is carried filled to the lip that must not be slopped. The instruction went to the wool and the river wind and never to the basket. \emph{Keep it off his ears. It's the river wind}, she told the fold, and told the wind nothing, for the wind was the one thing in the yard that had never asked anybody to be told anything. \person[Corin]{The boy} felt the small jolts of her walking come up through the straw into his ribs, and with them the field as a surgeon takes a field before the light, by the small sounds first because the small sounds do not lie. One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from his ear, gone harder through the dry heat, its corner standing a hair off the wool; the empty place beside it kept its name to itself and no third came into the afternoon to make the row come out even. The thread at his mouth had frozen in the dry kiln heat and was unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread. Cold had come to cheek, temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, and had stopped there as at the end of a line written out for it; \lore{the Afterdrop} waited beyond the stopping, past a warming nobody in this house would perform. He wanted the crust lifted the two inches, the thread wiped away, and a hand that would close on the pot handle and bring it within reach. Both were true and the second was louder and neither won. The argument went on under the wool without a verdict and without a witness, and the argument was the work.

Three hundred and twenty-eight stood exact behind him, six months and one hundred and forty-eight days told by nobody under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill}, \place{Grath}. Nothing that had happened on the twenty-eighth came into the yard as sign, lesson, mending, cause or payment. The fingers at \place{the rack end} that morning had gone in and come out empty and that emptiness was not repeated here; the thumb's resting on the uncut seventh at the kindling stayed one object on its own place and was not made again, because an afternoon had not earned the making of it. Under the floor \lore{the carried fire} gave four low pulls and kept the silence where the fifth should have stood, and that silence had stood three hundred and twenty-eight days with no payer named beneath it. \person{Garl Vail} stayed where he had been kept, in objects and not in a story. Bone went on without a boundary drawn. The older women named it by the weather that had not come and the slatemen by their count of Account-days, and neither habit was set right or left wrong; the month carried both names the way a pot carries two handles and is none the worse. Bone 30 (Day 329), the month's third and last reading, stood one morning ahead in the direction of \place{the Cross} and was not brought into a day that had not earned it; the reading block and the bell and the thane with his box were a direction and nothing more this afternoon.

She went around the seven places with a look and a wide step and no number, as a woman goes around a pail of hot water set where a child might knock it. The clean dark line at the Vail corner where the \lore{Ash} ridge had turned lay unswept, and the finer grey hairline beside it that the yard called \lore{the thaw} lay unswept beside it; the basket went wide of both. Under \place[the drying racks]{the lowest rack} the broken birth-slates lay where no broom went and no hand entered. On the stone lip of \place{the yard well} the round ring where the shallow clay pan had once stood kept its shape with no scrape, no crumb, no print, no chip and no word. The water in \place{the trough} had a skin on it from edge to edge. Out on \place{the worked strips} the field kept its empty place, and in the returning line the place where a hand had gone in on Seed 15 and had not come out was gone around with a look and a wide step and no figure spoken over the going. Seven places. No eighth likeness stood up among them as a row, and no cause was laid across any two, because a cause wants a payer put under it and none stood.

Three coverings at the well stayed weather and were entered nowhere. The white that frost-bloom had written on the well's north lip on Bone 3 had gone while the yard looked elsewhere, and its going was not a sign. The skin on the trough broke under a pail's knock and closed again over the water without a word spoken to it. The plate of ice clouded with silt stood on \place{the flat stone by the well} out of the path of feet where she had set it on Frost 9 and told the weather where to stand. There was a finger between every pair. The boy read the three as he would read a covering laid over a depth, then the same covering over another depth, then a piece of the depth lifted and set where no foot goes; a fourth likeness would have stood up as a rule, and a rule wanted a payer, and none stood. He kept the three where they were, not a lesson written on the air and not a mending anybody had performed.

At \place{the rack end} the dressed grain lay turned under wool on the cool stone away from the hearth. She lifted a corner and the dry rasp of the grain came up small and unmistakable, the sound a thing makes when it has given up its water and is going on giving it up. She put her fingers in. They went in and came out and did not close. No handful was lifted. The wool came down again over the place where the fingers had been and she went on to the next thing without a look back, making no show of not repeating it. The handful let fall back through her fingers on Bone 22 sat on a place of its own with a finger between it and the four held changes on their four places and a finger between it and his own kept gaps, and the empty fingers of this afternoon went beside it as a keeping and not as a second, for two would have been a habit and a habit would have wanted a payer. Nobody under the roof had spoken a number over the grain and nobody had looked toward the basket.

\person{Ilva} was at \place{the yard well} drawing, with the pail against her knee and the rope stiff in the dry cold. The rope gave its small dry rasp as the bucket came up, the sound of hemp that has given up its water and will not give it back until the thaw, and she drew on it in the way a woman draws who has done the same thing every morning of her life and expects the weight to be the weight it was. Clay had dried pale in the crescents of her nails and the finer grey dust of \place{the drying racks} was worked into the seam of one thumb, and she did not scrub the seam out. Her hands knew the weight of a pail and the edge of a wet board and had never been soft, and went on knowing these things without reporting on them to anybody. People noticed the pail first and the sleeve not at all, which was the order she preferred. The boy, set down at the edge of the work where no slate lay, took her in as he took in everything under this sky, by what was there and by what was not.

A woman waiting her turn with a pot asked what the firings of Bone had taken. It was a small question put in a small voice, the sort of question that goes into the air with no owner standing behind it and arrives nowhere in particular; but it wanted a figure, and a figure wants a payer, and the yard had long ago decided it would give the slate neither.

Ilva's hand went toward her sleeve and stayed. The short strip of notched birch bark lay where it lay, pale where a thumb had rubbed the notches and dark in the uncut grain between, and it was not taken out, not read, not copied, not counted and not joined to the charcoal beneath \place{the loaf shelf}, or the cord, or the open slate lying a pace away on the stone lip, or the knot-string, or the ownerless saying that came over the boards from a quarter league off. No figure came off the wood. Her hand put itself flat once on the sleeve, the way a hand is laid on a wound to keep something in, and stayed there while her eyes went to the woman who had asked.

Her mouth opened after both, with a half-beat between the looking and the word. “Ask the pot what it cost. I keep the count, not the account,” she said, answering the question put and not the one behind it. She put the flat of her hand once more on the sleeve and went back to the pail, and nobody turned twice. The bucket knocked the well lip, the rope gave its small rasp again, and the water came up the same weight it always came.

The boy took the order of her reaching apart as a surgeon takes a chart apart, by what was there and by what was not. Hand to the record before eyes to the person; eyes before mouth. The half-beat between the looking and the word was the small distance between intention and arrest, the same interval he had measured all his life in rooms whose floors did not touch this one. He filed the shape on a place of its own as the ninth record-first hand in the district, with a finger between it and \person{Hessa Rale}'s eighth, and filed no tenth. He made no figure of the open slate's nearness and did not make her a reader of her own keeping, which she was not and had never wanted to be. She was not Collector \person{Marra} with her neat dark hand, not the soft-handed man of \place{Slate Row}, not the soft-cuffed clerk set below \place{the north bridge} for a measure nobody called, not Hessa with her edge-on slate going up toward \place{Greybank}, not the green-mouthed woman at the pot board and not the Drawing collector with grey fingertips. He joined her to none of them. Two likenesses were not a cause, and a cause wanted a payer standing under it, and none stood; a rule made from two keepings would have been an account that had found somebody to pay, and no payer stood here either.

The cost asked at the well went to the pot and the weather and the river as such questions always went, and left no entry. It was a direction judged by the carrying arm and the weather, like the hare come down from \place{Cold Acre} and the cracked jar in its sling of rope; it was not a figure and did not become one. A chart left blank where the number should stand was a chart still, and he entered neither the firings nor the price in it. A second person in this district was keeping a figure where no slate could take it off, and the knowing sat quiet in him beside the other knowings with a finger between, and did not stand up and call itself a lesson.

At the shed's edge \person{Jorra Vail} came through the finger's-width door off \place{the Vail wheel-shed} with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bent little finger and white in the creases where a fist had opened; it was not the bone-coloured \lore{slip} of the wheel. Three or more men had gone low into \place{the back room} before first grey and he gave the house no name for them and did not say the room was cold. He kept dusty forearms and clean ones on one tray and parted them as a surgeon parts clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had been broken nor whether it was a birth-slate. He spoke to the wheel before he spoke to any person. \emph{It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady}, he said to the wheel, and to a pot \emph{You'll do}, in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either. He knew the \lore{full note} that pot would give three turns before it left his hands, with air under it and a want in it, and kept the knowing on the near-full tray beside the hearings and never among them; he would not say it before a kiln-master come up from \place{High Kiln} who would call it early and make a song of it, singing before its time and singing itself out early.

Before Sella had finished telling him the fire was low his bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper}, took the cold out of the fingertip, moved the tongue a hair's breadth and left it standing so that the end of her instruction arrived at the pot. One piece of iron had two bites in it, the holding and the taking, and there was no word in the district's speech for both mercies, so the house had made do with one word and gone on. He did not look toward the basket. \emph{He's not Vail} stayed sealed as tightly as a lid nobody had tried to lift in three years, and the promise of a name to the men who burned slates was neither given nor withdrawn. The burned-slate word kept its five with no sixth due on a day that had not earned one, and the row and the well-saying kept the places that were theirs. Five arithmetics of the year stood behind one printed figure up \place{the clay road}, one in twelve unchallenged, the figure read still not the figure paid, five readings five and not one account; the finding sat on a place of its own with no payer and was not laid across this house's \lore{not-doings}, the covered grain, the four held changes, the handful, the thumb's resting or his own gaps. The edges came near to touching. The finger went between.

At \place{the kindling} \person{Tarn} kept his six where the house could check and the seventh at the lip beyond it, beside a collector sentence that had never been forged and lay there without a name on the man who had turned back at the frozen ford and without a figure on the debt. He set the small knife at the very edge of its sill, where a man's hand could check it and where a man's hand could also slip it off, and told the wood \emph{Six counted. The next I don't count} in the tone of a man stating the weather. The announcement went to nobody, because the yard had already been told. When the sentence about an ear under this roof being worth more than the debt that held it came up in him it came all the way to the word that would call the loan and stopped there, with no figure and no name --- a remembered ninth stopping with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end and still ungiven. He drew the breath from Seed 16 that should have gone in at the top of the reach and did not spend it, and his chest had been tight since the dark. Ash was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line and no scrubbing lifted it; the pale clip-ring at his left wrist held what little light there was and gave it back, and he lifted the wrist a moment toward the grey as though to see whether the light had come to anything. His stroke went over the shallow bright patch on \place{the flagstones} without the half-check, and the place where it had stopped on another day stayed where it was. The warm loft stone lay cold in its unmarked pot, unreconciled by not trying, which is not forgiveness.

The thumb's resting on the uncut seventh on Bone 26 was one object on its own place, and he checked it and did not make it again. It was not a tenth stopping, not the seventh year given, not a payment, not a fifth held change, not a hearing, not a near-full answer, not a mending and not a rule, and no payer stood under it. His thumb went back down to the six where the house could check, and the knife lay where it lay. No collector came up \place{the clay road} or went down it, and the debt against a firing of tiles circled without a name and was worse for being uncalled. The boy kept the two ears under one roof in their old equality and did not join the debt's stopped sentence to the ninth hand at the well or to any of his own gaps. Five of one thing and nine of another were not a cause.

The whole holding tray was checked and left as it lay. The burned slate stood at five with no sixth and no witness and no hand at the flame; the row at five askings with one shaping and no witness and expressly no sixth; the well-saying at three shapings with the eighth hand on its own place and a finger between; the shut-room tellings at two; the three pot sayings with the Smoke 14 note on its own place and no fourth likeness made a cause; nine record-first hands with Ilva's ninth unread in its sleeve and no tenth, the count staying in wood where no slate could take a figure off it; nine stoppings with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end and the loan uncalled; the stayed-at-the-inch object and the early letting-go, the handful and the thumb's resting, four objects on four places with a finger between every pair and none made again on the twenty-ninth. Six hearings of \lore{the full note} stood beside and never among, five of them from four sources and the sixth the small flat knock out of a woman as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go and never among the things that had come out of vessels; the seventh place stayed empty and none was invented to fill it, for an empty place kept empty is a finding and a finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged. \person{Garl Vail} stayed in objects and not in a story: the salt-stiff coat with the crease at the left shoulder on its peg behind the door, the wheel-seat in the shed keeping its twenty-year hollow that nobody filled, the clean dark line and the finer grey unswept, the twice-mended pot with both proverbs gone to the pot and the fire and never to a person. The debt against a firing of tiles circled without a name and was worse for being uncalled. At the going of the light Sella carried him up at the half-pace from every open slate and never once went into \place{the Debt Loft}. She set him on \place{the bed} under the roof, put the fold right at his ear without a look at his face, her hand resting the half-beat longer than the setting needed before it went away, and left the thread where it lay. From above and behind came the small dry scrape of the loft, filed on its own place and entered nowhere; no interior was opened and no interior was entered. Under the floor the carried fire gave four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer unnamed three hundred and twenty-eight days; overhead \place{the boards} kept four nails on the near board, four on the next and a thumb-width knot where a fifth might have been driven and was not; through the finger's-width door the river kept \lore{water over water}, a low thing over a lower thing, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming and not numbered among the hearings. Three counts in three places. Agreeing any two would have wanted a payer put under both, and no payer stood, so the three stayed where they were. The file lay open on \place{the towel} at the head of the bed with every object on its own place and every gap left gap. Two hundred and thirty-one honest answers stood on the sixth when the file was opened.

In the small hours, with the yard quiet and the river's note at its lowest under the finger's-width door, the hand outside the wool went up an inch toward the sound. It took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole, let the intake go on the same low note, and came straight down and laid itself back against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. The note had done before the hand came down, so the letting-go was neither early again nor stayed, and the hand did not stay. The inch was not a reach and the intake was not a breath kept for warmth; the interval was not a stopping of the river and the note was not his. He filed it as answer two hundred and thirty-two: exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment, borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the five arithmetics, the bell's gap, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the ninth hand at the well, the stayed object, the early letting-go nor his own kept gaps. Two hundred and thirty-two honest answers stood on the sixth place, each an object and not a power, and neither the number nor the small hand-movings that had made it stood up and called themselves a doctrine, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood.

The surgeon took his small clean satisfaction in a field left as found and in an answer entered where nobody could see it. The infant underneath took none of it. One stiff brown-edged crust lay two inches from his ear with its empty place unnamed and no third; the thread lay frozen and unwiped; cold had stopped at cheek, temple and the wrist-joint as at the end of its line and the afterdrop waited past a warming nobody would perform. He wanted the crust lifted and the thread wiped and a hand that would close on the pot handle. Both were true and the second was louder and neither won, and the argument went on without a verdict. The boy's attention had passed from the strip in the sleeve to the reading one morning ahead without joining the two, checked by the old rule that two likenesses are not a cause and a rule wants a payer; Bone 30 stood where it stood and was not brought into a day that had not earned it. Three hundred and twenty-eight stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in Low Kiln, Sill and Grath; six months and one hundred and forty-eight days were told by nobody; the carried fire's fifth was unpaid the same number of days with its payer unnamed; and the name of the man the coat kept was not spoken under the roof the same number. Nothing on the twenty-ninth had been opened, joined, named, called, paid or ended. The keeping of the number was the only honest work there had been to do, and it had bought nothing at two inches of air and was not for sale, and it went on because attention is the one thing no institution can tax. At the kindling the thumb was drawn back down to the six where the house could check, and the knife lay at the edge of its sill with the seventh notch uncut and the unforged sentence beside it, and outside the wool the hand lay back against the fold on answer two hundred and thirty-two while the river's low note went quiet under the finger's-width door and Bone went on without a boundary drawn.
