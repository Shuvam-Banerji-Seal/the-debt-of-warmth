Not yet reached, the house waited at the end of \place{the clay road} the way a pot waits on a shelf with its handle turned inward. \person{Sella Vail} carried \person[Corin]{the boy} down at the half-pace she kept from every open slate, the basket held as a vessel is held filled to the lip that must not be slopped, and the instruction went to the wool and to the river wind and never to the basket. \emph{Keep it off his ears. It's the river wind}, she told the fold, and told the wind nothing, for the wind in this district had never once asked anybody to be told anything. The market thinned behind them as the road took it, the last of Bone's three readings going up ahead of them in a locked box with six true subtractions inside and no signature on the space where the price should have stood. Two hundred people had heard the figure twice and gone back to their pigs; under the roof this came to nothing anybody would enter.

He took the road and then the yard as a field is taken before the light, by the small sounds first, because the small sounds do not lie. One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from his ear, gone harder in the dry kiln heat, its corner standing a hair off the wool; the empty place beside it kept its name to itself and no third had come in on the thirtieth to make the row come out even. The thread of drool at his mouth had frozen and was unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread and a basket set down was a pot set down and left. Cold had stopped at cheek, temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, as at the end of a line written out for it with neither an order to go further nor an order to go away; \lore{the Afterdrop} waited beyond the stopping, past a warming nobody in this house would perform. He knew the composed face that is not composure, the ease cold puts on a face which is no ease, the patient who looks better before he is worse because a cold vessel cannot clot and the blood stands where it should not; such a patient must be warmed from the outside and slowly, never hurried, never rubbed, never set too near the fire. He wanted the crust lifted the two inches to his mouth, the thread wiped away, a hand on the pot handle brought within reach. Both were true and the second was louder and neither won. The argument went on under the wool without verdict or witness, and the argument was the work.

In \place{the Clay Yard} the seven places went by with a look and a wide step and no number: the clean dark line at the Vail corner with the finer grey hairline beside it called \lore{the thaw}, unswept both; the space beneath \place{the lowest rack} where the broken birth-slates lay; the round ring on the well's stone lip where the shallow clay pan had once stood, with no scrape, crumb, print, chip or word; the skinned \place{the trough}; the field's empty place out on \place{the worked strips}; the returning-line place where a hand had gone in on Seed 15 and had not come out. No eighth likeness stood up among them and no cause was laid across any two. The three coverings kept a finger between every pair: the frost gone off the well's north lip on Bone 3 while the yard looked elsewhere, which was not a sign; the skin on the trough breaking and closing under the knock of a pail; the silt-clouded plate of ice on \place{the flat stone by the well}, standing out of the path of feet. All three were weather and were entered nowhere. \person{Garl Vail}'s ridge was the clean dark line and the new grey beside it, and neither had been swept since the year turned.

Bone went out as it had come on, without a boundary drawn. The older women named it by the weather that had not come and the slatemen by their count of Account-days, and neither habit was set right or left wrong in so many words; the month carried both names the way a pot carries two handles and is none the worse. Nothing in the going of the light was a sign of what the dark month would bring, and the boy entered the yard as he found it.

\scenebreak

A man come down from \place{Cold Acre} waited at the well with a hare across his shoulders, the hind legs tied and the weight judged by the carrying arm and not by any figure. A woman with a pot asked what the carrying had cost. The man shifted the weight onto his other shoulder and told her to ask the weather, which had carried the snow in and could carry the price out, and went off toward the clay road with nobody weighing the hare and nobody opening a slate. The boy read it as a chart left blank where the number should stand and entered neither the hare nor the answer. Another man stood with a cracked jar in a rope sling, the rope bitten in where the crack had opened, and asked what the month's mending would want. Sella, passing with the basket, told him to ask the pot; and the pot, being a pot, said nothing. No figure came off the jar and nothing was entered.

At \place{the kindling} a woman with a basket of kindling asked what the seventh year would want when it came. Two men mending a strip of fence said the thaw would be early and late, one with a spike in his fist and one with his thumb on the rail. \person{Tarn} said \emph{Yes, house} to each, agreeing so thoroughly that there was nowhere for an argument to find a foot; and he kept the late, because a late thaw puts off the paying, though the paying had stood in the ruts since the water went. The woman went off with her basket and the men with their strip, and no collector came up the clay road or went down it.

The small knife lay at the very edge of its sill where the house could check it and where a man's hand could also slip it off; six years stood in the worn thumb-mark where the house could look, the seventh at the lip beyond where it could not, and Garl's unforged collector sentence lay beside it with no name on the man who turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on the debt. Tarn had carried his thumb up to that uncut notch on Bone 26, rested it there the length of one breath and drawn it back to the six, telling the wood \emph{Six counted. The next I don't count} in the tone of a man stating weather; he had not done it again on the twenty-eighth or the twenty-ninth and did not do it on the thirtieth. The sentence about an ear under this roof being worth more than the loan that held it had come up to the word that would call the loan and stopped there, a ninth stopping with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end, and still ungiven. The breath that should have gone in at the top of the reach stayed unspent, a small interval at the top of every reach; his chest had been tight since the dark. \place{Ash} was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line and no scrubbing lifted it, and the pale clip-ring at his left wrist held what little light there was and gave it back.

\scenebreak

The boy kept the held things as he had found them, each on the place of its own, and checked them by not making them again. Tarn's thumb's resting on the uncut seventh was one object and was not made again on Bone 28, 29 or 30: not a tenth stopping, not the seventh year given, not a payment, not a fifth held change, not a hearing, not a mending and not a rule. A finger went between it and the nine stoppings, the four held changes, the handful and his own kept gaps. It had been a touch that took nothing and paid nothing, and he kept it because it was true and not because it had bought anything. The handful let fall back through her fingers at \place{the rack end} on Bone 22 stayed one object and was not made again; the staying at the inch of Bone 15 and the early letting-go of Bone 17 stayed two objects on two places and were not made again. Four held changes sat on four places and were not a rule. Nothing had stood up and called itself the fifth of anything.

Six arithmetics of the year, with their true subtractions, rode locked up the clay road in \person{Berr Clay}'s box toward \place{High Kiln}; one in twelve stood unchallenged, the figure read was still not the figure paid, no signature stood on the space between, and six were six and not one account. The finding sat on a place of its own with no payer under it, not a lesson, not a grievance, not a mending, not a doctrine and not a rule, and was not laid across the house's \lore{not-doings}, the dressed grain covered under wool and read over by a number that did not know its cost, the four held changes, the handful, the thumb's resting, the two small objects of the hand or his own gaps. The edges came near to touching. The finger went between. No doctrine stood up out of any of it, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood. The reading had answered nothing for the hand that had gone in on Seed 15 and did not come out, nothing for the man carried out on Day 188, nothing for the grain under wool, nothing for the silence where the fifth pull of \lore{the carried fire} should have stood.

The whole tray was checked and left as it lay. The burned slate stood at five with no sixth, no witness and no hand at the flame. The ownerless row stood at five askings with one shaping and no witness, and expressly no sixth; the well-saying stood at three shapings with \person{Hessa Rale}'s eighth hand on its own place and a finger between, and no fourth had been made a cause. The shut-room tellings stood at two. Three pot sayings stood with the Smoke 14 note apart on its own place. Nine record-first hands stood with \person[Ilva]{Ilva}'s ninth in a sleeve and no tenth, the wood keeping its count where no slate could take a figure off it, and she was not made a reader of her own keeping. Nine stoppings stood with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end, the loan uncalled. Six hearings of the full note stood beside and never among, the seventh place empty and none invented to fill it, the sixth the small flat knock out of a woman as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go and never among the things that had come out of vessels. Five openings in five months stood with the sixth far off at \place{Ash} 2 and not brought near. Seven green stood a colour and seven shape a shape with no eighth. The heard bell stayed in its square beside the told bell of \lore{the Long Night}, and no collector came up or went down the clay road.

Garl stayed where he had been kept, in objects and not in a story. The salt-stiff coat kept the crease at the left shoulder on its peg behind the door; the wheel-seat in the shed kept its twenty-year hollow on that side and nobody filled it; the clean dark line and the finer grey kept their places unswept. The twice-mended pot stood on \place{the pot board} with the hairline of the original break showing between the old-brass staple and the tighter wire, and both proverbs went to the pot and to the fire and never to a person, so that the repair stayed apart from restoration. The debt against a firing of tiles circled without a name, neither paid nor settled nor done with and worse for being uncalled. His name had not been spoken under this roof three hundred and twenty-nine days, and the carried fire's fifth had been unpaid the same number with its payer unnamed; no lesson had been made of any of it.

\person{Jorra Vail} came through the finger's-width door off \place{the Vail wheel-shed} at the going of the light with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bent little finger and white in a fist-crease where a hand had opened, and it was not the bone-coloured \lore{slip} of the wheel. Three or more men had gone low into \place{the back room} before first grey and he gave the house no name for them and did not say the room was cold. He kept dusty forearms and clean ones on one tray and parted them as clot is parted from vessel, asking neither which slate had been broken nor whether it was a birth-slate. He spoke to the wheel before he spoke to any person --- \emph{It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady} --- and told a pot \emph{You'll do} in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either. He knew \lore{the full note} that pot would give three turns ahead, with air under it and a want in it, and kept the knowing on the near-full tray beside the hearings and never among them; he would not say it before a kiln-master come up from High Kiln who would call it early and make a song of it. When Sella had not finished telling him the fire was low, his bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper} ahead of her unfinished sentence, took the cold out of the fingertip, moved the tongue a hair's breadth and left it standing, so that the end of her instruction arrived at the pot and the household straightened around his silence. One piece of iron had two bites, the holding and the taking, and there was no \place{Grathic} word for both mercies. \emph{He's not Vail} stayed sealed as tightly as a lid nobody had tried to lift in three years, and the promise of the boy's name to the men who burn slates was neither given nor withdrawn.

At the going of the light Sella carried him up from the yard at the half-pace from every open slate, went wide around the seven walked-around places with a look and a wide step and no number, and set him on \place{the bed} under the roof and never in \place{the Debt Loft}. There was a sound from above and behind only, a foot on a stair that did not come down, and no interior was entered. She put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face, let her hand rest the half-beat longer than the setting needed before it went away, and left the thread where it lay. She said nothing about where they had been or what had been read over the basket, and the instruction went to the fire, the wool and the pot and never to the basket. \emph{The house will keep it}, she said where another woman would have said I. \lore{The widow's mark} hung where it hung and was not touched, because a going of the light and a bed are not openings and five openings in five months are five and not a rule; the sixth stood far off at Ash 2 and was not brought near. She went to \place{the hearth} before she went to the basket, told the coals it was the thaw and turned them with the back of a broken pot, and the fire answered four low pulls with the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer unnamed three hundred and twenty-nine days. Her finger lay on top of \place{the loaf shelf}'s front edge so the charcoal tally stayed unread; her lips moved along a two-gapped row and figured neither gap; she glanced once at the clay jar standing cold under \lore{lead glaze} and shut her hand half over nothing, the little-door burn on the back of her left hand closing on air. The twice-mended pot came up with her thumb first where the wire crossed the crack and both proverbs went to the pot and the fire with the crack left showing. The dressed grain lay turned under wool on the cool stone with no figure and no handful lifted; her fingers went in and came out and did not close. The pot rim stood two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat and no look toward the bed. The file lay open on \place{the towel} at the head of the bed with every object on its own place and every gap left gap.

Under the floor the carried fire gave four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood. Overhead \place{the boards} kept four nails on the near board, four on the next, and a thumb-width knot where a fifth might have been driven and was not. Through the finger's-width door \lore{water over water} went on as a low thing over a lower thing, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming, not numbered among the hearings. Three counts in three places, and none rose into another, because agreeing any two would have wanted a payer put under both and none stood to any. The bell's gap left in the square was not fetched in to make a fourth, and the heard bell stayed in its square beside the told and the told figure beside the paid with no signature between.

The hand outside the wool went up an inch toward the river's note. It took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole, let the intake go on the same low note, and came straight down and laid itself back against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. The note had done before the hand came down, so the letting-go was neither early again nor stayed, and the hand did not stay. The inch was not a reach and the intake was not a breath kept for warmth; the interval was not a stopping of the river and the note was not his. He filed it as answer two hundred and thirty-four: exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment, borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the six arithmetics, the bell's gap, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the stayed object, the early letting-go nor his own kept gaps. Two hundred and thirty-four honest answers stood on the sixth place, each an object and not a power, and neither the number nor the small hand-movings that had made it stood up and called themselves a doctrine, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood.

The surgeon took his small clean satisfaction in a field left as found and in an answer entered where nobody could see it; the infant underneath took none of it. One stiff brown-edged crust lay two inches from his ear with its empty place unnamed and no third; the thread lay frozen and unwiped; cold had stopped at cheek, temple and the wrist-joint as at the end of its line and the afterdrop waited past a warming nobody would perform. He wanted the crust lifted and the thread wiped and a hand on the pot handle. Both were true and the second was louder and neither won, and the two registers quarrelled without a verdict in the small hours under the roof. Three hundred and twenty-nine stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill} and \place{Grath}; six months and one hundred and forty-nine days were told by nobody; the carried fire's fifth was unpaid the same number of days with its payer unnamed; and the name of the man the salt-stiff coat kept was unspoken the same number. Nothing on the thirtieth had been opened, joined, named, called, paid or ended. Bone went out without a boundary drawn and neither of the district's two habits of naming its months was set right, and the keeping of the number was the only honest work there had been to do; it had bought nothing at two inches of air and was not for sale, and it went on because attention is the one thing no institution can tax. The silt-clouded plate of ice stood on its flat stone out of the path of feet; the file lay open on the towel with every gap left gap; the hand lay back against the fold on answer two hundred and thirty-four; and the river's low note went quiet under the finger's-width door while Bone went out without a boundary drawn. Ash 1 stood one morning ahead and Ash 2, the sixth opening, two mornings ahead, and neither was brought into a day that had not earned it.
