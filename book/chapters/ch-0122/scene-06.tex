\person[Corin]{The boy} took the afternoon's small sounds first, because the small sounds do not lie and the large ones are mostly weather. A pail went down on its stone at \place[the yard well]{the well} with the flat knock of a thing set where a thing has always been set, and the rope settled into its knot with the stiff dry rasp that cold puts into hemp, a sound without any grain of intention in it. A loaf was pushed a finger's width along a board and stopped; nobody called the stopping a figure. Barley passed from wall to wall in the breath between two voices and was never made into a number. The yard had not been told what the day was, and the basket had not been told to be still.

A woman came to the well with a pail and set it down where the other pail had stood, and the rope gave its small rasp again. She did not look at the basket. “Mind the rope,” she said in the tone of a person telling a pot where to stand, not looking up from the pail. “It'll take a finger in this cold.” Another woman, passing with barley held against her breast in a fold of her apron, said, “It took mine last week,” and went on about her business, and the first woman went back to the pail. The boy took the order of it apart by the place of each part: the hand on the rope, the eye on the pail, the mouth after both, and the half-beat lying in the gap where a name would have stood. There was no name in the gap. He gave the women no interior and the words no owner beyond the mouths they came out of. A voice in a yard is a voice in a yard, and the cold takes a finger and takes another; that was the whole of what the words were for. The pail went down on its stone, and the yard went on around it.

He went around \place{the seven walked-around places} once and not twice, with a look and a wide step and no number: the stone lip, the worn boards, the strip of hard mud, the place where a wheel had stood, the two narrow gaps between the stacked pots, and \place{the returning-line place}, widest of all, where a hand had gone in on \emph{Seed 15} and had not come back. The wide step was a keeping and not a sign. A figure set against that space would want a payer standing under it, and none stood. He did not look twice; looking twice is how a finding becomes a habit, and a habit wants the hand that put the thing there.

\place[the trough]{The trough} stood bare and dark. The thin skin that had lain on it through the turn was gone, and the rim kept the line where the water had been, a lower country marked on the side of a vessel. The gone pan held its shallow round print on a place of its own, and he did not lay that ring across the loaf's stopping or across the ridge at the corner. At \place{the Vail corner} the clean dark line kept its finer grey beside it, thickened in the dry cold, with the faint sour tang of \lore{Ash} coming up off it where no broom had gone in two years. The called-gone grey stood beside the left-in-place ridge with a finger between. No agreement had been made to stop looking at the ridge, and a thing left in place is not a thing forgiven. The tang was thinner in this afternoon's air and the grey lay a hair less distinct along one stretch. He found the softening where put and did not look twice, for a finding looked at twice becomes a habit, and a habit is an account that has gone and found its payer. He did not make the softening a sign, a fourth covering or a payment on a tend-day that had earned only its twenty-fifth.

He had once read a field at rest the way other men read a room: by where the weight had been and where it had been lifted away. The trough's line was a level taken in a vessel nobody had calibrated; the ridge at the corner was a surface drawn together over a cold cavity that would not close by being looked at. He knew the difference between a thing closed and a thing only surface-together, and he knew that a face composed by cold is not composure. The yard gave him no instrument and no light to work by, only the small sounds and the habit of arriving in the order they arrived, and the infant in him had no use for any of it. The infant wanted the thing at his ear gone. The surgeon wanted the line on the trough left exactly as it was. Both were true, and the yard kept its own arithmetic in pails and gaps, and neither of them could change a single figure of it.

Through \place{the finger's-width door} \place{the river} kept its note of \lore{water over water}, a low thing over a lower thing under the winter skin, softer at one edge where the low note had run nearer under it. The note came from under the covering and not from the covering; it was not a hearing, not one of \lore{the three coverings}, not a seventh of anything and not a payer, and no hand stood under it. He found it where put and called it weather. Four low pulls breathed below \place{the boards} with the silence where a fifth should have stood, and nobody lifted the floor to supply it.

Costs came to the yard as directions and went away as weather and left no entry. A man come down \place{the clay road} brought a number up out of a shut fist toward the well, held it where the light could see it, looked at it, and put it back into his sleeve; it arrived nowhere. He took the order apart as he took every field apart --- fist, eye, mouth, the half-beat lying in the gap --- and gave the fist no figure and the sleeve no motive, for a motive is a payer wearing a coat. A woman sent to ask what a pot cost was sent to the pot and got a note and not a price. The dressed grain lay covered and unfigured at \place{the rack end}; the figure read over it in its own morning had not known what it cost, and no signature stood between the figure and the price. \person{Berr Clay}'s two readings of the year stood behind one unsigned figure beside the twelve old arithmetics of Y.H. 813, two readings two and not one account, the first not made a thirteenth by the second, the figure read still not the figure paid, no doctrine out of two. He did not lay the twelve across the two to make a row come out even. The key to the box had gone up the clay road in a coat pocket that never came back down, and paper is not a payer.

The keepers stood where the morning had left them. The soft-cuffed clerk's entered figure, \person{Tarn}'s degree toward \place{the out-of-line landing} below \place{the north bridge}, and the face at the yard's edge stayed on three places with a finger between every pair; the figure was a report brought up the yard and never a sighting, the calling of no loan and no rule. The first man's face, the second man's direction and his narrow slate turned to the sleeve, and the asking that had come up among the bucket-round stayed each on a place of its own with a finger between every pair. No name, owner, answer carried, witness, reader, sale, tenth hand or paying stood under any of them. The asking was not joined to Tarn's valuable ear or to \person{Jorra Vail}'s ungiven promise and was not called the calling of the loan. A reaching with no hand under it gathers interest toward a far-off calling and a far-off hand, and is not that calling and not that hand. It was not told again on an afternoon that had earned only its twenty-fifth, and the direction was not made a custom by being remembered.

The fifth shaping of \lore{the well-saying} sat on its own tray between \person[Hessa Rale]{Hessa}'s eighth record-first hand and the saying of the road from \place{the Cross} to \place{Greybank}, with a third finger between it and the four ownerless directions; Hessa's narrow slate stayed unread up the road and her hand stayed eighth. \person{Ilva}'s notched strip stayed in her sleeve with her ninth hand nine, and no figure came off the wood. The green at \place{the pot board} and the shape at \place{the market's edge} stayed sightings with no eighth of either. Six hearings of \lore{the full note} stood beside and never among, with the seventh place empty and not dug for, and \place{the Drawing house}'s flat knock stayed outside them on its own ground; no seventh came on this day, and the boy did not go looking for one.

At the going of the light \person{Sella Vail} came to \place{the hearth} before she came to the basket. She told the coals it was \lore{the thaw} in the voice another woman would have used for a child who could not hear her, and turned them with the back of a broken pot. The back scraped over them and left the blind ash where it lay, and the coals gave off the dry smell of a fire that had been banked and not forgotten. She gave the small intake, the hold and the letting-go toward no opening, figuring neither of the two gaps. \emph{The house will keep it} went to the fire and not to the basket, because a thing said aloud becomes a thing the Court can enter. Her finger lay along the top of \place{the loaf shelf} so the charcoal tally beneath stayed unread, and her lips moved on a two-gapped row with neither gap figured. One glance went to the clay jar standing cold under \lore{lead glaze} beyond the pot board; the hand shut half over nothing, and the little-door burn on the back of her left hand closed on air and went back to the pot. She said nothing of the figure sent twice over a basket, nothing of the man whose slate had gone down the road with the writing turned away, nothing of the softening nobody owned. The instruction went to the pot, the fire and the wool, and never to the thing in the wicker.

She said to the fire, in the tone of a woman who has told the same thing to the same fire for three winters, \emph{The fire doesn't know you're sorry. Feed it}, and put two fingers of coal where the coals had thinned, and did not look to see whether the basket had heard. Her hands went on counting when they had nothing to do --- three spoons, a nail, the small breath a child makes that nobody numbers --- and came back to the hairline at the wire as hands come back to a wound they have dressed. The burn on the back of her left hand folded itself shut over the empty air where a cup would have been, and she lifted the pot without looking at the bed.

Her thumb went first to where the tighter wire crossed the crack, and only then did the weight come on the handle. The hairline of the original break showed between the old-brass staple and the wire, a dark thread with a hand's work on either side, and both proverbs went to the pot and the fire and never to a person: \emph{Leave the crack showing. People trust a thing they can see has been handled} and \emph{If it's worth mending it's worth mending twice. The second time is the real one}. Repair was not restoration; a restored thing hides the hand that handled it, and a mended thing leaves the hand showing, so that a vessel carrying its break can be read by the next woman who takes it up. At \place{the rack end} she turned the dressed grain piece by piece under the wool with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child, set each down where it had lain, took no figure from it and no handful, and drew the wool back with the flat of her hand. Then she set the pot rim two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat and no look toward \place{the bed}. The half-beat was the distance between the intention and the arrest; she did not measure it and did not name it, for a named gap wants a payer put under it and none stood.

She carried him up the nineteen steps at the half-pace she keeps from every open slate, correcting the tilt at every turn of the landing before it settled into the wicker. The instruction went to the wool, the river wind, the pot, the fire and the banked fire, and never to the basket; the basket was not told what the day was and was not told to be still. She went around the seven walked-around places once and not twice, with a look and a wide step and no number, widest at the returning-line place. She set him under the roof at the yard's edge where the light came sideways and never in \place{the Debt Loft}. \lore{The widow's mark} hung where it hung and was not touched: an afternoon, a hearth, a shelf, a yard and a going of the light are not openings, seven openings in seven months are seven and not a rule, and no eighth came due on a day that had earned only its twenty-fifth. She put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face and added no second fold. The thread stayed stiff and unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever given an instruction about the thread.

\place{The towel} came out at the head of the bed as instruments come out when a case is done and before the next is called, rough from the wash and smelling of tallow and low smoke. He went over the day by the place of each thing and left every gap a gap.

The surgeon in him laid the day out on the towel the way he had once laid out instruments, each thing in the place the next hand would expect it, handles out and edges in, nothing that could roll. Exactness was the whole of the tray and worth nothing at two inches of air; a field read correctly does not bleed less for being read. He had once been able to name what a man would not survive by the colour of his lips and the way he held his shoulder, and here he could not lift his own hand to his own chin. Both kinds of knowing lay on the towel with a finger between them, and he did not lay one across the other to make a row come out even, for paper is not a payer and the body under the wool kept its own count in cold and crust and thread.

The clerk's entered figure, Tarn's degree and the face at the yard's edge stayed on three places with a finger between every pair. The first man's face, the second man's slate and direction and the well-asking stayed on places of their own with a finger between every pair, and no name, owner, answer carried, witness, sale, tenth hand or rule stood under any of them; the asking was a reaching gathering interest toward the far-off calling and the far-off hand, and was neither. The fifth shaping stood on its tray between the eighth hand and the road saying with a third finger between it and the four ownerless directions, and Hessa's slate and Ilva's strip stayed unread with their hands eighth and ninth and no tenth. The sentence at \place{the kindling} was found where put and not made a habit; \lore{the tile debt} circled without a figure and the loan stayed uncalled; the softening at the river's skin was found where put and not made a sign. Berr's low saying about kindness was entered on a place of its own with no payer standing, and his \emph{your house} stood beside it, the speaker taken out of his own sentence as a hand is drawn from a field before the dressing goes on; his belief stayed up the road with the box.

The twelve old arithmetics stood behind one unsigned figure beside the first and second readings of the year --- two readings two and not one account, the first not made a thirteenth by the second, the figure read still not the figure paid, no signature on the space and no doctrine out of two. Six hearings of the full note stood beside and never among, with the seventh place empty and the Drawing's flat knock outside on its own ground. The heard bell stayed in its square beside the told, four, four and a gap, and its gap was not folded into \lore{the carried fire}'s four and a gap below the boards, the boards' four and four and a thumb-width knot, or the two readings behind one unsigned figure beside the twelve: three counts in three places staying three, because agreeing any two would want a payer put under both and none stood. Nine record-first hands stood with no tenth and nine stoppings with no tenth; four rooms kept the same gap and no Court room was a fifth likeness. \person{Garl Vail}'s name had gone unspoken four hundred and nineteen days and the fifth pull below the floor had gone unpaid the same number, the fire and the man standing side by side without becoming one. \lore{The keeping at the fold} was found where put and stayed a finding and not a placing, with no second fold, third crust, name, gift, payment or payer.

The hand outside the wool came up an inch toward the note. The inch was not a reach. He took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole on the note's own finishing under the covering --- neither early nor stayed --- and let it go as the note ended. Then the hand came straight down and laid itself flat against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. He did not wipe the thread and did not lift the crust. Four low pulls breathed under the floor and the fifth was not supplied.

Answer four hundred and fourteen came out and was filed on the sixth place. It was exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the twelve readings, the first and second of the new count, the printed figure, the fifth shaping, the clerk's entered figure, the well-asking, the second man's slate, Tarn's degree, the face at the yard's edge, Jorra's ungiven promise, the withheld sentence, the softening, the bell's gap, the flat knock, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the held breath, the two bites of iron, the winter skin, the widow's mark nor the keeping at the fold. Six honest answers stood on the sixth place, 409 through 414, two to each of its three days, and not one was a doctrine.

Count four hundred and nineteen exact, six months and two hundred and thirty-nine days told by nobody who would have known what a number counted. He did not stand it up as a threshold or a figure and borrowed no payer from the arithmetics gone up the road, for paper is not a payer. No eighth opening, seventh hearing, sixth shaping, tenth hand, tenth stopping, eleventh arithmetic, signature, payer, witness or reader stood on any of the three days.

The surgeon's exactness and the infant's want stood side by side and neither bought the other; under them, louder, the wanting went on. The crust lifted two inches, the thread wiped, a hand on the handle of the pot. He wanted the three things without knowing that wanting them was a want and got none of them. Both were true. The second was louder. Neither won, and no verdict was looked for; the quarrel was the work, and the work went on in silence in a room whose floor does not touch his. The asking stayed where put on its own place with a finger between it and the direction and the slate, not told again, not joined, not called; a voice in a yard was a voice in a yard and not a door. The well had given back the weather. Nothing had been paid, named or ended, and the house went on as a house goes when a pot is set down in its place and the fire kept --- forward, into a morning not yet lived and not brought into this one.
