The afternoon came on without a bell and without a word said over the basket. The yard kept the small grammar it had kept all morning, and \person[Corin]{the boy} took the small sounds first because they do not lie and the large ones are mostly weather. A pail went down on its stone with the small solid knock a pail makes when it is set where a pail has always stood. The rope settled into its knot with the stiff dry rasp that cold puts into hemp, a sound with no grain of intention in it. A loaf was pushed a finger's width along a board and stopped there, and nobody called the stopping a figure. Barley went from wall to wall in the breath between two voices and was never made into a number. Nothing in \place{the Clay Yard} had been told what the day was, and the basket had not been told to be still.

He went around \place{the seven walked-around places} once and not twice, with a look and a wide step and no number, as he had at morning and as he would again at the going of the light. They kept their places: the stone lip, the worn boards, the strip of hard mud, the place where a wheel had stood, the two narrow gaps between the stacked pots, and \place{the returning-line place}, widest of all, where a hand had gone in on \emph{Seed 15} and had not come out. The wide step was a keeping and not a sign; a figure set against that space would want a payer standing under it, and none stood. He did not look twice. Looking twice is how a finding becomes a habit, and a habit wants a hand that put the thing there.

\place[the trough]{The trough} stood bare and dark. The thin skin that had lain on it through the turn was gone, and the rim still showed the line where the water used to be. The gone pan kept its shallow round print on a place of its own; he did not lay that ring across the loaf's stopping or across the ridge. On \place{the pot board} the twice-mended pot kept the hairline of its first break showing between the old-brass staple and the tighter wire, a dark thread with a hand's work on either side, left showing for the next woman who took it up. Nothing had been agreed about the ridge, and a thing left in place was not a thing forgiven.

At \place{the Vail corner} the clean dark line kept its finer grey hairline beside it --- \lore{the thaw} thickened in the dry cold, with the faint sour tang of \lore{Ash} coming up off it where no broom had gone in two years. The sour tang was thinner in the air that had come off \place{the river} on the nineteenth. The called-gone grey stood beside the left-in-place ridge with a finger between; no agreement had been made to stop looking at it and no hand had come to own it. The grey lay a hair less distinct along one stretch, and the river's skin was softer at one edge where the low note ran nearer under it. He found the softening where put and did not look twice, for looking twice is how a finding becomes a habit. He did not make it a sign, a fourth covering of \lore{the three coverings}, a sixth shaping or a payment on a day that had earned only its twenty-fourth, and no hand and no payer stood under it.

A neighbour said the air had turned. Another said it would be back before morning. Neither asked the other what they meant, and nobody owned the day, and the yard went on at the pace of people who have learned to leave the weather alone.

Costs came to the yard as directions and went away as weather and left no entry. A man come down \place{the clay road} brought a number up out of a shut fist toward \place[the yard well]{the well}, held it where the light could see it, looked at it, and put it back into his sleeve. It arrived nowhere. He took the order apart as he took every field apart --- fist, eye, mouth, the half-beat lying in the gap --- and gave the fist no figure and the sleeve no motive, for a motive is a payer wearing a coat. A woman sent to ask what a pot cost was sent to the pot and got a note and not a price. At \place{the rack end} the dressed grain lay covered and unfigured; the figure read over it in its own morning had not known what it cost, and no signature stood between the figure and the price. Paper is not a payer.

The twelve old arithmetics stood behind one unsigned figure beside the first and second readings of the year, two readings two and not one account, the first not made a thirteenth by the second, the figure read still not the figure paid. He did not lay the twelve across the two to make a row come out even. The key to \person{Berr Clay}'s box had gone up the clay road in a coat pocket that never came back down, and there was no key under any roof and none in his hand.

The clerk's entered figure, \person{Tarn}'s degree toward \place{the out-of-line landing} below \place{the north bridge}, and the face at the yard's edge stayed on three places with a finger between every pair; the figure was a report brought up the yard and never a sighting, the calling of no loan, no tenth hand and no rule. The first man's face, the second man's direction and his narrow slate turned to the sleeve, and the asking that had come up among the bucket-round were kept each on a place of its own with a finger between every pair. No name, owner, answer carried, witness, reader, sale, tenth hand or paying stood under any of them. The asking was not joined to Tarn's valuable ear or to \person[Jorra Vail]{Jorra}'s ungiven promise and was not called the calling of the loan; a reaching with no hand under it gathers interest toward a far-off calling and a far-off hand, and is not that calling and not that hand. \person{Ilva} was offstage with her count still in wood and her ninth hand nine, and no figure came off the strip. \person[Hessa Rale]{Hessa}'s slate stayed unread up the road and her hand stayed eighth.

The fifth shaping of \lore{the well-saying} sat on its own tray between Hessa's eighth record-first hand and the saying of the road from \place{the Cross} to \place{Greybank}, with a third finger between it and the four ownerless directions, and no sixth was made due. Six hearings of \lore{the full note} stood beside and never among with the seventh place empty and not dug for, and \place{the Drawing house}'s flat knock stayed outside them on its own ground. Nothing was repeated, and the direction was not made a custom by being remembered.

\person{Sella Vail} came to \place{the hearth} before she came to the basket. She told the coals it was the thaw in the voice another woman would have used for a child who could not hear her, and turned them with the back of a broken pot. The back of the pot scraped over them and left the blind ash where it lay, and the coals gave off the dry smell of a fire that had been banked and not forgotten. She gave the small intake, the hold and the letting-go toward no opening, figuring neither of the two gaps. \emph{The house will keep it} went to the fire and not to the basket, because a thing said aloud becomes a thing the Court can enter. Her finger lay along the top of \place{the loaf shelf} so the charcoal tally beneath stayed unread, and her lips moved on a two-gapped row with neither gap figured. One glance went to the clay jar standing cold under \lore{lead glaze} beyond \place{the pot board}; the hand shut half over nothing, and the little-door burn on the back of her left hand closed on air, and went back to the pot. She said nothing of the figure gone twice over a basket, nothing of the man whose slate had gone down the road with the writing turned away, nothing of the softening nobody owned. The instruction went to the pot, the fire and the wool, and never to the thing in the wicker.

Her thumb went first to where the tighter wire crossed the crack, and only then did the weight come on the handle. The hairline of the original break showed between the old-brass staple and the wire, a dark thread with a hand's work on either side, and both proverbs went to the pot and the fire and never to a person: \emph{Leave the crack showing. People trust a thing they can see has been handled} and \emph{If it's worth mending it's worth mending twice. The second time is the real one}. Repair was not restoration; a restored thing hides the hand that handled it, and a mended thing leaves the hand showing, and a vessel that carries its break can be read by the next woman who takes it up. At the rack end she turned the dressed grain piece by piece under the wool with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child, set each down where it had lain, took no figure from it and no handful, and drew the wool back with the flat of her hand. Then she set the pot rim two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat and no look toward \place{the bed}. The half-beat was the distance between the intention and the arrest; she did not measure it and did not name it, for a named gap wants a payer put under it and none stood.

She carried him down the nineteen steps at the half-pace she keeps from every open slate, correcting the tilt at every turn of the landing before it settled into the wicker. The instruction went to the wool, the river wind, the pot, the fire and the banked fire and never to the basket; the basket was not told what the day was and was not told to be still. In the yard a pail went down on its stone where a pail always stood and the rope settled into its knot with its small dry rasp. She went around the seven walked-around places once and not twice with a look and a wide step and no number, widest at the returning-line place, where a hand had gone in on Seed 15 and had not come out. She set him under the roof at the yard's edge where the light came sideways and never in \place{the Debt Loft}. \lore{The widow's mark} hung where it hung and was not touched: a morning, an afternoon, a hearth, a shelf, a yard and a going of the light are not openings, seven openings in seven months are seven and not a rule, and no eighth came due on a day that had earned only its twenty-fourth. The thread stayed stiff and unwiped, because nobody had ever given an instruction about the thread. At the low angle of the loaf shelf she put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face and added no second fold.

At \place{the kindling} and \place{the flagstones} Tarn kept six pieces in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check and the seventh at the lip beside \person{Garl Vail}'s unforged collector sentence, with no name on the man who had turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on \lore{the tile debt}. The stack held its own small arithmetic of four, four and a space, and the space was not a fifth and was not filled. The small knife lay at the very edge of its sill. His hand went over blade and sill as a man passes a hand over a tool put back where it belongs, and came away with no breath held at the top of the reach --- the coming away the whole of the checking. He cut nothing, counted nothing and spent nothing. He told the wood in the tone of a man stating the weather, \emph{Six counted. The next I don't count}. The sentence was found where put and not made a habit, not a tenth stopping, not a payment and not a rule, and it was not said again on a day that had earned only its twenty-fourth, for a second silence would be a custom and a third a way and both want a payer where none stood. The silence of no further withholding was not repeated on an unearned day. The \emph{Seed 16} breath stayed unspent and the loan stayed uncalled at the very end of a term already ended. Ash stood worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line, and the pale clip-ring at his left wrist caught and gave back what little light there was; he was helpful when he was frightened and today he was not frightened, and the household read the quiet of him the way it read the quiet of Sella. He took the broom only after it had been given its instruction and said \emph{Yes, house} to the fire, and did not turn his head toward the landing.

Jorra came through \place{the finger's-width door} off \place{the Vail wheel-shed} with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past his bent little finger and white in a fist-crease where a hand had opened --- not the bone-coloured \lore{slip} of the wheel. He kept dusty forearms and clean ones on one tray and parted them as clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had been broken nor whether it had been a birth-slate; the men unnamed who had gone low into \place{the back room} before first grey got no name from him and no word that the room was cold, and the broken ones under the lowest rack stayed entered by nobody. He spoke to the wheel before any person --- \emph{It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady} --- and told a pot \emph{You'll do} in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either. He knew the full note that pot would give three turns before it left his hands, with air under it and a want in it, and withheld the knowing from any master come up from \place{High Kiln} who would call it early and sing a pot before its time; a pot called early is a pot whose note has been spent before it was earned, and no master under that roof had ever asked who paid for the singing. Sella had not finished telling him the fire was low when his bent finger went into the cold iron of \place{the damper} ahead of her sentence; one bite of the iron held and one took, and no word in Grathic covered both mercies. He took the cold out of the fingertip, moved his tongue a hair's breadth and came out without leaving the extra holding of Ash 26 a second time, for a second would be a habit and a third a way and both want a payer where none stood. \emph{He's not Vail} stayed sealed as a lid nobody had tried to lift in three years, and the promise under the shed stayed neither given nor withdrawn.

\place[the towel]{The towel} came out at the head of the bed as instruments come out when a case is done and before the next is called, rough from the wash and smelling of tallow and low smoke. He went over the day by the place of each thing and left every gap a gap. Six hearings of the full note stood beside and never among with the seventh place empty and not dug for, and the Drawing house's flat knock stayed outside them on its own ground; no seventh came on a day that had earned only its twenty-fourth. The heard bell stayed in its square beside the told, four, four and a gap, and its gap was not folded into \lore{the carried fire}'s four and a gap under \place{the boards}, the boards' four and four and a thumb-width knot, or the two readings behind one unsigned figure beside the twelve --- three counts in three places staying three, because agreeing any two would want a payer put under both and none stood. Four rooms held the same gap with a finger between each, and no Court room stood as a fifth likeness. Nine record-first hands stood with no tenth and nine stoppings with no tenth.

Under the boards the carried fire gave four low pulls and left the silence where the fifth should have stood, and nobody lifted a floor or supplied the pull. Its payer had gone unnamed four hundred and eighteen days. \person{Garl Vail}'s name had gone unspoken the same number. Fire and man stood side by side and did not become one. \lore[the keeping at the fold]{The keeping at the fold} was found where put and stayed a finding and not a placing, with no second fold, no third crust, no name, no gift, no payment and no payer. The kept things gathered toward the far-off hand that is not his.

Through the finger's-width door the river kept its note of \lore{water over water}, a low thing over a lower thing under its winter skin, softer at one edge where the low note ran nearer. The note came from under the covering and not from the covering; it was not a hearing, not one of the three coverings, not one of the six hearings of the full note, not a seventh of anything and not a payer, and no hand stood under it. He found it where put and called it weather and did not look twice.

The hand outside the wool came up an inch toward the note. The inch was not a reach. He took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole on the note's own finishing under the covering --- neither early nor stayed --- and let it go as the note ended. Then the hand came straight down and laid itself flat against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking.

Answer four hundred and twelve came out and was filed on the sixth place. It was exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the twelve readings, the first and second of the new count, the printed figure, the fifth shaping, the clerk's entered figure, the well-asking, the second man's slate, Tarn's degree, the face at the yard's edge, Jorra's ungiven promise, the withheld sentence, the softening, the bell's gap, the flat knock, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the held breath, the two bites of iron, the winter skin, the widow's mark nor the keeping at the fold. Six honest answers stood on the sixth place, four hundred and twelve the fourth of them, and not one was a doctrine.

Count four hundred and eighteen exact, six months and two hundred and thirty-eight days told by nobody who would have known what a number counted. The carried fire's fifth was unpaid the same number and Garl's name was unspoken the same number, and the fire and the man stood side by side without becoming one. He did not stand the number up as a threshold or a figure and borrowed no payer from the arithmetics gone up the road, for paper is not a payer. No eighth opening, seventh hearing, sixth shaping, tenth hand, tenth stopping, eleventh arithmetic, signature, payer, witness, reader or payment stood on a day that had earned only its twenty-fourth.

The surgeon's exactness and the infant's want stood side by side and neither bought the other; under them, louder, the wanting went on. The crust lifted two inches, the thread wiped, a hand on the handle of the pot. He wanted the three things without knowing that wanting them was a want and got none of them. Both were true. The second was louder. Neither won, and no verdict was looked for; the quarrel was the work, and the work went on in silence in a room whose floor does not touch his. The asking stayed where put on its own place with a finger between it and the direction and the slate, not told again, not joined, not called; a voice in a yard was a voice in a yard and not a door. The well had given back the weather. The softening was weather and was kept where put. The hand lay flat on the fold on answer four hundred and twelve, and the room went on holding its heat the way a cup holds water, with care, and with one crack that nobody had mentioned.
