\daymark{417}{Ilmen 23, Y.H. 814, Thirdday}

\dropcap{T}{he towel} came out at the head of \place{the bed} as instruments come out when a case is done and before the next is called, rough from the wash and smelling of tallow and low smoke, and \person[Corin]{the boy}'s hand was already down on the fold. It lay outside the wool, flat against \lore{the keeping at the fold} on answer four hundred and eight, as a man lays a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. The small hours held \place{the Vail kiln-house} the way a shut fist holds warmth --- not to keep it, only because there was nowhere for it to go. Beneath \place{the boards} \lore{the carried fire} gave four low pulls and left the silence where the fifth should have stood, and nobody lifted a floor or supplied the pull. Its payer had gone unnamed four hundred and sixteen days. \person{Garl Vail}'s name had gone unspoken the same number. Fire and man stood side by side and did not become one.

He took the field in the old order --- the surgeon's order, the order in which a body gives up its facts to a man with steady hands and no time to waste on comfort. One stiff brown-edged barley crust stood two inches from his ear and had gone past leather into old horn in the dry nights, its corner a hair off the wool, beyond the point where a child under this roof would still have tried to eat it. Beside it the empty place held the wool folded on \emph{Stone 15}, found where put and still a finding and not a placing, with no second fold, no third crust, no name spoken over it, no gift, no payment and no payer. The thread of drool at his mouth had stiffened in the dry kiln heat and was unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread. Cold had come to cheek, temple and the small bare joint at the wrist where the wool rode back and stopped there as at the end of a line written out for it. And \lore{the Afterdrop} waited past a warming nobody in this house would perform.

He knew what a careful rewarming would be: a basket set level out of the draught, heat coming to him sideways, slow, from the outside, never rubbed, never hurried, the surface lying pink over a cold core until the core caught up. He had once stood at a bay door and sorted twenty people by what they would not survive and had kept his hands steady doing it; now he could not lift a hand two inches to his own mouth. Both kinds of knowing stood in the room and neither bought the other.

Under the exactness, louder, the wanting went on. He wanted the crust lifted. He wanted the thread wiped. He wanted a hand on the handle of the pot. The three wants quarrelled without verdict, both true, the second louder, neither winning. The quarrel was the work. He did not know that wanting them was a want; he only wanted, with the whole small animal hunger of a body four hundred and sixteen days old, and the hand outside the wool stayed where it was.

First light came through \place{the finger's-width door} without a bell and without a word said over the basket. The count became four hundred and seventeen exact --- six months and two hundred and thirty-seven days, told by nobody who would have known what a number counted. He did not stand it up as a threshold, a doctrine or a figure, and he did not bring \place{Ilmen} 30 into a morning that had earned only its twenty-third; that date stood five mornings off, a date and not a day earned. The twelve old arithmetics of Y.H. 813 stood behind one unsigned figure beside the two readings of the year, two and not one account, the first not made a thirteenth by the second, the figure read still not the figure paid, no signature on the space and no doctrine out of two. He did not lay the twelve across the two to make a row come out even, for paper is not a payer, and the key to \person{Berr Clay}'s box had gone up \place{the clay road} in a coat pocket that never comes back down.

The soft-cuffed clerk's entered figure, \person{Tarn}'s degree toward \place{the out-of-line landing} below \place{the north bridge}, and the face at the yard's edge stayed on three places with a finger between every pair; the figure was a report brought up the yard and never a sighting, the calling of no loan, no tenth hand and no rule. The fifth shaping of \lore{the well-saying} sat on its own tray between \person[Hessa Rale]{Hessa}'s eighth record-first hand and the saying of the road from \place{the Cross} to \place{Greybank}, with a third finger between it and the four ownerless directions; Hessa's narrow slate stayed unread up the road and her hand stayed eighth. \person{Ilva}'s notched strip stayed in her sleeve with her ninth hand nine. Tarn's withheld sentence and the softening at \place{the river}'s skin stayed where put and were not spent as a custom or a sign on a day that had earned only its twenty-third. Six hearings of \lore{the full note} stood beside and never among, with the seventh place empty and not dug for, and \place{the Drawing house}'s flat knock stayed outside them on its own ground. The keeping at the fold was found where put and stayed a finding.

\person{Sella Vail} came to \place{the hearth} before she came to the basket. She told the coals it was \lore{the thaw} in the voice another woman would have used for a child who could not hear her, and turned them with the back of a broken pot. She gave the small intake, the hold and the letting-go toward no opening, figuring neither of the two gaps. \emph{The house will keep it} went to the fire and not to the basket, because a thing said aloud becomes a thing the Court can enter. Her finger lay along the top of \place{the loaf shelf} so the charcoal tally beneath stayed unread, and her lips moved on a two-gapped row with neither gap figured. One glance went to the clay jar standing cold under \lore{lead glaze} beyond \place{the pot board}; the hand shut half over nothing, and the little-door burn on the back of her left hand closed on air, and went back to the pot.

Her thumb went first to where the tighter wire crossed the crack, and only then did the weight come on the handle. The hairline of the original break showed between the old-brass staple and the wire, a dark thread with a hand's work on either side, and both proverbs went to the pot and the fire and never to a person: \emph{Leave the crack showing. People trust a thing they can see has been handled} and \emph{If it's worth mending it's worth mending twice. The second time is the real one}. Repair was not restoration; a restored thing hides the hand that handled it, and a mended thing leaves the hand showing. At \place{the rack end} she turned the dressed grain piece by piece under the wool with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child, set each down where it had lain, took no figure from it and no handful, and drew the wool back. Then she set the pot rim two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat and no look toward the bed. The half-beat was the distance between the intention and the arrest; she did not measure it and did not name it, for a named gap wants a payer put under it and none stood.

She carried him down the nineteen steps at the half-pace she keeps from every open slate, correcting the tilt at every turn of the landing before it settled into the wicker. The instruction went to the wool, the river wind, the pot, the fire and the banked fire and never to the basket; the basket was not told what the day was and was not told to be still. In \place{the Clay Yard} she went around \place{the seven walked-around places} once and not twice with a look and a wide step and no number, widest at \place{the returning-line place}, where a hand had gone in on \emph{Seed 15} and had not come out. The wide step was a keeping and not a sign. She set him under the roof at the yard's edge where the light came sideways and never in \place{the Debt Loft}. \lore{The widow's mark} hung where it hung and was not touched: a morning, a well, a yard and a basket are not openings, seven openings in seven months are seven and not a rule, and no eighth came due on a day that had earned only its twenty-third.

\scenebreak

At \place{the kindling} and \place{the flagstones} Tarn kept six pieces in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check and the seventh at the lip beside Garl Vail's unforged collector sentence, with no name on the man who had turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on \lore{the tile debt}. The small knife lay at the very edge of its sill. His hand went over blade and sill as a man passes a hand over a tool put back where it belongs, and came away with no breath held at the top of the reach --- the coming away was the whole of the checking. He cut nothing, counted nothing and spent nothing. He told the wood in the tone of a man stating the weather, \emph{Six counted. The next I don't count}. The sentence was found where put and not made a habit, not a tenth stopping, not a payment and not a rule, and it was not said again on a day that had earned only its twenty-third, for a second silence would be a custom and a third a way and both want a payer where none stood. The Seed 16 breath stayed unspent and the loan stayed uncalled at the very end of a term already ended. \lore{Ash} stood worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line, and the pale clip-ring at his left wrist caught and gave back what little light there was; he was helpful when he was frightened and today he was not frightened, and the household read the quiet of him the way it read the quiet of Sella.

\person{Jorra Vail} came through the finger's-width door off \place{the Vail wheel-shed} with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past his bent little finger and white in a fist-crease where a hand had opened --- not the bone-coloured \lore{slip} of the wheel. He kept dusty forearms and clean ones on one tray and parted them as clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had been broken nor whether it had been a birth-slate; the men unnamed who had gone low into \place{the back room} before first grey got no name from him and no word that the room was cold, and the broken ones under the lowest rack stayed entered by nobody. He spoke to the wheel before any person --- \emph{It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady} --- and told a pot \emph{You'll do} in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either. He knew the full note that pot would give three turns before it left his hands, with air under it and a want in it, and withheld the knowing from any master come up from \place{High Kiln} who would call it early and sing a pot before its time. Sella had not finished telling him the fire was low when his bent finger went into the cold iron of \place{the damper} ahead of her sentence; he took the cold out of the fingertip, moved his tongue a hair's breadth and came out without leaving the extra holding of Ash 26 a second time, for a second would be a habit and a third a way and both want a payer where none stood. \emph{He's not Vail} stayed sealed as a lid nobody had tried to lift in three years, and the promise under the shed stayed neither given nor withdrawn.

Ilva stood once at \place{the yard well} drawing with the pail against her knee, the rope stiff in the dry cold giving its small rasp, clay dried pale in the crescents of her nails and the finer grey dust of the racks worked into the seam of one thumb, which she did not scrub out. People noticed the pail first and the sleeve not at all, which was the order she preferred. Her hands knew the weight of a pail and the edge of a wet board and had never been soft. The short strip of notched birch lay in its fold in her sleeve, pale where a thumb had rubbed the notches and dark in the uncut grain between; it was not taken out, read, copied, counted or joined to anything, and no figure came off the wood.

Then the word came up. It came once, from mouth to mouth among the bucket-round, with no body standing under it --- a report and not a sighting. A neighbour with a pot against her hip said to nobody in particular that a man with a narrow slate under his arm had asked after a house-name under this roof. Another, at \place{the flat stone by the well}, said the man had been answered with the weather. The words went along the round and off into ordinary talk, and nobody turned twice. Nobody had seen the asking; no voice from it had carried into this yard; no slate had been opened within reach of the basket, no figure had come off any slate, no room had been entered and no threshold crossed; no name had been spoken over the asking or over the answer. \place{Slate Row} was a direction in a mouth and nothing more.

Ilva's hand went toward her sleeve before her eyes went to the asker and her mouth after both, the half-beat lying in the gap where a lesser observer would have put an intention. She was not the speaker of this word and was not made a reader by the nearness of it; her mouth said nothing that carried, and the flat of her hand went once more onto the sleeve and back to the pail. Her ninth record-first hand stayed nine.

The boy took the word apart by the order of its parts as a surgeon takes a field apart --- the asking, the house-name asked after, the weather that answered, the half-beat lying in the gap where a name would have stood. He gave the asking no name, no owner, no answer carried, no interior, no witness, no sale and no tenth hand. He filed it on a place of its own beside the second man's direction down the north bridge road and his narrow slate turned to the sleeve, the first man's face, the clerk's entered figure and the fifth shaping of the well-saying, with a finger between every pair. He joined it to none of them --- not to Tarn's valuable ear or Tarn's ended term, not to Jorra's ungiven promise, not to the clerk's inward slate, not to the face at the yard's edge, not to the fifth shaping. It was a reaching with no hand under it, gathering interest toward a far-off calling and a far-off hand that was not his, and it was \emph{not} the calling of the loan, not a second sighting of the man, not a sixth shaping, not a rule and not a door. The direction was not repeated on a day that had earned only its twenty-third, and the asking was not told again.

Costs arrived as directions and went away as weather and left no entry. A man come down the clay road brought a number up out of a shut fist toward the well, held it where the light could see it, looked at it, and put it back into his sleeve; it arrived nowhere. He took that order apart too --- fist, eye, mouth, the half-beat in the gap --- and gave the fist no figure and the sleeve no motive, for a motive is a payer wearing a coat. A woman sent to ask what a pot cost was sent to the pot and got a note and not a price. The dressed grain lay covered and unfigured at the rack end, and the two readings of the year, read over it in their own mornings, had not known what it cost; no signature stood on the space between the figure and the price and he set none there. Paper is not a payer. The key to the box had gone up the road in a coat pocket and did not come back down, and there was no key under any roof and none in his hand.

At \place{the Vail corner} the clean dark line kept its finer grey beside it, thickened in the dry cold with the faint sour tang of Ash coming up off it where no broom had gone in two years. The called-gone grey stayed beside the left-in-place ridge with a finger between, and a thing left in place is not a thing forgiven. The tang was thinner in this morning air than it had been, and he found the softening where put and did not look twice at it, for looking twice is how a finding becomes a habit, and he did not make it a sign, a fourth covering or a payment on a day that had earned only its twenty-third. \place{The trough} stood bare and dark with the gone pan's shallow round print on a place of its own, and he did not lay that ring across the loaf's stopping or the ridge.

\scenebreak

Through the finger's-width door the river kept its note of \lore{water over water}, a low thing over a lower thing under the winter skin, softer at one edge where the low note ran nearer on the nineteenth. The note came from under the covering and not from the covering; it was not a hearing, not one of \lore{the three coverings}, not one of the six hearings of the full note, not a seventh of anything, and no hand or payer stood under it.

The hand outside the wool came up an inch toward the note. The inch was not a reach. He took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole on the note's own finishing under the covering --- neither early nor stayed --- and let it go as the note ended. Then the hand came straight down and laid itself flat against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking.

Answer four hundred and nine came out and was filed on the sixth place. It was exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the twelve readings, the first and second of the new count, the printed figure, the fifth shaping, the clerk's entered figure, the well-asking, the second man's slate, Tarn's degree, the face at the yard's edge, Jorra's ungiven promise, the withheld sentence, the softening, the bell's gap, the flat knock, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the held breath, the two bites of iron, the winter skin, the widow's mark nor the keeping at the fold. Six honest answers began on the first of these three tending days, four hundred and nine the first of them, and not one was a doctrine.

Count four hundred and seventeen exact, six months and two hundred and thirty-seven days told by nobody. The carried fire's fifth was unpaid the same number and Garl's name was unspoken the same number, and the fire and the man stood side by side without becoming one. He did not stand the number up as a threshold and borrowed no payer from the arithmetics gone up the road, for paper is not a payer. No eighth opening, seventh hearing, sixth shaping, tenth hand, tenth stopping, eleventh arithmetic, signature, payer, witness, reader or payment stood on a day that had earned only its twenty-third.

The surgeon's exactness and the infant's want stood side by side and neither bought the other; under them, louder, the wanting went on. The crust lifted two inches, the thread wiped, a hand on the handle of the pot. He wanted the three things without knowing that wanting them was a want and got none of them. Both were true. The second was louder. Neither won, and no verdict was looked for; the quarrel was the work, and the work went on in silence in a room whose floor does not touch his. The asking was kept where put on its own place beside the direction and the slate with a finger between every pair, the well's answer nowhere under any roof, Ilva's pail on its stone and the strip in her sleeve. A voice in a yard was a voice in a yard and not a door. The well had given back the weather. Nothing had been joined, named, called, paid or ended; the house went on as a house goes when a pot is set down in its place and the fire kept --- forward, into a morning not yet earned and not brought into this one.
