The afternoon came on without a word said over the basket. It came the way heat comes off a banked kiln, not as a thing that arrives but as a thing that had been behind the wall all along and was let out a little into the yard. The light went off the high boards and lay flat on the clay; the shadow of the well rope shortened on the stone lip and then went no further, as though it had come to the end of an instruction. Nothing in the yard had been told what the day was, and the basket had not been told to be still.

Under the roof at the yard's edge, where the light came sideways, \person[Corin]{the boy} took the yard in the old order --- the surgeon's order, the order in which a field gives up its facts to a man with steady hands and no time to waste on comfort: the small sounds first, because the small sounds do not lie, and the large ones after, because the large ones are mostly weather. A pail went down on \place{the flat stone by the well} with the small solid knock a pail makes when it has been set where a pail has always stood. The rope settled into its knot with the stiff dry rasp that cold puts into hemp, a sound with no grain of intention in it. A loaf was pushed a finger's width along a board and stopped, and nobody called the stopping a figure. Barley went from wall to wall in the breath between two voices and was never made into a number. He took each of these apart by the place of it and let each be what it was; a yard that keeps its days in loaves and glances can keep them badly and go on none the worse for it.

Then the large sounds: the river under its skin, the clay road with nothing coming up or going down it that carried, the wind crossing the yard and going out of it again, and over all of them the ordinary Thirdday grammar of feet and pails and work that asked nothing of the basket. He had learned that a large sound will carry a long way and mean less than the small one under it. This was a thing he had known in another life with a different set of walls, and here it was useful in the same small way that knowing the feel of a true seam under a knuckle is useful. Both kinds of knowing stood where they stood and neither bought the other.

He went once around \place{the seven walked-around places} with a look and a wide step and no number. Once, not twice; a second round on a day that had earned only its twenty-third would be a custom, and a custom wants a hand that put the thing there twice, and a way wants a third, and both want a payer standing where none stood. The stone lip of \place{the yard well} came first, with the frost gone out of it and the wet dark under the bucket's round. The worn boards came next, with their edges polished by feet that had not thought about the polishing. The strip of hard mud held a boot's shallow print and gave it back slowly. The place where a wheel had stood was a pale circle in the dust with the weight of the wheel still in the shape of it. Two narrow gaps lay between the stacked pots, side by side and separate, and he kept them separate. Last came \place{the returning-line place}, where a hand had gone in on \emph{Seed 15} and had not come back, and there his step was widest of all. The wide step was a keeping and not a sign. A figure set against that space would want a payer standing under it, and none stood, so he set no figure against it and went on.

At \place{the Vail corner} the clean dark line kept its finer grey beside it. The line was called \lore{the thaw} by a woman who had not swept it in two years, and the grey had thickened in the dry cold with the faint sour tang of \lore{Ash} coming up off it, which no broom had gone near in those two years and which had been left as it lay. The called-gone grey stayed beside the left-in-place ridge with a finger between every pair. No agreement had been made to stop looking at the ridge, and nobody under the roof had said the word that would make a thing left in place a thing forgiven. The tang was thinner in this afternoon air than it had been in the morning; the grey lay a hair less distinct along one stretch, and the air off the river was less cold at one edge where the low note ran nearer under the skin. He found the softening where put and did not look twice at it, for looking twice is how a finding becomes a habit. He did not make it a sign, a fourth covering, a sixth shaping or a payment on a day that had earned only its twenty-third; no hand stood under it and no payer was named.

\place{The trough} stood bare and dark. The thin skin that had lain on it through the turn of the month was gone, and the rim still kept the line where the water used to stand, a clean mark low on the inside that the wet had drawn and left behind. The gone pan kept its shallow round print on a place of its own; he did not lay that ring across the loaf's stopping or across the ridge, for two absences laid together make a shape that wants an author, and no author stood in the yard.

Costs came to the yard as directions and went away as weather, and left no entry behind them. A man come down \place{the clay road} brought a number up out of a shut fist toward the well, held it where the light could see it, looked at it, and put it back into his sleeve. It arrived nowhere. He took the order apart as he took every field apart --- fist, eye, mouth, the half-beat lying in the gap --- and gave the fist no figure and the sleeve no motive, for a motive is a payer wearing a coat. A woman sent to ask what a pot cost was sent to the pot and got a note and not a price. The dressed grain lay covered and unfigured at \place{the rack end}; the figure read over it in its own morning had not known what it cost, and no signature stood on the space between the figure and the price. Paper is not a payer. The key to \person{Berr Clay}'s box had gone up the road in a coat pocket that never came back down, and there was no key under any roof and none in his hand.

The soft-cuffed clerk's entered figure stayed where it had been brought up the yard, a report and never a sighting, on its own place with a finger between it and \person{Tarn}'s degree toward \place{the out-of-line landing} below \place{the north bridge} and another between it and the face at the yard's edge. The first man's face, the second man's direction and his narrow slate turned to the sleeve, and the asking that had come up among the bucket-round were kept each on a place of its own with a finger between every pair. No name, owner, answer carried, witness, reader, sale, tenth hand or paying stood under any of them. The asking was not joined to Tarn's valuable ear or to \person{Jorra Vail}'s ungiven promise, and it was not called the calling of the loan; a reaching with no hand under it gathers interest toward a far-off calling and a far-off hand, and is not that calling and not that hand. The fifth shaping of \lore{the well-saying} sat on its own tray with a finger between it and every other kept thing, and no sixth was made due on a day that had earned only its twenty-third. \person{Ilva} was offstage with her notched strip in her sleeve and her ninth hand nine; nothing came off the wood and nobody made a reader of her at a distance. Nothing was repeated, and the direction was not made a custom by being remembered.

At the going of the light \person{Sella Vail} came to \place{the hearth} before she came to the basket. She told the coals it was \lore{the thaw} in the voice another woman would have used for a child who could not hear her, and turned them with the back of a broken pot. She gave the small intake, the hold and the letting-go toward no opening, figuring neither of the two gaps. \emph{The house will keep it} went to the fire and not to the basket, because a thing said aloud becomes a thing the Court can enter. Her finger lay along the top of \place{the loaf shelf} so the charcoal tally beneath stayed unread, and her lips moved on a two-gapped row with neither gap figured. One glance went to the clay jar standing cold under \lore{lead glaze} beyond \place{the pot board}; the hand shut half over nothing, and the little-door burn on the back of her left hand closed on air. The twice-mended pot went up thumb-first where the tighter wire crossed the crack, and the hairline of the original break showed between the old-brass staple and the wire. Both proverbs went to the pot and the fire and never to a person --- \emph{Leave the crack showing. People trust a thing they can see has been handled} and \emph{If it's worth mending it's worth mending twice. The second time is the real one}. Repair was kept apart from restoration, as a surgeon keeps a graft apart from a cure: a restored thing hides the hand that handled it, and a mended thing leaves the hand showing. At the rack end she turned the dressed grain piece by piece under the wool with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child, set each down where it had lain, took no figure from it and no handful, and drew the wool back. Then she set the pot rim two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat and no look toward \place{the bed}. The half-beat was the distance between the intention and the arrest; she did not measure it and did not name it.

At \place{the kindling} and \place{the flagstones} Tarn had kept six pieces in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check and the seventh at the lip beside \person{Garl Vail}'s unforged collector sentence, with no name on the man who had turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on \lore{the tile debt}. The small knife lay at the very edge of its sill. His hand had gone over blade and sill and away with no breath held at the top of the reach --- the coming away the whole of the checking --- and the sentence he had said to the wood in the tone of a man stating the weather was found where put and not made a habit, not a tenth stopping, not a payment and not a rule. It was not said again on a day that had earned only its twenty-third, for a second would be a custom and a third a way and both want a payer where none stood. The \emph{Seed 16} breath stayed unspent and the loan stayed uncalled at the very end of a term already ended. \lore{Ash} stood worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line; the pale clip-ring at his left wrist caught and gave back what little light there was. He took the broom only after it had been given its instruction and said \emph{Yes, house} to the fire, and did not turn his head toward the landing.

Jorra came through \place{the finger's-width door} off \place{the Vail wheel-shed} with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past his bent little finger and white in a fist-crease where a hand had opened --- not the bone-coloured \lore{slip} of the wheel. He kept dusty forearms and clean ones on one tray and parted them as clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had been broken nor whether it had been a birth-slate. He spoke to the wheel before any person --- \emph{It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady} --- and told a pot \emph{You'll do} in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either, knowing the \lore{full note} it would give three turns before it left his hands and withholding the knowing from any master come up from \place{High Kiln} who would call it early. Sella had not finished telling him the fire was low when his bent finger went into the cold iron of \place{the damper} ahead of her sentence; he took the cold out of the fingertip, moved his tongue a hair's breadth and came out without leaving the extra holding of Ash 26 a second time, for a second would be a habit and a third a way and both want a payer where none stood. \emph{He's not Vail} stayed sealed as a lid nobody had tried to lift in three years, and the promise under the shed stayed neither given nor withdrawn.

She carried him up the nineteen steps at the half-pace she keeps from every open slate, correcting the tilt at every turn of the landing before it settled into the wicker. The instruction went to the wool, the river wind, the pot, the fire and the banked fire and never to the basket; the basket was not told what the day was and was not told to be still. In the yard she went once around the seven walked-around places with a look and a wide step and no number, widest at the returning-line place, and set him under the roof and never in \place{the Debt Loft}. She put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face and added no second fold. \lore{The widow's mark} hung where it hung and was not touched: a morning, an afternoon, a shelf, a yard, a pot and a going of the light are not openings, seven openings in seven months are seven and not a rule, and no eighth came due on a day that had earned only its twenty-third.

In the small hours \place{the towel} came out at the head of the bed as instruments come out when a case is done and before the next is called, rough from the wash and smelling of tallow and low smoke. He went over the day by the place of each thing and left every gap a gap. Six hearings of the full note stood beside and never among, with the seventh place empty and not dug for, and \place{the Drawing house}'s flat knock outside them on its own ground; no seventh had come on a day that earned only its twenty-third, and the knock was not made a hearing. The heard bell stood in its square beside the told, four, four and a gap, and its gap was not folded into the four and a gap of \lore{the carried fire} beneath \place{the boards}, nor into the boards' four nails on the near board, four on the next and a thumb-width knot where a fifth might have been driven and was not, nor into the two readings of the year behind one unsigned figure beside the twelve old arithmetics of Y.H. 813. Three counts in three places stayed three, because agreeing any two would want a payer put under both and none stood. Four rooms held the same gap with a finger between each pair, and no Court room stood as a fifth likeness. Nine record-first hands stood with no tenth, and nine stoppings with no tenth; \person[Hessa Rale]{Hessa}'s slate stayed unread up the road with her hand eighth, Ilva's strip stayed in her sleeve with her hand ninth, and no tenth was made of a nearness.

Beneath the boards the carried fire gave four low pulls and kept the silence where the fifth should have stood, and nobody lifted a floor or supplied the pull. Its payer had gone unnamed four hundred and seventeen days, and Garl Vail's name had gone unspoken the same number; the fire and the man stood side by side and did not become one. \lore{The keeping at the fold} was found where put and stayed a finding and not a placing, with no second fold, no third crust, no name spoken over the place, no gift, no payment and no payer. The crust stood two inches from his ear with its corner a hair off the wool, gone into old horn in the dry nights; the thread of drool at his mouth was stiff and unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread; cold had stopped at cheek, temple and the small bare joint at the wrist where the wool rode back and stopped there as at the end of a line written out for it; and \lore{the Afterdrop} waited past a warming nobody in this house would perform. He knew a basket set level out of the draught where heat came sideways was the rewarming he would choose, and could not ask for it. Under the exactness, louder, the wanting went on: the crust lifted, the thread wiped, a hand on the handle of the pot. Both were true, the second was louder, neither won, and the quarrel was the work. He did not know that wanting them was a want; he only wanted, with the whole small animal hunger of a body four hundred and seventeen days old, and the hand outside the wool stayed where it was.

Through the finger's-width door \place{the river} kept its note of \lore{water over water}, a low thing over a lower thing under the winter skin, softer at one edge where the low note ran nearer. The note came from under the covering and not from the covering; it was not a hearing, not one of \lore{the three coverings}, not one of the six hearings, not a seventh of anything and not a payer, and no hand stood under it. He found it where put and called it weather and did not look twice.

The hand outside the wool came up an inch toward the note. The inch was not a reach. He took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole on the note's own finishing under the covering --- neither early nor stayed --- and let it go as the note ended. Then the hand came straight down and laid itself flat against the fold on answer four hundred and ten, as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking.

Answer four hundred and ten came out and was filed on the sixth place. It was exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the twelve readings, the first and second of the new count, the printed figure, the fifth shaping, the clerk's entered figure, the well-asking, the second man's slate, Tarn's degree, the face at the yard's edge, Jorra's ungiven promise, the withheld sentence, the softening, the bell's gap, the flat knock, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the held breath, the two bites of iron, the winter skin, the widow's mark nor the keeping at the fold. Six honest answers stood on the first of these three tending days, four hundred and nine and four hundred and ten, and not one was a doctrine.

Count four hundred and seventeen exact, six months and two hundred and thirty-seven days told by nobody who would have known what a number counted. The carried fire's fifth was unpaid the same number and Garl's name was unspoken the same number, and the fire and the man stood side by side without becoming one. He did not stand the number up as a threshold and borrowed no payer from the arithmetics gone up the road, for paper is not a payer. No eighth opening, seventh hearing, sixth shaping, tenth hand, tenth stopping, eleventh arithmetic, signature, payer, witness, reader or payment stood on a day that had earned only its twenty-third.

The surgeon's exactness and the infant's want stood side by side and neither bought the other; under them, louder, the wanting went on. The crust lifted two inches, the thread wiped, a hand on the handle of the pot. He wanted the three things without knowing that wanting them was a want and got none of them. Both were true. The second was louder. Neither won, and no verdict was looked for; the quarrel was the work, and the work went on in silence in a room whose floor does not touch his. The asking stayed where put on its own place with a finger between it and the direction and the slate, not told again, not joined, not called; a voice in a yard was a voice in a yard and not a door. The well had given back the weather. Nothing had been paid, named or ended, and the house went on as a house goes when a pot is set down in its place and the fire kept --- forward, into a morning not yet earned and not brought into this one.
