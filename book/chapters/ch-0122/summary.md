## Chronicle

Three days came under the Vail roof, and the house kept them badly, in loaves pushed a finger's width and stopped. The boy kept four hundred and seventeen, eighteen and nineteen exact and stood no number up as a threshold. Sella told the coals it was the thaw and lifted the twice-mended pot thumb-first with the hairline showing; repair was not restoration. Tarn kept six and the seventh at the lip; Jorra parted dusty from clean as clot from vessel.

On the twenty-third word came up once at the yard well with nobody under it: a man with a narrow slate had asked after a house-name here and been answered with the weather. Nobody saw the asking; no slate was opened, no name spoken. Ilva drew at the well, her hand to her sleeve before her eyes to the asker, her strip unread and her ninth hand nine, not the speaker. The boy filed the asking on a place of its own beside the second man's direction and slate, the first man's face, the clerk's entered figure and the fifth shaping of the well-saying, with a finger between every pair. Joined to none, it gathered interest toward a far-off calling and a far-off hand, not that calling. The well gave back the weather.

The other days kept the small grammar: a number rose out of a shut fist, looked at the light and went back into a sleeve; a woman sent to ask a pot's cost got a note and not a price. At the corner the called-gone grey stayed beside the left-in-place ridge, a thing left in place not a thing forgiven. The tang thinned and the river's skin softened at one edge — found where put, called weather. The widow's mark stayed untouched; seven openings were seven and not a rule.

Six answers, four hundred and nine through fourteen, two to each day, were filed on the sixth place, worth nothing at two inches of air and not one a doctrine. The count reached four hundred and nineteen exact, six months and two hundred and thirty-nine days, told by nobody; the fire's fifth was unpaid and Garl's name unspoken the same number. Two readings of the year stood behind one unsigned figure, two and not one account; the twelve were not laid across, for paper is not a payer and the key went up a coat pocket.

In the small hours the towel came out as instruments come out when a case is done. Six hearings stood beside and never among with the seventh place empty; nine hands stood with no tenth; the keeping at the fold was found where put. The surgeon's exactness and the infant's want stood side by side and neither bought the other; under them the wanting went on — the crust lifted, the thread wiped, a hand on the pot handle — and got none of the three. The hand came down flat on the fold; the house went on.
