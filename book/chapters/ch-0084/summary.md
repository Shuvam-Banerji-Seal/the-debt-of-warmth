## Chronicle

Bone came on without a boundary drawn. The boy came into his three hundred and third day and went out of the sixth at three hundred and five, six months and one hundred and twenty-five days told by nobody. Each morning the body made its old inventory: a crust two inches from the ear, the thread frozen and unwiped, cold stopped at cheek, temple and wrist, the afterdrop waiting past a warming nobody would perform. The surgeon knew a cold patient cannot clot; the infant wanted the crust lifted.

The carried fire kept four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should stand, its payer unnamed three hundred and five days; Garl's name had not been spoken under the roof the same number. Three counts in three places --- fire, river, boards --- did not become one, for agreeing any two would have wanted a payer and none stood. Three Frost arithmetics rode locked in Berr Clay's box, one in twelve printed and unchallenged, the figure read still not the figure paid.

Sella went to the hearth before the basket at both ends of every day, told the coals it was the thaw, said *The house will keep it* where another woman would have said *I*, kept the charcoal unread, set the pot rim two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat and no look toward the bed. A pot with a twice-mended handle stood on the board; she put her thumb where the wire crossed the crack before letting the weight come on the handle, and said the proverbs to the pot and the fire and never to a person.

On the fifth of Bone an ownerless voice came up from Slate Row over the boards, on the same breath as barley and the weather, saying a slate had been burned again. The boy laid it beside the four tellings behind it with a finger between every pair: a fifth telling of the burned slate, still not a third telling of the counting room shut in the war, not a hearing, not a witness, not a cause, not a payment. Five were five and not a rule. Jorra grew quieter whenever the saying went around; *he's not Vail* stayed sealed.

At the well on the sixth Ilva drew with the pail against her knee, clay pale in her nail crescents and rack dust in a thumb seam she did not scrub out. Asked what the month's firings had taken, her hand went toward her sleeve and stayed; the strip did not come out and no figure came off the wood. *Ask the pot what it cost. I keep the count, not the account.* He filed the order of reaching as the ninth record-first hand and filed no tenth. The hand came down on the river's low note, the hundred and eighty-sixth answer worth nothing at two inches of air; the file lay open on the towel with every gap left gap.
