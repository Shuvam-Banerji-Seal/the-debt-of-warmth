## The pull into Chapter 85

The final moment hands the next chapter its exact starting state: the hand laid back on the **186th** answer, the count at **305 exact** (six months and 125 days told by nobody), the fire's fifth unpaid the same number of days with its payer unnamed, Garl's name unspoken 305 days, the file open on the towel with every gap left gap, and the burned-slate word kept at five tellings with a finger between it and the four — five are five and not a rule. Chapter 85 opens on Day 306, Bone 7, Seventhday, in the small hours under the same roof, with the hand coming up for the **187th** answer and the count going to 306 — no recap, no boundary drawn, no sign taken from the fifth telling, the mended pot or Ilva's strip. The hook is ordinary and forward: Bone's last working week must be lived one dated day at a time, the yard still going around its seven places, the grain still covered and unfigured, Berr's box still locked up the road, Bone 10 (Day 309) three mornings ahead and not brought early. The fifth telling stays on its own place with no sixth, no witness and no payer; the uncalled loan, the warm stone in its loft pot, the ninth stopping with no tenth, the three shapings with no witness and the count kept in a sleeve are all carried unchanged into the days that will one day call them. The story moves because the hand keeps answering and the count keeps going, not because anything under the roof was settled.
