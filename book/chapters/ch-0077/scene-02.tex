The afternoon thinned without ending. It went out of the light the way heat goes out of a pot set off the coals and left standing on a cold stone --- not in a single breath, not with any word spoken over it, but by slow degrees, the warmth drawing back from \place{the bed} and the fold and sitting behind the pot where a hand would find it still if the hand went there. No hand went there. \person[Corin]{The boy} lay where he had been laid, and took the body as it presented itself, in the order the facts arrived, because there was no other order he had ever found trustworthy.

One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from his ear, gone past the point where a child would eat it, and the empty place beside it kept its name to itself; no third had come in the afternoon to make the row come out even. The thread of drool at his mouth had frozen in the dry heat and was unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. Cold had come to cheek and temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, and stopped there as at the end of a line written out for it, with neither an order to go forward nor an order to go away. The afterdrop waited beyond all three, past a warming nobody in this house would perform. He knew the composed face that is not composure. He knew a cold patient cannot clot and will seem better before he is worse, and that a man must be warmed from the outside and slowly. He wanted the crust lifted the two inches to his mouth. Both were true, and in a body six months and one hundred and two days old the second was louder; the surgeon took the inventory in its old order --- crust, gap, thread, cold, the waiting --- and entered none of it, not even on the place where he kept the things he did not enter.

Two hundred and eighty-two stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in \place{Sarn}; six months and one hundred and two days were told by nobody; \lore{the carried fire}'s fifth was unpaid the same number of days with its payer unnamed. The house kept its days in loaves, glances and unnamed spaces, and gave nobody a number to carry. Even the thinning of the light made no sound anybody could have entered.

\person{Sella Vail} came for him in the afternoon and carried him down. Her hand went under him the way a hand goes under a pot filled to the lip --- making no slop, asking nothing --- and the little-door burn on the back of her left hand closed on his weight and then on nothing as she lifted. She carried him at the half-pace from every open slate, and the half-pace was not haste and not fear but the exact distance a woman keeps between a pot and a place where a figure might be written. She gave the instruction to the wool before she gave it to the wind, and to the fire, the pot, the broom and the running water, and never to the basket. Out in \place{the Clay Yard} she went wide around the seven walked-around places with a look and a wide step and no number, as a woman goes around a pail of hot water set where a child might knock it.

The clean dark line at the Vail corner lay where it lay, and the finer grey hairline beside it, which the house called \lore{the thaw}, lay beside it unswept; she went around both and swept neither. Beneath \place{the lowest rack} of \place{the drying racks} the broken birth-slates kept their place and she went around the space above them without looking down. The round ring where the shallow pan had once stood kept no scrape, no crumb, no print and no word, and she went around the ring. \place{The trough} kept its skin over its water and the yard called the water weather, and she went around the trough. Out on \place{the worked strips} the field kept its empty place and she went around it as wide as if a man had been standing there. And in the returning line a hand had gone in on Seed 15 and had not come out, and she went around that place widest of all, with a look and a wide step and no number.

Seven were seven and not an account. The missing hand was carried, not named, not paid, not joined to the shelf's two gaps, the fold's one crust, the dressed grain under the wool at \place{the rack end} or anything else that waited with its own name kept.

The stayed \lore{frost-bloom} lay white on the north lip of \place{the yard well}, as white as it had lain on Frost 8 and as called weather on the days behind, and nobody had given it a figure or carried it away. The plate of ice lifted off the trough stood on \place{the flat stone by the well} out of the path of feet, clouded where the silt had risen into it, and the yard called that weather too. The surgeon's reading of a skin laid over a depth stayed on a place of its own, with a finger between it and the gone white of Frost 4; he made no fourth likeness of the three and no cause of any two, and the afternoon going out of the light paid none of it. Nobody named a payer to either the white or the ice.

\person{Garl Vail} stayed in objects and not in a story. The salt-stiff coat with the crease at the left shoulder kept his broader shape on its peg behind the door; the wheel-seat in \place{the Vail wheel-shed} kept its twenty-year lean and its hollow on that side, which nobody filled; the clean dark line at the corner kept its finer grey beside it, unswept both. The debt against a firing of tiles stood neither paid nor settled nor done with, and was worse for being uncalled, and his proverb had gone years ago to the pot and the fire and never to a person: leave the crack showing; people trust a thing they can see has been handled. His name had not been spoken under this roof for two hundred and eighty-two days. No collector had come up \place{the clay road} in the days behind and none had gone down it, and the household went around the debt as Sella went around \lore{Ash}, without a figure and without a name.

\person{Tarn} was at \place{the kindling} when the light went thin, with the small knife at the very edge of its sill. Six he counted in the worn mark worn almost flat by his thumb, where the house could check; the seventh he kept at the lip where it could not, and that was the one entry in this house the house could not check. Beside the knife lay Garl's unforged collector sentence, with no name on the man who had turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on the debt. \emph{Six counted. The next I don't count}, he told the wood, in the tone of a man stating the weather. He said \emph{Yes, house} to those who wanted the thaw early and to those who wanted it late, agreeing with both so thoroughly that no argument could find a foot, and keeping the late because it puts off the paying, though the paying stood in the ruts.

The sentence about an ear under this roof being worth more than the debt that held it came to his teeth and went as far as the word that would call the loan before it stopped, with no figure and no name --- a remembered stopping, the ninth, and no tenth stood up before the seventh year at its very end and still ungiven. He drew the breath that should have gone in at the top of the reach and did not spend it, and his chest was tight as it had been since the dark. Ash was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line and no scrubbing lifted it; the pale clip-ring at his left wrist held what light there was and gave it back. His stroke went on over \place{the flagstones} past the shallow bright patch without the half-check, the stones keeping the place where it had stopped.

Up in \place{the Debt Loft} there was a sound with a direction and never a room he was taken into. The warm stone in its unmarked pot was cold, and unreconciled by not trying, which is not forgiveness.

At the edge of the Vail wheel-shed \person{Jorra Vail} came through with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bent little finger and under the nails, not bone-coloured \lore{slip}; he kept his dusty forearms and his clean ones on one tray and parted them as a surgeon parts clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had been broken nor whether it was a birth-slate. He spoke to the wheel before he spoke to any person. \emph{It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady}, he told it, and he told a pot \emph{You'll do} in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either. He knew the \lore{full note} that pot would give three turns before it left his hands, with air under it and a want in it, and withheld the knowing on the near-full tray beside the hearings and never among them. Before Sella's sentence had finished, his bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper} ahead of it, moved the tongue a hair's breadth and left it standing; the end of the instruction arrived at the pot and the household straightened around his silence. The boy read one iron with two bites --- the holding and the taking --- with no \lore{Grathic} word for both mercies, joined to no kiln and entered in no slate.

\place{The back room} kept its finger's width of open and its sound of low voices. Men unnamed had gone low into it before first grey, and neither Jorra nor Sella had given the house a name for them, and nobody had said the room was cold. The burned-slate word stood at four from \place{Slate Row}, a direction over \place{the boards} and not a fifth telling; the two tellings of the counting room shut in the war stood at two and had not become three; the three sayings about a pot stood together with the premature Smoke 14 note on a place apart and no fourth likeness made a cause.

No sixth asking came over the boards. The ownerless question stayed at five askings with one shaping, a finger between every pair, no witness under any and no row named; the silence was a keeping and not a sixth witness. No sixth hearing came, and the empty seventh place was not filled to make a row come out even.

At the going of the light Sella carried him up at the half-pace from every open slate and set him on his bed under the roof and not in the Debt Loft. She put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face, left the thread frozen where it lay, and told the fire it was \emph{the thaw}. At \place{the loaf shelf} her finger stayed on top of the front edge so the charcoal beneath remained unread, and her lips moved along a row standing with two gaps and gave neither gap a figure; she glanced once at the clay jar standing cold under its \lore{lead glaze} and shut her hand half over nothing. She stood the pot rim two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat and no look toward the bed. She gave no instruction at any hour to the basket. \lore[The widow's mark]{The widow's mark} hung where it hung on its cord at her throat and her hand did not go to it at a going of the light, because a going of the light is not an opening.

Two private keepings went on side by side and neither was opened: the charcoal under the shelf, and the count a woman somewhere in the yard kept in wood where no slate could take a figure off it. The boy's curiosity about what a hand keeps to itself was checked by the rule that a keeping is not his to read. The check was not envy and not virtue, only the exact order of another person's reaching, filed apart. The finding that a house keeps by \lore{not-doings} stayed on a place of its own with the boy's gaps on another and a finger between, and neither was laid across the other for want of a payer.

In the small hours the room whose floor does not touch his gave its low counting --- the small intake, the hold, the letting-go --- and the boy took only what the hand could take. The hand outside the wool went up an inch. It took the small intake of one counted breath from that room and the hold of the next, set them side by side with a finger between, left the letting-go between them where it was, and came down on the river's next low note of \lore{water over water}. Two intakes held apart as objects on the sixth place, neither made to agree with the fire's missing fifth. It was an honest coordination, exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air: not a reach, not a hearing, not a seventh, not a stopping, not a payment, not a held change, not a key, not a power, not a doctrine and not a rule. One hundred and forty stood beside one hundred and thirty-nine.

Two hundred and eighty-two stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill}, \place{Grath}; six months and one hundred and two days were told by nobody; the fire's fifth was unpaid the same number of days with its payer unnamed. The boy lay with the crust two inches off and its empty place unnamed, the hand laid down on the hundred and fortieth answer and the letting-go between the two intakes left where it was, and the file open on \place{the towel} at the head of the bed with every gap left gap. Beyond the finger's-width door the frost lay white on the well lip where the yard had left it, called weather and entered nowhere, and Frost 20 (Day 289), the month's second Account-day, stood five mornings ahead and was not brought into this day. The river's low note went quiet after the hand came down.
