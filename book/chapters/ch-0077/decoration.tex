  % woman waiting her turn spoke to the boards and the well-roof, not into the
  % basket: the runner who carried a slate had once carried a figure that named
  % a person she knew. No owner stood to be asked; the saying arrived nowhere.
  % The boy filed it as a shaping on a place of its own, beside an eighth hand
  % with a finger between --- not a hearing, not a confession, not paid. Beside
  % it, Ilva's notched strip stayed in her sleeve: the count kept in wood, the
  % account not kept. Both go home with the weight; neither is called an account.
