The afternoon went on without arriving anywhere. Heat drew back from the fold the way it draws out of a pot lifted off the coals and set upon a cold stone, going first from the rim and then sitting behind the pot where a hand would find it if a hand went there; no hand went there. Frost went out of the district without a boundary drawn, and neither of the two habits the district has for naming its months was set right or left wrong in so many words. No lesson was made of Frost's first reading, and none was made now. The next opening stood in the first firing of a later month, far off, not hoped for and not counted toward. \person[Corin]{The boy} lay carried at the edge of the work in \place{the Clay Yard}, \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill}, \place{Grath}, and took the body as it presented itself, in the order the facts arrived, because there was no other order he had ever found trustworthy.

One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from his ear, gone past the point where a child would eat it, and the empty place beside it kept its name to itself; no third had come in the afternoon to make the row come out even. The thread of drool at his mouth had frozen in the dry heat and was unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. Cold had come to cheek and temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, and stopped there as at the end of a line written out for it, with neither an order to go forward nor an order to go away. The afterdrop waited beyond all three, past a warming nobody in this house would perform. He knew the composed face that is not composure. He knew a cold patient cannot clot, and will seem better before he is worse, and that a man must be warmed from the outside and slowly. He wanted the crust lifted the two inches to his mouth. Both were true, and in a body six months and one hundred and four days old the second was louder. The surgeon took the inventory in its old order --- crust, gap, thread, cold, the waiting --- and entered none of it, not even on the place where he kept the things he did not enter. He had once reduced a shoulder in the dark by feel alone and could not now close two inches on a pot handle; the thought came and went without bitterness, which was its own small betrayal.

Two hundred and eighty-four stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in \place{Sarn}. Under the floor \lore{the carried fire} gave four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer unnamed the same number of days, and nobody lifted the floor and nobody supplied the pull. At the finger's-width door the river kept its note of \lore{water over water}, a low thing over a lower thing, never numbered among the hearings; overhead \place{the boards} kept four nails on the near board, four on the next and a thumb-width knot where the fifth nail should have stood. Three counts in three places, and none rose into another, because agreeing any two would want a payer put under both and no payer stood to any. Six hearings of \lore{the full note} stood beside and never among, five from four sources and the sixth, the small flat knock out of a woman as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go and never with the things that came from vessels. The seventh place kept its emptiness and none was invented to fill it. The heard bell's four, four and a gap stayed in the square at \place{the Cross} beside the told bell of \lore{the Long Night}, not folded into the three and not heard as a seventh of the full note. In the room whose floor does not touch his a woman gave its low counting to the work --- the small intake, the hold, the letting-go --- and the boy took only what the hand could take and left the letting-go where it was. It was her breath and her count and not his.

Out at \place{the yard well} the stayed \lore{frost-bloom} lay white on the north lip where the yard had left it, past the going of the light that was still to come, and the yard called it weather. The rope stiffened in the hand and gave its small dry rasp; the water in \place{the trough} kept its skin; the plate of ice lifted off the trough stood on \place{the flat stone by the well} out of the path of feet, clouded where the silt had risen into it. None of it was entered, and no sign, cause, lesson, mending or payer was made of any of it. The yard went around its walked-around places with a look and a wide step and no number: the clean dark line at the Vail corner and the finer grey hairline beside it called \lore{the thaw}, unswept both; the space beneath \place{the lowest rack} at \place{the drying racks} where the broken birth-slates lay; the round ring where the shallow pan once stood with no scrape or crumb; the skinned trough; the field's empty place out on \place{the worked strips}; and the returning-line place where a hand went in on Seed 15 and did not come out. Seven were seven and not an account; the missing hand was carried, not named, not paid and not joined to the shelf's two gaps, the fold's one crust or the dressed grain.

\person{Garl Vail} stayed in objects and not in a story: the salt-stiff coat with the crease at the left shoulder on its peg behind the door, the wheel-seat keeping its twenty-year lean and its hollow that nobody filled, the clean dark line with its finer grey beside it, and the debt against a firing of tiles neither paid nor settled nor done with, and worse for being uncalled. His proverb had gone years ago to the pot and the fire and never to a person: leave the crack showing; people trust a thing they can see has been handled. His name had not been spoken under this roof for two hundred and eighty-four days, and no collector had come up \place{the clay road} in the afternoons behind and none had gone down it.

At the going of the light \person{Sella Vail} came for him and carried him up at the half-pace from every open slate. Her hand went under him the way a hand goes under a pot filled to the lip, making no slop, asking nothing, and the little-door burn on the back of her left hand closed on his weight and then on nothing as she lifted. She set him on \place{the bed} under the roof of \place{the Vail kiln-house} and never in \place{the Debt Loft}, put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face, and left the thread frozen where it lay. She gave the instruction to the fire, the wool, the pot, the broom and the running water and never to the basket. \emph{It was the thaw}, she told \place{the hearth}, and turned the coals with the back of a broken pot, and said \emph{The house will keep it} where another woman would have said \emph{I}. At \place{the loaf shelf} her finger stayed on top of the front edge so the charcoal beneath remained unread; her lips moved along a row standing with two gaps and gave neither gap a figure. She glanced once at the clay jar standing cold under its \lore{lead glaze} and shut her hand half over nothing. She stood the pot rim two inches beyond the reaching hand, with a half-beat and no look toward the bed. \lore[The widow's mark]{The widow's mark} hung where it hung on its cord at her throat and her hand did not go to it at a going of the light, because a going of the light is not an opening and the hand goes to the mark before the kiln and at no other hour. Four openings were four and not a rule; the first firing of Frost stood behind the household with its face turned away, and the next stood in a later month's first firing, far off, not hoped for.

\person{Tarn} was at \place{the kindling} when the last of the light went off the yard, with the small knife at the very edge of its sill. Six he counted in the worn mark worn almost flat by his thumb, where the house could check; the seventh he kept at the lip where it could not, and that was the one entry in this house the house could not check. Beside the knife lay Garl's unforged collector sentence, with no name on the man who had turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on the debt. \emph{Six counted. The next I don't count}, he told the wood, in the tone of a man stating the weather. He said \emph{Yes, house} to those who wanted the thaw early and to those who wanted it late, agreeing with both so thoroughly that no argument could find a foot, and keeping the late because it puts off the paying, though the paying stood in the ruts.

The sentence about an ear under this roof being worth more than the debt that held it came to his teeth and went as far as the word that would call the loan before it stopped, with no figure and no name --- a remembered stopping, the ninth, and no tenth stood up before the seventh year at its very end and still ungiven. He drew the breath that should have gone in at the top of the reach and did not spend it, and his chest was tight as it had been since the dark. \lore{Ash} was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line and no scrubbing lifted it; the pale clip-ring at his left wrist held what light there was and gave it back. His stroke went on over \place{the flagstones} past the shallow bright patch without the half-check, the stones keeping the place where it had stopped. From above came only a sound with a direction from a room he could not enter, and the warm stone in its unmarked pot was cold and unreconciled by not trying, which is not forgiveness.

At the edge of \place{the Vail wheel-shed} \person{Jorra Vail} came through with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bent little finger and under the nails, not bone-coloured \lore{slip}; he kept his dusty forearms and his clean ones on one tray and parted them as a surgeon parts clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had been broken nor whether it was a birth-slate. He spoke to the wheel before he spoke to any person. \emph{It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady}, he told it, and he told a pot \emph{You'll do} in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either. He knew the full note that pot would give three turns before it left his hands, with air under it and a want in it, and withheld the knowing on the near-full tray beside the hearings and never among them, advanced and not paid. Before Sella's sentence had finished, his bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper} ahead of it, moved the tongue a hair's breadth and left it standing; the end of the instruction arrived at the pot and the household straightened around his silence. The boy read one iron with two bites --- the holding and the taking --- with no \lore{Grathic} word for both mercies, joined to no kiln and entered in no slate.

\place{The back room} kept its finger's width of open and its sound of low voices. Men unnamed had gone low into it before first grey, and neither Jorra nor Sella had given the house a name for them, and nobody had said the room was cold. The burned-slate word stood at four from \place{Slate Row}, a direction over the boards and not a fifth telling; the two tellings of the counting room shut in the war stood at two and had not become three; the three sayings about a pot stood together with the premature Smoke 14 note on a place apart and no fourth likeness made a cause. The hand outside the wool went up an inch toward the river's note. It took the held interval and the empty interval from the file, set them side by side with a finger between, borrowed no house count, supplied no fifth to the kiln, crossed no silence that belonged to another's breathing, and came down on the river's next low note of water over water. Neither interval was promoted into a reach, a hearing, a gift, a keeping, a seventh, a stopping, a payment, a held change, a key, a power, a doctrine or a rule. It was an honest coordination, exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air. One hundred and forty-four stood beside one hundred and forty-three. He made nothing of it and would not let it stand up as a gift, because a gift wants a taker and nobody under this roof had taken anything.

The turn was from completion to continuation. The surgeon who had once laid out a tray for a night's trauma knew every object by its weight and knew the reach was still two inches short; the inventory was complete and useless and he kept it anyway. His clean satisfaction at setting two intervals side by side was checked by the infant's plain want of the crust two inches off; the checking was the work and he did not call it a virtue. The file was not closed. The hand laid back against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking.
