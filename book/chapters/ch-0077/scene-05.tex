\daymark{284}{Frost 15, Fifthday, Y.H. 813}

In the small hours under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof the house had the shape it keeps when nobody is looking at it: the coals banked an hour earlier sitting under their ash, \place{the loaf shelf} holding its low angle, \place{the bed} set where the underside of the shelf made a roof, and the finger's-width door showing a strip of river that went on saying the same thing in the dark. Frost 15 came on in its own morning and was not brought into the day behind it. It was a Fifthday with no market, no gathering at \place{the Cross} and no bell; the month's second Account-day, Frost 20 (Day 289), stood five mornings off and was not brought into a morning that had not earned it. \person[Corin]{The boy} lay with his ear in the fold and came into his two hundred and eighty-fourth day with nobody to tell him so. Six months and one hundred and four days were told by nobody. The number was exact and complete, and against a house that kept its days in loaves, glances and unnamed spaces it was worth what a tally keeps under a finger that will not turn the board over.

One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from his ear, gone past the point where a child would eat it, and the empty place beside it kept its name to itself; no third had come in the night to make the row come out even. The thread of drool at his mouth had frozen in the dry heat and was unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. Cold had come to cheek and temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, and stopped there as at the end of a line written out for it, with neither an order to go forward nor an order to go away. The afterdrop waited beyond all three, past a warming nobody in this house would perform. He knew the composed face that is not composure. He knew a cold patient cannot clot, and will seem better before he is worse, and that a man must be warmed from the outside and slowly. He wanted the crust lifted the two inches to his mouth. Both were true, and in a body six months and one hundred and four days old the second was louder. The surgeon took the inventory in its old order --- crust, gap, thread, cold, the waiting --- and entered none of it, not even on the place where he kept the things he did not enter. He had once reduced a shoulder in the dark by feel alone and could not now close two inches on a pot handle; the thought came and went without bitterness, which was its own small betrayal.

Two hundred and eighty-three had gone where all the house's days go, into the pot and the fold and the space nobody named, and two hundred and eighty-four stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in \place{Sarn}. Under the floor \lore{the carried fire} gave four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer unnamed for two hundred and eighty-four days, and nobody lifted the floor and nobody supplied the pull. At the finger's-width door the river kept its note of \lore{water over water}, a low thing over a lower thing, never numbered among the hearings; overhead \place{the boards} kept four nails on the near board, four on the next and a thumb-width knot where the fifth nail should have stood. Three counts in three places, and none rose into another, because agreeing any two would want a payer put under both and no payer stood to any. Six hearings of \lore{the full note} stood beside and never among, five from four sources and the sixth, the small flat knock out of a woman as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go and never with the things that came from vessels. The seventh place kept its emptiness and none was invented to fill it. The heard bell's four, four and a gap stayed in the square at the Cross beside the told bell of \lore{the Long Night}, not folded into the three and not heard as a seventh of the full note.

In the room whose floor does not touch his a woman gave its low counting to the dark --- the small intake, the hold, the letting-go --- and the boy took only what the hand could take and left the letting-go where it was. It was her breath and her count and not his, and he had long since stopped wanting the number of it spoken.

\person{Sella Vail} went to the fire before the basket, as she had gone every morning of this month, and gave the instruction to the fire and not to the thing in the fold. The little-door burn on the back of her left hand closed on the pot-lifter and then on nothing; she pushed coal-black hair back with that wrist and the one grey streak at her temple showed pale off the coals. \emph{Keep it, then. The house will keep it}, she told \place{the hearth}, and turned the coals with the back of a broken pot. \emph{It was the thaw}, she told them, and the fire answered four low pulls and a silence and asked for nothing in return that a person could have paid. At the loaf shelf her finger stayed on top of the front edge so the charcoal beneath remained unread; her lips moved along a row standing with two gaps and gave neither gap a figure. She glanced once at the clay jar standing cold under its \lore{lead glaze} and shut her hand half over nothing. She stood the pot rim two inches beyond the reaching hand, with a half-beat and no look toward the bed, and carried the basket down at the half-pace from every open slate when the light came. \lore[The widow's mark]{The widow's mark} hung where it hung on its cord at her throat and her hand did not go to it at a morning, a hearth or a rack, because none of these is an opening and the hand goes to the mark before the kiln and at no other hour. The first firing of Frost stood behind the household with its face turned away; four openings were four and not a rule, and the next stood in a later month's first firing, far off, not hoped for.

At \place{the rack end} she lifted a corner of the wool from the dressed grain and turned it once for damp, with a touch lighter than the morning before and lighter again than the morning before that, and laid it back where it had lain. No figure went with the turning. The arithmetic that had read over it on Frost 10 did not know what it cost, and the space between the figure and the price had no signature on it. Frost's first true subtraction stayed locked up \place{the clay road} in \person[Berr Clay]{Berr}'s box toward \place{High Kiln}; the printed one in twelve stood unchallenged and the figure read was still not the figure paid. Nothing under this roof had opened the box or copied the slate, and nothing under this roof would bring the reading back as a lesson, a figure, a mending or a payment.

The yard had gone around its seven walked-around places the day before with a look and a wide step and no number, and would go around them again this afternoon: the clean dark line at the Vail corner and the finer grey hairline beside it called the thaw, unswept both; the space beneath \place{the lowest rack} at \place{the drying racks} where the broken birth-slates lie; the round ring where the shallow pan once stood with no scrape or crumb; the skinned \place{the trough} and its water called weather; the field's empty place out on \place{the worked strips}; and the returning-line place where a hand went in on Seed 15 and did not come out. Seven were seven and not an account; the missing hand was carried, not named, not paid and not joined to the shelf's two gaps, the fold's one crust or the dressed grain. Out at \place{the yard well} the stayed \lore{frost-bloom} lay white on the north lip where the yard had left it and called it weather, and the plate of ice lifted off the trough stood on \place{the flat stone by the well} out of the path of feet, clouded where the silt had risen into it. None of it was entered, and no sign, cause, lesson, mending or payer was made of any of it. \person{Garl Vail} stayed in objects and not in a story: the salt-stiff coat with the crease at the left shoulder on its peg behind the door, the wheel-seat keeping its twenty-year lean and its hollow that nobody filled, the clean dark line with its finer grey beside it, and the debt against a firing of tiles neither paid nor settled nor done with, and worse for being uncalled. His proverb had gone years ago to the pot and the fire and never to a person. His name had not been spoken under this roof for two hundred and eighty-four days, and no collector had come up the clay road on the mornings behind and none had gone down it.

The saying from the well the day before stood on a place of its own in the file, beside \person{Hessa Rale}'s eighth record-first hand with a finger between. One saying, no second shaping; no witness stood under it, no confession had been made, no hand at a slate had been seen, no figure had been spoken, and nobody had stood anywhere to be asked. The boy had taken it apart as a surgeon takes a wound apart and filed it as a shaping toward a far-off day, not a hearing, not a seventh, not a tenth hand, not a cause, not a joining, not a witness and not paid. He did not join it to the strip in a sleeve a pace away, or to the twice-wiped thumb, or to the charcoal under the shelf, or to the inward slate at the landing, and he did not make \person{Ilva}'s ninth hand a tenth to answer it. The held interval and the empty interval lay side by side on the sixth place with a finger between, and neither they nor the saying were allowed to stand up and call themselves a doctrine, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood to any of them. The other keepings stayed exact and unadvanced: five askings of the ownerless question with one shaping and no witness and no row named; nine stoppings with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end and still ungiven; nine record-first hands with the ninth unread; six hearings with no seventh; the seventh green a colour and the seventh shape a shape with no eighth; the shelf's two gaps and the fold's one crust and its unnamed empty place with no third gap and no fourth likeness made a cause. Up in \place{the Debt Loft} the small dry scrape of the sill lay on a place of its own and the carried man's pale line on another, neither laid across the other; the loft came to him only as a sound with a direction and never as a room he was taken into. \lore{Ash} lay in the unswept corner and was called the thaw; \lore{not-doings} kept the house and the boy kept his gaps on another place with a finger between, and neither was laid across the other.

In the small hours the room whose floor does not touch his gave another count --- the small intake, the hold, the letting-go --- and the hand outside the wool went up an inch toward the river's note. It took the small intake of the river's own going and let it go on the same note without holding, without carrying it across a silence that belonged to somebody else's counting and without borrowing a house breath to close it, and came down. The letting-go was kept whole. It was not an ending made early, not a freedom and not a gift, for a freedom wants a taker and nobody under this roof had taken anything. It was an honest coordination, exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air: not a reach, not a hearing, not a seventh, not a stopping, not a payment, not a held change, not a key, not a power, not a doctrine and not a rule. One hundred and forty-three stood beside one hundred and forty-two.

The turn was from one saying that had gathered no witness to a letting-go that gathered no claim. The surgeon's clean observation of how a rhythm ends was checked by the infant's plain want of the crust two inches off; the checking was the work and he did not call it a virtue. Nothing had stood up out of either thing, no lesson from a voice at a well, no doctrine from a hand coming down on a note. The hand laid back against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking.

Two hundred and eighty-four stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill} and \place{Grath}; six months and one hundred and four days were told by nobody; the carried fire's fifth was unpaid the same number of days with its payer unnamed. The boy lay with the crust two inches off and its empty place unnamed, the hand laid down on the hundred and forty-third answer, and the file open on \place{the towel} at the head of the bed with every gap left gap, every tray parted by a finger's width and nothing that had moved. Beyond the finger's-width door the dressed grain lay covered under wool at the rack end, read over by a number that did not know its cost and still not knowing what it cost; the stayed frost lay white on the well lip where the yard had left it, called weather and entered nowhere, and the plate of ice stood where it had been set, clouded where the silt rose into it. Frost 20 (Day 289), the month's second Account-day, stood five mornings off and was not brought into this day. The river's low note went quiet after the hand came down.
