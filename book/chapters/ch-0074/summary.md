## Chronicle

Frost came on in its own light, and the boy came into it two hundred and seventy-three days old, carried and told by nobody what a number counted. The working week began by degrees and without announcement, with no market, Cross or bell. A brown-edged crust lay two inches from his ear, its empty place unnamed; the thread at his mouth froze in the dry heat and was left unwiped; cold came to cheek and temple and the bare wrist, and the afterdrop waited past a warming nobody would perform. He took the inventory as a surgeon takes a field. He wanted the crust lifted two inches. That was the louder truth.

Three counts stood in three places: four low pulls under the floor with the fifth withheld, water over water at the door, four nails and four and a thumb-width knot overhead. None rose into another, for agreeing any two would want a payer put under both, and none stood. Sella went to the fire before the basket, told the coals it was the thaw, turned them with the back of a broken pot, said The house will keep it, and carried him at the half-pace, going wide around seven places with a look and no number, among them the returning line where a hand went in on Seed 15 and did not come out. The grain lay under wool at the rack end, turned for damp and given no figure. Tarn kept six and a seventh at the knife's lip and stopped the ear-sentence at the word that would call the loan. Ilva kept her count in wood; Garl was kept in objects, his debt circling without a name.

On the Fifthday Sella's finger went beneath the shelf's front edge and stayed for two counted breaths where on Day 166 it had stayed for one, then came away without a word. The charcoal was not read, and the two gaps above were given no number. The boy filed the returned beat on the shelf's own place, beside the fraction at the damper and the older held beat, and would not let it stand up as a fourth likeness, a gift or a rule. A house could change by two held breaths and say nothing.

Six times the hand went up an inch toward the river's note --- for the small intake, for a holding carried across the little silence, for a held beat returned, for two intakes kept apart, for an ending made early --- and came down, borrowing no house count and supplying no fifth. At the close the held interval and the empty interval lay side by side with a finger between, neither made to agree with the missing fifth. Two hundred and seventy-five stood exact, spoken by nobody. Nothing had been opened, named, called, paid or ended; the file lay open on the towel with every gap left gap, and the hand lay back against the fold, the river's low note going quiet after it.
