\daymark{275}{Frost 6, Sixthday, Y.H. 813}

Frost came on its own morning, earned by the light at the finger's-width door and not brought into a day that had not earned it, and the count went from two hundred and seventy-four to two hundred and seventy-five the way a man lays one board beside another and does not measure the seam. Six months and ninety-five days were told by nobody under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof in \place{Low Kiln}. There was no market on a Sixthday and no Cross and no bell, and the carried fire's fifth was unpaid two hundred and seventy-five days with its payer unnamed, as it had gone unpaid on the Fourthday and the Fifthday before this one and on every morning since the coal was first tipped under a broken pot.

He took the body in the order the facts presented themselves, which is the order a field is taken when the light is poor and the wounds must be found by the hand. One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from his ear, gone past the point where a child would eat it, and the empty place beside it kept its name to itself; no third had come in the night to make the row come out even. The thread of drool at his mouth had frozen in the dry heat and was unwiped, because nobody under any roof in \place{Sarn} had ever been given an instruction about the thread, and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. Cold had come to cheek and temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, and stopped there as at the end of a line written out for it, with neither an order to go forward nor an order to go away. The afterdrop waited beyond all three, past a warming nobody in this house would perform. He knew the composed face that is not composure. He knew a cold patient cannot clot and will seem better before he is worse, and that a man must be warmed from the outside and slowly. He wanted the crust lifted the two inches to his mouth. Both were true, and in a body two hundred and seventy-five days old the second was louder; the surgeon took the inventory and let \person[Corin]{the boy} want, and entered neither, not even on the place where he kept the things he did not enter.

The small hours smelled of hot clay cooled under tallow and of wet wool and, under both, the sour mineral breath of the unswept corner called \lore{the thaw}; rime stood on the step where the Warm had lately gone out, and the house would call it weather by daylight. Through the finger's-width door the river kept its low note over a lower note, \lore{water over water}, never numbered among the hearings. Under the floor \lore{the carried fire} gave four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood, with no fire-sitter to supply it and no house count borrowed to carry any of it across. Overhead \place{the boards} kept four nails on the near board, four on the next, and a thumb-width knot where the fifth nail should have stood. Three counts in three places, and none rose into another, because agreeing any two would want a payer put under both and none stood to any. The woman's low counting in the room whose floor does not touch his came through the wall as a sound with a direction and no name, the small intake, the hold, the letting-go, and the boy took the beginning and the holding of it and left the letting-go where it was.

\scenebreak

The grammar \person{Sella Vail} kept held as it had held through the week. She went to \place{the hearth} before the basket and gave the instruction to the fire and to the wool and to the pot and to the broom and to the running water, and never once to the basket, and said \emph{The house will keep it} where another woman would have said \emph{I}. She told the coals it was the thaw and turned them with the back of a broken pot, and the pot's foot set down on stone gave its small flat sound and went out into the room's ordinary noise with no lesson made of it. She set the pot rim with its handle two inches beyond the reaching hand, with a half-beat and no look toward \place{the bed}. She put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face, and left the thread where it lay. At \place{the loaf shelf} she kept her finger on top of the front edge so the charcoal tally underneath stayed where it was, unread, uncopied and unfigured, and moved her lips along a row standing with two gaps and gave neither gap a figure. On this morning she did not let the finger go beneath the edge; the two held breaths of the Fifthday stayed where they had been laid, a held change on its own place and not a rule. She glanced once at the clay jar standing cold under its \lore{lead glaze} and shut her hand half over nothing. She pushed a strand of coal-black hair back with the wrist that carries the burn, and the one grey streak at her temple showed pale off the coals, and the little door of the burn shut on nothing. She had touched \lore{the widow's mark} once before the kiln on Frost 2 and had not touched it since, not at a hearth, a shelf, a rack, a yard, a basket or a morning, because none of these is an opening and the hand goes to the mark before the kiln and at no other hour.

On the cool stone at \place{the rack end} the dressed grain lay under wool away from the hearth. She lifted a corner and turned it once beneath the wool with a touch lighter than the morning before, and set it back where it had lain, changed in state and given no figure. The arithmetic that would read over it in the days to come did not know what it cost, and the space between the figure that would be read and the price that had been paid stayed unsigned. No number went into the fold and no number came out of it.

The first firing of Frost stood behind the household and was not looked at again, not as a sign, not as a lesson, not as a mending anybody would name and not as a payment. Four openings in four months were four and not a rule, for a rule wants a payer standing under it and none stood. The household's refusal to make a doctrine of its own openings was itself a finding, and the boy kept that finding on a place of its own with a finger between it and the house's not-doings and another between it and his own kept gaps; to lay any two together would want a payer, and there was no payer under any of them. He did not call the refusal kindness and did not call it tuition, and nothing he had kept was made to stand up as a doctrine because it had happened four times. Frost 10 stood four mornings ahead and was not brought early into a day that had not earned it.

\person{Garl Vail} was kept in objects and not in a story, his name unspoken under this roof for two hundred and seventy-five days. The salt-stiff coat with the crease at the left shoulder held a broader man's shape on its peg behind the door; the wheel-seat in the shed kept its twenty-year lean and its hollow on that side, and no one had been allowed to fill it; the proverb had gone years ago to a pot with no crack in it and was said now to the pot and the fire and never to a person; the debt against a firing of tiles lay where it lay, neither paid nor settled nor done with, circled without a name and worse for being uncalled. No collector came up \place{the clay road} on Frost 4, 5 or 6 and none went down it.

In the yard the seven walked-around places were gone around with a look and a wide step and no number: the clean dark line at the Vail corner and the finer grey hairline beside it called the thaw, the space beneath \place{the lowest rack} where the broken birth-slates lay, the round ring where the shallow clay pan had stood with no scrape or crumb or print or chip or word, \place{the yard well} with \place{the trough} bare and dark and its water called weather, the field's empty place out on \place{the worked strips}, and the returning-line place where the hand had gone in on Seed 15 and had not come out. The missing hand was carried and not named, not paid and not joined to the shelf's two gaps or the fold's one crust or the covered grain. Seven were seven and not an account, because an account wants a payer standing under it and none stood.

\scenebreak

At \place{the kindling} \person{Tarn} took the broom only after it had been given its instruction, and said \emph{Yes, house} to those who wanted the thaw early and to those who wanted it late, and kept the late because it puts off the paying, though the paying stood in the ruts. Six he counted in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check, and the seventh at the very lip of the sill where it could not, and he told the wood \emph{Six counted. The next I don't count} in the tone of a man stating the weather. The sentence about an ear under this roof being worth more than the debt that held it came to his teeth and went as far as the word that would call the loan before stopping, with no figure and no name, a remembered ninth stopping and no tenth before the seventh year at its very end and still ungiven. He drew the breath that should have gone in at the top of the reach and did not spend it, his chest as tight as it had been since the dark; \lore{Ash} was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line and no scrubbing lifted it, and the pale clip-ring at his left wrist held what light there was and gave it back. His stroke went on over \place{the flagstones} past the shallow bright patch without the half-check, the stones still keeping the place where it had stopped. From a room above and behind there came the small dry scrape of a pot's foot set down on stone, a sound with a direction, and the warm stone in its unmarked pot in \place{the Debt Loft} stayed cold and unreconciled by not trying. The boy set the sound on its own place and did not lay it across the shelf's held beat returned on the Fifthday, for that joining would want a payer and none stood.

\person{Jorra Vail} came through from \place{the Vail wheel-shed} with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal into both forearms past the bend of his little finger and white in the creases of a fist that had opened, not the bone-coloured slip of the wheel. He kept dusty forearms and clean ones on one tray and parted them as a surgeon parts clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had been broken nor whether it was a birth-slate. He spoke to the wheel before he spoke to any person --- \emph{It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady} --- and told a pot \emph{You'll do} in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either. The \lore{full note} that pot would give three turns ahead stood on the near-full tray with air under it and a want in it, known and withheld, advanced and not paid. His bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper} ahead of Sella's unfinished sentence, moving the tongue a hair's breadth and leaving it standing so that the end of the instruction arrived at the pot; the boy read one piece of iron with two bites, the holding and the taking, with no \lore{Grathic} word for both mercies, joined to no kiln and entered in no slate. Three or more men went low into the finger's-width-open \place{the back room} before first grey and got no name from the house, and the room kept its finger's width of open and its sound of low voices and got no name from anybody. The burned-slate word came from \place{Slate Row} as a direction over the boards, told four times and not a fifth; the two tellings of the counting room shut in the war stood at two and did not become three; the three sayings about a pot kept the premature Smoke 14 note on its own place with no fourth likeness made a cause. The promise of the boy's name to the men who burn slates was neither given nor withdrawn, and \emph{he's not Vail} stayed sealed.

In the small hours a woman's low counting came through the wall from the room whose floor does not touch his, flattened by work and given nobody's face, the small intake, the hold, the letting-go. Outside the wool the hand went up an inch toward the river's note through the finger's-width door. It took the small intake and the hold of one of those counted breaths, and came down on the river's next low note while that house's count was still letting go. The letting-go was left where it was, outside the holding and entered nowhere, not crossed and not filled, and no house count was borrowed to carry the hand's own inch across and no fifth pull was supplied to the carried fire under the floor. A separation had been made by ending early and not by filling, which is a cheaper thing and a cleaner one; the two intakes held apart on the Fifthday stayed on their own place with a finger between, and no likeness between them and this early ending was made a cause, because a cause wants a payer and none stood. One hundred and twenty-five was filed on the sixth place beside one hundred and twenty-four, exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air: an honest ending and not a reach, not a hearing, not a seventh, not a stopping, not a payment, not one of the held changes, not a key, a power, a doctrine or a rule. He made nothing of it and would not let it stand up as a freedom, because a freedom wants a man to take it and there was nobody here who could take anything. He could not lift the crust two inches to his mouth. He could decide where a holding stopped, and did not call it one.

The hand lay back against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. Two hundred and seventy-five stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in Sarn, \place{Sill} or \place{Grath}; six months and ninety-five days were told by nobody, and the fire's fifth was unpaid the same number of days with its payer unnamed. The letting-go in the next room went on where it was, entered nowhere, and Frost went toward its seventh morning without a boundary drawn.
