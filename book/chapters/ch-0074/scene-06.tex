Heat went out of the afternoon the way it goes out of a pot set off the coals: not all at once and with no boundary drawn in the going, but by degrees, drawing back from the rim and sitting down behind the pot where it had always been. The light went with it off \place{the boards} and out of \place{the Clay Yard}, and Frost went out of the day without a line drawn under it, and neither of the district's two habits of naming the months was set right or left wrong by anybody under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof in \place{Low Kiln}. Four openings stood behind the household as four and not a rule, for a rule wants a payer standing under it and none stood; the next opening stood in the first firing of a later month, far off and not hoped for. Nothing had been made of the week's small movements, and nothing would be.

One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from his ear, gone past the point where a child would eat it, and the empty place beside it kept its name to itself; no third had come into the afternoon to make the row come out even. The thread of drool at his mouth had frozen in the dry heat and was unwiped, because nobody under any roof in \place{Sarn} had ever been given an instruction about the thread and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. Cold had come to cheek and temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, and stopped there as at the end of a line written out for it, with neither an order to go forward nor an order to go away. The afterdrop waited beyond all three, past a warming nobody in this house would perform. He knew the composed face that is not composure, and that a cold patient cannot clot and will seem better before he is worse, and that a man must be warmed from the outside and slowly. He wanted the crust lifted the two inches to his mouth. Both were true, and in a body two hundred and seventy-five days old the second was louder; the surgeon took the inventory in its old order --- crust, gap, thread, cold, the waiting --- and entered none of it, not even on the place where he kept the things he did not enter.

\person{Garl Vail} was kept in objects and not in a story, his name unspoken under this roof for two hundred and seventy-five days. The salt-stiff coat with the crease at the left shoulder held a broader man's shape on its peg behind the door; the wheel-seat in the shed kept its twenty-year lean and its hollow on that side, and no one had been allowed to fill it; the proverb had gone years ago to a pot with no crack in it and was said now to the pot and the fire and never to a person; the debt against a firing of tiles lay where it lay, neither paid nor settled nor done with, circled without a name and worse for being uncalled. A debt uncalled can be gone around every day of a man's life and never be nearer done, which is the cruellest arithmetic in Low Kiln and the one nobody there will enter. No collector came up \place{the clay road} on Frost 4, 5 or 6 and none went down it.

In the yard the seven walked-around places were gone around with a look and a wide step and no number, the way a woman carries a full pail past a child who might knock it: the clean dark line at the Vail corner where the \lore{Ash} ridge had turned and the finer grey hairline beside it called \lore{the thaw}, unswept both; the space beneath \place{the lowest rack} at \place{the drying racks} where the broken birth-slates lay; the round ring where the shallow clay pan had stood, with no scrape or crumb or print or chip or word to say where it had been; \place{the trough} beside \place{the yard well}, bare and dark, its water called weather by everybody who drew from it and by nobody who had to pay for it; the field's empty place out on \place{the worked strips}; and the returning-line place where the hand had gone in on Seed 15 and had not come out. The missing hand was carried and not named, not paid and not joined to the shelf's two gaps, the fold's one crust or the dressed grain under wool at \place{the rack end}. Seven were seven and not an account, because an account wants a payer standing under it and none stood. \person[Corin]{The boy} went around them with his eyes and kept them on seven places that did not touch.

At the going of the light \person{Sella Vail} carried him up at the half-pace from every open slate and set him on \place{the bed} under the roof and not in \place{the Debt Loft}, the way a pot is set where it will not be knocked and not where it is most convenient to reach. She put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face, and left the thread where it lay. She went to \place{the hearth} before the basket and told the fire it was the thaw, and turned the coals with the back of a broken pot, and the pot's foot gave its small flat sound on stone and went out into the room's ordinary noise with no lesson made of it. \emph{The house will keep it}, she said, where another woman would have said \emph{I}. She had said it on every morning of the week and would say it again tomorrow, and the saying had not grown truer by repetition and had not grown less true either; it was the grammar the house used to keep a thing without entering it.

At \place{the loaf shelf} she kept her finger on top of the front edge so the charcoal tally underneath stayed where it was, unread, uncopied and unfigured, and moved her lips along a row standing with two gaps and gave neither gap a figure. On the Fifthday the finger had gone beneath that edge and stayed two counted breaths and come away without a word; tonight it stayed on top, and the two breaths kept the place they had been given and did not stand up and call themselves a rule. She glanced once at the clay jar standing cold under its \lore{lead glaze} and shut her hand half over nothing, and set the pot rim two inches beyond the reaching hand, with a half-beat and no look toward the bed. At no hour of this Sixthday did she give an instruction to the basket. The instruction went to the fire and the wool and the pot and the broom and the running water, and never to the thing lying in the basket, which was a keeping and not a conversation.

\lore{The widow's mark} hung where it hung on its cord at her throat, a thumb-length of clay under lead glaze with the Vail kiln scratched into it. She had touched it once before the kiln on Frost 2, held it one breath, let go, and did not go back; she had not touched it at a shelf, a well, a rack, a road, a basket, a yard, a morning, a hearth, an afternoon, a dusk or a going of the light on Frost 4, 5 or 6, because none of these is an opening and the hand goes to the mark before the kiln and at no other hour. The first firing of Frost stood behind the household with no lesson, sign, mending anybody would name or payer, and the boy did not look at it again. He had learned that an opening is an opening and that four are four, and had been careful not to let the learning become a doctrine, because a doctrine is an account that has found a payer.

In the small hours the file was turned out on the towel as instruments are turned out for a man who will never be called to use them, each thing laid in its own place with room between, exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air. The surgeon who had once laid out a tray for a night's trauma and known every instrument by its weight found the tray complete and the reach two inches short, and made nothing of the finding.

Six hearings of \lore{the full note} stood beside and never among, five from four sources and the sixth, the small flat knock out of a woman as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go and never with the things that came from vessels; no seventh stood in the empty place, and none had been invented to fill it, for an empty place kept empty is a finding and a finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged. The heard bell's four, four and a gap stayed in the square beside the told bell of \lore{the Long Night}, not folded into the three counts under this roof and not heard as a seventh of the full note. The premature pot note from Smoke 14 lay on its own place apart from the three sayings about a pot, and no fourth likeness had been made a cause, no pot lost, no name given to what ailed the man carried out on Day 188.

The burned-slate word stood at four from \place{Slate Row}, a direction over the boards and not a fifth telling; the two tellings of the counting room shut in the war stood at two and had not become three; the sayings about a pot stood at three. The question about the row stood at five askings with one further shaping beside them and a finger between, no witness anywhere to be asked and no row named; no sixth asking had come over the boards on Frost 4, 5 or 6, and the silence was a keeping and not a sixth witness. \person{Tarn}'s sentence about an ear under this roof being worth more than the debt that held it had come to the word that would call the loan and stopped there on each of the three days, a remembered ninth stopping and no tenth before the seventh year at its very end and still ungiven; the Seed 16 breath stayed unspent at the top of every reach, and the loan lay uncalled in the ruts.

Nine record-first hands stood on nine places with a finger between every pair: the midwife's slate before the fold, the thane's finger over the figure, the keeper's finger beneath the board, the runner's strap and wrist, the clerk's number before the eyes found the buyer, \person[Berr Clay]{Berr}'s twice-wiped thumb, the soft-cuffed clerk's inward entry, \person[Hessa Rale]{Hessa}'s edge-on slate, and \person{Ilva}'s thumb-nail crossing the notched birch at the well, her strip unread in her sleeve and no figure coming off it. No tenth hand had been filed and the nine were not a rule. The seventh green stood a colour at a silent woman's mouth and the seventh shape a shape in cast-off clothes at a market's edge, with no eighth of either and no name spoken over either. Smoke's third arithmetic sat locked in Berr's box up the clay road, unread under this roof; the printed figure stood where it had been printed and the figure read was still not the figure paid; the dressed grain lay covered under wool on the cool stone at the rack end, turned once more with a lighter touch for damp and given no figure, and the arithmetic that would read over it in four mornings did not know what it cost.

The finding that the house keeps itself by its not-doings stayed on its own place with the boy's own kept gaps on another and a finger between, and he had not laid the finding across the coat, the uncalled loan, the unread charcoal, the missing fifth or the crust in the fold, for to lay any two together would want a payer and there was no payer under any of them. The week had added small movements to the file --- a finger under a board for two breaths on the Fifthday, two intakes held apart on that same day, an early ending on this Sixthday --- and each stayed where it was laid as a held change on its own place beside the Day 161 fraction, the Day 166 held beat and the Tallow 22 flagstone half-check, with no fourth likeness standing up and no doctrine made of three. The kiln-master's saying about pots that sing early was a saying, the wrong wind's sweet glaze a direction arrived nowhere, and the white the frost had written on the well lip was weather by noon and a white thing kept on a place of its own by the boy; none of these had been joined, and the week had not taught him a lesson he could spend.

The held interval and the empty interval lay side by side on the sixth place with a finger between, and neither had been made to agree with the fifth pull missing under the floor, for agreeing any two would want a payer put under both and none stood to either. The shelf's held beat returned at two breaths on the Fifthday was a held change on its own place, not a fourth likeness, not a key, not a doctrine; the two intakes held apart stayed apart; the early ending stayed an ending and not a freedom. He had spent the week refusing to promote what he could not use, and the refusing cost him nothing and gained him nothing, which was the only honest work a body two hundred and seventy-five days old could do. A man who cannot lift a crust two inches can still decide where a holding stops, and the temptation to call that a gift had come and gone each time without his giving it a name.

In the next room whose floor does not touch his a woman's low counting came through the wall, flattened by work and given nobody's face, the small intake, the hold, the letting-go, and the little silence after it. Outside the wool the hand went up an inch toward the river's note through the finger's-width door, \lore{water over water}, low over lower. It took the small intake of one of those counted breaths and the hold of it, and stayed past the letting-go and through the little silence after it, and set the held interval and the empty interval side by side with a finger between, borrowing no house count to carry the inch across and supplying no fifth pull to \lore{the carried fire} under the floor. It took nothing from the row with two gaps in the other room and nothing from the charcoal under the shelf and nothing from the covered grain at the rack end. It came down on the river's next low note with the woman's letting-go left where it had been, outside the holding and entered nowhere.

One hundred and twenty-six was filed on the sixth place beside one hundred and twenty-five, exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air: an honest coordination and not a reach, not a hearing, not a seventh, not a stopping, not a payment, not one of the held changes, not a key, a power, a doctrine or a rule. He made nothing of it and would not let it stand up as a gift, because a gift wants a taker and nobody under this roof had taken anything; the restraint was the work and he did not promote it. The hand laid back against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking.

Two hundred and seventy-five stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in Sarn, \place{Sill} or \place{Grath}; six months and ninety-five days were told by nobody, and the fire's fifth was unpaid the same number of days with its payer unnamed. The file lay open on the towel with every gap left gap, every tray parted from its neighbour and nothing that had moved. Nothing had been opened, named, called, paid or ended on this Sixthday; the first firing of Frost stood behind the household without a lesson, the next opening stood far off in a later month's first firing, and Frost went on toward its seventh morning without a boundary drawn. The river's low note went quiet after the hand came down, and the boy lay with the crust two inches off and the empty place beside it unnamed, carried forward and unresolved.
