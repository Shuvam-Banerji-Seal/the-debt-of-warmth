The gap stayed unfilled under the Vail roof. It had sounded in the square at the second reading and gone up the clay road with the rest of the day, and nothing in this room had crossed itself to answer it. \person[Corin]{the boy} lay at the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf} with the hand resting where the ninety-third answer had laid it down, and the house had come to the hour in which the woodsmoke gives up its separate smells and lies flat under the rafters. \person{Sella Vail} was gone to her own bed. There was no breathing number anywhere under the roof and no fire-sitter over the coals. Through the finger's-width door the river kept its note of \lore{water over water}, low over lower, and under the floor \lore{the carried fire} pulled four times and kept the silence where the fifth should have stood, and the two sounds went on in two places without meeting. A day spent in a square does not become another day by being carried home in a basket. It was still Smoke 20 under this roof; the count could go forward in the dark without dragging a morning up by its heels. Two hundred and fifty-nine stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in \place{Sarn}, \place{Sill} or \place{Grath}. Six months and seventy-nine days were told by nobody. The fire's fifth was unpaid the same number of days and its payer had no name.

Dry heat lay behind the pot and came round into the fold, and the wool smelled sour with a body's long lodging. He took the body in the order the facts presented themselves, as a surgeon takes a field and asks only what is there. One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from his ear, gone past the point where a child would have eaten it, and the empty place beside it kept its name to itself; no third had come in the night to make the row come out even. The thread of drool at his mouth had frozen in the dry heat and was unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. Cold had come to cheek and temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, and had stopped there as if a line had been written out to that length; it neither went forward nor went away. The afterdrop waited beyond all three, past a warming nobody in this house would perform. He knew the composed face that is not composure. He knew a cold patient cannot clot. He wanted the crust lifted the two inches to his mouth. Both were true and the second was louder in a body of two hundred and fifty-nine days, and the surgeon took the inventory and entered none of it, not even on the place where he kept the things he did not enter.

Overhead \place{the boards} kept four nails on the near board, four on the next, and a thumb-width knot where the fifth nail should have stood. Three counts in three places: the fire's four and its gap under the floor, the river's low over lower through the door, the boards' four and four and knot above him. None rose into another, because agreeing any two would want a payer put under both and no payer stood to any. Six hearings of \lore{the full note} stood beside and never among, five from four sources and the sixth, the small flat knock out of a woman as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go. The empty place where a seventh would stand was left empty; a finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged. The heard bell of four, four and a gap stayed in the square where it had sounded, beside the told bell of \lore{the Long Night}, not folded into the three and not heard as a seventh of the full note.

The arithmetic of the day was behind him and unread under this roof. \person{Berr Clay}'s narrow slate and the box with the true subtraction inside had gone up \place{the clay road} toward \place{High Kiln} before the light went; \person{Hessa Rale}'s slate had gone up unread toward \place{Greybank} before noon, the writing turned to her sleeve; the soft-cuffed clerk's inward slate stayed where it had been set below \place{the north bridge}, at the out-of-line landing where a fifth step should have stood. None had been opened. None had been copied. None had been brought near the basket. A slate carried past a basket is not a slate opened beside it, and a figure that goes up the road in a locked box has not therefore come home in anybody's mouth.

The figure read aloud over the covered grain at \place{the Cross} was still not the figure paid. The dressed grain lay under wool on the cool stone at \place{the rack end}, away from the hearth, changed in state and carrying no figure; it had been turned for damp and gathered by the handful and covered, and the arm that had gathered it knew what it weighed and not what it cost. The space between the figure that had gone over it in the square and the price of that grain had no signature to lay upon it. He kept the two figures on two places and did not average them, the way a surgeon keeps a chart beside a wound and does not write one into the other. A thing covered is not therefore paid for; a thing not entered is not therefore free. There was a small cold satisfaction in that, and the satisfaction was the knowledge that the reading had never been meant to know the grain. It had done its work perfectly. It knew the figure and not the cost, and that was the whole of what it knew.

In the small hours he turned the file out as a surgeon turns instruments out on a towel at the beginning of a case, each thing laid where the next hand will find it without looking, and found the whole of it exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air. There was no hand to enter any of it and no slate within reach, and the towel lay on nothing but the fold. He went through it in its order, and the order was the whole of the keeping; a thing laid on the wrong tray is a thing made to answer a question nobody had asked it.

Six hearings stood beside and never among, five from four sources and the sixth, the small flat knock out of a woman as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go and never with the things that came from vessels. No seventh stood in the empty place and none had been invented to fill it. Three near-full answers stood advanced and not paid, with air under them and a wanting in them, and beside them three shapings of the pot saying with the premature note of Smoke 14 on its own place; no fourth likeness had stood up to call itself a cause, and no pot had been lost. The burned-slate word stood at four from \place{Slate Row}, a direction over the boards and not a fifth telling; the two tellings of the counting room shut in the war stood at two and had not become three, though \lore{the damper} in the shed held its double bite under one piece of iron and there was no word in \lore{Grathic} for both mercies. The heard bell stood beside the told bell, its four, four and a gap not laid across the fire's four and missing fifth or the boards' four nails and thumb-width knot. The told figure stood beside the paid figure, and Smoke's second arithmetic lay locked up the clay road in Berr's box with the true subtraction inside, answering nothing this towel could hold. Nine stoppings stood with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end, and the seventh year had still not given; the Seed 16 breath lay unspent and the loan uncalled.

The Day 161 fraction at the damper lay on its own place; the Day 166 held beat beneath the loaf shelf lay on a second; the flagstone half-check of Tallow 22 on a third. The small dry scrape of a pot's foot from the loft sill lay on a fourth, the pale line of the carried man on a fifth, and the held inch on a sixth with its honest answers standing on it. Six places and a finger between every pair, and nothing laid across two of them, for a crossing would have wanted a payer set under both and no payer stood to any. Nine record-first hands stood on their own places with a finger between every pair, and the ninth was \person{Ilva}'s, taken from the firing work and not from any house or Court; her notched strip of birch stayed in her sleeve, unread, unasked about, not taken out and not joined to the reed-cutter's cord turned so its knots stood, and no tenth hand was filed from a hand that did not reach. The seventh green was a colour and the seventh shape was a shape, with no eighth of either and no name spoken over either. The dressed grain lay covered and unfigured; the pan's gone ring, the fold's one crust and its empty place, the shelf's two gaps and the field's empty place lay on trays that did not touch. The returning-line place where a hand had gone in on Seed 15 was gone around and not named. The ownerless question stood at five askings on its seventh place with no owner, no room, no hand at the kiln, no cause, no witness and no row named. Five askings are not one witness and do not make a sixth; he had not opened the wound a second time to see whether it had changed its mind. A witness stands somewhere and can be asked, and none had stood. The towel held the whole of it and the whole of it was worth nothing at two inches of air; a thing worth nothing is not therefore free, and a thing kept is not therefore settled.

Two voices from the afternoon came to the basket as directions and not as speakers. At \place{the kindling} \person{Tarn} had taken the broom only after it had been instructed, and had said \emph{Yes, house} to \lore{the thaw}, the early and the late, agreeing with both so thoroughly that no argument could find a foot, and had kept the late because it puts off the paying. He had told the wood \emph{Six counted. The next I don't count}, with the small knife at the very edge of its sill and six where the house could check and the seventh at the lip where it could not. The sentence about an ear under this roof being worth more than the debt that held it had come to the word that would call the loan and had stopped there, with no figure and no name. It was a remembered stopping now, the ninth, and not a tenth standing up to make the nine into a rule; a rule wants a payer under it, and none stood. The loan stayed uncalled. The Seed 16 breath stayed unspent at the top of the reach, his chest tight as it had been since the dark, \lore{Ash} worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line, the pale clip-ring at his left wrist holding what light there was and giving it back. Up in \place{the Debt Loft}, which reached the basket only as a sound with a direction, the warm stone Sella had given him in the winter Garl died lay in its unmarked clay pot, two years gone and cold long since; not tried is not forgiven, and not forgiven is not entered.

At the edge of \place{the Vail wheel-shed} \person{Jorra Vail} had come through with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bent little finger and white in the creases of a fist, and had spoken to the wheel before he spoke to any person: \emph{It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady}. He had told a pot \emph{You'll do} in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either, and had known the full note it would give three turns ahead, with air under it and a wanting in it, and withheld the knowing on the near-full tray beside the hearings and never among them. When Sella's sentence had gone low and unfinished at the hearth his bent finger had gone into the cold iron of the damper ahead of it and had held until the end arrived at the pot. It was a remembered fraction now, held in the holding and not in a word, one piece of iron with two bites and no Grathic word for both mercies. His promise of the boy's name to the men who burn slates was neither given nor withdrawn, and \emph{he's not Vail} stayed sealed as tightly as the day it was spoken. The men who had gone low into \place{the back room} before first grey had got no name from him and he had not said the room was cold; the room kept its finger's width of open and its sound of low voices, which came to the basket as a direction and nothing more.

The second reading had answered nothing for the man carried out on Day 188, and no name had been given to what ailed him. He had been lifted through the glaze-room door with the sweet edge of \lore{lead glaze} on the wind behind him and a pale line across the back of one hand, and the line was on its own place and had not been joined to the green at a woman's mouth or to any ear; the boy had kept the small flat knock that came out of him as the sixth hearing, filed where a thing out of a person must go, and had not made a name of it in three years. No collector had come up the clay road for \person{Garl Vail}'s debt against a firing of tiles, and none had gone down it; the debt was neither paid nor settled nor done with, and the salt-stiff coat kept its broader shape on the peg with the crease at the left shoulder, and the wheel-seat kept its twenty-year hollow, and the proverb had gone years ago to a pot with no crack in it. His name had not been spoken under this roof for two hundred and fifty-nine days, and the keeping of him was maintenance and not a story. No collector had come for the hand that went in on Seed 15 and did not come out, and that place in the returning line was gone around with a look and a wide step and no number, and no figure had ever been given for it. A line left where it is is still a line kept.

He put the fourth question of the chapter to his hand. There was no ceremony in it and no answer he expected; a man tests a tool before he trusts it, and the testing is the whole of the work. Through the finger's-width door the river went on with its note, low over lower, and the wall between that sound and the bed did not touch anything but itself. There was no house count to borrow and no fire-sitter over the coals to give one; the only silence available was the little one after a count that had ended in the room that does not touch, and it was an object lying where it lay and not a gap that wanted filling.

The hand outside the wool went up an inch toward the low note and took one rhythm, from the note to the one following it. Then it carried that rhythm across the wall that does not touch, taking the little silence after the count that had ended in the other room as an object in its own right, crossing no second count against it and filling nothing to make a fifth stroke in a row that had none. An inch was a measurable thing and a silence was an empty thing, and he kept both as they were. The hand came down on the river's next low note, down on it and not past it. It was an answer and not an asking. It was ninety-four, filed on the sixth place beside ninety-three with no payer named, an object among objects, exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air: not a longer reach, not a miss entered as a finding, not a hearing of the full note, not a seventh of it, not a tenth stopping, not a payment, not one of the held changes, not a key, a power or a rule. He made nothing of it. The hand came down and lay back against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking.
