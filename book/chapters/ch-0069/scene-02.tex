The afternoon thinned without ending. It went the way heat goes out of a pot set off the coals --- not in one giving-up but by degrees, the heat drawing back behind the pot and into the fold where \person[Corin]{the boy} lay, so that a hand laid on the wool still found warmth while the air above it had gone cool. Nothing in the yard called the hour an end. The light went on being light, only less of it, and the work went on with less of it, and the boy took the body in the order the facts presented themselves, as a surgeon takes a field in the order the wounds present themselves, and entered none of it. One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from his ear, hardened past the point where a child would have eaten it, and the empty place beside it kept its name to itself; no third had come in the afternoon to make the row come out even. The thread of drool at his mouth had frozen in the dry heat and was unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. Cold had come to cheek, temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, and had stopped there as if a line had been written out for it to that length and no farther; it neither went forward nor went away. The afterdrop waited beyond all three, past a warming nobody in this house would perform. He knew the face severe cold wears, the composed face that is not composure, and he knew that a cold patient cannot clot and will seem better before he is worse. Both were true and the boy wanted the crust lifted the two inches to his mouth, and the second was louder, and the surgeon took the inventory and let the wanting stand and entered neither, not even on the place where he kept the things he did not enter.

Under the roof the afternoon was a house keeping itself the way a pot keeps heat --- without comment, and with one crack in it nobody would mention until the thaw. \person{Garl Vail}'s salt-stiff coat held a broader man's outline on its peg behind the door, the crease at the left shoulder still where a man had set his weight against a bench to reach a high shelf, and nobody had touched it. The wheel-seat under the shed kept its twenty-year lean and its hollow on that side. Nobody had spoken his name in two hundred and fifty-eight days and nobody spoke it now; the keeping of him was maintenance and not a story, and a line left where it is is still a line kept. His proverb had gone years ago to a pot with no crack in it, said now over a corner where the repair had been rubbed out --- leave the crack showing; people trust a thing they can see has been handled --- and the saying cost more with every saying of it, for there was nobody left to say it in his own voice. The debt against a firing of tiles went around in the household's mouth without a name put to it, circled the way a woman goes around Ash left in a corner, neither paid nor settled nor done with. No collector had come up \place{the clay road} on Smoke 19 and none had gone down it; the road kept its ruts and its silence, and the debt went on living by nobody saying the word for it.

\person{Sella Vail} carried the basket at the half-pace from every open slate. That was the whole of her rule and she did not have to decide it in an afternoon any more than a woman has to decide which hand takes the pail; the instruction went to the wool, the river wind, the bucket, the broom and the fire, and never to the basket, and she said \emph{the house} where another woman would have said \emph{I}. Out in \place{the Clay Yard} she went around seven things, and she went around them as wide as a pail of hot water set where a child might knock it. At the Vail corner she went around the clean dark line where \lore{Ash} had turned, and around the finer grey hairline called \lore{the thaw} that had gathered beside it, and gave neither a look long enough to make a cause of it. Under \place[the drying racks]{the lowest rack} she went around the space where the broken birth-slates lay, with a look and a wide step and no number. She went around \place{the pan's gone ring}, the round ring where the shallow clay pan had stood, with no scrape, crumb, print, chip or word to say what had been lifted from it. Beside \place{the yard well} she went around \place{the trough}, bare and dark, its water called weather by the yard, the ice gone out of it and the grey showing through. Out on \place{the worked strips} and up \place{the terrace} she went around the field's empty place. And in the returning line, where a hand had gone in on Seed 15 and had not come out with the last hands on Seed 27, she went around the empty place with a look and a wide step and no name.

Seven in a row were seven. They were not an account until a payer stood under them, and none stood; a ring is not a gap, a strip is not a rack, and a hand that did not come back is not therefore a name. He kept them on seven trays that did not touch, with a finger between every pair, and would not let them stand up and call themselves a doctrine, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood to any of these.

At the well \person{Ilva} drew with the pail against her knee, clay dried pale in the crescents of her nails and the finer grey rack dust worked into the seam of one thumb, which she did not scrub out. A woman with a pot asked what the firings of Smoke had cost. Ilva's hand stayed in her sleeve; the short strip of birch bark with its notches stayed where it was, pale where a thumb had rubbed it and dark in the uncut grain between, not taken out, not read, not asked about, not joined to the reed-cutter's cord turned so its knots stood or to any other private keeping. Her eyes went to the asker and her mouth answered the question put and not the one behind it. \emph{Ask the pot what it cost. I keep the count, not the account}, she said, and went on with her water, and nobody in the yard turned to look at her twice. The boy filed no tenth record-first hand. The ninth stayed on its place of its own beside the eighth with a finger between it and every other private keeping, and nine likenesses did not stand up and call themselves a rule, for a rule is an account that has found a payer and none stood to this one. No figure came off the stick.

At \place{the kindling} the broom had its instruction before any person had one, and \person{Tarn} took it up as a man takes up a tool already named. \emph{Yes, house}, he said to an early thaw and a late, agreeing with both so thoroughly that no argument could find a foot, and keeping the late because it puts off the paying, though the paying had stood in the ruts since the water went. \emph{Six counted. The next I don't count}, he told the wood, in the tone of a man stating the weather. The small knife lay at the very edge of its sill, six in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check and the seventh at the lip where it could not, with the unforged collector sentence beside it --- the sentence about a man sent for a collector who turned back at the frozen ford, with no name on the man and no figure on the debt. He had been that man and gave no name to the man. The sentence about an ear under this roof being worth more than the debt that held it came to his teeth and went as far as the word that would call the loan, and stopped there. It was a remembered stopping and not a new one, the ninth with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end and still ungiven; he would not stand a tenth up to make the nine into a rule, because a rule wants a payer under it and none stood. The Seed 16 breath was drawn at the top of the reach and not spent, a small interval at the top of each reach where it should have gone in; his chest had been tight since the dark. Ash was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line and no scrubbing lifted it, and the pale clip-ring at his left wrist, in place since Y.H. 806, held what light there was and gave it back. Up in \place{the Debt Loft}, which came to the boy only as a sound with a direction and never as a room he was taken into, the warm stone Sella had given him in the winter Garl died lay in an unmarked clay pot, two years gone and cold long since, and not tried is not forgiven. His stroke went on over \place{the flagstones} past the shallow bright patch without the half-check and without a turn of the head, and the stones kept the place where it had stopped, joined to no cause and to no ear.

At the edge of \place{the Vail wheel-shed} \person{Jorra Vail} came through with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bent little finger and under the nails, white in the creases where a fist had opened, and not bone-coloured \lore{slip}. Three or more men had gone low into \place{the back room} before first grey and he gave the house no name for them and did not say the room was cold; the room kept its finger's width of open, and its sound of low voices arrived at the basket as a direction and nothing more. He kept dusty forearms and clean ones on one tray and parted them as a surgeon parts clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had been broken nor whether it was a birth-slate; the broken birth-slates lay beneath the lowest rack where the yard went around them and entered them into nothing. He spoke to the wheel before he spoke to any person. \emph{It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady}, he said, and told a pot \emph{You'll do} in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either. He knew \lore{the full note} that pot would give three turns before it left his hands, with air under it and a want in it, and withheld the knowing on the near-full tray beside the hearings and never among them, advanced and not paid. The burned-slate word had come up over the boards four times now out of \place{Slate Row}, a direction and not a cause, and the two tellings of the counting room shut in the war stood at two and did not become three; the promise of the boy's name to the men who burn slates was neither given nor withdrawn, and \emph{he's not Vail} stayed sealed as tightly as a lid nobody had tried to lift in three years. When Sella's voice went low at \place{the hearth} and the sentence did not finish --- \emph{The fire's low. It wants---} --- his bent finger had gone into the cold iron of \lore{the damper} ahead of it, taken the cold out of the fingertip, held while the rest of the sentence came to its end at the pot, moved a hair's breadth and been left standing. One piece of iron, two bites: the bite that holds a vessel whole and the bite that can take the hand off a man, with no word in \lore{Grathic} for both mercies, filed as an object joined to no kiln and worth nothing at two inches of air.

\scenebreak

Sella carried him up the clay road at the going of the light at the half-pace, went wide around the seven places, and set him on \place{the bed} under the roof and not in the Debt Loft. She put the fold right without a look at his face and left the thread frozen, told the fire it was the thaw, turned the coals with the back of a broken pot, said \emph{The house will keep it} where another woman would have said \emph{I}, and stood a moment with the back of the pot in her hand counting under her breath toward no opening --- the small intake, the hold, the letting-go --- as if she were setting a row of tools in order on a board and would not have the row called a number. The grey streak at her temple showed pale off the coals; she pushed a strand of coal-black hair back with the wrist that carries the burn, the little-door burn closing on nothing and opening again to the work, and went to her own bed. Overhead \place{the boards} kept four nails on the near board, four on the next, and a thumb-width knot where the fifth nail should have stood. Through the finger's-width door the river kept its note of \lore{water over water}, a low thing over a lower thing, never numbered among the hearings. Under the floor \lore{the carried fire} pulled four times and kept the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer without a name for two hundred and fifty-eight days. Three counts in three places, and none rose into another, because agreeing any two would want a payer put under both and no payer stood to any of them; a river is not a fire and a knot is not a nail. \lore{The widow's mark} hung where it hung on its cord at her throat, a thumb-length of lead-glazed clay with the Vail kiln scratched into it, and she had not touched it at any shelf, well, rack, road, basket, afternoon or going of the light, because none of these was an opening. The one opening before Smoke 1 lay behind the household with no lesson, no sign, no mending and no payment, and no payer named to any of it.

In the small hours he turned the file out on the towel as a surgeon turns instruments out on a towel, and found the whole of it exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air. Six hearings of the full note stood beside and never among, five from four sources and the sixth, the small flat knock out of a woman as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go and never with the things that came from vessels. No seventh stood in the empty place and none had been invented to fill it, for an empty place kept empty is a finding and a finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged. Three near-full answers stood advanced and unpaid on their own tray; beside them, on a tray that did not touch, stood three shapings of the pot saying with the premature note from Smoke 14 on its own place and no fourth likeness made a cause. Four low things stood in four places that stayed four, and water over water was a fifth low thing on a place of its own, not a hearing and not a vessel's note; the told bell sat beside the heard bell, and the told figure sat beside the paid figure, with Smoke's first arithmetic gone up the road in a locked box and unread under this roof, and no signature on the space between the figure and its price. Nine stoppings stood with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end and still ungiven. Three held changes stood on three places, and with them the small dry scrape of a pot's foot set down on stone in the loft on a fourth, the pale line at the gum of a carried man on a fifth, and the held inch on a sixth with ninety-one answers that were objects and not reachings. Nine record-first hands stood each on its own tray, Ilva's ninth among them with a finger between it and the eighth and her strip of notched birch unread in her sleeve; the seventh green was a colour and the seventh shape a shape with no eighth. The covered grain lay under wool with no figure on it. The pan's gone ring and the shelf's two gaps and the fold's crust with its gap and the rack space and the bare trough and the field's empty place and the returning-line place stood on trays that did not touch, with no third gap and no fourth likeness made a cause. The ownerless question stood at five askings on a seventh place with no witness and no row named, and no sixth asking had been supplied to make the row of askings come out even. He kept the trays parted with a finger between every pair and laid no finger across any two, for absences standing in a row will call themselves a doctrine if nobody keeps a hand between them, and a doctrine is an account that has found a payer.

Through the finger's-width door the river kept its low note. Under the floor the carried fire held at four with its silence where the fifth should have stood. Between the two the wall did not touch anything but itself, and there was no breathing number anywhere in the house and no fire-sitter standing over the coals, so the only silence he borrowed was the little one after a count that had ended in the room that does not touch his. He put the sixth question to his hand. The hand outside the wool went up an inch toward the low note and held for the small intake only --- not for the whole interval, not across into the little silence after it, not borrowed from the room that does not touch --- and came down before the letting-go, down on the river's next note and not past it. An inch was a measurable thing and the intake was a length of water going over water, and he kept the two apart and borrowed no house count. It was an answer and not an asking. It was ninety-two, filed on the sixth place beside ninety-one with no payer named, an object among objects, exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air: not a longer reach, not a miss entered as a finding, not a hearing of the full note, not a seventh of it, not a tenth stopping, not a payment, not one of the held changes, not a key, a power or a rule. He made nothing of it. The hand came down and lay back against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking.

Two hundred and fifty-eight stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in \place{Sarn}, \place{Sill} or \place{Grath}; six months and seventy-eight days were told by nobody. Ninety-two honest answers stood on the sixth place and none of them was a reach. The carried fire stopped at four with its fifth unpaid the same number of days and its payer unnamed; the widow's mark hung where it hung; the dressed grain lay covered and unfigured; the seventh year stood at its very end and had still not given. Smoke went on without a boundary drawn and neither of the district's two names for the months was set right or left wrong. Smoke 20 stood one morning ahead and had not been brought into a day that had not earned it; the third reading stood beyond that and was not reached for, and the first firing of Frost stood far off and unbrought. The towel lay on the fold with every tray parted and every gap left gap, and the hand rested against the fold, and answer ninety-two lay filed on the sixth place, an object and not a power.
