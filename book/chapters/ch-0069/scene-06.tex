The day went on from the small hours into its afternoon without a boundary drawn in it, the way Smoke goes on, and nothing in the yard marked the crossing. \person[Corin]{the boy} lay where the hand had put itself down, at the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf} under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof, and the warmth that had gathered in the fold through the morning drew back from it by degrees as heat draws back from a pot set off the coals. He took the body in the order the facts presented themselves, as a surgeon takes a field in the order the wounds present themselves, and entered none of it. One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from his ear, hardened past the point where a child would have eaten it, and the empty place beside it kept its name to itself; no third had come in the afternoon to make the row come out even. The thread of drool at his mouth had frozen in the dry heat and was unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. Cold had come to cheek and temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, and had stopped there as though a line had been written out for it to that length and no farther; it neither went forward nor went away. The afterdrop waited beyond all three, past a warming nobody in this house would perform. He knew the face severe cold wears, the composed face that is not composure, and that a cold patient cannot clot and will seem better before he is worse. Both were true and the boy wanted the crust lifted the two inches to his mouth, and the second was louder in a body of two hundred and sixty days, and the surgeon took the inventory and let the wanting stand and entered neither, not even on the place where he kept the things he did not enter.

Under the roof the afternoon was a house keeping itself the way a pot keeps heat, without comment, and with one crack in it nobody would mention until \lore{the thaw}. \person{Garl Vail}'s salt-stiff coat held a broader man's outline on its peg behind the door, the crease at the left shoulder still where a man had set his weight against a bench to reach a high shelf, and nobody had touched it. The wheel-seat under the shed kept its twenty-year lean and its hollow on that side. Nobody had spoken his name in two hundred and sixty days and nobody spoke it now; the keeping of him was maintenance and not a story, and a line left where it is is still a line kept. His proverb had gone years ago to a pot with no crack in it, said over a corner where the repair had been rubbed out --- leave the crack showing; people trust a thing they can see has been handled --- and the saying cost more with every saying of it, for there was nobody left to say it in his own voice. The debt against a firing of tiles went around in the household's mouth without a name put to it, circled the way a woman goes around \lore{Ash} left in a corner, neither paid nor settled nor done with. No collector had come up \place{the clay road} on Smoke 19, 20 or 21 and none had gone down it; the road kept its ruts and its silence, and the debt went on living by nobody saying the word for it.

At \place{the kindling} the broom had its instruction before any person had one, and \person{Tarn} took it up as a man takes up a tool already named. \emph{Yes, house}, he said to an early thaw and a late, agreeing with both so thoroughly that no argument could find a foot, and keeping the late because it puts off the paying, though the paying had stood in the ruts since the water went. \emph{Six counted. The next I don't count}, he told the wood, in the tone of a man stating the weather. The small knife lay at the very edge of its sill, six in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check and the seventh at the lip where it could not, with the unforged collector sentence beside it --- the sentence about a man sent for a collector who turned back at the frozen ford, with no name on the man and no figure on the debt. He had been that man and gave no name to the man. The sentence about an ear under this roof being worth more than the debt that held it came to his teeth and went as far as the word that would call the loan, and stopped there. It was a remembered stopping and not a new one, the ninth with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end and still ungiven; he would not stand a tenth up to make the nine into a rule, because a rule wants a payer under it and none stood. The Seed 16 breath was drawn at the top of the reach and not spent, a small interval at the top of each reach where it should have gone in; his chest had been tight since the dark. Ash was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line and no scrubbing lifted it, and the pale clip-ring at his left wrist, in place since Y.H. 806, held what light there was and gave it back. Up in \place{the Debt Loft}, which came to the boy only as a sound with a direction and never as a room he was taken into, the warm stone \person{Sella Vail} had given him in the winter Garl died lay in an unmarked clay pot, two years gone and cold long since, and not tried is not forgiven. His stroke went on over \place{the flagstones} past the shallow bright patch without the half-check, the stones keeping the place where it had stopped, and the small dry scrape from the loft sat on a place of its own with no cause and no owner.

At the edge of \place{the Vail wheel-shed} \person{Jorra Vail} came through with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bent little finger and under the nails, white in the creases where a fist had opened, and not bone-coloured \lore{slip}. Three or more men had gone low into \place{the back room} before first grey and he gave the house no name for them and did not say the room was cold; the room kept its finger's width of open, and its sound of low voices arrived at the basket as a direction and nothing more. He kept dusty forearms and clean ones on one tray and parted them as a surgeon parts clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had been broken nor whether it was a birth-slate; the broken birth-slates lay beneath \place[the drying racks]{the lowest rack} where the yard went around them and entered them into nothing. He spoke to the wheel before he spoke to any person. \emph{It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady}, he said, and told a pot \emph{You'll do} in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either. He knew \lore{the full note} that pot would give three turns before it left his hands, with air under it and a want in it, and withheld the knowing on the near-full tray beside the hearings and never among them, advanced and not paid. The burned-slate word had come up over \place{the boards} four times now out of \place{Slate Row}, a direction and not a cause, and the two tellings of the counting room shut in the war stood at two and did not become three; the promise of the boy's name to the men who burn slates was neither given nor withdrawn, and \emph{he's not Vail} stayed sealed as tightly as a lid nobody had tried to lift in three years. When Sella's voice went low at \place{the hearth} and the sentence did not finish --- \emph{The fire's low. It wants---} --- his bent finger had gone into the cold iron of \lore{the damper} ahead of it, taken the cold out of the fingertip, held while the rest of the sentence came to its end at the pot, and left standing; the household straightened around the silence as cloth straightens when a weight is lifted, and nobody spoke of the waiting.

She carried him down in the afternoon and up again at the going of the light, and the going was the same careful business as the coming. She went at the half-pace from every open slate and went around seven things as wide as a pail of hot water set where a child might knock it. At the Vail corner she went around the clean dark line where Ash had turned, and around the finer grey hairline called the thaw that had gathered beside it, and gave neither a look long enough to make a cause of it. Under the lowest rack she went around the space where the broken birth-slates lay, with a look and a wide step and no number. She went around \place{the pan's gone ring}, the round ring where the shallow clay pan had stood, with no scrape, crumb, print, chip or word to say what had been lifted from it. Beside \place{the yard well} she went around \place{the trough}, bare and dark, its water called weather by the yard, the frost gone out of the stone lip under the bucket and the grey showing through. Out on \place{the worked strips} and up \place{the terrace} she went around the field's empty place. And in the returning line, where a hand had gone in on Seed 15 and had not come out with the last hands on Seed 27, she went around the empty place with a look and a wide step and no name.

Seven in a row were seven. They were not an account until a payer stood under them, and none stood; a ring is not a gap, a strip is not a rack, and a hand that did not come back is not therefore a name. He kept them on seven trays that did not touch, with a finger between every pair, and would not let them stand up and call themselves a doctrine, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood to any of these.

She set him on \place{the bed} under the roof and not in the Debt Loft, put the fold right without a look at his face and left the thread frozen, told the fire it was the thaw, turned the coals with the back of a broken pot, said \emph{The house will keep it} where another woman would have said \emph{I}, and stood a moment with the back of the pot in her hand counting under her breath toward no opening --- the small intake, the hold, the letting-go --- as if she were setting a row of tools in order on a board and would not have the row called a number. The grey streak at her temple showed pale off the coals; she pushed a strand of coal-black hair back with the wrist that carries the burn, the little-door burn closing on nothing and opening again to the work, and went to her own bed. Overhead \place{the boards} kept four nails on the near board, four on the next, and a thumb-width knot where the fifth nail should have stood. Through the finger's-width door the river kept its note of \lore{water over water}, a low thing over a lower thing, never numbered among the hearings. Under the floor \lore{the carried fire} pulled four times and kept the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer without a name for two hundred and sixty days. Three counts in three places, and none rose into another, because agreeing any two would want a payer put under both and no payer stood to any of them; a river is not a fire and a knot is not a nail.

\lore{The widow's mark} hung where it hung on its cord at her throat, a thumb-length of lead-glazed clay with the Vail kiln scratched into it, and she had not touched it at any shelf, well, rack, road, basket, afternoon or going of the light, because none of these was an opening. The one opening before Smoke 1 lay behind the household with no lesson, no sign, no mending and no payment, and no payer named to any of it; the next real opening was the first firing of Frost, far off and unbrought. Smoke went on without a boundary drawn in it and neither of the district's two names for the months was set right or left wrong.

In the small hours he turned the file out on the towel as a surgeon turns instruments out on a towel, and found the whole of it exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air. Six hearings of the full note stood beside and never among, five from four sources and the sixth, the small flat knock out of a woman as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go and never with the things that came from vessels. No seventh stood in the empty place and none had been invented to fill it, for an empty place kept empty is a finding and a finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged. Three near-full answers stood advanced and unpaid on their own tray; beside them, on a tray that did not touch, stood three shapings of the pot saying with the premature note from Smoke 14 on its own place and no fourth likeness made a cause. Four low things stood in four places that stayed four, and water over water was a fifth low thing on a place of its own, not a hearing and not a vessel's note. The told bell sat beside the heard bell, and the told figure sat beside the paid figure, with Smoke's second arithmetic gone up the road in a locked box and unread under this roof and no signature on the space between the figure and its price. Nine stoppings stood with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end and still ungiven. Three held changes stood on three places, and with them the small dry scrape of a pot's foot set down on stone in the loft on a fourth, the pale line at the gum of a carried man on a fifth, and the held inch on a sixth with ninety-five answers that were objects and not reachings. Nine record-first hands stood each on its own tray, \person{Ilva}'s ninth among them with a finger between it and the eighth and her strip of notched birch unread in her sleeve; the seventh green was a colour and the seventh shape a shape with no eighth. The covered grain lay under wool with no figure on it. The pan's gone ring and the shelf's two gaps and the fold's one crust and its unnamed empty place lay on trays that did not touch, and the five askings of the ownerless question stood on a seventh place of their own with no witness and no row named. Nothing had been copied, opened or brought near the basket; the second reading had gone up the clay road behind him and changed the district a second time, and the change had entered no room of this house.

Through the finger's-width door the river kept its low note. Under the floor the carried fire held at four with its silence where the fifth should have stood. Between the two the wall did not touch anything but itself, and there was no breathing number anywhere in the house and no fire-sitter standing over the coals, so the only silence he borrowed was the little one after a count that had ended in the room that does not touch his. He put the sixth question to his hand. The hand outside the wool went up an inch toward the low note and held for the small intake only --- one rhythm measured by the water's own going, from the note to the one following it --- and let that rhythm go without carrying it across. Then, with the hand still at its inch, he took the little silence after a count that had ended in the other room and carried one rhythm across that empty interval as a second object in its own right, neither filling it to make another stroke in a row that had none nor laying that interval across the first. Two objects lay on the sixth place side by side and apart, and neither was borrowed from the house and neither was made to answer for the other; then the hand came down on the river's next low note, down on the sound and not past it. It was an answer and not an asking. It was ninety-six, filed on the sixth place beside ninety-five with no payer named, an object among objects, exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air: not a longer reach, not a miss entered as a finding, not a hearing of the full note, not a seventh of it, not a tenth stopping, not a payment, not one of the held changes, not a key, a power or a rule. He made nothing of it. The hand came down and lay back against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking.

Two hundred and sixty stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in \place{Sarn}, \place{Sill} or \place{Grath}; six months and eighty days were told by nobody. Ninety-six honest answers stood on the sixth place and none of them was a reach. The carried fire stopped at four with its fifth unpaid two hundred and sixty days and its payer unnamed; the widow's mark hung where it hung; the dressed grain lay covered and unfigured on the cool stone at \place{the rack end}; the seventh year stood at its very end and had still not given. Smoke went on without a boundary drawn and neither of the district's two names for the months was set right or left wrong. Smoke 30, the third and last reading, stood nine mornings beyond and was not reached for, and the first firing of Frost stood far off and unbrought. The count went forward into Smoke 22, a day that had not earned its heading. The file lay open with every tray parted and every gap left gap, and the hand rested against the fold on the ninety-sixth answer, an object and not a power.
