The afternoon of the third of Bone went out of the yard the way colour goes out of wet slip left standing in a shallow dish: not by a boundary drawn and not at an hour anybody called, but by degrees the eye could not catch while it was happening, so that \place{the boards} were grey before a hand had gone to them and the last white had gone off the north lip of the well without anybody seeing it go. Bone went on. The older women named the months by what the weather did, and the slatemen named them by how many Account-days had gone into them, and the two habits lay side by side in the yard like two hands hauling on one rope, neither set right and neither left wrong in so many words. There was no market, no Cross and no bell on Thirdday, and the district wore the face it wears on a day when nothing is due: a pot set down, a broom drawn over stone, water going over water behind the racks. \person{Sella Vail} carried \person[Corin]{the boy} down at the half-pace she kept from every open slate, with the fold laid over his ears against the river wind and the instruction going to the wool and the wind and never to the basket. Men come down from \place{Cold Acre} with a hare across their shoulders had gone off toward the north gate road with the day's carrying still in the arm that had done it; the repairs that waited on the thaw were named by the weather that would bring them and not by any figure. A cost asked of the month was answered with the weather and a direction, and the answering left no entry anywhere. Nobody under any roof marked the going of the light or said what it counted.

She went around the seven walked-around places with a look and a wide step and no number: the clean dark line at the Vail corner where \lore{Ash} had turned, and the finer grey hairline beside it that the house called \lore{the thaw}, unswept both; the space beneath \place{the lowest rack} where the broken birth-slates lay; the round ring where the shallow pan had once stood with no scrape, no crumb, no print, no chip and no word; the skinned water in \place{the trough}; the field's empty place out on \place{the worked strips}; and the returning-line place where a hand had gone in on Seed 15 and not come out. Each got the look and the wide step, and she gave the broom its instruction before she gave it to anybody, and did not sweep any of them. The stale \lore{frost-bloom} had left the north lip of \place{the yard well} while the yard was looking elsewhere; the water wore its skin from edge to edge; and the plate of ice lifted off it stood on \place{the flat stone by the well} out of the path of feet, clouded with the silt that had risen into it as it froze. Three coverings in three places, and the yard called all three weather and entered none of them. He kept them on three places of his own with a finger between every pair, and made no fourth likeness of them to call a cause, and named no payer to any. The stayed frost had been a covering laid over a depth; the skinned water was the same covering laid over a different depth; the clouded plate was a piece of the depth lifted up and set where no foot would go. They were three, and a joining of any two would have wanted a payer put under both, and none stood. He did not call the weather a kindness, or a promise, or the face of anything that had been left. It was weather, and out of the path of feet.

At \place{the rack end} the dressed grain lay under wool away from \place{the hearth}, and she lifted a corner and turned it beneath the wool with a touch lighter than the morning's and gave it no figure. Three readings had gone over it in the district and not one had known what it cost, and the space between the figure and the price had no signature on it. The surgeon's hand turned a wound to see the hidden part and turned it back; the boy's hand lay outside the wool and went nowhere. Frost's three arithmetics, with their three true subtractions, rode locked up \place{the clay road} in \person{Berr Clay}'s box toward \place{High Kiln}, one in twelve printed and unchallenged and the figure read still not the figure paid, no signature upon the space between. Three readings were three and not one account. The finding sat on a place of its own with no payer under it, not a lesson, not a grievance, not a mending, not a doctrine; he did not lay it across the house's \lore{not-doings}, or across the covered grain, or across his own kept gaps. Some of those edges nearly touched, and the finger went between them. A joining would have wanted a payer put under both and none stood to either, and a finding filled in to make a row come out even is a finding forged.

Heat had been drawing back from him since the middle of the afternoon the way warmth goes out of a cup set on a stone that has never known the fire: it left his cheek first, then his temple, then the small bare joint of his wrist where the wool had ridden back, as though it had come to the end of a line written out for it and had neither an order to go further nor an order to stay. He took the inventory in the old order because the old order had never lied to him and no other had promised to. One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from his ear, gone past the point where a child would eat it, its empty place beside it keeping its name to itself and no third come in the going of the light to make the row come out even. The thread of drool at his mouth had frozen in the dry heat and was unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. Cold had stopped at cheek and temple and the small bare joint, and \lore{the Afterdrop} waited beyond all three, past a warming nobody in this house would perform.

He knew the composed face that is not composure. He knew the ease cold puts on a face is not ease, knew a cold patient cannot clot and will seem better before he is worse, knew a man must be warmed from the outside and slowly, never hurried, never rubbed, never set too near the fire. He wanted the crust lifted the two inches to his mouth and could not close two inches on the handle of the pot set beyond his reach. Both were true and the second was louder, and the wanting went on under the knowing the way a coal goes on under ash. Once he had reduced a shoulder in the dark by feel alone and now could not lift a crust two inches; the two facts stood edge to edge and made no doctrine, and the surgeon's knowing bought nothing at two inches of air. He had stopped expecting to be told what any of the numbers counted, which was not the same as not wanting it.

Sella came to the hearth before she came to the basket at the going of the light as she had come to it at first light, because it was the grammar of the house and the grammar held at both ends of the day. She told the coals it was the thaw when the sour breath came off the unswept corner, turned them with the back of a broken pot, and said \emph{The house will keep it} where another woman would have said \emph{I}. She stood a moment with the back of the pot in her hand counting something under her breath toward no opening --- small intake, hold, letting-go --- and gave neither of the two gaps a figure. Her finger stayed on top of \place{the loaf shelf}'s front edge so that the charcoal tally beneath remained unread; her lips moved along a row standing with two gaps and neither gap was entered; her eye went once to the clay jar standing cold under its \lore{lead glaze} on the wall shelf, and her hand shut half over nothing without touching it. She pushed a strand of coal-black hair back with the wrist that carries the burn, and the one grey streak at her temple showed pale off the low light, and the little-door burn on the back of her hand shut on nothing.

\lore{The widow's mark} hung where it hung on its cord at her throat, a thumb-length of lead-glazed clay with the Vail kiln scratched into it under a working thumb, and she did not touch it, because the hand goes to the mark before the kiln and at no other hour, and a going of the light is not an opening. Five openings in five months were five and not a rule; the four behind them stood where they stood and were not looked at again as signs, payments or menders, and the firing of two mornings ago had mended nothing anybody would name and paid nothing to the missing fifth under the floor. When she set the pot down on \place{the pot board} she set the rim two inches beyond the reaching hand with its handle turned toward it, and there was a half-beat between the setting and her going away, and she did not look toward \place{the bed}. The half-beat was not a gift and the handle turned toward the hand was not an instruction to take; a gift wants a taker, and he was not given the means to close two inches of air. She put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face, gave the instruction to the wool and never to the basket, and left the thread where it lay. A basket set down is a pot set down and left.

At \place{the kindling} \person{Tarn} had the small knife at the very edge of its sill, where it could be checked and where it could also slip, and six years stood in the worn thumb-mark while the seventh lay at the lip beyond the mark where the house could not check it. Beside the knife lay \person{Garl Vail}'s unforged collector sentence about the man who had been sent for a collector and turned back at the frozen ford, with no name on the man and no figure on the debt. \emph{Six counted. The next I don't count}, he told the wood, in the tone of a man stating the weather. The sentence about an ear under this roof being worth more than the debt that held it came up to the word that would call the loan and stopped there, with no figure and no name, a remembered ninth stopping with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end and still ungiven. He drew the breath that should have gone in at the top of the reach and did not spend it, and the Seed 16 breath stayed unspent with it, a small interval at the top of each reach where the breath should have gone. Ash was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line and no scrubbing lifted it; the pale clip-ring at his left wrist held what light there was and gave it back. His stroke went on over \place{the flagstones} past the shallow bright patch without the half-check, the stones keeping the place where it had stopped and nobody naming it. He said \emph{Yes, house} to an early thaw and a late, agreeing with both so thoroughly that no argument could find a foot, and went on wanting the late because it puts off the paying, though the paying had stood in the ruts since the water went. No collector came up the clay road and none went down it. Up in \place{the Debt Loft}, which reached the boy only as a sound with a direction, the warm stone in its unmarked pot was cold and unreconciled by not trying, which is not forgiveness. The loan stayed uncalled.

\person{Jorra Vail} came through the finger's-width-open \place{the back room} off \place{the Vail wheel-shed} with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bent little finger and under the nails, not bone-coloured \lore{slip}. He kept dusty forearms and clean ones on one tray and parted them as a surgeon parts clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had broken nor whether it was a birth-slate. Men unnamed had gone low into the room before first grey and he gave the house no name for them and did not say the room was cold; to the boy it was a direction and a sound. He spoke to the wheel before he spoke to any person --- \emph{It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady} --- and told a pot \emph{You'll do} in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either. He knew the \lore{full note} that pot would give three turns before it left his hands, with air under it and a want in it, and withheld the knowing on the near-full tray beside the hearings and never among them, advanced and not paid. When Sella had not finished telling him the fire was low, his bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper} ahead of her unfinished sentence, moved the tongue a hair's breadth and left it standing, so that the end of the instruction arrived at the pot and the household straightened around his silence. The boy read one iron with two bites, the holding and the taking, with no \lore{Grathic} word for both mercies, joined to no kiln and entered in no slate. The burned-slate word stood at four from \place{Slate Row}, a voice over the boards and not a fifth telling; the two tellings of the counting room shut in the war stood at two and did not become three; \emph{he's not Vail} stayed sealed as tightly as a lid nobody had tried to lift in three years, and the promise of the foundling's name to the men who burn slates was neither given nor withdrawn.

Garl stayed in objects and not in a story. The salt-stiff coat with the crease at the left shoulder kept a broader man's shape on its peg behind the door; the wheel-seat in the shed kept its twenty-year lean and its hollow on that side that nobody filled; the clean dark line at the Vail corner lay beside the finer grey called the thaw and neither had been swept; the proverb gone years ago to a pot with no crack in it went to the pot and the fire and never to a person; the debt against a firing of tiles was circled without a name and was worse for being uncalled, for a debt uncalled can be neither paid nor settled nor done with. His name had not been spoken under this roof three hundred and two days. \lore[the carried fire]{The carried fire} he had first kept still answered four low pulls with the silence where the fifth should have stood, and nobody lifted the floor to supply it, and the surgeon knew a reconnection paid for by somebody and knew also that the somebody here was not named.

He turned out the file the way he had once turned instruments out on a towel, each to its own place, and found it as he had left it, every gap left gap.

Six hearings of \lore{the full note} stood beside and never among, five from four sources --- a cup across a birth room, a pot in the wheel-shed through a finger's width of door, the district's slate twice at \place{the Cross} --- and the sixth, the small flat knock out of \person{Dessa Silt} as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go and never with the things that came from vessels. The seventh place kept its emptiness and none was invented to fill it, for an empty place kept empty is a finding and a finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged. The bell's four, four and a gap, heard on the last market of Frost with not one hand in two going to a face, stayed in its square beside the told bell of \lore{the Long Night}, and was not folded into the three counts and was not heard as a seventh.

The rest of the keepings stayed where they had been laid and were not brought forward. Nine stoppings with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end, the ear-sentence brought to the word that would call the loan and stopped there. The ownerless row question at five askings with one shaping and no witness, and expressly no sixth asking; \person{Hessa Rale}'s record-first hand at the eighth, and the three shapings of the Frost 14 well-saying beside it with a finger between every pair --- three shapings were three and not a witness, for a witness stands somewhere and can be asked and none stood, and a shaping is not an opening of a slate nor a figure put into a runner's hand. Nine hands stood with \person{Ilva}'s ninth unread in her sleeve a pace off and not under this roof, no tenth filed, the count staying in wood where no slate could take a figure off it. The seventh green stood a colour at a silent woman's mouth and the seventh shape a shape in cast-offs at the market's edge, each on its own place with no eighth of either and no name spoken over either. The charcoal tally lay beneath the shelf unread; the reed-cutter's wool-grey cord lay where no Court thumb had read it; the soft-cuffed clerk's slate stood inward at the out-of-line landing for a measure nobody called. None of these was a sign, a lesson, a mending, a cause or a payment. Nothing was joined, named, opened, called, paid or ended, and the house's not-doings stayed on a place apart from his own gaps with a finger between, and neither was laid across the other, and edges that nearly touched were kept from touching. None of it stood up and called itself a rule, for a rule is an account that has found a payer and none stood to any of these.

Outside the wool the hand came up an inch toward \lore{water over water}. From the next room, whose floor does not touch his, a small intake came through the wall as a thread of sound comes through cloth --- a woman's breath at her own work, the intake and the hold of it --- and under the floor the carried fire held its fourth low pull with the silence where the fifth should have stood. At the very end of the day the intake stood whole and was let go on the same note. He did not carry it into the fire's missing fifth, or into the bell's gap left behind in the square, or into the river's low over lower; he did not spend it to make any of those rows come out even or borrow a house count to stand under it. It had no payer and he named none.

The hundred and eightieth answer was exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air. It was promoted into no reach, no hearing, no seventh, no stopping, no payment, no held change, no key, no power, no freedom, no gift, no doctrine and no rule, and no payer was named to it. The older man in him took a clean satisfaction in the exact placing of one small sound against the note it had left on, and the infant in him wanted the crust two inches off at his ear, wanted the thread wiped, wanted a hand that could close two inches on a pot handle. The two voices argued and neither won, and the argument itself was the work, and he did not call it a virtue, and nothing he had done could reach two inches. He had once decided which of twenty injured people would live and which would die and had never found a way to make that feel clean; here there was no field, no clamp, no body under his hands, only a breath through a wall held whole and set down, and the wanting went on under the knowing the way a coal goes on under ash.

Three hundred and two stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill} and \place{Grath}; six months and one hundred and twenty-two days were told by nobody; the carried fire's fifth was unpaid the same number of days with its payer unnamed, and Garl Vail's name had not been spoken under the roof the same number of days. A hundred and eighty honest answers stood on the sixth place, each an object and not a power, and neither the number nor the small hand-movings that made them stood up and called themselves a doctrine, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood. Nothing was opened, joined, named, called, paid or ended in the going of the light on the third of Bone: no sixth asking, no tenth hand, no tenth stopping, no bell's gap folded into a count, no fifth pull supplied, no collector on the road, no sign taken from the opening or from the hare already gone into the weather. Bone 4 and Bone 10 stood ahead in the dark beyond the finger's-width door and were not brought into a day that had not earned them.

The hand laid itself back against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. The file lay open on \place{the towel} at the head of the bed with every gap left gap, every tray parted by the width of a finger and nothing that had moved. The river's low note went quiet under the finger's-width door, the plate of ice stood clouded with silt on the flat stone out of the path of feet, and Bone went on without a boundary drawn.
