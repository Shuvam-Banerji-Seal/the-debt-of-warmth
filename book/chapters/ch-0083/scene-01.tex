\daymark{300}{Bone 1, Firstday, Y.H. 813}

\dropcap{T}{he hand} had been laid down against the fold at the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf} as a man puts a tool where the next hand will find it without looking, and it lay there still in the small hours under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof when the first of Bone came on the way cold comes into a banked house, taking the room one board at a time and asking nothing of the child in it. \person[Corin]{The boy} came into his three hundredth day with nobody under any roof to tell him so. Six months and one hundred and twenty days were told by nobody in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill} and \place{Grath}; the count had gone from two hundred and ninety-nine to three hundred the way one board laid beside another gives a dry knock and asks nothing of the seam. A hundred and seventy-four honest answers stood behind him, and neither the number nor the small hand-movings that made them stood up and called themselves a doctrine, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood. Under the floor \lore{the carried fire} kept its fifth unpaid three hundred days with its payer unnamed, and nobody lifted the floor and nobody supplied the pull.

Bone came on without a boundary drawn. The older women named the months by the weather and the slatemen named them by the number of Account-days, and the two habits went on side by side like two hands hauling on one rope, neither set right and neither left wrong in so many words; the month went the way Frost had gone out, asking nothing of the door it came in by. Firstday had no market, no reading at \place{the Cross} and no bell, and the district kept the face it turns to the world on any morning when nothing is due: a pot set down, a broom drawn over stone, water going over water somewhere behind \place{the drying racks}. Three hundred stood exact and complete and unspent, spoken by nobody in Low Kiln, Sill and Grath; six months and one hundred and twenty days were told by nobody; the carried fire's fifth was unpaid the same number of days with its payer unnamed, and the name of \person{Garl Vail} had not been spoken under the roof the same number of days. The round figure was a threshold and not a payment. He noticed it the way a man notices the tenth instrument laid out on a tray and knows the set is whole without putting a hand to it, and no hand under any roof lifted it and spent it or said it over the basket. He had stopped expecting to be told what the number counted. That was not the same as not wanting it, and the wanting went on under the expectation the way a coal goes on under ash, giving off nothing anybody could hold.

The small hours smelled of cooled tallow and wet wool laid over tallow-heat, and under that the sour mineral breath of the unswept corner the house calls \lore{the thaw}. Heat had gone out of the fold by degrees, as it goes out of a pot lifted off the coals and set on a cold stone: it left his cheek first and his temple next and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool had ridden back last, as though it had come to the end of a line written out for it and had neither an order to go further nor an order to stay. He lay where he had been laid and took the inventory in the order it presented itself, because that order had never lied to him and no other had promised to. One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from his ear on \place{the bed}, gone past the point where a child would eat it, its empty place beside it keeping its name to itself and no third come in the night to make the row come out even. The thread of drool at his mouth had frozen in the dry heat and was unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread, and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. Cold had stopped at cheek and temple and the small bare joint, and the afterdrop waited beyond all three, past a warming nobody in this house would perform.

He knew the composed face that is not composure. He knew a cold patient cannot clot and will seem better before he is worse, and that a man must be warmed from the outside and slowly, never hurried, never rubbed, never set too near the fire; he had once known it in a white room with a body on a table and knew it now under a roof of wet wool and cooled tallow, and the knowing did not change by one degree the two inches of air between his mouth and the crust. Once he had reduced a shoulder in the dark by feel alone and now he could not close two inches on the rim of the pot set beyond his hand. Both facts stood, and the second was louder, and neither bought the lifting of the crust. The surgeon took the inventory in its old order and entered none of it, not even on the place where he kept the things he did not enter; the older kept the inventory and the younger kept the wanting, and neither had authority over the other.

\scenebreak

Through the finger's-width door the river kept its note of \lore{water over water}, a low thing over a lower thing, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming, never numbered among the hearings. Under the floor the carried fire gave four low pulls with the silence where the fifth should have stood; overhead \place{the boards} kept four nails on the near board, four on the next and a thumb-width knot where the fifth nail should have stood. Three counts in three places, and none rose into another, because agreeing any two would have wanted a payer put under both and none stood to any. A fire-sitter would have had to be invented to stand under the floor, or a nail driven to stand under the door, and neither was there; no house count came to be borrowed and no fifth pull was supplied to make the row come out even. The three stayed where they were, a rough music in three rooms that did not resolve, and the boy let them be three.

Six hearings of \lore{the full note} stood beside and never among, five from four sources and the sixth, the small flat knock out of a person as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go and never with the things that came from vessels; the seventh place kept its emptiness and none was invented to fill it, for an empty place kept empty is a finding and a finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged. The bell's four, four and a gap, heard at the market the morning before with not one hand in two hundred going to a face, stayed in its square beside the told bell of \lore{the Long Night}, a heard thing beside a told thing, not folded into the three counts under the roof and not heard as a seventh of the full note. Frost's first, second and third arithmetics, with their three true subtractions, rode locked up \place{the clay road} in a box toward \place{High Kiln} with one in twelve printed and unchallenged; the figure read was still not the figure paid and the space between them had no signature on it. Three readings were three and not one account. He kept them on three places with a finger between every pair, and kept the finding on a place of its own with no payer under it --- not a lesson, not a grievance he could carry up the road and set down at a door, not a mending, for nothing had been mended, and not a doctrine, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood to any of it. The finding was exact and worth nothing at two inches of air, and he did not call it a virtue and did not spend it.

The dressed grain lay covered under wool at \place{the rack end}, turned on other mornings with a progressively lighter touch and given no figure, read over three times by numbers that did not know what it cost, the space between the figure and the price still unsigned. Nine stoppings stood with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end; the row stood at five askings with one shaping and no witness; the burned-slate word stood at four and the shut-room tellings at two; the three shapings of the Frost 14 well-saying stood beside the eighth record-first hand with a finger between every pair, three and not a witness, for a witness stands somewhere and can be asked and nothing of that saying stood anywhere to be asked. A short strip of notched wood stayed unread in a sleeve not under this roof, the ninth hand stayed nine, and nine must not stand up and call themselves a rule, for a rule is an account that has found a payer and none stood to these. The edges of the keepings had nearly touched on other days; the finger had gone between them and stayed there. He did not make a fourth likeness of the stayed frost, the skinned trough and the clouded plate of ice to call them a cause, and he did not make the house's \lore{not-doings} into a story about himself, and nothing in the keepings was brought into this morning as a sign, a lesson, a mending or a payment.

\scenebreak

She came to \place{the hearth} before she came to the basket. That was the grammar of the Vail kiln-house, and it held at first light as strictly as at the going of the light or on an Account-day: the instruction went to the fire, the wool, the pot, the broom and the running water, and never to the basket. \person{Sella Vail} told the coals it was the thaw when the sour breath came off the unswept corner, turned them with the back of a broken pot, and said \emph{The house will keep it} where another woman would have said \emph{I}. To the fire she gave the house's old instruction in the words it had had from a man who was not named under this roof --- \emph{Feed the fire before you feed yourself} --- and to the pot, \emph{Don't set that near the door. You'll pay for it in the thaw.} Neither sentence was addressed to the basket. She stood a moment with the back of the pot in her hand, counting something under her breath toward no opening --- small intake, hold, letting-go --- and gave neither gap a figure. Her finger stayed on top of the loaf shelf's front edge so the charcoal tally beneath remained unread; her lips moved along a row standing with two gaps and neither gap was entered; her eye went once to the clay jar standing cold under its \lore{lead glaze}, and her hand shut half over nothing without touching it. She pushed a strand of coal-black hair back with the wrist that carries the burn, and the one grey streak at her temple showed pale off the low light, and she did not look toward the bed.

The house kept its dead man the way it kept its heat: in an object and a saying, never in a story. The salt-stiff coat with the crease at the left shoulder kept a broader man's shape on its peg behind the door, and the wheel-seat in the shed kept its twenty-year lean and its hollow on that side, and Sella would let nobody fill it. His sayings had gone years ago to the pot and the fire and never to a person --- feed the fire before you feed yourself, leave the crack showing, people trust a thing they can see has been handled --- and went to the coals again this morning with nothing in them that could be entered. The debt against a firing of tiles stood neither paid nor settled nor done with, and was worse for being uncalled; no collector had come up the clay road for it and none had gone down. The keeping of him was maintenance and not a story, and the boy took the objects in their order under the fold as he took the body's inventory, entering none of them.

\lore{The widow's mark} hung where it hung on its cord at her throat, a thumb-length of lead-glazed clay with the Vail kiln scratched into it under a working thumb, and she did not touch it, because the hand goes to the mark before the kiln and at no other hour and a morning is not an opening. Four openings in four months were four and not a rule; the next stood in Bone's first firing if the household fired, earned by Bone's own light, and was not brought into a day that had not earned it. When she set the pot down on \place{the pot board} she set the rim two inches beyond the reaching hand with its handle turned toward it, and there was a half-beat between the setting and her going away, and she did not look toward the bed. The half-beat was not a gift and the handle turned toward the hand was not an instruction to take; a gift wants a taker and he was not given the means to close two inches of air. She put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face and left the thread where it lay. The basket was set down and left, and a basket set down is a pot set down and left.

Outside the wool the hand came up an inch toward the river's note. From the next room, whose floor does not touch his, a small intake stood whole --- the small intake, the hold, the letting-go, kept as one object on one note --- and he held it against the kiln's fourth low pull at the moment that pull gave, without carrying it into the silence where the fifth should have stood. Then he let it go whole on the same note, borrowing no house count and supplying no pull, and brought it into neither the missing fifth under the floor nor the bell's gap left behind in its square nor the river's low over lower under the door. The hundred and seventy-fifth answer was exact and complete and worth nothing at two inches of air. It was promoted into no reach, no hearing, no seventh, no stopping, no payment, no held change, no key, no power, no freedom, no gift, no doctrine and no rule, and no payer was named to it. The surgeon's clean satisfaction at the exact coordination was checked by the infant's louder want of the crust two inches off at his ear, and the checking was the work and he did not call it a virtue. Nothing bought by the checking could reach two inches.

Three hundred stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in Low Kiln, Sill and Grath; six months and one hundred and twenty days were told by nobody; the carried fire's fifth was unpaid the same number of days with its payer unnamed, and Garl Vail's name had not been spoken under the roof the same number of days. A hundred and seventy-five honest answers stood on the sixth place, each an object and not a power, and neither the number nor the small hand-movings that made them stood up and called themselves a doctrine, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood. Nothing was opened, joined, named, called, paid or ended in the first of Bone: no third reading into one account, no figure into a price with a signature under it, no bell's gap into a count, no fourth likeness made a cause, no tenth stopping and no tenth hand stood up, no payer supplied to the floor. The three arithmetics stayed locked in the box up the road, the dressed grain stayed covered and unfigured, the three shapings stayed unpaid, the charcoal stayed beneath the shelf, the loan stayed uncalled and the promise stayed ungiven. The file lay open on \place{the towel} at the head of the bed with every gap left gap, every tray parted by a finger's width and nothing that had moved. The hand came down on the river's low note as it always did, and the gap under the floor stayed a gap, and Bone went on without a boundary drawn.
