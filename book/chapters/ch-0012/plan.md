## Scene 1 — The Slack Frame

- **File:** `scene-01.tex`. Opens with `\daymark{48}{Ilmen 19, Y.H. 813}` then a paragraph of at least three lines beginning `\dropcap{T}{he frost}`. The chapter's only drop cap and only `\daymark` for Day 48.
- **Day and time:** Day 48, Ilmen 19, Y.H. 813, Ninthday, first thin light at the yard well, into mid-morning and a turn of the wind off the water.
- **Place:** The yard well and its stone lip; the Vail window with its oiled paper frame; the drying racks and the Slip Brook behind them; the road down toward the Fishstairs and the Cross; the Vail kiln-house into which he is carried at the day's turn.
- **Present:** Corin (forty-eight days old, carried in the basket, fold at his chin), Sella Vail, Tarn (one silent yard-beat with the broom), rack-neighbours at the well including the woman who used to say the ice held and the man who once said it was honeyed (walk-ons). Berr, Alda, Mira, Jorra and the Fishstairs clerk offstage; Dessa dead and stays dead. Garl present as coat, seat, clean dark line and proverb under the roof.
- **Beats, in order:**

  1. Open on the day and a physical detail, not on a thought and not on a recap: a fresh frost-bloom stands in white fronds on the stone lip of the yard well where the Warm has lately gone out of the stone. A neighbour lets the bucket down; the woman who used to say the ice held now says nothing at all, and the man who once called the ice honeyed keeps his counsel too. The argument that used to end in weather has no argument left to end. Sella tells the fire it is the thaw and feeds it, giving the instruction to the fire and the bucket (T085, T077, T058).
  2. The oiled paper in the Vail window sits slack in its frame --- it no longer lifts hard as it once did. Tarn's eye catches it before the light does as he sweeps past; he says nothing, and the boy files the slack frame beside the honeyed ice and the frost-bloom as another shape the district has not entered (T085, T076, T071/T072).
  3. The boy is forty-eight days old and carried to the well's shade out of the draught with the fold turned right under his chin. One crust lies in the fold; the empty place beside it is carried into the new day without being named. When the wind turns off the water the thin breath of barley that used to come from the smoky crust does not come; the boy feels the absence with the exactness of a wound and writes no cause across it (T086, T060, T044).
  4. The spent crust from Stone 15 remains stiff past smelling where it lay, uneaten, unlifted, unnamed; no third crust has come. The three hands --- Sella's, Alda's, Mira's --- stay on their tray and do not become a cause (T086, T091).
  5. Six hearings of the full note stay six, five from four sources and one from a person; no seventh comes at the well or anywhere this morning. The nearly-full note of Ilmen 6 stays on its own tray, advanced and not paid (T061, T082).
  6. The body humiliates him exactly: the thread of drool at his mouth, the head going where it wants with the small wet sound, cold arriving at cheek then temple then wrist, the hand outside the wool going up an inch toward the bucket and coming down (T071/T072).
  7. Sella gives every instruction to the fire, the wool and the bucket and never to the basket; she says *the house* where another woman would say *I*, and goes around an open slate set near the rack end rather than set the basket near it (T058, T084).
  8. At the turn of the wind she carries him in from the well and sets him under the roof, not in the Debt Loft, and puts the fold right without looking at his face; behind him the carried fire keeps under blind ash and the back of a broken pot, missing one breath in every four (T060, T065, T083).

- **Emotional turn:** A season that everyone has been arguing about loses its arguer --- the ice held, the ice was honeyed, now neither man says anything --- while the boy's private file gains another unentered signature and carries the empty place across into a new day. The surgeon's exactness registers the slack frame and the missing smell as precisely as it once registered a chart, and buys him nothing he can lift a hand to.
- **Sensory anchors:** white frost-bloom on the stone lip; a bucket going down and a hand kept off the lip; oiled paper slack in its frame; a silence where an opinion used to stand; silt and wet wool and woodsmoke; one crust in the fold and an empty place; no barley smell when the wind turns; drool freezing; the hand up an inch and down; blind ash and the back of a broken pot; four, four and a gap.

## Scene 2 — One in Twelve

  2.
