\person{Sella Vail} carried him down into \place{the Clay Yard} in the afternoon at the half-pace she kept from every open slate, the way a vessel is carried filled to the lip and not to be slopped. The instruction went to the wool and the river wind and never to the basket. \emph{Keep it off his ears. It's the river wind}, she told the fold, and told the wind nothing, which had never asked to be told anything. The jolts of her walking came up through the straw into \person[Corin]{the boy}'s ribs; the crust two inches from his ear had gone leathery in the dry heat with its corner standing a hair off the wool; the thread at his mouth had frozen again and was unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread. Cold had come to cheek, temple and the bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, and had stopped there as at the end of a line written out for it. \lore{The Afterdrop} waited past the stopping, past a warming nobody here would perform. He took the inventory in its old order, by the small sounds first because the small sounds do not lie. The infant underneath wanted the crust lifted two inches and the thread wiped and went on wanting them while the surgeon took the field. Both were true and the second was louder and neither won; the argument was the work.

Three hundred and twenty-five stood exact behind him and before him, six months and one hundred and forty-five days told by nobody under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill}, \place{Grath}. \lore{The carried fire}'s fifth was unpaid the same number of days with its payer unnamed, and the name of the man the coat kept had not been spoken the same number. Nothing from the morning's file came down into the yard as sign, lesson, mending, cause or payment; answer two hundred and twenty-five lay where it had been set down, a tool put away where the next hand would find it without looking, and nothing in the yard stood up to be its likeness. Bone went on without a boundary drawn, carrying the weather's name and the slatemen's count of Account-days the way a pot carries two handles.

She went around the seven places with a look and a wide step and no number. The clean dark line at the house's corner where the \lore{Ash} ridge turned lay unswept, and the finer grey hairline beside it that the yard called \lore{the thaw} lay unswept beside it; she carried the basket wide of both as though a pail of hot water stood there that a child might knock. Under \place[the drying racks]{the lowest rack} the broken birth-slates lay where no broom went and no hand entered. On the stone lip the shallow clay pan's gone place kept its round ring with no scrape, crumb, print, chip or word. The water in \place{the trough} had a skin over it. Out on \place{the worked strips} the field had its empty place, and in the returning line the place where a hand went in on Seed 15 and had not come out was gone around with a look and a wide step and no figure spoken over the going. Seven places; no eighth likeness stood up among them and no cause was laid across any two, for a cause wants a payer and none stood.

Three coverings at the well stayed weather and were entered nowhere. The frost that had stayed on the well's north lip on Bone 3 while the yard looked elsewhere was gone, and its going was not a sign. The skin on the trough broke under a pail's knock and closed again without a word spoken to it. The plate of ice clouded with silt stood on \place{the flat stone by the well} out of the path of feet where she had set it on Frost 9 and told the weather where to stand. There was a finger between every pair; a fourth likeness would have stood up as a rule wanting a payer, and none stood. The boy kept the three where they were, not a lesson written on the air and not a mending anybody had performed.

\scenebreak

At \place{the rack end} the dressed grain lay under wool on the cool stone away from the hearth. Sella lifted a corner and turned what lay beneath with a touch lighter than the last and gave no figure to it. The dry rasp came up small and unmistakable, the sound a thing makes when it has given up its water and is going on giving it up. Her fingers went in and came out. They did not close; they did not take a small handful and let it fall back grain by grain and all at once the way they had on Bone 22. The wool came down over the place where no handful had fallen, and she went on as though there had been no place to notice. The boy watched the not-doing as carefully as another boy would have watched a doing. The handful stayed one object on its own place; the empty fingers were a keeping beside it and not a second, for two would have been a habit wanting a payer, and none stood. He put a finger between the keeping and the object and between both and the four held changes on their four places, and forbade them to stand up together and call themselves a rule.

At the edge of the yard stood \place{the yard well}, its stone lip worn smooth by pails and ropes and the years of hands. \person{Ilva} was drawing when Sella carried him past, and the boy took in the small sounds first because the small sounds do not lie. The rope was stiff in the dry cold and gave its small dry rasp as the bucket came up, a sound like a hand dragged over dry boards, and the pail was set against her knee while she got the rope's weight. Clay had dried pale in the crescents of her nails, and the finer grey dust of the drying racks was worked into the seam of one thumb, which she did not scrub out. Her hands knew the weight of a pail and the edge of a wet board and had never been soft, and went on knowing it without reporting on them. She was of the firing work and not of a house; people noticed the pail first and the sleeve not at all, which was the order she preferred.

A woman with a pot came and asked what the firings of Bone had taken, in the level tone of a person asking a price and expecting a figure back. Ilva's hand went toward her sleeve and stayed. The short strip of notched birch bark lay where it lay; pale where a thumb had rubbed the notches and dark in the uncut grain between, it did not come out, was not taken out, read, copied, counted or joined to the charcoal beneath \place{the loaf shelf}, the cord, the open slate lying a pace away, the knot-string or the ownerless saying. No figure came off the wood. The boy watched the motion arrest itself at the sleeve the way he had once watched a hand stop short of a field it had been about to enter, before the dressing went on and while there was still time to keep the field clean.

Her eyes went to the asker and her mouth opened after both, with a half-beat between the looking and the word --- the hand to the record before the eyes to the person, the eyes before the mouth, an order as exact as the order in which a field is opened and the instrument laid into it. “Ask the pot what it cost,” she said. “I keep the count, not the account.” There was no proverb in it and no instruction to a pot or a fire; that belonged to the houses. She answered the question put and not the one behind it, gave no name to the thing she kept, and put the flat of her hand once more on the sleeve before going back to the pail. Nobody turned twice; the woman went off with her answer level.

\scenebreak

The boy took the order of her reaching apart as a surgeon takes a chart apart, by what was there and by what was not, and filed it. It was the ninth such hand in the district and it stayed nine. He put a finger between it and \person{Hessa Rale}'s eighth, and a finger between both and every other hand on the tray, and would not let the nine stand up and call themselves a rule, for a rule is an account that has found a payer and none stood. The count was a thing he kept and not a thing he could spend. He did not make her a reader of her own keeping, which she was not and had never wanted to be; a person can carry a record for years and never be its reader, and the wood stays wood whether anybody reads it. He did not take a figure off the strip, did not make the slate a pace away into a cause, and did not join the nearness of the two into anything that wanted a witness.

She was not \person{Marra}, not the soft-handed man, not the soft-cuffed clerk set below the north bridge for a measure nobody called, not Hessa, not the green-mouthed woman at \place{the pot board}, not the woman whose voice had come over the boards, and not the Drawing collector, and she was joined to none of them. Each had been filed on a place of its own with a finger between, and the finger went between now. The old exact pleasure in noticing rose in him, the pleasure of a man finding a thing where nobody thought a thing was kept, and it was checked at once by the rule that two likenesses are not a cause. A second person in the district kept a figure where no slate could take it. That was all. To join the strip to the handful under the wool, or either to the charcoal beneath the shelf, would have wanted a payer put under both and none stood; the edges nearly touched and the finger went between.

Late in the afternoon a man came up the yard with a cracked jar in a sling of rope and asked what the month's mending would want; a woman with him said ask the pot, and the pot, being a pot, said nothing. The price went off toward \place{the clay road} with nobody weighing the jar and nobody opening a slate. The boy read it as a chart left blank where the number should stand and entered neither question nor answer. Bone's hunts and repairs were directions judged by the arm and the weather, never figures.

\scenebreak

At the shed's edge \person{Jorra Vail} came through the finger's-width door off \place{the Vail wheel-shed} with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bent little finger, not the bone-coloured \lore{slip} of the wheel. He kept dusty forearms and clean ones on one tray and parted them as a surgeon parts clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had been broken nor whether it was a birth-slate. Three or more men had gone low into \place{the back room} before first grey and he gave the house no name for them and did not say the room was cold. He spoke to the wheel before he spoke to any person. \emph{It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady}, he said to the wheel, and to a pot \emph{You'll do}. He knew the full note that pot would give three turns ahead, with air under it and a want in it, and kept the knowing beside the hearings and never among them; he would not say it before a kiln-master come up from \place{High Kiln} who would call it early. Before Sella had finished telling him the fire was low his bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper}, took the cold out of the fingertip, moved the tongue a hair's breadth and left it standing, so that the end of her instruction arrived at the pot. One piece of iron had two bites, the holding and the taking, and there was no word in the district's speech for both mercies. He did not look toward the basket. \emph{He's not Vail} stayed sealed, and the promise of a name to the men who burned slates was neither given nor withdrawn.

\scenebreak

At \place{the kindling} \person{Tarn} did the thing the day had been walking toward without announcing it. He set the small knife at the very edge of its sill, where a man's hand could check it and could also slip it off, and stood a moment with the check unfinished. Six years stood in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check; the seventh stood at the lip beyond, where the house's eye did not go and no tally reached. His thumb went up the worn mark, past the six, and rested on the seventh notch at the lip for the length of one breath.

He did not cut it; the knife lay at the edge of its sill with its edge turned away. He did not count the notch or bring the ear-sentence to the word that would call the loan. The sentence about an ear under this roof being worth more than the debt did not come up to the calling word at all, and stood somewhere behind the breathing unsaid. The breath from Seed 16 that should have gone in at the top of the reach did not go in, and his chest kept its tightness. Ash was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line, and no scrubbing lifted it; the pale clip-ring at his left wrist held what little light there was and gave it back. The unforged collector sentence lay beside the seventh notch with no name on the man who turned back at the ford and no figure on the debt. The warm stone he had kept two years in an unmarked loft pot lay cold, and he did not try to reconcile it, which was not forgiveness.

Then he drew the thumb back down to the six where the house could check, and told the wood \emph{Six counted. The next I don't count}, in the tone of a man stating the weather. The announcement went to nobody, because the yard had already been told. He put the knife back where it could be checked and went on with the broom, and the seventh notch lay where it had lain, uncut, unnumbered and unpaid for the touching.

The boy took the hand apart as he took everything apart, by what was there and by what was not. A hand had gone to a place a man had decided not to open and had left it lying there unopened. He filed the touch on a place of its own with a finger between it and the nine stoppings, between it and the four held changes, between it and the handful under the wool, and between it and his own kept gaps. It was not a tenth stopping, for no sentence had been brought to the calling word; not the seventh year given, not a payment, not a fifth held change, not a hearing, not a near-full answer, not a mending and not a rule. No payer stood under it. He did not join it to the strip in the sleeve; two likenesses were not a cause and a rule wanted a payer. It took nothing and bought nothing and was kept because it was true, and the boy did not make it again. The ninth stopping stayed nine with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end, the loan uncalled, and no collector came up the clay road or went down it. The edges of the things came near to touching and the finger went between.

\scenebreak

The holding tray came out unchanged. The burned-slate word stood at five with a finger between the fifth and the four, no sixth, no witness, no hand at the flame and no payer, gathering interest against Jorra's ungiven promise. The row stood at five askings with one shaping and no witness and expressly no sixth; the well-saying at three shapings with Hessa's eighth hand on its own place and a finger between; the shut-room tellings at two; the three pot sayings with the Smoke 14 note apart and no fourth likeness made a cause. Six hearings of \lore{the full note} stood beside and never among with the seventh place empty, the sixth the small flat knock out of a woman as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go and never among the things that came out of vessels. Five openings in five months were five and not a rule, the next at \place{Ash} 2 far off; seven green a colour and seven shape a shape with no eighth. The heard bell stayed in its square beside the told bell of \lore{the Long Night}; no collector came up the clay road or went down it.

\lore{The widow's mark} hung where it hung at the rack, the well, the kindling, the shed and the bed, because none was an opening; the hand goes to the mark before the kiln and at no other hour. The man whose absence kept the house in its shape stayed in objects and not in a story: the salt-stiff coat with the crease at the left shoulder, the hollow wheel-seat nobody filled, the clean dark line and the finer grey unswept, both proverbs gone to the pot and the fire and never to a person, the debt against a firing of tiles circling without a name and worse for being uncalled. \person{Garl Vail}'s name had not been spoken under the roof three hundred and twenty-five days, the keeping of him maintenance and not a lesson.

\scenebreak

At the going of the light Sella carried him up at the half-pace from every open slate and never once into \place{the Debt Loft}. She set him on \place{the bed} under the roof, put the fold right at his ear without a look at his face, and left the thread where it lay. Three counts stood in three places and did not become one. Under the floor the carried fire gave four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer unnamed three hundred and twenty-five days; overhead \place{the boards} kept four nails on the near board, four on the next and a thumb-width knot where a fifth might have been driven and was not; through the finger's-width door the river kept \lore{water over water}, a low thing over a lower thing, nearer at the going than at the coming and not numbered among the hearings. Agreeing any two would have wanted a payer put under both, and none stood, so the three stayed side by side. The bell's gap was not fetched in to make a fourth, and the heard bell stayed beside the told.

In the small hours, with the yard quiet and the river's note at its lowest under the finger's-width door, the hand outside the wool went up an inch toward the sound. It took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole, let the intake go on the same low note, and came straight down and laid itself back against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. The note had done before the hand came down, so the letting-go was neither early again nor stayed, and the hand did not stay. The inch was not a reach, the intake not a breath kept for warmth, the interval not a stopping of the river, the note not his. He filed it as answer two hundred and twenty-six: exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment, borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the five arithmetics, the bell's gap, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the stayed object, the early letting-go, the strip in the sleeve nor his own kept gaps. Two hundred and twenty-six honest answers stood on the sixth place, and neither the number nor the hand-movings that made it stood up and called themselves a doctrine, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood.

One stiff brown-edged crust lay two inches from his ear with its empty place unnamed and no third, and the surgeon took his clean satisfaction in the field left as found while the infant wanted the crust lifted and the thread wiped and got neither; both were true and the second was louder and neither won. Three hundred and twenty-five stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in Low Kiln, Sill and Grath; six months and one hundred and forty-five days were told by nobody; the carried fire's fifth was unpaid the same number of days with its payer unnamed; and the name of the man the coat kept was not spoken the same number. Nothing on the twenty-sixth had been opened, joined, named, called, paid or ended. The file lay open on \place{the towel} with every gap left gap, the hand lay back against the fold, and the river's low note came under the finger's-width door while Bone went on without a boundary drawn, four mornings short of the month's third and last reading.
