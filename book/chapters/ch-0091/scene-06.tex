The light went off \place{the Clay Yard} in the ordinary way, without a boundary drawn, the way a hand goes out of a room and leaves the shape of itself on the chair. It went from the tops of \place{the drying racks} first and came down the stone lip of \place{the yard well} last, and between the two it passed over a yard that had not been waiting for it and had no word for its going. \place{Bone} kept both its names and neither was set right or left wrong in so many words: the older women called it by the weather, the slatemen by the number of Account-days, and the month carried the two names the way a pot carries two handles and is none the worse for it. Nothing from the morning came down into the afternoon as sign, lesson, mending, cause or payment. The note that had done before the hand came down had gone quiet and was not fetched back to stand over the going of the light. The day was simply the same day, one tend further along, and the yard went on about its own work the way water goes over water.

\person{Sella Vail} carried \person[Corin]{the boy} down at the half-pace she kept from every open slate. The instruction went to the wool and the river wind and never to the basket: \emph{Keep it off his ears. It's the river wind}, and the wind took it and the wool kept what it could. Under the fold he took the yard in the order it arrived, by the small sounds first, because the small sounds do not lie and the large ones generally do. The rope at the well gave its small dry rasp as a pail came up and went down; a board shifted under a hand; somewhere a pot was set down and answered with a note that was not quite full and not quite flat, and he filed it where a near-full thing must go and gave it no seventh place. The air had the clay-heavy weight of the end of a dry day, slip dust ground small as meal riding under it, and under that the faint sour mineral edge of \lore{Ash} that had been in this yard longer than any of the hands working in it.

She went wide around the seven walked-around places with a look and a wide step and no number. The clean dark line at the Vail corner and the finer grey hairline beside it called \lore{the thaw} lay unswept both; the space beneath \place{the lowest rack} kept its broken birth-slates; the round ring where the shallow clay pan once stood had no scrape, no crumb, no print, no chip and no word; the \place{trough} kept its skin; out on \place{the worked strips} lay the field's empty place; and in the returning line stood the place where a hand had gone in on Seed 15 and had not come out, gone around with a look and no figure. Seven places, each with its wide step, and no eighth likeness stood up as a row, and no cause was laid across any two of them. A cause wanted a payer put under it, and none stood.

Three coverings stayed weather entered nowhere. The frost had gone off the well's north lip on Bone 3 while the yard looked elsewhere, which was not a sign; the skin on the trough broke under a pail's knock and closed again; and the silt-clouded plate of ice on \place{the flat stone by the well} stood out of the path of feet as though a man had set it there to keep it and had then forgotten to name the keeping. Three coverings, with a finger between every pair, and no fourth likeness made a cause of them. The boy kept them on three places and asked nothing of any of them, because he had learned early that a covering is a covering and not a statement, and that a thing covered is not the same thing as a thing mended.

The month's hunts and repairs went around the yard as directions judged by the carrying arm and the weather, never as figures. A man come down from \place{Cold Acre} with a hare across his shoulders answered the question of what the carrying had cost with the weather, which had carried the snow and could carry the price, and went off toward \place{the clay road} with nobody weighing the hare and nobody opening a slate. Another with a cracked jar in a rope sling was told to ask the pot, and the pot, being a pot, said nothing. The boy read both as a chart left blank where the number should stand and entered neither. Cost that arrives as a direction is a cost that has not yet found its figure, and a figure that has not found its payer is not a payment; he kept the two observations apart on the tray and nothing on the tray grew legs and walked into the other.

\scenebreak

A woman with a basket of kindling came to the edge of the work and asked what the seventh year would want when it came. She asked it of nobody in particular, the way the yard asked most of its questions, and went on shifting the weight of the basket from one hip to the other while she waited.

Two men mending a strip of fence answered her between them. The one with the mallet said the thaw would be early; the other, who had his collar up against the river wind, said it would be late. They said it twice each, not loudly, with the practical air of men who had each measured the weather in his own bone and found it wanting in the other man's mouth.

\person{Tarn} was at \place{the kindling} with the small knife on its sill, six years standing in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check and the seventh at the lip beyond it where it could not. He straightened a length of wood that did not need straightening and gave the woman his answer in the tone of a man stating the weather.

“Yes, house,” he said to the one who wanted the thaw early.

“Yes, house,” he said to the one who wanted it late.

He agreed so thoroughly that there was nowhere for an argument to find a foot. He kept the late, because a late thaw puts off the paying, though the paying had stood in the ruts since the water went and would stand there when the water came again. His chest had been tight since the dark, and the breath that should have gone in at the top of the reach did not go in, so that a small interval stood where the breath should have been, the way a gap stands where a fifth nail might have been driven and was not. \lore{Ash} was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line and no scrubbing lifted it; the pale clip-ring at his left wrist held what little light there was and gave it back. He did not bring the ear-sentence to the word that would call the loan, and he did not spend the breath from Seed 16, and \person{Garl Vail}'s unforged collector sentence lay where it lay beside the seventh notch with no name on the man who had turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on the debt.

The woman with the kindling went off with her basket, and the two men went back to their strip, and the yard took up its own noise again as though no question had been asked and no answer entered. No collector came up \place{the clay road} or went down it. The boy kept the early and the late as two directions on two places with a finger between them, and made no third thing of the agreeable man who wanted both.

\scenebreak

He checked the thing the chapter had been carrying and found it where it had been left. The thumb that had rested on the seventh notch at the kindling on Bone 26 was one object on its own place, and it was not made again on Bone 27. It was not a tenth stopping, for no sentence had been brought to the calling word; it was not the seventh year given, not a payment, not a fifth held change, not a hearing, not a near-full answer, not a mending and not a rule, with no payer standing under it. The boy put a finger between it and the nine stoppings, and between it and the four held changes on their four places, and between it and the handful, and between it and his own kept gaps, so carefully that the edges nearly touched and did not touch. It had been a touch that took nothing and paid nothing, and he kept it because it was true and not because it bought anything. A thing kept because it is bought is an account; a thing kept because it is true is only a thing kept, and the difference was the whole of what he had been doing with his days.

The handful let fall at the rack end on Bone 22 stayed one object and was not made again. The dressed grain lay under wool at \place{the rack end} with Sella's fingers going in and coming out without closing, and the empty fingers of this afternoon went beside the handful as a keeping and not as a second. The staying at the inch of Bone 15 and the early letting-go of Bone 17 stayed two objects on two places, each with a finger between it and the other and between it and everything else there, neither made again, neither a habit, a way or a fifth held change, both wanting a payer who did not stand. Two would have been a habit and three a way, and a habit and a way are both accounts that have found somebody to pay; nobody had.

Five arithmetics of the year stood behind one printed figure and rode locked up the clay road in \person{Berr Clay}'s box toward \place{High Kiln}: one in twelve unchallenged, the figure read still not the figure paid, no signature on the space between, five readings five and not one account. The finding sat on a place of its own with no payer under it, not a lesson, not a grievance, not a mending, not a doctrine and not a rule. It was not laid across the house's \lore{not-doings}, or across the dressed grain under wool, or across the four held changes, or across the handful, or across the thumb's resting on the seventh notch, or across the two small objects of the hand, or across his own gaps. The edges came near to touching, so near that a less careful hand would have pushed them together and called the touching a cause; the finger went between. He had once taken a hand out of a field before the dressing went on, and this was the same motion done with nothing but attention for a tool. No doctrine stood up out of any of it, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood.

The holding tray came out unchanged when he turned it over in the small hours before the carrying-up. The burned slate stood at five with no sixth and no witness and no hand at the flame; the row at five askings with one shaping and no witness and expressly no sixth; the well-saying at three shapings with \person{Hessa Rale}'s eighth hand on its own place and a finger between; the shut-room tellings at two; the three pot sayings with the Smoke 14 note standing apart; the nine stoppings with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end and the loan uncalled; nine record-first hands with \person[Ilva]{Ilva}'s ninth unread in its sleeve and no tenth, the wood keeping its count where no slate could take a figure off it; six hearings of \lore{the full note} standing beside and never among, the sixth the small flat knock out of a woman as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go and never among the things that came out of vessels, the seventh place empty and none invented to fill it; five openings in five months with the next far off at \place{Ash} 2; the seventh green a colour and the seventh shape a shape with no eighth; the heard bell in its square beside the told bell of \lore{the Long Night}. Two hands were not a cause and nine were not a rule, and an empty place kept empty was a finding and a finding filled to make a row come out even was a finding forged. Nothing on the tray had grown in the afternoon or been made to touch its neighbour. Nobody had come up the clay road and nobody had gone down it, and no collector had called anything by its name.

\person{Garl Vail} stayed where he had been kept, in objects and not in a story: the salt-stiff coat with the left-shoulder crease on its peg behind the door, the wheel-seat in the shed keeping its twenty-year hollow that nobody filled, the clean dark line and its finer grey unswept both, the twice-mended pot with its crack showing and both proverbs gone to the pot and the fire and never to a person. His name had not been spoken under the roof three hundred and twenty-six days, and the debt against a firing of tiles was neither paid nor settled nor done with and was worse for being uncalled. The carried fire's fifth was unpaid the same number of days with its payer without a name. No lesson was made of any of it, and the boy did not lay his own kept gaps across the absent man, for the absence of a man and the gap in a row of nails are two different empties and want two different payers, and neither payer stood.

At the shed's edge \person{Jorra Vail} came through the finger's-width door with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bent little finger and white in the creases where a fist had opened, and it was not the bone-coloured \lore{slip} of the wheel. He kept dusty forearms and clean ones on one tray and parted them as a surgeon parts clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had been broken nor whether it was a birth-slate. He spoke to \place{the Vail wheel-shed}'s wheel before he spoke to any person --- \emph{It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady} --- and told a pot \emph{You'll do} in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either. He knew the full note that pot would give three turns before it left his hands, with air under it and a want in it, and kept the knowing on the near-full tray beside the hearings and never among them, withheld from a kiln-master come up from High Kiln who would have called it early and made something of it, singing before its time and singing itself out early. When Sella had not finished telling him the fire was low, his bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper}, took the cold out of the fingertip, moved the tongue a hair's breadth and left it standing, so that the end of her instruction arrived at the pot and the household straightened around his silence. One piece of iron had two bites, the holding and the taking, and there was no word in the district's speech for both mercies. He did not look toward the bed or the basket, and \emph{he's not Vail} stayed sealed as tightly as a lid nobody had tried to lift in three years, the promise of the boy's name to the men who burn slates neither given nor withdrawn. The boy kept the two silences under the roof side by side and put no word between them.

Sella carried him up at the going of the light at the half-pace and set him on \place{the bed} under the roof, and never in \place{the Debt Loft}. From above and behind came the small sound of a foot on the loft stair, and nothing more: no door opened onto him, no interior was entered, and the sound stayed where sounds from that place belong, a thing heard and not gone into. She put the fold right at his ear without a look at his face, her hand resting a half-beat longer than the setting needed before it went away, and left the thread of drool frozen and unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. One stiff brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from his ear with its corner standing a hair off the wool; the empty place beside it kept its name to itself and no third came in the going of the light to make the row come out even. Cold had stopped at cheek, temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, as though it had come to the end of a line written out for it with neither an order to go further nor an order to go away, and \lore{the Afterdrop} waited past the stopping, beyond a warming nobody in this house would perform. He knew what the ease on a face meant, and that a cold vessel cannot clot and will seem better before he is worse and must be warmed from the outside and slowly, never rubbed, never set too near the fire. The knowing bought him nothing at two inches of air. He wanted the crust lifted the two inches to his mouth, the thread wiped, a hand that would close on the pot handle set within reach. Both were true and the second was louder and neither won, and the argument went on under the wool without a verdict and without a witness, and the argument was the work.

The file lay open on \place{the towel} at the head of the bed with every object on its own place and every gap left gap. He turned it over in his mind the way he had once turned instruments out on a towel at the end of a list, not to find what was missing but to see that nothing had grown there while nobody was looking. Nothing had. The staying and the early letting-go, the handful, the thumb's resting, the four held changes and the returned half-check beside the Day 161 fraction, the Day 166 shelf beat and the Frost 5 returned beat kept their places and were not made again, four objects and four and not a doctrine. Three hundred and twenty-five exact stood behind him and three hundred and twenty-six stood where he was, six months and one hundred and forty-six days told by nobody under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill}, \place{Grath}. The count was not a milestone and had never been one; the round three hundred had gone over on Bone 1 and been kept as a threshold, and the days had gone on into every number after it with nobody telling him what any of them counted. He had stopped expecting to be told, which was not the same as not wanting it, and the wanting went on under the expectation the way a coal goes on under ash.

The yard went quiet. The river kept its note of \lore{water over water} through the finger's-width door, a low thing over a lower thing, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming, never numbered among the hearings; under the floor \lore{the carried fire} gave four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood; overhead \place{the boards} kept four nails on the near board, four on the next, and a thumb-width knot where a fifth might have been driven and was not. Three counts in three places, and none rose into another, because agreeing any two would have wanted a payer put under both and no payer stood to any. The bell's gap left behind in the square was not fetched in to make a fourth, and the heard bell stayed in its square beside the told.

At the very end of the day, when the note had come down to its lowest under the finger's-width door, the hand outside the wool went up an inch toward the sound. It took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole, let the intake go on the same low note, and came straight down and laid itself back against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. The note had done before the hand came down, so the letting-go was neither early again nor stayed, and the hand did not stay. The inch was not a reach and the intake was not a breath kept for warmth; the interval was not a stopping of the river and the note was not his. He had merely put his small hand into the order of things for the length of one note and taken it out again, and the order went on whether he did it or not. He filed it as answer two hundred and twenty-eight: exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment, borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the five arithmetics, the bell's gap, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the stayed object, the early letting-go nor his own kept gaps. Two hundred and twenty-eight honest answers stood on the sixth place, each an object and not a power, and neither the number nor the small hand-movings that had made it stood up and called themselves a doctrine, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood.

Three hundred and twenty-six stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in Low Kiln, Sill and Grath; six months and one hundred and forty-six days were told by nobody; the carried fire's fifth was unpaid the same number of days with its payer unnamed; and the name of the man the coat kept was not spoken under the roof the same number. Nothing on the twenty-seventh had been opened, joined, named, called, paid or ended. The keeping of the number was the only honest work there had been to do all day, and it had bought nothing and was not for sale, and it went on because attention is the one thing no institution can tax. The silt-clouded plate of ice stood on its flat stone out of the path of feet; the file lay open on the towel with every gap left gap; the hand lay back against the fold on answer two hundred and twenty-eight; and the river's low note went quiet under the finger's-width door while \place{Bone} went on without a boundary drawn, three mornings short of the month's third and last reading and not brought into a day that had not earned it.
