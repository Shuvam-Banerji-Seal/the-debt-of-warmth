\daymark{325}{Bone 26, Sixthday, Y.H. 813}

Bone 26 came in under the roof without a boundary drawn, the way a day comes into a house that has not been waiting for it: by a change in the air no hand had made, the boards giving back what they had held, the dark thinning at no command. Nothing from the twenty-fifth crossed over as sign, lesson, mending, cause or payment. The handful let fall at the rack end stayed where it had fallen, one object on its own place, and was not made again; the note that had done before the hand came down on the last day had gone quiet and was not fetched back to stand over this one like a figure carried forward. The month kept both its names, the weather the older women counted by and the Account-days the slatemen counted by, and neither was set right or left wrong in so many words; it carried the two the way a pot carries two handles and is none the worse for it.

\person[Corin]{the boy} came into his three hundred and twenty-fifth day with nobody to say what had been reached. Three hundred and twenty-four exact stood behind him and three hundred and twenty-five stood in front, and six months and one hundred and forty-five days were told by nobody under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill}, \place{Grath}. Under the floor \lore{the carried fire} gave four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer without a name the same number of days; the salt-stiff coat with the crease at the left shoulder kept a broader man's shape on its peg, and the sayings went to the pot and the fire and never to a person, and the name of the man the coat kept had not been spoken under the roof the same number. He had stopped expecting to be told what any of it counted, which was not the same as not wanting it; the wanting went on under the expectation the way a coal goes on under ash.

He took the body as a wound is taken, in its old order, because the order was the one thing in the room that had not moved. One stiff brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from his ear, gone leathery through the dry heat, its corner standing a hair off the wool as though a hand had set it down badly and gone away; the empty place beside it kept its name to itself and no third came in the small hours to make the row come out even. The thread of drool at his mouth had frozen and was unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. Cold had come to cheek, temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back and had stopped there as at the end of a line written out for it, with neither an order to go further nor an order to go away; \lore{the Afterdrop} waited beyond the stopping, past a warming nobody in this house would perform.

He knew what the ease on a face meant. A patient who looks composed at the end of such a line is not composed; the pink that comes back to the skin is the core still cold and the blood going where it should not, and a cold vessel cannot clot, and must be warmed from the outside, and slowly, never rubbed, never set too near the fire. He had once known it as he knew the third rib, or the difference between pulsing blood and welling, and the knowing had never once made the warmth come. It bought him nothing at two inches of air. He had been the man who decided which bodies got a bed and which got a slate, and here he was the body being decided over, and the grammar of it had not changed, only the side of the door. The infant underneath wanted the crust lifted the two inches to his mouth, the thread wiped away, a hand that would close on the pot handle set within reach. Both were true and the second was louder and neither won; the argument went on under the wool without a verdict and without a witness, and the argument was the work.

Before the light he read the room by its small sounds, because the small sounds do not lie and the large ones generally do: the boards giving back their heat by degrees, the fourth pull under the floor not followed by a fifth, the river through the finger's-width door keeping its low note of \lore{water over water}, a low thing over a lower thing, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming. He let them arrive in their own order and did not arrange them, because arranging them wanted a hand to put under them and none stood.

\scenebreak

\person{Sella Vail} went to \place{the hearth} before she went to the basket. That was the order of the house and had been the order before any day the boy could count, and she kept it the way another woman might keep a habit of crossing herself. The coals lay grey under their blind ash with a little heat at the heart of them, and she told them it was \lore{the thaw} in the flat tone she used for the pot and the broom and the wool, and turned them with the back of a broken pot so the ash fell where she meant it to fall. \emph{The house will keep it}, she said to the fire, where another woman would have said I, and the house kept it, and the coals answered four low pulls from under the floor with the silence where the fifth should have stood. She stood a moment with the back of the pot in her hand and counted something under her breath --- a small intake, a hold, and a letting-go --- toward no opening, and gave neither gap a figure. The three small words went into the room the way the note went under the door, and the boy kept the hearing of them on its own place with a finger between it and his three, for two people keeping the same sound under one roof is not one sound and two likenesses are not a cause.

The grey streak at her temple showed pale where she pushed the coal-black hair back with the wrist that carries the burn. At \place{the loaf shelf} her finger lay on top of the front edge, resting a little longer than the checking needed, so that the charcoal tally beneath stayed unread; her lips moved along a row standing with two gaps in it, and she figured neither gap. She glanced once at the clay jar standing cold under \lore{lead glaze} on its shelf by the wall, and shut her hand half over nothing, the burn on the back of her left hand --- two straight sides and a lintel and a threshold, the skin inside puckered and pale --- closing on air the way a little door closes on a room nobody goes into. She did not touch \lore{the widow's mark}. A morning was not an opening and a hearth was not an opening; five openings in five months were five and not a rule, and the sixth stood at \place{Ash} 2 in the first firing, far off, not hoped for and not counted toward.

On \place{the pot board} the twice-mended pot stood with the hairline of the original break showing between the old-brass staple and the tighter wire. She put her thumb first where the wire crossed the crack, felt what the thumb had to feel, and only then let the weight come on the handle. \emph{If it's worth mending it's worth mending twice. The second time is the real one}, she said to the pot, and \emph{Leave the crack showing. People trust a thing they can see has been handled}, she said to the fire, and never to a person. The sayings were \person{Garl Vail}'s and had improved in the keeping and were very likely not as good as he had said them. The crack showed. Repair was one thing and a whole vessel another, and the house had long ago decided which of the two it would keep. He read the pot the way he had once read a wound closed in a place the hand would not have chosen: the first staple set honestly, the second wire drawn tighter than it needed and left longer, the hairline of the original break showing dark between them. A vessel that has been mended carries its break and is the truer for showing it, which was the whole of the saying and was not a lesson anybody had paid for.

Before the basket went up she was out at \place{the rack end}, where the dressed grain lay under wool on the cool stone away from the hearth. She lifted a corner and the dry rasp came up small and unmistakable, the sound a thing makes when it has given up its water and is going on giving it up; she turned what lay beneath with a touch lighter than the last time and gave no figure to it. Nothing lifted a hand and nothing fell. The empty fingers of this morning went beside the handful on its one place as a keeping and not as a second, for two would have been a habit wanting a payer and three a way wanting one, and none stood. She set the pot rim two inches beyond the reaching hand, with a half-beat before the setting and no look toward \place{the bed}, and went on to the next thing as though the half-beat had not been spent. The small arrest between the meaning and the setting-down was a space he had once measured in another room and entered nowhere; he kept it as an object on its own place and asked nothing of it.

\scenebreak

Three counts stood in three places and did not become one. Under the floor the carried fire gave four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer unnamed three hundred and twenty-five days; overhead \place{the boards} kept four nails on the near board, four on the next, and a thumb-width knot where a fifth might have been driven and was not; through the finger's-width door the river kept water over water and was not numbered among the hearings at all. Three counts in three places. Agreeing any two would have wanted a payer put under both, and no payer stood to any of them, so the three stayed where they were, side by side and not joined. The bell's gap left behind in the square was not fetched in to make a fourth, and the heard bell stayed in its square beside the told bell of \lore{the Long Night}.

Six hearings of \lore{the full note} stood beside and never among, the sixth the small flat knock out of a woman as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go and never among the things that came out of vessels; the seventh place stayed empty and none was invented to fill it, for an empty place kept empty is a finding and a finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged. The told figure stood beside the paid with no signature on the space between. Five arithmetics of the year stood behind one printed figure: one in twelve unchallenged, the figure read still not the figure paid, five readings five and not one account, their true subtractions riding locked up \place{the clay road} in \person{Berr Clay}'s box toward \place{High Kiln}, unread under this roof and not brought back as an answer to anything. The finding sat on a place of its own with no payer under it, not a lesson, not a grievance, not a mending, not a doctrine and not a rule. It was not laid across the house's \lore{not-doings}, or across the dressed grain under wool, or across the four held changes on their four places, or across the handful, or across the two small objects of the hand, or across his own kept gaps. The edges came near to touching, so near that a less careful hand would have pushed them together and called the touching a cause; the finger went between. Bone 30 (Day 329), the month's third and last reading, stood four mornings ahead and was not brought into a day that had not earned it.

The holding tray came out unchanged when he turned it over: the burned slate at five with no sixth and no witness and no hand at the flame; the row at five askings with one shaping and no witness and expressly no sixth; the well-saying at three shapings with \person{Hessa Rale}'s eighth hand on its own place and a finger between; the shut-room tellings at two; the three pot sayings with the Smoke 14 note standing apart; the nine stoppings with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end and the loan uncalled; nine record-first hands with the ninth still unread in its sleeve and no tenth, the wood keeping its count where no slate could take a figure off it; the seven green a colour and the seven shape a shape with no eighth; the heard bell in its square. Two hands were not a cause and nine were not a rule, and a rule wanted a payer and none stood. Nothing on the tray had grown in the night or been made to touch its neighbour.

The file lay open on \place{the towel} at the head of the bed with every object on its own place and every gap left gap. He turned it over in his mind the way he had once turned instruments out on a towel at the end of a list, not to find what was missing but to see that nothing had grown there while nobody was looking. The staying of Bone 15 and the early letting-go of Bone 17 sat each on its own place with a finger between them and between them and everything else there, neither made again, neither a habit, a way or a fifth held change, both wanting a payer who did not stand. The four held changes kept their four places and were not a rule; the handful kept its one place and was not made a fifth; the returned half-check kept its own place beside the Day 161 fraction, the Day 166 shelf beat and the Frost 5 returned beat, four objects and four and not a doctrine. Two hundred and twenty-four honest answers stood on the sixth when the light came, each an object and not a power, and neither the number nor the small hand-movings that had made it stood up and called themselves a doctrine, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood.

First light came into the room without a market, \place{the Cross} or a bell, an ordinary Sixthday going on about its own work the way water goes over water. No slate was opened within reach of the basket and no figure came near his hand. The surgeon in him took a small clean satisfaction in a field left as it was found, in the deliberate not-repetition of it, in the knowledge that a thing held by being left alone was still held and had not been spent to buy a likeness; the infant underneath took none of it and wanted the crust lifted and the thread wiped and got neither. Both were true and the second was louder and neither won. He kept the count because attention was the one thing no institution could tax, and he had stopped expecting anybody to tell him what it counted, which was not the same as not wanting it.

At the going of the water, when the river's note had come down to its lowest under the finger's-width door, the hand outside the wool went up an inch toward the sound. It took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole, let the intake go on the same low note, and came straight down and laid itself back against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. The note had done before the hand came down, so the letting-go was neither early again nor stayed, and the hand did not stay. The inch was not a reach and the intake was not a breath kept for warmth; the interval was not a stopping of the river and the note was not his. He had merely put his small hand into the order of things for the length of one note and taken it out again, and the order went on whether he did it or not. He filed it as answer two hundred and twenty-five: exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment, borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the five arithmetics, the bell's gap, the covered grain, the handful, the stayed object, the early letting-go nor his own kept gaps. Two hundred and twenty-five honest answers stood on the sixth place.

Three hundred and twenty-five stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in Low Kiln, Sill and Grath; six months and one hundred and forty-five days were told by nobody; the carried fire's fifth was unpaid the same number of days with its payer unnamed; and the name of the man the coat kept was not spoken under the roof the same number. Nothing on the twenty-sixth had been opened, joined, named, called, paid or ended. One stiff brown-edged crust lay two inches from his ear with its empty place unnamed and no third; the thread lay frozen and unwiped; cold had stopped at cheek, temple and the wrist-joint as at the end of its line and the afterdrop waited past a warming nobody would perform. The file lay open on the towel with every gap left gap, the hand lay back against the fold, and the river's low note came under the finger's-width door while Bone went on without a boundary drawn, four mornings short of the month's third and last reading and not brought into a day that had not earned it.
