# Chapter 124 — beat sheet: *Three Times Over the Basket*

**Arc:** Arc 1 — *The Cold Stone* / "a warm stone, already going cold". **Mode:** normal, three lived days in six full scenes. **Days:** 423–425 = Ilmen 29, Ninthday; Ilmen 30, Tenthday, the month's third and last Account-day and market; Silt 1, Firstday, Y.H. 814. Each day opens with its own dated `\daymark` in the first scene of that day (Scenes 1, 3 and 5) and is lived in two scenes of at least eight hundred words. No day is skipped, summarised or poured into another; there is no recap at the head of any scene.

**Opening state (from Chapter 123).** Small hours of Ilmen 28's end. The boy, four hundred and twenty-two days old and six months and two hundred and forty-two days told by nobody, lies under the Vail roof at the low angle of the loaf shelf, never in the Debt Loft; hand flat on the fold on answer **420**, count exact at **422**, six honest answers 415–420 on the sixth place, none a doctrine. Carried fire at four low pulls with the fifth unpaid; Garl's name unspoken the same 422 days, the two counts side by side and not become one. Two readings of the year (Ilmen 10, Ilmen 20) gone up the clay road in Berr's locked box behind the twelve of Y.H. 813, two being two and not one account, figure read still not figure paid. Ilva's offered figure gone back into its fist and its sleeve on Ilmen 26, filed beside her ninth record-first hand and the fifth shaping of the well-saying; the well-asking answered with the weather beside the second man's direction and turned slate and the first man's face; the clerk's entered figure reported at the landing; six hearings with the seventh empty and the Drawing's flat knock outside; nine record-first hands and nine stoppings with no tenth; softening at the river's skin kept as weather; crust hard as old horn two inches from the ear, thread stiff and unwiped, cold stopping at cheek/temple/wrist, Afterdrop waiting.

**Closing state.** Small hours of Silt 1's end. Count exact at **425**, six months and two hundred and forty-five days told by nobody, answers **421–426** filed two to each day; **three readings** of the year now behind one unsigned figure, three being three and not one account, no signature, figure read still not figure paid; two new sightings planted at the Ilmen 30 market on separate trays (first reaching of the adult romance, years ahead and unspent); everything else found where put and not spent. Hand flat on the fold on answer 426. Forward, not closed.

## Scene 1 — The Twenty-ninth Morning

- **Day and time:** Day 423, Ilmen 29, Y.H. 814, Ninthday. Small hours just after the previous chapter's close, then first light and the carrying-down into the ordinary morning work; ends at the morning's setting-down of the basket under the roof.
- **Place:** The Vail kiln-house — the bed at the low angle of the loaf shelf, the hearth, the pot board, the rack end; the nineteen steps down into the Clay Yard, the yard well, the flat stone by the well, the trough, the seven walked-around places, the kindling and flagstones, the Vail wheel-shed and the cold damper. The finger's-width door and the river heard, not entered.
- **Present:** Corin/Dan; Sella Vail; Tarn at the kindling and flagstones; Jorra Vail at the wheel-shed edge. Garl Vail in objects only (salt-stiff coat, hollow wheel-seat, clean dark line with its finer grey, twice-mended pot, unforged collector sentence beside the seventh notch). Walk-ons at the bucket-round. Offstage: Ilva (strip in her sleeve, count in wood), Hessa (slate unread up the road), Berr (box locked up the road). No Berr or Hessa *on stage*, no collector, officer, reading, market, Cross or bell.
- **Beats, in order:**
  1. Open on the hand laid flat on the fold. The surgeon takes the body in the old order: horn-hard barley crust two inches from the ear with its corner a hair off the wool; the empty place holding the fold found where put; the thread frozen in the dry heat and unwiped because nobody under any roof has ever given an instruction about the thread; cold stopping at cheek, temple and the bare wrist-joint where the wool rides back, as at the end of a line written out for it; the Afterdrop waiting past a warming nobody here will perform. He knows a basket set level out of the draught, where heat comes sideways, is the rewarming he would choose and cannot ask for; a face composed by cold is not composure; a cold patient cannot clot. The infant wants the crust lifted, the thread wiped and a hand on the pot handle. Both true, the second louder, neither winning; the quarrel is the work.
  2. First light. Sella comes to the hearth before the basket; the household straightens around the order. She tells the coals it was the thaw in the voice another woman would use for a child who could not hear her, turns them with the back of a broken pot, gives the small intake, the hold and the letting-go toward no opening and figures neither gap. *The house will keep it* goes to the fire, not to the basket.
  3. Her finger lies along the top of the loaf shelf so the charcoal tally beneath stays unread; her lips move on a two-gapped row; one glance goes to the cold clay jar under lead glaze beyond the pot board, the hand shuts half over nothing and the little-door burn on the back of her left hand closes on air. The widow's mark is untouched: a Ninthday morning is not an opening, seven openings in seven months are seven and not a rule.
  4. She lifts the twice-mended pot thumb-first where the tighter wire crosses the crack; the hairline shows between the old-brass staple and the wire; both proverbs go to the pot and the fire and never to a person — *Leave the crack showing. People trust a thing they can see has been handled*; *If it's worth mending it's worth mending twice. The second time is the real one*. Repair is not restoration. She turns the dressed grain under wool with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child, with no figure or handful, and sets the pot rim two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat and no look toward the bed.
  5. Carrying-down: nineteen steps at the half-pace from every open slate; the instruction goes to the wool, the river wind, the pot, the fire and the banked fire and never to the basket; seven walked-around places once and not twice, widest at the returning-line place; fold righted without a look at his face and no second fold.
  6. In the yard the small sounds come first — pail on the flat stone, the rope settling into its knot with the cold-stiffened rasp, barley passed wall to wall and never made into a number. A cost arrives as a direction and goes away as weather; a woman sent to ask a pot's price gets a note and not a price. The boy takes each apart by fist, eye, mouth and the half-beat in the gap and gives the gap no intention, for an intention wants a payer where none stands.
  7. Tarn: six pieces where the house can check and the seventh at the lip beside Garl's unforged sentence; *Six counted. The next I don't count* said to the wood once and not twice; the hand over blade and sill and away with no breath held at the top of the reach; the loan uncalled in a term already ended and the valuable ear exposure, not safety. Jorra: dusty forearms parted from clean as clot from vessel; speaks to the wheel before any person; withholds the full note he knows three turns ahead from any master up from High Kiln; bent finger into the cold damper ahead of Sella's unfinished sentence, one bite holding and one taking, out again without a second holding of Ash 26; *he's not Vail* sealed, the promise to the slate-burners neither given nor withdrawn.
  8. Answer **421** comes out and is filed on the sixth place. Count reaches **423** exact, six months and two hundred and forty-three days told by nobody. He does not stand it up as a threshold and does not lay the twelve old arithmetics and the two readings gone up the road across one another, for paper is not a payer. Ilmen 30 waits one morning off as a date and is not brought into a day that has earned only its twenty-ninth.
- **Emotional turn:** From the humiliating stillness of the body's inventory toward the small competence of the morning's order — not a recovery, only the arguable peace of work done exactly — and then the quiet re-arrest of the pot rim set two inches out of reach. The infant's want loses again without a verdict; the surgeon's register is worth nothing at two inches of air.
- **Sensory anchors:** Dry heat; horn-hard barley crust and the hair-lifted corner; stiffened thread; woodsmoke, wet wool and the faint sour mineral tang off the Vail corner; cold-stiffened hemp; the back of a broken pot scraping coals; lead glaze cold beyond the pot board; the little-door burn closing on air; old-brass staple and tighter wire with the hairline between; wool over dressed grain; two inches of air at the pot rim; the river's low note under its winter skin.
- **Threads advanced (none paid):** T060/T086/T097 (body inventory, three wants, keeping found where put); T071/T072 (423 exact, answer 421, six honest answers, none a doctrine); T058/T079/T100 (whole hearth grammar, charcoal unread, mark untouched, pot hairline showing, grain unfigured, pot rim two inches beyond reach); T009/T013/T092 (left things kept, no eighth opening); T098 and T009/T013/T092 (seven walked-around places once and not twice, returning-line place widest, wide step a keeping not a sign); T036/T076 and T035/T031/T062 (Tarn's six and seventh, sentence said once, loan uncalled; Jorra's dust, damper's double bite, sealed *he's not Vail*, promise ungiven); T077 (costs as directions, note not price); T083/T085/T092 (softening under the river's skin found and called weather).
- **Foreshadowing planted / paid off:** Nothing paid. Planted and carried: the third reading waits one morning off as a date; the twice-wiped order of reaching and the fist that returns to its sleeve go on their own trays; Berr's kindness and *your house* go up the road with the box; the softening under the skin gathers toward a thaw nobody will own; Tarn's valuable ear and ended term, Jorra's ungiven promise, the burned slate at five, the green at the pot board and the shape at the market's edge stand where put and do not repeat. No sixth hearing, tenth hand, tenth stopping, eighth opening, sixth shaping, signature or payer.
- **Exact last image:** The pot rim stands two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat and no look toward the bed, while under the boards the carried fire keeps four low pulls with the fifth unsupplied.

## Scene 2 — What the Twenty-ninth Left

- **Day and time:** Day 423, Ilmen 29, Ninthday. Afternoon through the going of the light and the small hours closing the day. Day already dated in Scene 1; no new `\daymark`.
- **Place:** The Clay Yard (yard well and flat stone, trough, drying racks, rack end, returning-line place, the seven walked-around places, the Vail corner); the finger's-width door and the boards; the hearth and pot board at the going of the light; the bed and towel at the head of the bed in the small hours.
- **Present:** Corin/Dan, Sella Vail, Tarn, Jorra Vail; Garl Vail in objects. Walk-ons at the ordinary Ninthday grammar. Ilva offstage with strip in sleeve and ninth hand nine, not made a reader by a reach in an empty yard. No offered figure a second time, no well-telling, no collector up or down the road.
- **Beats, in order:**
  1. Afternoon small sounds first because the small sounds do not lie and the large ones are mostly weather: pail set on the flat stone with the flat knock of a thing set where a thing has always been set, rope settling into its knot, a loaf pushed a finger's width along a board and stopped with nobody calling the stopping a figure. He has once sorted twenty people by what they would not survive; here he sorts a yard by what it will not say, and the sorting buys him the order of things and nothing more.
  2. He goes once around the seven walked-around places with a look and a wide step and no number, widest at the returning-line place where a hand went in on Seed 15 and did not come back. The wide step is a keeping, not a sign; he does not look twice, for looking twice is how a finding becomes a habit and a habit wants the hand that put the thing there.
  3. The trough stands bare and dark with the gone pan's shallow round print on a place of its own; he does not lay that ring across the loaf's finger's-width stopping or across the ridge at the corner, for two absences laid across one another want a single payer and none stands.
  4. At the Vail corner the clean dark line keeps its finer grey called the thaw, thickened in the dry cold with the faint sour tang of Ash coming up where no broom has gone in two years; beside it the called-gone grey of the ridge lies left in place with a finger between — a thing left in place is not a thing forgiven. The air off the river is less cold at one edge where the low note runs nearer under the skin, and the tang comes thinner on that edge; he finds the softening where put and calls it weather, and does not make it a sign, a fourth covering, a custom or a payment on a day that has earned only its twenty-ninth.
  5. Costs arrive as directions and go away as weather and leave no entry: a number comes up out of a shut fist toward the well, is held where the light can see it, looked at, and put back into a sleeve and arrives nowhere; a woman sent to ask what a pot cost is sent to the pot and gets a note and not a price; the dressed grain lies covered and unfigured and the arithmetic read over it does not know what it cost. He gives the fist no figure and the sleeve no motive, for a motive is a payer wearing a coat.
  6. The offered figure and its return are found where put on a place of its own beside Ilva's ninth hand and the fifth shaping, with a finger between every pair. It is not offered again, not spent, not named, not made a second reaching by being remembered, and not joined to the fist-to-nowhere number, the note-not-price, the asking answered with the weather, the clerk's entered figure, the second man's turned slate, the face at the yard's edge or Tarn's degree. The clerk's figure, the three likenesses of Slate Row and the fifth shaping keep their separate places with fingers between and no witness, reader, sale or tenth hand standing. Tarn's withheld sentence is found where put and not made a habit; Jorra's promise is neither given nor withdrawn.
  7. At the going of the light Sella comes to the hearth before the basket, tells the coals it was the thaw, turns them with the broken pot's back, gives the intake, hold and letting-go with neither gap figured, keeps her finger above the unread charcoal and her lips on a two-gapped row, glances once at the clay jar with the hand half over nothing, does not touch the widow's mark, lifts the twice-mended pot thumb-first with both proverbs to pot and fire, turns the dressed grain under wool, and sets the pot rim two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat and no look toward the bed. She says to the fire *The fire doesn't know you're sorry. Feed it* once on this day, puts two fingers of coal where the coals had thinned, carries him up the nineteen steps at the half-pace and sets him under the roof, never in the Debt Loft, adding no second fold.
  8. Small hours. The towel comes out at the head of the bed as instruments do when a case is done. Six hearings of the full note stand beside and never among with the seventh place empty and not dug for, the Drawing's flat knock outside on its own ground, the heard bell in its square beside the told bell with both gaps in their own squares, three counts in three places staying three, nine record-first hands and nine stoppings with no tenth. Answer **422** comes out and is filed on the sixth place; the count stands at **423** exact, six months and two hundred and forty-three days, told by nobody. He does not wipe the thread and does not lift the crust. Ilmen 30 waits one morning off.
- **Emotional turn:** From the day's small exactness toward the long undignified interval of the small hours — not grief, not settlement, only the holding of a field in which every gap is left a gap. The surgeon's file is exact, complete and worth nothing; the infant's three wants go unanswered and the wanting goes on.
- **Sensory anchors:** Flat knock of a pail set on stone; cold-stiffened hemp; a loaf stopped a finger's width along a board; the gone pan's shallow round print in bare dark water; the clean dark line, the finer grey, the faint sour tang of Ash; softer air at one river edge; a fist closing on a figure that arrived nowhere; tallow and low smoke off the towel; four low pulls under the floor and the missing fifth.
- **Threads advanced (none paid):** T071/T072 (423 exact, answer 422, six honest answers, none a doctrine); T098 and T009/T013/T092 (walked-around places, returning-line place, no figure, left things); T083/T085/T092 (softening and thinned tang found where put, called weather, not made again); T077 (costs as directions arriving nowhere); T080 with T090/T102 (offered figure and return found where put, not repeated or joined; clerk's figure, three likenesses, fifth shaping kept apart); T058/T079/T100 and T009/T013/T092 (whole hearth grammar at the going of the light, widow's mark untouched because a going of the light is not an opening); T060/T086/T097 (keeping found where put, crust and thread untouched); T061/T082 (six hearings beside and never among, seventh empty, three counts in three places).
- **Foreshadowing planted / paid off:** Nothing paid. The softening under the skin, the ridge left in place, the fist that arrived nowhere and the offered figure that went back into the sleeve gather interest toward the far-off hand and a thaw nobody will own; the held sentence, the uncalled loan, the ungiven promise, the burned slate at five, the green and the shape stay planted. No seventh hearing, tenth hand, eighth opening, sixth shaping or eleventh arithmetic; Ilmen 30 is a date and not a day earned.
- **Exact last image:** The hand outside the wool comes up an inch toward the river's note, the inch not a reach, holds the interval whole on the note's own finishing under the winter skin, lets it go as the note ends — neither early nor stayed — and comes straight down and lays itself flat against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking, and there is no next hand in the room.

## Scene 3 — The Third Reading

- **Day and time:** Day 424, Ilmen 30, Y.H. 814, Tenthday. Small hours opening on answer 422 and the count exact at 423; first light; the carrying-down; the third and last Account-day and market of Ilmen, earned by its own morning; the reading itself in the middle of the morning and the bell going through it.
- **Place:** The Vail kiln-house and the nineteen steps down; the Clay Yard; the Cross and the reading block under the moot-post with the moot-post's finger's width of shade; the clay road up toward High Kiln and on to Greybank; the north bridge and the out-of-line landing reported, never entered.
- **Present:** Corin/Dan (basket set in the moot-post's finger's width of shade); Sella Vail standing half a pace off through the reading and the bell; Tarn and Jorra at their morning work before the carrying-down; Berr Clay come up short off the clay road to the reading block; Hessa Rale taking the slate up the road before noon; Garl Vail in objects. Walk-ons: the two hundred at the Cross, eight pigs over the wet boards, a Sill assessor writing them down with hand to slate before eyes to the rail, a man with a number in a shut fist sent to a slate at the watch, the soft-cuffed clerk at the out-of-line landing reached only as a word brought up the yard.
- **Beats, in order:**
  1. Small hours: the surgeon takes the body in the old order and the infant's three wants quarrel without verdict; both true, the second louder, neither winning.
  2. First light and the carrying-down at the half-pace from every open slate; instruction to the wool, the river wind, the pot, the fire and the banked fire, never to the basket; seven walked-around places once and not twice; widow's mark untouched, for a market and a reading are not openings.
  3. At the Cross, Sella sets the basket in the moot-post's finger's width of shade where no slate lay and stands half a pace off, giving no instruction and no reason to the basket; she does not look into it and does not touch the mark.
  4. Berr comes up short off the clay road with the red wool cord of his office folded square under his coat, sweetish green of tasted lead glaze at one mouth-corner, hot clay over the smell of nothing at all. His large clean hands wipe the thumb twice before touching nothing — once for the slate and once for the figure that will be on it — hold a narrow slate unopened to the forearm with the writing inward, pass a finger over the figure through the sleeve, let his eyes rest on the writing a breath longer than the writing requires.
  5. He reads *One in twelve, as the notice says* low into the goers-by and sends the figure twice over the basket, once for the moot and once for the households that came late, never looking into it. He calls the levy and denies it — *The levy is called. It is not called by me*; *your house*, never *I* — taking himself out of his own sentence as a surgeon draws a hand from a field before the dressing goes on. To the man with the number in a shut fist he sends the number into a slate at the watch, and it goes from fist to mouth to shoulder and arrives nowhere.
  6. Something low he says to the block about kindness being entered, in the tone of a man setting a cup where a cup belongs; the stone answers nothing; there is no anger in it and no triumph and no payer standing under it. He goes off up the clay road toward High Kiln with the cord folded, the narrow slate under his arm and the box locked, Ilmen's third true subtraction riding inside, believing he has done a kindness.
  7. **Three readings of the year now stand behind one unsigned figure** — Ilmen 10, Ilmen 20, Ilmen 30. Three are three and not one account; the first is not made a thirteenth by the second or the third; the figure read is still not the figure paid; no signature stands on the space and no doctrine comes out of three. The boy does not lay the twelve old arithmetics or the two earlier readings across the third to make a row come out even, for paper is not a payer and the key went up in a coat pocket that never comes back down. Berr's low saying about kindness and his *your house* stay each on a place of its own with a finger between.
  8. Hessa goes up the clay road before noon with the slate held edge-on to her forearm and the writing turned to her sleeve, a runner and not a reader; no figure comes off the slate and her eighth hand stays eighth. Below the north bridge the soft-cuffed clerk stays set at the out-of-line landing with his entered figure reported on its own place, never a sighting, a witness or a tenth hand. The bell goes four, four and a gap with nobody crossing himself; the heard bell keeps its square beside the told bell, its gap not folded into the carried fire's gap or the boards' knot, three counts in three places staying three.
  9. Answer **423** comes out and is filed on the sixth place; the count reaches **424** exact, six months and two hundred and forty-four days told by nobody. At the going of the light Sella carries him home at the half-pace, saying nothing of what was read over him, of the bell's gap, of Hessa's slate or of the clerk's figure.
- **Emotional turn:** The crowd leans in and goes back to the pigs; the reading changes the district's arithmetic and changes nothing under the roof. The boy's register notes the order of Berr's reaching — hand, eye, mouth, the half-beat — and files it with no intention under it; the third reading is a change in the world's account and not a payment of the boy's.
- **Sensory anchors:** Wet boards under eight pigs; the red wool cord folded under a coat; sweetish lead-glaze green at a mouth-corner; hot clay over the smell of nothing; thumb wiped twice on an apron; a narrow slate held inward with writing turned away; a bell longer than a church bell, four, four and a gap; the cold iron of the moot-post at his cheek in the finger's width of shade; road-mud and bake-heat off the slate runner.
- **Threads advanced (none paid):** T015/T041/T047 (third reading, three readings three and not one account, no signature, figure not price, twelve not laid across three, Berr's self-removal and kindness on their own trays); T058/T079 (basket in the shade, half-pace, half-beat at setting, widow's mark untouched at a market and a reading); T080 (Hessa's eighth hand, slate unread, no figure, not made a reader by three figures); T090 with T080/T102 (clerk reported at the landing, never a sighting or witness); T078 (heard bell beside told, four, four and a gap, three counts in three places); T071/T072 (424 exact, answer 423, six honest answers, none a doctrine); T060/T086/T097 (body inventory and three wants carried into the Account-day); T077 (fist to mouth to shoulder arriving nowhere); T009/T013/T092 (no eighth opening on a reading day).
- **Foreshadowing planted / paid off:** Nothing paid. Berr's kindness on Ilmen 10, 20 and 30 grows toward the day it will be remembered against him; his *your house* toward the day a hand that was in a field must be found to have gone away before the dressing went on. The three readings, the key gone up in a coat pocket, the clean voice's missing name and the Kindling collector's reason go on their own trays toward the far-off accounting. Silt 10 (Day 434), the month's first reading and the year's fourth, waits beyond the close and is not brought. No tenth hand, eleventh arithmetic, signature, payer or seventh hearing.
- **Exact last image:** Berr's narrow slate goes up the clay road with its writing turned to his sleeve and the third true subtraction locked in the box under his arm, while the bell's fourth stroke falls into a gap nobody fills.
