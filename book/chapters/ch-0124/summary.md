## Chronicle

Three days came under the Vail roof and the house kept them in loaves pushed a finger's width and stopped. Ilmen 29 was an ordinary Ninthday; Ilmen 30 its Account-day and market; Silt 1 came on with no boundary drawn. The boy carried four hundred and twenty-three, four hundred and twenty-four and four hundred and twenty-five exact. Each morning the surgeon took the body in the old order — horn-hard crust, fold found where put, thread frozen and unwiped, cold stopping at cheek and wrist, the Afterdrop waiting past a warming nobody would perform — and the infant wanted the crust lifted, the thread wiped and a hand on the pot handle. Both were true, the second louder, neither won, and the quarrel was the work.

On the thirtieth Sella set the basket in the moot-post's shade and stood half a pace off. Berr Clay came up off the clay road, wiped his thumb twice, held the narrow slate unopened, passed a finger over the figure through his sleeve, and read *One in twelve, as the notice says* twice over the basket. He called the levy and denied it with *your house*, never I, sent a fist-number to a slate at the watch where it arrived nowhere, said something low to the block about kindness being entered, and went up the road with the box locked, believing he had done a kindness. Three readings stood behind one unsigned figure; three were three and not one account, the figure read still not paid. The bell went four, four and a gap; nobody crossed himself. Hessa carried the slate toward Greybank unread.

At the market a woman with clay in her nail crescents and a pale cord-mark on one hand turned a cup a quarter and set it back where it stood, saying nothing. At the edge a foundling in cast-offs turned his head a half-beat after a pot's knock and note and stopped mid-turn. The boy filed a colour and a shape on separate trays, joined to no name, sale or eighth. Nothing from the square crossed under the roof.

At each morning and going of the light Sella told the coals it was the thaw, lifted the mended pot thumb-first with the hairline showing, and set the rim two inches beyond the reaching hand. The widow's mark was untouched; Tarn kept six and the seventh at the lip; Jorra took his finger from the damper. Costs went away as weather; the softening at the river's skin was called weather. Six answers, four hundred and twenty-one through six and twenty-six, were filed, worth nothing at two inches of air and not one a doctrine. The count reached four hundred and twenty-five exact, told by nobody; the fire's fifth was unpaid and Garl's name unspoken. In the small hours the towel came out as instruments do when a case is done. The hand came down flat on the fold. The well had given back the weather, and the house went on.
