\daymark{425}{Silt 1, Y.H. 814, Firstday}

Small hours came on under the Vail roof without a boundary drawn and without a word said over the turning of the month. \person[Corin]{The boy} lay at the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf} in \place{the Vail kiln-house}, \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill}, \place{Grath}, under the roof and never in \place{the Debt Loft}, held where the heat came to him sideways and not where he would have chosen; the room kept its heat the way a cup keeps water, with care, and with one crack nobody had mentioned. \place{The finger's-width door} kept its own thin dark and the dark thinned by no degree anybody measured. The hand outside the wool lay flat on \lore{the keeping at the fold} where it had been laid on the last answer of Ilmen 30. Four hundred and twenty-four exact, and six months and two hundred and forty-four days told by nobody who would have known what a number counted, and no voice under the roof to tell it.

Silt had come on in the dark as Ilmen had gone. No line had been drawn, no hand set to any boundary, neither of the district's two habits of naming its months set right or left. The yard would call the new month mud and the slate would call it a date, and the two names would stand side by side without agreeing and without owing anything to one another. Nothing crossed into the morning from the day behind as a sign or a lesson or a mending or a cause or a payment or a payer. The third reading of Ilmen had gone up the road locked in \person{Berr Clay}'s box and the two sightings at the market had stayed where they stood, a quarter-turned cup and a head stopped in a half-beat, and neither had come under this roof to explain the other. A month turning is a thing the Court enters and a household endures; nobody under this roof had entered anything, and nobody had endured more than usual.

The surgeon took the body in its old order, because the body gave him nothing else to take. One stiff brown-edged barley crust stood two inches from his ear, gone past leather into old horn through the dry nights, with its corner a hair off the wool. The empty place beside it held the keeping where put and still a finding, with no third crust, no name, no gift, no payment and no payer; a hand that finds is not a hand that places, and a second placing would be a habit and a third a way, and both want a payer where none stood. The thread of drool at his mouth had frozen in the dry kiln heat and was unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread. Cold came to cheek and temple and the small bare joint at the wrist where the wool rode back, and stopped there as at the end of a line written out for it. A face composed by cold is not composure. A cold patient cannot clot. \lore{The Afterdrop} waited past a warming nobody in this house would perform. He knew that a basket set level out of the draught, where the heat came to him sideways, was the rewarming he would choose and could not ask for.

The infant wanted the crust lifted, the thread wiped, a hand on the pot handle. Both were true. The second was louder. Neither won. The quarrel was the work, and the work went on in the dark in a body that could not lift a crust two inches to its own mouth. Above and behind, the loft kept whatever small sound it kept and did not repeat it, and he left it where it was.

First light came grey at the door and then along \place{the pot board}, and the household straightened around the order of it as though the month had brought no change anybody needed to name. \person{Sella Vail} went to \place{the hearth} before she went to the basket. She told the coals it was \lore{the thaw} in the voice another woman would have used for a child who could not hear her, and turned them with the back of a broken pot; the back scraped over them and left the blind ash where it lay. She gave the small intake, the hold and the letting-go toward no opening, figuring neither of the two gaps, and said \emph{The house will keep it} to the fire and not to the basket, because a thing said aloud becomes a thing the Court can enter. Her finger lay along the top of the loaf shelf so the charcoal tally beneath stayed unread, and her lips moved on a two-gapped row. One glance went to the clay jar standing cold under \lore{lead glaze} beyond the board; the hand shut half over nothing, and the little-door burn on the back of her left hand, two straight sides with a lintel and a threshold, closed on air and went back to the pot. She smelled of woodsmoke and wet wool, and under it the faint sour tang of the corner that had not been swept in two years, and she carried the smell the way she carried the mark, without comment.

The \lore{widow's mark} hung where it hung and she did not touch it. A new month's first tending day is not an opening; seven openings in seven months are seven and not a rule, and no eighth came due on a Firstday that had earned only its first. She touched the mark before she opened the kiln and at no other hour, and the kiln was not open this morning. She lifted the twice-mended pot thumb-first where the tighter wire crossed the crack. The hairline of the original break showed between the older brass staple and the wire, a dark thread with a hand's work on either side, and both proverbs went to the pot and the fire and never to a person: \emph{Leave the crack showing. People trust a thing they can see has been handled}; \emph{If it's worth mending it's worth mending twice. The second time is the real one}. Repair was not restoration. A restored thing hides the hand that handled it; a mended thing leaves the hand showing, so that a vessel carrying its break can be read by the next woman who takes it up. The boy read the hairline as he read all honest seams, with the eye of a man who has set a clamp and known the difference between what holds and what only looks joined.

She turned the dressed grain under wool at \place{the rack end} with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child, set each piece where it had lain, took no figure from it and no handful, and drew the wool back with the flat of her hand. Under the wool it lay covered and unfigured, and a figure read over it would not know what it cost. Then she set the pot rim two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat and no look toward \place{the bed}. The half-beat was the distance between the intention and the arrest; she did not measure it and did not name it, for a named gap wants a payer put under it and none stood. Two inches of air bought no warmth and she knew it as well as he did, and neither of them said so.

At the carrying-down she went down \place{the nineteen steps} at the half-pace from every open slate, correcting the tilt at each turn of the landing before it settled into the wicker. The instruction went to the wool, the river wind, the pot, the fire and the banked fire, and never to the basket; the basket was not told what the day was and was not told to be still. In \place{the Clay Yard} she went wide around \place{the seven walked-around places} once and not twice, widest around \place{the returning-line place} where a hand had gone in on \emph{Seed 15} and had not come back. No name for the hand, no body found, no figure for what the month had cost; the wide step was a keeping and not a sign, and a figure set against that space would want a payer standing under it and none stood. At \place{the trough} the bare dark water kept the shallow round print of the clay pan lifted away, a ring on a place of its own, and she did not lay it across the loaf's finger's-width stopping or across the ridge at \place{the Vail corner}, for two absences laid across one another want a single payer and none stood.

The yard went about its small grammar while she carried him across it. A pail went down on \place{the flat stone by the well} with the flat knock of a thing set where a thing has always been set. A rope settled into its knot with the cold-stiffened rasp. Barley went wall to wall without a number. A loaf was pushed a finger's width along a board and stopped, and nobody called the stopping a figure. He had once sorted twenty people by what they would not survive; here he sorted a yard by what it would not say, and the sorting bought him the order of things and nothing more.

At the Vail corner the clean dark line kept its finer grey called the thaw beside it, thickened in the dry cold with the faint sour tang of \lore{Ash} coming up where no broom had gone in two years; beside it the called-gone grey of the ridge lay left in place with a finger between --- a thing left in place is not a thing forgiven. The air off \place{the river} was less cold at one edge where the low note of \lore{water over water} ran nearer under the winter skin, and the tang came thinner on that edge. He found the softening where put and called it weather. He did not look twice, did not make it a sign, a fourth of \lore{the three coverings}, a payment or a custom on the first day of a new month, and did not make it again. The sound came from under the covering and not from the covering, and the note ended on its own finishing before any hand of his went up to it.

Costs came as directions and went away as weather and left no entry. A man waiting his turn at the well opened a shut fist toward the water, held what was in it where the light could see, looked at it, shut the fist again and put it back into a sleeve; the number arrived nowhere. A woman sent to ask what a pot cost was sent to the pot and got a note and not a price. Two women at the rail said the rope would take a finger if a body was careless and that cold was cold and fingers were fingers, and went back to their pails; another said the cold had got into the rope and would not come out before the thaw, and another said a rope that held was a rope that held. The boy gave the fist no figure, the note no payer and the women no interior beyond the mouths the words came out of, for a motive is a payer wearing a coat and the half-beat in the gap is a gap with nothing standing under it.

At \place{the kindling} and \place{the flagstones} \person{Tarn} kept six pieces in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check and the seventh at the lip beside \person{Garl Vail}'s unforged collector sentence, with no name on the man who had turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on \lore{the tile debt}. The small knife lay at the very edge of its sill; his hand went over blade and sill as a man passes a hand over a tool put back where it belongs and came away with no breath held at the top of the reach, the coming away the whole of the checking. “Six counted. The next I don't count,” he told the wood in the tone of a man stating the weather, once and not twice, for a second would be a custom and a third a way and both want a payer where none stood. The sentence about an ear under this roof being worth more than the debt that held it came to the word that would call the loan and stopped there, a remembered ninth stopping with no tenth and no figure and no name. The loan stayed uncalled in a term already ended and his valuable ear was exposure and not safety; a term ending is not a payment, a forgiveness or a freedom, and leaves a man more exposed than it found him. His chest had been tight since the dark, Ash worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line, the pale clip-ring at his left wrist catching and giving back what little light there was. He took the broom only after it had been given its instruction, said “Yes, house” to the fire and did not turn his head toward the landing.

\person{Jorra Vail} came through the finger's-width door off \place{the Vail wheel-shed} with the meal-fine grey of slate dust ground small past his bent little finger and white in a fist-crease where a hand had opened; it was not the bone-coloured \lore{slip} of the wheel. He parted dusty forearms from clean as clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had been broken nor whether it was a birth-slate, for a question joined to a cause wants a payer standing and none stood. Men unnamed had gone low into \place{the back room} before first grey and he gave the house no name for them and no word that the room was cold, and the broken ones under the lowest rack stayed entered by nobody. He spoke to the wheel before any person. “It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady.” To a pot he said “You'll do” in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness, and knew the \lore{full note} that pot would give three turns before it left his hands, with air under it and a want in it, and withheld the knowing from any master come up from \place{High Kiln} who would call it early and sing a pot before its time. Sella had not finished telling him the fire was low when his bent finger went into the cold iron of \place{the damper} ahead of her sentence; one bite of the iron held and one took, and no \place{Grathic} word covered both mercies. He took the cold out of the fingertip, moved his tongue a hair's breadth and came out without the extra holding of Ash 26, gathered once and not made again. “He's not Vail” stayed sealed, and the promise to the men who burn slates stayed neither given nor withdrawn.

Answer four hundred and twenty-five came out and was filed on the sixth place. It was exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the twelve arithmetics of Y.H. 813, the three readings of the year gone up the road, the printed figure, the fifth shaping of \lore{the well-saying}, the offered figure, the clerk's entered figure, the well-asking, the turned slate, the degree, the face, Tarn's withheld sentence, Jorra's ungiven promise, the softening, the widow's mark nor the keeping at the fold. Eleven honest answers stood on the sixth place, 415 through 425, two to each of the five days behind and one to this morning, and not one was a doctrine. He stood no number up as a threshold and borrowed no payer from the arithmetics gone up the road, for paper is not a payer; the key had gone up in a coat pocket that never comes back down and no key came under any roof.

Four hundred and twenty-five exact, six months and two hundred and forty-five days told by nobody who would have known what a number counted. \lore{The carried fire}'s fifth was still unpaid and Garl's name still unspoken the same number, the man and the fire standing side by side without becoming one. Three readings stood behind one unsigned figure, three being three and not one account, the figure read still not the figure paid, no signature on the space and no doctrine out of three; the fourth waited nine mornings off as a date and was not brought into a day that had earned only its first. Berr's low saying about kindness being entered and his \emph{your house} kept each a place of its own with a finger between and went up the road with him, believed and not owed. The offered figure kept its place beside \person{Ilva}'s ninth record-first hand and the fifth shaping, with a finger between every pair, not offered again, not made a custom by a new month, not a sale, a reader, a tenth hand or a payer. \person{Hessa Rale}'s narrow slate stayed unread up \place{the clay road} with her eighth hand eighth; the soft-cuffed clerk's entered figure stayed a report brought up the yard from \place{the out-of-line landing} below \place{the north bridge}, never a sighting, a witness or a tenth hand; the three likenesses of \place{Slate Row} and the well-asking kept their places with a finger between every pair. The two sightings of the market behind stayed on separate trays with no eighth of either, no name, no loaf, no sale, no joining and no crossing under this roof as sign or lesson or mending or cause or payment or payer. No sixth shaping, seventh hearing, tenth stopping, eleventh arithmetic or eighth opening stood on the first of Silt, and no slate was opened within reach of the basket.

Sella carried him back up the nineteen steps at the half-pace and set him under the roof at the edge of the light where the heat came sideways, never in the Debt Loft, and put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face and added no second fold. The keeping in its empty place was found where put and stayed a finding, with no third crust, no name, no gift, no payment and no payer. The thread at his mouth stayed stiff and unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread. Under \place{the boards} the carried fire kept four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood, and nobody lifted the floor to supply it. The dressed grain lay covered under wool with no figure and no handful, and the pot stood where she had set it, its rim two inches beyond the reaching hand with the half-beat unmeasured and no look toward the bed. The well gave back the weather, the house went on as a house goes when a pot is set down in its place, and the hand lay flat on the fold in a room whose floor did not touch his --- forward, into an afternoon not yet lived and not brought into this morning.
