The afternoon came on under the Vail roof with nobody marking it, the light drawing off \place{the pot board} an inch at a time, the room keeping its heat the way a cup keeps water, with care, and with one crack nobody had mentioned. \person[Corin]{The boy} lay where \person{Sella Vail} had set him at the edge of the light, held sideways out of the draught and not where he would have chosen, the hand outside the wool lying flat on \lore{the keeping at the fold} where it had been laid on the last answer. Nothing had crossed under the roof from the market behind --- not the cup turned a quarter and set back where it stood, not the head that stopped in its half-beat, not the third reading gone up the road in \person{Berr Clay}'s locked box. An afternoon keeps its own small accounts and owes nothing to the day behind it. He had once sorted twenty people by what they would not survive; here he would sort the yard by what it would not say, and the sorting bought him the order of things and nothing more.

She carried him down before the afternoon was well begun, \place{the nineteen steps} at the half-pace from every open slate, correcting the tilt at each turn of the landing before it settled into the wicker. The instruction went to the wool, the river wind, the pot, the fire and the banked fire and never to the basket; the basket was not told what the day was and was not told to be still. In \place{the Clay Yard} she went wide around \place{the seven walked-around places} once and not twice, widest around \place{the returning-line place} where a hand had gone in on \emph{Seed 15} and had not come back. No name for the hand, no body found, no figure for what any month had cost; the wide step was a keeping and not a sign, and a figure set against that space would want a payer standing under it and none stood.

The small sounds came first because the small sounds do not lie and the large ones are mostly weather. A pail went down on \place{the flat stone by the well} with the flat knock of a thing set where a thing has always been set. A rope settled into its knot with the cold-stiffened rasp. Barley went wall to wall without a number. A loaf was pushed a finger's width along a board and stopped, and nobody called the stopping a figure. He took each apart by fist, eye, mouth and the half-beat in the gap, and gave the gap no intention, for an intention wants a payer where none stands.

At \place{the trough} the bare dark water kept the shallow round print of the clay pan lifted away, a ring on a place of its own. He did not lay that ring across the loaf's finger's-width stopping or across the ridge at \place{the Vail corner}, for two absences laid across one another want a single payer and none stood. The water-line stood level in a vessel nobody had calibrated and he read the level without a figure, as a man reads a level in a basin and leaves the arithmetic where it lies.

The yard kept its own time badly. A woman at the well knocked a pail against her knee and did not look at the sleeve of the one beside her; a girl with clay to her wrists passed a cup from hand to hand without lifting it from the board and set it back where it stood, and nobody called the quarter-turn a figure. He had learned to read a room by the order in which a hand went to a record, a face and a mouth, and here the order was pail, rope, board, with the half-beat lying in the gap where a lesser observer would put an intention; there was no intention standing under it, only the cold and the work and the going of the light coming on. He gave the half-beat no owner and the order no author, for an author is a payer wearing a coat.

At the Vail corner the clean dark line kept its finer grey called \lore{the thaw} beside it, thickened in the dry cold with the faint sour tang of \lore{Ash} coming up where no broom had gone in two years; beside it the called-gone grey of the ridge lay left in place with a finger between --- a thing left in place is not a thing forgiven. The air off \place{the river} was less cold at one edge where the low note of \lore{water over water} ran nearer under the winter skin, and the tang came thinner on that edge. He found the softening where put and called it weather. He did not look twice, did not make it a sign, a fourth of \lore{the three coverings}, a payment or a custom on the first afternoon of a new month, and did not make it again. The sound came from under the covering and not from the covering, and the note ended on its own finishing before any hand of his went up to it.

Costs came as directions and went away as weather and left no entry. A man waiting his turn at \place{the yard well} opened a shut fist toward the water, held what was in it where the light could see, looked at it, shut the fist again and put it back into a sleeve; the number arrived nowhere. A woman sent to ask what a pot cost was sent to the pot and got a note and not a price. The dressed grain lay covered under wool at \place{the rack end} and a figure read over it would not know what it cost, and between the figure and the price there stood an ink-neat space with no signature on it. He gave the fist no figure, the note no payer and the space no name, for a motive is a payer wearing a coat and the half-beat in the gap is a gap with nothing standing under it.

Two women at the rail said the rope would take a finger if a body was careless and that cold was cold and fingers were fingers, and went back to their pails; another said the cold had got into the rope and would not come out before the thaw, and another said a rope that held was a rope that held. The asking that had come up at the well on a day behind was found where put beside the second man's direction down \place{the north bridge} road and his narrow slate turned to the sleeve, with a finger between every pair; it was not told again, not made a custom and not given a name, owner, answer carried, witness, sale or tenth hand. It was a reaching gathering interest toward a far-off calling and a far-off hand, and was not that calling. \person{Tarn}'s withheld sentence was found where put and not made a habit, and \person{Jorra Vail}'s promise to the men who burn slates stayed neither given nor withdrawn.

At the going of the light \person{Sella Vail} came to \place{the hearth} before she came to the basket, and the household straightened around the order of it as though nothing had turned all afternoon. She told the coals it was \lore{the thaw} in the voice another woman would have used for a child who could not hear her, and turned them with the back of a broken pot; the back scraped over them and left the blind ash where it lay. She gave the small intake, the hold and the letting-go toward no opening, figuring neither of the two gaps, and said \emph{The house will keep it} to the fire and not to the basket, because a thing said aloud becomes a thing the Court can enter. Her finger lay along the top of \place{the loaf shelf} so the charcoal tally beneath stayed unread, and her lips moved on a two-gapped row. One glance went to the clay jar standing cold under \lore{lead glaze} beyond the board; the hand shut half over nothing, and the little-door burn on the back of her left hand, two straight sides with a lintel and a threshold, closed on air and went back to the pot. She smelled of woodsmoke and wet wool, and under it the faint sour tang of the corner nobody had swept in two years, and she carried the smell the way she carried the mark, without comment.

Her hands were never still. When they had nothing to do they counted --- spoons, nails, the breaths of a child nobody numbered --- and came back to the hairline at the wire. He watched the count the way he had once watched a nurse's hands in a bay whose doors would not stay shut, reading the work and not the woman, and the reading was the whole of what he was allowed. One grey streak at her temple said winter and she said nothing about the winter, and the burn on the back of her hand shaped like a little door closed on air when there was nothing for it to hold. She said the house where another woman would say I, and the boy where another would say my son, and a thing said aloud becomes a thing the Court can enter, so she said none of it aloud.

The \lore{widow's mark} hung where it hung and she did not touch it. A going of the light is not an opening; seven openings in seven months are seven and not a rule, and no eighth came due on a first day of \place{Silt} that had earned only its first. She touched the mark before she opened the kiln and at no other hour, and the kiln was not open this evening. She lifted the twice-mended pot thumb-first where the tighter wire crossed the crack. The hairline of the original break showed between the older brass staple and the wire, a dark thread with a hand's work on either side, and both proverbs went to the pot and the fire and never to a person: \emph{Leave the crack showing. People trust a thing they can see has been handled}; \emph{If it's worth mending it's worth mending twice. The second time is the real one}. Repair was not restoration. A restored thing hides the hand that handled it; a mended thing leaves the hand showing, so that a vessel carrying its break can be read by the next woman who takes it up. He read the hairline as he read all honest seams, with the eye of a man who has set a clamp and known the difference between what holds and what only looks joined. \person{Garl Vail}'s laugh had ended in a cough and his proverbs had survived him improved by use, and neither the hairline nor the sayings bought this absence a payment, a name or a mending.

She put two fingers of coal where the coals had thinned, turned the dressed grain under wool at \place{the rack end} with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child, set each piece where it had lain, took no figure from it and no handful, and drew the wool back with the flat of her hand. Then she set the pot rim two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat and no look toward \place{the bed}. The half-beat was the distance between the intention and the arrest; she did not measure it and did not name it, for a named gap wants a payer put under it and none stood. Two inches of air bought no warmth and she knew it as well as he did, and neither of them said so. The thread at his mouth stayed stiff and unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread.

She carried him back up the nineteen steps at the half-pace and set him under the roof at the edge of the light where the heat came sideways, never in \place{the Debt Loft}, and put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face and added no second fold. \lore{The keeping at the fold} in its empty place was found where put and stayed a finding, with no third crust, no name, no gift, no payment and no payer. Under \place{the boards} \lore{the carried fire} kept four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood, and nobody lifted the floor to supply it. The two counts stood side by side --- his name unspoken and the fire's fifth unpaid --- and did not become one. The well gave back the weather, the house went on as a house goes when a pot is set down in its place, and the hand lay flat on the fold in a room whose floor did not touch his.

Small hours came on and \place{the towel} came out at the head of the bed as instruments come out when a case is done and before the next is called, rough from the wash and smelling of tallow and low smoke. He went over the day by the place of each thing with every gap left a gap. The file turned out on the towel as instruments turn out on a towel, and found exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air. He had reduced shoulders in a room that smelled of antiseptic and had known the exact moment a vessel took or did not take; here he could not reduce the distance between his hand and a pot handle, and the distance was two inches of air and air buys no warmth. The chart in the old life had carried a note turned down and a hand going past it to a signature; here the space between figure and price carried no note and no signature, and no hand came to put one there.

Six hearings of \lore{the full note} stood beside and never among, with the seventh place empty and not dug for, and \place{the Drawing house}'s flat knock stayed outside them on its own ground and was not made a hearing. The heard bell in its square kept beside the told bell, four, four and a gap, with both gaps in their own squares; the carried fire's gap below the boards and the boards' four, four and a thumb-width knot stayed three counts in three places, because agreeing any two would want a payer put under both and none stood. Nine record-first hands stood with no tenth, and nine stoppings stood with no tenth, and no name was given to the gap after the ninth.

Three readings stood behind one unsigned figure --- the three of \place{Ilmen} gone up the road in Berr's box --- three being three and not one account, the first not made a thirteenth by the second or the third, the figure read still not the figure paid, no signature on the ink-neat space and no doctrine out of three. He did not lay the twelve arithmetics of Y.H. 813 across the three of the new count to make a row come out even, for paper is not a payer; the key had gone up in a coat pocket that never came back down and no key came under any roof. The fourth waited nine mornings off as a date and was not brought into a day that had earned only its first. Berr's low saying about kindness being entered and his \emph{your house} kept each a place of its own with a finger between, and went up the road with him, believed and not owed.

Offstage every keeping was found where put. \person{Hessa Rale}'s narrow slate stayed unread up \place{the clay road} with her eighth hand eighth; \person{Ilva}'s strip stayed unread in her sleeve with her ninth hand nine, the offered figure and its return on a place of its own with a finger between it and the ninth hand and another between it and the fifth shaping of \lore{the well-saying}. The soft-cuffed clerk's entered figure stayed a report brought up the yard from \place{the out-of-line landing} below the north bridge, a figure entered before the eyes went up, on a place of its own with a finger between it and \person{Tarn}'s degree and another between it and the face at the yard's edge. The first man's face, the clerk's figure and the fifth shaping kept their places; the second man's turned slate and direction and the asking answered with the weather kept theirs beside them. The two market sightings stayed on separate trays with no eighth of either, no name, no loaf, no sale, no joining and no crossing under this roof as sign, lesson, mending, cause, payment or payer. No joining stood among any of them, no witness, no reader, no sale, no tenth hand and no paying. Tarn's loan stayed uncalled in a term already ended and his valuable ear stayed exposure rather than safety; Jorra's \emph{he's not Vail} stayed sealed; the softening at the river's skin stayed weather; the green at the pot board and the shape at the market's edge stayed sightings with no eighth. The burned slate stood at five and the four ownerless directions stood where put. Nothing was paid.

The surgeon's exactness and the infant's want stood side by side and neither bought the other. Under them, louder, the wanting went on --- the crust lifted, the thread wiped, a hand on the pot handle --- and got none of the three. Both were true. The second was louder. Neither won. No verdict was looked for and the quarrel was the work, and the work went on in the dark in a body that could not lift a crust two inches to its own mouth. One stiff brown-edged barley crust stood two inches from his ear, gone past leather into old horn, with its corner a hair off the wool. Cold had come to cheek and temple and the small bare joint at the wrist where the wool rode back, and stopped there as at the end of a line written out for it. A face composed by cold is not composure; a cold patient cannot clot; \lore{The Afterdrop} waited past a warming nobody in this house would perform. He knew that a basket set level out of the draught, where the heat came to him sideways, was the rewarming he would choose and could not ask for.

Answer four hundred and twenty-six came out and was filed on the sixth place. It was exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the twelve readings, the three of the new count, the printed figure, the fifth shaping, the offered figure, the clerk's figure, the well-asking, the turned slate, the degree, the face, the two market sightings, the withheld sentence, the softening, the bell's gap, the flat knock, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the held breath, the two bites of iron, the winter skin, the widow's mark nor the keeping at the fold. Six honest answers stood on the sixth place, 421 through 426, two to each of the three days, and not one was a doctrine. He set the file down where the next hand would find it without looking, and there was no next hand in the room.

Four hundred and twenty-five exact, six months and two hundred and forty-five days told by nobody who would have known what a number counted. He did not stand the number up as a threshold or a figure and borrowed no payer from the arithmetics gone up the road, for paper is not a payer; a number counted is a number with air around it and air buys no warmth. The unsent call that had waited nine summers was not paid again and did not cross under any roof. No eighth opening, seventh hearing, sixth shaping, tenth hand, tenth stopping, eleventh arithmetic, signature, payer, witness or reader stood on any of the three days, and no slate had been opened within reach of the basket.

Through \place{the finger's-width door} the river kept its note of water over water, a low thing over a lower thing under the winter skin, softer at one edge where the low note ran nearer under it, found where put on all three days, called weather, not a hearing, not a sign, not a fourth of the three coverings, not a seventh of anything and not a payer. The hand outside the wool came up an inch toward the note, the inch not a reach. He took a small intake from the room whose floor did not touch his, held the interval whole on the note's own finishing under the winter skin, let it go as the note ended --- neither early nor stayed --- and came straight down and laid itself flat against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. He did not wipe the thread and did not lift the crust. Four low pulls breathed under the floor and the fifth was not supplied; the well gave back the weather, the house went on, and the hand had come down.
