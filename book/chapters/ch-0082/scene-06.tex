The walk home had the shape of every other walk home, and that was the only comfort in it. \person{Sella Vail} carried him down from \place{the Cross} at the half-pace she kept from every open slate, in the basket under the fold of wool like a pot filled that must not be slopped, and gave the instruction to the wool and to the river wind and never to the basket: keep it off his ears, it's the river wind. \person[Corin]{The boy} lay with the square going out of his hearing board by board, the two hundred leaning, the wet boards, the pigs that were eight and had been eight, the bell's hollow that nobody had filled; the yard took them all back the way a cold stone takes the heat out of a pot set down on it, by degrees, the belly of the noise holding longest and the rim going first. She did not look into the basket and said nothing of what had been read over it. There was nothing to say. A figure had gone over him twice and taken nothing from him that anyone could name, and the household had not been made poorer or richer by the saying of it.

In \place{the Clay Yard} she went wide around the seven walked-around places with a look and a wide step and no number. The clean dark line at \place{the Vail corner} kept the finer grey hairline beside it that the house calls \lore{the thaw}, and neither had been swept; the space beneath \place{the lowest rack} held its broken birth-slates, entered by nobody; the round ring where the shallow pan had once stood kept its shape with no scrape, no crumb, no print, no chip and no word; \place{the trough} beside \place{the yard well} held its skin from edge to edge; out on \place{the worked strips} the field kept the empty place the planting had not filled, and in the returning line stood the place where a hand had gone in on Seed 15 and had not come out, gone around as wide as a pail of hot water set where a child might knock it. On \place{the flat stone by the well}, set out of the path of feet, the lifted plate of ice stood clouded with grey silt. The stayed frost on the north lip, the skin on the water, the plate on its stone were three coverings in three places, and the yard called all three weather; the boy kept them with a finger between every pair and made no fourth likeness of them and no cause, for a cause wants a payer standing under it and nobody stood.

Frost went out in its last tend without a boundary drawn. The older women named the month by the weather and the slatemen named it by the number of Account-days, and the two habits went on side by side like two hands on one rope; neither was set right and neither was left wrong in so many words, and the month went the way Frost 1 had come, asking nothing of the door it went out by. It had been a month of three readings. Frost's first arithmetic and Frost's second had gone up \place{the clay road} locked in \person{Berr Clay}'s box, and Frost's third had followed them that morning with its true subtraction inside, behind the red cord folded square under the coat and the green of tasted \lore{lead glaze} at one mouth-corner, and now all three rode toward \place{High Kiln} with nobody under any roof able to open the box or read the slate. One in twelve stood printed and unchallenged on the notice at the Cross. The figure read was still not the figure paid, and the space between them had no signature on it, and the arithmetic of the district had changed a third time where two hundred people stood and could not be changed back.

Three readings were three and not one account. The boy kept them on three places with a finger between every pair, and kept the finding on a place of its own with no payer under it. He did not lay it across the house's \lore{not-doings} --- the two unswept lines, the widow's mark untouched, the charcoal unread beneath the shelf's front edge, the fifth pull unsupplied under the floor, the loan uncalled at the kindling, the crust in the fold not lifted two inches to a mouth that wanted it --- and he did not lay it across the dressed grain lying covered under wool at \place{the rack end}, read over three times that month by numbers that did not know what it cost, or across his own kept gaps beside them. The edges had nearly touched that morning under the moot-post's thin shade; the finger had gone between them and stayed there. Three readings and three omissions standing edge to edge were still three readings and three omissions, and a payer invented to make the two agree would have been a finding forged about the yard as surely as one forged about the floor. It was not a lesson. It was not a grievance he could carry up the road and set down at a door, nor a mending, for nothing had been mended, nor a doctrine, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood to any of it. The finding was exact and worth nothing at two inches of air, and he did not call it a virtue and did not spend it. The account had gone up the road and changed nothing under the roof.

\scenebreak

The light went out of the yard the way a pot goes cold when it is lifted off the coals and set on a cold stone, not all at once but by degrees. Heat drew back from the fold. It left his cheek first and his temple next and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool had ridden back last, as though it had come to the end of a line written out for it and had neither an order to go further nor an order to stay. He lay where he had been laid, under the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf}, and took the inventory in the order it presented itself, because that order had never lied to him and no other had promised to. One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from his ear on \place{the bed}, gone past the point where a child would eat it, its empty place beside it keeping its name to itself and no third come in the night to make the row come out even. The thread of drool at his mouth had frozen in the dry heat and was unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread, and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. Cold had stopped at cheek and temple and the small bare joint, and the afterdrop waited beyond all three, past a warming nobody in this house would perform.

He knew the composed face that is not composure. He knew a cold patient cannot clot and will seem better before he is worse, and that a man must be warmed from the outside and slowly, never hurried, never rubbed, never set too near the fire; he had once known it in a white room with a body on a table and knew it now under a roof of wet wool and cooled tallow, and the knowing did not change by one degree the two inches of air between his mouth and the crust. Once he had reduced a shoulder in the dark by feel alone and now he could not close two inches on the rim of the pot set beyond his hand. Both facts stood, and the second was louder, and neither bought the lifting of the crust. The surgeon took the inventory in its old order and entered none of it, not even on the place where he kept the things he did not enter; the older kept the inventory and the younger kept the wanting, and neither had authority over the other.

Through the finger's-width door the river went \lore{water over water}, a low thing over a lower thing, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming. Under the floor \lore{the carried fire} gave four low pulls with the silence where the fifth should have stood; overhead \place{the boards} kept four nails on the near board, four on the next and a thumb-width knot where the fifth nail should have stood. Three counts in three places, and none rose into another, because agreeing any two would have wanted a payer put under both and none stood to any. Six hearings of \lore{the full note} stood beside and never among, five from four sources and the sixth, the small flat knock out of a person as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go and never with the things that came from vessels; the seventh place kept its emptiness and none was invented to fill it, for an empty place kept empty is a finding and a finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged. The heard bell of that morning, four and four and a space, stayed in the square behind them beside the told bell of \lore{the Long Night}, a heard thing beside a told thing, not folded into the three counts under the roof and not heard as a seventh of the full note. Nine stoppings stood with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end; the row stood at five askings with one shaping and no witness; the burned-slate word stood at four, the shut-room tellings at two; the three shapings of the Frost 14 saying stood beside the eighth record-first hand with a finger between every pair, and a strip of notched wood stayed unread in a sleeve not under this roof, and nine must not stand up and call themselves a rule, for a rule is an account that has found a payer and none stood to these.

She came to \place{the hearth} before she came to the basket. That was the grammar of \place{the Vail kiln-house}, and it held at the going of the light as strictly as at first light or at an Account-day: the instruction went to the fire, the wool, the pot, the broom and the running water, and never to the basket. She told the coals it was the thaw when the sour breath came off the unswept corner, turned them with the back of a broken pot, and said \emph{The house will keep it} where another woman would have said \emph{I}. She stood a moment with the back of the pot in her hand counting something under her breath toward no opening --- small intake, hold, letting-go --- and gave neither gap a figure. Her finger stayed on top of the loaf shelf's front edge so the charcoal tally beneath remained unread; her lips moved along a row standing with two gaps and neither gap was entered; her eye went once to the clay jar standing cold under its lead glaze, and her hand shut half over nothing without touching it. She pushed a strand of coal-black hair back with the wrist that carries the burn, and the one grey streak at her temple showed pale off the low light, and she did not look toward the bed.

\lore{The widow's mark} hung where it hung on its cord at her throat, a thumb-length of lead-glazed clay with the Vail kiln scratched into it, and she did not touch it, because the hand goes to the mark before the kiln and at no other hour and a going of the light is not an opening. Four openings in four months were four and not a rule; the next stood in a later month's first firing, far off, not hoped for and not counted toward, to be earned by that morning's own light if the household fired at all. At the rack end she lifted a corner of the wool from the dressed grain to feel for damp and laid it back with a touch lighter than the one before, and no figure came off the grain and no cost was named to it; three numbers had gone over it that month without knowing what it cost, and the space between the figure and the price stayed unsigned. When she set the pot down on \place{the pot board} she set the rim two inches beyond the reaching hand with its handle turned toward it, and there was a half-beat between the setting and her going away, and she did not look toward the bed. The half-beat was not a gift and the handle turned toward the hand was not an instruction to take; a gift wants a taker and he was not given the means to close two inches of air. She put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face and left the thread where it lay. The basket was set down and left, and a basket set down is a pot set down and left.

At \place{the kindling} \person{Tarn} kept six where the house could check and the seventh at the lip where it could not. The small knife lay at the very edge of its sill with the worn thumb-mark almost worn flat, six standing in it and the seventh at the lip beside \person{Garl Vail}'s unforged collector sentence about the man who turned back at the frozen ford, with no name on the man and no figure on the debt. He told the wood \emph{Six counted. The next I don't count} in the tone of a man stating the weather, and the sentence about an ear under this roof being worth more than the debt that held it came to his teeth and went as far as the word that would call the loan before stopping, with no figure and no name. It was a remembered stopping, the ninth, and he did not make a tenth of it before the seventh year at its very end. He drew the breath that should have gone in at the top of the reach and did not spend it, the small interval showing where it should have gone, and the breath from Seed 16 stayed unspent with it. His chest had been tight since the dark.

\emph{Yes, house} he said to those who wanted the thaw early and to those who wanted it late, agreeing with both so thoroughly that no argument could find a foot, and went on wanting the late because it puts off the paying, though the paying had stood in the ruts since the water went. His stroke went on over \place{the flagstones} past the shallow bright patch without the half-check, the stones keeping the place where it had stopped and joined to no cause and to no ear. \lore{Ash} was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line and no scrubbing lifted it, and the pale clip-ring at his left wrist, in place since Y.H. 806, held what light there was and gave it back. Up in \place{the Debt Loft}, which came to the boy only as a sound with a direction, the warm stone in its unmarked pot was cold and unreconciled by not trying, which is not forgiveness. The loan stayed uncalled and no collector came up or went down the clay road on Frost 30, and nobody said the omission had a name.

\person{Jorra Vail} came through the finger's-width-open door of \place{the Vail wheel-shed} with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bend of his little finger and under the nails, not bone-coloured slip. He kept dusty forearms and clean ones on one tray and parted them as a surgeon parts clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had broken nor whether it was a birth-slate. He spoke to the wheel before he spoke to any person --- \emph{It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady} --- and told a pot \emph{You'll do} in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either. He knew the full note that pot would give three turns before it left his hands, with air under it and a want in it, and withheld the knowing on the near-full tray beside the hearings and never among them. Before Sella had finished telling him the fire was low his bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper}, moved the tongue a hair's breadth and left it standing, so that the end of her instruction arrived at the pot and the household straightened around his silence. He did not say the back room was cold and did not look toward the bed or the basket. Men unnamed had gone low into \place{the back room} before first grey, and he gave the house no name for them. The burned-slate word stood at four from \place{Slate Row}, a direction over the boards and not a fifth telling; the two shut-room tellings stood at two and did not become three; three sayings about a pot stood together with the premature Smoke 14 note on its own place and no fourth likeness made a cause. \emph{He's not Vail} stayed sealed as tightly as a lid nobody had tried to lift, and the promise of the foundling's name to the men who burn slates was neither given nor withdrawn. He did not ask about Tarn's ear and Tarn did not ask about the room.

Garl Vail stayed in objects and not in a story. His salt-stiff coat with the crease at the left shoulder kept a broader man's shape on its peg behind the door, and the wheel-seat in the shed kept its twenty-year lean and its hollow on that side, and nobody in the house let it be filled. The clean dark line at the Vail corner kept its finer grey beside it and neither had been swept; the proverb had gone years ago to a pot and the fire and never to a person --- feed the fire before you feed yourself, leave the crack showing, people trust a thing they can see has been handled --- and the debt against a firing of tiles stood neither paid nor settled nor done with, and was worse for being uncalled. His name had not been spoken under this roof in two hundred and ninety-nine days, and no collector had come up the clay road for the debt and none had gone down it. The keeping of him was maintenance and not a story, and no lesson had been made of it.

The small keepings stayed small through the going of the light. \person{Ilva}'s short strip of notched birch lay in her sleeve out at the yard well, unread, unfigured, not taken out for any slate, and no tenth record-first hand was filed to stand beside the ninth and make a rule of nine. The three shapings of the Frost 14 well-saying stood on a place of their own beside \person{Hessa Rale}'s eighth hand with a finger between every pair, three and not a witness, not a cause, not a confession, not a joining and not paid, and no fourth came to stand up and be asked. The runner's slate went up the road unread and no shaping made her a reader, for a witness stands somewhere and can be asked, and nothing of that saying stood anywhere to be asked. The ownerless question about the row stood at five askings with one shaping and no sixth, and no witness stood and no row was named. At the pot board the seventh green was a colour and at the market's edge the seventh shape was a shape, each on a place that did not touch the other, with no eighth of either and no name spoken over either and no joining to loaf, slate, sale, hearing, cause or romance. Under the floor the carried fire kept its fifth unpaid two hundred and ninety-nine days with its payer unnamed, gave four low pulls with the silence where the fifth should have stood, and nobody lifted the floor and nobody supplied the pull.

Outside the wool the hand came up an inch toward the river's note. There, at the very end of the day, a small intake stood whole --- breath from the room whose floor did not touch his, the small intake, the hold, the letting-go, all kept as one object on one note --- and he let it go on the same note without carrying it into the fire's missing fifth, the bell's gap left behind in the square, or the river's low over lower. The hundred and seventy-fourth answer was exact and complete and worth nothing at two inches of air. It was promoted into no reach, no hearing, no seventh, no stopping, no payment, no held change, no key, no power, no freedom, no gift, no doctrine and no rule, and no payer was named to it. The surgeon's clean satisfaction at the exact coordination was checked by the infant's louder want of the crust two inches off at his ear, and the checking was the work and he did not call it a virtue. Nothing bought by the checking could reach two inches. The hand came down on the river's note as it always did and the gap stayed a gap, and he did not fill the gap to make the row come out even.

Two hundred and ninety-nine stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill} and \place{Grath}; six months and one hundred and nineteen days were told by nobody; the fire's fifth was unpaid the same number of days with its payer unnamed; Garl Vail's name had not been spoken under the roof the same number of days. One hundred and seventy-four honest answers stood on the sixth place, each an object and not a power, and neither the number nor the small hand-movings that made them stood up and called themselves a doctrine, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood. Nothing was opened, joined, named, called, paid or ended in the going of the light: no third reading into one account, no figure into a price with a signature under it, no bell's gap into a count, no green into a name, no shape into a story, no boy's kept gap into the house's not-doings, no fourth likeness made a cause and no tenth hand or stopping stood up. No thread was opened and none was closed. The three arithmetics stayed locked in the box up the road, the dressed grain stayed covered and unfigured at the rack end, the three shapings stayed unpaid, the charcoal stayed beneath the shelf, the loan stayed uncalled and the promise stayed ungiven.

Bone stood a direction in the dark beyond the finger's-width door and was not brought into a day that had not earned it. It would come on its own morning, the hunting-and-repair month with a hunter's carrying arm, and the next opening stood in Bone's first firing if the household fired, earned by Bone's own light, five if it fired and not a rule. The boy did not hope for it and did not count toward it; a day that has not been lived has not earned the things that will be read over it, and he had long ago stopped bringing tomorrow's arithmetic into tonight's fold to see what it would weigh.

The hand laid itself back against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. The file lay open on \place{the towel} at the head of the bed with every gap left gap, every tray parted by a finger's width and nothing that had moved. The river's low note went quiet under the finger's-width door, and out on the flat stone by the well the plate of ice stood clouded with silt out of the path of feet, and Frost went out without a boundary drawn.
