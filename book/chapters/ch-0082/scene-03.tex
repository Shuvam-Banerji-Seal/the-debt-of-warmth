\daymark{298}{Frost 29, Ninthday, Y.H. 813}

Frost 29 came on in the small hours under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof the way heat comes into a banked room, by degrees and without a word addressed to anybody. It brought nothing in with it. The day before had stood at a well with a pail against a knee and a strip of wood in a sleeve, and this morning stood under a roof with a fold at a child's ear, and the two did not touch; nothing seen there was carried here as a sign, a lesson, a mending, a witness or a payment. \person[Corin]{The boy} came into his two hundred and ninety-eighth day with nobody under any roof to tell him so. Six months and one hundred and eighteen days were told by nobody in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill} and \place{Grath}; the count had gone from two hundred and ninety-seven to two hundred and ninety-eight the way one loaf pushed along a board gives the next loaf its place and asks nothing of either. Under the floor \lore{the carried fire} kept its fifth unpaid two hundred and ninety-eight days with its payer unnamed, and nobody lifted the floor and nobody supplied the pull. There was no market, no reading, no bell. The morning was an ordinary Ninthday and asked nothing of the child in the fold except that he lie where he had been laid and keep what warmth the house had given him.

The room smelled of hot clay cooled under tallow and wet wool, and under those the sour mineral breath of the unswept corner the house calls \lore{the thaw}. Through the finger's-width door the river went \lore{water over water}, a low thing over a lower thing, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming, and behind the wall the kiln answered in fours with the silence where the fifth should have stood --- a kiln's answer and not a bell, and nobody crossed himself. One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from his ear with its empty place unnamed and no third to make the row come out even. The thread of drool at his mouth had frozen in the dry heat and was unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread, and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. Cold had come to cheek and temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, and stopped there as at the end of a line written out for it, with neither an order to go forward nor an order to go away. The afterdrop waited beyond all three, past a warming nobody in this house would perform.

He knew the composed face that is not composure. He knew a cold patient cannot clot and will seem better before he is worse, and that a man must be warmed from the outside and slowly, never hurried, never rubbed, never set too near the fire; the ease cold puts on a face is not ease, and the surgeon in him read the small bare joint the way he had once read the last clean margin of a wound. He wanted the crust lifted the two inches to his mouth and could not close two inches on the pot handle set beyond his reach. Both were true, and in a body six months and one hundred and eighteen days old the second was louder; the surgeon took the inventory in its old order --- crust, gap, thread, cold, the waiting --- and entered none of it, not even on the place where he kept the things he did not enter. Two men were under the roof and only one of them had a body that could be warmed; the older kept the inventory and the younger kept the wanting, and neither had authority over the other, and the naming bought nothing at two inches of air. He had once reduced a shoulder in the dark by feel alone and could not lift a crust to his mouth; the thought came and went without bitterness, which is its own small betrayal.

The oiled paper at the window took the first grey and gave it back thin, and the thread at his mouth held stiff as wire drawn through tallow and did not soften with the room. Nothing in this house wiped a thread, and no instruction had ever gone out about one; a woman who wiped a child's face would have been doing a thing no slate had asked of her, and the house did only what could be entered. He lay with the cold at the bare joint and the crust at two inches and the grey coming on the paper, and took the three as a surgeon takes three observations that must not be made to agree simply because they stand near one another.

Under \place{the boards} four nails stood on the near board, four on the next, and a thumb-width knot where the fifth should have stood; under the floor the fire gave four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood; at the finger's-width door the river gave water over water with no fifth in it either. Three counts in three places, and none rose into another, because agreeing any two would have wanted a payer put under both and none stood to any. A house that counted its days in loaves, glances and an unnamed space at the row's end could not have been made to agree with a boy who counted his in breath and board and bare joint without putting a man under the sum; there was no man under any of them, and the silence where the fifth should have stood was not a debt anyone had forgotten to call.

The file lay open on \place{the towel} at the head of \place{the bed}, turned out as instruments are turned out on a tray where the next hand will find each in its place. Six hearings of \lore{the full note} stood beside and never among: five from four sources --- a cup across a birth room, a pot in the wheel-shed through a finger's width of open door, the district's slate twice at the reading --- and the sixth, the small flat knock out of a person as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go and never with the things that came from vessels. The seventh place kept its emptiness and none was invented to fill it; an empty place kept empty is a finding and a finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged. The bell's four, four and a gap stood in the square beside the told bell of \lore{the Long Night}, and no bell had come to be lent an ear it had not earned. The told figure stood beside the paid with \person{Berr Clay}'s two arithmetics locked behind and unread in his box up \place{the clay road}, and one in twelve printed and unchallenged and the figure read still not the figure paid; the space between figure and price had no signature, and the boy did not put one there.

Three shapings of the Frost 14 well-saying stood beside \person{Hessa Rale}'s eighth record-first hand with a finger between every pair, not a witness, not a cause, not a confession and not paid; a witness stands somewhere and can be asked, and nothing of that saying stood anywhere to be asked. Nine record-first hands stood with \person{Ilva}'s ninth strip unread in her sleeve and no tenth filed, and nine must not stand up and call themselves a rule, for a rule is an account that has found a payer and none stood to these. The row question stood at five askings with one shaping and no witness; the burned-slate word stood at four from \place{Slate Row}, a direction over the boards and not a fifth telling; the two tellings of the counting room shut in the war stood at two and had not become three; nine stoppings stood with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end. The seventh green stayed a colour and the seventh shape a shape with no eighth. Three held changes --- the fraction at the damper, the beat beneath the shelf, the returned beat at the fire --- lay on three places and had not been laid together; a fourth likeness would have wanted a payer and none stood. \person{Garl Vail} stayed in objects --- the salt-stiff coat on its peg behind the door, the hollow wheel-seat, the clean dark line with its finer grey beside it, the proverb gone to the pot and the fire --- and his name had not been spoken under this roof in two hundred and ninety-eight days, and the keeping of him was maintenance and not a story.

\person{Sella Vail} came to the fire before she came to the basket. That was the grammar of the house and it held on an ordinary morning as strictly as on an Account-day: the instruction went to the fire, the wool, the pot, the broom and the running water, and never to the basket; a basket set down is a pot set down and left. She told the coals it was the thaw and turned them with the back of a broken pot, and said \emph{The house will keep it} where another woman would have said \emph{I}. She stood a moment with the back of the pot in her hand counting something under her breath toward no opening --- small intake, hold, letting-go --- and gave neither gap a figure, and her finger stayed on top of \place{the loaf shelf}'s front edge so the charcoal tally underneath remained unread. Her lips moved along a row standing with two gaps and neither gap was entered; her eye went once to the clay jar standing cold under its \lore{lead glaze} and her hand shut half over nothing without touching it. She pushed a strand of coal-black hair back with the wrist that carries the burn, and the one grey streak at her temple showed pale off the low light, and she did not look toward the bed.

\lore{The widow's mark} hung where it hung on its cord at her throat, a thumb-length of lead-glazed clay with the Vail kiln scratched into it, and she did not touch it, because the hand goes to the mark before the kiln and at no other hour and a morning is not an opening. Four openings in four months were four and not a rule; the next opening stood in a later month's first firing, far off, not hoped for and not counted toward. At \place{the rack end} she lifted a corner of the wool from the dressed grain to feel for damp and laid it back with a touch lighter than the one before, and no figure came off the grain and no cost was named to it; the arithmetic standing a morning off would read over a thing whose price it did not know, and the space between the figure and what the grain had cost would stay unsigned. On the well lip the stayed frost, in the trough its skin of ice, and on the flat stone the plate clouded with silt were three coverings in three places called weather, and weather was entered nowhere, and no fourth likeness was made a cause.

\scenebreak

Outside the wool the hand went up an inch toward the room whose floor does not touch his. Under the floor the carried fire gave its fourth low pull and then the silence where the fifth should have stood, and the boy held a small intake against that fourth the way a surgeon holds pressure in a cavity while he looks for the vessel that is bleeding. He did not carry the intake into the missing fifth. He did not supply a beat under the floor to make the row come out even, for a fifth supplied to please a total is a finding forged and a payer invented to stand under it. He did not cross the room's letting-go with the river's low note, did not borrow a house count, did not promote the held breath into a reach, a hearing, a seventh, a stopping, a payment, a held change, a key, a power, a freedom, a gift, a doctrine or a rule. The letting-go went on belonging to the woman who let it go, and was not crossed or borrowed; it was her breath and her count and not his.

The hundred and seventy-first answer was filed exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air. It was a small intake held against the kiln's fourth low pull and let go whole on the same note, and it bought nothing he could spend and nothing anybody could have spoken. The clean satisfaction the surgeon felt at the exact coordination was checked by the infant's plain want of the crust two inches off, and the checking was the work and he did not call it a virtue; nothing bought by the checking could reach two inches of air. He had once spent a life finding the one error that makes a set of numbers agree, and here the finding was to leave them as they stood.

Frost 30 stood one morning off. It did not come into this morning and was not made a sign of it; a day that has not been lived has not earned the things that will be read over it, and the boy had long ago stopped bringing tomorrow's arithmetic into today's fold to see what it would weigh. \place{The Cross}, \place{the reading block under the moot-post}, \place{the pot board} and the out-of-line landing stood where they stood, a morning away and not anticipated into a room that had earned only its own quiet. He counted the days because there was nothing else under the roof to count that would stay counted --- the loaves went, the glances went, the line at the corner did not grow into a number --- and the count bought him nothing he could hold and nothing he could have told anybody, for there was nobody under the roof who would have known what the number counted. He had learned that a number spoken by somebody who knew what it counted was a different thing from a number kept in a child's head; one was an account and the other was a habit, and the habit had no payer and no ear.

The hand laid itself back against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. One hundred and seventy-one honest answers stood on the sixth place, each an object and not a power, exact, complete and worth nothing. Two hundred and ninety-eight stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in Low Kiln, Sill and Grath; six months and one hundred and eighteen days were told by nobody; the fire's fifth was unpaid the same number of days with its payer unnamed. Nothing was opened, joined, named, called, paid or ended in this morning; no thread opened to a hearing, no hearing to a seventh, no gap to a figure, no figure to a price with a signature under it, no holding to a freedom and no count to a payer. The file lay open on the towel at the head of the bed with every gap left gap, every tray parted by a finger's width and nothing that had moved. The river's low note went quiet under the finger's-width door after the hand came down. The crust lay two inches off with its empty place unnamed and no third, and under the floor the fire stayed short of its fifth pull.
