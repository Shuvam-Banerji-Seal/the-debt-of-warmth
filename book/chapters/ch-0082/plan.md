## The pull into Chapter 83

The final moment leaves the boy awake and silent under the Vail roof with the 174th answer put away, the count at 299 exact, the fire's fifth still unpaid, and Frost going out without a boundary drawn. Nothing was joined, named, opened, called, paid or ended, so the forward motion is carried by what has **not** been spent: the third arithmetic gone up the clay road in Berr's locked box with its true subtraction unread, the dressed grain still covered and unfigured at the rack end, the three shapings of the well-saying still waiting for the runner's far-off choosing, Ilva's stick still in her sleeve, the charcoal still under the shelf, the loan still uncalled with Tarn's seventh year at its very end, Jorra's promise still ungiven, and Garl's name unspoken 299 days. Bone is a direction in the dark beyond the finger's-width door — the hunting-and-repair month, a hunter's carrying arm, a first firing that must be earned by Bone's own morning — arriving on its own day rather than being brought into this one. Chapter 83 opens in the small hours of Day 300, Bone 1, Firstday, with the hand still on the 174th answer and the count still at 299, and lets Bone come on without a boundary drawn in the narration.
