\daymark{297}{Frost 28, Eighthday, Y.H. 813}

\dropcap{T}{he hand} had been laid down against the fold at the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf} as a man puts a tool where the next hand will find it without looking, and it lay there still in the small hours under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof while the twenty-eighth of Frost came on the way cold comes into a banked house, taking the room one board at a time and asking nothing of the child in it. \person[Corin]{The boy} came into his two hundred and ninety-seventh day with nobody under any roof to tell him so. Six months and one hundred and seventeen days were told by nobody in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill} and \place{Grath}; the count had gone from two hundred and ninety-six to two hundred and ninety-seven the way one board laid beside another gives a dry knock and asks nothing of the seam. A hundred and sixty-eight honest answers stood behind him, and neither the number nor the small hand-movings that made them stood up and called themselves a doctrine, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood. Under the floor \lore{the carried fire} kept its fifth unpaid two hundred and ninety-seven days with its payer unnamed, and nobody lifted the floor and nobody supplied the pull. Frost 28 had come on its own ordinary Eighthday, with no market, no reading at \place{the Cross} and no bell, and it asked nothing of the boy under the fold except that he lie there and be warm where he could.

The room had its morning smell before it had its morning light: wet wool lifting from the fold, tallow gone cold in its dish, a thread of clay dust in the crease of the bedding, and under all of it the sour mineral breath of the unswept corner the house calls \lore{the thaw}. Through the finger's-width door the river kept its low note of \lore{water over water}, a thing over a thing, and behind the wall the kiln breathed in fours with a gap where the fifth should have stood. First light came in as a narrow bar through the door and did not move; it found the dust in the air and then the rim of the pot on its board, and went no further into the room than the fold. The boy lay at the angle where the shelf becomes a roof and took what came as it came, in the order the facts arrived, because that order had never lied to him and no other had promised to. Counting was an old habit and not a gift; he had counted the exits of rooms before he had ever counted the nails of a floor, and nobody here had asked him to count anything.

He took the body first. One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from his ear on \place{the bed}, gone past the point where a child would eat it, and the empty place beside it kept its name to itself; no third had come in the night to make the row come out even. The thread of drool at his mouth had frozen in the dry heat and was unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread, and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. Cold had come to cheek and temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, and stopped there as at the end of a line written out for it, with neither an order to go forward nor an order to go away. The afterdrop waited beyond all three, past a warming nobody in this house would perform.

He knew the composed face that is not composure. He knew that a cold patient cannot clot and will seem better before he is worse, and that the ease a man sees on a face at that stage is not ease, and that a man must be warmed from the outside and slowly, never hurried, never rubbed, never set too near the fire. He wanted the crust lifted the two inches to his mouth. Both were true, and in a body six months and one hundred and seventeen days old the second was louder; the surgeon took the inventory in its old order --- crust, gap, thread, cold, the waiting --- and entered none of it, not even on the place where he kept the things he did not enter. The dry heat had drawn the water out of the thread and left it stiff as wire drawn through tallow; he could not lift a hand to wipe it, and no instruction had ever been given to anybody to wipe it, and the two facts stood side by side without either becoming a complaint.

There were two men under the roof and only one of them had a body that could be warmed. The older kept the inventory and the younger kept the wanting, and neither had authority over the other; the older could name the afterdrop and the younger could only be cold, and the naming did not warm him. He had once reduced a shoulder in the dark by feel alone and now could not close two inches on the pot handle set beyond his reach; the thought came and went without bitterness, which was its own small betrayal. He had signed a chart once without reading the note in the margin and carried that shape into a country where notes are turned face-down toward floorboards; here he read every note he was not given and entered none of them. It was an old habit, this standing ready with a fact nobody would ask for, and he kept it the way a house keeps its charcoal under a finger and does not look. A man who has spent his life putting things right in rooms where somebody always called for him finds it a strange discipline to lie under a shelf and call nothing. He had been learning it since his first morning under this roof and it had not got easier.

\scenebreak

Three counts stood in three places and none rose into another. Under the floor the carried fire gave four low pulls with the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer without a name for two hundred and ninety-seven days; at the finger's-width door the river went water over water, a low thing over a lower thing; overhead \place{the boards} kept four nails on the near board, four on the next and a thumb-width knot where the fifth nail should have stood. Agreeing any two would have wanted a payer put under both and none stood to any, so the three stayed where they were, exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air. The knot had the width of a thumb and was not a nail; a row made even by calling it one would have been a row made even by a lie.

In the small hours he turned the file out on \place{the towel} as a surgeon turns instruments out on a tray, each in its place, none of them a cure. Six hearings of \lore{the full note} stood beside and never among, five from four sources and the sixth, the small flat knock out of \person[Dessa Silt] as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go and never with the things that came from vessels; the seventh place kept its emptiness and none was invented to fill it, for an empty place kept empty is a finding and a finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged. The bell's four, four and a gap, heard at the market on Frost 20 and held since, stayed in its square beside the told bell of \lore{the Long Night}; no bell had come on this morning and he would not lend an ear to one that had not earned it. The heard and the told lay side by side with a finger between, two sayings of one sound and not one hearing borrowed to stand under the other.

The told figure stood beside the paid and the space between them had no signature. Frost's first arithmetic and Frost's second rode locked up \place{the clay road} in \person[Berr Clay]'s box toward \place{High Kiln}, and the printed one in twelve stood unchallenged, and the figure read was still not the figure paid; two readings were two sayings and not one account, and neither had closed the hand that went in on Seed 15 or the man carried out on Day 188. The dressed grain lay covered under wool away from \place{the hearth} at \place{the rack end}, turned with a progressively lighter touch and given no figure, so that any number travelling over it would not know what it cost. A figure that does not know what a thing cost can pass over it twice and go away clean; the boy had seen a man do that with a chart and a good reason, and the good reason had not made the number true.

The Frost 14 saying stood on a place of its own with its first, second and third shapings, a finger between every pair, beside \person[Hessa Rale]'s eighth record-first hand with a finger between that and the nearest shaping; not a witness, not a cause, not a confession, not a joining and not paid, for a witness stands somewhere and can be asked and none of the three stood anywhere to be asked. \person{Ilva}'s ninth hand stayed nine with the notched birch strip unread in her sleeve and no figure coming off the stick, and he filed no tenth hand; the count stayed in wood where no slate could take a figure off it. The row question stood at five askings with one shaping and no witness; the burned-slate word stood at four, a direction over the boards and not a fifth telling; the shut-room tellings stood at two and had not become three; three pot sayings stood together with the Smoke 14 note on its own place and no fourth likeness made a cause; nine stoppings stood with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end. The seventh green stood a colour and the seventh shape a shape, and no eighth came to either. Nine hands were nine and not a rule, and nine stoppings were nine and not a payment; a tenth supplied to make either stand up as a doctrine would have wanted a payer and none stood.

\person{Garl Vail} stayed in objects and not in a story. His salt-stiff coat with the crease at the left shoulder kept a broader man's shape on its peg behind the door, and the wheel-seat kept its twenty-year lean and its hollow on that side, and nobody in the house let it be filled. The clean dark line at the corner kept its finer grey beside it, unswept; the proverb had gone years ago to a pot and the fire and never to a person --- feed the fire before you feed yourself, leave the crack showing, people trust a thing they can see has been handled --- and the debt against a firing of tiles stood neither paid nor settled nor done with, and was worse for being uncalled. His name had not been spoken under this roof in two hundred and ninety-seven days. \person{Tarn}'s loan stayed uncalled and no collector came up or down the clay road; \person{Jorra Vail}'s promise stood ungiven and \emph{he's not Vail} stayed sealed; from \place{the Debt Loft}, which came to the boy only as a sound with a direction, the warm stone in its unmarked pot was cold and unreconciled by not trying, which is not forgiveness. The house's keepings by \lore{not-doings} stayed on a place apart from the boy's own gaps with a finger between them, for want of a payer under either. Nothing stood up and called itself a rule, and the keeping of a man by omission was maintenance and not a story.

\scenebreak

\person{Sella Vail} went to the fire before the basket, as she had gone every morning of her life in this house, and gave the instruction to the fire and never to the basket. She told it the thaw and turned the coals with the back of a broken pot, and said \emph{The house will keep it} where another woman would have said \emph{I}. The small hours had laid a film of grey over everything, and she moved through it with her hands already knowing what they had to do: the wool told to keep the river wind off his ears, the pot told its word, the broom told to go around, and never a word spoken to the child in the fold who could not have answered. She carried no number into the room and took none out; what she counted she counted with her lips and kept where nobody was shown. When she came to the loaf shelf her finger stayed on top of the front edge so the charcoal tally beneath was unread, and her lips moved along a row standing with two gaps and gave neither gap a figure; she glanced once at the clay jar standing cold under its \lore{lead glaze} and shut her hand half over nothing. \lore{The widow's mark} hung where it hung on its cord at her throat, a thumb-length of lead-glazed clay with the Vail kiln scratched into it, and she did not touch it, because a morning is not an opening and the hand goes to the mark before the kiln and at no other hour. She pushed a strand of coal-black hair back with the wrist that carries the burn, the little door of it closing on nothing, and the one grey streak at her temple showed pale off the coals, and she did not look toward the bed.

At the rack end she lifted a corner of the wool over the dressed grain to feel for damp and laid it back with a touch lighter than the one before, and no figure came off the grain and no cost was named to it. The wool went down again over the grain the way a fold goes down over a face that is not to be looked at. Out in \place{the worked strips} the field kept an empty place the planting had not filled; beneath \place{the lowest rack} the broken birth-slates lay where the yard had put them; on the stone lip of \place{the yard well} the round ring where the shallow pan had stood kept no scrape, no crumb, no print and no word to say what had been set there; \place{the trough} beside it had a skin over its water from edge to edge; and on \place{the flat stone by the well}, set out of the path of feet, the lifted plate of ice stood clouded with silt. The stayed frost, the skinned water and the clouded plate were three coverings in three places, and the boy kept them with a finger between every pair. He made no fourth likeness of them and no cause; a cause wants a payer standing under it and nobody stood. The three were weather, entered nowhere, and no figure came off any of them. She did not bring any of it under the roof as a lesson or a mending, and the boy did not make a sign of any of it.

When she set the pot down on \place{the pot board} she set the rim two inches beyond the reaching hand with its handle turned toward it, and there was a half-beat between the setting and her going away, and she did not look toward the bed. The half-beat was not a gift and the handle turned toward the hand was not an instruction to take; a gift wants a taker and he was not given the means to close two inches of air. The boy watched the going of her burn-wrist the way he had learned to watch a surgeon's hand leave a field --- for what it did not touch --- and filed the not-touching on its own place, maintenance and not a message. Instructions went to the fire, the wool, the pot, the broom and the running water; the basket was set down and left, and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. He wanted the thread wiped and the crust lifted and a number spoken by somebody who would have known what a number counted, and he got none of the three and could not ask for any of them. There was no bitterness in the not-asking either, which was the second small betrayal of the morning, and he did not give it a place to stand.

\scenebreak

Outside the wool the hand went up an inch toward the next room. At the end of the morning a small intake came from the room whose floor does not touch his --- the breath of a woman counting what she will not say aloud --- and he took it at its top and let it go on the same note, whole and unjoined. He did not carry it into the carried fire's missing fifth, did not cross the room's letting-go with the river's low note of water over water, did not borrow a house count, did not supply a beat under the floor to make the row come out even, for a fifth supplied to please a total is a finding forged and a payer invented to stand under it. The letting-go went on belonging to the woman who let it go and was not crossed or borrowed; it was her breath and her count and not his. The hand came down on the river's note as it always did, and the gap stayed a gap.

The hundred and sixty-ninth answer was filed exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air. It was not promoted into a reach, a hearing, a seventh, a stopping, a payment, a held change, a key, a power, a freedom, a gift, a doctrine or a rule. The clean satisfaction the surgeon felt at the exact coordination was checked by the infant's plain want of the crust two inches off, and the checking was the work and he did not call it a virtue; nothing bought by the checking could reach two inches of air. He had once spent a life finding the one error that makes a set of numbers agree, and here the finding was to leave them as they stood.

The hand laid itself back against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. One hundred and sixty-nine honest answers stood on the sixth place, each an object and not a power, exact, complete and worth nothing. Two hundred and ninety-seven stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in Low Kiln, Sill and Grath; six months and one hundred and seventeen days were told by nobody; the fire's fifth was unpaid the same number of days with its payer unnamed. Frost 30 stood two mornings off and was not brought into a morning that had not earned it or made a sign of anything. Nothing was opened, joined, named, called, paid or ended; no thread was opened and none was closed. The file lay open on the towel at the head of the bed with every gap left gap, every tray parted by a finger's width and nothing that had moved. The river's low note went quiet under the finger's-width door after the hand came down. The crust lay two inches off with its empty place unnamed, and nothing that had been counted under the roof had bought the lifting of it.
