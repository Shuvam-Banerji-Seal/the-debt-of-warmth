The afternoon thinned without ending. It went out of the room the way heat goes out of a pot lifted off the coals and set down on a cold stone, not all at once but by degrees, the belly of it holding longest and the rim going first, until the stone had taken what the pot had held and neither of them was the richer for the exchange. Heat drew back from the fold. It left \person[Corin]{the boy}'s cheek first and his temple next and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool had ridden back last, as though it had come to the end of a line written out for it and had neither an order to go further nor an order to stay. He lay where he had been laid, under the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf}, and took the inventory in its old order because there was nothing else to take and the order itself was a kind of instrument he had learned to trust.

One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from his ear, gone past the point where a child would eat it, its empty place beside it keeping its name to itself and no third come in to make the row come out even. The thread of drool at his mouth had frozen in the dry heat and was unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread, and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. Cold had stopped at cheek and temple and the bare joint. The afterdrop waited beyond all three, past a warming nobody in this house would perform.

He knew the composed face that is not composure. He knew a cold patient cannot clot and will seem better before he is worse, and that a man must be warmed from the outside and slowly, never hurried, never rubbed, never set too near the fire; the ease cold puts on a face is not ease. He wanted the crust lifted the two inches to his mouth and could not close two inches on the pot handle set beyond his reach. Both were true, and in a body six months and one hundred and eighteen days old the second was louder. The surgeon took the inventory in its old order --- crust, gap, thread, cold, the waiting --- and entered none of it, not even on the place where he kept the things he did not enter. The older kept the inventory and the younger kept the wanting, and neither had authority over the other, and the naming bought nothing at two inches of air.

Through the finger's-width door the river went \lore{water over water}, a low thing over a lower thing, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming. Under the floor \lore{the carried fire} gave four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer unnamed two hundred and ninety-eight days and nobody lifting the floor or supplying the pull. Above, \place{the boards} kept their four nails, four and a thumb-width knot. Three counts in three places, and none rose into another, because agreeing any two would have wanted a payer put under both and none stood to any. He did not make them agree. A finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged, and a payer invented to stand under a silence is no payer at all.

\person{Sella Vail} came to the basket only after she had come to the fire. That was the grammar of \place{the Vail kiln-house} and it held in the thinning afternoon as strictly as at first light: the instruction went to the fire, the wool, the pot, the broom and the running water, and never to the basket. She told the coals it was \lore{the thaw} and turned them with the back of a broken pot, and said \emph{The house will keep it} where another woman would have said \emph{I}. She stood a moment with the back of the pot in her hand counting something under her breath toward no opening --- small intake, hold, letting-go --- and gave neither gap a figure, and her finger stayed on top of the shelf's front edge so the charcoal tally underneath remained unread. Her lips moved along a row standing with two gaps and neither gap was entered; her eye went once to the clay jar standing cold under its \lore{lead glaze} and her hand shut half over nothing without touching it. She pushed a strand of coal-black hair back with the wrist that carries the burn, and the one grey streak at her temple showed pale off the low light, and she did not look toward \place{the bed}.

\lore{The widow's mark} hung where it hung on its cord at her throat, a thumb-length of lead-glazed clay with the Vail kiln scratched into it, and she did not touch it, because the hand goes to the mark before the kiln and at no other hour. Four openings in four months were four and not a rule; the next opening stood in a later month's first firing, far off, not hoped for and not counted toward. Then she lifted him, the little-door burn closing first on nothing and then on his weight, and carried him down at the half-pace from every open slate, out into \place{the Clay Yard}.

The yard had the look of a room after a sick person has been carried out of it: everything in its place and the places gone around. She went wide around the seven walked-around places with a look and a wide step and no number. The clean dark line where \lore{Ash} had turned its corner lay along the Vail wall with the finer grey hairline beside it that the house calls the thaw, unswept both. The space beneath \place{the lowest rack} held its broken birth-slates. The round ring where the shallow pan had once stood kept its shape on the clay. At the well \place{the trough} had skinned over. Out on \place{the worked strips} the field kept its empty place, and in the returning line stood the place where a hand had gone in on Seed 15 and had not come out, gone around as wide as a pail of hot water set where a child might knock it and entered by nobody. She gave each a look and a wide step and no number, and the boy kept the order of the going-around as he kept all orders, by what was there and by what was not.

At \place{the yard well} three coverings stood in three places. Stayed white frost sat in fronds on the north lip under the bucket, grey now where the rope had knocked it and not gone with the sun. The water in the trough had a skin drawn edge to edge. On \place{the flat stone by the well}, set out of the path of feet, the lifted plate of ice stood clouded with grey silt. Three coverings in three places called weather, and weather was entered nowhere, and no fourth likeness was made a cause and no payer named to any. The boy kept a finger between them the way he kept a finger between every pair that wanted to become a row. A likeness is not a cause because it is near, and three near things standing together are three things until a man stands under them and is asked to pay.

At \place{the rack end} she lifted a corner of the wool from the dressed grain to feel for damp and laid it back with a touch lighter than the one before, and no figure came off the grain and no cost was named to it. The arithmetic standing one morning off would read over a thing whose price it did not know, and the space between the figure and what the grain had cost would stay unsigned. He watched the touch lighten and kept that too, the progressively lighter hand and the thing under it given no figure, because a number spoken over grain that does not know what it cost is a figure with no signature under it.

At \place{the kindling} \person{Tarn} kept six where the house could check and the seventh at the lip where it could not. The small knife lay at the very edge of its sill with the worn thumb-mark worn almost flat, six standing in it and the seventh at the very lip beside \person{Garl Vail}'s unforged collector sentence about the man who turned back at the frozen ford, with no name on the man and no figure on the debt. He told the wood \emph{Six counted. The next I don't count} in the tone of a man stating the weather, and the sentence about an ear under this roof being worth more than the debt that held it came to his teeth and went as far as the word that would call the loan before stopping, with no figure and no name. It was a remembered stopping, the ninth, and he did not make a tenth of it before the seventh year at its very end. He drew the breath that should have gone in at the top of the reach and did not spend it, the small interval showing where it should have gone, and the breath from Seed 16 stayed unspent with it. His chest had been tight since the dark.

\emph{Yes, house} he said to those who wanted the thaw early and those who wanted it late, agreeing with both so thoroughly that no argument could find a foot, and went on wanting the late because it puts off the paying, though the paying had stood in the ruts since the water went. His stroke went on over \place{the flagstones} past the shallow bright patch without the half-check, the stones keeping the place where it had stopped and joined to no cause and to no ear. Ash was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line and no scrubbing lifted it, and the pale clip-ring at his left wrist, in place since Y.H. 806, held what light there was and gave it back. Up in \place{the Debt Loft}, which came to the boy only as a sound with a direction, the warm stone in its unmarked pot was cold and unreconciled by not trying, which is not forgiveness. The loan stayed uncalled and no collector came up or down \place{the clay road}, and nobody said the omission had a name.

\person{Jorra Vail} came through the finger's-width-open door of \place{the Vail wheel-shed} with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bend of his little finger and under the nails, not bone-coloured slip. He kept dusty forearms and clean ones on one tray and parted them as a surgeon parts clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had been broken nor whether it was a birth-slate; the broken birth-slates stayed under the lowest rack and were entered by nobody. Three or more men had gone low into \place{the back room} before first grey and he gave the house no name for them and did not say the room was cold. To the boy it was a direction and a sound, and no more a room than the loft was.

He spoke to the wheel before he spoke to any person. \emph{It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady}, he told it, and told a pot \emph{You'll do} in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either. He knew the \lore{full note} that pot would give three turns before it left his hands, with air under it and a want in it, and withheld the knowing on the near-full tray beside the hearings and never among them, advanced and not paid. The burned-slate word stood at four from \place{Slate Row}, a direction over the boards and not a fifth telling; the two tellings of the counting room shut in the war stood at two and had not become three; three sayings about a pot stood together with the premature Smoke 14 note on its own place and no fourth likeness made a cause; the row question stood at five askings with one shaping and no witness. \emph{He's not Vail} stayed sealed as tightly as a lid nobody had tried to lift in three years, and the promise of the foundling's name to the men who burn slates was neither given nor withdrawn.

Before Sella had finished telling him the fire was low his bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper}, ahead of her unfinished sentence, moved the tongue a hair's breadth and left it standing, so that the end of her instruction arrived at the pot and the household straightened around his silence the way cloth straightens when a weight is lifted. The boy read one iron with two bites --- the holding and the taking --- with no \lore{Grathic} word for both mercies, joined to no kiln and entered in no slate. He did not look toward the bed or into the basket, and did not ask about the ear, and Tarn did not ask about the room; the two silences kept their old shape under one roof with no word between them.

Back under the roof, with the going of the light coming on, the boy kept the house's \lore{not-doings} the way he kept everything else that would not stay kept by anybody else. He kept the two lines at the corner unswept, the old clean dark one and the finer grey beside it; he kept the mark untouched at a shelf, a yard, a rack, a road, a basket, an afternoon, a going of the light; he kept the charcoal beneath the shelf's front edge unread; he kept the fifth pull of the carried fire unsupplied under the floor; he kept the loan uncalled at the kindling and no collector on the road; he kept no number spoken over the basket and the crust in the fold not lifted two inches to a mouth that wanted it. He kept these as a man keeps a set of instruments laid out on a towel, each in its place, with nothing promoted out of its place into a reach, a hearing, a seventh, a stopping, a payment, a held change, a key, a power, a freedom, a gift, a doctrine or a rule. The house kept itself by not-doing, and the keeping was maintenance and not a story, and no payer stood under any of it.

He very nearly laid the house's omissions across his own empty intervals. The two lines unswept wanted to lie across the gap at his ear; the uncalled loan wanted to lie across the unspent breath; the unsupplied fifth pull wanted to lie across the empty seventh place of the hearings; the mark untouched at every hour wanted to lie across the nine stoppings with no tenth. They lay that way in the mind for a breath, edge to edge, and the edges very nearly touched. Then the finger went between them and stayed there. A joining would have wanted a payer put under both, and none stood under the house's not-doings and none stood under the boy's kept gaps, and a payer invented to make the two agree would have been a finding forged about himself as surely as one invented about the floor. He did not make the near-joining into a lesson, a grievance, a doctrine, a mending or a cause. The absence of the joining was itself kept and not spent, and it went on its own place with a finger between it and everything it might have touched.

Three shapings of the Frost 14 well-saying stayed three and not a witness. They stood beside \person{Hessa Rale}'s eighth record-first hand with a finger between every pair, not a cause, not a confession, not a payment and not joined to her slate; a witness stands somewhere and can be asked, and nothing of that saying stood anywhere to be asked. Nine record-first hands stayed nine with \person{Ilva}'s strip unread in her sleeve and no tenth filed, and nine must not stand up and call themselves a rule, for a rule is an account that has found a payer and none stood to these; the strip stayed in wood where no slate could take a figure off it, and she was offstage and did not know she had been read. The row question stayed at five askings with one shaping and no witness and no row named, and no sixth asking came to make the fifth into a witness. Six hearings of the full note stood beside and never among, five from four sources and the sixth, the small flat knock out of a person as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go; the seventh place kept its emptiness and none was invented to fill it. The heard bell's four, four and a gap stood in the square beside the told bell of \lore{the Long Night} and was not folded into the three counts under the roof or heard as a seventh. The told figure stood beside the paid with \person{Berr Clay}'s two arithmetics locked behind and unread in his box up the clay road, one in twelve printed and unchallenged, the figure read still not the figure paid, and the space between figure and price without a signature.

From the loft overhead came a small dry scrape --- the foot of a pot set down on stone, a sound with a direction and no room, no hand at a slate and no word spoken over it. He filed it on a place of its own. He did not join it to the fraction at the damper where Jorra's finger had waited, or to the returned beat beneath the shelf's front edge, or to the half-check the flagstones kept where Tarn's stroke had stopped. Four small movements lay on four places with a finger between every pair, and a fourth likeness would have wanted a payer and none stood, so the scrape stayed a scrape and the loft stayed a direction and a sound. The finding was exact and worth nothing at two inches of air, and he did not call it a virtue and did not spend it.

Outside the wool the hand went up an inch toward the room whose floor does not touch his. At the very end of the day a woman's low counting came through --- small intake, hold, letting-go --- and he took only what the hand could take and left the letting-go where it was; it was her breath and her count and not his. He held the small intake whole on the same note and let it go whole, and carried it into neither the carried fire's missing fifth nor the river's low note, borrowed no house count, supplied no pull, and promoted the held breath into no reach, hearing, seventh, stopping, payment, held change, key, power, freedom, gift, doctrine or rule.

The hundred and seventy-second answer was filed exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air. The clean satisfaction the surgeon felt at the exact coordination was checked by the infant's plain want of the crust two inches off, and the checking was the work and he did not call it a virtue; nothing bought by the checking could reach two inches. Two hundred and ninety-eight stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill} and \place{Grath}; six months and one hundred and eighteen days were told by nobody; the fire's fifth was unpaid the same number of days with its payer unnamed; \person{Garl Vail}'s name had not been spoken under this roof two hundred and ninety-eight days. Nothing was opened, joined, named, called, paid or ended in the afternoon; no thread opened to a hearing, no hearing to a seventh, no gap to a figure, no figure to a price with a signature under it, no house's not-doing to a boy's kept interval, no holding to a freedom and no count to a payer.

Frost 30 stood one morning off. It did not come into this afternoon and was not made a sign of it; a day that has not been lived has not earned the things that will be read over it, and \place{the Cross} and the bell and the box stood where they stood, a morning away and not brought into a room that had earned only its own quiet. The hand came down on the river's low note as it always does and the hand laid itself back against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. The file lay open on \place{the towel} at the head of the bed with every gap left gap, every tray parted by a finger's width and nothing that had moved. The river's low note went quiet under the finger's-width door, and out on the flat stone by the well the plate of ice stood clouded with silt out of the path of feet, and the count closed at two hundred and ninety-eight exact.
