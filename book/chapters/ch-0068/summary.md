## Chronicle

For three days the house at Low Kiln kept its grammar and the boy kept his count, from two hundred and fifty-five days to two hundred and fifty-seven, and nobody said so. The carried fire answered four pulls under the floor and kept the silence where the fifth should stand; the river and the boards' four nails, four and a knot, kept their own counts beside it. Three counts in three places; he would not agree any two, for agreeing them would want a payer and none stood to any. No market, no Cross and no bell; each day earned its own Smoke by its own light.

He inventoried his body as a surgeon inventories a wound: one crust two inches from his ear, its gap unnamed and no third; the thread frozen; cold at cheek, temple and wrist; the afterdrop waiting past a warming nobody would perform. He knew the face of a man going cold and wanted the crust. Sella Vail went to the fire before the basket, told it the thaw, turned the coals, said *The house will keep it*, kept her finger above the charcoal, and set the pot handle two inches beyond his reach with no look toward the bed. The widow's mark hung where it hung, untouched since before the kiln on Smoke 1.

She turned the dressed grain under wool and gathered it by the handful; on the seventeenth morning she gathered it in for the last time before Smoke's second reading and set it under wool away from the hearth. It gained no figure, and the arithmetic to be read over it would not know what it cost. Ilva kept her notched strip in her sleeve, answering *I keep the count, not the account.* Frost wrote white on the well lip; the bucket knocked it grey, and the yard called it weather. Tarn told the kindling *Six counted. The next I don't count*, set six where the house could check and the seventh at the lip, and stopped the ear-sentence at the calling word. Jorra told a pot *You'll do*, withheld its note three turns ahead, and held his bent finger in the damper ahead of Sella's unfinished sentence.

Six times he questioned the hand and six times it answered: down at once on the river's note, a borrowed count and its silence carried across a wall, held to the river's own interval, held for the small intake, one rhythm carried over a borrowed silence, and an empty interval kept as an object, not filled to make a row come out even. Answers eighty-five through ninety stood on the sixth place, each an object and not a power. He turned the file out on the towel and found it exact, complete and worth nothing. Two hundred and fifty-seven stood exact, unspent and spoken by nobody, and the count went forward into Smoke 19, a day that had not earned its heading.
