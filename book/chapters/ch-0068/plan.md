## How the final moment pulls into Chapter 69

The close leaves the boy on his bed under the Vail roof with the hand laid against the fold on the ninetieth answer, the count exact at two hundred and fifty-seven, the carried fire stopped at four with its fifth unpaid and its payer unnamed, and the file open on the towel with every tray parted. Smoke 19 (Day 258) is one morning beyond the close and has not earned its heading; Smoke 20 (Day 259), the second Account-day and market of the second firing, stands two mornings beyond and was not reached for. The dressed grain is covered and unfigured under wool, so the arithmetic that will be read over it on Smoke 20 will not know what it cost; Smoke's first arithmetic stays locked in Berr's box with the true subtraction inside, the figure read still not the figure paid. Chapter 69 therefore opens in the small hours of Day 258 with the hand laid down on the ninetieth answer and the count at two hundred and fifty-seven, earns Smoke 19 by its own light, and goes to the Cross on Smoke 20 where Berr wipes his thumb twice, reads *one in twelve, as the notice says* twice over the basket, changes the district's arithmetic for the second time in Smoke and goes off with the box locked, believing he has done a kindness. Nothing under the roof is settled by it: no seventh hearing, no tenth stopping, no sixth asking, no witness, no tenth hand, no figure on the grain, no name on the Day 188 man, no calling of the loan, no opening before the first firing of Frost, and no ending.
