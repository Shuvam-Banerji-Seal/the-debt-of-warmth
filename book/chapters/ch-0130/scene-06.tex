The afternoon of the nineteenth came on as a fire comes on under blind ash --- not with a flame but with a change in the colour of the room, the grey light going thin and then a little less thin, and the low note of \place{the river} coming up through \place{the finger's-width door} as though the covering over it had grown an ear. \person[Corin]{The boy} lay where \person{Sella Vail} had set him, in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill}, \place{Grath}, out of the straight draught, with the wicker's shadow across his wrist and the warmth arriving sideways out of the wall the way it always arrived and never from the direction he would have chosen. He had woken in the small hours to a crust gone hard as old horn and a thread stiff on his lip and cold stopped at cheek and temple and the bare joint of the wrist, and had taken the three wants in the old order and given them no verdict; the afternoon found him with the quarrel still open on the one page, the register and the wanting, neither signed. Nothing had come up out of the yard to close it. No fist had opened again toward the road, and no number had arrived from any direction, and the not-seeing of the day stood where it had been put. A day that had earned only its nineteenth earned no more than its nineteenth, and the house took up its grammar as though nothing anywhere had turned.

She carried him down the nineteen steps at the half-pace from every open slate when the afternoon had settled, and the instruction went to the wool and the wind off the river and the pot and the fire and never to the basket; the basket was not told what the hour was or what the day was or told to be still. She set him at the edge of \place{the flagstones} where \place{the Vail corner} turned, out of the way of pails and feet, and went back to the small honest work of a Ninthday afternoon, and the household straightened around her going the way a bed straightens when a weight is lifted off one corner. A pail went down with its flat knock somewhere beyond him; a rope settled into its knot with the cold-stiffened rasp; barley lay wall to wall without a number. He took the small sounds first because they do not lie and the large ones are mostly weather, and took each apart by fist and eye and mouth and the half-beat in the gap, and gave the gap no intention, for an intention wants a payer where none stood.

The clean dark line lay where it had always lain, where the \lore{Ash} ridge turned the corner of the house and the broom had gone the other way for two years. Beside it the finer grey called \lore{the thaw} kept its place, thickened in the dry cold into a hairline the colour of the ridge in wet weather, and along a second stretch where no broom had gone it lay a hair less distinct than along the first --- not gone, not swept, only less sharp at the edge, as a line drawn in dust loses a little of its edge when the air is dry and nobody comes near it. The faint sour tang of Ash came up off it into the basket and went no further than the basket. He found it where put. He did not look twice. He called it weather, and the calling bought nothing and cost nothing, and there was no hand under the grey to make it a gift or a claim or a sign. Not a sign, not a fourth covering, not a fourth likeness, not a sixth shaping, not a payment; the greys had no owner and the lessening had no payer, and the finding stayed a finding.

Through the finger's-width door the air off the river came thinner at one edge, softer where the low note of \lore{water over water} ran nearer under \lore{the winter skin}, a low thing over a lower thing under a covering that did not move. The note came from under the covering and not from the covering, and ended on its own finishing. The softer edge was where it had been on the sixteenth and where it had not been called on the seventeenth or the eighteenth or the morning of this nineteenth, and nobody had gone to it with a finger or a broom or a word. He found it where put and did not look twice and called it weather. A fourth covering wants a hand to lay it and a fourth likeness wants a payer standing under both, and none stood. The tang came thinner at that edge and thicker at the corner, two small differences of air on two places of their own, and he kept a finger between them and did not lay one across the other to make a row come out even.

The line had not been made to answer the month's turn or the reading that had gone up \place{the clay road} in \person{Berr Clay}'s locked box, and the softer edge had not been made to answer it either. Four readings of the year stood behind one unsigned figure, four being four and not one account, and the finding at the corner stood on a place of its own with a finger between it and them, gathered and not spent, keeping and not payment. Nobody had passed a finger along the second stretch. Nobody had swept it into a row. A thing left in place is not a thing forgiven, and the two greys at the corner kept a finger between the called-gone beside the left-in-place --- the hair-thin lessening of Silt 7 on its own tray beside this second one of Silt 19, each called weather and neither made to answer the other. He made no doctrine out of two softenings, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood under either line.

\scenebreak

Sella, at \place{the hearth} when she came back in, told the coals it was the thaw and did not tell the basket. She said it in the voice another woman would have used to a child who could not hear her, and turned the coals with the back of a broken pot, the dry scrape of iron over stone going into his ear ahead of the warmth and leaving the blind ash where it lay. What she said low went to the pot and the coals and the fire; what she meant to do she called \emph{the house}; of the child under the shelf she said \emph{the boy}. A thing said aloud becomes a thing the Court can enter, and she had decided long ago that the greys at the corner would not be entered by her.

The shape of the two lines met him from another room and another country, and he read it by where the lines had stopped and not by the name anybody had given the stopping. In a ward at the end of a winter the frost had come off the inside of a window in two lines that ran down the glass and met at a corner, one line longer than the other, and a nurse with her sleeves rolled had glanced at them, called it the weather and gone on with her count. She had not put her finger on the glass. She had not called a payer under the lines. The frost was gone by the next round and the count had gone on unchanged, four and four and whatever the figure was, and the window had kept its corner all the same. He had been a man in that room with a tray in his hands and had not thought twice about the lines then, and he did not think twice about them now. The shape paid nothing. No break came into the kiln-house from that room and no voice spoke in it; the recollection went over the afternoon like a hand over a closed field and came away without dressing it. Reading by where a thing stopped and not by the word given the stopping was the whole of what he had brought with him, and it was a habit and not a gift, and it bought no warmth for a boy who could not close two inches of air.

\scenebreak

The light went off the yard in the order it always went, first the top of the wall and then the flagstones and then the trough, and Sella came to the basket when the dark was already in her sleeves. She carried him up the nineteen steps at the half-pace from every open slate, taking the tilt out of the landing before the wicker had settled, and the instruction went to the wool and the river wind and the pot and the fire and the banked fire and never to the basket. She said nothing to the basket of the second stretch of grey, of the softer edge at the door, or of what the day had been; a pot carried up is a pot carried up, and no woman explains the afternoon to a pot.

Under the roof she went to the hearth before the basket and the household straightened around the order of it. She told the coals it was the thaw and not the basket; she gave the small intake, the hold and the letting-go toward no opening, figuring neither gap, for a figured gap wants a payer standing in it and none stood; her finger went along the top of \place{the loaf shelf} so the charcoal tally beneath stayed unread, no hand beneath, no mark copied, no row numbered; her lips moved on a two-gapped row that was neither prayer nor count and wanted no answer; one glance went to the clay jar standing cold under \lore{lead glaze} beyond \place{the pot board}, the hand shut half over nothing and the little-door burn on the back of her left hand closing on air. She lifted the twice-mended pot thumb-first where the tighter wire crossed the crack, the hairline of the original break showing between the older brass staple and the wire, and gave both proverbs to the pot and the fire and never to a person, and kept repair apart from restoration as a surgeon keeps a graft apart from a cure. \emph{Leave the crack showing. People trust a thing they can see has been handled}, to the pot. \emph{If it's worth mending it's worth mending twice. The second time is the real one}, to the fire. The dressed grain lay turned under wool and unfigured, and the pot rim stood two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat and no look toward \place{the bed}, the half-beat the distance between the intention and the arrest, unmeasured and unnamed. Two inches of air bought no warmth and she knew it as well as he did and neither of them said so.

\person[Garl Vail]{Garl} was in the room the way he had been in it for four hundred and forty-three days: the salt-stiff coat with its crease at the left shoulder on the peg behind the door, the hollow wheel-seat nobody was allowed to fill, the unforged collector sentence at the kindling with no name on the man who had turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on \lore{the tile debt}, the clean dark line at the corner with its second grey unswept. His laugh had ended in a cough and his proverbs had survived him improved by use, and neither the hairline nor the sayings bought the absence a payment, a name or a mending. \lore{The widow's mark} hung where it hung and she did not touch it; a going of the light on a Ninthday is not an opening, and seven openings in seven months are seven and not a rule, and no eighth came due on a day that had earned only its nineteenth. She touched the mark before she opened \place{the kiln mouth} and at no other hour, and the kiln mouth was not open. She put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face and added no second fold, and \lore{the keeping at the fold} was found where put, still a finding and not a placing, with no name, no gift, no payment and no payer. What had happened in the yard had not mended anything under the roof and had not been made a lesson of there.

The small hours came down under the roof as a banked fire comes down when nobody tends it --- not out but inward, drawing its heat into itself and leaving the edges of the room to the cold. At the head of the bed \place{the towel} came out as instruments come out when a case is done and before the next is called, rough from the wash and smelling of tallow and low smoke. He had once laid a tray in the order things would be wanted and named them again as they came away, and known the difference between a finding and a want without having to decide which was which; that was another room and another pair of hands and paid nothing here. He went over the day by the place of each thing with every gap left a gap and did not add them, for a surgeon who adds findings before the last is in has already made up the answer he wanted before he cut.

Six hearings of \lore[full note]{the full note} stood beside and never among, with the seventh place empty and \place{the Drawing house}'s flat knock outside on its own place; the heard bell stood beside the told bell with both gaps in their own squares, and three counts in three places stayed three, for agreeing any two would want a payer put under both and none stood; nine record-first hands stood with nine stoppings and no tenth, and seven openings in seven months were seven and not a rule. The clean dark line and its second finer grey stood on a place of their own, beside the called-gone grey of Silt 7 and the softer river air of Silt 16, with a finger between every pair and no hand under any of them; a thing left in place is not a thing forgiven, and none of them was made to answer the month's turn or the reading gone up the road. The direction come up at the well on the eighteenth stood on its own place beside the fist-to-nowhere arrivals and \person{Ilva}'s offered figure and its return, with a finger between every pair, and was not told again on a day that had earned only its nineteenth; the strip of notched birch stayed in its fold in her sleeve with no figure coming off the wood, and she was offstage with her fear unpaid and was not made a reader by any nearness.

\person{Tarn}'s withheld sentence of the fifteenth stood on its own place beside the older stopped sentence and Garl's unforged collector sentence, three sentences and not one rule, the loan uncalled in a term already ended; Tarn and \person{Jorra Vail} were offstage at the kindling and the wheel-shed with their keepings found where put, and the two quiet men under one roof kept their places on trays of their own, their silences maintenance and not a compact. The second asking of Silt 12, the face at the yard's edge on Silt 13, the reported second slate of Silt 6, the neighbour's near-reaching at the loaf shelf, the clerk's entered figure brought up the yard as a report and never a sighting, the fifth shaping of the well-saying, the three likenesses of Slate Row, \person{Hessa Rale}'s eighth record-first hand and \lore{the burned slate} standing at five, the four ownerless directions and the two market sightings on separate trays stood each on its own place with a finger between every pair, none a reader, a witness, a tenth hand, a custom, a payment or a payer, and none made to answer another on a day that had earned only its nineteenth. No eighth opening, seventh hearing, tenth hand, tenth stopping, sixth shaping, eleventh arithmetic, signature, payer, witness, fifth reading or calling of the loan had stood on this day.

Four readings of the year stood behind one unsigned figure up the clay road, four being four and not one account, the first not made a thirteenth by the three that followed, the second supplying no signature, the third no doctrine, the fourth no payer, the twelve of Y.H. 813 not laid across the four to make a row come out even, the figure read still not the figure paid and no signature on the space. None of them was made to answer any kept thing under this roof, for paper is not a payer and no key came under any roof. Silt 20, the month's second reading and the year's fifth, stood one morning off as a date and was not brought; it had earned nothing yet and would be earned by its own morning. The unsent call to \person{Nina} stayed where it was put and was not paid again, nine summers and nine promises and a boat never bought left in the dark where a small cold rectangle of light had once lain.

Answer four hundred and sixty-two was filed on the sixth place, exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment and not a doctrine, laid beside the others with a finger between every pair, and the seventh place stayed empty. Six honest answers, four hundred and fifty-seven through four hundred and sixty-two, stood there, two to each of the three days, not one a doctrine, and four hundred and sixty-two answers stood there in all. He kept four hundred and forty-three exact, six months and two hundred and sixty-three days told by nobody who would have known what a number counts. \lore{The carried fire}'s fifth went unpaid and Garl's name went unspoken the same four hundred and forty-three, the man and the fire standing side by side without becoming one, for a number counted is a number with air around it and air buys no warmth.

Through the finger's-width door the river kept its low note of water over water under the winter skin, softer at one edge where the note ran nearer under the covering and the tang came thinner, and the note came from under the covering and not from the covering and ended on its own finishing. The hand outside the wool came up an inch toward it --- the inch not a reach, not a wanting made into a claim, only the body's small assent to a sound --- took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole on the note's finishing, let it go as the note ended and came straight down and laid itself flat against the fold on answer four hundred and sixty-two as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. There was no next hand in the room. He did not wipe the thread and did not lift the crust. Under \place{the boards} the four low pulls kept their silence where the fifth should have stood and nobody supplied the pull; the greys at the corner kept their finger's width and the soft edge at the door kept its own place, called weather and not a sign, and the fire's fifth and the man's name stood side by side without becoming one, counted and uncounted, kept and unpaid. Forward, into a twentieth day not lived and not brought.
