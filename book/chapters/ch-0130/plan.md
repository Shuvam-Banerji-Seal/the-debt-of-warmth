## Scene 1 — The Seventeenth Morning of Silt

- **Day and time:** Day 441, Silt 17, Y.H. 814, Seventhday; small hours opening into first grey and the morning's work.
- **Place:** Vail kiln-house under the loaf shelf's low angle; the hearth, pot board, rack end; the nineteen steps down; the kindling and flagstones; the wheel-shed edge and cold damper.
- **Present:** Corin/Dan (carried), Sella Vail, Tarn, Jorra Vail; Garl Vail in objects only. Ilva offstage with her count still in wood and her fear unpaid; Hessa's slate unread up the road, Berr up the clay road with the box locked and the key gone up in a coat pocket, the soft-cuffed clerk reported at the out-of-line landing and never a sighting. No market, Cross, bell, Account-day, collector or runner.

**Beats in order**

1. The chapter's only `\dropcap`, then the first `\daymark{441}{Silt 17, Y.H. 814, Seventhday}`; small hours open on the hand flat on the fold on **answer 456**, count **440 exact**, six months and 260 days told by nobody, the carried fire's fifth unpaid and Garl's name unspoken the same number, four readings of the year behind one unsigned figure up the clay road in Berr Clay's locked box behind the twelve of Y.H. 813, four being four and not one account, the figure read still not the figure paid and no signature on the space, and **no next hand in the room**. Tarn's sentence told the wood on Silt 14, withheld on Silt 15 and said again on Silt 16, the hair-thin thaw of Silt 16 and the softer river air stand each on its own place with a finger between and are **not** told again on a morning that earned only its seventeenth; do not recap them, do not make a lesson of the fourth reading.
2. The surgeon takes the body in its old order: crust gone past leather into old horn two inches from the ear with its corner a hair off the wool; keeping at the fold found where put in the empty place, still a finding and not a placing, no third crust, no name, no payer; thread stiff in the dry kiln heat and unwiped, because nobody under any roof has ever been given an instruction about the thread; cold stopped at cheek, temple and the bare wrist-joint where the wool rides back as at the end of a written line; a face composed by cold is not composure and a cold patient cannot clot; the Afterdrop waiting past a warming nobody here will perform, with the level basket and sideways heat being the rewarming he would choose and cannot ask for.
3. The infant's three wants — crust lifted two inches, thread wiped, a hand on the pot handle — quarrel with the surgeon's register; both true, the second louder, neither buying the other, the quarrel the work and no verdict.
4. First grey: Sella carries him down the nineteen steps at the half-pace from every open slate, instruction to wool, river wind, pot and fire and never to the basket, basket not told the day or the hour or told to be still; goes to the hearth before the basket; tells the coals it is the thaw and turns them with the back of a broken pot, a dry scrape of iron over stone; gives the small intake, hold and letting-go toward no opening with neither gap figured; finger along the shelf's top so the charcoal stays unread; lips on a two-gapped row; one glance at the cold clay jar with the hand shut half over nothing and the little-door burn closing on air.
5. Twice-mended pot lifted thumb-first where the tighter wire crosses the crack, hairline showing between older brass staple and wire, both proverbs to pot and fire and never to a person; dressed grain turned under wool unfigured; pot rim two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat and no look toward the bed; widow's mark untouched — a Seventhday morning is not an opening and seven openings in seven months are seven and not a rule.
6. Tarn at the kindling and flagstones: six where the house can check, seventh at the lip beside Garl's unforged collector sentence with no name on the man who turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on the tile debt; small knife at the edge of its sill, hand over blade and sill and away with no breath held at the top of the reach, the coming away the whole of the checking; the sentence found where put and not made a habit, a tenth stopping, a payment or a rule; ear-sentence stopped at the word that would call the loan, term ended, loan uncalled; Ash in the knuckle creases, pale clip-ring at the left wrist, *Yes, house* to the fire.
7. Jorra at the wheel-shed edge: meal-fine grey slate dust ground small past the bent little finger and white in a fist-crease; dusty forearms parted from clean as clot from vessel; wheel and pot spoken to before any person; full note known three turns ahead and withheld from any High Kiln master who would call it early; bent finger into the cold damper ahead of Sella's unfinished sentence, one bite holding and one taking, no Grathic word covering both, coming out without a second holding of Ash 26; *he's not Vail* sealed, promise neither given nor withdrawn against the burned slate at five.

## Scene 6 — What the Nineteenth Left

- **Day and time:** Day 443, Silt 19, Y.H. 814, Ninthday; afternoon at the Vail corner and the finger's-width door, going of the light, small hours closing the chapter.
- **Place:** the Vail corner and the clean dark line with its finer grey; the finger's-width door and the river beyond; under the Vail kiln-house roof at the loaf shelf and bed.
- **Present:** Corin/Dan, Sella Vail; Tarn and Jorra Vail offstage at kindling and wheel-shed with their keepings found where put; Garl Vail in objects; Ilva offstage with her count in wood and her fear unpaid. Day already dated in Scene 5.

**Beats in order**

1. In the afternoon, at the Vail corner, the clean dark line keeps its finer grey called the thaw a hair less distinct along a **second** stretch where no broom has gone in two years, thickened in the dry cold with the faint sour tang of Ash coming up off it; through the finger's-width door the air off the river is softer at one edge where the low note of water over water runs nearer under the winter skin, the tang thinner there. He finds both where put, does not look twice, calls them weather — not a sign, not a fourth covering, not a fourth likeness, not a sixth shaping, not a payment, with no hand or payer under either grey and not made again. The finding is not the sixteenth's finding made again: the line has not been made to answer the month's turn or the reading that has gone up the road, and nobody has passed a finger along the stretch or swept it into a row (T083/T085/T092 with T009/T013/T092).
2. Sella tells the fire it is the thaw and does not tell the basket. The two greys at the corner keep a finger between the called-gone beside the left-in-place; a thing left in place is not a thing forgiven. The old-life register supplies the shape of frost leaving a ward window in two lines that met at a corner and a nurse calling it the weather and going on with her count, read by where the lines stopped and not by the name given them — past tense, untagged, paying nothing, with no present-tense break spent and no `\memory{}` tag used (T004 held).
3. Sella carries him home at the going of the light at the half-pace, says nothing to the basket of the greys or of what the day was; the whole grammar comes back as though nothing had turned, widow's mark untouched, fold righted without a look at his face and no second fold, keeping found where put (T058/T079, T060/T086/T097).
4. Small hours: the towel comes out at the head of the bed as instruments come out when a case is done, rough from the wash and smelling of tallow and low smoke. He goes over the day by the place of each thing with every gap a gap and does not add them. Six hearings beside and never among with the seventh place empty and the Drawing's knock outside; heard bell beside told with both gaps in squares and three counts in three places staying three; nine record-first hands with nine stoppings and no tenth; seven openings in seven months and not a rule. The well-direction of Silt 18 stands on its own place beside the fist-to-nowhere arrivals and the offered figure and its return; Tarn's withheld sentence of Silt 15, the hair-thin thaw of Silt 16 and softer air, the second asking of Silt 12, the Slate Row face of Silt 13, the reported second slate of Silt 6, the neighbour's near-reaching, the hair-thin thaw of Silt 7, the clerk's entered figure brought up the yard as a report, the fifth shaping of the well-saying, the three likenesses of Slate Row, the burned slate at five, the four ownerless directions and the two market sightings on separate trays stand each on its own place with a finger between every pair (T077, T061/T082/T078, T080/T090/T102, T097, T065/T095, T050/T051).
5. Four readings of the year stand behind one unsigned figure — four being four and not one account, the first not a thirteenth, the second no signature, the third no doctrine, the fourth no payer, the twelve of Y.H. 813 not laid across the four, the figure read still not the figure paid — and none is made to answer any kept thing under this roof; Silt 20 (Day 444), the month's second reading and the year's fifth, stands one morning off as a date and is not brought (T015/T041/T047). The unsent call to Nina stays where it was put and is not paid again (T020).
6. **Answer 462** filed on the sixth place, exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment; six honest answers **457–462** stand there, two to each of the three days, not one a doctrine, and 462 answers stand there in all. Count **443 exact**, six months and 263 days told by nobody; the carried fire's fifth unpaid and Garl's name unspoken the same number, the man and the fire standing side by side without becoming one, for a number counted is a number with air around it and air buys no warmth (T088).
7. Through the finger's-width door the river keeps its low note of water over water under the winter skin, softer at one edge, the note coming from under the covering and ending on its own finishing. The hand outside the wool comes up an inch toward it — the inch not a reach, not a wanting made into a claim, only the body's small assent to a sound — takes a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, holds the interval whole on the note's finishing, lets it go and comes straight down and lays itself flat against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. There is no next hand in the room. He does not wipe the thread and does not lift the crust. Under the boards the four low pulls keep their silence where the fifth should have stood and nobody supplies the pull.

- **Emotional turn:** the second stretch of grey is found and called weather, the file closes over the day without a verdict, and the wanting goes on; the two advances stand on their own places and buy no payment.
- **Sensory anchors:** the hair-thin grey along the second unswept stretch, sour tang of Ash thickened in dry cold, river air softer at one edge with the low note nearer under the skin, tallow and low smoke, the rough wash-towel, four low pulls under the boards.
- **Threads advanced, none paid:** **T083/T085/T092 with T009/T013/T092** (thaw's second stretch and river softness advanced and NOT paid, found where put, called weather, not a fourth covering, sign or payment); T077 (well-direction held on its own place and not repeated); T061/T082/T078 (file, bells, three counts in three places); T080/T090/T102 and T050/T051 (kept things unjoined); T015/T041/T047 (four behind one unsigned figure, not made to answer); T004 held; T060/T086/T097 and T058/T079 (grammar, fold, keeping found where put); T071/T072 (answer 462, count 443); T088; T020 (unsent call not paid again).
- **Foreshadowing planted / paid off:** nothing paid. The well-direction of Silt 18, the withheld word of Silt 15 and the two greys of Silt 16 go on gathering interest toward the far-off hand that will lay the district's private keepings together and call them an account — a hand not his that has not come. No eighth opening, seventh hearing, tenth hand, tenth stopping, sixth shaping, eleventh arithmetic, signature, payer, witness, reader, fifth reading, third crust, name for a gap or calling of the loan stood.
- **Exact last image:** the hand comes down flat on the fold on answer 462; there is no next hand in the room; the four low pulls under the boards keep their silence where the fifth should have stood with nobody supplying it; forward into a twentieth day not lived and not brought.

## How the final moment pulls into Chapter 131

The chapter closes on the small hours of Silt 19 with the hand laid flat on **answer 462**, the count **443 exact**, six months and two hundred and sixty-three days told by nobody, six honest answers 457–462 on the sixth place and not one a doctrine, the carried fire's fifth unpaid and Garl's name unspoken the same number. The well-direction of Silt 18 stands on its own tray beside the fist-to-nowhere arrivals and the offered figure and its return; Tarn's withheld sentence of Silt 15 stands on its own beside the older stopped sentence; the thaw's second stretch of Silt 19 stands on its own beside the called-gone grey and the softer river edge, each called weather and not a sign. Nothing from any of them has crossed under the roof as sign, lesson, mending, cause, payment or payer, and none is told again on an unearned day. There is no next hand in the room and the river goes on with its low note under its winter skin.

Chapter 131 opens in the small hours of **Silt 20 (Day 444), Tenthday**, with the hand still on answer 462 and the count still exact at 443, the house taking up its grammar as though nothing had turned. Silt 20 is the month's second Account-day and market and the year's fifth reading, earned by its own morning: Berr Clay reads *one in twelve, as the notice says* twice over the basket, calls the levy, denies it with *your house*, never *I*, says something low to the block about kindness being entered, and goes off up the clay road with the box locked and the fifth subtraction inside, believing he has done a kindness. **Five readings of the year will stand behind one unsigned figure** — five being five and not one account, the four behind not laid across the fifth to make a row come out even, the figure read still not the figure paid, no signature on the space and no doctrine out of five — and the finding will stay on a place of its own with no payer, not a sixth arithmetic, not a signature and not a doctrine, not made to answer the well-direction, the withheld sentence, the second stretch of grey or the neighbour's near-reaching. Sella sets the basket in the moot-post's finger's width of shade and stands half a pace off through the reading and the bell; Hessa carries the narrow slate up the road unread and is not made a reader by five figures; the soft-cuffed clerk stays reported at the out-of-line landing; the green and the shape stay sightings with no eighth of either and the two Ilmen 30 sightings stay on separate trays, never Fenna or Ulda in the narration. Nothing planted here is spent there; Silt 30 (Day 454) stands further off as a date and is not brought. Forward, not closed.
