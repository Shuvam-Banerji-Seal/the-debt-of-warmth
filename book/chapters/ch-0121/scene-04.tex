The afternoon came on without a sound of its own, the way warmth comes into a room from a wall that has been holding it all morning. There had been no market and no reading on the twenty-first and no bell struck through it; the day had earned only its own going, and from the low angle of the basket \person[Corin]{the boy} took \place{the Clay Yard} as he found it, by its small sounds first, because the small sounds did not lie and the large ones were mostly weather. A pail went down on its stone at \place{the yard well} where a pail always stood, and the stone gave back the same small wooden knock it had given back on the twentieth, neither more nor less. A rope settled into its own knot with a rasp stiff as the week's dry cold, the fibres arguing among themselves a moment and then giving the argument up. A loaf went a finger's width along a board and stopped, and nobody called the stopping a figure; the board kept the shallow place where the loaf had lain. Barley passed from wall to wall in the breath between two women's voices and was never made into a number, the price carried on the sound of it like silt held in water and let go again before it could settle. He had learned early that a yard keeps its accounts in such things --- a knock, a rasp, a finger's width, a breath between voices --- and that none of them is a figure until some hand goes looking for one.

At \place{the Vail corner} the clean dark line kept its finer grey beside it, the hairline the yard calls \lore{the thaw}, thickened in the dry cold and carrying the faint sour tang of \lore{Ash} up off it where no broom had gone in two years. The air that had come off \place{the river} on the nineteenth was less cold than it had been, and the tang was thinner for it, and the grey lay a hair less distinct along one stretch. He found the softening where he had put it and did not look at it a second time, for looking twice is how a finding becomes a habit, and a habit wants a hand that placed twice and a way wants a third, and both want a payer standing where none stood. The called-gone grey stayed beside the left-in-place ridge with a finger between, and no agreement had been made to stop looking at this one; a thing left in place is not a thing forgiven, and the looking stayed a looking and did not turn into a custom. Out on \place{the worked strips} the field's empty place held the shape of a thing taken out, the way a bed holds the shape of a body that has got up, and he did not lay that shape across the loaf's stopping or across the ridge. \place{the trough} stood bare and dark with its skin gone, and the gone pan's round ring gave back its shallow print on \place{the flat stone by the well}, on a place of its own out of the path of feet. A figure set against any of these would want a payer standing, and none stood.

He went around \place{the seven walked-around places} once and not twice, with a look and a wide step and no number: the clean dark line and its grey, the space beneath the lowest rack where the broken birth-slates lay, the gone pan's ring, the bare trough, the field's empty place out on the strips, and \place{the returning-line place} where a hand had gone in on \emph{Seed 15} and had not come back. The seventh he went widest around, giving it the whole width of the basket's small world, as though a wide enough step might keep a hand from ever having gone in there. The wide step was a keeping and not a sign. He kept the step and gave the figure up, for a keeping that stands up and calls itself a rule is an account that has found a payer, and none stood.

Neighbours at the well disagreed about the air and nobody owned the day. One woman said it had turned and a man said it would be back before morning, and neither asked the other what they meant, and the words went off into ordinary talk the way a word goes down a well and leaves no ring behind it. This was the same lessening he had found on the nineteenth and found again here, filed where put and not spent on a day that had earned only its twenty-first; it was weather, not a sign, not a fourth covering, not a sixth shaping, not a payment, and no hand or payer stood under it. He did not look twice at it. \person[Sella Vail]{Sella} had told the fire it was the thaw two mornings running and had not told the basket, and no word came up at the well that the man who had gone down the bridge road the night before with his writing turned away had asked after a house-name under this roof. The direction and the slate stayed where he had put them and were not repeated, and he did not go looking along the road to make a habit of them.

Costs came to the yard as directions and went away as weather, and left no entry behind them. A man come down \place{the clay road} brought a number up out of a shut fist toward the well, held it where the light could see it, looked at it, and put it back into his sleeve. It arrived nowhere. The boy took the order apart as he took every field apart: hand first, then eye, then the mouth that did not open, and the half-beat lying in the gap between the hand and the eye. He gave the fist no figure and the sleeve no motive; a sleeve is a sleeve and not an intention, and a half-beat is a place where a thing might have stood and did not. A woman came asking what a pot cost and was sent to the pot with a practical word; from the pot she got a note and not a price, for pots keep their own accounts and do not speak them. The dressed grain lay covered under wool on the cool stone at \place{the rack end}, and the arithmetic read over a basket in another yard on the twentieth had not known what it cost. No signature stood on the space between the figure and the price. He did not set one there.

The clerk's figure entered before a face was looked at stayed a report brought up the yard and never a sighting from the basket; \person{Tarn}'s degree toward \place{the out-of-line landing} below \place{the north bridge} stayed a shape found where put and was not repeated in the afternoon; the face at the yard's edge, filed among the older record-first hands, stayed uncounted; and the second man's direction down the bridge road with the slate turned to the sleeve stayed on a place of its own beside them. No name stood under any of them, no owner, no answer carried, no witness, no sale, no tenth hand and no paying. He kept a finger between every pair and did not lay them across one another, for joining any two would want a payer standing under both and none stood. The fifth shaping of \lore{the well-saying} sat on its own tray between \person[Hessa Rale]'s eighth hand and the saying of the road from \place{the Cross} to \place{Greybank}, with a third finger between it and the four ownerless directions; \person[Ilva]'s strip stayed in her sleeve away from this yard with her ninth hand nine, and no figure came off any wood. No sixth was made due by the quiet of the day, and no man from \place{Slate Row} stood at the yard's edge in the afternoon.

At \place{the kindling} Tarn kept six pieces in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check and the seventh at the lip beyond it, beside \person{Garl Vail}'s unforged collector sentence with no name on the man who turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on \lore{the tile debt}. The small knife lay at the very edge of its sill. His hand went over blade and sill as a man passes a hand over a tool put back where it belongs and came away with no breath held at the top of the reach --- the coming away was the whole of the checking. He cut nothing, counted nothing, spent nothing, and the sentence he had said on the nineteenth and twentieth and withheld on the eighteenth was found where put and not made a habit, not a tenth stopping, not a payment and not a rule. Ash was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line, and the pale clip-ring at his left wrist caught and gave back what little light there was. His chest had been tight since the dark. He was helpful when he was frightened and on this afternoon he was not frightened, and the household read the quiet of him the way it reads the quiet of Sella.

At \place{the Vail wheel-shed} \person{Jorra Vail} came through \place{the finger's-width door} with meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past his bent little finger and white in a fist-crease where a hand had opened, and it was not the bone-coloured \lore{slip} of the wheel. Three or more men unnamed had gone low into \place{the back room} before first grey and he gave the house no name for them and did not say the room was cold. He parted dusty forearms from clean as clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had been broken nor whether it was a birth-slate, for a question joined to a cause wants a payer standing and none stood. He spoke to the wheel before any person: \emph{It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady}. He told a pot \emph{You'll do} in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness, and kept the \lore{full note} it would give three turns ahead from any master come up from \place{High Kiln} who would call it early and sing a pot before its time. His bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper} ahead of Sella's unfinished sentence and came out without the extra holding of \emph{Ash 26} a second time; one piece of iron had two bites, the holding and the taking, and no Grathic word covered both mercies. \emph{He's not Vail} stayed sealed as a lid nobody had tried to lift in three years, and the promise was neither given nor withdrawn.

The going of the light came on in the ordinary way, without a bell and without a word said over the basket, and Sella came to \place{the hearth} before she came to it, as she had at both ends of every day since the boy was laid under this roof. She told the coals it was the thaw in the voice another woman would have used for a child who could not hear her, and turned them with the back of a broken pot, and gave the small intake, the hold and the letting-go toward no opening, figuring neither of the two gaps. \emph{The house will keep it} went to the fire and not to the basket, because a thing said aloud becomes a thing the Court can enter. Her finger lay along the top of \place{the loaf shelf} so the charcoal tally on its underside stayed unread, and her lips moved on a two-gapped row with neither gap figured. One glance went to the clay jar standing cold under \lore{lead glaze} beyond \place{the pot board}; the hand shut half over nothing, the little-door burn on the back of her left hand closing on air, and went back to the pot. The back of the broken pot checked the coals and set them, and the fire took what it was given in its own order and gave back nothing that could be carried under a roof as a figure.

Her thumb went first to where the tighter wire crossed the crack, and only then did the weight come on the handle; the hairline showed between the old-brass staple and the wire, a dark thread with a hand's work on either side. Both proverbs went to the pot and the fire and never to a person: \emph{Leave the crack showing. People trust a thing they can see has been handled} and \emph{If it's worth mending it's worth mending twice. The second time is the real one}. Repair was not restoration; a restored thing hides the hand that handled it, and a mended thing leaves the hand showing, and the boy, who had once closed a man's artery with a clamp and left the clamp in for the next hand to find, knew the difference without having a word in this world for it. She said \emph{The fire doesn't know you're sorry. Feed it} to the fire, which did not know and was fed. At \place{the rack end} she turned the dressed grain piece by piece under the wool with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child, set each down where it had lain, took no figure from it and no handful, and drew the wool back. Then she set the pot rim two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat and no look toward \place{the bed}. The half-beat was the distance between the setting down and the stopping of the hand, and she did not measure it and he did not name it, and no instruction went to the basket about either.

She carried him up the nineteen steps at the half-pace she keeps from every open slate, correcting the tilt at every turn of the landing before it settled into the wicker. The instruction went to the wool, the river wind, the pot, the fire and the banked fire and never to the basket; she did not tell the basket what the day was and did not tell it to be still. Out in the yard she went around the seven walked-around places again with a look and a wide step and no number, once and not twice, widest around the returning-line place, and set him under the roof and never in \place{the Debt Loft}. She put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face and added no second fold. The thread at his mouth stayed frozen and unwiped, because nobody under any roof has ever been given an instruction about the thread. Garl stayed where Garl stays, in objects and not in a story: the salt-stiff coat with its crease at the left shoulder on its peg behind the door, the hollow wheel-seat in the shed keeping its twenty-year hollow on that side that nobody is allowed to fill, the clean dark line at the Vail corner with the finer grey beside it called the thaw, thicker in the dry cold with the faint sour tang of Ash coming up off it where no broom had gone in two years, the twice-mended pot on the board with the hairline showing, the unforged collector sentence lying beside the seventh notch at the kindling, and the tile debt against a firing of tiles circling without a name and worse for being uncalled. His name had not been spoken under this roof for four hundred and fifteen days and the two counts --- his name and \lore{the carried fire}'s fifth --- stood side by side without becoming one.

\lore{The widow's mark} hung where it hung at a well, a road, a yard, a shelf, a going of the light and a bed and was not touched; a well and a road are not openings, seven openings in seven months are seven and not a rule, and no eighth came due on a day that had earned only its twenty-first. The hand that goes to the mark goes before the kiln and at no other hour, and there had been no kiln this evening. She went back to her fire saying nothing at all --- not of the man who had gone down the bridge road with his writing turned away, not of the number that had gone from a fist to a sleeve and arrived nowhere, not of the softening in the air she had told the fire was the thaw, not of the two readings gone up the road in a box with no key, not of the sentence found where put at the kindling. What she put down in the room was a pot in its place and a fire kept, and the house went on as a house goes when those two things are done.

In the small hours \place{the towel} came out at the head of the bed as instruments come out when a case is done and before the next is called, rough from the wash and smelling of tallow and low smoke. He went over the day by the place of each thing and left every gap a gap. One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust stood two inches from his ear with its corner a hair off the wool, gone past the point where a child would still eat it and hardened through the dry nights into old horn; \lore{the keeping at the fold} lay folded where it had been put, found where put and still a finding, not a placing, with no second fold, no third crust, no name and no gift over it; the thread of drool at his mouth was frozen and unwiped, because nobody under any roof has ever been given an instruction about the thread. Cold had come to cheek, temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, and stopped there as at the end of a line written out for it, with the afterdrop waiting past a warming nobody in this house would perform.

Six hearings of \lore{the full note} stood beside and never among, with the seventh place empty and not dug for or filled, and \place{the Drawing house}'s small flat knock stayed outside them on its own ground; neither the knock nor a told gap was made a seventh hearing. The heard bell stayed in its square beside the told bell of \lore{the Long Night}, four, four and a gap, and its gap was not folded into the carried fire's four and a gap under \place{the boards}, into the boards' four and four and a thumb-width knot where a fifth nail might have been driven and was not, or into the two arithmetics behind one unsigned figure beside the twelve old ones; three counts in three places stayed three, because agreeing any two would have wanted a payer put under both and none stood. The carried fire gave four low pulls with the silence where the fifth should have stood, and nobody lifted the floor or supplied the pull; its payer was unnamed four hundred and fifteen days and Garl's name was unspoken the same number, and the fire and the man stood side by side without becoming one. Four rooms kept the same gap with a finger between every pair and no Court room came under the roof as a fifth likeness. Nine record-first hands stood with no tenth and nine stoppings stood with no tenth; the clerk's entered figure, Tarn's degree and the face at the yard's edge stayed on three places with a finger between every pair, and no witness, reader, sale, payer or tenth hand stood under any of them.

The second man's narrow slate with its writing turned to the sleeve and his direction down the bridge road sat on their own places with a finger between them and the first man's face, the clerk's entered figure and the fifth shaping, and no name, owner, answer, sale, witness, tenth hand or rule stood under any of them. The direction had not been repeated on a day that had earned only its twenty-first and he did not set it down as a custom or look for it a second time to make a habit of it; it was a shape gathering interest toward a far-off hand that was not his and had not come, and he let it gather without calling it. The fifth shaping sat on its own tray between Hessa's eighth hand and the road saying, with a third finger between it and the four ownerless directions; Hessa's narrow slate stayed unread up the road and Ilva's strip stayed in her sleeve with her ninth hand nine, and no figure came off any wood. Tarn's withheld sentence was found where put and not made a habit; the softening at \place{the river}'s skin was found where put and called weather, not a sign, not a fourth covering, not a payment and not made again. \person[Berr Clay]'s low saying about kindness being entered stayed on a place of its own with no payer standing, his \emph{your house} beside it, his belief left up the road with the box where it had gone. The twelve old arithmetics stood behind one unsigned figure beside the two readings of the year, two and not one account, the first not a thirteenth, the figure read still not the figure paid, no signature on the space and no doctrine out of two. Every kept thing lay where it lay, advanced and not paid, gathering interest separately toward a far-off hand that was not his; none stood up and called itself a rule.

The hand outside the wool came up an inch toward the note under the winter skin. The inch was not a reach. Through the finger's-width door the river kept its note of \lore{water over water}, a low thing over a lower thing under the covering, softer at one edge where the low note had run nearer under it on the nineteenth, found where put and not made a sign. He took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole on the note's own finishing under the covering --- neither early nor stayed --- and let it go as the note ended. Then the hand came straight down and laid itself flat against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. He did not wipe the thread and did not lift the crust.

Answer four hundred and six came out and was filed on the sixth place. It was exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the twelve readings, the first and second of the new count, the printed figure, the fifth shaping, the clerk's entered figure, the face, the degree, the softening, the bell's gap, the flat knock, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the held breath, the two bites of iron, the winter skin, the widow's mark nor the keeping at the fold. Six honest answers began on the new tend and not one was a doctrine. Count four hundred and fifteen exact, six months and two hundred and thirty-five days told by nobody who would have known what a number counted. The carried fire's fifth was unpaid the same number and its payer unnamed the same number; Garl's name was unspoken the same number, and the fire and the man stood side by side without becoming one. Under the exactness, louder, the infant wanted the crust lifted, the thread wiped and a hand on the pot board's handle, and got none of the three; both were true, the second was louder, neither won, and the quarrel was the work. The crust's corner stood a hair off the wool and the thread was frozen and unwiped; four low pulls breathed below the floor with the fifth unsupplied; the two readings of the year were up the clay road in a locked box with no key in his hand; the second man's slate and direction were kept where put and not repeated; the softening was kept as weather; the bell's gap stayed in its square. The house went on as a house goes when a pot is set down in its place and the fire kept --- forward, into a morning not yet earned and not brought into this day. Count four hundred and fifteen exact, and no number stood up as a threshold.
