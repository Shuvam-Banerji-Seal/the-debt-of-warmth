\daymark{416}{Ilmen 22, Y.H. 814, Secondday}

The hand lay flat against the fold where the going of the light had left it, and the small hours came down under the roof the way a fine dry cold comes through a wall that has no name for the place it enters --- not arriving, only being there. Answer four hundred and six lay under the hand, a small intake held whole on the river's note's own finishing and let go as the note ended, exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air; four hundred and fifteen days stood behind it, six months and two hundred and thirty-five of them told by nobody. This was the twenty-second of \place{Ilmen}, an ordinary Secondday of the new year's third working week, with no market and no reading due and no bell struck for it. Nothing from the twenty-first had crossed under the roof as sign, lesson, mending, cause, payment or payer. First light was still a direction beyond \place{the finger's-width door} and not a thing in the room. \person[Corin]{The boy} lay at the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf} in \place{the Vail kiln-house}, \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill}, \place{Grath}, under the roof and never in \place{the Debt Loft}, and the heat came to him sideways from \place{the hearth} and not where he would have chosen.

One stiff brown-edged barley crust stood two inches from his ear, no longer leathery now but hardened through three dry nights into old horn, with its corner standing a hair off the wool the way a thing set down by a careful hand will keep the shape of that hand's leaving. Beside it \lore{the keeping at the fold} lay folded once where it had been put, found where put on the mornings and goings of the light of the seventeenth, eighteenth, nineteenth, twentieth and twenty-first, still a finding and not a placing, with no second fold, no third crust, no name and no gift over it. The thread of drool at his mouth had frozen in the dry kiln heat and was unwiped, because nobody under any roof has ever been given an instruction about the thread. Cold had come to cheek, temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, and stopped there as at the end of a line written out for it, with the afterdrop waiting past a warming nobody in this house would perform.

Taking the body in its old order was the only work the room allowed him, and he took it: crust, fold, thread, cold, the waiting heat. He knew a face composed by cold is not composure, that the grimace of a cold man wears the shape of a smile and the smile means nothing, that pink returning is the surface still lying warm over a core still cold, and that a cold patient cannot clot and must be rewarmed from outside, slowly, never hurried, never rubbed, never set too near the fire. A basket set level out of the draught where the warmth came sideways was the rewarming he would have chosen and could not ask for. He had once stood at a bay door and sorted twenty people by what they would not survive, and that knowing had hands two paces long and a body that could not lift a crust to its own mouth. Under all of it, louder than the register, the wanting went on: the crust lifted, the thread wiped, a hand on the handle of the pot. He wanted the three things without knowing that wanting them was a want. He got none of the three. Both were true. The second was louder. Neither won, and no verdict was looked for; the quarrel was the work, and the work went on in silence in a room whose floor does not touch his.

First light came up \place{the clay road} side of the room without a boundary drawn across the dark, and the count became four hundred and sixteen exact, six months and two hundred and thirty-six days, told by nobody. It was a round-sounding number, six and two hundred and thirty-six, and round numbers are the ones men stand up and call a line crossed; he did not stand this one up as a threshold, a doctrine or a figure, because a number stood up as a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood. The twelve arithmetics of Y.H. 813 stayed twelve behind one unsigned figure, and the two readings of the year gone up the road in the locked box stayed two and not one account, the first not made a thirteenth by the second, the second supplying no signature, the figure read still not the figure paid. He did not lay the twelve old findings across the two new ones to make a row come out even, for a row made even by a hand that has no key is made even on paper and paper is not a payer; the key had gone up in a coat pocket that never comes back down and there was no key under any roof and none in his hand.

\person{Sella Vail} went to the hearth before the basket, as she had at both ends of every day since he came under the roof. She told the coals it was \lore{the thaw} in the voice another woman would have used for a child who could not hear her, turned them with the back of a broken pot, and gave the small intake, the hold and the letting-go toward no opening, figuring neither of the two gaps. \emph{The house will keep it} went to the fire and not to the basket, because a thing said aloud becomes a thing the Court can enter. Her finger lay along the top of the loaf shelf so the charcoal tally on its underside stayed unread, and her lips moved on a two-gapped row with neither gap figured. One glance went to the clay jar standing cold under \lore{lead glaze} beyond \place{the pot board}; the hand shut half over nothing, the little-door burn on the back of her left hand closing on air, and went back to the pot. Her thumb went first to where the tighter wire crossed the crack, and only then did the weight come on the handle; the hairline showed between the old-brass staple and the wire, a dark thread with a hand's work on either side. Both proverbs went to the pot and the fire and never to a person: \emph{Leave the crack showing. People trust a thing they can see has been handled} and \emph{If it's worth mending it's worth mending twice. The second time is the real one}. Repair was not restoration; a restored thing hides the hand that handled it, and a mended thing leaves the hand showing. She said \emph{The fire doesn't know you're sorry. Feed it} to the fire, which did not know and was fed. At \place{the rack end} she turned the dressed grain piece by piece under the wool with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child, set each down where it had lain, took no figure from it and no handful, and drew the wool back. Then she set the pot rim two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat and no look toward \place{the bed}. The half-beat was the distance between the setting down and the stopping of the hand, and she did not measure it and he did not name it, and no instruction went to the basket about either.

\person{Garl Vail} stayed where Garl stays, in objects and not in a story: the salt-stiff coat with its crease at the left shoulder on its peg behind the door, the hollow wheel-seat in the shed keeping its twenty-year hollow on that side that nobody is allowed to fill, the clean dark line at \place{the Vail corner} with the finer grey beside it called the thaw, thickened in the dry cold with the faint sour tang of \lore{Ash} coming up off it where no broom had gone in two years, the twice-mended pot on the board with the hairline showing, the unforged collector sentence lying beside the seventh notch at the kindling, and \lore{the tile debt} against a firing of tiles circling without a name and worse for being uncalled. His name had not been spoken under this roof for four hundred and sixteen days and the two counts --- his name and \lore{the carried fire}'s fifth --- stood side by side without becoming one.

Before first light she carried him down the nineteen steps at the half-pace she keeps from every open slate, correcting the tilt at every turn of the landing before it settled into the wicker. The instruction went to the wool, the river wind, the pot, the fire and the banked fire and never to the basket; she did not tell the basket what the day was and did not tell it to be still. Out in \place{the Clay Yard} the dark still held \place{the seven walked-around places}, gone around with a look and a wide step and no number, once and not twice, widest around \place{the returning-line place} where a hand had gone in on \emph{Seed 15} and had not come out. The wide step was a keeping and not a sign; a figure set against it would want a payer standing and none stood. \lore{The widow's mark} hung where it hung and was not touched; a morning, a shelf, a yard and a basket are not openings, seven openings in seven months are seven and not a rule, and no eighth came due on a day that had earned only its twenty-second. The hand that goes to the mark goes before the kiln and at no other hour, and there had been no kiln this morning.

At \place{the kindling} \person{Tarn} kept six pieces in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check and the seventh at the lip beyond it, beside Garl's unforged collector sentence with no name on the man who turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on the tile debt. The small knife lay at the very edge of its sill. His hand went over blade and sill as a man passes a hand over a tool put back where it belongs and came away with no breath held at the top of the reach --- the coming away was the whole of the checking. He cut nothing, counted nothing, spent nothing, and the sentence he had said on the seventeenth, nineteenth, twentieth and twenty-first and withheld on the eighteenth was found where put and not made a habit, not a tenth stopping, not a payment and not a rule. The silence of the eighteenth was not repeated on a day that had earned only its twenty-second; a second silence would be a custom and a third a way, and both want a payer where none stood. The \emph{Seed 16} breath stayed unspent and the loan uncalled at the very end of a term already ended. Ash was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line, and the pale clip-ring at his left wrist caught and gave back what little light there was. His chest had been tight since the dark. He was helpful when he was frightened and on this morning he was not frightened, and the household read the quiet of him the way it reads the quiet of Sella.

At \place{the Vail wheel-shed} \person{Jorra Vail} came through the finger's-width door with meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past his bent little finger and white in a fist-crease where a hand had opened, and it was not the bone-coloured \lore{slip} of the wheel. Three or more men unnamed had gone low into \place{the back room} before first grey and he gave the house no name for them and did not say the room was cold. He parted dusty forearms from clean as clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had been broken nor whether it was a birth-slate, for a question joined to a cause wants a payer standing and none stood. He spoke to the wheel before any person: \emph{It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady}. He told a pot \emph{You'll do} in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness, and kept the \lore{full note} it would give three turns ahead from any master come up from \place{High Kiln} who would call it early and sing a pot before its time. His bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper} ahead of Sella's unfinished sentence and came out without the extra holding of \emph{Ash 26} a second time; one piece of iron had two bites, the holding and the taking, and no Grathic word covered both mercies. \emph{He's not Vail} stayed sealed as a lid nobody had tried to lift in three years, and the promise was neither given nor withdrawn.

At \place{the yard well} \person{Ilva} drew with the pail against her knee, the rope stiff in the dry cold giving its small rasp, clay dried pale in the crescents of her nails and the finer grey of the racks worked into the seam of one thumb, which she did not scrub out. People noticed the pail first and the sleeve not at all, which was the order she preferred. No question was put to her this morning and she volunteered no figure; the short strip of birch bark lay in its fold in her sleeve, pale where a thumb had rubbed the notches and dark in the uncut grain between, not taken out, read, copied or counted, and no figure came off the wood. Her hand went once toward the sleeve and stayed there and went back to the pail. She was seen and not asked, and was expressly not the speaker of any word that came up the yard; her ninth record-first hand stayed nine, joined to no tenth and to no reading of the printed figure gone up the road.

The going of the light came on in the ordinary way, without a bell and without a word said over the basket, and Sella came to the hearth before she came to it, as she had at both ends of every day since he was laid under this roof. She told the coals it was the thaw, turned them with the back of the broken pot, gave the small intake, the hold and the letting-go toward no opening with neither gap figured, kept her finger above the unread charcoal and her lips on a two-gapped row, glanced once at the clay jar with the hand shut half over nothing and the little-door burn closing on air, lifted the twice-mended pot thumb-first where the wire crossed the crack with the hairline showing and both proverbs gone to the pot and the fire, turned the dressed grain under wool with no figure or handful, and set the pot rim two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat and no look toward the bed. Then she carried him up the nineteen steps at the half-pace she keeps from every open slate, correcting the tilt at every turn of the landing before it settled into the wicker, went around the seven walked-around places once and not twice with a look and a wide step and no number, widest around the returning-line place, and set him under the roof and never in the Debt Loft. She put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face and added no second fold. The thread at his mouth stayed frozen and unwiped, because nobody under any roof has ever been given an instruction about the thread. The widow's mark hung where it hung at a morning, a shelf, a yard and a basket and was not touched; no eighth opening came due on a day that had earned only its twenty-second. She went back to her fire saying nothing at all --- not of the man whose slate had gone down the bridge road with the writing turned away, not of the figure the clerk had entered before he looked up, not of the two readings up the road in a box with no key, not of the sentence found where put at the kindling, not of the softening in the air she had told the fire was the thaw. What she put down in the room was a pot in its place and a fire kept, and the house went on as a house goes when those two things are done.

\place{The towel} came out at the head of the bed as instruments come out when a case is done and before the next is called, rough from the wash and smelling of tallow and low smoke. He went over the day by the place of each thing and left every gap a gap. Six hearings of \lore{the full note} stood beside and never among, with the seventh place empty and not dug for or filled, and \place{the Drawing house}'s small flat knock stayed outside them on its own ground; neither the knock nor a told gap was made a seventh hearing. The heard bell stayed in its square beside the told bell of \lore{the Long Night}, four, four and a gap, and its gap was not folded into the carried fire's four and a gap under \place{the boards}, into the boards' four and four and a thumb-width knot where a fifth nail might have been driven and was not, or into the two arithmetics behind one unsigned figure beside the twelve old ones; three counts in three places stayed three, because agreeing any two would have wanted a payer put under both and none stood. Four rooms kept the same gap with a finger between every pair and no Court room came under the roof as a fifth likeness. Nine record-first hands stood with no tenth and nine stoppings stood with no tenth; the clerk's entered figure, Tarn's degree toward \place{the out-of-line landing} below \place{the north bridge} and the face at the yard's edge stayed on three places with a finger between every pair, and no witness, reader, sale, payer or tenth hand stood under any of them. The carried fire gave four low pulls with the silence where the fifth should have stood, and nobody lifted the floor or supplied the pull; its payer was unnamed four hundred and sixteen days and Garl's name was unspoken the same number, and the fire and the man stood side by side without becoming one.

The second man's narrow slate with its writing turned to the sleeve and his direction down the bridge road sat on their own places with a finger between them and the first man's face, the clerk's entered figure and the fifth shaping, and no name, owner, answer, sale, witness, tenth hand or rule stood under any of them. The direction had not been repeated on a day that had earned only its twenty-second and he did not set it down as a custom or look for it a second time to make a habit of it; it was a shape gathering interest toward a far-off hand that was not his and had not come, and he let it gather without calling it. The fifth shaping of \lore{the well-saying} sat on its own tray between \person[Hessa Rale]'s eighth hand and the saying of the road from \place{the Cross} to \place{Greybank}, with a third finger between it and the four ownerless directions; Hessa's narrow slate stayed unread up the road and Ilva's strip stayed in her sleeve with her ninth hand nine, and no figure came off any wood. Tarn's withheld sentence was found where put and not made a habit; the softening at \place{the river}'s skin was found where put and called weather, not a sign, not a fourth covering, not a payment and not made again. \person[Berr Clay]'s low saying about kindness being entered stayed on a place of its own with no payer standing, his \emph{your house} beside it, his belief left up the road with the box where it had gone. The twelve old arithmetics stood behind one unsigned figure beside the two readings of the year, two and not one account, the first not a thirteenth, the figure read still not the figure paid, no signature on the space and no doctrine out of two. Every kept thing lay where it lay, advanced and not paid, gathering interest separately toward a far-off hand that was not his; none stood up and called itself a rule, and he did not lay one across another to make a row come out even, for paper is not a payer and no key lay in his hand.

The hand outside the wool came up an inch toward the note under the winter skin. The inch was not a reach. Through the finger's-width door the river kept its note of \lore{water over water}, a low thing over a lower thing under the covering, found where put and not made a sign. He took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole on the note's own finishing under the covering --- neither early nor stayed --- and let it go as the note ended. Then the hand came straight down and laid itself flat against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking.

Answer four hundred and seven came out and was filed on the sixth place. It was exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the twelve readings, the first and second of the new count, the printed figure, the fifth shaping, the clerk's entered figure, the face, the degree, the softening, the bell's gap, the flat knock, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the held breath, the two bites of iron, the winter skin, the widow's mark nor the keeping at the fold. Six honest answers stood on the sixth place, four hundred and two through four hundred and seven, and a seventh would stand there by the chapter's close; not one was a doctrine. Count four hundred and sixteen exact, six months and two hundred and thirty-six days told by nobody who would have known what a number counted. The carried fire's fifth was unpaid the same number and its payer unnamed the same number; Garl's name was unspoken the same number, and the fire and the man stood side by side without becoming one. Under the exactness, louder, the infant wanted the crust lifted, the thread wiped and a hand on the pot board's handle, and got none of the three; both were true, the second was louder, neither won, and the quarrel was the work. The crust had gone hard as old horn with its corner a hair off the wool and the thread was frozen and unwiped; four low pulls breathed below the floor with the fifth unsupplied; the two readings of the year were up the clay road in a locked box with no key in his hand; the second man's slate and direction were kept where put and not repeated; the softening was kept as weather; the bell's gap stayed in its square. The house went on as a house goes when a pot is set down in its place and the fire kept --- forward, into an afternoon not yet earned and not brought into this morning. Count four hundred and sixteen exact, and no number stood up as a threshold.
