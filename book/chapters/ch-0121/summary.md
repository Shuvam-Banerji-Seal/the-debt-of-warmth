## Chronicle

Three days came under the Vail roof, and the house kept them badly, in loaves pushed a finger's width along a board and stopped. The boy kept four hundred and fourteen, fifteen and sixteen exact against it and stood no number up as a threshold. Sella came to the hearth before the basket, told the coals it was the thaw, and lifted the twice-mended pot thumb-first with the hairline showing. Repair was not restoration.

On the twentieth Berr Clay came up short off the clay road with the red cord of his office folded under his coat. He wiped his thumb twice, passed a finger over the figure through his sleeve, and read *One in twelve, as the notice says* twice over the basket. He called the levy, denied it with *your house*, never *I*, sent a man's number from a shut fist to a slate at the watch, and went off with the box locked, believing he had done a kindness. Something low he said to the block about kindness, and the stone answered nothing.

Two readings of the year stood behind one unsigned figure; two were two and not one account, the first was not made a thirteenth by the second, the figure read was still not the figure paid. He did not lay the twelve old arithmetics across the two new ones to make a row come out even, for paper is not a payer and the key had gone up a road. Hessa carried the slate up before noon unread; the clerk below the north bridge stayed a report; the bell went four, four and a gap.

On the twenty-first a second man from Slate Row went down the north bridge road before first grey with a narrow slate under his arm and the writing turned toward his sleeve, and no word of his carried. The boy took the passing apart by the order of its parts and filed the direction and the slate on places of their own beside the first man's face, the clerk's entered figure and the fifth shaping of the well-saying, with a finger between every pair, giving the man no name, owner or interior. At the well Ilva drew with her pail against her knee, her hand going to her sleeve before her eyes to the asker, her strip unread and her ninth hand nine.

The evenings gave back the ordinary grammar of pail, rope and loaf. The softening at the river's skin was found where put and called weather, and he did not look twice, for looking twice is how a finding becomes a habit. Six answers, four hundred and three through four hundred and eight, were filed on the sixth place, worth nothing at two inches of air, and not one a doctrine. The crust stood hard as old horn; the thread was frozen and unwiped; four low pulls breathed below the floor with the fifth unsupplied. The hand came down flat on the fold.
