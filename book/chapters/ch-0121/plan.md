**Place.** Small hours at the low angle of the loaf shelf; the hearth, the pot board, the drying racks, the rack end, the loaf shelf; the nineteen steps down and up; the kindling and the flagstones, the Vail wheel-shed edge. **Present:** Corin/Dan, Sella Vail, Tarn, Jorra Vail; Ilva glimpsed once at the well with the strip unread or kept offstage, her ninth hand nine either way; Garl Vail in objects only. Walk-ons at the ordinary Secondday grammar. **No** Berr, Hessa sighting, man from Slate Row, collector, officer, reading, market, Cross or bell; no second asking at the well, no repetition of the second man's direction, no sixth shaping, seventh hearing, tenth hand, eighth opening or eleventh arithmetic; no slate opened within reach of the basket. New day, new date, no recap; nothing from Ilmen 21 crosses forward as sign, lesson, mending, cause, payment or payer, and Ilmen 30 stands eight mornings off and is not brought.

**Beats, in order.**
1. Small hours. The hand is down on the fold on answer 406. The surgeon takes the body in its old order: the crust leathery and hardened through the dry nights into old horn two inches from the ear with its corner a hair off the wool, the fold found where put and still a finding, the thread frozen and unwiped, cold stopping at cheek, temple and the small bare wrist-joint as at the end of a line written out for it, the Afterdrop waiting past a warming nobody here will perform. He knows a basket set level out of the draught where the heat comes sideways is the rewarming he would choose and cannot ask for. The infant wants the crust lifted, the thread wiped and a hand on the pot handle; both true, the second louder, neither winning, the quarrel the work (T060/T086).
2. First light. Count becomes **416** exact, six months and two hundred and thirty-six days, told by nobody. He does not stand the number up as a threshold, a doctrine or a figure, and does not lay the twelve old arithmetics or the two readings of the year across one another to make a row come out even, for paper is not a payer and no key lies in his hand (T071/T072, T015/T041/T047).
3. Sella comes to the hearth before the basket, tells the coals it is the thaw, turns them with the back of a broken pot, keeps her finger above the unread charcoal and her lips on a two-gapped row with neither gap figured, glances once at the clay jar cold under lead glaze with the hand half over nothing, lifts the twice-mended pot thumb-first where the wire crosses the crack with the hairline showing and gives both proverbs to the pot and the fire, turns the dressed grain under wool with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child and with no figure or handful, and sets the pot rim two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat and no look toward the bed. Repair stays apart from restoration (T058/T079/T100).
4. The carrying-down and -up: nineteen steps at the half-pace from every open slate, instruction to the wool, the river wind, the pot, the fire and the banked fire and never to the basket; once around the seven walked-around places with a look and a wide step and no number, once and not twice, widest at the returning-line place; set under the roof and never in the Debt Loft; the fold righted at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face and no second fold added. The widow's mark hangs where it hangs: a morning, a shelf, a yard and a bed are not openings, seven openings in seven months are seven and not a rule and no eighth came due (T009/T013/T092, T098, T058/T079).
5. Tarn at the kindling keeps six pieces in the worn thumb-mark where the house can check and the seventh at the lip beside Garl's unforged collector sentence, the small knife on its sill, his hand over blade and sill and away with no breath held at the top of the reach. The sentence is found where put and not made a habit, and the silence of the eighteenth is not repeated on a day that earned only its twenty-second; the Seed 16 breath stays unspent and the loan uncalled at the very end of a term already ended (T036/T076/T006 held). Jorra's meal-fine grey dust, the wheel spoken to before any person, the pot told *You'll do* with its full note withheld, the damper's two bites and the withheld note hold; *he's not Vail* stays sealed and the promise neither given nor withdrawn (T062/T035/T031 held).
6. Answer **407** filed on the sixth place: exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer. Six honest answers on the sixth place grow to seven by the chapter's close; not one is a doctrine.

**Emotional turn.** From the exact, worthless inventory into the day's honest work and back to another answer filed where the others stand. The count reaches a round-sounding number and is not stood up as a line.

**Sensory anchors.** Crust hardened into old horn; stiff unwiped thread; dry kiln heat; the pot's hairline between staple and wire; the wheel-shed's grey dust; the knife at the edge of its sill; cold at the small bare wrist-joint; the river wind through the finger's-width door.

**Threads advanced (none paid).** T060/T086 (body inventory, quarrel of the three wants); T071/T072 (416 exact; answer 407); T015/T041/T047 held (two readings not laid across twelve, no key); T058/T079/T100 (hearth grammar, widow's mark untouched); T009/T013/T092 and T098 (seven walked-around places); T036/T076/T006 and T062/T035/T031 held.

**Foreshadowing planted / paid off.** Planted: nothing new; the second man's direction, the fifth shaping, the clerk's entered figure, Tarn's withheld sentence and the softening gather interest on their separate places and do not repeat. Paid off: nothing.

**Exact last image.** The hand outside the wool laid flat against the fold on answer 407, the count exact at 416 and no number stood up as a threshold.

## Scene 6 — The Writing Kept Where Put (Day 416 · afternoon → going of the light → small hours; chapter close)

**Place.** The Clay Yard: yard well, Vail corner, trough, returning-line place; then the hearth, the pot board, the rack end and the bed under the roof. **Present:** Corin/Dan, Sella Vail; Garl Vail in objects. Offstage with keepings found where put: Tarn at kindling and flagstones; Jorra at the wheel-shed and cold damper; Ilva with her strip in her sleeve and her ninth hand nine; Hessa's slate unread up the road with her eighth hand eighth; Berr up the clay road with the box locked and the two readings of the year inside; the clerk's inward slate and entered figure reported at the landing; the first man from Slate Row's face and the second man's narrow slate with its writing turned to the sleeve and his direction down the north bridge road on their own places; the fifth shaping on its own tray; the softening kept as weather; the green at the pot board and the shape at the market's edge as sightings with no eighth. Walk-ons only at the yard grammar. No second well-telling, no market, no collector, no present-tense break and no `\memory{}` tag; no sixth hearing, tenth hand, tenth stopping, eighth opening, sixth shaping, eleventh arithmetic, signature, payer, witness or reader. Day already dated in Scene 5.

**Beats, in order.**
1. Afternoon. The boy takes the yard's small sounds first because they do not lie and the large ones are mostly weather. He goes around the seven walked-around places once and not twice with a look and a wide step and no number, widest at the returning-line place; the wide step is a keeping and not a sign, for a figure set against it would want a payer standing and none stood (T098, T009/T013/T092). The trough stands bare and dark, the gone pan keeps its round print and the called-gone grey stays beside the left-in-place ridge with a finger between.
2. Costs arrive as directions and go away as weather and leave no entry: a figure in a fist goes toward the well, looks at the light and goes back into a sleeve and arrives nowhere; a woman sent to ask what a pot cost gets a note and not a price; the dressed grain lies covered and unfigured and the first and second readings of the year, read over it in their own mornings, did not know what it cost and no signature stands on the space between (T077).
3. Through the finger's-width door the river keeps its note of water over water under its winter skin, softer at one edge where the low note ran nearer on the nineteenth, found where put on that day and not made a sign, a fourth covering or a payment on a tend-day that earned only its twenty-second (T083/T085/T092). He does not look twice.
4. Going of the light. Sella keeps the whole hearth grammar with repair kept apart from restoration: the twice-mended pot lifted thumb-first, the hairline showing between old-brass staple and tighter wire, both proverbs to the pot and the fire and never to a person, the grain turned under wool with no figure or handful, the pot rim two inches beyond the reaching hand with its half-beat and no look toward the bed, the nineteen steps at the half-pace, the fold righted without a second fold. The widow's mark hangs where it hangs and no eighth opening comes due (T058/T079/T100, T009/T013/T092).
5. Small hours. The towel comes out at the head of the bed as instruments come out when a case is done and before the next is called, rough from the wash and smelling of tallow and low smoke. He goes over the field by the place of each thing with every gap left a gap:
   - the clerk's entered figure, Tarn's degree and the face at the yard's edge on three places with a finger between every pair; the first man's face and the second man's slate and direction on their own places with a finger between every pair and no name, owner, answer, sale, witness, tenth hand or rule under any of them, a reaching gathering interest toward the far-off calling (T080/T090 advanced, not paid);
   - the fifth shaping on its own tray between Hessa's eighth hand and the road saying, with a third finger between it and the four ownerless directions; Hessa's slate unread and Ilva's strip unread, the hands eighth and ninth with no tenth;
   - Tarn's withheld sentence found where put and not made a habit; the softening at the river's skin found where put and not made a sign;
   - Berr's low saying about kindness entered on a place of its own with no payer standing, his *your house* beside it, his belief left up the road with the box;
   - the twelve old arithmetics behind one unsigned figure beside the first and second readings of the year — two readings two and not one account, the first not made a thirteenth by the second, the figure read still not the figure paid, no signature on the space and no doctrine out of two (T015/T041/T047);
   - six hearings of the full note beside and never among with the seventh place empty and the Drawing's flat knock outside on its own ground; no seventh came on any of the three days;
   - the heard bell in its square beside the told, four, four and a gap, its gap not folded into the fire's four and a gap, the boards' four and four and a knot, or the two readings behind one unsigned figure beside the twelve — three counts in three places staying three, because agreeing any two would want a payer put under both and none stood (T078, T061/T082, T081);
   - nine record-first hands with no tenth and nine stoppings with no tenth; four rooms with the same gap and no Court room a fifth likeness;
   - the carried fire's four pulls with the fifth unsupplied, its payer unnamed four hundred and sixteen days and Garl's name unspoken the same number, the fire and the man standing side by side without becoming one (T088);
   - the keeping at the fold found where put, still a finding and not a placing, no second fold, third crust, name, gift, payment or payer (T060/T086/T097).
6. Answer **408** comes out and is filed on the sixth place: exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the twelve readings, the first and second of the new count, the printed figure, the fifth shaping, the clerk's entered figure, the second man's slate, Tarn's degree, the face at the yard's edge, Jorra's ungiven promise, the withheld sentence, the softening, the bell's gap, the flat knock, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the held breath, the two bites of iron, the winter skin, the widow's mark nor the keeping at the fold. Six honest answers stand on the sixth place, 403 through 408, and not one is a doctrine. Count four hundred and sixteen exact, six months and two hundred and thirty-six days, told by nobody who would have known what a number counted; the fire's fifth unpaid and Garl's name unspoken the same number. He does not stand four hundred and sixteen up as a threshold or a figure and borrows no payer from the arithmetics gone up the road, for paper is not a payer. No eighth opening, seventh hearing, sixth shaping, tenth hand, tenth stopping, eleventh arithmetic, signature, payer, witness, reader or payment stood on any of the three days.
7. The surgeon's exactness and the infant's want stand side by side and neither buys the other; under them, louder, the wanting goes on: the crust lifted two inches, the thread wiped, a hand on the handle of the pot. He wants the three things without knowing that wanting them is a want, and gets none of them. Both are true. The second is louder. Neither won, and no verdict was looked for; the quarrel was the work, and the work went on in silence in a room whose floor does not touch his. A voice in a yard would be a voice in a yard and not a door; the second man's direction and the slate turned to the sleeve are kept where put and not called.
8. The hand outside the wool comes up an inch toward the river's note, the inch not a reach. He takes a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, holds the interval whole on the note's own finishing under the covering — neither early nor stayed — and lets it go as the note ends. Then the hand comes straight down and lays itself flat against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. He does not wipe the thread and does not lift the crust. Four low pulls breathe below the floor with the fifth unsupplied. Nothing has been joined, named, called, paid or ended, and the house goes on as a house goes when a pot is set down in its place and the fire kept — forward, into a morning not yet earned and not brought into this one. Count four hundred and sixteen exact, and no number stood up as a threshold.

**Emotional turn.** From the yard's kept absences into the small-hours review, where every gap is left a gap and exactness is worth nothing at two inches of air; the chapter closes on the hunger that is louder than the knowing and on a reaching that has been given no name.

**Sensory anchors.** Pail on stone; rope's rasp; the thinned Ash tang at the corner; the twice-mended pot's hairline under the thumb; tallow and low smoke off the towel; the river's low note under its winter skin; the stiff unwiped thread; the crust's hair-lifted corner; four low pulls under the floor and the silence where the fifth should stand.

**Threads advanced (none paid).** T098, T009/T013/T092, T077, T083/T085/T092 (held), T058/T079/T100, T080/T090 (**second man's slate and direction advanced, not paid**), T015/T041/T047 (two readings two, not one account), T061/T082, T078, T081, T088, T060/T086/T097, T071/T072 (answer 408, count 416 exact), T004 (no present-tense break and no tagged memory).

**Foreshadowing planted / paid off.** Planted: the second man's direction and turned slate (new T102) gather interest toward the far-off calling of the loan that can hear and the far-off hand that will lay the district's private keepings together; the two readings and Berr's kindness go on their own trays toward the day the kindness is remembered against him; Tarn's valuable ear and ended term, Jorra's ungiven promise, the burned slate at five, the green, the shape and the four Ash and Slate directions stand where put and do not repeat. Ilmen 30 (Day 424) stands eight mornings off as a date. Paid off: nothing — no sixth hearing, tenth hand, tenth stopping, eighth opening, sixth shaping, eleventh arithmetic, signature, payer, witness, reader, payment, third crust, name for a gap or ending, and the unsent call to Nina not paid again (T020 held).

**Exact last image.** The hand laid flat against the fold on answer 408 as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking; the crust's corner a hair off the wool and the thread unwiped; four low pulls under the floor with the fifth unsupplied; the second man's slate and direction kept on their own places with a finger between them and every other kept thing.
