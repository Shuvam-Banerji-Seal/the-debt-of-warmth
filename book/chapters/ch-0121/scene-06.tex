The afternoon came on without a bell and without a word said over the basket, the way light comes through a finger's-width door --- not arriving, only being there longer than it had been. \person[Corin]{The boy} sat low in the wicker at the edge of \place{the Clay Yard}, where the world reached him in pieces: a stone's edge, a length of rope, the undersides of things, the small smell of damp hemp and cold clay that the afternoon keeps after the sun has gone off it. The large sounds went over him and the small sounds came down to him, and he took the small ones first because they do not lie and the large ones are mostly weather. A pail went down on its stone where a pail always stood. A rope settled into its knot with the stiff rasp that cold puts into hemp. A loaf was pushed a finger's width along a board and stopped, and nobody called the stopping a figure. Barley passed from wall to wall in two voices that never met in a number. He took the order of each small thing as he had once taken a field at rest --- what was there, what was not, and the half-beat lying in the gap where an answer would have stood. Nothing in the yard had been told what the day was, and the basket had not been told to be still.

He went around \place{the seven walked-around places} with a look and a wide step and no number, once and not twice, though the going of the light was still ahead of him and he would go around them again then as he had gone around them at morning. They kept their places under the afternoon as they kept them under the dark: the stone lip, the worn boards, the strip of hard mud, the place where a wheel had stood, the two narrow gaps between the stacked pots, and \place{the returning-line place}, widest of all, where a hand had gone in on \emph{Seed 15} and had not come out. The wide step there was a keeping and not a sign. A figure set against it would want a payer standing and none stood. He did not look twice. Looking twice is how a finding becomes a habit, and a habit wants a hand that put the thing there, and there was no such hand under any roof. Keeping the step was not the same as making a rule of it; a rule is an account that has found a payer, and none stood.

\place{The trough} stood bare and dark, the thin skin that had lain on it since the turn gone and its rim still showing the line where the water used to be. The gone pan kept its shallow round print on a place of its own, a ring where a thing had been lifted away, and he did not lay that ring across the loaf's stopping or across the ridge. At \place{the Vail corner} the clean dark line kept its finer grey beside it, thickened in the dry cold with the faint sour tang of \lore{Ash} coming up off it where no broom had gone in two years. The called-gone grey stayed beside the left-in-place ridge with a finger between. No agreement had been made to stop looking at the ridge, and a thing left in place is not a thing forgiven. The tang was thinner in this afternoon air than it had been, as it had been on the nineteenth, and he found the softening where put and did not look twice at it, for it was weather entered nowhere and not made again. He did not promote the Ash's memory into a sign.

Costs arrived as directions and went away as weather and left no entry. A man come down \place{the clay road} brought a number up out of a shut fist toward \place{the yard well}, held it where the light could see it, looked at it, and put it back into his sleeve; it arrived nowhere. He took the order apart as he took every field apart --- fist, eye, mouth, the half-beat lying in the gap --- and gave the fist no figure and the sleeve no motive, for a motive is a payer wearing a coat. A woman sent to ask what a pot cost was sent to the pot and got a note and not a price. The dressed grain lay covered and unfigured at \place{the rack end}, and the two readings of the year, read over it in their own mornings, had not known what it cost; no signature stood on the space between the figure and the price and he set none there. Paper is not a payer. The key to the box had gone up the road in a coat pocket that never comes back down and there was no key under any roof and none in his hand.

Through \place{the finger's-width door} \place{the river} kept its note of \lore{water over water}, a low thing over a lower thing under the winter skin, softer at one edge where the low note had run nearer on the nineteenth. He found it where put and did not make a sign of it, a fourth covering, a payment or a lesson on a tend-day that had earned only its twenty-second of \place{Ilmen}. The note came from under the covering and not from the covering; it was not a hearing, not one of \lore{the three coverings}, not one of the six hearings of \lore{the full note}, not a seventh of anything, and no hand or payer stood under it. It ended on its own finishing, before any hand went up and after no hand stayed it. The afternoon went on over it as an afternoon goes, with a pail on its stone and a rope in its knot, and nobody owning the day.

The going of the light came on without a bell and without a word said over the basket, and \person{Sella Vail} came to \place{the hearth} before she came to it, as she had at both ends of every day since he was laid under this roof. She told the coals it was \lore{the thaw} in the voice another woman would have used for a child who could not hear her, turned them with the back of a broken pot, gave the small intake, the hold and the letting-go toward no opening, figuring neither of the two gaps. \emph{The house will keep it} went to the fire and not to the basket, because a thing said aloud becomes a thing the Court can enter. Her finger lay along the top of \place{the loaf shelf} so the charcoal tally beneath stayed unread, and her lips moved on a two-gapped row with neither gap figured. One glance went to the clay jar standing cold under \lore{lead glaze} beyond \place{the pot board}; the hand shut half over nothing and the little-door burn on the back of her left hand closed on air, and went back to the pot.

Her thumb went first to where the tighter wire crossed the crack, and only then did the weight come on the handle; the hairline showed between the old-brass staple and the wire, a dark thread with a hand's work on either side. Both proverbs went to the pot and the fire and never to a person: \emph{Leave the crack showing. People trust a thing they can see has been handled} and \emph{If it's worth mending it's worth mending twice. The second time is the real one}. Repair was not restoration; a restored thing hides the hand that handled it, and a mended thing leaves the hand showing. She said \emph{The fire doesn't know you're sorry. Feed it} to the fire, which did not know and was fed. At the rack end she turned the dressed grain piece by piece under the wool with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child, set each down where it had lain, took no figure from it and no handful, and drew the wool back. Then she set the pot rim two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat and no look toward \place{the bed}. The half-beat was the distance between the intention and the arrest; she did not measure it and did not give it a name, for a named gap wants a payer put under it and none stood.

She carried him up the nineteen steps at the half-pace she keeps from every open slate, correcting the tilt at every turn of the landing before it settled into the wicker; the instruction went to the wool, the river wind, the pot, the fire and the banked fire and never to the basket. She went around the seven walked-around places once and not twice with a look and a wide step and no number, widest around the returning-line place, and set him under the roof, in \place{the Vail kiln-house} at \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill}, \place{Grath}, and never in \place{the Debt Loft}. She put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face and added no second fold. The thread at his mouth stayed frozen and unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread.

\person{Garl Vail} stayed where Garl stays, in objects and not in a story: the salt-stiff coat with its crease at the left shoulder on its peg behind the door, the hollow wheel-seat in the shed keeping its twenty-year hollow on that side that nobody was allowed to fill, the clean dark line at the corner with the finer grey beside it called the thaw, the twice-mended pot on the board with the hairline showing, the unforged collector sentence lying beside the seventh notch at \place{the kindling} with no name on the man who turned back at the frozen ford, and \lore{the tile debt} against a firing of tiles circling without a name and worse for being uncalled. His name had not been spoken under this roof for four hundred and sixteen days, and the two counts --- his name and \lore{the carried fire}'s fifth --- stood side by side without becoming one. \lore{The widow's mark} hung where it hung and was not touched; a shelf, a yard, a going of the light and a bed are not openings, seven openings in seven months are seven and not a rule, and no eighth came due on a day that had earned only its twenty-second.

She went back to her fire saying nothing at all --- not of the man whose slate had gone down the bridge road with the writing turned away, not of the figure the clerk had entered before he looked up, not of the two readings up the road in a box with no key, not of the sentence found where put at the kindling, not of the softening kept as weather. What she said to the fire was the thaw and the house, and the words went into the coals and stayed there as a stain stays in a board. He had once sorted twenty people at a bay door by what they would not survive and had kept his hands steady doing it; now the hands were soft and small and could not close two inches on the rim of a basket. Both kinds of knowing stood side by side in the room and neither bought the other.

\place{The towel} came out at the head of the bed as instruments come out when a case is done and before the next is called, rough from the wash and smelling of tallow and low smoke. He went over the day by the place of each thing and left every gap a gap. Six hearings of the full note stood beside and never among, with the seventh place empty and not dug for or filled, and \place{the Drawing house}'s small flat knock stayed outside them on its own ground; neither the knock nor a told gap was made a seventh hearing, and no seventh had come on any of the three days.

The clerk's entered figure, \person{Tarn}'s degree toward \place{the out-of-line landing} below \place{the north bridge}, and the face at the yard's edge stayed on three places with a finger between every pair, and no witness, reader, sale, payer or tenth hand stood under any of them. The first man's face and the second man's narrow slate with its writing turned to the sleeve and his direction down the bridge road sat on places of their own with a finger between them and every other kept thing; no name, owner, answer, sale, witness, tenth hand or rule stood under any of them, and the direction had not been repeated on a day that had earned only its twenty-second. It was a shape gathering interest toward a far-off calling and a far-off hand that had not come, and he let it gather without calling it. A voice in a yard would be a voice in a yard and not a door; the slate turned to the sleeve was kept where put and not opened within reach of the basket.

The fifth shaping of \lore{the well-saying} sat on its own tray between \person[Hessa Rale]{Hessa}'s eighth record-first hand and the saying of the road from \place{the Cross} to \place{Greybank}, with a third finger between it and the four ownerless directions. Hessa's narrow slate stayed unread up the road and \person{Ilva}'s strip stayed in her sleeve with her ninth hand nine, and no figure came off any wood; no sixth shaping, tenth hand or eleventh arithmetic stood on any of the three days, and no reader was made by the carrying or the counting. \person[Berr Clay]'s low saying about kindness being entered stayed on a place of its own with no payer standing, his \emph{your house} beside it, his belief left up the road with the box where it had gone. He had gone off believing he had done a kindness, and that belief stayed up there where it went and did not come under any roof as lesson or cause.

The twelve old arithmetics stood behind one unsigned figure beside the two readings of the year, two and not one account, the first not made a thirteenth by the second, the figure read still not the figure paid, no signature on the space and no doctrine out of two. He did not lay the twelve across the two to make a row come out even, for paper is not a payer and no key lay in his hand; the key had gone up in a coat pocket and did not come back down. Tarn's withheld sentence was found where put and not made a habit, not a tenth stopping, not a payment, not a hearing and not a rule; the softening at the river's skin was found where put and called weather, not a sign, not a fourth covering, not a payment and not made again. Every kept thing lay where it lay, advanced and not paid, gathering interest separately toward a far-off hand that was not his; none stood up and called itself a rule.

The heard bell stayed in its square beside the told bell of \lore{the Long Night}, four, four and a gap, and its gap was not folded into the carried fire's four and a gap under \place{the boards}, into the boards' four and four and a thumb-width knot where a fifth nail might have been driven and was not, or into the two arithmetics behind one unsigned figure beside the twelve; three counts in three places stayed three, because agreeing any two would have wanted a payer put under both and none stood. Nine record-first hands stood with no tenth and nine stoppings stood with no tenth; the clean-voice speaker was drawn out of his own sentences on a place of his own and the face at the yard's edge was filed beside the hands and not counted among them. Four rooms kept the same gap with a finger between every pair and no Court room came under the roof as a fifth likeness. The carried fire gave four low pulls with the silence where the fifth should have stood, and nobody lifted the floor or supplied the pull; its payer was unnamed four hundred and sixteen days and Garl's name was unspoken the same number, and the fire and the man stood side by side without becoming one. \lore{The keeping at the fold} was found where put and stayed a finding and not a placing, with no second fold, third crust, name, gift, payment or payer.

The hand outside the wool came up an inch toward the note under the winter skin. The inch was not a reach. Through the finger's-width door the river kept its note of water over water, found where put and not made a sign. He took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole on the note's own finishing under the covering --- neither early nor stayed --- and let it go as the note ended. Then the hand came straight down and laid itself flat against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking.

Answer four hundred and eight came out and was filed on the sixth place. It was exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the twelve readings, the first and second of the new count, the printed figure, the fifth shaping, the clerk's entered figure, the second man's slate, Tarn's degree, the face at the yard's edge, \person[Jorra Vail]{Jorra}'s ungiven promise, the withheld sentence, the softening, the bell's gap, the flat knock, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the held breath, the two bites of iron, the winter skin, the widow's mark nor the keeping at the fold. Six honest answers stood on the sixth place, four hundred and three through four hundred and eight, the six the chapter had earned, two to each of its three days, and not one was a doctrine. Count four hundred and sixteen exact, six months and two hundred and thirty-six days told by nobody who would have known what a number counted. The carried fire's fifth was unpaid the same number and its payer unnamed the same number; Garl's name was unspoken the same number, and the fire and the man stood side by side without becoming one. He did not stand four hundred and sixteen up as a threshold or a figure and borrowed no payer from the arithmetics gone up the road, for paper is not a payer. No eighth opening, seventh hearing, sixth shaping, tenth hand, tenth stopping, eleventh arithmetic, signature, payer, witness, reader or payment stood on any of the three days.

The surgeon's exactness and the infant's want stood side by side and neither bought the other; under them, louder, the wanting went on. The crust lifted two inches, the thread wiped, a hand on the handle of the pot. He wanted the three things without knowing that wanting them was a want and got none of them. Both were true. The second was louder. Neither won, and no verdict was looked for; the quarrel was the work, and the work went on in silence in a room whose floor does not touch his. He returned once to the direction down the bridge road and the slate turned to the sleeve, found them where put, and did not call them, for a voice in a yard is a voice in a yard and not a door and a reaching given no name is still only a reaching. The river's low note ended on its own finishing under the covering and the crust stood with its corner a hair off the wool and the thread frozen and unwiped. He did not wipe the thread and he did not lift the crust. Four low pulls breathed below the floor with the fifth unsupplied, and the silence stood as a silence and not as a debt anybody owed him. Nothing had been joined, named, called, paid or ended. The house went on as a house goes when a pot is set down in its place and the fire kept --- forward, into a morning not yet earned and not brought into this one. Count four hundred and sixteen exact, and no number stood up as a threshold.
