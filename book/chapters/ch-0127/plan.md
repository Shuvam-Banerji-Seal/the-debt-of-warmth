# Chapter 127 — beat sheet: *The Fourth Figure*

Days 432–434, Silt 8–10, Y.H. 814, Eighthday to Tenthday. Normal chapter, six scenes, two per day, ~19,900 target words. Three days lived, none skipped, none poured into another. Count opens at 431 exact on answer 438 and closes at 434 exact on answer 444, six honest answers 439–444 on the sixth place, two to each day, not one a doctrine. The carried fire's fifth is unpaid and Garl's name unspoken 432, 433 and 434 days, the two counts side by side and never one. Three readings of the year stand behind one unsigned figure at the opening; after Silt 10 four stand, four being four and not one account, the figure read still not the figure paid.

**The chapter's one large change:** Silt 10, the fourth reading of Y.H. 814 and the first of Silt, is lived by its own morning — advanced and NOT paid (T015/T041/T047). Nothing from Silt 6's reported second slate or Silt 7's hair-thin thaw crosses into any of these three days as sign, lesson, mending, cause, payment or payer; neither is repeated, made a custom, a sixth shaping, a sign, a witness, a reader or a payer, and the account-day is not made to answer the neighbour's near-reaching at the shelf. No present-tense break (Chapter 113 spent the volume's one break; 114–126 paid none) and no `\memory{}` tag. Point of view never leaves the basket: the Debt Loft a sound from above and behind, the back room a shape and a dust, the Drawing a room seen and not entered, the landing a report brought up the yard and never a sighting.
