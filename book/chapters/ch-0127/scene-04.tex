The light went over the ridge and lay at a new angle on the same boards, and the afternoon of the ninth day opened without an edge to it, as the morning had opened. \person[Corin]{The boy} was carried down again after the mid-day and set out of the draught in \place{the Clay Yard}, \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill}, \place{Grath}, where \person{Sella Vail} could keep an eye on him between her pail and her broom. The instruction went to the wool, the river wind, the pot, the fire and the banked fire and never to the basket, and the basket was not told what the hour was or told to be still. Two inches from his ear the crust stood where it had stood in the morning, gone into old horn with its corner a hair off the wool. Beside it the empty place held \lore{the keeping at the fold}, found where put and still a finding, with no second fold, no third crust, no name and no payer. At his mouth the thread had gone stiffer in the dry heat and was unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread. Cold had stopped at cheek, temple and the small bare joint at the wrist where the wool rode back, and had stopped there as at the end of a line written out for it; \lore{The Afterdrop} waited past a warming nobody in this house would perform.

He took the small sounds before the large, because the small ones did not lie and the large ones were mostly weather. A pail went down on \place{the flat stone by the well} with a flat knock that had no note in it and wanted none. At \place{the yard well} the rope settled into its knot with the cold-stiffened rasp, complaining while the weight came on it and going quiet when the weight was taken away. Barley went wall to wall without a number. A loaf was pushed a finger's width along a board and stopped, and nobody called the stopping a figure. He took each apart by the place of it --- the fist that arrived, the eye that checked, the mouth that said or held its peace, and the half-beat between them, a place and not a person, an interval with no intention standing in it, for an intention wants a payer under it and none stood.

The bucket-round had taken up the rope and set it down again where it belonged. “A rope that holds is a rope that holds,” a woman said, and let the sentence rest on the stone beside the pail. Another answered the question put and not the one behind it. “The cold has got into it,” she said. “It won't come out before \lore{the thaw}.” Neither owned the day. The argument ended where yard arguments end, at the bucket on its stone, with the rope holding what the rope was given and asking nothing back. He took the two sayings apart by the place of each and let them stand where they fell: a rope that holds is a rope that holds, and the cold is in the rope and will not come out. Both were true and neither bought the other. The half-beat between them he left a gap, for an account that has found a payer wants a man standing under it and none stood. A number said twice is two numbers, and the cleanest part of the slate is the space where the signature would stand.

A man waiting his turn opened a shut fist toward the water, looked at what the light made of it, shut the fist and put it back into a sleeve; the number arrived nowhere. “What'll it want,” he said, and the answer came back as weather, carried on the wind off the river and gone again on the same wind. A woman sent to ask what a pot cost was given a note folded once with a mark on it that was not a price; she turned the note over, put it in her sleeve and went on about her errand. The figure read over the dressed grain covered at home did not know what the grain had cost, and the ink-neat space between figure and price carried no signature. Costs came as directions and went away as weather and left no entry under any roof.

\scenebreak

He filed each on a place of its own with a finger between every pair. Beside the pail's knock and the rope's rasp and the loaf's stopping stood the offered figure gone back into its sleeve, the well-asking answered with weather, the second man's turned slate and direction, the first man's patient face, the clerk's entered figure brought up the yard as a report and not a sighting, the fifth shaping of the well-saying, the three likenesses of \place{Slate Row}, the neighbour's near-reaching at the loaf shelf, the reported second slate of Silt 6 and the hair-thin thaw of Silt 7. None was stood up as a rule, a witness, a tenth hand, a sixth shaping or a doctrine, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood. The two sayings of the bucket-round he filed on their own places too, and did not lay one across the other, for a rope that holds and a rope that has taken the cold are two arithmetics and not one account. He made no fourth likeness of the soft river edge and no sixth shaping of the two greys, and gave the gap no intention. There was no hand in the yard to make a joining of any of it, and the hand that one day would was far off and not his.

\scenebreak

Sella went once around \place{the seven walked-around places} with the basket, with a look and a wide step and no number, once and not twice. Widest was the step around \place{the returning-line place}, where a hand had gone in on \emph{Seed 15} and had not come back out. No name went with the wide step, no body, no figure for what any month had cost, no accident written across the space. The step was a keeping and not a sign; a sign would want somebody standing under it to say what it meant, and nobody stood. At \place{the trough} the bare dark water kept the shallow round print of the clay pan lifted away, a ring on a place of its own, not laid across the loaf's stopping and not laid across the ridge. The water-line stood level in a vessel nobody had calibrated, and level is not honest and a level is not a promise.

At \place{the Vail corner} the clean dark line kept its finer grey beside it, the hairline called the thaw, thickened in the dry cold with the faint sour tang of \lore{Ash} coming up off it where no broom had gone in two years. Beside it the called-gone grey of the ridge lay left in place with a finger between, a thing left in place not a thing forgiven. Both were found where put and called weather, and he did not go around them a second time or look twice, and made no custom, no fourth covering, no sixth shaping, no sign and no payer of the finding. Through \place{the finger's-width door} \place{the river} kept its note of \lore{water over water}, a low thing over a lower thing under the winter skin, softer at one edge where the low note ran nearer under the covering; the sound came from under the covering and not from the covering, and the note ended on its own finishing. The softness was found where put and called weather, and nobody owned the day.

At \place{the kindling} and \place{the flagstones} \person{Tarn} kept six pieces in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check and the seventh at the lip, beside \person{Garl Vail}'s unforged collector sentence with no name on the man who turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on \lore{the tile debt}. Ash was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line, and the pale clip-ring at his left wrist caught and gave back the little light there was. The small knife lay at the very edge of its sill; his hand went over blade and sill as a man passes a hand over a tool put back where it belongs, and came away with no breath held at the top of the reach. The coming away was the whole of the checking. The sentence he had told the wood in the morning, \emph{Six counted. The next I don't count}, was found where put and stayed there; he did not say it a second time this afternoon and did not make a silence of the not-saying, for a second silence would be a custom and a third a way and both want a payer where none stood. The ear-sentence about an ear under this roof worth more than the debt that held it came all the way to the word that would call the loan and stopped there, a remembered ninth stopping with no tenth, at the very end of a term already ended. The Seed 16 breath stayed unspent. The loan was uncalled, and a term ending was not a payment, a forgiveness or a freedom; it left a man more exposed than it had found him, his valuable ear a thing that could hear and had never been called, the fear of being entered again for vagrancy still his without a figure, and the want he did not voice still there for a slate with his own name and no mark against it. He owned nothing and knew where every small thing was, and did not turn his head toward the landing.

\person{Jorra Vail} kept \place{the Vail wheel-shed} edge with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past his bent little finger and white in a fist-crease where a hand had opened, and it was not the bone-coloured \lore{slip} of the wheel. Three or more men unnamed had gone low into \place{the back room} before first grey and he gave the house no name for them and no word that the room was cold, while the broken ones under the lowest rack stayed entered by nobody. He kept dusty forearms and clean on one tray and parted them as clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had been broken nor whether it was a birth-slate. To the wheel he did not speak a second time. He knew the \lore{full note} a pot would give three turns before it left his hands, with air under it and a want in it, and withheld the knowing from any master come up from \place{High Kiln} who would call it early and sing a pot before its time. His bent finger went into the cold iron of \place{the damper} ahead of Sella's unfinished sentence; one bite of the iron held and one took, and he came out without leaving the extra holding of Ash 26 a second time, gathered once and not made again. There was no Grathic word for the two bites of one piece of iron and he gave the house neither. \emph{He's not Vail} stayed sealed and the promise to the men who burn slates stayed neither given nor withdrawn, the burned slate at five and the four ownerless directions going on gathering without being called or paid. The boy kept the two quiet men on places of their own under the one roof, his sentence beside Jorra's silence, maintenance and not a compact, and made no cause, custom or joining of them.

\scenebreak

At the going of the light Sella came to \place{the hearth} before she came to the basket, and the household straightened around the order of it as a table straightens when a weight is lifted off it. She told the coals it was the thaw and did not tell the basket, and said it in the voice another woman would have used for a child who could not hear her. She turned the coals with the back of a broken pot; the back scraped over them with the small dry sound of a tool that had done this work before and expected to do it again, and left the blind ash where it lay. She gave the small intake, the hold and the letting-go toward no opening, figuring neither of the two gaps; a gap measured is a gap with a payer put under it, and none stood. Her finger lay along the top of \place{the loaf shelf} so the charcoal tally beneath stayed unread; no hand went beneath, no mark was copied, no row was numbered. Her lips moved on a two-gapped row. One glance went to the clay jar standing cold under \lore{lead glaze} beyond \place{the pot board}; the hand shut half over nothing, and the little-door burn on the back of her left hand, two straight sides with a lintel and a threshold, closed on air and went back to the pot.

“\emph{Leave the crack showing. People trust a thing they can see has been handled},” she said to the pot; “\emph{If it's worth mending it's worth mending twice. The second time is the real one},” she said to the fire, and never to a person. She lifted the twice-mended pot thumb-first where the tighter wire crossed the crack, and the hairline of the original break showed between the older brass staple and the wire, a dark thread with a hand's work on either side. Repair was not restoration; a restored thing hides the hand that handled it, and a mended thing leaves the hand showing so that the next woman who takes it up can read that it was carried. At \place{the rack end} she turned the dressed grain under wool with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child, set each piece where it had lain and drew the wool back with the flat of her hand; no figure went over it and no handful was counted. Then she set the pot rim two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat and no look toward \place{the bed}. The half-beat was the distance between the intention and the arrest; she did not measure it and did not name it, for a named gap wants a payer put under it and none stood.

\lore{The widow's mark} hung where it hung and she did not touch it. She touched it before she opened \place{the kiln mouth} and at no other hour, and the kiln mouth was not open. A morning, a shelf, a pot, a yard, a basket and a going of the light are not openings; seven openings in seven months are seven and not a rule, and no eighth came due on a Ninthday that had earned only its ninth.

She carried him up \place{the nineteen steps} at the half-pace from every open slate and set him on his bed under the roof at the low angle of the loaf shelf, never in \place{the Debt Loft}. She put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face and added no second fold. The keeping in its empty place was found where put and stayed a finding, with no third crust, no name, no gift, no payment and no payer. The crust stood unmoved two inches from his ear, horn-hard in the dry cold with its corner a hair off the wool; the thread at his mouth stayed stiff and unwiped. The pot stood where she had set it, its rim two inches beyond the reaching hand, and the warmth in it went the way warmth goes in a cold room --- out, slowly, by the whole of its surface, and faster where the metal met the air. Under \place{the boards} \lore{the carried fire} kept four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood, and nobody lifted the floor to supply it. Its payer had been without a name for four hundred and thirty-three days. Garl Vail's name had gone unspoken the same four hundred and thirty-three days, the man and the fire standing side by side without becoming one; the salt-stiff coat kept its crease at the left shoulder on its peg behind the door, the wheel-seat in the shed kept its twenty-year hollow that nobody was allowed to fill, and the unforged collector sentence lay beside the seventh notch at the kindling with no figure on the tile debt. Neither the debt nor the fifth pull was called, paid, forgiven or made to answer the other. The kiln behind the wall breathed in fours with its gap, and the river under the door kept its low over lower under a skin, three counts in three places that did not become one, for agreeing any two would want a payer put under both and none stood.

The small hours came down under the roof the way silt comes down through still water --- not in a fall but in a settling, gathering first at the skirting and lying longest where nothing stirred it. \place{The towel} came out at the head of the bed as instruments come out when a case is done and before the next is called, rough from the wash and smelling of tallow and low smoke. He went over the day by the place of each thing with every gap left a gap: the pail's flat knock, the rope's cold-stiffened rasp, the two sayings of the bucket-round and the half-beat between them, barley wall to wall without a number, the loaf stopped a finger's width with nobody calling the stopping a figure; the seven walked-around places gone round once and not twice, widest at the returning-line place; the trough's round print on its own place; the two greys at the corner with a finger between; the softening at the river's skin found where put and called weather. Beside each, on places of their own with a finger between every pair, stood the offered figure and its return, the well-asking, the turned slate and its direction, the first man's face, the clerk's entered figure brought up the yard as a report and not a sighting, the fifth shaping, the three likenesses, the neighbour's near-reaching, the reported second slate and the hair-thin thaw. None was stood up as a rule, a witness, a tenth hand, a sixth shaping or a doctrine, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood.

Six hearings stood beside and never among, with the seventh place empty and \place{the Drawing}'s flat knock outside; the heard bell stood beside the told with both gaps in their own squares, and three counts in three places stayed three. Nine record-first hands and nine stoppings stood with no tenth; seven openings in seven months were seven and not a rule. \person{Ilva} was offstage with her count still in wood, her strip in her sleeve and her hand ninth with no tenth; \person{Hessa Rale}'s narrow slate stayed unread up \place{the clay road} with her eighth hand eighth; the soft-cuffed clerk's entered figure stayed a report brought up the yard from \place{the out-of-line landing} below \place{the north bridge}, never a sighting, a witness or a tenth hand; \person{Berr Clay} was gone up the road with the box locked and the key in a coat pocket that did not come back down, his \emph{your house} and his low saying about kindness entered each on a place of its own with a finger between, believed and not owed. Nothing from the reported second slate or the hair-thin thaw crossed under the roof as sign, lesson, mending, cause, payment or payer; a rope that holds was left holding and the cold in it was left where it had got in, and no argument at the well made a figure or a payer of either. Silt 10 waited one morning off as a date and was not brought into a Ninthday that had earned only its ninth.

The file turned out exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air. It was a restraint and not a payment. The surgeon's exactness and the infant's want stood side by side and neither bought the other: he knew the crust was old horn and would not nourish, that the thread was a small dry nothing, that a hand on the pot handle was a hand arriving at two inches of air and stopping; and he wanted the crust lifted, the thread wiped and a hand on the pot handle all the same. Both were true. The second was louder. Neither won and neither settled anything, and the quarrel went on in the dark without a verdict, because a verdict wants a hand to enter it and none stood.

Four hundred and thirty-three exact, six months and two hundred and fifty-three days, told by nobody who would have known what a number counted. He stood no number up as a threshold, a doctrine or a figure, for paper is not a payer and a number counted is a number with air around it and air buys no warmth. The carried fire's fifth was unpaid and Garl Vail's name unspoken the same number, and he did not lay the two counts together to make a row come out even. Answer four hundred and forty-two was filed on the sixth place --- exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air --- and laid across neither the reported second slate, the thaw, the neighbour's near-reaching nor any other kept thing. No sixth hearing, tenth hand, tenth stopping, eighth opening, sixth shaping, thirteenth arithmetic, signature or payer stood on a day that had earned only its ninth.

Through the finger's-width door the river kept its low over lower under its winter skin, softer at one edge where the note ran nearer under it, ending on its own finishing. The hand outside the wool came up an inch toward it --- the inch not a reach, not a wanting made into a claim, only the body's small assent to a sound --- took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole on the note's own finishing, let it go as the note ended, and came straight down and laid itself flat against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. There was no next hand in the room. He did not wipe the thread and did not lift the crust. Forward, into a tenth day not yet lived and not brought into this one.
