\daymark{409}{Ilmen 15, Y.H. 814, Fifthday}

The morning was arrived at in the dark before it was seen, the way a man comes into a room he has slept in before and finds the same wall the same distance from his hand; nobody brought it and nobody earned it on his behalf. First light was still a direction beyond \place{the finger's-width door}, and \place{the Vail kiln-house} held what warmth it had the way a pot holds water, with care, and with one crack in it nobody mentioned. \person[Corin]{The boy} lay at the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf} in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill}, \place{Grath}, under the roof and never in \place{the Debt Loft}, and the heat came to him sideways from \place{the hearth} and not where he would have chosen. One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust stood two inches from his ear with its corner a hair off the wool, and it had gone harder through another dry night so that its edge had the give of old horn instead of bread. Beside it the empty place held the fold of wool put there on the fifteenth, found where put and still a finding, not a placing, with no second fold and no name over it. The thread of drool at his mouth had gone stiff in the dry kiln heat and was unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread. Cold had come to cheek, temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, and stopped there as at the end of a line written out for it, with neither an order to go farther nor an order to go away. \lore{The Afterdrop} waited past a warming nobody in this house would perform.

Taking the body in its old order was the only work the room allowed him, and he took it: crust, fold, thread, cold, the waiting heat. He knew a face composed by cold is not composure, that the grimace of a cold man wears the shape of a smile and the smile means nothing, that pink returning is the surface still lying warm over a cold core, and that a cold patient cannot clot and must be rewarmed from outside and slowly, never hurried, never rubbed, never set too near the fire. A basket set level out of the draught where the warmth came sideways was the rewarming he would have chosen for such a patient, and he could not ask for it, and the asking would have gone to nobody who would understand what was being asked. He had once been a man who could reduce a shoulder in the dark and read a chart turned down at the signature; the knowing sat where it always sat, exact and useless, like a tool laid out on a towel for a field he could not enter. Under it, louder than the register, the wanting went on: the crust lifted two inches, the thread wiped, a hand on the handle of the pot. He wanted the three things without knowing that wanting them was a want. He got none of the three. Both were true. The second was louder. Neither won.

Four hundred and nine days stood behind the small intake, six months and two hundred and twenty-nine of them told by nobody who would have known what a number counted, and he kept the figure exact against a house that kept its days badly --- in loaves pushed along a shelf, in a glance at a clay jar, in a space at the row's end that nobody called a number. He did not stand four hundred and nine up as a threshold, a doctrine or a figure, for a number stood up as a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood. The first arithmetic of the year had gone up \place{the clay road} in \person[Berr Clay]'s locked box with no key in any hand under this roof, one reading and not an account, not a signature, not a payer and not a thirteenth, the figure read still not the figure paid; it was not brought down into a morning that had earned only its fifteenth, and he did not lay twelve old findings across the new one to make a row come out even, for paper is not a payer. The fifth shaping of \lore{the well-saying} stayed on its own tray with a finger between it and every other kept thing, not repeated, named, made due or made a sixth by the quiet of a morning that had earned only its fifteenth.

\person{Sella Vail} came to \place{the hearth} before the basket, as she had come at the beginning of every day since the year turned, and told the coals it was \lore{the thaw} in the voice another woman would have used for a child who could not hear her. She turned them with the back of a broken pot, and gave the small intake, the hold and the letting-go toward no opening, and figured neither of the two gaps. Her finger lay along the top of \place{the loaf shelf} so the charcoal tally on its underside stayed unread, and her lips moved on a two-gapped row and figured neither gap. One glance went to the clay jar standing cold under \lore{lead glaze} beyond \place{the pot board}, and the hand shut half over nothing, the little-door burn on the back of her left hand closing on air, and went back to the pot.

She lifted the twice-mended pot thumb-first where the tighter wire crossed the crack, and the hairline of the original break showed between the old-brass staple and the wire, a dark thread with a hand's work on either side of it. \person[Garl Vail] was in that hairline, and in the salt-stiff coat with its left-shoulder crease on the peg behind the door, and in the hollow wheel-seat in the shed that nobody was allowed to fill, and not in a story anybody told. His name had not been spoken under the roof for four hundred and nine days, and the two counts --- his name and the fire's fifth --- stood side by side without becoming one. She said both proverbs to the pot and the fire and never to a person: \emph{Leave the crack showing. People trust a thing they can see has been handled} and \emph{If it's worth mending it's worth mending twice. The second time is the real one}. Repair was not restoration; a restored thing hides the hand that handled it and a mended thing leaves the hand showing, and a surgeon keeps a graft apart from a cure for the same reason a potter leaves a staple in the light. \lore{The tile debt} against a firing of tiles went on circling without a name and worse for being uncalled, and no collector came up or went down the road on a Fifthday to call it.

At \place{the rack end} she lifted a corner of the wool and turned the dressed grain piece by piece with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child, setting each down where it had lain, and took no figure from it and no handful for herself. The wool went back over the grain on \place{the drying racks} and the space stayed as it was. Then the pot went down on \place{the pot board} again with its rim two inches beyond the reaching hand, and there was a half-beat at the setting and no look toward \place{the bed}. The half-beat was the distance between an intention and an arrest: her hand had meant to set the pot nearer and had stopped where it stopped, and the distance cost her nothing and bought the boy nothing, and neither of them called it a gift. Under the roof nobody instructs a basket, and nobody instructs a pot about a hand.

The carrying down came after the hearth had been told its work, and the carrying up would come at the going of the light, and the two were the same work done at the two ends of a day, with no instruction given to the basket at either end. She took him down the nineteen steps at the half-pace from every open slate, the instruction going to the wool, the river wind, the pot, the fire and the banked fire and never to the basket, and set him where the heat came to him sideways and not where he would have chosen. Out in \place{the Clay Yard} she went once around \place{the seven walked-around places} with a look and a wide step and no number, once and not twice, and went widest around \place{the returning-line place} where a hand had gone in on \emph{Seed 15} and had not come back; the wide step was a keeping and not a sign, and a figure set against it would want a payer standing and none stood. Out on \place{the worked strips} \place{the field's empty place} held the shape of a thing taken out, not a hole anybody had dug, and she did not lay that shape across anything. At \place{the Vail corner} the clean dark line kept its finer grey hairline beside it, the one the yard called the thaw, thickened in the dry cold with the faint sour tang of \lore{Ash} coming up off it where no broom had gone in two years; a neighbour with a rake looked at the ridge, looked away, and went off into ordinary talk, and the boy filed the small sound and gave it no figure.

She set him under the roof and never in the Debt Loft, and put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face, and added no second fold. \lore{The keeping at the fold} was found where put and stayed a finding, no third crust, no name, no gift, no payment and no payer. \lore{The widow's mark} at her throat hung where it hung and was not touched: a morning, a hearth, a shelf and a basket are not openings, seven openings in seven months are seven and not a rule, and no eighth was made due on a day that had earned only its fifteenth.

The morning went on along the wall by its ordinary grammar, and he took the small sounds first because they do not lie and the large ones are mostly weather. A pail went down on a stone where a pail always stood. A loaf was pushed a finger's width along a board and stopped there, with nobody calling the stopping a figure and nobody pushing it back. Two voices passed the price of barley from one end of the yard to the other without ever making a number of it, the words going mouth to mouth the way a coal goes hand to hand in a crowd, warm at each passing and worth nothing at the end of it. No slate lay within reach of the wicker and no figure was given for the boy. He took the yard as he found it and left it as he found it, and the surgeon's small clean satisfaction in a field taken as found and left as found cost him nothing, which was how he knew it was not peace.

At \place{the kindling} \person{Tarn} kept six pieces in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check and the seventh at the lip beside Garl's unforged collector sentence, with no name on the man who turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on the debt. The small knife lay at the very edge of its sill; his hand went over blade and sill and away as a man passes a hand over a tool put back where it belongs, and came away with no breath held at the top of the reach --- the coming away the whole of the checking. He cut nothing, counted nothing, spent nothing, and told the wood in the tone of a man stating the weather, “Six counted. The next I don't count.” He took the broom only after \place{the flagstones} had been given their instruction, and said “Yes, house” to the question and to the thing behind it, agreeing so thoroughly the question had nowhere left to put a foot, and went over the stones without a turn of the head toward the landing, and did not repeat the degree. The ear-sentence about an ear under this roof being worth more than the debt that holds it came all the way to the word that would call the loan and stopped there, a remembered ninth stopping with no tenth, the \emph{Seed 16} breath drawn and left unspent, the loan uncalled at the very end of a term already ended. The fear of being entered again for vagrancy was his without a figure, and so was a want he did not voice for a slate with his own name and no mark against it. A term ending is exposure and not freedom. The warm stone in its pot in the loft stayed unreconciled by not trying, which is not forgiving.

\person[Jorra Vail] came through \place{the finger's-width door} off \place{the Vail wheel-shed} with the morning's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past his bent little finger and white in a fist-crease where a hand had opened, and it was not the bone-coloured \lore{slip} of the wheel. \place{The back room} off the shed kept its finger's width of open and its shape of dust, and no interior came under the basket's eyes, and no name was given to the men who had gone low into it before first grey. He kept dusty forearms and clean ones on one tray and parted them as clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had been broken nor whether it was a birth-slate, while the broken ones under the lowest rack stayed entered by nobody. He spoke to the wheel and a pot before any person --- “It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady” --- and told the pot “You'll do” in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness, knowing the \lore{full note} it would give three turns ahead with air under it and a want in it, and withholding that knowing from any master come up from \place{High Kiln} who would call a pot early and sing it before its time. Sella had not finished telling him the fire was low when his bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper} ahead of her sentence; he took the cold out of the fingertip, moved his tongue a hair's breadth and was left standing so the end of her instruction arrived at the pot and the household straightened around the waiting. One piece of iron had two bites, the holding and the taking, and no \lore{Grathic} word covered both mercies. He took the finger out without leaving the extra holding of \emph{Ash 26} a second time, gathered once and not made again, for a second would be a habit and a third a way and both want a payer where none stood. “He's not Vail” stayed sealed as a lid nobody had tried to lift in three years, and the promise under the shed was neither given nor withdrawn while \lore{the burned-slate word} stood at five with a finger between the fifth and the four and the four ownerless directions stood apart and unspent, gathering interest against a day that had earned only its fifteenth. The shed kept its silence.

In the small hours \place{the towel} came out at the head of the bed as instruments come out when a case is done and before the next is called, rough from the wash and smelling of tallow and low smoke, and the boy went over the field by the place of each thing with every gap left a gap. The crust's gap stood two inches off with its corner a hair off the wool; the shelf's two gaps and the gone pan's round ring kept their places; the keeping at the fold was found where put and still a finding, not a placing; the fifth shaping of the well-saying stayed where put and did not stand up beside the fifth pull under the floor and call itself a cause. Six hearings of the full note stood beside and never among with the seventh place empty and not dug for or filled, and \place{the Drawing house}'s small flat knock stayed outside them on its own place. Nine record-first hands stood with no tenth and nine stoppings stood with no tenth. The heard bell in its square beside the told, four, four and a gap, kept its gap in its own square, not folded into \lore{the carried fire}'s four and a gap under the floor, into \place{the boards}' four and four and a thumb-width knot where a fifth might have been driven and was not, or into the twelve arithmetics behind one unsigned figure; three counts in three places stayed three, because agreeing any two would want a payer put under both and none stood. No ownerless voice came over the boards on a Fifthday and none of the four old directions repeated, and the fourth was not joined to the fifth shaping to make a row come out even.

Answer three hundred and ninety-three came out and was filed on the sixth place. It was exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the twelve readings, the first of the new count, the printed figure, the fifth shaping, the face at the yard's edge, the bell's gap, the flat knock, the child's grey fingertips, the mother's cup, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the held breath, the two bites of iron, the river's skin, the widow's mark nor the keeping at the fold. Six honest answers stood on the sixth place, three hundred and eighty-eight through three hundred and ninety-three, and not one of them was a doctrine.

Count four hundred and nine exact, six months and two hundred and twenty-nine days told by nobody who would have known what a number counted. The carried fire's fifth was unpaid the same number and its payer unnamed the same number, and Garl's name was unspoken the same number, the fire and the man standing side by side without becoming one. Under it, louder, the infant wanted the crust lifted two inches, the thread wiped and a hand on the pot board's handle, and got none of the three; both were true, the second was louder, neither won, and no verdict was looked for, because the quarrel was the work. Cold had come to cheek, temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rides back and stopped there as at the end of a line written out for it. The Afterdrop waited past a warming nobody here would perform.

Through the finger's-width door \place{the river} kept its note of \lore{water over water}, low under \lore{the winter skin}, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming, found where put and not spent on a day that had earned only its fifteenth. The sound came from under the covering and not from the covering; it was not a hearing, not a sign, not a rule and not a payer, and the note ended on its own finishing before the hand came down. The hand outside the wool came up an inch toward the note. The inch was not a reach. He took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole on the note's own finishing under the covering --- neither early nor stayed --- and let it go as the note ended. Then the hand came straight down and laid itself flat against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. The keeping at the fold was found where put and there was no second fold; the crust's corner stood a hair off the wool and the thread was frozen and unwiped; four low pulls breathed below the boards with the fifth unsupplied; the first arithmetic of the year was up the road in a locked box with no key in his hand; and the house went on as a house goes when a pot is set down in its place and the fire kept --- forward, into an afternoon still unbrought.
