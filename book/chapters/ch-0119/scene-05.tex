\daymark{410}{Ilmen 16, Y.H. 814, Sixthday}

The morning came on in the dark before it was seen, the way a man comes into a room he has slept in before and finds the same wall the same distance from his hand; nobody brought it, nobody earned it on his behalf, and the sixteenth had not yet been spent on anything. \person[Corin]{The boy} lay at the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf} in \place{the Vail kiln-house}, \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill}, \place{Grath}, under the roof and never in \place{the Debt Loft}, and the heat came to him sideways from \place{the hearth} and not where he would have chosen. First light was a direction beyond \place{the finger's-width door} and not yet a thing in the room. Four hundred and nine days stood behind the hand laid flat on the fold; answer three hundred and ninety-four was under it, exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment, and the small hours had not yet been asked to give up their one small answer.

He took the body in its old order, because the body was a field and a field is taken in the order it lies, not in the order a man would wish it. The crust stood two inches from his ear with its corner standing a hair off the wool, and it had gone leathery past the point where a child would still put it in his mouth --- brown-edged, stiff as old horn, with the faint sweet-sour of barley that had been dry too long. Beside it the empty place held the fold of wool put there on the fifteenth, found where put and still a finding, not a placing, with no second fold and no name spoken over it. The thread of drool at his mouth had gone stiff in the dry kiln heat and was unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread. Cold had come to cheek, temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, and stopped there as at the end of a line written out for it, with neither an order to go farther nor an order to go away. \lore{The Afterdrop} waited past a warming nobody in this house would perform. He knew a face composed by cold is not composure, that the grimace wears the shape of a smile, that pink returning is the surface still lying over a cold core, and that a cold patient cannot clot and must be rewarmed slowly from the outside, never hurried, never rubbed; he knew that a basket set level out of the draught where the heat came sideways was the rewarming he would choose and could not ask for. Under the knowing, louder, the three wants quarrelled without a verdict: the crust lifted two inches, the thread wiped, a hand on the handle of the pot. He wanted them without knowing that wanting them was a want. Both were true. The second was louder. Neither won, and no verdict was looked for, because the quarrel was the work.

He kept four hundred and ten exact. Six months and two hundred and thirty days, told by nobody who would have known what a number counted. He kept them against a house that kept its days badly, in loaves pushed a finger's width along a shelf, in glances at a clay jar cold under glaze, in the unnamed space at the row's end where a loaf had stood, and he did not stand the number up as a threshold, a doctrine or a figure, for a number stood up as a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood. The first arithmetic of the year had gone up \place{the clay road} in \person[Berr Clay]'s locked box with no key in any hand under this roof, and twelve arithmetics of the last year stood unchanged behind one unsigned figure beside the first of a fresh count; he did not lay the twelve old findings across the new one to make a row come out even, for paper is not a payer, and he did not bring the key down out of a coat pocket that never came back. One reading was one and not an account. A reset was not a signing. The figure read was still not the figure paid.

The clerk's entered figure stayed on a place of its own. So did \person{Tarn}'s degree toward \place{the out-of-line landing}, and so did the face at the yard's edge filed among the older record-first hands and not counted among them. He put a finger between every pair and left them there. The entered figure and the degree did not stand up together and call themselves a rule, for a rule is an account that has found a payer and none stood under either; the figure belonged to no man standing under it, because no man stood, and the degree stayed a shape found where put, not repeated as a habit and not made a cause. The soft-cuffed clerk was still a report brought up the yard and never a room entered, his hand to his inward slate before his eyes to the landing, and nobody under any roof had seen the hand or the slate. \person[Ilva] was offstage with her strip of notched birch bark in her sleeve, her count staying in wood and her ninth record-first hand staying nine; \person[Hessa Rale] was not a sighting, her eighth hand on its own tray joined to none. The fifth shaping of \lore{the well-saying} was found where put between the eighth hand and the saying of the road from \place{the Cross} to \place{Greybank}, with a finger between every pair, and no second telling at the well and no sixth shaping came due on a day that had earned only its sixteenth.

\person{Sella Vail} came to the hearth before the basket, as she had come at the beginning of every day since the boy was brought under the roof. She told the coals it was \lore{the thaw} in the voice another woman would have used for a child who could not hear her, and turned them with the back of a broken pot. She gave the small intake, the hold and the letting-go toward no opening, and figured neither of the two gaps. Her finger lay along the top of the loaf shelf so the charcoal tally on its underside stayed unread, and her lips moved on a two-gapped row and figured neither gap. One glance went to the clay jar standing cold under \lore{lead glaze} beyond \place{the pot board}, and the hand shut half over nothing, the little-door burn on the back of her left hand --- two straight sides, a lintel and a threshold --- closing on air, and went back to the pot. She said \emph{The house will keep it} to the fire and not to the basket, because a thing said aloud becomes a thing the Court can enter.

She lifted the twice-mended pot thumb-first where the tighter wire crossed the crack, and the hairline of the original break showed between the old-brass staple and the wire, a dark thread with a hand's work on either side of it. \person{Garl Vail} was in that hairline, and in the salt-stiff coat with its left-shoulder crease on the peg behind the door, and in the hollow wheel-seat in the shed that nobody was allowed to fill, and not in a story anybody told. She gave both proverbs to the pot and the fire and never to a person: \emph{Leave the crack showing. People trust a thing they can see has been handled} and \emph{If it's worth mending it's worth mending twice. The second time is the real one}. Repair was not restoration; a restored thing hides the hand that handled it and a mended thing leaves the hand showing, and a surgeon keeps a graft apart from a cure for the same reason a potter leaves a staple in the light. \lore{The tile debt} against a firing of tiles went on circling without a name and worse for being uncalled, and no collector came up or went down the road on a Sixthday to call it. At \place{the rack end} she lifted a corner of the wool and turned the dressed grain piece by piece with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child, setting each down where it had lain, and took no figure from it and no handful for herself. The wool went back over the grain on \place{the drying racks} and the space stayed as it was. Then the pot went down on the board again with its rim two inches beyond the reaching hand, and there was a half-beat at the setting and no look toward \place{the bed}. The half-beat was the distance between an intention and an arrest; it cost her nothing and bought the boy nothing, and neither of them called it a gift. Under the roof nobody instructs a basket, and nobody instructs a pot about a hand.

She carried him down the nineteen steps at the half-pace from every open slate, and the instruction went to the wool, the river wind, the pot, the fire and the banked fire and never to the basket; the basket was not told what the day was and was not told to be still. Out in \place{the Clay Yard} she went once around \place{the seven walked-around places} with a look and a wide step and no number, once and not twice, and widest around \place{the returning-line place} where a hand had gone in on \emph{Seed 15} and had not come back; the wide step was a keeping and not a sign, and a figure set against it would want a payer standing and none stood. Out on \place{the worked strips} \place{the field's empty place} held the shape of a thing taken out, not a hole anybody had dug, and she did not lay that shape across the loaf's stopping or the ridge. At \place{the Vail corner} the clean dark line kept its finer grey beside it, the one the yard called the thaw, thickened in the dry cold with the faint sour tang of \lore{Ash} coming up off it where no broom had gone in two years; a neighbour with a rake looked at the ridge, looked away, and went off into ordinary talk, and nothing from the yard or the well or the word at the well came under the roof as sign, lesson, mending, cause, payment or payer. She set him under the roof and never in the Debt Loft and put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face. She added no second fold. \lore{The keeping at the fold} was found where put and stayed a finding, no third crust, no name, no gift, no payment and no payer. At her throat \lore{the widow's mark} hung where it hung and was not touched: a morning, a hearth, a shelf, a yard, a well and a bed are not openings, seven openings in seven months are seven and not a rule, and no eighth was made due on a day that had earned only its sixteenth.

At \place{the kindling} Tarn kept six pieces in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check and the seventh at the lip beside Garl's unforged collector sentence, with no name on the man who turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on the debt. The small knife lay at the very edge of its sill; his hand went over blade and sill and away as a man passes a hand over a tool put back where it belongs, and came away with no breath held at the top of the reach --- the coming away the whole of the checking. He took the broom only after \place{the flagstones} had been given their instruction, and said “Yes, house” to the question and to the thing behind it, agreeing so thoroughly the question had nowhere left to put a foot. He cut nothing, counted nothing, spent nothing, and told the wood in the tone of a man stating the weather, “Six counted. The next I don't count.” He went over the stones without a turn of the head toward the landing and did not repeat the degree. The ear-sentence about an ear under this roof being worth more than the debt came all the way to the word that would call the loan and stopped there, a remembered ninth stopping with no tenth, the \emph{Seed 16} breath drawn and left unspent, the loan uncalled at the very end of a term already ended. The warm stone in its pot in the loft stayed unreconciled by not trying, which is not forgiving.

\person[Jorra Vail] came through the finger's-width door off \place{the Vail wheel-shed} with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past his bent little finger and white in a fist-crease where a hand had opened, and it was not the bone-coloured \lore{slip} of the wheel. \place{The back room} off the shed kept its finger's width of open and its shape of dust, and no interior came under the basket's eyes and no name was given to the men who went low into it before first grey. He kept dusty forearms and clean ones on one tray and parted them as clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had been broken nor whether it was a birth-slate, while the broken ones under the lowest rack stayed entered by nobody. He spoke to the wheel before any person --- “It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady” --- and told a pot “You'll do” in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness, knowing the \lore{full note} it would give three turns ahead with air under it and a want in it, and withholding that knowing from any master come up from \place{High Kiln} who would call a pot early and sing it before its time. Sella had not finished telling him the fire was low when his bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper} ahead of her sentence; he took the cold out of the fingertip, moved his tongue a hair's breadth and was left standing so the end of her instruction arrived at the pot. One piece of iron had two bites, the holding and the taking, and no \place{Grathic} word covered both mercies; he took the finger out without leaving the extra holding of \emph{Ash 26} a second time, gathered once and not made again, for a second would be a habit and a third a way and both want a payer where none stood. “He's not Vail” stayed sealed as a lid nobody had tried to lift in three years, and the promise under the shed was neither given nor withdrawn while \lore{the burned-slate word} stood at five, the shut-room tellings at two and the four ownerless Ash and Slate directions apart and unspent.

In the small hours \place{the towel} came out at the head of the bed as instruments come out when a case is done and before the next is called, rough from the wash and smelling of tallow and low smoke, and the boy went over the field by the place of each thing with every gap left a gap. The clerk's entered figure sat on its place of its own beside the inward slate, with a finger between it and Tarn's degree and another between it and the face at the yard's edge; the order of it stood hand, eye, mouth, with the half-beat in its gap, and no man was put under any of the three. The fifth shaping of the well-saying stayed between the eighth hand and the road saying with a finger between every pair. The first arithmetic of the year was up the road in Berr's box with no key, and twelve arithmetics of the last year stood unchanged behind one unsigned figure beside the first of a fresh count, not laid across it to make a row come out even, for paper is not a payer. The keeping at the fold was found where put and still a finding, and nothing crossed under the roof from the day as sign, lesson, mending, cause, payment or payer, nor was made to answer for anything. \place{The boards} kept four nails on the near board, four on the next and a thumb-width knot where a fifth might have been driven and was not. \lore{The carried fire} gave four low pulls under the floor with the silence where the fifth should have stood, and nobody lifted the floor or supplied the pull. The told bell kept its gap in its square beside the bell heard from the Cross, four, four and a gap, its gap not folded into the fire's four and a gap, into the boards' four and four and a knot, or into the twelve arithmetics behind one unsigned figure; three counts in three places stayed three, because agreeing any two would want a payer put under both and none stood. Six hearings of the full note stood beside and never among with the seventh place empty and not dug for or filled, and \place{the Drawing house}'s small flat knock stayed outside them on its own place. Nine record-first hands stood with no tenth and nine stoppings stood with no tenth; four rooms kept the same gap with a finger between every pair and no Court room came under the roof to make a fifth. On \lore{the gap tray} the silence after the name from Weighing, the clean voice's missing name and the Long Night story's empty name sat each in its own place with no name set into any of them. No sixth hearing, tenth hand, tenth stopping, eighth opening or sixth shaping stood, and nothing was opened, joined, named, called, paid, forgiven or ended.

Answer three hundred and ninety-five came out and was filed on the sixth place. It was exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the twelve readings, the first of the new count, the printed figure, the fifth shaping, the clerk's entered figure, the face at the yard's edge, the bell's gap, the flat knock, the child's grey fingertips, the mother's cup, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the held breath, the two bites of iron, the river's skin, the widow's mark nor the keeping at the fold. Six honest answers stood on the sixth place, three hundred and ninety through three hundred and ninety-five, and not one of them was a doctrine. Count four hundred and ten exact, six months and two hundred and thirty days told by nobody who would have known what a number counted. The carried fire's fifth was unpaid the same number and its payer unnamed the same number, and Garl's name was unspoken the same number, the fire and the man standing side by side without becoming one.

The quiet after a figure entered before a face was looked at was kept as a quiet, and the boy's exact count ran on under a house that could not say what it counted. He took a small clean satisfaction in a field taken as found and left as found, and knew it cost nothing, which is how he knew it was not peace. Under it, louder, the wanting went on: the crust lifted two inches, the thread wiped, a hand on the pot board's handle. He wanted the three things without knowing that wanting them was a want, and he got none of the three. Both were true. The second was louder. Neither won, and no verdict was looked for; the quarrel was the work. Cold had come to cheek, temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rides back, and stopped there as at the end of a line written out for it, with neither an order to go farther nor an order to go away; the Afterdrop waited past a warming nobody here would perform.

Through the finger's-width door \place{the river} kept its note of \lore{water over water}, a low thing over a lower thing under \lore{the winter skin}, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming, found where put and not spent on a day that had earned only its sixteenth. The sound came from under the covering and not from the covering; it was not a hearing, not a sign, not a rule and not a payer, and the note ended on its own finishing before any hand came down. The hand outside the wool came up an inch toward the note. The inch was not a reach. He took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole on the note's own finishing under the covering --- neither early nor stayed --- and let it go as the note ended. Then the hand came straight down and laid itself flat against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. The clerk's entered figure was kept on its own place with a finger between it and the degree; the crust's corner stood a hair off the wool and the thread was frozen and unwiped; four low pulls breathed below the floor with the fifth unsupplied; the first arithmetic of the year was up the road in a locked box with no key in his hand; and the house went on as a house goes when a pot is set down in its place and the fire kept --- forward, into an afternoon not yet earned and not brought into this one.
