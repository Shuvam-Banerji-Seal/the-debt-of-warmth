The afternoon came on without an announcement, the way a pot goes on cooling under a fold --- nobody told it and nobody entered it, and by small gains the light lay flat on the stone and the yard held what warmth it had the way a pot holds water, with care, and with one crack in it nobody mentioned. \person[Corin]{The boy} was set where the heat came to him sideways and not where he would have chosen, in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill}, \place{Grath}, under the roof and never in \place{the Debt Loft}, and the instruction went to the wool, the river wind, the pot, the fire and the banked fire and never to the basket. He took the small sounds first because they do not lie and the large ones are mostly weather.

There was the small rasp of a rope on the stone lip of \place{the yard well}, a sound no bigger than a thumbnail and truer than anything said over a wall. The rope settled into its own knot and stayed there. The bucket knocked the stone, went down and came up, and the water was not entered. A loaf went a finger's width along a board and stopped, with nobody calling the stopping a figure and nobody pushing it back. Two voices passed the price of barley from one end of \place{the Clay Yard} to the other without ever making a number of it, the words going mouth to mouth the way a coal goes hand to hand in a crowd, warm at each passing and worth nothing at the end of it. From the basket's low angle the well was a stone lip and a bucket-round and nothing more. No slate lay within reach of the wicker and no figure was given for the boy. He filed each small sound on the place where it was found and gave none of them a standing, because a figure set against a pail or a loaf would want a payer standing under it and none stood.

Then the word came up the yard.

It came from mouth to mouth and not from any room, travelling the way a spark travels in wet straw --- slowly, from one to the next, with nobody carrying it and nobody owning it. A man at the wall said it to a woman with a pail, and the woman said it low to her pail, and by the time it reached the shade where the basket stood it had no body under it at all. The word was this: the soft-cuffed clerk still set below \place{the north bridge} at \place{the out-of-line landing} had entered a figure on his inward slate before he looked up to find the man who had given it. The cuff was grey from washing and folded back twice; the slate was turned inward, its writing to the forearm. The hand had gone to the slate. The eyes had gone to the person after. The mouth, if there was a mouth in it, had come after both.

Nobody under any roof had seen the hand or the slate. No man stood under the figure to be asked about it. No room was entered, no name was spoken, no figure was brought under a roof, and no slate was opened within reach of a basket. It was a report and not a sighting, a word travelling the yard without a body under it, and the boy took it as he took the field of a wound --- by what was there and by what was not.

He took it apart in the order its parts came. A surgeon opening a field does not ask the history first; he sees where the hand has been, and where the eye went, and only then what the mouth says about it. Hand to the slate. Eye to the person. Mouth after both. And the half-beat lay in the gap between the first and the second, a small dark interval with nothing standing in it, the place where a lesser observer would have put an intention and found nothing but the space between two motions. He had seen that order in a midwife laying slates before she turned to a fold, in a thane passing a finger over a figure before the crowd, in \person[Berr Clay]'s twice-wiped thumb, in a runner's strap and wrist, in a collector's hand to the slate before his eyes to the pallet, and in \person[Ilva]{Ilva}'s hand toward her sleeve before her eyes went to the asker. It was an order, and an order is not an answer.

He filed it on a place of its own. He put a finger between it and \person{Tarn}'s degree toward the landing, and another finger between it and the face at the yard's edge that had been filed among the older record-first hands and not counted among them. Three places, three fingers' widths, no joining. It was the next reaching toward the loan that can hear, and it was not the calling of that loan. It was not a tenth hand, not a rule, not a sighting, not a witness, not a reader made and not a payment. The figure belonged to no man standing under it, because no man stood. The degree toward the landing stayed a shape found where put and was not repeated as a habit or made a cause. Ilva was offstage with her strip of notched birch bark in her sleeve, her count staying in wood and her ninth record-first hand staying nine, and she was expressly not the speaker of any word that came up at this well: the well had given up a report and the woman at the well had given a count, and the two went on separate places with a finger between. \person{Hessa Rale} was not a sighting; her eighth hand stayed on its own tray, joined to none.

He did not lay the entered figure across Tarn's ended term, or across the fifth shaping of \lore{the well-saying} found where put on its own tray between the eighth hand and the saying of the road from \place{the Cross} to \place{Greybank}, or across the face on its tray, or across the clerk's inward slate up the road, or across the first arithmetic gone up \place{the clay road} in Berr's locked box with no key in any hand under this roof, or across \person[Jorra Vail]'s promise neither given nor withdrawn. Likenesses stood up want a man put under every one of them and no man stood. A doctrine is an account that has found a payer, and he had watched a district make one out of twelve true subtractions behind a single unsigned figure without ever finding the hand that signed it; he would not make another out of a word that had no body under it.

When the light slackened off the walls, \person{Sella Vail} went once around \place{the seven walked-around places} in the yard with a look and a wide step and no number, once and not twice, and widest around \place{the returning-line place} where a hand had gone in on \emph{Seed 15} and had not come back; the wide step was a keeping and not a sign, and a figure set against it would want a payer standing and none stood. Out on \place{the worked strips} \place{the field's empty place} held the shape of a thing taken out, not a hole anybody had dug, and she did not lay that shape across the loaf's stopping or the ridge. At \place{the Vail corner} the clean dark line kept its finer grey beside it, the one the yard called \lore{the thaw}, thickened in the dry cold with the faint sour tang of \lore{Ash} coming up off it where no broom had gone in two years; a neighbour with a rake looked at the ridge, looked away, and went off into ordinary talk. Nothing from the yard, the well or the word at the well came under the roof as sign, lesson, cause, payment or payer.

At the going of the light she came to \place{the hearth} before the basket, as she had come at the beginning of the day, and told the coals it was the thaw in the voice another woman would have used for a child who could not hear her. She turned them with the back of a broken pot. She gave the small intake, the hold and the letting-go toward no opening, and figured neither of the two gaps. Her finger lay along the top of \place{the loaf shelf} so the charcoal tally on its underside stayed unread, and her lips moved on a two-gapped row and figured neither gap. One glance went to the clay jar standing cold under \lore{lead glaze} beyond \place{the pot board}, and the hand shut half over nothing, the little-door burn on the back of her left hand closing on air, and went back to the pot.

She lifted the twice-mended pot thumb-first where the tighter wire crossed the crack, and the hairline of the original break showed between the old-brass staple and the wire, a dark thread with a hand's work on either side of it. \person{Garl Vail} was in that hairline, and in the salt-stiff coat with its left-shoulder crease on the peg behind the door, and in the hollow wheel-seat in the shed that nobody was allowed to fill, and not in a story anybody told. His name had not been spoken under the roof for four hundred and nine days, and the two counts --- his name and the fire's fifth --- stood side by side without becoming one. She said both proverbs to the pot and the fire and never to a person: \emph{Leave the crack showing. People trust a thing they can see has been handled} and \emph{If it's worth mending it's worth mending twice. The second time is the real one}. Repair was not restoration; a restored thing hides the hand that handled it and a mended thing leaves the hand showing, and a surgeon keeps a graft apart from a cure for the same reason a potter leaves a staple in the light. \lore{The tile debt} against a firing of tiles went on circling without a name and worse for being uncalled, and no collector came up or went down the road on a Fifthday to call it.

At \place{the rack end} she lifted a corner of the wool and turned the dressed grain piece by piece with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child, setting each down where it had lain, and took no figure from it and no handful for herself. The wool went back over the grain on \place{the drying racks} and the space stayed as it was. Then the pot went down on \place{the pot board} again with its rim two inches beyond the reaching hand, and there was a half-beat at the setting and no look toward \place{the bed}. The half-beat was the distance between an intention and an arrest: her hand had meant to set the pot nearer and had stopped where it stopped, and the distance cost her nothing and bought the boy nothing, and neither of them called it a gift. Under the roof nobody instructs a basket, and nobody instructs a pot about a hand.

She carried him up the nineteen steps at the half-pace from every open slate, set him under the roof and never in the Debt Loft, and put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face. She added no second fold. \lore{The keeping at the fold} was found where put and stayed a finding, no third crust, no name, no gift, no payment and no payer. At her throat \lore{the widow's mark} hung where it hung and was not touched: a hearth, a shelf, a yard, a well, a going of the light and a bed are not openings, seven openings in seven months are seven and not a rule, and no eighth was made due on a day that had earned only its fifteenth.

At \place{the kindling} Tarn kept six pieces in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check and the seventh at the lip beside Garl's unforged collector sentence, with no name on the man who turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on the debt. He took the broom only after \place{the flagstones} had been given their instruction, and said “Yes, house” to the question and to the thing behind it, agreeing so thoroughly the question had nowhere left to put a foot. The small knife lay at the very edge of its sill; his hand went over blade and sill and away as a man passes a hand over a tool put back where it belongs, and came away with no breath held at the top of the reach --- the coming away the whole of the checking. He cut nothing, counted nothing, spent nothing, and told the wood in the tone of a man stating the weather, “Six counted. The next I don't count.” He went over the stones without a turn of the head toward the landing and did not repeat the degree. The ear-sentence about an ear under this roof being worth more than the debt came all the way to the word that would call the loan and stopped there, a remembered ninth stopping with no tenth, the \emph{Seed 16} breath drawn and left unspent, the loan uncalled at the very end of a term already ended. The warm stone in its pot in the loft stayed unreconciled by not trying, which is not forgiving.

Jorra came through \place{the finger's-width door} off \place{the Vail wheel-shed} with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past his bent little finger and white in a fist-crease where a hand had opened, and it was not the bone-coloured \lore{slip} of the wheel. \place{The back room} off the shed kept its finger's width of open and its shape of dust, and no interior came under the basket's eyes and no name was given to the men who went low into it before first grey. He kept dusty forearms and clean ones on one tray and parted them as clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had been broken nor whether it was a birth-slate, while the broken ones under the lowest rack stayed entered by nobody. He spoke to the wheel and a pot before any person --- “It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady” --- and told the pot “You'll do” in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness, knowing the \lore{full note} it would give three turns ahead with air under it and a want in it, and withholding that knowing from any master come up from \place{High Kiln} who would call a pot early and sing it before its time. Sella had not finished telling him the fire was low when his bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper} ahead of her sentence; he took the cold out of the fingertip, moved his tongue a hair's breadth and was left standing so the end of her instruction arrived at the pot. One piece of iron had two bites, the holding and the taking, and no \place{Grathic} word covered both mercies; he took the finger out without leaving the extra holding of \emph{Ash 26} a second time, gathered once and not made again. “He's not Vail” stayed sealed as a lid nobody had tried to lift in three years, and the promise under the shed was neither given nor withdrawn while \lore{the burned-slate word} stood at five, the shut-room tellings at two, the row at five askings with no witness and the four ownerless Ash and Slate directions apart and unspent. The shed kept its silence.

In the small hours \place{the towel} came out at the head of the bed as instruments come out when a case is done and before the next is called, rough from the wash and smelling of tallow and low smoke, and the boy went over the field by the place of each thing with every gap left a gap. \place{The boards} kept four nails on the near board, four on the next and a thumb-width knot where a fifth might have been driven and was not. \lore{The carried fire} gave four low pulls under the floor with the silence where the fifth should have stood, and nobody lifted the floor or supplied the pull. Answer three hundred and ninety-four came out and was filed on the sixth place. It was exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the twelve readings, the first of the new count, the printed figure, the fifth shaping, the clerk's entered figure, the face at the yard's edge, the bell's gap, the flat knock, the child's grey fingertips, the mother's cup, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the held breath, the two bites of iron, the river's skin, the widow's mark nor the keeping at the fold. Count four hundred and nine exact, six months and twenty-nine days told by nobody who would have known what a number counted. The carried fire's fifth was unpaid the same number and its payer unnamed the same number, and Garl's name was unspoken the same number, the fire and the man standing side by side without becoming one. Under it, louder than the register, the wanting went on: the crust lifted two inches, the thread wiped, a hand on the handle of the pot. He wanted the three things without knowing that wanting them was a want, and he got none of the three. Both were true. The second was louder. Neither won, and no verdict was looked for, because the quarrel was the work. Cold had come to cheek, temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rides back, and stopped there as at the end of a line written out for it. \lore{The Afterdrop} waited past a warming nobody here would perform.

Through \place{the finger's-width door} \place{the river} kept its note of \lore{water over water}, low under \lore{the winter skin}, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming, found where put and not spent on a day that had earned only its fifteenth. The sound came from under the covering and not from the covering; it was not a hearing, not a sign, not a rule and not a payer, and the note ended on its own finishing before the hand came down.
