The afternoon came on by small gains that nobody entered, the way warmth comes into a room that has been shut: nothing brought, nothing earned on his behalf, the sixteenth still unspent and the light not yet gone. \person[Corin]{The boy} was carried down the nineteen steps at the half-pace from every open slate, out of \place{the Vail kiln-house} in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill}, \place{Grath}, and the instruction went to the wool, the river wind, the pot, the fire and the banked fire and never to the basket; the basket was not told what the day was and was not told to be still. In \place{the Clay Yard} \person{Sella Vail} went once around \place{the seven walked-around places} with a look and a wide step and no number, once and not twice, and widest around \place{the returning-line place} where a hand had gone in on \emph{Seed 15} and had not come back. The wide step was a keeping and not a sign. A figure set against it would want a payer standing, and none stood.

He took the field as he found it and left it as he found it, taking the small sounds first because they did not lie and the large ones were mostly weather: a pail set down on its stone where a pail always stood, a rope settling into its own knot, a loaf pushed a finger's width along a board and stopped with nobody calling the stopping a figure, barley passed from wall to wall and never made into a number. At \place{the Vail corner} the clean dark line kept its finer grey beside it, the one the yard called \lore{the thaw}, thickened in the dry cold with the faint sour tang of \lore{Ash} coming up off it where no broom had gone in two years. There was a word in the yard for grey put down and another for grey gone, and he kept the called-gone grey beside the left-in-place ridge with a finger between, and no agreement had been made to stop looking at this one. Out on \place{the worked strips} \place{the field's empty place} held the shape of a thing taken out, not a hole anybody had dug, and he did not lay that shape across the loaf's stopping or the ridge. From the basket's low angle \place{the trough} stood bare and dark beside \place{the yard well}, and the round ring where a pan had stood gave back its shallow print on a place of its own. Nothing crossed under the roof from any of it as sign, lesson, mending, cause, payment or payer, and nothing was made to answer for anything.

Costs arrived as directions and went away as weather and left no entry. A man come down \place{the clay road} brought a number up out of a shut fist toward the well, held it where the light could see it, looked at it as a man looks at a thing he has not decided to keep, and put it back into his sleeve; it arrived nowhere. He took that order apart as he took every field apart --- hand, eye, mouth, the half-beat lying in the gap --- and did not give the fist a figure or the sleeve a motive. A woman sent to ask what a pot cost was sent to the pot and got a note and not a price. The dressed grain lay covered under wool on the cool stone at \place{the rack end}, and the arithmetic read over it on Ilmen 10 had not known what it cost; the space between the figure and the price carried no signature, and nobody set one there. The first arithmetic of the year was up the road in \person{Berr Clay}'s locked box with no key in any hand under this roof, and twelve arithmetics of the last year stood unchanged behind one unsigned figure beside the first of a fresh count; he did not lay the twelve old findings across the new one to make a row come out even, for paper is not a payer, and the figure read was still not the figure paid. One reading was one and not an account. A reset was not a signing. The arithmetic of a covered thing does not know the weight of the thing it covers.

At the going of the light \person{Sella Vail} came to \place{the hearth} before the basket, as she had come at the beginning of every day since the boy was brought under the roof. She told the coals it was the thaw in the voice another woman would have used for a child who could not hear her, and turned them with the back of a broken pot. She gave the small intake, the hold and the letting-go toward no opening, and figured neither of the two gaps. Her finger lay along the top of \place{the loaf shelf} so the charcoal tally on its underside stayed unread, and her lips moved on a two-gapped row and figured neither gap. One glance went to the clay jar standing cold under \lore{lead glaze} beyond \place{the pot board}, and the hand shut half over nothing, the little-door burn on the back of her left hand --- two straight sides, a lintel and a threshold --- closing on air, and went back to the pot. She said \emph{The house will keep it} to the fire and not to the basket, because a thing said aloud becomes a thing the Court can enter.

She lifted the twice-mended pot thumb-first where the tighter wire crossed the crack, and the hairline of the original break showed between the old-brass staple and the wire, a dark thread with a hand's work on either side of it. \person{Garl Vail} was in that hairline, and in the salt-stiff coat with its left-shoulder crease on the peg behind the door, and in the hollow wheel-seat in the shed that nobody was allowed to fill, and not in a story anybody told. She gave both proverbs to the pot and the fire and never to a person: \emph{Leave the crack showing. People trust a thing they can see has been handled} and \emph{If it's worth mending it's worth mending twice. The second time is the real one}. Repair was not restoration; a restored thing hides the hand that handled it and a mended thing leaves the hand showing, and a surgeon keeps a graft apart from a cure for the same reason a potter leaves a staple in the light. At the rack end she lifted a corner of the wool and turned the dressed grain piece by piece with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child, setting each down where it had lain, and took no figure from it and no handful for herself. The wool went back over the grain on \place{the drying racks} and the space stayed as it was. Then the pot went down on the board again with its rim two inches beyond the reaching hand, and there was a half-beat at the setting and no look toward \place{the bed}. The half-beat was the distance between an intention and an arrest; it cost her nothing and bought the boy nothing, and neither of them called it a gift.

She carried him up the nineteen steps at the half-pace from every open slate and went wide around the seven walked-around places once and not twice, widest around the returning-line place, and set him under the roof and never in \place{the Debt Loft}. She put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face and added no second fold. \lore{The keeping at the fold} was found where put and stayed a finding, still not a placing, with no third crust, no name, no gift, no payment and no payer. \lore{The tile debt} against a firing of tiles went on circling without a name and worse for being uncalled, and no collector came up or went down the road on a Sixthday to call it. At her throat \lore{the widow's mark} hung where it hung and was not touched: a morning, a hearth, a shelf, a yard, a well, a going of the light and a bed are not openings, seven openings in seven months are seven and not a rule, and no eighth was made due on a day that had earned only its sixteenth.

The quiet after a figure entered before a face was looked at was kept as a quiet, and the boy's exact count ran on under a house that could not say what it counted. He took a small clean satisfaction in a field taken as found and left as found and knew it cost nothing, which is how he knew it was not peace. He had once stood at a bay door and sorted twenty people by what they would not survive, and the same hand lay now outside the wool against a fold of a kind nobody here would have thought to keep; the two kinds of knowing stood side by side and neither bought the other. Under them, louder, the wanting went on: the crust lifted two inches, the thread wiped, a hand on the pot board's handle. He wanted the three things without knowing that wanting them was a want, and he got none of the three. Both were true. The second was louder. Neither won, and no verdict was looked for; the quarrel was the work, and the work went on in silence in a room whose floor does not touch his. Cold had come to cheek, temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rides back, and stopped there as at the end of a line written out for it, with neither an order to go farther nor an order to go away; \lore{the Afterdrop} waited past a warming nobody here would perform.

Through \place{the finger's-width door} \place{the river} kept its note of \lore{water over water}, a low thing over a lower thing under \lore{the winter skin}, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming, found where put and not spent on a day that had earned only its sixteenth. The sound came from under the covering and not from the covering; it was not a hearing, not a sign, not a rule and not a payer, and the note ended on its own finishing before any hand came down. The hand outside the wool came up an inch toward the note. The inch was not a reach. He took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole on the note's own finishing under the covering --- neither early nor stayed --- and let it go as the note ended. Then the hand came straight down and laid itself flat against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. The clerk's entered figure was kept on its own place with a finger between it and the degree; the crust's corner stood a hair off the wool and the thread was frozen and unwiped; four low pulls breathed below the floor with the fifth unsupplied; the first arithmetic of the year was up the road in a locked box with no key in his hand; the fifth shaping sat on its own tray with a finger between it and every other kept thing. The house went on as a house goes when a pot is set down in its place and the fire kept --- forward, into a morning not yet earned and not brought into this one.
