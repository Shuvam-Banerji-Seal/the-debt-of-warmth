\daymark{408}{Ilmen 14, Y.H. 814, Fourthday}

\dropcap{T}{he hand} lay flat against the fold where it had been put down, and the small hours came on under the roof without asking to be let in, the way dry cold comes through a wall that has no crack for it --- not arriving, only being there. Answer three hundred and ninety lay under the hand, a small intake held whole on the river's note's own finishing and let go as the note ended, exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air; four hundred and seven days stood behind it, six months and two hundred and twenty-seven of them told by nobody who would have known what a number counted. First light was still a direction beyond \place{the finger's-width door} and not yet a thing in the room. \person[Corin]{The boy} lay at the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf} in \place{the Vail kiln-house}, \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill}, \place{Grath}, under the roof and never in \place{the Debt Loft}, and the heat came to him sideways from \place{the hearth} and not where he would have chosen. One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust stood two inches from his ear with its corner a hair off the wool; beside it the empty place held the fold of wool put there on the fifteenth, found where put and still a finding, not a placing, with no second fold and no name over it. The thread of drool at his mouth had gone stiff in the dry kiln heat and was unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread. Cold had come to cheek, temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, and stopped there as at the end of a line written out for it, with neither an order to go farther nor an order to go away. \lore{The Afterdrop} waited past a warming nobody in this house would perform.

Taking the body in its old order was the only work the room allowed him, and he took it: crust, fold, thread, cold, the waiting heat. He knew a face composed by cold is not composure, that the grimace of a cold man wears the shape of a smile and the smile means nothing, that pink returning is the surface still lying warm over a cold core, and that a cold patient cannot clot and must be rewarmed from outside and slowly, never hurried, never rubbed, never set too near the fire. A basket set level out of the draught where the warmth came sideways was the rewarming he would have chosen for such a patient, and he could not ask for it, and the asking would have gone to nobody who would understand what was being asked. He had once stood at a bay door and sorted twenty people by what they would not survive, and the knowing was of the same order as the knowing that the extremities give up heat before the core and take it back the same way, and it was as useful here as a hand two paces long. Under all of it, louder than the register, the wanting went on: the crust lifted two inches, the thread wiped, a hand on the handle of the pot. He wanted the three things without knowing that wanting them was a want. He got none of the three. Both were true. The second was louder. Neither won.

He kept four hundred and seven exact through the small hours and did not stand the number up as a threshold, a doctrine or a figure, for a number stood up as a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood. The house kept its days badly, in loaves pushed along a shelf, in a glance at a clay jar, in a space at the row's end that nobody called a number, and its not keeping them was neither a cruelty nor a mercy that could be entered on a slate. The first arithmetic of Y.H. 814 had gone up \place{the clay road} in \person[Berr Clay]'s locked box with no key in any hand under this roof, one reading and not an account, not a signature, not a payer and not a thirteenth, the figure read still not the figure paid. Twelve arithmetics of Y.H. 813 stood unchanged behind one unsigned figure beside the first of a fresh count, and he did not lay twelve old findings across the new one to make a row come out even, for a row made even by a hand with no key is made even on paper, and paper is not a payer. Nothing from the road had crossed under the roof as sign, lesson, mending, cause, payment or payer, and that not-crossing was itself kept, not celebrated.

The fifth shaping of \lore{the well-saying} sat on its own tray between \person[Hessa Rale]'s eighth record-first hand and the saying of the road from \place{the Cross} to \place{Greybank}, with a finger between it and the four ownerless directions out of \place{Slate Row} --- \lore{the burned-slate word} joined to \lore{the counting room} shut in the war and the two shapes further named by nobody. It was found where put and was not made again, not named, not repeated, not joined, not made due, and a sixth was not made due by the quiet of a tend-day that had earned only its fourteenth. \person{Ilva}'s strip of notched birch bark stayed in her sleeve offstage with her ninth hand staying nine and no figure coming off the wood; the face at the yard's edge stayed on its own tray, filed and not counted among the hands, never a sale and never a witness; \person[Jorra Vail]'s promise lay neither given nor withdrawn, and the degree of the head toward the landing lay a shape found where put. Each kept its own place with a finger between, and a finger between is not a joining and does not want a payer standing under it.

First light came along the wall the way water comes into a dry ditch, by inches and without comment, and the room took on the smell of woodsmoke and tallow in wet wool, the smoke lying low under the rafters and the tallow sweetish where a wick had been pinched the night before. \person{Sella Vail} came to the hearth before the basket, as she had come at the beginning of every day since he came under the roof, and told the coals it was \lore{the thaw} in the voice another woman would have used for a child who could not hear her. She turned them with the back of a broken pot. She gave the small intake, the hold and the letting-go toward no opening, and figured neither of the two gaps. Her finger lay along the top of the loaf shelf so the charcoal tally on its underside stayed unread, and her lips moved on a two-gapped row and figured neither gap. One glance went to the clay jar standing cold under \lore{lead glaze} beyond \place{the pot board}, and the hand shut half over nothing, the little-door burn on the back of her left hand closing on air, and went back to the pot. At her throat \lore{the widow's mark} hung where it hung and was not touched: a morning, a hearth, a shelf and a basket are not openings, seven openings in seven months are seven and not a rule, and no eighth was made due on a day that had earned only its fourteenth.

She lifted the twice-mended pot thumb-first where the tighter wire crossed the crack, and the hairline of the original break showed between the old-brass staple and the wire, a dark thread with a hand's work on either side of it. \person[Garl Vail] was in that hairline, and in the salt-stiff coat with its left-shoulder crease on its peg behind the door, and in the hollow wheel-seat in the shed that nobody was allowed to fill, and not in a story anybody told. His name had not been spoken under the roof for four hundred and seven days, and the two counts --- his name and the fire's fifth --- stood side by side without becoming one. She said both proverbs to the pot and the fire and never to a person: \emph{Leave the crack showing. People trust a thing they can see has been handled} and \emph{If it's worth mending it's worth mending twice. The second time is the real one}. Repair was not restoration; a restored thing hides the hand that handled it and a mended thing leaves the hand showing, and a surgeon keeps a graft apart from a cure for the same reason a potter leaves a staple in the light. \lore{The tile debt} against a firing of tiles went on circling without a name and worse for being uncalled, and no collector came up or went down the road on a Fourthday to call it.

At \place{the rack end} she lifted a corner of the wool and turned the dressed grain piece by piece with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child, setting each down where it had lain, and took no figure from it and no handful for herself. The wool went back over the grain on \place{the drying racks} and the space stayed as it was. Then the pot went down on the pot board again with its rim two inches beyond the reaching hand, and there was a half-beat at the setting and no look toward \place{the bed}. The half-beat was the distance between an intention and an arrest: her hand had meant to set the pot nearer and had stopped where it stopped, and the distance cost her nothing and bought the boy nothing, and neither of them called it a gift. Under the roof nobody instructs a basket, and nobody instructs a pot about a hand.

She carried him down the nineteen steps at the half-pace she keeps from every open slate, correcting the tilt at every turn of the landing before it had time to settle into the wicker, and the instruction went to the wool, the river wind, the pot, the fire and the banked fire and never to the basket. \place{The seven walked-around places} were gone around with a look and a wide step, once and not twice, widest around \place{the returning-line place} where a hand had gone in on \emph{Seed 15} and had not come back, and the wideness was a keeping and not a sign, because a figure set against it would want a payer standing under it and none stood. At \place{the Vail corner} the clean dark line kept its finer grey hairline beside it called the thaw, thickened in the dry cold with the faint sour tang of \lore{Ash} coming up where no broom had gone in two years, and the ridge lay where a yard had left it, called dealt with in one place and left in another with a finger between. He was set under the roof and never in the Debt Loft, and the fold was put right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face, and \lore{the keeping at the fold} was found where put and stayed a finding, no third crust, no name, no gift, no payment and no payer.

Out in \place{the Clay Yard} the morning went on in its ordinary Fourthday grammar, a pail set on a stone where a pail always stood, a loaf pushed a finger's width along a board and stopped there with nobody calling the stopping a figure, barley passed from one end of the wall to the other without ever becoming a number, a neighbour with a rake raising it at the ridge and setting it down with nothing said. \place{The yard well} was a stone lip and a bucket-round from the basket's low angle and nothing more; no slate lay within reach of the wicker and no figure was given for the boy. He took the small sounds first because they do not lie and the large ones are mostly weather: the rope settling into its own knot, the rasp of a pot's foot on stone, a voice going off into ordinary talk. The boy took the yard as he found it and left it as he found it, and the surgeon's small clean satisfaction in a field taken as found and left as found cost him nothing, which was how he knew it was not peace.

At \place{the kindling} \person{Tarn} kept six pieces in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check and the seventh at the lip beside Garl's unforged collector sentence, with no name on the man who turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on the debt. The small knife lay at the very edge of its sill and his hand went over blade and sill and away as a man passes a hand over a tool put back where it belongs, and came away with no breath held at the top of the reach --- the coming away the whole of the checking. He cut nothing, counted nothing, spent nothing, and told the wood in the tone of a man stating the weather, “Six counted. The next I don't count.” He took the broom only after the flagstones had been given their instruction and said “Yes, house” to the question and to the thing behind it, agreeing so thoroughly the question had nowhere left to put a foot, and went over the stones without a turn of the head toward the landing. The ear-sentence about an ear under this roof being worth more than the debt that holds it came all the way to the word that would call the loan and stopped there, a remembered ninth stopping with no tenth, the \emph{Seed 16} breath drawn and left unspent, the loan uncalled at the very end of a term already ended, the fear of being entered again for vagrancy his without a figure, and a want he did not voice for a slate with his own name and no mark against it. A term ending is exposure and not freedom. The warm stone in its pot in the loft stayed unreconciled by not trying, which is not forgiving.

Jorra came through the finger's-width door off \place{the Vail wheel-shed} with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past his bent little finger and white in a fist-crease where a hand had opened, and it was not the bone-coloured \lore{slip} of the wheel. \place{The back room} off the shed kept its finger's width of open and its shape of dust, and no interior came under the basket's eyes and no name was given to the men who went low into it before first grey. He kept dusty forearms and clean ones on one tray and parted them as clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had been broken nor whether it was a birth-slate, while the broken ones under the lowest rack stayed entered by nobody. He spoke to the wheel and a pot before any person --- “It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady” --- and told the pot “You'll do” in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness, knowing the \lore{full note} it would give three turns ahead with air under it and a want in it, and withholding that knowing from any master come up from \place{High Kiln} who would call a pot early and sing it before its time. Sella had not finished telling him the fire was low when his bent finger went into the cold iron of the damper ahead of her sentence; he took the cold out of the fingertip, moved his tongue a hair's breadth and was left standing so the end of her instruction arrived at the pot, and came out without leaving the extra holding of \emph{Ash 26} a second time. One piece of iron had two bites, the holding and the taking, and no \lore{Grathic} word covered both mercies. “He's not Vail” stayed sealed as a lid nobody had tried to lift in three years, and the promise under the shed was neither given nor withdrawn while the burned slate stood at five, the shut-room tellings at two, and the four ownerless directions apart and unspent.

The going of the light came down off the walls by small gains nobody entered, and Sella carried him up the nineteen steps at the half-pace, went once around the seven places with a look and a wide step, set him under the roof of the Vail kiln-house in Low Kiln, Sill, Grath, and did not set him in the loft. She put the fold right without a look at his face and added no second fold to the empty place. Nothing from the yard, the well or the reported landing came under the roof as a cause, and the afternoon had earned only its own going of the light.

In the small hours \place{the towel} came out at the head of the bed as instruments come out when a case is done and before the next is called, rough from the wash and smelling of tallow and low smoke, and he went over the field by the place of each thing with every gap left a gap. The crust's gap stood two inches off with its corner a hair off the wool; the shelf's two gaps and the gone pan's round ring kept their places; \place{the field's empty place} out on \place{the worked strips} held the shape of a thing taken out, not a hole anybody dug; the keeping at the fold was found where put and still a finding, not a placing. The told bell kept its gap in its square beside the bell he had heard from the Cross, four, four and a gap, its gap not folded into \place{the carried fire}'s four and a gap under the floor, into the boards' four and four and a thumb-width knot where a fifth might have been driven and was not, or into the twelve readings behind one unsigned figure; three counts in three places stayed three, because agreeing any two would want a payer put under both and none stood. Four rooms kept the same gap with a finger between every pair and no Court room came under the roof to make a fifth. Six hearings of the full note stood beside and never among with the seventh place empty and not dug for or filled, and \place{the Drawing house}'s small flat knock stayed outside them on its own place; nine record-first hands stood with no tenth and nine stoppings stood with no tenth. The clerk's inward slate and the degree toward the landing stayed on separate places, Berr's low saying about kindness stayed on a place of its own beside neither the twelve findings nor the new figure, and the green at the pot board and the shape at the market's edge stayed sightings with no eighth of either. On \lore{the gap tray} the silence after the name from Weighing and the clean voice's missing name and the Long Night story's empty name sat each on its own place, and no name was set under any of them. From above and behind, where the Debt Loft kept its floor, a small dry sound moved and was not explained, and the boy filed it on the place where such sounds go and did not enter the room.

Answer three hundred and ninety-one came out and was filed. It was exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the twelve readings, the first of the new count, the printed figure, the fifth shaping, the bell's gap, the flat knock, the child's grey fingertips, the mother's cup, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the held breath, the two bites of iron, the river's skin, the widow's mark, the seventh notch, the clerk's inward slate, Berr's low saying nor the keeping at the fold. Six honest answers stood on the sixth place, three hundred and eighty-six through three hundred and ninety-one, and not one of them was a doctrine. The count came to four hundred and eight exact, six months and two hundred and twenty-eight days told by nobody who would have known what a number counted. The carried fire's fifth was unpaid the same number and its payer unnamed the same number, and Garl's name was unspoken the same number, the fire and the man standing side by side without becoming one. Nothing had been opened, joined, named, called, paid, forgiven or ended, and no door had been drawn across the tend. Under it, louder, the infant wanted the crust lifted two inches, the thread wiped and a hand on the pot board's handle, and got none of the three; both were true, the second was louder, neither won, and no verdict was looked for, because the quarrel was the work. Cold had come to cheek, temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back and stopped there as at the end of a line written out for it. The Afterdrop waited past a warming nobody here would perform. Through the finger's-width door \place{the river} kept its note of \lore{water over water}, low under \lore{the winter skin}, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming, found where put and not spent on a day that had earned only its fourteenth.

The hand outside the wool came up an inch toward the river's note under the winter skin. The inch was not a reach. He took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole on the note's own finishing under the covering --- neither early nor stayed --- and let it go as the note ended. Then the hand came straight down and laid itself flat against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. The crust's corner stood a hair off the wool and the thread was frozen and unwiped; four low pulls breathed below the floor with the fifth unsupplied; the first arithmetic of the year was up the road in a locked box with no key in his hand; the fifth shaping sat on its own tray with a finger between it and every other kept thing; and the house went on as a house goes when a pot is set down in its place and the fire kept --- forward, into a morning not yet earned and not brought into this one.
