The afternoon came down off the yard walls the way Ash comes down off a shelf --- by small gains nobody entered, a little at a time and without comment, until the light lay flat on the flagstones and the whole yard held what warmth it had the way a pot holds water, with care, and with one crack in it nobody mentioned. \person[Corin]{The boy} was carried out at the half-pace and set where the heat came to him sideways, and the instruction went to the wool, the river wind, the pot, the fire and the banked fire and never to the basket. Under the roof and out beneath the sky the day went on by its own grammar, and the surgeon took the small sounds first because they do not lie and the large ones are mostly weather.

There was a pail set on a stone where a pail always stood. There was a loaf pushed a finger's width along a board and stopped there, with nobody calling the stopping a figure and nobody pushing it back. Two voices passed the price of barley from one end of \place{the Clay Yard} to the other without ever making a number of it --- the words going mouth to mouth the way a coal goes hand to hand in a crowd, warm at each passing and worth nothing at the end of it. A rake rasped at the ridge at \place{the Vail corner}, lifted, held a moment, and set aside with nothing done and nothing said. At the corner the clean dark line kept its finer grey hairline beside it, the one the yard called \lore{the thaw}, thickened in the dry cold with the faint sour tang of \lore{Ash} coming up off it where no broom had gone in two years. A neighbour looked at the ridge, looked away, and went off into ordinary talk. He filed each small sound on the place where it was found and gave none of them a figure, because a figure set against a pail or a loaf would want a payer standing under it and none stood.

\place{The yard well} was a stone lip and a bucket-round from the basket's low angle and nothing more; the rope settled into its own knot, the bucket knocked the stone, and the water went down and came up without being entered. No slate lay within reach of the wicker and no figure was given for the boy. He took the yard as he found it and left it as he found it, and the surgeon's small clean satisfaction in a field taken as found and left as found cost him nothing, which was how he knew it was not peace. Under it, louder than the register, the wanting went on: the crust lifted two inches, the thread wiped, a hand on the pot board's handle. He wanted the three things without knowing that wanting them was a want, and he got none of the three. Both were true. The second was louder. Neither won, and no verdict was looked for, because the quarrel was the work.

When the light slackened off the walls he went around \place{the seven walked-around places} with a look and a wide step, once and not twice, and with no number. The wideness was at \place{the returning-line place} where a hand had gone in on \emph{Seed 15} and had not come back, and the wide step was a keeping and not a sign; a keeping asks nothing of the ground it goes around, while a figure set against it would want a payer standing under it and none stood. Out on \place{the worked strips} \place{the field's empty place} held the shape of a thing taken out, not a hole anybody had dug, and he did not lay that shape across the loaf's stopping, the ridge or the gone pan's round ring. There was a word in the yard for grey put down and another for grey gone, and the boy kept the called-gone grey beside the left-in-place ridge with a finger between, made no likeness a cause and entered into no agreement to stop looking at this one. No sixth shaping of the well-saying was made due by a quiet afternoon that had earned only its fourteenth.

\place{Slate Row} was a direction beyond the yard, and from that direction a man stood again at the yard's edge. He had been there in the morning. He was there now. From the basket's low angle he was first a pair of hands and then a face: soft hands of a man who sits at a table, with no clay ground into the creases and no rope-work in the palm, held loose at his sides; and the patient face of a man who can wait in a doorway without shifting his weight. Nobody asked what he wanted and he said no word that carried. No loaf passed to him, no slate was shown, no figure was read and no threshold was crossed under any roof. The boy filed the face on a place of its own beside the older record-first hands and did not count it among them, made no tenth of it, called the waiting no sale and the man no witness, and did not lay the face across \person[Tarn]'s ended term, the degree of the head toward the landing or \person[Jorra Vail]'s promise neither given nor withdrawn, to make a cause stand up where no man stood.

Costs came as directions and went away as weather and left no entry. A man come down \place{the clay road} brought a number up out of a shut fist toward the well, held it where the light could see it, looked at it as a man looks at a thing he has not decided to keep, and put it back into his sleeve; it arrived nowhere. A woman sent to ask what a pot cost was sent to the pot and got a note and not a price. The boy filed the order of each --- fist, light, sleeve; question, pot, note --- and gave no figure to either, because a cost that arrives nowhere has no space for a signature and the space between a figure and its price has no signature in it either. Nothing under any roof was made a reader by the carrying of a number or the asking of a pot.

\person{Sella Vail} came to \place{the hearth} before the basket at the going of the light, as she had come at the beginning of the day, and told the coals it was \lore{the thaw} in the voice another woman would have used for a child who could not hear her. She turned them with the back of a broken pot. She gave the small intake, the hold and the letting-go toward no opening, and figured neither of the two gaps. Her finger lay along the top of \place{the loaf shelf} so the charcoal tally on its underside stayed unread, and her lips moved on a two-gapped row and figured neither gap. One glance went to the clay jar standing cold under \lore{lead glaze} beyond \place{the pot board}, and the hand shut half over nothing, the little-door burn on the back of her left hand closing on air, and went back to the pot. At her throat \lore{the widow's mark} hung where it hung and was not touched: a going of the light, a hearth, a shelf and a basket are not openings, seven openings in seven months are seven and not a rule, and no eighth was made due on a day that had earned only its fourteenth.

She lifted the twice-mended pot thumb-first where the tighter wire crossed the crack, and the hairline of the original break showed between the old-brass staple and the wire, a dark thread with a hand's work on either side of it. \person[Garl Vail] was in that hairline, and in the salt-stiff coat with its left-shoulder crease on its peg behind the door, and in the hollow wheel-seat in the shed that nobody was allowed to fill, and not in a story anybody told. His name had not been spoken under the roof for four hundred and eight days, and the two counts --- his name and the fire's fifth --- stood side by side without becoming one. She said both proverbs to the pot and the fire and never to a person: \emph{Leave the crack showing. People trust a thing they can see has been handled} and \emph{If it's worth mending it's worth mending twice. The second time is the real one}. Repair was not restoration; a restored thing hides the hand that handled it and a mended thing leaves the hand showing, and a surgeon keeps a graft apart from a cure for the same reason a potter leaves a staple in the light. \lore{The tile debt} against a firing of tiles went on circling without a name and worse for being uncalled, and no collector came up or went down the road on a Fourthday to call it.

At \place{the rack end} she lifted a corner of the wool and turned the dressed grain piece by piece with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child, setting each down where it had lain, and took no figure from it and no handful for herself. The wool went back over the grain on \place{the drying racks} and the space stayed as it was. Then the pot went down on the pot board again with its rim two inches beyond the reaching hand, and there was a half-beat at the setting and no look toward \place{the bed}. The half-beat was the distance between an intention and an arrest: her hand had meant to set the pot nearer and had stopped where it stopped, and the distance cost her nothing and bought the boy nothing, and neither of them called it a gift. Under the roof nobody instructs a basket, and nobody instructs a pot about a hand.

She carried him up the nineteen steps at the half-pace from every open slate, went once around the seven places with a look and a wide step, widest around the returning-line place, set him under the roof and never in \place{the Debt Loft}, and put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face. She added no second fold. \lore{The keeping at the fold} was found where put and stayed a finding, no third crust, no name, no gift, no payment and no payer. Nothing from the yard, the well, the face at the edge or the reported landing came under the roof as a cause, and the afternoon had earned only its own going of the light.

At \place{the kindling} Tarn kept six pieces in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check and the seventh at the lip beside Garl's unforged collector sentence, with no name on the man who turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on the debt. He took the broom only after the flagstones had been given their instruction, and said “Yes, house” to the question and to the thing behind it, agreeing so thoroughly the question had nowhere left to put a foot. The small knife lay at the very edge of its sill; his hand went over blade and sill and away as a man passes a hand over a tool put back where it belongs, and came away with no breath held at the top of the reach --- the coming away the whole of the checking. He cut nothing, counted nothing, spent nothing, and told the wood in the tone of a man stating the weather, “Six counted. The next I don't count.” He went over \place{the flagstones} without a turn of the head toward the landing and did not repeat the degree. The ear-sentence about an ear under this roof being worth more than the debt came all the way to the word that would call the loan and stopped there, a remembered ninth stopping with no tenth, the \emph{Seed 16} breath drawn and left unspent, the loan uncalled at the very end of a term already ended. The warm stone in its pot in the loft stayed unreconciled by not trying, which is not forgiving.

Jorra came through the finger's-width door off \place{the Vail wheel-shed} with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past his bent little finger and white in a fist-crease where a hand had opened, and it was not the bone-coloured \lore{slip} of the wheel. \place{The back room} off the shed kept its finger's width of open and its shape of dust, and no interior came under the basket's eyes and no name was given to the men who went low into it before first grey. He kept dusty forearms and clean ones on one tray and parted them as clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had been broken nor whether it was a birth-slate, while the broken ones under the lowest rack stayed entered by nobody. He spoke to the wheel and a pot before any person --- “It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady” --- and told the pot “You'll do” in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness, knowing the \lore{full note} it would give three turns ahead with air under it and a want in it, and withholding that knowing from any master come up from \place{High Kiln} who would call a pot early and sing it before its time. Sella had not finished telling him the fire was low when his bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper} ahead of her sentence; he took the cold out of the fingertip, moved his tongue a hair's breadth and was left standing so the end of her instruction arrived at the pot. One piece of iron had two bites, the holding and the taking, and no \lore{Grathic} word covered both mercies; he took the finger out without leaving the extra holding of \emph{Ash 26} a second time, gathered once and not made again. “He's not Vail” stayed sealed as a lid nobody had tried to lift in three years, and the promise under the shed was neither given nor withdrawn while the burned slate stood at five, the shut-room tellings at two, and the four ownerless directions apart and unspent. The shed kept its silence.

In the small hours \place{the towel} came out at the head of the bed as instruments come out when a case is done and before the next is called, rough from the wash and smelling of tallow and low smoke, and he went over the field by the place of each thing with every gap left a gap. The crust's gap stood two inches off with its corner a hair off the wool; the shelf's two gaps and the gone pan's round ring kept their places; the field's empty place out on the worked strips held the shape of a thing taken out, not a hole anybody dug; the keeping at the fold was found where put and still a finding, not a placing. The fifth shaping of \lore{the well-saying} sat on its own tray between \person[Hessa Rale]'s eighth record-first hand and the saying of the road from \place{the Cross} to \place{Greybank}, with a finger between it and the four ownerless directions out of Slate Row --- \lore{the burned-slate word} joined to \lore{the counting room} shut in the war and the two shapes further named by nobody. It was not repeated, named, made due, joined or made a sixth by a tend-day that had earned only its fourteenth. \person{Ilva}'s strip of notched birch bark stayed in her sleeve offstage with her ninth hand staying nine and no figure coming off the wood. \person[Berr Clay]'s first arithmetic of the year was up the clay road in a locked box with no key in any hand under this roof, one reading and not an account, not a signature, not a payer and not a thirteenth; twelve arithmetics of Y.H. 813 stood unchanged behind one unsigned figure beside the first of a fresh count, and he did not lay twelve old findings across the new one to make a row come out even, for paper is not a payer. The clerk's inward slate at the landing and Tarn's degree toward the landing stayed on separate places with a finger between; the man from Slate Row's face stayed filed and not counted among the hands, and nothing from the well or the road had crossed under the roof as sign, lesson, cause, payment or payer.

The told bell kept its gap in its square beside the bell he had heard from the Cross, four, four and a gap, its gap not folded into \lore{the carried fire}'s four and a gap under the floor, into the boards' four and four and a thumb-width knot where a fifth might have been driven and was not, or into the twelve readings behind one unsigned figure; three counts in three places stayed three, because agreeing any two would want a payer put under both and none stood. Four rooms kept the same gap with a finger between every pair and no Court room came under the roof to make a fifth. Six hearings of the full note stood beside and never among with the seventh place empty and not dug for or filled, and \place{the Drawing house}'s small flat knock stayed outside them on its own place. Nine record-first hands stood with no tenth and nine stoppings stood with no tenth. The green at the pot board and the shape at the market's edge stayed sightings with no eighth of either; Berr's low saying about kindness stayed on a place of its own beside neither the twelve findings nor the new figure; and on \lore{the gap tray} the silence after the name from Weighing, the clean voice's missing name and the Long Night story's empty name sat each on its own place, and no name was set into any of them. No sixth hearing, tenth hand, tenth stopping, eighth opening or sixth shaping stood, and nothing was opened, joined, named, called, paid, forgiven or ended.

Answer three hundred and ninety-two came out and was filed. It was exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the twelve readings, the first of the new count, the printed figure, the fifth shaping, the face at the yard's edge, the bell's gap, the flat knock, the child's grey fingertips, the mother's cup, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the held breath, the two bites of iron, the river's skin, the widow's mark nor the keeping at the fold. Six honest answers stood on the sixth place, three hundred and eighty-seven through three hundred and ninety-two, and not one of them was a doctrine. The count came to four hundred and eight exact, six months and two hundred and twenty-eight days told by nobody who would have known what a number counted. The carried fire's fifth was unpaid the same number and its payer unnamed the same number, and Garl's name was unspoken the same number, the fire and the man standing side by side without becoming one. Under it, louder, the infant wanted the crust lifted two inches, the thread wiped and a hand on the pot board's handle, and got none of the three; both were true, the second was louder, neither won, and no verdict was looked for, because the quarrel was the work. Cold had come to cheek, temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rides back and stopped there as at the end of a line written out for it. \lore{The Afterdrop} waited past a warming nobody here would perform.

Through \place{the finger's-width door} \place{the river} kept its note of \lore{water over water}, low under \lore{the winter skin}, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming, found where put and not spent on a day that had earned only its fourteenth. The sound came from under the covering and not from the covering; it was not a hearing, not a sign, not a rule and not a payer, and the note ended on its own finishing before the hand came down. The hand outside the wool came up an inch toward the note. The inch was not a reach. He took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole on the note's own finishing under the covering --- neither early nor stayed --- and let it go as the note ended. Then the hand came straight down and laid itself flat against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. The crust's corner stood a hair off the wool and the thread was frozen and unwiped; four low pulls breathed below the floor with the fifth unsupplied; the first arithmetic of the year was up the road in a locked box with no key in his hand; the face at the yard's edge was filed and not counted; and the house went on as a house goes when a pot is set down in its place and the fire kept --- forward, into a morning not yet earned and not brought into this one.
