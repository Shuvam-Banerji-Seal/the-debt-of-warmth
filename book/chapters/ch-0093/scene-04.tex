The afternoon came on without a boundary drawn across it, as the morning had come on, and nobody under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill}, \place{Grath} gave the change a word. \person[Corin]{The boy} had been carried down after the first firing with the fold laid over his ears against the river wind, and \person{Sella Vail} carried him now at the half-pace she kept from every open slate, the pace of a woman carrying a pot filled to the lip that must not be slopped, giving the instruction to the wool and the wind and never to the basket. Behind them the yard kept the heat of the opening in its pots and its flagstones and went about its work as though no lid had been lifted anywhere, because no lid had been lifted anywhere that anybody would enter. Nobody made a feast of the dark month's first firing and nobody crossed himself. It was an ordinary tending afternoon, and the boy took it as the only honest record there was.

She went wide around the seven walked-around places with a look and a wide step and no number: the clean dark line at the Vail corner with the finer grey hairline beside it called \lore{the thaw}, unswept both; the space beneath \place{the lowest rack} where the broken birth-slates lay; the round ring where the shallow clay pan had once stood with no scrape, crumb, print, chip or word; the skin on \place{the trough}; the field's empty place out on \place{the worked strips}; the returning-line place where a hand had gone in on Seed 15 and had not come out. Seven, and no eighth likeness made a cause across any two of them, and no payer standing under any of them to be asked. The stayed frost had gone off the well's north lip on Bone 3 while the yard looked elsewhere, which was not a sign; the skin on the trough broke and closed under a pail's knock; the silt-clouded plate of ice stood on \place{the flat stone by the well} out of the path of feet. Three coverings called weather, entered nowhere, with a finger between every pair. In the first cold of \place{Ash} the rope at the well stiffened, the skin on the water lay longer, and the coals were banked an hour earlier at the going of the light; the yard gave nobody a figure for any of it.

He was carried everywhere and knew the small humiliations of being carried. One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from his ear, its corner standing a hair off the wool, the empty place beside it unnamed and no third come to make the row come out even. The thread at his mouth had frozen in the dry kiln heat and was unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. Cold had stopped at cheek, temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, as at the end of a line written out for it, with \lore{the Afterdrop} waiting past a warming nobody in this house would perform. He wanted the crust lifted the two inches, the thread wiped, and a hand that would close on the pot handle and bring it within reach. Both were true and the second was louder and neither won. The surgeon took the inventory in its old order and the infant underneath wanted what the inventory could not give, and the argument went on under the wool without a verdict. He had reduced a thousand shoulders in the dark and could not lift his own chin from a fold, and the joke of it lived in the exactness and not in the telling.

At \place{the yard well} \person{Ilva} drew with the pail against her knee, the rope stiff in the Ash cold and giving its small dry rasp as the bucket came up. Clay had dried pale in the crescents of her nails and the finer grey dust of the racks was worked into the seam of one thumb, which she did not scrub out. Her hands knew the weight of a pail and the edge of a wet board and had never been soft, and went on knowing these things without reporting on them to anybody; people noticed the pail first and the sleeve not at all, which was the order she preferred. She was of the yard and not of a house, and stood at the edge of the work where a slate had been left open a pace away on the stone lip, the writing turned toward its owner and nobody reading it aloud.

A woman waiting her turn asked what the firings of Ash had taken. Ilva's hand went toward her sleeve and stayed. The short strip of notched birch bark lay where it lay, pale where a thumb had rubbed the notches and dark in the uncut grain between; it was not taken out, read, copied, counted or joined to the open slate a pace away, the charcoal beneath \place{the loaf shelf}, the cord, the knot-string or the ownerless saying, and no figure came off the wood. Her eyes went to the asker and her mouth opened after both, with a half-beat between the looking and the word. \emph{Ask the pot what it cost. I keep the count, not the account}, she said, answering the question put and not the one behind it. She put the flat of her hand once more on the sleeve and went back to the pail, and nobody turned twice.

The boy took the order of her reaching apart as a chart is taken apart, by what was there and by what was not: hand to the record before eyes to the person, eyes before mouth. He filed it on a place of its own as the ninth record-first hand in the district, with a finger between it and \person{Hessa Rale}'s eighth, and filed no tenth. He made no figure of the slate's nearness, made her no reader of her own keeping, which she was not and had never wanted to be, and joined her to none. Nine objects were nine and not a rule, for a rule is an account that has found a payer and none stood to this one. The count stayed in wood where no slate could take a figure off it, and she did not know she had been read.

The month's hunts and repairs went past as directions and came to no entry. A man come down from \place{Cold Acre} with a hare across his shoulders shifted the weight when a woman asked what the carrying had cost, told her to ask the weather, which had carried the snow and could carry the price, and went off toward \place{the clay road} with nobody weighing the hare and nobody opening a slate. Another with a cracked jar in a sling of rope asked what the month's mending would want and was told to ask the pot; the pot, being a pot, said nothing. The boy took both answers apart the way he took apart a voice over the boards, by what was there and by what was not, and filed the blank where the number should have stood. A chart left blank where the number should stand is a chart still, and he entered neither the hare nor the jar nor their prices in it.

At \place{the rack end} the dressed grain lay covered under wool away from the hearth, turned with a lighter touch and given no figure. Sella's fingers went in and came out without closing. The handful she had lifted and let fall through her fingers on Bone 22 stayed one object on a place of its own and was not made again on the second of Ash, not a fifth held change, not a sign, not a payment, and she made no show of not repeating it; the empty fingers went beside it as a keeping and not as a second. The grain lay under wool and did not know what any figure would have cost.

At \place{the kindling} \person{Tarn} kept six years in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check and the seventh at the lip beyond it, where it could not, with \person{Garl Vail}'s unforged collector sentence lying beside the seventh and no name on the man who had turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on the debt. The small knife lay at the very edge of its sill, where a hand could check it and where a hand could also slip it off. \emph{It's back. You can check}, he told the empty yard in the tone of a man stating the weather, and then to the wood, \emph{Six counted. The next I don't count}. The sentence about an ear under this roof being worth more than the debt that held it came up to the word that would call the loan and stopped there, a remembered ninth stopping with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end, and still ungiven. The breath that should have gone in at the top of the reach stayed unspent, a small interval at the top of every reach, and his chest had been tight since the dark. Ash was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line and no scrubbing lifted it; the pale clip-ring at his left wrist, in place since Y.H. 806, held what little light there was and gave it back. His stroke went over the shallow bright patch on \place{the flagstones} without the half-check, and the place where it had stopped on another day stayed where it was. The warm loft stone lay cold in its unmarked pot, unreconciled by not trying, which is not forgiveness. His thumb's resting on the uncut seventh on Bone 26 was checked and found where it had been left and was not made again on Ash 2: not a tenth stopping, not the seventh year given, not a payment, not a fifth held change, not a hearing, not a mending, not a rule. No collector came up the clay road or went down it.

\person{Jorra Vail} came through the finger's-width door off \place{the Vail wheel-shed} at the going of the light with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bent little finger and white in the creases where a fist had opened, and it was not the bone-coloured \lore{slip} of the wheel. Three or more men had gone low into \place{the back room} before first grey and he gave the house no name for them and did not say the room was cold. He kept dusty forearms and clean ones on one tray and parted them as clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had been broken nor whether it was a birth-slate. He spoke to the wheel before he spoke to any person: \emph{It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady}; and told a pot \emph{You'll do} in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either. He knew \lore{the full note} that pot would give three turns ahead, with air under it and a want in it, and kept the knowing where nobody could make a song of it, withheld from a kiln-master come up from \place{High Kiln} who would have called it early and made something of it. When Sella had not finished telling him the fire was low his bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper}, took the cold out of the fingertip, moved the tongue a hair's breadth and was left standing, so that the end of her instruction arrived at the pot and the household straightened around his silence. One piece of iron had two bites, the holding and the taking, with no Grathic word for both mercies. \emph{He's not Vail} stayed sealed as tightly as a lid nobody had tried to lift in three years, and the promise of the boy's name to the men who burn slates was neither given nor withdrawn. The burned slate stood at five with a finger between the fifth and the four and no sixth due on a day that had not earned one.

Garl stayed where he had been kept, in objects and not in a story: the salt-stiff coat with the left-shoulder crease on its peg behind the door, the wheel-seat in the shed keeping the hollow twenty years of another man's weight had worn into it and that nobody filled, the clean dark line and its finer grey unswept, the twice-mended pot on \place{the pot board} with the hairline of the original break showing between the old-brass staple and the tighter wire. Both proverbs went to the pot and the fire and never to a person, so that repair stayed apart from restoration. The debt against a firing of tiles was neither paid nor settled nor done with and was worse for being uncalled. His name had not been spoken under this roof three hundred and thirty-one days, and no lesson had been made of any of it.

At the going of the light Sella carried him up at the half-pace, went wide around the seven walked-around places again with a look and a wide step and no number, and set him on \place{the bed} under the roof and never in \place{the Debt Loft}. She put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face, let her hand rest the half-beat longer than the setting needed before it went away, and left the thread where it lay. A sound came from above and behind, a foot on the loft stair, and no interior was entered and no word was spoken about it. She gave the instruction to the fire, the wool, the pot and the running water and never to the basket, and said \emph{The house will keep it} where another woman would have said I. Then she went to \place{the hearth} before the basket, told the coals it was the thaw and turned them with the back of a broken pot, and banked them for the cold; her finger lay on top of the loaf shelf so the charcoal tally stayed unread, her lips moved along a two-gapped row and figured neither gap, and her eye went once to the clay jar cold under its glaze with her hand shut half over nothing, the little-door burn closing on air. She lifted the twice-mended pot with her thumb first where the wire crossed the crack, said both proverbs to the pot and the fire, and left the crack showing; the rim went down two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat at the setting and no look toward the bed. The widow's mark hung where it hung and was not touched. An afternoon and a going of the light and a bed are not openings; six openings in six months were six and not a rule, and the hand had gone to the mark before the kiln and at no other hour.

Behind the wall the kiln she had opened in the morning breathed four low pulls with the silence where the fifth should have stood, and under the floor \lore{the carried fire} gave four low pulls with the same silence, its payer unnamed three hundred and thirty-one days. Overhead \place{the boards} kept four nails on the near board, four on the next and a thumb-width knot where a fifth might have been driven and was not. Through the finger's-width door \lore{water over water} went low over lower, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming, and was not numbered among the hearings. Three counts in three places, and none rose into another, because agreeing any two would have wanted a payer put under both and none stood. The bell's gap left behind in the square was not fetched in to make a fourth; the heard bell stayed in its square beside the told bell of \lore{the Long Night}, and the told figure stood beside the paid figure with no signature on the space between. Six hearings of the full note stood beside and never among with the seventh place empty, the sixth still the small flat knock out of a woman as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go and never among the sources. Six arithmetics of the year rode locked up the clay road in \person{Berr Clay}'s box, one in twelve unchallenged, the figure read still not the figure paid, six readings six and not one account; the finding sat on a place of its own with no payer under it and was not laid across the house's \lore{not-doings}, the dressed grain covered under wool, the four held changes, the handful, the thumb's resting, the two small objects of the hand or his own gaps. The edges came near to touching. The finger went between.

The file lay open on \place{the towel} at the head of the bed with every object on its own place and every gap left gap. In the small hours, with the yard quiet and the river's note at its lowest under the finger's-width door, the hand outside the wool went up an inch toward the sound. It took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole, let the intake go on the same low note, and came straight down and laid itself back against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. The note had done before the hand came down, so the letting-go was neither early again nor stayed, and the hand did not stay. The inch was not a reach and the intake was not a breath kept for warmth; the interval was not a stopping of the river and the note was not his. He filed it as answer two hundred and thirty-eight: exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment, borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the six arithmetics, the bell's gap, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the sixth opening nor his own kept gaps. Two hundred and thirty-eight honest answers stood on the sixth place, each an object and not a power, and neither the number nor the small hand-movings that had made it stood up and called themselves a doctrine, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood.

The surgeon took his small clean satisfaction in a field left as found and in an answer entered where nobody could see it. The infant underneath took none of it. One stiff brown-edged crust lay two inches from his ear with its empty place unnamed and no third; the thread lay frozen and unwiped; cold had stopped at cheek, temple and the wrist-joint as at the end of its line and the afterdrop waited past a warming nobody would perform. He wanted the crust lifted and the thread wiped and a hand on the pot handle. Both were true and the second was louder and neither won, and the two registers quarrelled without a verdict in the small hours under the roof. No comfort was given and none was taken. Three hundred and thirty-one stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in Low Kiln, Sill and Grath; six months and one hundred and fifty-one days were told by nobody; the carried fire's fifth was unpaid the same number of days with its payer unnamed; and the name of the man the salt-stiff coat kept was unspoken the same number. Nothing on the second of Ash had been opened, joined, named, called, paid or ended except the one opening at the kiln mouth, which changed the house's count and changed nothing anybody would enter. His exact noticing was the only record there was, and it was worth nothing at two inches of air and was not for sale. Ilva's strip lay in her sleeve with no figure off the wood; the opened kiln missed its fifth behind the wall for the three hundred and thirty-first day; the file lay open on the towel with every gap left gap; and the river's low note went quiet under the finger's-width door while the third stood one morning ahead and was not brought into a day that had not earned it.
