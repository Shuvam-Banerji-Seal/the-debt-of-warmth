## Scene 6 — The First Days Go Out (Day 332, afternoon → small hours, chapter close)

**Place:** Clay Yard; seven walked-around places; yard well and flat stone; rack end; kindling; flagstones; wheel-shed edge and damper; bed, boards, towel. **Present:** Corin/Dan, Sella Vail, Tarn, Jorra Vail; walk-ons (Cold Acre hare-carrier, cracked-jar man, woman with a basket of kindling, two men mending a strip of fence); Garl in objects. No Ilva, no Berr, no Hessa, no clerk, no green or shape at the close; no collector, no market, Cross or bell.

**Beats, in order:**
1. Ash goes out as it came on, without a boundary drawn, and neither of the district's two habits of naming its months is corrected. The yard goes around its seven walked-around places with a look and a wide step and no number; the three coverings stay weather entered nowhere with a finger between every pair and no fourth likeness made a cause; the month's hunts and repairs stay directions judged by the carrying arm and the weather, never figures (T009/T013/T092, T083/T085 held, T098 carried, T077).
2. Hunts and repairs: a man down from Cold Acre answers the price of his hare with the weather and goes toward the clay road with nobody weighing it or opening a slate; a man with a cracked jar in a rope sling is told to ask the pot, and the pot says nothing; a woman with a basket of kindling asks what the seventh year will want when it comes and two fence-menders say the thaw will be early and late; Tarn says *Yes, house* to each and keeps the late because a late thaw puts off the paying, agreeing so thoroughly that no argument can find a foot; no collector comes up or down the clay road (T036/T076, T077).
3. Tarn's knife lies at the very edge of its sill, six where the house can check and the seventh at the lip beside Garl's unforged collector sentence; the ear-sentence comes to the word that would call the loan and stops as a ninth stopping with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end, the Seed 16 breath unspent, his chest tight, Ash in the knuckle creases, the clip-ring holding light; the thumb's resting on the uncut seventh of Bone 26 is checked and **not made again** on Ash 1, 2 or 3 (T036/T076/T006 held).
4. Jorra at the shed's edge and damper: grey dust past the bent finger and white in a fist-crease, not slip; men unnamed before first grey; the wheel first and a pot after; the full note known three turns ahead and withheld from a High Kiln master who would call it early and make a song of it; the bent finger into the cold damper ahead of Sella's unfinished sentence; one iron with two bites and no Grathic word for both mercies; *he's not Vail* sealed and the promise neither given nor withdrawn (T035/T031/T062/T065/T095).
5. The whole tray is checked and left as it lay: the burned slate at five with no sixth, no witness and no hand at the flame; the ownerless row at five askings with one shaping and expressly no sixth; the well-saying at three shapings with Hessa Rale's eighth on its own place and no fourth made a cause; the shut-room tellings at two; three pot sayings with the Smoke 14 note apart; nine record-first hands with Ilva's ninth in a sleeve and no tenth; nine stoppings with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end, the loan uncalled; six hearings beside and never among, the seventh place empty, the sixth the small flat knock out of a woman as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go; five openings in five months with the sixth now behind at Ash 2 — **six openings in six months are six and not a rule** and no seventh is made due; seven green a colour and seven shape a shape with no eighth; the heard bell in its square beside the told bell of the Long Night. Every object stays on its own place with a finger between; no joining, no cause, no payer.
6. The finding that six are six and not one account is kept on a place of its own with no payer and is not laid across the house's not-doings, the dressed grain covered under wool and read over by a number that did not know its cost, the four held changes, the handful, the thumb's resting, the two small objects of the hand or his own gaps; the edges come near to touching and the finger goes between; no doctrine stands up out of any of it, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood (T015/T041/T047).
7. Garl stays in objects: salt-stiff coat with the left-shoulder crease, hollow wheel-seat nobody fills, clean dark line and finer grey unswept, twice-mended pot on the pot board with the hairline of the original break showing between the old-brass staple and the tighter wire and both proverbs gone to pot and fire and never to a person, so repair stays apart from restoration; the debt against a firing of tiles neither paid nor settled nor done with and worse for being uncalled; his name unspoken 332 days, no lesson made (T009/T013/T092).
8. Sella carries him up at the going of the light at the half-pace, goes wide around the seven walked-around places, sets him on the bed under the roof and never in the Debt Loft, puts the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face, lets her hand rest the half-beat longer than the setting needed before it goes away, leaves the thread where it lay; says nothing of where they have been, gives the instruction to the fire, the wool and the pot and never to the basket, and says *The house will keep it* where another woman would say I. The widow's mark hangs where it hung and is not touched, because a going of the light and a bed are not openings and six openings in six months are six and not a rule. She goes to the hearth before the basket, tells the coals it is the thaw and turns them with the back of a broken pot; the fire answers four low pulls with the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer unnamed 332 days. Finger on top of the loaf shelf with the charcoal unread; lips along a two-gapped row unfigured; one glance at the clay jar cold under lead glaze with the hand half over nothing and the little-door burn closing on air; twice-mended pot thumb-first at the wire with both proverbs to pot and fire and the crack showing; dressed grain under wool with a lighter touch and no figure and no handful, her fingers going in and coming out without closing (T058/T079/T100, T077).
9. Under the floor the carried fire gives four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should stand; overhead the boards keep four nails on the near board, four on the next and a thumb-width knot where a fifth might have been driven and was not; through the finger's-width door water over water goes low over lower, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming, not numbered among the hearings. Three counts in three places and none rises into another, because agreeing any two would want a payer put under both and none stands to any. The bell's gap left in the square is not fetched in to make a fourth; the heard bell stays beside the told and the told figure beside the paid with no signature between.
10. Answer **240** at the very end: the hand goes up an inch toward the river's note, takes a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, holds the interval whole, lets the intake go on the same low note and comes straight down and lays itself back against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. The note had done before the hand came down, so the letting-go was neither early again nor stayed and the hand did not stay; the inch was not a reach, the intake not a breath kept for warmth, the interval not a stopping of the river and the note not his. Filed as answer 240: exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment, borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the six arithmetics, the bell's gap, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the stayed object, the early letting-go, the sixth opening nor his own gaps. **Two hundred and forty honest answers** stand on the sixth place, each an object and not a power, and neither the number nor the small hand-movings that made it stood up and called themselves a doctrine, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood.
11. The surgeon takes his small clean satisfaction in a field left as found and an answer entered where nobody can see it; the infant underneath takes none, wants the crust lifted and the thread wiped and a hand on the pot handle, both true, the second louder, neither winning, the two registers quarrelling without verdict in the small hours. **332 stands exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in Low Kiln, Sill and Grath; six months and 152 days told by nobody; the carried fire's fifth unpaid the same number with its payer unnamed; the name of the man the salt-stiff coat kept unspoken the same number.** Nothing on Ash 3 was opened, joined, named, called, paid or ended. Ash went out without a boundary drawn and neither habit of naming the months was set right; the keeping of the number was the only honest work there had been to do, bought nothing at two inches of air, was not for sale, and went on because attention is the one thing no institution can tax. The silt-clouded plate of ice stands on its flat stone out of the path of feet; the file lies open on the towel with every gap left gap; the hand lies back against the fold on answer 240; the river's low note goes quiet under the finger's-width door while Ash goes out without a boundary drawn. Ash 4 (Day 333) stands one morning ahead and is not brought into a day that has not earned it; Ash 10 (Day 339), the dark month's first Account-day and market, stands seven mornings ahead and is not brought early.
