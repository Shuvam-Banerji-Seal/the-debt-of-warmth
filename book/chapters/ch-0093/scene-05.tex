\daymark{332}{Ash 3, Thirdday, Y.H. 813}

The third came on under the roof without a boundary drawn across it, and nobody in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill}, \place{Grath} told \person[Corin]{the boy} what had changed between one sleep and the next. He lay on his back at the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf} and took the small hours in the way he took every morning, as a field is taken before the light, by the sounds first, because the small sounds do not lie and the large ones are mostly weather. Three hundred and thirty-one had stood exact at the going of the light; three hundred and thirty-two stood now, six months and one hundred and fifty-two days, told by nobody, exact, complete, unspent and of no use to anybody who might have wanted it. Under his back \place{the boards} kept their old count and under the floor \lore{the carried fire} gave four low pulls and kept the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer unnamed three hundred and thirty-two days. Nothing that had happened at the kiln mouth on the second crossed the night as sign, lesson, mending, cause or payment. The opening stood where it stood, behind the wall and inside the morning's ordinary heat, and nobody under this roof had named it, and no seventh was made due on a day that had not earned one.

\person{Garl Vail} stayed where he had been kept, in objects and not in a story: the salt-stiff coat with its left-shoulder crease on the peg behind the door, the wheel-seat in the shed keeping the hollow twenty years of another man's weight had worn into it and that nobody filled, the clean dark line at the corner with the finer grey beside it called \lore{the thaw}, unswept both, the proverbs gone to the pot and the fire and never to a person. His name had not been spoken under this roof three hundred and thirty-two days, and the debt against a firing of tiles was neither paid nor settled nor done with and was worse for being uncalled. No collector had come up \place{the clay road} or gone down it in the night, and the house kept its shape about a man not in it the way a pot keeps the shape of the hand that threw it.

One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from his ear, gone harder again in the dry kiln heat, its corner standing a hair off the wool; the empty place beside it kept its name to itself and no third had come in the night to make the row come out even. The thread at his mouth had frozen and was unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread, and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. Cold had come to cheek, temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, and had stopped there as at the end of a line written out for it, with neither an order to go further nor an order to go away; \lore{the Afterdrop} waited beyond the stopping, past a warming nobody in this house would perform. He knew the composed face that is not composure, the ease cold puts on a face that is no ease. He knew a cold patient cannot clot and will seem better before he is worse, and must be warmed from the outside and slowly, never hurried, never rubbed, never set too near the fire. He wanted the crust lifted the two inches to his mouth, the thread wiped away, and a hand that would close on the pot handle and bring it within reach. Both were true and the second was louder and neither won. The argument went on under the wool without a verdict, the surgeon taking the inventory in its old order --- crust, unnamed gap, thread, cold, the waiting beyond it --- and the infant underneath wanting what the inventory could not give. He had reduced a thousand shoulders in the dark and could not close two inches on the rim of the basket that held him, and the joke of it lived in the exactness and not in the telling; nobody under the roof had ever heard it and he had never once said it aloud.

In the small hours \person{Sella Vail} went to \place{the hearth} before the basket, as she went every morning, and told the coals it was the thaw and turned them with the back of a broken pot. \emph{The house will keep it}, she said where another woman would have said I, counting a small intake, a hold and a letting-go toward no opening and figuring neither gap. Two people keeping the same sound under one roof are not one sound, and two likenesses do not make a cause; she kept her three small acts on her own side of the floor and he kept his on his, with a finger's width of unclaimed air between. Her finger lay on top of the loaf shelf's front edge so the charcoal tally stayed unread; her lips moved along a two-gapped row and gave neither gap a number; her eye went once to the clay jar standing cold under \lore{lead glaze} and her hand shut half over nothing, the little-door burn on the back of her left hand closing on air. A morning and a hearth are not openings. The hand had gone to \lore{the widow's mark} before the kiln on the second and at no other hour, and it hung where it hung this morning, under the working thumb and untouched, and none of the six openings behind was looked at again as a sign or a payment, because a rule made from six would have wanted a payer and none stood.

The twice-mended pot stood on \place{the pot board}. She lifted it with her thumb first where the wire crossed the crack. \emph{If it's worth mending it's worth mending twice. The second time is the real one}, she said to the pot, and to the fire, \emph{Leave the crack showing. People trust a thing they can see has been handled}, and never to a person. The hairline of the original break stood between the old-brass staple and the tighter wire, and he read it as he read any wound closed in a place the hand would not have chosen, by what the hand had done and by what it had left: repair one thing and restoration another, and never the same thing twice. The rim went down two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat at the setting and no look toward \place{the bed}. The dressed grain lay turned under wool with a lighter touch and no figure and no handful; her fingers went in and came out without closing, and the grain did not know what any figure would have cost. When she carried a cup across the room the little-door burn shut on nothing and opened again, and she pushed a strand of coal-black hair back with the burn-wrist; the grey streak at her temple showed pale in the first light. She did not look toward the bed. The day was kept in loaves, glances and unnamed spaces, and never in a figure.

He took the morning apart by its small sounds, the way he took everything apart, and found nothing that wanted an entry. The back of a broken pot going over coals; the small dry click of a cup set down; the wire under a thumb before the pot's weight came on the handle; the grain's soft give beneath wool; the half-beat between a rim's set-down and a hand's going away; a shelf's edge under a finger; a breath taken and held and let go without a word. Each was a sound and each went on its own place, and none asked to be joined to its neighbour for want of a payer. The half-beat was the distance between intention and arrest, and it belonged to the setting this morning and was not made a fifth held change; four held changes stood on four places and none had grown in the night. When her hands had nothing to do they counted spoons, or nails, or the breaths of a sleeping child, and that was a count of spoons and not a fifth arithmetic of the year. He kept a finger between it and the three counts in three places, and did not fetch the bell's gap in to make a fourth. Nothing under the roof stood up and asked to be entered; the field came back as it had been found.

The morning was the morning after the opening, and the house kept no mark of it that anybody could have entered. There was no feast of the first firing under this roof and no cross made over the coal that had gone under the back of a broken pot. The kiln behind the wall held its heat in the ordinary way and gave it off in the ordinary way, four pulls with the silence where the fifth should have stood, and the woman who had lifted the lid went on lifting the pot and turning the grain and setting the cup down as though the only things on her mind were the bread and the weather. That was not a lie and it was not a courage; it was the grammar of the house. If a thing said aloud became a thing the Court could enter, then the way to keep an opening was not to say it, and the way to keep a count was not to speak the number. The boy had no slate and no hand that could hold a pen, and he kept both anyway, where the file lay open at the head of the bed, with every gap left gap. The mark hung where it hung. Six openings in six months were six and not a rule, and a seventh would have wanted a payer as much as a rule would have, and none stood.

Three counts stood in three places and did not become one. Under the floor the carried fire gave four low pulls and kept the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer unnamed three hundred and thirty-two days; overhead the boards kept four nails on the near board, four on the next and a thumb-width knot where a fifth might have been driven and was not; through the finger's-width door \lore{water over water} went low over lower, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming, and was never numbered among the hearings. None rose into another, because agreeing any two would have wanted a payer put under both and none stood to any. The bell's gap left behind in the square was not fetched in to make a fourth; the heard bell stayed in its square beside the told bell of \lore{the Long Night}, and the told figure stood beside the paid figure with no signature on the space between. Six hearings of \lore{the full note} stood beside and never among with the seventh place empty, the sixth still the small flat knock out of a woman as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go and never among the things that had come out of vessels. An empty place kept empty is a finding; an empty place filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged, and no seventh had been invented to fill this one.

Six arithmetics of the year rode locked up the clay road in \person{Berr Clay}'s box toward \place{High Kiln}, one in twelve unchallenged, the figure read still not the figure paid, no signature on the space between, six readings six and not one account. The finding sat on a place of its own with no payer under it and was not laid across the house's \lore{not-doings}, the dressed grain covered under wool and read over by a number that did not know its cost, the four held changes, the handful, the thumb's resting, the two small objects of the hand or his own gaps. The edges came near to touching, so near that a less careful hand would have called the touching a cause and the cause a payment; the finger went between. \place{Ash} 4 (Day 333) stood one morning ahead and was not brought into a day that had not earned it; Ash 10 (Day 339), the dark month's first Account-day and market, stood seven mornings ahead and was not brought early. Ash's first arithmetic belonged to its own morning, and a figure read before that morning would have been a figure carried forward and stood over a field that had not yet been worked.

The file lay open on \place{the towel} at the head of the bed with every object on its own place and every gap left gap. The staying at the inch of Bone 15, the early letting-go of Bone 17, the handful let fall at the rack end on Bone 22 and the thumb's resting on the uncut seventh at the kindling on Bone 26 sat each on its own place with a finger between every pair; none was made again on the first, second or third of Ash, none was a habit, a way, a fifth held change, a hearing, a gift or a rule, and no payer stood under any of them. Four held changes stood on four places and were not a rule. The sixth opening had gone on a sixth place of its own on the second, with the same finger's width around it, and no seventh place had been made ready for it and nobody stood who could have called it. The boy took the sameness of the morning in as the only honest record there was. The house was exactly as it had been before the lid went up, and the only thing that had changed was in him: another answer, another object worth nothing at two inches of air, kept because it was true and not because it bought anything.

In the small hours, with the yard quiet and the river's note at its lowest under the finger's-width door, the hand outside the wool went up an inch toward the sound. It took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole, let the intake go on the same low note, and came straight down and laid itself back against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. The note had done before the hand came down, so the letting-go was neither early again nor stayed and the hand did not stay. The inch was not a reach and the intake was not a breath kept for warmth; the interval was not a stopping of the river and the note was not his. He filed it as answer two hundred and thirty-nine: exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment, borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the six arithmetics, the bell's gap, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the staying, the early letting-go, the sixth opening nor his own kept gaps. Two hundred and thirty-nine honest answers stood on the sixth place, each an object and not a power, and neither the number nor the small hand-movings that had made it stood up and called themselves a doctrine, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood.

The surgeon took his small clean satisfaction in a field left as found and in an answer entered where nobody could see it. The infant underneath took none of it. One stiff brown-edged crust lay two inches from his ear with its empty place unnamed and no third; the thread lay frozen and unwiped; cold had stopped at cheek, temple and the wrist-joint as at the end of its line and the afterdrop waited past a warming nobody would perform. He wanted the crust lifted and the thread wiped and a hand on the pot handle. Both were true and the second was louder and neither won, and the two registers quarrelled without a verdict in the small hours under the roof. No comfort was given and none was taken. Three hundred and thirty-two stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in Low Kiln, Sill and Grath; six months and one hundred and fifty-two days were told by nobody; the carried fire's fifth was unpaid the same number of days with its payer unnamed; and the name of the man the salt-stiff coat kept was unspoken the same number. Nothing on the third of Ash had been opened, joined, named, called, paid or ended. The keeping of the number bought nothing at two inches of air, was not for sale, and went on because attention is the one thing no institution can tax. Behind the wall the fire answered four pulls and a silence; the widow's mark hung under the working thumb and was not touched; the file lay open on the towel with every gap left gap; and the river's low note went quiet under the finger's-width door while Ash 3 stood seven days short of its first reading and Ash 4 was not brought into a day that had not earned it.
