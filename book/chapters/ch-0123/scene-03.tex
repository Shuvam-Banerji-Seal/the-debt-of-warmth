\daymark{421}{Ilmen 27, Y.H. 814, Seventhday}

The small hours had a weather of their own, and it came through a finger's width of door. \place[The towel]{The towel} lay at the head of \place{the bed} as instruments lie when a case is done and before the next is called, rough from the wash and smelling of tallow and low smoke, and the hand outside the wool lay flat on the fold where it had been laid at the last answer. Four hundred and sixteen exact, and the count standing at four hundred and twenty, and nobody under any roof knowing what a count was. It was a new day. Nothing had crossed into it from the one behind --- not as a sign, not as a lesson, not as a mending, not as a cause, not as a payment and not as a payer. \person[Corin]{The boy} lay at the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf} in \place{the Vail kiln-house}, \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill}, \place{Grath}, under the roof and never in \place{the Debt Loft}, held where the heat came to him sideways and not where he would have chosen; the room kept its heat the way a cup keeps water, with care, and with one crack nobody had mentioned. Above and behind, the loft kept a sound that was not a footfall and was not repeated, and he left it where it was.

He took the field in the old order, because the order was the only instrument he had. One stiff brown-edged barley crust stood two inches from his ear, gone past leather into old horn in the dry nights, with its corner a hair off the wool; a child under this roof would have tried to eat it four nights ago and would not try now. Beside it the empty place held \lore{the keeping at the fold}, found where put through the mornings and goings of the light behind him, still a finding and not a placing, with no second fold, no third crust, no name, no gift, no payment and no payer. The thread of drool at his mouth had frozen in the dry kiln heat and was unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread. Cold had come to cheek and temple and the small bare joint at the wrist where the wool rode back, and had stopped there as if it had reached the end of its own instruction; a face composed by cold is not composure, and a cold patient cannot clot. The Afterdrop waited past a warming nobody in this house would perform. He knew that a basket set level out of the draught where the heat came sideways was the rewarming he would choose, and he could not ask for it.

The infant wanted three things without knowing that wanting them was a want. He wanted the crust lifted two inches, the thread wiped, and a hand on the handle of the pot. Both registers were true; the second was louder; neither won. It was not a verdict but the work, and the work went on in silence in a room whose floor did not touch his.

Under the flat hand the file kept its old shape. Six hearings of \lore{the full note} stood beside and never among, with the seventh place empty and not dug for, and \place{the Drawing house}'s flat knock outside them on its own ground. The bell heard from \place{the Cross} kept its square beside the told bell, four, four and a gap, and its gap was not folded into \lore{the carried fire}'s four and a gap below \place{the boards}, or the boards' four and four and a thumb-width knot where a fifth nail might have been driven and was not, or the two readings of the year behind one unsigned figure beside the twelve arithmetics of Y.H. 813 --- three counts in three places staying three, because agreeing any two would want a payer put under both and none stood. The two readings stood two and not one account, the first not made a thirteenth by the second, the figure read still not the figure paid, no signature on the space and no doctrine out of two; \person{Berr Clay}'s box had gone up \place{the clay road} locked and the key had gone up in a coat pocket that never came back down, and paper is not a payer.

Nine record-first hands stood with no tenth and nine stoppings with no tenth; \person[Hessa Rale]{Hessa}'s narrow slate stayed unread up the road, and \person{Ilva}'s short strip of notched birch stayed unread in a sleeve in the yard, her hand ninth with no tenth made of it. The offered figure sat where it had been put on a place of its own beside her ninth hand and the fifth shaping of \lore{the well-saying}, with a finger between every pair --- fist, eye, mouth, the half-beat in the gap, the figure gone back into the sleeve and arriving nowhere. It was not offered again on a morning that had earned only the twenty-seventh, not made a custom, not a sale, not a tenth hand, not a reader made of the strip, not a payer and not an account. The clerk's entered figure stayed reported at \place{the out-of-line landing} below \place{the north bridge}, a figure entered before the eyes went up, on a place of its own with a finger between it and \person{Tarn}'s degree toward the landing and another between it and the face at the yard's edge --- a report brought up the yard and never a sighting, not the calling of the loan, not a tenth hand and not a rule. The asking answered with the weather stayed beside the second man's direction down the bridge road and his narrow slate turned to the sleeve, and the fifth shaping sat on its own tray between the eighth hand and the road saying with a third finger between it and the four ownerless directions. No joining stood among them, no witness, no reader, no sale, no tenth hand and no paying. \person{Garl Vail}'s name had gone unspoken four hundred and twenty days and the fifth pull below the boards had gone unpaid the same number, the fire and the man standing side by side without becoming one.

First light came thin through the finger's-width door, the kind that shows dust rather than warms it. \place[The river]{The river} kept its note of \lore{water over water} under the winter skin, a low thing over a lower thing, softer at one edge where the low note ran nearer under it, and the boy found it where put and called it weather, not a hearing, not a sign and not a fourth of \lore{the three coverings}. The count became four hundred and twenty-one exact, six months and two hundred and forty-one days, told by nobody who would have known what a number counted. He did not stand it up as a threshold, a doctrine or a figure, and did not lay the twelve old arithmetics across the two readings of the new year to make a row come out even, for a row made even on paper is not a payment and no key came under any roof. A number counted is a number with air around it; it bought no warmth and borrowed no payer. Ilmen 30 stood three mornings off, a date and not a day earned, and he did not bring it into a morning that had earned only the twenty-seventh.

\person{Sella Vail} came to \place{the hearth} before she came to the basket. She told the coals it was \lore{the thaw} in the voice another woman would have used for a child who could not hear her, and turned them with the back of a broken pot; the back scraped over them and left the blind ash where it lay, and the coals gave off the dry smell of a fire banked and not forgotten. She gave the small intake, the hold and the letting-go toward no opening, figuring neither of the two gaps. \emph{The house will keep it} went to the fire and not to the basket, because a thing said aloud becomes a thing the Court can enter. Her finger lay along the top of \place{the loaf shelf} so that the charcoal tally beneath stayed unread, and her lips moved on a two-gapped row with neither gap figured. One glance went to the clay jar standing cold under \lore{lead glaze} beyond \place{the pot board}; the hand shut half over nothing, and the little-door burn on the back of her left hand closed on air and went back to the pot. She did not touch \lore{the widow's mark}. A morning, a hearth, a shelf, a yard and a basket are not openings; seven openings in seven months are seven and not a rule, and no eighth came due on a day that had earned only its twenty-seventh.

She said to the fire, in the tone of a woman who has told the same thing to the same fire for three winters, \emph{The fire doesn't know you're sorry. Feed it}, and put two fingers of coal where the coals had thinned, and did not look to see whether the basket had heard. Her hands went on counting when they had nothing to do --- three spoons, a nail, the small breath a child makes that nobody numbers --- and came back to the hairline at the wire as hands come back to a wound they have dressed. At \place{the rack end} the dressed grain lay covered under wool. She turned it piece by piece with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child, set each piece down where it had lain, took no figure from it and no handful, and drew the wool back with the flat of her hand. Then she lifted the twice-mended pot thumb-first where the tighter wire crossed the crack. The hairline of the original break showed between the old-brass staple and the wire, a dark thread with a hand's work on either side, and both proverbs went to the pot and the fire and never to a person: \emph{Leave the crack showing. People trust a thing they can see has been handled} and \emph{If it's worth mending it's worth mending twice. The second time is the real one}. Repair was not restoration. A restored thing hides the hand that handled it; a mended thing leaves the hand showing, so that a vessel carrying its break can be read by the next woman who takes it up. She set the pot rim two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat and no look toward the bed. The half-beat was the distance between the intention and the arrest; she did not measure it and did not name it, for a named gap wants a payer put under it and none stood.

At the carrying-down she went nineteen steps at the half-pace from every open slate, correcting the tilt at each turn of the landing before it settled into the wicker. The instruction went to the wool, the river wind, the pot, the fire and the banked fire, and never to the basket; the basket was not told what the day was and was not told to be still. In \place{the Clay Yard} she went around \place{the seven walked-around places} once and not twice, with a look and a wide step and no number: the stone lip of the well, the worn boards, the strip of hard mud, the place where a wheel had stood, the two narrow gaps between the stacked pots, and \place{the returning-line place}, widest of all, where a hand had gone in on \emph{Seed 15} and had not come back. The wide step was a keeping and not a sign, for a figure set against that space would want a payer standing under it and none stood. She did not look twice; looking twice is how a finding becomes a habit, and a habit wants the hand that put the thing there. She set him under the roof at the yard's edge where the light came sideways and never in the Debt Loft, put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face, and added no second fold. The thread stayed stiff and unwiped.

Tarn was at \place{the kindling} and \place{the flagstones} when the basket came down. Six pieces stood in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check, and the seventh lay at the lip beside Garl's unforged collector sentence, with no name on the man who had turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on \lore{the tile debt}. The small knife lay at the very edge of its sill. His hand went over blade and sill as a man passes a hand over a tool put back where it belongs, and came away with no breath held at the top of the reach --- the coming away the whole of the checking. In the tone of a man stating the weather he told the wood, \emph{Six counted. The next I don't count}, cutting nothing, counting nothing, spending nothing. The sentence was found where put and was not made a habit, not a tenth stopping, a payment, a hearing or a rule; it did not gather a second silence on an unearned day, for a second silence would be a custom and a third a way and both want a payer where none stood. His chest had been tight since the dark, \lore{Ash} worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line, the pale clip-ring at his left wrist catching and giving back what little light there was, his stroke over the flagstones going without the half-check. He took the broom only after it had been given its instruction and said \emph{Yes, house} to the fire. He did not turn his head toward the landing; the degree stayed a shape found where put and was not made a second time.

The ear-sentence came all the way to the word that would call the loan and went no further. It was about an ear under this roof being worth more than the debt that held it, and it stopped there with no figure and no name, a remembered stopping with no tenth, the \emph{Seed 16} breath unspent and the loan uncalled in a term already ended. A term ending is exposure and not freedom, and leaves a man more open than it found him. The boy filed the stopping on a place of its own beside the sentence told the wood and the seven walked-around places, and laid none of them across the clerk's entered figure, the well-asking, the fifth shaping or the face, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood. The fear of being entered again for vagrancy stayed Tarn's without a figure, and so did the want he did not voice for a slate with his own name and no mark against it.

\person{Jorra Vail} came through the finger's-width door from \place{the Vail wheel-shed} with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past his bent little finger and white in a fist-crease where a hand had opened --- not the bone-coloured \lore{slip} of the wheel. Three or more men unnamed had gone low into \place{the back room} before first grey and he gave the house no name for them and did not say the room was cold; the broken ones under the lowest rack stayed entered by nobody. He kept dusty forearms and clean ones on one tray and parted them as clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had been broken nor whether it was a birth-slate, for a question joined to a cause wants a payer standing and none stood. He spoke to the wheel before any person, \emph{It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady}, and told a pot \emph{You'll do} in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness. He knew the \lore{full note} that pot would give three turns before it left his hands, with air under it and a want in it, and withheld the knowing from any master come up from \place{High Kiln} who would call it early and sing a pot before its time, for a pot called early is a pot whose note has been spent before it was earned and no master under that roof had ever asked who paid for the singing. Sella had not finished telling him the fire was low when his bent finger went into the cold iron of \place{the damper} ahead of her sentence. One bite of the iron held and one took, and no Grathic word covered both mercies; he took the cold out of the fingertip, moved his tongue a hair's breadth, and was left standing so the end of her instruction arrived at the pot and the household straightened around the waiting. He took the finger out without leaving the extra holding of Ash 26 a second time, gathered once and not made again, for a second would be a habit and a third a way and both want a payer where none stood. \emph{He's not Vail} stayed sealed and the promise of the boy's name to the men who burn slates stayed neither given nor withdrawn. The boy filed Tarn's withheld sentence and Jorra's withheld knowing on places of their own with a finger between, two quiet men under one roof and not a custom, not joined to the offered figure gone back into the sleeve or to any payer.

Answer four hundred and seventeen came out and was filed on the sixth place. It was exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment supplying no payer, laid across neither the twelve readings, the two of the new count, the printed figure, the fifth shaping, the offered figure, the clerk's entered figure, the well-asking, the turned slate, the degree, the face, the withheld sentence, the softening, the bell's gap, the flat knock, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the held breath, the two bites of iron, the winter skin, the widow's mark nor the keeping at the fold. Nine honest answers stood on the sixth place, 409 through 417, one to this morning and two to each of the four days behind it, and not one was a doctrine.

The surgeon's exactness and the infant's want stood side by side and neither bought the other. Under them, louder, the wanting went on --- the crust lifted, the thread wiped, a hand on the pot handle --- and got none of the three. Both were true. The second was louder. Neither won, and no verdict was looked for; the quarrel was the work and the work went on in silence in a room whose floor did not touch his. The hand outside the wool went up the inch toward the river's note, the inch not a reach, held the interval whole on the note's own finishing under the winter skin, let it go as the note ended --- neither early nor stayed --- and came straight down and lay flat against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. He did not wipe the thread and did not lift the crust. The offered figure was kept where put on its own place beside the ninth hand and the fifth shaping, the stick kept its notch, the well had given back the weather, and four low pulls breathed below the floor with the fifth unsupplied. The pot rim stood two inches beyond the reaching hand, the crust's corner sat a hair off the wool, and the house went on as a house goes when a pot is set down in its place and the fire kept --- forward, into an afternoon not yet lived and not brought into a morning that had earned only its twenty-seventh.
