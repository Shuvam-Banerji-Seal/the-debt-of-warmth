## Chronicle

Three days came under the Vail roof and the house kept them badly, in loaves pushed a finger's width and stopped. The boy carried four hundred and twenty, twenty-one and twenty-two exact and stood no number up. Each morning the surgeon took the body in the old order — horn-hard crust, frozen thread, cold stopped at cheek and wrist, the Afterdrop waiting past a warming nobody would perform — and each morning the infant wanted the crust lifted, the thread wiped and a hand on the pot handle. Both were true, the second louder, neither won, and the quarrel was the work.

At the yard well on the twenty-sixth a woman brought a number up out of a shut fist toward the strip in Ilva's sleeve. Ilva's hand went flat over the fold before her eyes went to the asker and her mouth after both. She answered the question put and not the one behind it: *Ask the pot what it cost. I keep the count, not the account.* The figure looked at the light and went back into the sleeve, arriving nowhere; the stick kept its notch and learned no name. *Then it's a stick*, the woman said, and went back to the round. The boy filed the offer and its return beside Ilva's ninth hand and the fifth shaping, with a finger between every pair — a reaching toward a far-off hand, and not that hand. Offstage after that, she was not made a reader and no figure was offered twice.

Sella told the coals it was the thaw and lifted the twice-mended pot thumb-first with the hairline showing; repair was not restoration. Tarn kept six where the house could check and the seventh at the lip, told the wood *Six counted. The next I don't count*, and let his hand go over blade and sill and away. Jorra parted dusty from clean as clot from vessel and took his bent finger out of the cold damper without the extra holding of Ash 26.

Costs came as directions and went away as weather: a fist opened toward the well and shut again, a woman sent for a pot's cost got a note and not a price. The called-gone grey stayed beside the left-in-place ridge, a thing left in place not forgiven; the river's air softened at one edge and was called weather. Seven openings were seven, not a rule.

Six answers, four hundred and fifteen through twenty, were filed on the sixth place, worth nothing at two inches of air and not one a doctrine. The count reached four hundred and twenty-two exact, told by nobody; the fire's fifth was unpaid and Garl's name unspoken the same number. In the small hours the towel came out as instruments do when a case is done; six hearings stood with the seventh empty, nine hands with no tenth. The hand came down flat on the fold. The well had given back the weather, and the house went on.
