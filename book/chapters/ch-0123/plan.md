## How the final moment pulls into Chapter 124

Chapter 124 opens in the small hours of Day 423, Ilmen 29, Ninthday, with the hand still laid down on answer 420 and the count exact at 422, six months and two hundred and forty-two days told by nobody, the carried fire's fifth unpaid and Garl's name unspoken the same number, the offered figure, the well-asking and the fifth shaping found where put and not spent.
