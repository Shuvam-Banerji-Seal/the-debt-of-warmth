The afternoon came on without anybody under the roof giving it a name, the way the dry half of the year comes on: not as a threshold crossed, not as a thing the morning had owed and paid, but as light sliding off the near boards and up the far wall of the shed, so slowly that a man watching would have sworn the wall had grown lighter of its own accord. Nothing in \place{the Clay Yard} had been told what the day was. \person[Corin]{The boy} had not been told to be still. The basket sat where \person{Sella Vail} had set it, under the eave where the heat came sideways, and the yard went on at the scale the yard keeps --- pail, rope, board, stone --- and not at the scale of the number he carried under his tongue.

He took the small sounds first because the small sounds do not lie. A pail went down on \place{the flat stone by the well} with the flat knock of a thing set where a thing has always been set, and the sound told him the stone was dry and the pail was empty and the arm that set it down had not been in a hurry. The rope settled into its knot with the stiff dry rasp that cold puts into hemp, a sound with no grain of intention in it, and he let it be a rope and nothing more. Somewhere a loaf was pushed a finger's width along a board and stopped --- stopped by a hand, but nobody called the stopping a figure, and nobody set a second loaf beside the first to see which had travelled the farther. Barley went from wall to wall in a woman's two hands and never once became a number. The yard kept its accounts in weight and distance and in the warm places on a stone, and none of them wanted a figure put against them.

The large sounds were mostly weather. \place[the river]{The river} worried at its skin under \place{the finger's-width door}; the wind came down off the sill and went around the stacked pots; a shutter knocked once somewhere and was answered by nothing. He had learned to leave the weather where it lay. A man who makes a hearing out of weather ends by putting a payer beneath it, and there was no payer in the wind. At the rail two women went on with the bucket-round as if the morning's small commerce had never stopped, one saying the rope would take a finger off a careless hand and the other saying cold was cold and fingers were fingers, and both going back to their pails. He gave the words no owner beyond the mouths they came out of and the cold no motive. A voice in a yard is a voice in a yard, and the taking of a finger is not an argument.

He went around \place{the seven walked-around places} once and not twice. He had gone around them in the morning when the pail went down; he would go around them again at the going of the light when Sella carried him up; and between the two he went around them now in the afternoon with a look and a wide step and no number. That was the whole of it --- a look, a wide step, and no number. The stone lip of the well, worn smooth on the side where knees had rested; the boards worn hollow before the kiln; the strip of hard mud that kept the print of a wheel; the place where the wheel itself had stood for twenty years with nobody filling it; the two narrow gaps between the stacked pots, each gap gone around with the same wide step; and \place{the returning-line place}, widest of all, where a hand had gone in on \emph{Seed 15} and had not come back. The wide step at that one was wider than at the others, and the widening was a keeping and not a sign. A figure set against that space would want a payer standing under it and none stood. He did not look twice. Looking twice is how a finding becomes a habit, and a habit wants the hand that put the thing there, and there was no such hand to be found in the afternoon light.

\place[the trough]{The trough} stood bare and dark in the lee of the wall. The thin skin that had lain on it through the turn was gone, and the rim kept the line where the water had been, a lower country marked on the side of a vessel that no longer held it. He read the line as a level taken in a cup nobody had calibrated. The gone pan kept its shallow round print on a place of its own, and he did not lay that ring across the loaf's finger's-width stop or across the ridge at the corner. Two places in a yard that happen to hold a curve are not therefore the same curve, and a third thing is not made by looking at them together.

At \place{the Vail corner} the clean dark line kept its finer grey beside it, the one called \lore{the thaw}, thickened in the dry cold with the faint sour tang of \lore{Ash} coming up off it where no broom had gone in two years. The called-gone grey stood beside the left-in-place ridge with a finger between them. No agreement had been made to stop looking at the ridge; nobody had swept it and nobody owned the day on which it had been left, and a thing left in place is not a thing forgiven. In this afternoon's air the tang came thinner off the corner than it had at morning, and the grey lay a hair less distinct along one stretch where the light fell across it. He found the softening where he had put it and did not look at it a second time. He did not make it a sign, a fourth of \lore{the three coverings}, a sixth shaping or a payment on a day that had earned only its twenty-sixth. The air was less cold at one edge where the low note ran nearer under the skin; that was weather, and weather arrives nowhere and is owed nothing. The ridge kept its place. Nothing under the roof had been paid to make it keep its place, and nothing had been paid to have it go.

Costs came to the yard as directions and went away as weather and left no entry behind them. A man come down \place{the clay road} brought a number up out of a shut fist toward the well, held it where the afternoon light could see it, looked at it, and put it back into his sleeve, where it arrived nowhere. He had given the number to no slate and the slate to no person; the hand had gone fist, eye, sleeve, and the half-beat had lain in the gap where a motive would have stood had there been one. The boy gave the fist no figure and the sleeve no motive, for a motive is a payer wearing a coat. A woman sent to ask what a pot cost was sent to the pot and got a note and not a price; the note was an instruction to the clay and did not name what the clay wanted. At \place{the rack end} the dressed grain lay covered and unfigured, and the figure that had been read over it in its own morning had not known what the grain cost, and no signature stood on the space between the figure and the price. Paper is not a payer, and the key to the box of true subtractions had gone up the road in a coat pocket that never came back down. The offered figure was not laid across any of these. Two arrivals that reached nobody do not become one arrival by being set side by side on the same afternoon.

The offered figure and its going-back were found where put. They sat on a place of their own in the file, beside \person{Ilva}'s ninth record-first hand and the fifth shaping of \lore{the well-saying}, with a finger between every pair. The fist had held it toward the wood; the wood had kept its notch; the figure had looked at the light and gone back into the sleeve. It was not offered a second time on a day that had earned only its twenty-sixth, and the boy did not make a second reaching of it by remembering it. A reaching remembered a third time wants the hand that made it, and no hand stood here. It was not made a habit, not a sale, not a tenth hand, not a reader made of the strip, not an account and not a payer. It was not joined to the fist-to-nowhere number, the note-not-price, the asking that had come up at the well, the clerk's entered figure reported from below \place{the north bridge}, the second man's turned slate and direction, or the face at the yard's edge. Those stood each on a place of its own with a finger between every pair: the clerk's figure entered before the eyes went up, \person{Tarn}'s degree toward the landing, the patient face at the yard's edge, the first man's face, the second man's slate with its writing turned away and the direction down the bridge road, and the asking answered with the weather. No joining stood among them, no witness, no reader, no sale, no tenth hand and no paying. Six hearings of \lore{the full note} stood beside and never among with the seventh place empty, and the Drawing's flat knock stayed outside them on its own ground; no hearing was made of the river's note, and the offered figure was not made a seventh of anything.

At the going of the light Sella came to \place{the hearth} before she came to the basket. She told the coals it was the thaw in the voice another woman would have used for a child who could not hear her, and turned them with the back of a broken pot; the back scraped over them and left the blind ash where it lay, and the coals gave off the dry smell of a fire banked and not forgotten. She gave the small intake, the hold and the letting-go toward no opening, figuring neither of the two gaps. \emph{The house will keep it} went to the fire and not to the basket, because a thing said aloud becomes a thing the Court can enter. Her finger lay along the top of \place{the loaf shelf} so that the charcoal tally beneath stayed unread, and her lips moved on a two-gapped row with neither gap figured. One glance went to the clay jar standing cold under \lore{lead glaze} beyond \place{the pot board}; the hand shut half over nothing, and the little-door burn on the back of her left hand closed on air and went back to the pot. She did not touch \lore{the widow's mark}. An afternoon, a yard, a shelf, a hearth and a going of the light are not openings; seven openings in seven months are seven and not a rule, and no eighth came due on a day that had earned only its twenty-sixth.

She said to the fire, in the tone of a woman who has told the same thing to the same fire for three winters, \emph{The fire doesn't know you're sorry. Feed it}, and put two fingers of coal where the coals had thinned. Her hands went on counting when they had nothing to do --- three spoons, a nail, the small breath a child makes that nobody numbers --- and came back to the hairline at the wire as hands come back to a wound they have dressed. She lifted the twice-mended pot thumb-first where the tighter wire crossed the crack. The hairline of the original break showed between the old-brass staple and the wire, a dark thread with a hand's work on either side, and both proverbs went to the pot and the fire and never to a person: \emph{Leave the crack showing. People trust a thing they can see has been handled} and \emph{If it's worth mending it's worth mending twice. The second time is the real one}. Repair was not restoration; a restored thing hides the hand that handled it, and a mended thing leaves the hand showing so that a vessel carrying its break can be read by the next woman who takes it up. At the rack end the dressed grain was turned under wool with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child, with no figure and no handful, and the pot rim was set two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat and no look toward \place{the bed}. The half-beat was the distance between the intention and the arrest; she did not measure it and did not name it, for a named gap wants a payer put beneath it and none stood.

At the carrying-up she went nineteen steps at the half-pace from every open slate, correcting the tilt at each turn of the landing before it settled into the wicker. The instruction went to the wool, the river wind, the pot, the fire and the banked fire, and never to the basket; the basket was not told what the day was and was not told to be still. She went around the seven walked-around places once and not twice, widest at the returning-line place, and set him under the roof at \place{the Vail kiln-house} where the light came sideways and never in \place{the Debt Loft}. She put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face and added no second fold. \lore[the keeping at the fold]{The keeping at the fold} was found where put and stayed a finding. The thread remained stiff and unwiped. \person{Garl Vail}'s name went unspoken a fourth hundred and twentieth day and his fifth pull under \place{the boards} was not supplied; the two counts stood side by side and did not become one. \person{Jorra Vail} kept the silence of \place{the Vail wheel-shed}, parting dusty forearms from clean as clot from vessel and taking his bent finger out of the cold \place{the damper} without the extra holding of Ash 26 a second time; \emph{he's not Vail} stayed sealed and the promise under the shed stayed neither given nor withdrawn. Tarn's six stood where the house could check and the seventh at the lip, and the sentence told the wood was found where put and not made a custom by being said again. None of it came to the basket as a lesson, and the boy made no doctrine of any of it.

In the small hours \place[the towel]{the towel} came out at the head of the bed as instruments come out when a case is done and before the next is called, rough from the wash and smelling of tallow and low smoke. He went over the field by the place of each thing and left every gap a gap. Six hearings of the full note stood beside and never among, with the seventh place empty and not dug for or filled, and \place{the Drawing house}'s flat knock outside them on its own ground. The bell heard from \place{the Cross} kept its square beside the told bell, four, four and a gap, and its gap was not folded into \lore{the carried fire}'s four and a gap below the boards, or the boards' four and four and a thumb-width knot where a fifth nail might have been driven and was not, or the two readings of the year behind one unsigned figure beside the twelve arithmetics of Y.H. 813 --- three counts in three places staying three, because agreeing any two would want a payer put under both and none stood. Four rooms in the district kept the same four things with a finger between each, and no Court room was brought under the roof as a fifth likeness. Nine record-first hands stood with no tenth and nine stoppings with no tenth; \person[Hessa Rale]{Hessa}'s narrow slate stayed unread up the road and Ilva's strip stayed in its sleeve with her count in wood, her ninth hand nine, and the offered figure gone back where it came from.

The carried fire gave four low pulls below the boards with the silence where the fifth should have stood, and nobody lifted the floor to supply it. Its payer had gone unnamed four hundred and twenty days and Garl's name had gone unspoken the same number, the fire and the man standing side by side without becoming one; the keeping at the fold was found where put, still a finding and not a placing, with no second fold, no third crust, no name, no gift, no payment and no payer. He did not stand four hundred and twenty up as a threshold or a figure and borrowed no payer from the arithmetics gone up the road, for paper is not a payer. The offered figure sat on its own place with a finger between it and the ninth hand and the fifth shaping, not a sale, not a reader, not a tenth hand, not a payer, not an account and not joined to the asking, the clerk's entered figure, the turned slate or the face. The surgeon's exactness and the infant's want stood side by side and neither bought the other. Under them, louder, the wanting went on --- the crust lifted, the thread wiped, a hand on the pot handle --- and got none of the three. Both were true. The second was louder. Neither won, and no verdict was looked for; the quarrel was the work and the work went on in silence in a room whose floor did not touch his.

Answer four hundred and sixteen came out and was filed on the sixth place. It was exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer. Eight honest answers stood on the sixth place, 409 through 416, two to the day gone by and two to each of the three before it, and not one was a doctrine. The hand outside the wool went up the inch toward the river's note, the inch not a reach, and held the interval whole on the note's own finishing under the winter skin, and let it go as the note ended --- neither early nor stayed --- and came straight down and lay flat against the fold. He did not wipe the thread and did not lift the crust. The offered figure was kept where put; the stick kept its notch; the well had given back the weather. Four low pulls breathed below the floor and the fifth was not supplied, and the house went on as a house goes when a pot is set down in its place and the fire kept --- forward, into a morning not yet lived and not brought into this day that had earned only its twenty-sixth.
