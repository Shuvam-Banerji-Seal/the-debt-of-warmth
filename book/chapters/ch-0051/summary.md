## Chronicle

Three mornings came up over Low Kiln as the last working week ran on, and the house kept its dead man without saying his name. The boy came into his two hundred and fourth day, then the fifth, then the sixth, and nobody told him. The carried fire missed its fifth; the river kept water over water; the boards kept four nails, four and a thumb-width knot. Three counts in three places, none becoming the others, for agreeing them would have wanted a payer and none stood to any. The salt coat held a broader shape, the seat its hollow, the clean line and the finer grey lay unswept, the proverb went to the pot, the tile debt unnamed.

Sella Vail came to the fire before the basket, gave the instruction to wool, fire, pot, broom, running water and river wind, never to the child, and said the house where another woman would say I. She fed him from her own dish with the spoon on her sleeve, set the pot handle two inches beyond his hand, moved her lips along a two-gapped row, kept her finger from beneath the charcoal, and carried him home around the pan's gone ring. Cost-questions went to the pot and the weather and went away level. The widow's mark had not been touched since before the kiln on Ember 1.

On the second morning a word came up from Slate Row, spoken over the boards and not to the basket: a slate had burned in the night. Nobody said whose, or why. He filed it beside Jorra's dust and the two shut-room tellings, a hand between the three, and made no cause of it. Jorra grew quieter, withheld a note from a wrong wind whose saying about a pot singing early cost no pot, and held his bent finger in the damper until the sentence reached the pot --- remembered, not made again. Tarn told the kindling six counted and the next he did not count, brought the ear-sentence to the word that would call the loan and stopped. Nine stoppings stood with no tenth before the seventh year was earned, the Seed 16 breath unspent, the loan uncalled, no collector came.

Five times he questioned his hand: down at once, one small intake, one small intake, a whole breath down with the breath, down before the breath finished. Then, in the small hours of the last day, with no count at Sella's fire to hold against, it went up an inch toward the river's note and stayed for the river's own low interval, coming down on the next low note --- a coordination with the one rhythm through the door. He filed it on its sixth place, no hearing, seventh, stopping, payment, key, power or rule, no payer named. He still could not close two inches or lift the crust. Two hundred and six stood exact, unspent, spoken by nobody; the river went on over water without noticing the inch.
