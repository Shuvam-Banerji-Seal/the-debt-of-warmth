## Chronicle

On the twelfth of Seed the boy was one hundred and one days old and nobody said so. The house kept its days in loaves and an unnamed space. A neighbour reached past the bare board and said in one breath that the old space came in with the planting and the empty place at his ear was a child's appetite, never seeing they were one absence. Sella Vail said the house where another would say I, instructed pot, fire and broom but never the basket. Tarn swept the collector's road without turning; his knife held six counted years and a seventh nearly gone, and he told the kindling, Six counted. The next I don't count.

On the thirteenth Tarn agreed the thaw early and late, yet wanted the late, which puts off the paying. Berr Clay wiped his thumb twice, read one in twelve, called the levy and put himself out of his sentence with There'll be a slate at the watch, then went toward High Kiln with his box locked and the true subtraction inside. A neighbour mentioned a counting room shut in the war — a mention, not a telling. Jorra Vail put his finger in the damper ahead of Sella's sentence and the household straightened around his silence; he would not name a pot's note before a High Kiln master. The boy read the iron as one bite that holds and takes.

At the Fishstairs Sella set the basket where no slate lay. Mira Silt answered her own question and moved eel-bones while deciding whether to lie; when a buyer named a figure a second cord, twisted twice, showed beneath the grey, neither knot counted; her board lay beside Orra Weir's and did not touch. In House Silt Alda Reed laid her slates before the fold, crossed two fingers at the flower unknowing and covered the sign with a dead woman's date. Warm and breathing, that's all I've set down — exact and not true. She touched the tin inside her bodice unopened; her sixth page stayed face-down, six unread. Below the sill a woman wound wool round a dark foot and called it the cold; the yard called it weather.

On his bed the boy, one hundred and three days old and told by nobody, checked his file: six hearings of the full note, no seventh; three near-full answers advanced and not paid; the word forty unspoken. The shelf kept two unnamed gaps; in the fold one crust lay stiff beside its empty place, unnamed, and no third. He held the three absences apart; his hand was small and outside the wool. Sella told the fire it was the thaw; it answered four low pulls and a gap, unnamed for a hundred and three days. The river's note came through the door, the fire's gap through the floor. He was the only adult and the least use; his hand went up an inch and came down. The count stood at one hundred and three, unspent.

## Continuity

- **Exact final moment / day and time.** Day 103, Seed 14, Fourthday, Y.H. 813, at the going of the light. The chapter stops under the Vail kiln-house roof with Corin on his bed (not in the Debt Loft), one crust stiff in the fold, Sella at the fire in the next room. Last image: the river's low muttered note through the finger's-width-open door from the Ilm and the fire's four, four and a gap through the floor under blind ash, the two sounds in two separate places; the boards overhead keeping four nails and four and a fifth that is a knot the width of a thumb; his hand outside the wool going up an inch toward the note of water going over water and coming down. Final line: the count at one hundred and three, unspent, the dark under the roof keeping it without a figure.
- **Present in the last scene:** Corin and Sella Vail only. All others offstage at the close. No recap opened any scene; each of the three days has its own `\daymark` and is lived in full.

- **Days covered (3):** Day 101 (Seed 12, Secondday), Day 102 (Seed 13, Thirdday), Day 103 (Seed 14, Fourthday). None is an Account-day (Seed 10 was Day 99; Seed 20 is Day 109). Day 104 belongs to Chapter 22.

- **Where every on-stage character is now:**
  - **Corin/Dan:** on his bed under the roof with the fold put right at his ear; drool frozen and unwiped, head wandering with the small wet sound, cold cheek–temple–wrist with the wool riding back, hand outside the wool going up an inch and down. Wants the crust, the bucket's lip, the chipped cup and the number of his days said aloud by someone who would know what it counted; can reach or get none. Count at 103, three months and thirteen days.
  - **Sella Vail:** at the fire under the roof, having carried him up at the half-pace from every open slate and not looked at his face; told the fire it was the thaw and turned the coals with the back of a broken pot. Touched the widow's mark at no hour (no opening came in three days). Private roll unshown; said nothing about the writing, the tin or the fold pressed too long. Wants the fire, the house and the keeping held and never explained.
  - **Tarn:** about the yard and house, broom set away, small knife on its sill with six counted and the seventh nearly gone and still held; to the kindling, *Six counted. The next I don't count*. Said *Yes, house* to an early and a late thaw and kept wanting the late (it puts off the paying). The unforged collector sentence from the frozen ford stays kept with the seventh year, neither entered.
  - **Jorra Vail:** in the wheel-shed; on Day 102 men came before first grey and spoke low in the finger's-width-open back room (not entered, not named) and he came through with grey slate dust past the bent little finger, put his finger into the damper ahead of Sella's sentence and straightened the household by silence. Knows a Vail pot's full note and will not say it before a High Kiln kiln-master. *He's not Vail* stays sealed; the foundling's name is neither given nor withdrawn.
  - **Berr Clay:** went off up the clay road toward High Kiln on Day 102 with the office cord folded under his coat, the box in his house locked and the true subtraction inside, believing he had done a kindness. Never says *I*.
  - **Alda Reed:** in House Silt three doors down on Day 103, wrote her dates, left the sixth narrow slate face-down toward a floorboard (six pages unread), touched the little tin in her bodice without opening it, and covered the birth-flower sign with a dead woman's date. Mercy-lie curdled to a fact nobody alive can correct.
  - **Mira Silt:** at the eels at the Fishstairs on Day 103; grey cord at her waist with the second cord twisted twice beneath it, knots uncounted, board beside Orra's knot-string and not touching. Did not look into the basket.
  - **Offstage throughout:** Orra Weir (present only as her knot-string), Garl Vail (wheel-seat, coat and proverbs), Dessa Silt (dead, stays dead), Marra (not in Sill), Ulda (does not appear in this chapter), Rusk, the Waiting Room clerk and all the Earth characters.
