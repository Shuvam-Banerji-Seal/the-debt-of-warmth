## Chronicle

On Stone 13 the kiln opened under a hand that touched the widow's mark and let it go. Sella Vail fed the boy from the house's dish, barley thinned with milk; she said *the house*, never *I*, and nothing was entered for the warmth. A pot waited on the bench. The boy turned his head before Jorra Vail set it down, expecting the flat knock; the pot gave nothing, and he kept the interval where the note should have been. Tarn saw it and stopped his broom half a stroke at the empty set-down. Two ears under one roof, and no word between them.

Jorra spoke to the clay, slip bone-pale on his forearms and no slate dust; Sella went around the number of days to Kindling; Tarn counted six years and held the seventh back, and stopped for two breaths when she said he had done well. The boy laid the death-house bench beside the two ridges of Ash, the charred slate and the barrow of grey --- four nails and four and a knot --- and joined none. His drool froze; his hand rose an inch and fell. At dusk she banked the coals and did not touch the mark.

On Stone 14 she carried him to the Fishstairs. Mira Silt worked among eels, pale slime on her sleeves, the top of her left ear gone --- a hook by her account, a knife by the scar. She gave Berr Clay four eels where six should go; he had paid her two marks short for six years. Asked whose was the basket, she said the child was warm, off the slate and not on one; when a number was spoken her hand went under her apron to a cord. The boy saw the hand, not the knots. A collector crossed the bridge, and for two sentences a cafeteria at four in the morning hummed and a wrapper was eaten to its last crumb. Sella bought eels and carried him home.

On Stone 15 he was set in the bridge's shade. Dessa Silt stood at the end of nineteen steps, called the pits' cough weather, counted loaves and bits to the woman with the basket, and did not look in. Across the market a girl with green at her mouth looked at pots; Mira's grey cord lay at her waist, a second cord under it never taken to Nine Pots. She put a brown-edged crust in the fold --- a crust, not a sermon --- and he could smell the barley and feel the warmth and could not lift it to his mouth. At dusk Sella counted her loaves twice; Tarn put the knife back; Jorra moved the damper. The boy lay with the crust warm against him, his hand going up an inch and coming down. Four, four and a gap, and the gap was the part nobody had thought to enter.

## Continuity

- **Exact final moment:** Night of Stone 15. The boy lies on his bed under the Vail roof, not in the loft, with the brown-edged barley crust still warm in the fold at his side --- a gift Sella did not put there and will not take out. The coals hold under their ash, the grey ridge runs to the corner and turns it without a break, the door stands a finger's width open to the wind, and his hand outside the wool goes up an inch toward the warmth and comes down. Last counted thing: four, four and a gap. Present under the roof: Corin, Sella, Tarn, Jorra; Garl as coat, hollow seat, ridge and proverb. No one has spoken to the basket.
- **Day and time at the end:** Day 9 of the new life, Stone 15, Y.H. 812, Fifthday, dusk into night. Nine days old. Day 7 (Stone 13, Thirdday) and Day 8 (Stone 14, Fourthday) were lived in full; no gaps, no passage mode.
- **Where everyone is and their condition:**
  - **Corin/Dan:** carried everywhere --- basket at hip, set in shade, lifted back to bed. Drool frozen at mouth; head goes where it wants with a small wet sound when too far; hand rises an inch to pot, cord, crust, pocket-place and comes down. No roll, no speech, no Grathic, no teeth. Cold arrives cheek, temple, wrist. Wants the breast and the crust and cannot take either.
  - **Sella Vail:** at the fire after banking with the back of a broken pot, then counting loaves and bits with her lips twice and returning to the same place. Little-door burn shut on nothing; hand did not go to the widow's mark at the bank. Carried him to the Fishstairs twice and the market once; stood a half-pace from every open slate; said the wind and the house, never *I*. Neither thanked nor refused the crust. Wants the fire, the house and the keeping, never an explanation.
  - **Tarn:** in the yard and then under the roof, broom set against the wall, small knife back on the sill and announced unasked. Six years of seven counted, the seventh held back. Stopped altogether for two breaths at *you've done well*. On Day 7 he saw the boy turn his head before the pot was set down and stopped his own broom half a stroke at the set-down that gave nothing; he has said nothing for three days and will say nothing. Wants his exact account and grudges the house the loan of a bed.
  - **Jorra Vail:** in from the shed with bone-coloured slip, no slate dust on Day 7 or Day 9, bent little finger; spoke to pots and the wheel (*it doesn't care whose hands*), shifted the damper before Sella finished, did not say the back room was cold tonight. *He's not Vail* still sealed. Wants the foundling outside the name and the hollow seat for himself.
  - **Garl Vail:** absent; coat salt-stiff on its peg, seat hollow on the left, ridge unswept, proverbs spoken to the fire and to pots, tile debt uncalled.
  - **Mira Silt:** at the eel board at the Fishstairs on Days 8 and 9; pale slime on her sleeves, left ear gone (hook / knife scar), eel-bones flicked as a tell, question-answered-with-question idiolect. Short-weighted Berr two eels against his two marks short for six years. Cord of grey wool at her waist touched three times in two mornings, with a second cord twisted twice under it never taken to Nine Pots. Gave the crust briskly into the fold without looking in. Never says she knows the child.
  - **Berr Clay:** at the Fishstairs on Day 8, thumb wiped before his slate, lead-glaze green at the mouth; *three days to the Five*, finger passed over the figure. Box still locked; wants the account to stand.
  - **Dessa Silt:** at the end of nineteen steps in the market shade on Day 9; slip-grey nails, clay at her hem, sieve-wire scar, cough called weather, door-shut smile shut three years. Counted him among the bits with the loaves and never looked into the basket; has not said number or name for three years. Believes him spent among the Forty. Dies on Day 38, uncollected.
  - **Fenna Clay:** a silhouette across the market with green at the corner of her mouth, looking at pots, no dialogue, unnamed on the page.
  - **Collector on the bridge:** walk-on, soft cuff turned back twice, flat satchel, hand to clasp before eyes to stalls; no speech, no name, not Marra.
  - **Alda Reed:** offstage; her two written notes remain turned down, the tin inside her bodice unopened.
- **Items, money, powers:**
  - **Gained:** the barley crust with brown smoked edge, lying warm in the fold at his side, cannot be eaten or lifted. Knowledge of the grey cord and the second cord, the eel-bones, nineteen in a thumb, the Fishstairs landing that is not a step, the green at a girl's mouth.
  - **Lost/unchanged:** the small pale river stone is still in the fold on the abandoned board-bed in House Silt, unmentioned. The bench knife is back on the sill; the charred slate is still in the kiln ash; basket and wool are Sella's.
  - **Money:** none to the boy. Sella laid marks for two pair of eels; Mira's six-year short-changing of Berr (four eels for six, two marks short) is an unspoken war. Every warmth has a named payer and a recipient who cannot refuse.
  - **Powers:** none used, no change; cracked, unranked, below Thin, twelve to fifteen embers at best, flaw unnamed, rate of loss unmeasured. Hearing count unchanged at five from four sources; the pot's missing note is an interval, not a sixth hearing. Present-tense Chicago fragments cost him homesickness for an appetite, not for a room.
- **Facts, lies, debts:** Sella's rule of the mark holds at the bank as well as before the opening. A pot can be set down and not answer. Tarn has witnessed the boy's ear and keeps it. The number of days to Kindling goes around as a closed fist and an absence. Mira's hook is a knife; her two eels short and his two marks short are never named. Dessa's cough is weather, four loaves on the seventh of Stone in eight hundred and twelve, and the boy is put among the bits unrecognized. The crust is a crust, not a sermon, and is entered nowhere. Garl's tile debt remains uncalled; Berr still says three days to the Five and will not say what he counts.
