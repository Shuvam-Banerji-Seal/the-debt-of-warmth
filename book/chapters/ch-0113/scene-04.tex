The afternoon came down thin through \place{the finger's-width door} and went without a boundary drawn, the way the last of a pot's pour goes not because a hand closed the lip but because the pot has given all it had. There was no hour in it and no edge to it. \place{The Five} stood where they had stood since the dark, \lore{Weighing} with its clean voice gone back up the yard into the mouths that had borrowed it, and nothing under this roof had been made to turn upon the day's name. \person[Corin]{The boy} lay on \place{the bed} at the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf} and watched the light thin on \place{the boards}, four nails on the near board, four on the next and a thumb-width knot where a fifth might have been driven and was not. He had come into the count of three hundred and ninety-one and found it where it had been left, exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, and he did not look for a verdict in it. A count is not a verdict, and nobody under this roof had ever asked one of it.

\person{Sella Vail} came to \place{the hearth} before the basket, told the coals it was \lore{the thaw} and turned them with the back of a broken pot, and said \emph{The house will keep it} where another woman would have said I. Then she took the basket down the nineteen steps at the half-pace from every open slate, correcting the tilt at each turn of the landing before it had time to settle into the wicker, so that he felt the small correction before he felt the stair. The instruction went to the wool, to the river wind and to the fire she had banked against their coming back, and never to the basket. Out in \place{the Clay Yard} the dry cold stood in the afternoon and took the light by degrees, and the yard went around its seven walked-around places with a look and a wide step and no number, once and not twice, widest around \place{the returning-line place} where a hand went in on Seed 15 and did not come out.

The clean dark line at \place{the Vail corner} went around with its finer grey hairline beside it called the thaw, thickened in the dry cold with the faint sour tang of \lore{Ash} coming up off it where no broom had gone in two years. The space beneath \place{the lowest rack} went around, where \lore{the broken birth-slates} lay and were entered by nobody. The gone pan's round ring at \place{the rack end} went around with no scrape, crumb, print, chip or word; \place{the yard well} with its rope stiff in the cold and giving its small dry rasp went around, and \place{the trough} beside it went around with its skin drawn edge to edge; \place{the flat stone by the well} went around with the silt-clouded plate standing on it out of the path of feet. \place{the field's empty place} out on \place{the worked strips} went around, and the hollow beside the seventh notch at \place{the kindling} went around. Nothing in the yard was counted or called across, and the seven were seven and not a row with a sum at the end of it.

The frost had gone off the well's north lip; the trough kept its skin; the clouded plate kept its flat stone. Three coverings, and a finger between every pair so that they should not touch and call themselves a cause. Beyond \place{the finger's-width door} \place{the river} kept \lore{the winter skin} over its note of \lore{water over water}, a low thing over a lower thing under a covering put on by the cold and taken off by the cold, found where put and not spent on a holy day, with no hand that laid it, no payer under it and no source standing to be asked. The boy kept that covering on its own place, and did not lay it across the trough's skin or the plate on the stone, for a covering is a covering and three of them agreeing would want a payer put under all three, and none stood.

She gave the broom its instruction before she gave it to anybody --- round the line, round the rack, round the place where the hand went in; it cost enough to come in and it will cost more to take out --- and \person{Tarn} took the broom only after the words had gone to the bristles. He was bent at the shoulders from six years in the slip pit, which made twenty-two look older than its term, and the grey Ash was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the very colour of the clean dark line at the corner. The pale clip-ring at his left wrist caught what little light there was and gave it back. He called everyone house, including her, and said \emph{I owe} where another man would have said \emph{I am}, and he never argued; he agreed so thoroughly that an argument had nowhere to put a foot.

A man down from \place{Cold Acre} came into the yard with a hare across his shoulders, and put a question about its price not to Tarn's face but to the space in front of it, the way a man asks a wall what it is worth. Tarn did not weigh the hare and did not open a slate. \emph{Ask the weather}, he said. \emph{It carried the snow. It can carry the price.} The man went off toward \place{the clay road} with the hare unweighed and nobody entering it, and the price went with him as a direction goes, arriving nowhere. A man with a cracked jar in a rope sling asked what the firing would want for a jar in that state. Tarn sent him to a pot, and the pot, being a pot, said nothing.

A woman with a basket of kindling asked what the seventh year would want when it came. Tarn's chest had been tight since the dark, and the thumb of his broom hand went over the worn wood once as a man checks a tool and not as a man counts money. \emph{Yes, house}, he said, and gave her no figure. Two fence-menders came at him from either side of the well wanting the thaw early and the thaw late, one with the argument that an early thaw would let them at the ruts, the other with the argument that a late one would leave the clay workable another week, and neither of them hearing the other because each had already heard his own answer in Tarn's mouth. \emph{Yes, house}, Tarn said to the early, and \emph{Yes, house} to the late, and he kept the late because a late thaw puts off the paying, agreeing so thoroughly that no argument found a foot to stand on. The woman went off with her basket and the men went off with their thaw, and the yard closed over them as water closes over a stone dropped in it, with no ring entered and no figure left standing.

The boy took the day's costs as the yard gave them --- as directions judged by the carrying arm and the weather, never as figures. A hare answered by the weather, a cracked jar answered by a pot that kept its own counsel, an early thaw and a late both answered with the same two words and the late kept for the paying it put off. He filed no fourth beside them and made no row come out even. The walkers gave no interior and no name; a question asked in the yard is not a person produced, and an answer that goes away as weather is not a payment that stays. Cost arrived as a direction and went away as a direction, and what was left was the yard going around its places once and not twice, and the broom going over \place{the flagstones} without a number in it.

At the kindling six pieces stood in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check them, and the seventh lay at the lip beyond it, beyond the mark and beyond the house's checking, beside \person{Garl Vail}'s unforged collector sentence. There was no name on the man who had turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on the debt. The \lore{tile debt} against a firing of tiles circled without a name and was worse for being uncalled, the way a pot set down in a draught is worse for nobody having told it where to stand. The small knife lay at the very edge of its sill, where a hand could be checked and where a hand could also slip it off. Tarn passed his hand over blade and sill as a man passes a hand over a tool put back where it belongs, and the hand went away when the checking was done with no breath held at the top of the reach. The coming away was the whole of the checking. He cut nothing, counted nothing, spent nothing.

\emph{Six counted}, he said to the wood, in the tone of a man stating the weather. \emph{The next I don't count.}

The boy watched the hand go up and come away, and took it apart as a surgeon takes a field apart: not the hand that works and not the hand that only rests, but the hand that checks and goes away, which is a third thing and has its own order. The hand had gone to the knife before it went to the man, the way another hand goes to the record before it goes to the person, and the mouth had opened after both. He filed the order on a place of its own with a finger between it and the other reachings, and did not make of it a tenth hand or a rule. A rule wants a hand that has reached the same way twice and a payer under both, and no payer stood at the sill.

The Ash 14 held breath and the Bone 26 thumb's resting were found where put and were not made again, lying beside the five held objects and the pot-crack resting with a finger between every pair --- not a sixth held object, not a seventh of anything, not a tenth stopping. He passed over them as a man passes over tools in a closed box, without lifting the lid. Then Tarn brought the sentence about the ear worth more than the debt all the way to the word that would call the loan, and stopped there. It was a remembered ninth stopping with no tenth before the seventh year now at its very end. The Seed 16 breath was drawn and left unspent at the top of the reach; the loan stood uncalled; he did not look along the road. Two ears, two silences, a loan that can hear and has never been called, and nobody standing anywhere to enter it.

A term ending is not a payment, a forgiveness or a freedom. Tarn had counted every day of six years and would not count the seventh, and the end of it left him more exposed than the beginning had, the way taking a coat off a man in a cold yard leaves him more exposed and not more free. He wanted a slate with his own name on it and no mark against it, and thought he wanted freedom; what he wanted was to be called something that was not a house's possession, and what he needed was a way to keep warm without being spent, which in this district is another category of account altogether. The fear that he would be entered again for vagrancy when no house stood ready to take him stayed his without a figure. He had never said that fear aloud and did not say it now. When he was frightened he became helpful, which in a kiln-house means doing three things at once and quietly putting the fourth right; his chest had been tight since the dark, and he put the fourth right without letting the tightness show in the work.

His stroke went on over the flagstones and across the shallow bright patch without the half-check, the stones keeping the place where the stroke had stopped with the returned half-check of Bone 8 staying on them as a held change and not a rule. The Ash in his knuckle creases was the colour of the clean dark line at the corner; the clip-ring at his left wrist gave back the little light as though the iron had kept a small measure of the day and was returning it by degrees. Sella did not ask him about his seventh year and did not ask about his ear. The instruction went to the broom, the wool, the pot and the fire, and never to the man, and he took what was given and called her house. Under the roof the boy kept the stopping on the interval tray, and kept the fear where Tarn had put it, without a figure, without a name, without a hand from this house laid across it to make a row come out even.

\person{Jorra Vail} came through the finger's-width door off \place{the Vail wheel-shed} in the afternoon with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bent little finger and white in a fist-crease where a hand had opened, and it was not the bone-coloured \lore{slip} of the wheel. Three or more men unnamed had gone low into \place{the back room} before first grey; he gave the house no name for them and no word that the room was cold. He kept his dusty forearms and his clean ones on one tray and parted them as clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had been broken nor whether it had been a birth-slate while the broken ones under the lowest rack stayed entered by nobody. He spoke to the wheel before he spoke to any person. \emph{It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady}, he said, and to a pot on the bench he said \emph{You'll do} in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either. He knew the full note that pot would give three turns before it left his hands, with air under it and a want in it, and he withheld the knowing from any master come up from \place{High Kiln} who would call it early and sing a pot before its time.

The boy kept Jorra's knowing on a tray of its own beside the hearings and never among them, with a finger between it and the six, for a knowing is not a hearing and wants no source standing under it. Six hearings of \lore{the full note} stood beside and never among with the seventh place empty and the flat knock from two doors down outside them, and nothing Jorra knew went in to fill the empty place.

Sella had not finished telling him the fire was low when his bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper} ahead of her sentence, took the cold out of the fingertip, moved the tongue a hair's breadth and was left standing so that the end of her instruction arrived at the pot and the household straightened around the waiting. One piece of iron had two bites, the holding and the taking, and no Grathic word covered both mercies. He took the finger out without leaving the extra holding of Ash 26 a second time; that holding had been gathered once and stood where nobody entered it. Neither looked at the other and neither said what the iron had been asked to carry.

\emph{He's not Vail} stayed sealed as a lid nobody had tried to lift in three years, and the promise of the boy's name under the wheel-shed was neither given nor withdrawn. The \lore{burned-slate word} stood at five, the shut-room tellings at two, the row at five askings with no witness, the well-saying at four shapings; the collector's reason and the silence after the clean voice's name went on their own trays, joined to none. Four directions stood where put and did not repeat and bought no lesson, and no fifth shaping of them was made on a day that had not earned one. The boy kept his finger between every pair, and did not let the silence and the promise and the burned slate stand up together and call themselves an account, for an account wants a hand to lay three likenesses together and a payer under all three, and no such hand stood and no such payer stood.

At the going of the light Sella carried him up the nineteen steps at the half-pace from every open slate and set him on the bed under the roof and never in \place{the Debt Loft}. She put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face and added no second fold. One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from his ear with its corner standing a hair off the wool, and the empty place beside it held the fold put there on the fifteenth, found where put and not made again, still a finding and not a placing. The thread of drool at his mouth had frozen in the dry kiln heat and was unwiped, because nobody under any roof has ever been given an instruction about the thread. Cold had come cheek, temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, and stopped there as at the end of a line written out for it; \lore{the Afterdrop} waited past a warming nobody in this house would perform. Under the exactness the infant wanted the crust lifted, the thread wiped and a hand on the pot handle, and wanted them without knowing that wanting them was a want. Both were true. The second was louder. Neither won, and no verdict was looked for.

At \place{the pot board} the hearth grammar went on as it had gone before the basket. She told the coals it was the thaw, turned them with the back of a broken pot, stood a moment counting a small intake, a hold and a letting-go toward no opening with neither of the two gaps figured, her finger along the top of the loaf shelf so the charcoal tally on its underside stayed unread, her lips moving along a two-gapped row and figuring neither gap. One glance went to the clay jar standing cold under \lore{lead glaze} beyond the pot board, the sweetish edge of it coming and going on a wrong wind; her hand shut half over nothing, the little-door burn on the back of her left hand closing on air. The twice-mended pot was lifted thumb-first where the wire crossed the crack, the hairline of the original break showing dark between the old-brass staple and the tighter wire; both proverbs went to the pot and the fire and never to a person, improved in the keeping and very likely not as good as the man had said them, for a saying kept in a house grows smoother than the mouth that made it. Repair stayed apart from restoration: a restored thing hides the hand that handled it, and a mended thing leaves that hand showing. The dressed grain was turned under wool with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child, changed in state and lifting no figure and taking no handful. The pot rim was set two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat and no look toward the bed; his hand went toward it and stopped where the half-beat had stopped it. The cord of her \lore{widow's mark} at her throat was oiled where a working thumb had touched it ten thousand times and was not touched: a going of the light and a bed are not openings, seven openings in seven months are seven and not a rule, and no eighth was made due on this day.

\place{The towel} came out at the head of the bed as instruments come out when a case is done and before the next is called. He went over the field by the place of each thing, and checked three silences on three separate places with a finger between every pair: the silence after the name from the reading, which no mouth under this roof would take up; Tarn's ear-sentence stopped at the calling word with the Seed 16 breath unspent; Jorra's promise under the wheel-shed neither given nor withdrawn. Three likenesses and not one account. He did not let them stand up and call themselves a rule, for a rule is an account that has found a payer and none stood. Nine record-first hands kept no tenth; nine stoppings kept no tenth; six hearings of the full note stood beside and never among with the seventh place empty; twelve arithmetics stood behind one unsigned figure with no signature on the space and no doctrine out of twelve; the keeping in the crust's empty place was found where put and stayed a finding. Under the floor \lore{the carried fire} gave four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer without a name for three hundred and ninety-one days, and nobody lifted the floor and nobody supplied the pull. The fire that warmed the house had no name on its giver, and he kept that as a finding and not a cause.

Through the finger's-width door the river kept its note of water over water, a low thing over a lower thing under the winter skin, muffled as a voice is muffled by a shut door. The note had done before any hand in the room came down. The hand outside the wool came up an inch toward it --- did not reach, and the inch is not a reach --- took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole on the note's own finishing under the covering, let the intake go as the note ended and came straight down and laid itself flat against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. The letting-go was neither early nor stayed, and the hand did not stay.

He filed answer three hundred and fifty-eight: exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the twelve readings, the bell's gap, the flat knock, the child's grey fingertips, the mother's cup, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the held breath, the two bites of iron, the river's skin, the widow's mark, the fourth direction nor the keeping at the fold. The count stood at three hundred and ninety-one exact, six months and two hundred and eleven days told by nobody who would have known what a number counted; the carried fire's fifth was unpaid the same number and its payer unnamed the same number; Garl Vail's name was unspoken the same number, kept in the coat, the hollow seat, the hairline and the unswept line and nowhere spoken. The surgeon took his small clean satisfaction in a field taken as found and left as found, and it cost him nothing, which was how he knew it was not peace; under it, louder, the infant wanted the crust lifted, the thread wiped and a hand on the pot handle, and got none of the three. The hand lay flat on the fold on answer three hundred and fifty-eight, the loan uncalled and the promise ungiven, the name from Weighing still held behind the household and not entered on any tray but its own, the crust's corner standing a hair off the wool and the thread frozen and unwiped, and the count went on under the roof in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill}, \place{Grath}.
