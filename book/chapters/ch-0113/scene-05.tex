\daymark{392}{the Long Night, Y.H. 813}

The small hours came up under the Vail roof without an edge to them, the way a pot gives back its heat after the fire has been banked, by degrees and never all at once. \person[Corin]{The boy} lay at the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf} and came into the count of three hundred and ninety-one where it had been left the day before, exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air. Answer three hundred and fifty-eight stood on the sixth place and did not ask to be added to. \place{The Five} stood where they had stood since the dark, \lore{the Long Night} setting its name on this day and no tend-day with it, and nothing under this roof had been made to turn upon the name. The measure taken on Kindling stayed behind the household with the collector's ordinary writing hand; the clean voice of Weighing stayed where the yard had let it go, a name spoken once and then held in the mouth. Neither bought a teaching here. The boy kept them on their own trays with a finger between, and did not look toward the room two doors down or up the road by which the voice had travelled.

The surgeon took the body in the order it had learned to expect, which is the order a wound is taken, by what is there and by what is not. One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from his ear with its corner standing a hair off the wool. Beside it the empty place held the fold put there on the fifteenth and found where put at every morning and every going of the light since, still a finding and not a placing, with no second fold and no third crust and no name spoken over it. The thread of drool at his mouth had frozen in the dry kiln heat and was unwiped, because nobody under any roof has ever been given an instruction about the thread. Cold had come to cheek, temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, and stopped there as though it had reached the end of a line written out for it, with neither an order to go farther nor an order to go away; \lore{the Afterdrop} waited past a warming nobody in this house would perform. A basket set level out of the draught where the heat came sideways was the rewarming he would choose and could not ask for. He knew a face composed by cold is not composure, that pink returning is the surface still lying over a cold core, that a cold patient cannot clot and must be warmed from the outside and slowly, never hurried, never rubbed. He knew it the way a man knows the feel of a shoulder before he has put his hands on it, and the knowing was worth nothing at two inches of air.

Under the exactness the infant wanted the crust lifted, the thread wiped and a hand on the pot handle. Both were true. The second was louder. Neither won, and no verdict was looked for; the quarrel was the work.

\person{Sella Vail} came to \place{the hearth} before the basket, and banked the fire early against the going of the light, for on this night no fire would be lit after dusk. She told the coals it was \lore{the thaw} where another woman would have said I, and turned them with the back of a broken pot, and said \emph{The house will keep it} to the pot and not to any person standing near. She stood a moment counting a small intake, a hold and a letting-go toward no opening, and figured neither of the two gaps. Her finger lay along the top of \place{the loaf shelf} so the charcoal tally beneath stayed unread; her lips moved on a two-gapped row and figured neither gap. One glance went to the clay jar standing cold under \lore{lead glaze} beyond \place{the pot board}, the sweetish edge of it coming and going on a wrong wind, and her hand shut half over nothing, the little-door burn on the back of her left hand closing on air. The twice-mended pot went up thumb-first where the wire crossed the crack, the hairline of the original break showing dark between the old-brass staple and the tighter wire; both proverbs went to the pot and the fire and never to a person, improved in the keeping and very likely not as good as the man who made them had said them. Repair stayed apart from restoration, for a restored thing hides the hand that handled it and a mended thing leaves that hand showing. The dressed grain was turned under wool with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child, changed in state and lifting no figure and taking no handful. The pot rim was set two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat and no look toward \place{the bed}; his hand went toward it and stopped where the half-beat had stopped it.

Then she took the basket down the nineteen steps at the half-pace she keeps from every open slate, correcting the tilt at each turn of the landing before it had time to settle into the wicker, so that he felt the small correction before he felt the stair. The instruction went to the wool, to the river wind and to the fire she had banked against their coming back, and never to the basket. Out in \place{the Clay Yard} the dry cold stood in the last of the light and took it by degrees, and the yard went around its seven walked-around places with a look and a wide step and no number, once and not twice, widest around \place{the returning-line place} where a hand went in on Seed 15 and did not come out. The clean dark line at the Vail corner kept its finer grey hairline beside it called the thaw, thickened in the dry cold with the faint sour tang of \lore{Ash} coming up off it where no broom had gone in two years. The space beneath \place{the lowest rack} went around where the broken birth-slates lay and were entered by nobody. The gone pan's round ring at \place{the rack end} went around with no scrape, crumb, print, chip or word; \place{the yard well} with its rope stiff in the cold and giving its small dry rasp went around, and \place{the trough} beside it went around with its skin drawn edge to edge; \place{the flat stone by the well} went around with the silt-clouded plate standing on it out of the path of feet. \place{The field's empty place} out on \place{the worked strips} went around, and the hollow beside the seventh notch at \place{the kindling} went around, and \person{Garl Vail}'s unforged collector sentence lay beside it with no name on the man who turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on \lore{the tile debt}, which circled without a name and was worse for being uncalled. Nothing in the yard was counted or called across, and the seven were seven and not a row with a sum at the end of it.

Beyond \place{the finger's-width door} the river kept \lore{the winter skin} over its note of \lore{water over water}, a low thing over a lower thing, found where put and not spent on a holy day, with no hand that laid it, no payer under it and no source standing to be asked. Three coverings stood in the yard and a finger went between every pair so that they should not touch and call themselves a cause: the frost gone off the well's north lip, the skin on the trough, the silt-clouded plate on its flat stone, and this skin of the river's over all three, entered nowhere. Sella carried him back up the nineteen steps and set him on his bed under the roof and never in \place{the Debt Loft}. She put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face and added no second fold. The keeping was found where put and stayed a finding, and the thread stayed frozen and unwiped.

\scenebreak

No fire was lit after dusk. The house sat down into the dark holding its own shape the way a vessel holds what it has, with a crack in it that nobody mentioned. Under the floor \lore{the carried fire} gave four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer without a name for three hundred and ninety-two days, and nobody lifted the floor and nobody supplied the pull. The fire that warmed the house had no name on its giver, and the boy kept that as a finding and not a cause. The cord of her \lore{widow's mark} at Sella's throat was oiled where a working thumb had touched it ten thousand times and was not touched: the dark is not an opening, seven openings in seven months are seven and not a rule, and no eighth was made due on this night.

In the dark under the roof the boy lay where the heat came sideways and heard the small sounds first, because the small sounds do not lie and the large ones are mostly weather. The wool was heavy on him and did not reach his chin; the crust stood hard at two inches with its corner lifted off the wool; the thread at his mouth had gone stiff and would go stiffer before morning. Through the floor the fire's fourth pull came and went and the place where the fifth should have stood gave back nothing. Beyond the finger's-width door the river kept its low note under the covering, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming, a sound that had done before any hand in the room came down. He did not make a measuring of these things and did not lay them together; the dark held them apart the way a shelf holds cups, each in its own place, with a finger between every pair.

Neighbours making ready for the dark went down the yard and spoke the bell as four, four and a gap, passing the telling from one mouth to another the way a coal is passed in a dish from hearth to hearth, each house saying it had kept the count and none saying where the count began. A woman covered her banked coals with the back of a broken pot and told them the thaw; a man went past with a dish of the year's coal held level against his chest and gave no name to where he had got it; somebody called a child in off the flagstones by a name that was not repeated after the calling. These gave no interior and no figure and were not followed into their houses. No bell was rung; it was a told bell and not a heard one, and the boy kept it in its square beside the bell he had heard from \place{the Cross}, four, four and a gap, with its gap not folded into the fire's four and a gap, nor into the boards' four and four and a thumb-width knot, nor into the twelve arithmetics standing behind one unsigned figure, and not made a seventh hearing or a fourth count. The three counts in three places stayed three and none rose into another, because agreeing any two of them would want a payer put under both and no payer stood to either. Six hearings of \lore{the full note} stood beside and never among, the seventh place empty and the flat knock from two doors down outside them; a told bell is not a hearing and a gap in a telling is not a source standing under a sound. He set no hand to join them, for a joining wants a payer under the joined things and none stood.

The stories of the Return were spoken of as coming around in the dark, with gaps in them the shape of vessels' intervals: a pause where a name should stand, a space where a counting should be, an interval that is not a hearing. No story was yet told under this roof and no voice came over \place{the boards}. The boy did not make a seventh hearing of any story spoken beyond the finger's-width door, and did not lay a story's gap across the told bell's gap or across his own kept gaps, for a gap is a gap and two of them agreeing would want a payer put under both and none stood. The Ash 5 joining and the Ash 17, Ash 23 and Stone 23 directions stood where put and did not repeat and bought no lesson; no fifth shaping of them was made on a night that had not earned one, and no owner came over the boards to stand behind the shapes and be asked.

The dark went on without a boundary drawn, and the house kept itself by not-doings: no fire after dusk, no eighth opening, no new hearing, no supply of the fifth pull. There was no market, no Cross, no collector and no officer under this roof, and nothing from Kindling's measure or Weighing's clean voice crossed in as sign, lesson, mending, cause, payment or payer. The widow's mark hung where it hung. Tarn's loan stood uncalled at the very end of a seventh year, and Jorra's promise under the wheel-shed was neither given nor withdrawn, but neither man came under the roof this night and neither was brought in to stand beside the dark and call it a rule. The keeping stayed a finding; the thirteen findings were findings still and no hand had placed a fourth crust or a second fold. The burned-slate word stood at five, the shut-room tellings at two, the row at five askings with no witness, the well-saying at four shapings, nine record-first hands with no tenth and nine stoppings with no tenth; four rooms kept the same gap with a finger between every pair, and no Court room was brought under the roof as a fifth likeness. No doctrine came out of any of it, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood.

\scenebreak

\place{The towel} came out at the head of the bed as instruments come out when a case is done and before the next is called. He went over the field by the place of each thing and left every gap a gap. The crust's gap, the shelf's two gaps, the gone pan's round ring, the field's empty place, the keeping at the fold, the told bell's gap, the silence after the name from Weighing, the stopped ear-sentence, the promise under the wheel-shed --- each lay on its own place with a finger between, and none was made to stand up and call itself a rule.

The hand outside the wool came up an inch toward the river's note under the finger's-width door, did not reach, and the inch is not a reach; took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole on the note's own finishing under the winter skin, let the intake go as the note ended and came straight down and laid itself flat against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. The letting-go was neither early nor stayed, and the hand did not stay. The note had done before the hand came down.

He filed answer three hundred and fifty-nine: exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the twelve readings, the bell's gap, the flat knock, the child's grey fingertips, the mother's cup, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the held breath, the two bites of iron, the river's skin, the widow's mark, the fourth direction nor the keeping at the fold. The count stood at three hundred and ninety-two exact, six months and two hundred and twelve days told by nobody who would have known what a number counted; the carried fire's fifth was unpaid the same number and its payer unnamed the same number; Garl's name was unspoken the same number, kept in the salt-stiff coat with its crease at the left shoulder, the hollow wheel-seat, the hairline on the pot and the unswept line at the corner, and nowhere spoken. Three counts in three places stood side by side and did not become one, and he set no hand to join them. The surgeon took his small clean satisfaction in a field taken as found and left as found, and it cost him nothing, which was how he knew it was not peace; under it, louder, the infant wanted the crust lifted, the thread wiped and a hand on the pot handle, and got none of the three.

The hand lay flat on the fold on answer three hundred and fifty-nine, no fire after dusk under the Vail roof and the carried fire breathing four below the floor with its fifth unsupplied, the bell's gap kept in its square and the stories waiting in the dark, the crust's corner standing a hair off the wool and the thread frozen and unwiped, and the count went on under the roof in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill}, \place{Grath}.
