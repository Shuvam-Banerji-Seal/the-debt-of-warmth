\daymark{391}{Weighing, Y.H. 813}

The hand outside the wool lay flat on the fold where answer three hundred and fifty-six had left it, and the dark under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill}, \place{Grath} thinned without being lifted away, the way a banked fire spends itself under blind ash. The count stood at three hundred and ninety, exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, and nobody under any roof would have known to speak it. This was \lore{Weighing}, the second of \lore{The Five} --- a day with a name and no tend-day and no market --- and it had come the way weather comes, without a boundary drawn, with no doing of this house to have earned it. The measure taken yesterday under another roof sat on a tray of its own with a finger between it and every other hand and bought no reading here. A taking is a taking and a reading is a reading; the one did not pay for the other, and the boy did not lay them together to make a row come out even. Nothing from \lore{Kindling} crossed under this roof as sign, lesson, mending, cause, payment or payer.

\person[Corin]{The boy} lay at the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf} on \place{the bed}, carried everywhere and told by nobody, and the surgeon took the body in the order it came because the order was a way of being awake. The crust came first: one stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust two inches from the ear, gone harder in the dry kiln heat, its corner standing a hair off the wool. Beside it the empty place held the fold put there on the fifteenth, found where put, not made again, still a finding and not a placing. The thread of drool at the lip had frozen in the dry heat and was wiped by nobody, because nobody under any roof has ever been given an instruction about the thread and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. Cold had come cheek, temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, and stopped there as though it had come to the end of a line written out for it, with neither an order to go farther nor an order to go away. \lore{The Afterdrop} waited past a warming nobody in this house would perform. He knew a face composed by cold is not composure, and pink returning the surface still lying over a cold core; that a cold patient cannot clot and must be warmed from the outside, slowly, never hurried, never rubbed; that a basket set level out of the draught where the heat comes sideways is the rewarming he would choose and could not ask for. The knowledge was exact and belonged to a man with another name in another country. The body it was spent on was three hundred and ninety days old and could not lift a crust two inches to its own mouth.

Under the exactness the infant wanted the crust lifted, the thread wiped and a hand on the pot handle, and wanted them without knowing that wanting them was a want. The two registers quarrelled without verdict. Both were true. The second was louder. Neither won, and no verdict was looked for; the quarrel was the work, and the work went on in silence in a room whose floor does not touch his.

Overhead \place{the boards} kept four nails on the near board, four on the next and a thumb-width knot where a fifth might have been driven and was not. Through \place{the finger's-width door} \place{the river} kept its note of \lore{water over water} under \lore{the winter skin}, a low thing over a lower thing, found where put and not spent on a holy day, with no hand that laid it, no payer under it and no source standing to be asked. Three counts stood in three places and none rose into another: the boards' four and four and a knot; the four low pulls under the floor and the silence where the fifth should stand; the river's low over lower under its skin. Agreeing any two would have wanted a payer put under both, and no payer stood.

\person{Sella Vail} came to \place{the hearth} before the basket and told the coals it was \lore{the thaw} and turned them with the back of a broken pot, and said \emph{The house will keep it} where another woman would have said I. She carried him down the nineteen steps at the half-pace from every open slate, correcting the tilt at every turn of the landing before it had time to settle into the wicker, so that he felt the small correction before he felt the stair. The instruction went to the wool, to the river wind and to the fire she had banked against their coming back, and never to the basket. Out in \place{the Clay Yard} the second of the Five stood thin and dry, and the yard went around its seven walked-around places with a look and a wide step and no number, once and not twice, widest around the returning-line place where a hand went in on Seed 15 and did not come out. The clean dark line at \place{the Vail corner} went around with its finer grey beside it called the thaw, thickened in the dry cold with the faint sour tang of \lore{Ash} where no broom had gone in two years; the space beneath \place{the lowest rack} where \lore{the broken birth-slates} lay went around and was entered by nobody; the gone pan's round ring at \place{the rack end} went around with no scrape, crumb, print, chip or word; the skinned \place{trough}, the field's empty place out on \place{the worked strips} and the hollow beside the seventh notch at \place{the kindling} went around. Nothing in the yard was counted or called across, and the three coverings stayed weather entered nowhere with a finger between every pair.

The word of the reading came up the yard in the middle of the morning, when the light had stood a while and the early goers were coming back from it. It did not come as news and it did not come as a command. It came as a voice people had been holding in the ear and were repeating without meaning to, the way a man repeats the last line of a song because the tune has left its shape in his mouth. A woman setting down a pail said it to the pail; a man passing with a coil of rope said it to the rope; a girl following her mother up the path said it to the dust. \emph{The whole is returned}, the woman said to the pail, and left the rest of it standing in the air. None of them addressed the basket and none of them explained what had been read. The boy took the sayings as he took everything else in a yard --- as things said and not as sources standing to be asked.

The voice wore the grammar of \place{the Cross} in a temple's clothes. It was calm and clean, and the cleanness was the part that landed: no gravel in it, no haste, no small catch where a man might have put a feeling if he had been made to feel what he was saying. The sentences were whole and well joined and the speaker was not in them. He had taken himself out of his own words the way another man takes his hand out of a field before the dressing goes on, leaving the work neat behind him. The boy had seen that order of reaching before --- the hand to the record before the eye to the person, the mouth after both --- and had filed every instance on a place of its own; this one went on a place of its own too, and he put a finger between it and all the others, because two clean voices standing touching will call themselves a rule if nobody keeps a hand between.

What the voice announced was a whole return for somebody absent from every room in this yard: the measure kept, the account squared, a person made whole again on a slate and set down where the person had been. It said the name cleanly, once, setting it into the sentence as a woman sets a cup on a shelf --- exact, unhurried, the flat of the hand under it --- and then went on to the next measure and did not say the name again. The yard took up the grammar and did not take up the name. The neighbours who repeated the sentences left the space where the name had been standing, the way a ledge keeps the round pale place where a cup stood and has been carried away. Under this roof the name would not be repeated. The boy did not write it into any file and did not look toward the house where the person might have been, because a voice at a distance is not a room entered and a name spoken is not a person produced. He kept the silence after it on the gap tray, beside the told bell's gap and the unfigured spaces, with no payer standing under it and no cause written across it. A silence is a silence; filled to make a row come out even it is a silence forged, and he did not fill it.

\scenebreak

The man who had read was not \person{Berr Clay}, who had come up short off \place{the clay road} on the thirtieth with the cord folded square under his coat and the box locked; he was not the Sill assessor who entered eight pigs before anybody could ask which eight he meant; he was not the Drawing collector who had taken the first measure under another roof with a hand that went to the slate before his eyes went to the bed. The boy did not join the clean voice to the ordinary writing hand of yesterday, or to the neat dark hand whose small stop had been seen and filed, or to \person{Hessa Rale}'s turned wrist going up the road with the writing toward her own sleeve, or to any of the four directions standing where put and not repeating. A speaker taken out of his sentences is not a tenth hand and not a reader; a reader wants a page under the eye and a payer under the page, and none stood. The speaker went on a place of his own, filed apart, joined to none. Nine record-first hands kept no tenth, and the clean voice did not make a tenth of itself by being clean.

There were four rooms that kept the same gap, and the reading did not make a fifth. The birth room in \place{House Silt}, this kiln-house, the room where the slate was read on market-day and \place{the Drawing house} seen two doors down stood each on its own place with a finger between every pair. Four likenesses and not one account: an account wants a hand to lay them together and a payer under all four, and no such hand stood and no such payer stood. No Court room was brought under the roof as a fifth likeness and no new gap was opened by a voice from a reading; Weighing was a voice at a distance, and a voice at a distance does not come into a house to set a fifth thing beside its four. The boy kept his finger between every pair and did not let the clean voice sit down among them, and the silence after the name stayed on the gap tray with a finger between it and the told bell's gap.

At the going of the light \person{Sella Vail} carried him up the nineteen steps at the half-pace from every open slate and set him on his bed under the roof and never in \place{the Debt Loft}. She put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face and added no second fold, and said nothing of the voice, the reading or the name. The hearth grammar went on as it had gone before the basket in the morning. She told the coals it was the thaw and turned them with the back of a broken pot, stood a moment to count a small intake, a hold and a letting-go toward no opening with neither of the two gaps figured, her finger along the top of the loaf shelf so the charcoal tally on its underside stayed unread, her lips moving along a two-gapped row and figuring neither gap. One glance went to the clay jar standing cold under \lore{lead glaze} beyond \place{the pot board}, the sweetish edge of it coming and going on a wrong wind; her hand shut half over nothing, the little-door burn on the back of her left hand closing on air. The twice-mended pot was lifted thumb-first where the wire crossed the crack, the hairline of the original break showing dark between the old-brass staple and the tighter wire; both proverbs went to the pot and the fire and never to a person, improved in the keeping and very likely not as good as the man had said them, for a saying kept in a house grows smoother than the mouth that made it. Repair stayed apart from restoration: a restored thing hides the hand that handled it and a mended thing leaves that hand showing. The dressed grain was turned under wool with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child, changed in state and lifting no figure and taking no handful. The pot rim was set two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat and no look toward the bed; his hand went toward it and stopped where the half-beat had stopped it. The interval was bought at nothing and was not a gift, and the stopping was his.

The keeping in the crust's empty place was found where put and stayed a finding: the wool folded once exactly where a hand put it on the fifteenth, changed by nobody, with no third crust, no name spoken over the place, no gift, no payment and no payer. A habit wants a hand that placed twice and a way that a third, and both want a payer where none stands; a hand that only reads adds no placing however many times it reads. The finding sat on a place of its own beside the crust's gap, the shelf's two gaps, the gone pan's round ring and the field's empty place, with a finger between every pair. The cord of her \lore{widow's mark} at her throat was oiled where a working thumb had touched it ten thousand times and was not touched: a holy day is not an opening, seven openings in seven months are seven and not a rule, and no eighth was made due on this day, and the empty place where one might have stood was not dug for and not filled. The salt-stiff coat on its peg behind the door kept \person{Garl Vail}'s broader shape at the left shoulder and the hollow of the wheel-seat in the shed kept its twenty-year hollow that nobody filled, and neither was given his name; the \lore{tile debt} against a firing of tiles circled without a name and was worse for being uncalled. Under the floor \lore{the carried fire} gave four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer without a name for three hundred and ninety-one days, and nobody lifted the floor and nobody supplied the pull. The fire that warmed the house had no name on its giver, and the boy kept that as a finding and not a cause. Twelve arithmetics of the year stood behind one unsigned figure with no signature on the space and no doctrine out of twelve; the new year's first figure waited beyond the Five and was not brought into a day that had not earned it.

\place{The towel} came out at the head of the bed as instruments come out when a case is done and before the next is called. He went over the field by the place of each thing and checked every object and left every gap a gap, and supplied nothing to make a row come out even, for a finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged. Nine record-first hands kept no tenth; nine stoppings kept no tenth; six hearings of \lore{the full note} stood beside and never among with the seventh place empty and \lore[The Hearth Drawing]{The Drawing}'s flat knock outside them; four rooms with the same gap were held apart with a finger between every pair; twelve arithmetics stood behind one unsigned figure. The burned-slate word stood at five, the shut-room tellings at two, the row at five askings with no witness, the well-saying at four shapings; the loan at the kindling stood uncalled at the very end of a seventh year with the Seed 16 breath unspent and the fear of being entered again for vagrancy still his without a figure; the promise under the wheel-shed was neither given nor withdrawn; the cold foot was uncured and the cause of the second chill withheld. The told bell stood in its square beside the heard bell, four, four and a gap, and its gap was not folded into the fire's four and a gap or into the twelve behind the figure. The Ash 5 joining and the Ash 17, Ash 23 and Stone 23 directions stood where put and did not repeat and bought no lesson. The silence after the name from the reading lay on the gap tray beside the told bell's gap and the unfigured spaces, with no payer standing and no cause written across it, and he did not let it stand up as a witness or a reader or a payment, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood.

Through \place{the finger's-width door} the river kept its note of water over water, a low thing over a lower thing under the winter skin, muffled as a voice is muffled by a shut door. The note had done before any hand in the room came down. The hand outside the wool came up an inch toward it --- did not reach, and the inch is not a reach --- took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole on the note's own finishing under the covering, let the intake go as the note ended and came straight down and laid itself flat against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. The letting-go was neither early nor stayed, and the hand did not stay.

He filed answer three hundred and fifty-seven: exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the twelve readings, the bell's gap, the flat knock, the child's grey fingertips, the mother's cup, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the held breath, the two bites of iron, the river's skin, the widow's mark, the fourth direction nor the keeping at the fold. Weighing kept its day without a boundary drawn, and the Five were not made a door out of it: the three that stood beyond stayed beyond and were not brought into an hour that had not earned them. The count stood at three hundred and ninety-one exact, six months and two hundred and eleven days told by nobody who would have known what a number counted; the carried fire's fifth was unpaid the same number and its payer unnamed the same number; Garl Vail's name was unspoken the same number, kept in the coat, the hollow seat, the hairline and the unswept line and nowhere spoken. The surgeon took his small clean satisfaction in a field taken as found and left as found, and it cost him nothing, which was how he knew it was not peace; under it, louder, the infant wanted the crust lifted, the thread wiped and a hand on the pot handle, and got none of the three. Both were true. The second was louder. Neither won, no verdict in the quarrel and none looked for. The hand lay flat on the fold on answer three hundred and fifty-seven, the name from the reading still unspoken under this roof and the silence after it kept on the gap tray with a finger between it and every other kept thing, the crust's corner standing a hair off the wool and the thread frozen and unwiped, and the count went on under the roof in Low Kiln, Sill, Grath.
