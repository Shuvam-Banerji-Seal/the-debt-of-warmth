The morning came up thin and dry over \place{the Clay Yard} and stood in a narrow bar on \place{the boards} of the room two doors down, where a high window had been rubbed clear in one place and left clouded everywhere else, so that the room was a shape before it was a room. \person{Sella Vail} carried him down the nineteen steps from \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill}, \place{Grath} at the half-pace she keeps from every open slate, correcting the tilt at every turn of the landing before it had time to settle into the wicker, so that he felt the small correction before he felt the stair. The instruction went to the wool, to the river wind, to the fire she had banked against their coming back, and never to the basket. She told the wool to keep his ear covered; she told the wind nothing, because a wind is owed no instruction it cannot keep. A basket set down is a pot set down, and a pot is told where to stand and not why.

It was \lore{Kindling}, the first of \lore{The Five}, a day with a name and no tend-day and no market; it had arrived and would go without a boundary drawn, and nobody under this roof would have known to call it a beginning. She set the basket at the edge of the light where the bar of it ended on the floor, with his head a little to the dark side of the line, so that he could see into the room and not be seen to join it. Then she stood half a pace off and gave the basket no instruction at all. The room stayed a sound and a shape from a basket, the way a Drawing had stayed a sound and a shape in another month; a room is not entered by being looked at from its edge.

\person[Corin]{The boy} took it as he took a field before the light, by its small sounds first, because the small sounds do not lie and the large ones are mostly weather: the rasp of a bench dragged a hand's breadth along the boards, a cough held in and lost, the small dry click of a latch, the scrape of a chair's foot going back. The room had a shape a basket at its edge could hold --- a table, a board-bed against the far wall, the rubbed place in the window, a smell of cold clay and tallow with something sour under it that was not the ridge along the skirting board at home and was not made into a cause because it smelled like one. Under the wool the infant wanted the tilt corrected the other way, toward the crust standing its hair off and the thread frozen at his lip, and got none of it. Both wants were true and the second was louder and neither won. That was the quarrel, and the quarrel was the work.

The man came in out of the cold with a flat leather satchel held against his forearm, the flap open and the slate inside turned toward himself. His cuff had gone soft from washing and was folded back twice, and the grey of the wash was worked into the seam at the wrist so that the seam looked drawn there rather than sewn. He set the satchel on the bench without looking at it, the way a man sets down a tool he will want in a moment and does not need to check. Out of a roll of wool at his elbow he took a cold iron tube, grey where the light from the window touched it and darker where his fingers had it, and his fingertips were grey at the tips the colour of skimmed milk gone off. He did not speak to the room. He did not look into the basket.

His hand went to the slate before his eyes went to the bed. The boy took the order apart as a surgeon takes a field apart: the hand to the record first, then the eyes to the person lying under the tube, and the mouth after both. There was nothing hurried in it and nothing ritual; it was the order a man uses who has done the same thing in the same light until the doing of it has gone into the muscle and left the mind free for other work. The boy had seen the order in a midwife laying slates before she turned to a fold, in a thane passing a finger over a figure before he looked at the goers-by, in a keeper's finger beneath a board, in a clerk going inward at a landing below the bridge. He had filed every one of them on a place of its own. He would file this one on a place of its own too, and put a finger between it and all the others, because a row of hands standing touching will call itself a rule if nobody keeps a hand between.

He said once, low, to the slate, not to the room and not to the basket: \emph{The measure is banked. The account stands. The return is recorded.} The hand that wrote was ordinary. There was no pause in it, no catch at the wrist, no small check where a man might have put one if he had been made to feel what he was doing; the writing went on in the same flat line from the first word to the last, and the last was set down with the same hand that had set down the first. The neat dark hand had a small stop in it that had been seen and filed and never joined to anything; this hand had no stop at all. The boy looked for the stop because the other hand had taught him to look for one, and found none, and filed the finding as a finding and not as a likeness, for a finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged.

He laid what he had taken on a tray of its own, the way instruments are laid out in the order they will be wanted and not in the order they were handled. Hand to the slate before eye to the bed: that was the shape, and the shape was all he kept. The ordinary writing hand with no pause in it went on the tray with a finger between it and the neat dark hand, whose small stop had been seen on another day and stayed where it had been seen, joined to nothing. He put a finger between it and \person{Hessa Rale}'s eighth, the turned wrist and the writing going to her own sleeve on the road; a finger between it and \person{Ilva}'s ninth at the well, her hand going to her sleeve before her eyes went to the asker and her mouth opening after both; a finger between it and \person{Berr Clay}'s twice-wiped thumb, passed over a figure through the cloth of his sleeve before his eyes went to the goers-by; a finger between it and the soft-cuffed clerk's inward slate set below the bridge for a measure nobody called; a finger between it and the Drawing collector's ordinary hand, filed out of a room that had been seen from a basket and never entered. Nine places stood and a tenth did not stand among them. The fingers went between every pair, because two ordinary hands are two ordinary hands and nine of them standing touching will call themselves a keeping if a man stands them up long enough and asks them what they mean. He did not stand them up and did not ask.

This man was not \person{Marra}. He was not the soft-handed man behind the Court table, whose slates lay flat where the person speaking could not see them; he was not the Fishstairs clerk who entered a buyer's number before he looked up to find the buyer; he was not the Drawing collector whose hand was already on its own tray with a finger around it. He was a man who had come in out of the cold with a satchel and a tube and written three sentences to a slate in a flat hand with no pause in it, and the boy did not make a tenth of him. No tenth hand stood up in the empty place. The empty place stayed empty and was not dug for and was not filled, and no rule came out of nine and one, for a rule is an account that has found a payer and none stood to any of these hands.

The neighbour under the tube was a woman and was not named and gave nothing of herself but a sound. It was a small sound, low in the throat and gone almost as soon as it had started, the sound a person makes when something is taken out of them that they had been holding for a long time and had not expected to lose in this room, on this morning, in this light. It did not fill the room and did not ask to be entered. The boy did not make a seventh hearing of it. Six hearings of \lore{the full note} stood beside and never among, the sixth the small flat knock out of \person{Dessa Silt} as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go and never among the things that had come out of vessels; the seventh place was empty and was not dug for and was not filled with a sound out of this room. \lore[The Hearth Drawing]{The Drawing}'s flat knock stayed outside them on its own place, a knock with no source standing to be asked and no room entered to put it in. After the small sound there was a pale round place on the woman's arm where the tube had been, not a wound and no blood, the round of it the colour of the inside of a shell. She covered it with her sleeve. Nobody said what it was and nobody said what it cost, and the boy put the pale round on no tray and wrote no cause across it.

He did not read the man's reason into the room. The man believed he kept the world alive by taking what was already going, and believed it the way a man believes the fire will take if he feeds it: not as a thought he had arrived at and could set aside, but as the shape of the work he had done for years and meant to go on doing. The boy did not know this and would not have entered it if he had, for a reason is a thing a man carries in him and not a thing standing in a room to be asked. The man went away with the belief whole in him, with no anger and no triumph in it, and that belief, not the anger and not the triumph, is the part of him that will one day be remembered against him. The boy put nothing of it on a tray. He did not join the man's hand to the twelve arithmetics behind the one unsigned figure, or to the four directions that stood where put and did not repeat. The Ash 5 joining and the Ash 17, Ash 23 and Stone 23 directions stood where they had been put, gathering dust in a corner nobody swept, and this morning's taking added no fifth shaping to them and bought no lesson and supplied no payer. Nothing under the roof had been opened by it, or named, or paid.

The going of the light came on without a boundary drawn, the colour going out of the rubbed place in the window a little at a time the way warmth goes out of a pot set off the fire, so that no hour could say where the morning had ended and the evening would begin. The man was gone with his satchel and his tube and his ordinary hand. The woman was gone with her sleeve down over the pale round place. The room two doors down went on being a shape and a sound from a basket, and shut itself behind the household the way a pot shuts behind a hand that has set it where it belongs.

At the going of the light \person{Sella Vail} carried him up the nineteen steps at the half-pace from every open slate and set him on \place{the bed} under the roof and never in \place{the Debt Loft}. She put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face and added no second fold, and said nothing of the room, the tube, the slate, the voice or the woman. The instruction went to the wool and said nothing to the basket. Under the wool the crust stood its hair off two inches from the ear, gone harder in the dry heat, and the thread at his lip was frozen and unwiped, because nobody under any roof has ever been given an instruction about the thread and a basket set down is a pot set down and left.

At \place{the hearth} the grammar went on as it had gone before the basket in the morning. She told the coals it was \lore{the thaw} and turned them with the back of a broken pot, and said \emph{The house will keep it} where another woman would have said I, standing a moment to count a small intake, a hold and a letting-go toward no opening with neither of the two gaps figured. Her finger lay along the top of \place{the loaf shelf} so that the charcoal tally on its underside stayed unread; her lips moved along a two-gapped row and figured neither gap. One glance went to the clay jar standing cold under \lore{lead glaze} beyond \place{the pot board}, the sweetish edge of it coming and going on a wrong wind; her hand shut half over nothing, the little-door burn on the back of her left hand closing on air. Both proverbs went to the pot and the fire and never to a person, improved in the keeping and very likely not as good as the man had said them, for a saying kept in a house grows smoother than the mouth that made it. Repair stayed apart from restoration: a restored thing hides the hand that handled it, and a mended thing leaves that hand showing. The dressed grain was turned under wool with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child, changed in state and lifting no figure and taking no handful. The pot rim was set two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat and no look toward the bed; his hand went toward it and stopped where the half-beat had stopped it. The interval was bought at nothing and was not a gift, and the stopping was his.

The faint sour tang of \lore{Ash} came up off the unswept clean dark line at \place{the Vail corner}, thickened in the dry cold where no broom had gone in two years; it was a smell and not a cause. The salt-stiff coat on its peg behind the door kept the broader shape at the left shoulder, and the hollow of the wheel-seat in the shed kept its twenty-year hollow that nobody filled, and neither was given the name of the man they kept. The \lore{tile debt} against a firing of tiles circled without a name and was worse for being uncalled. The cord of her \lore{widow's mark} at her throat was oiled where a working thumb had touched it ten thousand times and was not touched. A collection and a going of the light are not openings; seven openings in seven months are seven and not a rule, and no eighth was made due on this day, and the empty place where one might have stood was not dug for and not filled. The keeping in the crust's empty place was found where put and stayed a finding: the wool folded once exactly where a hand put it on the fifteenth, changed by nobody, with no third crust, no name spoken over the place, no gift, no payment and no payer. A habit wants a hand that placed twice and a way that a third, and both want a payer where none stands; a hand that only finds adds no placing however many times it finds. The finding sat on a place of its own beside the crust's gap, the shelf's two gaps, the gone pan's round ring and the field's empty place, with a finger between every pair. The far-off finger that would one day lay shelf and fold together and call the joining an account was still far off, not his and not come; the boy did not reach toward it by wanting it.

Under the floor \lore{the carried fire} gave four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer without a name for three hundred and ninety days, and nobody lifted the floor and nobody supplied the pull. The fire that warmed the house had no name on its giver, and the boy kept that as a finding and not a cause; the pull that did not come was not made to stand up and call itself a debt anybody owed him. \lore{The Afterdrop} waited past a warming nobody in this house would perform. He knew a face composed by cold is not composure, that pink returning is the surface still lying over a cold core, that a cold patient cannot clot and must be warmed from the outside and slowly, never hurried, never rubbed, and that a basket set level out of the draught where the heat comes sideways is the rewarming he would choose and could not ask for. The knowing was exact and belonged to a man with another name in another country; the body it was spent on could not lift a crust two inches to its own mouth.

In the small hours \place{the towel} came out at the head of the bed as instruments come out when a case is done and before the next is called. He went over the field by the place of each thing and checked every object and left every gap a gap, and supplied nothing to make a row come out even, for a finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged. Nine record-first hands kept no tenth, the ordinary hand of this morning sitting on its own tray with a finger between it and every other; nine stoppings kept no tenth; six hearings of the full note stood beside and never among with the seventh place empty and the Drawing's flat knock outside them; four rooms with the same gap --- the birth room in \place{House Silt}, this kiln-house, the room where the slate was read on market-day and \place{the Drawing house} seen two doors down --- were held apart with a finger between every pair, four likenesses and not one account, an account wanting a hand to lay them together and a payer under all four, and no such hand and no such payer standing; twelve arithmetics stood behind one unsigned figure with no signature on the space and no doctrine out of twelve. The burned-slate word stood at five, the shut-room tellings at two, the row at five askings with no witness, the well-saying at four shapings; the loan at the kindling stood uncalled at the very end of a seventh year with the Seed 16 breath unspent and the fear of being entered again for vagrancy still his without a figure; the promise under the wheel-shed was neither given nor withdrawn; the cold foot was uncured and the cause of the second chill withheld. The told bell stood in its square beside the heard bell of \lore{the Long Night}, four, four and a gap, and its gap was not folded into the fire's four and a gap or into the twelve behind the figure. The Ash 5 joining and the Ash 17, Ash 23 and Stone 23 directions stood where put and did not repeat and bought no lesson. No ownerless voice came over the boards. Nothing under the roof had been opened, joined, named, called, paid, forgiven or ended.

Through \place{the finger's-width door} \place{the river} kept its note of \lore{water over water}, a low thing over a lower thing under \lore{the winter skin}, muffled as a voice is muffled by a shut door. The note had done before any hand in the room came down. The hand outside the wool came up an inch toward it --- did not reach, and the inch is not a reach --- took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole on the note's own finishing under the covering, let the intake go as the note ended and came straight down and laid itself flat against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. The letting-go was neither early nor stayed, and the hand did not stay.

He filed answer three hundred and fifty-six: exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the twelve readings, the bell's gap, the flat knock, the child's grey fingertips, the mother's cup, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the held breath, the two bites of iron, the river's skin, the widow's mark, the fourth direction nor the keeping at the fold. Kindling kept its day without a boundary drawn, and the Five were not made a door out of it: the four that stood beyond stayed beyond and were not brought into an hour that had not earned them. The count stood at three hundred and ninety exact, six months and two hundred and ten days told by nobody who would have known what a number counted, the carried fire's fifth unpaid the same number and its payer unnamed the same number; \person{Garl Vail}'s name was unspoken the same number, kept in the coat, the hollow seat, the hairline and the unswept line and nowhere spoken. The surgeon took his small clean satisfaction in a field taken as found and left as found, and it cost him nothing, which was how he knew it was not peace; under it, louder, the infant wanted the crust lifted, the thread wiped and a hand on the pot handle, and got none of the three. Both were true. The second was louder. Neither won, no verdict in the quarrel and none looked for. The hand lay flat on the fold on answer three hundred and fifty-six, the crust's corner standing a hair off the wool, the thread frozen and unwiped, with the room two doors down shut behind the household and no name spoken over its pale round place, and the count went on under the roof in Low Kiln, Sill, Grath.
