The stories came at last, sitting down in the dark the way neighbours sit down --- with a scrape of wood on stone, a settling of coats, a little coughing before anyone spoke. No fire had been lit to sit by. The year's last dark had come down the nineteen steps ahead of them and found the rooms already full of it, and they made themselves at home in it as people make themselves at home in a house they have known all their lives, without asking who built it or who pays for the keeping.

Under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof \person[Corin]{the boy} lay at the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf}, held where the heat came sideways and not where it would have chosen to come. He had been set there and not asked about it. The dark did not change the order of the body; it only took away the small consolations that daylight brings without anybody's ordering them. One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust stood two inches from his ear, its corner a hair off the wool. Beside it \place{the bed} kept the fold in the empty place where a hand had put it on the fifteenth, found where put through all the days since, still a finding and not a placing, with no second fold and no third crust and no name spoken over it. The thread at his mouth had gone stiff and would go stiffer before morning, unwiped, because nobody under any roof has ever been given an instruction about the thread. Cold had come to cheek, temple and the bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, and stopped there as at the end of a line written out for it; \lore{the Afterdrop} waited past a warming nobody in this house would perform. He knew a basket set level out of the draught where the heat came sideways was the rewarming he would choose, slow and from the outside and never rubbed, and could not ask for it. Under the exactness the infant wanted the crust lifted, the thread wiped and a hand on the pot handle. Both were true. The second was louder. Neither won, and no verdict was looked for; the quarrel was the work.

\person{Sella Vail} sat in the dark near \place{the hearth} with her hands in her lap, and her hands were not still. In the dark she counted what the light had shown her at both ends of the day: the spoons on \place{the pot board}, the nails in \place{the boards}, the breaths of a child who had not yet been given a number by anybody under this roof. The cord of her \lore{widow's mark} lay against her throat with the oil of ten thousand working thumbs in it, and her hand did not go to it; the dark was not an opening, and seven openings in seven months were seven and not a rule with no eighth made due on a night that had not earned one. Once she put her fingers on the hairline of the twice-mended pot where the old-brass staple met the tighter wire and left them there a breath longer than any check required, then took them away, not making a sixth held object of the interval and not writing a cause across it. She said nothing to the basket and gave it no instruction. What she said, low, went to the banked coals: something about \lore{the thaw}, in the voice another woman would have used for a child who could not hear her. The coals answered with their small red eye under the broken pot's back, and the house straightened around her not saying what she meant.

Beyond \place{the finger's-width door} the neighbours sat where they had sat for the telling of \lore{the Return} in every year this house had known --- on the doorsteps, on \place{the flat stone by the well}, on an overturned pail outside a house whose fire had also been banked early. Their voices came through the winter air without being addressed to any room, the way a coal is passed in a dish from hearth to hearth and nobody says where the dish was filled. \place{The Clay Yard} held the dark between its seven walked-around places, and the stories went around among them with gaps in them the shape of vessels' intervals: a pause where a name should stand, a space where a counting should be, an interval that was not a hearing, though it had the shape of one. A vessel holds what it holds and the space between its warm places is still part of the vessel; the boy had learned that much from listening to pots before he had learned the word for it. But a gap in a story is not a sound with a source standing under it. He did not make a seventh hearing of any story told beyond the door, and did not lay a story's gap across the told bell's gap, nor across the silence after the name from \place{Weighing}, nor across his own kept spaces, for two gaps agreeing would want a payer put under both and none stood to either.

Other voices went around the telling as well, because a story of the Return is never carried by only one mouth in a yard where every house has kept its own dead. A woman said that in her grandmother's time the count of the returned had stopped at four and the fifth had been left for the weather, and she said it with a small intake before the word for the fifth, as though the word itself were a coal too hot to hold. A man whose voice had been at the well said that a name had once been called from \place{the Cross} and nobody had come to answer it, and that the calling had gone on being a calling after the man who called it went away. Neither story had a figure in it and neither had a source standing; the boy kept them on their own places and gave no interior to the people sitting in the dark, for a voice in a yard is a voice in a yard and not a room entered. He had learned early that a pause is not a person. He had learned it from pots: a cracked vessel answers flat, and the flatness is a fact about the vessel and not a confession from whoever held it. The neighbours went on around their gaps as the yard went around its seven places, with a look and a wide step and no number, and the gaps stayed gaps, the shape of vessels' intervals and not the intervals themselves.

One of them told of a whole return announced in a clean voice for somebody who was not in any room to hear it. The teller had the Cross's grammar in his mouth and a temple's clothes on his meaning --- calm, clean, with every speaker taken neatly out of his own sentences, so that the announcement arrived without a man inside it to be asked. It was the form of the telling that caught the seam, not the words; the boy had heard that form before a voice up the yard had left a name in the air and taken it back. The neighbour went on in the dark and came to the place where a name would stand, and left it standing empty. He did not say the name. Nobody in the dark supplied it. His voice went quiet where the name would have been, and the quiet had the exact weight of a thing set down and not picked up; then he went on around it, the way a man steps around a hole in a floor he has not been asked to mend.

The boy put that quiet on the gap tray beside the told bell's gap, the silence after the name from Weighing, the crust's two inches, the shelf's two gaps, the gone pan's round ring, the field's empty place and the unfigured spaces, with a finger between every pair so that none should touch another and call itself a rule. He wrote no payer over it and no cause across it. He did not let it stand up as a witness, for a witness wants a person standing; did not let it stand up as a reader, for a reader wants a hand on a slate; did not let it stand up as a payment, for a payment wants somebody to be paid; and did not let it stand up as a doctrine, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood.

The page over the speakers says a name. The white room does not look up, and his hands stay in the field.

Then it was \lore{the Long Night} under the Vail roof again, and the neighbour going on around the name he had left out, and nothing had been paid. The form had shown itself for two sentences and gone back into the mouth where it is kept, neither spent nor explained, borrowing no house count and supplying no payer. The boy's hand outside the wool did not move; the infant under it wanted the crust lifted and the thread wiped and was given neither.

The bell was told once more before the stories were done, four, four and a gap, passed from one mouth to another down the yard as a coal is passed in a dish, each house saying it had kept the count and none saying where the count began. No bell was rung. The boy kept the told bell in its square beside the bell he had heard from the Cross, four, four and a gap, with its gap not folded into the fire's four and a gap under the floor, nor into the boards' four and four and a thumb-width knot, nor into the twelve arithmetics standing behind one unsigned figure, nor made a seventh hearing of \lore{the full note}. The heard bell stayed heard and the told bell stayed told; a gap in a telling is not a source standing under a sound. Three counts in three places stayed three, and none rose into another, because agreeing any two would want a payer put under both and no payer stood to either. He set no hand to join them.

The stories went on after the telling of the bell, because on a night with no fire there is nothing to do but sit in the dark and let the old sayings go around; and the house under the roof kept its own shape, with the crust at two inches and the thread stiffening by degrees and the fourth pull of the fire coming and going below the floor. Nobody under any roof checked the fifth pull. Nobody lifted the floor. The wanting of the crust, the thread and a hand on the pot handle went on quarrelling with the inventory and went on losing in the same way it had won: both true, the second louder, neither winning. It was a small thing to be awake for, and it was the whole of the night.

\scenebreak

When the last of the stories had gone around and the yard had settled into its own small sounds, the boy went over the day in the order it had arrived, because a field taken out of order is a field read wrong. The small sounds came first: the coals turning under the broken pot's back, the fire's four low pulls under the floor and the silence where the fifth should have stood, the river's note under its covering, the voices in the yard going quiet where a name would have been, the told bell's four, four and a gap. None of these was a hearing and none was added to another. Then the kept things, each on its own place with a finger between: the crust's gap, the shelf's two gaps, the gone pan's round ring, \place{the field's empty place}, the keeping at the fold found where put and still a finding, the told bell's gap, the silence after the name from Weighing, the place the neighbour's story had left empty, \person{Tarn}'s stopped ear-sentence, \person{Jorra Vail}'s promise under \place{the Vail wheel-shed}, and \person{Garl Vail}'s unforged collector sentence beside the seventh notch. Four rooms kept the same gap in their count --- the birth room in \place{House Silt}, the Vail kiln-house, the room where the slate was read on market-day and \place{the Drawing house} --- and were held apart with a finger between every pair; no Court room was brought under the roof as a fifth likeness. He did not let the silences stand up together and call themselves a rule, for a rule is an account that has found a payer and none stood.

He had seen the same order of reaching in all of them: hand to the record before eye to the person, eye before mouth, with the small stop in the gap where another man would have put an intention. \person{Hessa Rale}'s slate went up the road unread; \person{Ilva}'s strip stayed in her sleeve with no figure coming off the wood; the soft-cuffed clerk's inward slate stayed at the out-of-line landing; the collector who took the year's first measure went on his own tray with the ordinary writing hand that had no pause in it. Nine record-first hands and no tenth stood up in the empty place, though the yard's voices tonight had left a row of gaps that wanted only a hand to lay them together and a payer to stand under them. He supplied neither. A man who files likenesses without a hand to join them has filed nothing but likenesses, and an account wants a payer standing under all of them; none stood.

Tarn's loan stood uncalled at the very end of a seventh year with the Seed 16 breath unspent and the fear of being entered again for vagrancy still his without a figure; a term ending is not a payment, a forgiveness or a freedom. Jorra's promise under the wheel-shed was neither given nor withdrawn, and \emph{He's not Vail} stayed sealed where it had been put. Sella's widow's mark hung where it hung, untouched, because a holy day is not an opening and seven openings in seven months are seven and not a rule with no eighth made due. The burned-slate word stood at five, the shut-room tellings at two, the row at five askings with no witness and expressly no sixth, the well-saying at four shapings with no fifth; nine record-first hands kept no tenth and nine stoppings kept no tenth. Six hearings of the full note stood beside and never among, the seventh place empty and the flat knock from two doors down outside them; a story's gap was the shape of a vessel's interval and not a source standing under a sound. Twelve arithmetics stood behind one unsigned figure --- the three of \place{Frost}, the first, second and third of \place{Bone}, the first, second and third of \place{Ash}, \place{Stone}'s first, second and last --- twelve and not one account, the figure read still not the figure paid, no signature on the space and no doctrine out of twelve. Their twelve true subtractions went up \place{the clay road} in a locked box with the key gone up in a coat pocket and not coming back down, and the boy had no key.

The count came to three hundred and ninety-two exact, six months and two hundred and twelve days told by nobody who would have known what a number counted. \lore{The carried fire}'s fifth was unpaid the same number and its payer unnamed the same number. Garl's name was unspoken the same number, kept in the salt-stiff coat with the crease at the left shoulder on its peg behind the door, in the hollow wheel-seat nobody filled, in the hairline of the twice-mended pot between old-brass staple and tighter wire, in the clean dark line at \place{the Vail corner} with the finer grey beside it called the thaw, thickened in the dry cold with the faint sour tang of \lore{Ash} coming up off it where no broom had gone in two years --- kept there, and nowhere spoken. Three counts in three places stood side by side and did not become one: the fire's four and a gap under the floor, the boards' four and four and a knot overhead, the bell's four, four and a gap in its square. He set no hand to join them, for a joining wants a payer put under the joined things and none stood to any of them.

\scenebreak

Through the finger's-width door the river kept its note of \lore{water over water}, a low thing over a lower thing under \lore{the winter skin}, and the note had done before any hand in the room came down. The covering lay from bank to bank since the first day of Stone, put on by the cold and taken off by the cold, entered nowhere, found where put and not spent on a holy day, with no hand that laid it, no payer under it and no source standing to be asked. It was not a hearing and not a sign, and the boy kept it on its own place with a finger between it and the story's empty name, the told bell's gap and every other kept thing. The river went on being water over water whether any room listened or not.

The hand outside the wool came up an inch toward the note, and the inch is not a reach. It took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole on the note's own finishing under the covering, let the intake go as the note ended and came straight down and laid itself flat against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. The letting-go was neither early nor stayed, and the hand did not stay. The note had done before the hand came down, so the interval ended on the note's own finishing and not on any decision of the hand.

\place{The towel} came out at the head of the bed as instruments come out when a case is done and before the next is called. He went over the field by the place of each thing and left every gap a gap. The crust's gap, the shelf's two gaps, the gone pan's round ring, the field's empty place, the keeping at the fold, the told bell's gap, the silence after the name from Weighing, the place the neighbour left empty, the stopped ear-sentence, the promise under the wheel-shed --- each lay on its own place with a finger between, and none was made to stand up and call itself a rule. He filed answer three hundred and sixty: exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the twelve readings, the bell's gap, the flat knock, the child's grey fingertips, the mother's cup, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the held breath, the two bites of iron, the river's skin, the widow's mark, the fourth direction nor the keeping at the fold.

The surgeon took his small clean satisfaction in a field taken as found and left as found, and it cost him nothing, which was how he knew it was not peace. Under it, louder, the infant wanted the crust lifted, the thread wiped and a hand on the pot handle, and got none of the three. The quiet in the house was not the quiet of an answer; it was the quiet of a room kept the way a vessel is kept, with a crack in it that nobody mentions and a fire under the floor that nobody lifts to look at.

He had closed fields like this one before, in a life whose rooms had no names under this roof: the field taken in the order it arrived, the instruments counted out, the gaps in the covering left where the gaps were, the note written with nothing entered that had not been seen. Nothing from those rooms had crossed except the form of a voice announcing a name over people who did not look up, and the form had gone back where it came from without a payer and without a figure. He did not try to bring the rest of it across. A man who brings a whole room across for the sake of two sentences will end by putting a payer under both lives, and there was none standing under either.

The hand lay flat on the fold on answer three hundred and sixty, the crust's corner standing a hair off the wool and the thread frozen and unwiped, with the dark of the Long Night going on without a fire and the carried fire breathing four below the floor with its fifth unsupplied, the bell's gap kept in its square and the story's empty name on its own tray, and the river keeping its note under the winter skin beyond the finger's-width door. The count went on under the roof in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill}, \place{Grath}, six months and two hundred and twelve days told by nobody, and nothing had been opened, joined, named, called, paid, forgiven or ended.
