## Chronicle

Three holy days came under the Vail roof — Kindling, Weighing and the Long Night — with names and no tend-days and no market. Each morning the surgeon inventoried the body: the crust two inches from the ear, the fold in the empty place, the frozen thread nobody was told to wipe, the afterdrop waiting past a warming nobody would perform. The infant wanted the crust lifted, the thread wiped, a hand on the pot handle. Both true; the second louder; neither winning.

On Kindling the year's fire came up the Clay Yard in a clay dish, hearth to hearth, before first light; nobody said where it had first been lit or what it cost. A collector took the year's first measure. His hand went to the slate before his eyes went to the bed, and he said once: *The measure is banked. The account stands. The return is recorded.* The hand that wrote had no pause in it; the boy filed it apart, not a tenth. The man went away believing he kept the world alive by taking what was going; that belief will be remembered against him.

On Weighing a clean voice went up the yard in the grammar of the Cross wearing a temple's clothes. It announced a whole return for somebody absent, said the name once and no more; the household would not repeat it. The speaker had taken himself out of his sentences; the boy filed him apart. Tarn kept six at the mark and a seventh at the lip, and said, *Six counted. The next I don't count.* His seventh year ended with the loan uncalled. Jorra put a bent finger into the cold damper, parting dusty forearms from clean. The promise under the shed was neither given nor withdrawn.

On the Long Night no fire was lit after dusk. Sella banked the coals early and set the pot rim two inches beyond his reach. The bell went four, four and a gap in the yard's told voices, beside the heard bell. Neighbours told the Return in the dark, with gaps the shape of vessels' intervals. One told of a whole return announced in a clean voice for somebody absent, and left the name standing empty. For two sentences the grammar broke: *The page over the speakers says a name. The white room does not look up, and his hands stay in the field.* Then it fell back into the dark, and nothing was paid.

Six answers were filed, 355 through 360. The count stood at 392 exact, six months and two hundred and twelve days told by nobody; the carried fire's fifth was unpaid and its payer unnamed, and Garl's name unspoken the same number. His satisfaction in a field taken as found and left as found cost nothing; it was not peace. The infant wanted the crust lifted, the thread wiped and a hand on the pot handle, and got none of the three.
