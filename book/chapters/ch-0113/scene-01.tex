\daymark{390}{Kindling, Y.H. 813}

\dropcap{T}{he hand} outside the wool lay flat on the fold where the going of the last light had left it, and the dark under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill}, \place{Grath} thinned by degrees, the way heat goes out of a banked fire under blind ash --- not lifted away but spent slowly, the coals behind the wall giving back what they had in fours and leaving the fifth where it had always been left. The count stood at three hundred and eighty-nine, exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, and no voice under any roof would have known to speak it. This was \lore{Kindling}, the first of \lore{The Five}: a day with a name and no tend-day and no market, and the boy did not call it an opening, a payment or a boundary. A holy day arrives the way weather arrives, and no doing of this house had earned it. Nothing from the twenty-eighth, twenty-ninth or thirtieth of \place{Stone} had crossed under this roof as sign, lesson, mending, cause, payment or payer; nothing crossed in these small hours. The Five stood here, unbrought and unnamed by anybody who would have known what a name was worth.

\person[Corin]{The boy} lay at the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf} on \place{the bed}, carried everywhere and told by nobody, and the surgeon took the body in the order it came because the order was a way of being awake. The crust came first: one stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust two inches from the ear, gone harder in the dry kiln heat with its corner standing a hair off the wool. Beside it the empty place held the fold put there on the fifteenth, found where put, not made again, still a finding and not a placing. The thread of drool at the lip had frozen in the dry heat and was wiped by nobody, because nobody under any roof has ever been given an instruction about the thread and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. Three things found as they had been left, and none the better for his finding them. Cold had come cheek, temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, and stopped there as though it had come to the end of a line written out for it, with neither an order to go farther nor an order to go away. \lore{The Afterdrop} waited past a warming nobody in this house would perform.

He knew a face composed by cold is not composure. He knew pink returning is the surface still lying over a cold core, a better colour on a worse patient; that a cold patient cannot clot and must be warmed from the outside and slowly, never hurried, never rubbed; that a basket set level out of the draught where the heat comes sideways is the careful rewarming he would choose and could not ask for. The knowledge was exact and belonged to a man with another name in another country. The body it was spent on was three hundred and eighty-nine days old and could not lift a crust two inches to its own mouth.

Under the exactness the infant wanted the crust lifted, the thread wiped and a hand on the pot handle, and wanted them without knowing that wanting them was a want. The two registers quarrelled without verdict. Both were true. The second was louder. Neither won, and no verdict was looked for; the quarrel was the work, and the work went on in silence in a room whose floor does not touch his.

Overhead \place{the boards} kept four nails on the near board, four on the next and a thumb-width knot where a fifth might have been driven and was not. Through \place{the finger's-width door} \place{the river} kept its low note of \lore{water over water} under \lore{the winter skin}, a covering put on by the cold and taken off by the cold and entered nowhere, found where put and not spent on a holy day, with no hand that laid it, no payer under it and no source standing to be asked. Three counts stood in three places and none rose into another: the boards' four and four and a knot; the four low pulls under the floor and the silence where the fifth should stand; the river's low over lower under its skin. Agreeing any two would have wanted a payer put under both, and no payer stood.

\person{Sella Vail} came to \place{the hearth} before the basket, as she had come before it at the beginning of every day since the boy came under the roof. She told the coals it was \lore{the thaw} and turned them with the back of a broken pot, and said \emph{The house will keep it} where another woman would have said I, standing a moment to count a small intake, a hold and a letting-go toward no opening with neither of the two gaps figured. Her finger lay along the top of the loaf shelf so that the charcoal tally on its underside stayed unread; her lips moved along a two-gapped row and figured neither gap. One glance went to the clay jar standing cold under \lore{lead glaze} beyond \place{the pot board}, the sweetish edge of it coming and going on a wrong wind; her hand shut half over nothing, the little-door burn on the back of her left hand closing on air. The instruction went to the wool, to the fire and to the river wind, and never to the basket.

The cord of her \lore{widow's mark} at her throat was oiled where a working thumb had touched it ten thousand times and was not touched. A holy day is not an opening; seven openings in seven months are seven and not a rule, and no eighth was made due on this morning, and the empty place where one might have stood was not dug for and not filled. The faint sour tang of \lore{Ash} came up off the unswept clean dark line at \place{the Vail corner}, thickened in the dry cold where no broom had gone in two years; it was a smell and not a cause. The salt-stiff coat on its peg behind the door kept \person{Garl Vail}'s broader shape at the left shoulder, and the hollow of the wheel-seat in the shed kept its twenty-year hollow that nobody filled, and neither was given his name. The \lore{tile debt} against a firing of tiles circled without a name and was worse for being uncalled.

On the pot board the twice-mended pot stood with the hairline of the original break showing between the older brass staple and the tighter wire. Sella lifted it thumb-first where the wire crossed the crack before she let the weight come on the handle, and the hairline showed dark; she left it showing. \emph{Leave the crack showing. People trust a thing they can see has been handled} and \emph{If it's worth mending it's worth mending twice. The second time is the real one} went to the pot and the fire and never to a person, improved in the keeping and very likely not as good as the man had said them, for a saying kept in a house grows smoother than the mouth that made it. Repair stayed apart from restoration: a restored thing hides the hand that handled it, and a mended thing leaves that hand showing. The dressed grain at the cool stone was turned under wool with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child, changed in state and lifting no figure and taking no handful. The pot rim was set two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat and no look toward the bed; his hand went toward it and stopped where the half-beat had stopped it. The interval was bought at nothing and was not a gift, and the stopping was his.

\scenebreak

She carried him down the nineteen steps at the half-pace she keeps from every open slate, and the instruction went to the wool, the river wind and the fire she would bank against their coming back, and never to the basket. Out in \place{the Clay Yard} the year's first morning stood in the dry cold with its light not yet come, and the yard went around its seven walked-around places with a look and a wide step and no number, once and not twice, widest around the returning-line place where a hand went in on Seed 15 and did not come out. The clean dark line at the Vail corner went around; the space beneath \place{the lowest rack} where \lore{the broken birth-slates} lay went around and was entered by nobody; the gone pan's round ring at \place{the rack end} went around with no scrape, crumb, print, chip or word; the skinned \place{trough}, the field's empty place out on \place{the worked strips} and the hollow beside the seventh notch at \place{the kindling} went around. Beside that seventh piece lay Garl's unforged collector sentence, with no name on the man who turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on the debt. \place{The flagstones} gave back what little light there was without the half-check, and nothing in the yard was counted or called across.

The \place{drying racks} stood with the month's grey worked small into the seams of their lowest bars, and the three coverings stayed where the weather had put them, with a finger between every pair: the stayed frost gone off the north lip of \place{the yard well}, the skin drawn edge to edge across the trough, the plate of ice clouded with silt standing on \place{the flat stone by the well} out of the way of feet. Weather entered nowhere and was not made a cause, a sign, a payment or a promise. Out on the worked strips the field kept its empty place and did not fill it. Beyond the yard the \place{Ilm} lay under its covering, found where put and not spent on the holy day; a line left where it is is still a line kept, and a sign without a payer is weather and not a promise. He kept the note and the covering on separate places, so that no row came out even by his doing.

Before first light the year's fire came up the Clay Yard in a clay dish, from hearth to hearth. Hands passed it in the dark; a woman at one door said her house had kept the flame, and a man at the next said the same, and a girl further down said it the way the others had said it, and nobody said where it had first been lit or what it had cost to keep. The dish came up warm and went on warm, and the boy, carried at the edge of the passing, took the saying as he took everything else --- as a thing said and not as a source standing. The coal came under the back of a broken pot at \place{the kiln mouth} with no hand under this roof making an opening of it and nobody praising what it mended. The fire took. Under the floor \lore{the carried fire} gave four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer without a name for three hundred and eighty-nine days, and nobody lifted the floor and nobody supplied the pull. The fire that warmed the house had no name on its giver, and the boy kept that as a finding and not a cause; the pull that did not come was not made to stand up and call itself a debt anybody owed him.

There was an order to the morning that he could not place: a clean space wiped where nothing had been set, a place kept ready that nobody named. He filed it as an order and not an arrival. The man who would come with the light was not in the yard and not under the roof, and the boy did not bring his hour into an hour that had not earned him; an hour earns what comes in it and not what is coming to it. Twelve arithmetics of the year --- the three of \place{Frost}, the first, second and third of \place{Bone}, the first, second and third of \place{Ash}, and Stone's first, second and last --- stood behind one unsigned figure, one in twelve read twice over the basket on the thirtieth and sent twice, once for the moot and once for the households that came late; the figure read was still not the figure paid, no signature stood on the space between, twelve were twelve and not one account, and no doctrine came out of twelve. No thirteenth was brought into a holy day that had not earned it. \lore[The Hearth Drawing]{The Drawing}'s flat knock stayed outside the six hearings on its own place; six hearings of \lore{the full note} stood beside and never among, the seventh place empty and not dug for or filled; the heard bell stayed in its square beside the told, four, four and a gap, and its gap was not folded into the fire's four and a gap or into the twelve behind the figure. The Ash 5 joining and the Ash 17, Ash 23 and Stone 23 directions stood where put, did not repeat and bought no lesson. No ownerless voice came over the boards.

\scenebreak

At the going of the light she carried him up the nineteen steps and set him on his bed under the roof and never in \place{the Debt Loft}, and put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face and without a second fold. The keeping in the crust's empty place was found where put and stayed a finding: the wool folded once exactly where a hand had put it on the fifteenth, changed by nobody, with no third crust, no name spoken over the place, no gift, no payment and no payer. A habit wants a hand that placed twice and a way that a third, and both want a payer where none stands; a hand that only reads adds no placing however many times it reads. The finding sat on a place of its own beside the crust's gap, the shelf's two gaps, the gone pan's round ring and the field's empty place, with a finger between every pair. The far-off finger that would one day lay shelf and fold together and call the joining an account was still far off, not his and not come; the boy did not reach toward it by wanting it. The thread stayed frozen at his lip and unwiped. The hearth grammar was kept at both ends of the day --- fire before basket, coals told the thaw, the twice-mended pot thumb-first with the hairline showing, the dressed grain turned under wool and given no figure or handful, the pot rim two inches beyond the reaching hand --- and the widow's mark was not touched at a morning, a hearth, a yard, a going of the light or a bed, because none of them was an opening and seven openings in seven months are seven and not a rule with no eighth made due.

\place{The towel} came out at the head of the bed as instruments come out when a case is done and before the next is called. He went over the field by the place of each thing and checked every object and left every gap a gap, and supplied nothing to make a row come out even, for a finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged. The burned-slate word stood at five, the shut-room tellings at two, the row at five askings with no witness, the well-saying at four shapings; nine record-first hands kept no tenth and nine stoppings kept no tenth; four rooms with the same gap --- the birth room in \place{House Silt}, this kiln-house, the room where the slate was read on market-day and \place{the Drawing house} --- were held apart with a finger between every pair, four likenesses and not one account, an account wanting a hand to lay them together and a payer under all four, and no such hand standing. The keeping was a finding; twelve findings and twelve readings made no habit and no doctrine; the loan at the kindling stood uncalled at the very end of a seventh year with the Seed 16 breath unspent and the fear of being entered again for vagrancy still his without a figure; the promise under the wheel-shed was neither given nor withdrawn; the cold foot was uncured and the cause of the second chill withheld, and neither was spent on the holy day or made to open anything. Nothing under the roof had been opened, joined, named, called, paid, forgiven or ended.

Through the finger's-width door the river kept its note, a low thing over a lower thing under the winter skin, muffled as a voice is muffled by a shut door. The note had done before any hand in the room came down. The hand outside the wool came up an inch toward it --- did not reach, and the inch is not a reach --- took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole on the note's own finishing under the covering, let the intake go as the note ended and came straight down and laid itself flat against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. The letting-go was neither early nor stayed, and the hand did not stay.

He filed answer three hundred and fifty-five: exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the twelve readings, the bell's gap, the flat knock, the child's grey fingertips, the mother's cup, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the held breath, the two bites of iron, the river's skin, the widow's mark, the fourth direction nor the keeping at the fold. Kindling kept its day without a boundary drawn, and the Five were not made a door out of it: the four that stood beyond stayed beyond and were not brought into an hour that had not earned them. The count stood at three hundred and ninety exact, six months and two hundred and ten days told by nobody who would have known what a number counted; the carried fire's fifth was unpaid the same number and its payer unnamed the same number; Garl Vail's name was unspoken the same number, kept in the coat, the hollow seat, the hairline and the unswept line and nowhere spoken. The surgeon took his small clean satisfaction in a field taken as found and left as found, and it cost him nothing, which was how he knew it was not peace; under it, louder, the infant wanted the crust lifted, the thread wiped and a hand on the pot handle, and got none of the three. Both were true. The second was louder. Neither won, no verdict in the quarrel and none looked for. The hand lay flat on the fold on answer three hundred and fifty-five, the crust's corner standing a hair off the wool, the thread frozen and unwiped, with the fire gone on under the floor in fours and the fifth where it had been left, and the count went on under the roof in Low Kiln, Sill, Grath.
