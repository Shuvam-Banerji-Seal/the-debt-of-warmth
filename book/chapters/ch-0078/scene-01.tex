\daymark{285}{Frost 16, Sixthday, Y.H. 813}

\dropcap{T}{he hand} lay back against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. Outside the wool the small hours went on at their own pace under the roof of \place{the Vail kiln-house} in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill}, \place{Grath}, and \person[Corin]{the boy} lay carried, awake and silent, with the hundred and forty-fourth answer put down behind him the way a pot is set on a shelf where nobody will knock it. Two hundred and eighty-four stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in \place{Sarn}; six months and one hundred and four days were told by nobody; the fire's fifth was unpaid the same number of days with its payer unnamed. The hand had gone up an inch, taken the held interval and the empty interval from the file, set them side by side with a finger between, borrowed no house count and supplied no fifth to the kiln, and come down on the river's next low note. That was done. It was not recalled as growth, a gift, a reaching or a sign; it was a thing put away in its place, and the place was the sixth, and the sixth was a place and not a power. Under the floor \lore{the carried fire} kept its four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood, and the lean quarter of its warmth stayed where it was with nobody to lift the floor and nobody to supply the pull.

He took the body as it presented itself, in the order the facts arrived, because that was the only order he had ever found trustworthy. One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from his ear, gone past the point where a child would eat it, and the empty place beside it kept its name to itself; no third had come in the night to make the row come out even. The thread of drool at his mouth had frozen in the dry heat and was unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. Cold had come to cheek and temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, and stopped there as at the end of a line written out for it, with neither an order to go forward nor an order to go away. The afterdrop waited beyond all three, past a warming nobody in this house would perform. He knew the composed face that is not composure; he knew a cold patient cannot clot and will seem better before he is worse, and that a man must be warmed from the outside and slowly. He wanted the crust lifted the two inches to his mouth. Both were true, and in a body six months and one hundred and four days old the second was louder; the surgeon took the inventory in its old order --- crust, gap, thread, cold, the waiting --- and entered none of it, not even on the place where he kept the things he did not enter. He had once reduced a shoulder in the dark by feel alone and now could not close two inches on a pot handle; the thought came and went without bitterness, which was its own small betrayal.

Through the finger's-width door the river kept its note of \lore{water over water}, a low thing over a lower thing, never numbered among the hearings; overhead \place{the boards} kept four nails on the near board, four on the next and a thumb-width knot where the fifth nail should have stood. Three counts in three places, and none rose into another, because agreeing any two would want a payer put under both and no payer stood to any. No fire-sitter stood over the coals and no house count came to be borrowed. Six hearings of \lore{the full note} stood beside and never among, five from four sources and the sixth, the small flat knock out of a woman as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go and never with the things that came from vessels; the empty place kept its emptiness and none was invented to fill it. The bell's four, four and a gap stayed in the square at \place{the Cross} beside the told bell of \lore{the Long Night}, not folded into the three and not heard as a seventh of the full note. In the room whose floor does not touch his a woman gave its low counting to the work --- the small intake, the hold, the letting-go --- and the boy took only what the hand could take and left the letting-go where it was. It was her breath and her count and not his.

The house kept its days in loaves, in glances and in unnamed spaces, and gave nobody a number to carry. Frost the thirteenth, fourteenth and fifteenth lay behind with no boundary drawn between them and the morning that had come on in its own time; the sixteenth came on its own first light and was not brought into a day that had not earned it. There was no market, no gathering at the Cross and no bell on this Sixthday, and the reading of the tenth was not looked at again as a sign, a lesson, a mending or a payment. Frost the twentieth (Day 289), the month's second Account-day, stood two mornings beyond the close and was not brought into a morning that had not earned it. In the small hours the count crossed from two hundred and eighty-four to two hundred and eighty-five the way a man lays one board beside another without measuring the seam. Two hundred and eighty-five stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in Low Kiln, Sill and Grath; six months and one hundred and five days were told by nobody; the carried fire's fifth was unpaid the same number of days with its payer unnamed.

First light came up the yard without announcing itself, the way heat comes back into a room that has been cold all night, by degrees and from the outside. With it came \person{Sella Vail}. She went to \place{the hearth} before the basket, as she had gone on every morning since the boy came under the roof, and gave the fire its instruction before she gave one to anything that breathed. \emph{It was the thaw}, she told the coals, and turned them with the back of a broken pot, and said \emph{The house will keep it} where another woman would have said \emph{I}. The little-door burn on the back of her left hand closed on the broken pot and then on nothing, and she pushed a strand of coal-black hair back with that wrist so the one grey streak at her temple showed pale against the dark. The fire answered four low pulls and kept its silence where the fifth should have stood; she neither praised it nor supplied the pull, and made no lesson of the first firing of Frost now that it stood behind her. Four openings in four months were four and not a rule. The next stood in a later month's first firing, far off, not hoped for and not counted toward.

At \place{the loaf shelf} her finger stayed on top of the front edge so the charcoal beneath remained unread; her lips moved along a row standing with two gaps and gave neither gap a figure. She glanced once at the clay jar standing cold under its \lore{lead glaze} and shut her hand half over nothing. \lore[The widow's mark]{The widow's mark} hung where it hung on its cord at her throat and her hand did not go to it at a morning, a hearth and a basket, because none of these is an opening and the hand goes to the mark before the kiln and at no other hour. She took the wool at his ear between two fingers and drew it up, telling the wool and not the boy what it was for. \emph{Keep it off his ears. It's the river wind}, she said, in the flat practical voice she kept for inanimate things, and her burn shut on the wool and then on nothing. The instruction went to the fire, the wool, the pot, the broom and the running water and never to the basket. She set \place{the bed} right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face, and left the thread frozen where it lay. The pot rim she stood two inches beyond the reaching hand, with a half-beat and no look toward the bed, as she had stood it on every other morning and would stand it again.

Out beyond the finger's-width door the stayed \lore{frost-bloom} lay white on the north lip of \place{the yard well} where the yard had left it, past the going of the light and into another morning, and the yard called it weather. The rope stiffened in the hand and gave its small dry rasp; the water in \place{the trough} kept its skin; the plate of ice lifted off the trough stood on \place{the flat stone by the well} out of the path of feet, clouded where the silt had risen into it. None of it was entered, and no sign, cause, lesson, mending or payer was made of any of it. The surgeon's reading of a covering laid over a depth stayed on a place of its own beside the gone white of Frost 4, and no fourth likeness of three was made a cause.

At the rack end the dressed grain lay covered and unfigured under wool on the cool stone, away from the hearth; a corner of the wool had been lifted and the grain turned once for damp with a touch lighter than the morning before, and set back where it had lain with no figure going with it. The arithmetic that had read over it on Frost 10 did not know what it cost, and the space between the figure and the price had no signature on it. Frost's first arithmetic stayed locked up \place{the clay road} in \person{Berr Clay}'s box toward \place{High Kiln}; the printed one in twelve stood unchallenged, and the figure read was still not the figure paid. Nothing from the reading had come back under this roof as lesson, figure, mending or payment.

The file lay open on \place{the towel} at the head of the bed and he walked the shorter inventory in its order, because a thing hurried is a thing entered wrongly. The five askings of the ownerless question stood with one shaping and no witness and no row named, a finger between every pair; the nine stoppings stood with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end and still ungiven; the record-first hands stood at nine with a finger between the ninth and the eighth and nobody standing anywhere to be asked. The seventh green was a colour and the seventh shape a shape, with no eighth of either and no second look joined to the first. The shelf's two gaps kept their place above the charcoal, the fold's one crust kept its empty place beside it, and the pan's gone ring, the field's empty place and the returning-line place where a hand went in on Seed 15 and did not come out stayed each on a tray of its own. No third gap came to make a row come out even, and no fourth likeness was made a cause. Keeping by \lore{not-doings} held the house the way a clamp holds a seam: the fifth pull unsupplied, the charcoal unread, the debt uncalled, the grain unfigured, the thread unwiped, the crust unlifted. He kept the house's keeping on one place and his own gaps on another with a finger between, and neither standing up and calling itself a rule, for a rule is an account that has found a payer and none stood to any of them.

The room whose floor does not touch his went on with its counting, and the boy took from it only what the hand could take. Outside the wool the hand went up an inch toward the river's note. It took the small intake of the river's own going through the finger's-width door and carried it across the little silence left by one of the counted breaths in the next room, borrowing no house count and supplying no fifth to the kiln, and came down on the river's next low note of water over water. It was one house rhythm carried across the river's interval on an ordinary morning, exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air. It was kept as an honest coordination on the sixth place; it was not a reach, a hearing, a seventh, a stopping, a payment, a held change, a key, a power, a freedom, a gift, a doctrine or a rule. The surgeon's clean satisfaction at setting one sound against another was checked by the infant's plain want of the crust two inches off; the checking was the work and he did not call it a virtue. One hundred and forty-five stood beside one hundred and forty-four. He made nothing of it and would not let it stand up as a gift, because a gift wants a taker and nobody under this roof had taken anything.

The hand laid back against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. Two hundred and eighty-five stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in Low Kiln, Sill and Grath; six months and one hundred and five days were told by nobody; the carried fire's fifth was unpaid the same number of days with its payer unnamed. Nothing was opened, named, called, paid or ended. The file lay open on the towel at the head of the bed with every gap left gap, every tray parted by a finger's width and nothing that had moved. Beyond the finger's-width door the frost lay white on the well lip where the yard had left it, called weather and entered nowhere, and the plate of ice stood where it had been set, clouded where the silt rose into it. Frost 20 (Day 289), the month's second Account-day, stood two mornings beyond the close and was not brought into a morning that had not earned it. The boy lay with the crust two inches off and its empty place beside it unnamed, the hand laid down on the hundred and forty-fifth answer, while the ordinary first light came up on a day with no market in it. The river's low note went quiet after the hand came down.
