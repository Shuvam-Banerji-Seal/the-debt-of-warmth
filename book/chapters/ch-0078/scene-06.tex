The note went quiet and the quiet went on, and the afternoon came over it the way a pot comes off the coals and is set upon a cold stone: not ending, only giving up its heat by degrees, the loss itself a work the yard performed without anybody naming it. Frost's last working day thinned in this manner. Neither of the district's two habits of naming its months was set right or left wrong by the thinning, and no lesson was made of Frost's first reading and none made now. The next opening stood in the first firing of a later month, far off, not hoped for, not counted toward; four openings in four months were four, and four were not a rule. \person[Corin]{The boy} lay at the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf} under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill}, \place{Grath}, and the heat drew back out of the fold and sat behind the pot on the side that had last faced the fire, as a man's warmth draws back from a hand he has taken from a door and does not go with him.

He took the body as it presented itself, in the order the facts arrived, because that order had never lied to him and no other had ever promised to. One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from his ear, gone past the point where a child would eat it, and the empty place beside it kept its name to itself; no third had come in the afternoon to make the row come out even. The thread of drool at his mouth had frozen in the dry heat and was unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. Cold had come to cheek and temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, and stopped there as at the end of a line written out for it, with neither an order to go forward nor an order to go away. The afterdrop waited beyond all three, past a warming nobody in this house would perform. He knew the composed face that is not composure; he knew a cold patient cannot clot and will seem better before he is worse, and that a man must be warmed from the outside and slowly. He wanted the crust lifted the two inches to his mouth. Both were true, and in a body six months and one hundred and seven days old the second was louder; the surgeon took the inventory in its old order --- crust, gap, thread, cold, the waiting --- and entered none of it, not even on the place where he kept the things he did not enter. He had once reduced a shoulder in the dark by feel alone and now could not close two inches on the pot handle set beyond his reach; the thought came and went without bitterness, which was its own small betrayal.

Three counts stood in three places and none rose into another. Under the floor \lore{the carried fire} gave four low pulls with the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer without a name for two hundred and eighty-seven days; at the finger's-width door the river went \lore{water over water}, a low thing over a lower thing; overhead \place{the boards} kept four nails on the near board, four on the next and a thumb-width knot where the fifth nail should have stood. Agreeing any two would want a payer put under both and none stood to any, so the three stayed where they were, exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air. Six hearings of \lore{the full note} stood beside and never among, five from four sources and the sixth, the small flat knock out of a woman as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go and never with the things that came from vessels; the seventh place kept its emptiness and none was invented to fill it, for an empty place kept empty is a finding and a finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged. The bell's four, four and a gap, heard at \place{the Cross} on Frost 10, stayed in the square beside the told bell of \lore{the Long Night}, not folded into the three and not heard as a seventh of the full note, but a heard bell beside a told bell.

\person{Sella Vail} carried him up at the going of the light at the half-pace she kept from every open slate, and set him on \place{the bed} under the roof and not in \place{the Debt Loft}. She put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face, and left the thread frozen where it lay, because nobody had told her about the thread and she had told nobody about it either. At \place{the kindling} \person{Tarn} kept six where the house could check and the seventh at the very lip of the sill where it could not, with \person{Garl Vail}'s unforged collector sentence lying beside it --- no name on the man who turned back at the frozen ford, no figure on the debt. He told the wood \emph{Six counted. The next I don't count} in the tone of a man stating the weather, and said \emph{Yes, house} to those who wanted the thaw early and to those who wanted it late, agreeing with both so thoroughly that no argument could find a foot and keeping the late because it puts off the paying, though the paying stood in the ruts. The sentence about an ear under this roof being worth more than the debt that held it came to his teeth and went as far as the word that would call the loan, and stopped there with no figure and no name --- a remembered ninth stopping, with no tenth before the seventh year at its very end and still ungiven. He drew the breath that should have gone in at the top of the reach and did not spend it, and his chest was tight as it had been since the dark. \lore{Ash} was worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line and no scrubbing lifted it, and the pale clip-ring at his left wrist held what light there was and gave it back. Up in the Debt Loft, which came to the boy only as a sound with a direction and never as a room he was taken into, the warm stone in its unmarked pot was cold and unreconciled by not trying, which is not forgiveness.

\person{Jorra Vail} came through from \place{the Vail wheel-shed} with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal into both forearms past the bent little finger and under the nails, not bone-coloured slip, and he kept dusty forearms and clean ones on one tray and asked neither which slate had been broken nor whether it was a birth-slate. He spoke to the wheel before he spoke to any person --- \emph{It doesn't care whose hands. He knew the full note that pot would give three turns before it left his hands, with air under it and a want in it, and withheld the knowing on the near-full tray beside the hearings and never among them, advanced and not paid. Before Sella had finished telling him the fire was low his bent finger went into the cold iron of the damper ahead of her unfinished sentence, moved the tongue a hair's breadth and left it standing, so that the end of her instruction arrived at the pot and the household straightened around his silence the way cloth straightens when a weight is lifted.
