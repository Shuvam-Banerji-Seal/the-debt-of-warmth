## Chronicle

The sixteenth day of Frost came on in its own light. Under the Vail kiln-house roof in Low Kiln the boy lay carried and awake, two hundred and eighty-five days old by a count nobody kept, with one barley crust two inches from his ear and the empty place beside it unnamed. He took the body in the order the facts arrived — crust, gap, thread frozen in the dry heat and unwiped, cold stopped at cheek, temple and the bare wrist-joint, the afterdrop waiting past a warming nobody would perform. He knew a cold patient cannot clot and must be warmed slowly from the outside; the knowing bought him nothing at two inches of air. He wanted the crust lifted and could not close two inches on the pot handle. Both were true, and the second was louder.

Sella went to the fire before the basket and said *The house will keep it* where another woman would have said *I*. She turned the coals with a broken pot, kept the charcoal unread, set the pot rim two inches beyond the reaching hand, turned the dressed grain and gave it no figure, and left the widow's mark untouched, for none of these is an opening. The yard went around its seven walked-around places with a look and a wide step and no number — the two unswept lines, the broken birth-slates, the pan's gone ring, the skinned trough, the field's empty place and the returning-line hand. The stayed frost lay white on the well lip and the plate of ice stood clouded with silt, and the yard called both weather.

On the seventeenth Ilva drew water at the well with the pail against her knee, clay pale in her nail crescents and rack dust in one thumb seam. To the woman who asked what the month's firings had taken, now the district's arithmetic had changed where two hundred stood, her hand went toward her sleeve and stayed there, and she answered: *Ask the pot what it cost. I keep the count, not the account.* The boy read her order of reaching and filed no tenth hand; the birch stayed in her sleeve.

On the eighteenth the file was turned out on the towel in the small hours as instruments on a tray — six hearings with no seventh, five askings with no witness, nine stoppings, nine hands, each on its own place with a finger between. The hand took the held interval and the empty interval, set them side by side and came down on the river's low note. One hundred and fifty answers stood beside one hundred and forty-nine, exact and worth nothing at two inches of air; a round number did not stand up as a doctrine, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood. The hand laid back against the fold, 287 stood exact and spoken by nobody, and the river went quiet. Nothing was opened, named, called, paid or ended.

## Continuity

- **Exact final moment:** Day 287, Frost 18, Eighthday, the small hours under the Vail kiln-house roof. The boy lies on his bed under the roof (never the Debt Loft) with the fold at his ear, one crust two inches off and its empty place unnamed, the thread frozen and unwiped, the hand laid back against the fold on the hundred and fiftieth answer. The file lies open on the towel with every gap left gap and every tray parted by a finger's width. Beyond the door the frost lies white on the well lip, called weather, and the plate of ice stands clouded with silt on its flat stone. The river's low note goes quiet after the hand comes down. No other character is on stage at the close; the chapter ends on continuation, not a drawn line.

- **Day and time at the end:** Day 287, small hours, six months and one hundred and seven days, told by nobody. Days 285 (Frost 16, Sixthday), 286 (Frost 17, Seventhday) and 287 (Frost 18, Eighthday) were each opened by their own dated `\daymark` and lived in two full scenes. The carried fire's fifth is unpaid 287 days with its payer unnamed. Frost 20 (Day 289), the second Account-day and market, stands two mornings beyond the close and was not brought into any unearned day.

- **Corin/Dan:** carried everywhere, awake, silent, cannot roll, stand, speak, read Grathic, reach a cup, close two inches on the basket rim, lift a crust to his mouth. Cold stops at cheek, temple and the bare wrist-joint; the afterdrop waits past a warming nobody will perform. The cracked low-capacity vessel is unchanged, below Thin, twelve to fifteen embers, flaw unnamed, rate of loss unmeasured. The only movement is the hand up an inch toward a sound. He wants the crust lifted and the thread wiped and gets neither. No rank, no power, no experiment, no new cast to any spell.

- **Where everyone is and wants:** Sella Vail, keeper of the fire, goes to it before the basket, says *The house will keep it*, keeps the charcoal unread and the pot rim two inches out, leaves the thread frozen, never touches the widow's mark except before a kiln (last on Frost 2), and carries him up at the going of the light; she wants the house kept whole by not-doings. Tarn, at the kindling, keeps six checkable and a seventh at the lip beside Garl's unforged sentence, stops the ear-sentence at the word that would call the loan (a remembered ninth stopping, no tenth), chest tight, Seed 16 breath unspent, Ash in the knuckles, pale clip-ring at the wrist; he wants the paying put off. Jorra Vail, at the wheel-shed edge, parts dusty forearms from clean, speaks to the wheel and a pot before any person, withholds the note that pot will give three turns ahead, and puts his bent finger into the cold damper ahead of Sella's sentence; *he's not Vail* stays sealed and the promise of the boy's name is neither given nor withdrawn. Garl Vail stays in objects — salt-stiff coat, wheel-seat with its hollow, clean dark line and finer grey beside it, the tile debt worse for being uncalled, his name unspoken 287 days. Ilva is glimpsed once on Frost 17 at the well and wants her count kept in wood where no slate can take a figure off it; she does not know she was read. Unnamed men go low into the back room before first grey (a direction and a sound only) and a woman with a pot puts the question to Ilva. Berr, Hessa, the soft-cuffed clerk, the green-mouthed woman and the foundling stay offstage.

- **Items, money, powers:** Nothing gained, lost, bought, sold or bonded; no money at any point and no slate opened within reach of the basket. The printed one in twelve stands unchallenged, read four times over the basket (Smoke 10, 20, 30 and Frost 10) and not again; the figure read is still not the figure paid. Coals turned with the back of a broken pot; grain turned beneath wool with no figure; pot rim set two inches out. No usable power was used; the six additions to the held inch are honest hand-coordinations, not abilities.

- **Facts, lies, promises, debts:** No new fact is revealed and no lie told. Ilva reaffirms that she keeps the count, not the account, and gives no figure. The Frost 14 well-saying is held as one saying with **no second shaping** on any of these three days, no witness, no confession, no joining, not paid, never making Hessa a reader. Garl's tile debt and Tarn's loan remain uncalled; Jorra's promise remains ungiven. No payer is named for any of the kept things.

- **Threads advanced, none paid:** T071/T072 and T088 (count 284 → 287, answers 145–150, fire's fifth unpaid); T061/T082 (the sixth place — 145 one house rhythm carried across the river's interval; 146 two intakes held apart; 147 intake held through the silence; 148 bridge laid down as an object; 149 letting-go whole on the same note; 150 held interval and empty interval side by side — a round number held as an object and **not a doctrine**); T080 (nine hands, Ilva's ninth unread; well-saying held, not paid); T058/T079 and T100 (widow's mark, four openings not a rule, charcoal unread); T083/T085 held (stayed frost, plate of ice); T078 (heard bell beside told); T101 (five askings, one shaping, no witness); T036/T076 held (nine stoppings, no tenth); T006, T035/T031/T062, T065/T095, T064 (damper's double bite, Jorra's dust and withheld note, burned slate at four, two shut-room tellings, three pot sayings with the Smoke 14 note apart); T015/T041/T047 and T077 (Frost's arithmetic in Berr's box, grain unfigured); T060/T086, T009/T013/T092, T097 held, T098 carried, T050/T051, T033/T034.

- **Foreshadowing planted / held for later:** Frost 20 (Day 289) is kept two mornings off and unbrought. The second shaping of the well-saying (a voice over the boards asking what a runner carries in a slate that has a name in it) is reserved for Chapter 79, still not a confession, joining or payment. The covered grain, Frost's locked subtraction, Ilva's notched strip, the charcoal under the shelf and the plate of ice all wait unread and unfigured. No new thread was opened and none was closed.

- **What Chapter 79 must not forget:** Opens Day 288, Frost 19, Ninthday, small hours, hand laid down on the hundred and fiftieth answer, count at 287 exact, six months and one hundred and seven days, fire's fifth unpaid the same number of days. Frost 19 is an ordinary tending day with no market, Cross or bell; Frost 20 stands one morning ahead and is not brought early. The hand answers six times (151–156) and the count reaches 290 exact. A second shaping of the Frost 14 well-saying may gather beside the first with a finger between, never making Hessa a reader. Do not let a round number, four months' openings or a hundred and fifty-six hands stand up as a doctrine, and do not open, join, name, call, pay or end anything on an unearned day.
