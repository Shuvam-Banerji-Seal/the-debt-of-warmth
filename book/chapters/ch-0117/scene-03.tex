\daymark{403}{Ilmen 9, Y.H. 814, Ninthday}

The small hours came on without being announced, the way a draught comes under a door that nobody has opened, and the hand lay where it had been laid, flat against the fold, with answer three hundred and eighty under it and four hundred and two days behind it exact. Ninthday was the working name of this morning and nothing under \place{the Vail kiln-house} made a threshold of it or a signing. \person[Corin]{The boy} lay at the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf}, where the heat came to him sideways and not where he would have chosen, and the dry kiln heat had gone deep into the wool with woodsmoke and tallow, and below those the faint sour tang of \lore{Ash} lifting out of \place{the Vail corner} where no broom had gone in two years. Under \place{the boards} \lore{the carried fire} gave four low pulls and kept the silence where the fifth should have stood, and nobody lifted the floor to supply what was wanting below it. The house kept its days badly, in loaves pushed along a shelf and glances at a jar and a space at the row's end nobody called a number. He kept them exactly. The two keepings occupied the same room and did not touch, and his exactness bought him nothing, which was how he knew it was not peace.

The surgeon took the body in the order it arrived, because a field read out of order is a field read wrong. The barley crust had gone harder through the dry nights, leathery and brown-edged, two inches from his ear with its corner standing a hair off the wool; beside it the empty place held the fold put there on the fifteenth, found where put again in the dark and not made again, with no second fold, no third crust and no name spoken over the place. \lore{The keeping at the fold} was a finding however many hands found it, and a hand that finds is not a hand that places; a habit wants a hand that placed twice and a way wants a third, and both want a payer standing where none stood. The thread of drool at his mouth had gone stiffer in the dry heat and was unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread. Cold had come to cheek and temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, and stopped there as though it had reached the end of a line written out for it; \lore{the Afterdrop} waited past a warming nobody in this house would perform. A face composed by cold is not composure. Pink returning is the surface still lying over a cold core, and a cold patient cannot clot, and the rewarming he would choose is a basket set level out of the draught where the heat comes sideways, slow and from the outside, never hurried and never rubbed, and he could not ask for it. He had once stood at a bay door and sorted twenty people by what they would not survive, and the knowledge of how a body gives up heat and takes it back, extremities before core, surface before middle, was of the same order and as useless here, for there was no hand two paces long to put any of it to work.

Under the knowing the infant wanted three things and would not be argued out of them: the crust lifted two inches, the thread wiped away, a hand on the pot board's handle. Both registers were true and the second was louder and neither won, and the quarrel between them was the whole of the work, going on in a body that could not hold up its own head.

First light came thin through \place{the finger's-width door}, not arriving so much as being allowed. \person{Sella Vail} came to \place{the hearth} before she came to the basket, as she had come at the turning of every dark since the year turned and before that. “It's the thaw,” she said to the coals, in the voice another woman would have used for a child who could not hear her, and turned them with the back of a broken pot so the small red eye under the ash showed and shut and showed again. “The house will keep it,” she said to the pot, where another woman would have said I. She stood a moment with her hands gone still, taking a small intake, a hold and a letting-go toward no opening, with neither gap figured, the way a woman stands over a wound she has dressed and will not look at twice. Her finger lay along the top edge of the loaf shelf so the charcoal tally underneath stayed unread; her lips moved on the two gaps in the loaf row and gave nobody a figure. A morning was not an opening, and she made one out of it no more than she made a third crust out of the fold.

On \place{the pot board} the twice-mended pot waited with the hairline of the original break showing dark between the older brass staple and the tighter wire. She lifted it thumb-first where the wire crossed the crack, putting her thumb there before the weight came on the handle, and left the hairline showing. “Leave the crack showing,” she said to the pot. “People trust a thing they can see has been handled.” To the fire she said, “If it's worth mending it's worth mending twice. The second time is the real one.” Both proverbs went to the pot and the fire and never to a person, and repair stayed apart from restoration as a surgeon keeps a graft apart from a cure: a restored thing hides the hand that handled it, and a mended thing leaves that hand showing for anybody who cares to look. The pot gave back its flat note as she set it down, the note of a thing that carries its break and goes on holding, and she had never once called it mended. Repair that holds mends nothing anybody will name, and no figure went over the hairline and no proverb was offered to the basket.

She turned the dressed grain under wool at \place{the rack end} with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child, and no figure went over it and no handful was lifted. The arithmetic that would one day be read over that covered grain would not know what it cost, and the space between the figure and the price would have no signature. Then she set the pot rim two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat and no look toward \place{the bed}. The half-beat was the distance between the hand's intention and the hand's arrest, bought at nothing and not a gift. The instruction went to the wool, \place{the river} wind, the pot, the fire and the banked fire, and never to the basket. \lore{The winter skin} lay where the cold had put it, found where put and not spent on a Ninthday morning, and \lore{the three coverings} stayed weather entered nowhere.

She carried him down the nineteen steps at the half-pace she keeps from every open slate, and went wide around the seven walked-around places with a look and a wide step and no number, once and not twice, widest around the returning-line place where a hand went in on Seed 15 and did not come out. The look stayed the length of a step before it went off the place; the wide step was a keeping and not a sign, and he gave it no name, no cause and no figure, for a figure would want a payer standing and none stood. She set him under the roof and never in \place{the Debt Loft}, and put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face, and added no second fold. At her throat \lore{the widow's mark} hung where it hung, a thumb-length of lead-glazed clay oiled by ten thousand working thumbs, and a morning, a hearth, a shelf and a basket were not openings; seven openings in seven months were seven and not a rule, and no eighth was made due under a roof that had done nothing but keep a fire.

\scenebreak

Out in the yard a wrong wind came off \place{High Kiln} and crossed the boards with the sweet edge of \lore{lead glaze} on it, and went out again on the wind that brought it. There was no hand at any kiln under that edge, no name standing beneath it, no figure and no errand; it arrived nowhere and departed to the same place. The boy took it as weather and filed it as weather, on a place of its own beside \person{Berr Clay}'s green at his own mouth and the greens at past market pot boards, with a finger between every pair. No eighth green came of it, no name was laid under the colour, no hearing made of a taste on a wind and no cause made of a direction. A source is an account that has found a payer, and none stood beyond the wall; the wind had brought the edge and the wind had taken it, and that was the whole of what had happened. He gave it no standing and it bought him no lesson. The arithmetic that would be read on the morrow was not brought into this morning: it stood one morning off as a reset and not a signing, and he did not stand it up as a threshold or lay twelve old findings across a figure not yet read to make a row come out even, for a row made even by a hand that has no key is made even on paper and paper is not a payer. No door was drawn across the tend, and the morning was left as it had been found.

At \place{the kindling} \person{Tarn} took up the broom only after it had been given its instruction. Sella had told \place{the flagstones} they wanted sweeping, which is a thing a woman says to stone when she has a man within earshot and does not want to give him an order he could carry to a slate; he said “Yes, house,” to the question and to the thing behind it, agreeing so thoroughly that the question had nowhere left to put a foot, and swept the same shallow bright patch he had swept the day before because it was the patch the house could check. Then he went back to the wood. Six pieces stood in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check and the seventh at the lip beyond it, beside \person{Garl Vail}'s unforged collector sentence with no name on the man who turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on \lore{the tile debt}. The small knife lay at the very edge of its sill and his hand went over blade and sill as a man passes a hand over a tool put back where it belongs, and came away with no breath held at the top of the reach. The coming away was the whole of the checking. He told the wood in the tone of a man stating the weather, “Six counted. The next I don't count,” and cut nothing, counted nothing toward the house's keeping and spent nothing.

He did not turn his head toward \place{the out-of-line landing} below \place{the north bridge}, where the soft-cuffed clerk was set with his slate turned inward for a measure nobody called, hand to the record before eyes to the landing, calling nothing and writing no name, a shape brought up by word and seen by nobody under this roof. The degree his head had once gone toward it and back to the broom stayed a shape found where put and not made a second time, a habit, a way or a rule. The ear-sentence about an ear under this roof being worth more than the debt that held it came all the way to the word that would call the loan and stopped there, a remembered ninth stopping with no tenth, no figure and no name; the \emph{Seed 16} breath was drawn and left unspent at the top of the reach, and the loan uncalled at the very end of a term already ended. A term ending is not a payment, a forgiveness or a freedom, and leaves a man more exposed than it found him. The fear that he would be entered again for vagrancy stayed his without a figure, and so did the want he did not voice, of a slate with his own name on it and no mark against it. His chest had been tight since the dark, Ash worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line, the pale clip-ring at his left wrist catching and giving back what little light there was, his stroke over the flagstones going without the half-check. He was helpful when he was frightened, and this morning he was not frightened, and the household had learned to read the quiet of him as carefully as the quiet of Sella.

At the edge of \place{the Vail wheel-shed} \person{Jorra Vail} kept the shed's silence without anybody asking him to keep it. He had come through the finger's-width door with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past his bent little finger and white in a fist-crease where a hand had opened, and it was not the bone-coloured \lore{slip} of the wheel. Three or more men unnamed had gone low into \place{the back room} before first grey and he gave the house no name for them and did not say the room was cold; the broken ones under the lowest rack stayed entered by nobody. He kept dusty forearms and clean on one tray and parted them as clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had been broken nor whether it was a birth-slate, for a question joined to a cause wants a payer standing and none stood. He spoke to the wheel before any person. “It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady,” he said, and told a pot “You'll do” in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness. He knew the \lore{full note} that pot would give three turns before it left his hands, with air under it and a want in it, and withheld the knowing from any master come up from High Kiln who would call it early and sing a pot before its time; the knowing went on a tray of its own beside the hearings and never among them. “He's not Vail” stayed sealed as a lid nobody had tried to lift in three years, and the promise of the boy's name made to men who burn slates stayed neither given nor withdrawn, going on gathering interest against \lore{the burned-slate word} at five and the four old directions with a finger between every pair. The burned slate stood at five, the shut-room tellings at two, the row at five askings with one shaping and no witness, the well-saying at four shapings, and the Ash and Slate directions stood where put and did not repeat and bought no lesson. Nothing under the shed was made a sign of, and the dust stayed dust.

\scenebreak

In the small hours \place{the towel} came out at the head of the bed as instruments come out when a case is done and before the next is called. It was rough from the wash and smelled of tallow and the low smoke that gets into cloth when a house has no chimney worth the name, and it lay under the resting hand with the weight of a thing put down for the next hand to find. He went over the field by the place of each thing and left every gap a gap. The crust's gap stood two inches from his ear with its corner standing a hair off the wool. The shelf kept its two gaps, and the gone pan's round ring lay on the board where a pan had stood and a pan no longer stood, a round place and not a hole. \place{The field's empty place} out on \place{the worked strips} lay where it lay. The keeping at the fold was found where put and was still a finding, not a placing. The told bell's gap lay in its square beside the bell he had heard from \place{the Cross}, four, four and a gap, its gap not folded into the fire's four and a gap under the floor, into the boards' four and four and a thumb-width knot where a fifth nail might have been driven and was not, or into the twelve arithmetics behind one unsigned figure; three counts in three places stayed three, because agreeing any two would want a payer put under both and none stood. On \lore{the gap tray} the silence after the name spoken over \place{the Remembering} sat beside a clean voice's missing name and the empty name the neighbours had left in their story, three emptied names with a finger between every pair, none filled, none made a witness or a reader. The neighbour's called-gone grey lay beside the left-in-place ridge at the Vail corner, two states on two places with a finger between, and neither was laid across the other to make a row come out even. Tarn's ear-sentence lay stopped at the word that would call the loan at the very end of a term already ended; Jorra's promise under the wheel-shed lay neither given nor withdrawn; Garl's unforged collector sentence lay beside the seventh notch with the knife at the sill; the soft-cuffed clerk's inward slate lay at the landing and Tarn's degree lay beside it on a separate place; the face of the man from \place{Slate Row} at the yard's edge lay on a further place of its own, a shape seen from a low angle with no name and no interior, filed and not counted among the nine record-first hands. Every one lay where it lay, advanced and not paid, and none stood up and called itself a rule.

Nine record-first hands stood with no tenth and nine stoppings stood with no tenth; six hearings of the full note stood beside and never among with the seventh place empty and \place{the Drawing house}'s small flat knock outside on its own place, and neither the flat knock nor a story's gap nor a told bell was made a seventh hearing. Four rooms kept the same gap with a finger between every pair and no Court room came under the roof as a fifth likeness. Counting was an old-life habit and not a gift: it gave him no key, no slate with his own name, no hand to lift a crust two inches, only the exactness of the field and the cost of it, which was nothing. A man may count his own breaths and learn nothing from the count but the counting, and go on counting because it is the only instrument left him. The arithmetic that would be read on the morrow was not made a door: one reading would be one and not an account, and twelve old findings were not laid across it to make a row come out even, for paper is not a payer and a figure read is not a figure paid. He left the space unsigned.

Through the finger's-width door the river kept its note of \lore{water over water}, a low thing over a lower thing under the winter skin, the sound coming from under the covering and not from the covering, and he gave it no source, for none stood beyond the door. The note had done before any hand in the room came down on it; it ended on its own finishing and not on any decision of the hand. The hand outside the wool came up an inch toward it. The inch was not a reach. He took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole on the note's own finishing under the covering, let it go as the note ended --- neither early nor stayed --- and came straight down and laid itself flat against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. He did not wipe the thread. He did not lift the crust.

Answer three hundred and eighty-one came out and the hand came down. It was exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the twelve readings, the bell's gap, the flat knock, the child's grey fingertips, the mother's cup, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the held breath, the two bites of iron, the river's skin, the widow's mark, the fourth direction, the clerk's inward slate, the face at the yard's edge nor the keeping at the fold. Six honest answers stood on the sixth place, three hundred and seventy-six through three hundred and eighty-one, and not one of them was a doctrine. The count came to four hundred and three exact, six months and two hundred and twenty-three days told by nobody who would have known what a number counted. The carried fire's fifth was unpaid four hundred and three days and its payer unnamed four hundred and three days, and Garl's name was unspoken the same number, the two counts standing side by side without becoming one. Nothing was opened, joined, named, called, paid, forgiven or ended; no slate lay open within reach of the basket, no figure was given for the boy, and no boundary was drawn around the morrow. The keeping was found where put with no second fold, the crust's corner stood a hair off the wool and the thread was frozen and unwiped, four low pulls breathed below the floor with the fifth unsupplied, and the house went on as a house goes when a pot is set down in its place and the fire kept --- forward.
