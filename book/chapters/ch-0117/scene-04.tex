Afternoon came on without anybody calling it, the light thinning off the clay until the yard held no shadow worth the name, and \person[Corin]{the boy} lay where the morning had set him with the day going on around him in the grammar it had used since the year turned. He took the field as he found it and left it as he found it. That was not a decision come to and written down; it was the only work available to a hand two inches from a crust it could not lift. The surgeon in him read the yard as a wound at rest, clean edges, no track of anything going in, and the infant under the reading wanted three things and would not be argued out of them: the crust lifted, the thread wiped, a hand on the pot board's handle. Both registers were true. The second was louder. Neither won, and the quarrel between them was the whole of the work, going on in a body that could not hold up its own head.

The small sounds came first because they did not lie and the large ones were mostly weather. A woman set a pail down where she always set it and the stone took the weight without comment. Two voices passed the price of barley from one end of \place{the wall} to the other and never made a figure of it, the price going mouth to mouth the way a coal goes in a dish. A man counted kindling into a stack and did not count the stack. A neighbour lifted a rake at \place{the Vail corner}, looked at the clean dark line and the finer grey beside it, and set the rake down again with nothing said under any roof. A loaf went a finger's width along a board and stopped, and nobody called the stopping a number. The dry kiln heat had gone deep into the wool with woodsmoke and tallow, and below those the faint sour tang of \lore{Ash} lifting out of the unswept corner where no broom had gone in two years.

\person{Sella Vail} carried him down at the half-pace she keeps from every open slate, and went around the seven walked-around places with a look and a wide step and no number, once and not twice. She went widest around \place{the returning-line place} where a hand went in on \emph{Seed 15} and did not come out; the look stayed the length of a step and then went off the place, and the wide step was a keeping and not a sign. He gave it no name, no cause and no figure, for a figure would want a payer standing and none stood. The clean dark line at the corner lay with the finer grey the cold had put beside it called \lore{the thaw}, thickened in the dry weather, and the neighbour's rake had looked at it and away. The space beneath \place[the drying racks]{the lowest rack}, the gone pan's round ring, \place{the trough} bare and dark, \place{the field's empty place} out on \place{the worked strips} and the returning-line place kept their places, seven and not a rule, and no eighth was made due on an ordinary afternoon.

A man come down \place{the clay road} brought a number up out of a fist toward \place{the yard well}, held it where the light could see it, and put it back into his sleeve; the figure arrived nowhere and the yard went on around him the way it goes around a stone that has always lain in it. A woman sent to ask what a pot cost was sent to the pot and got no figure from a pot, and went back with the question still in her mouth. Costs came as directions and went away as weather and left no entry. He watched the order of the reaching, fist, light, sleeve, hand to the pot before the mouth asked, and filed it on a place of its own with no payer put under it and no lesson taken from it. A figure that goes back into a sleeve has not paid anybody, and a question answered by a pot has not been entered on any slate.

The arithmetic that would be read on the morrow stood one morning off and was not brought into this afternoon. He did not stand it up as a threshold or a signing, and he did not lay twelve old findings across a figure not yet read to make a row come out even, for a row made even by a hand that has no key is a row made even on paper and paper is not a payer. One reading would be one and not an account; a reset is not a signature. Twelve true subtractions rode up the clay road in a box locked with the key gone up in a coat pocket that never came back down, and he had no key. His satisfaction in a field taken as found and left as found cost nothing, and that was how he knew it was not peace. Under it, louder, the infant wanted \place{the pot board}'s handle under a hand and got none of the three.

At \place{the kindling} \person{Tarn} kept six where the house could check and the seventh at the lip beyond it, beside \person{Garl Vail}'s unforged collector sentence with no name on the man who turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on \lore{the tile debt}. The small knife lay at the very edge of its sill and his hand went over blade and sill as a man passes a hand over a tool put back where it belongs, and came away with no breath held at the top of the reach. The coming away was the whole of the checking. He told the wood in the tone of a man stating the weather, “Six counted. The next I don't count,” and cut nothing, counted nothing toward the house's keeping and spent nothing. The degree his head had once gone toward \place{the out-of-line landing} below \place{the north bridge} and back to the broom stayed a shape found where put and not made a second time, not a habit, not a way, not a rule. The ear-sentence about an ear under this roof being worth more than the debt came all the way to the word that would call the loan and stopped there, a remembered ninth stopping with no tenth; the \emph{Seed 16} breath was drawn and left unspent, and the loan uncalled at the very end of a term already ended. His chest had been tight since the dark, Ash worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line, the pale clip-ring at his left wrist catching and giving back what little light there was, his stroke over \place{the flagstones} going without the half-check. He was helpful when he was frightened, and this afternoon he was not frightened, and the household read the quiet of him the way it read the quiet of Sella.

At the edge of \place{the Vail wheel-shed} \person{Jorra Vail} kept the shed's silence without anybody asking him to keep it. He had come through \place{the finger's-width door} with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past his bent little finger and white in a fist-crease, and it was not the bone-coloured \lore{slip} of the wheel. He kept dusty forearms and clean on one tray and parted them as clot from vessel, asking neither which slate had been broken nor whether it was a birth-slate, for a question joined to a cause wants a payer standing and none stood. He spoke to the wheel before any person. “It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady,” he said, and told a pot “You'll do” in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness. He knew the \lore{full note} that pot would give three turns before it left his hands, with air under it and a want in it, and withheld the knowing from any master come up from \place{High Kiln} who would call it early and sing a pot before its time. Sella had not finished telling him the fire was low when his bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper} ahead of her sentence; he took the cold out of the fingertip, moved his tongue a hair's breadth and was left standing so the end of her instruction arrived at the pot and the household straightened around the waiting. One piece of iron had two bites, the holding and the taking, and no \lore{Grathic} word covered both mercies; he took the finger out without leaving the extra holding of Ash 26 a second time, and neither looked at the other nor said what the iron had been asked to carry. “He's not Vail” stayed sealed as a lid nobody had tried to lift in three years, and the promise of the boy's name made to men who burn slates stayed neither given nor withdrawn, going on gathering interest against \lore{the burned-slate word} at five and the four old directions with a finger between every pair. The burned slate stood at five, the shut-room tellings at two, the row at five askings with one shaping and no witness, the well-saying at four shapings, and the four old directions stood where put and did not repeat and bought no lesson. Nothing under the shed was made a sign of, and the dust stayed dust.

The light went and Sella came to \place{the hearth} before she came to the basket. “It's the thaw,” she said to the coals in the voice another woman would have used for a child who could not hear her, and turned them with the back of a broken pot so the small red eye under the ash showed and shut and showed again. “The house will keep it,” she said to the pot, where another woman would have said I. She stood a moment with her hands gone still, taking a small intake, a hold and a letting-go toward no opening, with neither gap figured, the way a woman stands over a wound she has dressed and will not look at twice. Her finger lay along the top edge of \place{the loaf shelf} so the charcoal tally underneath stayed unread; her lips moved on the two gaps in the loaf row and gave nobody a figure. One glance went to the cold clay jar under \lore{lead glaze} beyond the board, and the hand shut half over nothing, the little-door burn on the back of her left hand closing on air, and went back to the pot. An afternoon was not an opening, and she made one out of it no more than she made a third crust out of the fold.

On the pot board the twice-mended pot waited with the hairline of the original break showing dark between the older brass staple and the tighter wire. She lifted it thumb-first where the wire crossed the crack, putting her thumb there before the weight came on the handle, and left the hairline showing. “Leave the crack showing,” she said to the pot. “People trust a thing they can see has been handled.” To the fire she said, “If it's worth mending it's worth mending twice. The second time is the real one.” Both proverbs went to the pot and the fire and never to a person, and repair stayed apart from restoration as a surgeon keeps a graft apart from a cure: a restored thing hides the hand that handled it, and a mended thing leaves that hand showing for anybody who cares to look. The pot gave back its flat note as she set it down, the note of a thing that carries its break and goes on holding. She had never once called it mended, and no figure went over the hairline.

She turned the dressed grain under wool at \place{the rack end} with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child, and no figure went over it and no handful was lifted. The arithmetic that would be read on the morrow over that covered grain would not know what it cost, and the space between the figure and the price would have no signature. Then she set the pot rim two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat and no look toward \place{the bed}. The half-beat was the distance between the hand's intention and the hand's arrest, bought at nothing and not a gift. The instruction went to the wool, \place{the river} wind, the pot, the fire and the banked fire, and never to the basket. She carried him up the nineteen steps at the half-pace from every open slate, set him under the roof and never in \place{the Debt Loft}, and put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face, and added no second fold. At her throat \lore{the widow's mark} hung where it hung, a thumb-length of lead-glazed clay oiled by ten thousand working thumbs, and an afternoon, a going of the light, a shelf and a basket were not openings; seven openings in seven months were seven and not a rule, and no eighth was made due under a roof that had done nothing but keep a fire.

In the small hours \place{the towel} came out at the head of the bed as instruments come out when a case is done and before the next is called. It was rough from the wash and smelled of tallow and the low smoke that gets into cloth when a house has no chimney worth the name, and it lay under the resting hand with the weight of a thing put down for the next hand to find. He went over the field by the place of each thing and left every gap a gap. The crust's gap stood two inches from his ear with its corner standing a hair off the wool; beside it the empty place held \lore{the keeping at the fold}, found where put and still a finding, not a placing, with no second fold, no third crust, no name spoken over the place and no payer. The shelf kept its two gaps, and the gone pan's round ring lay on the board where a pan had stood and a pan no longer stood, a round place and not a hole. The told bell's gap lay in its square beside the bell he had heard from \place{the Cross}, four, four and a gap, its gap not folded into \lore{the carried fire}'s four and a gap under the floor, into the boards' four and four and a thumb-width knot where a fifth nail might have been driven and was not, or into the twelve arithmetics behind one unsigned figure; three counts in three places stayed three, because agreeing any two would want a payer put under both and none stood. On \lore{the gap tray} the silence after the name spoken over \place{the Remembering} sat beside a clean voice's missing name and the empty name the neighbours had left in their story, three emptied names with a finger between every pair, none filled, none made a witness or a reader. The neighbour's called-gone grey lay beside the left-in-place ridge at the Vail corner, two states on two places with a finger between, and neither was laid across the other to make a row come out even.

Nine record-first hands stood with no tenth and nine stoppings stood with no tenth: the clerk's reported hand on its own tray at the landing, hand to slate before eyes, calling nothing and writing no name; the clean-voice speaker taken out of his own sentences on a place of his own, a clean voice not being a hand; the face at the yard's edge filed beside them and not counted among them, a shape seen from a low angle with no name and no interior. Six hearings of the full note stood beside and never among with the seventh place empty and \place{the Drawing house}'s small flat knock outside on its own place, and neither the flat knock nor a story's gap nor a told bell was made a seventh hearing. Four rooms kept the same gap with a finger between every pair and no Court room came under the roof as a fifth likeness. Counting was an old-life habit and not a gift: it gave him no key, no slate with his own name, no hand to lift a crust two inches, only the exactness of the field and the cost of it, which was nothing. A man may count his own breaths and learn nothing from the count but the counting, and go on counting because it is the only instrument left him. He did not stand the morrow's reading up as a door: one reading would be one and not an account, and twelve old findings were not laid across it to make a row come out even, for paper is not a payer and a figure read is not a figure paid. He left the space unsigned.

Through the finger's-width door the river kept its note of \lore{water over water}, a low thing over a lower thing under \lore{the winter skin}, the sound coming from under the covering and not from the covering, and he gave it no source, for none stood beyond the door. The note had done before any hand in the room came down on it; it ended on its own finishing and not on any decision of the hand. The hand outside the wool came up an inch toward it. The inch was not a reach. He took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole on the note's own finishing under the covering, let it go as the note ended --- neither early nor stayed --- and came straight down and laid itself flat against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. He did not wipe the thread. He did not lift the crust.

Answer three hundred and eighty-two came out and the hand came down. It was exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the twelve readings, the bell's gap, the flat knock, the child's grey fingertips, the mother's cup, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the held breath, the two bites of iron, the river's skin, the widow's mark, the fourth direction, the clerk's inward slate, the face at the edge nor the keeping at the fold. Six honest answers stood on the sixth place, three hundred and seventy-seven through three hundred and eighty-two, and not one of them was a doctrine. The count came to four hundred and three exact, six months and two hundred and twenty-three days told by nobody who would have known what a number counted. The carried fire's fifth was unpaid four hundred and three days and its payer unnamed four hundred and three days, and Garl's name was unspoken the same number, the two counts standing side by side without becoming one. Nothing was opened, joined, named, called, paid, forgiven or ended; no slate lay open within reach of the basket, no figure was given for the boy, and no boundary was drawn around the morrow. The keeping was found where put with no second fold, the crust's corner stood a hair off the wool and the thread was frozen and unwiped, four low pulls breathed below the floor with the fifth unsupplied, and the first arithmetic of the year waited one morning off without a boundary drawn around it. The house went on as a house goes when a pot is set down in its place and the fire kept --- forward.
