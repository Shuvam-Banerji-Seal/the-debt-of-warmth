The afternoon came down off the yard walls without anybody marking its arrival, as a thing comes due without anybody calling the date. It was not the fire that brought it and no hand opened a door to let it in; it was the slow addition of small gains that nobody entered anywhere, a beam holding its weight a little longer, coals kept under ash, the sideways heat finding the basket where it lay and staying. The house held its warmth the way a pot holds water, with care, and with one crack in it that nobody mentioned. \person[Corin]{The boy} lay set out of the draught where the heat came to him sideways and not where he would have chosen, with the crust two inches from his ear and its corner standing a hair off the wool, the fold found where put in the empty place, the thread at his mouth stiff in the dry kiln heat and unwiped, because nobody under any roof has ever been given an instruction about the thread. Cold had come to cheek and temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, and stopped there as at the end of a line written out for it. \lore{The Afterdrop} waited past a warming nobody in this house would perform.

Out in \place{the Clay Yard} the neighbours went about the ordinary grammar of the tend, and the grammar was the only thing in the afternoon that could be relied on to be exactly itself. A woman set a pail on the stone where a pail always stood and did not look down to see whether it stood where it had stood before. A loaf went a finger's width along a board and stopped, and nobody called the stopping a figure, because a figure wants a hand to put it there and no hand had. The price of barley passed from one end of the wall to the other in the ordinary mouths of the yard and arrived at the far end without ever having been a number. A neighbour raised a rake at the ridge in \place{the Vail corner}, where the clean dark line kept its finer grey hairline beside it, thickened in the dry cold with the faint sour tang of \lore{Ash} coming up off it where no broom had gone in two years, and set the rake down again with nothing done and nothing said. The ridge lay where it lay. There was a word in the yard for grey put down and another for grey gone, and the two words were kept on two places with a finger between them, for a thing called gone is a thing somebody has agreed to stop looking at, and no such agreement had been made here.

He took the small sounds first, because the small sounds do not lie and the large ones are mostly weather: a pail set on stone, a rake's rasp on the ridge, a rope settling after a pig had gone past the boards and the boards gone quiet after the market, \place{the river} keeping \lore{water over water} low under \lore{the winter skin} through \place{the finger's-width door}, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming, found where put and not spent on an Account-day. Under \place{the boards} \lore{the carried fire} gave four low pulls and kept the silence where the fifth should have stood, and nobody lifted the floor to supply what was wanting below it. Three counts in three places stayed three, because agreeing any two would have wanted a payer put under both and none stood.

The carrying went around \place{the seven walked-around places} with a look from the basket's low angle and a wide step that belonged to somebody else, once and not twice, widest around \place{the returning-line place} where a hand had gone in on \emph{Seed 15} and had not come back, and the wide step was kept as a keeping and not made a sign. Beyond the last house \place{the field's empty place} held the shape of a thing taken out of a field and not the shape of a hole anybody had dug. He did not lay that shape across the loaf's stopping or the ridge or the two words for grey. Likenesses stood up want a man put under every one of them, and no man stood.

Up \place{the clay road} the first arithmetic of Y.H. 814 had gone with \person{Berr Clay} in the box locked, and the key had gone up in a coat pocket that does not come back down, and there was no key in his hand. He had said \emph{One in twelve, as the notice says} low into the goers-by and sent it twice over the basket, once for the moot and once for the households that came late, and had taken himself out of his own sentence with \emph{your house}, never \emph{I}, as a surgeon draws a hand from a field before the dressing goes on. Something low he had said to the block about kindness being entered, and the block had answered nothing. That was all. The arithmetic was gone up the road and nothing came down it addressed to the basket; there was no second reading, and the market did not hold after the going of the light. It was one reading and not an account, the first of a fresh count beside twelve that stood behind one unsigned figure, the figure read still not the figure paid, no signature on the space and no doctrine out of one. He did not lay twelve old findings across the new figure to make a row come out even, for a row made even by a hand that has no key is made even on paper, and paper is not a payer. Berr's saying about kindness entered lay on a place of its own, with a finger between it and the twelve findings and another between it and the new figure, so that the three should not touch and make a row come out even by his doing.

\person{Hessa Rale} had gone up the road before noon with the narrow slate edge-on to her forearm and the writing turned to her sleeve, giving no figure and not made a reader by the carrying; her eighth hand stayed eighth and joined to none. Below \place{the north bridge} the soft-cuffed clerk stayed set at \place{the out-of-line landing} for a measure nobody called, his slate turned inward, his hand to the slate before his eyes to the landing, reported by word and not witnessed under any roof. \person[Ilva]{Ilva} kept her place at \place{the yard well} with the notched strip in her sleeve, no figure coming off the wood and no tenth hand standing. \person{Tarn} was at \place{the kindling} and \place{the flagstones}, and \person{Jorra Vail} at \place{the Vail wheel-shed} and the cold \lore{damper}, and the man from \place{Slate Row} was on his tray, each kept where put and not made again; nothing in the afternoon called any of them up to stand beside the basket and be counted.

The exactness was the habit of a man with a name in another country, and the name had not gone up the road with the box. It was spent here on a basket that could not close two inches on a pot's handle, on a crust that could not be lifted and a thread nobody had been told to wipe, and on a number spoken over him by a man who had put himself outside his own sentence. He had once stood in rooms where the chart went past the nurse's note to a signature and nobody turned the page back; he knew the order of a field closed by a hand that had gone away. He filed the day as he found it --- exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air --- and the infant's louder want came up from underneath and asked for the crust lifted, the thread wiped and a hand on the pot board's handle. Both were true. The second was louder. Neither won, and no verdict was looked for; the quarrel was the work, and the work went on in silence in a room whose floor does not touch his.

\person{Sella Vail} went to \place{the hearth} before the basket at the going of the light, as she had gone at both ends of every day since he came under the roof of \place{the Vail kiln-house} in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill}, \place{Grath}. She told the coals it was \lore{the thaw} in the voice another woman would have used for a child who could not hear her, and turned them with the back of a broken pot, and said \emph{The house will keep it} where another woman would have said I, standing a moment with a small intake, a hold and a letting-go toward no opening and figuring neither of the two gaps. Her finger lay along the top of \place{the loaf shelf} so the charcoal tally on its underside stayed unread; her lips moved on a two-gapped row and figured neither gap. One glance went to the clay jar cold under \lore{lead glaze} beyond the board, and the hand shut half over nothing, the little-door burn on the back of her left hand closing on air, and went back to the pot. At her throat \lore{the widow's mark} hung where it hung and was not touched. An Account-day was not an opening; a market, a shelf, a going of the light and a bed were not openings; seven openings in seven months were seven and not a rule, and no eighth was made due on a day that had earned one reading and no more.

She lifted the twice-mended pot from \place{the pot board} thumb-first where the tighter wire crossed the crack, and the hairline of the original break showed between the old-brass staple and the wire, a dark thread with a hand's work on either side of it. \person{Garl Vail} was in that hairline and in the salt-stiff coat on its peg behind the door and in the hollow seat in the shed that nobody filled, and not in a story anybody told; his name had not been spoken under the roof for four hundred and four days, and the two counts --- his name and the fire's fifth --- stood side by side without becoming one. She said both proverbs to the pot and the fire and never to a person: \emph{Leave the crack showing. People trust a thing they can see has been handled} and \emph{If it's worth mending it's worth mending twice. The second time is the real one}. Repair was not restoration. A restored thing hides the hand that handled it and a mended thing leaves the hand showing, and a surgeon keeps a graft apart from a cure for the same reason a potter leaves a staple in the light.

At \place{the rack end} the dressed grain lay covered under wool on the cool stone away from the hearth. She lifted a corner of the wool and turned what was under it with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child, piece by piece, setting each down where it had lain, and took no figure from it and no handful for herself. The arithmetic read that morning over grain covered at home did not know what the grain cost; it had gone up the road with the rest of them, and the space between the figure and the price had no signature on it. She, who could not enter a figure anywhere and had no hand to lift a crust two inches, set none against it. The wool went back over the grain with the same light touch, and the space stayed as it was.

Then the pot went down on the board again with its rim two inches beyond the reaching hand, and there was a half-beat at the setting and no look toward \place{the bed}. The half-beat was the distance between an intention and an arrest: her hand had meant to set the pot nearer and had stopped where it stopped, and the distance cost her nothing and bought the boy nothing, and neither of them called it a gift. Under the roof nobody instructs a basket, and nobody instructs a pot about a hand.

She carried him up the nineteen steps at the half-pace she keeps from every open slate, correcting the tilt at every turn of the landing before it had time to settle into the wicker, and the instruction went to the wool and the river wind and the pot and the fire and the banked fire and never to the basket. The seven walked-around places were gone around with a look and a wide step and no number, once and not twice, widest around the returning-line place, and she set him under the roof and never into \place{the Debt Loft}. She put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face and added no second fold to the crust's empty place. \lore{The keeping at the fold} was found where it had been put, and was still a finding and not a placing: no third crust, no name spoken over the place, no gift, no payment and no payer. A fold put down once and found again on the mornings and goings of the light of many days does not become a habit, for a habit wants a hand that placed twice and a way wants a third, and both want a payer standing where none stood.

The widow's mark hung where it hung through the carrying and the setting and the righting of the wool. Her hand went to it before the opening of a kiln and at no other hour, and a hearth and a going of the light are no opening. He kept the not-touching as a finding beside the left-in-place ridge and the called-gone grey, two states on two places with a finger between, and made no fourth likeness a cause.

In the small hours \place{the towel} came out at the head of the bed as instruments come out when a case is done and before the next is called, rough from the wash and smelling of tallow and low smoke, and he went over the field by the place of each thing with every gap left a gap. The crust's gap stood two inches from the ear with its corner a hair off the wool; the shelf's two gaps stood in a room that does not touch this one; the gone pan's round ring lay where a pan had been lifted and not where a pan was; the field's empty place held the shape of a thing taken out and not a hole dug; the keeping at the fold was found where put and still a finding, with no name over it and no payer under it. The told bell's gap of \lore{the Long Night} stood in its square beside the bell he had heard from \place{the Cross}, four, four and a gap with nobody crossing himself, its gap not folded into the fire's four and a gap under the floor, into the boards' four and four and a thumb-width knot where a fifth nail might have been driven and was not, or into the twelve readings behind one unsigned figure; three counts in three places stayed three, because agreeing any two would have wanted a payer put under both and none stood. On \lore{the gap tray} three names had been emptied out and the emptiness was kept with a finger between it and the clean voice's missing name and the story's empty name, none filled, none made a witness or a hearing. The neighbour's called-gone grey stood beside the left-in-place ridge at the Vail corner, two states on two places, with a finger between. Tarn's ear-sentence had come all the way to the word that would call the loan and stopped there, a remembered ninth stopping with no tenth, in a term that had run to its very end and was ended; a term ending is not a payment, a forgiveness or a freedom, but leaves a man more exposed than it found him. Jorra's promise under the wheel-shed stayed neither given nor withdrawn. Garl's unforged collector sentence lay beside the seventh notch at the kindling with no name on the man who turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on \lore{the tile debt}, and his name was unspoken the same number of days as the fire's fifth unpaid, the two counts side by side and never one. The soft-cuffed clerk's inward slate stayed at the out-of-line landing, reported and not witnessed; Tarn's degree toward it stayed a shape found where put and not repeated. Berr's low saying about kindness entered lay on its own place beside neither the twelve findings nor the new figure. The man from Slate Row's face lay on its tray, filed and not counted among the nine hands, no loaf, no slate and no sale passing under any name.

Six hearings of \lore{the full note} stood beside and never among, with the seventh place empty and not dug for or filled, and \place{the Drawing house}'s small flat knock outside on its own place, and neither the knock nor any told gap was made a seventh. Nine record-first hands stayed nine with no tenth, and nine stoppings stayed nine with no tenth. No eighth opening stood, no seventh hearing, no thirteenth arithmetic, no signature, no payer, no witness, no reader and no payment; Hessa's slate stayed unread up the road toward \place{Greybank}, Ilva's strip stayed in her sleeve, the clerk's slate stayed inward below the north bridge, and the face stayed on its tray. The four rooms that keep the same gap stayed four with a finger between every pair, and no Court room was brought under the roof as a fifth likeness. The Ash 5 joining of \lore{the burned-slate word} to \lore{the counting room} shut in the war, the Ash 17, Ash 23 and Stone 23 directions and the fourth shaping stood where put, advanced and not paid, and did not repeat on a day that had earned its own arithmetic. No ownerless voice came over the boards.

The first arithmetic of the year stood gone up the road in Berr's locked box with the key gone up in the coat pocket that never comes back down and no key in any hand under this roof. It was one reading and not an account; the figure read was still not the figure paid, and the district's arithmetic had begun again and could not be undone. He did not lay twelve old findings across it to make a row come out even, for paper is not a payer, nor did he make a doctrine of one. Nothing in the square had addressed the boy it would carry.

The hand outside the wool came up an inch toward the river's note under the winter skin. The inch was not a reach. He took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole on the note's own finishing under the covering --- neither early nor stayed --- and let it go as the note ended. Then the hand came straight down and laid itself flat against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking.

Answer three hundred and eighty-four came out and the hand came down. It was exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the twelve readings, the new figure, the bell's gap, the flat knock, the child's grey fingertips, the mother's cup, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the held breath, the two bites of iron, the river's skin, the widow's mark, the fourth direction, the clerk's inward slate, the green at the pot board, the shape at the market's edge, Berr's low saying nor the keeping at the fold. Six honest answers stood on the sixth place, three hundred and seventy-nine through three hundred and eighty-four, and not one of them was a doctrine. The count came to four hundred and four exact, six months and two hundred and twenty-four days told by nobody who would have known what a number counted. The carried fire's fifth was unpaid the same number and its payer unnamed the same number; Garl's name was unspoken the same number, and the fire and the man stood side by side without becoming one. Nothing was opened, joined, named, called, paid, forgiven or ended; no slate lay open within reach of the basket, no figure was given for the boy, no door was drawn across the tend, and Ilmen 20 stood ten mornings off and was not brought into a day that had earned only its tenth.

The surgeon's small clean satisfaction in a field taken as found and left as found cost him nothing, which was how he knew it was not peace; under it, louder, the infant wanted the crust lifted, the thread wiped and a hand on the pot board's handle, and got none of the three. The crust's corner stood a hair off the wool and the thread was frozen and unwiped; four low pulls breathed below the floor with the fifth unsupplied; the first arithmetic of the year was up the road in a locked box with no key in his hand, and the house went on as a house goes when a pot is set down in its place and the fire kept --- forward.
