# Chapter 117 — beat sheet: The First Figure of the Year

Days 402–404 · Ilmen 8–10, Y.H. 814, Eighthday to Tenthday. Three lived days, six scenes, two full scenes to each day, every day opened by its own dated `\daymark` and never skipped, poured into another or summed up. Normal mode. Ilmen 10 is the new year's **first** Account-day and market, earned by its own morning: one reading is one and not an account, a reset is not a signature, not a thirteenth arithmetic, not a payer and not a doctrine. Ilmen 20 (Day 414), the month's second reading, stands ten mornings beyond the close and is not brought forward. Answers **379–384**; count **404 exact**, six months and two hundred and twenty-four days told by nobody. No present-tense break and no `\memory{}` tag; the white room, the page over the speakers and the hands that stayed in the field go on gathering interest from Chapter 113 and are not spent. Point of view never leaves Dan/Corin; no Court room entered, no collection interior, no Debt Loft entered, no back room entered.

## The pull into Chapter 118

The chapter closes with the first arithmetic of Y.H. 814 already gone up the clay road in Berr's locked box, the key gone up in a coat pocket that never comes back down, and the boy with no key: one reading, not an account, not a signature, not a payer, the figure read still not the figure paid. Under the roof nothing has changed hands — grain covered and unfigured, pot rim two inches beyond the reaching hand, fold found where put, thread unwiped, fifth pull unsupplied — and the boy's hand has just come down on answer 384 with the count exact at 404. Chapter 118 opens on Ilmen 11 (Day 405) with that hand still laid on the fold and that exact count: three ordinary tending days after the first reading, the arithmetic gone up the road behind the household and not brought back, Ilmen 20 ten mornings off and unbrought, the clerk still set at the landing, the Slate Row face still on its tray, Tarn's term ended and Jorra's promise ungiven, the keeping at the fold still a finding, and the fire's fifth still without a payer. Nothing from this Account-day crosses forward as sign, lesson, mending, cause, payment or payer; the saga continues by the accumulation of days, not by a settlement.
