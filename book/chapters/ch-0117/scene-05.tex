\daymark{404}{Ilmen 10, Y.H. 814, Tenthday}

The small hours of the tenth of \place{Ilmen} came down under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof in \place{Low Kiln}, \place{Sill}, \place{Grath}, and the hand outside the wool lay where the going of the light had left it, flat against \lore{the keeping at the fold}, with answer three hundred and eighty-two under it and four hundred and three days behind it exact. This was the first Account-day of the new year and the first market of it; the arithmetic stood in this morning and had been earned by the morning, and nothing from the eighth or the ninth had crossed under the roof as sign, lesson, mending, cause, payment or payer. Twelve arithmetics of Y.H. 813 stood unchanged behind one unsigned figure. A reset was not a signing, and a tend-day was not set against a holy day. \person[Corin]{The boy} lay in the dark with the day waiting on him the way an uncalled case waits on a surgeon --- known in outline, unwanted in particular, certain to arrive.

He took his body in its old order, as a surgeon takes a field off a towel before the light is good enough to trust: the crust first, stiff and leathery-brown at the edge, two inches from the ear with its corner standing a hair off the wool; the empty place beside it holding the fold put there on the fifteenth and found where put on the mornings and goings of the light since, never made again, still a finding and not a placing; the thread of drool frozen at the lip in the dry kiln heat and wiped by nobody, because nobody under any roof has ever been given an instruction about the thread. Three things found as they had been left and none the better for his finding them. Cold had come to cheek, temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, and stopped there as though it had reached the end of a line written out for it, with neither an order to go farther nor an order to go away. \lore{The Afterdrop} waited past a warming nobody in this house would perform. Under \place{the boards} \lore{the carried fire} gave four low pulls and kept the silence where the fifth should have stood, and nobody lifted the floor to supply what was wanting below it. Through \place{the finger's-width door} \place{the river} kept its note of \lore{water over water}, a low thing over a lower thing under \lore{the winter skin}, found where put and not spent on a reading that had not yet been read.

He knew a face composed by cold is not composure; that pink returning is the surface still warm over a core still cold; that a cold patient cannot clot and must be warmed from the outside and slowly, never hurried, never rubbed. He knew that a basket set level out of the draught where the heat came sideways was the careful rewarming he would choose for such a patient and could not ask for. The knowledge was exact and belonged to a man with a name in another country. The body it was spent on was four hundred and three days old and could not lift its own head. Under the exactness the infant wanted the crust lifted two inches, the thread wiped and a hand on \place{the pot board}'s handle, and wanted them without knowing that wanting them was a want. The two registers quarrelled without verdict. Both were true. The second was louder. Neither won, and no verdict was looked for; the quarrel was the work, and the work went on in silence in a room whose floor does not touch his.

\person{Sella Vail} went to \place{the hearth} before the basket, as she had at both ends of every day since he came under the roof. She told the coals it was the thaw and turned them with the back of a broken pot, and said \emph{The house will keep it} where another woman would have said I, standing a moment with a small intake, a hold and a letting-go toward no opening and figuring neither of the two gaps. Her finger lay along the top of \place{the loaf shelf} so the charcoal tally on its underside stayed unread; her lips moved along a two-gapped row and figured neither gap. One glance went to the clay jar cold under \lore{lead glaze} beyond the board, and the hand shut half over nothing, the little-door burn on the back of her left hand closing on air, and went back to the pot. At her throat \lore{the widow's mark} hung where it hung and was not touched; an Account-day was not an opening, and seven openings in seven months were seven and not a rule.

Before first light she carried him down the nineteen steps at the half-pace she keeps from every open slate, correcting the tilt at every turn of the landing before it had time to settle into the wicker. Out in \place{the Clay Yard} the dark still held \place{the seven walked-around places}, gone around with a look and a wide step and no number, once and not twice, widest around \place{the returning-line place} where a hand had gone in on \emph{Seed 15} and had not come out; a going-around on a market morning was not a lesson and he took none. The instruction went to the wool and the river wind and never to the basket. She did not tell the basket what the day was and did not tell it to be still.

At \place{the Cross} the market had begun to assemble in the cold before the top of the morning: boards going up, a rope of pigs somewhere behind the square, the first of the two hundred finding places to lean. \place{The reading block under the moot-post} stood with its finger's width of thin shade on the north side, and Sella set the basket there, where no slate lay, the cold iron of \place{the moot-post} at his cheek, and stood half a pace off through the reading and the bell. She gave no instruction and no reason to the basket. A basket set down is a pot set down and left, and nobody under any roof instructs a basket about what goes over it.

\scenebreak

Eight pigs went over the wet boards and no figure was called aloud for any of them. The assessor wrote what he wrote on a slate and the pigs went on, and the two hundred leaned to the block. From the low angle of the basket the square came by its small sounds first --- the rope under a pig's belly, the rasp of a slate laid flat on a board, the small flat knock of an iron rim on stone, a cooper's mallet checking a hoop. The small sounds did not lie and the large ones were mostly weather. A neighbour passed the bell from mouth to mouth along the rail without making a number of it, and the number that was coming was not yet in anybody's mouth.

By the time the top of the crowd had begun to lean, \person{Berr Clay} came up short off \place{the clay road} to the block with the red wool cord of his office folded square under his coat instead of worn across it and the sweetish green of tasted lead glaze at one mouth-corner; under the hot clay of him was the smell of nothing at all, which is the most expensive smell in Grath. His hands were large and clean. He wiped the thumb twice on his apron before touching nothing, once for the slate and once for the figure that would be on it, held a narrow slate unopened against his forearm with the writing inward, passed a finger over the figure through the cloth of his own sleeve, and let his eyes rest on the writing a breath longer than the writing required, the way a man checks that a number is still where he put it.

Then he read low into the goers-by, \emph{One in twelve, as the notice says}, and sent the figure twice over the basket --- once for the moot and once for the households that came late --- never looking into it. He called the levy and denied it. \emph{The levy is called. It is not called by me}; \emph{Your house wants it again, there'll be a slate at the watch}; \emph{If your household has heard otherwise, your household has heard wrong} --- \emph{your house}, never I --- taking himself out of his own sentence as a surgeon draws a hand from a field before the dressing goes on, leaving the work clean and the hand that did it nowhere in it. To a man who had brought a number up out of a shut fist he did not give the number back: the man was sent to a slate at \place{the watch}, and the number went from the fist to a mouth to a shoulder going up the road and arrived nowhere.

Something low he said to the block about kindness being entered, in the tone of a man setting a cup where a cup belongs, and the block answered nothing. There was no anger in it and no triumph, and no payer stood under it for the boy to file; the sentence went into the stone and stayed there as a stain stays in a board. He went off up the clay road toward \place{High Kiln} with the cord folded, the narrow slate under his arm and the box locked, the first true subtraction of the new year riding inside, believing he had done a kindness. The boy watched the going as he had watched the coming, by the order of the parts: the thumb wiped before the slate was touched, the finger over the figure before the eyes to the crowd, the sentence said and the speaker taken out of it before the dressing went on. It was the order of a man who removes his hands from a field so that somebody else must close it. Berr had done a kindness and believed it, and that belief, not anger, was the part of him that would one day be remembered against him; the boy filed it on a place of its own without anger and without triumph, because a finding filed in anger is a finding forged.

He took Berr's order of reaching apart as a surgeon takes a field apart --- the thumb wiped before the slate was touched, the finger passed over the figure through the sleeve before the eyes went to the goers-by, the sentence said with the speaker taken out of it --- and filed it on a tray of its own beside the other record-first hands, with a finger between it and the face of the man from \place{Slate Row} at the yard's edge that was filed and not counted, and between it and the runner's eighth that would come up the road before noon. No tenth stood up in the empty place. He made no doctrine of the twice-wiped thumb and no payer of the man who had wiped it; he kept the order as an order, the way a surgeon keeps a hand that was in a field and went away again.

He filed the arithmetic. It was the first of Y.H. 814 and not a thirteenth. The twelve behind it stood behind one unsigned figure, and this one stood beside them as the first of a fresh count, one reading and not an account, one in twelve printed and unchallenged, the figure read still not the figure paid, no signature standing on the space between and no doctrine coming out of one. The reset supplied no payer to the old twelve or to this one. He did not lay twelve old findings across the new figure to make a row come out even, for a row made even by a hand that has no key is made even on paper and paper is not a payer. The new true subtraction rode up the clay road in the locked box with the key gone up in a coat pocket that does not come back down and no key in his hand. Berr's low saying about kindness entered he filed on a place of its own, with a finger between it and the twelve findings and another between it and the new figure, so that the three should not touch and make a row come out even by his doing.

\scenebreak

The bell went four, four and a gap with nobody crossing himself, the strokes striking through the morning and the afternoon and longer than a church bell. The heard bell stayed in its square beside the told bell of \lore{the Long Night}, its gap not folded into the carried fire's four and a gap under the floor, into the boards' four and four and a thumb-width knot where a fifth nail might have been driven and was not, or into the twelve readings behind one unsigned figure; three counts in three places stayed three, because agreeing any two would have wanted a payer put under both and none stood. Six hearings of \lore{the full note} stood beside and never among with the seventh place empty and \place{the Drawing house}'s small flat knock outside on its own place, and neither the knock nor a told gap was made a seventh hearing.

\person{Hessa Rale} went up the clay road toward \place{Greybank} before noon with Berr's narrow slate held edge-on to her forearm and the writing turned to her sleeve, the thumb along the grain of the frame and nothing pressing where the writing was, the second strap let out for the long road and dried pale scurf of road-mud unbrushed on the shortened skirt. The small flat correction of the strap and the turn of the wrist came first, her hand to the slate before her eyes to the crowd. To a woman at the rail who asked what she carried she answered the question put and not the one behind it: \emph{I don't read it. I carry it. There's a difference, and the road doesn't care which}; and to the one who wanted more, \emph{Cross to Greybank. Reading goes up before noon. I've got a road to walk}. She gave no figure, read nothing aloud and did not look into the basket, and went on at the pace of a person who has made a bargain with distance and keeps it without thinking. This first arithmetic of the year did not make her a reader; her eighth record-first hand stayed eight with no tenth, joined to none --- not to the twice-wiped thumb up the road, not to the charcoal under the loaf shelf, not to the inward slate at the landing.

Below \place{the north bridge} the soft-cuffed clerk stayed set at \place{the out-of-line landing}, where a fifth step should have stood, for a measure nobody called, his cuff soft from washing and folded back twice and grey from washing, his slate turned inward and his hand to the slate before his eyes to the stalls. He was a shape brought up by word and not a sighting from the basket, reported and not witnessed, and nobody under any roof had seen his face. \person{Tarn}'s head had gone a degree toward the landing on a day that was not this one and back to the broom without a word, and the degree stayed a shape found where put and not repeated on this market morning. Across the square at the pot board a silent woman turned a cup a quarter and set it back where it stood, the sweetish green of taste at one corner of her mouth staying a colour joined to no ear; at the market's edge a small shape in cast-offs caught the light on a green sliver and went dull, a shape with no name and no house. Neither was given a loaf, a slate or a sale. There was no eighth of either, and he never called the colour by a woman's name or the shape by a child's. A colour is not a name and a shape is not a house.

The dressed grain covered under wool at home lay turned once and unfigured, read over on this morning by a figure that did not know what it cost. The space between that figure and its price had no signature, and he, who could not spend a single one of his own numbers, set none against it.

At the going of the light she carried him home, saying nothing of what had been read over the wicker, up the nineteen steps and never into the Debt Loft, correcting the tilt at every turn of the landing, and set him on \place{the bed} under the roof. She put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face and added no second fold to the crust's empty place; the thread at his mouth stayed frozen in the dry kiln heat and unwiped, because nobody under any roof has ever been given an instruction about the thread. \person{Garl Vail}'s twice-mended pot stood on the board with the hairline of the original break showing between the older brass staple and the tighter wire, and nobody under the roof had carried its price into the square or brought the square's figure back to it.

In the small hours \place{the towel} came out at the head of the bed as instruments come out when a case is done and before the next is called. He went over the day by the place of each thing and left every gap a gap: the figure sent twice over the basket and not into it; the man's number that went from fist to mouth to shoulder and arrived nowhere; the kindness said low to the block on a place of its own; the twelve behind one unsigned figure beside the new first; the bell's gap in its square; the runner's eighth going up the road unread; the clerk's inward slate reported from below the bridge; the green and the shape with no eighth of either; the covered grain read over by a number that did not know its cost. Every one lay where it lay, advanced and not paid, and none stood up and called itself a rule. The district's arithmetic had begun again and could not be undone, and nobody in the square had addressed the boy it would carry.

The hand outside the wool came up an inch toward the note under the winter skin. The inch was not a reach. He took a small intake from the room whose floor does not touch his, held the interval whole on the river's note's own finishing under the covering, let it go as the note ended --- neither early nor stayed --- and came straight down and laid itself flat against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking.

Answer three hundred and eighty-three came out and the hand came down. It was exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the twelve readings, the new figure, the bell's gap, the flat knock, the child's grey fingertips, the mother's cup, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the held breath, the two bites of iron, the river's skin, the widow's mark, the fourth direction, the clerk's inward slate, the green at the board, the shape at the market's edge, Berr's low saying nor the keeping at the fold. Six honest answers stood on the sixth place, three hundred and seventy-nine through three hundred and eighty-four in progress, and not one of them was a doctrine. The count came to four hundred and four exact, six months and two hundred and twenty-four days told by nobody who would have known what a number counted. The carried fire's fifth was unpaid the same number and its payer unnamed the same number; Garl's name was unspoken the same number, and the fire and the man stood side by side without becoming one. Nothing was opened, joined, named, called, paid, forgiven or ended; no slate lay open within reach of the basket, no figure was given for the boy, no door was drawn across the tend, and Ilmen 20 stood ten mornings off and was not brought into a day that had earned only its tenth. The surgeon's small clean satisfaction in a field taken as found and left as found cost him nothing, which was how he knew it was not peace; under it, louder, the infant wanted the crust lifted, the thread wiped and a hand on the pot board's handle, and got none of the three. The crust's corner stood a hair off the wool and the thread was frozen and unwiped; four low pulls breathed below the floor with the fifth unsupplied; the first arithmetic of the year was up the clay road in a locked box with no key in his hand, and the house went on as a house goes when a pot is set down in its place and the fire kept --- forward.
