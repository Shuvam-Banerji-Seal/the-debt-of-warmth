\daymark{243}{Smoke 4, Fourthday, Y.H. 813}

\dropcap{T}{he hand} lay against the fold where it had come down on the river's next low note, and \person[Corin]{the boy} set the coordination down the way a man lays a tool on a bench at the end of work and leaves it where the next hand will look for it. It was not watched. It was not named. It was not asked to stand up and say what it was. The sixtieth answer had crossed two rhythms in one inch of air across a wall that does not touch, and now the inch was finished and the hand was only a hand again, small, soft-nailed, outside the wool, with the fold at the ear and one crust lying two inches from it. He had once reduced a shoulder in the dark by feel alone and now could not lift that crust to his mouth; both were true, and neither stood up to call itself a sign. Under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof in \place{Low Kiln} one room kept fire and the other kept river, and neither stopped for the ear between them. The file lay open on the towel where he had left it, exact, complete and worth nothing, six hearings standing beside \lore{the full note} and never among, with no seventh and none invented to fill the empty place. There was no towel, no instruments and no case, only the order of the laying, and that was the whole of the discipline: the sharp thing by the sharp thing, the blunt by the blunt, every edge where a hand would look for it first.

He was two hundred and forty-three days old and six months and sixty-three days, and nobody in \place{Sarn}, \place{Sill} or \place{Grath} said either number in his hearing or over his head. The count had gone forward in the dark from two hundred and forty-two to two hundred and forty-three with no hand to enter it and no voice to say so, exact, complete, unspent. He had stopped expecting to be told, which is not the same as not wanting it, and the wanting went on under the expectation the way a coal goes on under ash. Smoke went on the way a firing goes, without a boundary drawn across it, no stroke to mark the hour, neither of the district's two names for the months set right or left wrong in the telling. There was no market on a Fourthday and no \place[the Cross]{Cross} and no bell. Smoke 10 stood six mornings ahead, the first Account-day and market of the second firing, and it stood beyond this day and had not been brought into a day that had not earned it.

Hot clay came first under the roof, hot clay under tallow and wet wool under that, and under all three the sour \lore{Ash} breath of the unswept corner the house called \lore{the thaw}. The day's first sound was not a bell. Behind the wall the kiln drew in four low pulls and stopped where the fifth should have stood, and the stopping stood where it stood with nobody under any roof to put a name to what stood in it. He took the smells in the order they arrived and the sound in the order it did not, the way he had once taken a room in the dark: what was under the hand first, what was behind it second, and what the ear wanted to hear last. A man who had learned to find the bleeder before the bleed knew that the ear lies and the hand does not, and he had learned it here again in a house where the ear was told nothing and the hand was given everything it could not use.

In the small hours the coals kept themselves and no breathing number stood anywhere in the house. The fire's keeper had gone to her own bed before the dark settled and left the fire to its four low pulls, and there was no fire-sitter over the coals and no count kept aloud under this roof. The boy lay on \place{the bed} at the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf}, awake and silent, with the fold at his ear and the room that does not touch his keeping its own quiet on the other side of the wall. The oiled paper at the window hung slack in its frame in the dry heat and lifted for nothing, and the little light there was came from the banked coals and went no further than the hand. He borrowed no rhythm from the room that does not touch his; he had not borrowed one on any of the three days behind this one, and he did not borrow one now.

He used the three counts the way a man uses three instruments that will not read against one another. Under the floor \lore{the carried fire} gave four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have been, its payer without a name for two hundred and forty-three days. Through the finger's-width door the river kept its note of \lore{water over water}, a fifth low thing never counted among the hearings. Overhead \place{the boards} kept four nails on the near board, four on the next, and a thumb-width knot where the fifth nail should have stood. Fire under the floor, river through the door, nails overhead: three counts in three places, and none rose into another. A man who wanted them to agree would have to put a payer under all three, and no payer stood to any of them, so he kept them on three trays and let the trays lie where they lay. Three instruments that will not read against one another are still three instruments, and an instrument is a thing you use, not a thing you believe. The boards were overhead and the river was through the door and the fire was under the floor, and a man could stand under the three of them all his days without their ever adding up to a voice.

One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from that ear. The empty place beside it kept its name to itself. No third came to lie down there and make the row come out even, because a kindness with a figure on it is the beginning of an account and this house had learned to give without entering. The thread at his mouth had frozen in the dry heat and was unwiped, and would stay unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. Cold had stopped at cheek, temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, as though it had come to the end of a line written out for it and had neither an order to go further nor an order to leave. The afterdrop waited on the far side of all three, past a warming nobody in this house would perform. He had watched men come up out of the cold and go worse before they went better, and knew the shape of it: the blood leaving the hands for the middle, the shaking, the slow mind, the warmth that must be put in from the outside and slowly, and the fact that nobody under this roof would put it in. A cold patient cannot clot and the face of severe cold wears the look of a smile; he had seen both, and had seen what comes of rubbing a man and setting him too near a fire. Here there was no fire to set him near and no hand to do the rubbing, and the warming was put off onto a far side of the house where nobody stood. He knew the afterdrop when he saw it. He wanted the crust. Both were true, and the second was louder in the small hours, because a child's hunger keeps no hours at all and the adult's register goes on being exact while the body in which it sits goes on being hungry. The surgeon's register took the inventory and entered it nowhere.

\scenebreak

Before first grey \person{Sella Vail} went to the fire before the basket, as she had gone on Smoke 1 and Smoke 2 and Smoke 3 and went now on Smoke 4. She gave the instruction to the wool to keep the river wind off his ears, told the fire it was the thaw, turned the coals with the back of a broken pot, and gave the instruction to the pot, the broom, the running water and the river wind and never to the basket, because the basket had not been given an instruction and had no need of one. She said \emph{The house will keep it} where another woman would have said \emph{I}. Standing a moment with the back of the pot in her hand, she counted under her breath toward no opening --- small intake, hold, letting go --- a count she kept toward nothing and had never offered to any ear under this roof. She pushed a strand of coal-black hair back with the wrist that carries the burn, and the grey streak at her temple showed pale off the coals. The burn across the back of her left hand had the shape of a little door, lintel and threshold and two straight sides, and it closed on nothing now as it closed on nothing when she carried a cup. Nothing was priced in the giving and nothing was entered; a cost sent to the pot and the weather comes back with no figure on it, and a figure that never comes back is not a figure refused.

\lore{the widow's mark} hung where it hung on its cord at her throat, a thumb-length of clay under \lore{lead glaze} with the Vail kiln scratched into it. It had been touched once before the kiln on Smoke 1, held one breath, let go, and not touched since: not at a shelf, not at a well, not at a rack, not on \place{the clay road}, not at a basket, not at an afternoon or a dusk on any of the three days behind this one. The hand goes to the mark before the kiln and at no other hour, and the rule held as it had held on the first opening of Tallow and the first opening of Ember. The one opening lay behind the household and was not looked at again, not as a sign, not as a lesson, not as a mending performed, not as a payment. Nobody under the roof said what it had mended and nobody made a cause of it, and the boy did not look back at it either, because the hand goes to the mark before the kiln and at no other hour.

The household straightened around the instruction without anybody giving an order to a person. The door stood a finger's width open with the river wind coming through and the fire's four pulls coming through the floor, and the two sounds stayed in two places. The instruction went down onto the things that could hear it and nowhere else, and the boy on his bed at the low angle took it in as a man takes a room's temperature before he decides where to stand, and decided nothing. The heat of the opening held in the house without becoming a lesson or a figure or a sign, and the keeping of it went on as the fire keeps coals under ash, by covering and not by counting.

The dressed grain lay under its fold of wool near \place{the hearth}, covered and unfigured. In the morning she had lifted it for damp, felt it to the knuckle, spread it thin on the flat stone to finish, gathered it back by the handful and set the fold flat; it had changed state and gained no figure, and now it lay as it lay, counted in glances and handfuls and by the heft of an arm that knew the weight and not the cost. A muscle remembers a load and not what the load was worth; he had spent his life learning to tell the two apart and had no word here in which to enter either. He had dressed a hundred wounds and knew the difference between a fold laid flat to keep and a fold laid flat to hide, and this was the first kind and was entered nowhere. The figure that had gone over this grain in the square at Grain's last market was not a figure this room had entered, and the two were left apart and not averaged. No slate came near the fold and no figure was laid across any of the absences.

\person{Garl Vail} was kept in objects and not in a story: the salt-stiff coat on its peg with the crease at the left shoulder, the wheel-seat in the shed with its twenty-year hollow, the clean dark line where the Ash ridge had turned the corner and the finer grey beside it called the thaw, both left unswept. His name had not been spoken under this roof for two hundred and forty-three days. No collector had come up or gone down the clay road for the tile debt on Smoke 1, 2 or 3 and none had gone down now on Smoke 4; the debt circled without a name, neither paid nor settled nor done with, and worse for being uncalled. His proverb went to the pot and the fire and never to the basket --- leave the crack showing; people trust a thing they can see has been handled --- and went still to a pot with no crack in it, over a corner where the repair had been rubbed out. The keeping of him was maintenance, and nobody had made a story out of it and nobody would. A line left where it is is still a line kept.

\scenebreak

He put the question to his hand the first way of the chapter. The hand outside the wool went up an inch toward the low note through the finger's-width door and came down at once on the river's note. It was down before any intake had settled, a small single motion toward the one sound, borrowed from nothing in the room that does not touch his and held for no count. He filed it on the sixth place with no payer named. It was not a longer reach, not a miss entered as a finding, not a hearing of the full note, not a seventh, not a tenth stopping, not a payment, not one of the held changes, not a key, not a power and not a rule. Sixty-one answers stood on the sixth place now, exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air. Once he had taken a shoulder apart in the dark by feel alone and now he could not close the two inches to a pot handle, lift a hand to his own chin or carry a crust to his mouth; exactness and humiliation were one motion in that small hand and both were true, and neither stood up and said what the other was. The hand went up and came down and the motion was over before a witness could have stood to it, and no witness stood. The river kept its note at the finger's-width door and the fire kept its four pulls under the floor and the boards kept their knot overhead, and he kept the one motion on the sixth place and let the three counts lie where they lay. He had learned to file a thing without asking what it was worth, which is the whole of the discipline and the whole of the humiliation. The child in him wanted the crust. The adult kept the tray. Neither was promoted into a finding.

Two hundred and forty-three stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in Sarn, Sill and Grath; six months and sixty-three days told by nobody; the fire's fifth unpaid the same number of days, its payer without a name; the widow's mark touched once before the kiln on Smoke 1 and at no other hour, not at a shelf, a well, a rack, a road, a basket, an afternoon or a dusk on any of the days behind this one; the seventh year at its very end and still ungiven. Smoke went on without a boundary drawn and neither of the district's two names for the months was set right or left wrong, and Smoke 10 stood six mornings off and unbrought.

The hand came down at once on the river's note. The file lay open on the towel, exact, complete and worth nothing, and the count went forward into the day that had earned its heading.
