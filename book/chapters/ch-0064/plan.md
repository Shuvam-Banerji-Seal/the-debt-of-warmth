# Chapter 64 — What the Notches Keep

Days 243–245, Smoke 4–6, Fourthday to Sixthday, Y.H. 813. Normal chapter: three lived days, six scenes, two entries to a day. Every day has its own `\daymark` in the first scene of that day only — `\daymark{243}{Smoke 4, Fourthday, Y.H. 813}`, `\daymark{244}{Smoke 5, Fifthday, Y.H. 813}`, `\daymark{245}{Smoke 6, Sixthday, Y.H. 813}` — and each is lived in full in two scenes of well over eight hundred words. No day skipped, poured into another or summarised. Weekdays checked against *t* = 336 + *N* − 1: 243 = Smoke 4, Fourthday; 244 = Smoke 5, Fifthday; 245 = Smoke 6, Sixthday. Smoke 4–6 are the second working week of the second firing. There is no market, no Cross and no bell on any of the three days; Smoke 10 (Day 249), the first Account-day and market of Smoke, stands four mornings ahead at the close and is not brought early.

## Chapter-level invariants (hold in every scene)

- **The count.** Opens at 242 exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody ("two hundred and forty-two" in the small hours of Day 243, never as a milestone, never as a number anybody says); reaches 243, 244 and 245 across the three `\daymark`s, six months and sixty-three days to six months and sixty-five days, told by nobody (T071/T072). The carried fire's fifth is unpaid 243, 244 and 245 days (T088). The seventh year stands at its very end and has still not given; nine stoppings stand, no tenth; Tarn's Seed 16 breath is unspent and the loan uncalled; the warm stone is cold in its unmarked loft pot, unreconciled, which is not forgiving (T036/T076, T006).
- **The opening stays behind.** Sella touched the widow's mark once, before the kiln on Smoke 1, held one breath, let go, tipped the carried coal under a broken pot and the fire took, still missing its fifth. In this chapter she does not touch it at a shelf, a well, a rack, a road, a basket, an afternoon or a dusk, because the hand goes to the mark before the kiln and at no other hour. The opening is not recalled as a sign, a lesson, a mending performed or a payment; no slate entered, no payer named (T058/T079, T088).
- **Three counts in three places.** The kiln's four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should stand under the floor; the river's note of water over water through the finger's-width door; the boards' four nails, four and a thumb-width knot. Agreeing any two would want a payer and none stands to any, so the trays lie apart (T061/T082, T088).
- **The file.** Six hearings beside and never among, the sixth out of a person, no seventh and none invented to fill the empty place; three near-full answers advanced and unpaid; three shapings of the pot saying with no fourth likeness made a cause; the fourth burned-slate telling and the two shut-room tellings on their own places; the told bell beside the heard and the told figure beside the paid, with Grain's third subtraction locked in Berr's box; three held changes plus the loft scrape, the pale line and the held inch on six places; the seventh green a colour and the seventh shape a shape with no eighth; the dressed grain covered and unfigured; the pan's gone ring, the shelf's two gaps, the fold's one crust and gap, the field's empty place and the returning-line place on trays that do not touch; the row question on its seventh place with four askings and no witness (T061/T082, T064, T065/T095, T101, T060/T086, T098).
- **The body humiliates him.** One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust two inches from the ear, its empty place unnamed and no third; thread of drool frozen in the dry heat and unwiped because nobody under any roof has ever been given an instruction about the thread and a basket set down is a pot set down and left; cold stopped at cheek, temple and the bare joint of the wrist where the wool rides back, as though it had reached the end of an order written out for it; the afterdrop waiting on the far side of a warming nobody in this house will perform. He knows the afterdrop when he sees it and wants the crust; both are true and the second is louder. The surgeon takes the inventory and enters it nowhere. The held inch stays an honest physical fraction; no answer becomes a reach, a hearing, a key, a power or a rule.
- **The household grammar.** Instruction to the wool, the fire, the pot, the broom, the running water and the river wind, never the basket; *the house* and *The house will keep it*; the half-pace from every open slate; the pot handle two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat at setting and no look toward the bed; Garl in objects and not in a story, his name unspoken, no collector up or down the clay road; costs sent to the pot and the weather with nothing priced, refused or entered.
- **The answer count.** One hand question and answer per scene: **sixty-one** through **sixty-six**, each filed on the sixth place with no payer named, each an object and not a power. The sixty-sixth repeats the two-rhythm coordination of the sixtieth but is remembered, not a change made again. No answer is a longer reach, a miss entered as a finding, a hearing of the full note, a seventh, a tenth stopping, a payment, a held change, a key, a power or a rule.
- **Forbidden in this chapter.** No seventh hearing, no tenth stopping, no fifth asking and no witness for the row question, no third telling of the shut counting room, no fifth telling of the burned slate, no fourth likeness of the pot saying, no third gap, no fourth likeness made a cause, no eighth green or eighth shape, no name spoken over either, no second look joined, no sale, no romance, no calling of the loan, no naming of a payer, no reading of the charcoal, no opening of a tin, slate, box or drawer, no Court, collector or back-room interior, no present-tense Chicago break, no `\memory{}` tag, no departure from the boy's eyes, no offstage death, no ending. Dessa's name and number are not spoken. Marra, Ulda and Fenna are not named in narration. The foundling's loaf and the green-mouthed woman's ear stay years ahead.

## The one meaningful change

A **ninth record-first hand-shape** is seen at the yard well on Smoke 5: a woman of the firing work draws a short strip of notched birch bark from her sleeve and passes the nail of a thumb across the notches before her eyes go to the person who asked and before her mouth answers. This is **T080 advanced and not paid** — no new thread is opened, because the tally holds nothing hidden (it is a count of firings, plain as a firing is plain, kept in wood because a slate would want a figure put on it) and the only thing on the page is the order of reaching. The boy files the ninth hand on a place of its own beside the eighth, a finger between every pair, and refuses to let nine likenesses stand up and call themselves a rule, for a rule is an account that has found a payer and none stands to any. The woman is given a real but quiet role and a new slug, `ilva`, whose sheet is written when the chapter closes; she is not Marra, not Hessa Rale, not the soft-cuffed clerk and not an officer, and she is joined to none of them.

**Exact last image.** The hand outside the wool goes up an inch toward the low note through the finger's-width door and comes down at once on the river's note; answer sixty-one lies on the sixth place, and the file lies open on the towel, exact, complete and worth nothing.

## Scene 2 — Four Nails and a Knot

**Day and time.** Day 243, Smoke 4, Fourthday. Afternoon into the small hours. Second entry of Day 243, no new `\daymark`.

Men unnamed went low into the back room before first grey and got no name; the room keeps its finger's width of open and its cold.
