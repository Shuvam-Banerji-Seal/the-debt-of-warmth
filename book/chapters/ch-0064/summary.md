## Chronicle

Three days came under the Vail roof and went without a boundary drawn. He came into his two hundred and forty-third day, then the two after, and nobody said any number over him. He kept the count like an instrument: exact, complete, unspent, worth nothing at two inches of air. Fire under the floor missed its fifth; river through the door went over water; the boards held four nails, four and a knot. He kept them on three trays; agreeing would want a payer and none stood to any.

One leathery brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from his ear, its empty place unnamed and no third. The thread froze unwiped; cold stopped at cheek, temple and wrist, and the afterdrop waited beyond all three. He knew the shape of a man going cold and wanted the crust. He kept the tray.

Sella Vail went to the fire before the basket, said *The house will keep it*, and counted toward no opening. The grain came out from under wool for damp, was spread thin and gathered by the handful; it changed state and gained no figure. Cost-questions went to pot and weather; a man's number went back into his sleeve. The widow's mark hung where it hung, for the hand goes to it before the kiln and at no other hour.

At the kindling Tarn put six where the house could check and the seventh where it could not. The ear-sentence reached the calling word and stopped; nine stoppings stood, no collector on the clay road. Jorra parted dust from slip and withheld the note he knew three turns ahead; the burned slate stood at four, the shut-room tellings at two. Garl was kept in objects, his name unspoken two hundred and forty-five days.

At the yard well on the fifth morning Ilva of the firing work passed a thumb-nail across notched birch bark before her eyes went to the woman who asked and before her mouth answered: *Five to this morning*. The boy took the order of reaching and nothing else: he set a ninth record-first hand beside the eighth, a finger between, and would not let nine likenesses call themselves a rule. Next morning a wrong wind carried the sweet edge of glaze over the boards, touched the fold and went out on the wind that brought it; one pot saying stood again, and no fourth likeness was made a cause.

Six times he put the question to his hand, the sixty-first through the sixty-sixth, and filed each on the sixth place: down at once, down before the letting-go, held to the river's interval, two rhythms crossed in the inch, one rhythm held through Sella's silence, crossed again. None was a reach, a hearing or a power. At the close the hand lay against the fold like a tool put away and the file lay open, exact and worth nothing; the count went forward into a day unearned.
