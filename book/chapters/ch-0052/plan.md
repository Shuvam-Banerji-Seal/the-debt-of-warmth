# Chapter 52 — working plan: The Third Reading

**Days covered:** 207, 208, 209 — Ember 28, 29, 30; Eighthday, Ninthday, Tenthday, Y.H. 813. Three lived days, each with its own `\daymark`, each present in two scenes and well over eight hundred words; nothing skipped, poured into another or summarised. Normal chapter, six scenes, roughly 17,900 words. Ember 30 (Day 209) is the third and last Account-day and the last market of the kiln month; Grain 1 (Day 210) belongs to Chapter 53.

**Where we are:** Chapter 51 closed on Day 206, Ember 27, Seventhday, small hours under the Vail kiln-house roof, with the hand up an inch toward the river's note and held for the length of the river's own low interval with no count anywhere in the house to hold against, down on the next low note — a coordination with the one rhythm through the door, filed on a sixth place, no payer, no sign. That day is done and is not revisited. The count goes forward into 207, six months and twenty-seven days, spoken by nobody. A word from Slate Row that a slate burned in the night is a direction filed on its own place beside Jorra's dust and the two shut-room tellings with a hand between the three; it is not a cause, not a third telling and not joined to the men in the back room.

**Binding discipline for every scene below:** close third-person past tense inside Dan/Corin's eyes; no recap, no Court or back-room interior, no departure from the boy's eyes; no present-tense Chicago break and no `\memory{}` tag (T004); no new hearing beyond six (the sixth from a person, beside and never among), no seventh, no tenth stopping before the seventh year is earned, no third gap, no fourth likeness made a cause, no third telling of the shut counting room, no seventh green and no seventh sighting of the foundling, no calling of the loan, no spending of the Seed 16 breath, no payer named, no private record read, no collector called, no ending. The market does not get to read the inch. No boundary is drawn at the going out of Ember and neither of the district's two month-names is corrected.

**The chapter's one meaningful change, advanced and not paid:** Ember's third and last Account-day and last market are read over the basket on Day 209. Berr Clay comes up short off the clay road to the reading block under the moot-post with the red wool cord of his office folded square under his coat and the green of tasted lead glaze at one corner of his mouth, wipes his thumb twice before touching nothing, holds a narrow slate unopened to his forearm, passes a finger over the figure and reads *One in twelve, as the notice says* low into the top of the crowd — the figure twice over the basket, once for the moot and once for the households that come late. He says *The levy is called. It is not called by me* and *your house*, never *I*, and goes off up the clay road with the box locked and the true subtraction inside, believing he has done a kindness. The district's arithmetic changes for the third time in the kiln month and cannot be changed back; the printed figure stands unchallenged and a figure read aloud is still not the figure paid. The bell goes four, four and a gap with nobody crossing himself, heard beside told; Hessa takes the slate up the road unread, the soft-cuffed clerk stays set at the out-of-line landing for a measure nobody called. The reading answers nothing for the glaze-room hand carried out on Day 188: no figure, collector, slate, name, cause or offstage death. This is the third shaping of a repeating account and settles no long thread (T015/T041/T047/T077/T078, T080, T090 advanced, none paid).

**Second advancement, also unpaid:** in the small hours of Day 209, with Sella gone to her own bed and no count anywhere in the house, the hand goes up an inch toward the river's note and comes down at once against the fold — a miss under the very conditions that gave the one-rhythm holding on Day 206. The asking is not always answered and there is no failure in the miss. Filed on the sixth place beside the other answers, not a hearing, seventh, stopping, payment, one of the three held changes, key, power or rule; no payer named and nothing stood up out of it to call itself a sign (T061/T082). It keeps the inch honest: it is an object an infant body gives and refuses, not a gift growing toward a power.

**Dominant texture, held not changed:** the household's keeping of Garl in objects at the very end of the kiln month — the salt-stiff coat holding a broader man's shape with the crease at the left shoulder, the seat worn hollow with a twenty-year leftward lean, the clean dark line where the Ash ridge turned with the newer finer grey beside it called the thaw, the proverb gone to the pot and the uncalled debt against a firing of tiles, worse for being uncalled because it can neither be paid nor settled nor stopped. Nobody makes a lesson of any of it, nobody calls a collector and Garl is not collected; his name has been unspoken for two hundred and nine days. Sella's private roll stays unshown, Dessa's cost unentered, the widow's mark untouched since the opening before the kiln on Ember 1 — no opening in twenty-nine days — the charcoal unread, the warm stone cold and unreconciled in its unmarked loft pot. The burned-slate word stays a direction arrived nowhere. The sweet edge of lead glaze on a wrong wind stays a direction, no cause named in Grathic, no second edge paid so soon after the carrying-out and no pot lost. The sixth green stays a colour and the foundling a shape, with no seventh of either at Ember's last market; the sale to the Waiting Room clerk and Fenna's ear remain years away.

## Scene 1 — The Last Working Week Runs Out

  2. The boy is two hundred and seven days old and six months and twenty-seven days and nobody says so; he has stopped expecting to be told, which is not the same as not wanting it.   3.   4.   5.   8. The next I don't count*; *Yes, house* to an early and a late thaw, agreeing with both while wanting the late because it puts off the paying though the paying has stood in the ruts since the water went; chest tight since the dark, Ash worked into the knuckle creases, pale clip-ring at the left wrist; the ear-sentence to the word that would call the loan and stopped with no figure and no name, the Seed 16 breath unspent, nine stoppings nine and the loan uncalled; stroke over the bright patch without the half-check, no collector on the road (T036/T076).

## Scene 2 — The Shape of a Man Who Is Not Named

- **Day and time:** Day 207, Ember 28, Eighthday. Afternoon into the small hours; second entry of the same day, no new `\daymark`.

- **Beats in order:**

  9. Small hours: the file is laid out as instruments on a towel and found exact, complete and worth nothing; then the hand goes up an inch toward the note and holds for the length of the small intake only, down before the letting go, filed on its sixth place beside the others and never laid across them — not a hearing, seventh, stopping, payment, key, power or rule (T061/T082).

- **Exact last image:** The hand has come down before the letting go, the thread at his mouth still frozen, and the salt coat on its peg holds a broader man's shape while the seat below it keeps a hollow no one fills.

The district's arithmetic changes for the third time in the kiln month and cannot be changed back; the printed figure stands unchallenged and a figure read aloud is still not the figure paid (T015/T041/T047/T077).
  5. Hessa Rale takes the slate up the clay road toward Greybank before noon, edge-on to her forearm with the writing turned to her sleeve, her hand arriving at the slate before her eyes arrive at the crowd; to the women who ask what she carries she answers the question put and not the one behind it — *I don't read it. I carry it. There's a difference, and the road doesn't care which* — a runner and not a reader, and no figure comes off the slate (T080 advanced, not paid).
  6. Below the north bridge the soft-cuffed clerk stays set at the out-of-line landing for a measure nobody called, cuff grey from washing and grey again by midday, hand to slate before eyes to stalls; the market sells over him as water goes over a stone that lies where a stone lies (T090).
  7. The surgeon's register reads the twice-told figure and the taking of the speaker out of his own sentence as the grammar of charts and signatures; the crowd is sorted in ninety seconds by a man with no hand to carry any of it. The reading answers nothing for the glaze-room hand carried out on Day 188: no figure, collector, slate, name, cause or offstage death, and the pale line stays on its own place (T077, T064).
  8. Across the square the sixth green stays a colour and at the market's edge the foundling stays a shape; no seventh of either comes at Ember's last market and neither is staged or named as Fenna or Ulda in the narration (T050/T051 and T033/T034 held). The market does not get to read the inch and no payer is named to any of it.
- **Emotional turn:** The district's arithmetic changes for the third time in one square with every withholding together and none of them read. The boy is a chart-reader who cannot enter a chart, and the day's greatest competence belongs to a man who goes home believing he has done a kindness. The turn is not to revelation but to the ordinary walking-home of the market after the reading.
- **Sensory anchors:** the moot-post's finger's width of shade and cold iron at his cheek; wet boards under eight pigs; the bell's four, four and a gap; green glaze at one mouth-corner; red wool cord folded square under a coat; the twice-wiped thumb; a narrow slate held writing-inward; the pale scurf on a runner's shortened skirt; grey cuffs at an out-of-line landing.
- **Threads advanced:** T071/T072, T088, T061/T082, T058/T079, T078, T015/T041/T047/T077, T080, T090, T050/T051 held, T033/T034 held, T064. None paid.
- **Foreshadowing planted/paid off:** The third and last Account-day of Ember promised since earlier chapters is lived and read; it opens no long thread and pays none. Hessa's unread slate and the clerk's inward slate go on gathering toward the far-off hand that will lay the private records together; Grain 10 (Day 219) is held as the first Account-day and market of Grain, where the seventh green and the seventh sighting are owed and must not come here. No figure, collector, name or cause closes the carried man's account.
- **Exact last image:** The basket sits in the moot-post's thin shade while the crowd leans and goes back to its pots and pigs, the bell's gap has nobody crossing himself, and the red wool cord goes up the clay road folded square under Berr's coat with the locked box and the difference inside.

## Scene 6 — What the Last Day Did Not Enter

- **Day and time:** Day 209, Ember 30, Tenthday. Afternoon into the small hours; sixth entry, chapter close, no new `\daymark`.
- **Place:** The loaf shelf's low angle, the hearth, the salt coat and the hollow seat, the kindling, the wheel-shed edge, the clay road at the going of the light and the bed.
- **Present:** Corin/Dan, Sella, Tarn, Jorra. Garl as the shape of the house. Unnamed men into the back room before first grey.
- **Beats in order:**
  1. Ember goes out without a boundary drawn in the narration and neither of the district's two month-names is corrected; the last working week ends and Grain is not named under the roof. The afternoon thins without ending; heat stays longest under the shelf, behind the pot and in the fold where one stiff leathery brown-edged crust lies two inches from the ear with its empty place unnamed and no third; thread frozen and unwiped, cold at cheek, temple and the bare wrist-joint, the afterdrop waiting.
  2. Sella stands with her back to the basket, moves her lips along a row with two unnamed gaps and gives nobody a figure, keeps her finger on top of the shelf's front edge so the charcoal stays unread, uncounted and uncopied, glances once at the clay jar and shuts her hand half over nothing without a touch, tells the fire it is the thaw, turns the coals with the back of a broken pot, says *The house will keep it*, counts under her breath toward no opening, pushes coal-black hair back with the burn-wrist so the grey streak shows pale, sets the pot rim out with its handle two inches beyond the reaching hand, lets her hand rest a half-beat longer at the setting and does not look toward the bed (T058/T079, T100).
  3. Garl is kept to the last day without a story: the salt coat on its peg keeps the broader shape and the crease at the left shoulder, the seat keeps its hollow and leftward lean, the clean dark line and the finer grey are gone around with a look and a wide step and not swept, the proverb went to the pot and the debt against the tiles is circled without a name, worse for being uncalled. No lesson made, no collector called, the name unspoken for two hundred and nine days (T009/T013/T092).
  4. The widow's mark hangs where it hung, touched once before the kiln on Ember 1 and at no shelf, hearth, well, rack, road, basket, yard, market, morning, afternoon or dusk since — no opening in twenty-nine days (T058/T079).
  5. Tarn keeps six counted where the house can check and the seventh at the lip where it cannot, the knife at the very edge of its sill with the unforged collector sentence beside it; told the wood *Six counted. The next I don't count*; brings the ear-sentence all the way to the word that would call the loan and stops there with no figure and no name, draws the breath he should have spent and does not spend it; leaves nine stoppings standing with no tenth before the seventh year is earned, the seventh nearer its end than ever and still not given, the Seed 16 breath unspent and the loan uncalled; sweeps past the shallow bright patch without the half-check and over the road a house sends a man down when it wants a collector without once turning along it, and no collector comes. The warm stone lies cold and unreconciled in its unmarked pot in the Debt Loft and the small loft-sill scrape stays on its fourth place (T036/T076, T006).
  6. Jorra gives no name to the men who went low into the back room before first grey, comes through with meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past the bent finger and under the nails, parts dust from slip as a surgeon parts clot from vessel, keeps dusty and clean forearms on one tray, speaks to the wheel before anyone and tells a pot *You'll do*, withholds the knowing of its note from a man on a wrong wind whose saying stays a saying and costs no pot, and holds his bent finger in the cold damper until Sella's sentence reaches the pot as a remembered repetition, the iron one piece with two bites joined to no kiln; *he's not Vail* stays sealed, the promise of the boy's name stays ungiven, the burned-slate word stays a direction arrived nowhere beside the dust and the two tellings with a hand between and no third telling comes (T035/T031/T062, T065/T095).
  7. At the going of the light she carries him home around the pan's gone round ring, the bare dark trough, the rack space with its broken birth-slates, the field's empty place and the place the carriers crossed, each with a look and a wide step and no number, and sets him on his bed under the roof and not in the Debt Loft, fold right without a look at his face and thread left frozen (T009/T013/T092/T098).
  8. In the small hours he turns out the file as a man lays instruments on a towel and finds it exact, complete and worth nothing: six hearings beside and never among with the sixth out of a person and no seventh, three near-full answers advanced and unpaid, told bell beside heard, told figure beside paid, nine stoppings with no tenth, three held changes on three places with the loft scrape on a fourth, the pale line on a fifth and the held inch on a sixth with all its answers, the burned-slate word on its own place, and the crust's gap, the shelf's two gaps, the pan's gone ring and the field's empty place on trays that do not touch — no third gap, no fourth likeness, no finger of his drawn across them (T061/T082, T100, T060/T086/T097). Nothing in the keeping has been joined, named, opened, called, spent or ended.
  9. Then he puts the question to his hand. Sella has gone to her own bed and left the coals to keep themselves and there is no breathing number anywhere in the house, only the rhythm that comes through the door. Under the very conditions that gave the one-rhythm holding on Day 206, the hand goes up an inch toward the note of water over water and comes down at once against the fold — a miss, and the asking is not always answered and there is no failure in the miss. He files it on the sixth place beside the others, not a longer reach, stronger hand, hearing, seventh, stopping, payment, one of the three held changes, key, power or rule, no payer named and nothing standing up out of it to call itself a sign (T061/T082, advanced and not paid).
  10. He still cannot close the two inches to the pot handle, lift his chin the two inches to the crust or speak a number that anybody under any roof has been asked to believe. The body humiliates him and moves by an inch in the same motion, and both are true. Ember goes out without a boundary drawn; the count goes forward into Day 210, Grain 1, Firstday, and the river does not stop for any of it.
- **Emotional turn:** The chapter closes where it opened, on the exact file and the small body that can neither enter it nor let it go; the one true change of the district happened in a square the boy could only be carried through, and the last living thing on the page is a hand refused under the conditions that once answered. The turn is forward: nothing gained, nothing spent, the month gone out and the count still his to carry.
- **Sensory anchors:** heat stopped at the shelf's edge and in the fold; the brown-edged crust two inches from the ear; frozen drool thread; the clay jar's cold glaze; the broken pot turning coals; salt in the coat and the hollow seat; Ash in Tarn's knuckles and the pale clip-ring; grey slate dust white in Jorra's knuckles and the cold damper; the river's low interval through the finger's-width door.
- **Threads advanced:** T071/T072, T088, T061/T082, T058/T079, T100, T009/T013/T092/T098, T036/T076, T006, T035/T031/T062/T065/T095, T060/T086/T097. None paid.
- **Foreshadowing planted/paid off:** Nothing paid and no new thread opened. The cold foot stays uncured and unnamed (T093/T094/T096 carried toward the far-off child found again); the six hearings stay six with the sixth from a person; the near-full answers, the loft scrape, the pale line, the three held changes and the inch stay on separate places with a hand between them; the uncalled debt, the pressed seventh year, the Seed 16 breath and the uncalled loan gather toward their distant payings. The day's miss keeps the inch an object and not a power.
- **Exact last image:** The hand has come down at once against the fold, the thread at his mouth still frozen and unwiped, one stiff leathery crust two inches from his ear with its empty place unnamed and no third; two hundred and nine stands exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody under any roof, the carried fire's fifth has had no payer for two hundred and nine days, and the fire goes on in the room that warms him while the river goes on in the room he cannot enter and neither stops for the ear under the roof.

**How the final moment pulls into Chapter 53:** The boy lies with the hand down against the fold and the file exact and worth nothing; Ember has gone out without a boundary drawn and neither of the district's two month-names has been corrected, so Grain comes on in the dark beyond the door as Ember came on before it, with no mark at the turning. The count goes forward unspent into Day 210, Grain 1, Firstday, six months and thirty days old and told by nobody; the carried fire's fifth will have had no payer for two hundred and ten days; the knife will still lie at the lip of its sill with the Seed 16 breath unspent and no tenth stopping before the seventh year is earned, now at its very end; the burned slate will still be a direction arrived nowhere, the widow's mark untouched, the charcoal unread, the stone cold, the green a colour and the foundling a shape. Chapter 53 opens on the ordinary morning of a new month with no market and no Account-day — Grain 10 (Day 219) is the first — and the inch asked again as the days ask, held or refused as the body answers. Forward, not closed.
