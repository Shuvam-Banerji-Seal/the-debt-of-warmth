The afternoon came on in the house's key. Nothing in it announced itself. A pail went down on stone where the pail always went down; a latch lifted, shut; a loaf was shifted along a board, and the price of barley passed from one end of the wall to the other without anybody making a figure of it. \person[Corin]{The boy} lay in the basket against the warm side of the wall where the heat came sideways and the wind did not reach, and took the yard as a field at rest. He read it by its small sounds first, because the small sounds do not lie and the large ones are mostly weather.

The man from \place{Slate Row} had not moved. He was not at work and he was not going anywhere. From the basket's low angle he was first a pair of hands and then a face: hands loose at the sides with no clay ground into the creases and no rope-work in the palm, and the patient face of a man who can wait in a doorway without shifting his weight. Nobody had asked him what he wanted and he had not said. No loaf had passed, no slate had been shown between them, no word had carried as far as the wicker, no figure had been read and no threshold crossed. He had no name under this roof and no interior in the boy's field, and the boy gave him neither. He had looked once in the morning and did not look again. A second look would have made a habit of the looking, and a habit wants a hand that has placed twice and a payer standing under it. The man kept his place and bought no lesson from the afternoon; the yard went around him as it goes around a stone that has always lain in it.

The boy refused to lay the face across the other kept things. It would have been easy, which was the danger. Set the soft hands beside \person{Jorra Vail}'s promise under the wheel-shed, beside \person{Tarn}'s ended term and the degree his head had gone toward \place{the out-of-line landing} below \place{the north bridge} and back to the broom without a word, beside the twelve arithmetics behind one unsigned figure, beside the soft-cuffed clerk's slate turned inward for a measure nobody had called, beside the collector's reason and the clean voice's missing name, beside \lore{the keeping at the fold} and the four directions out of Slate Row, and call the whole row one account. They stood alike in one thing only. Each wanted a payer put under it, and none stood. A likeness stood up as one would want a man under all of them, and no man stood. He left them on their several places with a finger between every pair, and the waiting went on gathering interest without being called. Danger that is not named does not therefore go away; it stops being a story and stays a thing in the yard.

\person{Sella Vail} told \place{the flagstones} they wanted sweeping before she told Tarn anything, and he took the broom after the telling and not before. “Yes, house,” he said, and went to \place{the kindling}. He took it as another man takes up an instrument he has already been asked to play, with no flourish in the hand and no reluctance; the instruction had gone to the ground and the broom and only then to him, and he had been included in it the way a tool is included.

Six pieces stood in the worn thumb-mark where the house could check. The seventh lay at the lip beyond it, where the house could not check and where a hand could take it, beside \person{Garl Vail}'s unforged collector sentence with no name on the man who turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on \lore{the tile debt}. The small knife lay at the very edge of its sill. His hand went over blade and sill as a man passes a hand over a tool put back where it belongs, and came away. There was no breath held at the top of the reach. The coming away was the whole of the checking. He cut nothing. He counted nothing. He spent nothing, and said to the wood in the tone of a man stating the weather, “Six counted. The next I don't count.”

He did not turn his head toward the landing. The degree his head had gone on Ilmen 3 stayed where it had been put, a shape found and not made a second time, not repeated as a habit, a way, a rule or a danger come due. The ear-sentence about an ear under this roof being worth more than the debt that held it came all the way to the word that would call the loan and stopped there --- a remembered ninth stopping with no tenth stood up, no figure and no name. The \emph{Seed 16} breath was drawn and left unspent. The loan was uncalled. A term ending is not a payment, a forgiveness or a freedom; it leaves a man more exposed than it found him, and the fear that he would be entered again for vagrancy stayed his without a figure, and so did a want he had never voiced, for a slate with his own name on it and no mark against it. The warm loft stone lay in its unmarked pot above the shed and was not reconciled by not trying, which is not forgiveness. His chest had been tight since the dark. \lore{Ash} worked into the creases of his knuckles in the colour of the clean dark line, and the pale clip-ring at his left wrist gave back the little light there was. His stroke went over the shallow bright patch of the flagstones without the half-check, and the stones kept the place where the stroke had stopped, joined to no cause and no ear. Found where put and not made again were \lore{the held breath} at the knife-sill on Ash 14 and the Bone 26 thumb's resting, each with a finger between it and the next, not a sixth held object, not a tenth stopping, and with no payer standing anywhere.

At the wheel-shed Jorra kept the edge, and the household straightened around his silence without a word from anybody. He came through \place{the finger's-width door} with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past his bent little finger and white in a fist-crease where a hand had opened, and it was not the bone-coloured \lore{slip} of the wheel. Dust ground small is a thing that got where it got by being broken; slip is a thing somebody mixed on purpose. He kept the two states on one tray with a finger between, parted his dusty forearms from his clean ones as a surgeon parts clot from vessel, and asked neither which slate had been broken nor whether it had been a birth-slate. The broken ones under \place[the drying racks]{the lowest rack} stayed entered by nobody.

He spoke to the wheel before he spoke to any person. “It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady.” To a pot on the bench he said, “You'll do,” in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either. He knew the \lore{full note} that pot would give three turns ahead, with air under it and a want in it, and withheld the knowing from any master come up from \place{High Kiln} who would call the note early and sing a pot before its time. A note called before its time is a note spent before it is owed. The boy filed the knowing on a place of its own beside the hearings and never among them, and no seventh hearing stood up in the empty place to take it.

Garl was most present in the shed in the things he had left: the wheel-seat worn hollow on that side after twenty years, which nobody was allowed to fill, and the salt-stiff coat with its crease at the left shoulder on its peg behind the door, which nobody took down. His name was unspoken under this roof, and the silence around the hollow had the shape of a man's absence rather than the shape of a story.

Sella had not finished telling him the fire was low when his bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper} ahead of her sentence. He took the cold out of the fingertip, moved his tongue a hair's breadth and was left standing so that the end of her instruction arrived at the pot and the household straightened around the waiting. One piece of iron had two bites in it, the holding and the taking, and no word in \lore{Grathic} covered both mercies in one breath. The boy read \lore{the two bites of iron} as a surgeon reads a clamp that is mercy and mutilation in the same bite, and gave them no name that would make the two into one account; they were an object on a place of their own. The finger came out of the iron without leaving the extra holding of \emph{Ash 26} a second time. A second would be a habit and a third a way, and both want a payer where none stood. Neither of the two looked at the other or said what the iron had been asked to carry. \emph{He's not Vail} stayed sealed as a lid nobody had tried to lift in three years, and the promise under the wheel-shed, to men who burn slates, was neither given nor withdrawn.

The \lore{burned-slate word} stood at five with a finger between the fifth and the four, and no sixth telling came over the boards in the afternoon. The shut-room tellings stood at two and no third; the row question at five askings with one shaping and no witness standing, and expressly no sixth asking; the Frost well-saying at four shapings and no fifth. The Ash 5 joining of the burned-slate word to \lore{the counting room} shut in the war, and the Ash 17, Ash 23 and Stone 23 directions out of Slate Row, stood each on its own place with a finger between every pair, so that four should not touch and make a row come out even. Nobody repeated them and nobody bought a lesson from them. They were shapes in a yard, not facts with a room named or a hand at a flame.

The ledger was checked in the afternoon before the going of the light. Six hearings of the full note stood beside and never among, the seventh place empty and not dug for or filled. The small flat knock out of \person{Dessa Silt} as the men lifted her stayed where a thing out of a person must go and was not made a seventh hearing; \place{the Drawing house} kept its flat knock outside the six on its own place. Nine record-first hands stood with no tenth: the neat dark hand, \person{Marra}'s, the finger beneath the shelf, \person{Berr Clay}'s twice-wiped thumb, \person{Hessa}'s eighth going up unread, \person{Ilva}'s ninth with her strip unread, the Drawing collector's ordinary hand, the Kindling collector's ordinary hand, and the soft-cuffed clerk's reported hand from the landing, reported and not witnessed. The face at the edge sat on its tray beside them but was not counted among them, and the waiting was never called a sale, for a sale wants a loaf, a slate or a figure passed between two hands and nothing had passed. The clerk stayed a shape brought up the yard by word, not a sighting from the basket and not a witness; the clean-voice speaker stayed taken out of his own sentences and was not made a tenth hand of, for a clean voice is not a hand. Nine stayed nine.

Found where put and not spent on a tend-day were \lore{the three coverings} and the note of \lore{water over water} under \lore[the winter skin]{the river's winter skin}; \place{the river} kept its low thing over a lower thing, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming, and the sound came from under the covering and not from the covering. The neighbour's called-gone grey stood beside the left-in-place ridge at \place{the Vail corner}, two states on two places with a finger between, and nobody made the one pay for the other. The cold foot below the sill stayed uncured and unnamed, and \lore{the second chill} kept its withheld cause, carried through the afternoon and not spent; no silt-leach, cure, recognition or treatment was named over it.

He took a small clean satisfaction in the field as he found it and left it: every object in its place, nothing laid across anything else, each gap kept as a gap. A field taken as found and left as found costs nothing, and that was how he knew it was not peace. Under it, louder, the infant wanted the crust lifted two inches from his ear, the thread at his mouth wiped, and a hand on the handle of the pot on \place{the pot board}, and got none of the three. The wanting and the having-not went on being the work the way a wound goes on being a wound under a dressing that does not close it. He judged neither register. He had once sorted twenty bodies in ninety seconds and decided who would be left in the field; he could not lift a crust two inches to his own mouth. That was a joke with a blade in it, and he found it funny without laughing, which cost less.

At the going of the light the hearth grammar repeated beat for beat. She came to \place{the hearth} before she came to the basket, and told the coals it was \lore{the thaw} in the voice another woman would have used for a child who could not hear her, and turned them with the back of a broken pot. She gave the small intake, the hold and the letting-go toward no opening, with neither gap figured and neither made to stand for a number, and kept her finger along the top of \place{the loaf shelf} so the charcoal tally underneath stayed unread. Her lips moved on the two gaps in the loaf row and gave nobody a figure. One glance went to the clay jar cold under \lore{lead glaze} beyond the pot board, and her hand started toward it and shut half over nothing, the little-door burn on the back of her left hand closing on air. She lifted the twice-mended pot thumb-first where the tighter wire crossed the crack, left the hairline showing between the old-brass staple and the new binding, and said both proverbs to the pot and the fire and never to a person. Repair was kept apart from restoration as a surgeon keeps a graft apart from a cure: a restored thing hides the hand that handled it, and a mended thing leaves that hand showing. She turned the dressed grain under wool with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child and with no figure over it and no handful lifted, and set the pot rim two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat and no look toward \place{the bed}. The instruction went to the wool and the pot and the fire and the banked fire and the river wind, and never to the basket. At her throat \lore{the widow's mark} hung where it hung; a going of the light is not an opening, seven openings in seven months are seven and not a rule, and no eighth was made due on a Sixthday.

At the head of the bed \place{the towel} came out as instruments come out when a case is done and before the next is called, and he went over the field by the place of each thing and left every gap a gap. The crust's gap with its corner standing a hair off the wool; the shelf's two gaps; the gone pan's round ring; \place{the field's empty place} out beyond the last house; the keeping at the fold found where put and still a finding; the told bell's gap in its square beside the heard bell, four, four and a gap in two places and a gap in a third, three counts in three places staying three because agreeing any two would want a payer put under both and none stood; the silence after the name from \place{Weighing} on \lore{the gap tray} beside a clean voice's missing name and the empty name the neighbours had left in the story of \place{the Remembering}, each with a finger between; the neighbour's called-gone grey beside the left-in-place ridge; the held breath at the knife-sill and the thumb's resting found where put; \lore{the handful} let fall back at \place{the rack end} on Bone 22 as one object and not made again; the promise under the wheel-shed, the degree held as a shape, and the man at the yard's edge on a place of its own with a finger between every pair. The three coverings and the river's winter skin stayed found where put and unspent; the cold foot below the sill stayed uncured and the second chill kept its withheld cause, carried through the dark and not spent on an ordinary Sixthday.

Through the finger's-width door the river kept its note under its skin, and under the floor \lore{the carried fire} gave four low pulls with the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer without a name for four hundred days now, and nobody lifted the floor to supply what was wanting below it. Garl's name was unspoken the same number, and the fire and the man stood side by side in the house's keeping without becoming one keeping. The boy gave the missing pull no cause and the silence no source, for a source is an account that has found a payer and none stood under the floor.

The count came to four hundred exact, six months and two hundred and twenty days told by nobody who would have known what a number counted. The hand outside the wool came straight down and laid itself flat against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. The man at the edge of the yard stood with his hands loose and no word passing, held as a shape on a place of its own and not brought under the roof as sign or lesson or payer.
