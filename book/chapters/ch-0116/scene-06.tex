The afternoon came down off the yard walls without anybody marking its arrival. It was not the fire that brought it and no hand opened a door to let it in; it was the slow addition of small gains that nobody entered anywhere, a beam holding its weight a little longer, coals kept under ash, the sideways heat finding the basket where it lay and staying. The house held its warmth the way a pot holds water, with care, and with one crack in it that nobody mentioned. \person[Corin]{The boy} was carried down the nineteen steps at the half-pace Sella kept from every open slate, and the instruction went to the wool, the river wind, \place{the pot board}, the fire and the banked fire, and never to the basket. He was set in the shade where the heat came to him sideways and not where he would have chosen, and the fold was put right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face. No second fold was added to the empty place. The thread at his mouth had frozen in the dry kiln heat and was unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread.

Out in \place{the Clay Yard} the neighbours went about the ordinary grammar of the tend, and the grammar was the only thing in the afternoon that could be relied on to be exactly itself. A woman set a pail down where she always set it and did not look at the place where it had stood. A man pushed a loaf along a board and the loaf went a finger's width and stopped, and nobody called the stopping a figure. Two voices passed the price of barley from one end of the wall to the other without making a number of it, the way a coal is passed in a dish from one hearth to the next and is not counted until it is spent. A neighbour with a rake looked at the ridge at \place{the Vail corner}, where the clean dark line kept its finer grey hairline beside it, thickened in the dry cold with the faint sour tang of \lore{Ash} coming up off it where no broom had gone in two years, and looked away again, and nothing was said of it under this roof. The ridge lay where it lay. The yard had a word for grey put down and another for grey gone, and the two words were kept on two places with a finger between them, because a thing called gone is a thing somebody has agreed to stop looking at, and no agreement had been made here.

The boy went around the seven walked-around places with a look and a wide step and no number, once and not twice, from the basket's low angle and with no hand of his own to take the step. He went widest around the returning-line place where a hand had gone in on Seed 15 and had not come back, and he gave the wide step no name and no cause and no figure, because a figure would have wanted a payer standing under it and none stood. He kept the wide step as a keeping and not a sign. Beyond the last house \place{the field's empty place} held its own shape, which was the shape of a thing taken out of a field and not the shape of a hole anybody had dug. He did not lay it across the loaf's stopping or the ridge or the two words for grey. Likenesses stood up want a man put under all of them, and no man stood.

\person{Sella Vail} went to \place{the hearth} before she came to the basket at the going of the light, as she had at the going of every light since the year turned and before that. She told the coals it was \lore{the thaw} in the voice another woman would have used for a child who could not hear her. “You'll want more than that come the drying,” she said, and turned them with the back of a broken pot, and did not wait for the coals to answer. She gave a small intake, a hold and a letting-go toward no opening, with neither gap figured, and kept her finger along the top of \place{the loaf shelf} so the charcoal tally underneath stayed unread. Her lips moved on the two gaps in the loaf row and gave nobody a figure. One glance went to the clay jar cold under \lore{lead glaze} beyond the pot board, and her hand started toward it and shut half over nothing, the little-door burn on the back of her left hand closing on air. Her hands were never still. When they had nothing to do they counted spoons, nails, the breaths of a child nobody had numbered, and came back to the hairline at the wire.

She lifted the twice-mended pot thumb-first where the tighter wire crossed the crack, and left the hairline of the original break showing between the old-brass staple and the new binding, and said both proverbs to the pot and the fire and never to a person. “Don't set that near the door. You'll pay for it in the thaw.” That to the pot. “The fire doesn't know you're sorry. Feed it.” That to the coals. Repair was kept apart from restoration as a surgeon keeps a graft apart from a cure: a restored thing hides the hand that handled it, and a mended thing leaves the hand showing. The boy had once been paid to know the difference under a light that had nothing to do with this one, and the knowledge had crossed with him without being asked and without buying him anything. She turned the dressed grain under wool with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child and with no figure over it and no handful lifted. “Lie down,” she said to the grain. “You'll keep better there.” Then she set the pot rim two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat and no look toward \place{the bed}, and carried him up the nineteen steps and set him under the roof and never in \place{the Debt Loft}. She put the fold right at his ear and under his chin without a look at his face, and added no second fold. The instruction went to the wool and the pot and the fire and the river wind and never to the basket. At her throat \lore{the widow's mark} hung where it hung; she touched it before she opened a kiln and at no other hour, and a going of the light was not an hour for it. A morning, a hearth, a shelf and a basket are not openings. Seven openings in seven months are seven and not a rule, and no eighth was made due on a Seventhday.

The towel came out at the head of the bed as instruments come out when a case is done and before the next is called. \place{The towel} was rough from the wash and smelled of tallow and the low smoke that gets into cloth when a house has no chimney worth the name, and it lay under the resting hand with the weight of a thing put down for the next hand to find. He went over the field by the place of each thing and left every gap a gap. The crust's gap stood two inches from his ear with its corner standing a hair off the wool. The shelf kept its two gaps, and the gone pan's round ring lay on the board where a pan had stood and a pan no longer stood, a round place and not a hole. The fold was found where put in the empty place and was still a finding; however many hands found it, it remained a keeping found and not made, for a second placing would make a habit and a third a way, and both want a payer standing where none stood. The thread at his mouth was frozen and unwiped. He left it frozen. He had no hand to lift the crust two inches, no cloth to take the thread away, no purchase on the pot board's handle, and the three wants went on quarrelling under the register without verdict, both true, the second louder, neither winning.

The told bell's gap lay in its square beside the bell he had heard from \place{the Cross}, four, four and a gap in two places, its gap not folded into the fire's four and a gap under the floor, into the boards' four and four and a thumb-width knot where a fifth nail might have been driven and was not, or into the twelve arithmetics behind one figure. Three counts in three places stayed three, because agreeing any two would want a payer put under both and none stood. The bell of \place{Weighing} was a bell told from mouth to mouth and passed from one house to another the way a coal is passed in a dish, and nobody said where the count began. The silence after the name spoken over the yard on \place{the Remembering} sat on \lore{the gap tray} beside the clean voice's missing name and the empty name the neighbours had left in their story, each with a finger between every pair, none filled, none made a witness or a reader. The Long Night story's empty name and the place the Remembering did not fill lay where they had been laid and were not dug for. A silence is not a person, and a gap kept on a tray is not a door.

The neighbour's called-gone grey lay beside the left-in-place ridge at the Vail corner, two states on two places with a finger between, and neither was laid across the other to make a row come out even. \person{Tarn}'s ear-sentence about an ear under this roof being worth more than the debt that held it stood at the very end of a term already ended, stopped at the word that would have called the loan --- a remembered ninth stopping with no tenth stood up, no figure and no name, the Seed 16 breath unspent. A term ending is not a payment, a forgiveness or a freedom; it leaves a man more exposed than it found him, and the exposure was his, kept on his own place and not spent on this evening. \person{Jorra Vail}'s promise under \place{the Vail wheel-shed} stood neither given nor withdrawn, going on gathering interest against the burned slate at five and the four directions with a finger between every pair. The soft-cuffed clerk's inward slate stood at \place{the out-of-line landing} below \place{the north bridge}, a reported shape brought up the yard by word and never a sighting from the basket, and Tarn's head having gone a degree toward it and back to the broom without a word was held as a shape and not repeated as a habit, a way, a rule or a danger come due. \person{Garl Vail}'s unforged collector sentence lay beside the seventh notch at \place{the kindling} with no name on the man who turned back at the frozen ford and no figure on \lore{the tile debt}, and the debt against a firing of tiles circled without a name, neither paid nor settled nor done with and worse for being uncalled. His name was unspoken under this roof four hundred and one days, and the two silences stood side by side without becoming one. The face of the man from \place{Slate Row} at the yard's edge lay on a place of its own beside the older record-first hands, a shape seen from a low angle with no name and no interior, no loaf passed, no slate shown, no word that carried. It was filed and not counted among them, never called a sale and never stood up as a witness. The waiting was not named, and the promise under the shed was not laid across it to make a cause, for a doctrine is an account that has found a payer and none stood.

The ledger was checked in the small hours before the going out of the light and was found unchanged. Nine record-first hands stood with no tenth: the neat dark hand, \person{Marra}'s, the finger beneath the shelf, \person[Berr Clay]'s twice-wiped thumb, \person[Hessa Rale]{Hessa}'s eighth going up unread, \person{Ilva}'s ninth with her strip unread, the Drawing collector's ordinary hand, the Kindling collector's ordinary hand, and the soft-cuffed clerk's reported hand on its own tray, reported and not witnessed. The face at the edge lay beside them and was not counted among them, and the clean-voice speaker stayed taken out of his own sentences on a place of his own and was not made a tenth of, for a clean voice is not a hand. Nine stoppings stood with no tenth, and no new stopping had been made on any of the three days of the working week. Six hearings of \lore{the full note} stood beside and never among, the seventh place empty and not dug for or filled, and \place{the Drawing house} kept its flat knock outside them on its own place. The small flat knock out of \person{Dessa Silt} as the men lifted her stayed where a thing out of a person must go and was not made a seventh; her number and her name were not spoken to the boy.

The burned-slate word stood at five, the shut-room tellings at two, the row at five askings with one shaping and no witness, the well-saying at four shapings, and the four old directions stood where they had been put and did not repeat and bought no lesson: \lore{the burned-slate word} joined to \lore{the counting room} shut in the war with no room named and no hand at any flame, and the three shapings that had come after it from the direction of Slate Row, each with no figure, no date and nobody standing to be asked. Green was a colour on a place of its own and the foundling was a shape on a place of its own, with no eighth of either and no name given under this roof. The cold foot below the sill stayed uncured and unnamed, and \lore{the second chill} kept its withheld cause, carried and not spent; no silt-leach, cure, recognition or treatment was named over it. \place{The river} under \lore{the winter skin} lay beside the note of \lore{water over water}, a low thing over a lower thing, and the four rooms that keep the same four things with the same gap stayed four with a finger between every pair, and the three counts in three places stayed three. The twelve arithmetics of Y.H. 813 stood unchanged behind one unsigned figure, twelve and not one account, the figure read still not the figure paid, no signature on the space and no doctrine out of twelve. He did not lay his twelve old findings across a new figure to make a row come out even, for a row made even by a hand that has no key is a row made even on paper, and paper is not a payer. He kept the shape of the lock without asking for the key.

Through \place{the finger's-width door} the river kept its note under its winter skin, a low thing over a lower thing, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming. The note had done before any hand in the room came down; it ended on its own finishing and not on any decision of the hand. The sound came from under the covering and not from the covering, and he gave it no source, for a source is an account that has found a payer and none stood beyond the door. The covering lay where the cold had put it and lay still; nothing in the note asked to be lifted, and nothing in the room lifted it. He had learned, in a life where the cold was a thing that could be measured on a sheet, that a patient cannot clot once the core has gone and that the surface may be pink while the inside is still cold, and the knowledge went on doing its quiet work in him and bought him no warmth. The basket sat level out of the draught where the heat came sideways, which was the rewarming he would have chosen, slow and from the outside, never hurried and never rubbed, and which he could not ask for. He took the note as it came and did not turn it into a second covering or a third count or a fourth room.

The hand outside the wool came up an inch toward the note. The inch was not a reach. He took a small intake from the room whose floor did not touch his, held the interval whole on the note's own finishing under the covering, let it go as the note ended --- neither early nor stayed --- and came straight down and laid itself flat against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. He did not wipe the thread and did not lift the crust. The fold was found where put and was still a finding; the keeping had not been made again, and no hand had placed a second wool or named the first or given it to anybody. The crust's corner stood a hair off the wool. Under the floor \lore{the carried fire} gave four low pulls with the silence where the fifth should have stood, its giver without a name for four hundred and one days, and nobody lifted the floor and nobody supplied the pull. Overhead the boards kept four nails on the near board, four on the next, and a thumb-width knot where a fifth might have been driven and was not.

The file came out on the towel and the hand came down on answer three hundred and seventy-eight, exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the twelve readings, the bell's gap, the flat knock, the child's grey fingertips, the mother's cup, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the held breath, the two bites of iron, the river's skin, the widow's mark, the fourth direction, the clerk's inward slate, the face at the yard's edge nor the keeping at the fold. Six honest answers stood on the sixth place, three hundred and seventy-three through three hundred and seventy-eight, and not one of them was a doctrine. Count four hundred and one exact, six months and two hundred and twenty-one days told by nobody who would have known what a number counted. The carried fire's fifth was unpaid the same number and its payer unnamed the same number; Garl's name was unspoken the same number, and the fire and the man stood side by side without becoming one.

The surgeon took his small clean satisfaction in a field taken as found and left as found, every object in its place and nothing laid across anything else, and knew that it cost nothing, which was how he knew it was not peace. Under it, louder, the body wanted the crust lifted two inches, the thread at the mouth wiped and a hand on the pot board's handle, and got none of the three, the wanting and the having-not going on being the work the way a wound goes on being a wound under a dressing that does not close it. He had once left a job unfinished in a white room and had carried the sentence into a country with no word for the room or the job, and the old life went on gathering interest in the register and was not paid tonight any more than it had been paid on the six days behind. He did not stand the number up as a threshold or a signing; he kept it edge to edge against a house that kept its days in loaves, glances and an unnamed space at the row's end and could not have told you what day it was by any of them.

Nothing had been opened, joined, named, called, paid, forgiven or ended. No slate had been opened within reach of the basket, no figure given for the boy, no door drawn across the tend, no payer set under any of the kept things. The face at the yard's edge stayed a shape on its place gathering interest without being called; the promise under the wheel-shed stayed neither given nor withdrawn; Tarn's degree stayed a shape and not a rule; the twelve subtractions stayed in a box up the road with the key gone up in a coat pocket, and the first arithmetic of the year waited three mornings off for a hand not his and not yet come. The hand lay flat on the fold with the crust's corner standing a hair off the wool and the thread frozen and unwiped; four low pulls breathed below the floor with the fifth unsupplied. The house went on as a house goes when a pot is set down in its place and the fire kept --- forward.
