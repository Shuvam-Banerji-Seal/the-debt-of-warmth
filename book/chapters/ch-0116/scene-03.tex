\daymark{400}{Ilmen 6, Y.H. 814, Sixthday}

The sixth morning opened in the small hours with \person[Corin]{the boy} taking his body in the old order before anybody had come to move him. One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust stood two inches from his ear, gone harder through the dry nights, its corner standing a hair off the wool; beside it the empty place held the fold put there on the fifteenth and found where put so many mornings now that the finding had become the whole of it, a finding still and never a placing. The thread of drool at his mouth had frozen in the dry kiln heat and was unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread. Cold had come to cheek and temple and the small bare joint at the wrist where the wool rode back, and stopped there as at the end of a line written out for it, with no order to go farther and no order to go away. \lore{The Afterdrop} waited past a warming nobody in this house would perform. He knew a face composed by cold was not composure, that a cold patient could not clot and must be rewarmed slowly from the outside, never hurried, never rubbed, and that pink returning would be the surface still lying over a cold core. The infant wanted the crust lifted two inches, the thread wiped, and a hand on the pot's handle. Both were true. The second was louder. Neither won.

He had carried three hundred and ninety-nine exact days into the dark and he kept them exact now, edge to edge, with nobody who would have known what a number counted to tell them. The house kept its days in loaves pushed along \place{the loaf shelf}, in glances at a clay jar, in a space at the row's end nobody called a number, and he did not lay his own account across any of them to make a row come out even. The year's first arithmetic stood four mornings off at Ilmen 10, a reset and not a signing, and he did not bring it into a small hours that had not earned it. Twelve arithmetics of the year gone stood unchanged behind one unsigned figure, twelve and not one account, the figure read still not the figure paid, their true subtractions locked in a box up \place{the clay road} with the key gone up in a coat pocket that never came down and no key in his small hand. He did not ask the figures to stand for him.

The instruction went to the wool, the river wind, the pot, the fire and the banked fire, and never to the basket. \person[Sella Vail]{Sella} came to \place{the hearth} before she came to him, as she came to it before every basket, and told the coals it was \lore{the thaw} in the voice another woman would have used for a child who could not hear her; she turned them with the back of a broken pot and kept her finger along the top of the shelf so the charcoal tally underneath stayed unread. Her lips moved on a two-gapped row and gave nobody a figure. The \lore[the widow's mark]{widow's mark} hung where it hung; a morning, a hearth, a shelf and a basket were not openings, seven openings in seven months were seven and not a rule, and no eighth was made due on a Sixthday. The \lore{the keeping at the fold} was found where put and was still a finding. Nothing from the day behind crossed under the roof as sign, lesson, mending, cause, payment or payer; the small hours took their own body and their own heat and were answerable to nobody.

Before first grey, when the yard was still a shape and not yet a day's work, three or more men unnamed went low into \place{the back room} off \place{the Vail wheel-shed}, keeping its finger's width of open and its sound of low voices. They went in with their heads down and their collars high, and the household straightened around the going-in as cloth goes on holding its shape after a weight has been lifted off it. She gave them no name and did not say the room was cold. From the basket nothing of the room could be seen but the finger's width of dark and the dust that came out of it; no interior of that room belonged to the boy, and no name, no figure, no reason and no witness stood under any of it. The men went in and the door kept its open and the low voices kept low, and the yard went on being the yard.

\person{Jorra Vail} came through \place{the finger's-width door} with the day's meal-fine grey slate dust ground small as meal past his bent little finger and white in a fist-crease where a hand had opened, and it was not the bone-coloured \lore{slip} of the wheel. Dust ground small is a thing that got where it got by being broken; slip is a thing somebody mixed on purpose. He kept the two states apart the way the boy kept clot apart from vessel, on one tray with a finger between, and asked neither which slate had been broken nor whether it had been a birth-slate. The broken ones under \place[the drying racks]{the lowest rack} stayed entered by nobody. He parted his dusty forearms from his clean ones without a word passing between the two states, and the household straightened around the parting without a word from anybody.

He spoke to the wheel before he spoke to any person. “It doesn't care whose hands. It cares if they're steady.” To a pot on the bench he said, “You'll do,” in a voice with no welcome in it and no unkindness either. He knew the \lore{full note} that pot would give three turns ahead, with air under it and a want in it, and withheld the knowing from any master come up from \place{High Kiln} who would call it early and sing a pot before its time. A note called before its time is a note spent before it is owed. The boy filed the knowing on a place of its own beside the hearings and never among them; no seventh hearing stood up in the empty place to take it.

Sella had not finished telling him the fire was low when his bent finger went into the cold iron of \lore{the damper} ahead of her sentence. He took the cold out of the fingertip, moved his tongue a hair's breadth and was left standing so that the end of her instruction arrived at the pot and the household straightened around the waiting. One piece of iron had two bites in it, the holding and the taking, and no word in \lore{Grathic} covered both mercies in one breath; \lore{the two bites of iron} were an object on a place of their own, and the boy read them as a surgeon reads a clamp that is mercy and mutilation in the same bite, and gave them no name that would make the two into one account. He came out without leaving the extra holding of \emph{Ash 26} a second time. A second would be a habit and a third a way, and both want a payer where none stood. The finger went in and the finger came out, and neither of the two looked at the other or said what the iron had been asked to carry.

\scenebreak

At first light she took up the basket and carried him down the nineteen steps at the half-pace she keeps from every open slate, the instruction going past him to the wool and the river wind and the pot and the fire and the banked fire and never to the basket. The yard came up around them in its ordinary grammar: a pail set where it always stood, a latch, a loaf shifted along a board, two voices passing the price of barley from one end of the wall to the other without ever making a figure of it. She went wide around the seven walked-around places with a look and a wide step and no number, once and not twice. Around the clean dark line at \place{the Vail corner} and the finer grey beside it called the thaw, thickened in the dry cold; around the space beneath the lowest rack where the broken birth-slates lay and nobody entered them; around the gone pan's round ring, the bare dark trough, \place{the field's empty place} out beyond the last house. Widest of all she went around \place{the returning-line place}, where a hand had gone in on Seed 15 and had not come out, and the look stayed the length of a step and then went off the place as a man takes his eye off a thing he has no business examining. Nobody swept, named, counted or entered any of them.

It was from the basket's low angle that he saw the man. A man from \place{Slate Row} whose name was on no roll stood at the edge of the yard with his hands loose at his sides, not working, not going anywhere, standing where no work was going on. From that low angle a man is first a pair of hands and then a face: the soft hands of a man who sits at a table, with no clay ground into the creases and no rope-work in the palm, and the patient face of a man who can wait in a doorway without shifting his weight. The hands were loose, not empty of intent and not full of it; they simply hung. Nobody asked him what he wanted and he did not say. No loaf was passed between them, no slate was shown, no word carried as far as the basket, no figure was read and no threshold was crossed. He had no name under this roof and no interior in the boy's field; he was a shape seen and not a witness standing.

The yard went on around him. A woman set a pail down where she always set it. A man counted kindling into a stack and did not count the stack. The man at the edge kept his place and bought no lesson from the morning.

The boy filed the face on a place of its own. It went beside the older record-first hands, each on its own tray with a finger between: the neat dark hand, \person{Marra}'s, the finger beneath the shelf, \person[Berr Clay]'s twice-wiped thumb, \person{Hessa}'s eighth going up unread, \person{Ilva}'s ninth with her strip unread, the \place{the Drawing house} collector's ordinary hand, the Kindling collector's ordinary hand, the soft-cuffed clerk's reported hand from \place{the out-of-line landing} below \place{the north bridge}. The face was not made a tenth of them. The waiting was not called a sale; a sale wants a loaf, a slate or a figure passed between two hands, and nothing had passed. The men in the back room were not made witnesses; witnesses stand where a thing can be asked of them, and these had gone in with their heads down and kept to their low voices. The clean-voice speaker stayed taken out of his own sentences on a place of his own and was not made a tenth hand of, for a clean voice is not a hand. Nine hands stayed nine.

He kept the face at the edge, the promise under the wheel-shed and the degree \person{Tarn}'s head had gone toward the landing on Ilmen 3 on three places with a finger between every pair, and did not lay them together to make a cause. The promise went on gathering against \lore{the burned-slate word} at five and the four directions out of Slate Row, a betrayal waiting to be called, neither given nor withdrawn, and neither it nor the valuable ear was paid. Tarn's term stayed ended at \place{the kindling} with the loan uncalled, the \emph{Seed 16} breath unspent, the ninth stopping standing with no tenth, and the degree held as a shape found where put and not repeated as a habit, a way, a rule or a danger come due. A doctrine is an account that has found a payer, and no payer stood under any of the three. Two likenesses stood up side by side will always want a man put under both; he had learned to leave them standing side by side with the space between them kept as a space.

The surgeon read the waiting as he would read any thing in a field: a man standing where no work was going on, exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air. He did not diagnose a man from the shape of his hands. Soft hands mean a man who sits at a table, and that is all they mean; a diagnosis wants a history, an exposure, a hand that can be laid on a vessel, and none of these stood in the yard. He did not give the patience a name. Patience is what a man's face does when nobody has asked him to move, and a name stood over it would be a payer stood under it. A face on a tray is a face on a tray and not a cause. Under that clean reading, louder, the infant wanted the crust lifted two inches, the thread at his mouth wiped and a hand on the pot board's handle, and got none of the three, and the wanting and the having-not went on being the work the way a wound goes on being a wound under a dressing that does not close it. He judged neither register. It was a kind of triage and not a quiet mind.

\scenebreak

The morning's work came down to the hearth and held its grammar beat for beat. She came to the coals again and gave them their instruction first. “It's the thaw,” she said, and turned them with the back of a broken pot. She gave the small intake, the hold and the letting-go toward no opening, with neither gap figured and neither made to stand for a number, and kept her finger along the top of the shelf so the charcoal tally underneath stayed unread. Her lips moved on the two gaps in the loaf row and gave nobody a figure. One glance went to the clay jar cold under lead glaze beyond \place{the pot board}, and her hand started toward it and shut half over nothing, the little-door burn on the back of her left hand closing on air. She lifted the twice-mended pot thumb-first where the tighter wire crossed the crack, left the hairline showing between the old-brass staple and the new binding, and said both proverbs to the pot and the fire and never to a person. Repair was kept apart from restoration as a surgeon keeps a graft apart from a cure: a restored thing hides the hand that handled it, and a mended thing leaves that hand showing. She turned the dressed grain under wool with a touch lighter than a woman would use on a sleeping child and with no figure over it and no handful lifted, and set the pot rim two inches beyond the reaching hand with a half-beat and no look toward \place{the bed}. The instruction went to the wool and the pot and the fire and the banked fire and the river wind, and never to the basket.

Through the finger's-width door the river kept its note of \lore{water over water} under \lore[the winter skin]{the river's winter skin}, a low thing over a lower thing, nearer at the going of the water than at its coming, a covering put on by the cold and taken off by the cold, found where put and not spent on a tend-day. The sound came from under the covering and not from the covering, and he gave it no source, for a source is an account that has found a payer and none stood beyond the door. Under the floor \lore{the carried fire} gave four low pulls with the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer without a name for four hundred days now, and nobody lifted the floor to supply what was wanting below it. \person{Garl Vail}'s name was unspoken the same number, and the fire and the man stood side by side in the house's keeping without becoming one keeping. The ridge at the Vail corner lay where it lay and she gave no answer about it to any neighbour; the called-gone grey stayed on its own place beside the left-in-place ridge, with a finger between.

The file came out on \place{the towel} at the head of the bed as instruments come out between cases and before the next is called, and he went over the field by the place of each thing and left every gap a gap. The crust's gap with its corner standing a hair off the wool; the shelf's two gaps; the gone pan's round ring; the field's empty place; the keeping at the fold found where put and still a finding; the told bell's gap in its square beside the heard bell, four, four and a gap in two places and a gap in a third, three counts in three places staying three because agreeing any two would want a payer put under both and none stood; the silence after the name from \place{Weighing} on \lore{the gap tray} beside a clean voice's missing name and the empty name the neighbours had left in the story of \place{the Remembering}, each with a finger between; the neighbour's called-gone grey beside the left-in-place ridge; \lore{the held breath} at the knife-sill and \lore{the thumb's resting} found where put; \lore{the handful} let fall back at the rack end on Bone 22 as one object and not made again; the promise under the wheel-shed, the degree held as a shape, and the man at the yard's edge on a place of its own with a finger between every pair. Each lay where the morning had left it, advanced and not paid, and none stood up and called itself a doctrine. \lore{the three coverings} and the river's winter skin stayed found where put and unspent; the cold foot below the sill stayed uncured and \lore{the second chill} kept its withheld cause, carried and not spent on an ordinary morning.

Answer three hundred and seventy-five was filed exact, complete, worth nothing at two inches of air, a restraint and not a payment borrowing no house count and supplying no payer, laid across neither the twelve readings, the bell's gap, the flat knock, the child's grey fingertips, the mother's cup, the covered grain, the handful, the thumb's resting, the held breath, the two bites of iron, the river's skin, the widow's mark, the fourth direction, the clerk's inward slate, the promise, the degree, the face at the edge nor the keeping at the fold. The answers stood on the sixth place and not one of them was a doctrine, and three hundred and seventy-five stood since the first. The count came to four hundred exact, six months and two hundred and twenty days told by nobody who would have known what a number counted. The carried fire's fifth was unpaid the same number and its payer unnamed the same number; Garl's name was unspoken the same number, and the two stood side by side without becoming one. Nothing had been opened, joined, named, called, paid, forgiven or ended; no slate had been opened within reach of the basket, no figure given for the boy, no sale made of the waiting, no witness made of the men, no door drawn across the tend, and the year's first arithmetic waited four mornings off where it had been left.

The hand outside the wool came straight down and laid itself flat against the fold as a man puts away a tool where the next hand will find it without looking. The man at the edge of the yard stood with his hands loose and no word passing, and Sella's hand shut half over nothing beyond the pot board, the little-door burn closing on air. Answer three hundred and seventy-five lay flat on the fold with the crust's corner a hair off the wool and the thread frozen and unwiped, the count standing at four hundred exact and told by nobody, and four low pulls breathing below the floor with the fifth unsupplied.
