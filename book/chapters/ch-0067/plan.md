  2. Carry the count from 251 to **252**; six months and seventy-one days to six months and seventy-two; nobody told. Fire's fifth unpaid 252 days.   3. Three counts in three places — kiln's four low pulls and missing fifth under the floor, river's water-over-water through the finger's-width door, boards' four nails, four and a thumb-width knot. None rises into another; agreeing two would want a payer and none stands to any.

  7. At the kiln mouth: pot rim set out, handle two inches beyond the reaching hand, half-beat at setting, no look toward the bed; finger on top of the shelf's front edge so the charcoal stays unread; one glance at the clay jar, hand shut half over nothing.

## How the final moment pulls into Chapter 68

The last image is not a revelation but an instrument put away: the hand comes down on the river's next low note, answer eighty-four is filed beside eighty-three, and the file lies open and worth nothing. The count has reached **254** and the fire's fifth is still unpaid, so the only thing that can move forward is the count itself. Forward, not closed.
