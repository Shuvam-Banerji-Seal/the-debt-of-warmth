## Chronicle

For three days the house at Low Kiln kept its grammar and the boy beneath its roof kept his count, from his two hundred and fifty-second day, six months and seventy-two days, to his two hundred and fifty-fourth, six months and seventy-four days, and nobody said so. The carried fire answered four pulls under the floor and kept the silence where the fifth should stand, its payer nameless; the river and the boards' four nails, four and a knot, kept their own counts beside it. Three counts in three places, and he would not agree any two, for agreeing them would want a payer and none stood to any.

He inventoried his body as a surgeon inventories a wound: one stiff brown-edged crust two inches from his ear, its empty place unnamed and no third; the thread frozen and unwiped; cold at cheek, temple and the bare wrist-joint; the afterdrop waiting past a warming nobody would perform. He knew the face of a man going cold and wanted the crust. Sella Vail went to the fire before the basket each morning, told it the thaw, turned the coals with a broken pot, said *The house will keep it*, counted toward no opening, kept her finger above the charcoal, and set a pot handle two inches beyond the reaching hand. The widow's mark she touched once before the kiln on Smoke 1 and nowhere since.

The dressed grain was spread, turned, gathered by the handful and laid flat, changed in state and given no figure. Ilva at the well answered *I keep the count, not the account*, her notched strip unread in her sleeve. Tarn told the kindling *Six counted. The next I don't count* and stopped the ear-sentence at the word that would call the loan, the ninth stopping, the seed-breath unspent. Jorra told a pot *You'll do*, knew its note three turns ahead and withheld it, and set his bent finger in the cold damper ahead of Sella's unfinished sentence. A pot set down wrong at the racks gave its high thin note before its time; the woman swept around it, and the boy filed it beside the three sayings and made no fourth likeness and no cause. The question about the row did not come a sixth time and stood at five askings with no witness.

Six times he put a question to the hand, and six times it answered --- down at once on the river's note, down before a borrowed count was spent, held to the river's own interval, held one rhythm through a little silence, and twice crossing two rhythms across a wall that does not touch, remembered and not made again. Eighty-four honest answers stood on the sixth place, each an object and not a power. He turned the file out at the close and found it exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air; the count went forward into Smoke 16, a day that had not earned its heading.
