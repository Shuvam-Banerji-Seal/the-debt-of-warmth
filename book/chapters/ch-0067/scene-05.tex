\daymark{254}{Smoke 15, Fifthday, Y.H. 813}

The dark went on the way a banked fire goes on, held under ash and giving less than it kept. \person[Corin]{the boy} lay on \place{the bed} at the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf} under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof in \place{Low Kiln}, with the fold at his ear and the hand where he had let it lie the night before, small and soft-nailed and going about its own animal business. Two hundred and fifty-four had come down out of the close without a sound, exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in \place{Sarn}, \place{Sill} or \place{Grath}; six months and seventy-four days told by nobody. He did not hold the number the way a man holds a thing he expects to be handed back. He held it the way an instrument is held in its case, sharp by sharp, and the case had opened in the night and the count had gone forward into this day and no voice under any roof had said so. The wanting of a number went on under the not-expecting the way a coal goes on under ash. Under the floor \lore{the carried fire} kept its fifth unpaid two hundred and fifty-four days, and its payer had no name, and the silence where the fifth should have stood had the shape of a held breath with no chest under it to hold it.

The smells came in their old order, the near before the far: hot clay under tallow, wet wool under that, and under both the sour mineral breath of \lore{Ash} in the unswept corner the house called \lore{the thaw}. The oiled paper at the window hung slack in its frame and let the cold come through in a slow sheet that found the wall and stopped where the wall was, the way water finds a bank. There was a small sound in the wool when he breathed, small and animal and entirely his own. The first sound from outside himself was not a bell. It was the kiln drawing in fours under the floor, pull, pull, pull, pull, and the silence after the fourth. Smoke 15 was that morning's work and no market had been brought into the small hours; there was no Cross and no bell, for a Fifthday keeps its own quiet. The bell that would go on the tenth of the next tend, four, four and a gap, had sounded in the square two mornings before and had stayed in the square; it was not heard here and was not borrowed. Smoke 20 (Day 259), the second Account-day and market of the second firing, stood five mornings beyond the close, a date and not a day, and was not brought early into a stretch of dark that had not yet earned its full light. A day is a small vessel, and a day not yet lived will not be poured into one that is.

The inventory came in the order the facts arrived and not in the order he would have chosen. One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from his ear, gone hard in the dry warmth, and the empty place beside it kept its name to itself; no third had come to lie down and make the row come out even, for a third entering a fold where two had stood would be the beginning of an account and this house gave without entering. The thread of drool at his mouth had frozen in the dry heat and was unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. Cold had come to cheek, temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, and had stopped there as if a line had been written out for it to that length and no farther; it neither went further nor went away. The afterdrop waited beyond all three, past a warming nobody in this house would perform. He knew the severe face cold wears, knew that a cold patient cannot clot, knew that the warming, if ever it came, must come from outside and slowly and would seem to worsen before it mended. He knew all of it and wanted the crust. Both were true, and the small hours made the second no quieter. The surgeon took the facts in their old order and entered none of them, not even in the place where he kept the count. \lore{the widow's mark} hung where it hung on its cord at a throat that was not his, touched once before the kiln on Smoke 1, held one breath, let go, and touched at no shelf, well, rack, road, basket, afternoon or dusk since; the one opening before Smoke 1 lay behind the household and was not looked at again, had no lesson made of it and no payer named under it.

Through the finger's-width door the river kept its note of \lore{water over water}, a low thing going over a lower thing and never counted among the hearings; under the floor the carried fire gave four low pulls and a gap; overhead \place{the boards} kept four nails on the near board, four on the next, and a thumb-width knot where the fifth nail should have stood. Three counts in three places, and none rose into another, for agreeing any two would want a payer put under both and no payer stood to any. Six hearings of \lore{the full note} lay beside and never among, five from four sources and the sixth, the small flat knock out of a woman as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go; no seventh had come and none had been invented to fill the empty place, for an empty place kept empty is a finding and a finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged. The bell's four, four and a gap stayed where it had sounded, beside the told bell and not folded into the three. The told figure stayed beside the paid figure, and Smoke's first arithmetic, gone up \place{the clay road} in a locked box two mornings before, answered nothing here; a thing not priced is not therefore free but a thing whose payer has not been named.

\scenebreak

Before first grey the house stirred the way a pot stirs when a hand goes to it in the dark, without a word and without a decision. \person{Sella Vail} went to \place{the hearth} before she went to the basket. She told the fire the thaw, turned the coals with the back of a broken pot and said \emph{The house will keep it} where another woman would have said \emph{I}. She stood a moment with the back of the pot in her hand and counted under her breath toward no opening --- the small intake, the hold, the letting-go --- and pushed the coal-black hair back with the wrist that carried the burn, so the one grey streak showed pale off the coals and the burn shaped like a little door closed on nothing and opened again to the work. The fire answered four low pulls and kept its fifth where it had always kept it, and she fed it as if it had given her no reason to count what it lacked. When she spoke it was to the fire, the pot, the wool and the weather, and never to the basket; the boy had learned long ago to hear the instruction go past him the way a man hears water go over a stone that lies where a stone lies.

At \place{the drying racks} the dressed grain came out from under its wool for damp. She put her fingers into it to the knuckle, felt the cold that had settled in it overnight, spread it thin on the flat stone to finish, turned it with the back of a hand, gathered it by the handful and set the fold flat again. It had changed state and gained no figure. The arm that carried it knew weight and not cost, and the count of it lived in glances and handfuls and in what the arm remembered of yesterday's weight, entered on no slate and laid across none of the absences. The figure spoken over it at the first Account-day had gone up the road without knowing what it cost, and the space between that figure and the grain's price had no signature; she did not put one there and the boy did not put one there for her. A figure read over a covered thing knows the cover and not the thing under it. He watched the handfuls go out and come back and the fold go flat, and the work was exact and unfigured and of no use to him, and that was enough.

At the loaf shelf she moved her lips along the row with its two gaps and gave nobody a figure, the way a woman runs a thumb along a hem to find the tear and does not say what she found. Her finger stayed on top of the shelf's front edge, not beneath it, so the charcoal tally under the board stayed unread and uncounted, a mark kept where nobody thinks to look above a row kept badly on top; a mark nobody reads is still a mark kept, and a mark read wants a figure put under it, and a figure wants a payer. Her eye went once to the clay jar cold under its glaze on the wall and her hand shut half over nothing and came away; she did not touch the jar and did not say what it held. Then she set a pot rim out at \place{the kiln mouth} with its handle two inches beyond the reaching hand, with a half-beat at the setting that was neither a look toward the bed nor a look away from it. Two inches of air was the whole distance and the hand could not close it; the setting went on being a setting and not a kindness entered. Two inches, a mark above the edge, a hand shut on nothing: three keepings in one room that did not touch one another, and the boy filed them where they were without making an account of the three.

A neighbour come up from the strips with a pot hitched against her hip asked what the keeping and the storing would take now the grain was under wool, and Sella sent the question to the pot and the weather with nothing priced. \emph{Ask the pot what a wet thing costs set down there. The house has said it.} She carried the unanswered question away level so nothing slopped, and the weather answered first and the price of barley after, and no row was named and no witness stood. Then a man come down the clay road with dried pale scurf of mud on his coat skirt brought a number up out of a fist toward \place{the yard well}, looked at it, and put it back into his sleeve, so that the figure arrived nowhere. It was not a missing figure; a sleeve is no slate and a figure kept is not a figure lost. He read the fist as a man reads a chart turned face-down --- the shape of the paper, not the writing under it --- and entered neither the shape nor the writing, and the morning went on around him at the hearth and the racks and the kiln mouth.

\scenebreak

In the hour when the wall held what the morning had given it and the yard had not yet given it back, the question about the row did not come over the boards. It had come five times and stood where it stood, and the boy did not ask it a sixth time to make a row of askings come out even. Five askings stood on the seventh place with a finger between each pair: Grain 21, Grain 23, Grain 26, Grain 29, and the afternoon of Smoke 8, each in a voice with no owner, no room, no hand at the kiln and no cause, low into the boards and not into the basket. Each time the weather had answered first and the price of barley after it, and each time the voice had gone off up the terrace and the row had stood where it stood with nobody leaning in to take the answer down. There was no witness in five askings, and there was not one witness in them; a witness stands somewhere and can be asked, and none stood and none could be asked.

He had opened the fifth asking as a surgeon opens a wound and found no payer at the bottom of it, and he did not open a sixth to see whether the wound had changed its mind. Five askings were not a hearing of the full note and did not make a seventh of it. They were not a fourth shaping of the pot saying, which was a proverb and not a question and stood on its own place with three companions; they were not a fifth telling of the burned slate, which came as a direction over the boards from a quarter league off, and the quarter league was the whole of the difference between the two trays; they were not a third telling of the counting room shut in the war, which stood at two and did not become three. They were not a cause and not a name and not a row named. A row is a place, a place wants a witness, a witness wants a day when somebody stands somewhere and can be asked, and until such a day the askings lay on their seventh place as five objects with a finger between each pair, held toward the far-off day when a witness might stand somewhere and be asked, which was not this day and could not be brought into it.

The three counts stayed three. The carried fire kept its four low pulls under the floor and the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer unnamed two hundred and fifty-four days, and neither the river's note through its finger's-width door nor the boards' four, four and a knot was folded into it; agreeing two of the three would want a payer put under both and none stood to any. The bell's four, four and a gap stayed in the square beside the told bell, and the told figure stayed beside the paid figure. Smoke's first arithmetic, locked in \person{Berr Clay}'s box and gone up the clay road two mornings before, answered nothing for the man carried out on Day 188 and nothing for the hand that went in on Seed 15 and did not come out; the man and the hand kept their separate places with a finger between them, and no figure was laid across either. A thing not priced is not therefore free but a thing whose payer has not been named, and the silence under the floor had kept its own counsel for two hundred and fifty-four days without asking the house to enter it.

\scenebreak

Then he put the fifth question of the chapter to his hand. There was no ceremony in the asking and no answer he expected; he had learned to ask the way a man tests a tool before he trusts it, with the question small enough that a no would cost him nothing. In the room that does not touch his, Sella had counted under her breath toward no opening --- the small intake, the hold, the letting-go --- and the little silence after the count had ended lay where she had left it, an interval with its own edges. The hand outside the wool went up an inch and took that borrowed count and the silence after it, and held them; then it crossed the two against the river's own low note through the finger's-width door, and the crossing was in the inch and nowhere else, because the wall did not touch the room of the fire and the river did not come into the house. He had done this once before and filed the doing as an object; the memory of it was no warrant for a change made again, and the hand did not make one. It was two rhythms crossed across a wall that does not touch, remembered and not done anew, not a reach, not a miss entered as a finding, not a hearing of the full note, not a seventh of it, not a tenth stopping, not a payment, not one of the held changes, not a key, a power or a rule. When the river's next low note came the hand came down on it and lay back against the fold. It was answer eighty-three, filed on the sixth place beside eighty-two with no payer named, an object among objects, exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air.

The hand came down and lay where the next hand would find it without looking. It had crossed two rhythms in the inch and come down on the river's next low note, and the crossing stayed remembered and was not made again; the file grew by one honest object and nothing more. Under the floor the carried fire stopped at four and the silence where the fifth should have stood stood where it stood, its payer unnamed two hundred and fifty-four days. Two hundred and fifty-four stood exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in \place{Sarn}, \place{Sill} or \place{Grath}; six months and seventy-four days were told by nobody. The count went forward into the rest of Smoke 15, a day that had earned its heading and would be lived to its end.
