\daymark{253}{Smoke 14, Fourthday, Y.H. 813}

The dark had not thinned and the house kept its breathing under the floor, four low pulls and the silence where the fifth should have stood. \person[Corin]{the boy} lay on \place{the bed} at the low angle of \place{the loaf shelf} under \place{the Vail kiln-house} roof in \place{Low Kiln}, with the fold at his ear and the hand where he had let it lie, small and soft-nailed and going about its own animal business. Two hundred and fifty-three had come down out of the close without a sound, exact, complete, unspent and spoken by nobody in \place{Sarn}, \place{Sill} or \place{Grath}; six months and seventy-three days told by nobody. He did not hold the number as a man holds a thing he expects to be handed back. He held it the way an instrument is held in its case, sharp by sharp, and the case had opened in the night and the count had gone forward into this day and nobody under any roof had said so. The wanting of a number went on under the not-expecting the way a coal goes on under ash. Under the floor \lore{the carried fire} kept its fifth unpaid two hundred and fifty-three days, and its payer had no name, and the silence where the fifth should have stood had the shape of a held breath with no chest under it to hold.

The smells came in their old order, the near before the far: hot clay under tallow, wet wool under that, and under both the sour mineral breath of \lore{Ash} in the unswept corner the house called \lore{the thaw}. The oiled paper at the window hung slack in its frame and let the cold come through in a slow sheet that found the wall and stopped where the wall was, the way water finds a bank. There was a small sound in the wool when he breathed, small and animal and entirely his own. The first sound from outside himself was not a bell. It was the kiln drawing in fours under the floor, pull, pull, pull, pull, and the silence after the fourth. Smoke 14 was that morning's work and no market had been brought into the small hours; there was no Cross and no bell. The bell that would go on the tenth of the next tend, four, four and a gap, was not heard here and was not borrowed. Smoke 20 (Day 259), the second Account-day, stood six mornings beyond the close, a date and not a day, and was not brought early into a stretch of dark that had not yet earned its full light. A day is a small vessel, and a day not yet lived will not be poured into one that is.

Through the finger's-width door the river kept its note of \lore{water over water}, a low thing going over a lower thing and never counted among the hearings; under the floor the carried fire gave four low pulls and a gap; overhead \place{the boards} kept four nails on the near board, four on the next, and a thumb-width knot where the fifth nail should have stood. Three counts in three places, and none rose into another, for agreeing any two would want a payer put under both and no payer stood to any. Six hearings of \lore{the full note} lay beside and never among, five from four sources and the sixth, the small flat knock out of a woman as the men lifted her, filed where a thing out of a person must go; no seventh had come and none had been invented to fill the empty place, for an empty place kept empty is a finding and a finding filled to make a row come out even is a finding forged. The bell's four, four and a gap stayed in the square beside the told bell and was not folded into the three. The told figure stayed beside the paid figure, and Smoke's first arithmetic, gone up \place{the clay road} in a locked box two mornings before, answered nothing here.

The inventory came as it always came, in the order the facts arrived and not in the order he would have chosen. One stiff leathery brown-edged barley crust lay two inches from his ear, gone hard in the dry warmth, and the empty place beside it kept its name to itself; no third had come to lie down and make the row come out even. The thread of drool at his mouth had frozen in the dry heat and was unwiped, because nobody under any roof had ever been given an instruction about the thread and a basket set down is a pot set down and left. Cold had come to cheek, temple and the small bare joint of the wrist where the wool rode back, and had stopped there as if a line had been written out for it to that length and no farther; it neither went further nor went away. The afterdrop waited beyond all three, past a warming nobody in this house would perform. He knew the severe face cold wears, knew a cold patient cannot clot, knew that the warming, if it ever came, must come from outside and slowly and would seem to worsen before it mended. He knew all of it and wanted the crust. Both were true and the small hours made the second no quieter. The surgeon took the facts in their old order and entered none of them, not even in the place where he kept the count. \lore{the widow's mark} hung where it hung on its cord at a throat that was not his, touched once before the kiln on Smoke 1 and at no shelf, well, rack, road, basket, afternoon or dusk since; the one opening before Smoke 1 lay behind the household and was not looked at again and had no lesson made of it and no payer named under it.

\scenebreak

Before first grey the house stirred the way a pot stirs when a hand goes to it in the dark, without a word and without a decision. \person{Sella Vail} went to \place{the hearth} before she went to the basket. She told the fire the thaw, turned the coals with the back of a broken pot and said \emph{The house will keep it} where another woman would have said \emph{I}. She stood a moment with the back of the pot in her hand and counted under her breath toward no opening --- the small intake, the hold, the letting-go --- and pushed the coal-black hair back with the wrist that carried the burn, so the one grey streak showed pale off the coals and the burn shaped like a little door closed on nothing and opened again to the work. The fire answered four low pulls and kept its fifth where it had always kept it, and she fed it as if it had given her no reason to count what it lacked. When she spoke it was to the fire, the pot, the wool and the weather, and never to the basket; the boy had learned long ago to hear the instruction go past him the way a man hears water go over a stone that lies where a stone lies.

At \place{the drying racks} the dressed grain came out from under its wool for damp. She put her fingers into it to the knuckle, felt the cold that had settled in it overnight, spread it thin on the flat stone to finish, turned it with the back of a hand, gathered it by the handful and set the fold flat again. It had changed state and gained no figure. The arm that carried it knew weight and not cost, and the count of it lived in glances and handfuls and in what the arm remembered of yesterday's weight, entered on no slate and laid across none of the absences. The figure spoken over it at the first Account-day had gone up the road without knowing what it cost, and the space between that figure and the grain's price had no signature; she did not put one there and the boy did not put one there for her. A figure read over a covered thing knows the cover and not the thing under it, and a thing not priced is not therefore free but a thing whose payer has not been named. He watched the handfuls go out and come back and the fold go flat, and the work was exact and unfigured and of no use to him, and that was enough.

At \place{the loaf shelf} she moved her lips along the row with its two gaps and gave nobody a figure, the way a woman runs a thumb along a hem to find the tear and does not say what she found. Her finger stayed on top of the shelf's front edge, not beneath it, so the charcoal tally under the board stayed unread and uncounted, a mark kept where nobody thinks to look above a row kept badly on top. A mark nobody reads is still a mark kept, and a mark read wants a figure put under it, and a figure wants a payer. Her eye went once to the clay jar cold under its glaze on the wall and her hand shut half over nothing and came away; she did not touch the jar and did not say what it held. Then she set a pot rim out at \place{the kiln mouth} with its handle two inches beyond the reaching hand, with a half-beat at the setting that was neither a look toward the bed nor a look away from it. Two inches of air was the whole distance and the hand could not close it; the setting went on being a setting and not a kindness entered. Two inches, a mark above the edge, a hand shut on nothing: three keepings in one room that did not touch one another, and the boy filed them where they were without making an account of the three.

A neighbour come up from the strips with a pot hitched against her hip asked what the keeping and the storing would take now the grain was under wool, and Sella sent the question to the pot and the weather with nothing priced. \emph{Ask the pot what a wet thing costs set down there. The house has said it.} She carried the unanswered question away level so nothing slopped, and the weather answered first and the price of barley after, and no row was named and no witness stood. Then a man come down \place{the clay road} with dried pale scurf of mud on his coat skirt brought a number up out of a fist toward \place{the yard well}, looked at it, and put it back into his sleeve, so that the figure arrived nowhere. It was not a missing figure; a sleeve is no slate and a figure kept is not a figure lost. He read the fist as a man reads a chart turned face-down --- the shape of the paper, not the writing under it --- and entered neither the shape nor the writing, and the morning went on around him at the hearth and the racks and the kiln mouth.

\scenebreak

Under the low angle of the racks, in the hour when the wall held what the morning had given it and the yard had not yet given it back, a woman come to turn her pots set one down wrong. She had carried it in her shadow, the way the yard carried everything that must not take the wall's heat too soon, and she set it out of the shadow and near the warm side of the wall, and the pot gave its note before its time. It was a high thin note, small and bright and early, nothing like the full note a sound vessel gives under the knuckle and nothing like the flat knock of a thing cracked all the way through. It was not the seventh of the full note, and it was not the small flat knock out of a woman as the men lifted her. It was a pot set down wrong giving its note before its time, and the woman heard it and swept around it with her broom the way the yard went around the clean dark line, and said something practical about the \lore{slip}. \emph{Don't set that where the wall's warm. You'll have the note off it before it's fired. And that's slip on your hands --- wipe it, or it'll show in the pot.} She did not look toward the basket and the boy did not look toward her face; the edge went into her sweeping and the morning went on.

The boy filed the note on a place of its own beside the three sayings, advanced and not paid: that a pot singing before its time generally sings itself out early; that the pot which sang early last month had not been heard from since; that every row goes quiet come the thaw. He made no fourth likeness of them and made no cause of any of them. No pot was lost in the yard that morning, and no name was given to what ailed the man carried out on Day 188, and the sweet edge on the wrong wind was not joined to the note. He had once held a clamp in a hand and known from the resistance under his fingers when a vessel would take and when it would tear; here he had heard a note before its time and had put it where it belonged, on its own tray, with a finger between it and the hearings. An almost-note can be filed without being spent. The household took the edge into its sweeping and went on; the boy took it into the file and went on; neither took it into an account, because an account wants a payer and none stood under the note.

\scenebreak

Toward the boards a wrong wind came off \place{High Kiln}, bringing with it the sweet green edge of \lore{lead glaze}, the taste of a thing heated with a metal that gives off a sweetness and a sickness together. It went over \place{the boards}, touched the fold at his ear, and went out again on the wind that brought it, a direction arrived nowhere and not a second edge paid. He knew the taste of lead in a body the way another man knows the smell of rain: the sweetish green of it, the line at the gum, the short breath with the pause at its top, the tremor in the wrist, the care with which a man sets a dipper down when his fingers have stopped telling him where it is. All of it was exact and complete and worth nothing at two inches of air. He did not join the wind to the pot's early note or to the sayings or to the man carried out on Day 188 or to the pale line kept on its own place; a direction that arrives nowhere is still a direction kept, and the keeping of it did not ask him to spend it.

At the fire Sella told it the thaw and fed it, and the fire answered four low pulls and kept the silence where the fifth should have stood, its payer without a name for two hundred and fifty-three days. She stood a moment with the back of the broken pot in her hand and counted under her breath toward no opening, and the little-door burn closed on nothing and opened again to the work. The boy kept the three counts in three places and did not agree any two of them.

Then he put the third question of the chapter to his hand. There was no ceremony in the asking and no answer he expected; he had learned to ask the way a man tests a tool before he trusts it, with the question small enough that a no would cost him nothing. In the room that does not touch his there was no breathing number to borrow and no fire-sitter standing over the coals; the only rhythm in the house that he had not made himself was the river's own note through the finger's-width door, a low thing going over a lower thing at its own interval. He held the hand outside the wool to that interval and took no count from the house to steady it against. There was no small intake borrowed, no held breath of a woman counting toward no opening, no little silence after a count that had ended somewhere else; there was only the river going over itself and the hand going up an inch and waiting for the river's own next low note. He kept to the interval and did not cross it with anything. When the note came the hand came down on it and lay back against the fold. It was not a longer reach, a miss entered as a finding, a hearing of the full note, a seventh of it, a tenth stopping, a payment, one of the held changes, a key, a power or a rule. It was answer eighty-one, filed on the sixth place beside eighty with no payer named, an object among objects, exact, complete and worth nothing at two inches of air. He had kept the hand to the river's own interval because there was no count in the house to borrow, and he would not ask the whole of that to stand for more. The hand came down and lay where the next hand would find it without looking, and the count went forward into the rest of Smoke 14, a day that had earned its heading and would be lived to its end.
