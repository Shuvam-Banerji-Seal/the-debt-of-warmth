## Chronicle

On the morning after the blessing Sella Vail set the boy below the north bridge as a pot is set, out of the draught, the fold at his chin. A brine-gull went off upriver with a coal from a banked fire not yet cold, and the warmth went into no account; the boy, forty-five days old, kept the subtraction nobody else would. Orra Weir came down with eels, and the knot at her waist she could not account for had a shape he could now read --- a double turn, tail tucked, tied left over right, set with the weight of a thumb --- though he had no name for it and the word she had said into his basket was not said again. She watched; he could do nothing.

Under the Vail roof that evening Sella went around the month in loaves and glances, giving every instruction to the fire, the pot and the wool and saying the house where another woman would say I. She touched the widow's mark once before the kiln opened and not at the bank. Tarn's knife lay on its sill with six years counted and the seventh held back; his broom went over the collector's road without turning and no collector came, because a late thaw puts off the paying. Jorra's bent finger went to the damper before she had finished, and he told a pot it would do.

On Ilmen 17 Alda Reed came short with soap-and-milk and birth-slates, touched the tin inside her bodice unopened, pressed the fold too long and laid exact dates where a wonder should stand; warm and breathing, she said, which was exact and not true. In the afternoon the wind turned off the water and the smell of barley did not come. One crust lay where two had lain --- the smoky one Mira had put there weeks before, the warmth he had most wanted and could never reach --- gone, and nobody said who had taken it or that it was gone. He kept the empty place as he kept a wound, without dressing it in a cause.

On Ilmen 18 frost wrote in white fronds on the well-lip where the Warm had lately gone out, and by noon the yard called it weather. A number went from fist to mouth to shoulder and arrived nowhere; Berr Clay wiped his thumb twice before touching nothing, said a number goes into a slate, not into a well, and went off with his box still locked. Word came up that the clerk sat still at the Fishstairs for a measure nobody had called. A weir woman said a board in the plank walk would not carry a man when the river rose. At dusk Sella set him on his bed, forty-seven days old, one crust in the fold and an empty place beside it, the fire keeping four, four and a gap. Nothing had been joined, paid or explained. The count went on.

## Continuity

- **Exact final moment.** The boy lies on his bed under the Vail roof, forty-seven days old, with one crust in the fold where two had lain and the empty place at his ear beside it, carried home at dusk from the yard well. Sella set him there, not in the Debt Loft, and put the fold right without looking at his face. Behind him the carried fire keeps under blind ash and the back of a broken pot, warming the boards by degrees and missing one breath in every four; the clean dark line where the Ash turned is unswept; Garl's coat and hollow seat keep their places. His hand outside the wool goes up an inch toward the warmth and comes down. Nothing has been joined, paid, explained or solved. The count goes on.
- **Day and time at the end.** Day 47, Ilmen 18, Y.H. 813, Eighthday, evening under the Vail roof, after the frost has gone off the well-lip and the day's figures have gone into nobody's slate. Three lived days, one dated entry each: Day 45 (Ilmen 16, Sixthday) opens below the north bridge and runs on under the roof into evening; Day 46 (Ilmen 17, Seventhday) runs from the morning fire through dusk; Day 47 (Ilmen 18, Eighthday) opens at the yard well and runs on into dusk under the roof. No recap, no skipped day, no present-tense break, no old-life voice, no memory tag. Day 44 is finished and not revisited.
- **Where everyone is now.** Corin: on his bed under the roof, carried everywhere, mute, drool frozen and unwiped, head going where it wants with the small wet sound, cold at cheek–temple–wrist, hand up an inch and down. Sella Vail: at the fire and the bed, having carried him at a half-pace from every open slate, saying *the house*, giving every instruction to fire, pot and wool, never to the basket; she touched the widow's mark once before the opening on Day 45 and not since, not at a bank or a well; her private roll stays unshown and neither crust nor its absence is named by her. Tarn: broom put away, small knife back on its sill, announced to the empty yard on Days 45 and 46 and to nobody on Day 47 because the yard had already been told; six of the seven counted, the seventh held back, Ash in the knuckles no scrubbing lifts; his broom went over the collector's road twice without turning and no collector came; wants the thaw late. Jorra Vail: in from the wheel-shed with bone-coloured slip dried to old bone on both forearms and no grey past the little finger, bent finger ahead of his hand at the damper ahead of Sella's instruction, telling the pot *You'll do*; he did not say the back room was cold and did not look toward the bed. Alda Reed: came short on Day 46 with soap-and-milk and birth-slates, touched her tin once unopened, covered the flower with a date, went on giving dates after the question had stopped; offstage at the close. Berr Clay: up the yard on Day 47, red cord under his coat, green of glaze at his mouth, thumb wiped twice, slate held unopened, gone off toward High Kiln with his box locked; never looked into the basket. Mira Silt: at the far racks on Day 45 with slime dried on her sleeves and her head held right, eel-bones moving while she decided whether to lie, grey cord and second cord shown; offstage since. Orra Weir: down from the Weir on Day 45 with eels, watched the boy watching the knot, said nothing further, took the cord back upriver to the Eel House. Garl Vail: absent, kept as coat, hollow seat, clean dark line and proverbs said to the fire and pot. Dessa Silt: dead since Day 38, uncollected, stays dead.
- **Items gained, lost or used.** Gained: the readable shape of the forty-first knot (double turn, tail tucked, left over right, the weight of a thumb) without its name; the frost-bloom written on the well-lip and gone by noon; the heard-of weak board in the plank walk; the figure that went from fist to mouth to shoulder and was given to nobody; the clerk still set at the Fishstairs, filed apart. Lost: **one crust** --- the smoky barley crust Mira put into the fold on Stone 28, gone by the afternoon of Day 46, not eaten in his sight, not lifted, not named, not solved; Sella's hand, Alda's hand and Mira's hand are set on three places on a tray and are not allowed to become a cause. Also gone: a coal from a banked fire carried off by a brine-gull on Day 45, warmth out of the carried fire's account entered in no slate and missed in no account. Unchanged: the spent crust from Stone 15, stiff past smelling, uneaten and unlifted; no third crust; the small pale river stone on the abandoned bed in House Silt, unmentioned; the bench knife on its sill; the charred slate in the kiln ash; the clay jar on the wall shelf; basket and wool lent without a word; Alda's tin shut in her bodice, her notes toward a floorboard; Sella's roll unshown; Berr's box locked; Mira's two cords and Orra's knot-string unread, knots uncounted; the clean dark line left where it lies.
- **Money and figures.** No money reaches the boy. One in twelve stays printed and unchallenged and the true subtraction is unspoken; the number for the thaw goes from fist to mouth to shoulder on Day 47 and is never given; Berr keeps a figure he will not give and says a number goes into a slate, not into a well; eels still change hands six for five; the house goes around its days and stores in loaves and glances and the space where a loaf should stand, with no figure spoken; warmth given, taken and lost is entered nowhere. The word *forty* is never spoken a second time and the boy still does not know it is a count of children or that he is one of them.
- **Powers and cost.** No usable power, no rank change, no experiment, no clamp, no heresy. Cracked, low-capacity, unranked, below Thin, twelve to fifteen embers at best, with a flaw nobody has named. Counting is an old-life habit, not a gift. The ear still holds six hearings of the full note --- five from four sources (a cup across the birth room, a pot in the Vail wheel-shed through the wall, the district's slate twice at the Cross, a man giving at the Drawing) and the sixth from a person, out of Dessa as the men lifted --- beside them and never among them, and no seventh came on any of the three days. The nearly full note of Ilmen 6 stays on its own tray, advanced and not paid; the told bell stays beside and never on the heard bell; five pauses stay unjoined; the absence with the outline of a collection is filed beside the intervals and never among them; the six like rooms stay in six places. The word stays a saying on its tray, the shape of the knot beside it and the weight of a thumb apart from both. No Grathic word for the flat note, for nearly, or for a thing taken and not spoken of. Knowing the shape exactly has bought him no hand to act with.
- **Facts, lies, promises, debts.** The lie told for mercy is six days old and now curdles against a death nobody entered: Alda says *warm and breathing*, exact and not true, and goes on laying dates over it; nobody corrects her. Nobody said the crust was missing, who took it, or that it was given to the fire or the eels. No collector was called for the woman the brook took nine mornings ago, nobody found a word for the omission, and nobody made a doctrine of it; the clerk's slate and the unentered body stay two facts that do not touch. Garl's debt against the firing of tiles stays neither paid nor settled nor done with; the clean dark line is not swept, because a line left where it is is still a line kept. Tarn will not count the seventh year. Jorra's sentence about the foundling stays sealed. No new promise is made and nothing is explained.
- **Threads advanced.** T024 (the knot's shape legible, word unspoken again, the watching continued), T059 and T025 (Mira's two cords on a board beside Orra's, Nine Pots unvisited, neither knot counted), T090 and T080 (Fishstairs clerk still set for the uncalled measure, hand to slate before eyes, filed apart from Marra, the Drawing collector and the Court-table clerk), T088 (the coal the gull took, and the smoke crust gone, both unentered), T061 and T082 (six hearings stay six; nearly-full note, five pauses, told bell, absence-shaped-like-a-collection, word and knot all kept apart), T086 (first edge paid: one crust gone, unsolved), T058, T060 and T044 (unanswerable kindness become unanswerable absence; house's arithmetic in loaves and glances; gruel from the house's dish), T089, T023, T008 and T073 (tin touched once unopened; exact dates over a wonder; lie curdling; name and number unsaid), T036, T076, T009 and T013 (seventh withheld; collector's road swept without turning; no collector called; clean dark line unswept), T035 (damper moved early, bone-coloured forearms, back room unsaid), T079 (widow's mark touched once before an opening, not at a bank), T065 and T083 (four, four and a gap; hairline, higher brook, frost-bloom and missing step kept in separate places), T085 (ice going soft before its time, Singing Sill early, all called weather), T077, T041 and T047 (number withheld, speaker taken out of his own sentence, passive grammar), T071/T072 (the exact count kept and never spent).
- **Foreshadowing planted and paid.** Pays the first edge of the two-crusts thread planted on Stone 28 and carried through ten chapters: a gift entered on no slate has become an absence entered on no slate, and the reader can suspect every woman in the yard, including the girl who put the crust there. Carries the coal gone up with the bird toward the unnamed payer of the carried fire and, far off, the Hearth as a wound; carries the readable shape of the knot toward the day the cord is read as a chart and the long vigil that watches for an answer; carries the frost where the Warm has lately left, the ice going soft before its time, the higher brook, the early low note and the weak board heard-of but not trodden toward the Breaking in Silt and the day the river puts the Fishstairs out of line; carries the clerk still set and the woman still uncollected toward a standing charge on a district that has already spent its dead. Joins nothing else and explains nothing.
- **The next chapter must not forget.** Day 48, Ilmen 19, Y.H. 813, Ninthday, opens at the yard well: frost-bloom on the stone lip where the Warm has lately left, the oiled paper slack in its frame, and a neighbour who used to say the ice held now saying nothing. The boy is forty-eight days old, still carried in the basket with the fold at his chin, and his hand outside the wool goes up an inch toward the bucket and comes down. One crust is gone and nobody has said who took it; the coal is entered nowhere; the weak board at the Weir is still heard-of, not trodden; the clerk is still at the Fishstairs; no collector has been called for the woman in the brook and the omission still has no word. Keep the count six from four sources and one from a person, four, four and a gap, the nearly-full note on its own tray, the two cords and the knot-string on separate boards, the word unspoken and the trays unjoined. Nothing has been explained except a shape that can now be read and a taking nobody will name. No recognition of the mother, no name or number from Dessa, no Marra, no one in three, no explanation of the Forty, no quest, no ending. Forward, not closed.
